 One family had only 3 daughters and no boys. One day, 3 girls carried hoes to plow the ground. The third sister is young, has little strength, and can't plow the ground as fast as her older sister or second sister. She sighed and said to herself, “Alas, I'm so tired! If anyone can help me plow the land, that would be great; I would love to marry him and be his wife.” As soon as she spoke, a large frog in the field stood on its back and said, “I can help you finish plowing this area right away.” The third sister looked down. It turned out to be a frog talking. She thought it was teasing her, and then scolded, “You little frog, as long as I hit the hoe, I'll smash you to death right away. Can you still dig the ground?” But the frog smiled and said, “Girl, don't get mad, close your eyes for a while.” As soon as the three sisters heard it, they were both angry and funny. They actually closed their eyes: “I'm dependent on you. Close your eyes and see what you can do.” Strangely enough, as soon as the three sisters closed their eyes for a while, the frog immediately cleared up this area. “The ground is ready; you should marry me.” The frog repeatedly asked the three sisters to fulfill their promise and be their wives. This infuriated the three sisters; the eldest sister and second sister didn't agree. They raised their hoes and chased to smash the frog to death. But the frog was bouncing and jumping, and couldn't beat it at all; “Third sister, if you talk, you have to count; you should be my wife.”
At noon, the three sisters went home, and the frog followed them and came to their house. The frog entered the door, jumped on the beam, and said to the three sisters, “You said you wanted to marry me, go back with me. Otherwise, I could flood your house with just one sip of water.” As soon as the parents heard it, they were all furious: “There is a girl in the world marrying a frog! Get out quickly, or I'll kill your little frog with a bamboo pole!” As soon as the frog heard it, it was furious: “None of you keep your credit. Look, I'm about to spit.” It drooled half of its mouth while talking, and immediately flooded the house in half. The whole family was terrified. Only then did they learn that frogs are amazing frogs. They hurriedly asked the frog to say, “Take back the water soon. We have agreed to marry your third sister.” The frog took its big mouths one by one, and in one go, it sucked half of the room's water back into its mouth.
This time the whole family stopped talking. As a result, the three sisters put on new clothes and left the cabinet on horseback, and the frog jumped forward to guide the way. After walking for a while, they arrived at a bamboo forest. There was a stone cave in the forest, and the entrance was covered with all kinds of beautiful flowers and plants. The frog said, “When you get home, sister, close your eyes, I'll take you down.” As soon as the three sisters closed their eyes, they felt that their bodies had fluttered away, and soon it seemed like they were being pulled away. The frog opened her eyes. As soon as the three sisters opened their eyes, they were dazzling and dazzling: the tall, spacious house, all kinds of utensils, and all kinds of gold and silver treasures dazzled the three sisters.
What's even more strange is that when the frog made the three sisters close their eyes again, the frog suddenly changed and removed the frog skin. When the third sister opened her eyes again, standing in front of her was a very handsome and beautiful young man. The three sisters were surprised and happy, so they both became friends on the same day, and have lived a happy and happy life ever since.
