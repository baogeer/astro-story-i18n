The story takes place in the dormitory building of a supply and marketing cooperative on Binyang Road in the county town. At 11 a.m., a young man in jeans pried open the door to room 101. He gently pushed the door in. Hey, I was shocked. There was a kid sleeping on the floor in front of the door.
The thief was creeping out of the door, but the kid woke up. The child looked like he was only about three years old. He probably cried a lot before going to bed, so his face was stained. The kid didn't even live. When I saw him, I sweetly shouted, “Uncle, who are you looking for?”
I looked around in my jeans, and when I didn't see anyone in sight, I smiled and tentatively said, “Kid, don't you know me? I'm your dad's colleague ah, is your dad home?”
As soon as the kid heard it, he said, “Looking for dad?” Little finger, “My dad is in the bathroom.”
As soon as I heard about the jeans, there was an adult in the room. They were shocked and quickly lowered their voice and said, “Let's just go to the bathroom. Your uncle is in an emergency right now, look for him next time.” After speaking, “pedaling” ran out of the door and ran downstairs as if escaping his life.
“Fuck, disgusting!” The thief was wandering around the street, sighing, and suddenly thought: No! If adults are at home, how can they make children cry and sleep on the floor? Huh, I was almost shocked by a kid who had a milky smell and hadn't dried up. So he turned around and went upstairs quickly. After listening at the door for a while, there was no movement, so he quickly pry open the door and walked in with a big swing.
I saw that kid was sleeping on the floor again, so the jeans flew from room to room, and from this drawer to cabinet. The harvest was actually quite small; the cash alone took almost 5,000 yuan. The jean's excited eyes flashed straight through, and he thought to himself: if he wasn't insightful himself, would he still be able to eat this good fruit?
At this point, the child woke up, rubbed his eyes, and strangely asked, “Uncle, haven't you left?” The thief made a clever move and said, “I've finished the emergency and am waiting for your dad.” The kid still had a small finger and said, “My dad is in the bathroom.” The jeans “hehehe” laughed: “You little devil still lied to my uncle.” The child said, “I don't lie. Dad said lying to people is not a good kid.” Speaking of which, he climbed up from the floor, grabbed the small ball thrown on the side, and ran “dong-dong” towards the small yard behind the house. As soon as I saw that the kid had thrown the jeans into the yard to play, I quickly found a bag and put the money and stolen “loot” in it. I was about to go back and leave. Suddenly my eyes were dark and I fell to the ground.
What's going on with this? It turned out someone punched him in the back. This person is either someone else, or the child's father. His name is Lu Feng.
When I woke up in my jeans, I found that my hands were tied behind my back, and the tall Iron Tower Man was standing in front of me. I couldn't help but secretly cry out bitterness in my heart: “You, how are you...”
“Would you like to know?” Lu Feng glared at him, “Tell you, I'm the child's father. I have no intention of catching thieves today; I didn't expect you to take the initiative to deliver them to your door. I heard that recently there was a “Sun and Moon Theft,” and they commit crimes stealing things day and night. I'm afraid it's just you, dude?”
The jeans didn't squeak; that seemed to be the default. However, he was quite puzzled and asked, “Why did you show up all of a sudden?”
Lu Feng proudly patted his son's head and laughed: “I owe this to my son. Aren't you careful? When our house was being renovated, I asked someone to move the bathroom to the back of the yard. Today, my wife went to work in the morning, and my son was outside playing in the water under the faucet. I scolded him a few words. He didn't listen, so he took advantage of me to plug the bathroom on while I was outside. Did you just think my son was in the yard to play? Hehe, he opened the door for me! You need to know that when your intellect is blind, your IQ isn't as good as that of a three-year-old!”
