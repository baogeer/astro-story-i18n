Lao Geng left the city, turned a yellow clay path, and rode a short distance. As soon as the horn around him sounded, a voice shouted, “Wow, this isn't our Comrade Lao Geng!”
Lao Geng took a look. He is a big fat guy. He drives a brand-new motorcycle. He is familiar. If you think about it carefully, isn't this the corrupt director he caught himself three years ago?
Fat Man, nicknamed Zhang Fat Zi, used to be the director of the Administration for Industry and Commerce. He was reported for corruption three years ago. After investigation, he went to the Public Security Bureau and interrogated by Lao Geng and several others. Lao Geng's wrists are very stiff. He gave Zhang Fat “a few strokes”. Fat Zhang continued to proudly say, “After being in prison for two years, even though the director stopped working, I set up my own washing powder factory. Hehe, God bless, you did a good job.
