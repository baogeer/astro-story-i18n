 Wang Zhaojun is a famous historical figure with a brief history. Over the past 2,000 years, everyone in the poetry world, historical giants, and even the older generation of revolutionaries have forged an indissoluble bond with her. Among them are Du Fu, Jiang Quan, Wang Anshi, and Ma Zhiyuan. Today's people include Dong Biwu, Guo Moruo, Fan Wenlan, Jian Bozan, Cao Yu, Lu Zhenyu, and Weng Dujian. Writers chose rhymes to write poems and find words to create an image of a beautiful Han dynasty woman leaving her home country and being close to the Huns; historians sought to restore that period of historical facts buried in dust through the confusing smoke of history; the older generation of revolutionaries celebrated the historical story of ethnic integration.
The historical exploits of Shojun and Kazuchin are self-evident. At the beginning of the founding of the country, the Han dynasty met with the military and war of the Huns, which covered the sun with smoke and shook the sky with drums. “Since the war moved, I felt that the world was small.” People are in exile, idyllic hills are ruined, and there are no peaceful years on the northern border. Since Zhaojun and his family, the two sides turned Gan Ge into jade, and forged swords and hoes. On the northern border, there was a peaceful scene of “Bian City Closed, Cow and Mabune, Third Generation Police, No Dog Barking Police, Lai Shu No Battle.” The wadang fragments unearthed at the Han Tomb in Baotou in 1954, such as “Only Heqin,” “Long Live a Thousand Years,” and “Changle Weiyang,” are evidence of this period of history. Before the Qin and Han dynasties, ethnic minorities in the north were not governed by the Central Plains dynasty. “Since three generations prosper, Hu and Yue have no choice but to accept Zheng Shuo,” the frontier dynasty was only placed under the leadership of the Central Dynasty, thus laying the foundation for the reunification of the motherland. The Huns were infiltrated and influenced by Han culture because of their closeness. In order to make the son of Zhaojun born a single one, he changed the father-son succession system to a fraternal succession system, and added the word “Ruochun” before his heir title. “The Huns called filial piety. They have been close to Han since the Han dynasty surrendered. They saw that Emperor Han always had filial piety, and adored him. Up to the point where their children have recovered, the list is as follows, they are all known as Ruyi.” (“The Book of the Later Han Dynasty: The Biography of the Southern Huns”) In terms of other lifestyle customs, the Huns also deliberately imitated the Han dynasty, thus speeding up the process of national integration. It was in view of this that Dong Biwu, a revolutionary of the older generation, wrote the poem “Zhaojun has a long history of success; Hu Han and his family are highly knowledgeable.”
The magnitude of the achievements of the Han dynasty and pro-women was neither due to her level of talent, nor to her good looks; it was the strength or weakness of the Han dynasty. If the Han dynasty was powerful and had no faraway roots, women who were close to them would be respected; if the Han dynasty declined, women who were close will be snubbed. When Zhaojun was close, just when Han was strong and Hu was weak, this gave her an opportunity to succeed. Huhan Xedan, married to Zhaojun, had a terrible and bitter experience: he was the eldest son of Lao Dan Yu. He was supposed to be heir to the throne, but he was quickly promoted by Yu Xian Wang Tu Qitang, who took the lead, claiming to have taken the lead. Huhan Xie had to flee to take refuge in his wife's father Wu Zhan Mu. Holding Yanqu Danyu caused domestic discontent due to violence. Wu Chanmu took the opportunity to endorse Han Xie as a single, and attack with soldiers to seize Yanyu, seize Yanqu, and commit suicide in military defeat. At a time when the Han dynasty was full of ambition and regaining momentum, there were also people who coveted for the throne. First, the “five orders were fought,” and then the three orders were tied together. The heroes fought for the deer, and the Huns once again fell into chaos. After a fierce battle, there were only two people left, Hu Han Xie and his brother Chang Zhi. Hu Han Xie was defeated in the war. They were in a state of desperation and had no choice but to convert to the Han dynasty. When the Han dynasty slaughtered his rival Zhi Zhidan, he “rejoiced and fearful” and “wished his son-in-law Han to take care of himself.” At this time, the demonic forces of Han Han were exhausted and depended on others; if they were to do anything, they were at ease; if they betrayed Han, they were in danger. His rise and fall between the glory and shame of the Han dynasty was entirely between the thoughts of the gods of the Han dynasty. When Zhaojun is close to his family under these circumstances, how dare the entire country of the Huns slack off? It makes sense that she is revered as “Mr. Ning Hu”. Some commentators exaggerate, saying that Zhaojun stepped up and generously answered the call to complete the mission of reconciliation between China and Hungary. It seems that she was a close relative of the Han dynasty. Only she could save the mess from the past. This is an unrealistically extravagant statement!
“Huan Peiying shakes Aotsuka Moon, and the sound of pipa stops autumn in Heijiang.” As far as Zhaojun's personal experience is concerned, her act of being friendly was actually a tragedy. This is a political marriage, and as Engels said, “It is the family's interests, not individual wishes, that determine this type of marriage. Under these circumstances, how can love have the final say on marriage?” Zhaojun was nothing more than a bargaining chip for Emperor Han Yuan to take control of the Huns. There were a total of 9 women and relatives in the Han dynasty. None of them were the daughters of Heavenly Son. Not a last resort. Who would want to stay married in a foreign country? Although Zhaojun volunteered for action, it was a way of fighting back out of anger because “I can't stand guard for a few years.” There are many opinions about “Miscellaneous Notes of Xijing” that Zhaojun was unable to get the fortune of Tianzi because he refused to bribe the painter Mao Yanshou. However, Du Fu and Wang Anshi have no doubt about this, as evidenced by Du Fu's verses “Draw a picture of the province to know the spring style, ring the sky to the night soul of the Moon” and Wang Anshi's “The origin of the intention was to not be able to paint; Mao Yanshou was murdered in vain at the time.” Zhaojun lived in Qionglu, from the beautiful scenery of Jiangnan to northern Mobei, where the climate was severe and the climate was cold. He had very different customs. He didn't understand the language. Huhan Xie was more than twice her age. It was impossible for the two to meet and fall in love at first sight. Zhaojun refused and “wrote a book to seek return, and the emperor's royal decree followed nonsense.” Since there is no point in falling flowers and there is no reason to return to the mountains when the clouds pass away, Zhaojun had no choice but to cry again with tears and have two more daughters.
