 In ancient times, there was a girl named Zhu Yingtai. She was born smart and beautiful. Not only could she embroider and cut phoenixes, but she also loved to write and read. When she reached the age of 15 or 6, she wanted to study at a foreign school.
However, girls were not allowed to study at that time. What should I do? Yingtai and Ya Huan discussed a good idea: pretend to be a boy and go to school. As a result, Zhu Yingtai dressed up like a boy, and Ya Huan dressed up as a book boy. The two looked at each other. They looked quite similar, and couldn't help laughing happily.
Zhu Yingtai's father was drinking tea in the hall. Suddenly, he saw a bookboy leading a boy to salute him. He hurriedly got up and gave up his seat. He also asked the boy to honor his name. Zhu Yingtai even hid it from her father when she saw it; not to mention how happy she was. She then unpretended to reveal the truth, which surprised her father and blamed her daughter for being naughty and unruly. Zhu Yingtai took the opportunity to talk to her father about her idea of studying abroad. My father said, “How many women have gone out to study since ancient times? Even if you're pretending to be a man, there are many inconveniences about living outside.” However, Zhu Yingtai was determined to go. Her father couldn't resist her, so he had to agree.
Zhu Yingtai pretended to be a man. He looked very handsome and chic. Ya Huan pretended to be a bookcase and left home to study. After walking, they felt hot, so they went to a small pavilion on the side of the road to rest. At this point, a student and a bookboy walked along the way, and also went to the pavilion to rest their feet. They greeted each other. Only then did Zhu Yingtai learn that the student's name was Liang Shanbo, and he also studied at the Academy. Zhu Yingtai and Liang Shanbo had a very speculative conversation. The two became brothers in the pavilion. Yang Shanbo was two years older than Zhu Yingtai, so Zhu Yingtai called Liang Shanbo his brother, Liang Shanbo called Zhu Yingtai his brother, and then happily hit the road together.
Zhu Yingtai and Liang Shanbo visited the academy and met their teachers. The teacher was very happy to see these two smart and handsome teenagers studying.
The teacher arranged for them to study at the same desk. Liang Shanbo cared and cared for Zhu Yingtai like his younger brother. The two were together from morning to night and became the best friends.
Zhu Yingtai shared the same room with Liang Shanbo. In order not to let Liang Shanbo discover that she was a woman, she separated the two bookcases between their beds. She also filled the bookcase with a pot of water. She told Liang Shanbo to be honest when sleeping. If he messed around and spilled the water in the basin, she could tell the teacher to punish him severely. Therefore, Liang Shanbo always has rules, never messed around, and never found out that Zhu Yingtai was a girl.
However, the fact that Zhu Yingtai is dressed as a man has long been seen by careful teachers and daughters. The teacher called Zhu Yingtai in front of her to unravel the truth. Zhu Yingtai asked her mother to keep her secrets. The teacher agreed, and she took more careful care of this smart girl. If Zhu Yingtai had any difficulties or feelings, she would also talk to her mother.
Three years in a flash. One day, Zhu Yingtai received a letter from her family saying that her father was sick and wanted her to go home quickly. Zhu Yingtai took a leave of absence from her teacher and came back to find her teacher. She said that she and Liang Shanbo had been classmates for three years. Liang Shanbo was sincere and enthusiastic, studied hard, and she was already deeply in love with him. She handed over a jade fan pendant to her teacher, and asked her to act as a mediator. After she left, she loved Liangshan Bertie for her.
When Zhu Yingtai leaves home, Liang Shanbo must personally deliver her. The two were attached to each other along the way and were always unwilling to break up. Zhu Yingtai wanted to reveal his love to Liang Shanbo, but it was not convenient to say it directly; he had to use many examples to inspire Liang Shanbo.
When they saw a pair of geese in the river, Zhu Yingtai sang:
I came to a river in front of me. There was a pair of geese swimming in the river. The male goose swims ahead, and the female goose is called an older brother.
The honest and kind Liang Shanbo didn't understand what she meant and kept moving forward. Zhu Yingtai also sang several songs that compare the love between a man and a woman. Liang Shanbo still didn't understand it. Zhu Yingtai joked, “You really are a stupid goose!”
Zhu Yingtai also pointed to a pair of mandarin ducks in the pond and sang:
There are green lotus leaves and clear water ponds, and mandarin ducks come in pairs. If England and Taiwan were red makeup girls, brother Leung, would you like to “pair up with mandarin ducks”?
Liang Shanbo sighed and said, “Too bad you're not a girl with red makeup!”
When Zhu Yingtai saw that Liang Shanbo still didn't understand, she said, “My family has nine sisters. She and I look exactly like me. I would like to work as a mediator to make nine girls a couple with you. Would you like to?” Liang Shanbo originally loved Zhu Yingtai's talent. As soon as he heard that Jiu Mei was born exactly like her, she happily agreed.
They spent 18 miles together and arrived at the riverside, only then did they fall in love and broke up reluctantly. When they parted ways, Zhu Yingtai and Liang Shanbo agreed to go on a blind date at Zhu's house on July 7. Liang Shanbo watched Zhu Yingtai's figure on the other side of the river from afar, getting farther and farther away, and gradually became invisible.
Zhu Yingtai returned home. Her father's illness had long since improved. He changed Zhu Yingtai's costume to a girl and didn't let her go out to study. At this point, it just so happened that a rich owner with the surname Ma came to ask for a family, and the father gave Zhu Yingtai Xu to the son of the Ma family. Zhu Yingtai definitely did not agree to this family affair. She told her father that she had fallen in love with Liang Shanbo and that she had trusted her teacher to work for the media. However, the father objected, “The marriages of children have always been controlled by parents, and girls themselves are looking for men outside. What kind of words?” I really want Zhu Yingtai to get married to the Ma family.
Since Liang Shanbo said goodbye to Zhu Yingtai that day, he went back to the academy and continued to study hard. Actually, he completely forgot about going to the house to wish her family on July 7. It wasn't until her teacher brought a jade fan pendant to explain that Zhu Yingtai invited her to raise her family. Liang Shanbo suddenly realized that Zhu Yingtai was originally a woman. The nine sisters she mentioned were Zhu Yingtai herself! Liang Shanbo immediately took time off from his teacher and rushed to Zhu's house to meet Zhu Yingtai.
Liang Shanbo came to Yingtai's home and saw that Zhu Yingtai had completely recovered her appearance as a woman, and looked even more beautiful and cute. He mentioned that her teacher proposed family for them. As soon as she heard this, Zhu Yingtai burst into tears. She said, “Brother Leung, why are you here so late? My father is already pressing to give me a license to the Ma family!” As soon as Liang Shanbo heard it, he was surprised and sad, and his heart was broken. They both held their heads and cried. They swore to each other that no one could ruin their deep love and that they would stay together forever. Their cries were heard by Zhu Yingtai's father. Zhu Ying-tai rushed upstairs in anger, kicked Liang Shanbo out of the house, and took strict care of Zhu Yingtai.
When Liang Shanbo returned home, he was extremely sad. He missed Zhu Yingtai. He couldn't eat or sleep. He fell ill. His condition got worse and worse, and soon died. Before his death, he told his family that after his death, he would be buried on the side of the road leading from Zhu Jia to Ma's house.
The day for Ma's family to welcome their family has arrived. They carried the flower car to Zhu's doorstep, whistled, and had a good time. However, Zhu Yingtai was crying and crying, and did not want to get on the bus at all. Under her father's orders, many people pushed and pulled, and forced Zhu Yingtai to push the car away.
Halfway up the car, a strong wind suddenly came, and the person who raised the car couldn't move. At this point, Ya Huan told Zhu Yingtai that Liang Shanbo's tomb was in front of him. When Zhu Yingtai stepped out of the car despite being stopped by others, he must go to Liang Shanbo's tomb to pray.
Zhu Yingtai came to Liang Shanbo's tomb and cried loudly. He was in pain and didn't want to live. He threw his whole body on the tomb. In an instant, lightning thunder, wind and rain raged, and the tomb suddenly cracked a big slit. Zhu Yingtai shouted Liang Shanbo's name, and suddenly jumped into the tomb.
After a while, the rain stopped, the clouds cleared, and a rainbow appeared in the sky. I saw a pair of beautiful butterflies flying from the grave and dancing around the grave. Everyone says that this pair of butterflies is a transformation of Liang Shanbo and Zhu Yingtai. People still call this large butterfly with a black pattern, emerald green spots, and two long streamers on its tail.
