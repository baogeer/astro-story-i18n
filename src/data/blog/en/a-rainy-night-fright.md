 Late in the middle of a stormy night, there were no pedestrians on the street for a long time. A taxi had just delivered a guest and was driving alone on the way back. Suddenly, lightning struck, and the taxi driver vaguely saw a long-haired woman in a white dress reaching out to stop the car on the side of the road. Although the driver wanted to collect the car and go home, thinking about a lone woman on such a rainy night, she felt compassionate and decided to send this last guest.
After sitting in the back seat, the woman coldly said, “Cemetery.” The driver couldn't help but shiver. Although he doesn't believe in ghosts, he still feels a little fuzzy when he goes to that kind of place so late. The driver started the car with a stiff head and casually glanced at the rearview mirror. Why was there no one in the back?! The driver was shocked. Turning her head around, the woman in white sat nicely in the back seat and smiled at him, laughing very unnaturally. I didn't see anyone in the rearview mirror just now. Am I dazzled? But why is she laughing so strangely at me? The driver thought about it and unwittingly took another look at the rearview mirror, but there was still no one! Looking back quickly, the woman was indeed still sitting there, but this time she didn't laugh and glared at him. The driver didn't dare to face it; he quickly turned his head around. I heard that ghosts can't see in a mirror, is that true? While thinking about it, he looked back into the rearview mirror. There's still no one, I've seen a ghost! Suddenly, there was a thunder, he shivered all over his body and braked abruptly! Then slowly... take a look back. A flash of lightning struck him, and he could see clearly. The woman in white had a white face, had blood in her nose, and was staring at him angrily! Oops, ghosts! The driver fainted...
In the police car, the woman told the police what had happened. “I'm working the night shift today. I'm very tired, caught up with the heavy rain, and I'm in a very bad mood. I stopped this taxi after work. I live in a neighborhood next to the Martyrs' Cemetery, and since I'm not in a good mood, I just said 'cemetery'. After a while, I felt that my nose was itchy, and I wanted to get rid of it. Of course it's not good for a girl to be seen cutting her nose, so I bowed my head. But as soon as I looked down, the driver turned his head and looked at me. I quickly looked up and smiled embarrassingly at him. After he turned his head, I just bowed my head again. He was so annoyed that he looked at me again. I was so angry that I stared at him. He probably felt embarrassed too, so he quickly turned his head back. I think he won't bother me this time around, so I bowed my head in peace. But I don't know what happened. Suddenly he braked suddenly. I moved forward, cut out my nose, and messed up my hair. I looked up at him angrily, and he fainted when he opened his mouth...”
