 Twenty years ago, on a clear spring day, I was wandering around a street in Hongkou District and saw a large, newly opened hotel. It was very stylish. The name of the restaurant on the sign was “Three Dianshui”. I was surprised at the time. When I walked into the lobby, the waiters greeted me with a smile and sat down. I asked if the manager is there? She went in right away. Soon, a middle-aged man came out and politely asked me, respect my last name? Is there anything expensive about it? He then sat across from me and waited for my answer. I didn't answer his question positively at the time; instead, I asked him, how did the manager think of taking the name of this store? Is there a reason? He answered with a wry smile. There really is no reason; he always wants to use a fresh store name to attract customers. At the time, they applied for a business license from the Shanghai Municipal Administration for Industry and Commerce. They thought that the name of this “Three Point Water Restaurant” was too vulgar. Eventually, it was approved by the Hongkou District Administration for Industry and Commerce, and the level of the store was lowered. After the manager said this, I went on to say to him, “If you are interested, I'll tell you the origin of this “3 o'clock water” hotel. The manager immediately got up and led the waiter to pick up the tea, then said he wanted to listen to this story.
There is a “Luyang Village” hotel on the beach. In the early years, due to poor business, the owner came up with a couplet where customers could eat for free after leaving. This trick certainly worked. Not only were there more diners, but there were also many literati people among the customers.
One day in the winter, a customer dressed as a book reader came into the “Luyang Village” hotel to drink. He had just finished ordering the food. As soon as the store's junior picked it up, he touched the wine cup. The wine was cold, so he asked the store's junior 2 to heat the wine before drinking it. Mr. Dianjiao first stuck his finger into the cup and then wrote the three words “frozen wine” on the table. He also said, sir, if you talk to my cashier, you can also let the gentleman eat nothing. He also continued to write “1 point, 2 point, 3 point” under “frozen wine,” which he also wrote by sticking his finger into a wine cup and dipping it in wine. He also repeated that he saw a point on the word “water,” two points next to the word “east,” and three points next to the word “rooster.” It was too difficult. The scholar was humiliated on the spot, so he got up and left. Since then, not a single diner has played against this link. Before I finished speaking, the manager couldn't wait to ask me, can anyone respond to this link? I drank tea. At this point, the waiter had already served the dish. The four cold bowls ordered by the manager were: white cut meat, white belly, white cut chicken (later collectively known as “three yellow chicken”), salted water shrimp, and a pot of wine. I asked in a fun way: Miss, is it too hot? I asked Nong for hot wine; please don't leave the link. She laughed. The manager is still questioning the downlink. I said that until the spring blossoms bloomed the following year, when the lilacs bloomed at the entrance of the “Luyang Village” hotel, Sui Jing immediately entered the store and shouted, “The treasurer's, the bottom link is here. Lilac flowers, 100, 1000 heads, 10,000 heads.” He explained that the letter “Ding” is one horizontal, the word “hundred” is one horizontal; the word “incense” is “thousand” on the top; “incense” is the beginning of the word “thousand”; “flower” and “ten thousand” are both “cursive letters”, so the first letter on “flower” is also the first letter above the word “ten thousand”, isn't it “ten thousand heads”? Customers all over the store applauded.
Frozen wine, 1 point, 2 point, 3 point
Lilac flowers, hundred heads, thousand heads, ten thousand heads
After listening to it, the manager of the “Three o'clock water” restaurant immediately got up and toasted me, and promised that visiting our restaurant in the future will also be free, and you will never have to pay a bill. After we exchanged business cards, he said that if I had heard this story a long time ago, if I had submitted this story with the application report to the Shanghai Municipal Administration for Industry and Commerce to apply for a business license, I definitely wouldn't have been transferred to the district, and I wouldn't be downgraded. How culturally significant is our “three point water.”
