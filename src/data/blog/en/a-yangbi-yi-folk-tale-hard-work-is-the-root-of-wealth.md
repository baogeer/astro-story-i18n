 A long time ago, there was a loving couple who only had a son in middle age. They treated him as their sweetheart. They loved him from an early age, and everything depended on him. Therefore, from an early age, this kid developed clothes to reach out for him, eat meals that are easy to eat, and have a lazy temper; he has to rely on his parents to cook everything for him.
Day by day, year after year, 30 years of spring and autumn have passed in a blink of an eye. Everyone in the village watched this only son grow up and understand his habits, so no one family wants to marry their own girl as his daughter-in-law. His parents are already old, and they are worried about his marriage. The East Family went in and the West Family went out to talk about the media, but none of them helped. It was really sad and anxious.
One night, the older couple discussed a “magic trick” and called their son to the front, saying, “We're old and can't live for a few years. Once we die, you can only cook everything yourself. We think about your future life, so we planted some gold and silver for you in the open space behind the house a few years ago, but they've forgotten the details of the burial. If you don't eat or wear anything, go and dig the ground patiently.”
Soon, both of his parents died, and since then he has become an unreliant single person. At first, I was able to make my own food and drink, but after all, I had to sit and eat in the mountains, but then I had to go house to eat mixed meals. After a long time, the neighbors and townspeople all bothered him. A middle-aged man, he was also embarrassed by his strong self. Lying in bed hungry one day, I suddenly remembered that my parents had confessed before my birth, but now that they have no choice, they have decided to dig up those gold and silver treasures. However, after a few days, all the vacant land was dug up repeatedly, and no gold or silver was found, so I had to borrow some seeds from my neighbor and learn how to grow food. Since the ground had been carefully cultivated by him and the wind and rain were smooth, the crops grew very well, and the grain was harvested very well. Having a secure life, he also experienced the hardships of life during his work, and also enjoyed the joy of harvest. He understood that hard work and frugality are fundamental to his survival.
The changes in his habits and life made a girl in his neighborhood fall in love with her, so he married his daughter-in-law and became a family and had children. He learned his own lessons, made strict requirements for his future generations from an early age, and taught them to love labor, thrift, and economy. Only by working hard in life and learning some skills can they find “gold and silver” in the land, and can they also create wealth.
Thanks to the hard work of their family, their days were getting better, and gradually lived a happy life full of clothing and food. Later, they became famous monopolies near and far.
