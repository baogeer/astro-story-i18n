Once upon a time, there was a child who wasn't good at speaking, so his father found a gentleman to teach him.
One day, the gentleman led him to the forest. A woodpecker grabbed the trunk of a tree and pecked the wood with its steel-like mouth. A thunderous sound broke the silence of the forest. “Ye-ju, Ju-ju, is this cluttered? (Chinese meaning: teacher, teacher, what is this?)” The gentleman said, “Dong dong, there are foodies in Dong Dong.” The kid remembers it.
Soon, they came to a large road, and a cow was defecating. “Play Joo, Play Joo, is this cluttered?” The gentleman said, “Clap, clap, keep burning buckwheat cakes.” The kid remembers it.
After a while, they came to a meadow, and a swarm of green flies fell on the poop and flew away. “Play Joo, Play Joo, is this cluttered?” The gentleman said, “Flies are buzzing on the head of dog poop, can't stand up?” The kid remembers it.
Soon, they arrived at the edge of a persimmon tree, and the red persimmons were all over the branches. “Juju, Juju, this crap?” The gentleman said, “Red lanterns don't have a treasure cover; thousands of people love them.” The kid remembers it.
Later, they came to the family's door. The gentleman knocked on the door, and the child experienced the scene: “Dong dong dong, there are foodies inside.” The gentleman kept knocking on the door, and he kept shouting. The people inside were having a happy event and couldn't hear them knocking on the door. When they heard it later, they thought their name was Hanako, so they refused to open the door. Finally, the deacon prime minister heard it and let them in. In the yard, the kid saw a group of people around the table gambling. The kid said, “Flies are buzzing on the head of the dog poop, can't stand up?” People stared in amazement. Next, the child saw the cook baking cookies and said, “Clack, clap, keep cooking buckwheat cakes.” A few old people heard a thumbs up praising the child as a prodigy. The child became even more proud. Seeing that the short and fat bride came out of the happy room with a red face, busily saying, “Red lanterns, no treasure cover; thousands of people love them.” The bride was very happy to hear it and gave him a big red envelope.
The owner invited them to dinner and praised the children for their ingenuity and extraordinary memory. Back home, the gentleman told the child's father about the day's events. The father gave the gentleman twice his daily wage and fired him. “No need to teach, my kids can talk.”
