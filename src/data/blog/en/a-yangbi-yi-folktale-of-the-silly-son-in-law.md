 According to legend, there used to be a couple. The daughter-in-law was very smart and intelligent, but the man was dumbfounded. People call her man Grandpa Han. Below are a few stories of this gracious old lady.
(1) Borrow a chisel
One day, Grandpa Han's family had to work as a carpenter and needed a chisel, so the daughter-in-law sent Grandpa Han to borrow one from her own mother's house.
Grandpa Han climbed the mountain and came to her father-in-law's house. Having just walked through the door, the old father-in-law asked him what happened to his son? But he was stunned; for a moment, he couldn't even remember what he was actually doing. The old father-in-law felt strange when he saw him open his mouth, but since he was an idiot, he didn't compare himself with him; he just asked him to take a break. Grandpa Han then climbed to the top of the stove by herself, rode on corrugated wood framed in the void, and looked up at the roof while hanging her body upside down. I just sat there thinking and thinking about it...
When the old mother-in-law saw the situation, she couldn't help but yell at him and say, “Ah, what are you doing? Hurry up and get out of here; I'm afraid you want to die!” Unexpectedly, as soon as Grandma Han heard it, she immediately turned over and stood up and shouted, “Ma'am, ma'am, ma'am, I'm not trying to die; I'm here to borrow a chisel!”
(2) Being born with an “heck smash”
On this day, Han's grandpa and daughter-in-law gave birth to a big fat kid. The daughter-in-law asked Grandpa Han to quickly return to her mother's house to give good news. When she was about to leave the house, the daughter-in-law was afraid that he would return to his old father-in-law's house, and repeatedly advised him to say, “When the doll's grandpa and grandma asked what they were born with, you must remember to answer them that they gave birth to a son!”
Grandpa Han listened to her daughter-in-law and left home in a hurry. Along the way, for fear of going back to his father-in-law's house and saying the wrong thing, he kept nagging to himself: “Son, son...” While passing through a large water storm, he couldn't help but make a “smash” sound in his mouth when he jumped so hard. Since then, the “son, son” talk in his mouth has turned into a self-talk of “smash, smash...”
When Grandpa Han was excited to walk into his old father-in-law's house, the old mother-in-law asked him what happened. He excitedly said, “His mom gave birth to the doll!” The old mother-in-law was pleasantly surprised and asked, “What were you born with?” But in a daredevil response, he answered, “I was born to smash one!”
The old mother-in-law was immediately fooled by him. She quickly asked him out loud in a hurry, “What kind of 'heck' did they give birth to?” Now, Grandpa Han suddenly murmured in a very fiery, murmuring voice: “I was just born to 'smash it', and what else was 'ah smash'!”
(3). “The water has risen to the bottom of the rice”
The old mother-in-law found out that when her daughter had a big fat kid, she couldn't help but be so happy. She hurriedly packed up a liter of rice and 30 or 40 eggs for Grandpa Han to take home. She advised him to take good care of his confining daughter-in-law, and repeatedly taught him, “Remember to beat eggs when the pot is spicy, and only serve rice when the water is hot!”
Grandpa Han rushed home barefoot. At this point, the sun was hot in the sky. He accidentally stepped on a burnt bluestone plate with his bare feet, causing him to jump all of a sudden. He suddenly remembered what his old mother-in-law said, “The pot is spicy, beat the eggs,” so he immediately took out the eggs that his old mother-in-law gave him and put them all on this hot blue stone plate. Afterwards, he continued on his way. After walking for a short time, dark clouds suddenly rolled over the sky, and torrential rain poured in. Grandpa Han had to hide under a big tree to shelter from the rain. After the rain cleared up, Grandpa Han discovered that the water in a small river around him had risen. Remembering his old mother-in-law's instructions to “the water rose to the bottom of the rice,” he quickly put down the rice he was carrying and dumped it all into the river.
When Grandpa Han walked into her house, her daughter-in-law saw him empty-handed and asked, “Why didn't his grandmother send you the doll?” Grandpa Han then told her everything he had done on the way back. When the daughter-in-law listened, she could only stare at him with dry eyes; she was so angry that she couldn't say a word.
