 In Damaidi Natural Village, Yangjiang Town, Yangyi Yi Autonomous County, they live in the Luo Wu branch, a Yi ethnic group surnamed Luo Chang. A hundred years ago, the Luo Chang family lived quite well in Damaidi. Others looked blushing and were dissatisfied. Wanting to take over this place, he couldn't fight against the Luo and Chang families. He went to Wangfu to report that the Luo Chang family wanted to conspire against the throne in the Daimadi region. They had no choice but to get rid of the two surnames, and they would go against you. Wangfu had no idea when they heard it. I heard what you said, you went, and that person followed his orders. Wangfu thought left and right. This mountain range is bound to have a “treasure”. If you take the treasure, it's fine. Shortly after the incident, Wangfu sent a close friend, bringing a few thousand people and horses to Santaipo in the Daimaidi Mountains. They were stationed at the head station to dig “mountain ridges” to get the treasure. After several months, nothing was found, they had run out of food and grass, and the troops were withdrawn back home. That person continued to inform Wang Fu. More than a year had passed. Wang Fu brought soldiers and horses to the place where they had dug a mountain 1,000 meters down the mountain range. After several months of digging, they also yielded nothing, and withdrew the troops back home. That person kept on informing. After a few years, they sent people from Wangfu to dig for the third time. They went down the mountain range for about 2 kilometers. After several months of running out of food and grass, nothing was found, and the soldiers and horses were withdrawn back to camp. It just so happened that the chief who led the team stepped on the stirrup to get on the horse. Suddenly, you idiots said, “I'm not afraid to dig a lot, I'm not afraid of stepping on the stirrup with your back hand. The officer doesn't move when he steps on the stirrup. He asked his subordinates to pick up the harrow and dig it backwards. At first glance, he dug up a little doll. Alas, when I picked it up, I saw that it was a “golden doll.” The chief who led the team was extremely happy. He ordered his subordinates not to say it. If you say it, cut anyone's head off. He collected the golden doll and brought it back. This golden doll is the “Jimbo” who blessed the Daimai Diro and Chang families and was taken away by the royal family. On the day after the chief arrived home, he told his mother that his mother was doing needlework and that his son was doing needlework, and his son was happy to say, “You're blind, you've cut off our posterior pulse. She was holding a needle and cursing her finger. It just happened to be a needle poking her son's right eye, and his right eye was pierced.” This person was later dubbed “One-Eyed Dragon.”
Mountain Treasure was taken away, and society changed, affecting both the Luo and Chang families, such that they didn't feed chickens and chickens for a long time. Unexpected events occurred, the families had poor luck, and life was becoming more and more difficult. Especially during the Kuomintang rule, the arrests of soldiers, dispatching funds, chauffeurs, and exorbitant taxes increased unabated. Arrest of the soldiers, three children were drawn one, five children were also taken. Men from 14 to 30 years old were caught. The elderly and poor are divided into Zhuang Ding (local soldiers) and others are classified as national soldiers. They use the warlord system to manage, abuse, and corporal punishment. People don't care about life; they die when they are sick; those who disobey military officers are beaten and scolded; for example, those who disobey until corporal punishment to death. This situation is well known to all. The parents are so cold that they don't want their son caught. Some hid their son in Tibet every day, but he still hasn't escaped. Crops in the village are uncultivated, barren fields, wasteland, and not desolate; they must still be handed over. The people live a life that is not as good as cows and horses.
Under the leadership of the Communist Party of China, guerrillas were organized under the leadership of the underground party in Yunnan Province, called the Shangjiang River Guerrillas. The guerrillas (in Eryuan County) cooperated closely. Our Xiajiang guerrillas gradually grew, and the Shangjiang Guerrillas fought together, and sometimes fought separately with the Kuomintang Security Corps. The guerrillas are often active in Maidi, Jinzhan, Shangyi, Daimaidi, Chedapo, etc., so several powerful men from Daimai were also hooked up to join the Jinmai guerrilla Squadron. At the time, they had a one-line connection and were absolutely confidential. In 47, the Jinmai guerrilla squadron fought fiercely with the Kuomintang security corps at the crossroads of Ganmaidi Street in Shangyi. Unfortunately, the guerrillas were injured and seriously injured. The guerrillas immediately dispersed and Yang was sent overnight to the Shangjiang Guerrillas in Eryuan County for rescue. The wounded Yang Lichang died a few days later. After the peaceful liberation of Yunnan in 1949, land reform, rent reduction and leasing of public food and taxes were reduced.
The new Chinese people have been freed, and people of all ethnic groups all over the country have worked hard, especially since Comrade Deng Xiaoping of the Party Central Committee proposed the reform and opening-up policy after the Third Plenary Session of the 11th CPC Central Committee. We are members of the Yi people in the same country as Tiruo and Changsheng. We have enjoyed the fruits of reform and opening-up, settled our sustenance, and are moving towards becoming rich and well-off. Today, the Yi people in Daimaidi have basically achieved “three connections”, road access, electricity, and water communication. Our Yi family has already crossed the road to the doorstep of the house. The household lights are on, running water is delivered to the house, and the thatched house and yellow board house have been destroyed. The house has built large white tile houses, and the house-to-household roads have concrete roads, concrete garden dams, and floor tiles in some of the interiors. Every household has a few rice cookers and mobile phones. Even livestock are equipped with mobile phones. Many households have also been mechanized, and most fields and fields have also been excavated. Small agricultural transporters, rotary tillers, etc. have also been used, and it is also possible to scientifically cultivate fields, raise livestock, etc. Households' income from food has increased, animal husbandry has developed, and walnuts have become the main economic income. In addition, the country has also reduced the burden on farmers, free from public food, tax exemptions, subsidies for every mu of field and land, etc., and people from the old Yi region also live a happy and fulfilling life. There is already food left over from the family, and some households have money left over. We are also reminded of ancient legends and dream stories: one day when Maitreya Buddha ascends the throne, ordinary people will be able to live a life of eating three bites of white rice and one bite of meat. Today, a small Yi village realized this dream.
