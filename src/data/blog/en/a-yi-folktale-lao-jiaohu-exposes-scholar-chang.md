Because the tea man surnamed Tea Man at the time was recognized by the government as a “one tree, two pieces of sand, no grass, no tiles,” he was not allowed to participate in the scientific research. After Grandpa Chang Gong was admitted to Gong's birth as he wished, he had a big feast for the guests.
