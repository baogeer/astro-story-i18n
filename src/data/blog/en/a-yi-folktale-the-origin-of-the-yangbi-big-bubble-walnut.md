Walnuts are called “dense in color” in the Yi dialect. “color” means to like or like, and “dense” means delicious. “Color density” is because it is delicious, so it is a popular food. There are three types of walnuts that are common in Yangyi. One is called iron walnut (Yi language: colored dense fruit, wild walnut); one is called pickled walnut (Yi: color dense, home-grown walnut); another type is called cotton walnut (Yi: Magomamen). It is neither an iron walnut nor a soaked walnut. It is said that it is an iron walnut. It is not as hard as an iron walnut, and a walnut kernel is larger than an iron walnut; it is said that it is a soaked walnut. But it matures earlier.
Of the three types of walnuts, the one that can get the “color density” award is obviously soaked walnuts. So who actually cultivated soaked walnuts? Who Invented the Traditional Leather Patch Technique? Who Invented the Ancient Art of Refining Walnuts?
According to an old Yi ethnic group, since our ancestors of the Yi ethnic group Laloba fell in the middle of Cangshan Mountain after heavy floods. After being rescued, the brother and sister settled at the foot of Cangshan Mountain. After several generations of reproduction, the population is large, and life is basically worry-free. However, as the population grew, eating oil became a big problem. As a result, people do not get enough oil and fat, and most people are weak and sick, unable to work, and the average lifespan is less than 30 years. In order to solve the fuel consumption problem, the leader of the tribe, called on everyone to come up with ideas and find solutions, and he also meditated and thought hard himself. At night, the leader saw an old man with white hair and told it: If you want to get oil, unless you dig up thousands of years of snow on the highest peak in Shangcang Mountain, you can find a type of walnut tree on the ground. It is planted at the foot of a field, and the fruit that grows can be pressed for oil.
The next day, the leader told everyone about his dream. Everyone said, “Dreams are unbelievable.” Some say, “Dreams are the opposite.” The leader said, “So did everyone find a better way?” Everyone didn't make a sound. “If you don't have one, then if you have a dream, there's hope, and there's nothing wrong with looking for it. Tomorrow, in addition to women, the elderly, and children, everyone will bring a wooden knife and some sacks to get to the top of Cangshan Mountain.”
After dawn, everyone followed the leader up the mountain. When they first went up the mountain, thorns and vines blocked their way; they had to overcome the thorns and set out a path. Further up, the bushes blocked their way again, so they had to cut down trees with a knife and keep moving forward. Continuing upward, the tall trees gradually became sparse, and a white landscape appeared in front of you. Continuing up, the snow had already exceeded my knee. To walk ahead, you have to push the snow on both sides and drive a path. At this point, the sun set, and the leader called everyone back and come back tomorrow.
At noon on this day, people came to the bottom of the snowy peak and looked at the steep snowy peak. There was no grass, no trees, and no place to step foot. Many people lost their confidence. The leader asked everyone what method they would use to climb up. Some people say there's no other way but to put up a ladder. Someone immediately retorted that ladders weren't that long. The leader said, “This is easy to do. Just tie up the ladder with a rope and connect it.” Some people said, “It may take dozens of ladders to get to the top of the mountain, and we can't stand them up.” The leader said, “Why don't you just put them up one after the other.” At this point, the sun set again, and the leader called everyone back and come back tomorrow.
For two days in a row, everyone cut down trees, make ladders, make ladders. They started picking up ladders on the third day, and only reached the top of the mountain on the fourth day. On the fifth, sixth, and seventh days, three days passed. On the first day, they dug up one person's height, and the next day it was filled with snow again. In the evening, the leader organized everyone to find a way. Some people suggested using fire and heat the snow to turn it into water, saving both effort and trouble. The leader said, “This is a good method. When everyone goes up the mountain tomorrow, they will carry more pine wool to burn.”
Heavy smoke billowed, blowing with the wind, and flames blazing. The entire snow at the top of Cangshan Mountain instantly turned into water. The water became a flood and washed down the mountain, breaking out of thousands of mountains and valleys, and breaking out of Shimen Pass, a spectacle of the world. The top of the mountain came out, and the walnut fruit came out, but it washed away with the flood. The leader of the Yi ethnic group said it was too late. He jumped down with one arrow and grabbed three walnuts. Some people threw him a rope before they were swept away by the flood. Some of the walnuts that were washed away by the floods fell along the Qinggou and self-destructed into wild walnuts, or iron walnuts. Two of the three walnuts that the leader brought back were shared with everyone. Everyone ate them and they were crispy and fragrant. Some people said, “Yummy, yummy, really tasty!” Some people say, “It's so fragrant, it's so fragrant!” Some people say, “I love it to death!” The leader said, “This thing is called 'color density'! The rest was planted in the heart of my yard.”
A year has passed before the walnut tree is as thick as chopsticks and as tall as the Eight Immortals table. They sprout in spring, just like bracken sprouts. It grew tall one after another like a question mark, then the question mark became an inverted exclamation mark. Later, it gradually unfurled its leaves and became an open green umbrella. After another 20 or 30 days, new shoots grew on the top of the umbrella. The buds became branches, and the branches grew leaves, but they never branched. At the end of summer, they stop growing, and in winter, they shed their leaves and go dormant. The leader of the Yi ethnic group didn't know the growth rules of walnuts. At the end of summer, he saw the walnut leaves gradually turning yellow, thinking they were about to die. They were in a hurry like ants on a hot pot. He thought: There is a lot of rain in summer. Maybe there is too much water. Dig a trench around the walnut tree to drain water. Ten days and a half months have passed, and it still doesn't seem like they've survived. He thought it had been infested by a pest; gently peeling off the root of the walnut, there were no insects. Oh right, there must be a shortage of fertilizer. He applied fertilizer that had been fermented for half a year around the walnut saplings. Autumn is over, and the walnut leaves are getting more and more yellow. Seeing that it didn't work anymore, the leader had to tell everyone about it. An old man with two balds said, “Oops, it's fine. The walnut tree has to take off its clothes and go to sleep; it only wakes up and puts on new clothes after the Spring Festival! A walnut tree is just like a hemp oak tree, a mahogany tree, and a mulberry tree. It doesn't die; it's not scary!” Everyone took a look and only discovered that although the walnut leaves are dead, the branches are still dark green and still radiant, as if they had been covered with a layer of varnish. The leader was falsely shocked, and only then did he show an honest smile on his face.
In the second year, the stem was thick, and there were no branching results; it did not bear six fruits until the fifth year. The leader organized everyone to discuss the fruit distribution plan. Finally, they agreed and distributed it to six families for planting. After that, each family distributed the seeds and only shared the remaining walnuts.
A strange thing happened when eating walnuts. Boss's walnuts are big and puffy, while others' walnuts are small and hard. Everyone doubts whether others have stolen walnuts and replaced them with iron walnuts. All of these people said no. The leader said, “Don't worry, wait for everyone to get results, then let's talk about it.”
The leader's first walnut tree can bear thousands of fruits. The walnuts he distributed were still small and hard, and there was too much of a difference between them and the seeds. He held another meeting to let everyone find out why. Some say that the first seed was hard to come by; it was the result of the hard work of the leader and everyone. Some people say, is it still necessary to burn ice and snow at the top of Shangcang Mountain? Some say that yellow fruits and oranges can be grafted, and walnuts can also be grafted. The leader said, “Everyone can try it. The buds come to my house. Whoever grafts live is the master. Everyone can learn from him.”
Later, a person named Ah Tai succeeded in sticking rectangular sprouts on iron walnuts and padded walnuts, and this is how the thick, bubbly walnuts developed.
With walnuts, an Ah Gu person cooked the walnut kernels, put them in a pestle, use a pestle to make the noodles, and put them in the vegetable soup, and actually drifted off a layer of oil. Since then, he invented the oil refining method: he first put the walnuts in a pitcher and crushed them, then fry them in a pan, fry until the aroma comes out, then pour in the pan and continue cooking until the oil comes out. If the oil doesn't come out, you can burn some boiling water, but it's hard to cook. After the oil comes out, scoop it into a pot of boiling water and stir continuously. The bright yellow cigarette floats on the surface of the water. Use a spoon to scoop the oil from the head into a small pot and cook until the water vapor evaporates clean, and the walnut sesame oil is ready.
Refining iron walnuts is more troublesome than soaking walnuts. First, you need to find a flat and thick stone, then prepare a wooden hammer, place an iron walnut on the stone, and smash it with a wooden hammer. If you use too much force, the broken iron walnuts will fly all over the place. They don't have enough strength, and they won't break. Proficiency can only be achieved through repeated training. The more crushed the iron walnuts, the better. The crushed iron walnuts must be sifted through a thick sieve. If they don't fall off, put them in the pan and sift again until you can't see the walnut kernels in the iron walnut scraps. However, I still put it in a pot of boiling water and cooked it for about 2 hours before pouring it out for the pigs to eat. The crushed noodles that have been sifted are mixed in a pitcher. The subsequent processing process is the same as soaking walnuts. However, the oil yield of iron walnuts is only about 4%. Padded walnuts are between 8% and 12%, and large soaked walnuts can reach about 20%. If you have soaked walnuts, you have sesame oil; if you have sesame oil, you have deep-fried rice dumplings and deep-fried dried slices. Since then, Yi people have sacrificed their ancestors without these two types of deep-fried foods until now.
Later, followers of Islam, the Hui people, came to Weishan, Dali, and Yangjiao. They didn't eat pork. They particularly favored Yangjiao walnut oil, which also played a positive role in promoting the development of Yangjiao walnuts. The old Yi ethnic group said, “Walnuts will always be precious; our ancestors cultivated them with hard work and sweat. Golden fruit and silver fruit are not as good as my walnuts. Even if I can't sell them, refined edible oil is green, natural, beautiful, and slimming.”
