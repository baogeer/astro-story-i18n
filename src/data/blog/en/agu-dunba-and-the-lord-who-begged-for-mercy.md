 One day, Agutomba ran to a lord's house to borrow yaks and leather pockets. The lord said, “You don't carry food, and you don't have any food to transport at all. Why borrow yaks and leather pockets? No good!”
Agutomba thought to herself: If it doesn't work, I'm just letting you know that I want yaks and leather pockets. As a result, he didn't say anything in front of the lord, looked like he didn't care at all, and immediately went back.
When Agutomba got home, he scattered all around with his yak and leather pockets. Because everyone was willing to help, the owner's cow and the western family had two pockets. It didn't take much effort or time, so they quickly rounded up 50 cows and 100 pockets.
One day, he filled all of these 100 pockets with sand and carried them on the back of this 50-head cow. He deliberately passed by the door of the lord who refused to borrow yaks and leather pockets. Fearing that the lord wouldn't know, he let go of his voice and sang:
Cow cow, go fast,
Donba, my heart is happy and relaxed,
A large amount of food has arrived.
Since then, I don't have to worry about eating or wearing anything.
Cow cow, run fast,
Do you know Donba's feelings,
After the first trip, I still have to catch two trips.
Once you've packed the food, you have to carry gold treasure.
The singing voice floated into the lord's garden and into the lord's bedroom. It touched the lord's financial fans and stimulated the lord's greed. The lord peeked out his big head from the tall building window and asked Agutomba:
“Agutomba! You've chased so many yaks, what are you carrying?”
“Food!”
“Where did all this food come from?”
“It takes a long time to talk about! It's not early, let's talk about it later.” Agutomba knew the lord's feelings and deliberately made fun of him by pretending to be careless. “Uh, stop! Let me ask you something.” The lord couldn't help but jump out of the window all of a sudden, but he also knew that his life would be in danger. So I hurriedly rushed downstairs and ran to Agutomba. At this point, Agutomba had already stopped on the side of the road, while the Yaks were slowly moving forward. “Hey, it's Agutomba! Tell me, how did your food come from? You must tell me in detail.”
“Grandpa, don't hide it from you; all of this food was donated.” Agutomba mysteriously said, “This is the situation. I met a philanthropist Buddha in my dream the night before yesterday. He said to me, “I know you're poor. Tomorrow you bring yaks and leather pockets to me and bring back some food! I live in an old small temple on Qingsong Hill. Finally, there are two roads leading to Qingsonggang. One is to walk along Shashi Mountain; the other is on Qingcao Mountain. However, when you go, you must walk Shashi Mountain, not Qingcao Mountain; when you return, you must walk Qingcao Mountain, not Shashi Mountain; otherwise, you will be in danger. ' Today I got this food from him”
When the lord heard these words, he was greedy, and evil thoughts suddenly sprang up. Immediately go back and take 100 yaks and 200 leather pockets. Follow the route I just heard and run straight to an ancient temple in Qingsonggang.
When Agutomba got home, she immediately brought a set of living Buddha's crown that had been prepared in advance and walked to the small temple in Qingsonggang. Because he walked Mount Qingcao, he walked very fast step by step when going up the mountain; while the lord walked up the mountain, the road was very steep, almost two steps backwards, so he walked very slowly.
Agutomba got there first. After taking a break, he immediately put on his Buddha costume, put on his Buddha hat, and sat seriously on the Buddha's throne, waiting for the lord who came to kneel and ask for alms.
After a while, the lord came. After putting those yak arrangements in place, he seemed extremely pious and careful, and slowly walked into the small temple. Seeing “Buddha,” he immediately knelt down and bowed his head and prayed. I couldn't help but think the words: “Lord Buddha, who is merciful and compassionate, and saves suffering, is on top! I'm as poor as Agutomba! Pray for Buddha and give some food...”
“Buddha” said, “Reach out your hand!”
The lord obediently extended his right hand. As soon as the “Buddha” grasped it, he pulled out a sharp, glowing sword from his waist and scolded the lord:
“Agutomba is poor. You are the lord. On weekdays, you oppress the crowd, and today you are deceiving Buddha again. I'm going to cut off your arm and cut off your head!”
“No, no! Excuse me! “Buddha...” The lord's head was hitting the bluestone ground one after another, and painstakingly begged for forgiveness.
“OK, I can leave your dog dead today, but you have to honestly promise me a few conditions: keep all the yaks and leather pockets you brought; cancel all debts owed to you by poor people around you; in the future, Agutomba, if they want to borrow something from you, you must take care of them unconditionally” Wait until the lords agree one by one before “Grandpa Buddha” frees him.
When I went back, since Agutomba walked down Sandstone Mountain, I quickly got home; the lord walked Mount Green Grass and was only able to walk the difficult step by step, so I came back very late. By the time the lord came back, Agutomba was already waiting at his house! As soon as Agutomba saw the lord, he said, “I'm here waiting for your good news!”
“Good news? Alas, I almost lost my life!”
