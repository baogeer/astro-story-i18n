 On the banks of the Nianchu River, there is a bald wealthy man. People say that the reason he became bald was because he did bad things and was too immoral.
One evening, the herder Zaxipentso and his son Awang returned from herding. They were passing in front of a bald rich owner and were suddenly stopped by him. “Zaxi Peng Cuo! Come over to me!” The herdsman walked up to him in shock and asked, “Is there anything wrong with asking me, sir?” The rich owner pointed to the two bulls tied to the corner of the house and said to Zaxipengtso, “You are a herder; of course you can herd. Now I'll give it to you, and you'll graze for me for two months starting tomorrow. During this time, cow dung is yours, but you have to hand me two buckets of milk every day.”
The herdsman said, “There's nothing wrong with asking me to lay them for you for two months, but these two are bulls, so how can you pump milk them?” The wealthy man immediately showed an outrageous and ferocious expression, shouting with strong words: “All milk is squeezed out of cows; bulls are also cows.”
As the saying goes: You can't tighten your arms and thighs. Under these circumstances, herder Zaxipengtso was very aggrieved to take those two bulls back. The herdsman and his son were walking back and forth with no energy. It just so happened that Agutomba was on their way. They were very familiar. Agutomba asked, “Zaxipentso, why did you go back so late today?”
“Delayed by the rich dog...” The herdsman wanted to say something more, but his son rushed to Agutomba, telling Agutomba everything about the matter as if making an abacus, and asked, “Uncle Agutomba, can you think of a solution for us? Poor grandpa was in such a hurry that he almost went crazy!” At this point, Agton Bashun took a look at this kid's father, but wasn't he? His saggy hands are still shaking! Agutomba immediately comforted him and said, “Brother Zaxi Pengtso, don't worry; I'll deal with this.” Then he said to the child, “After five days, I'll go to you. Let's go to that rich dog's house together.”
The kid said, “I won't go until five days; this won't work. The dog owner said to my dad, “I have to send milk every day.” Agton Barr said, “Ignore his style, just listen to me. The insurance is correct.” Afterwards, he once again comforted herder Zaxi Peng Cuo, “It's not early, go home, father and son. Five days later, Agutomba led the herdsman's son to meet the rich owner. On the way, Agutomba clearly explained the countermeasures he had in mind — all at once to the child. When the rich man saw them coming, he didn't seem to see Agutomba standing next to him. Instead, he asked the kid, “Today is the sixth day since your family has taken my bull. Why haven't they brought milk? What about your dad?” The child answered, “My dad is lying in bed and confining!”
The rich owner scolded: “It's just nonsense! Is your dad in confinement? Can men still have children?” The child asked, “Since you know men can't have children, how can bulls have milk?” The dog owner had a furrowed brow and eyelids blurry when questioned. Only then did he take a look at Agutomba and thought to himself, this must be the ghost behind his back!
Agutomba saw that he was ignoring himself and said, “When I went to Zaxipentso's house, I saw how he had a difficult delivery. I'm afraid his life is still in danger! Mr. Wealthy, do you want them to graze and milk your two bulls?” The cunning dog owner listened to this enigmatic statement and thought it over carefully. He felt that there was something in his words; the situation was not good! Thought: Two cows are no joke. He immediately sent someone to Zaxi Pengtso's home to bring back his two cows, lest their lives be in danger due to “difficult delivery” in the future...
