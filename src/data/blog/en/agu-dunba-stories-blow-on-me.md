 When Agutonba was only 12 and 3 years old, he worked as a servant in the family of a senior.
That leader seems to have been born with a love for speaking out and being aggressive, and even more fond of bragging about himself and bluffing. For example, originally he only had one manor, but he had to say ten in front of others; originally he didn't even know a single Tibetan alphabet, but he had to brag about himself in front of others to be highly educated and proficient in all kinds of scriptures...
One day, he called Agutomba and said:
“Tomorrow is my birthday — my 50th birthday. Some distinguished guests will come to wish me a happy birthday. You're a clever person, and you should find a way to blow me off in front of those guests...”
Agutomba agreed.
The next day, of course, it was full of visitors. The birthday ceremony began. While the leader was sitting in the center of the lobby, facing the guests, full of spring breeze and feeling comfortable, Agutomba walked through the crowd without hesitation. It blew hard into the head's face. When people saw it, they all felt baffled. The head hurriedly waved at him and asked in surprise and anger:
“Bastard! What are you doing?”
Agutomba was afraid of how noisy people would be, and he couldn't hear the sound clearly. He deliberately raised his voice and asked in a loud voice:
“Hey? Didn't you ask me to brag about you in front of your noble guests yesterday?”
For a moment, it made everyone laugh. The owner was so ashamed that he blushed and had almost no place to be self-reliant.
