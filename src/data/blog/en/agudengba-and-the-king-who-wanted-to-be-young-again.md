 Once upon a time, there was a king. He is already in his 70s. He is still looking for beautiful women everywhere, and has threatened to have his 20th wedding in the near future.
Once, he went on a “visit” to a neighboring country and saw that the king of the neighboring country had a young princess, who was well-mannered, smart and intelligent, and was called an “absolute beauty.” So he came up with a bad idea.
One day, this old fox quietly walked up to the princess, expressed “love” to her, and said, “As long as you are willing to marry me, I will satisfy you no matter what you ask. Now please tell me about some of the conditions and requirements!”
The princess began to feel very strange and angry, and thought: Isn't this a joke? An old man with white hair and a silver cheek. Half of his body has been unearthed, and he is actually looking for “love” for himself. This is simply an insult to himself! But I thought again: he is a king. If he flatly refused, I'm afraid it would be rude, so I wanted to use “conditions” to embarrass him. “Dear King, if you wanted to marry me, I wouldn't be able to disobey, but there is one condition—only one condition, which is that you must go back to your current age, 50, and become a handsome young man. Otherwise, good things will be difficult to achieve, and you won't be able to get married!”
“This... this...” The king was stunned as soon as he heard it, so embarrassed that he couldn't speak for a moment. Seeing him acting like a fool, the princess learned the king's stuttering and went on to say, “This... this... this... this is my only condition. As soon as you become younger, I'll marry you.”
On the night the king returned home, ministers were summoned to come up with ideas for him. Some people suggested that the king issued a notice and ordered folk physicians from all over the world to make every effort to collect elixirs for “returning to old age,” or to collect the relevant magic recipes, give them for a limited period of time, and sentence them to death if they don't expire... This vicious suggestion was immediately adopted by the king. Early the next morning, this criminal notice was revealed.
How can poor folk physicians get any “elixirs” and “magic recipes” to restore old age and children? One month, two months... half a year has passed, and folk physicians from all over the world have been forced to donate only the heads and blood of so many innocent people! Agutomba, who is revered by the people, saw such a miserable situation, and his heart was as painful as being cut with a sword. He was determined to use his bravery and wisdom to defeat that devilish king in order to save the people from this increasingly serious disaster as soon as possible.
On a harsh winter morning, Agutomba, wearing only a pair of single pants, ran into the palace shirtless and out of breath to see the king, saying that he had come to donate “the house” for the king's “returning child.”
When the king saw this young man, he was naked on such a cold morning and couldn't help but ask, “I'm wearing so many clothes and still feel cold. Why are you wearing only one pair of single pants and still so hot and sweaty?”
Agutomba answered, “The reason for this needs to be discussed in detail; it takes a long time!
The king smartly said, “Well, I see a lot of articles here.” Then he said to Agutomba, “Just keep it brief!”
As a result, Agutomba made up the following “myth” about the king: “I am a leader. I am not hiding from you. Based on my actual age, I am 75 years old this year. Five years ago, when I was celebrating my 70th birthday, the daughter of a leading man from abroad was very beautiful. She followed her father to wish me happy birthday. I poured out my love for her, but she graciously declined. Why did she reject me? There's only one reason: I don't think I'm too old. Just when I was extremely distressed and unable to do anything, with the blessing of the gods, I suddenly became 50 years younger, and finally became close to that fairy-like beauty...”
Hearing this, the king suddenly interjected and asked, “Uh, tell me how that god blessed you? How can I make you 50 years younger all of a sudden?”
The king is most concerned about this, and he is also most interested in it. Because he heard that the basic situation Agutomba was talking about was almost exactly the same as his situation; the only difference was the head man, one was the king; one was the head's daughter, and the other was the king's princess. Agutomba understood the king's mentality better, and answered according to the king's question: “One night, when I was falling asleep, I had a dream where a 'god' quietly approached me and leaned down and said to me: Go over Tianpan Mountain, cross the Diling River, and surrounded by mountains, there is a large lake with a flat wall like a mirror and crystal clear bottom. It's a sacred place for immortals to bathe. As long as you have sincerity, you can go naked and bathe during the harsh winter season. The old ones can return to being young, the little ones can return to being young, white hair can turn dark, and the silver lining on your cheeks can be reduced to nothing. Not only that, after bathing in fairy water, it can also keep you young forever without fear of severe cold in winter or extreme heat in summer. Afterwards, I followed the divine spirit's dream, and that's true!”
“Bathing in the lake in winter won't freeze your body?” the king asked skeptically.
“I remember when I first went down, it was really cold, but I'm convinced that the gods won't lie to me. Maybe it's “Cheng Ze Ling”! Soon, the cold feeling gradually disappeared; instead, the more you wash, the warmer it became. In the end, I didn't go ashore because I felt too hot to go ashore.” Agutomba recounts it honestly with an attitude.
“Then take me there now!” The king said hastily.
“Absolutely. However, before we leave, you have to prepare some fine wine and dinner so we can eat and drink enough. On the one hand, I wish the king a safe and successful journey; on the other hand, let's eat and drink enough, so we can go fast and work hard!” Actually, for Agutomba, eating is fake; drinking alcohol is real, and at this moment, how necessary is drinking to protect against the cold for Agutomba! Needless to say, of course, the king treated Agutomba warmly.
The wise Agutomba led the king to the lake and said to him, “Dear King, please untie your dragon robe and take your jade down the lake to bathe!”
The king was already shivering incessantly in the face of the howling northerly wind. I looked at the lake again. It was frozen and thick everywhere, and I couldn't help but timidly ask, “This... how do I go down!” “Never mind, I'll break a hole for you.”
The hole was smashed. The king looked at the hole, and still hesitated. At this point, Agutomba took out a rope and said to him, “For insurance, use it to tie your body. I'm holding it on the lakeshore. If you really can't hold on after getting out of the water, move the rope, and I'll pull you ashore. Are you optimistic?” The king listened and was a bit tempted. However, when he approached the cave entrance, he was a little hesitant. At this point, Agutomba, standing behind him, helped him out and sneered, “Go to yours, devil!”
