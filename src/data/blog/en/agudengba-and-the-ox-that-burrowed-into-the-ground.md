 There is a lord in Agutomba's village. He has countless cows and sheep and lots of land, but his greed is getting bigger and bigger, and he can't help but return all the cattle, sheep, and land in the world to him. The people around him hated him to the bone.
One day, a group of poor guys who are very good with Agutomba came to his house. Everyone was chatting, and they all told him one wish: they wanted to eat beef. After some thought, Agutomba said to his friends, “OK, tonight I'm going to cook to relieve everyone's cravings, so let's have a nice and enjoyable meal of beef.”
However, all of his friends knew that Agutomba had neither cows nor money, so they asked him, “Where can I get beef?” “Don't worry, I have my own way.” Agutomba said it with full certainty.
Agutomba ran to the lord's house and said to the lord, “Master, I'll lend you a cow.” “Why borrow a cow?” the lord asked arrogantly.
“There is a plot of land that hasn't been cultivated yet!”
“Hmm...” the lord nodded and went on to say, “But you have to pay the rent.”
“Of course! Be sure to pay according to your rules.”
“By the rules? No good! Now is the busy farming season. Everyone has to use cows, and they have to double the rent!”
“No problem, double it!” Agton Barts answered very simply. This greedy lord heard about “doubling,” and of course he readily agreed. “Go grab it yourself.”
Agutomba walked into the lord's bullpen, picked a fat and big yak, and walked back and forth. But he didn't go far, but went back to the lord's house and said to the lord, “Master, I found your cow; it's a bit dishonest. I was walking on the ground with it just now, but it was trying hard to drill underground...”
Before I finished speaking, the lord scolded: “Nonsense, I haven't heard of it; cows can go underground!”
“Really. I'm never lying, old man.” Agutomba argued uneasily.
“Go to yours! If it were true, I'd pretend I didn't have it.” As soon as Agutomba heard the lord say this, he stopped talking to him and rushed home quickly with the cow.
As soon as he got home, he ordered the crowd to work together to slaughter the cow. On this night, in addition to being kind to the visitors, he also distributed the leftover beef, three and five pounds of each family, to the left and right neighbors, so that everyone had a beautiful meal.
The next day, Agutomba walked to the field with a cow's tail. It just so happened that I saw that lord also come this way from afar. Agutomba hurriedly planted one end of the cow's tail in the ground, but held the other end tightly with both hands, acting as if pulling it hard, and shouted one after another at the lord who was faltering over there: “That's amazing! That's amazing! Your cow is in the ground, come and pull it!”
As soon as the lord heard that his cow had gone into the ground, he hurriedly flew over, trying to help Agutomba. As soon as he got on, he didn't have time to work hard, so Agutomba pulled out his tail. As soon as the tail of the cow was unearthed, Agutonba looked like it was being overworked and leaned backwards, and the lord fell to the ground. Agutomba lazily climbed up from the ground, patted the dirt on her body, and said to the lord, “Oops, the cow's tail was cut off by us! I've said before that you're an unhonest cow!” The lord seemed to be paralyzed. It took half a day to get up with trouble. I saw his greedy little eyes staring at that cow's tail that had just been “cut off”. He felt sad and started crying with sadness...
