 Agutomba is an expert hunter. Not only is he clever and brave, and has excellent marksmanship, he is also familiar with the personalities and tempers of all kinds of birds and animals. Somehow, a mountain king who loved to hunt learned about this, so Agutomba was summoned by the king to be his hunting assistant.
On a hot summer day, the king took Agutomba to go hunting in the wild on horseback. The king rode a very brave horse, while Agutomba rode a weak and lazy horse. Agutomba was very angry at the king's extreme selfishness, but he tried his best to suppress it and not let his anger take the form of anger.
One day, they were hunting, and suddenly dark clouds suddenly rose and thunder roared. The weather in the mountains is indeed variable.
At this point, when the king saw that torrential rain was coming, he rode his horse and sped away. The horseshoe “laughed,” and there was a muddy burst of smoke and dust on the road leading to the royal palace.

The torrential rain was about to pour down, Agutomba was in a hurry and had nothing to do for a moment!
