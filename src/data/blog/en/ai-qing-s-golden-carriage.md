Ai Qing left. After 26 years of Chunqiu, he went a long way. In my mind, he just traveled a long distance and never died out.
In my dreams, I often see Ai Qing, but she is in the background. Ai Qing always runs with his Poetry “pure gold tricycle,” and I followed him. We walked through a muddy path, to a grassland covered with wild flowers, through a jungle, past a forest ranger's cabin, and were stopped and checked. Ai Qing said, “The car is full of poems; we write poems.” So let it go. Further ahead was a wave of reeds, and we walked past the waves. Suddenly, there was a sound of waves, and I looked up, just like raging waves.
When I woke up, Ai Qing and his carriage were gone. I remembered Ai Qing's “Poetry Tales”: “Our god of poetry drives a pure gold three-wheeled horse-drawn carriage and rode through the wilderness of life. Those three wheels, flashing the same light and vibrating with the same solemn loudness, are true, kind, and beautiful.”
Afterwards, I left the long period before dawn to think back, go back in time, and pick up those little things from the past. Past events that are too long will be overshadowed, like dreams; past stories that are too beautiful will linger in the mind, like vines.
The first time I saw Ai Qing's name was in a language textbook for the fifth grade of elementary school. There is a poem by him on it — “Spring Girl.” The idea of the poem is that the spring girl is here. In the willow basket she is carrying, there are many things, red flowers, green grass... The teacher took us to read it aloud and then explained it. I raised my hand and said, “Chun Girl's willow basket is missing a type of flower, Chongming's rapeseed. Teacher, can you give Ai Qing a suggestion?” The students laughed out loud, but the teacher walked up to my seat and gently touched my head and said, “If you don't think and don't have imagination, you wouldn't have this kind of thought. This is amazing! But I couldn't find Ai Qing. Xu Gang is still young. Maybe I'll have a chance to meet Ai Qing in the future and tell him in person.” There was silence in the classroom.
Since then, I have remembered the distant and unfamiliar name “Ai Qing.” A farmhouse boy was moved by a poem and a poet's name. He hazy and hazy saw a little glimmer of light hanging in the distance, flickering leisurely, making people move forward and give birth to clear dreams. All dreams face two possibilities: one is that you can't keep dreaming and the dream is interrupted; the second is that you will have a series of seemingly random encounters and collisions, which will cause the dream to continue.
Since “Spring Girl,” more poets and their works have appeared one after another: Yuan Ying's “A Gift for the Old Man” in the sixth-grade language textbook; when I was in middle school at Sanle Middle School, I read Guo Xiaochuan's “Starry Sky” in the November 1959 issue of “People's Literature”... I read, carried, and recited it to my classmates. I gradually saw the sky of poetry, the sky of the poet's beautiful or melancholy imagination. I was deeply moved because I was deeply moved Among them, I don't know what evening this evening is. These strange people and poems stirred the subtle light in my dream and made it brighter. I even think there is someone from afar beckoning; is that a poem or a poet? Can't see clearly, even those who are ignorant.
In the dark, the blue lanterns and yellow rolls lead me under the moonlight to find the subtle light of my dreams. That is the way to travel at night. I learned to trek and be patient, lick my wounds in the grass on the side of the road, write my dreams on the ground with reeds, and write “Torches” by Ai Qing, and pass from cloud to cloud. When I was looking for a place to stay at night, I met Mr. Wang Yao who was walking. After listening to the instructions, I didn't notice that the dawn had come. In the woods around Weiming Lake, I heard the sound of larks...
That's how I arrived in Beijing.
On a winter night in 1976, when the north wind called, Big Brother Yang Zhaoxiang, the poetry editor of “People's Literature,” said, “Go, I'll take you to see Ai Qing.” At that time, Ai Qing had already returned to Beijing from Xinjiang and temporarily stayed in a large complex in Shijia Hutong. In a small living room, Ai Qing smiled and shook hands with me. I finally saw Ai Qing. I actually met Ai Qing! His hands are so big, so warm, and so strong. His wife, Big Sister Gao Ying, was also with him. Ai Qing wouldn't let me be called “Mr.,” or “Teacher,” and wouldn't let me be called “Ai Lao.” He likes the title “Lao Ai”: “It's kind. I'm used to it. Yang Zhaoxiang and the children in the Corps are all called that.” Next, let's talk about homesickness. I talked about “Spring Girl” in the 5th grade elementary school language textbook, Chongming's rapeseed flowers, and the language teacher's words. Both Ai Qing and Gao Ying are happy. Ai Qing said, “Your teacher is a good teacher. He protected a child's imagination.” Still not exhausted, he placed special emphasis on the importance of elementary and middle school language teachers: “In almost every way, they are the shapers of the mind, such as fun, morality, thinking, imagination, and appreciation and understanding of beauty, etc.” He also said, “It's actually not important if you don't see Ai Qing. What matters is that he gave a child a dream of poetry. This is an amazing dream. It touches on the pyramid of literature, and it is linked to the poetic spirit of the Chinese nation.” Sister Gao Ying said, “Ai Qing rarely had this kind of conversation. He is happy tonight.”
When they said goodbye, Ai Qing insisted on going out together, saying they wanted to go to the toilet. There was no toilet at his place, and the public toilet was across the street. I supported him; he wouldn't let me; he just held my hand forcefully. When he went out, he looked up at the night sky. The new moon was like a hook. He waved to let us go: “Xu Gang, come when you have time!” Since then, I've become a regular customer of Ai Qing's family.
At that time, too, Ai Qing entered a new peak of creativity. Every time there was a new work, Yang Zhaoxiang, and Zhou Ming, always got a sneak peek at it. Long poems, such as “Hymn of Light,” have pages for “People's Literature”; short poems are included in the “Earth” supplement to “People's Daily”. Other than requesting manuscripts, it's mostly chat. We ate at Ai Qing's house or a nearby restaurant. Ai Qing doesn't have good teeth; he likes to eat “rotten stewed meat.” Meanwhile, Ai Qing moved to the Beiwei Hotel to stay temporarily, just a stone's throw away from No. 31 Huaren Road, where I live. At that time, the sites of “Guangming Daily” and “Poetry Magazine” were all nearby, and Ai Qing's place of residence became a gathering place for poets. It was a gathering place for poetry, language, and passion. This kind of gathering was also so pure: just to see Ai Qing, just because of her love for poetry. Those warm words were the sound of “pure gold three-wheeled horse-drawn carriages racing in the wilderness of life.”
The regular guests of Ai Qing's residence at Beiwei Hotel include Zou Difan, Cai Qilong, Zhou Liangpei, Sun Jingxuan, Han Zuorong, and me, some amateur writers at the Beijing Workers' Cultural Palace, as well as poets from other provinces such as Hu Zhao. “Hymn of Light” was the most talked about at the time. Zou Difan said, “There is a popular phrase in the literary world recently — 'Ai Qing is back'!” Others recited in unstandard Mandarin: “The bonfire in the mountains is beautiful/ The lighthouse in the harbor is beautiful/ The summer night stars are beautiful/ The fireworks celebrating victory are beautiful/ Everything is beautiful with light/...” There was applause in the room, and tea was used instead of wine to cheer for Ai Qing. Ai Qing looked at the poets with his charming smile and even thanked them. Sun Jingxuan is usually really excited and like crazy: “Let's sit in Ai Qing's tricycle, let's go Mercedes-Benz!” When the poets returned happy, it was already the beginning of the moon and the sky. Ai Qing sent everyone out. Sister Gao Ying smiled and said, “A lovely bunch of crazy people!”
Ai Qing never told me how to write a poem, but he once warned me, “Xu Gang, you have to brush your hair.” At that time, despicable people hadn't had time to go bald. They had random hair. They lay on their heads when there was no wind, and scattered all over the place when there was wind. I probably didn't listen to Ai Qing; my hair was still messy. Once, after reading “Walnuts, Pearl Bei and Fish Fossils - Ai Qing's Silhouettes”, he smiled and said, “It seems that a person's words have little to do with his hair.” This is the only time Ai Qing mentioned my text.
On March 27, 1983, Ai Qing's birthday. Sister Gao Ying said, “Ai Qing doesn't like to spend her birthday on her birthday. You can just go have a light meal and spend time with him.” I learned from my colleague that there is a pretty small flower shop on the west side of Chang'an Street, so I went there after work. There is a begonia plant in the store. It is more than two meters tall. The flowers that have already bloomed are white and warm, and the buds that have not bloomed are scrambling and waiting for me. Begonia sells for 65 yuan. The owner is a young old man: “Authentic Xifu Begonia, you can have fun!” Unwittingly, I remembered Lin Daiyu's poem: “If you stole Li Rui's three white triplets, you can borrow a drop of soul from a plum blossom.” I paid for it. I asked the boss to move the Begonia to a corner and take care of it safely.
After work the next day, I hurriedly rushed to the flower shop, picked up the flower pot, and rushed to No. 21 Fengshou Hutong. The flower pot was very heavy. The begonia tree swayed back and forth, and sometimes the petals fell on pedestrians. A beautiful girl with braids held the petals in her hand and said, “It's so beautiful, thank you!” On the way, I carefully placed the flower pot on the teeth of the street to take a breath. Passers-by all came to watch. People in the know said, “Nishifu Begonia is rare!” When I think of the little old shopkeeper, I think he is very cute.
Pushing into Ai Qingyuan's gate, Sister Gao Ying said, “Xu Gang held a big tree!” Ai Qing came over and looked at Begonia with a smile. Begonia, who had not calmed down since running all the way, faced Ai Qing's smile with trembling branches. Ai Qing said, “She also had a big smile on her face.”
With this living begonia tree, Ai Qing talked about truth, goodness, and beauty: “First, this begonia is real; it's not a plastic flower. This is really the first principle — what is displayed in front of you is real. Second, the person who sent this begonia tree is real and has a real personality. Why didn't they give it to me and not to the yard next door? Plants are the closest to human life and help the most. They are good and beautiful.”
Ai Qing's old house was demolished, and the new house was completed. When I moved again, I needed to send away some flowers from the yard. Sister Gao Ying later told me, “Don't give Xu Gang away!”
I remember saying goodbye at Fengshou Hutong. It was somewhat bleak. The cold moon shone backwards, and the lights were dim. I said, “Lao Ai, you should rest.” Ai Qing let go of her hand and said, “You have to come back often.” I let Ai Qing go home; he won't. “I'll be back when you leave.” Looking back, Ai Qing was still standing at the entrance of the courtyard; looking back, the shadow was blurry.
The bell rang ten times at Beijing Railway Station. I'm walking in Beijing, which is still full of traffic. I feel rich. I am rich because I treasure Ai Qing's three-wheeled carriage in my heart. That pure gold shone in my heart, and there was light in front of my eyes, and the alley without street lights was also splendid.
At my desk, there is always a voice that encourages me to write and move forward in loneliness: “The beauty that exists in poetry is expressed through the poet's emotions, a kind of spark in human ambition. This kind of flickering is like some sparks splashing in the dark; it's also like sparks blazing on a rock with a chisel and an axe.” “Simplicity is an abandonment of the extravagance of rhetoric, a healthy expression of getting rid of Chinese clothing, an unbridled step in breaking free from the restraints of form, and a broad laugh at empty techniques.”
What am I supposed to do? Nobody asked me to do anything. Nowadays, various “forms of poetry” are becoming popular. But I want to run, hold up Ai Qing's poems and “Poetry Theory,” and let them fly like flags, fly on the highlands of Chinese poetry, and let true, kind, and beautiful three-wheeled carriages “ride in the wilderness of life” and see that “broad laughter.”
