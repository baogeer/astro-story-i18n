When Grandpa Munemoto got this message, he sneered a few times and said:
“Wow, as long as his Agutomba head is iron and his legs are cast in bronze, let him come!” However, just in case, Zōben arranged for his cronies and minions to guard against Agutomba. Time went by so fast. After a blink of an eye, Grandpa Munemoto's happy period came. Just after noon this day, close relatives and friends arrived at the Zongben family one by one. Suddenly, there was a lot of noise and lots of guests, so it was really lively!
At dusk, the beautiful young bride, riding a large maroon horse, arrived hugging the ground after calling. For a while, there were lots of people in front of Grandpa Munemoto's door, drums were blaring in unison, and the crowd was in turmoil. In the midst of this chaos, suddenly in the crowd, a daring man wearing a fox fur hat and a long-sleeved robe appeared. He pointed his hands and feet there, talking about Dongxi. After a while, he went to the kitchen and said to the chef, “Let's make five more tables!” After a while, I ran to the one who was cooking and yelled, “The ghee tea is too light. Put a few more pieces of ghee!” The guests admired his gracious, busy and undisturbed manner. The woman thought, “It's amazing that the groom's family has such a smart and hardworking housekeeper!” The man's family thought, “The bride's family was really thoughtful. In order to organize tonight's wedding, they also sent such a competent manager!”
Actually, this smart and capable man is neither a woman's manager, nor a man's manager; he is just Agutomba who is famous and has come to make fun of Grandpa Munemoto!
After the climax of the wedding, Agutomba went into the kitchen and saw that the chefs who handled specific tasks and the maids who ran the dishes, made tea, served wine, toast, and tableware were sitting around in groups, eating and drinking. They said very sincerely and enthusiastically:
“Thank you all for your hard work! I'm here to have a few drinks with you!” Having said that, he sat up, and the people present received him very warmly. Thoughts: This manager is really reasonable and approachable. He even ate and drank with us, and had no shelves at all!
Needless to say, Agutomba of course took the opportunity to eat and drink beautifully and thoroughly.
After eating, Agutomba kindly said to the crowd:
“It's not early, please go take a break! The rest, I'll cook tomorrow, no problem.” The male and female servants thanked him one by one and resigned.
Neighbors have gone back one after another, and visitors from afar have gone to bed one after another. Seeing that there was no sound upstairs, Agudunba quietly walked into the stable and replaced the “Xining horse” Zumoto was riding with a yak, a horse whip for a long knife, and a long gun tied to the wall for a wooden bar. As a result, they brought in a few large pieces of wood and blocked the door.
When he put things downstairs to a standstill, he quietly climbed downstairs. First, when I walked to the place where the guests were staying, I saw them lying in Japanese clothes one by one, snuggling up and living like a dead dog. Agutomba lightly tied the girls' hair in an interlaced manner, and tucked a stone into each male guest's sleeve before turning around and walking towards Grandpa Munemoto's cave room. He listened at the door of the house. There was a deathly silence; even the green lamp had gone out. He locked it backwards with an iron lock on the outside. Thought: If you don't let me in, I won't let you out!
“BANG, BANG!” Agutomba knocked on the door of Grandpa Munemoto's house from outside.
“Who?” Old Munemoto asked inside.
“I am Agutomba!” Then he said, “Master Munemoto, you slept so early, I just wanted to amuse you!”
As soon as he heard that it was Agutomba, he was immediately enraged. Hearing his unscrupulous remarks, he was even more exasperated. He couldn't stand it, so he crawled out of bed with a grudge.
Agutomba heard the sound of him waking up outside the door, so he said in a serious manner:
“Tonight is your wedding night, so don't wake up, let's meet again another day!” After speaking, Agutomba hurriedly went downstairs. Walking to the stairway, he pulled a few peas out of his arms and scattered them on the stairs. Then, two more piles of poop were scattered there, and many flower needles were inserted into the poop...
By the time Grandpa Munemoto said his power, it was so easy to open the door. Agudomba had already walked out of the gate.
“Catch Agutomba soon! Catch Agutomba soon!” Munemoto screamed one after another. They chased them downstairs while shouting. Unexpectedly, his hands were stuck all over the place with flower needles. The pain caused him to hurry to remove the needle with his teeth. As a result, it stained his mouth with poop again...
At this point, I only heard him yell viciously at the people upstairs and downstairs:
“Are all of you idiots dead? Go after Agutomba! Hurry up, go after him!”

As for the maids lying in the straw shed outside, not to mention that the door was blocked, they were able to get out, and they couldn't help Grandpa Munemoto.
When Zumoto saw nothing to ask for help, he ran into the stable alone, pulled out the “Xining Horse” he had ridden, removed his own long gun from the wall, and set out to hunt down Agutomba. As soon as I touched the bolt of the gun, I knew it was a wooden bar!
What is the use of wooden bars? He also knows that the strong Agutomba doesn't eat this!
