 In the late Qing dynasty, there was a Xinqiao village about two miles northwest of Mingguangjin. Next to the village, there is a tomb of a senior official from the late Qing dynasty. It covers an area of about ten acres. Stone people and horses are lined up on both sides of Shinto in front of the tomb, which is spectacular. There is a couplet on the stone pillar in front of Shinto. It reads: Gongwei, the Great Wall of North Korea, and the famous city of Shenzhou establishes the foundation of the country. The owner of this tomb is Jin Wu of Xiao Jinying, and Jin Yunchang, the governor of Urumqi in western Xinjiang during the late Qing dynasty. Jin Yunchang is the biggest official in the history of Xiao Jinying. The entire village and the people around him are proud. Because Jin Wu was born into a poor family and has a fondness for the common people, he respects him very much and enshrines him as the “Holy Duke of Jin Wu.” The graveyard is known as the tomb of “Lord Kim”.
Qing history records: Jin Yunchang, Zi Jingting, from Xuyi County, Anhui Province (Jinli belonged to Xuyi at the time), a young orphan. After being banished due to banditry, she was adopted by the Cao family, the mother of Guo Baochang, the general soldier of Fengyang, and took the surname Guo. As an adult, enlisting in the military is defensive. He was promoted from guerrillas, general, to admiral, and restored the surname Kim due to his repeated achievements in the war. In the 8th year of Tongzhi, Baochang chose Yunchang to send his army down to defend Suide and besieged Jinji Fort with the Xiang Army's Liu Jintang department. After repeated and bitter battles, Yunchang struggled to finally defeat the People's Army. Its leaders Chen Lin and Ma Hualong led the crowd back down. The imperial court gave Yunchang a yellow coat of honor and gold. Second, they tried to quell the rebellion of Ma Shengfu of the Gansu People's Army, while Jin Fengtou was stationed in Baotou. In the second year of Guangxu, Zuo Zongtang dispatched from Changxi to the west. In the summer of the following year, he arrived in Urumqi. The imperial court appointed him the governor of Urumqi to guard western Xinjiang. After a long period of war and turmoil, people's livelihood was ruined. Yunchang built water conservancy, built bridges, developed agricultural production, and distributed huge sums of money to help the victims. These all upheld the fate of her mother-in-law Guo Cao. Li Hongzhang invited him to the imperial court and commended him at the Special Construction Hall. In the 11th year of Guangxu, Yunchang quit his job due to illness and returned home. He died the following year. He received the same pension as system, and was enshrined at the Zhaozhong Temple of the Zhuo Long Army. His wife and concubines, Wang, Ma, and Zhang, successively died at the festival, and were all honored.
As can be seen from the above historical records, Jin Yunchang's life was quite legendary, and there are many stories about Mr. Kim. I've collected and compiled a few articles here for the benefit of readers; at the same time, to commemorate this “King Dame” loved by ordinary people.
(1) The sound of reading outside the window
Jin Yunchang was formerly known as Jin Wu when he was a child. His parents died one after another, and his family was impoverished. As a young boy, Kim Woo has to spend his days asking for food. At that time, people in the village didn't have a very good time; they often went around the village and couldn't get a bite to eat, because the villagers were also hungry and full. As a young boy, Jin Woo carried a bowl of work and dragged a dog stick to Jinli Town a few miles away to ask for food. Because there are so many people in the town, so many businesses, and so many large families, I happened to be able to buy food one day and not be able to finish eating it for two or three days.
There is a private academy at Dogou in Jinzu-ri, and every day when Jin Go enters town, he has to pass through the entrance of the academy. Dozens of kids in the school were all about the same age as Kim Woo. They sat down at the table one by one and read with their heads shaking their heads. When Kim Woo saw it, I was very envious. At the age I just recorded, Kim Woo heard her parents say that if a person doesn't study, they will struggle for the rest of their lives to be literate. “Learning is a blade; he can cut through the roots of people's poverty.” Kim Woo often dreams that like these kids, he is sitting in a spacious school reading, writing, and studying with his teacher. As soon as I woke up, I couldn't help but shed tears with bitterness. Strangely enough, every day when Jin Wu walks to the entrance of the school, his legs look like they are filled with lead, unable to lift, and walk. Mr. Cheng began to quietly crouch under the window and spoke. When Mr. Jin read a sentence, Jin Wu read it in silence outside the window, then simply lay down on the window sill. Mr. read along, and Mr. Woo wrote it. Mr. Jin Woo used a rice bowl with water and dipped his fingers in water to write on the window sill. Because Kim Woo isn't noisy and doesn't affect other kids, the gentleman thought Kim Woo was curious for a while, so he didn't care and let him lie on the window sill. Day by day, more than a month passed. One day the student asked one student to read Li Bai's “Jinling Xinting,” and the students stood up in a hurry and were very sweaty. Kim Woo, who was lying on the window sill at this point, couldn't help but groan loudly:
The scenery of Jinling is good, and the new Haoshiji Pavilion.
Looking at the mountains and rivers, he hurt Zhou Kai's feelings.
Sitting on all sides, the prisoner is sad; he is not worried about the situation.
Prince Wang was generous and famous for thousands of years.
After Jin Wu Yin, the students in the class applauded him. The teacher was very surprised, so he led him to the school and asked, “Where did you learn the song “Jinling Xinting”?” Kim Woo said, “I learned with my teacher while lying outside the window.” The gentleman also asked, “Do you know what this poem means?” Jin Wu nodded and said, “This poem was written by Li Bai in Jinling in the 15th year of Tianbao. The Anshi Rebellion destroyed the stability and unity of the country and caused people in the Central Plains region to be devastated. In his poem, Li Bai used stories from Dongjin Wang and others at the Xinting rally to criticize the laid-back attitude of 'not worrying about the situation, 'reflecting his desire for unification.” The gentleman was so surprised that he was a bit dumbfounded. He asked Kim Woo who was his name and where he came from. Kim Woo told the truth one by one. He was deeply moved: a child begging for food, so attentive and memorable. It's rare, rare. Since then, Mr. has promised that Jin Wu will study for free, and that he will provide paper, ink, pens, and inkstones free of charge. Kim Woo is smart and clever. He immediately knelt down on both knees and knocked his head. This is considered a homage to his teacher.
Since then, there have been more students in the school. Jin Wu is naturally brilliant and wise, has studied hard day and night, and has progressed greatly in his studies over the past few years. As Jin Wu grew older, the gentleman wanted Jin Wu to stay and be his assistant, but he felt that Jin Wu had more to do and not let this big fish nest in this small pond. One day, Mr. will fix a book. He wants to recommend Jin Wu to the Guo Baochang Department of the Fengyang General Army; maybe he can stand out in the future. As soon as Jin Woo heard that the gentleman wanted him to leave, he burst into tears and said, “Don't you want me anymore, sir?” The gentleman shook his head, “Which is not necessary?” The gentleman shared his thoughts with Jin Wu. Jin Wu understood it and knelt down to the ground and knocked, “Sir, treat me like a biological child, and Kim will be well rewarded in the future.”
Kim Woo is indeed a righteous person. After he became a general soldier, I heard that the gentleman was old and did not accept any more apprentices. Every year, they set aside a portion of their salary and send them to Jinli quickly. Later, when they fought in Hunan, Inner Mongolia, and Xinjiang, they all commissioned the Xuyi County government to advance two pieces of silver. Later, when the local elderly told stories to their descendants, they only mentioned a section on Ji Eun's must report, and they took Kim Woo to retire for his husband as an example.
(2) Guo Cao, the old mother of the General Soldier, took Jin Wu as her son
Jin Wu carried a letter of recommendation from Mr., said goodbye to Mr., and went all the way to Fengyang Province to go to General Army Guo Baochang. Guo Baochang read his letter of recommendation and looked at Jin Wu up and down. He didn't see anything special from his appearance. After a few simple questions, he first arranged for the battalion to become a famous guard.
Guo Baochang is a martial arts expert who has read books and learned martial arts. He is also a gentleman with a strong sense of courtesy and reputation. They treat soldiers like brothers on weekdays. They often take the lead when fighting, and are loved by lower-level military officers and soldiers. There are five brothers Guo Baochang. He is the boss. Among the brothers, Guo Wangchang and Lao Wu Guo Jianchang all followed their older brother in East and West discussions. They were born and died. In a battle, Lao Wu Guo Jianchang was killed in action. Since Lao Wu is the son of Lao Ba that his mother Guo Cao loved the most, the brothers Baochang and Wangchang kept the news of Lao Wu's death in action so as not to make their mother sad. Guo Mu missed her old son and called Boss Bao Chang and Lao Si Wang Chang several times to bring Lao Wu back for her to take a look. Baochang and Wangchang are always busy with military affairs. The old five are out with soldiers and can't come back for a while. Guo Mu often washes her face with tears, and her eyes are blurred from crying over time. As soon as someone comes in front of her eyes, she shouts, “Has Jianchang Wu come back to see her?” Baochang and Wang Changshi couldn't bear it. They had to tell their mother the truth a few times, but they were also worried that their mother wouldn't accept it and something would go wrong. The two brothers were helpless and discussed what to do. Wang Chang, the senior, proposed: Can someone with a similar voice and figure as Lao Wu be impersonated in the camp to meet Lao Wu's mother? Baochang had no better way; he had to agree and try walking across the river by touching a stone.
The search for people is handled by the senior. The next day, I watched every person close to the old five heads all over the camp. As a result, Jin Wu was selected and brought to the boss Bao Chang to discern. He had a voice and figure similar to that of the old five. When I asked again, I knew it was the young man recommended by Mr. Tsuri a few days ago. Baochang regrets why he didn't pay attention at the time. Baochang explained to Jin Wu that he had asked him to go home and impersonate Lao Guo Jianchang to comfort his old mother. Jin Wu nodded and agreed, so the boss and senior taught him how to deal with it, and Jin Wu wrote them down one by one.
As soon as Jin Wu arrived at Guo's house, she saw that Guo Muci had the same kind face as her mother in her heart. Tears immediately burst out. She kneeled in front of Guo Mu with a “bang” and cried, “Ma'am!” The filial son Goko is back.” As soon as Guo Mu heard Wu Er come back, she was surprised and delighted. She hugged Jin Wu tightly in her arms. She was in tears and speechless for a moment. After crying for a while, Guo Mu's hands caressed Jin Wu's face, head, and ear. Finally, her hand stayed on the “horse leash” in her left ear. Guo Mu said, “Little Wuzi, why haven't you come back to see your wife all these years? My mother misses you so much.” Kim Woo said, “Ma'am, the filial girl has been out with the military all day. She hasn't been able to come back to visit her. I hope she forgives her.”
The boss and sir stood next to them and watched as the “maiden” had no end of being affectionate. They were in a hurry and were afraid that they would break down over time. While busy, he interjected, “Fifth brother, you still have military service.” At this point, Kim Woo also woke up from her mother-son relationship and stood up and said, “Ma'am, I have to go back and see you next time.” Guo Mu dragged Jin Wu and turned her head to Guo Baochang and said, “If the enemy hits the doorstep, do you want to hang up too?” As soon as Baochang saw that the lady had a wrong face, she didn't dare to squeak any more. He even looked down at Lao Si and asked her to help out. Lao Si hurriedly stepped forward and said, “Ma'am...” The following words haven't been uttered. Guo Mu sank her face and said, “Without you, my mother has numbers. Neither you nor the boss are good things.” The boss and the elder first heard their mother scolding. They knew they were really angry. No one dared to get angry anymore. Just stand by and listen to the “maiden” talking in a daze. Guo Mu spoke up again: “Don't stand still. The boss sat down. The senior went to the kitchen and told them to add food for the evening. I'm going to have dinner with the three of you tonight.”
Baochang and Wangchang were in such a hurry that they were caught by a cat, but they were afraid to disobey their mother's life and did it one by one. Kim Woo is also a bit nervous at this point, and he is afraid that showing off his horse will not end well after a long time. Fortunately, Guo Mu didn't ask about her childhood; most of her questions were about fighting outside. Although Jin Wu was a soldier for only a few days, he spent the whole day listening to the veterans telling stories about the war, as well as stories about how brave Lao Wu Guo Jianchang fought. Jin Wu continued to talk endlessly, and a stone gradually fell to the ground in Baochang's heart.
While eating, the family had fun without saying anything. In the evening, Guo Mu asked her boss, senior, and fifth to go to her room to talk. The boss, senior, and Jin Wu all felt like 15 hanging buckets of water - seven or eight times down. I don't know who the old lady will attack again. They all followed their mother to the bedroom. The boss, senior, and Jin Wu sat down one by one, and quietly listened to what the mother had to teach. Mu Guo sighed and called Boss, Senior, and Old Five. The three people here also agreed one by one. Guo Mu said, “Tonight I'll tell you three brothers a story.” The boss said, “The three of us will listen attentively to whatever story you want to tell, mother.” Guo Mu ignored the boss and only said to herself, “In the Song dynasty, there was a general named Yue Fei. When Yue Fei received martial arts to bid farewell to her mother, the mother-in-law had to embroider her son on his back...” The senior interjected, “Ma'am, you've told this story since you were a kid. She was not happy, but she also said, “OK, then I'll tell you the story of General Yang of the Song Dynasty tonight. Grandpa Yang died in battle. Hearing this, Jin Wu understood everything in his heart. He couldn't help but “flap” and kneel in front of Guo Mu: “Ma'am, we made a mistake; we misunderstood your old man.” As soon as Baochang and Wangchang saw Jin Wu, they couldn't help but exhale their breath. They also “knocked, knocked” in front of their mother and asked for forgiveness from their mother one by one. Guo Mu's heart calmed down a lot at this point. She picked up her boss, senior, and Jin Wu, who were kneeling in front of her. After sitting down, Guo Mu asked about Jin Wu's situation again. Jin Wu came one by one. Guo Mu said, “Boss, listen to me, senior. This Jin Wu is a material that can be made. In the future, it is determined by you. I just heard your old reprimand. The child admires you even more. I am your son, then I will call you your wife, and I will never do anything humiliating to your old family.” After speaking, I didn't wait for Guo Mu to answer “Boom, Boom, Boom” and knocked three noises.
Since Guo Mu came into contact with Jin Wu in the afternoon, she felt that this kid was very intelligent, and Guo Mu's level of happiness has increased. Hearing that he is an orphan, has also increased her motherly compassion, and she also wants to recognize him as a righteous child. She doesn't want this kid to speak up first. It fits Guo Mu's feelings. Guo Mu is happy to accept it.
Boss Bao Chang was very curious. How did his mother discover that Kim Woo was impersonating him? How did the mother know that her fifth brother had died in action? He was puzzled. The next day, the Guo family held a ceremony according to local customs. Since then, Guo Mu has had a son, and Jin Wu also has a mother. Jin Wu changed his surname to Guo and took the name Guo Yunchang according to the Chang family. Since then, General Soldier Guo Baochang has treated Jin Wu as his own brother and cultivated it carefully.
(3) Promotion to the governor of Tokyo
Guo Baochang has more good brothers, more good guerrillas in the military camp, one more fierce general on the battlefield, and one more senior officer in the camp.
Guo Yunchang lived up to expectations. He rose from guard to guerrilla. There were minor riots around Fengyang, or to destroy a small cottage. Guo Baochang often sent Guo Yunchang to take troops, and Yunchang never dishonored his mission every time, because of his ingenuity and bravery in his battles. Every time a victory comes back, Baochang must hold a celebration conference to reward the meritorious generals and enhance Yunchang's prestige in the military. Through Guo Baochang's thoughtful promotion and strong recommendation, Guo Yunchang went from guerrilla to deputy general, and became the most powerful general around Guo Baochang in a few years.
In the 8th year of Tongzhi (1870), the People's Army of Jinji Fort fought against the government, under the leadership of its leaders Chen Lin and Ma Hualong. The local government was unable to return to the People's Army. The Qing dynasty government reorganized Fengyang Huaiyong Chu Shengjun into Zhuo Shengjun and cooperated with Zeng Guofan's Xiang Army Liu Kam Tong to besiege the People's Army in Jinji Fort. Guo Baochang's mother Guo Cao was seriously ill at the time. Seeing that she couldn't get through many days, the boss Guo Baochang wanted to stay at home and do his best. He strongly recommended Guo Yunchang to take the lead of the General Army and downgrade the defense against Suide, and the imperial court followed suit. Before leaving, Guo Cao was lying in his hospital bed and advised Guo Yunchang: “If you are an official, you must take care of the people and profit for the people. People all over the world have good officials. People are like water, government offices are like boats. Water can carry boats, and can also cover boats.” Guo Yunchang nodded, “My son keeps in mind the mother's words one by one.”
Zhuo Shengjun fought bravely and was invincible. Wherever he went, Guo Yunchang comforted the people and put an end to the riots. He divided the rebels into three categories: the first category was the culprit and resolutely killed; the second category was the attackers, who were not allowed to be killed as long as they did not resist; the third category was forced to join forces, and these people were not allowed to be killed. After the war, he was willing to follow Zhuo Shengjun's welcome and treat them equally. This not only divided and dismantled the rebellious army, but also expanded the military resources of Zhuo Shengjun. In the end, the People's Army was defeated without a fight. Leaders Chen Lin and Ma Hualong successively surrendered. Guo Yunchang's department won a major victory. The imperial court gave Guo Yunchang an official approval as a general soldier of Zhuo Shengjun, and gave him a yellow coat and garrisoned for some money.
The following year, Ma Shengfu of the People's Army returned to the Gansu People's Army and confronted the imperial court again, killing officers and besieging the city. In time, fighting broke out again in Gansu. Former military leaders Chen Lin and Ma Hualong, who surrendered, always followed General Soldier Guo Yunchang. Guo suspected that they were brothers, and General Chen and Ma never had a chance to repay General Guo. The current Gansu People's Army riots were a good opportunity for both of them to go and talk about the surrender. General Chen and Ma called for war. Guo Yunchang analyzed that General Chen and Ma Er were also former leaders of the People's Army. Although they were not in one place, the customs of returning to the people were similar. He said that the surrender was certain, so he wrote to the imperial court to go to war. The imperial court is also trying to resolve the chaos. It was very happy to receive Guo Yunchang's petition for war, and immediately sent Guo Yunchang, general soldier of the Zhuo Shengjun, to bring troops to Gansu to calm the unrest. Chen Lin and Ma Hualong became the pioneer forces of the Zhuo Shengjun. Since most of the old members of Chen and Ma are still in their own army, they are all refugees. Everywhere Chen and Ma Fu promoted how good Guo Yunchang was. In addition, Chen and Ma Bu went back to their own clans, so there was no big war. They only made small knocks. “Fight less, kill less” was also Guo Yunchang's purpose of the campaign. At the end of the day, only a small number of Ma Shengfu's headquarters troops resisted. Only General Chen and Ma Er easily won the victory, captured Ma Shengfu, and calmed down the civil unrest of the Gansu returning army and people. Since Guo Yunchang called for war this time, the entire imperial court was very concerned. Seeing Guo Fei's efforts to calm the civil army chaos, the emperor was very happy. He promoted Guo Yunchang's official uniform and officially became General Zhuo Sheng.
In the 2nd year of Guangxu (1877), Zuo Zongtang and Li Hongzhang sent Guo Yunchang to advance west to pacify western Xinjiang. Guo Yunchang led the department to investigate along the way. Guangxu arrived in Urumqi in the summer of three years and was appointed by the imperial court as the governor of Urumqi to guard western Xinjiang.
People in western Xinjiang were ruined by years of war. Guo Yunchang kept in mind Guo Mu's teachings before the conquest, “Water can carry boats, but they can also cover boats”, cut rents and interest rates, vigorously develop production, build water conservancy, and build bridges. The region was poor. Unable to pay much money for a while, Guo Yunchang took most of his annual salary, supported them, and distributed huge sums of money to help the victims. Agriculture and animal husbandry production in western Xinjiang developed rapidly in a few years, and there have been no further riots in western Xinjiang since then.
When Guo Yunchang was leading the people to resume agricultural production in western Xinjiang, the family spread the sad news of Guo Mu's death. Guo Yunchang and his fourth brother Guo Wangxing set up a memorial hall a thousand miles away for seven to 35 days, praying and praying every day. After the death of her mother-in-law, Guo Yunchang obtained the consent of her eldest brother Guo Baochang to submit details of her birth to the imperial court and requested approval to restore her last name to Jin, and still used the name Yunchang.
In the 11th year of Guangxu (1886), Jin Yunchang returned home due to illness. He died of illness the following year. The imperial court was worried and buried in his hometown of Jinri.
