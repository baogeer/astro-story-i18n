 There is a young man from the Hani tribe named Arjuni. He worked for three months at the family of the rich Matsumon family, but he didn't get any copper money. Arjuni thought that her blood and sweat must not be wasted; she must find a way to recover this salary.
One day, while the Matsumon family was drinking, Arjuni went to the table and said, “Master, there is so much meat on your table, can you let me taste the aroma?”
“You can eat meat, bring the money.” Matsumon reached out and asked Arjuni for money.
“I don't have any money. However, I have an ancestral treasure worth tens of thousands of dollars!” Arjuni was very serious.
Matsumon smiled and said, “Come on, sit down and eat some meat, then go get it when you're full!”
When Arjuni had enough to eat, Matsumon forced him to pick up the baby.
After a while, Arjuni walked over with a finger-big ball of snow-white glutinous rice wrapped in a broken red cloth. “Grandpa, don't grab it; if you lose it, you have to pay for it!” While talking, gently unwrapped the red cloth and exposed the snow-white sticky rice puff. When Matsumon saw that snow-white round bead, I really thought it was a priceless treasure. Just reached out to get it. As soon as Arjuni's hands shook, the glutinous rice pudding fell to the ground and was eaten by a white rooster next to her. Arjuni pretended to be anxious and angry and said, “Grandpa, you lost my family's ancestral baby, you have to pay for it!”
Matsumon said, “That baby is in the stomach of a chicken, you can't lose it.” After dark, the chickens went into the chicken's nest one by one. Matsumon left the white rooster that had eaten the “baby” in the nest, and caught another white rooster and mentioned it in front of Arjuni: “Your baby was eaten by this rooster. OK, I even gave you the chicken.”
Arjuni said, “I just want my baby, not your chicken.”
Matsumon said, “I touched your baby, and I also have responsibilities. Let's do it, take the chicken back and I'll give you another 30 silver dollars.” He thought, after eating the baby's chicken, locked it in the chicken nest, and paid 30 silver yuan to buy an ancestral baby. It's so cheap!
After receiving the silver dollar, Ahjuni thought: these three months of work have not been in vain. He walked two steps while holding the chicken and said, “Grandpa, I'll take the money and chicken back first, but I have to make it clear. If the chicken takes the baby out, I don't want any of this 30 yuan; if the baby doesn't come out, you still have to use your assets to pay for my baby.”
Matsumon took out another 20 yuan and handed it to Arjuni: “Sure, this 50 yuan is my compensation for you. Baby, you can only ask the rooster to ask for it; if the chicken doesn't eat baby, I'm not responsible.”
With a white rooster in her hand and silver dollars in her pocket, Arjuni easily and happily went down the bamboo tower at Matsumon Gate.
