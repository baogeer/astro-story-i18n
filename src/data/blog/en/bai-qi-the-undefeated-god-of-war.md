 Bai Qi, a native of Qin Guoxian County (present-day Baijia Village, Changxing Town, Meixian County, Shaanxi Province) during the Warring States period, was another outstanding military man and commander after Sun Wu and Wu Qi in Chinese history. Along with Lian Po, Li Mu, and Wang Jian, he is known as one of the four famous generals of the Warring States. Bai Qi's father is a die-hard fan of Wu Qi. He hopes that when Bai Qi grows up, he can become an excellent soldier like Wu Qi, and he will name his son Qi Qi.
Main achievements: Bai Qi has fought on the field for more than 30 years and has commanded many important battles. He has fought more than 70 battles in his lifetime, and has not experienced defeat. The Battle of Yi Que destroyed the 240,000 allied forces of Han and Wei, and completely cleared the way for the Qin army to advance eastward.
