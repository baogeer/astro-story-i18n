“The Seven Commandments” includes seven chapters: Weakness, Couple, Prudence, Motherhood, Concentration, Qu Cong, and Uncles. It was originally a private textbook to teach the daughter of the class family, but unexpectedly, the Beijing Family scrambled to copy it, and soon became popular all over the country.
In the “Humble” section, Ban Chao quoted the phrase in “Shijing: Xiaoya”: “Having a boy means building a long Zhang, and having a daughter means making a tile.” Thinking that women are not meant to be compared to men, they must “go to bed late and work early, don't be afraid to stay up late; doing business is easy.” Only then can we do our part.
In the “Couple” section, it is believed that a husband is older than heaven, and that they must also serve with due respect. “If a woman is not wise, she has no role; if a woman is not a husband, then her righteousness falls apart.
