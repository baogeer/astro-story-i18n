 Shen Linsheng said, “While taking a nap in the summer, in the midst of haze, he saw a woman lift the curtains and walk to the back room, wrapped in white cloth and mourning dress. The old man thought it might be a neighbor's woman looking for his wife. But I thought again, why wear unlucky clothes to other people's homes? While she was puzzled, the woman had already walked out of the back room. He took a close look. This woman was in her 30s. Her face was yellow and swollen, her eyebrows were not stretched, and she looked horrible. The woman hesitated not to leave and gradually got closer to the old man's bed. The old man pretended to be asleep to see what happened.
Not long ago, the woman put on her clothes and went to bed, putting pressure on the old man's stomach. The old man felt like he weighed several hundred pounds. Although I understood everything in my heart, if I wanted to raise my hands, my hands were tied; if I wanted to lift my feet, my feet were weak and unable to move. I was in a hurry to cry for help, and I was too worried that I couldn't make a sound. Next, the woman sniffed his face with her mouth; sniffed his cheeks, nose, eyebrows, and forehead. The old man felt that her mouth was as cool as ice, and the chill was penetrating through her bones. He was in a hurry and wanted to bite her hard when she smelled it on her cheek. It didn't take long. Sure enough, I could smell the edge of her cheek. The old man took advantage of the force to bite her cheekbone, and all of her teeth went into the flesh. The woman felt pain and wanted to leave quickly. While struggling, she cried. But the harder the old man bit him, he intuitively felt blood flowing down his cheek and soaked the pillow.
While they were struggling, hearing the voice of his wife in the yard, the old man hurriedly shouted, “There is a ghost!” As soon as she relented, the woman fled. The wife ran into the house and saw nothing. She just laughed at him for having a nightmare. The old man explained the strange incident in detail and said that there were blood stains on the pillow as proof. The two of them checked, and it was true that there was something like water leaking from the roof, wet the pillows and mats. Lie down and sniff. The fishy smell is abnormal. The old man was so nauseous that he threw up a lot. After a few days, there was still a smell left in his mouth.
