 A long time ago, dozens of hardworking and kind farmers lived in Willow Village, where the mountains and clear water were beautiful and the soil was fertile. When working hard during the day or relaxing after a meal, people like to drink herbal tea and chat under a centuries-old willow tree next to the village. Willow Tree Village is named after this willow tree.
I don't know when the chickens, pigs, sheep, cows, and even boys and girls in the village will suddenly disappear after a period of inexplicably sluggishness. People don't know why they are fearful; some are planning to go out and take refuge, and some are preparing to relocate.
When Carpenter Zhang from the neighboring village heard about this situation, he brought a little apprentice to Willow Village to do wood work. In fact, he came to observe and understand the strange phenomenon in Willow Tree Village. A few days later, Carpenter Zhang discovered that many of the strange things in the village were probably related to old willow trees. He set up a shed in an open space not far from the willow tree, set up a carpentry site here, and food and lodging were also here. There are no carpenters in Willow Tree Village. Carpenter Zhang has set up a wood factory. Everyone who wants to make tables, benches, and cabinets has prepared wood to use Carpenter Zhang to do it. Carpenter Zhang and his little apprentice are also extremely busy.
A few days later, Carpenter Zhang discovered that there was a young and beautiful girl who was unique. Every afternoon, she had to chat with her little apprentice. Within a few days, the little apprentice looked dazed and sluggish. Carpenter Zhang remained silent and observed carefully. After counting in mind, they chose to destroy the fairies on the Golden Moon Golden Day of the Five Elements. In the afternoon of this day, the beautiful woman came to the woodworking factory as usual to chat and laugh with her little apprentice. After the girl left, the little apprentice laughed for a long time and was very tired. After dinner, Carpenter Zhang told the little apprentice to go to bed early. After the little apprentice went to bed, he fell into a heavy sleep. Carpenter Zhang drew a spell on the carpentry axe, measured the proper distance from his apprentice using Luban's scale, and fell asleep in a uniform. At three o'clock at night, a beautiful girl floated in. First, he pushed Carpenter Zhang with his hands. Carpenter Zhang pretended to be awake. The girl then leaned over the apprentice and stuck her bright red tongue into the apprentice's mouth. Carpenter Zhang saw that the time had come. Holding a sharp axe in his hand, he violently slashed the girl's thigh. The girl screamed and drifted in the direction of the old willow tree. The axe was cut into her leg bone and taken away by her, leaving behind a speck of blood along the direction she fled. Carpenter Zhang kept an eye on her whereabouts and did not catch up. The girl disappeared when she reached under the willow tree. Carpenter Zhang didn't see eye to eye all night until dawn woke up his little apprentice. Carpenter Zhang took Lu Ban's ruler and asked his little apprentice to take a mountain axe and follow the blood trail to the bottom of the willow tree. He saw that the sharp axe with the spell was cut deep into the trunk of the willow tree. The apprentice was surprised and asked, “Master, what's going on with this?” Carpenter Zhang talked about the situation last night. The little apprentice was still half-convinced and half-skeptical. Carpenter Zhang used Luban ruler to measure the proper position on the willow tree and asked his apprentice to cut down the old willow tree with a mountain axe. When the little apprentice cut down a tree, every time he cut down an axe, the willow made a terrible groan, and blood came out of the cut. After cutting a few axes, Willow made a vocal cry to Carpenter Zhang and said, “Master, please spare me. I would like to be your faithful slave and listen to your call.” Carpenter Zhang said, “You are a poisonous fairy. If you take the essence of the Sun and the Moon and the aura that touches the earth, you can live a long life. You can also cruelly draw blood from humans and animals, hurt lives and damage spirits, and harms people on one side. I want to exterminate the damage for the people today, and I will never be merciful!” He also asked his apprentice to keep cutting down trees. The little apprentice had a lot of energy at this point, and the more he cut, the more energetic he got. When the villagers heard the sound of trees being cut down, they all gathered around to take a look. When Carpenter Zhang saw that most households from all over the village had arrived, he told everyone about the harm caused by the willow tree when it was refined. Both of their mentors and apprentices specially came to exterminate the damage for the people, so they can live and work peacefully in the future. The crowd happily thanked Carpenter Zhang's mentors and apprentices. A few young and strong boys took turns to help cut down the tree. When the tree was quickly cut down, the axe on the trunk loosened. Carpenter Zhang had words in his mouth, spilled a bowl of clean water on the willow tree, reached out to remove the axe, and said, “Willow tree essence has been eradicated, everyone can rest assured.”
Since then, there have been no old willows, and Willow Village is still called Willow Tree Village.
