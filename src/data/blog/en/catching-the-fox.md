 Sun Laoweng, the uncle of Sun Qingfu in my family, has always been very brave. During the day, he was lying down to rest, feeling as if something had climbed onto the bed, and then felt his body shaky, like driving in the fog. He thought in his heart, was he misled by the Vixen? Then I squinted and peeked quietly. I saw something as big as a cat. It had yellow fur, but it had a green mouth, and was slowly creeping up from the side of my feet. It squirms softly, as if fearful of waking up an old man. After a while, it was attached to Sun Laoweng's body. Next to his foot, his foot was paralyzed; when he leaned on his leg, his leg was soft. When it had just climbed to its stomach, Sun Laoweng suddenly sat up, pressed it down violently, grabbed it, and held it by the neck with both hands. It screamed in a hurry, but it couldn't break free.
Sun Laoweng hurriedly called his wife, tied her waist with a rope, tightened both ends of the rope, and smiled and said, “I heard you are good at change. Today I'm watching you here to see how you can change.” While talking, it suddenly shrank its stomach like a thin tube, and almost escaped by removing the rope. Sun Laoweng was shocked and hurriedly tightened the rope with force. But it bloated again, and it was as thick as the mouth of a bowl, and it couldn't hold back. Sun Laoweng's energy loosened a bit, but it shrunk again.
Sun Laoweng was afraid that it would run away, and asked his wife to quickly kill it with a knife. The old lady looked around in a panic and didn't know where to put the knife. Sun Laoweng shook his head to the left to indicate where the knife was released. When I looked back, I only had an empty rope cover like a ring in my hand, and the fox didn't know where it was going.
