 Hou Yi came to the world by the will of Emperor Yu Huang, and used his divine bow to shoot nine suns down eight, leaving one to illuminate the people. Afterwards, he surrendered to some demons and ghosts. There was a peaceful scene in the world. People all called Hou Yi the hero who shot the Sun and the god of conquering demons. When Hou Yi returned to Tianting, Emperor Jade assigned him the fairy Chang'e Xu.
When Hou Yi saw that Emperor Yu valued himself, he also became arrogant; there were no other immortals in his eyes. He took Chang'e east and west all day long. He was a little displeased; either arguing with this or with that. Those immortals were originally unconvinced that Emperor Yu gave Chang'e's promise to Hou Yi; they thought to themselves, wasn't Hou Yi just a martial artist who could pull a bow and archery? There's nothing remarkable about it. They ran to Emperor Yu for three days and said bad things about Hou Yi. Over time, Emperor Jade thought: I can't do anything for a Hou Yi to offend the immortals. Emperor Yu made up an excuse to seal Hou Yi as the emperor of a poor country, and asked him to take Chang'e to sit in rivers and mountains, and not be allowed to return to heaven.
Hou Yi brought Chang'e to a poor country, and heard that people were praising him, compiling his exploits for shooting Japan into a song. Her heart was sweet and heartwarming. Later, he wanted to expand his power and turn the southeast, northwest, and four sides into his world. He brought his troops to attack a few small surrounding countries. As soon as those small countries heard that they had shot at Japan, they surrendered because they were too scared to fight. After taking over a few small neighboring countries, his ambitions grew even greater, so he went to attack Emperor Mudu of the East. This Emperor Mude of the East is no different; it is Fu Xi of the Snake Head, who is far more capable than Hou Yi. Hou Yi and Fu Xi fought a war in the East China Sea and were beaten to the bone. Hou Yi came back from defeat in the East China Sea and was depressed. He was too lazy to take care of the Korean affairs. He wanted Chang'e to go hunting with him all day long. Within a few days, he didn't like to fight, so he had fun shooting people with arrows. He steamed and ate those who had been shot down, whether alive or dead. As a result, Hou Yi became the devil who ate people. People hide when they see him, and they all curse him to die quickly.
When Hou Yi learned that there is a type of Ganoderma lucidum in the West that can last a long time after eating it, he sent many people to find Ganoderma lucidum. Someone actually got that Ganoderma lucidum back. Hou Yi handed Ganoderma lucidum to Chang'e and said they would eat it together when he chose a lucky day.
Also, Chang'e. Originally, she was used to living the days of fairies in heaven. She never got used to being in a mortal world with Hou Yi; she always complained about Hou Yi in her heart. If I didn't marry him, how could I go to the mortal world to be sinned? Later, when I saw that Hou Yi was getting more and more ferocious, I was even more dissatisfied with him. Sometimes she wanted to persuade Hou Yi to do good deeds and gain virtue, but before she finished speaking, Hou Yi made a loud noise and couldn't listen to them at all. After Chang'e got Ganoderma lucidum, she thought to herself, Hou Yi, now you have become a tyrant. Don't do good things, do all the bad things. People want you to die sooner; do you still want to live forever? If you really live forever, you won't have a good life on earth! She quietly ran to the mountain and hid a poisonous mushroom about the same as Ganoderma lucidum.
On this day, Hou Yi called Chang'e to take out the Ganoderma lucidum, and Chang'e gave him the poisonous mushroom. He wanted to swallow it alone, and he also wanted Chang'e to go to the throne with him. To put it bluntly, let Chang'e eat with him. When Chang'e arrived, he said that he was the king of a country, so let him eat it alone first, then send someone to eat it the next day. Later, Yiba was not allowed to do this; he picked up the poisonous mushroom and swallowed it. After a while, I saw him rolling on the ground with a blue face and a stomach ache. At this point, Chang'e took out the Ganoderma lucidum and said to Hou Yi, “Hou Yi, you and I have finally had a match. I want you to understand. What you just ate wasn't Ganoderma lucidum; it was a poisonous mushroom I picked for you. You've done all bad things; keeping you is a scourge. The relationship between you and my husband comes to an end today. True Ganoderma lucidum is here, watch me eat it!” After speaking, she ate Ganoderma lucidum one by one, and slowly ascended to heaven.
Hou Yi was fooled by Chang'e and tried to shoot her down with a bow and arrow. Unbeknownst to him, it was toxic. He was no longer able to pull the bow, and fell to the ground, bleeding to death.
