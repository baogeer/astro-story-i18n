Wu Sangui's rush to the crown and fury
After entering Beijing, Chen Yuanyuan became Tian Hongyu's family actor. Tian Hongyu gradually lost power due to the death of his noble wife. In order to consolidate his position and find support in the troubled world, he intended to befriend Wu Sangui, who was very famous at the time and had a strong army. Tian Hongyu once invited Wu Sangui to his family dinner. “They were all outstanding. A person who wears light makeup, takes all the beauty before everyone else, and is passionate and charming.” And this light-looking diva is Chen Yuanyuan. Wu Sangui was amazed by Chen Yuanyuan's beauty, “Unbeknownst to him, he was distracted” (Lu Ciyun's “The Legend of Yuan Yuan”). At the request of the Three Gui Gui, Tian Hong gifted Yuanyuan to Wu Sangui, bought a rich makeup kit, and sent it to Wu Fu. After Li Zicheng's peasant army occupied Beijing, Yuanyuan was taken by Liu Zongmin. Wu Sangui originally wanted to surrender to the peasant army, but after learning that Yuanyuan had been robbed, he was furious and relented. “Ming History. “Ryukou” said, “In the beginning, the Sangui Fengzhao enlisted in Shanhaiguan, and the Beijing Division fell and did not hesitate to advance.
