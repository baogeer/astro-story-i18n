 There was a scholar from the Han Dynasty named Liu Yanchang (Liu Xiang). When he went to Beijing to rush for the exam, he visited Mount Hua along the way. There is a temple on Mount Huashan. The Three Madonna of the Temple Gods (in one word, three daughters) is a beautiful and kind fairy. Since being sent to Huashan by the royal mother, she has lived a lonely and lonely life. On this day, while singing and dancing in the temple, she suddenly discovered that a scholar had walked through the temple gate. She hurriedly ascended to the lotus throne and turned into a statue. Walking into the main hall, Liu Yanchang saw the beautiful, gentle, and laid-back statue of the Three Madonna at a glance. Liu Yanchang was deeply fascinated and wanted to be happy to be able to marry her as a wife! Unfortunately, this is just a statue with no sense of emotion. With deep regret, Liu Yanchang was unable to contain his inner excitement. He took out his pen and ink and affectionately expressed his love for the Three Notre Dame on the wall.
The Three Madonna watched all this in silence, and couldn't help but feel mixed in her heart. The scholar in front of me was so handsome and well-literate. He was deeply attached to himself, yet why wasn't he deeply attracted to him, and why didn't he fall in love with him? However, if one is a fairy from above and the other is a mortal below, how can a marriage be formed? Seeing Liu Yanchang go away in despair and relentlessly, the Three Notre Dame can no longer calm down. She groaned over and over again, and finally decided to become a couple with Liu Yanchang despite Tiantian prohibitions. As a result, the Three Madonna turned into a folk woman, caught up with Liu Yanchang and revealed her true feelings to him. As a result, the two became attached to each other and became husband and wife, and were extremely affectionate. Liu Yanchang's exam period is approaching. The Three Noble Mother is already pregnant. When she said goodbye, Liu Yanchang gifted the Three Saints a piece of ancestral agarwood incense, saying that having children in the future can be called “incense.” The two were sent ten miles to each other, and it was hard to be separated.
Liu Yanchang hit the list in Beijing and was appointed as the Yangzhou Provincial Patrol Officer. Just as he took office, the Three Notre Dame was in distress. It turns out that this was the birthday of the royal mother and daughter. The Peach Festival was held in Tiangong. Immortals from all walks of life came to celebrate their birthday, but when the Three Madonna were pregnant, they got rid of the infection and stayed in Huashan. Who knows, the true feelings were discovered by Jiro God, the older brother of the Three Blessed Virgin. Jiro God was furious and blamed his sister for marrying a mortal person privately, violating the rules of heaven, and being punished by heaven. The Three Noble Madonna is righteous and fearless. Furthermore, she also carries with her a treasure, the Lotus Lantern. This item is the treasure of the Three Notre Dame's Zhenshan Mountains. No matter which demon or immortal side, as long as the Lotus Lantern shines brightly, they will be deterred and admired, and they will be taken captive. Jiro knew he was invincible, so he stole his tengu while resting on the Three Madonna. In this way, the poor Three Madonna was crushed by the god Jiro in Hei Yun Cave under Mount Hua. The Three Madonna gave birth to her son Agarwood in a dark cave. To prevent an accident, she secretly begged Ya Huan to send her son to Yangzhou and stay with her father Liu Yanchang.
When Agarwood grew up, she gradually became sensible. After learning that her mother was being crushed and suffering under Mount Hua, she wanted to save her Three Notre Dame. He shared his thoughts with his father. Helplessly, Liu Yanchang was just an illiterate scholar; he only sighed and shook his head. So Agarwood left home alone and went to see her mother. After much hard work, he finally reached Mount Huashan. But where's the mother? This eight-year-old kid was overwhelmed and started crying loudly. Terrible and painful cries echoed in the empty valley, shocking the Thunderbolt Daxian who passed by here. The kind-hearted fairy asked for the cause of her love. The Three Blessed Virgin, who was deeply kind, and the suffering child were unhappy, but there was nothing they could do. So he brought agarwood back to his place. Under the direction of the immortal, Agarwood studied hard and earnestly, and gradually learned six strategies, a hundred martial arts, and seventy-three changes. On her 16th birthday, Agarwood resigned from her master and wanted to go to Huashan to save her mother. Daisen claimed that he had ambition and gave him a Kayaka Kaisan Axe (it is said that Jiro used this magic axe to save his mother back then).
Agarwood drived in the fog and arrived in front of Hei Yun Cave in Huashan Mountain. He called out to his mother, and the sound penetrated the heavy rock layers and reached the ears of the Three Madonna. The Three Notre Dame couldn't help but feel a sense in her heart. She knew that her son had grown up and had a sense of filial piety to save herself. Excited, she brought incense to the front of the cave. The Three Noble Madonna knows that her older brother Jiro has great abilities, and even Sun Wukong, who made a big fuss in the Tiangong back then, couldn't help him; incense was still young, and Jiro God also stole the Lotus Lantern. How could her son be an opponent? Therefore, the Three Noble Madonna called Agarwood to fall in love with her uncle.
Incense flew to Jiro's temple and prayed bitterly for Jiro. Who knows that Jiro has an ironstone heart. Not only did he refuse to let go of the Three Madonna, but instead, he danced a three-pointed, two-edged sword, and wanted to attack agarwood. Agarwood was furious and felt that Jiro had lied too much, so she threw up a magic axe and fought with him. In the clouds and fog, the two went back and forth; in the mountains and water, they became dragons and fish; they killed from the sky to the ground, and then slaughtered back to Tiangong; they directly killed the mountains and the ground shook and the river turned the sea, and the sky was dark. I was so shocked by this incident that I sent four immortals to find out. The Four Immortals watched in the clouds for a while, and felt that Jiro was an uncle, treating a child so harshly was so ruthless and unjust. As a result, they looked at each other, and secretly helped Agarwood. The more you fight incense, the more brave it is. The Vietnam War god, Jiro God, couldn't help but run away in the desert, and the lotus lantern fell into the hands of agarwood.
Agarwood immediately flew back to Mount Huashan, raised Xuanhua Kaisan's magic axe, and fought hard to attack. I only heard a loud “roar”. The earth shook the mountain, and Mount Huashan split. Agarwood quickly found Black Cloud Cave and rescued her mother. After 16 years of suffering, the Three Notre Dame saw the sun again. She held her son tightly, mixed feelings, and burst into tears.
Later, Jiro Jin also confessed his mistake to the Three Notre Dame and Agarwood; Agarwood was also crowned by Emperor Jade. Since then, the Three Saints, Liu Yanchang, and their heroic son, Incense, have been reunited and have lived happily together forever.
