 The Prairie Tourism Festival was held simultaneously from July 15 to August 30 in several grasslands in Namuda, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region. It mainly focuses on events such as horse racing, Mani meetings, Ordos weddings, and memorial services for Genghis Khan's tomb. At that time, you can also watch ethnic customs shows, enjoy the beautiful scenery of the prairie, and visit herdsmen's homes. Experience the open-minded and exuberant temperament of the herders.
From July to August, the Guizhou Famous Wine Festival was held in Zunyi City, Maotai Town in Renhuai City, and Shizhangdong in Chishui City in Guizhou Province, respectively. The event mainly included visiting the Zunyi Wine Culture Museum, Maotai Distillery, the Zunyi Conference Site, Chishui Shizhangdong Waterfall... But for drinkers, the most appealing thing is that they can try famous local wines all over.
The Tibetan Shetton Festival means yogurt feast in Tibetan. The Shetton Festival will be held in August in Lhasa, Tibet. It offers visitors exciting programs such as basking the Big Buddha and traditional Tibetan opera performances.
The Xinjiang Grape Festival was held in Turfan, Xinjiang in late August. It entertains guests with beautiful Uyghur songs and dances, and hosts fun cantaloupe matches, grape tastings, etc.
The Qingdao International Beer Festival was held in Qingdao, Shandong in mid-August. It carries out a series of cultural, economic and trade activities, and beer technology displays and exchanges around beer, and is sure to be an eye-opener for beer enthusiasts.
The Jiuhua Temple Fair was held in Jiuhua Mountain, Anhui in late August (July 30 of the lunar calendar). It mainly focuses on activities such as the Land and Water Festival, Buddha statues illuminated, sutras with high priests, and the folk “Baizi Meeting”. Visitors can also visit the beauty of Mount Jiuhua.
The Qiangtang Jockey Club was held in late August in the Nagqu region of Tibet. It is mainly ethnic horse racing, archery competitions, and equestrian shows. It is exciting, and it is a must-see for those who want to enjoy the Tibetan style to the fullest.
