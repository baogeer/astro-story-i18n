 Turning over the 5,000 year history of the Chinese nation, we can see that there are many female heroes who have defended the land, protected the country and the people, and made immortal exploits. Among these star-studded female heroes in Chinese history, there was a leader of an ethnic minority who was called “Lingnan Merry” and “An Excellent Model” by later generations. Premier Zhou Enlai also highly praised her as “the first female hero in our country's history.” The leader of this ethnic minority is Mrs. Qiao, an outstanding female politician and military figure in ancient China who experienced the Liang, Chen, and Sui dynasties.
Mrs. Qiao Guo is also known as Mrs. Xian. Mrs. Xian lived in the Liang, Chen, and Sui dynasties, and was a famous ethnic minority leader through the ages. She dedicated her whole life to maintaining the unity and unity of the motherland and opposing rebellion, plunder, and corruption. She was forward-looking, brave and resourceful, defended the land, protected the country and the people, maintained peace and stability in the Lingnan region for 50 years, and promoted national integration and local economic development.
Mrs. Xian is the daughter of the Xian clan in the Lingnan region. During Emperor Liang Wu of the Southern Dynasty, she married Feng Bao, Taishou of Gaoliang County as his wife. During the Northern and Southern Dynasties, the Central Plains were destroyed and military disasters linked, and the Lingnan region was never affected by the flames of war. First, it was probably because Lingnan was still not a part of the country, and then it was true that Mrs. Lai Xian had plans and plans to soothe the people. After her death, she was also banished as a “Honest Lady.”
According to “The Biography of Lie Nu” in “Sui Shu,” “Mrs. Qiao Guo is also a daughter of the Gao Liang Xian clan. He is the leader of South Vietnam. He has straddled a mountain cave with over 100,000 tribes.” The Lingnan Xian clan was originally a tribal leader with 100,000 households, and crossed the mountainous regions of Enping and Yangjiang in Guangdong. Not only was she able to slay the sword and fight with enemies, but she was also deeply involved in the method of marching. In August of the 2nd year of Emperor Liang Wu, Hou Jing rebelled in Shouyang and collaborated with King Linhe Xiao Zhengde to encircle Emperor Liang Wu in a small city. As soon as Guangzhou Governor Xiao Bo learned about the military situation, he quickly recruited troops to help, but Gaozhou assassin Li Qianshi raised an army to rebel.
