 Lu You is a leader of the Jiuli tribe. I've heard that Luyou's army was made up of his 981 brothers. They all had bronze heads and iron arms. They were all very powerful; they were simply the elite of the world.
After Tenjin learned about this incident, he felt that it was very unfair. If one day, Luyou and they felt that they wanted to bully the common people, even if the common people were to resist, Luyou would not be their opponent. Therefore, only we have the power to solve him. Failure to solve him is simply a disaster for the world, one disaster after another for the people!
Therefore, Tenjin wrote a challenge book and gave it to Lu You. Because Tenjin felt that they were definitely no match for my army, he had three days to prepare. After receiving the challenge, Luyou summoned his brother to discuss countermeasures.
Three days later, Tenjin came with an army, and Lu You also brought an army. At the beginning, Tenjin sent Rain God. Rain God first sent Rain God. However, Luyou's army didn't retreat and rushed directly up to defeat Rain God. Second, Tenjin sent God of Fog. Tenjin wanted to win victory by making Luyou's army invisible. However, Luyou also used a compass, causing the god of fog to be defeated.
When Tenjin saw this scene, he was furious. When Lu You saw it, they set off rockets. Tenjin just wanted to retreat, but was intercepted by Luyou's army. Fortunately, Tenjin's army arrived, and Tenjin successfully retreated. However, in this war, Tenjin lost troops and generals, and lost a lot of money.
Tenjin's subordinates said to him, “Although we have many troops and are strong, and we have an absolute advantage to win, we were defeated just because His Majesty was too proud and underestimated them!” After Tenjin listened to it, he humbly accepted it.
After Tenjin, there are no more challenges with Chi You!
