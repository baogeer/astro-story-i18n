 At the end of Chong Zhen's year, Wu Sangui brought Qing soldiers into customs and went south to attack Li Zicheng. Li Zicheng withdrew from Beijing in defeat. For a while, war raged, and refugees poured in from north to south like a tide. Tens of thousands of refugees gather along the north-south main road in Guizhou County. These refugees have no money to buy food, so they have to spend their days begging, and the front of their families is often overcrowded. Strangely enough, not a single refugee dared to beg in front of the house of the great man, Wen Shaogui.
There are always exceptions. On this day, Wen Shaogui opened the house and found a beggar in his 20s lying asleep on the straw heap in front of the door. Wen Shaogui approached and kicked the beggar and said, “Go away!” Who knows, the beggar ignored it, turned over and continued to snore.
Not bothered, Wen Shaogui turned back to the yard and pulled out a dog. This fruit is called “Wangcai”. It is of Tibetan mastiff descent. It is three feet tall, tall, and has a strong body, and spits out its teeth. It looks like a devil that eats people. Turns out, refugees were afraid to go to Wen Shaogui's house to beg because of this evil dog. As the saying goes, “A dog sees people as low; they bite the poor, not the rich.” Wang Cai bit at least 20 beggars. If it's light, it breaks his skin; if it's heavy, it's broken. County officials have all run away in this troubled world; how can anyone handle these matters. As a result, the beggars spread to ten, and ten to hundred. The family all knew that Wen Shaogui's family had an evil dog, and no one dared to beg in front of his house again.
Wen Shaogui pulled Wangcai to the door. Strangely enough, Wangcai didn't do the same thing as before. He rushed out and bit the beggar, smashed his nose, pinched his tail, and didn't move at all.
Seeing that Wang Cai refused to go out, Wen Shaogui had no choice but to grab the collar around his neck and pull it hard. Unexpectedly, Wang Cai actually competed with Wen Shaogui and didn't want to leave the yard alive. Wen Shaogui got angry and dragged Wangcai out of the yard until he was in front of the beggars. What stunned Wen Shaogui was that when Wang Cai saw the beggar, he actually fell down. His two claws were lying in front of the beggar, his head was attached to his claws, and he shivered all over his body.
“What happened to this dog?” Wen Shaogui was baffled; vaguely felt that there must be some mystery in it. Wangcai was given to Wen Shaogui by a friend. The friend said that Wangcai is very clever and can identify people. At the time, Wen Shaogui didn't believe it, so she changed into the servant's thick cloth clothes, then asked the servant to wear his own Chinese costume and came to Wang Cai. Wang Cai was really powerful. He barked at his servant more than once, but he rubbed his head on Wen Shaogui to please him. Only then did Wen Shaogui believe him. After that, I tried it a few more times, and it worked again and again.
Wangcaishman has never made a mistake, but now he is lying in front of a beggar and shivering in horror... Isn't this person in front of him a real beggar? Wen Shaogui suddenly remembered that he had a party with the townspeople not long ago. He heard that Li Zicheng had destroyed the city of Beijing, and that many members of the royal family fled south for their lives and fell to the people. Is it true that this person in front of you is out of character?
Thinking about this, Wen Shaogui took another careful look at the beggar in front of him. He only saw that his fingers were slender and his skin was white. At first glance, he was not a hard worker. Let's take another look at Wang Cai's posture. He lies on the ground with his front paws and his head resting on his claws. Isn't that a position of bowing his head and kneeling? Is it true that the person in front of you is actually a descendant of Ming's royal family and a grandson of Fengzi Dragon?
Thinking of this, Wen Shaogui hurriedly woke up the beggar: “Wake up quickly and stay warm in the house with me.”
The beggar rubbed his eyes, took a look at Wen Shaogui, and said, “Forget it, I'll go.” When Wen Shaogui heard the beggar speak in pure Beijing language, he couldn't help but feel a little more sure about his own judgment.
“This army is insane, where can you go? Just stay in my house.” Wen Shaogui kept them over and over again, dragged the beggars into the house, and let the servants serve them with good wine and good meat. Faced with a full table full of meals, the beggars were unkind. On the sidelines, Wen Shaogui carefully observed the beggar's eating behavior, only to see that he was a native rat. After eating, Wen Shaogui knocked on the sidelines and asked, “May I ask your last name, sir, where is it high?”
The beggar hesitated a bit and said, “My name is Wei Wei. I am a native of Cangzhou, Hebei. I have always cultivated land at home, but due to years of war and turmoil, I had no choice but to wander south and live in the world.”
Wen Shaogui listened to Wei Wei's elegant language and couldn't help but nod his head secretly. After seeing him meditate for half a day before saying this, he knew he was saying a lie. Yes, the military is in turmoil right now. If you reveal your identity, it will probably immediately cause trouble to your upper body. Wen Shaogui secretly thought: The current situation is complicated, and it is difficult to clearly see victory or defeat. Although Ming is gradually declining, it actually has a 300-year foundation. If he leaves this person behind, once Ming regains his power, he is a hero...
As a result, Wen Shaogui left Wei at home and treated him every day with good wine and good food. On this day, Wei Wei was intoxicated by Wen Shaogui. While he was confused, Wen Shaogui asked him carefully about his identity again in his ear. I only heard Wei Wei say drunkenly, “I am Prince Zhu...”, and he fell drunk to the ground before his words fell to the ground.
After hearing it, Wen Shaogui secretly lost his joy; he couldn't help but have other plans. It turns out that he is nearly 50 years old and has no children. He has only one daughter, named Wen Fengying. At the beginning of the year, Grandpa Xian's son had a crush on Wen Fengying and sent a medic to talk to his family. Wen Shaogui graciously agreed. He has already accepted the wedding, but he hasn't gone through the door yet. Unexpectedly, last month, the county grandpa heard that the Qing soldiers had gone south and was so scared that they left the county office. The whole family fled in a hurry. There is no news yet.
Wen Shaogui thought that instead of treating her daughter Xu as the son of a county governor, she should seize the opportunity in front of her and rise to the royal family. At that time, even if the county grandpa had returned and was a relative of the imperial family, he would have no choice but to take himself.
After making a good calculation, Wen Shaogui found her daughter and expressed her own thoughts. As soon as Wen Fengying heard, her father actually repented and wanted to marry a beggar. She immediately burst into tears and refused to agree at all. When Wen Shaogui saw the situation, she made up her mind and said to her daughter, “Alas, Feng Ying, let me tell you the truth. Your fiance's whole family was killed by robbers as early as half a month ago. I found his body and buried it. I never dared to tell you; I'm afraid you'll be sad. This is a troubled world. Looking for a husband for you is also afraid that something will happen to me and your mom; after all, there will be a man to protect you!”
After hearing this remark, Wen Fengying cried for half a day, and finally had to agree. Wen Shaogui was afraid that the gossip would change in the middle, so she took advantage of the heat to host a wedding for Wei Wei and her daughter, and let the two go into a cave room.
After Wen Fengying and Wei Wei became a couple, they were also quite affectionate. However, Wen Fengying was always stubborn and blamed her father for marrying a beggar. Wen Shaogui quietly said to her daughter, “What do you know, Wei Wei's real identity is the imposing Prince Zhu. It's just that this is a troubled world, and if you ask him, he won't admit it.” While talking, I also told my daughter about Wangcai's actions. Wen Fengying also knows that Wangcai is an intelligent person; she had no choice but to believe her father's words.
Things often went beyond people's expectations. Wen Shaogui wholeheartedly hoped that Ming would rebuild the country. Unexpectedly, the Qing army was invincible, and soon invaded Guizhou County. Overnight, the Ming Guizhou County became the Qing Guizhou County. Wen Shaogui originally wanted to be part of the royal family, but he didn't expect Wei Wei to become a burden now; he had to knock down his front teeth and swallow his blood. What he didn't expect even more was that after more than two years, the world gradually became peaceful. Liu Qinghao, the son of the county governor, came back and said he wanted to marry Wen Fengying. Wen Shaogui had no choice but to tell the other party that she thought he had died and had married her daughter to someone else.
Liu Qinghao was speechless. However, he didn't give up on Moon Fung-ying, so he spent money to buy through a family. After getting the news out of the blue, Jia Ding finally made an appointment with Wen Fengying. Seeing her former fiance, Wen Fengying learned that her father had deceived her and couldn't help but cry. She brought out her father's thoughts and plans, and also revealed Wei Wei's identity. Liu Qinghao made Wen Feng-ying run off with herself, but Moon Fung-ying cried and said, “Marry a chicken and marry a dog. This has come to this point; I have to spend the rest of my life with Wei Wei.”
Liu Qinghao listened and was outraged. According to the etiquette of the time, Moon Fung-ying was originally Liu Qinghao's wife who never went through the door. She was dressed in a green hat for no reason. Liu Qinghao couldn't help it. Suddenly, he thought: Didn't Wen Fengying say Wei Wei was Prince Zhu? Even though Emperor Shunzhi has pardoned the world now, he will definitely not let go of the Ming dynasty. Thinking of this, Liu Qinghao grew evil out of courage and went straight to the Guizhou County Office to file a lawsuit against the county commissioner. As soon as the county commissioner heard that this had happened, he was shocked and immediately sent someone to take Wei Wei and Wen Shaogui to the county office.
Hearing that Wei Wei was Prince Zhu's third prince had been revealed, Wen Shaogui couldn't help but look up to the sky and sigh: “Heaven is going to destroy me!” Unexpectedly, Wei Wei shouted, “Wrong, wrong! The villagers are nothing more than Prince Zhu's cooks. After Li Zicheng entered Beijing, I fled Beijing with Prince Zhu. I didn't expect to get lost along the way and went into exile in Guizhou County.”
The county commissioner asked, “Then tell me, why didn't the Wenjia fortune bite you?”
Wei Wei smiled bitterly: “Actually, the little one was just a dog butcher. They have been killing dogs for generations and have done a good dog meat feast. It's already the 10th generation here for me. Prince Zhu is addicted to dog meat, so he recruited me to Fuchu to become his full-time cook. After entering the province, I rarely did physical work, so I grew lean and tender, and I saw some scenes. This time they were exiled to Guizhou County. Hunger was intolerable. I heard that Wenjia's dog likes to bite people, so they tried to trick them into killing them. I didn't expect Mu to be really smart. He found out that I killed dogs, so he lay on the ground and asked for forgiveness, but was misunderstood by my father-in-law.”
Hearing this, there was still some disbelief in the prefectural order, which made people take a bad dog from the backyard. Sure enough, when the vicious dog saw Wei Wei, he was lying on the ground and afraid to move. At this point, the county governor completely believed it, so Wei Wei was released on the spot.
Wen Shaogui saved her life and secretly called “luck” in her heart. However, it also spread that he relied on his dog to choose a butcher's son-in-law. The family all smiled and said, “Only then did Wen Shaogui really grow a pair of eyes.”
