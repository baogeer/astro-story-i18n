 Once upon a time, in the Wuyou region of later Tibet, there was a person named Kanggadewa. He acted as his father outside and had fun everywhere; he left his wife Sam Jumma at home and ignored him all year round. Sammjuma was in great pain and secretly fell in love with Mafudan in the estate.
One day, while drinking horses at the well, Sam Jumma came by and grabbed the rope to fetch water and wanted to make fun of him. The next dan got anxious and sang:
Ajasam Jumma,
Please let it go, let it go!
I'm not an old man or young man, but a slave who herds horses.
There are not one or two horses, but 3,600 horses.
Sam Jumma quickly put down the barrel rope and sang:
My brother is second time, huh,
Please don't get mad, don't get mad!
I don't want any old man or young man,
I want to be with you.
Who knows, just in time for Kangadewa to return. Hearing a song sung by Sam Jumma, he said to Zidan indiscriminately, “Bad boy, how dare you molest my wife when I'm not here. Look, your brother is my steward, and my younger brother is my Morimoto. I'll give you three days to go home, say anything, and do whatever you need to do. Today one day, tomorrow, when the sun rises the next morning, come see me at the manor house.
I felt very aggrieved the second day, but I couldn't explain it clearly to the old man, even if you had a thousand mouths. When he got home, he stayed with his grandmother for two days, thought about it with his head down for two days, and smoked for two days. By the time the star rose on the third day, the next day she woke up early, washed clean, and sang to Grandma from the room:
Grandma heh grandma,
Please give my horse some water,
I won't be bothering you again for the rest of my life!
Grandma heh grandma,
Please feed my horse.
I won't be bothering you again for the rest of my life!
After listening to his song, Grandma was very puzzled; she knew that her son had a bad temper and didn't want to ask too many questions, so she drank and fed him food. The next day I came to the yard, I sang to Grandma again:
Grandma, grandma,
Please take out my holiday clothes.
I haven't worn them before, so what am I going to wear today!
Grandma, grandma,
Please take out my engagement ring,
I haven't worn it before; I want to wear it today!
The next day, I put the holiday clothes and engagement ring in the same way in the horse and got ready to go on the horse. Who knew his horse was rolling on the ground, but he wouldn't get up at all. The next time, pat the horse on the back and sing:
OK, Marolin, go ahead,
Don't sleep, clear up.
Otherwise, we won't be able to catch up with the good times!
The horse climbed up from the ground and rode up the next day. The horse kept cutting its hooves, and tears fell like rain. The next time, they touched the horse's mane and sang;
OK, Marolin makes a deal, 'OK,
Don't cry, please be happy,
Otherwise, my heart would be even saddened!
At this point, Grandma really couldn't hold back. She hugged Cidan's leg and asked in tears:
Next time, next time,
Did you steal something?
Are you telling a lie?
If you don't tell Grandma if you don't say anything, who do you talk to?
If you don't talk to Grandma, who are you going to talk to?
Next dan held Grandma's hand, touched his forehead three times, and sadly answered:
Grandma, grandma,
Your son hasn't stolen the east side.
Your son never told a lie;
It's just Ajay Samjuma,
You sang a song to me!
As soon as Grandma heard it, she was so frightened that she didn't wake up in a long time.
When the sun rose from the snowy mountain, he rode to the boss's manor the next day. The old man Kanggadewa was waiting for him at the wheat farm, and the surrounding people were also invited to watch. Kangadwawei sat down on the mat and ordered Zidan's older brother, who was the housekeeper, and his younger brother, who was an attendant, to kill Zidan by hand.
I had already anticipated this day the next day, and I laughed “hehe”. He opened his horse basket, took out his clothes for the holiday season, and sang:
This hidden robe with an otter skin edge,
For the brother of a compatriot, you;
These new pants,
Leave it to my closest brother to wear.
Please both of you kill me,
That's a bit of processing money.
This golden hat,
For the brother of a compatriot, you;
These embroidered hidden boots,
Leave it to my closest brother to wear.
Please do it with the two of you,
That's a bit of compensation.
After speaking, the next day, I took out a dagger, a ring, and sang:
This sharp knife,
Give it to the old man to make a self-propelled knife,
This gemstone ring,
Give it to Sam Jumma as an engagement ring.
Thank you, old man, for the good arrangement.
The next time I run out of words, I won't say anything else!
As soon as Cidan's song was finished, Kangadewatou called Cidan's older brother and younger brother, and killed him by hand; blood flowed in front of the manor, and the body was left in the Brahmaputra River. The common people all said, “After all, death is a blunder! ”
After his death, his soul became a bird and flew around the estate all day long. One day, Grandpa Kangadewa was riding a horse across the bridge, and a bird flew out from under the bridge, which surprised him to jump around. The old man fell off his horse, and a sharp knife around his waist just got into his chest and stabbed him to death.
Furthermore, when Sam Jumma learned that she had been killed the next time, she was anxious, scared, and seriously ill and couldn't get up while lying on the mat. Suddenly, I saw a small bird flying on a branch outside the window and singing:
good luck! good luck!
I'm a bird that changes from time to time, hehe,
The old man just died on the bridge, hehe,
Ah Jia, what are you thinking? good luck.
As soon as Sam Jumma heard, his illness was more than half better. He held out his hands and said:
Little bird, little bird!
If you're really stubbornly, little bird,
Please fall on my shoulder, little bird,
Please fall on my lap, little bird.
The bird is certainly very humane. As soon as it flew over Samjuma's shoulder and then on her lap, Sammjuma held the bird with both hands and gently caressed it with her face. At this point, the bird sang again:
Hi, hey,
If you really love me, hehe,
Please pack me in a box, hehe,
I'll see you on the 15th night of January, good luck.
Sammjuma did according to its orders. By the night of the 15th of the first month, when a full moon was rising from the snowy mountain, she opened the box and took a look. Mafu Cidan was coming out of the box with a smile. He was stronger and more beautiful than before.
