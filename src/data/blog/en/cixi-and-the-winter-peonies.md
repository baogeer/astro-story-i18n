 At the end of the Qing Dynasty, Empress Dowager Cixi was authoritarian, assertive, and whatever she wanted. She imitated Wu Zetian, the emperor of the Tang dynasty, and made peonies bloom in winter.
In the middle of the winter season, Cixi was tired of seeing Beijing's traditional flowers such as plum and begonia, and wanted to see the magnificent peonies. One day, Cixi asked her subordinate, “Where in the world has the best peonies?” The ministers unanimously said, “The peonies in Caozhou, Shandong are the best, and the world is famous.” The Empress Dowager Dowager ordered the Caozhou Government to pay tribute to peonies. The sooner the better, the better, without delay.
The Governor of Caozhou was both afraid and happy when it received the order. What I'm afraid of is that peonies bloom in spring. It's a whimsical thing that goes against the seasons; the Empress Dowager blames her life; the good news is that the small governor, in the eyes of the Empress Dowager, was an unknown junior. Today, the Dowager Dowager is making a big splash. If she can make progress on schedule and get her what she wants, she will definitely be relegated to the post, and the blue clouds will rise straight ahead. This is a blessing from all over the world. The prefectural government weighed in, stepped aside, seized the opportunity, and quickly accepted the plan. So they sent people to Peony Township overnight.
Officials from Caozhou Province announced to the flower owners in Peony Township that in order to present blooming Caozhou peonies to the imperial palace, flowers must be grown within January. There are many rewards for the success of the project; if they don't grow, all the peonies in Peony Township will be cut out!
As the saying goes, “Gu Yu saw peonies in the three dynasties.” Where can peonies bloom now in the harsh winter months, ice and snow? Flower farmers are worried day and night, and no one can think of a good solution. Some old flower farmers have heard their elders say that there used to be a way to bake flowers in a fire place, but it hasn't been used for many years. It's lost, and no one knows it anymore. The government also knows that there are no peonies in winter, and they also send people to pressure them every day, making them uneasy to sit back and lie down.
There is an old flower farmer in Wanhua Village who has been growing flowers for many years. They know all about the characteristics of each type of peony. If you look at the roots in winter, you can tell what kind of peony it is. He has heard old people talk about roasting peonies in winter, but he has never seen it with his own eyes, let alone practiced it himself. Now, in order to overcome the disaster in Peony Township, he is determined to experiment with baking flowers. They led the whole family to break through the cellar, make pots in the cellar, plant peonies in the pot, put cow dung on it, and heat it.
Certainly, the effort was worth the effort. In a few days, I baked two big red leaves. Good news broke. Men, women, and children from Peony Township clapped their hands and cheered, and sent the dried big huhong to the Caozhou government house. The prefectural government introduced peonies as scheduled; indeed, the official fortune was successful, and the blue clouds rose. The thousand-mu peony in Peony Township was also spared a disaster of extinction.
In Yu Pengnian's “Caozhou Peony Spectrum: Supplementary Provisions,” there is also a record about roasting flowers: “Today, there are three types of flowers in Caozhou that can be burned: Hu's Red, or White, and Purple Crown Group.”
