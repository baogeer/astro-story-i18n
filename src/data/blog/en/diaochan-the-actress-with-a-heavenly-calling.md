 Diao Chan is not recorded in ancient books, but is often described in literary works. He was an important figure in the dispute between the Three Kingdoms at the end of the Han Dynasty. Luo Guanzhong further described and portrayed Diao Chan in “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms”, which had a great influence. Folklore is particularly legendary, and is almost a well-known female superstar known to women and children. According to scholar Meng Fanren's research, the novel's character is Diao Chan, whose surname is Ren, in the small letters Hongchang, was born in Mu'er Village, Jiuyuan County, Bingzhou County. At the age of 15, he took charge of the Diao Chan (hat for a Han Dynasty servant official) worn by the courtier, and has since changed his name to Diao Chan. The court was in turmoil at the end of the Han Dynasty, and Diao Chan left the palace and was taken as a righteous daughter by Situ Wang Yun. Soon Dong Zhuo had exclusive power. Wang Yun used Dong and Lu to make Diao Chan carry out a “serial plan,” which finally prompted Lu Bu to kill Dong Zhuo and make a merit. After that, Diao Chan became Lu Bu's concubine. Lu Bu of Bai Men Lou died, and Cao Cao repeated the “serial plan” with the Taoyuan brothers, so he gave it to Guan Yu. In order not to harm the Taoyuan brothers, Diao Chan “led her neck and prayed for death”. She was protected by Guan Yu and escaped and became a nun. Cao Cao arrested Diao Chan after learning about it, and Diao Chan resolutely killed himself by throwing a sword. We can't imagine how the laid-back, graceful and graceful Diao Chan, who has the appearance of a “closed moon,” takes to the edge of the sword. It's been an earth-shattering number of years for Diao Chan to “not hesitate to use his golden body, why be afraid of dangerous students”.
There have been many great dramas. The main character is a young girl with a thin face, and the appearance of a closed moon has gone to waste.
Two powerful men acted as supporting characters. One gave power to the world, and the other was invincible, yet one woman's acting skills left her bewildered and bewitched.
History has given her the role of an actor. An actor has no choice; they must complete the role of an actor according to the director's consciousness.
Glamorous eyes ignited Jiang's jealousy. Cao Cao repeated the “serial plan” to wreck the three Taoyuan brothers. However, the female tycoon brought the conspiracy of a generation of emperors to nothing; a woman became an actor, and many tragedies were unfolding on the historical stage.
The drama has been going away for a thousand years, yet so many people still can't get out of the loud sound of drums...
