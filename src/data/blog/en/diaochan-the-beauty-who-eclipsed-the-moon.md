 After the Yellow Scarf Uprising, the Eastern Han dynasty survived. Dong Zhuo covered the sky with one hand and maimed loyalty, causing widespread dissatisfaction among the courtiers. However, his righteous son Lu Bu had excellent martial arts and was a barrier for Dong Zhuo. The courtiers all dared to get angry and afraid to speak up. Archbishop Wang Yun worried day and night about this. One day he was walking in his garden without showing any sadness. He overheard someone sigh secretly. In the past, he saw that it was Diao Chan. Wang Yun asked why Diao Chan lamented. Diao Chan said, “I am a Montesan who took me in, and I have never been able to repay your favor. Seeing you day and night now, I can't share my worries with you, so I sigh.
Wang Yun heard her say this, and also took a close look at her perfect appearance under the moon, taking it to heart. So he summoned Diao Chan into the house and said something, frightening Diao Chan from being able to speak for a long time, but in order to repay her kindness, she promised to act according to Wang Yun's plan.
The next day, Wang Yun invited someone to give Lu Bu a golden crown and invited Lu Bu to his home to be treated warmly. Lu Bu was very happy. After three rounds of drinking, Diao Chan slowly walked out of the inner room. Lu Bu's eyes lit up and made a big fuss. Wang Yun took advantage of the momentum to say that she would marry Diao Chan as his wife and send her to Fuchu in two days. Lu Bu was very happy.
Two more days passed. Early in the morning, Wang Yun invited Dong Zhuo to dinner at his home, and Dong Zhuo gladly agreed. During the event, Wang Yun asked Fuchu prostitutes to perform songs and dances. The lead dancer was Diao Chan. Dong Zhuo could see his saliva flowing all over the table. Wang Yun said she wanted to dedicate Diao Chan to Dong Zhuo as her handmaiden. Dong Zhuo graciously accepted it and brought Diao Chan back to Fuchu that night. When Lu Bu came to Wang Yun to pick up Diao Chan, Wang Yun cried and said that Dong Zhuo had taken Diao Chan. Lu Bu hurriedly arrived at Dong Zhuo's place. Diao Chan from behind the curtain conveyed his love to him. Dong Zhuo watched Lu Bu stare behind the screen, and when he looked back, he saw Diao Chan there and said in anger, “Can you play I love Ji?” Lu Bu went back to his house.
Within a few days, Diao Chan led Lu Bu to Fengyi Ting, saying that she missed Lu Bu a lot. Dong Zhuoqiang had taken over her. Lu Bu was so angry that Lu Bu gnawed his teeth. At this point, Dong Zhuo was looking for Diao Chan. He saw Lu Bu with Diao Chan and hurled the Fang Tianhua fan on his side, and was knocked out by Lu Bu.
Wang Yun also took Lu Bu to his home, saying that he wanted revenge for Lu Bu. Together, the two planned how to assassinate Dong Zhuo.
On the 1st, Lieutenant General Li Su of the Light Vehicle was ordered to visit Dong Zhuo in Yuwu. It is said that Tianzi is angry and wants to meet the Minister of Culture and Wu at Weiyang Temple to discuss passing on the throne to the Grand Master. Dong Zhuo immediately left for Beijing. While walking into the Weiyang Temple, he was ambushed by a soldier who was ambushed inside the temple. Lu Bu stabbed Dong Zhuo's throat with a punch. After Dong Zhuo was stabbed, the whole family was affected and died.
Lu Bu found Diao Chan, brought him home, and paid off his wish. However, in the end, Lu Bu was caught and killed by Cao Cao in Xiapi because he couldn't bear Diao Chan. There are many opinions about Diao Chan's whereabouts. Some say she died by suicide, while others say she was killed by Guan Yu.
