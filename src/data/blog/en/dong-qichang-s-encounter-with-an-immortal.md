 Legend has it that when Dong Qichang was the chief priest of the Nanjing Ministry of Worship, he once went back to Songjiang to sacrifice his ancestors and wrote a plaque for the newly built Yilan Tower at Chaoguo Temple's request. The word “lanlou” is very well written; the word “one” is always bad at writing.
At the time, a lame beggar huddled in the onlookers and sneered, “How difficult is this?” I saw him take off his sandals, soak them in ink, and as soon as they waved, the character “1” was imposing. It was really a good character. Dong Qichang just wanted to talk to him. Suddenly, the beggar had no idea where he was going.
After a few days, Dong Qichang met the beggar again in Wuxi and invited him to the Songjiang River. The two climbed the tower together to enjoy the scenery of Fucheng.
The beggar said that he was an iron gaiter, so Dong knelt down to pray and ask for the immortals.
Tieguai Li said, “Grandpa Hong Fu Qi Tian. Unfortunately, there is no blessing.”
Dong is willing to abandon glory and wealth; he will definitely want to learn. Tieguai Li wanted Dong Qichang to cross the Xilin Tower together. While Dong was hesitating, Tieguai Li had already jumped past and smiled and waved at the top of the Xilin Tower.
After a few days, Dong Qichang went to an ancient temple and visited a familiar old abbot. The abbot just so invited Dong to eat with him. Stop for a moment. Eight people have arrived at the clothing threshold. One of them is a woman. After asking questions, everyone took their seats. During the dinner, I saw that the dishes on the plate were all cooked with children's hands, feet, and first class. Dong Qichang didn't dare to move his chopsticks. He only saw eight people swallowing it up. He had already eaten it to the bottom of his stomach for a while.
At this point, a lame monk ran out of the back of the palace and licked the soup from the pot while cleaning up the bowl. One of Dong Qichang's family members was hungry and saw that there was half a bowl of soup left. He picked up the bowl and drank it. By the time the owner stopped drinking it, the soup had already puffed up his stomach.
By the time the old abbot brought back the guests, Dong Qichang questioned him for daring to eat human flesh in front of the imperial court. Did you know it was against the law? The old abbot laughed and said, “This is an ebony tree. It's rare to grow in the shape of a thousand years. If you eat it, you can live forever.” Dong Qichang lamented that he was unhappy, but he never died and immediately sent his family to recover the gang. The family followed him all the way on fast horses, and at every fork in the road, they had to be instructed. In the end, they finally caught up with the lame one, but they never went back. The family grabbed him half the waist of a mountain with an iron cane in his hand. Li Xian slapped her family on the back, and the family threw up. She only felt that the sky was spinning and her feet were empty. Fortunately, when she fell down the mountain, she clutched the crutches with her hands and used that stick for support when she landed on the ground, causing no injury. However, when I asked the locals, it turned out that it was already on the Guangdong border.
That family has gone through a lot of hard work. When he returned to Songjiang, it coincided with the “People Chao Dong” incident. Dong Fu had been burnt to a rubble field by tens of thousands of farmers. That family returned to Ye Xie Shui Yue Monastery to become monks.
