 Love at first sight
The Seven Fairies, the daughter of the Jade Emperor, admires life on earth because she feels alone and lonely in Tiangong. One day, I visited Ling Xutai with six older sisters. Occasionally, I saw Dong Yong, a young farmer who sold his father in the lower world. I was moved by his loyalty and honesty, and I developed a love for him.
The elder sister saw through the little sister's heart and helped her defy the situation despite the strict rules of the Tiangong Palace. When they left, they also gave away a bottle of difficult incense so that the little girl could burn incense and ask for help in case of crisis. The Seven Fairies came to the world, and after getting along with Grandpa Earth, they became husband and wife with Dong Yong. In order to help her husband redeem herself, the Seven Fairies went to Fu Yuan to work outside the home. An outsider deliberately harassed her and limited her to weaving ten brocades in one night.
If so, Dong Yong's long working period will be changed from three years to 100 days; otherwise, three years will be changed to six years. The Seven Fairies ignited a bad scent in the computer room and asked for help. The six sisters helped each other and weaved ten silk silk overnight. Fu Yuanwai had no choice but to keep his promise.
After Dong Yong's work period was completed, the husband and wife both returned home happily. On the way, Dong Yong discovered that his wife was pregnant and hurriedly asked for water to quench her thirst. At this point, strong winds suddenly broke out, and the Heavenly Soldier appeared in the air. He handed down the Jade Emperor's commandment. The Seven Fairies were limited to returning to Tiangong at 3 p.m., and if they disobeyed orders, Dong Yong's corpse was destroyed 10,000 times. The Seven Fairies couldn't bear her husband's innocent victimization; she had to explain her history to Dong Yong, engrave the oath “One Heart in Heaven and Earth” on the Huaiyin tree, and return to the heavenly court with a sense of grief and indignation.
