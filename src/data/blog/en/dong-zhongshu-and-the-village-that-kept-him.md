 In Kangxi's “Historic Sites” and “Historic Sites” of Kangxi in the Qing Dynasty, a place name called “Liu Dongli” is recorded. Why is it called “Liu Trang Li”? This is related to Dong Zhongshu, a great Confucian in the Han Dynasty.
The records in the county register about staying in Dongli say, “The scenery of Hebei is 25 miles southwest of the county. Legend has it that Han Dong Zhong Shu passed through this place. There was an old man who met him without asking his last name. They made friends for a few months and left with the word “Dong.” After that, the old man said, “He called Dong Ye!” Again, far from being famous. Kojungdo is not renamed as such. If old people don't spread the word, you can also imagine their lofty meaning! Now there is Dongzi Temple.” According to local legends, Dong Zhongshu passed by here when he was young, and when he experienced continuous rain, he became ill again and was unable to move forward. At this point, he met an elderly local man. The old man saw that he was a foreigner wandering outside, sick, and had nowhere else to go, so he invited him to live and take care of his illness. Dong Zhongshu ate and stayed at the old man's house for several months, and the old man never asked him his name. After Dong Zhongshu had recovered, he wrote the word “Dong” on the table and left. After a while, when Dong Zhongshu returned, he passed by here again and met the old man again. The old man said, “Didn't you have the last name Dong?” They invited him to his home again and never asked Dong Zhongshu's name. From this legend, it can be seen that the folk customs here are simple, and the name is not unique.
Dong Zhongshu is a native of Guangchuan. Hirochuan is about 300 kilometers from present-day Jingxian County in Heze City. According to “Historical Records” and “Han Shu,” Dong Zhongshu studied “Chunqiu” very much, and “didn't look into the garden for three years” when he was reading the book. Han Jingdi, then Ph.D., taught “Spring and Autumn of the Ram”. In the first year of Emperor Han Wu Yuanguang, Emperor Han Wu issued a commandment to seek a strategy for governing the country. Dong Zhongshu combined Confucian thought with the social needs of the time in his famous “Measures to Be Good,” and absorbed theories from other schools, and created a new system of thought centered on Confucianism, which was deeply appreciated by Emperor Han Wu. He systematically put forward the “natural sense” and “unification” doctrine and the proposition that “no one in the six arts or the Confucius practitioner should go hand in hand” and “oust the hundred families and respect Confucianism alone,” which was adopted by Emperor Han Wu. This was an epoch-making event in the history of Chinese feudal society, and provided theoretical support for the feudal ruling class to govern society after the Han Dynasty. From this point on, Confucianism began to become the orthodoxy of feudal society and profoundly influenced the history of China. After that, Dong Zhongshu served as Minister of State for 10 years; Yuanshuo was King of Jiaoxi for 4 years (125 before), he resigned and returned home after 4 years to write. Since then, every time the imperial court had major discussions, the emperor ordered messengers and court officers to go to the Dong family to ask about his suggestions, showing that Dong Zhongshu was still respected by the emperor. Dong Zhongshu went through three dynasties in his life and passed through the heyday of the Western Han dynasty. He died of illness in 104 BC.
The place name “Liudangli” no longer exists. According to research, this place is present-day Wangzhuang Village, a Jiunyu market town about 25 miles southwest of Chengwu County. According to the Qing Dynasty County Register, Dongzi Temple was built in the village, but after 100 years of ups and downs, the temple no longer exists. Currently, there is the Xuandi Temple in the north of the village, the “Xuandi Temple Chronicles” monument from the 10th year of Ming Chongzhen, and the “Renovation of the Yuan Emperor Temple” stone monument from the 1st year of Qing Daoguang, which are the former sites of the ancient Dongzi Temple. Emperor Xuan is Emperor Zhenwu the Great. Zhu Di, the founder of Ming Cheng, vigorously promoted the worship of Emperor Zhenwu after launching the “Jingnan Incident” to seize the throne, so that the belief of Emperor Zhenwu reached its peak in the Ming Dynasty, and the court and people built a large number of Zhenwu temples. When the Xuandi Temple was built in the Ming Dynasty, it was probably co-built with the Dong Zi Temple. The Xuandi Temple was taken seriously by the public because of the emperor's promotion, and the Dong Zi Temple was replaced by it. Later, on March 3 of the lunar calendar, people nearby invited troupes to sing and hold temple fairs.
According to the “Xuandi Temple Chronicles” monument erected during Ming Chongzhen's time, “When Dong Village stayed 25 miles southwest of Wenshan, Dong Zi once stopped in Sri Lanka. His father and elder Xian Panyuan left him for a while, and he still couldn't bear to listen to it, and later became famous because of that.” Judging from the monument written by the person who signed “Zhong Tao Yusheng,” the real reason for “staying in Trang Li” is probably that Dong Zhongshu used to stay here in a horse-drawn carriage. The local parents and elders knew that he was a Grand Ju, and they all wanted to keep him for a while, so this place was named “Liu Dongli.” “Li” was a grass-roots administrative division unit during the Qin and Han dynasties, equivalent to a village. According to villagers, this stone monument from the Ming Dynasty was excavated and rebuilt from an old bridge in recent years. Judging from the fact that the inscription was not included in the Lixiu County Chronicles of the Qing Dynasty, the person who wrote the County Chronicles probably didn't see this stone monument; the stone monument was used for bridge construction around the end of the Ming Dynasty. In the Qing Dynasty, Daoguang's “Renovation of the Temple of the Yuan Dynasty” records: “The county government is about 20 miles southwest. The old city was Liudong City. The residents were rich, the customs were strong, and there were no fewer than 100 traders...” It can be confirmed from the stone monument records that this place is ancient Trang, and it is also a large commercial bazaar. Dong Zhongshu has certainly passed by since then.
Why did Dong Zhongshu use martial arts? Due to lack of information, it is now impossible to know the exact reason. However, judging from his study content and ideas, it should have something to do with his study trip to Qilu. In “Historical Records: The Biography of Rulin,” which chronicles Dong Zhongshu's life, Sima Qian mentioned Confucius' “self-defense return to Lu” several times in his preface and his exclamation after hearing “Western Qin Lun.” These related locations are all within Heze. Most importantly, Sima Qian said that during the period from the Warring States period to the unification of the six kingdoms by Qin, the succession and research of Confucianism in various regions had been seriously abandoned. “However, among Qilu, scholars were left alone.” This shows that despite the war, there are still many scholars in Qilu who continue to study and study Confucianism.
Dong Zhongshu advocates Confucianism. In response to Emperor Han Wu's question on how to expand the great cause of the Han dynasty, he quoted Confucius many times in his “Three Heavenly People's Policies,” and also quoted the stories of Yu Shun and Confucius. Dong Zhongshu started with “Chunqiu”. This book was compiled and revised by Confucius and is one of the Confucian classics. Dong Zhongshu studied “Chunqiu” and became a Ph.D., and he also knows Confucius' personal stories very well, especially when Confucius traveled around the world to study. Confucius traveled through places such as Chengwu County and Dingtao County, and left behind quite a few classic chapters of “The Analects” along the way. Yu Shun, who is admired by Dong Zhongshu, used to “cultivate the mountains, fish for lightning, and gray in Changyang,” while “Razer” and others are all within present-day Heze, about a hundred miles away from Chengwu County. Wenting Mountain in Chengwu County is the place where counselor Lu Jia persuaded Liu Bang to govern the world by “combining culture and martial arts, and governing for a long time.” Due to Lu Jia's persuasion and offering 12 “New Words,” Liu Bang went specifically to Qufu to sacrifice Confucius, pioneering the emperor sacrificing Kong. As the hometown of Yao Shun, the place of Qilu where Confucianism was not abolished, and a turning point where the emperor sacrificed Kong, Dong Zhongshu had to visit no matter what. However, what he experienced in Chengwu County, whether it was the warm hospitality of the legendary elderly without asking his name, or the warm retention of the local parents and elders recorded in the Ming Monument, made him really feel the strong and unfamous Qilu style when he thought about it.
Because of Dong Zhongshu's passing, Liu Dongli attracted many writers from later generations to visit and express their feelings. “Mr. Keting,” Zhao Yujing, who taught in Chengwu County in the Qing Dynasty, wrote the long poem “Living in Dongcun”.
