 At the beginning of September of the lunar calendar, the two suns are similar, so it is called “Chongyang,” and Chung Yeung Festival is also “Old People's Day.” On this day, the elderly either enjoyed chrysanthemums to cultivate their sentiments, or climbed high to exercise their physical fitness, adding endless fun to the evening view of Sang Yu.
On this day in Chongyang, people enjoy chrysanthemums, wear dogwood, go hiking with alcohol, and enjoy drinking. In Chongyang in September, the sky is high and the clouds are light, and the golden wind is refreshing. It is a good season to climb high and have a great view. Therefore, climbing high has become an important custom during the Chung Yeung Festival. People living in the Jiangnan Plain suffer from not having mountains to climb, so they prevent rice noodles and pastries, and put a small colored triangular flag on the cake to show that it is a remote disaster.
Chung Yeung Festival also has customs such as planting dogwood, drinking chrysanthemum wine, and eating chung yang cake. Dogwood, also known as Vietnamese pepper, is an important plant. It has a strong smell and can prevent bad turbidity. Chongyang flower cake is a seasonal dish made from japonica rice.
Chung Yeung Festival customs
The Chung Yeung Festival is refreshing in autumn. If you go up high, you can enjoy a panoramic view of the grass, mountains, and rivers. This is actually a kind of field trip, a traditional sporting activity for our people.
Enjoy chrysanthemums. Chrysanthemums, also known as yellow flowers, belong to the Asteraceae family and have many varieties. During the Chongyang season, when chrysanthemums are in full bloom, drink chrysanthemum wine. “Art and Literature Gathering” quoted “Continuing the Autumn of Jinyang” as saying, “Every 9 days until (September), people climb mountains and drink chrysanthemum wine.” It is said that ancient chrysanthemum wine was specially brewed during the Chung Yeung Festival in the first year for the Chung Yeung Festival in the second year. On September 9, first blooming chrysanthemums and some green leaves are harvested, mixed with the food prepared for winemaking, then used together to make wine, and left to drink until September 9 of the following year. Legend has it that drinking this type of alcohol can prolong life. From a medical point of view, chrysanthemum wine can brighten eyesight, cure dizziness, and lower blood pressure. It has the benefits of losing weight, lightening the body, supplementing liver gas, calming the stomach, and blood.
