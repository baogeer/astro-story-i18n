 Du Mu, a native of Xi'an, comes from a famous family. His grandfather Du You was prime minister and the author of 200 volumes of the “General Dictionary”. He is a senior academic official. However, Du Mu didn't rely on Zu Yin to prosper. As a child, he was a “school bully” and became an instant hit; the 26-year-old champion and No. 1, supposedly the champion, was originally the champion, only because the chief examiner Cui Yan was relegated to 5th place. For a while, “Famous Jingyi”, Du Mu couldn't help but expand a bit.
Tang Mengyi's “Talented Poetry: Gao Yi No. 3” said that as soon as Du Mu's “College Entrance Examination” was over, he invited several students from the same year to enjoy the local tour and met an old monk sitting silently at Wengong Temple in the south of the city. Du Mu was attracted by an old monk wearing a brown monk's uniform and no expression in front of him, and took the initiative to greet him. “It was surprising.” The old monk then asked who is Du Mu's name, and Du Mu, who is full of “everyone in the world knows me,” proudly told the truth. The old monk asked him “what career to practice,” and the “same year” who came together couldn't help it. You don't even know him? I “read it over and over” that Du Mu became famous for his weak position, tried it out and passed the test, etc. Who knows, the old monk laughed when he heard it: “Nobody knows.”
People are no stranger to this bridge section. In Yang Jie's version of “Journey to the West,” after Sun Monkey reported that his family “I am Qi Tian Daisheng who made a big noise in Tiangong 500 years ago,” there were also elderly people or children who answered with the three words “I don't know,” and Sun Monkey just stomped his feet in anger. Hearing the old monk's “I don't know” answer, how many people had expressions. It was not described in the book, but Du Mu was very surprised to write the song “Giving the Old Monk the Last Name in Nanshan”: “I don't have a famous name; at first, I felt that the Zen Monk had a long smell.” Some people say that this is a self-deprecating poem by Du Mu; I think even more, this is his poem of introspection and self-warning.
Du Mu, who has a high starting point in his work and hobby, is known as “Xiao Du”, whether he is a staff member of Fang Zhen, or is promoted to supervise the Imperial History, Huangzhou and Chizhou Assassin, Zhizhi, and Shusha, and serve the country; his literary and artistic creations always use fine work to open up new horizons of literature and art, and are known as “Xiao Du.”
What few people know is that Du Mu was also an excellent military man. When introducing Du Mu, “The Book of the New Tang Dynasty, Volume 91,” it took 80% of the space to endorse him. Not only did he annotate the thirteen chapters of “Sun Tzu's Art of War” established by Cao Cao, he is also the author of essays such as “War Theory” and “Defense Theory,” but he also used the military like a god. Huichangzhong (844), Huichangzhong defeated the Huijiazi army, and the Hui tribe retreated to the south of the Great Desert. Du Mu persuaded then-Prime Minister Li Deyu to take the opportunity. The reason was that “the two Han dynasties attacked Hungary, often in the fall and winter. At that time, the Huns had strong arms and horses, and they fought against them, so losing more was better than less. “Now, if the elite cavalry from Youzhou and Bingzhou and the soldiers from Jiuquan are recruited in midsummer, they can all be annihilated in one fell swoop.” Li Deyu agreed very much, and believes that Du Mu is a military genius. Unfortunately, this ingenious plan came to an end when moderation forced Liu Yi to boycott Liu Yi, and Du Mu offered Li Deyu a decisive victory plan: “To the northwest of Heyang, go 100 miles to the Tiangguanqiang, use 10,000 people as a base, don't fight the deep wall... if the two armies of Zhongwu and Wuning are used today, Yiqingzhou Jingjia 5,000 and Xuanrun Crossbowmen 2,000.” According to the “animal husbandry plan,” the Zezhou and Luzhou rebellions in Zhaoyi Town quickly settled down.
Born in the middle of the “Li Niu Party Feud,” Du Mu has an outspoken personality, and doesn't disdain greetings. Although his career isn't going well, he is much better than the famous Li Shangyin. This is more or less related to the old monk of Wengong Temple, who “took the lead” when he first debuted. Du Mu, who is used to looking at real problems and personal experiences from the depths of history, has decadent elements in his poems, but they are not limited and dark. Not only did he leave behind many popular golden phrases such as “There was a lot of rain during the Qingming season, and pedestrians on the road wanted to break their spirits”, “It's hard in the world to laugh, the chrysanthemums must be plugged in,” “Stop and love the maple forest at night, frost leaves turn red in February,” but he also always formed a special level of poetry with bright images, handsome spirit, and a unique sense of history.
