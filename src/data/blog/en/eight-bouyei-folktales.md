 1.
One father went up the mountain to work, but it was dark and he never returned. The family said, “It's all so late, it's already dark, why haven't we come home yet?”
While talking, the kid came close to home, with his father's leg on his shoulder. Hearing the family's concerns, he said:
Father never comes back
Legs hanging ding-ding-dang
This father's son was later chopped up by the family with a knife and axe, then placed in a stone pedal, crushed into powder, and then scattered in the air. This debris falls down and becomes the most painful long-legged mosquito, flying in groups.
2.
In ancient times, there was a family with many brothers, and food was scarce. The mother couldn't resist the children's grievances day by day, so she had to go to her mother's house. When the mother left, she advised, “I'll give you a bamboo tube. You can go up the mountain to pick wild strawberries. Wait until one day the bamboo tube is full, and your two brothers will return home one day, and then I will return from Grandma's house.”
The two brothers then took bamboo tubes and went up the mountain. However, the brothers dropped their wild strawberries one by one, and they were not satisfied with how many they dropped, no matter how many they put in the bamboo tube. It turns out that the mother made a round hole in the bottom of the bamboo tube, and the bamboo tube will never be full.
I don't know how long the years have passed. Both brothers' clothes are all decayed, and their bodies are covered with hair.
When the mother got home, carrying the rice cake she had brought back from her mother's house, she called out to her child:
Oh---
Kid, kid
Come home and eat mochi
I brought the mochi back from my grandmother's house
The child answered her in the mountains:
We have no return to our food
Eating sesame seeds won't return
From here to the end
Turned into a monkey, leaving behind her mother
When the two children came home, their mother patted four red rice cakes on their butts and said:
Let's go---
You can't get enough of what you've eaten
There is no return to eating sesame
From here to the end
Turned into a monkey, leaving behind her mother
As a result, the two brothers returned to the mountains and became wild monkeys.
Remarks:
Mochi is a type of cake made during the Buyei New Year. Cooked rice is poured into a vessel, knocked out many small balls, and kneaded. They are flat and round. They are usually white as rice, but are also dyed red.
Le ru-en (le ru-en), an inaccurate transliteration, is a type of wild fruit.
3.
One day, a mother said to her children, “I want to go to Grandma's house. Call the neighbor's wife to stay with you, kids.” The mother left after giving advice.
At night, the two children called for the neighbor's wife at home. However, the old woman was deaf and had never heard of it, but the cow woman in the mountains heard it, so the cow woman came to join the two children. Grandma Niu said, “Baby boy, my mother-in-law has hemorrhoids. She brought a hoop bucket to sit with her.” The cow lady was able to hide her tail in that hoop.
While sleeping, Mrs. Niu said, “Kid, let my brother sleep with me at the bedside; you stay at the end of your brother's bed.”
When I slept until the middle of the night, the cow ate my younger brother. There was a chewing sound in her mouth, and blood flowed all over the bed.
The brother asked, “Mother-in-law, what are you eating?”
The cow woman answered, “I'm eating fried corn kernels, kid.”
“Why is the bed wet?” This brother asked again.
Mrs. Niu answered, “This is your brother bed-wetting, kid.”
But when this brother found out, he said, “Mother-in-law, mother-in-law, I want to get rid of it.”
Grandma Niu said, “Just go to your dad's bedside to solve it.”
“Dad wants to scold me when he comes back.”
“Just go to your mom's bedside to solve it.”
“No, Mom will have to curse when she comes back.”
“Then go out and go to the stable.” Grandma Niu said, “I use a pair of leggings to tie your legs. I'll take this one, and you shake the leggings once you've unwrapped them. I'll drag you back. Don't be afraid.”
This brother is a smart kid. When he loosened his hands, he tied the leggings to the horse's leg. As soon as the horse shook, the cow woman asked, “Kid, have you finished removing your hands?” No one answered. The cow pulled again, the horse shook again, and never saw the child come back.
The cow lady was in a hurry. She said, “Boy, kid, where are you, why haven't you come?”
At this point, the child had already climbed a persimmon tree in front of the door and escaped. The kid said, “Mother-in-law, I'm on a persimmon tree, mother-in-law, do you want to eat persimmons?”
The cow woman said, “Eat, eat.”
“If you bring some tung tree oil and apply it to the persimmon tree, you can climb up.” The kid said.
As the cow said, she went and put some tung oil on it. How can an oiled tree still go up? This kid said, “Mother-in-law, mother-in-law, if you want to eat a persimmon, go to my dad's bedside and get a fork; I'll eat it for you.”
The cow also complied, took a long fork and handed it up the tree. The child said, “Grandma, grandma, if you want to eat a persimmon, just open your mouth and wait.”
The cow widened her mouth and stood under the tree and waited. The kid then slashed a long fork in his hand, which penetrated the cow's throat. The crying cow turned into a thicket of thorns and besieged the persimmon trees. When the child's parents got home, they were cut off with a scythe. Apart from being born again, the thickets of thorns cannot be removed in any way.
Remarks:
Cow wa, literally translated, cow boy (bao wa-i), cow woman (ya wa-i) are all monsters and usually live in mountain forests or caves.
4.
Once upon a time, there was a baby named Jingnan, who became a couple. On weekdays, when his parents went out to work, they put him in a flat bamboo basket and slept by the fire pond. I know that after his parents went out, he became an old man. One day he caught a chicken from the house, cooked it, and ate it. Before his parents got home, he turned back to being a baby. He fell asleep in that bamboo basket or cried.
His father and mother were puzzled: “Why lose one chicken a day, lose one a day?”
A familiar neighbor said, “Who killed the kid in your family.”
Naturally, his parents didn't believe it. They said, “My kids are this young and still can't walk and talk. Why did they kill chickens to eat?”
The neighbor said, “If you don't believe it, don't believe it; just wait and see.”
After a few more days, chickens are still being lost day by day. On this day, the couple, carrying backpacks, water, and hoes, locked the door and pretended to go out to work, but quietly hid back upstairs.
Close to noon, Jingnan repeated his old trick, turning it into an old man killing a chicken. While fiddling with the fire, his mother took out the water bottles in her back basket and fell some of them from upstairs.
Jingnan said, “Huh, you mouse, go pee. Wait a minute, I'll finish it and give you something to eat in your stomach.”
His parents went downstairs, and Jingnan hurriedly wanted to become a baby. He lay back in the bamboo basket by the fire pond, but it was too late. Seeing that his upper body had changed as a baby, he hung a leg around the bamboo basket, and he was covered with sweat unique to adults.
Remarks:
Jingnan (jin-eng n-ang). Inaccurate transliteration, monster.
5.
In the past, there was a guy who was very hungry. At one point, he served at someone else's house, pulled down the bacon that was still being grilled, and ate it raw.
Once again, her grandson begged to eat meat. She said, “Wait until Grandma makes it for you to eat, wait until Grandma makes it for you to eat.” Just turn around and go into her room. There are many crock pots in the corner of her room, and I don't know what they are. It is said that she often excavates the graves of young babies, retrieves the dead bodies, and shatters them in sauerkraut. Sure enough, she took a baby palm out of one of the cans and came back to cook and eat with her grandchildren. When she was bumped into by her son, this Asian poop was severely beaten.
Remarks:
Yga-a bia-a (yga-a), an inaccurate transliteration, or medicine woman, is usually a woman who thinks that her soul can get out of her mind, turn it into something else, and can be drugged on people. It is also commonly thought of as something that suppresses people in the middle of the night.

When his father came back, he saw a child lying upside down at the corner of a wall and staying at home. He also saw Wu's mother still crying. His father then said:
Niangou, niangou

7.
In ancient times, there was a person called Jia Jing. He often made pranks and harassed the townspeople. The townspeople said, “Jiajing, you are wrong; we need to take you to heaven.”
“Trust me to the sun if you want, not the moon.” Jia Jing said.
Soon, the townspeople actually used bamboo poles to hold Jiajing up to the sky. When they got to the sun, Jia Jing said, “It's so cool, it's so cool,” and they also held him to the moon. Jia Jing also said, “It's hot, too hot.”
Neighbors said, “You are so harmful, we will punish you and trust you to that hot moon.”
Later, on a clear moonlit night, I could clearly see a shadow on the moon, which looked like a big tree, and there was another person carrying that tree underneath.
Remarks:
Scenery A, transliteration, person's name. It is said that the folk drama “Kageyuki” is still being performed in some places.
8.
In the past, the Han people lived at the top of the mountain, while the Buyei lived on the flatlands under the mountain. The Han people can make tofu, but the Buyei people can't.
A Han Chinese person went to a Buyei family member to serve as his servant. One day, the servant made tofu outside the house. Outside the staff thought tofu was a good thing, and said to the servant, “You also worked hard. I kept the tofu scum, so I'll give you the tofu water. You can take it back and dispose of it whatever you want.”
The servant brought tofu water home, added sour water to make tender white tofu, and delivered it to the staff the next day. Waiyuan said with envy, “What did you use to make it, why is this so much tastier than the one I took?” The servant said, “Give me the leftover tofu water, and I'll take it back and make this delicious dish for you as usual.” Outside the staff said, “Let's not stop, come and live with us.”
At this point, Han talents have moved from the top of the mountain to live at the bottom of the mountain.
