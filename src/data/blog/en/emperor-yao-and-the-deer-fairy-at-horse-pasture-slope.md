 Legend has it that Yao Wang's Mumapo is located in the Beixian Cave of Mount Gushi in ancient times. The top of the mountain is full of green grass, the hillside is overgrown with shrubs, and a forest sea underneath the ditch. In spring, plants sprout and are full of life. The trees are lush in summer, the mountains are full of flowers, the maple leaves are red like fire in the fall, and the pine cypress is frosty and snowy in the winter. Legend has it that “the tree is planted by Yao Wang”, is Yao Wang's Fangma Mountain. Today, there are Shengma Zhuang in the north and Mu Ma Beach in the south. The names of the villages of Shengma and Erkoma are all names handed down from Yao Wang's time. In the “Chronicles of Linfen County”, this place is one of the Eight Scenic Sites of Linfen. On the west side of Yamashita Fenshui, East Ma Zha and West Ma Zha are rumored to be Yao Wang's horse farm. After getting married to King Yao, the Deer Fairy also managed the horse farm in this area.
Di Yao and the Deer Fairy
The Deer Fairy was born with ice and snow skin, and a beautiful figure, just like a virgin. She has a kind heart, helps the needy and the dangerous, and exterminates harm for the people. A black dragon lurks in Heilongtan near Xiandonggou. It often bothers pedestrians, and also runs up the river to injure deer herds in the Lugou area. The Deer Fairy cares about the safety of the people and the survival of the deer herd, and is determined to subdue the Black Dragon. One day, she came to Heilongtan to challenge Black Dragon. Black Dragon rose from the pool, spread her teeth and went straight to the fairy. The deer fairy reached out and pointed a finger at the black dragon. All of a sudden, the black dragon fell paralyzed on the riverside, asked for forgiveness from the deer fairy, and expressed his desire to serve the fairy for the rest of his life. The Deer Fairy spared it from death and imprisoned it in the Black Dragon Cave and turned it into a mount she drove herself. Since then, the Black Dragon's harm to life in the Xiandonggou area has been eliminated.
At a time of distress, Di Yao was ordered to “go out side by side for ten days,” and then floods flooded and flooded the countryside. Tribes and countries from all over the world divided the country and dominated the country. Emperor Yao relied on the support of Hou Yi and other tribal leaders to level down the powerful forces, reunify the Central Plains, and lead the masses in the fight against floods and droughts. The people praised him. One day, he visited Mukumapo in Sendo and talked with herders about animal husbandry methods. Incidentally, the herders also told stories of deer fairies killing people. In between, I saw a fairy drifting towards the Fairy Cave. The herdsmen were pleasantly surprised and pointed out to Di Yao that it was a deer fairy known as the Goddess of Fire. Since Di Yao returned to Yaodu, the image of a deer fairy has always surfaced in his mind and lingered in his mind. At night, I dreamed that the deer fairy floated down from the sky, stared and smiled, walked up to him, joined hands with him, exchanged feelings, and traveled with him in the sky.
Di Yao danced to Gushi Mountain and walked to Xiandong. From afar, she saw a young woman dancing on the forest lawn. She was graceful, suddenly vacated and hid, walking through a stone like a human, and walking empty like a flat ground. A fawn accompanied her by her. Di Yao thought to herself that she must be a deer fairy, so she stepped forward and bowed to her. Surprisingly, she didn't even answer. She hid behind a pine tree and brushed her hair in a shy manner. As Yao approached, she brushed the tree to the top and smiled at the back of another tree. Di Yao also chased after him with a smile. Unwittingly, he arrived at a secluded place. Suddenly, a giant python flew out of the valley. He spouted red letters and stumbled at Yao with his head held high. Di Yao was too late to retreat and was stumbled over by the grass on the ground.
At this critical moment, the deer fairy saw the situation and jumped in front of Di Yao to protect him. She leaned her hand. She saw that the giant python was shaking all over, paralyzed to the ground, and followed the instructions and retreated back to the valley. This python was created by the Black Tiger Fairy; the intention was to harm Di Yao. In addition to being terrified, Di Yao repeatedly thanked the Deer Fairy for saving her life. On their way back to Sendo, the two shared their feelings with each other and agreed. Di Yao stayed in Xiandong that night. The next day, the deer fairy led Di Yao on a mountain tour. The deer fairy pointed to the glittering mirror stone and said, “I often look at it for plastic surgery.” Walking to the side of the stone table under the jiangou, the deer fairy said, “I often sit on this table and brush my hair. My family inherits it as my dressing table.” She pointed to the layers of stone steps on the opposite shore and said that she often picked up the steps from there, known as the Fairy Ladder. She said that she often rides a black dragon to bathe and play in the water at Longsu Falls in Hougou o “I love this amazing nature and enjoy living freely, but since I saw you, I have admired your ambition to help the world, and am willing to help you in the Guangdi industry.” After hearing it, Di Yao was very pleased and expressed his wish to play with Wings and Chirps. The two then entered into a marriage contract and decided to get married on an auspicious date. Di Yao and the Deer Fairy tied the knot in Fairy Cave, using the cave as their new house. The candle mountain on the opposite side is dazzling, illuminating Nanxian Cave as if it were daytime. Later, people called this wedding night “The Night of Candlelight in the Cave House.”
After Di Yao got married, she was busy managing state affairs, and the Deer Fairy also often took care of the horse farm. The next year, the Deer Fairy gave birth to a boy. Yao was very happy and named him “Zhu.” The Deer Fairy raised her son and grew little by little. When she heard that a giant python was devouring herders at Wrangler Beach, she thought it must have been the Black Tiger Fairy who is growing monsters again. So I decided to surrender to that evil python. She chased the python from Wrangler Beach and followed it to the bottom of the dressing table. The python was about to return. The deer fairy leaped forward and stabbed the python in the throat with a sword. The giant python was stabbed to death. Black Tiger escaped, and later left the Monty Python Caves there.
The Black Tiger Fairy is increasingly jealous of the Deer Fairy and is looking for ways to harm the Deer Fairy. The Deer Fairy had no choice but to report it to the Emperor. The Emperor sent the Heavenly Soldier to capture the Black Tiger Fairy and crushed the Black Tiger Fairy under the hill southeast of the Yangtze in order to crouch on Mount Tiger. At the same time, Emperor Tien also punished the Deer Fairy and cut ties with Emperor Yao. The Deer Fairy was helpless and returned the infant Zhu Er to Di Yao. Since then, she has been in seclusion deep in the mountains. Di Yao sent people to search everywhere. There was no trace. He personally went to Gushe Mountain to find him. He called out clearly and night, and there was no response. Di Yao also married a daughter from the Sanyi clan and gave birth to seven boys and two daughters. Zhu Er was later sealed in Danji (Fushan), so it is known as Danzhu Yi. Later, people appreciated the merits of the Deer Fairy and commemorated the Deer Fairy statue in a small cave on the left side of Nancian Cave in Heilongdong. Incense has continued to burn for thousands of years.
