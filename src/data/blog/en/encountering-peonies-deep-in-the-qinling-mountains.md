 In Peony Township in Caozhou, anyone who talks about the origin of peonies in Caozhou must mention the name of Zhao Ruibo. Legend has it that during the Ming Wanli years (more than 400 years ago), Zhao Ruibo, a famous young doctor from Zhao Lou, went to Shaanxi to practice medicine. On the way back, he took a detour in the Qinling Mountains to collect medicine. Just near the foot of the mountain, I saw a beautiful young woman wearing a blue umbrella, riding a donkey, and singing the “Flower” mountain song while walking:
The blue umbrella, the donkey, and the khan jiayi
On the Qinling Mountains,
My eyes are full of peonies.
My brother found Little Sister.
Among the flowers,
The insatiable words that keep coming from the head.
Ripple has long been familiar with the medicinal properties of peony root bark. It is spicy and slightly cold. It takes enough of the negative effects of the lunar calendar, has diarrhea, breaks blood accumulation, passes through the meridians, and treats the five pains of stroke... However, they are scarce locally. They can be purchased from afar, and are expensive. If you can grow your own, it is very convenient. Thinking about this, he was even more motivated to go up the mountain and moved forward with the woman's singing. Climbing a few small hills, the woman disappeared as soon as she turned a corner, and her singing voice disappeared. He was worried. Looking at the hillside in front of him, it was really a “dark willow and bright”. The red, purple, white, pink, colorful, and really beautiful. So I took a big step and walked there. When I saw it recently, my eyes were full of tall, low, and large peony flowers. Because they are wild, and no family plants are big, and they are all single-petaled, but they are brightly colored, but they seem to surpass those grown at home. He is extremely happy. Although he is deep in the mountains, he doesn't feel alone at all. He feels that every peony flower around here is like a woman with beautiful makeup. He is looking forward to someone taking himself to the world and dedicating his youth and beauty to those hardworking, brave, and kind people. He pulled out a medicine shovel from his waist, picked the best colored ones, dug them up, wrapped them in a straw rope, and put them in his backpack; after tightening his belt, he happily went down the mountain from the original path and found the right path back to Kanto. I ate and slept in the open air all the way, didn't feel any pain at all, and returned home in a few days.
Ripple chose the best plot of land in the house, planted the peonies they had moved, and carefully managed it.
However, good flowers are hard to grow. In the spring of the following year, these immigrated peonies had no flowers or leaves. They had just revealed a few green shoots, and soon withered.
Ripple was very sad, and his friend advised him, “Your failure this time was the wrong season for transplanting. August 15 is the peony's birthday. It is best to transplant the peony around its birthday to survive.” When Ripple heard this, Maosai opened up and decided to go to the Qinling Mountains until the fall of August.
Ripple set out at the end of July. He climbed the Qinling Mountains twice. After going for a month, he came home full of loads. Cut the peonies you brought back to their original location. In the spring of the following year, strong young shoots certainly grew. No effort was spared. After the rain, the peonies that came to bloom. They are red, white, pink, and purple, and are still single.
Ripple analyzed the growth habits of peonies, fertilized them in a timely manner, and managed them carefully. Within a few years, they changed from single flowers to double petals, and built buildings, which are very beautiful.
Ripple felt that using this limited number of dozens of peonies to make “dandelions” is not enough; it must also be cultivated in large quantities to add new varieties. He heard that although Luoyang was attacked by the Jin and Yuan dynasties, peonies fell into decay and were almost extinct, but in the countryside, more than 10 colored varieties such as Erqiao, Yao Huang, and Wei Zi have been preserved. He also worked tirelessly. He traveled a long distance to Luoyang to retrieve more than 10 new varieties and expand his garden.
