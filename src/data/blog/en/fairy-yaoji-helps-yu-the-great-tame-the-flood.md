 In the days when Xia Yu was in charge of water, the 23rd daughter of Western Wangmu lived in the Yaochi Palace. Her name was Yao Ji. She is smart and beautiful, kind-hearted, lively and cheerful, and can't stand the lonely life in the palace. On August 15, she invited the eleven sisters around her to drive through the clouds and travel all over the world. When they arrived in Wushan, they only saw 12 evil dragons making waves, and Daewoo, who was managing the water, was also besieged by floods. Yao Ji admired Dayu's spirit of managing water without entering the house and decided to help him manage the water.
Dayu was then given a copy of the “Shang Qing Bao Sutra”. Before Yao Ji had time to tell Dayu how to decipher this celestial book, she was taken back to the palace by Heavenly Soldiers sent by the Western Queen's Mother. The Twelve Fairies have long been tired of living in the Fairy Palace. They broke free from the chains and returned to the world to help Dayu clear the channel and eliminate flooding. Since then, Yao Ji has fallen in love with the Three Gorges and has been running between the peaks of Wushan Mountain all day to remove water demons for the boaters, drive tigers and leopards for the woodworkers, and pour clouds and rain for the farmers... Sister 12 forgot about returning to the palace. Over time, they have turned into 12 beautiful and beautiful mountains standing on both sides of the Wuxian Gorge. Yao Ji is an outstanding representative of the Twelve Fairies. It is the highest peak. Every day it welcomes the morning glow for the first time, it has earned the fame of “Wangxia Peak.”
