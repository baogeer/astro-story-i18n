 Legend has it that when Fan Lihua marched west, he brought soldiers and horses to Mulei County in Xinjiang. At this point, a large river blocked the way.
Fan Lihua had no choice but to ask Bing Ma to stop and set up camp and wait for a while. She brought eight companions herself to the foot of the mountain. She looked around and saw that there were many pine trees growing on the mountain. As a result, the soldiers were ordered to go up the mountain to cut down the pine trees and use them to build a bridge. Since the surface of the river is too wide, it is impossible to build on it. As a result, Fan Lihua ordered the soldiers to put up a pile in the water and use wood as a bridge. Only then did Fan Lihua successfully pass through with his soldiers and horses.
Because the bridge is built of wood, it is called a “wooden bridge.” Later, people called this river Mulei River.
