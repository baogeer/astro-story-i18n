Feng Xiaoqing is the concubine of a rich man surnamed Feng from Jiangnan. She is extremely beautiful, fair, and thin. She cares for herself every day. We also have a daffodil like this in China. Her name is Feng Xiaoqing, and she self-destructed in narcissism. Feng Xiaoqing was originally the daughter of the Guangling family (present-day Yangzhou). Her father was also crowned Taishou of Guangling. Her childhood was blessed with golden clothes and jade, and she called for slaves. When Feng Xiaoqing was 10 years old, an old woman from Taishou Fuchu came to take her out of the family. “If you can't bear to give up, don't let her study and be literate; she may still have 30 years of good life!” However, late. Soon, Yan Wang and Zhu Di took the throne, and the Feng family was slaughtered. The old Feng Xiaoqing went out with Mrs. Yang, a distant relative at the time. She survived the disaster. In the midst of panic, she fled to Hangzhou with Mrs. Yang. Also, according to research, Feng Xiaoqing was a “thin Yangzhou horse” before getting married. This is a group of young girls who are being supported. They have gone through physical training, vocal training, and beauty and skin care courses since childhood. Not only were they not allowed to get close to Xiaoqing, but they also deducted Xiaoqing's living expenses.
