 Every year on July 7 of the lunar calendar, it is the Seven Begging Festival, also known as Girls' Day. According to folklore, on this night, magpies flew to the Milky Way in groups and built a magpie bridge, so that the cowherd and the weaver met on the Galaxy Magpie Bridge. A folk custom called “begging for wisdom and dexterity” on the night of Tanabata is to sacrifice her for wisdom and craftsmanship. On the day of Tanabata, every household mixed the flour with sugar until firm, cut it into 2-inch long strips, and twisted into a tangled shape. After frying, it was crunchy and sweet. Famous day chocolate is a must-have offering for Beggar Day. Wu Di also used a teacup to fill the garden with mandarin duck water (a mixture of well and river water), and mix the dew in the garden. After sunrise, the girls put in a small needle to make the needle float on the surface of the water and see the needle on the bottom of the water. If it looks like a cloud and a flower, it's a “clever sign”; if it looks like a pestle, it's a “bad sign.” There are also customs such as piercing pinholes with thread to discern good and bad skills.
Rolling Fairy
Legend has it that April 14 of the lunar calendar is the birthday of Lu Dongbin, one of the Eight Immortals, commonly known as the “fairy birthday.” The Temple of the Immortals is in Xiatang, Hongqiao, Changmen, the former Fu Ji Temple. In the old days, there was an endless flow of people who went to the Temple of the Immortals to worship incense on April 14. Among them, prostitutes, doctors, and the pharmacy industry were the most fanatical. Rumor has it that among the bustling crowd of people with strange illnesses, people with strange illnesses often get treatment, so the crowds are particularly crowded. Everyone wants to try their luck and get the blessings of the immortals, commonly known as “rolling the immortals.” Many small vendors also visited the Hongqiao to Gaoqiao area in Changmen to sell small products such as clay toys, flower bonsai, fish, bugs, flowers, and birds. Today, the superstitious activity of entering the Temple of the Immortal Incense is extinct, but the custom of punching immortals is not easy to follow. Every time the Immortals Day, the area around the Temple of the Immortals is still full of people, and there are all kinds of small items for daily use and viewing. “Rolling the Immortal” has actually become a folk temple fair event.
Ishiko Kushizuki
Every year on August 18 of the lunar calendar, Suzhou has an old custom of boating at Shihu Lake to enjoy the moon. It is called a “stone lake with the moon”, also known as a “stone lake tour.” Shihu Lake is located in the southwest of Suzhou city, 25 miles from the lake. Under the clear moonlight, the lake and mountains, pagoda and picture bridges, sailing and fishing boats, the scenery is beautiful and fascinating. At night, the moonlight was reflected in the water through the Xingchun Bridge hole in Shihu Lake. It is also said that the moonlight passed through the chain ring hole in the tower above, and the reflection just happened to be a string. People from Suzhou visited Shihu Lake in unprecedented success. At that time, large and small boats floated out of the city. Cruise boats and lanterns were intertwined. There was a lot of music on the lake, and there were many vendors on the shore. Cai Yun's “Wu Gu” poem day: “The painting on the side of the Xingchun Bridge stopped, and the autumn lights were bright and bright for ten miles. In the middle of the night, the tide watched the moon, and a few people were drunk watching the lake pavilion.”
Rizaizaizo
Legend has it that June 24 of the lunar calendar is Lei Zun's birthday. Lei Zun, or Lei Gong, is the mythical god who throws thunder. When you eat “Leizai Vegetarian,” you are a vegetarian in order to worship Leizun. It is said that if you take leizai, you can relieve disasters and avoid the epidemic and keep yourself safe. In the past, people in Suzhou who ate “lei zhaisu” usually started in early June until Lei Zun's birthday, for almost a month. On the day of the opening of the meat, the Songhelou Restaurant on Guanqian Street launched a special seasonal noodle dish, braised duck noodles, made with fresh fat duck, which is fresh and delicious. Therefore, there is an afterword in Suzhou: “Lei Zhai vegetarian meat - braised duck noodles.”
Reveal the Book and Read the Bible
Every year on June 6th of the lunar calendar, there is a custom of reading in Suzhou. Picture books were displayed in court on this day to prevent insects, cavities, and corrosion, which was particularly effective. Various monasteries and temples carried out their collection of scriptures to the sun. The monks took the opportunity to summon old rural women to have a “scripture reading session” where they were exposed to the sun, claiming that “after going through the scriptures ten times, they can become men.” There is also a folk saying, “June 6th, dog rotten bath.” (Bathing is called rotten bath in Suzhou dialect) On a daily basis, dogs and cats are taken to the river to bathe to avoid lice and fleas.
