 When Hao Shijue Garden, known as the “best real estate,” opened, Feng Shui master Jia Fugui in the corner of the venue was a crowd taker. Almost half of the magnesium lights and cameras focused on him alone. The property market is now in high demand, spawning a group of Feng Shui masters who refer to homesteaders. In addition, more and more rich people are obsessed with Buji taking the lead, Feng Shui and his entourage can simply be described as popular. No. Apart from a group of reporters who wanted to see the “true face of Lushan Mountain,” the onlookers were more Feng Shui enthusiasts who wanted to worship this “greatest master” as a teacher.
In Feng Shui, the word “accurate” comes first; the more accurate, the more famous. It's not easy to think a thousand things out of 100, called “Master of the Thousand Engineers,” which is enough to make one party famous; and being foolproof is very rare; it is very rare; it is honorable as “Master Wan Zhenshi.” There are fewer than ten Wan Zhenshi in the country; their popularity can be seen clearly.
Jia Fugui is a new Master Wan Zhenzhen. He has a leather suit and a red face. He is also the vice president of the Yixue Research Association, which has been recognized by 800 countries. So he has enough strength and a straight back. He holds a microphone and humbly said, “Thank you to all the people for their love, but we still need to choose another lucky day for the sake of receiving an apprentice.”
“The day after tomorrow, Venus will rush south, and Jiachen will meet. It is good to worship teachers and take apprentices.” Suddenly, a clear voice came from the crowd.
Everyone followed their popularity; this remark actually came from a 178-year-old guy.
However, Jia Fugui was neither embarrassed nor annoyed. After thinking about it for a moment, his bright light flashed. He immediately approved of this day by Shunshui Tuzhou: “Forty and three despises people. No children yet, that's all! An apprenticeship conference will be held the day after tomorrow. Anyone who has passed my three levels can worship me as a teacher. Please spread the word to your media friends.”
Under the “clap bang” flash, Jia Fugui bowed deeply. Actually, he's stealing fun. In Feng Shui, apart from “accuracy,” “word of mouth” is the second principle. Now, thanks to free advertisements from the media, after the apprenticeship conference, Jia Fugui's “Wan Zhenshi” name naturally went unabated. At that point, his fortune telling fees and entrance fees won't be as simple as doubling or doubling.
The auspicious day has arrived, and a good day has been chosen. The solemn reception was successfully held in the conference hall of a luxury hotel.
Jia Fugui wore a blue shirt and said to the hundreds of students attending the worship service, “Feng Shui masters are different from witches and gods in feudal society. We have a textbook called “Zhou Yi,” an instrument called a compass, and a purpose: to promote natural coexistence between heaven, earth, and people, and add bricks and tiles to a harmonious society!” Some remarks drew warm applause, and Jia Fugui also said, “So this is the first test for basic knowledge.” At the end of the day, a few assistants sent everyone a test paper with questions and answers printed on it.
This bothers those unlearned guys. Who can carry “Zhou Yi”, let alone how to use a compass. Nearly one-third of the participants left the venue in a hurry. This scene once again drew applause from reporters and bystanders.
After answering the questions, Jia Fugui reviewed them one by one. From time to time, he shook his head and sighed, and finally erased out one-third of the answers that failed.
In a blink of an eye, there were only about 30 students left at the venue. Jia Fugui began to laugh and talk: “The universe has three principles: truth, goodness, and beauty. You all have real material, so the second level will test your understanding of 'good'.” Jia Fugui turned on the projector, and an example popped up on the screen: the competition between the two major real estate companies in a certain city was fierce. In order to crush their rivals, they did not hesitate to pay bribes to the Feng Shui company and let the Feng Shui company take advantage of each other in an attempt to curb the competitor's development. It just so happened that these two real estate agents found the same Feng Shui company. The question is: If you were in charge, how would you handle it?
The students scratched their ears one by one, and the reporters on the scene also pondered with relish. As soon as it was time to answer the questions, Jia Fugui reviewed the answers one by one.
“Oops, some people are so foolish that they first serve oolong for one family and then another.” The conference hall laughed. Jia Fugui explained, “Really, we can't overdo it! If you only seek the truth, you are probably doing evil!”
Although many people walked in the hall, the applause was particularly loud this time.
“Others suggest donating money to charity, calling it a cure for nothing.” Jia Fugui smiled bitterly and continued to comment, “I don't agree. It costs people money to solve disasters for others, and the company needs capital to operate. I don't like unrealistic hypocrisy. In fact, this was a project I picked up in 2000. As some people answered, I placed an 'oolong' on both sides, but I also placed a glass purple gold ball in the middle. In this way, it became a double dragon jewel.”
The venue was silent, and everyone looked at Jia Fugui with admiration.
“Okay, listen to the remaining nine. The science of Feng Shui is vast and profound, and the wording of Bu Gu is also very varied. However, customers always like to please Geely, yet we cannot deceive them even if we are aggressive. Here is the third hurdle, the third logic of the universe, beauty.”
In this case, roughly speaking, a wealthy businesswoman divined for her daughter-in-law who was pregnant in March, but the guessing appeared to be a major culprit, causing a dispute between the couple and upset her, and asked how to gently feed back the gossip to the wife.
Jia Fugui read nine people's rhetoric and nodded. In the end, he chose only three. There is one that is best written: “Tianjuanjia is a gift for talented people, and I'm afraid they will cut love and want to take it back.” In just a few minutes, the gossip was written into poetry, describing the premature death of the fetus as a genius of natural jealousy. While satisfying the wife's vanity, it also gave her a warning and hope.
Jia Fugui targeted this prospective apprentice signed “Liu Pangui”. He was actually only 17 or 8 years old. It was the young man who indicated the date of his mentor in the sales hall the day before yesterday!
Seeing that the conference was coming to an end, Jia Fugui sincerely confessed, “Friends from all walks of life, becoming Master Wan Zhenshi is not as easy as many people think. At the age of 25, I was supposed to enjoy glory and wealth, but I decided to go out and break through and worship the famous senior Cui Daniu as my mentor. It can be described as going through many hardships...”
Speaking of “exploring the gorge and visiting the river under the scorching sun,” a prospective apprentice got up violently and left the venue.
Speaking of “sleeping with dead bodies in the mountains,” another cursed “pervert,” and also abstained.
Seeing that the two “childcare” performed brilliantly, Jia Fugui anticipated that the results had already been achieved. After all, he didn't want to scare the real apprentice, so the conversation turned around: “Real gold is not afraid of fire. You are my true apprentice!”
As expected, the reporter saw this meaningful scene and hurriedly turned on the camera, which had already been turned off, and the audience that had intended to leave the venue sat back in their original position. The thunderous applause announced the climax of the conference. It was just different from what everyone expected to see. Liu Pangui was like a block of wood, neither had a happy expression, nor did he take the stage to sign an agreement. It wasn't until the applause disappeared that he coldly said, “Master Jia, after passing your three levels, I can worship you as my teacher. But you have to answer a few of my questions before you can take me as a prisoner.”
“But it's OK to ask.” Hearing him sell things off, Jia Fugui was a bit alarmed, but he still held his ground and was as kind as ever.
“Two years ago, a poor rural woman with a ruined reputation lied about being a stranger and asking for gossip. Who had no mercy to say 'have no rich children, die without companion' and end up in depression?” Without waiting for an argument, Liu Pangui also asked, “Well, the Double Dragon Ball cost tens of millions of dollars. Who will pay for it in the end? All I know is that housing prices in Ssangyong City went up from 7,000 to 10,000 overnight. Isn't this good for ordinary people?”
Jia Fugui was blushing and blushing; he was poor at reason and speechless. He was trying to give his best support, and Liu Pangui stood up and said, “I still want to unravel the scandal someone did when he was 25! He is gifted and intelligent, and became a Believer at the age of 25. However, there was a time when the fortune telling for my wife turned out to be “futile.” This hypocritical luckier ran away from home in order not to fail in his divination... was this really “futile”? His wife was pregnant when he left, and later did not hesitate to self-destruct her face to prevent other men from craving her beauty...”
Looking at Jia Fugui, who was paralyzed to the ground, Liu Pan, who choked a few times, clenched his teeth, like an angry leopard: “Huh! Wan Zhenshi! Just because of your truth, I've been living under my mother's bitter lies for 18 years!”
Once again, the venue was silent.
