 Caves are a form of residential housing unique to northern Shaanxi. The ancestors of the Chinese nation survived, thrived, and grew in caves. There are many types of caves: earthen caves, stone caves, brick caves, earth-based caves, Liujiazi caves, Liubazi caves, and interface caves. People use the characteristics of loess. Caves built by digging holes are called earthen caves. They are generally 7-8 meters deep, more than 3 meters high, about 3 meters wide, and up to 20 meters deep. There are two types of windows. One is a small square window of about 1 square meter; the other is a round window of about 3-4 square meters. It is characterized by being warm in winter and cool in summer. Stone is used as a building material. The stone arch cave is 7-9 meters deep and about 3 meters wide and high. It is called a stone cave. The style and construction method of the brick kiln is the same as that of a stone cave, and the appearance is beautiful. First hospital caves usually have 3 or 5 holes. The middle kilns are regular kilns. Some are divided into front and back kilns, and some have 1 in 3 holes. Caves are usually built on the side of a mountain or at the foot of a mountain in a sunny spot, and many trees, flowers and plants are planted around the top of the cave. The cave is a product of the Loess Plateau and a symbol of farmers in northern Shaanxi. Here, the deep culture of ancient yellow land has been deposited, and the people of northern Shaanxi have created unique cave art (folk art) here. A farmer has worked hard all his life. The most basic wish is to build a few holes in the cave. Having a kiln and marrying a wife is considered a family and a business. The men dug in the yellow earth, while the women did housework in earthen caves. The folk art of decoupage in northern Shaanxi was created by farm women to beautify their own caves.
Starting with choosing a site for a cave, before building a cave, you must first ask Mr. Yin and Yang Feng Shui to look at the terrain, determine the direction, and choose good fortune. In the minds of local people, building a cave is a major event relating to the rise and fall of families and the prosperity of children and grandchildren. Therefore, when building trees, there are many folklore customs, which are very solemn. You must live in a shared house, choose a good place, and those who have a strong mountain after home are called “rich in mountains”. As the saying goes, “Backed by a golden mountain face facing south, ancestors have been blessed with great officials”; those who have no support behind their homes are often called “back to the mountains and the sky.” After completion, Yin and Yang chose the date to carry out Helongkou. Before they officially lived, they also had customs such as Anchang, Azujin, and warm kilns. No matter how strong the land is or how wide the field of view is, as long as Mr. Yin and Yang says that Feng Shui is bad, it must be replaced. The time of Helongkou is also a specific form of sacrifice to heaven and earth. This is even more so when it comes to Azuchi God and the sacrifice of Heaven, Earth, and Earth in the middle of the two holes. The reason for this situation is that people in this area have relatively common pantheistic beliefs and believe that “there is a god three feet up.” Even common stone mills are common. Legend has it that it was changed by a blue dragon. The rotating stone roller is the eye of the blue dragon. It is also said that Qinglong is suspected of fighting off. As a result, many rural people in northern Shaanxi have experienced a red and white happy event and passed through a stone mill. The owner always had to cover it with a red cloth, but they were afraid they would rush to the Blue Dragon. Every time during the Spring Festival, the owner pasted the four characters “Qinglong Daikichi” horizontally on the stone mill to avoid evil and relieve disasters. Most of the folk activities in this area are also related to gods and ghosts. For example, the four-person scene in the Da Yang Ge, evolved from the ancient sorcerer's dancing god; the Yang Ge team followed the gate to celebrate the New Year, supposedly begging for blessings and disaster relief for every household. However, the folk song Zen-9, some people say it comes from the 18th year of the Yellow River during the ancient war; in fact, it is a representation of yin and yang gossip. “Turn the nine songs”, also known as “turn the lights,” is a folk custom memorial service. People seek the blessings of the gods and spirits to save themselves from disasters within a year, be safe, have plenty of grains, be healthy, and be happy and beautiful. This kind of communication room tradition is inseparable from religious beliefs. According to legend, the three daughters of the Three Immortals in “Fengshenbang” (Yunxiao, Qiongxiao, and Bixiao) fought a battle in Xiqibu to report the murder of their brother, called the “Nine Tracks.” After establishing the Jade Emperor, Sanguan Temple (Tianguan, Earth Officer, Human Officer), and the Ancient Buddha Temple, it was changed to a “Jiuqu Lantern” to commemorate the death of the three maidens to avenge their brother's death. Since then, this custom has become popular among the people, and it has been passed down from generation to generation, and continues to flourish for a long time. The impact on local interpersonal relationships. Caves have a huge impact on the formation of local interpersonal connections, which are mainly reflected in the following points. First, a cave is generally a family with three holes, five holes, and seven holes, and the people who live there are close blood relatives. Before liberation, in some places, a large family lived in a courtyard, but until now, this pattern has basically disappeared; second, although large families don't live in one courtyard, their homes are still relatively concentrated. Some are distributed at the top of a hill, and some are distributed on several neighboring mountains. Finally, the former deep house compound disappeared, and the walls became shorter, making part of the family life public. There are many relationships between people, and the distance is relatively small.
