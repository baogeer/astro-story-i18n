Princess Binh Duong is the daughter of Tang Gaozu and Li Yuan. Of course, her last name is Li. However, there is no historical record of her famous name. The attitude towards women in Chinese history books changed one by one in five generations. Five generations ago, no matter what kind of woman, most history books would leave their names. However, after the Song dynasty, women's names were no longer publicly recorded in the annals of history. The more honorable a woman is, the more so. Instead, so-called “female thieves” are often directly called by their name in history books. The “Old Tang Book” and “New Tang Book”, which document the deeds of Princess Pingyang, were written in the later Jin Dynasty and the other in Northern Song, but none of them record the name or age of Princess Pingyang. Therefore, having future generations write martial arts novels like Huang Yizhe had no choice but to randomly name her Lee Soo-ning.
Tang Gaozu and Li Yuan had a total of 19 daughters, and Princess Pingyang is in third place. Of course, these 19 daughters can't all be born to the same mother. Princess Taiping's family
Before introducing Princess Taiping, we should talk about her family. Because the Tang Dynasty was in the transition phase from an aristocratic society to a bureaucratic society, the power of the aristocrats also played an important role in the politics of the Tang Dynasty, especially in the early Tang period. The division and restructuring among aristocrats at various levels in various regions was one of the important elements of early Tang politics. In this general social environment, birth and family have a huge impact on a person's life experience, especially for people at the center of the political vortex, such as Princess Taiping. Princess Taiping's father is Tang Gaozong Li Zhi. Although the Lee family were members of the royal family, they were not a first-class noble family at the time. Since Tang Gaozu founded the Tang dynasty, several generations of emperors have adopted a policy of fighting back and forth against traditional first-class families, especially the Shandong warriors, and mainly restrained them. In the fourth year of Tang Gaozong's Xianqing (659), he also issued a decree prohibiting the Taiyuan Wang clan, the Xingyang Zheng clan, the Qinghe Cui clan, and the Fan Yang Lu clan from “self-made marriages” (“General Guide to the Administration of Ziji” vol. 200) to weaken the power of the Shandong warrior family. At that time, the royal family also often married famous ministers or the Guanzhong and Daibei aristocrats, and they basically didn't get along well with the Shandong shih family. This policy directly affected Princess Taiping's marriage.
Princess Taiping's mother is Wu Zetian. The Wu family is an authentic tribe. Wu Zetian's father is probably a timber merchant, while her mother, Yang, is of unknown origin.
