 Fu Bi, Zi Yan Guo, famous minister of the Northern Song Dynasty, native of Luoyang. When he was a teenager, he was extremely intelligent, and he read books too well. Fan Zhongyuan saw him, said that he had “Wang Zuo's talent,” and recommended him to the imperial court; Yan Shu saw him and said that he had excellent talents and married his daughter to him. He was the best negotiator in the Northern Song Dynasty. One word thwarted the Khitan country's attempt to invade the border. He lived for 7 years and lived a long time. He died at the age of 80.
Among the prime minister of the Northern Song Dynasty, Tomibu and Moon Hyon-bak are the oldest living stars: Fumihyo lived to be 92, and Fu Bi lived to 80. After 80 years of leisurely time and 60 years as an official and eunuch, Fu Bi's people grew up in a majestic river. He only captured a few waves here to watch his whole life.

I think someone has the same name and surname as me.” The person who scolded him “heard it so bad” and quickly apologized to Fu Bi.
The younger Fu Bi clearly pretended to be confused, but he showed his intelligence and wisdom. The Song dynasty had a blowout of many talents: Fan Zhongquan, Sima Guang, Ouyang Xiu, Wen Yanbo, Su Dongpo, Wang Anshi, etc., all easily passed the academic examination, got ahead of the exam, and achieved fame. Fu Bi's career didn't go very well. This young man is Fu Bi and is known as a “Luoyang Talent.” Fan Zhongyuan was already quite famous at the time. After getting to know Fu Bi, he greatly appreciated Fu Bi, saying that he had “Wang Zuo's talent,” and recommended his article to the then Prime Minister Yan Shu. Yan Shu is a genius. When he was less than 20 years old, he entered the entrance exam and wrote the famous phrase “No choice but to lose the flower; it seems like I have met Yan before”. As soon as he saw that Fu Bi had a future, he asked Fan Zhongquan, “Has this Luoyang talent ever been married?” Fan Zhongyuan replied, “I'm not married yet.” Yan Shu nodded in satisfaction. At the time, Yan Shu still had a girl waiting for her girlfriend, and she was asking Gong Ju Chen Xiang to help choose her son-in-law. Chen Xiang said to Yan Shu, “I am impressed by Fu Bi's writing; he has the talent of a prime minister. When Fan Zhongyuan saw that Yan Shu liked Fu Bi, he matched with Chen Xiang. Fu Bi eventually became Yan Shu's son-in-law of the East Bed.
Fu Bi was admitted to the entrance examination in the 8th year of Tiansheng (AD 1030). He was 27 years old at the time, and he belonged to the Maocai Department of Heterogeneity and No. 1. After starting his career, he first went to Heyang (present-day Mengzhou) to serve as an official in the Jiedu Judicial Office, then in Jiangzhou (present-day Xinyi, Shanxi) and Yunzhou (present-day Dongping, Shandong). A few years later, there were many mutiny in the Shandong region. Some state and county governors saw that the bandits were strong, weak, and incompetent. Not only did they not suppress them, but instead opened up their doors and accepted them as a courtesy. The imperial court later investigated and dispatched a task force to conduct a thorough investigation.
Fan Zhongyuan sat down to deal with these things. Fu Bi said to Fan Zhongyuan, “These state and county governors, with imperial court salaries, actually condoned rapes. They should all be convicted of death; otherwise, no one would fight against bandits in the future.” Fan Zhongyuan, on the other hand, said, “You don't know, the bandits are powerful. They are far away in the mountains and forests, so it is difficult to encircle them. The local government has insufficient military strength, and rashly encircled them; they only hurt their teachers and their wealth, causing ordinary people to suffer in vain. They are holding on to their forces in an attempt to slow down the attack. This is probably an expedient measure to protect the people.” Fu Bi disagreed with Fan Zhongyuan's opinion, blushing and arguing with her mentor. Someone advised Fubil, “You've gone too far. Have you forgotten Mr. Fan's great kindness and virtue to you?” It turns out that after Fu Bi entered the exam, it was just when the emperor issued a decree to seek wisdom, and he had to personally inspect the world's corporals. Upon hearing this news, Fan Zhongyuan immediately sent someone to call Fu Bi and prepared him with a study room and books so that he could concentrate on writing his views on political affairs. As a result, Fu Bi was appreciated by the emperor.
However, Fu Bi replied, “My relationship with Mr. Fan is a gentleman's relationship. Mr. recommended me not because I always shared his views, but because I dared to express my own opinions when things happened. How can I give up my claim because I want to repay him?” Fan Zhongyuan later said, “Fu Bi is not popular. I admire him because of this.”
Fu Bi had opinions about the incident, did not follow blindly, and did not follow others casually, even to Tenshi.
two
Song Renzong and Zhao Zhen at the time saw the shortcomings of the Song dynasty policy and needed to carry out some reforms. In the plan submitted by the minister, he liked the plan of Fan Zhongyuan and Fu Bi, so he implemented the ten reforms proposed by them all over the country. Historically, this reform has been called the “Qingli New Deal.”
In order to implement the New Deal, Fan Zhongyuan visited various places with ministers such as Han Qi and Fu Bi to screen all supervisors (inspectors) (“road” is the name of the administrative division of the Song dynasty). Once, while reviewing a list of supervisors and supervisors, Fan Zhongyuan discovered that there were corrupt and wrongdoing officials, and he wrote down the names of these people one by one to prepare to select others. Fu Bi watched it on the sidelines. She was a little unbearable, and said to Fan Zhongyuan, “Gong Fan, you can make this whole family cry.” Fan Zhongyuan said seriously, “Yan Guo, if I don't let the families of these officials cry, then it will hurt people all the way to cry.” This time, Fu Bi is no longer arguing with Fan Zhongyuan. He felt that Fan Zhongyuan was right, and since then he has been more realistic in his affairs.
In the 2nd year of the Qingli era (AD 1042), Fu Bi was promoted to the government and inspected the prison in Beijing. In the spring of the year, the Khitan army pressured the situation and threatened to use force to wipe out the Central Plains and demand compensation from the Great Song dynasty before settling the army. The Song royal family, panicked, hurriedly discussed sending ministers to Khitan to seek peace, but the ministers of North Korea and China all carried out dangerous acts because of this, and they were afraid to go. Some people recommended Fu Bi. Fu Bi was appointed as an ambassador, served as a Privy Clerk, and traveled to Khitan.
Seeing the Khitan emperor Liao Xing Zong, Fu Bi asked, “Why did they send troops to attack Song?” Liao Xingzong answered, “It was you who broke the contract first and blocked the barrier, so we need to attack. If you divide the land and seek peace, we can withdraw our troops.” Tomibi said, “I know you don't want to send troops yourself, but your courtiers are yelling about going to war. Did you know they all did it for their own profit?” Liao Xingzong was surprised and asked, “How do you say this?” Fu Bi said, “We have conquered the empire for thousands of miles in the Song dynasty, and have one million elite soldiers. If you want to use troops, can you guarantee victory? Even if you were lucky enough to win, you would lose more than half. Can your belligerent ministers make up for these losses? If we were still as friendly as before, wouldn't the money that Daesong gave you every year be at your disposal alone?”
Liao Xing Zong thought about it and nodded yes. Fu Bi also said, “As for the blockage of our country's borders, it was intended to prevent rebels, not to attack others. If it's to attack, we won't block the barrier; instead, we have to break through the barrier and go on the offensive!” Liao Xingzong felt that everything Fu Bi said was true. He invited Fu Bi to go hunting with him the next day, but he proposed a condition: “If land can be ceded to Khitan, then the two countries can be repaired for a long time.” Fu Bi asked, “Why?” Liao Xingzong said, “We are all ashamed to receive your money every year.” Fu Bi immediately retorted, “You are proud to have gained land; we must be ashamed to lose it! Are you saying that's the case? If you want us to divide our land and seek peace, never do it!”
Faced with both good arguments and tough arguments, Liao Xing Zong felt helpless and finally stopped asking for land cessation. In this way, Fu Bi did not avoid life or death, and did not dishonor his mission. By saying only one word, he dismissed Liao's plan to invade the country and turned the two countries into jade. The two countries lived peacefully for decades thereafter.
Fu Bi later went to Khitan and also had great success, showing his superhuman courage and diplomatic ability. When he first went to Khitan, his family reported that his daughter had died and he ignored it; before going to Khitan for the second time, his wife gave birth to a boy, and he was too late to take a look, so he set off. When he arrived in Khitan, every time he got a book, he didn't even tear it down. The attendant inexplicably asked, “This is a family book, why not tear it down?” He answered, “Dismantled, in vain!”

Fu Bi is dedicated to the country and was honored by Song In-jong, who wanted to promote him to the position of Deputy Privy Council. He couldn't resist it. Please accept it.” Only then did he accept the position of Deputy Privy Council.
In June of the fourth year of the Qingli era (1044 AD), war broke out between Khitan and Western Xia. The Khitan sent 100,000 troops to the Western Expedition and sent to Song to demand that the Great Song Dynasty sever diplomatic ties with Western Xia. Fan Zhongyuan suspected that the Khitan was attacking the east and west, and that they might join forces with Western Xia to attack Song, and suggested that the imperial court immediately increase troops to help the western border. After observing calmly, Fu Bi thought that Khitan would not join forces with Western Xia to kill the Great Song Dynasty again. At the time, the entire court was very worried, and there was a lot of discussion: if Da Song were attacked, would you, Fu Bi, apologize for the crime with your head? Fu Bi said very calmly, “Don't worry, this won't happen.” As a result, as Fu Bi had anticipated, Khitan did not invade the Great Song border.
In the second year of Shiwa (AD 1055), Fubi was awarded a bachelor of Shosha Ping and Jihyeonden University, and worshiped on the same day as Moon Yeon-bak. Later, along with the Minister of the DPRK and China, Tomiji persuaded the Emperor Ninzong to become an early emperor to calm people's hearts.
After Song Shenzong succeeded to the throne, Wang Anshi was summoned to Beijing in April of the first year of Xining (AD 1068) to reform the law in order to strengthen the country's military. Shinto wanted to ask Fu Bi about his views on the transformation of the law, because Fu Bi was a veteran of the three dynasties, so he shouldered his shoulder to the temple. Fu Bi saw that Song Shenzong reused Wang Anshi and knew it was impossible for him to fight against him, so he claimed that he was sick and retired and wanted to return to Luoyang. He was only allowed to go back to Luoyang dozens of times. Before he left, he went to the Shushen sect and said that now Wang Anshi has changed the law and used many villains. Coupled with earthquakes and droughts, national affairs are worrying. Shinto asked him who could replace him after he went, and he recommended Moon Yeon-bak. Shinto also asked, “How is Wang Anshi?” Tomibu was silent and did not answer.
As a result, Wang Anshi's son Wang Yi hated Fu Bi and privately said, “If Xiao Fu Bi is the first in the city, then the law will be enforced.” Fu Bi then retreated to Luoyang and formed the “Luoyang Senior Club” with 13 people, including Wen Yanbo and Sima Guang. Every day, he made alcohol in his home to make peace with poetry, but every time a major national event occurred, he still made suggestions and suggestions, and spoke freely.
In August of the 6th year of Yuan Feng (AD 1083), Fu Bi, 80, died of illness in Luoyang. Before his death, he gave a message to God: there are now many villains in speculative drilling camps in North Korea. They are not a blessing for the country, and should be clarified.
