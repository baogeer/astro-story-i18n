 Zuo Hanchen's oral description u2002u2002u2002 collected and sorted by Zuo Dehui
His father, Zuo Wenwei, was the township chief of Nanpian Township (now the three townships of Brachang, Longtan, and Jijie) in Yangyi County before the founding of the People's Republic of China or before 1949. Although he was the head of his hometown, he never used his power to bully people, or even retaliated his grievances with his virtue. As a result, when the land was reformed, landowners, large and small, were fought to varying degrees (mass meetings were held, and those who were combated were punched and kicked with high hats made of paper and kneeled; heavy ones were punched and kicked, tied up with flowers, hung from a beam, and fell on their feet), and the father did not ask for a fight, so he was spared this robbery.
Grandpa, however, is different from his father. He has a rough personality; he admits money and doesn't recognize people. Alba Yang Chunsheng borrowed money for several years and never paid it back on the grounds that he had no money. One day, Grandpa went to Yang Chunsheng's house to collect debts. Without paying back the money, Grandpa went to the horse stall and was beaten by Yang Chunsheng, who had lost control of his emotions. Grandpa had no choice but to come back in a hurry. When he left, he put down his words and said, “Wait, I'll have my son take you to jail.”
After the father learned about this incident, he only criticized Grandpa: “Our family has little money, we don't have much, we don't have much. It's really difficult for Yang Chunsheng's family. You don't want people's lives when you go to Ramah? It's not easy for Alba to cut a load of firewood; how big a slope will he have to climb! As the saying goes, be forgiving and forgiving. You treat the disease first, and you don't care about anything else.”
Yang Chunsheng knew it was wrong. When he saw his father coming from afar, he immediately hid under the road. The father said, “Big brother, you keep hiding; it's all my dad's fault; you shouldn't have pulled your horse. These five oceans are treated as if I were paying for my father.” After speaking, I left the ocean on the side of the road.
Later, when my father built the house and dug the foundation, Yang Chunsheng helped for more than ten days without receiving a single penny.
