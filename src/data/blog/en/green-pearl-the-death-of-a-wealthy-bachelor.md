 Author: Hou Hongbin
Lu Zhu, last name is Liang. Clever, beautiful and dignified, and able to sing prom poems. During the Taikang era of the Western Jin Dynasty, Shi Chong hired her as a concubine with three dendrobium pearls and built the Golden Valley Garden in the imperial capital Luoyang. At a time when King Zhao and Simalun were in power, Sun Xiu, the leader of Lun's party, coveted the Green Pearl and was unsuccessful in demanding the Green Pearl from Shi Chong. He was extremely angry. The lead army surrounded Jingu Garden, and the Green Pearl fell to his own death. Tang Dynasty poet Du Muyong's poem “Golden Valley Garden” said, “The bustling events scattered dust, and the flowing water ruthlessly brought forth spring; the east wind blames birds at dusk, and the flowers fall like people falling from a building.”
In fact, Shi Chong himself isn't displeased; he is just too rich. As soon as he accidentally gets on the rich list, he is on the pig slayer list, and he has no choice but to be murdered.
During the reign of Emperor Jin Wu, Shi Chong was a son of a family. He was a master assassin in Jingzhou. He was a long-sleeved dancer. He had a strong heart and a strong hand. Many of his industrial companies were spun off and listed overseas. The gold and silver were like mountains, and there were countless treasures. He simply retired early and didn't get paid as much as a civil servant in the imperial court. Simply absorbing the money from shareholders and retail investors was enough for him to live for ten years.
In Taikang's early years, Shi Chong made a mission to inspect the operation status of his family business, in Vietnam today. Passing through Shuangkakuzan in Hakushu, I met a beautiful woman who was playing the flute. I've seen a lot of beautiful women, Shi Chong, but if girls wanted to stand out, they usually had multiple licences such as certified actuaries, certified public accountants, senior interpreters, and financial analysts to get close to industry elites like Shi Chong and Wang Kai. If you think about dealing with a group of women who talk every day and have to pay a fee every six seconds, aren't you bothered? ——However, this little girl from a small border town obviously doesn't know anything other than singing, dancing, and playing the flute. This gave Shi Chong a sense of security. As a result, Shi Chong spent three dendrobium pearls as a dowry and married this girl named Green Pearl back home.
Shi Chong was originally a person with fighting spirit, courage, vocal rules, understanding art, and knowing how to enjoy life. He composed the song “Ming Jun Song,” taught “The Dance of Forgetting Worries,” designed Miki's costumes, designed the garden landscape, and created a special atmosphere. This kind of knowledgeable and interesting man is nothing short of a diamond king in the minds of young, middle, and young women — although they have had a concubine, but as long as he likes them, they can get married one by one. Shi Chong is also a real estate pioneer in Luoyang, Beijing. He developed a real estate “Golden Valley Garden” in Jinguyuan on the outskirts of the city. The pavilions, exotic flowers, fish and plants, raise monkeys and horses, and peacocks walk downstairs. Green Pearl lives in the deepest waterfront villa and lives a happy life in paradise on earth.
Shi Chong is very idle, and he has so much money that he has no place to spend. He can't help but often compete with the imperial family and relatives to compete for victory. Once Emperor Jin Wu gave his uncle Wang Kai a two-foot-tall coral tree. Wang Kai enthusiastically ran to the Jingu Garden to brag about it to Shi Chong; who could have inadvertently crushed it with iron Ruyi. Wang Kai was shocked. Shi Chongxin calmed down and his servant took out the coral trees he had hidden in his home and listed them on the table. There were 67 trees that were three or four feet tall, and many more were about two feet tall. Wang Kai was dumbfounded, hugged a plant at will, and left Jingu Garden in a state of confusion. As a result, Shi Chong was added to the top of the wealth list by Xi Jin. At the time, there were people who wanted to levy taxes on him, others wanted to tie his tickets, and others wanted to rob him of his money.
Just in time for the Hachiou Rebellion, King Zhao and Simalun are in full swing, and his subordinates have a ruthless character, Sun Xiu. Sun Xiuhu pretended to be Tiger Wei and wanted to ask Shi Chong for the Green Pearl. Shi Chong was so angry that he actually asked me for my little wife? Are you also too disrespectful to national entrepreneurs? Don't give it! After rejecting Sun Xiu, Shi Chong was not afraid, so he found Pan An, a famous beautiful man. The two urged Sima Yun, the king of Runan, to rebel. As a result, things fell apart. Wang Simalun ordered Shi Chong, Pan An, etc. to be apprehended and brought to justice. Sun Xiu led the brigade and surrounded the Jingu Garden Group in a menacing manner. Shi Chong was enjoying a drink with Green Pearl upstairs. Suddenly he rode to the door, and when he knew something was wrong, he said to Green Zhu, “I offended someone for you today. What should I do?” With tears in her eyes, Green Pearl said, “Let the thieves not succeed before they die!” Having said that, Chaolan Gan took a leap forward and spilled blood at Jingu Garden. Shi Chong couldn't stop him; he just picked up a torn dress.
In fact, Shi Chong seems to be passionate; in fact, he is unaffectionate. What does failure to rebel on one's own have anything to do with the beautiful woman in the family? If Green Pearl wasn't a girl with a simple nature and a chivalrous heart, but a female actuary, then she would simply settle her interests right away and join Sun Xiu.
Sun Xiu originally wanted to capture Shi Chong, seize his family property, and take his family home. He couldn't imagine that Green Pearl had died, so he took Shi Chong directly to Dongshi for execution without interrogation. Shi Chong lamented before the sentence: “My slaves are greedy for my family's wealth!” Only then did I understand; it was too late.
