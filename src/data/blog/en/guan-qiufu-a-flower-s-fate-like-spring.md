 Some people say, “Home flowers don't smell as good as wild flowers.” If a man and woman become a couple, keep up with each other morning and night, know everything, and live a long time, they can easily lose their freshness and appeal. As a result, many merry masseuses left their lovely wives aside, keen to go to Fireworks Willow Alley to find charm and enjoy the taste of “wild flowers.” However, this is not the case for a truly passionate couple. Their relationship is like alcohol in a cellar. The longer it is, the more aromatic it becomes, the more delicious it becomes. They can always create a variety of charms in an ordinary family life. They are intoxicated by each other, and they will never lose their appeal. Guan Qiufu, a talented woman from Jiangnan, and her husband Jiang Tan are such a loving couple. Speaking of the strong feelings between them, it is quite inspiring and evokes people's dreams.
Guan Qiufu, Ming Ying, a native of Qiantang, was born in the 3rd year of the Qing Xuanzong Daoguang in a talented family. Father Guan read a lot of poetry books but his career didn't go well. He often indulged in poetry and wine with a group of literary friends and masseuses. Guan Qiufu grew up to be charming and loved by her father's friends, so Wei Zibo taught her how to learn poetry, Yang Zhubai taught her how to learn painting, and Li Yufeng taught her piano. Although she was not a famous girl, she was a true legend of poetry, painting, and piano masters at the time, and became an outstanding woman from all walks of life.
Women who are so talented are, of course, “nice gentlemen.” The Guan family did not want to borrow their daughter to raise Gaozhi; instead, they naturally assigned Guan Qiufu Xu to her aunt and cousin Jiang Tan. Jiang Tan also grew up in Shuxiangmen. His family wasn't rich, but his poetry was passed down from generation to generation, making him a very cultured and temperamental celebrity. The Guan and Jiang families live very close together. Guan Qiufu and Jiang Tan have always played together since childhood. They are close friends. Growing up, they admired each other's talents and were happy with each other. Both parents deeply understood their children's concerns, went with the flow, and achieved 100 years of happiness for them. The marriage was completed in the fall of Daoguang 23. Amidst the fragrance of laurel, Guan Qiufu stepped into Jiang's family with joy and timidity. The banquet was over three years old. Red candles flashed in the cave room, and the warmth was melting. Guan Qiufu sits on the edge of the bed, wearing a red silk shirt, with her head down and smiling. Her white, tender, red-dyed face looked prettier and more charming than usual. Jiang Tan, the bridegroom who had just delivered the welcome guests, walked into the cave and looked obsessively at the happy bride. Her heart fluttered, and her footsteps fluttered a bit. Yesterday she was still his cousin, and today she became his beautiful wife. In a trance, he felt that this cute newlywed was both familiar and unfamiliar. Jiang Tan walked to the bedside and sat down next to Qiu Fu. He was silent for a while, quietly holding her plump little hand and holding it tightly in the palm of her hand. Qiu Fu slowly turned her head. Yang Ximao's shy gaze met Jiang Tan, and the four bundles of eyes clasped tightly together. Slowly reading each other's eyes, it was as if they had gone back to their childhood without guessing. That layer of strangeness like smoke and fog completely disappeared, and their gaze and palm connected them tightly. So they opened up the conversation. Together, they recalled childhood games and teenage ridicule. The two have worked hand in hand to pick flowers in the yard, catch butterflies, watch ants climb trees, and pinch villains; the two also write and paint, and play the piano side by side to seek sorrow in order to create new poems... All of these beautiful past events are all owned by them. You're talking and laughing at me word for word, and before you know it, the morning bell is already ringing outside. The little girl brought by Qiu Fu knocked on the door softly and shouted in a low voice, “The sky is already dawning, miss, please get up and get your makeup done!” It was only then that the bride and groom, who were talking about the excitement in the house, woke up like a dream. They just sat on the edge of their bed for a night of fireworks in their cave.
Life after marriage was very pleasant. Although the Jiang family was not very rich, they also had some real estate. If you rely on rent to make a living, you don't have to worry about food or clothing. At this point, Jiang Tan had already passed the county exam, got his talents, and was ready to take the rural exam course, so his mission was to study. When her husband studied, Qiu Fu naturally acted as a “red sleeve with fragrance,” but in addition to adding fragrance, she inevitably had to add some tenderness and tenderness, causing Jiang Tan to fall to her gentle hometown, and she inevitably neglected quite a bit in her schoolwork.
In spring, the garden was full of flowers. Jiang Tan was sitting in front of the study window and was able to see a few peach blossoms blooming under the courtyard wall as soon as he looked up. There was just a shower of spring rain last night. The spring rain knocked down the pink peach blossoms to the ground. At this point, Qiu Fu was crouching on the ground and holding up those disheartened Luoying, “She must be sad about falling flowers again!” This thought flashed through Jiang Tan's mind.
Qiu Fu had been squatting under the peach tree for a long time, putting around a bunch of flower petals, and didn't know what she was doing. Jiang Tan couldn't help it. He put down his books, climbed out the study room door, and quietly walked behind Qiu Fu. “Ahhh! It's so beautiful!” He exclaimed in his heart, and he held back without shouting. It turned out that Qiu Fu was making up a phrase from those peach petals; most of them had already been made up. Jiang Tan quietly read it behind her. This was a “visit to the Golden Gate”:
Halfway through spring, the life of flowers is as short as spring; overnight, the red blossoms gradually fill up, and the wind is raging...
It's a wonderful idea to save spring with falling flowers and have both body and mind! When Jiang Tanzheng secretly complained, a gust of cold wind suddenly scattered all the petals that had been placed on the ground. The word “spring” had not yet had time to finish the final touch. “It's a good 'wind and wild spring'!” Jiang Tan couldn't help but blurt out, “Why do you know I didn't create the word 'no matter' behind me?” Surprised by him, Qiu Fu turned her head and asked her husband. Jiang Tan hugged his lovely wife, who had just stood up, and quipped, “We both have a close relationship. I don't know the sentence you have in mind yet!” As soon as Qiu Fu smiled brightly and patted her husband's chest, the feeling of frustration caused by the wind and the scattered flowers just now evaporated.
The couple co-planted plantains in spring, and the leaves are already wide and colorful by fall. The autumn night was bleak. The cold rain hit the banana leaf, and Jiang Tan sat in the shadows in front of his desk and listened to it. He couldn't help but feel the same kind of nostalgia for the poet. I was so worried that I couldn't read the book anymore. I simply ran to the hospital, picked a banana leaf, dried it with a cloth, and wrote a few idle words: “Who grows bananas all the time, is always cool, and at night!” Then I called a maid and asked her to show the banana leaf to the lady who was playing the piano in her bedroom.
After a while, the little lady brought back to the study room with banana leaves. Jiang Tan thought she hadn't been able to get it to Qiu Fu. Who knows, after taking a look, the title was continued with two more lines: “You are so ungrateful. When you grew bananas, you also resented bananas!” As soon as Jiang Tan saw it, he knew it was his lovely wife's play. There was a warm current in his heart, and a heartfelt smile appeared on his face.
Although he spent many days together, Jiang Tan inevitably had some social interactions, and sometimes had to go out for a few days. These days, Lycheuf is neither sitting nor standing; she doesn't even feel like playing the piano. The phrase “butterfly loves flower” shows how sad she is:
There was no pond for a few days, and the willows were also dark. I wanted to do the clear rain; half a night of tea smoke and dream cooking, and paint a few Jiangnan trees on the screen.
Don't let the wind roll. At dusk, don't teach to move the kite; if you don't go upstairs, the mountains are green; the sunset is returning to nowhere.
The wrench index passed day by day, and finally the day came when my husband returned home. Qiu Fu woke up early this day, carefully dressed up, changed into her husband's favorite clothes, and looked out of the courtyard over and over again. At this point, all the scenes became bright and cheerful in her eyes, and the feeling of joy filled with this “Bodhisattva Mania”:
Xiaolou was in the east last night. There were not enough flowers in spring; the oblique moon had green window paper, and I dreamed of smelling Ma Huh. The pear blossoms looked like snow, and opened the door with a smile and said, “The lights came out last night; now I'm home!”
From his wife's words, Jiang Tan discovered the sadness and joy that his departure and return brought to his wife, so he couldn't bear to leave his wife easily. He was fascinated by the affectionate world created by his wife.
There must be gains and losses. Jiang Tan and Qiu Fu were immersed in a passionate affair and enjoyed the couple's love, yet they inevitably abandoned their academic career. Jiang Tan participated in the triennial village exam twice in a row, all famous for Sun Mountain. In the midst of disappointment, Jiang Tan gradually disregarded the painstaking work of fame, and drew a “picture of Qiu Lin's books”, showing that he was happy to read books and had no intention of being famous. As far as Qiu Fu is concerned, if her husband gets a place on the gold list, she can also enjoy the joy of being a husband's wife. This book is something that ordinary book readers dream of; however, since her husband is tired, she is also very understanding. She has to go on a blind date and fall in love with each other. Being poor and happy! As a result, Qiu Fu wrote these words on her husband's “Qiu Lin's Book Map”:
The family is fishing, and the whole family relies on fishing poles; many people are mistaken in their lives, so how can the woodcutter not laugh to death.
The husband and wife are dependent and indifferent for a living. Jiang Tan was very grateful for his wife's understanding, and he took the couple's affairs more and more seriously.
