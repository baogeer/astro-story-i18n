 One year, an epidemic broke out in Hangzhou. It just so happened to be a desolate year. The citizens were poor and sick, and it was very miserable.
One day, a large boat was parked by the lake in the city, and a beautiful woman sat on the boat. She takes care of the poor and sick. If someone pays to buy her, she lives in his home, serves him, and gets money to help the people.
People on the shore scrambled to buy her, and when they couldn't get along, they used the method of investing money. Whoever threw money at her would welcome her back.
As a result, copper coins, gold, and silver were all thrown down one after another, and the bow of the boat was piled up, but none of them fell on her. Everyone was so disappointed that they had to give up.
The woman smiled, clasped her hands to thank the people on the shore, and gave every donation to the poor.
The news spread and shocked the entire city of Hangzhou. The rich were moved by her chivalrous act and gave generously. As a result, the sick get medicine, the poor get money, the hungry get food, and the hearts of the people are all comforted and satisfied.
Suddenly, the woman's boat was full of colors and bright lights, and a bodhisattva with a solemn law minister smiled. Everyone was amazed.
She said, “I am the Buddha of Guanzeyin. I am here to inspire and awaken everyone's kindness. Compassion and compassion are the noblest sentiments. Helping others is the most sacred responsibility. Helping the weak is a duty that people cannot quit. Today, your performance is very commendable, and everyone will be happy.”
The crowd was both moved and delighted, and they all joined hands to celebrate Kanzeon Bodhisattva.
Guanzeon Bodhisattva gave them the images. Her promise has come true; she really lives in the home of every person who pays for good.
This story has spread widely in China, increasing people's belief in Guanshiyin.
