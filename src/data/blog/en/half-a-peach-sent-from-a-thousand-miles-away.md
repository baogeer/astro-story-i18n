 As the saying goes, “Send goose feathers a thousand miles, be polite and respectful”. The story of “Sending Half a Peach in a Thousand Miles” is even more emotional.
In the past, a family with the surname Ding gave birth to a boy and a girl. My older brother is older than my younger sister to be in her teens. When my brother started a family, my younger sister wasn't sensible. Her parents later died, and the little sister lived with her brother and sister. My sister-in-law is a wise person. They treat my little sister better than her own brother, teach her to read, teach her to embroider, and sometimes have fun with her. The two sisters-in-law have never quarreled. Neighbors boasted and said in jingle: “Good brother, look at sister-in-law, sister-in-law, good sister-in-law, only really good!”
Once, while playing outside, the girl found a peach kernel and brought it back, and her sister-in-law planted it in the patio. One day three, three days nine, my sister-in-law brought water and my sister poured it, and the peach tree slowly grew. But I didn't wait for Momoki Yutomo, and my younger sister got married a thousand miles away.
For many years, this peach tree has bloomed every spring, but it doesn't grow peaches. This year, peach trees finally bear peaches. Strangely enough, a big peach tree only produces one big peach. They look forward to it every day. One day, when the peaches were ripe, my brother picked them and had to eat them. My sister-in-law stopped them and said, “No! I planted peach trees with your sister. Now that Baba has a peach, I should have shared it with my sister.”
My brother said, “Girl? The girl murmured thousands of miles away!”
My sister-in-law said, “It doesn't matter. I'm leaving tomorrow to send half a peach for my sister.”
My sister-in-law spent thousands of miles, day and night, delivering half a peach to the girl. The girl held a peach and looked at her sister-in-law's burnt face and lame legs, and said, “This is really sending half a peach a thousand miles. It's a blessing!”
Later, some people said, “Send half a peach for a thousand miles, treat kindness with kindness”; “Send goose feathers for a thousand miles, be light and respectful.” The statement is different; in fact, it means the same thing.
