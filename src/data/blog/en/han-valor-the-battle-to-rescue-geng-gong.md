The situation is urgent: in March of this year, the Huns only sent Zuolu Li Wang (Liyinli) to compete with the Han Empire for the western region. The Huns army broke ground, defeated the country after the chariots attached to the Han Empire, and recruited small countries such as Yanqi in the northern part of the western region. The western region where the Han Empire had just taken office protected Chen Mu and died in battle. The Guan Chung Department, which was stationed in Liuzhongcheng, and Geng Gong, which was stationed in Jinpu City, were surrounded by only a few hundred people!! Once these two forces are destroyed, the Huns drive straight into southern Shannan, and the entire western region will fall into the hands of the Huns.
However, it was already October when I received this letter of distress!! It's been over half a year, and no one knows what the current situation is in the western region. More than a thousand armies versus 20,000. Do these imperial armies still exist? They dared to send military reinforcements. Without the support of the castle, they could easily be destroyed by the aggressive Hungarian cavalry, not to mention that it was already winter. The weather was poor, the road was long, and the logistics were difficult.
The ministers shook their heads. It seemed that the loss of the western region was inevitable; they had to wait to find opportunities later, and those warriors were already destined to die for the country!
However, the minister, represented by Situ Bao Yu, still strongly advocated rescue. His words deeply moved the young emperor's heart: the Han Empire never cooled down the heroes' enthusiasm, and even if this rescue was doomed to failure, they must declare to the world that the Han Empire would never give up the warriors who fought for him!
The Han army left Yumen Pass in the midst of wind and snow to find less than one percent hope!
Let's take another look at the situation in the western region: after Geng Gong's department was besieged in Jinpu City, Geng Gong took advantage of the heavy rain and led his army to launch an attack on the enemy. In the heavy rain, the enemy couldn't see how many troops our side actually had, so they retreated, but they still formed a long-distance encirclement trend. Geng Gong's department's achievements made it out of heavy encirclement. By May, Geng Gong had transferred troops to Shule City due to water problems. There are streams flowing through Shule City, which can guarantee drinking water in summer; at the same time, Shule City is the main road between the mountains and the north, which can prevent the Huns from conquering small countries in the western region of southern Shannan.
The Huns soon discovered Geng Gongbu's intentions and once again surrounded Geng Gongbu in Shule City. Countries in Shannan sent reinforcements to destroy themselves without fighting.
The brutal siege has begun!

While Geng Gong arranged for the Han army to guard the city, he also ordered the excavation of wells. But I've been digging 15 feet (about 40 meters now) and I haven't seen any water! This is a very serious matter. Without drinking water, there are only two roads to die or fall. The thirsty general used a cloth to squeeze the juice out of horse manure to drink! Seeing that the Han army was under siege for a long time, she was anxious. After a few more months, the Han army in the city continued to die of battle, illness, and starvation. As a result, there were only a few dozen people left, and no one wanted to surrender to the Huns.
After being trapped for a long time, what kind of hardship did it reach? The soldiers were so hungry that they had to put the leather armor on their bodies in a pot, cook it soft, then cut it into pieces to fill their stomachs. Afterwards, they finished eating even the leather armor. As a last resort, they removed the crossbow and cooked the strung leather strip on it and ate it in the same way as the beast tendon used as a bow string. Protective gear and weapons are undoubtedly a fighter's second life, and they can't worry about it in order to fill their stomachs a little.
At this point, the Huns only visited the castle in person and knew that the Han army in the city was extremely tired, yet they still didn't surrender. Although the Huns were ferocious, they had a straight heart and respected the hero, so he showed respect for them, so they summoned Geng Gong and promised to make him king.
At this point, a shocking scene happened — Geng Gong promised to surrender, tricked the Hungarian messenger into the city, killed him with his own hands, and then cut the flesh of the corpse and grilled it in the city against the army of the Huns! Geng Gong used such actions to completely cut off the path of surrender. It was not so much that they were so hungry that they tricked a “food” in; it was more about lying down one's heart and sworn to death.

In the first month of the following year, the Han Empire's rescue army finally reached Liuzhongcheng. The entire army was destroyed. Under such severe cold weather, the rescue army thought that the more difficult Geng Gong department was even less likely to exist, so the commanding generals Qin Peng, Wang Meng, and Huang Fu Yuan all decided to return. After all, they couldn't risk putting the entire army at risk. However, Fan Qiang cried and demanded that he visit Shule City. Leading the army, Fan Qiang, braved the snow and traveled north along the mountain. After much hardship, they finally reached the bottom of the city. There was not much left of the Han army in the city, and none of them were alive with any fighting ability. In the middle of the night, they noticed that an army was coming, and they thought it was the Huns who took advantage of the snow to fight. I was shocked and desperate.
