 Han Xiangzi was originally a legendary character, but after he took his place as one of the Eight Immortals, writers and scholars also joined the fun to examine his life, but what is really powerful are those popular folk stories.
Legend has it that Han Xiang Zi, Zi Qingfu, is Han Yu's nephew. Some say that he was born with sacrum and has an extraordinary personality. He is tired of being gorgeous. He likes quiet, beautiful and beautiful women. He is not tempted at all, has good wine and food, and can't indulge in his ambition. What he is passionate about is the Daoist method of practice.
Some say that he was born indulgent and not good at studying; he had no choice but to drink alcohol. From generation to generation, his studies became immortal. He fell in love with mountains and rivers at the age of 20 and never went back. There was no audio for over 20 years. During Tang Yuan's years, he suddenly returned to Chang'an. His clothes were worn out and his behavior was strange. Han Yu asked him to study with the students, but Han Xiangzi didn't say a word during discussions with his students. He only gambled with his subordinates. When he was drunk, he slept in the stable for three or five days, or slept on the street. Han Yu was very worried and asked him, “Everyone has strengths, even hawkers. What can you do in the future?” Han Xiangzi said, “I also have a skill. You just don't know.” Han Yu asked, “So what can you do?” He said, “Let the peonies bloom in several colors,” and it was just in the early winter season, so the potted soil was scattered and bloomed in an instant. Han Xiangzi's later legends learned from Lu Dongbin and ranked in the Senban class.
In addition, there is another way of saying that Han Yu's strong persuasion cannot shake his ambitions. Han Xiang Zi became a fairy and wanted Han Yu to be born, but Han Yu always didn't believe. One year, on Han Yu's birthday, Xiang Zi returned from a long trip. At the dinner party, Han Yu deliberately made it difficult for Xiang Zi and wanted him to become a flower in an instant. The flowers that have come out of Xiang Zi are more beautiful than peonies. There are also two lines on the flower: Where is Yunheng's home in Qinling, Xue Yong, Lan Guan Ma is not moving forward. Han Yu didn't understand the meaning of the poem. Xiang Zi said, “He tested himself on a daily basis. He cannot be predicted.” After the dinner party, Xiang Zizhong went on a long trip again. Legend has it that Han Xiang Zi eventually became an immortal.
