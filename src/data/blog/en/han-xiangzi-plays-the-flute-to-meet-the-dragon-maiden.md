 Han Xiangzi is a good-natured scholar in the Eight Immortals. The masterpiece in his hand is called Zijin Xiao, and it was made from a bamboo plant in the Zizhu Forest in the South China Sea. It is said that Han Xiang Zi, the god Xiao, was given to him by the Seven Princesses of the Dragon King in the East China Sea!
One year, Han Xiangzi roamed the famous mountains and rivers to the coast of the East China Sea. I heard that there are dragon girls in the East China Sea. They are good at music, and are good at singing and dancing. I would love to meet her for a while. As a result, he goes to the seaside every day to blow the sound. This day, March 3, is the day of the East China Sea Dragon Girl going out to sea for a spring trip. At night, the dragon girl was stunned to hear a melodious and pleasant sound coming from the seaside.
Han Xiang Zi's cry disturbed the dragon girl's heart. The sound of this wonderful song seemed to have drawn her soul, so she couldn't help but walk to the seaside, turning into a silver eel that would blow in the air.
Han Xiangzi stopped playing the song, and the Great Lake retreated ten miles away.
At this point, he discovered that there was a stranded silver eel on the beach that had missed the tide. He was looking up at him with tears in his eyes.
Looking at her look, she seemed to be still enchanted by the sound of the music. Han Xiangzi was cheerful and funny and said, “Eel, eel, eel, can you also understand the mystery? If you're an acquaintance, please spread my feelings to the Crystal Dragon Palace!”
The eel listened and even nodded.
Han Xiangzi was astonished. Out of curiosity, he blew up the Yuping Xiao again. Unexpectedly, silver eels are so familiar with humanity that their mother-in-law actually danced a magical dance under the bright moonlight. Her beautiful dance posture and strange demeanor are rare in the world. Even Han Xiangzi, who wandered through rivers and lakes and visited famous mountains, was stunned.
Under the moonlight, the silver eel kept flashing, dancing, spinning... The speed was getting faster and tighter. Suddenly, the silver light flashed, and the eel disappeared. I only saw a dragon girl standing like a fairy in the moonlight, with willow eyebrows, apricot face, jade bamboo hands, a thin willow waist, a gold yarn dress, and a lotus flower skirt. Relieving the waist is like a Chang'e dance. After the starting race, Yan swept the water and confused Han Xiangzi.
Dragon Girl dances and sings: Lonely Dragon Palace hears the sound of Hsiao.
Let you sing the phoenix with a song, and the concubine should dance with you until dawn.
Amidst the sound of singing and dancing, the moon is slowly falling west, the tide is slowly rising, and the sky is almost bright. Suddenly, a wave surged in, and the eel and dragon girl disappeared. This kind of scene happened for three nights in a row.
On this day, Han Xiangzi went to the seaside to play a show again. For some reason, after blowing for most of the day, Dragon Girl just didn't go out to sea. Did Yu Ping Xiao fail? He was so angry that he broke his beloved Jade Chapter, and Dragon Girl still hasn't come from there.
Han Xiangzi was walking back and forth in frustration when she heard someone behind him shouting at him. Looking back, she was an unfamiliar old fisherman. The old fisherman said, “My husband, the princess thanks you for your beauty. I'm so sorry to tell the story. Unsurprisingly, it was the Seven Princesses of the East China Sea Dragon King who sang and danced under the moon the other night. Due to the unraveling of the incident, they were imprisoned by Dragon King in the Deep Palace, so they were unable to meet. Today, she asked me to dedicate a branch of bamboo from Putuo in the South China Sea to be used by Xiang Gong Xian Xiao. Wang Xiang Xiang became a fairy and wrote a divine song to save the dragon girl from a sea of suffering!”
After all, the old fisherman handed over the bamboo branch, and a fresh breeze disappeared.
Han Xiang Zi turned Shenzhu into a purple golden bow. Since then, he has cut off the idea of messing around in the earthly world, and has gone deep into ancient caves and played composing songs day and night. Sure enough, he has developed an extraordinary ability.
Later, the Eight Immortals crossed the sea, and Han Xiang's son, Xiang Xiang's god, collected the snake demon, and Aoyu in Miaoqu Town showed the genius of the Immortal Family; but what about the East China Sea Dragon Girl? However, in order to smuggle a branch of bamboo, she was punished as a handmaiden by Master Kannon, and she was never allowed to escape.
Fu said that fishermen in the East China Sea still often hear a deep cry at sea. That is, Han Xiangzi misses the dragon girl. Her heart is irritated, and the depression is blowing in the sky!
