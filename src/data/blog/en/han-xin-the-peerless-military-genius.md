 Han Xin (? —B.196), Early Han military man. A native of Huaiyin (now Jiangsu). My parents both died when I was young, and my family was poor, but I studied hard, practiced military law, and determined my country's ambition. Suffering from a lack of means of livelihood, as a last resort, they eat dinner at an acquaintance's house, and sometimes go fishing at Huai Shui in exchange for money. They are repeatedly discriminated against and treated coldly by people around them. Once, a group of scoundrels publicly humiliated Han Xin. A butcher said to Han Xin, “Although you are tall and big, you like to carry a knife and sword, you were very timid at the time. If you have the skills, would you dare to stab me with your sword? If you don't dare, just go under my crotch. Han Xin knows that he only has a video list; the hard fight will definitely lose. As a result, in front of many onlookers, he drilled through the butcher's crotch. In history books, it is called “the humiliation of stepping down.”
In 209 BC, Chen Sheng and Wu Guang rose to prominence. Han Xin joined the army with a sword and joined Xiang Liang's Western Chu army. After Xiang Liang died in battle, he followed Xiang Yu, but was not reused by Xiang Yu; he only acted as a sword guard. He proposed suggestions to Xiang Yu many times, but none of them were adopted. As a result, he angrily escaped from Chu Camp and defected to Han King Liu Bang. Liu Bang did not initially use him as a general; he only appointed him as Captain Zhi Su. Han Xin saw that Liu Bang would refuse to use it again and decided to leave Hancamp. Prime Minister Hsiao Ho was aware of Han Xin's talent. When he heard the news, he immediately chased him on a moonlit night and persuaded him back. This left a beautiful story about “How many months did Hsiao chase Han Xin”. Later, under repeated persuasion from Xiao He, Liu Bang personally discussed military affairs with Han Xin. Convinced that Han Xin was a rare talent, he held a ceremony to worship the general.
In May of the first year of Han Gaozu (206 BC), Han Xin sent people to restore the boardwalk that was burnt down when Liu Bang entered Hanzhong, and confuse Wang Yong, but he led his army quietly along the old road of Southern Zheng and left Chencang in the east, defeating Zhang and Hanjun, taking over the Guanzhong region in one fell swoop, enabling Liu Bang to return to Sanqin.
In February of the 2nd year of Han Gaozu (205 BC), Han Xin led troops out of Hanggu Pass and drove Bing Feng to Luoyang. The feudal states to which Xiang Yu belonged, such as Han Wang Zheng Chang and Yin Wang Sima Qiong, surrendered successively. They then collaborated with Qi and Zhao to attack Chu. In April, the army had already entered Pengcheng, Chudu. The intensity of the attack was amazing. Unexpectedly, after Liu Bang entered Pengcheng, he would leave defense aside and search for beautiful treasures all over the place. Xiang Yu, who was fighting with the Qi army, heard that Pengcheng had fallen and rushed back with 30,000 elite horses to defeat Liu Bang in the first battle. Upon hearing the news, Han Xin immediately rushed to gather the defeated army and join forces with Liu Bang in the Luoyang region. They also successfully fought an interdiction war, defeating the Chu army by a big defeat between Beijing County and Suoting, causing Xiang Yu's military advance to a standstill. The front finally stabilized in Xingyang.
Liu Bangbing lost to Pengcheng, and Qi, Zhao, Wei, etc. once again rebelled against Ge Xiang Chu. In August, Liu Bangfeng and Han Xin became Prime Minister Zuo and led the attack on Wei. Wei Wangbao and Chen Zhongbing are in Puzan on the east bank of the Yellow River. Han Xin deployed a large number of ships in Linjin, opposite of Kamasaka, and pretended to cross the river head-on. They secretly used wooden frames to tie up tiles to make temporary river crossing, and attacked Anyi from upstream. They suddenly appeared behind Wei Jun, wrecked Wei Jun, and captured Wei Wangbao.
In September of the 3rd year of the Han Gaozu (204 BC), Han Xin sent another army to the east to seize the Xia dynasty. At this point, Liu Bang ordered Han Xin to quickly deploy his forces to Xingyang to strengthen the area's defense, so Han Xin only brought more than 10,000 troops east to Jingjing to attack Zhao. Zhao Wangxie and Zhao Jun's commander Cheng Anjun and Chen Yu concentrated 200,000 troops at Jingxingkou in the Taihang Mountains to occupy favorable terrain and prepare for a decisive battle with Han Xin. Han Xin first took a night detour and ambushed back to the back of Zhao Jun's battalion with 2,000 light riders. After Tianming, he personally led the main forces out to the riverside and lined up behind the river, inducing Zhao Jun to go out of camp to attack. The Han army fought back to the river; there was no way back; everyone fought to the death. Two thousand light riders bowed down in advance and invaded Zhao Jun's air camp and planted the Han army's red flags all over. When Zhao Jun saw the situation, the military was in turmoil. Han Xinhuai's army took advantage of the opportunity to fight back, breaking 200,000 of Zhao Jun, killing Commander Chen Yu, and capturing Zhao Wangshe alive. Next, Han Xin surrendered Yan using the method of “attacking the army.”
In November of the 4th year of Han Gaozu (203 BC), Han Xin also used a heavy military raid to defeat Linyi in Qidu. Chu Jianglong also quickly led 200,000 Malaysian aid, joined forces with the defeated Qi army in Gaomi, and then confronted the Han army across the Huai Shui River. Han Xin secretly sent people to use more than 10,000 sandbags to use the dark night to block Huai Shui upstream. After Tianming, part of the army was sent across Huai Shui and attacked the Chu army from behind, then feigned defeat. Dragon also mistook the Han army for being timid and led the main force to go to Huai Shui in pursuit. Han Xin ordered his subordinates to excavate the upstream dam and split the Chu army into two sections. The Han army used the method of attacking in half a ferry to completely destroy the Chu army, which had already crossed the water, and the dragon was killed. The allied forces of Qi and Chu, which did not cross the water, destroyed themselves without fighting. Han Xin took advantage of the opportunity to pursue and destroy the fugitive enemy, captured Qi Wang and Tianguang, and pacified everything.
After Han Xin captured all the land, Xiang Yu was terrified and hurriedly sent people to lobby Han Xin, hoping that Han Xin would go against the Han League on the condition of 3 points in the world, and was rejected by Han Xin. Han Xin's mentor Kuai Tong advised him, “Haven't the generals heard the reason that those who brave the earthquake are in danger, and those who have built the world aren't rewarded? ... The general now has both the fame of the earthquake lord, and his great exploits that are hard to appreciate. Returning to China, Chu is unbelievable; returning to Han, the Han King is terrified. If you don't stand on your own feet as king, where will you end up?” Han Xin listened and waved his hand: “Please don't talk about it again. Han Wang treated me very kindly. He gave me his car, put his clothes on, and fed me his food. The ancients said: If you ride someone's car, you should share your worries; if you wear someone else's clothes, you should also share your worries; if you eat someone else's food, you should work for others. How can I see profit and ignorance?” As a result, Kuai Tong's suggestion was declined. However, it was initially decided that a king should be established to take charge of the government to reassure the people. Therefore, Han Xin has sent an envoy to revise the book and ask Liu Bangli to make him a fake King Qi (acting for King Qi). At the time, Liu Bang was stuck in Xingyang by Xiang Yu. He was too busy taking care of himself. After reading the book, he was furious and didn't want to accept it. Later, after listening to the opinions of Zhang Liang and Chen Ping, he also said, “It's okay to calm down that the princes are real kings. Why be fake kings!” As a result, Han Xin was appointed as King Qi, and his forces were recruited to attack Chu.
In December of the 5th year of Han Gaozu (202 BC), the Chu and Han armies fought a decisive battle in Gaixia (present-day Lingbi South, Anhui). Liu Bang, with Han Xin as the lead general, uniformly commanded all forces. Xiang Yu commanded the 100,000 Chu army and attacked the Han army's positions from the front. Han Xin used a typical flank attack tactic, causing the Han army to retreat a little later, avoid the strength of the Chu army, and then unfold both wings, carry out side attacks, and then force the Chinese army to advance, and suddenly complete the siege. At night, Han Shun ordered the Han army to sing the Chu Song on all sides. Eventually, the Chu army lost its fighting spirit and was annihilated by the Han army in one fell swoop. Seeing that the general trend had passed, Xiang Yu generously prayed for herself along the Wujiang River. The five-year Chu-Han War ended with Han King Liu Bang taking over the world.
Han Xin used his humble status as a defender in charge of the sword under Xiang Yu. He took the stage for a few years, built a strong prodigy, and eventually became a member of the left and right in the war between Chu-Han. Kuai Tong praised this powerful military figure by saying “slightly out of the world.” His way of using the military was admired by later generations of soldiers. According to “Han Shu: The Chronicles of Art and Literature,” he wrote three chapters of “Han Xin's Art of War,” but unfortunately these have been lost. Han Xin's military talents made Liu Bang extremely uneasy, so after Xiang Yu was defeated, he took military power and transferred to King Chu, then deposed as the Prince of Huaiyin, and placed under house arrest with Liu Bang. One day, Liu Bang asked Han Xin, “How many soldiers do you think I can bring?” Han Xin replied, “Your Majesty can only carry an army of 100,000.” Liu Bang also asked, “Then what about you?” Han Xin paused and suddenly laughed arrogantly: “The more I am, the better!” Han Xin's extraordinary military prowess and talent finally brought death. In the 11th year of Han Gaozu (196 BC), Lu Hou and Xiao He lured Han Xin to the bell room of Changle Palace to kill him under the pretext of conspiring against him. A generation of famous generals died out of place; it was really sad.
