 People often liken a good woman marrying a crappy husband to “a flower on cow dung.” The life of the female poet He Shuangqing in the early Qing dynasty could not have been more appropriately summed up in this sentence. Although He Shuangqing was tortured by his “cow dung,” her poetry became more and more outstanding in the midst of hardship, revealing a different kind of splendor.
In the fall of '54, Kangxi added another daughter to a farmhouse named He under Siping Mountain in Danyang, Jiangsu. This is the second daughter of the He family, so she was named Shuang Qing. Xiao Shuangqing is very well-behaved. She rarely cries, and often looks at the outside world from all sides with her clear black and white eyes. She seemed to know that her family was poor. Her parents were busy making a living all day, and had no intention of taking care of herself, so she grew up obedient. Unbeknownst to me, Xiao Shuangqing can walk and talk. Don't look at her, she doesn't like to talk a lot; she is smart and intelligent when it comes to speaking. Shuang Qing's uncle works as a handyman at the school in town. The town is not far from He Shuangqing's home. Little Shuangqing, who has no one to take care of, often runs to the academy alone to quietly stand outside the window and take a peek. The students inside were studying, and she also imitated “yay ah ah”. It was also very fun to learn. Later, when the teacher at the school saw that this little girl was smart and studious, he agreed to attend the classroom as an exception. Xiao Shuangqing was so happy that her heart was even sweeter than eating honey. After class, she was busy delivering water and tea to Mr., throwing fans to catch flies in the summer, and adding charcoal to set off fires in the winter to thank Mr. for his special care. Just like that, Xiao Shuangqing attended the academy for free for three years, learned how to read and write, and also entered the art of writing poetry.
Three years have passed, and He Shuangqing is ten years old. Mom thought the girl was older and ran around all day. She didn't look like a crazy girl. She was afraid she wouldn't be able to get married in the future, so she stopped running to the town school every day, but left her at home to learn how to be a female celebrity. Xiao Shuangqing didn't know how to fight back against her mother, because all the other little sisters in the village didn't leave the house easily. Most women are illiterate, and they can't even write their own names. As a mother when they marry and have children as usual, the He family has been able to let Shuang Qing run outside for a few years, which is quite open enough.
I did housework with my mom at home, and I still couldn't let go of the pen, ink, and poetry in the school. Her Uncle Yang bought paper, pen and ink, and as soon as he had free time, he sat down at the dinner table to write poems and essays. I also asked my uncle to take her studies to the school and ask the gentleman to correct them. He often criticized her studies with words of encouragement, and was also secretly amazed at her rapid progress.
The crops in the field have been planted and harvested again. In a blink of an eye, He Shuangliao is 18 years old and has grown into a big girl standing tall. The inner show with good poetry and ability makes her more subtle and profound than other country girls. Although He Shuangqing has excellent poetic talent and charm, in the eyes of the country people, this money is not worth it. The He family's parents didn't think their daughter was any different from others. When the daughter reached the age of marriage, they made their own proposition that Shuang Qing married Zhou Dawang from the neighboring village. Zhou Dawang is also from a farmer. They only know that he is burying his head in growing crops. What the parents in the He family like is that he is strong and a good crop player, and they think that their daughter actually lives with him.
Zhou Dawang is as strong as a cow and has a hot temper. Dou Da's characters are ignorant. The contrast with He Shuangqing's weak physique, gentle temperament, and smart talent is extremely discordant. He Shuangqing, a newlywed, looked at her unfamiliar husband and felt that she had been thrown into the wilderness. Her future fate was uncertain whether good or bad.
Newlywed Yan Er, Zhou Dawang is very fascinated by his gentle and slender wife. It wasn't enough to hug her at night and rub her. Although he didn't speak softly, his broad chest and strong arms made He Shuangqing experience a kind of solid care. Although he didn't have the kind of talented and beautiful romance in the book, unfortunately, she was also slightly satisfied. Anyway, farm girls get married this way.
However, after entering the Zhou family for three days, He Shuangqing began to taste some bitterness. Grandma Yang is a spirited and capable woman. Young and widowed, one person supported his only son Zhou Dawang. Now that the daughter-in-law enters the door, the son is actually fascinated. By the time she, who became a wife, there were fewer of them. It really was “I married my daughter-in-law and forgot my wife”! She counted her hate on He Shuangqing's head. She decided that this vixen had stolen her son's heart, and began picking bones in eggs to make trouble. The new daughter-in-law began cooking on the third day. He Shuangqing made a bowl of sugar soup balls for her mother-in-law and carefully delivered them to her. Yang took it in disguise, scooped one into her mouth with a spoon. As soon as she took a bite, she suddenly furrowed her brow, “slapping” the gnocchi that had been eaten in her mouth, and at the same time knocked the bowl in her hand against the table. She immediately stood up and angrily said, “You little prostitute want to burn me to death? Where do you need to put so much sugar to make gnocchi; they just want to defeat our Zhou family, right? What a broom star!”
The spit out of soup was spitting on the hem of He Shuangqing's dress. She was shocked, then another smelly scolding hit her head over her face, overwhelmed her, and tears the size of a bean fell uncontrollably.
“Gee! Is it OK for the old lady to say a few words? Let me tell you, the old lady is still the head of the family here. You can't do whatever you want; if you don't clean up the things on the ground quickly, you still have to feed the pigs in the circle and the chickens in the fence! We are farmers, but we can't support idle people!” Yang didn't seem to have vented enough; it was another reprimand to He Shuangqing. Then he turned his head again and shouted at the son in the room, “Wang Er, if you keep your daughter-in-law every day, you can protect the millet? Let's not get down to work in the field, see how lazy you are!” The newlyweds had just taken a break for three days, and Yang had a great deal of anger in her heart. She felt that her sons were all about to be taken by their daughter-in-law.
Zhou Dawang, who had real relationships, was used to listening to his mother's modus operandi since childhood. Naturally, he was afraid to resist. He had to obediently carry his hoe down the field, and he still blamed his anger on his wife. I felt that my mother was angry and that she was provoked by her, so I couldn't help but cut my love and pity for my wife in half.
Since that day, He Shuangqing has been living under the oppression of his mother-in-law every day. Miscellaneous tasks such as cleaning the house, cooking, feeding chickens, raising pigs, and chugging all fell to her head. Originally, she was in poor health, so she seldom did these things in her mother's family. At first, I couldn't get used to it. Either she forgot or missed it, and she was so busy. Not only did the mother-in-law sitting on the sidelines not help, but she also thought she was too slow. It wasn't here, it wasn't right there.
In the beginning, He Shuangqing was wronged by her mother-in-law, and also quietly told her husband that he wanted some comfort from her husband. However, the husband slowly listened to his mother-in-law's arrangement, and also determined that He Shuangqing was clumsy and lazy. He often stood by his mother to help reprimand his wife. He Shuangqing was extremely sad.
