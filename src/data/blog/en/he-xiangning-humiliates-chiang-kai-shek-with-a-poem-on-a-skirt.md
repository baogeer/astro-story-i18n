 Author: Liu Jixing
After the “9/18” incident, Chiang Kai-shek adopted a reactionary policy of “settling down before the outside world”, did not resist the Japanese invaders, and carried out a crackdown on the Communist Party, which aroused great indignation among the people of the whole country. In July 1935, He Yingqin, acting chairman of the Kuomintang North China Military Division, signed the “Homey Agreement” with Miyazu Mijiro, the commander of the Japanese army invading China, to lose power and humiliate the country, and surrendered most of the sovereignty of the Hebei and Chahar provinces, leaving North China alive. When the news came, Ho Hsiang-ning was outraged. He immediately sent someone to send a pair of her old dress to Chiang Kai-shek in a package. She wrote a poem on the dress and paid for it:
Calling himself a boy, he is willing to be tempted by Japanese slaves.
If you don't fight, send mountains and rivers, the world will be ashamed.
Women of my age would like to die in the sand field.
I'm going to buy my women's dress for yours!
This poem angrily criticized Chiang Kai-shek and his army's traitor against Japan, and expressed her determination to be a female hero and go to the front line of resistance against Japan.
The couplets of the Continuing Fan Pavilion attached to the package are:
The lone frog at the bottom of the well is small and arrogant,
The strange stone in the toilet didn't fit, and it was smelly and smelly.
Ho Hsiang-ning used this couplet from Fan Ting to refer to Chiang Kai-shek (in name: Zhong Zheng) as a strange toilet stone that is incorrect, smelly, and hard. It can be described as being invigorating!
Chiang Kai-shek received the package. When I opened it, it was an old dress, which was somewhat baffling. However, after reading the couplets between Ho Hsiang-ning's poem and Xu Fanting, I was very annoyed. However, since He Xiangning was a pioneer of China's democratic revolution, an outstanding politician and social activist, the famous Mrs. Liao (wife of Kuomintang left leader Liao Zhongkai), and followed Sun Yat-sen in her early years, she was the first female member of the League. Therefore, when Chiang Kai-shek was abused, he did not dare to blatantly retaliate; he had no choice but to keep quiet and stop.
He Xiangning is not only a revolutionary and leader of the women's movement, but also an accomplished painter and poet. Her poems are generous and passionate, full of national spirit and revolutionary pride. She had a very close relationship with Liu Zhongkai after her marriage. When her husband wanted to go to the front line, she didn't have the humility of a little girl. She generously wrote a poem encouraging: “It's hard to die if you haven't reported a national feud. You can bear to cry and say goodbye. I urge you not to worry about being expensive, and remain famous in Chinese history.”
Chen Jiongming mutinied. When Liu Zhongkai was shackled and imprisoned by the rebels, she desperately broke into the prison to visit the prison and deliver food and change clothes. In 1925, when Liao Zhongkai was assassinated by the right-wing at the entrance of the Guangzhou Central Party Department, Ho Hsiang-ning was by her side, and her husband's blood covered her shirt. She forcibly endured her grief, buried her thoughts in her heart, and inherited Liao Zhongkai's unfinished career without the slightest fluctuation or compromise.
In 1927, Chiang Kai-shek and Wang Jingwei betrayed Sun Yat-sen's three major policies of joining the Russian Communist Party and supporting agricultural workers, removed the Communist Party from the party, government, and military systems, expelled Soviet advisers, and completely relied on the European and American powers and the Chinese bureaucratic bourgeoisie. He Xiangning resolutely resigned from all positions in the Kuomintang. She said in a strict manner that I want to resign from all positions within the Kuomintang, and I want to continue my dealings with the Communist Party and the Soviet Union. The Kuomintang law cannot punish me. I am abiding by Sun Yat-sen's last words, “Wu Ning lives a hard life with a brush, and is unwilling to join forces and become a criminal of the nation.” Ho Hsiang-ning made a public statement to resign as a member of the Kuomintang Central Executive Committee, and broke up with the Nanjing government without taking a penny from Chiang Kai-shek.
From the beginning of the “9/18” incident in 1931 to the end of the War of Resistance against Japan in 1945, He Xiangning has carried out anti-Japanese rescue campaigns in Shanghai, Hong Kong, Guangdong, Guangxi, etc., and has written more than 30 popular anti-Japanese poems. Yu Haoran's enthusiasm, worried about the country and the people, is moving and inspiring.
The “9/18” incident broke out in 1931, and Japan invaded the three northeastern provinces of our country. Upon hearing the news, He Xiangning immediately ended her comfortable and comfortable life in Paris, France, and returned to Shanghai to actively participate in the anti-Japanese rescue campaign. He also wrote the poem “Sensibility”:
I'm afraid to hear a blow break the national chant; it's even more sad to wander along the road.
How important is the right to sacrifice, and where to find lost rivers and mountains?
Xiao Xiao, Ye Ye, and the Geese flew south, leaving thousands of miles to return to their homeland.
Eight years before and after the Central Plains taught people to remember tears.
In the “1.28” war of resistance in Songhu in 1932, He Xiangning actively supported the anti-Japanese activities of the 19th Route Army, founded a hospital for the wounded with Song Qingling and others, and raised funds to organize a volunteer army ambulance team to go to the front line of resistance against Japan in Northeast China. However, the Kuomintang government signed the “Songhu Armistice Agreement” with Japan on May 5, 1932. In June, Chiang Kai-shek re-gathered 630,000 troops to launch a fourth military “encirclement” against the revolutionary base in order to implement its reactionary policy of “settling the country first.” Ho Heung-ning was “extremely sad and indignant” against Chiang Kai-shek for his act of passively resisting Japan and starting a civil war. In “Sending Huangpu Students After “January 28,” he wrote: Quiet and silent, the river and mountains are not what they used to be. I can't stand to look back on 13 years, with a thousand tears in my eyes. Q What are your requirements for admission? Kill enemies for the survival of the nation. Over the years, I have failed you, sacrificed in the civil war, hypocritical exploits, and no friends or enemies. Remembering history has become an exhibition. Extremely sad and outraged! I'm staring at the mountains, rivers, and the sun; where are you looking? Terrible relatives! Follow your ancestors. Don't live up to your ambitions and attack Japanese slaves quickly!
In “Giving to Former Enemy Generals,” He Xiangning wrote in eclectic, plain and fluent language: “Japanese slaves invaded, and their ambitions are not dead. According to our three northeastern provinces, we have regained the land of our Shenjiang River. Lament my great rivers and mountains. Today is not what it used to be. How many of my cities have been burnt down, how many of my compatriots have been murdered, and how many women have raped me? Shame! You guys are bloody soldiers, how can you get this mad?” In “A Gift to the Dear Wounded Soldiers,” it is written: “You shed blood, I shed tears, and the splendid river was taken. Boost your courage and quickly go to the sand field to restore our land. Good boy, I'm not afraid to die to save the country. die! Remain famous for all generations.” Call on all officers and soldiers to fight hard to resist the Japanese invaders.
On April 10, 1933, He Xiangning published a five-word poem entitled “Be brave, be a good boy” in the “Greater China Weekly”:
Brave, good boy! I'm not afraid to die in the sand field.
To endure pain and swallow sound is a disgrace to the nation of Tuxue.
The nation is not independent, and the bloodshed continues.
Seeing the country die, I was traumatized and shed tears.
Get hurt and charge to take back the ground I lost.
Instead of humiliating life, my mother would rather die for the country!
Mr. Ho Hsiang-ning often makes paintings to raise funds for the war of resistance, or presents poems with inscriptions to inspire anti-Japanese soldiers. In October 1935, she made a Chinese painting of Plum Blossoms and wrote a poem with the title:
A plum tree is accompanied by daffodils, and the north wind is still strong.
Frost and snow are still putting pressure on the heart, and overcoming the cold winter is even stronger.
While personally organizing women from all walks of life in Shanghai to make cold clothes for anti-Japanese officers and soldiers, He Xiangning wrote, “Women are in the middle of their hands and are looking for personal jackets. “The needle involved enemies, and Tsutomu killed the enemy Yi” (“Rei General”); “The children of the family are collecting clothes, and there is a fixed period for recovering the rivers and mountains. Together they must be afraid of the enemy, and work together to inspire the war with one heart and one spirit”. (“Comfort the Athletes Who Struggle to Kill the Enemy”); “The former sacrificed the latter's mentor, and the children of the family sent warm clothes; Gan Jun bravely went to the beach and remained famous for a long time.” (“A Gift to the Frontline Soldiers”). Encourage anti-Japanese generals to follow suit and swear to the death against the Japanese invaders; call on men, women, and children to contribute to the war of resistance.
On November 12, 1937, Shanghai fell. Mr Ho Heung-ning moved to Hong Kong. On August 26, 1938, she was invited to attend the first anniversary conference of the Hong Kong Women's Comfort Association. When she saw that some of the men and women attending the conference were still “all embroidered and greased and powdery,” she felt “like embroidered on the back.” Three poems from “The Boy Is Beautiful” were written on the spot:
The country is like flowing water, horses are like dragons, and the boy is in the seat.
When the Liyuan Dance Festival dusk comes to an end, it is Ai Hong.
Erlang Fu Fen women are greased, and more Ling Ling is sewing and embroidered clothes,
The money was exported for hostile use, and the compatriots were being bombarded with meat.
Hong Kong women compete for prosperity, and their cloisonny-colored makeup looks just like flowers.
With the cost of singing and dancing all night, the disaster area was able to support 100 families.
At the end of 1938, Wang Jingwei sent out a “flashlight” in Hanoi, openly rebelling against the country and surrendering to the enemy. At this point, the Japanese army had already taken over half of my rivers and mountains. Some people lost confidence in winning the war, and some even acted as devil traitors. In 1939, He Xiangning wrote a poem titled “Mei”, expressing his strong belief that he will persevere in the war of resistance and never waver:
Ridge started late and thought; wind and rain hit the highest branch.
Observe half the differences in rivers and mountains, and stand firm with an iron heart and heart.
In 1941, in the face of internal worries and the difficult situation where people did not talk about their lives, she wrote the poem “Huaiyue Famine Warning”:
The dangerous building sat alone and watched the plane. What was the purpose of a bomb falling on me?
Win the martyrdom as a reward for your dead friends, and thank everyone for their hunger.
Hong Kong fell on December 25, 1941. In the poem “Feeling the Fall of Hong Kong”, she expressed her firm belief that the war of resistance against Japan would definitely be won and that Hong Kong would eventually be restored: “The turbulence is bleak for the Hong Kong River. After a hundred years of prosperity, it was impossible to pay for it. Looking back at the land leased in Kowloon, the map temporarily became an enemy country.”
On January 15, 1942, at the direction of the CPC Central Committee, Guangdong Dongjiang Water Guerrillas escorted He Xiangning and her daughter-in-law, grandson, and Liu Yazi's father and daughter from Hong Kong to Guilin, Guangxi via Haifeng, Guangdong. She wrote two poems, “Feelings on the Way Back to Guangdong” and “The Sun Accident Moved to Zhouzhong, Guangxi”:
“Feelings on the Way Back to Guangdong”:
Run out of water and food to cross the seas and bountiful, and dare to fight against time poverty with courage.
When we are impoverished, we are responsible for each other; that is, we die and leave the style of future generations.
“The Japanese Accident Moved to Zhouzhong, Guangxi”:
The clouds of war are spreading all over Shenzhou, so how can the hatred of the broken country end?
However, the military and the civilian population have the same enemy, and wandering in bad ships is also merry.
Due to Ho Heung-ning's great influence on society, Chiang Kai-shek has always wanted to win over her. In the fall of 1944, Hengyang fell, and the Japanese army approached Guangxi. When General Zhang Zhizhong arrived in Guilin from Chongqing, he visited Ho Hsiang-ning on the orders of Chiang Kai-shek, and once again asked her to take refuge in Chongqing with her family, and said that hundreds of thousands of yuan in travel expenses had been specially brought to her. He Xiang Ning flatly refused. To show her heart, she gave her an inspirational poem: “Wandering in the wilderness of Guilin, the country hates and invades each other. Why is it difficult to walk Shudao? There is no reason, but there is a reason.”
She also wrote another nostalgic poem: “Rongma Guanshan wants to dusk, and I'm afraid to go up to the building to see the reunion of the moon. Homesickness is even more painful, rivers and mountains are shattered, and there are two more years left after the robbery.” (Liu Jixing)
