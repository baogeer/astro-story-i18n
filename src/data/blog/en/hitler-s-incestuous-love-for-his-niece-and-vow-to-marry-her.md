 Why did Hitler never get married? Sometimes, we ask him this question... his answer doesn't address the reasons hidden deep in his heart — it's why he vowed to be celibate and didn't break this vow until the eve of his suicide.
Hitler bluntly explained that marriage would distract him, and that a head of state can only completely sacrifice himself for the happiness of his people if he dedicates everything he has to his people. He also gave examples of heads of state who had forgotten their responsibilities to the people because they were concerned about their families and worried about their families. Hitler said, “They had a character that had been trained to be extremely strong, but they were destroyed because of their family. We have also seen some men who have made up their minds to achieve great things on the path they have pioneered, and have become indecisive and come to nothing because of family reasons.” He sees his mission as important and does not allow him to be distracted from personal matters.
When Hitler said this, he had a serious face and an unquestionable tone. In the end, it not only satisfied our curiosity, but also convinced those who were skeptical. That's the end of the question. But the real reason he only got married a few hours before his death constituted the saddest aspect of his life.
He loved Glee Laubal, the daughter of his half-sister Angela, and he had such deep feelings for her that it was impossible for him to consider marrying another woman after the bizarre death of his niece. He often said to me that she had achieved the perfect ideal he placed on a woman, and that he would one day marry her, if not for the bizarre accident that took her life.
When Hitler, an uncle, brought Glee from Vienna, she was only 16 or 7 years old. She was a brown-skinned young girl with light brown eyes, tall stature, and a pleasant voice. In the beginning, Hitler treated her like an older child, made her take music lessons, and was jealous of her dealings with other men.
In 1927, when she was secretly engaged to her driver, Emil Morris, Hitler was furious, ordered the driver to cut ties with her, and threatened that if he ignored it, he would immediately be swept out of the house. Hitler was originally a rude and stubborn person, and he did not hesitate to use any means to break up this young man. Not only did he threaten Glee to kick her out of Munich, but he actually withdrew the financial support he sent to Glee's mother and other members of her family. He used to help them with money. In the summer of 1928, his blackmail triumphed and completely separated Glee and her driver. It didn't take long for young Glee to meet a painting artist in Linz. The painter fell in love with her charm and soon proposed to marry her. Hitler learned about this through his personal detective and used the same method to force his sister to oppose the marriage.
Hitler's motives for these acts are unquestionable. His feelings for his niece are far more than just a kind of love and care for their elders to their juniors. He was tormented by a strong sense of jealousy, and this jealousy was caused by love, but it was not easy for him to show it at the time.
I had the chance to see the young painter's love letter to Glee. In the love letter, the desperate suitor exhausts every reason to ask Glee to go with him. I've re-copied that love letter for Hitler, so now I can also quote the most characteristic passage from that letter here:
“Now, your uncle is aware of his influence on your mother and is shamelessly exploiting her weakness. Unfortunately, it's only when you're an adult that we can fight back against this kind of blackmail. He really put a lot of obstacles in front of our happiness. Yet he knew we were a match made in heaven. The year your mother asked us to separate before she agreed to get married only increased our love and attachment to each other. It's hard for my honesty and kindness to accept such despicable practices.”
“However, I can only explain what your uncle did for some essentially selfish reasons that linked him to you. He just wants you to be attached to him one day and not to anyone else.”
In another section of the letter, the young painter declared:
“Your uncle still sees you as a little girl who knows nothing about the world. He won't understand that you have grown up and will use your own hands to create your own happiness. Your uncle has a violent personality. In his party, everyone treated him as kindly and bowed down to his knees like slaves. I don't understand why someone as smart and sharp as him doesn't understand. His stubbornness and his bizarre opinions about marriage only turned into a fan before our love and wishes. He wants to beat us successfully this year, but he really doesn't understand your fervent feelings...”
It was during this period that Hitler made up his mind to marry Glee as his wife once he achieved his political ambitions. In 1930, he leased an entire floor of a house at 16 Prenzregentamplatz in Munich, and Glee moved to this apartment. The years he lived with Glee were, according to Hitler himself, the happiest period of his life.
Later, when he recalled this past, he was radiant and described to us in detail how they both had an enchanting and happy night together. The two of them always go shopping together, go to shows together, and attend concerts regularly. With a hint of bitterness, he told us some of Glee's little strange temper: “When I took her to the fashion store, she asked the clerk to unpack all the hats in the hat department, and also ordered the clerk to bring the hats displayed in the window. After all the hats in the store had been worn on her head, she told the clerk that no one was suitable for her to wear. She spoke so recklessly that I was very embarrassed. I whispered to Glee that she can't mess up the store like this and then leave the store without buying anything. But she gave me an angry smile and dropped a sentence from her mouth: 'But, Uncle Adolphe, isn't this what these people are here for? '”
Hitler cared for Glee, was always alert and jealous. Every time he goes out to promote his tours, Glee must solemnly swear to him that he won't take advantage of his time out to socialize with people he used to be nice to. It was only when she went back to her mother's that he wouldn't force her to accept his company. This continued until September 1931, when Hitler met a small clerk named Eva Braun in photographer Heinrich Hoffman's store. She had a crush on Hitler and believed she would conquer him. Hitler and her played together and scolded her.
On September 17, 1931, Hitler called Glee from Berchtesgaden, when Glee was resting there. The next day, they both had a big fight because Hitler suddenly decided to go to Nuremberg. Glee accused her uncle of calling her over when nothing happened. He was not at home, but she was prohibited from going to Vienna to have a vocal teacher check her voice. She was very angry about this. When they broke up the next morning, they both began to get involved in the Cold War. On the same day, while searching for her uncle's jacket, Glee found a personal letter from Ava Braun confessing her love to Hitler inside. Glee's bad mood instantly turned to despair. That night, she shot herself in the mouth with a handgun, crying out for life.
Hitler received the news and rushed back from Nuremberg in a hurry. He was very saddened by his niece's suicide. It almost ended his life. His deputy, Hess, had a hard time taking the pistol from him. After that, for a long time, he didn't want to eat. He walked back and forth in the room, wondering what exactly caused his niece to act so decisively.
When he resumed his diet, he was no longer able to eat meat. It was also from that day that he became an absolute vegetarian.
For several months, Hitler refused to meet friends and lived entirely in his memories of Glee, and Glee's room remained the same as when she died. He kept the house blooming with flowers every day, and every year since then he has hosted a birthday celebration for her. Until the war broke out, he had the keys to that room with him, and even Glee's room in Villa Berghof was always locked. Later, when he renovated the villa and expanded the space in the house, his niece's room on the side wing remained untouched. Her clothes, toiletries, and everything that belonged to her were kept where they were. Glee's mother wanted something she had used or some letters to keep as a memento, all of which were rejected by Hitler. All of Glee's letters were carefully kept by her jealous uncle. In April 1945, Hitler ordered his deputy Schaub to destroy those letters if he felt that Hitler had no chance of leaving Berlin. He also had people replicate many portraits based on Glee's photographs and hang them in his apartments in Munich, Berlin, and Villa Berghof. This tragedy once made him indulge in loneliness and was unable to help himself.
Six months after Glee committed suicide, the head of state's friends finally succeeded in rescuing him from loneliness. One night, Heinrich Hoffman took him to the cinema and successfully placed Ava Braun next to him, as if sitting together by chance.
Just like that, Hitler and Ava Braun started blushing again. After a few years, their flirtation turned into a strong relationship. One day Hitler told me honestly that he never had that kind of great love for Ava; he was just used to her. Another time, he said to me, “Ava is very kind, but in my life, only Glee can inspire a real feeling in my heart. I'll never have the idea of marrying my beloved as a wife. The only woman I could possibly combine with in my life is just Glee.”
In early 1945, during a conversation, someone implied that three women had attempted suicide for him. Those three women were Glee, Ava, and Miss Mitford. Hitler answered Glee's death: “She was the only woman I could be happy with, and the only woman I could ever marry. Her death was a terrible experience for me. However, looking back on the past, I'm beginning to believe that this might be the best solution for her, because I might never be able to give her the happiness she deserves.”
One night, in a Munich café, Hitler discovered a girl who looked surprisingly similar to Glee. He asked the girl to come to the table where he was sitting and got to know her. For a few years, he arranged for her to take drama classes, even though she had almost no stage acting talent. During this time, this protected girl lived a very indulgent life. After Hitler found out, he never visited her again, and completely stopped funding her.
In the first few years of her relationship with Hitler, Ava Braun was just a very ordinary, unconfident girl. Her cautious attitude in her dealings with Hitler left a good impression on Hitler and made Hitler fall in love with her.
She never attended official receptions, and Hitler never mentioned her in front of guests. At that time, she hadn't lived in Berghof, where she was a vicious caretaker and mother of the late Glee — the difficult Mrs. Laubal to deal with. Eva just had a room at the nearby Platterhof Hotel. Every now and then she went to the Berghof and stayed with Hitler for a few hours.
Mrs. Laubal fiercely criticized Ava's relationship with her half-brother, expressed extreme contempt for Ava, treated her as an intruder, and was very angry when she saw that she could not separate her from Hitler. Laubal often suggested that Hitler get married to an actress like Mrs. Amy Sonnemann, who later became Goring's wife. One day, in front of Goring, she couldn't help but say, “I'm so envious of you, Mr. Marshal of the Empire. I envy you for two things: first, you married Mrs. Sonnemann, and secondly, having a perfect servant like Robert to serve you. I'm so sorry my brother didn't do this to you.” With a satisfied smile, Goring replied, “I can give Robert to him if necessary. As for Amy Sonneman, I would never give it to him, never!”
Although from the outside, Ava Braun is just a weak, blond woman, she is energetic and strong-willed. Completely surrendering to the capriciousness and whims of her master, she gradually consolidated her position.
Eva was recognized by Hitler on the occasion of the 1936 Congress of the Nazi Party and officially became his lover. Mrs. Laubal criticized her on the grounds that Ava did not participate fully during the Nuremberg parade review. She told her brother about this, but what she never expected was that Hitler was completely on his girlfriend's side. Due to being harshly criticized by Mrs. Laubal, Ava did think about committing suicide. Hitler was greatly shocked by this desperate act, causing him to unkindly send his sister away and finally settle Ava in Burghof.
It was at that time that Eva officially entered the life of the head of the Third Reich. Hitler gave her a small villa in Munich, gave her a car, wrapped her in jewels and expensive dresses, and gave her a pension to meet all her whims.
Ava Braun knows how to adapt to high society's lifestyle. She wanted to become a noblewoman, try her best to imitate Mrs. Goebbels's demeanor, and use her as her own role model. However, all her efforts and expenses must not make others forget her origin. She's still such an ordinary girl, no different from thousands of ordinary girls. Their only interest is to dress up in fancy clothes, and they are terrified at the thought of gaining a few grams more. Ava is afraid of gaining weight, has a particularly irregular diet, and has to take laxatives after every meal. As a result of this diet and dietary restrictions, she developed stomach problems. When she had a stomach attack, Hitler completely panicked. At this point, he acted like a middle school student in love, caressing her hands and arms endlessly while calling her “my little baby.”
Eva doesn't always look beautiful. Her eyes are light brown and her eyelashes are very long, very charming. But as soon as she shrugged her mouth in anger, her beauty disappeared without a trace. At that time, the corners of her lips would hang down two deep lines, making her look particularly old. She is extremely sensitive and easily irritated because the unreal situations she struggles with make her constantly worried. The head of state was fearful all day long when she thought that some of the women responsible for taking care of him would cleverly commit adultery with him. At that time, she would have a horrible sense of inferiority complex. She wanted to know what they were chatting about. Every time someone revealed to her that some female guests showed love to her lover, she felt like she was dying.
Ava's personality makes it difficult for her to get along with people. She has an impulsive personality, doesn't know how to control herself, sometimes lose her temper, and is sometimes ecstatic. She unabashedly showed her dislike or affection for those close to her. She's selfish, except for her family members and close friends. Her capricious personality made her change people frequently. Since Hitler rarely brought her to public appearances, she was very upset; this emotion tormented her. Just thinking that at the party, he would be surrounded by a large group of beautiful women. All kinds of intoxicating words would come out of their mouths, like the scent of burning incense, yet she was forced to stay alone in an empty room and wait for him to return, and she went crazy. It was only when Hitler entertained guests in small groups that Ava was able to sit next to Hitler. I found that when she had this kind of opportunity, she always made every effort to make herself stand out. She stubbornly forces others to accept her opinions on various things. At Burghof, she was treated by the guests as the hostess of the house. At every dinner, Hitler had to change her face, but Ava always sat on Hitler's left. When leaving the table, Hitler always kissed her hand before kissing the female guest sitting on the right.
During the meal, Ava Braun seldom participated in conversation, at least for the first few years. A few years later, when she feels more confident, she will also participate in the conversation, but it depends on how she was feeling at the time. Every time after eating, Hitler hadn't left his seat and was still talking endlessly about topics she loved, and I found her very impatient. She unabashedly showed her impatience. During the war, she was convinced of her huge influence on Hitler, and even dared to cast rebuke at him or ask him out loud. What time is it now. As a result, Hitler would suddenly interrupt his rhetoric, get up and leave the seat, while apologizing for his endless chatter.
Hitler was used to Ava's easily agitated personality, but he never gave her the slightest concession on anything; she had to obey some very strict orders. For example, she can't sunbathe because her master doesn't like brown skin; if she wants to go to a dance party, she can only go secretly because Hitler hates dancing. Eva is an excellent athlete and loves swimming, skiing, and gymnastics. She likes to keep pets, and is surrounded by a shepherd dog, a short-legged hound, and two foxes (next to the left). In April 1945, Hitler raised another long-haired, droopy hunting dog. In addition, Ava was very patient and kept two black thrushes, which flew around freely in her suite.
Ava spends most of her time on makeup. Her dedication to this particular field is extraordinary. To this end, she made a filing cabinet. All the dresses in the cabinet were backed up with the same clothing and style, and numbered. In this way, she can see the clothes in her closet at a glance and quickly browse through them. Ava likes to categorize everything related to it, and this habit is definitely outstanding. Hitler admired her for this virtue, saying that she was very clean and tidy, and never criticized her for her minor negligence.
Ava is a frequent visitor to theaters and cinemas, and she never gets tired of it. Hitler often asked her what she thought about the dramas she had watched, but she was also often tempted to make mistakes, because Ava did not judge the merits of the performance in terms of practical significance, but on the more or less flattering attitude that the artists showed when they saw her. I've never seen her read a serious book; she only likes detective stories or popular literature. Her interest in reading is related to her level of education.
At the beginning of 1938, Miss Mitford was finally able to see Hitler more often than before. All of a sudden, Ava Braun was in a daze. She staged a second suicide farce. This attempted suicide caused her lover to return to her with remorse. Since then, her position has stabilized completely. As soon as Hitler thought that she might commit suicide again, he shuddered at the thought that one day a shocking scandal would break out. All in all, I can clearly feel that since then, Ava has finally become a famous woman. She is increasingly showing off her personality in her social life and is respected and valued by those around her.
Every year, she is allowed to take a few girlfriends on vacation to Italy, and every now and then she can show up in Berlin. In Berlin, however, she wasn't as visible as in Berghof. Hitler didn't forbid her to walk around Berlin, go shopping, go to beauty salons and tailor shops, and even watch one show after another during the drama festival, but she had to remain incognito at all times.
Ava's younger sister, Greet, married Herman Figlein, a liaison officer with Himmler, the national leader of the SS who closely followed the head of state, marking another big step on her path to liberation. Since then, in high society, people introduced her as Feiglein's aunt, so she loved this brother-in-law from the bottom of her heart. However, in any case, when Hitler ordered the execution of Figlein a few days before the fall of Berlin, she still couldn't keep her brother-in-law's head.
In early 1945, she asked me, “Don't you think I'm much freer than before? Previously, at official receptions, I didn't know what kind of attitude to make, but now I'm a person: I'm Major General Figline's aunt. He introduced me to a bunch of people I didn't know, and I also learned so many things I didn't know before.”
At the beginning of 1945, Ava was very disappointed during her two days in Berlin. Over the past year, Hitler followed a stricter vegetarian diet and also asked her to eat a vegetarian diet together. She complained, “We both fight about this every day. I couldn't swallow the horrible hodgepodge he loved to eat, and I noticed that the whole atmosphere here had changed. I used to be so excited to meet him in Berlin, but now I'm starting to regret it. Other than his diet and his dog, Adolf didn't talk to me about anything else. Burundi (Hitler's favorite German shepherd) This filthy beast made me angry. Sometimes I hit it hard with my foot under the table, and Adolf was very alarmed when he saw how crazy it was. It's my way of revenge.”
It can be said that from a political point of view, Ava is absolutely clueless, ignorant, and carefree. When she discovered through the depressed expressions on the faces of Hitler's staff and secretaries that something extraordinary had happened, she came to our trouble and wanted to know the reason.
She often complained that no one told her what had happened. When others told her unpleasant news, she always shouted with a naive face: “But, my kids, I have absolutely no idea about these terrible things.”
