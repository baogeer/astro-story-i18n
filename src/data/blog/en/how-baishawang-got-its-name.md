 There is Baisawang Township between Mingguang Jianxi and Zilaiqiao Town. It is a mountainous town, surrounded by mountains on all sides. The Song and Shihuai River basins are often affected by military disasters. People have fled, and cultivated land is barren. However, there are also occasional refugees from Jiangnan and Huaibei who fled to mountainous areas to take refuge in the disaster of the war. The area of present-day Mingguang City, which was in Caixin and Xuyi counties at the time, was in front of the Song Dynasty. The Song dynasty set up trade stalls, traded with Jinren, and was also a major trading center. Southern merchants such as Liuhe, Yangzhou, Huizhou, and Jinling continued to travel between Liuhe, Xuzhou, Caixin, and Lai'an. Huailiu Ancient Trail: Jingjin - 15 Milijing - Wangcun (now Baisha Wang) - Laokou - Sanli Temple - Zilai Bridge - Tuncang (Laianjing) - Liuhe. At the beginning, merchants shuttled back and forth without incident. Later, a group of mountain thieves who didn't know where they came from often murdered people and robbed goods. For a while, the road was interrupted and sparsely populated, and the trade debate was in a slump. Later, the Song dynasty received reports and learned that mountain robbers had murdered people and stolen goods in the mountains, and that merchants were afraid to take risks. The Song dynasty emperor was furious and sent General Yang into the mountains to fight the bandits.
In other words, General Yang wanted to go into the mountains to fight bandits. Yang Liulang personally took charge and accompanied Meng Liang and Jiao Zan. Because both Meng Liang and Jiao Zan are hunters in the Laojiashan area, they know dozens of miles of mountains, mountains, ridges, and canals. Before Yang Liulang left with his soldiers and horses, General Hu Yanqing found Yang Liulang and told Yang Liulang that his grandmother, grandfather, and uncle all lived in Xiaowang Village north of Yanwang Village. After not seeing each other for many years, Wang Family Ding may have grown. Inevitably, one or two people who have forgotten their interests will join the ranks of bandits. If so, they hope that Rokuro will focus on education and save his life. Rokuro asked Yanqing to list the name of the Wang family so that it would be easier to confess. This made it difficult for Yanqing, because these relatives had been gone for many years. Except for a few who still remember their names, they couldn't remember the rest. Young people who later grew up were even less able to know their names. Yang Liulang was embarrassed when he saw Huyanqing and was embarrassed to say, “Please don't worry, General. Catch mountain thieves and save anyone with the local surname Wang from death.” After listening to it, Hu Yanqing felt relieved and went back.
On this day, Yang Liulang set out to the southeast mountainous area of Zhaoxin County with his troops. Soon, the steep section of the mountain road was selected to support logging and build military camps. At the same time, counties such as Zhaoxin, Haozhou, and Xuyi were required to issue official documents to Yangzhou, Jinling, Huizhou and other places in the south, stating that Song General Yang Liulang was stationed in the mountainous area and patrolled throughout the day. If there were a few bandits or valuable goods, they could be escorted back and forth; if there were large caravans or valuable goods, they could be escorted back and forth to ensure a prosperous trade situation. After the official letter was issued, a small group of merchants first tested back and forth trade. Sure enough, it was safe, and military camps were set up in the most remote places along the way. The merchants spread the word to each other, and more people came and went back and forth. The merchants gradually grew bolder when they saw that military camps were stationed along the way. At the beginning, it was a small-batch, low-investment round-trip shipment. Later, they gradually let go of their hands. They were interested in what kind of goods were sold at the cost of being exported, and the goods were also carried by large carts instead of being carried by horses.
Besides those mountain thieves, I saw Yang Liulang personally stationed his troops on the mountain path to keep the merchants safe. Turtles were shrinking one by one, and they were afraid to touch the stone with eggs. Sitting and eating in the mountains, I couldn't stand it until long. The bold bandits looked for a gap, took advantage of the opportunity to quickly make a quick decision, and hit another place with one shot. In order to find out the details of the mountain thieves, Yang Liulang sent people dressed in casual clothes to go deep into the mountain village to investigate. I learned that with the exception of Coffin Ridge and Kimashu, there are two thief huts. The rest of the small bandits are all villagers in mountain villages, mostly hunters. After Yang Liulang found out the details of the bandits, he began a large-scale attack on the bandits. Before attacking the bandits, he remembered his promise to Huyanqing, so he handed out an order: do not kill any of the bandits with the surname Wang. “Don't kill the king” instructions were passed on among the soldiers.
When they catch mountain thieves, they first ask what their last name is. As long as the surname is not Wang, let alone kill them. The bandits also had the surname Wang, but they didn't belong to Uncle Hu Yanqing's family, but they survived. Soon, the bandits were slaughtered and ran; those who didn't kill and those who didn't run were surnamed Wang. It is true that there are people from Xiaowangcun among these people. They are cousins from Huyanqing, but there are also no such people; they rely on Xiaowangcun. Originally, there were only 20 or 30 families in Xiaowang Village. It was a small mountain village with no known name, but in the few years Yang Liulang fought the bandits, it suddenly grew to 450 households. People with different surnames in the village have all changed their surname to Wang in order to settle the knot. Villagers near and far replaced the original Xiaowangcun with the “Don't kill Wang” command, and called it Kaifang.
Legend has it that in the late Qing dynasty, the elders who didn't kill Wang Cun felt that the name “Don't Kill Wang” would be ferocious, so they changed the name. After several days of discussion, many alternative village names were proposed, but they were not suitable, and I was afraid that those that had changed their new names for a year and a half would not be able to be called. Finally, I wrote an article on the three words “Don't Kill the King.” An elder suggested that it would be more appropriate to change the name “White Sand King”. He explained to the villagers that after not killing Wangcun, there was a mountain stream. The mountain stream produced white sand. “White sand” was synonymous with “white sand” and “don't kill” was used to promote white sand, which is abundant in the local area, and the word “white sand” was used instead of the word “don't kill” to get aggressive. People all felt very good when they heard it, so the place name “Don't Kill King” was called for hundreds of years, and later it was changed to “White Sand King”.
