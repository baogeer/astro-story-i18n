 Special times have special rules of the game. In the Han Dynasty, the style of judging characters was popular, and fame directly influenced the career of Shizi. If you want to make yourself famous, the most direct way is to hype yourself up. Cao Cao put it into practice once. It was just a bit blatant and a bit overbearing. Once he got approval from famous people, he laughed out loud with satisfaction. Perhaps this is what makes Fierce Heroes unique.
The Han dynasty employed people and attached great importance to the evaluation of public opinion. Their job standards were mainly based on local reviews, or so-called clean-up reviews; in fact, it was a kind of public opinion appraisal. Only taxis who are praised by public opinion are likely to be selected. Due to the popular culture of judging characters, some people have become experts in articulating authority and appraising talents. They are known as famous people in the world. Their praise and disapproval of characters can influence local public opinion to a large extent, thus affecting the progress and decline of the careers of warriors. In order to win the praise of the priestess, they must engage in extensive social activities and seek mentors and friends to showcase and enhance their talents and reputation, and gain people's attention and favor. Special attention was paid to garnering praise from Qingyi Authority, so much so that some Qingyi Authority had guests all day long, and there were even cases where people seeking fame had gone thousands of miles.
And in this regard, Cao Cao really showed his great wisdom. First, he adopted the strategy of befriending young celebrities; second, he bowed his head to ask for advice from older celebrities. In this way, it is beneficial to seek knowledge and help from famous people, thereby improving one's reputation and expanding one's influence.
Cao Cao dated Yuan Shao when he was a teenager. Although there were some barriers between the two in adulthood, the relationship was still unusual. By the time Yuan Shao and Yuan Shu's mother were buried in Runan after their deaths, Cao Cao went to the funeral regardless.
Yeongcheon Lee Chan is the son of “Party Member” leader Lee Yeong, and later became Minister of State Dongping (like a county governor). Cao Cao is in a relationship with him, and they know each other very well. Li Chan was very appreciative of Cao Cao's talent. At the end of his life, Li Chan said to his son Li Xuan, “There is no hero in the world who can surpass Cao Cao. Zhang Yuzhuo (Zhang Miao) is my friend, and Yuan Benchu (Yuan Shao) is your maternal relative. Despite this, you must not depend on them; you must rely on Cao Cao.” Later, several of Lee Chan's sons followed their father's orders and certainly saved their lives in the troubled world.
He Yi from Nanyang, Zi Boqiu, traveled to Luoyang when he was young, and had good relationships with Tae student leaders such as Guo Tai and Jia Biao, and is very famous. When her friend Lu Weigao's father died, Ho Yi went to greet her. When she learned that her father had no revenge, she helped Lu Weigao take revenge and sacrificed his enemy's head in front of his father's tomb. It was very chivalrous.
He Yi and his bureaucrats and “party members” Chen Fan and Li Ying are on good terms. After Chen Shan and Li Ying were murdered by lieutenants, Ho Yi was also implicated and arrested, so she changed her name and fled to Runan to hide. Yuan Shao admired his name and had a private relationship with him. He Yi often sneaks into Luoyang to discuss plans with Yuan Shao to rescue “party members.”
Cao Cao also had a relationship with Ho Yi during this period. He talked about Confucian studies, discussed Baijia, “Shijing,” and talked about the art of war. Analyzing and commenting on actual factional struggles and the scourge of party confinement is very insightful. He showed that he was knowledgeable and talented in helping the world. He Yi privately said to others, “The Han family is about to perish. Whoever can secure the world must be this person.” When Cao Cao heard it, he was very grateful.
After making friends with famous people, Cao Cao became famous among the people, so he sought greater fame.
Among the many people at the time, Xu Shao was a very influential person. Whoever received his praise would be very beneficial to his career. Xu Shao, the character master, is a public figure in Nanping. They respect themselves in the name of honor and refuse to be called to serve as an official. Good at identifying and commenting figures. At the time, people recommended Qingyi's authority, and they all represented him and Taiyuan Guo Tai. Whoever can get Xu Shao's praise will be able to double his worth. On the first day of every month, Xu Shao often re-reviews local figures, called the “Moon Day Review.”
In order to get Xu Shao's praise, Cao Cao first visited Daming Shi Qiao Xuan, who has a high reputation in the judging community.
Hashi Xuan, Zi Gongzu, is a native of Liangguo Yayang. He has held positions such as County Clerk, Minister of State, Taishou, Shu Chang Shi, Master Master Master, Shaofu, Dahongping, Si Kong, Zishu, and Shu Ling. Corporal Hashi Hyun Kung is good at observing and evaluating people, and also enjoys a high reputation in the Qing court community. Cao Cao admired him. After talking with him, Qiao Xuan felt that Cao Cao was very unusual. He said:
“Now the world is about to turn into chaos; it is not people who have gone through the Bonji era who will not be able to stabilize the world. You are probably the one who can stabilize the world.”
After stopping for a moment, he said, “I've seen so many famous people in the world; none of them are like you. I'm old and would like to entrust my wife and children to you.”
Cao Cao listened and was very grateful. He introduced this old senior as his confidant. Qiao Xuan felt that Cao Cao was not famous yet, and advised him to befriend Xu Shao.
Because of Qiao Xuan's recommendation, and because he has been admired for Xu Shao for a long time, Cao Cao brought courtesy to see Xu Shao and asked Xu Shao to praise himself. On the one hand, Xu Shao felt that Cao Cao was unique; on the other hand, he probably understood Cao Cao's actions like those flying eagles; he didn't really admire him, so he declined to answer. But Cao Cao never relaxed and insisted on his demands. Xu Shao had no choice but to say, “You are a powerful minister in the Taiping era, an adulterous man in the turbulent times.” Cao Cao was very happy to hear this comment. Haha left with a big smile.
