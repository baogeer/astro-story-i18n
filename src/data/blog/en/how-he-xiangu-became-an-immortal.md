 People who know a little bit about traditional Chinese culture will surely be familiar with the term “Eight Immortals.” The so-called “Eight Immortals” refer to the eight immortals of Han Zhongli, Zhang Guolao, Tieguai Li, Uncle Cao, Lu Dongbin, Han Xiangzi, Lan Caihe, and He Xianguu. They are all from mortal backgrounds, and they have worked hard to become immortals. The only woman in the Eight Immortals is Ho Xian Gu. Among the Eight Immortals, she seems to be the only little red in the midst of all the greenery, so she is particularly remarkable.
He Xianguu, originally known as He Qiong, was born in the first year of Tang Gaozong Kaiyao to an ordinary family of shogunds in Lingling. Locals say that on the day He Qiong was born, a bright and bright purple atmosphere enveloped the cottage, and a group of cranes were flying up and down in the purple gas. Soon, a large sika deer carrying a girl with a small braid and red belly rushed into the house. At this point, Mother Ho gave birth to a white, chubby baby girl.
Speaking of He Qiong's hometown, Ling Ling, is a Feng Shui treasure with beautiful mountains and clear water. According to the “Historical Records”, “Shun was buried in Jiuyuan, Jiangnan, for Ling Ling.” Emperor Shengshun was buried in Jiuwei Mountain in Lingling. Lingling City is located where the two rivers of Xiao and Xiang meet. It overlooks Mount Yueheng in the north and the majestic Five Mountains in the south. It is the gateway to Chu and Guangdong. There is a mica mountain in the west of Lingling County. The mountain is rich in multicolored mica stones. Mica stone is an ancient medicine for people seeking immortality; a clear and gentle stream runs down the mountain, called Mica Creek. He Qiong's family is on the shores of the beautiful Mica Creek.
He Qiong, who grew up drinking mica water, had a beautiful and beautiful appearance. Since she was a child, she loved to play and roam by herself along Mica Creek. At the age of 14, she met a white-haired, long-bearded old man on the banks of Mica Creek. He Qiong answered them smartly one by one. He was very happy. He Qiong took a bright peach from his backpack and gave it to He Qiong. He Qiong took it, thanked Xie Laoweng, and then ate the peach three times five times. The old man watched her finish eating, nodded and turned away. After returning home, He Qiong didn't feel hungry for a few days, so she didn't want to eat, but she was more energetic than before. A month later, He Qiong met the old man by Mica Creek. This time, the old man took her to Mica Mountain to teach her how to collect mica and how to eat mica. He Qiong followed his words and went to Mica Mountain every day to pick mica, and gradually felt that he was as light as a swallow, walking to and from the top of the mountain like flying. In addition, she can also identify and pick all kinds of fairy herbs and elixirs in the mountains, treat various diseases for nearby people, and can predict personnel affairs, so everyone around her calls her “Ho Sian Gu.”
The news of He Qiong's attainment spread ten, ten to one hundred, and spread farther and farther. In the end, it spread to the ears of Wu Zetian, the royal official of Beijing.
Wu Zetian was the empress of Tang Gaozong at the time, but she held real power in the imperial court. Wu Zetian was influenced by her mother to believe in Buddhism from an early age, and even became an empress. She also tried to promote Buddhism within the palace and all over the country, hoping to overcome the Taoist momentum revered by the Li and Tang dynasties, and used certain Buddhist sutras as the theoretical basis for her usurping the throne. The Buddhist “Dharma Sang sect” promotes “two emptiness,” which means that we need to think of ourselves and everything as empty, so that we can reach the lofty realm where everything in the universe is one; in popular terms, we can just feel things with our heart and forget things, then we can soar in the daytime, drive in the clouds, and live forever. Wu Zetian was very convinced of this. When she heard that there was a Ho Xian-gu in the Lingling region. She was very interested in being able to freely travel to and from the top of the mountain without eating fireworks. She also sent someone to visit and gave Ho Xian Gu a morning glow suit. He Xianguu accepted the morning glow suit and wore it with great interest. People around him came from all sides to watch when they heard the news. They saw Ho Xiangu's body shining brightly. Her eyes were dazzling; the townspeople were shocked and couldn't help but kneel to the ground in unison to worship Ho Xiangu. He Xianguu felt quite comfortable, yet her mother was terrified and thought, “Who dares to marry a daughter like this!”
Not surprisingly, as expected by Ho Sang-gu. When Ho Sian-gu was 18, her mother hurriedly asked a matchmaker to choose her son-in-law. Although Ho Sin Gu came out as beautiful as a flower, no one dared to get married because she was so talented. Mother Ho was worried, but if nothing happened to Ho Sian Gu herself, she spent the whole day in the mountains and villages, busy collecting medicine and treating people, and had a very fulfilling life.
One day, He Xianguu went deep into the Mica Mountain jungle to gather medicine and met two amazing people. Among them was a lame old man with an iron hand and a huge wine gourd, dressed like a beggar; the other was wearing a clean blue cloth shirt, holding a medicine hoe and a medicine basket on his shoulder. Not far in front of Ho Xian-gu, these two sang together, and had words in their mouths. Soon, they actually vacated and suddenly disappeared. These two people were Tieguai Li and Lan Caihe from The Eight Immortals. He Xian Gu kept an eye on them and read the secret of stealing their studies. She wanted to actually be able to ride the wind and fly over the valley like them. Since then, she has often quietly gone deep into the mountains to practice alone, becoming more skilled and able to fly farther and farther. Using this kind of kung fu, she often flew to the mountains in the distance, and brought back some strange mountain fruits for her family to taste. The family felt that they were sweet and delicious, and their energy was doubled, but after all, they didn't know what kind of fruit it was.
Seeing that she left early and returned late every day, Mother Ho was worried and questioned where she had gone to do what. He Xianguu couldn't help her mother; she said that she went to famous mountains and fairy Buddhas every day to talk about Buddhism. Gradually, news spread again that He Xianguu was familiar with Buddhism. After hearing about it, Wu Zetian sent an envoy to Ling Ling to get ready for Luoyang, the eastern capital, to discuss Buddhism. Officials trekked the mountains and water with He Xianguu to the outside of Luoyang City. While waiting for the boat to cross Luoshui, the crowd suddenly disappeared, causing the minister to panic and rush around to search for it, but they didn't find any telltale signs. The crowd was so frightened that they sat by the Luo River in a daze. At dusk, He Xiangu fell into the air and hurriedly told the messenger, “I have already gone to the Forbidden Palace to see the divine; you can go back to North Korea and get back to life.”
The envoy returned to the Luoyang Palace in doubt. As soon as he inquired, it was true that He Xianguu visited Wu Hou that day and had a long conversation with her in the palace for half a day, which surprised the ministers.
It is said that in the palace, Ho Sin-gu talked with Wu Hou about the art of immortality. She persuaded Wu Hou that to live a long life, they must first achieve a laid-back desire, abandon voices, and see fame and fortune; secondly, they must do more good deeds, suppress torture, strictly prohibit false accusations, practice benevolent government, and cultivate good fortune. At the same time, she also discussed the way to govern the country and keep the country safe; it is essential to have close relatives, ministers, and young people; never use someone of another surname as the heir to the emperor. What she mentioned was actually very specific to the post-military era.
Empress Wu was an intelligent person. After hearing the sentiments of Ho Xianguu, she gradually took action. Soon, Lai Junchen and others were beaten; then she reused the wise man, Di Renjie: As for the imperial family, Empress Wu originally wanted to establish Wu Chengsi as the prince. Under Di Renjie's advice, she followed Xian Gu's admonition and abandoned her original intention to have a different surname. Wu Zetian, who is already addicted to lust and power, has never been able to do it. It is said that it has reached the point where it is difficult to get lost and find it difficult to return.
Wu Zetian ordered local officials from Lingling to build a majestic Huixian Hall at Fenghuangtai in the south of Lingling City as a place for Ho Sian Gu to teach the promotion of the law. In addition to preaching, Ho Sin Gu often sat on the stone steps in front of the museum, peeled a round fairy fruit, and threw away the core of the fruit four times. Later, a lychee tree grew around the Huixian Hall. The lychee that grew on these trees was actually all green, green, green lychee. People call it “On Phoenix Terrace, lychee hangs green.”
He Xianguu also wrote a poem called “Phoenix Terrace”:
Phoenix mica is like smallpox, made into hibiscus and white cloud buds;
The Laughing Madman has no ideas, and even more where to find Dansha.
This poem is ostensibly written about Phoenix Terrace; in fact, the poem contains the true meaning of how to eat mica, which is the way to eat mica. People say that she is a Taoist believer based on the characteristics of her worship and practice; others attribute her to a Buddhist disciple based on the ideological content of her sermon. In fact, Ho Sian Gu can be said to be both a Buddha and a person who is not a Buddha. It's a realm where heaven and human beings are united in itself; how can worldly Buddhism frame her!
One day, Ho Sian-gu suddenly got inspired and wrote a poem titled “Magu Feng”:
Magu laughed at me, I'm in love with the hustle and bustle; it's a long way away from Xian Fan;
Fly to Cangzhou to find out the moon and ride a yellow crane in reverse to listen to the piping.
This poem, which is full of fairy rhymes, seems to harbor some kind of omens. Sure enough, on a day in the first year of Tang Zhongzong's Jinglong year, he Xianguu, 26, was sitting on Fenghuang Terrace, looking up at the distant sky, and suddenly saw Tieguai Li standing in the clouds in the distance, dancing his iron abyss, and seemed to be greeting her. Unbeknownst to me, Ho Xian-gu's body rose up like a rainbow, and flew up into the sky, following Li Gui. A bead shoe from her foot fell to the ground. The next day, a well suddenly appeared at the place where the beads fell. The well water was clear and sweet, with a strong smell. It was surrounded by a well fence, shaped like a bow shoe. The local people built a Ho Xian Temple next to the well. It was very fragrant day by day, because the water in the well not only refreshes and quenches thirst, but also cures all kinds of drip diseases, so people near and far relish it.
He Xian Gu soared up during the day and became immortal, thus becoming the only red powder among the Eight Immortals, creating a slightly red situation in the midst of all the greenery. After becoming an immortal, He Xianguu never forgets the suffering of the world. She often travels around the south to spread clouds and rain, eliminate epidemics, and relieve suffering. Any kind person needs her help. Just by silently praying to the sky, she can arrive like “timely rain” and give people magical strength.
Tang Xuanzong Tianbao is nine years old. It has been more than 30 years since He Xianguo became a fairy. After a day of heavy rain, people in the Lingling region saw a colorful cloud floating leisurely. He Xianguu stood in the clouds in morning glow suits. Just as people kneeled down to worship, a bunch of yellow lilies fell from the sky onto Phoenix Terrace, with this verse written on it:
The banks of Mica Creek rise above the roof, and thousands of peach trees bloom;
Blow the jade flute through Huanglong Cave; don't lead the crane to cross the length.
A message to the Zhang Family and the Li Family, putting the dust out of the room;
The weak water in Horai is now shallow, and the ground is shaded with flowers to protect the moonlight.
The fairy has taken advantage of Ziwei, and it is still too late to look back at the water town;
I've kept Ike's shoes for a thousand years, and they say they are familiar with the Thatched Cottage Fairy.
The poem includes affection for the customs of the hometown, warm words for the townspeople, and an explanation of the quiet feelings about the life of an immortal, and persuades everyone to cherish their own lives. Really, “Everyone says that immortals are good. Who knows immortals have problems. When advising you to take mere mortals, don't wait for time to pass by itself!”
