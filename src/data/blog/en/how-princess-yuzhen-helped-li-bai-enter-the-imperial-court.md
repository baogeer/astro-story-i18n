 In the first year of Tang Xuanzong Tianbao (742 AD), Li Bai was 42 years old. In the first month of this year, Tang Xuanzong issued an edict: “Former financial officers and white people were proficient in Confucianism, excellent literature, and martial arts. They are recommended by name.” (“Old Tang Shu: The Book of Xuanzong”) Whether you are now an ordinary citizen or a national civil servant, as long as you are proficient in Confucianism, or are proficient in writing, military martial arts, or have skills, all in all, you are a useful person, and the local government can recommend you to the imperial court. The Tang dynasty emperors often promulgated such edicts to recruit talents. This was a rare opportunity for those with real talents, practical studies, and a desire to build meritorious careers. And the core element of this opportunity is getting strong recommendations, which means whether the right person will recommend you to the imperial court.
Li Bai left his hometown at the age of 24 and is now over 40 years old. He has been looking for political opportunities that suit him for more than ten years, so can he seize this opportunity this time around? Who else would recommend Li Bai to the imperial court and to Emperor Xuanzong?
The emperor didn't owe it. Li Bai finally found a very important recommender. This person has a very close relationship with Tang Xuanzong. She is Princess Yuzhen, the daughter of Tang Ruizong and the younger sister of Tang Xuanzong. Princess Yu Zhen believes in Taoism. During the Xuanzong dynasty, she became a Taoist. The law was supreme, and later she was given the name Master Zhi Ying. Princess Yu Zhen was born out of a royal family member as a Taoist. It was not uncommon in Tang Dynasty society. This had a lot to do with the social culture at the time. At the beginning of the establishment of the Tang dynasty, the rulers recognized Li Er, the founder of the Taoist family, as the ancestor of the Taoist family, in order to consolidate and deify their power, and defined Taoism as the “national religion.” Tang Taizong also issued a special edict to rank Taoists ahead of monks and nuns: “From now on, the practice of fasting has been established. As for the title, the female Taoist crown can be ahead of the monk and nun. The original customs of Shudun are common in Jiuyou; the style of respecting ancestors brings back ten thousand leaves.” (“Bringing the Taoist in Front of the Monk”) Later, the Taoist was also included as a type of examination in academic examinations. As a result, the status of Taoists in Tang dynasty society was very high.
Taoism and Taoist ideology flourished in the Tang Dynasty. The rulers of the Tang Dynasty, Tang Gaozu, Tang Gaozong, Tang Xuanzong, etc. all had close relationships with Taoist figures, such as Sima Chengzhen, the Taoist leader at the time, were summoned and treated kindly by the three emperors Wu Zetian, Tang Ruizong, and Tang Xuanzong. The emperors not only asked him to teach him the art of immortality, but also consulted them on how to govern the country. Taoism was a powerful social force and ideological trend in the Tang dynasty. The reason why the emperor paid great attention to Taoist activities and Taoist figures was based on the political interests of the entire Tang dynasty. In other words, the relationship between Taoist ideology, Taoist activities, and Taoist figures and Tang Dynasty politics is too close, or it can be said that Taoism itself was an important part of the political life of the Tang Dynasty. Therefore, starting with Taoism is a wise choice to enter the Tang dynasty's official business. The reason Li Bai wanted to have a relationship with Princess Yu Zhen was that he wanted to follow the path of becoming a member of the Daoist religion.
However, most readers in the Tang Dynasty were aware of the close relationship between Taoism and politics, as well as the relationship between Princess Yu Zhen and Xuanzong. How can they successfully connect Princess Yu Zhen and stand out from the many competitors? This requires critical help from another key person. Who is this person? This is Li Bai's friend Yuan Danqiu. We don't know much about Yuan Danqiu, but we are very familiar with Li Bai's poem “Almost Liquor,” which contains two sentences: “Cen Fuzi, Danqiu is born, close to wine, can't stop drinking, can't stop drinking. I want you to listen to it for me.” The birth Danqiu in the poem is Yuan Danqiu.
Li Bai met Yuandanqiu very early. He met Yuandanqiu when he was in his hometown of Sichuan in his 20s. When Li Bai was in his 50s, he wrote a poem “Autumn Refinery Tweezers Bai Fa Giving Yuan Six Brothers Lin Zong”. The poem said, “Thirty years of voting, we have enjoyed the prosperity and decline.” Explain that for more than 30 years, they have always been very close and good friends. Li Bai and Yuandanqiu once roamed Sichuan, Hubei Anlu, Xiangyang, Henan Songshan, and Shimen Mountain. Judging from Li Bai's letters and poems with Yuandanqiu, this Yuandanqiu was clearly a professional Taoist who lived in secluded mountains and forests. In “Li Hanlin Collection”, Wei Hao said, “Bai Jiuju lived in Eyi, reached with Master Danqiu Zhi Ying, and Bai also joined Hanlin because of this,” revealing that the key figures who recommended Li Bai to enter Chang'an were Princess Yuan Danqiu and Princess Yu Zhen.
Yuan Danqiu had a long and close relationship with Li Bai. He naturally understood Li Bai's aspirations, personality, and demands. Li Bai was able to meet Sima Chengzhen back then, and was also recommended by Yuan Danqiu. However, the relationship between Yuan Danqiu, Princess Yu Zhen, and Sima Chengzhen is even more unusual. Princess Yu Zhen was ordered by Emperor Xuanzong to build funds for Sima Seung-jeong. She and Sima Seung-jeong are considered to have a mentor-apprenticeship relationship. Although Li Bai had no chance to meet Princess Yu Zhen through Sima Chengzhen, this indirect relationship helped him get close to Princess Yu Zhen.
Around the first year of Tang Xuanzong and Tianbao, Princess Yu Zhen visited Zhenyuan Palace in Qiao County, Hebei to participate in Taoist activities. Later, Cai Wei, a Taoist of Hongdao in Chang'an, wrote “Princess Yu Zhen Visits Zhenyuan Palace in Qiao County, Shedao, Wangwushan, and Xianrentai.” This incident was recorded in detail. Tang Xuanzong personally wrote a monument for this monument. The inscription on the monument tells us that Yuan Danqiu was responsible for the construction of this monument. It can be seen that the relationship between Yuan Danqiu and Princess Yu Zhen is extraordinary. We can make a relatively reasonable guess: Perhaps Yuan Danqiu directly followed the princess to participate in this event. Perhaps, during this event, Yuan Danqiu recommended Li Bai to Princess Yu Zhen and presented the princess with “Yu Zhen Fairy Tales” written by Li Bai many years ago:
The Immortal Man of Yuzhen went to Taihua Peak in time.
Drums rang out early in the morning, and Ssangyong soared.
They never quit getting electricity; there was no trace of their trip.
When did they enter the junior room, the princess was supposed to meet.
The meaning of the poem is that Yu Zhen Taoists live on Mount Taihua and practice the method of hitting their teeth early in the morning, that is, the sound of the sky drums, and they are about to clap their upper and lower teeth. At this point, the wind raged, like a dragon twirling in a sea of clouds. Princess Yu Zhen has excellent spells. She keeps playing with Lightning Jade, and Teng Yun drives back and forth in the fog without a trace. It won't be long before the Immortals of Yu Zhen will be immortal and attained true fruit. When they go to Mount Shomuro, even Wang's mother and daughter will personally welcome her.
In this poem, Li Bai integrates the real world with the fairy world, vigorously beautifies Princess Yu Zhen, and weaves a very beautiful picture of the fairy. This romantic and exaggerated approach is different from Li Bai's self-recommendation books for ordinary officials. Instead of directly stating his life experiences and talents, nor directly boasting about each other, he uses a fairy world to support Princess Yu Zhen's magical abilities.
Speaking of the song “Yu Zhen Fairy Tales,” Princess Yu Zhen should have seen it a long time ago. It was around 19 years after Tang Xuanzong's founding. Li Bai, who was in his 30s at the time, came to Chang'an to seek a career; his fame was far less prominent than later. In order to make friends with powerful people, he wandered around and wrote the song “Yu Zhen Fairy Tales”, hoping to be recommended through his relationship with Princess Yu Zhen.
Later, Li Bai met Tang Xuanzong's horse, Princess Yu Zhen's niece and son-in-law Zhang, hoping Zhang could connect him with Princess Yu Zhen. Who knows, Zhang didn't really plan to help Li Bai. Li Bai had no choice but to stay alone at the Zhongnanshan Tower Guandai Princess Yuzhen Annex, waiting for the opportunity. Lou Guandai has been a Taoist resort dedicated to Laozi since the Han Dynasty. It is more than 100 miles from Chang'an City. Princess Yu Zhen not only has Yu Zhen Guan in Chang'an City, but has also built many annexes in places such as Louguantai Mountain. Many great poets from the Tang Dynasty, such as Wang Wei and Chu Guangxi, were visitors to the Princess Yuzhen Annex and wrote quite a few poems. It can be seen that Princess Yu Zhen had close relationships with writers, and many writers wanted to build a bridge with Xuanzong through Princess Yu Zhen. However, Li Bai was very unlucky that time. Not only did he not meet Princess Yu Zhen at the annex, but he was also very frustrated, let alone contribute to “Yu Zhen Fairy Tales.” This can be seen from “Princess Yu Zhen, Annex, Bitter Rain Presents Two Songs to Guard Zhang Qing”, which he wrote at the time. One of them wrote:
The Kinjagikan in the fall is not open in the middle of the day.

Overshadowy, grievous, and hateful.

This poem was filled with a bleak and lonely atmosphere. At the time, it was a time of continuous autumn rain, and the whole day was overcast. I hadn't seen a clear sky in a long time; I was in the same mood as this weather. It was very cloudy, and I could only drink a glass of alcohol to masturbate. At the end of Part 2, Li Bai wrote: “When is the golden plate, I recommend betel nuts. The accomplishment blew away and swayed around Cangzhou.” There is a person in South Korea called Liu Muzhi. His family is very poor; his wife's mother's family is rich. Once, he went to his mother-in-law's house to eat. After he was full, he ate a few more betel nuts. My mother's brother sarcastically said that betel nuts help digestion; people like you who can't eat enough every day don't need to eat betel nuts. Later, he became a senior official and invited his mother's brother to eat. After eating, he gave his mother's brother a large pot of betel nuts on a golden plate. It can be seen that although the situation was poor at the time, Li Bai felt alone and helpless, but he still believed that one day he would stand out.
Now, the years of painstaking search have finally come to an end. Through the introduction of his old friend Yuan Danqiu, Li Bai has been strongly recommended by Princess Yu Zhen, and he is about to meet Tang Xuanzong. It's also interesting to talk about. There has always been controversy in academia about Li Bai's recommendation. The main reason for this result is that Li Bai was vague about this issue. He never mentioned a specific recommender: “In the beginning of Tianbao, the five provinces were transferred, and they did not seek good knowledge.
Also, Yuko Mayaguchi, the famous master of Kyoto, was delighted to hear it and summoned to the ban.” (“Self-recommendation Form for Song Zhongcheng”) In the early years of Tianbao, although the imperial court was repeatedly called up, I was unwilling to go out of the mountains. Since I have been in seclusion in the mountains and forests for many years, and my fame has shaken Beijing, the saint admired me very much, so I was invited to the palace. Of course, this is a very general and vague statement. The key point is to rely on one's own strength to directly reach out to Heaven without involving a specific recommender at all.
We can make some reasonable assumptions. The reason why Li Bai was able to be personally summoned by Xuanzong this time is fundamentally through two channels: one is the Taoist channel, and the other is the Princess channel. Strictly speaking, none of these are bright and fair channels; there is a suspicion that they are walking through the back door. Li Bai doesn't want to clearly talk about the specific channels through which he entered North Korea; maybe he doesn't want his political image to always carry such a shameful braid.
In fact, Li Bai's career path was not uncommon in the Tang dynasty. For example, the famous poet Wang Wei used the power of a princess to achieve his goals. Wang Wei is proficient in musical rhythms and is good at playing pipa. He often travels to powerful people, “Qi Wang Zhong Zhong Zhi”. Wang Wei wanted to advance in the exam and asked Qi Wang for help. Qi Wang asked him to prepare ten excellent poems, a haunted pipa song. Then, dress up Wang Wei in a splendid costume and take him to meet a certain princess. At the feast, Qi Wang's celebrities came to perform to cheer up. Wang Wei “had a beautiful white year and a beautiful style, and stood ahead.” Princess Life and Wang Wei played the song. The tone of the song was sad, and the audience was full of emotion. The princess couldn't help but wonder. Wang Wei also took advantage of the opportunity to present her poems. The princess was surprised to say that these are all poems I usually recite. She thought they were written by the ancients, but she didn't expect the author to be right around the corner, so she entertained Wang Wei as a guest. Wang Wei had an elegant and elegant conversation at the feast, which was greatly appreciated by the powerful and powerful. Qi Wang took the opportunity to express Wang Wei's wishes to join the ranks. The princess agreed, and Wang Wei immediately took first place (see Tang Xue Yongjiao's “The Legend of Tang Caizi”, Song Xin Wenfang's “The Biography of Tang Caizi”).
Regardless of whether this story is true or false, at least it means that in the Tang dynasty, it was not uncommon for royal family members to ask for advice or recommendations, so Li Bai's actions are not surprising.
Li Bai is now about to enter Chang'an, to enter the political center of the Tang Empire, to meet Tang Xuanzong, the supreme ruler of the Tang Empire. Li Bai seems to be getting closer to his political ideals, and it seems that his many achievements and the heavy responsibilities of an assistant secretary will soon become a reality.
