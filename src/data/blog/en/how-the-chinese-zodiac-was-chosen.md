 The old scalper became an official today. He is both excited and busy. In order to ease the tension between humans and animals, Emperor Jade decided to pair 12 types of animals with humans. The selected animals are arranged year by year, and the animal year is implemented. People born in a certain animal year have a certain zodiac sign, so that humans and animals live in harmony for a lifetime. The scalper was designated as the convener of the conference representing the Chinese zodiac for livestock and poultry.
This is the first time a scalper has been an official. He is so busy that he gasps. Being an official really has to take care of the affairs is also very painful. He is illiterate. At the suggestion of the flower cat, he invited a mouse as a recorder.
The time has come for the meeting, and three people who were due to attend the meeting were absent.
The pig said, “I ate enough, slept well, and was stabbed when the time came. It doesn't matter if it's an improper representative; it's a lie. If there are too many delegates, it's OK to round up the numbers.”
Sister Goo and Sister Duck took leave of absence together. The reason is that the duck sister was deceived by a scammer and is heartbroken. Sister Goo wants to coax her to the riverside to relax — enlighten her.
Duck Girl and Cormorant were originally a good pair of little sisters. Under the cormorant's sweet talk, they both changed their feathers and mouths. After the exchange, the duck girl discovered that the cormorant had better floating water, but she couldn't dive to the bottom of the river to eat small fish and shrimp; the large mouth of the cormorant made it easier to eat the food the owner gave them, but it was useless to go to the riverside or meadow to catch bugs. Only then did I know that I had been scammed. Duck Girl went looking for a cormorant in exchange, but they stopped doing it. The duck girl thought that she was confused; don't worry about losing money in this life. The problem is that future generations will always be like this; they will eat this loss; the more she thinks about it, the more sad she becomes. Brother Duck learned about this, found a deal with the cormorant, and quarreled for seven days and seven nights. Brother Duck's voice was hoarse, and his voice has been hoarse ever since.
If three people are absent, the conference will still have to be held. Scalpers first talked about the importance of the zodiac, then asked the family to nominate or report their own names. As long as most people agree, that counts.
After a while, there were no nominations or self-reports. The recorder is a mouse with twists and turns, saying, “The Chinese zodiac accompanies people for a lifetime, so you have to be in close relationship with them. Niu Bo and Uncle Ma cultivate fields and set up carts and work sincerely for others. They are natural zodiac representatives, and they are honored labor models.”
The goat thought: Am I not close to people? You're a “bragging” dog thing, but they all nodded, that's all. The flower kitty cries, “I'm a domestic tiger. Who hasn't seen a tiger? Just take a look, you can do it. Don't look at the tiger; he only belongs to me; people call him a feline. So I think mine is a part of the zodiac.” Because no one raised any objections, it was approved.
The rooster whistled and said, “No one nominated their own newspaper. I wake up early every day to inspire people to do all kinds of work. I have a very close relationship with people, so that's one?”
The yellow dog howled and said, “Don't listen to her; she woke up early to report the news. Do you know why? This stupid thing turns out to be able to fly because she has a beautiful crown in front of her nose. She made friends with a blue snake in Qingyun Mountain. It was as good as a bowl of oil. The blue snake has been practicing for many years and claims to be a blue dragon, but it just doesn't have a front crown on its nose and can't fly in the sky. The blue snake lent him a front crown and told him to do something in heaven; at most, three days or less, it would be returned in one day. This idiot actually agreed. The blue snake borrowed the crown in front of the nose and never came back, so this idiot woke up every morning and shouted to the sky: 'Brother Dragon... give me back my crown..., Brother Dragon, give me back my crown... wow. ' She is collecting debts every day, where and what are the reports?”
Ma shook her head and said, “You must have made up this stupid story. No matter what, the rooster has objectively played a role in reporting for a long time. I agree that she counts as one.”
The old scalper listened and thought Ma was right, and nodded.
The rooster clutched its wings and blushed with excitement.
The goat brushed its beard and said, “We are inseparable from people, and people are inseparable from us.”
The yellow dog screamed again.
The goat said, “Dude, don't look at people with dog eyes. Since time immemorial, people would not be able to live today without us. What's more, there are people in the world who are making money on us — making good money. If you don't believe me, ask Boss Heng Yuanxiang.”
The yellow dog is still shouting, “I rushed them to graze, but it hasn't been reported yet; how can he report it first?”
Ma said, “Don't call the old dog. It's also hard work for you to take care of people and herd. That's one thing. There is no quota limit, so don't worry about being sloppy.”
The scalper finally said, “If the pig did not attend the meeting, that would count as one. Emperor Jade made an account; pigs are representative of a nation.”
After the meeting was adjourned, everyone was happy. Only Little White Rabbit had no nominations. He was timid and afraid to report it. He cried red with blushing eyes after the meeting. She found Mountain Rabbit to discuss it. The two prayed at the top of the mountain on the night of the full moon and called, “Sister Yutu, let's do it!” Yu Tu found out. He was busy looking for Fairy Chang'e and made a fixed quota.
