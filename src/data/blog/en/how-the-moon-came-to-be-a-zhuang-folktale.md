 Once upon a time, we had a beautiful girl named Sister Moon in Zhuangxiang. She planted three grapefruit trees in front of her door. Every time grapefruit is ripe, it is picked and given to everyone to eat.
She has a clever mind. She can tie lanterns, round ones like balls, flat ones like a mill, birds fly, and dance; she can also play sandals, the sandals she plays with red and green ears, which are lightweight and tight.
At that time of year, everything was fine; there was no moon or stars in the sky. As soon as the sun sets, the sky is dark and the ground is black and hemp. If you don't turn on the lights, you won't be able to see anything. People in this world are like being covered in a big iron pot. How uncomfortable should it be!
Sister Moon was curious. She thought, “How great would it be if I could hang a big lantern in the sky and shine as soon as it gets dark! But how can I hang the lanterns in the sky? Where is the way to heaven? She asked her white-bearded father-in-law and her self-haired grandmother, and everyone shook their heads.
Sister Moon thought: Doesn't it mean that the sky is connected to the earth, and the earth is connected to the sky; since they are connected, there must be a path to heaven.
At that time, Moon Sister was only a 17 or 8 year old girl. The 17- and 8-year-old girls looked like red flowers were blooming. When they thought about it, they lost their soul. They turned the lanterns around Moon Mei's house at night, picked her up, and sang to make fun of her, but Sister Moon was not moved; she wanted to take the lantern laurels to the sky.
You can't just think about it; you have to take a trip. Moon Girl is determined to find her way to heaven and hang lanterns in heaven.
Sister Moon went out, and the townspeople came to see her off.
Moon Girl said, “Let's all wait and see! If a lantern hangs up in the sky, I'll go to the sky. You can go to my house to pick grapefruit to eat. Later, my brother can cut firewood to pick water, and the girl can steam sweet cakes. Everyone should live a friendly and friendly life.”
Moon Girl brought three things when she went out. Which three? A large lantern, a straw bow, and a large pocket of incense sesame seeds.
The lanterns are ready to hang in the sky; the straw bow is used to hit the straw shoes and drive; the large bag of sesame seeds is used to remember the days. She believes that once a large bag of sesame is used up, she will definitely find a way to heaven.
This is how Tsukimi went to find her way to heaven.
Huh, go over mountains, cross mountains, water; get out of the sun and rain; keep asking; keep asking; if the sandals are rotten, wear sandals; lose a grain of sesame after a day.
I don't know how many steps she has taken, and I don't know how many days have passed. Her bow of sandals is only half a finger wide, sesame is only half small, and Sister Moon's hair is all white, yet she keeps on rushing. She made up her mind to hang a lantern in the sky to bring light to the night.
One day, she arrived at a strange place and saw that the ocean in front of her was vast, endless, but there was no water or waves in the sea; it was just thick layers of clouds and rolling around.
Sister Moon is so tired that she wants to find a place to rest her feet. It just so happened that there was a big stone on the seaside, round, white, like a big plate.
Sister Moon sat down and rested on this large plate while putting out her sandals and picking up her shoes.
Suddenly, a fresh breeze blew through, and the large plate slowly moved and rotated. Gradually, it drifted into the sea and dived into the clouds.
However, Sister Moon is still holding her head down and wearing sandals; it seems like she hasn't noticed this change.
When Moon Girl finished her sandals, she looked up. Huh, what kind of place is this? The top of the head is empty, and the surrounding area is empty. Look down, oops! Below are mountain rivers, housing fields. Ah, isn't this up in the sky?
Sister Moon was so happy that she immediately lit the lantern. Also, scatter all the sesame seeds around.
Oops! Sister Moon sat on a large plate, illuminating the lantern all over her body. Seen from the ground, the silver, silver and white one turned into a beautiful moon! The sesame floats in the sky and shines brightly in the moonlight, making it a star in the sky.
When the townspeople of Zhuang's hometown saw that a big lantern actually appeared in the sky, there were countless flashing little dots next to it. They knew it had been hung by Sister Moon, so they called it the moon. That day was August 15 in the lunar calendar, and that day became the moon's birthday.
On that day, if you take a close look at the moon, don't you think there's a girl playing sandals? However, now people don't call her Sister Moon, but Grandma Moon.
Later, everyone in the Zhuang family followed Yuemei's previous instructions. Whichever family had grapefruits, they all distributed them to everyone to eat. However, there are also petty people who don't want to separate their grapefruit. As a result, Zhuangxiang set a rule. On August 15, you can steal grapefruit and give it to everyone; it's not illegal; whoever can go to the rich owner's house to steal grapefruit is even the most competent.
