 The little bunny is lying on the grass, comfortably basking in the sun. Suddenly, a giant wolf and a fox came running in. The big duke showed his long teeth and said, “Little bunny, we're hungry and close to our backs; you can bask in comfort here. This is so unfair. Come get us a snack soon, okay?”
As soon as the little bunny heard it, it was too frightened. He hurriedly cried to Grand Duke Wolf: “Grandpa, look, I'm so thin, it's not as good as a kid's fist...”
The fox placed its paw on the bunny's back and smiled and said, “Little bunny, don't be kind. As the saying goes: Even though rabbits are small, they taste great! Big Brother Wolf, drink tea while it's hot, eat meat while it's fresh, let's do it!”
“Slow! Slow!” The rabbit jumped hard and got into the stingwood and said, “Master Wolf, Sister Fox, someone in the village invited me to drink happy wine tonight. Wait until I have had happy wine and then let you eat me, OK?”
“Drink a happy drink? What is drinking happy wine?” The wolf and the fox put down their claws at the same time and asked questions together. They thought this was pretty fresh.
“Are you drinking happy wine?” The little bunny slowly came out of the thorns, crouched on a small pile of dirt, and introduced them in a very good mood: “To drink happy wine is to attend a wedding! Singing, dancing, drinking, and eating meat, it's really the most interesting place in the world.”
The wolf and the fox discussed it and agreed to eat it again tomorrow. Tonight it will lead them to drink happy wine. The rabbit hurriedly answered, “OK! OK!”
In the evening, rabbits brought foxes and wolves to the house where the wedding was being held. They drilled through a gutter into the yard, then jumped from a small window into a warehouse containing barley wine and meat. The wolf, the fox, and the little bunny sat on the window sill, and saw many, many lights lit up in the yard, surrounded by many, many people. They offered haddahs and toasts to the groom and bride, and others were singing, dancing, and performing Tibetan shows. It was very lively.
The little bunny said, “It's not interesting to just look at it; let's have a wedding too!” The wolf and the fox asked, “How is it going to be held?” The little bunny said, “Mr. Wolf is powerful and powerful as the groom, and the sister Fox is young Peugeot as the bride. My little bunny has no money and power, so it would be nice to be a host.” With that said, wolves and foxes are very good at it. The rabbit picked up a broken sieve and put it on a male wolf's head, which is considered a “Sosha” hat; it also found a big cake, gnawed a hole in the middle, and placed it around the fox's neck as a gold necklace. I had a small fritter hanging from my ear, saying it was the host's earring.
After the wedding, the bunny announced, “Let's have a drink!” It opened the bar and invited the bride and groom to drink. Wolves and foxes have never drank anything this good, so they rushed to stick their heads into the wine table and drink it recklessly. By the time the bottle of wine had run out, both the wolf and the fox were so drunk that they were all over. As for the little bunnies, in order to be polite, they only wet their beards with alcohol.
After drinking enough, the little bunny shouted again, “Let's sing!” When I finished speaking, I sang “heheheh” myself first. The fox was intoxicated and screamed strangely. The old wolf jumped up and down in the warehouse, howled in his mouth, and let out a burst of smell from his butt, making a mess.
People attending the wedding didn't know who was making a lot of noise in the warehouse. They ran over to take a look. It turned out to be wolves and foxes. Some of them were holding wooden sticks, and some were armed with long knives, and surrounded the warehouse. The little bunny saw a bad situation. After “miso”, he jumped out of the window and ran away. The wolf and the fox also jumped on the window sill. However, the wolf had a big sieve on its head, and the fox had a big cake on its neck. They couldn't get out at all, and they were beaten to death.
