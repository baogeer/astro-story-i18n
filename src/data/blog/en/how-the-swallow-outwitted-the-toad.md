 One day, the toad took a bath in the North River, and a swallow flew in. Its wings were stained in the water, and it kept singing in its mouth.
The toad was very envious, and also very jealous, and jokingly said, “Brother Swallow, is it strange these past two days?” As soon as Swallow saw that it was a toad talking to him, he ignored it. Toad is a little annoyed: “Why, you don't look down on people? Do you think the clothes I'm wearing are broken and have patches on my body? That's better than yours too. Although you are well-dressed, with black silk and black satin, the family lives in one house, and they don't like to wash clothes all day long. Even though we wear patches, we take a bath a few times a day”. As soon as Swallow heard the toad harass itself, he was furious, but it didn't want to suffer from this loss, so he thought it was furious. It flew around on the surface of the water and dug up the bitter toad and said, “Dude Toad, you are really clean; it's not easy. There's one thing I don't understand: Why isn't anyone else making a sound like you, “Whoa,” why is that?” As soon as the toad heard it, it praised it and said, “You don't know this. I scream loudly. My stomach, thick neck, and mouth are so loud.” Swallow laughed and said, “Can you scream when your mouth and throat are thick? The tea basket that sells tea leaves has a thick stomach, mouth, and neck. Why hasn't it screamed all day?” The toad hummed in anger: “Do you understand? Isn't that bamboo?” The swallow said, “Isn't the bamboo's cry?” The toad said, “Can a bamboo make a noise?” The swallow said, “The flute is also made of bamboo. Why is it screaming as soon as it blows?” The toad said, “You mentioned that bamboo has eyes.” The swallow said, “If you have eyes, it makes a noise? That bamboo sieve isn't clean-eyed, why isn't it loud at all?” The toad bulged its stomach twice and said, “I told you, the bamboo sieve is round.” The swallow said, “The round one won't ring? Isn't the gong you hit also round? Why is “booming” louder than you shouting? The toad was so angry that it couldn't be said: “The gong he hit, the gong he hit, he had a “navel,” and that was the sound of the “navel.” Swallow said, “Doesn't that iron pot also have a “navel”; why doesn't it ring again?” The toad said, “It's made of iron; iron doesn't sound as good as copper.” Swallow said, “Iron doesn't sound as good as bronze. Isn't the bell that the monk in the temple knocked on all iron? Why is it still so loud?” The toad said, “Did you say that clock didn't rise?” The swallow said, “When you hang it up, it makes a noise? When you hang up that weigher, why doesn't it make a sound?” The toad was furious and said, “If you don't knock, say it's too small.” Swallow said, “It's small. The whistle that blows in the school isn't even smaller. Why is it still so loud when you blow it?” The toad said, “That's amazing.” Swallow said, “Impressive? When a car tire is full of air, why doesn't it make a noise at all?” I held back a toad: “That, that...” As soon as I worked hard, my stomach “fluttered” and exploded.
