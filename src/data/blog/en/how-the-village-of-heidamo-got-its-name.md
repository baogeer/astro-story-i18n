 Across from Xiatuan Mountain, where the ancient rock paintings on the western slope of Cangshan Mountain are located, in the middle of the mountainside on the south bank of the Yangliang River, there is a beautiful small mountain village called “Black Touch” shaded by greenery. There is an ancient local legend about the origin of the strange village name “Black Touch.”
Legend has it that a long time ago, the place Heidatou was not called Heidatou. Of course, not many people are clear about what it was called back then. But back then, even though it was a small mountain village with beautiful mountains and clear water, the landscape was very unfavorable. Crops are always a poor source of yellow disease, wet disease, and chicken fever continues one after another. What saddens the villagers the most is that it is always difficult for the dolls in the village to prosper. Almost five or six of the ten dolls often die before the age of eight or nine.
One day, the villagers, living in extreme distress and fear, suddenly saw an old man wearing crutches with a dragon head and a white beard. Standing under a large green tree at the head of the village, he pointed to the other side of the Yangjiao River with his crutches and said to the sad villagers, “Why don't you see any cow incidents all year round? There are so many natural and man-made disasters. Isn't that because the white tiger on Mount Daimen is acting strange? Everyone looked in the direction he was pointing, and couldn't help but be shocked, because they saw a white tiger on Xiatuan Mountain at the foot of Cangshan Mountain across the river, grinning at the village. Of course, when I rubbed my eyes and looked closely, I discovered that the white tiger turned out to be nothing more than a silver-white ash stone the size of a large dustpan, that is, the kind of stone used to burn lime.
The villagers hurriedly knelt down and knocked at the old man, begging him to show me a way to solve it. The old man touched his long white beard on his chest and groaned for half a minute before slowly saying, “It's not that there aren't any solutions; it's just a bit cumbersome.” Everyone is anxious to ask him what exactly is the method? Only then did the old man slowly tell the crowd that, in fact, this method is relatively easy to talk about; it just requires persistence year after year. This means that every year after the spring season, you can choose a dark night, send three or four young people to bring a pot of tobacco mixed with sesame oil or something, quietly climb over the river to Diamen Mountain, and paint that burnt stone black. But you must remember that you must not damage that stone in any way; otherwise, an even greater disaster will befall. Remember, remember!” Afterwards, the old man suddenly mysteriously disappeared without a trace.
Only then did the villagers learn that it turned out that the old man was not an ordinary person; he was probably a fairy master who came specifically to give them tips and tricks. Since then, the village has followed the instructions of the elderly and sent people to the other side of the river every year to paint this tiger-shaped burnt stone on Xiatuan Mountain black. Strangely enough, after hitting this, as long as the burnt stone was painted black, the soil and water will not only be smooth in the wind and rain, and the crops will be well harvested, but people and animals will be fine and safe. In particular, the dolls have had a good handle. Over time, people called this village a “black scum.” Since local accents are generally indistinguishable from “smelling” and “touching,” “dark smudging” slowly became known as “dark touching.” It is said that this kind of activity continued until the eve of the “Cultural Revolution.”
