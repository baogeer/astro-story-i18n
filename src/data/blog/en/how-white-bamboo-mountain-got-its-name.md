 Mount Hakuchu got its name because, according to legend, there are three silver white bamboo plants at the top of the mountain. According to local folklore, if you look up along these three towering white bamboo shoots inserted directly into the sky, you can see a heavenly wonderland. Unfortunately, appreciating the beauty of white bamboo has always been unattainable. These three mysterious white bamboo plants are said to have originally been the treasure of Mount Jinshan in Yongping. I don't know what year or generation. A high priest competed with a tiger in the mountains to fight for the Baotai Mountain method. After three days and three nights of competition, the giant tiger, which fell to the ground on three legs, had to pick up the white bamboo from the mountain and hurriedly flee east in the night. When it dawned, Shenhu found itself standing on a majestic mountain peak from Wanshan. It let go of the white bamboo in its mouth and set up camp again in this rich Feng Shui treasure. This mountain is today's Baizhu Mountain. According to local people, until now, every year when the first day of the Lunar New Year, many Yi people live in villages down the mountain and still see a pair of blue, quiet lights appear from time to time in the dense forest at the top of Baizhu Mountain. It is said that these are the eyes of Chaoshan tigers.
