 The oral literature “The Origin of Wrestling” is an oral literary work that is popular in Sani Shanzhai, Shilin Yi Autonomous County, Kunming City. The wrestling activities of the Sani people have a long history, with a history of hundreds of years. So far, wrestling and bullfighting are one of the main activities of the “Torch Festival” in Shilin.
Legend has it that a long time ago, a Han brother fled to Sani due to the panic of the soldiers in his hometown and the destruction of his home. He met and met a shepherd boy and became a brother worshiper. They ate and stayed together, and went up the mountain together to herd cows and sheep.
I herded cows in the mountains one day. Suddenly, a cow grazed, didn't drink water, and was standing still motionless. It got grass caries disease, and their stomachs were bloated and bulging, hurting the Yi and Han brothers in a hurry. They got smart in a hurry and found their own way to find herbal medicine and smash it into paste to feed the cow disease. Soon after, the cow pulled a bubble of poop, flung its tail, and came back eating. The two brothers happily hugged each other's necks and rolled straight on the ground with excitement. I was seen by an old man who thought they were fighting and asked why. The old man said, “Cows are the lifeblood of crop farmers. Cows are well sick, and it is worth being happy and celebrated. Who are you two stronger than?” The brothers said they would decide whether to win or lose. Otherwise, I wouldn't know which one had a lot of strength after a day of falling. The old man said, “OK, then lose by falling back on your back.” Three fights and two wins are a win. As a result, they fell and wrestled over and over again, so much to the point that they couldn't help but agree.
After a while, the Han brother's military disaster subsided. He went home. Afterwards, the Sunny brothers went to their Han brother's house to celebrate their meeting by wrestling. The Han brother also came to the Sonny brothers house. The Sunny brothers entertained him with buckwheat dipped in honey. In this way, they invited the whole village to watch his brothers wrestle.
In this way, wrestling has become a traditional sports activity for the Sani people.
The origin of wrestling is a good story about the Han people being linked to the Sani people of the Yi ethnic group and are as close as brothers. More importantly, “wrestling” is a traditional form of national sport that has continued to this day and continues to flourish.
