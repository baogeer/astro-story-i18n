 Huo Gubing (140-117 BC), a famous Western Han general, from Pingyang, Hedong (present-day Linfen, Shanxi), is the nephew of General Wei Qing, and is good at riding and shooting. At the age of 17, he was the servant of Emperor Han Wu. In the same year, he accompanied Wei Qing as Captain Yao, and led 800 elite riders to drive hundreds of miles to raid the rear of the Huns, kill 2028 Huns, capture Sang Guo and Dangdang of the Huns, and kill the grandfather and Ji Father of the Huns, who won the championship.
In the spring of the 2nd year of Yuanzari (121 BC), Emperor Han Wu appointed Huo Gubing as General Piaoqi and led 10,000 cavalry troops out of Longxi to attack King Youxian of the Xiong. He defeated the five kingdoms of the Huns for 6 days in a row. Next, they crossed Yanzhi Mountain for more than 1,000 miles, fought with the Huns under Gaolan Mountain, annihilated nearly 9,000 enemies, killed King Lu Hou and King Zhilan of the Hunzhe, and captured Prince Hunzhe, Xiang Guo, and many Captain Lieutenants. In the summer of the same year, Huo Gubing led tens of thousands of riders out of Beidi County, crossed Juyan Sea, fought fiercely with the Huns at the foot of the Qilian Mountains, annihilated more than 30,000 enemies, captured 5 Hungarian kings and more than 120 people, including Wang Mu, Shan Yu, Prince, Xiang Guo, and General, and surrendered to the Hunevil King of the Huns and 40,000 people, all occupying the Hexi Corridor. The Huns sang about this: “Losing my Qilian Mountains made my six animals unable to rest; losing me, Zhishan, made my wife colorless.”
In the spring of the 4th year of Yuangari (119 BC), Emperor Han Wu ordered Wei Qing and Huo Gubing, and each led 50,000 cavalry to Dingxiang and Dingxiang to go deep into northern Fujian to find the main forces of the Huns. Huo Koubing led his army more than 2,000 miles north, crossed Mount Hou, crossed the Gonglu River, and fought against King Zuo Xian of the Huns, annihilated 70,400 enemies, captured 3 people including King Tuntou and Han of the Huns, and 83 people including General, Sang-guk, Danghu, and Captain, and took advantage of the victory to hunt them down to Langjuxushan (present-day Mongolia), and held a sacred ceremony at Mount Guyan. The military forces pushed all the way to the Han Sea (present-day Lake Baikal). As a result of this war, “the Huns fled far away, and there was no royal court in Monan.” The cause of Huo Gou's illness was credited to Master Sima. His prestige surpassed Wei Qing, and many members of the Ministry of Health and Youth switched to Huo Gou's disease one after another.
In the 6th year of Genkari (117 BC), Huo Gou died of illness in Chang'an. He was only 23 years old. Emperor Han Wu made a special order to bury Maoling and built his tomb in the shape of the majestic Qilian Mountains. Large original stone carvings such as “Mata the Huns” were displayed in front of the tomb in recognition of his great achievements during his expedition to the Huns, and he was sealed as Jinghuanhou. On the day of his burial, the Hunnic generals, dressed in black and black armor, formed an army for tens of miles on both sides of the road from Chang'an to Maoling to be buried.
Huo Guying led an army against the Huns four times in his life, and all returned with great victory, annihilated more than 110,000 Huns, surrendered more than 40,000 Huns, and opened up the land of Hexi and Jiuquan, eliminating the threat the Huns posed to the Han dynasty. He fought bravely. He was a military genius. Emperor Han Wu once advised him to learn Sun Wu's art of war. He answered, “In order to be able to plan at any time, why must ancient laws be held back?” Emperor Han Wu built a luxurious mansion for his military exploits, but he said, “The Huns are immortal; they have no family.” Descendants highly admired Huo's illness. Li Bai's “Hu Wuren” poem said, “Yan Feng blew frost and seaweed, and Hu Ma was proud. There were 300,000 Han soldiers, and Huo Xuyao was the general and leader. A meteor white feather is inserted between the waist, and the sword flower autumn lotus light comes out of the box. Tianmen shouted under the snow in Yuguan, and captured arrows like sandblazing gold armor. Yunlong, Wind, and Tiger have returned, and Taibai's enemies will be destroyed when they enter the Moon. Enemies are destructible, and their heads are destroyed, and their bowels are covered in blood. Hang Hu Qing in the sky and bury Hu Zi in the middle. Hu Wuren, Han Daochang.”
