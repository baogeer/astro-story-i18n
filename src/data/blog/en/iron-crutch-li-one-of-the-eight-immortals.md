 Tieguai Li is also known as Li Tiejiao. Other names include Li Ningyang, Li Hongshui, Li Kongmu, Li Xuan, Li Yue, and Li Yuanzhong. There are also many opinions about the year he was born. It is generally believed that there are no real people in history; their true appearance and actions all come from legends. Legend has it that he was originally a talented young man. After entering the mountains, he once visited Western countries with Lao Jun and Wanqiu. Before leaving, he ordered his apprentice Yang Zi to keep his spirit (body), and agreed not to return for 7 days before incinerating it. They didn't expect it until the sixth day. Some people in Yang Zi's family rushed to make an emergency and said that his mother was in critical condition and just wait to see him. In the midst of hesitation, Yang Zi was unable to withstand the urging of her family, and finally incinerated Li Xuan's body. After Li Xuan's soul traveled west with Lao Jun, he returned to the mountain where he practiced for 7 days and went to Maozhai to find his soul. Only to see that his hair was gone, and his apprentice Yang Zi didn't know where he was going. Lee Hyun's wandering spirit wandered everywhere. Calling out of thin air day and night, he happened to encounter a hungry dead body lying upside down in the mountains. At this point, Lee Hyun suddenly remembered the old monarch's words when he left: “If you want an old body, just meet a new face”, he realized “this hungry corpse has no face.” As a result, no longer a horn, and no longer wandering around, it started with a hungry corpse. Since then, the burly and handsome teenager has changed to a pompous, unscrupulous, and beggar with purple crutches. Lee Hyun, who raised his body, was able to change at will. The crutches in his hand can be recognized as an iron cane. The world no longer knows his real name; they only loudly call him “Mr. Iron Kai,” and later more directly called him “Iron Guy.”
After Li Xuan became an “iron kidnapper,” she learned that her apprentice Yang Zi's mother had died, and she couldn't help but reflect and ask herself, “He who kept my dead body until the end was forced by my mother. The one whose mother died and didn't give up is exhausting for me. If I don't die and come back to life, he will hate him for the rest of his life!” The grievance turned into a heavy sense of sympathy, so he carried crutches and carried a gourd on his shoulder to the Yang family. He took a pill of the Necromancer taught by a stranger from the gourd, saved Yang Zi's old mother, clarified her identity, and cleared the wind.
