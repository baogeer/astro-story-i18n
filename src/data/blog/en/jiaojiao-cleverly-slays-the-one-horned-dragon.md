 There is Shangzhangjia Village in the east of Dinghai City. There is a 100-meter-high Lion Mountain at the back of the village, and there is a natural ancient cave in southern Shannan. The local people collectively call it the “Unicorn Dragon Cave.” Legend has it that there used to be a unicorn wrapped around this hole. It was very vicious and cruel. He also often turned into a beautiful man and broke into houses in the middle of the night to make a monster.
This unicorn was originally the youngest son of the Dragon King in the East China Sea. He was very ugly, but he was also very fond of looking for flowers and asking Willow. Some of the beautiful fish girls and shrimp girls in the aquarium were afraid to die when they saw him. Later, he felt that he couldn't find the girl he liked in the aquarium, so he came to a place called Shangzhangjiacun and became a white-faced scholar and made fun of the girls everywhere.
One day, he passed by Lion Rock and arrived at a bun shop at the foot of the mountain, so he took a break and bought a few cages of buns to fill his stomach. This bun shop was opened by a widow surnamed Lee. Widow Lee is over 30 years old. She has no in-laws and no children. She spends her time selling buns alone. Seeing that she was very beautiful, Unicorn visited the store every day to buy buns to eat. While eating buns, they also chatted with Widow Lee.
Over time, Widow Lee was seduced and turned into a unicorn dragon's head. Unicorn didn't want to go back to Dragon Palace either. He found a cave on the south side of Lion Rock. He slept well in the cave during the day, and sneaked into the village at night to hang out with Widow Lee. Later, Widow Lee slowly grew old and deteriorated, and Unicorns began to snub her, leaving her in the village to find new love.
Once, a girl named Zhao Jiaojiao passed by Zhangjiacun. This girl has learned good martial arts. She runs through rivers and lakes, travels south and north, and specializes in chivalry. After a day of walking, she was tired and hungry, so she went to the store to buy buns to fill her hunger. It just so happened that I ran into a unicorn in the store.
When the unicorn saw that it was petite, young and beautiful, its mouth dragged three feet long. He changed and looked like a second-year shopkeeper. He delivered a hot package of buns for Widow Lee, and secretly sprinkled a pack of sweat medicine into the bun. Jiao Jiao's stomach was so hungry that she ate it when she caught it. After some effort, she ate a perfect basket of buns, but she also fell asleep in a daze. The unicorn was not happy; he picked up Jiao Jiao and left. Back in the cave, the unicorn showed its true form. It blew a breath of cool breeze at Jiao Jiao with a big smile and shouted, “Wake up girl, wake up girl!”
Jiao Jiao yawned, opened her eyes, and was surrounded by a black monster. Seeing that she was at a loss, she asked loudly, “What are you? Dare to fool the girl!”
When the Unicorn woke up, the hippie smiled and said:
“Don't be afraid, girl. I am the 7th Prince of the Dragon King of the East China Sea. You and I are related in this life. When a girl enters my cave, she has endless glory and wealth!”
After all, Jiao Jiao is a woman who has broken through rivers and lakes and seen the world. After listening to this monster's words, she was afraid, but on the surface she was very calm. She thought that since she was in a daze, it would be impossible to get out of there all of a sudden. Even though I learned martial arts myself, I had my grandfather Jinbao on me. However, this monster is full of iron-scaled iron armor, and the Golden Dart is hard to break its irony. Right now, all you have to do is slowly find an opportunity to subdue the dragon. “The Seven Fairies of Heaven aren't afraid to break the rules of heaven. If I dare to get married to Mortal Dong Yong, can't I, Prince Long, marry a mortal woman as my wife?”
