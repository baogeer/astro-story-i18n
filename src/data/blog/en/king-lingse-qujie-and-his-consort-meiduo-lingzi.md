 Once upon a time, in the Chalong Ding region, there was a king named Ling Xue Qu Jie. He has a beautiful concubine named Mei Duolingzi. The king's older brother, Aku, is very fond of the beauty of his concubine Mei Duolingzi and always wants to beat her ideas.
At one point, the princess left Charongding to visit her parents in her hometown. Within a few days, Aku hid her younger brother Ling's twists and turns to pick Mei Duolingzi back to the palace. The concubine didn't know if it was fake. She quickly packed up her luggage; she followed Aku on her horse. On the way they passed, there was a very dangerous mountain, and many ferocious humans and bears lived on the mountain (the Tibetan word for “zemo”, which is said to be an old book). Aku smiled and said, “Princess, it's almost dark, so I had to spend the night here. Are you sleeping with me tonight? Or do you sleep with humans and bears?”
When the concubine heard it, she was frightened and shuddered like a leaf. She knelt down and prayed, “Akuhaku, I can't live with you, nor can I live with humans and bears. Let's just hurry and go back to the Charongding area! The twists and turns of King's Ridge are waiting for us! Abatana Rochin is waiting for us! Grandma Bai Meiming is waiting for us!” Seeing that Mei Duolingzi disobeyed his intentions, Aku pushed her to the edge of a human bear cave and walked away with a horse in tow.
When Mei Duolingzi climbed up from the ground, she met a scary adult bear like a devil. When she saw the beautiful concubine, she broke her big mouth and laughed strangely, and also danced a swaying dance. Mei Duolingzi had never seen this situation before, and passed out on the spot. However, instead of eating her, humans and bears took her into a cave, became their own wife, and took very careful and thoughtful care of her. It's just that every time people and bears go out looking for food, they always have to move a few large stones to block the cave, fearing that the girl will run away. Later, Mei Duolingzi became pregnant and gave birth to two little bears. The big bear screams so much that they don't block doors with big rocks anymore.
Once, a merchant in Chalong Ding passed by here and saw a naked woman with long hair wrapped all over her body, climbing like a wild beast to drink water. He was taken aback at first. When he took a closer look, he felt very much like Princess Mei Duolingzi. They kept rushing day and night to report this unfortunate news to King's Ridge in twists and turns. When the king heard the merchant's report, he was surprised and saddened. He rode a fast horse alone, carrying a knife, musket, chopsticks, and table salt, and rushed to Renxiong Mountain to save his concubine. He hid in a big tree and saw the big bear coming out of the cave and the little bear coming out of the cave. After a while, his concubine Mei Duolingzi also came out of the cave. Then she jumped off the tree and sprinkled salt and pickles while walking; Mei Duolingzi followed and ate salt and chopsticks. They arrived at a secluded place, and Ling Se-qu sang in unison:
Please listen to it.
Princess Mei Duolingzi,
Can't you remember,
Is it our velvet top?
Can't you remember,
Is that Abaddin?
Can't you remember,
Does Grandma have many white beauties?
Can't you remember,
Are the colors of Wangling Mountain in our country crooked?
After a while, Mei Duolingmei shook her head and sang:
Our place at the top of Chalong,
I really can't remember.
Abatana Rochin,
I really can't remember.
Grandma has many white beauties,
I really can't remember.
King's Ridge twists and turns,
I really can't remember.
When I finished singing, I went back to the human bear cave like a wild beast. King's Ridge was full of twists and turns, and he hid in a big tree early the next morning. He knows that when Mei Duolingzi eats salt and pickles, she can think of past events and speak people's languages. He also sprinkled snow-white salt, sprinkled scented pickles, led the concubine to a secluded place, and sang yesterday's song again. After a while, Mei Duolingzi had a painful expression on her face and tears swirling in her eyes. She answered:
Our place at the top of Chalong,
I'm reminded a little bit;
Abatana Rochin,
I'm reminded a little bit;
Grandma has many white beauties,
I'm reminded a little bit;
King's Ridge twists and turns,
I'm reminded a little bit.
My relentless brother Akku,
They left me at the edge of a human bear cave;
A full year and two years,
I haven't had a human life anymore.
The king was very sad to hear this; he shed sad tears while holding his poor concubine. The concubine told him that as the sky slowly gets dark, people and bears will return to the cave. If we run into humans or bears, our lives would all be over. You go up the mountain again tomorrow morning, bring a set of clothes, two horses, and pick me up and Little Bear. The concubine also prayed bitterly for King's Ridge not to kill that big bear.
The next day, the king brought clothes and two horses to pick up his concubine. The concubine wanted to walk with the two little people and bears. The king said, “Your horse doesn't have good foot strength, so get on and go! My horse is fat and has strong legs, and then I brought the little bear.” The king walked to the center of the river on horseback. Taking advantage of the concubine's carelessness, he pulled out a knife from his waist and killed Little Bear. The king walked to the other side on horseback. It just happened that the big bear chased it down from the mountain. While running, it tore up the trees it met along the way and yelled in a horrible voice: “My girl! My girl!”
The king and his wife, Mei Duolingzi, returned to Chalong Ding. Ordinary people learned about this incident, and they all said that the Akubi bear was still ruthless. A swarm of bees rushed to the royal palace and beat him to death.
