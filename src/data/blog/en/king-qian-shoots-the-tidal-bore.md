Therefore, the dykes on both sides of the Qiantang River are always repaired on this side, and then destroyed on that side. “The Yellow River repairs one bucket of gold every day, and the Qianjiang River heals one bucket of silver every day.” At that time, you can imagine the disaster that the tide brought to the people from this sentence.
By the end of the Tang dynasty, there was a Wu Yue Wang called Qian Yi, who was extremely brave. At the time, most people called him “King Qian.”
When Qian Wang governed Hangzhou, he felt that everything was still easy to do; that is, the Qiantang River seawall was not well repaired. Because it has just been repaired, the tide comes twice a day and night, which simply makes it impossible for people to build a seawall. As a result, Qian Wang's subordinates were anxious; they were all afraid that Qian Wang would lose his temper. They had no choice but to report that Qian Wang said, “Dawang, this seawall is still not going to be repaired; it's never going to be repaired. Because there is a tidal god in the Qiantang River who is against us, we only waited until we had the seawall repaired, and he made waves, raised the tide, and knocked down our seawall.”
Qian Wang was full of anger when he heard it. He was so angry that his beard grew straight. His eyes glared like bronze balls, and shouted loudly:
“Bark! You useless bastards! Why don't you drag that god of tide up to slaughter?”

August 18 arrived, and a giant tower was set up along the Qiantang River. Qian Wang went to the stage early in the morning to watch the movement and wait for the god of tide to arrive. However, the 10,000 elite archers selected from the local area came down one after another. When Qian Wang saw that they were reluctant, they drank so they had to immediately gather at the riverside and line up their positions.
