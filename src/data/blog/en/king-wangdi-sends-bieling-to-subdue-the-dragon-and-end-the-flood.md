 In other words, the ancestor of Shu Wang of the Qiang tribe was called Silkworm Cong. His next generation was called Bai Yi, and the next generation was called Yu Wei. They lived for three generations as long as hundreds of years, and later became gods and lived forever.
Their people also tend to deify themselves with them. Yu Fu became a god when hunting in Yulei Mountain, and now there is also a temple dedicated to him in Yulei Mountain, and people often sacrifice him. At that time, Shudi was sparsely populated. Later, a man named Du Yu fell from heaven to Zhu Ti; another woman came out of the well at the source of the Yangtze River and married him as his wife. As a result, he established himself as King Shu, known as Emperor Wang, and built all of them in Piji under Mount Ru. People who have been deified in the past have often come to the world again. After Emperor Wang was in power for more than 100 years, the Chu nation had a personal name called Yiling. He accidentally fell into the water and died. His body was lost and disappeared, and the Chu people searched seven and eight times to find it. It turned out that his corpse had flowed backwards into the river and floated to Pidi. He came back to life and met King Wang. Emperor Wang admired him and appointed him prime minister.
At that time, Yulei Mountain was flooded. Like the terrible floods in Yaoshi, Emperor Wang was unable to manage it. Emperor Wang then sent him to excavate Mount Yulei to vent the floods, and only then were people able to live safely.
Dragons fought against each other in Wushan. They gambled on the Yangtze River. The river couldn't flow down and flooded the people of Shudi. After Yu Ling went to fix the water, Emperor Wang had an affair with his wife. Later, Emperor Wang felt ashamed and thought he had bad character and was not as good as Turtle Spirit, so he relinquished the throne to him and left on his own...
Turtle Ling took over the throne and became known as Emperor Kaiming. After Emperor Wang left, he wanted to return to power again, but when he was unable to do so, he had no choice but to die and became a cuckoo. The cuckoo continued to cry sadly until its beak bleed. When the cuckoo called, it bleed its beak and dyed its beak red. Blood dripped on a type of flower and dyed it red, so people called it an azalea.
