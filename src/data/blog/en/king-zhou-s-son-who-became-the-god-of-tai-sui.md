So, is Wu Geng the prototype of Yin Jiao? Not at all.
The name “Yin Jiao” is actually not a name, but a place name. “Yin Jiao” is also a name we are familiar with, that is, “Makino” from the Battle of Muye in Shang and Zhou. It refers to the suburb of Chaoge, the capital city of the Shang dynasty, in the Xinxiang area of present-day Henan. Zhou Chu's literature “Shang Shu · Shepherd's Oath” says, “When the Jiazi dynasty is concerned, Muye, the dynasty,” refers to Jiazi Day, Wu Wang led Zhou Jun to Muye in the suburbs of Shangjiao; the Sengoku document “Lu Shi Chunqiu” also said that Wu Wang went to Lushui, and Minister Jiao Wei of the Chinese dynasty came to spy on the intelligence. Wu Wang told him “Jiazi will take Jiazi to the outskirts of Shangjiao”, in other words, the Zhou dynasty's army will arrive in the suburbs of Shangdu.
The Battle of Makino took place on Koshi Day, and was first recorded in the bronze “Rikan” at the beginning of the week. Li Gui said that King Wu Fayi. That day was Jiazi Day. The day was full of stars, and Zhou Jun invaded the commercial capital. By the time of Xin Weili, King Wu had nothing to say about his merits and gave the meritorious Ushilimetal. The “year star” here is Jupiter. In ancient times, the “age star age method” was popular, which means that the age was determined by the position of the star (Jupiter) in the sky. The year star rotates one week in 11.86. The ancients divided the sky into the zodiac. Wherever the year star moved, it was called the year by which position. “Mandarin” says, “King Wu Fayin; when you are old, you live in the fire,” and the guzzling fire is one of the twelve signs.
However, the one-week rotation of the year star is 11.86, which is not a complete 12 years. In order to overcome this deviation, the ancients fictitious a star with the opposite trajectory of the year star. It runs for exactly 12 years. This is the origin of “Taisui.” During the Warring States period, there was also a saying that being too old was taboo. The Sengoku document “Xun Zi” says, “King Wu's death was also used as a military rule on the day of the act, and he welcomed too old in the east.” Originally, King Wu was only born in the middle of heaven on that day, but during the Warring States period, it was spread that Wu Wang Fayi was a taboo day for soldiers; however, the year star used as an era was also misrepresented as Taisei, a symbol of disaster.
The combination of “Yin Jiao” and “Taisui” gave the image of Prince Yin Jiao.
From “Taisai” to Prince Prince
There is a popular saying called “too old to break ground”, which describes confrontation beyond one's control. It can be seen that “Taisui” is a very powerful star. According to the Ming Dynasty's “Yongle Festival” quoting the Tang Dynasty's “Four Ming Jing”, a person named Yao Bao soared up in the sun in the middle of the day, later reincarnated as Prince Liu, and was later crowned Taisui by the Emperor. He became “the first lord to respect gods at home and abroad” and “manage the gods within the world.” It can be seen that Taisui God has a high status. But in this story, Taisui God's name is Yao Bao.

The Shang dynasty did have a country named “ghosts,” which was the ancient country “ghost side” in the Shanxi region. “Ghost side” is called a “ghost”. Naturally, it has nothing to do with ghosts; it is a derogatory term for merchants. According to “Zhou Yi,” Shang Wang Wu Ding attacked the devil side and only subdued them in three years. However, according to Oracle records, the devil side was more submissive to the Shang dynasty, and even some ethnic people joined the Shang dynasty to serve as close ministers of the Shang Wang. This is probably the origin of the “ghost lord.” Entering the Zhou dynasty, the devil side was called Chidi (ghost).
