 In the past, there was a famous Lang Zhong on the side of the city in Zhaoxin County (present-day Mingguang Nushanhu Town). He is said to be a descendant of Kong Sheng. He is said to be a descendant of Kong Sheng. He specializes in treating injuries from falls. No one knew about it for a hundred miles, and there was an endless stream of people seeking medical treatment. Kong Yi is over half a hundred years old. Anyone with a leg injury has personally visited his home for treatment. He is particular about medical treatment fees: rich households pay enough for medicine; poor people don't pay a cent for medical treatment. As a result, Kong and his entourage have been medics for half their lives, and their family is still not well-off, and they eat vegetarian food all day long.
One day, Kong Yi went home from the doctor. Passing through the market in the middle of the city, he saw an Orion selling his prey. It was an injured giant geese. When Kong Yi stopped to look at the giant geese, the giant geese sang its wounded wings and cried, and tears still broke down in his eyes. The hunter recognized Kong Yi. When he saw Kong, he saw that the wild goose was busy and said, “Kong Lang really wanted to take this giant goose home. It's so fat.” Kong Yi couldn't help but touch his bag. The hunter mentioned the injured goose: “In Kong Lang, you don't have to pay; I gave it to you. You didn't have to pay for it last year. You treated my child's leg injury for nothing; I still lack your kindness.”
Kong brought home the injured giant geese. His experience was that he had hurt his left wing and was pierced several times by iron sand. The main bone was not broken, and he was able to recover after a few days of treatment. As a result, Kong Yi changed medicine for Geese every day to heal his wounds. Sure enough, it didn't take a few days for the wound to heal, and I was able to spread my wings. Early this morning, Kong Yitte bought soybeans to feed the geese. While feeding them, he said, “Big Geese, your wounds have healed. After eating these soybeans, go find your house.” After eating the soybeans, the giant geese flapped its wings and shed tears. Kong Yi didn't know what was going on, and asked Dayan, “How are you? Can't you fly?” The giant geese understood Kong Yi's words and shook his head. Finally, it pecked a gray feather on its wing and delivered it to Kong. Kong then said, “Are you leaving this as a memorial for me?” The giant geese still shook his head, grabbed the feather in his mouth and hung around Kong Yi's ear, then screamed a few times. Kong was taken aback when he heard it. He understood that the giant geese were saying, “Thank you to the kind person. I'm going to the south to find my loved ones, and I won't be back until next spring. I'll give you this feather. Please keep it with you. You can understand the language of birds as long as you hang it around you.” After speaking, they circled over Kong's family before they reluctantly left.
Since then, Kong has carried that feather with him every day when he visits the clinic. Sometimes when he encounters a group of birds “chirping”, he hangs the feather on his ear. He often hears birds chatting and talking about love and family life. One day, Kong Yi came back from the clinic. He passed by a haystack and saw many birds shouting at the grass heap. He hung a feather in his ear. It doesn't matter if he doesn't listen; I was shocked when I heard it. The birds said there would be a big storm three days later. The torrential rain was mixed with hail. The hail was as big as a goose egg, and the flat ground could accumulate four to five inches thick. Kong Yi went home and told his wife what the bird had said. The lady said she would buy some grass soon to spread the thick layer of grass on the tiles, and informed the people around her. Everyone else is laughing like crazy. How can the cool weather this fall cause hail? In the whole of Zhaoxin County, only Kong and his family spread a thick layer of grass on the tiled house.
Early in the morning of the third day, the sun seemed to be joking with Kong and woke up very early. The sky was clear, the wind and sun were beautiful, and people laughed even more. Kong Yi also doubted whether what the bird said was true. Unexpectedly, just after noon, the northwest sky seemed to have turned over an ink bottle completely black. Soon, the entire sky over Cushin City seemed to be covered with a layer of ink. After the thunderstorm, bean-sized drops of rain fell, as if a water tank had been spilled. After a while, I heard a loud “crackling” noise. The beave-like hail came down and smashed two bags of smoke before the dark clouds moved southeast with the wind. The frightened people saw the rain clear and ran out of the house one after another. There were no good tiles on the roof, but the thatched house was safe and sound. After the hail, Kong Yi cleaned up the grass on the tiles. His house's tile house was undamaged. Some people came back to it and asked Kong Yi how he knew it. Kong Yi didn't hide it; he told people the truth.
The fact that Kong Yi could understand the language of birds quickly spread within and outside the city. Some people who go on a long trip also look for Kong Yi and ask questions before going out, which is very clever every time.
On this day, Kong Yi heard another group of sparrows busy moving while saying, “Too bad, unfortunately, it won't take a few days for such a big city to sink to the bottom of the water. It's a shame!” The news is nothing short of small. If it actually spreads, it can save parents and people all over the city; if it is fake, it will cause people to feel anxious and uneasy. It would be strange that the county grandpa didn't kill me. Just thinking about it, another group of magpies flew by in the sky. Kong Yi heard it even more clearly. An old black and white magpie said, “After three days, the heavy rain will not stop for 7 days. This city will be over.” Another little magpie said, “The water recedes after the rain, isn't the city still a city?” The old magpie said, “The heavy rain doesn't stop, the water doesn't drain, and the lake is still being irrigated. This plot of land cannot withstand it; it will sink. At that time, houses will collapse. Most of the city is a vast ocean.” Kong Yi breathed a sigh of relief when he heard this: My God, I must talk about such a disaster. As a result, Kong ran home and met people and said, “I'm trying to find a way to move. In a few days, this county will sink into the water.” One transmission to ten, ten to one hundred, and the whole of Cushin City went up in flames. People are busy moving. But where to move in just a few days? The old man from the county knows that Kong Yi was to blame. He sent someone to arrest Kong Yi. The old man wanted to be promoted to the court to examine Kong Yi. The county government office was surrounded by three floors inside and three floors outside.
The county grandpa sat down in the courthouse and “knocked” on the ground: “Bold Kong Yi, how dare you spooky the crowd and make people feel anxious. Can you know it's a crime?” Kong Yi is a well-informed person. He cured the wounds of the giant geese and gave them feathers. From then on, he reported to the old man that he understood the language of birds one by one, and asked the old man to use the county government to issue a notice to save the people of the city. The old man from the prefecture usually knows that Kong is good, and he doesn't want to embarrass him. He said, “I'll try it first to see if I can understand the bird language.” Kong handed over a feather and hung it on the county's grandpa's ear, but the bird couldn't understand people's words. Every time the owner made fun of it, the owner would give it something tasty, so the yellow finch actually sang: “Poor, pathetic, really pathetic. I've been locked in a cage all day, eating, drinking, and having fun. I still think about the sky outside.” The crowd heard the yellow finch cry so clearly and pleasantly, but the old man from the county understood the lyrics, opened the bird cage and released the yellow finch, and said to the owner of the rice shop, “I will never let you raise birds in the future.” Mr. Jang Ji, the owner of the rice shop, was unable to touch his head, and it wasn't easy to turn his face off at the head of the prefecture for a bird. Kong understood in his heart that he must have understood the language of birds.
On the same day, the county grandpa posted a notice. The notice said, “There may be heavy rain for several days in the near future. I hope the people will prepare to take refuge from the disaster.” Since it wasn't too serious, many people didn't pay enough attention. However, some families have no doubt about Kong Yi. Seeing that Kong Yi abandoned his property and moved to the southwest with things he could take away, he also moved away with him. Sure enough, as was to be expected, the heavy downpour continued after three days, and water accumulated three feet deep on the flat ground for several days. Due to the large area of rain, not only did the accumulated water not flow away, but the lake water flowed back. A few days later, the ground sank. Except for the Jiayouyuan area of Cuxin City, which was preserved, everything else fell to the bottom of the water.
Kong Yi and his entourage moved out two days before the torrential rain stopped and built a home in Gaogang. This location is said to be Confucius Port, which is now a mile long on the west shore of Lake Qilihu.
