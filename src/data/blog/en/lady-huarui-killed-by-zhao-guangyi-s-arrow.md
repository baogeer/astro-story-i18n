 After the death of Qian Shu, Tang Zhuangzong later used Meng Zhixiang as the ambassador of the two rivers. After Meng Zhixiang arrived in Shu, Zhuang Zong was killed until after Tang Mingzong's death, Meng Zhixiang arrogated the title of emperor, but died within a few months, and Meng Chang succeeded to the throne.
Meng Chang is a person who knows how to have fun. He sought out beautiful women from Shudi to use as his harem, and his favorite was “Mrs. Huarui” Fei Guifei. Mrs. Huarui loves peonies and red mast flowers, so Meng Chang ordered the government and private people to grow peonies in large quantities. In addition to spending time day and night with Mrs. Huarui, Meng Chang also invited a feast to enjoy peonies. Changri's dinner was getting boring. Mrs. Huarui cooked white sheep's head with red ginger, suppressed it with a stone, and soaked it in wine. It was cut as thin as paper, and the flavor was endless. It is known as “Scarlet Lamb's Head.”
Meng Chang is most afraid of the heat. On the Mohe Pond, he built a crystal palace, using Kusunoki as columns, agarwood, coral windows, and jasper as the home. The surrounding walls do not use masonry, and use several large layers of glass. The Donnello tent quilt is extremely luxurious.
Just when the two were drowning in their dreams, Song Taizu, Zhao Kuangyin, “joined in a yellow robe,” and ordered Chung Wu to make Wang Quanbin lead an army of 60,000 to attack Shudi. The 140,000 Shu soldiers who defended Chengdu were destroyed by a thousand miles. Meng Chang left the city to surrender. In spring, when the mountains were full of flowers, Meng Chang and Mrs. Huarui were forced to leave their dream of life and death in Shudi Park and go to Bianliang. Zhao Kuangyin has been watching Mrs. Huarui for a long time. To see the beauty, he gifted Meng Chang and his family, and met Mrs. Huarui when they came to the palace to thank her. Seven days later, Meng Chang died violently. Many people think he was poisoned by Zhao Kuangyin. Meng Chang's mother was already ashamed of her son's request, and she also went on hunger strike to death. Taizu left Mrs. Huarui for a feast in the palace and asked her to sing a poem on the spot. Mrs. Huarui groaned: “When you first leave Shu Dao, your heart will be broken and your hatred will last. Spring is like a new year, and you will soon smell rhododendrons all the time. The women of the Three Thousand Palaces all looked good. They fought for Chanjuan and learned the best. Today, who knows this is a lie. This phrase was written on the wall of the post station when leaving Shu Guo and passing through Jia Meng Pass. Back then, in the Chengdu Palace, Shu lord Meng Chang personally wrote the “Wanli Chaotian Song.” He thought it was a trifecta for thousands of miles to the dynasty. Women wore high crowns; they all called it “Chatian,” but it was a reflection of the Song dynasty. Taizu ordered her to write another poem. Mrs. Huarui thought: “When the flag was lowered from a tree in the city of King King, where did concubines learn about it in the deep palace; 140,000 people were all deconstructed, let alone a son. Song Taizu felt the feelings of Mrs. Huarui's homeland and admired her even more. She went to bed with Mrs. Huarui, and was soon crowned a royal princess.
However, Mrs. Huarui has not forgotten her relationship with Meng Chang and often secretly worships his portraits. Once I was seen by Song Tae-jo and asked her who was in the portrait, she had no choice but to say that it was a gift to Zhang Xian as a popular gift. After this attack, every concubine in the palace also offered a statue of Zhang Xian.
Mrs. Huarui later angered Taizu's younger brother Zhao Guangyi due to a political dispute, and was shot dead by Zhao Guangyi with an arrow during a hunt.
