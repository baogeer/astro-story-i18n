 After the Yuan Ming Dynasty, when the “Eight Immortals” group gradually formed and became more popular and widely spread with the rendering of miscellaneous operas, novels, folklore, folk tales, folk art, etc., the people of Penglai were also greatly influenced and gave birth to many legends about the Eight Immortals. This is also a major landscape of the Eight Immortals culture in Penglai. There are quite a few famous legends about the Eight Immortals in Horai. Here are a few excerpts for people to enjoy.
1. Eight Immortals Crossing the Sea
According to local legend in Horai, the Eight Immortals went to the Horai Pavilion to drink. When the wine was in full swing, Tieguai Li proposed to take a trip to the sea. The immortals echoed in unison and said that each person should cross the sea according to the law and must not ride a boat.
Han Zhongli took the lead in throwing the big banana fan into the sea, lying on the fan with his back open chest, and drifted far away; He Xianguu threw the lotus flower into the water and instantly had a red light. He Xian Gu stood on top of the lotus flower and drifted with the waves. Afterwards, Lu Dongbin, Zhang Guolao, Uncle Cao Guo, Tieguai Li, Han Xiangzi, and Lan Caihe also threw their treasures into the water and used the treasures to explore the East China Sea.
Dragon Palace was shocked by the actions of the Eight Immortals. Dragon King Ao Guang of the East China Sea led shrimp, soldiers and crabs to watch. The words clashed with the Eight Immortals, causing a fight. Dragon King of the East China Sea took advantage of the Eight Immortals and captured Lan Caihe into Dragon Palace. The immortals were furious, and each showed their ingenuity and slashed two dragons in the lower back. Shrimps, soldiers, and crabs will not be able to resist it; they will go down to sea one after another and hide at the bottom of the water. Immortals, on the other hand, go back and forth at sea to call war. Dragon King from the East China Sea invited Dragon King from the South China Sea, the North Sea, and the West Sea to join forces to turn over the waters of the three rivers, five lakes, and the four seas, set off huge waves, and run to kill all the immortals. In a critical moment, Uncle Cao Guo's jade board was powerful. I saw him take the lead in front of the jade board, and the turbulent waves retreated to both sides. The immortals followed, safe and sound. The Four Seas Dragon King saw the situation and quickly mobilized the four Marines to prepare for a final battle. At this point, it just so happened that Guanyin in the South China Sea came in, drank both sides, and came forward to mediate until Dragon King of the East China Sea released Lan Caihe, and the two sides went on strike.
The Eight Immortals prayed goodbye to Kannon Bodhisattva. Each carried a treasure, chased the waves, and wandered away.
Second, Su Dongpo visits the Eight Immortals
Although Su Dongpo is from the Northern Song Dynasty, the Eight Immortals were formed during the Yuan Ming period, and the Penglai Dier made up a legend that Su Dongpo visited the Eight Immortals.
Legend has it that when Su Dongpo was an official in Dengzhou, he wanted to visit the Eight Immortals, but he didn't know where to find them. After inquiring around, an old man with all white hair told him that every year on March 3, the Eight Immortals have to go to a party at the Horai Pavilion; as to whether or not you can see them, it depends on fate.
Early in the morning of March 31st, Su Dongpo went up to the Penglai Pavilion, and while traveling east and west, he didn't see the Eight Immortals. When I arrived at Xianling Gate out of boredom, I saw two old men playing chess. One was red and one had a dark face. They were all over 80 years old and had to be completely white. When the red-faced old man saw Su Dongpo coming, he waved to ask him to be the referee. Su Dongpo was only a good fighter. However, when he watched the game recently, he was dumbfounded and couldn't understand the chess path. To avoid being humiliated, the excuse is speculative. The red-faced old man said to Su Dongpo, “The person you are looking for will definitely come today; we are waiting for them here too. 3. Jiudonghui Xianshan
Ulyang Mountain is about 30 miles southwest of Penglai City. There are nine peaks on the mountain, which are of various shapes, large and small. According to folklore, these nine mountains are also related to the Eight Immortals.
Legend has it that in early March of a certain year, 38 Immortals went to a party at Penglai Pavilion. When they passed Ulyang Mountain, they were attracted by the beautiful scenery. They pressed the clouds one after another to enjoy the light and water colors of the mountain. Lu Dongbin excitedly took off his robe and put on a large blue stone. When he performed his magic, the gods sat down and enjoyed the mountain view while handing over a cup and drinking. Soon, he was intoxicated. Tieguai Li leaned on a treasure gourd and was bewildered and said, “All you have to do is lie down on my treasure gourd and fool around.” Zhang Guolao stared dazed and was unconvinced and said, “Who wants you to have a broken gourd? I rode a donkey instead. As soon as I hit the donkey, it was over.” Several others also boasted about their abilities. Seeing that the quarrel didn't come to fruition, Han Zhong said with a big stomach, “Crossing the sea today, everyone is still doing their own mana. Please come and judge. Whose patience is high, they will know when the time comes.” As a result, Lu Dongbin took off his crown, sat on his knees with respect and reverence, closed his eyes slightly, and meditated. A ray of blue smoke rose from his head and turned into a golden light and went straight to the sky.
About a bag of smoke, a bright cloud floated in the southwest sky, and Taishang Laojun rode a blue cow. The Eight Immortals picked up their drunken state and stood up to welcome them. Taishang Laojun descended from the clouds and asked, “Dong Bin hurriedly sent Jin Guang to ask me, so why?” Lu Dongbin bowed down and said, “My mentors, kids, etc. have something difficult to solve. It's troublesome for the Holy Driver.” Then I'll talk about testing mana. After listening to it, Taishang Laojun groaned a bit and said, “The art of crossing the water is a small skill in a district; how good is that! How can people who practice like them take it easy to fight for supremacy and victory?” After listening to it, all the immortals were ashamed. The old ruler also said, “If you do good deeds and spread blessings according to chance, you are on the right path. The scenery here is beautiful, so why don't you use your own spells to benefit the world?” The immortals immediately realized, and they all stepped forward to thank them. Afterwards, Lu Dongbin wore a sword, and Bu Tiangang stepped on the ground. There were words in his mouth. He pointed the sword and drank a “rise” sound, and a steep mountain appeared. Zhang Guo was refreshed. Instead, he rode a donkey for a circle, and a mountain full of strange rocks appeared in front of him. Next, Tieguai Li, Han Zhongli, Cao Guojiu, Han Xiang Zi, He Xian Gu, Lan Cai, and Ye all performed their magic one after another, and each created a mountain. Taishang Lao Jun saw it and was overjoyed. He said, “Wait and stand by and wait until the old man builds one, so it's worth the trip.” After all, as soon as the dust blew up in my hands, a peak rose to the ground. Cui Wei was exuberant, reckless, and majestic, far above other mountains, astonished the immortals. By the time the Eight Immortals had come back to their senses, Taishang Laojun had already crossed the Blue Bull to lead the palace.
This is the origin of the nine peaks of Ulyang Mountain. Later, the “Jiudingshan Temple Fair” was formed every year on March 3. There is a record of “Jiudinghui Xianshan” in the inscriptions on the two giant monuments erected in front of the mountain gate.
4. Tang Huai
There is an acacia tree from the Tang Dynasty in the courtyard of Tianhou Palace in Danyashan, Penglai, but legend has it that this “Tang Huai” didn't exist a long time ago. After the Eight Immortals arrived in Penglai, Li Tieguai and Zhang Guolao (Lu Dongbin) played chess in this courtyard. The scorching sun shone in the face, and there was nothing to hide. Li Sui, an iron gourd, poured out a seed from his gourd and buried it in the ground. In a blink of an eye, green shoots appeared on the ground. It soon grew into a large acacia tree with lush foliage and a crown like an umbrella. Two Immortals continued to play chess under this tree.
Allegedly, the tree has not changed since it grew. Since they are spawned by immortals, they no longer grow or die; they always look like this. A fire broke out at Tianhou Palace during the Qing Daoguang years, burning down more than 30 temple buildings overnight, and this Tang Acacia tree survived the disaster, which inevitably made people think: was this acacia tree really planted by the Eight Immortals?
