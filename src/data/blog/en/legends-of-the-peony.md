 The legend of “purse peony”
In ancient times, on the 200-mile southeast road of Luoyang City, there was a state called Ruzhou, and there was a town on the western side of the state called Miaoxia. Surrounded by mountains, the scenery is pleasant, and there is also a wonderful custom: once young men and women settle down, the woman must personally send the man a purse embroidered with mandarin ducks. The meaning of this is self-evident. If the doll is close, the sister-in-law in the woman's family or the elder sisters in the neighborhood will also have to embroider one for a lifetime. There is a beautiful girl named Yu Nu, who lives in the town. Eighteenth year of Tamame. They are ingenious, naturally clever, and have excellent embroidery and weaving skills. In particular, the various flower patterns on embroidered purses often attract bees and butterflies to fall on top of them. You can see the depth of effort. For such a nice girl, the person who proposed her family crashed the threshold, but they were all graciously rejected by the girl's family one by one. It turns out that the girl has a favorite man, and the family has decided by default. Unfortunately, it has been two years since the young man joined the military outside Serbia. There has been no news, let alone a wallet. The Jade Girl looks forward to it day and night, and misses it, so every month she embroiders a purse to talk about her feelings of nostalgia and hangs it one by one on a peony branch in front of the window. Over time, purses formed strings and became what people call “purse peonies.”
The legend of the “golden peony”
On the shores of the Erhai Sea in Dali, Yunnan Province, there is a beautiful mountain called Diancang Mountain. In this deep mountain, a world-famous type of golden peony grows. Not only is it as colored as gold, but it also looks like an ingot, which is very appealing. According to local Bai brothers, it was changed from gold. Here's a moving story. At the end of the Yuan dynasty, there were often bandits in the mountains. An old local Bai man made a living by selling firewood. He was kidnapped by bandits one day to cut firewood, and let out the wind. His family was limited to three days, bringing 100 and two pieces of gold into the mountains to redeem it. Once the period was over, his life was not guaranteed. The old man's family has only one daughter, Ah Qing, who is dependent on her. She was very sad after hearing the news. My family is so poor, where can I get gold or silver? Extremely clever, she changed her mind and got an idea. On the third day, she went up the mountain with only a bag of gold-dyed stones and a sharp sword. The bandits saw a young, beautiful, and weak woman. They all looked right. How prepared were they? She threw the golden stone on the ground, and the bandits flocked to grab it. She took the plane to kill the bandit leader with a sword, and the rest scattered like birds and beasts. Ah Qing rescued her father; the two went down the mountain untouched. Later, just where she threw “gold,” golden peonies grew.
The legend of the “purple-spotted peony”
At the end of the Ming dynasty, there was a poor person with extensive knowledge, and was good at piano, chess, calligraphy, and painting. He only saw through “red dust,” refused to take refuge from the world, shaved his hair as a monk, and lived in seclusion at Baiyun Temple in Taibai Mountain under the name “Shi Yi Shou”. This temple is surrounded by mountains and water. The scenery is beautiful, and peonies are planted widely in the courtyard. In monasteries, apart from working on Buddhism, Yishou almost always studies ink and paints in his spare time. He is particularly good at painting peonies. His paintings are detailed and realistic, just like nature. Everyone in the audience clapped their hands. The fame of Yi Shou's paintings soon spread far and wide, and there was an endless flow of painters. One spring day, around the valley rain, peonies compete to attract good men and women from all sides to visit Chaoshan to worship Buddha and enjoy the flowers, hoping for wealth, good fortune, and well-being. This afternoon, Yi Shou was painting peonies in the courtyard, ignoring the loud voices in front of the hospital. Looking up, there were a few people dressed up in Zhuang Ding in the distance, crowding a “fat man” who looked rich. Wobble towards this side. When I got close, I saw that it was the famous local bully “Wang Dahan”. This person has always been rampant in the village, oppressing the people, and doing nothing wrong. “Wang Dahan” walked in front of Yi Shou and saw that he was a good painter and salivated. When he was in a hurry to get it, he instigated Zhuang Ding to ask for it. What kind of personality did Yi Shou have? How could he join forces with “Wang Dayi,” and now he refused. “Wang Da-yu” was annoyed and forced to take a picture. Yi Shou showed no weakness, tore up the painting, threw the brush into the inkstone, and left in anger. “Wang Dajian” saw that the onlookers were so angry that they had no choice but to remain quiet. Who knows, the ink that spilled out of the inkstone fell on the petals of a few nearby peonies, and also flowed down the petals to the base of the petals, condensing into purple spots. Since then, every year during the blooming season, people can clearly see the purple spots on the flowers, which is why they are called “purple-spotted peonies

The legend of “dead peony”
At the end of the Northern Song Dynasty, Jin Bing invaded the Central Plains. A general surnamed Bian led his team through Luoyang. The season was right in the middle of the winter season. It was full of desolate scenes of grass turning yellow and thousands of trees dying. The general was anxious to hasten the horse's journey. The horse's whip was broken, so he left behind a section of dead branch on the side of the road and headed east. After a few changes of war, one day he led his team to Biancang Town in Yancheng, Jiangsu Province. He was already stranded and in need of recuperation. The general got off the horse, looked around, and inserted dead branches of Quan Zuo's horse whip into the ground so that the Ministry would set up camp here... In the spring of the following year, the dead branch inserted into the ground actually took out shoots and displayed new leaves. As the rain passed, bright and beautiful flowers magically bloomed. The local people heard the incident and came from hundreds of miles to watch the flowers and burn incense. According to the drug farmer, it is known that it is a peony. People rushed to tell each other: “Big Song is hopeful, Da Song is hopeful.” These peonies are also called “dead branch peonies.” Later, local people donated money to build Taiwan and built a “dead branch peony garden” to enshrine these peonies.
The legend of “Liu Shi Ge”
At the end of the Sui dynasty, in the east of Miaoxia Town, Ruzhou, Henan, there was a place where the Liu family lived - the Liu Family House. There is a beautiful and naive girl here. She was born in Shuxiangmendi. She has understood everything since childhood, playing chess, calligraphy, and painting, and is loved by her relatives and neighbors. After that, her parents passed away one after another, and the girl came to live in Chang'an with her brother and sister-in-law, who worked as an official in Chang'an. After the fall of the Sui dynasty, her brother and sister passed away one after another, leaving her alone, with nowhere else to go. They also saw through the red dust, and went out of their own home to become a nun. When she left home, the girl brought white peonies that she had grown with her own hands in her home to the hermitage as a sign of devotion to the Buddhist family and purify herself. Under her careful management, white peonies grew very lush and beautiful. One plant has a thousand flowers. The flowers are large, and the petals are heavy. The flowers are white with a hint of redness, and crystal and moisturize, such as beautiful skin, boy's jade face. Viewers all praise and praise their virtues, so every April, many believers visit this temple to worship the Buddha and enjoy offering the Buddha, and the incense is more and more intense. Therefore, the flower comes from “His Excellency Liu Shi Ju”, hence the name “Liu Shi Ge”, also known as “Liu Shi Ge”. Later, this peony variety spread to Tianpeng in Sichuan Province, Heze, Shandong, etc., and the fragrance spread far and wide.
The legend of “Ten Thousand Books”
In the Ming Dynasty, there was a scholar in Bozhou, Anhui. His name was Ouyang Boyun, and the character was a hard writer. He was originally born into a family of government officials. Later, due to the family's situation, it gradually fell into disrepair and was very poor. However, scholar students are unwilling to accept fateful arrangements, and are even less willing to be placed under the guise of others. They are determined to gain “fame” and shine for their ancestors. How can they fall off the list year after year. A kind-hearted gentleman told him, “My post-generation skills are so poor. I still need to read 10,000 books before I can feel the gods of heaven and earth.” As a result, he spent the whole day copying books and studying literature more than once. However, my family was poor, and paper was too expensive, so I had to copy an article on the wall and door panel. One day he felt bored in his room and came to the backyard to relax. I was pleasantly surprised to see the peony bush in the backyard that hadn't bloomed for many years, so I suddenly had a whim. I went back to the room, fetched ink, and copied the article on peony petals, and used flowers instead of paper. The kind gentleman passed by and saw this scene and called this peony “ten thousand volumes”. “Flower God” was probably moved by this. The following year, Ouyang Boyun actually “raised people.”
The legend of “Outlaw Liu Huang”
In the old days, there was a descendant near Luoyang, whose name was Liu Mingdanting. Since childhood, he has loved flowers like crazy, and has become addicted to growing flowers. Among the flowers, peonies are particularly good. They have grown many flowers and peonies in front of and behind the hospital. However, precisely because he grows flowers so well, he is often harassed by urchins. He was very angry. Every time he caught him, the lighter one was given a minute's hard work, and the heavier one was punched several times. As a result, local urchins nicknamed him “Bad Liu.” In this way, it spread gradually, and over time, it took over his name and became a “big name.” Speaking of “Nao Liu”'s flower growing skills are truly remarkable. He grows more than 100 peonies, and there are many varieties with big and colorful flowers. One year, he cultivated a golden peony. Its color surpassed “Yao Huang”. Neighbors were amazed, and wealthy people sought gold and silver. Yellow peonies were very scarce at the time, so they multiplied in large numbers, and for a while they scrambled to plant them near and far, it became a fashion. Later, people named this flower “Bad Liu Huang”. This product name was included in books and spread.
