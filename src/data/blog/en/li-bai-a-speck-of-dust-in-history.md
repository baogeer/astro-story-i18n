 Recently, the animated film “30,000 Mile in Chang'an” has gone viral and has become the first choice for many families to watch movies together.
There are many aspects of this film that can be explained: Gao Shi's friendship with Li Bai, Li Bai's tortuous life experiences, etc. Actually, the story also involves “cold knowledge”: in Li Bai's life, he had the experience of being exiled, and the reason was actually “betrayal”!
It's hard to imagine that one of the greatest poets in the history of Chinese poetry was once an “anti-thief” who “plotted to subvert” the prosperity of Shengtang — in fact, this was a historical case spawned from the Anshi Rebellion. It's just that, this slightly “dishonorable” experience was overshadowed by Li Bai's other brilliance...
Be energetic and go to the East with the army
A new beginning in the career
Winter, in the south of the Tang dynasty, is still bitterly cold. However, on the riverside boat, there was a well-dressed scribe standing. He was a staff member of this Eastern Expedition army, and he was also a famous poet all over the world. As a poet who has witnessed the prosperity of Shengtang with his own eyes, he certainly likes the magnificence. Just like the verses he wrote, “Fly straight down three thousand feet” and “Let's fight alone”... When he saw an army with a flag covering the sun, he must have been in a strong and intense mood.
Yes, this poet is the brightest star in Datang poetry: Li Bai.
In order to write down the scene in front of him, Li Bai exclaimed, “Yongwang went out of his way to learn in the east, and Tianzi divided the dragon and tiger flags from afar. As soon as the boat building was quiet, the river and Han turned into Yanwu Pond.” It can be seen that this eastward advance was aimed at calming the storm, suppressing the rebels, and thus reviving the prestige of Shengtang. If successful, the country is settled, it will be an immortal achievement.
Although Li Bai was famous for his poems and was familiar to future generations, his career path was not going well, and it was difficult for him to achieve his ideals and ambitions. Although he once wrote the phrase “An Neng destroyed his eyebrows and the powerful and made me unable to be happy,” Li Bai's excitement, which had been silent for a long time, instantly recovered when the opportunity to join the military and establish a career was right in front of him. Of course, this strong feeling must be expressed through his best poetry.
According to “The Old Tang Book: The Legend of Wenyuan, Legend of Li Bai”, after the Anshi Rebellion, Tang Xuanzong was forced to go west and advance towards Shu Zhong. On the way, in order to calm down the rebellion, Li Yi, his sixteenth son, was appointed as the governor of Jianghuai and the ambassador of Yangzhou Jidu, while Li Bai visited Wang Yong in Xuanzhou (present-day Xuancheng, Anhui). Of course, Li Yi knows this famous fairy of poetry all over the world — back when Li Bai first arrived in Chang'an, the literary leader at the time, He Zhizhang already called him a “fairy.”
Most officials and citizens of the Tang dynasty knew that Li Bai had a desire to serve the country. If Li Bai could be conquered, he could not only have frequent discussions with famous poets, but also increase the prestige of his army. Of course, Yong Wang was happy to go with the water and push the beauty of adulthood, so he ordered Li Bai to do his own thing.
“Working” was not even part of the formal government system of the Tang dynasty. It was usually equivalent to a private secretary for someone in power, or a master whose salary was not established by the imperial court. This appointment also reflects Li Yi's intention: to use Li Bai's fame to add appeal to himself, and if he succeeds in the future, he can also help Li Bai achieve his “Dapeng Vision.”
The son took the power of the father, and the brothers turned their backs
Sudden change of identity
The story unfolds to this point, and you can even see the taste of the father and son working together in harmony with each other. However, the mystery or helplessness of history often lies in the fact that it will change the face of future generations according to the will of some superior person:
Tang Xuanzong and Li Longji are well known to posterity because of his afflictions with Yang Yuhuan. This gentle and moving love story involving the country, family, ideals, and power is enduring because of Bai Juyi's “Song of Eternity.” The key turning point in this story, Ma Yuyi's transformation, also became an inflection point for Li Bai's path.
The incident occurred on June 14, the 15th year of Tianbao (756). Prior to that, Tang Xuanzong had granted Li Yi the power to administer one side's troops and horses. After Ma Yuyi's mutiny, all of Tang Xuanzong's enthusiasm for the world mostly dissipated with Yang Yuhuan's death. Therefore, after a month, Prince Li Heng took the throne without permission, and even promulgated the “Emperor Ming Order Su Zong to the Throne” on August 18 to confirm and agree in it that in order to calm the rebellion, Xuanzong can also make corresponding arrangements according to his own judgment and report it to Su Zong for the record.
It can be seen that the Anshi Rebellion is actually a “rare opportunity” for Prince Lee Hyung. He knows that if he wants to use this rebellion to follow the trend, the most convenient method is to stay ahead and direct the battle as the leader of counterinsurgency. As a result, Lee Hyung did not go west with his father; instead, he stayed in the North to lead the rebellion. As mentioned before, Xuanzong didn't have much feelings about national affairs or even the world at this time, so he handed over the heavy responsibility of assisting Li Heng to Li Heng's half-brother, Li Lin, and proclaimed Li Yi as the governor of Jianghuai Bingma and the ambassador of Yangzhou Jidu (with expanded powers over his previous post, leading more soldiers and horses).
Some scholars in later generations believed that Lee Yong-gi's move was meant to confront Lee Hyung, but based on Lee Yong-gi's logical analysis, this was not the case. First, Xuanzong Lee Lung-gi was in central Sichuan at the time. This area was rich in products and difficult to attack. If he set up an army here, plus the appeal of the emperor of the world and the courage to kill the Yang family, he wouldn't worry about regaining his dominant position.
Hyeon-jong did not do this. Instead, in the far south, where there was no threat to Lee Hyung, he ordered Lee Yeong to set up an army and recruit talents; second, after the rebellion was settled, Hyeon-jong only had more than 600 guards around him, so it can be seen that he had no sense of confrontation. Combined with Ma Yuyi's experience, the more logical explanation is that Xuanzong no longer pursues the throne, rule, or power; he just wants to live his life in his recollection.
However, Hyeon-jong's move was not without checks and balances; he was actually reminding Lee Heng that if the rebellion cannot be settled, he still has a backseat. Since ancient times, the imperial family has been the most ruthless. How could Lee Hyung leave a legacy for himself? After Li Yi reformed the navy, went north to sea, landed in Youzhou, and recovered his lost ground, Su Zong Li Heng was afraid of his younger brother's “powerful shock,” and even more afraid that his father would repent and that his younger brother would grow up, completely deviated from “Enthroned”, used the excuse of poor communication in ancient times to declare Li Yi's department as rebels, and drastically changed history, leaving such records in his own personal history and Li Yi's biography, respectively:
“The Old Tang Book: The Chronicles of Suzong” says, “Yongwangping, the governor of Gangneung, is good at navigating the ship's way down to Guangling.” The word “disrespectful” not only showed disrespect for his father, but also erased the contributions of his brother and brother to recover lost ground and secure the country after breaking through the sea.
Of course, you have to do a full set of dramas. “Old Tang Shu: The Legend of Yong Wang Yi”, which corresponds to “Benji”, it is recorded like this: “Being good at Zhou Shi went east, and 5,000 people from the First Battle became Guangling.”
In the above two records, Li Heng deliberately erased the written records that Yong Wangyi was awarded by Tang Xuanzong as the governor of Jianghuai Bingma and the ambassador for temperate management in Yangzhou, tampering with his inauguration, and completely distorted and covered up the truth.
Meanwhile, Li Bai also went from being a staff member who was involved in planning operations to become an accomplice in plotting the rebellion. Of course, in the midst of the torrents of history, Li Bai was nothing more than a supporting character in this battle for the imperial family power, or to put it more harshly, no one even considered his experiences and feelings; he simply became a shining light and a stifling companion in this dark period of history.
Yoro no Nishi, Sad Moon
Comfort eventually becomes a lie
As the rebellion calmed down, Emperors Xuan and Su returned to Beijing one after another, but Li Bai was taken back to Chang'an and imprisoned as a traitor.
As a cultural celebrity, Li Bai almost never gets killed. After many requests for help, he finally saved his life with the help of friends such as Song Ruosi and Guo Ziyi, but was also condemned to exile Ye Lang (present-day Guizhou).
Coincidentally, as early as 78 years ago, Li Bai's friend Wang Changling was relegated and exiled as a result of the incident, Longbiao, a destination just west of Yelang. Li Bai was full of sorrow at the time, and he also wrote a farewell poem for his friend: “Yang Hua ran out of rules, and heard the dragon cross the Five Rivers. “I'm worried and the bright moon, and follow the wind until night and west.”
Ironically, however, Li Bai never thought at the time that he would be sent into exile because he had achieved his ideals and ambitions for a short time and because of his merit.
While Li Bai was on his way into exile, he received an order of pardon. He returned to Chang'an with great interest, and wrote, “In the midst of the clouds of Emperor Baidi, I returned a thousand miles to Gangneung in one day. The phrase “The monkeys on both sides of the Strait can't stop crying; the boat has already crossed Mount Manjou.”
Unfortunately, Li Bai was already old at this time, and even when he returned to Chang'an, he still didn't wait for an opportunity to achieve his ideals. His death became an unsolved case.
From the perspective of posterity, Li Bai's life was very glorious. There were so many places worth writing and recording that his tortuous experiences are little known today.
Yes, in the eyes of ordinary people, it seems that Li Bai was the main character in Tang Dynasty history. Unfortunately, in Li Bai's time, after all, he was just a pawn for those in power. Some people may say that when the dust of the times falls on a person, it is a mountain, but what is most breathtaking is that Li Bai's ideal of building a meritorious career with him is a grain of dust from the times.
