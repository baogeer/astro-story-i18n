 The famous quote from Shengtang poet Li Bai singing peonies has been sung by people for thousands of years. I'm talking about Tang Xuanzong and Yang Gui watching peonies at Chenxiang Pavilion one day. Singer Li Guinian led a group of children to sing. Tang Xuanzong said to Li Guinian, “Enjoying famous flowers, how can you sing old lyrics to the Queen? Let's do it, call Li Bai to write new words.” Li Guinian rushed to a famous restaurant on Chang'an Street to look for it. Sure enough, Li Baizheng enjoyed drinking with several writers, and was already very drunk. When Li Gui-nian conveyed the Holy Will to him, he was so drunk that he went to bed without thinking about it.
The sacrament was unmistakable. Li Guinian had no choice but to ask his entourage to drag Li Bai to the horse, to the front of the palace gate, and used a few people to support the left and right and push it in front of Tang Xuanzong. When Tang Xuanzong saw that Li Bai was drunk like mud, he called Tachen to rest on the jade bed and ordered that the soaking soup be brought. Yang Guifei asked someone to spray cold water on the surface to dissolve it. Li Bai lay on a jade bed and extended his foot to Collix and asked him to take off his boots. Collix was helpless. He had to hold his stomach and crouch down to get rid of him. After being busy for a while, Li Bai woke up from his drunken dream. Tang Xuanzong told him to write poetry quickly to cheer up. Li Bai smiled slightly and picked up the pen. After less than a moment of effort, he had already written three “Clear and Peaceful” lyrics:
(1)
Clouds want clothes, flowers want visitors, and the spring breeze blows down the window.
If I hadn't seen you at Yushan Mountain, I'd meet at Yaotai under the Moon.
(2)
One branch of red dew is fragrant, and the clouds and rain go to waste in Wushan.
Who in the Han Palace would be similar? Poor Feiyan relied on new makeup.
(3)
The two famous flowers enjoyed the country; they looked like a king with a smile.
Explain that Chunfeng is infinitely hateful, and that Agarwood is reliant on the north.
In these three poems, Peony and Yang Guifei are written interactively. A flower is a person, a person is a flower, and a person is a flower. A person's face is full of light, the same as Meng Tien. From a structural point of view, the first song was written in space and introduced into Moon Palace Langyuan. The second song was written in time and introduced to the balcony of King Chu Xiang, the court of Emperor Han Cheng. The third song returns to reality and points out the north of the Chenxiang Pavilion in the Tang Palace. The first Spring Breeze echoes the third Spring Breeze.
In the first sentence of the song, seeing clouds reminds me of the royal queen's neon feather coat, and when I see peonies, I think of Princess Yu Rong. The next sentence is Luhuanong. Further dyeing the peony flower makes him look beautiful in the crystal dew, making the face of the flower more spiritual. In the next two sentences, imagine ascending to the world of immortals such as Qunyu Mountain, Yaotai, and Moon Palace, where the royal mother and daughter live. These scenes can only be seen there; they really compare Yang Fei to a celestial girl.
The second song points out that King Chu Xiang is a Chinese goddess with a broken heart, and she is the most beautiful woman in front of her. Furthermore, Emperor Han Cheng's empress, Zhao Feiyan, had to rely on new makeup. There was no need for fat powder; it was all natural and stunning. Here, they are suppressing Goddess and Zhao Feiyan to exalt Consort Yang.
The first and second sentences of the third song combine Peony, Consort Yang, and Xuanzong. Beauty of the country refers to Consort Yang. The word “spring breeze” in the third sentence is synonymous with a monarch.
Tang Xuanzong was very satisfied with this poem. Later generations made up saying that Golix took off her boots and thought she was humiliated, and went to Yang Fei, saying that Li Bai used Feiyan's thinness, ridiculed Yang Fei's fat, and used Feiyan to confuse Red Phoenix and ridiculed Princess Yang's palace for not being inspected. This is unreliable.
