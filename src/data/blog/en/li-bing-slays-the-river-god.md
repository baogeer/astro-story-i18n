 Emperor Jiang, the dragon god who rules the Yangtze River in Chinese mythology, is said to require the residents of the basin to sacrifice two young girls every year; otherwise, there will be a flood. Later, Li Bing, an official from the Qin State, heard about this when he became a local official in Shu County, so he personally went and slaughtered Jiang Shen Dajun. After a vicious battle, Li Bing used spells to turn himself into a cow, and Jiang Shen also became a cow and fought with him. At this point, Li Bing's vassals took the opportunity to assassinate Jiang Shen, and the floods have since been lifted.
