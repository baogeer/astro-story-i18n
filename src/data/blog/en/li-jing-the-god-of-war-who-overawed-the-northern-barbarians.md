 Li Jing (571-649) was a military man in the early Tang dynasty. Formerly a pharmacist, a native of Sanyuan, Yongzhou (present-day northeastern Sanyuan County, Shaanxi). There are few “martial arts skills omitted.” His uncle, Han Zenhu, was a famous general in the Sui dynasty. He often discussed the art of war with him and once praised him, “Li Jing is the only person who can discuss Sun Wu's art of war with me.” At the end of Emperor Yang of the Sui dynasty, Li Jing became mayor of Mayi County. In 617 AD, Li Yuan's father and son set up an army against the Sui. Li Jing was taken prisoner in Chang'an. Li Yuan wanted to kill him. Li Shimin survived his death, so he followed Li Shimin and succeeded in the war to conquer Wang Shichong and was awarded the opening of a government.
In the 4th year of Wu De (621), Tang Gaozu and Li Yuan appointed Li Jing as marching chief to conquer Xiao Xian in the middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River with King Li Xiaogong of Zhao County. In September, Li Jing took advantage of the sharp rise in the water level of the Yangtze River and Xiao Xian's lack of defense to lead an army out of the Three Gorges and down the east of the river, attacking Gangneung City in one fell swoop, and forced Xiao Xian to surrender. After Li Jing led the army into Gangneung, military discipline was strict, no offenses were committed in autumn, and all the states and counties in Gangnam were attached. Li Jing honored Shangzhu Guo as governor of Yongkang County, and served as Jingzhou Assassin History. Next, Li Jing led an army south to Guangxi and sent people to various states to recruit them, so that 96 states in the Lingnan region and more than 600,000 households returned to the Tang dynasty. As a result, Li Jing was also appointed as the Lingnan Provincial Relief Ambassador and the Guizhou Supervisor of the Guizhou School.
In the 6th year of Wu Te (623), Fu Gongping reformed an army against Tang in Jiangnan. Gao Zu used Lee Hyo-gong as the marshal, and Li Jing as the deputy marshal to go to the crusade. Unexpectedly, Li Jing first defeated Fu Gongyi's navy, then led the lightly armed Tang army straight to Danyang, captured Fu Gong, and completely pacified Jiangnan. Since then, Li Jing has successively served as the chief minister of the Southeast Taiwan military department and the governor of Yangzhou Dadu.
In the 8th year of Wu De (625), the Turks attacked Taiyuan. Li Yuan ordered Li Jing as marching chief, led 10,000 Jiang Huai soldiers north to resist the Turks. In the same year, he was governor of the capital of Anzhou. In April of the 9th year of Wu Deng, Li Jing fought fiercely with the Turkic Jeri Kehan in Lingzhou for a day, forcing Jie Li to retreat north. Soon, he became the superintendent of the Lingzhou Road Marching Force.
In 627 AD, Tang Taizong and Li Shimin ascended the throne. Li Jing was successively appointed Chief of the Criminal Division, the General Secretary of the Public Prosecution Service, and the Senior Secretary of the Military Department.
In the 3rd year of Zhenguan (629), Taizong decided to fight back against the Turks in a big way and ordered Li Jing to become the chief marching commander of the Dingxiang Dingxiang Province, and summoned 100,000 troops to attack the Turks in six divisions. In the first month of the fourth year of Zhenguan, Li Jing personally led 3,000 elite riders to take advantage of the dark to take advantage of the dark to take over Dingxiang, the location of Jelly Kehan's dental tent. Taejong praised him and said, “In the past, Li Ling stepped down for 5,000 years; he inevitably surrendered to the Huns; he was still blessed with famous books and bamboo. With 3,000 light riders, Qing went deep into Ruting, Ke Fudingxiang, and Weizhen Beidi. This is something that has never been seen before.” As a result, Li Jing was enlisted as acting king of the country. In February, Li Jing also led 10,000 elite riders to raid Yinshan and captured more than 1,000 Turkic soldiers. Then, taking advantage of the fog to advance towards Iron Mountain, they broke through Jie Likehan's teeth in one fell swoop and captured Xie Likehan. The Eastern Turks, which had been affected by the Central Plains for many years, perished. Li Jing was shot by Dr. Ga Guang Lu for his success in defeating the Eastern Turks, and Bai Shang Shu's right-hand servant.
In the 8th year of Zhenguan (634), Tsugu Hun in the northwest was invaded. Li Jing took the initiative to go out. Taizong immediately appointed Li Jing as the chief commander of the West Sea Road March and commanded the army to conquer Tugohun. In the 9th year of Zhenguan, Li Jing was defeated by Tsugu Hun's army in Jishi Mountain. Tsugu hunkechan was killed, and all of his land returned to the Tang dynasty. In the 11th year of Zhenguan, Taizong changed the title of Li Jing to Duke Wei of Guo.
In the 23rd year of Zhenguan (649), Li Jing died of illness at the age of 78. Tang Taizong made a special order to bury the Zhaoling Mausoleum. His tomb was built in the shape of Iron Mountain and Mount Seishi in recognition of his brilliant achievements in defeating Eastern Turks and conquering Tugohun, and hanging his portrait in the Lingyan Pavilion.
After Li Jing returned to Tang, he defeated the Turks in the north, and Tugu Hun in the west, showing extraordinary military talent. Historians claim that Li Jing “had an opportunity to win against Ming,” and was a military man with a rich record and theory. He is the author of many military books, such as “Li Jing's Sixth Army Mirror,” but most of them have been scattered. Later generations compiled military books such as “Tang Taizong, Li Weigong asked the right questions” and “Defending the Army of War”. “Tang Taizong, Li Weigong, Asked Right” was designated as one of the “Seven Books of the Martial Arts” during the Northern Song Dynasty, and is a collection of ancient Chinese military studies.
