 In 755 AD, the Anshi Rebellion broke out. The following year, when Tang Xuanzong fled to Ma Yupo (present-day northwest Xingping County, Shaanxi), the accompanying generals executed Prime Minister Yang Guozhong and forced Yang Yuhuan to commit suicide. Historically known as the “Ma Yupo Mutiny.” After the mutiny, Tang Xuanzong fled west, and Prince Li Heng ascended the throne in Lingwu (present-day Ningxia Province) for Tang Suzong.
The imperial court was established in a situation of internal and external hardship. Tang Suzong felt that it was difficult, so he urgently called for Li Bixiang, who had gone back to the mountains and forests to avoid Yang Guozhong's power. After the two met, Tang Suzong was very happy. He consulted him every little thing, said nothing to him, and even slept together while sleeping.
Later, Li Bi saw Empress Zhang Liangdi of Tang Suzong and condoned her for being involved in politics; she also appointed lieutenant Li Fuguo's dictatorship. Under the struggle between these two factions, the country's government became more and more chaotic. Li Bi felt that her strength alone was weak; staying in the middle of the DPRK had become a mistake in the eyes of others. In order to avoid annihilation, Li Bi decided to retreat to the mountains and forests again.
Therefore, when Chang'an was recovered, Li Bi said to Tang Suzong, “Your kindness to me is as great as a mountain. Now I have shallowly repaid some of my blessings. From now on, I ask Your Majesty to allow me to be an idle person.”
Tang Suzong disagreed and said, “I have been worried for many years with my husband. I should have fun with him. Why did you leave me?” Li Bi said to Tang Suzong, who has a deep love for retention, that I have five things to keep. I hope you will let me leave after listening and avoid death.”
Tang Suzong hurriedly asked which five reasons not to stay.
Li Bi replied, “I met you too early. You took me too seriously, loved me too deeply, my credit was too high, and my deeds were too strange, so I couldn't stay in North Korea. If you don't let me go, just kill me.”
It was useless for Tang Suzong to be held back again and again; later, he had to agree to Li Bi's request.
As she left, Li Bi said to Tang Suzong, “You have so much trust and grace in me, I wouldn't dare to talk about the case of King Jianning being wrongfully murdered. I'm not saying this now to pursue this, but rather to remind you not to have similar situations in the future.”
Tang Suzong was surprised and said, “Jianning Wang listened to the villain, murdered his brother, and wanted to seize his seat. I had to give him to death. You must have made it very clear, sir!”
Li Bi shook her head: “If King Jianning had this feeling, his older brother, King Guangping, should hate him, but every time King Guangping talked to me about this, he cried and said that his younger brother had been wronged. Furthermore, when the rebellion was settled, you wanted King Jianning to be the world's military and horse marshal. I don't agree; please change to King Guangping. If King Jianning wanted to take the throne, he must have hated me very much. Why was he more respectful and kind to me later?”
Listening to it, Tang Suzong also felt it and couldn't help crying: “Sir, I know it was wrong, but it's over, and I don't want to hear about it anymore.”
Li Bi said, “I brought this up to make His Majesty be wary of the future. Back then, Tianhou (Wu Zetian) had four sons. The eldest son, Prince Lee Hong, changed his second son, Lee Hyun. Lee Hyun was afraid in his heart and wrote the song “Huangtai Melon Song”: “Under the platform of growing melons, ripe melons left. Picking once makes the melon good, then picking the melon thinner; picking three is especially good; picking four to bring back the vine. ' He was very affectionate. His intention was to move the diva, but in the end he went into exile and died in central Guizhou. Now that you've picked a melon, don't pick any more.” Tang Suzong was deeply moved and said that he would definitely live there himself, which reassured Li Bi. After this conversation, Li Bi immediately entered Hengshan. The reason she chose to say such heartfelt words during her farewell to Quanlin was because he felt that Empress Zhang Liangdi would love to establish her own son as Prince Prince. For this reason, she secretly made up rumors to frame King Quang Ping. Imperial Palace rituals have always been a sensitive and dangerous topic. There are many eyes from various forces, and the relationship is complicated. Li Bi was afraid to say it before leaving the court. He not only reminded Tang Suzong, but also raised his own safety factor, which can be described as having gone to great pains.
Li Bi knows that the fashion of suzong has no farsighted plans, omissions, and no merit, that is more important than personal feelings, ignorance, and suffering can be compromising with corporals. In times of peace, it is difficult to listen to advice. Accompanying the king is like a tiger; it is better to leave early.
