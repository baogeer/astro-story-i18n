 Talented woman from Jinan through the ages - Li Qingzhao
Li Qingzhao, a glorious name in the history of ancient Chinese literature. Because of her dazzling brilliance, the International Astronomical Association named a circular mountain on Mercury after her in 1987. This is enough to show that her achievements are as bright as stars. Not only does it twinkle above the earth of Shenzhou, but it also shines brightly in the vast starry sky, drawing the world's attention.
Li Qingzhao, the most talented woman in history, is recognized in history books. A native of Jinan, she was born in 1084 in Mingshui, Zhangqiu. His father, Li Gefei, was a scholar. He studied under Su Yi. He was a military commander in the Song Dynasty. His mother came from a famous family and was a good writer. Li Qingzhao lives in Shuxiangmen and is smart. His father invited the famous writer of the time, Chao Buzhi (good poetry, calligraphy, and wild person from Shandong) to work as Li Qingzhao's tutor. Li Qingzhao's works when he was a teenager were highly appreciated by his teacher. For example, Girls' Generation's little motto “Like a Dream Order” was written about her mental activity to cherish flowers on a stormy night. As soon as this phrase came out, it caused a stir in the literary world. Among them, “green, fat, red, and thin” has also become a famous phrase throughout the ages. Another song, “Like a Dream,” describes her elegant lifestyle: “When I was excited, I went back to the boat and got into the depths of the lotus flower. I was shocked by a beach of gulls and herons.” It has also been praised by people and spread to this day.
When Li Qingzhao was 16, her mother brought her sister and brother to Bianjing (Kaifeng, the capital of the Song Dynasty) to reunite with her father and settle there. Li Qingzhao married Zhao Mingcheng, a 21-year-old Thai student at age 18, in Bianjing. Zhao Mingcheng is from Zhucheng, Shandong. Father Zhao and Father Li were originally officials in the imperial court. Father Li was ousted due to a court fight and returned to his native Shandong with his family. Father Zhao later died of illness in Kyoto, and the Zhao family also returned to Shandong. Li Qingzhao began living in seclusion with Zhao Mingcheng at the age of 25 at their former home in Qingzhou for more than ten years. They live a quiet and elegant life here. They are passionate about piano, chess, calligraphy and painting, guessing books and fighting tea, and are passionate about collecting cultural relics, appreciating calligraphy, painting, and inscribed ancient books, and writing books. Zhao Mingcheng is a gold stone scholar and collector, and his book “The Golden Stone Record” has been passed down to future generations. Li Qingzhao is a famous lyricist. Their husband, Luanfeng and Ming, can be called an immortal couple. Li Qingzhao calls himself “Master Yi An”, and his office is called “Return to the Hall”, which is interpreted as Tao Yuanming's “Return to Xi Xi”. Later, Zhao Mingcheng was appointed as an official in Laizhou, and later transferred to Zizhou. During the couple's separation, she wrote the famous acacia phrase “One Cut of Plum,” which is “one kind of acacia, two places of idleness.” “I only lowered my eyebrows, but I was interested.” He showed a strong sense of nostalgia and was praised by future generations. Li Qingzhao wrote quite a few words during this period.
When Li Qingzhao was 43, the Jin people invaded, and Bian Jing soon fell. The Jin people captured Song Huizong, the father and son of Qin Zong and went north. Historically known as the “Jingkang Incident,” the Northern Song Dynasty came to an end. Later, Kang Wang and Zhao Gou ascended the throne in Jiankang (Nanjing) to change to Yuan Jianyan, and the Southern Song Dynasty began. The situation in the north is becoming increasingly tense. From time to time, scattered soldiers are harassed in Shandong. Li Qingzhao is very concerned about the cultural relics he has collected. At this time, she and Zhao Mingcheng also collected a large number of cultural relics at their home in Zizhou. The disaster was not alone. At this point, Zhao Mingcheng's mother died of illness in Jiangning. They packed up and selected 15 carts of cultural relics and were escorted by Zhao Mingcheng to Jiangning to evade war and death. Meanwhile, Li Qingzhao went back to Qingzhou to sort out the cultural relics from more than ten houses, and he couldn't help picking them over and over again. Qingzhou immediately fell into disrepair. In the midst of the panic, Li Qingzhao hastily brought some cultural relics and fled the refugee stream. The precious cultural relics from more than ten houses in Qingzhou were reduced to ashes. Li Qingzhao accompanied the refugees all the way across the Yangtze River by land and water to Jiangning to be reunited with Zhao Mingcheng. However, the flames of war soon approached Jiang Ning, and the couple became involved in the fugitive army. While passing through the Wujiang River (now in Ma'anshan, Anhui) on his way to escape, Li Qingzhao thought that Xiang Yu, the king of Western Chu, would not give up. He touched the scene and wrote a sad and pompous poem “Summer Excerpt”: Life is an outstanding person, and death is also a ghost. So far, I think about Xiang Yu and refuse to go beyond Jiangdong. This poem secretly satirizes the Song dynasty's greed for life and fear of death and defeat. Zhao Mingcheng was also appointed by the imperial court at this time, but he was unable to take office due to his illness. Zhao Mingcheng is 49 years old and Li Qingzhao is 46 years old.
Alone, she escorted the remaining cultural relics and continued to flee south with the refugees. From Zhenjiang to Shaoxing to Ningbo, to Zhoushan, to the sea via waterways to Wenzhou, they drifted in places such as Fujian, Zhejiang, etc., where they lived without a place to live. Finally, they followed the Southern Song dynasty and fled to Lin'an (Hangzhou, the capital of Southern Song). After a long walk, there were few cultural relics left with me. After Li Qingzhao arrived in Lin'an, he was alone and unattached. The loss of ancient books, calligraphy, and painting artifacts caused her great mental trauma. Her exile life brought her physical pain and suffering, leaving Li Qingzhao in a desperate situation where there was no way to go, poor, and sick. At this point, Zhang Ruzhou, a small imperial court official, had long coveted Li Qingzhao's leftover cultural relics, and his ingenious rhetoric tricked her trust. Li Qingzhao agreed to marry him when he thought that he was half a hundred years old and had nothing to rely on in his old age. Who expected Zhang Ruzhou to reveal his original form immediately after the marriage, forcing Li Qingzhao to hand over the cultural relics. Li Qingzhao naturally treated the relics like life, and Zhang committed domestic violence. At this point, Zhang Ruzhou's scum face was fully revealed. Li Qingzhao resolutely proposed a divorce, which definitely ended this three-month marriage. Since then, Li Qingzhao has been single and has lived a “cold, clean, desolate and miserable” life.
Li Qingzhao suffered the hardships of a broken country and the death of her family, her husband died of illness, wandering alone, and being deceived again. In the end, she lived a lonely old age in Lin'an. Despite going through countless tribulations, her obsession with literature never died out. She left us with her famous literary wealth through the ages with her fresh flowers and wonderful pens. In particular, her obsessive research and writing of lyrics was already famous in the literary world at the time, and was later called the “representative of the gracious school” and “a generation of words.” Although she wrote only 780 lyrics in her lifetime, these priceless literary treasures established her place in the history of ancient Chinese literature and had a profound impact on future generations. The style of Li Qingzhao's work is defined by Jingkang, and the style of content varies greatly from one to the other. Her early works truly reflect the elegant and quiet boudoir life. The white brush style is clean, bright, delicate and graceful, with the natural charm of a young girl, and the feelings she had when the couple separated. During this period, there was a song called “Drunken Flower Yin,” which was particularly appreciated by writers of all time. It says, “The fog is thick, the clouds are always sad, and the Swiss brain sells gold beasts. The festival is once again in Chongyang. Jade Pillow Chef cools down and clears up in the middle of the night. When Dongli puts wine at dusk, it has a dark fragrance. Mo Dao is indecisive. The wind sweeps the westerly wind, and people are thinner than yellow flowers.” This phrase describes the feelings I had when I separated after marriage. The meaning of the first two sentences is from the fog in the early morning to the thick clouds in the evening, and the long daylight makes people infinitely sad. The incense in the beast-shaped incense burner has finished burning, yet the sadness in my heart is still endless. From the scene to the feeling, it creates an atmosphere of desolation and loneliness. The following three sentences describe people's miserable feelings of loneliness, despair, and love each other in the middle of the night during the Chung Yeung Festival. “Dongli brings wine...” The lyricist drank alcohol alone in front of Dongli Chrysanthemum on the evening of Chung Yeung Festival to get rid of his worries. The last three sentences, “Don't lose your soul, the wind sweeps you, people are thinner than yellow flowers”. These last three sentences are a finishing touch. They directly express the feeling of sadness (no modo is ecstasy) and an embittered face (people are thinner than yellow flowers). One of the words “thin”, which can be described as “eye of the word,” sums up the essence of the whole word. She sent these words to her husband Zhao Mingcheng in a letter. After receiving it, Zhao really liked it, but was unwilling to fall behind, so he asked his boss for a leave of absence, closed his door to thank the guests for three days, wrote 50 lyrics at home, and mixed these three sentences into them and invited friends to read them. After reading it, a friend said, “Out of 50 songs, only the three sentences “Don't despair, wind in the wind, people are thinner than yellow flowers” are good sentences. At this point, Zhao Ming is sincerely happy and willing to accept the downside.
Li Qingzhao is nicknamed “Li Santhin”. She is good at giving the word “thin” an emotional color to describe people or flowers and express her feelings. In addition to the song mentioned above, there is also “Green, Fat, Red, and Skinny” in “Like a Dream.” Another phrase, “Memories of Blowing in Phoenix Terrace,” is also written about loneliness and sorrow. The last three sentences of the previous sentence are “Newly born to lose weight. It's not about being sick; it's not a sad fall.” The last three sentences of the last sentence are “A place to focus, added now, a new feeling of sadness”, which refers to staring in the distance and missing my husband and making people lose weight even more.
After going south, the country broke down, her family died, and exile in a foreign country had a profound impact on her. Her works are filled with an atmosphere of worry for the country and the people, sorrow and desolation. Most of her paintings are sad, sad and sad feelings and thoughts about her hometown. The tribulations of life have led her to create deafening and inspiring works. Among them, the evacuees were in exile in “Wulingchun,” which was written in Jinhua, Zhejiang: “I heard that Shuangxichun is good, and I also plan to go boating lightly. “I'm just afraid of Shuangxi and Mengzhou, and I can't carry much sorrow,” she wrote about her deep, deep sorrow that she couldn't dispel. The most famous song “The Sound Is Slow”: “Seek and Seek, Cold and Clear, Miserable, and Miserable. It's hardest to rest when it's warm or cold. It has a distinct color of the times. The entire lyrics come together in one go, like crying, and deeply moving. The first sentence uses 14 overlapping characters to graphically express the author's feelings. The following “bit by bit” goes back and forth, showing the author's melancholy and uneasy state of mind of loneliness. The whole word is full of tears, lingers with grievances, and is extremely artistic. Her works during this period have achieved high artistic achievements and established her position in the literary world.
Her words were already popular in manuscripts in the literary world when she was in her 20s. Later, “Song Shi · Art Literature Chronicles” wrote seven volumes of “The Works of Yi An Jushi” and rewrote six volumes of “The Lyrics of Yi An”. A collection of poems and lyrics was published and has long been scattered. Descendants edited the book “Yu Yu's Lyrics.” Today, people have various editions of “Li Qingzhao's Collection of Notes”.
Li Qingzhao wrote far fewer poems than words. At the age of 17, she wrote the epic poem “The Song of Zhongxing in Wuxi and Two Songs by Zhang Wenqian”. Not only is she very knowledgeable in history, but she is also highly poetic. When she took refuge in Jinling, she climbed the famous Eight Wing Tower. She looked around and saw the broken river and mountains. She felt a lot of emotion and wrote “The Eight Wing Lou”: The Seven Eighth Wing Lou, which was elegant throughout the ages, and left behind and sad for future generations. Water runs through the south for 3,000 miles, and the air pressure is on the 14 states of Jiangcheng. This poem is full of pride and sorrow. From this, it can be seen that Li Qingzhao directly addresses national affairs in his poetry, expressing his will to serve the country and his compassion for the people. His style is strong and not inferior.
After being stranded in Hangzhou, Li Qingzhao also retrieved her late husband's legacy “The Golden Stone Record” and wrote “The Chronicles of Jinshi” as a memoir. This long essay documents in detail the couple's process of collecting artifacts and appreciating ancient books, their lives in Qingzhou, and the loss of cultural relics during their escape, which is of great value to future generations of archaeology. Li Qingzhao is not only good at writing words; her famous academic paper “The Theory of Words” was the first systematic lexical monograph in ancient China, which aroused strong reactions at the time. According to “The Chronology of Li Qingzhao,” Li Qingzhao's “Tales” was written in the 2nd year of the Northern Song Dynasty (1108) (Li Qingzhao was 24 years old). At the time, Su Shi used poetry as words to cause controversy in the lyric world. People began to seriously think about issues such as the physicality, function, and characteristics of words. Chao Buzhi, Zhang Yi, Li Zhiyi, and Chen Shidao all expressed their opinions on these issues. Li Qingzhao elevated the experiences, feelings, and sporadic opinions of his predecessor relating to poetry into clear rational understanding and a more systematic theory of true colors, and put forward the idea of the phrase “not one family.” The division between words, poetry, and writing was emphasized, and the standards for excellent lyrics were explained in a systematic manner through evaluations of previous families. In other words, they strictly abide by the rules in terms of form, subjectively sentimental in terms of content, and elegant in style, so as to achieve strict adherence to the differences in poetry and maintain the true color of the words.
Li Qingzhao's ups and downs in her later life not only did not make her depressed, but instead made her achieve outstanding achievements in literature. Later generations often referred to her as “Three Li” along with Li Bai and Li Yu. As the couplets at Zhang Qiu and Li Qingzhao's former residence show:
