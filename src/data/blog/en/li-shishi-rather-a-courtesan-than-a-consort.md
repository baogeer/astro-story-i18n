 Author: Hou Hongbin
Li Shi Shi: A famous prostitute of both talents at the end of the Northern Song Dynasty. She is generously known as “General Fei.” Many of her stories are described in her notes, field history, and novel reviews. Earlier, you can see Zhang Duanyi's “Guier Collection”, Zhang Bangji's “The Record of Mozhuang”, and the Song Dynasty commentary “The Legacy of Xuanhe”. During Song Huizong's reign, he also traveled a lot. He was guided by several insiders to go to Li Shi Shi's home in small cars. He had relationships with famous writers Zhou Kongyan and Chao Chong. It is said that later Huizong summoned her to the Inner Palace and canonized as Lady Ying Guo or Consort Li Ming. Jin Bing invaded and Bian Jing fell, and the whereabouts of Li Shi Shi became widely debated and confused.
To explain why Song Huizong hooked up with Li Shi Shi, we must first start by talking about the nightlife during the Northern Song dynasty.
In 1077 AD, the total tax revenue of the Northern Song Dynasty reached 70,730,000 yuan. Of these, industrial and commercial tax accounts for 70%, and agricultural tax accounts for 30%. In today's rush of urbanization, this kind of industrial structure is simply envied and salivated. Bianjing in the Northern Song Dynasty and Lin'an in the Southern Song Dynasty were all large cities with a population of over 1 million, while Venice, the largest and busiest city in the West in the late Southern Song period, had a population of only 100,000 people; in addition, at night, Bianjing was involved in lighting projects, and the Song people went clubbing at night, intoxicated with gold fans, and were happy to death.
At the same time, Bianjing is also a cultural and art center. The Bianjing International Art Festival is held one after another, and there will also be a large number of peripheral exhibitions during the same period. However, at that time, it was neither called 798 nor Chuangku; it was called Washe or Gouban. And Emperor Song Huizong himself was the most famous painter at the time. Actually, what he is good at is not brushwork, flower and bird painting, but jewelry design. Unfortunately, he doesn't understand publicity at all, and the brand is overwhelmed. And Li Shi Shi is the chief singer of the biggest and most popular Fanlou KTV in Bianjing City. Both of them were great players in the art world at the time, and they had never been photographed.
For the first time, Song Huizong came to see her dressed up as a merchant. The meeting ceremony was “Two Purple Mushrooms, Two Haze Dies, Two Earthen Pearls, and Twenty White Gold (24 x 2)” from Naiku's collection. Wait, wait. Master Li only came to the site in the middle of the night. No fat powder was applied, and he was dressed in silicin. As soon as she saw Song Huizong, she didn't look good; he was good-looking, and his beard failed. Shi Li casually played the song “Falling Goose in the Sand” and walked away. As soon as Zhao Ji saw her arrogant attitude, she was immediately shocked: this is the supermodel I have always dreamed of. Voluptuous, beautiful, and naturally sexy stunners are everywhere. But the teacher is very thin and has very long eyes, very thick, very fleshy, and very cosmopolitan. Plus, they also have a naturally good voice, and are arrogant and tall. They never give the media a good face; things are rare and expensive; this is how the value of teachers and teachers is boosted.
Zhao Ji has been waiting for inspiration to come. However, every day, the beautiful women in the palace dangled around his eyes, leaving this jewelry designer with no taste. However, Shi Li's bare Indian hair was just right in line with his design philosophy. He went back to the palace in a nutshell. Master Li's agent was taken aback when he learned that this person who looked like a stupid doctor was the emperor. Shi Li is quiet and relaxed; he is not aloof: he only envies talents, not officials; we are not the kind of ignorant people.
Half a month later, Zhao Ji visited again and gave her teacher a gift box. When Shi Li saw the jewels designed by the emperor himself, he couldn't help but groan with happiness. This thing hasn't spread, and I still can't imagine what it looks like. It combines the solemnity of enamel, the simplicity of silverware, the brilliance of crystals, and the dazzling of diamonds. It seems that Zhao Ji is really an outstanding artist. Li Shi Shi couldn't help but secretly agree that Zhao Ji would dig a tunnel when she had time.
The agent immediately insured the jewelry. The insurance company considered that it was an isolated product designed by the emperor, and the request was that it must not be exposed while outdoors to prevent it from being robbed. As a result, every time Li Shishi went out, he wore a veil and covered his face with tulle or light cloth. As a result, no one in the world had a chance to see Zhao Ji's masterpiece in person. Instead, women all over the street imitated superstar Li Shishi's veil, thinking it was the most modern dress from a foreign country, saying it was a delicate balance between hiding and revealing. So, if you take a look at Zhang Zeduan's “Qingming River Map”, you can see that the new style invented by Li Shi Shi is very popular.
In fact, in addition to being on good terms with this high-ranking designer, Li Shishi also has ties with famous writers Yan Jidao, Qin Shaoyou, Zhou Bangyan, and even the painter Zhang Zeduan. Zhou Bangyan, in particular, has the same talent as Dou Wei, Lin Xi, and Zhang Yadong as the sum of his talents. For an outstanding Diva, Shi Li was nothing but sympathy. Once, Zhou Bangyan was in the middle of a conversation with his teacher, ignoring the arrival of the Holy Ghost. Zhou Bangyan hurriedly hid under the bed. Huizong warmly peeled the orange for his teacher. As soon as Huizong left, Zhou Bangyan climbed out from under the bed and wrote the lyrics of the song “Youth Tour”: “Together, Wu Yan wins the snow, breaking a new orange.” This phrase reached Huizong's ears. He was burning with jealousy. He ordered Cai Jing to drive Zhou Bangyan out of the country. Thinking about it and afraid of offending her, she recalled Zhou Bangyan.
It makes sense that Li Shi Shi would rather be a social flower; of course, there is a question of whether he can do it, but first of all, the question is whether he wants to do it — once in the palace, he will have to fight with those three grandmothers and six women for the rest of his life, and die every minute without a place to die; and in the act of doing things like old people every day, they can sing and reconcile with people who adore each other, and respect them as goddesses. Which destiny is better? Naturally, Master Li has his fair share.
