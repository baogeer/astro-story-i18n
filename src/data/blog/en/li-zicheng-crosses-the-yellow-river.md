 At the end of the Ming dynasty, the imperial court was corrupt, and the people didn't talk about their lives.
King Chuang Li Zicheng led the peasant revolt in Mizhi, Shaanxi, to cross the Yellow River and overthrow the rule of the Ming dynasty. It was early winter at the time, and the water in the Yellow River had not yet frozen, and only when the river froze to a solid ice force could pass smoothly.
What should I do? Chuang Wang was anxious day and night, so sad that his beard and hair all went white overnight.
Early the next morning, two people reported: “The Yellow River has frozen to a solid state.” Wang Daxi hurriedly ordered the three armies to cross the river quickly.
As soon as I got to the riverside to take a look, the river was still “buzzing” and flowing happily; it didn't freeze at all. However, there was a large pontoon bridge on it. Boat to boat, board to board, all flat and stable, just right for a large army to cross the river.
Chuang Wang was extremely puzzled. He was about to get off the horse to check. Suddenly, two people climbed up from the river, gave a punch to Chuang Wang, and loudly said, “Dawang, we are the boaters of the Yellow River. I heard that you wanted to cross the river for the benefit of the people, so it took a special night to build this pontoon bridge, so let's invite the army to hit the road.”
Chuang Wang was moved to tears: “I will definitely live up to the love of the people, and I must let everyone have a good life!” Having said that, I drove over to the bridge and led the troops east.
