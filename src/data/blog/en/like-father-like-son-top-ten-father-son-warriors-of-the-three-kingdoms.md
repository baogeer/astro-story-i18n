NO2 Ma Teng & Ma Chao
Ma Teng's life was glorified in the Romance of the Three Kingdoms, and he became a model for loyal martyrs. There is no way. Who made him Ma Chao's father? In history, Ma Teng wasn't that perfect, but he can also be considered a hero. At the end of the Eastern Han Dynasty, Ma Teng was a great power in the Gansu region. He joined the two most famous princes in Liangzhou at the time: Bian Zhang and Han Sui. At the time, the Eastern Han dynasty also sent Dong Zhuo, Zhang Wen, and Sun Jian to defeat the three lords. Later, Ma Teng and the others accepted the Han dynasty's offer and went to Chang'an to meet the emperor. He was canonized as General of the Western Conquest and was stationed in Meicheng. Later, Dong Zhuo was killed, Li Peng and Guo Yi were killed in Chang'an. Ma Teng brought the military and the king, was defeated by Guo Li, and returned to Liangzhou.
After Cao Cao became the prime minister of the Han dynasty, he fought against Yuan Shao. Yuan Shao ordered Gao Gan and Guo Yuan to attack the Guanzhong region. Zhong Yi asked Ma Teng for help, and Ma Teng sent Ma Chao to lead 10,000 cavalry to defeat Guo Yuan in the Hedong area, which can be said to have helped Cao Cao a great deal. Since that battle, Ma Chao's name has attracted worldwide attention. Later, Ma Teng had a bad relationship with Han Sui. Ma Teng wanted to move to another place and become an official in the imperial court. Cao Cao agreed to him, built a mansion for him in Xuchang, and made him a guard. Judging from these historical facts, Ma Teng's relationship with Cao Cao doesn't seem to be bad. Even Ma Teng moved his entire family of over 300 to Xuchang, fully demonstrating his trust in Cao Cao.
It didn't work out later. Cao Cao wanted to conquer Zhang Lu from Hanzhong. Ma Chao began to doubt whether Cao Cao would take the opportunity to annex him, and then set up an army to rebel. As a result, Cao Cao killed more than 300 members of Ma Teng's family. Everyone knows what happened later.
