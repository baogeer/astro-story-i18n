Still at the end of the Yuan Dynasty, it seemed that women who died out of love one by one always had to write history with those hegemons who fought for supremacy. This time it was a woman from the countryside. She moved the world with her ordinary stories.
On the banks of the Huai River, an inconspicuous village. At a private school, behind a table, there were two students who were confused about Dong Dong. The girl's name was Liu Cuicui, and the boy's name was Jin Ding. In today's middle school education, one common problem is the so-called early love, which hurts teachers' hearts and parents' hearts. However, judging by common sense, the attitude of teachers and parents even suppresses the sexual spirit.
Liu Cuicui and Jin Ding have gradually grown up. Liu Cuicui is elegant, and Jin Ding is smart and handsome. Just like today's middle school students occasionally hand a note and throw their eyes away. The two also gradually selfishly agree. Classmates also kept making fun of each other, saying that they are the same age, same window and table, and it is only natural that they will become a couple in the future. Liu Cuicui blushes every time she hears such remarks, even though she is shy, but she actually doesn't refute it; she acquiesces.
Soon after graduation, Jin Ding quietly handed Liu Cuicui a note, only to see that it read:
The twelve-railing cloisonne platform, the spring breeze shines everywhere;
Dongyuan peach trees and western garden willows, why not move to one place to plant them.
This is a very bold love poem full of provocative meaning. Liu Cuicui not only didn't dislike it, but she also took all the affectionate meaning between the lines and couldn't wait to unravel the secret of her heart:
Every time I hated Zhu Yingtai in my whole life, why didn't I open up early;

Compared with the phrase “there have been many blemishes since ancient times,” “many blessings have been lost since ancient times” more reflect the fate of ancient women.
Liu Cuicui caused her endless problems because she was born beautiful. It was Zhang Shicheng who set up troops around the Huai River at the end of the Yuan Dynasty. He took over Huai'an. Amidst military chaos, Liu Cuicui was discovered by Zhang Shicheng's general Li Hushan. He marveled at Liu Cuicui's beauty, so he couldn't help but agree with her.
When people go to an empty building, everything in the family naturally seems to Jin Ding that “things are people stop doing everything.” All the customs of the past, the sorrow of today, everything in the world is dead...
Rainy spring and summer are followed by clear autumn. Jin Ding misses Liu Cuicui all the time. He often wanders in the valleys and fields outside the city, exhausting himself — trying to resist his sorrow.
Order was slightly restored, and Jin Ding couldn't wait to leave his parents, carry his baggage on his back, and embark on a aimless journey to find his beloved wife. When he left home, his mother looked at him with kind and peaceful eyes: “Go, kid, don't miss the good weather.” They looked at each other for a while, then nodded to say goodbye. He gently brought the door up—so he left her and left her forever. He didn't know until his death; this is also his eternal farewell to his mother.
Wear the moon all the way, eat and sleep in the open air, and gradually run out of entanglements. The bladder was exhausted, and it was difficult to move, but I couldn't lose my heart. Jin Ding begged for people during the day and stayed at Broken Temple or at the bridge at night. He got the whereabouts of Li Hushan. Li Hushan was crowned general by Zhang Shicheng due to his military merit, and currently guards Huzhou. ,
Jin Ding arrived in Huzhou. General Li's mansion was a high-ranking man in Huzhou. He was very brave. Jin Ding stood outside the door hesitantly watching, fearful of hesitation, and didn't know how to do it. The hesitation was temporary, and Jin Ding gathered the courage to walk towards the General Palace. Jin Ding told the gatekeeper that he is from Huai'an. His sister and family have disappeared in the midst of military chaos. Over the past few years, there has been no news. Now I heard that she is living in General Lee's house. She has traveled thousands of miles herself, hoping to meet her sister. He paused for a moment, then said, “My name is Liu Jinding, and my younger sister is Liu Cuicui. She knows the scriptures and history. She is 17 years old when she disappeared. Now it's been 7 years; she should be 24.” The gatekeeper was a middle-aged man and a man who had experienced many vicissitudes. Although he had been at the public gate for a long time, he was simple and enthusiastic. Seeing that Jin Ding had gone through all the ups and downs, his face was full of grief, and immediately informed him.
Soon, Jin Ding was summoned from inside. There was a middle-aged warrior sitting in a tiger leather chair in the tall hall. This person is Li Hushan. Jin Dingqiang endured the “hate of taking his wife” and stepped forward to offer a ceremony.
Liu Cuicui was in the inner room at the time. Hearing that her brother had been found from his hometown, I felt a bit strange. Since there are no older brothers in the family, the person who came was undoubtedly her former husband, Jin Ding. She carefully dressed herself up and tried to restore her appearance from seven years ago. She suppressed her inner excitement and quickly walked out of it. They met. In front of Li Hushan, the two met as a brother and sister. Jin Ding kindly asked how Liu Cuicui was doing. Liu Cuicui wanted Jin Ding to stay in Fuchu for a while and take a good rest. Fortunately, they have similar looks, have so-called “husband and wife faces,” and have some similar demeanors. Li Hushan is convinced that they are indeed brothers and sisters whom he hasn't seen for many years.
Liu Cui Tsui is Li Hushan's favorite person. The scholar in front of me also traveled thousands of miles to visit Liu Cuicui's older brother. He loved the house and Wu. Li Hushan repeatedly told the servant to prepare meals, change clothes, and set the bed, and treated Jin Ding as a guest. The next day, Li Hushan obtained Jin Ding's approval, and even left Jin Ding as his office, his current secretary. Since then, Jin Ding has handled books and documents in the front office every day. He has been respectful and honest, has handled matters in an orderly manner, and has earned the respect of others. As a result, he has been relied upon even more by Li Hushan. Li Hushan often compliments Jin Ding to visitors.
Li Hushan is waiting hard for Jin Ding, but Jin Ding went to great lengths to find him this time, not to work for half an official, but to find a loving wife. Now that we have no chance to see each other again, there is nothing we can do about it. Facing the boudoir, far away, and in vain, we are calling for nothing.
