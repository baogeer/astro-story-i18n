Liu Lanzhi: Seeing Xu Ling in the Southern Dynasty, “New Song in the Yutai”. “The Peacock Flies Southeast” is the longest narrative poem in the folk song of Han Le Fu in China. The title is “An Ancient Poem by Jiao Zhongqing's Wife”. The creation period is roughly during the Jian'an years of Emperor Xian of the Eastern Han Dynasty. The author is unknown. The main story is that Liu Lanzhi married to the Jiao family; Jiao's mother did not allow her, but she was sent back to her mother's family, and her brother forced her to remarry. On her wedding night, Lan Zhi threw herself into the water, and Jiao Zhongqing also died as a martyr. From the late Han dynasty to the southern dynasty, this poem was widely distributed and continuously processed, and eventually became the most outstanding long-form narrative poem in the Han Dynasty folk songs of Lefu.
I believe many young girls made a vow when they were in their early ten years: never get married. The women in the textbook were all oppressed by feudal society, suffering and deep hatred. At that time, when I finished reading “The Peacock Flies Southeast,” I fell into a huge panic. I felt that once I got married, I would be abandoned by people every minute, and that there would definitely be a bad mother-in-law, forcing me to sweep the ground and feed the chickens to cook and polish every day. Maybe I still had a whip in my hand. Of course, the city later banned chicken breeding, and my nightmare came to an end. If I do have feminist tendencies today, don't doubt it, I was frightened by books when I was a kid.
Some basic descriptions of Liu Lanzhi are as follows: “Thirteen learned to knit, fourteen to learn tailoring. Fifteen volumes; sixteen poetry readings.” At that time, Liu Lanzhi was studying at the Lujiangfu School of Etiquette. It was managed in a closed manner, mainly training good daughters-in-law who are beautiful, beautiful, and knowledgeable. Everyone knows that over a thousand years ago, a lifelong career for women around the world was to find a good man (in fact, this is still the case today). However, in addition to cultural classes and art classes offered at the School of Etiquette, you also need to pass a devil test before you graduate. Devil-style training is: while weaving and tailoring clothes, a special person abuses them, and the little girl must bow and laugh all over her face. The more she curses her hands and feet, the more beneficial it is. If you don't cry or make noise for two weeks and don't look for life, you can graduate. Back then, the young lady who couldn't graduate was like a college student who couldn't get a degree certificate today; she couldn't find her grandmother's family.
Liu Lanzhi obtained the qualification of a special student with unrivaled perseverance. After graduation, she married Jiao Zhongqing, a junior governor of Lujiang Province. From then on, I began my life as a working woman. I wake up every day when the chicken cries and only go to bed late at night. I can't see the sun for a few days, so I'm all wrapped up in the computer room. Liu Yuzhi has woven five cloths in three days. There is no salary, and it doesn't reflect the value of labor at all. The devil's training is only a month, but once the Jiao family works, it takes a lifetime. I wonder how many teardrops are in my eyes, how can we prevent spring from flowing from winter to summer?
Fortunately, Jiao Zhongqing was an abusive person. Seeing his wife work so hard, she slipped home to spend time with Liu Lanzhi after work. However, Jiao Mu disagreed. What is she accusing Lanzi of being rude; she is simply accusing her that she seduced her son, made him indulge in love, lose his fighting spirit, and be willing to work as a mediocre, and that his career has not improved. Breaking them up and asking for a “Dongjiaxian woman” for her son is to save his son!
This is how Liu Lanzhi was evicted from the Jiao family, even though Jiao Zhongqing asked for it again and again. When Lan Zhi returned to her mother's house, her mother's family was unhappy, and the etiquette college she attended was even worse: how can we get our future graduates married? Based on the three-pack principle of “distribution of graduation packages, marriage with completion package, marriage with divorce package”, under the mediation of the School of Etiquette, the county magistrate proposed marriage for the third son, and Taishoulai proposed marriage for the fifth son, and there was a car and horse in front of the door for a while. As the playful Wilde said, “Women remarry because they hate their original husbands; men remarry because they love their original wives too much.” Laneige refused to get married again because she is still in love with her ex-husband. However, as soon as Lan Chi's older brother saw that her sister had a better future after her divorce, he urged her to remarry.
The love between Liu Lanzhi and Jiao Zhongqing apparently only developed after marriage. Amidst self-grievances and self-pity, their relationship is strong. The two “raised their bodies to clear the pond” and the other “hung their own southeast branch”, leaving behind two trees and a pair of birds at the top of the loess ridges, and sang for years.
