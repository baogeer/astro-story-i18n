 Legend has it that Dragon King lives in the Qiantang River. He has an aggressive temperament, causing the tide to rise and fall without a fixed period of time. The fields along both sides of the river are often flooded, causing people to live fearlessly all day long.
At that time, there was a poor family of fishermen living on the riverside, and the couple had a son, Liuhe. When he was six and five years old, his dad overturned his boat while fishing in the river and drowned.
You can't fish without a fishing boat. Since then, the Rokuhe family has been even poorer. The two wives used two bamboo sticks, each tied to a small round net. When the tide came, they ran barefoot in front of the tide to catch tidal head fish. Catching tidal fish is dangerous; running a little slower will cause the tide to roll away. In order to make a living, the two daughters had to take this risk again.
One day, when the two wives were fishing, unexpectedly, the tide came very fast and was very aggressive. When Liuhe saw that the momentum wasn't good, they held her hand and ran away, but it was too late. One wave came and dragged his mother into the vortex.
Liuhe no longer has a wife, and is even more lonely, clueless, and unreliable. He was also sad and angry. While crying, he did his best to remove large and small stones from the riverside hill and throw them hard into the river. He vowed to fill the Qiantang River with stones so that the tide would not run rampant and cause harm everywhere. He threw stones in his hands and cursed constantly in his mouth:
“Abominable tide, damn Dragon King! I want to move the mountain down and fill up your Qiantang River!”
The roof, doors and windows of the Crystal Palace were smashed into many holes by the stones lost by Liuhe. The stones were piled up on the steps in front of the Crystal Palace into a hill, which almost blocked the gate. Dragon King heard Liuhe's curse and walked to the entrance of the Crystal Palace. Unexpectedly, he was hit on the head by a stone left by Liuhe, which crooked one of his dragon horns. There was a big pimple on the back of my head, and the pain was screaming.
Liuhe cried on the riverside, cursed, and threw stones at the heart of the river. One day, two days... a whole seven, seven, forty-nine days. This day happened to be August 18. He suddenly heard a “thunderous” sound coming from far and near, and the tide of the Qiantang River was rushing in. An overbearing general Crab was standing at the head of the tide, leading a team of bowed shrimp minions, and the Dragon King was covered under the Yellow Luo umbrella in the back. Soon, Dragon King came to Liuhe and said:
“Kid, kid, don't cry, don't cry, don't throw stones. If you want gold, silver, jewelry, I'll give you whatever you say.”
“Dragon King, Dragon King, listen, I don't want your gold, silver, and jewels! You have to rely on me for two things. If you don't, I'll crush your Crystal Palace with stones and fill up this Qiantang River!”
“Which two things, talk about them.”
“The first one will be sent back to my mother right away; the second one will not allow the tide to rise randomly from now on; the tide is only allowed to follow the rules and follow the rules and rise to this point in the mountains.”
Dragon King was full of reluctance, but he was afraid that Liuhe would actually fill up the Qiantang River and collapse his Crystal Palace, but they all had to agree.
Dragon King sent Liuhe Nun right away. How happy Roku is! The two wives went home happy.
Since then, the tide in the Qiantang River has decreased a lot, and it has stabilized when it rises to the side of that small mountain. It is only on August 18 every year that the tide is higher than normal. This is because Dragon King has suffered the losses of Liuhe and is afraid that his subordinates will get into trouble again and go out to cruise the river himself. When people feel the tempers of the tide, they are no longer afraid of it, turning the deserted beaches along both sides of the river into good fields and growing green crops. To thank Liuhe for defeating King Dragon, posterity built a pagoda on the hill where he moved stones. This is the “Liuhe Pagoda.”
