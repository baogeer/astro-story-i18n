Wang Xiucai had a hard time getting into the city. Without money, Wang Xiucai was only able to wander around the city. He finally met a friend who does business in the city. His friend is a stingy person, but recently signed a lot of orders, so today he was very happy. He borrowed Wang Xiucai's large bag of money. The next day, he went to test people. Unfortunately, he didn't lose heart. Instead, he stayed in the city, but something magical happened in a dream. One day he decided to go back to the countryside to recuperate. The night before he left, he had a very strange dream. He had a very strange dream. The ancient craftsman Luban said to him, “There is a place that has just been flooded. The bridge there has just been a big flood. The bridge was destroyed. Now there is a great need for a bridge there, so go!” He went to a very poor place, but he heard his hometown say, “A few days ago, there was a lot of water and destroyed the only bridge here.” He immediately followed the method Luban taught him, but what Luban taught him was a stone bridge, but there was a serious shortage of stone there, so he thought of using wood instead of stone. However, wood is wood after all, stone is stone, and wood's ability to withstand the impact of water flow is still not as good as that of stone. Although the bridge was tough and persisted for a while, it eventually collapsed.
As a result, Wang Xiucai thought of using boats one by one as a bridge. At the beginning, he encountered some difficulties. The dangerous river didn't flow too fast; otherwise, the boat would spread out. Finally, the bridge was built. Since then, everyone can cross the river safely.
