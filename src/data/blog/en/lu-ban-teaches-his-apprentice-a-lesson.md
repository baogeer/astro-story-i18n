 Among Luban's many apprentices, one named Zhao Xian is a character who blinks his eyebrows. He was born smart, and he likes to cheat and skate for others. It's also strange that Luban looked away for a moment and didn't see his true face. During his three years as a teacher, Zhao Xian also learned some fur techniques.
“Master, why does everyone respect you so much but they don't take me seriously, I'm not convinced!” Suddenly, Zhao Xian proposed to compete with his master.
Luban saw that his apprentice was helpless and unhumble. Although he was a little annoyed, he could say it in his heart. Why don't you take this opportunity to teach this guy who doesn't know how rich he is and arrogant. Furthermore, he can test what real skills he really has and to what extent he has learned his craft. As a result, they gladly agreed and said to Zhao Xian, “OK, let's build each bridge tonight until the rooster crows. If anyone doesn't finish on time, even if someone loses, what do you think?”
Zhao Xian complied: “Yes! One word is for sure. However, we need to keep the distance between the two of us so as not to be interfered with each other. You go to Yingcheng to build a bridge, and I will go to Shajin to start construction.” Having said that, everyone is ready to go.
In other words, this day was the first day of February. Helplessly, the sky wasn't beautiful. Not only was there no moon at night, but the wind was raging, and torrential rain was pouring down, and it was so dark that I couldn't reach out for five fingers. In order to illuminate, Luban quickly borrowed a crystal lamp that was not afraid of wind and rain from the East China Sea and built the bridge overnight. Zhao saw that the weather was bad and he was unable to start construction. He was in a hurry to walk around the riverside, scratching his ears and burning his heart. But there was nothing he could do. I thought to myself that the weather was so bad, Master definitely wouldn't be able to start construction. Maybe he was lying under the covers, sleeping, and dreaming. It was so comfortable! In order to find out the truth, Zhao Xian decided to sneak away and find out. Far away, I saw a beacon hanging high, illuminating the ground as bright as daylight. The piers had already been erected on both sides of the river. Luban was sawing wooden boards to lay the bridge deck. There were also many people watching in the rain and cheering for the master. The scene was extremely popular. However, Luban didn't allow them to be busy; he was just working fast, silent, and in high spirits. Seeing that the master had accomplished his job, Zhao Xian frowned and thought it through. As soon as he hit his head, he said, “Yes, as long as you stop the master from building the bridge deck, then it's not a complete project; if you don't finish, it's not a win. As a result, he held up his head and stretched his neck, pinching his throat and learned how to chirp the rooster: “Whoa whoa -” Not to mention, his imitation was so vivid that it made all the chickens in Yingcheng chirp along.
When Luban heard the chicken barking, he had to say goodbye to the townspeople and stop working and go home. Halfway through, I was met by someone who fought back, and Gengfu asked with concern, “Master's Bridge completed?” Luban said, “I'm still short of breath; I haven't been able to get on the bridge deck. As we agreed in advance, the chicken will cry until it starts. Now that the chicken has called, I have no choice but to confess defeat!” Gan Fu shouted “yay” and said, “Strange! At this point, less than 2, why are the chickens barking?” The conversation didn't go away. It just happened that the soldiers on the night patrol walked over with a sneaky guy. Luban immediately recognized that the person who had been arrested was Zhao Xian. He was in doubt and hurriedly asked, “Why did the apprentice come here?” Zhao Xian was so ashamed that he bowed his head and didn't speak. When the soldiers saw that Luban knew Zhao Xian, they hurriedly bowed their hands and said, “This late-night stealing chicken cry disrupts people's hearts and affects dormancy. In order to maintain the peace of the Yingcheng side, we took it on the spot.” As soon as Ruban and his wife heard it, they immediately understood. Mindful of his mentor and apprenticeship, Luban relied on his face to seek personal affection from the officers and soldiers and let go of Zhao Xian. Zhao Xian was very grateful. She knelt down in front of her master and promised: “Thank Master for forgiveness. The disciples knew their mistakes and never dared again.” Chief Lu sighed, “As long as you have the sincerity to repent, be honest, learn the arts, and be a steady person, you will still be a good apprentice for me in the future.” After all, with the help of Zhao Xian, they turned around, and the two mentors and apprentices completed the construction of the bridge together.
The Dragon King from the Four Seas was amazed to hear that Luban had built a bridge overnight. They all gathered along the waterway to watch the bridge and congratulated Luban. Later generations called this bridge “Dragon Hui Bridge.” When the Four Seas Dragon Kings came to see the bridge, they all entered and left the watergate on the east side of Yingcheng, so people also called this watergate a “dragon gate.”
The following year, coinciding with the spring droughts in Chu, Luban led his disciples to build many water wheels and distribute them to the common people to collect water to fight the drought in order to solve urgent needs and eliminate the suffering of the people. After a few days, all the water in the mainland's ponds, weirs, and harbors was drained by cars, and the drought situation was still serious. Without watering, the farmland began to crack, and the seedlings turned yellow. The direct result of this development was that there was no harvest of particles. Luban saw it in his eyes and was anxious. While walking and thinking, he saw a crystal lamp that had not yet been returned to his home, so he had an idea and decided to ask Dragon King of the East China Sea to rescue him...
