 The 34th year of Guangxu, 1908 AD. Emperor Guangxu and Empress Dowager Cixi passed away a few days apart. A large number of people were fearful and uneasy. It seemed that as soon as Cixi died, the country lost its backbone and didn't know what to do. At this point, however, someone filled in the “Hundred Characters Order,” with a portrait of Cixi, which appeared in the newspaper: they scolded Cixi, an old demon woman who died in the country, and said that during the nearly half century of ruling the dynasty, she had knocked the rivers and mountains of the Qing dynasty into a mess. A large amount of territory on the Chinese frontier and a large amount of money in the treasury were given to the imperialist countries. When she went to Yin Cao's capital, she would definitely be afraid to meet Lu Hou, the ancestor of Han Dynasty, and Wu Zetian of the Tang dynasty. The words are as follows:
In the depths of the clouds, write a picture of Chanjuan, clothed in a light feather. It is forbidden to rise and die to hate sword-like heroism through the ages. Shield up the frontier, lose with fierce hands and lose lightly, wander underground, and be ashamed to encounter Han, Pheasants, and Tang Geese.
This “hundred character order” made the Qing government very angry, and it became sensational news. It was a long time later that people learned that its author was a young woman, Lu Bicheng.
Lu Bicheng is a native of Jingde, Anhui. He was born in the 9th year of Guangxu, that is, 1884 AD. Her father, Lu Fengqi, was a three-year junior in Guangxu, and his family is a student. Lu Bicheng and her sisters Lu Huiru and Lu Meisun claim to be “Huainan Sanlu, famous all over the world.” When Lu Bicheng was 12 years old, his achievements in poetry, calligraphy, and painting were already quite impressive. Fan Fanshan, who was famous as a talented person at the time, was in the same year as Lu Fengqi's Han Lin. After reading Lu Bicheng's poetry, he couldn't help but be amazed. When someone told him that it was just a work by a 12-year-old girl, he initially didn't believe that Lu Bicheng could write something so exciting at a young age.
With her own talents and the taunts of her parents, 20-year-old Lu Bicheng is already a young and famous girlfriend in the Beijing-Tianjin area. I often see her published articles in newspapers, and her whereabouts are often seen at various arts and literature gatherings. When “Tai Kung Pao” was first published, she was one of the main writers. Qiu Jin and Wu Zhiying fell in love with her at first sight. Qiu Jin founded “China Women's Daily”, and her handwriting was her handwriting.
When Yuan Shikai became the governor, funds were raised to set up Beiyang Women's College. Fu Zengxiang was the principal, and Lu Bicheng was specially nominated as the chief teacher. As a result, Lu Bicheng stayed at the highest school for women at the time for seven or eight years, and was later nominated to supervise the school. She combined traditional Chinese virtues with Western ideas of democracy and freedom; she combined traditional Chinese scholarship with Western natural science knowledge, making Beiyang Women's College one of the birthplaces of modern Chinese women's civilization. For example, Yuan Shikai's highly respected tutor Zhou Daoru, and Zhou Enlai's wife, Deng Yingchao, have all personally listened to Lu Bicheng's lectures here.
After Yuan Shikai became the interim president, Lu Bicheng entered the Xinhua Palace to serve as a confidential secretary of the Grand President's government office. This year, she was only 28 years old. She planned to do a big job, but as a result, the impact of life caused her to live a semi-secluded life from now on.
The first time life hit Lu Bicheng hard was when her father died. Lu Fengqi died in the year of the Sino-Japanese War. His wife, Yan Shi, returned to her hometown from Beijing to dispose of her ancestral property. Some members of the Lui family used despicable methods and instigated the fanatics to kidnap Yan. Lu Bicheng heard the news in Beijing, petitioned for help everywhere, and wrote letters to her father's friends and students asking for help. Meanwhile, all kinds of pressure came to all levels of government in Anhui, and all kinds of concerns came to the widows and orphans. Naturally, the matter was successfully resolved. The townspeople surnamed Wang, who had been close friends with Lu Bicheng since childhood, became wary. They thought that Lu Bicheng, at a young age, could call for wind and rain. She would pass through the door in the future and become a daughter-in-law of the Wang family. How could she shock the government office if she wasn't happy. “The small temple cannot provide for the Big Buddha.” The Wang family requested an divorce. The Lu family's orphan daughter and widow were unwilling to argue, and the matter was settled. If it were today, men and women got married since childhood. The environment they grew up in was different, and there was a huge difference in their level of knowledge and life experience. The two parties agreed to divorce, which would be an act of wisdom. However, in that era, when a girl quit her marriage to her mother-in-law's family, it was simply a great shame and a great disgrace. At one point, Lu Bicheng complained about herself. Thanks to her strong self-esteem, she survived and made her decide not to get married for the rest of her life.
Women who don't seem to be planning to get married, even though they are proud at work, always have a strong sense of loneliness and depression after finishing their work, especially when they are alone. In order to seek spiritual belonging, eight or nine people inevitably convert to religion, and Lu Bicheng is no exception. While working in Beijing, she often chatted with a generation of high priests and the monk Dixan. The monk Dixan said to her, “If you pay off your debts, you'll be fine; however, you know how hard it is to repay your debts, so remember not to borrow again.” The debt mentioned here should refer to all the evil debts in the world. The Buddha said there are eight hardships in life. Apart from life, old age, sickness, and death, there is also that resentment causes suffering; love separates from suffering; the five riches of suffering are abundant; suffering cannot be sought. They say hate causes suffering; they say they don't want to have a party but rather get together. The relationship between Lu Bicheng and Yuan Shikai made Lu Bicheng hide in deep resentment and suffering.
Lu Bicheng entered the Xinhua Palace to be nominated as a confidential government secretary to President Yuan Shikai. Later, Yuan Shikai actively prepared for the restoration of the imperial system, and a group of people from the Security Council acted as drummers for the restoration of Yuan Shikai's imperial system. Although it is difficult for Lu Bicheng to follow the folklore, he is unaccustomed to the despicable behavior of people who are generally pro-inflammatory. He left Beijing and went south. Bong Mu stayed in seclusion in Shanghai, studied behind closed doors, and did not know anything about the world. However, after Yuan Shikai's defeat, Lu Bicheng still couldn't escape justice and was scolded by the Chinese people. As a result, the idea of sheltering from the world surrounded her even more, making her feel like a dream. As a result, she went abroad to study literature at Columbia University in the US, then moved to Europe, toured places of interest and monuments on the European continent, and finally settled on the shores of Lake Geneva in Switzerland, dedicated to the “Campaign to Stop Nursing Students.” He fasted for many years, and his heart was full of Zen.
After the success of the Northern Expedition, Lu Bicheng gladly returned home. At this point, she was almost half a hundred years old. Although she was wearing a Western-style shirt and wearing light makeup, she was already feeling like stopping the water and was even more relaxed. She often recited “Namu Amitabha” and called herself Master Saint In. When she arrived in Beijing, she repeatedly chanted Li Qingzhao's “Things are people who stop doing everything; they want to shed tears first.” She poured all her emotions into “Miluo's Grudge.”
Cuigongping Peak, Hongqu Palace wall, still see the old Tianfu. Sad at Mai Xiu, he overcame the vicissitudes and got carried away by the bus. Recognising that the sun is shining in black, how many times have you hurried back and forth? Defeated with Jackdaw, and took over Chuiyang's past.
Gossiping about the past of the Southern Dynasty. Who is Zhong Qingyou, picking incense fragments, spreading wax in the Han Palace, Qin Jingying, and Yingxing. In one case, Luhua is unfounded? However, Jiangcheng is full of singing strings, and it is sad that Huangling is full of ups and downs. I'm also afraid to say that the flowers fall in the new pavilion, and the partridges are crying out in the past.
After returning home, Lu Bicheng still loved the landscape. When visiting Deng Wei in Suzhou, it was just when plum blossoms were in full bloom, a sea of snow, and fragrant for ten miles. Lu Bicheng stayed there for a long time, hoping to be buried in this place after his death. “Aoyama buries his bones and wishes for the past year. May all the plum blossoms be cherished.” However, it seemed difficult to find a place for her to stay at home. At the time, it was difficult for her to have a quiet place to recuperate in China. There were revolutionary movements everywhere; warlords were fighting everywhere; and the sound of invading foreign powers was everywhere. She went to Europe again.
World War II broke out, and the smoke in Europe was stronger than in China. Lu Bicheng heard the sound of his neighbor's piano late at night, all as if it were killing. She returned from Europe east to Hong Kong. She “had a rough life and a bleak death.” She died lonely in the guest house. She didn't know anything in her early years and fell out with her family. She once said to her family, “I won't see you until Huangquan.” When she died, no one loved one was around, and no one knew where her body was actually buried.
According to the Buddhist sutra story, there is a weak stream of water in front of Mount Ryoyama where the Buddha is located. Some people asked the Buddha, “Weak water is 3,000, how can it cross in one fell swoop?” The Buddha said, “There is no weak water in the first place, so why must there be ups and downs.”

Qionglou's thoughts are high in the cold, and his meditation has come to an end;
