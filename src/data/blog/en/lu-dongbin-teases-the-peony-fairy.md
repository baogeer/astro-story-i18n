 Legend has it that Lu Dongbin, one of the Eight Immortals, is known as Chun Yangzi. Legend has it that he is a native of Tang Jingzhao. In the middle of Huichang, he went out of favor. He wandered through rivers and lakes. He left power with Han Zhong and was awarded to Dan Zi, and became immortal. He once lived in seclusion at Zhongnan Mountain and later traveled all over the world. In legends about him, in addition to Jianghuai Zengjiao, Yueyang Nong He, and the Drunk Hotel, there is also the story of the Peony Flower Fairy.
Legend has it that Lu Dongbin traveled all over the world and came to Tongbai Mountain, known as Jinting Dongtian. He discovered that the earth was shaking, houses were collapsing, and Jiufeng wanted to collapse. He took a close look. It turned out to be a pangolin acting strange.
Annoyed, Lu Dongbin quickly summoned various mountain gods to discuss a plan to capture pangolins and save the people. The mountain gods said one after another, “This monster has worked for 5,000 years, and has perfected the art of climbing over the sea. We are no match against it. I hope Daxian will report to Emperor Yu and quickly send the Heavenly Soldier to capture this monster, save the people, and protect the mountains and forests.”
Lu Dongbin smiled and said, “A little pangolin acts as a monster. There is no need to shock the Heavenly Soldier; I can conquer it alone.” The mountain gods all gave thanks.
After the gods left, Lu Dongbin thought, “This monster is amazing, how can I defeat it? I was also saying big things for a moment. If I hadn't put this demon down, wouldn't the gods of the mountains laugh at me! This”
While Lu Dongbin was meditating, Taibai Jinxing said to Lu Dongbin, “If you want to surrender to pangolins, you have to use the Dingshan magic needle. This magic needle is a jade hairpin on the princess's head. If you can borrow it, it will soon be a success.” Lu Dongbin said, “How can this be done? The jade hairpin was originally a favorite thing of the princess, so I'm afraid no one can borrow it.” Taibai Jinxing said, “This is not difficult. “Mother Wang has a close handmaiden, the Fairy Peony. She has always had her own intentions. If you can touch her heart, you will definitely be able to get this done.”
The next day, Mrs. Wang held a peach festival at Yaotai in Xitian. All kinds of immortals were invited to the feast. Lu Dongbin and Taibai Baixing drove Yang Yun to the Pantao Club together.
At the peach party, the piano sounded melodious and danced beautifully. Daisen from all walks of life can drink for pain. After three rounds of drinking and five dishes, the royal mother and daughter ordered the maid, Fairy Peony, to pour wine to the various fairies. When the Peony Fairy was pouring wine for Lu Dongbin, Taibai Jinxing touched Lu Dongbin with his arm. Lu Dongbin knew this intention, so he took advantage of picking up the wine glass and gently pinched the Peony Fairy's hand. As soon as the Peony Fairy moved, she blushed and retreated with her head down.
After a while, Princess Wang ordered the Peony Fairy to present the fairy with a peach. Fairy Peony hesitated and came to Lu Dongbin. Big Platinum Star kicked Lu Dongbin with the tip of his foot. When Lu Dongbin was picking up the peach, he pressed the peach plate heavily. As soon as Peony Fairy's wrists were soft, she was ashamed like a peach blossom. She kept her head down and hurriedly walked along the back door towards Yaochi. Lu Dongbin followed suit.
The peony fairy walked straight to the Yao pool and gazed at the peony flowers blooming by the pool and meditated. Lu Dongbin quietly stood behind the Peony Fairy and said softly, “Fairy Peony, are you enjoying the flowers?”
Looking back at Fairy Peony, he saw Lu Dongbin and quickly covered his face and said, “You, do you know the rules of the fairy?”
Lu Dongbin smiled and said, “Not only do I know fairy rules, but I can also see through your thoughts.”
Fairy Peony shook her head. Lu Dongbin took a few steps and said, “You're very envious of the world, aren't you?” Fairy Peony slowly bowed her head again. Lu Dongbin said with emotion, “The world is so beautiful. There are green mountains and water everywhere, and birds and flowers are fragrant. I have traveled all over the world and seen quite a few famous mountains and rivers, scenery and gardens, such as the beautiful scenery of Suhang, the strange peak of Mount Taishan, the paradise of Penglai, the peony of Caozhou... it was so beautiful, ten times better than heaven”
Fairy Peony slowly looked up and said softly, “Really?” Lu Dongbin pointed a finger and said, “Peony Fairy, look there. There is a young couple who are happily cultivating the land. If you look over there, it's a couple of lovers enjoying the flowers in the garden.” Lu Dongbin looked back and saw that the Peony Fairy was still standing there watching the couple, so he said, “Fairy Peony, if I hadn't enjoyed some happiness on earth, it would be the greatest regret.”
Fairy Peony hesitated and said, “How easy is it to talk about the future. The rules of the fairy are so strict, how can they get what they want?”
Lu Dongbin smiled slightly: “Fairy Peony, if you have this intention, I'd like to help.”
Fairy Peony blushed and shyly said, “Is that true?”
Lu Dongbin said, “Really. But I'd like to ask you to do me a favor.”
Fairy Peony said, “What can I do for you?”
“Let me use the jade hairpin on Mama Wang's head.”
“Oops, then what's the deal? The jade hairpin is a favorite thing of the royal mother and daughter, and no one can borrow it.” Fairy Peony said it with embarrassment.
Lu Dongbin took a few steps and said, “Please take a look here!” The Peony Fairy passed through the clouds. I saw the area around Mount Tongbai. Houses collapsed everywhere, men crying and women howling.
Fairy Peony quickly closed her eyes and said, “Oops, the people are so pitiful.”
Lu Dongbin said, “This area around Tongbai Mountain used to have beautiful mountains and rivers, and the forest was rich in food. It is only because these pangolins are acting strange that this place has become such a bleak sight! I want to borrow a jade hairpin from the Queen Mother and get rid of this monster pangolin.”
Fairy Peony anxiously said, “I'd like to help, but...”
Lu Dongbin was overjoyed to see that the Peony Fairy promised to help. He gave such instructions and handed over another fake jade hairpin to the Peony Fairy.
Early the next morning, the princess finished bathing. While having the Peony Fairy brush her hair, Fairy Peony took the opportunity to steal the jade hairpin. I hid it in my sleeve and gave it to Lu Dongbin.
Lu Dongbin came to Mount Tongbai with a magic needle and quickly caught the mischievous pangolin.
After Lu Dongbin punished the pangolin, he joined Taibai Baixing to return Dingshan Shanshen to Xitian and requested that the Peony Fairy forgiveness for stealing the jade hairpin.
When Mrs. Wang learned, she was both happy, surprised, and angry. Although she was doing away with harm for the people, the Peony Fairy is always around. They have no dignity, and the rules of heaven are unbearable! When Mrs. Wang saw the two immortals making love, she said, “Look at the faces of the two immortals, don't let the peony die, but they must be driven out of the western world and reduced to normal!”
In this way, the people of Tongbai Mountain lived a peaceful and happy life again, and the Peony Fairy also fulfilled their desire to be on earth.
Later, in order to think about Lu Dongbin, people also talked about Lu Dongbin's play Peony for the People as a beautiful story, until today.
