 Lu Shunge's bizarre love was caused by the turbulent social environment at the time. Her life and death complex with Fan Xizhou stemmed from war, and they also separated due to war. However, she kept that little bit of infatuation, and finally got her wish. “May all the affectionate people in the world become family members. It was a marriage destined not to be missed in previous lives.” This sentence has the best explanation. Lu Shunge has been tormented by many ups and downs. She and Fan Xitong's Yuanjing have continued their relationship, adding a popular story to the scarred years of chaos in the Southern Song Dynasty.
After the Jingkang disaster, Song Gaozong built the capital Lin'an, and Jin Bing stepped back and took advantage of the attack. Four years in Jianyan, they went deeper into eastern Zhejiang and occupied Lin'an (present-day Hangzhou). However, due to the lack of succession and the trend of being isolated, they wantonly looted Lin'an and returned to the north. As a result, they were besieged by 8,000 people in Jiaoshan, Zhenjiang for 48 days by the famous anti-gold general Han Shizhong. Unfortunately, the imperial court relied on Qin Hui. He had contacts with the crown, stepped up peace discussions with Jin Renren, and at the same time stepped up sending personnel to various states to collect field taxes for the imperial court's use. This is where things began.
Lu Shunge's father, Lu Zhongyi, was sent to Fuzhou to work as a tax collector and brought his family to Chishi Town, Xianxia Ling. It just happened to happen that the people changed in Jianzhou. Jianzhou is now Jianou in Fujian. The people unanimously nominated Fan Ru as the leader and resisted the food expropriation policy. What was originally a very simple incident actually intensified under the impetus of interested people. Traffic was cut off, order was disrupted, and fires were set in high winds everywhere, killing people during the day. Lu Zhongyi and his entourage had to stay temporarily in Chishi Town.
A few days later, in the night, chaos spread to Akaishi Town. The streets were lit up, and residents scattered to flee. Lu Zhongyi also fled Ashi Town with his wife and little girl. Under the messy situation, Lu Shunge ran wild all the way forward. Unbeknownst to him, he lost his way, and never saw his parents. At dawn, he was forcibly taken away by a small group of rebels.
Fan Xizhou is the nephew of Fan Ruwei, the leader of the troublemaking people. Fan Xizhou's acquaintance and relationship with Lu Shunge was quite dramatic.
At this point, Fan Ruwei has declared himself to be the Grand Marshal of the Tianxiong Army and is preparing to make a big attempt to buy people's hearts. As a result, he generously rewarded the looted wealth and women to meritorious subordinates separately. Lu Shunge was assigned to an elementary school.
Lu Shunge is the daughter of a family of government officials. Naturally, she has fine skin and tender flesh. At the age of sixteen, she is also in the flower season, the age of breaking melons. The elementary school was very happy. He carried Lu Shunge on his shoulder and happily returned to his place of residence. When I was satisfied, I drank my own drink one cup after another, and binge-drank the yellow soup. Brother Lu Shun crouched down in the corner, frightened by the greedy eyes cast from time to time. The night was already deep, and the lights were dead, yet nothing happened. The little military officer, who was addicted to alcohol, was already drunk, and Lu Shunge took every opportunity to escape from the magic cave.
Lu Shunge deliberately made it look like he was unkempt and wandered aimlessly in the streets and alleys. After the turmoil. Ten rooms and nine empty rooms, pedestrians disappeared everywhere, and the family closed their doors. The hungry Lu Shunge finally fainted near a broken temple.
A few little bastards were cooking meat under the temple porch. After eating and drinking enough, they sat leisurely. Seeing someone lying upside down on the side of the road, they slowly poured the remaining broth into Lu Shunge's mouth. Lu Shunge slowly woke up, opened his eyes wide, and someone exclaimed, “It turned out to be a little girl!”
“From hunger and cold, I lost my heart, and I felt warm and lustful.” Everyone carried her into the temple with seven hands and eight feet. Some people grabbed her hair; some held her hands and feet; others forced her to peel off her clothes; some had already set up positions and were ready to practice as a bully. At a critical moment, someone kept her innocent for the rest of her life. Naturally, this person was Fan Xizhou. As for how Fan Xi Zhou came, there is no way to investigate; it was mostly a matter of God's will.
Lu Shunge was helped by a “noble person” in the midst of a very critical and miserable experience. In addition to being grateful to Dade, in the face of pity on the other side, with tears in his eyes, he wept and recounted his life and the civil changes he experienced on his way to office. The other party also revealed that he was the nephew of Fan Ruwei, the leader of the Rebellion, so Lu Shunge was brought to Fan Xizhou's home. Under the auspices of Fan Xi Zhou's mother, Miss Official Family Lady Lu Shunge became the lovely wife of the nephew of the leader of the troubled people.
In a situation where the military and the people are in turmoil, it is not surprising that a weak woman becomes the wife of the nephew of the leader of the troubled people; what is invaluable is later.
The suffering shows true feelings. The mother and son of the Fan family are happy to have married an official woman and take care of Lu Shun Ge even more. Meanwhile, Lui Shun-ge's love for her mother-in-law and her devotion to her husband gradually developed a strong sense of trust and happiness. Although she was unable to let go of the grief of being separated from her parents for a moment, the joy of being newly married and the nourishment of love made her feel a full and happy life.
After all, the chaos of the troubled people did not succeed. It quickly collapsed in front of Han Shi-chung's 30,000 army. The officers and soldiers made every effort to search for the remaining evil. Fan Hee-joo feared that a major disaster was imminent. In a hurry, they were unable to escape with their old mother and lovely wife, so the family of three hugged their heads and cried. It seems that only Fan Xizhou was able to escape alone. Fan Mu took the ancestral mandarin duck mirror from the bottom of the box, and her son and daughter-in-law each took their own sides. She hoped that they would meet again in the future and be reunited.
After Fan Xizhou escaped for her life, her mother-in-law and daughter-in-law, who were dependent on each other, were at the mercy of an unknown fate. A gang of troublemakers finally broke into this manless family one day, and while resisting and fighting, Fan Mu was stabbed to death with a sword. Lu Shunge took advantage of the chaos to walk out of the window. Amidst all the horror, he ran wild outside without life.
After suffering the hardships of exile, Lu Shunge finally met her father, who took office in Jianzhou. Lu Shunge honestly told her parents that she is already married to Fan Ruwei's nephew Fan Xi Zhou, and the couple are very affectionate. Lui Chung-yi listened and remained silent. He thought that his daughter was possessed by thieves and that it was a nightmare. He gently advised Lui Shun-ge not to talk about it again, and find a Ruyi Lang Jun for her later.
They met again after the robbery. Lu Shunge was very dissatisfied with her father's attitude. She vowed to die not to discuss marriage. Soon, her father was sent to serve in Fengzhou for military merit, which is now Fengchuan, Guangdong. The infatuated woman Lu Shunge's wish to break the mirror and come true seemed to be getting fainter. She held the bronze mirror her mother-in-law gave her all day and cried.
The world is inherently difficult to understand. One day, a person named He Chengxin, sent by a higher authority to supervise taxation came to the government office of Lu Zhongyi in Fengzhou. Naturally, the fool must be filmed. Lu Zhongyi hosted a banquet at her home to entertain this young man. Although this person is young but capable, she is both well-mannered and polite. Outside of her official duties, Lui Chung-yi naturally thought of her daughter. Unfortunately, this person is already married.
People left, and Lu Zhongyi came to the back room feeling the pain of suffering and loss. At this point, the daughter came and told him hopefully that she was eavesdropping on the visitor's conversation. The accent was very familiar; it was probably her separated husband. Her father thought that her daughter was simply out of her mind. One had the last name Fan and the other was He, which simply had nothing to do with Feng Ma and Niu.
Half a year has passed, and He Chengxin has once again arrived in Fengzhou for work. Lu Zhongyi couldn't help but mess around with her daughter Hu and had to invite He Chengxin to her home. Lu Shunge saw it really real from behind the screen. This is indeed her own husband. She suppressed her ecstasy and asked her servant to hand over a bronze mirror to the person named He Chengxin. As soon as He Chengxin pulled out the other bronze mirror from his arms, Lu Shunge rushed out at all costs, frightening his father who was sitting on the sidelines.
After all, it is a family of government officials. Not to mention the civil changes in Jianzhou, Lu Shunge and He Chengxin had another lively wedding. Amidst the cheerful sound of drumming and melodious shouting, they had two relationships, and their lovers remained family members for life. Woe, blessings overflow.
