 Lu Xiaoman is known as the first social flower to dare to get divorced in love at the beginning of the last century, but it really makes people wonder what her ideal model of love is?
Lu Xiaoman's marriage with her ex-husband was also a matter of will. After marriage, she was completely satisfied financially. However, outside of being in a relationship, she was also afraid of loneliness and felt that she had no spiritual support. She thinks Xu Zhimo is the true love she is looking for.
At one point, they were deeply in love, but after they were discovered, Xu Zhimo was under the greatest pressure. At this point, he received news of Tagore's disease. In order to avoid criticism from people on the cusp, he had to leave Lu Xiaoman temporarily in the name of visiting Tagore. When he left, he sent a letter to Lu Xiaoman: “I miss you, I feel sorry for you, and I comfort you. Even though my people are gone, my heart won't leave you -- you can rush up this time; it's a success even if you die! With me here, bolster your courage, go ahead and let us not disappoint each other.”
But after that, what did Lu Xiaoman do?
Xu Zhimo and Hu Shi took care of Xiaoman, but the two sparked a spark. Lu Xiaoman wrote a love letter to Hu Shi in English: I'm very worried about you these days, are you really not here anymore? I hope not because I know I won't trust you.
Another one: I just hope you can visit me soon. Don't take yourself too seriously. Life is too short, take it easy.
It can be seen that apart from Xu Zhimo, Lu Xiaoman and Hu Shi also have private relationships that cannot be explained. It's just that Hu Shi is afraid of internal affairs; he won't go to pieces like Xu Zhimo to fight for it.
After Lu Xiaoman and Xu Zhimo got married, the two often quarreled over whether to live in Beijing or Shanghai. Xu Zhimo is busy making money every day for Xiaoman, but Lu Xiaoman also takes on the singer Weng Ruiwu to massage her and smoke opiates with her. There is no evidence to test for the affair between the two, but when others see it, they always feel uneasy about Shima. Xu Zhimo actually excused them for this: “The couple's relationship is love; the relationship between friends is love; the relationship between friends is love. It's a cure; Hibiscus spits on the pillow and swallows up the clouds; at best, they can only talk; at best, they can't have sex.” Even Xu Zhimo said this; what else could anyone else say?
However, Xu Zhimo and Lu Xiaoman never had the same relationship they had before their marriage. Perhaps the love Lu Xiaoman wants is just passion; emotionally, she is someone who can't stay idle for the rest of her life. Why did she ever want to visit Shima? Share the pressure with him and don't let him get caught up in economic oppression and unable to write poetry. All she wanted was to get love from him, and she lost her temper when she wasn't happy.
After Xu Zhimo's death, Lu Xiaoman lived with Weng Ruiwu due to the stress of life. Hu Shi once mentioned that he would let her leave Weng Ruiwu to be responsible for her living expenses, but Lu Xiaoman refused on the grounds that they had been together for many years. Now that Weng is not in good health, she cannot get rid of him.
Looking at Lu Xiaoman's love life, think about Xu Zhimo's original partner Zhang Youyi. In fact, as Zhang Youyi said, among the women in Xu Zhimo, she is the only one who loves him the most.
But who can explain the love thing? What you think of as intense feelings is nothing more than a mirrored moon. Xu Zhimo was just Lu Xiaoman's dance partner who turned around once on the stage, but he took it seriously.
