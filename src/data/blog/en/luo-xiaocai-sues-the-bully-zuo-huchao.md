 Luo Xiaocai is a bricklayer in Heima Village. The Zuo Hu dynasty was a rich man in Heimatang. He was known as the “King of the Shunjiang River for a generation”. He had five sons under him, known as the “Five Tiger Generals.” Because the family is rich and has a lot of furniture, everyone around them has to borrow it from them, and everyone must respect them; otherwise, they will make excuses to bully boys and girls, and do nothing wrong. People hate them to the bone, but they are all against it, and they are angry and afraid to speak up.
At one point, Luo Xiaocai's mother died. While it was being opened, Zuo Hu's father and son also visited. Those sitting down at the table didn't put wine and food on the table where they were sitting first. Luo Xiaocai and his wife hurriedly went to their father and son to express their apologies, and even knelt down to ask for forgiveness. The prime minister immediately asked the bartender to sit down first. It was fine if Zuo Hu and his father and son were supposed to drink and eat, but the Five Tigers put up with that breath, turned over the table full of wine and food, and left with a curse grin.
Luo Xiaocai usually doesn't get used to Zuo Hu's aggressive, overbearing, and bullying people, but since he didn't mess with himself, the well water didn't violate the river. This time he provoked himself and couldn't help but sigh. He kept quiet and began planning to rectify the Zuo Hu dynasty. After sending his mother to the mountain for burial, he ruined the tableware and furniture in the family, then took him to Huacheng to sue Zuo Hu for his father and son. He went all the way thinking about how to completely overthrow the Zuo Hu dynasty? When he crossed the Yangjiangjiang River, he thought: The Zuo Hu dynasty was the king of the Shunjiang River. Locally, they had no national law, no county order, and Laozi was number one in the world. Even when his mother died, he had to “honor one person from the Zuo Hu dynasty first, then treat his mother's death.” On the way, he saw ducks looking for food in a gutter, and he thought, “Zuo Tiger is like hungry ducks searching ditches in the morning, and like hungry wolves searching mountains at night.” It's really abhorrent. He also saw a big tree on the side of the road. There were no plants growing around the tree. He also thought, “Big trees shade plants and trees are difficult to grow; bullies make people uneasy.” When he heard the thunder, he thought that if Lei didn't hit Takako, Zuo Hu Chao should have added the crime of “hitting my filial son and plucking my wife's hair and forcing her to eat suede meat.”
As soon as he arrived in Menghua City, he found a special person to write down all the requests he thought of along the way. In the shape of a paper scripture, the person sharpened the pen and described the Zuo Hu dynasty as an unforgivable villain. As soon as the paper was handed over, the county officials were furious. They immediately ordered the arrest of Zuo Hu and bring the father and son to justice. Their hearts were happy. Grandpa Fortuna brought it to the door.
However, after two days, Zuo Hu Chao's father and son were brought to the lobby. The county official sat majestically behind the case table, smashed a shock tree on the table, and loudly said, “Who kneeled down in the hall?”
“Kneeling in the hall are Zuo Hu Chao's father and son from Heimatang of the tile factory. I wonder what law we broke to get us arrested?” Zuo Hu said, bowing his head.
“In the Zuo Hu dynasty, Luo Xiaocai accused your father and son of smashing all the furniture and dishes, injuring Luo Xiaocai with their own hands, plucking his wife's hair, and forcing them to ask for suede meat when his mother hanged him. Whether it's true or not, it comes from the truth.”
“It's true that we overturned a table of wine and dishes, but we didn't smash all the furniture, beat filial piety, knock on Xiaozi's wife's hair, and force them to ask for chamois meat. Please ask the old man to check.” Zuo Huchao argued.
“Report the situation at the survey site as soon as the arrest is reported.” The county official said.
During the campaign in uniform: “The investigation of the site was as follows: five large shredded iron pans were destroyed, seven medium pots and three small pots; five copper pots; six holes of aluminum pans were broken; and countless pans and bowls were damaged. The total value is around 50 or so. There is indeed a woman in the family who is in a trance. I don't know if it is schizophrenia caused by tugging her hair.”
“The old man is wrong!”
“In the face of human and physical evidence, your Zuo Hu dynasty still dared to defy and serve you.”
“OK, OK, OK, let's admit it. If we lose 50, we lose 100, OK?”
“This really doesn't work. Don't Luo Xiaocai and his wife want to pay for medical expenses, lost work expenses, and litigation expenses?”
“Pay compensation, grandpa has the final say.”
“The total amount of compensation is 200 yuan, of which Luo Xiaocai is 150 and 2 large sums of compensation, and is responsible for 50 and 2 large sums of money for this lawsuit. Not convinced?”
“Dress up, be fair to adults.” Zuo Hu and the dynasty wanted to spend 200 or two large sums of money to get out of the disaster. I paid 200 silver bills.
“In the Zuo Hu dynasty, why did they accuse you of claiming to be the king of Shunjiang? Are you trying to rebel?”
“I'm joking about being able to enjoy the water freely on the Yangjiao River. There was no other intention.”
“I also heard that your son robbed other people's gold bars on the Bonan Trail. Is that true, can't be denied, honestly explained, and sought leniency?”
“This is a huge mistake. My son has a bad temper and loves to be a bit drunk. This is true, but I would never dare to do such a harmful thing.”
“You still dare to argue and wait for a big sentence.”
“Grandpa, you just beat me to death; I wouldn't dare to confess to this murder.”
“Then go back and learn about the situation. Your son stays in this prison, and I'll take good care of them. I'm waiting for news from your old man's family. Leave the hall—” Undisputed, and Zuo Huchao chased out of the lobby.
After Zuo Huzhao returned, news of his son calling for help came every day, and as a last resort, he sent ten gold bars from his family. Since then, my family has declined, and I don't dare to be a high-profile person.
In the old society, there were times when the rich were also afraid of the poor, because the government loved the poor to sue the rich. This was the best opportunity to blackmail the rich. Why not do they enjoy it? As the saying goes, “rotten sandals trip over people” is the truth.
