Bai Niang Zi: Seeing the Case: Vol. 28 of Feng Menglong's “Warning Notice”, “Bai Niangzi Yongzhen Leifeng Tower”. The blue and white snake spirits on Mount Emei envy life on Earth. They became young girls, Xiaoqing and Bai Suzhen, and visited the West Lake. Scholar Xu Xian and Baek Su-jeong met, fell in love with each other, and became a family after being matched by Xiaoqing. The monk of Jinshan Temple deceived Xu Xian and divorced his wife. Xu Xian listened to insults and abandoned her family and ran away from Jinshan. Baek Su-jeong went to Kanayama Sufu, and the bridge was broken in a fight with the French Sea. Later, Fa Hai used force to break up the couple and crushed Baek Su-jeong under the Leifeng Tower. Xiaoqing invited God Generals to burn down the Leifeng Tower and rescue Baek Su-jeong. “The Legend of the White Snake” is a famous Chinese opera. Ming native Chen Liulong wrote “The Legend of Leifeng Tower”, and the Qing people wrote the lyrics “Legend of the Righteous Demon”.
Before, there was one thing I always took for granted. That is, Tien Xian must marry an idiot, a rich family must marry a poor bastard, and a good girl must marry a bad guy. Under the careful education and upbringing of the proletarian class, this is just right; this is beautiful: just blame all evil in the old society; it is hard to tear people apart!
First, there was the cowherd and the weaver. The cowherd is very poor. He probably has some qualities only ordinary working people have: honest, sincere, and stupid. The weaver, on the other hand, is a heavenly fairy. She is young, beautiful, and has a smart mind. However, the weaver really liked the cowherd. She married every cowherd without permission, and gave birth to a pair of children. The most pitiful thing is that because they didn't have the consent of Weaver's father, they never got a temporary residence permit, got married, and couldn't live together; they were only able to see each other once a year.
Then there are Dong Yong and the Seven Fairies. Dong Yong was so poor that he sold himself as a slave, and he was stupid and had no claims. The Seven Fairies are beautiful. They know some fairy law, are smart and cute. They also have a group of equally beautiful sisters to help them fight against evil forces and take back Dong Yong. Both husband and wife returned their family.
There is also White Lady and Xu Xian. The white snake is beautiful and amazing, smart and capable, and unchanging; Xu Xian is stupid and foolish, easy to be fooled and irresponsible, and achieved nothing. Xu Xian betrayed her wife. She still fought for her life, death for him, and for him.
People in the West have the prototype of Cinderella, and most of us have the “Grey Boy” theme. Men want a pie to fall from the sky. They want to take advantage of being poor, stupid, and honest in exchange for a wife who is like a flower, rich and powerful, has a noble lineage, and takes the initiative to deliver it to the door. A beautiful woman with good manners like the Conch Girl, and poor kids feel uncomfortable — I don't think she's a famous person.
At the end of the day, even though successive generations of Chinese masculinity have given men so much power and staff, they still feel that their feet are weak, and they have no control over their own abilities and destiny. If you think about it, let women take the lead. Cost-effective.
After practicing for a thousand years, Bai Niang Zi became a beautiful woman, loaned Xu Xian an umbrella, and then fell in love with this young man. After that, Xing Xing went headlong in pursuit of others and trained him from being a drugstore boy to become a big pharmacy owner. As soon as she caught her inside and out, Xu Xian was dumbfounded and watched as this woman, who was like a fairy, did everything for him. Later, he turned White Lady into her original form and foolishly invited the French Sea to capture her. However, the White Lady risked her life several times to save Xu Xian, but was eventually crushed under the Leifeng Tower.
Other than the fact that the cowherd and Dong Yong don't have any bad intentions (you know, it's just because they haven't had a chance). I can't see their merits; basically all of their claims and decisions are implemented by their girlfriends. Even love is very passive. Xu Xian, on the other hand, basically concentrated all the faults of a small Chinese writer: foolish, cowardly, realistic, disloyal, passive, and doing nothing. He is a shameful person who succeeded.
If anyone argues that Xu Xian has a kind heart, then, Lord, please take away the kindness in me; I won't join this kind of person.
When Bai Niang Zi was unhappy, it was because she wanted to be a good woman in the traditional sense of the word. Well, she doesn't really know. Many of the rules of the game in the world are set for others to see. It is precisely those who have caused the greatest harm to this world, but who have no bad intentions, yet are ignorant, indifferent, weak, and self-righteous. The one who broke White Lady's heart was not Fa Hai, but Xu Xian. Even in normal times, what languages can Bai Niang Zi and Xu Xian share? What kind of happy life can you have?
As soon as the cowherd and Dong Yong got into trouble, they were probably about the same as Xu Xian.
