Legend has it that Zhu Yingtai is a beautiful and smart girl. Not only can she embroider and do needlework, but she can also read and learn calligraphy. One day, she suddenly wanted to study outside, but she also felt that her family was a woman and only went outside to study alone, which was quite inconvenient; it was not as good as a man in masquerade. So she put on a man's costume and left her hometown for a long time.
When she arrived at that place, worshipped the “saint” and the old gentleman, she met those classmates. Among these students, there is one named Liang Shanbo. In Zhu Yingtai's eyes, he is an extremely lovely teenager, because not only is he good-looking, but he also has a gentle temperament, and he works particularly hard at studying. As a result, her relationship with him became closer day by day. The two spent the whole day studying together and sleeping together. Although she loved him warmly, the precautions were very strict. She often placed her bookcase in the middle of the two bedsides, put a pot of water on top of the bookcase, and told Liang Shanbo, “When you sleep, you should stay calm. If you roll around in bed and spill water in the basin, I'll immediately report it to Mr. Lao and ask him to punish you!”
Liang Shanbo listened to her, but she had no choice but to do the rules and regulations and didn't dare to move. As a result, Zhu Yingtai never showed a trace in front of Liang Shanbo! Although Liang Shanbo is so nice to Zhu Yingtai, he has no doubt that she is a woman, because he is a bit nerdy and can easily be confused by her. Other classmates had no way of knowing her details.
