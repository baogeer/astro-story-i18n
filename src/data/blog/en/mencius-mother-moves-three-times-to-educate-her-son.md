 “Mencius was able to become an “ascent,” becoming a person second only to Confucius in the orthodox ideology system of Chinese feudal society, thanks a lot to his mother. Mencius's mother was a great woman. She worked hard to stick to her goals and raised her son. She was a typical Chinese mother. She was careful, inspirational, diligent, and studying, to making a marriage and making money. Decades were a day without relaxation. She not only made Mencius, but also left a complete parenting plan for future generations.
Mencius was born in the middle of the night of April 2, 3rd year of King Zhou Weilie in Xiafu Village, Ma On Shan, a suburb of Zoucheng. Most of the births of great people were accompanied by a magical sign. According to “Tianzhong”, “When Mencius was born, the mother dreamer came from Mount Taishan on a bright cloud and stopped over Meng's house. Her mother stared for a long time, and suddenly the clouds fell. All of Lu Alley was covered with a five-color cloud.” The sky is changing constantly, and Caiyun is not convinced that there are so-called magical signs at any time. Instead, it is a “prenatal education” statement, which is quite reasonable based on scientific experimental results.
Chinese culture has a long history, and the term “prenatal education” has been around for a long time. It is said that when Zhou Wenwang's mother was pregnant, she ignored bad eyesight, did not listen to obscene voices, and did not speak ill; during her pregnancy, Zhou Chengwang's mother did not stand up, sit well, lived alone. Even though she was angry and did not curse, Meng Mu deeply experienced the importance of “prenatal education.” “The Legend of Korean Poetry” contains a passage from her saying, “I am pregnant. I am pregnant. I don't sit or eat; it's also about prenatal education.”
Meng Zi's mother's last name is Feu, and her father's name is Meng Ji. Zi Gongyi is a gifted reader. He left his beautiful wife and young children, and traveled to Song Guo to study and seek service. Three years later, what he brought to Meng Mu was bad news like a thunderbolt from a clear day. Since then, Meng Meng Mu, who is isolated and helpless, began a rough life journey. She made up her mind to use her own hands to obtain clothing and food needs, and to use her own strength to raise her only son as a useful person.
According to “psychological” analysis, this is also a highly effective “compensatory effect.” Meng Mu not only carefully paid attention to her son's warmth and warmth in his life, but she also tirelessly used “words and teachings” and “physical education” to improve her son's personality. For the most part, a person's basic personality was formed before the age of six, and has been deeply rooted ever since. After the age of six, the scope of activities gradually expands, and naturally the surrounding environment affects the fragile personality model that has already been formed, facing intense trials and tests. At this time, “those close to Zhu are red, those close to Mexico are black,” and “dyed blue is blue, and dyed yellow is yellow”. The environment is very important. Meng Mu suddenly discovered that her son, who has always been smart and obedient, has been affected by the bad environment. Since then, in order to find a good living and study environment for her son, Meng Mu has begun a long relocation campaign, and the beautiful story of “Meng Mu's Three Migrations” still continues to pass down.
The Meng family used to be in a ghost village at the foot of Ma'anshan. The children in the village chased and played everywhere. From time to time, they also imitated the manners of adults and acted in groups of three or five. Mother Meng saw it in her eyes and pain in her heart. Nor could she always keep a lively and active child at home; the only way was to change the living environment.
After some setbacks, the mother and son of the Meng family moved from Fu Village to Miaohuying Village ten miles away. Every single day, on the 1st, 3rd, 5th, and 7th, people from near and far come to the bazaar to trade, bargain, and the hustle and bustle. This was very appealing and confusing for children. Meng Zi and some other children also learned how to compare the Japanese baht. Mu Meng was uneasy. After staying for half a year, she moved again.
Meng Mu neither wanted her son to be an obscure person, nor was she disdainful that her son was steeped in the temptation of profit. She must choose an environment suitable for her son to grow up. For the third time, she moved her home near the Academy Palace in Zoucheng. Although the house was completely deserted, Meng Mu settled in peace with her son.
There are often readers in the vicinity of the Academy Palace. The elegant atmosphere, calm demeanor, and conformist manners all have a subtle influence on the nearby residents. In particular, children who are new to human resources often gather under big trees to practice the manners of moving forward and backward in the Xuegong Palace. It is a kind, solemn scene that makes Mother Meng, who watched it from afar, very happy from the bottom of her heart: “This is the best living environment for children!” She wholeheartedly exclaimed.
Being a human requires honesty, so-called “words must be done, actions must bear fruit.” When the Meng family was still living next to the market in Miaohuying Village, someone killed pigs in the east. Mencius inexplicably asked her mother, “Why are the neighbors killing pigs?” Meng Mu was busy at the time, so she casually said, “Eat it for you!” Mencius was very happy to wait to eat meat. In order not to lose her trust in her son, Meng Mu set aside a portion of her living expenses to buy a piece of meat in order to keep her son happy.
You must be persistent in your work. Once you identify your goals, you will not be disturbed by the outside world. Mencius has natural spirituality and wisdom, but he also has a habit of being lazy and playful that is common among young children. One day, Mencius actually skipped school and spent half a day playing outside. When her son came home, Meng Mu silently picked up a pair of scissors and clicked the woven silk section and cut it into two sections. Just when Mencius was astonished, Meng Mu said, “Your abandonment of school is like me cutting silk weaving! A gentleman is famous when he asks, so if you live, you are at peace, and your actions are far from harm. If you don't study today, you can't get away from evil. From now on, you will only do small things that linger and do nothing in the middle. How can you live with your husband and not run out of food?” Mu Meng used “fraying” as a warning metaphor for “dropping out of school,” pointing out that the consequences of doing things halfway and giving up are very serious. The scene of “Inspector Broken Weave” left a vivid impression in Mencius's young mind that he was both surprised and fearful. Since then, he has worked hard and studied hard day and night.
In the Spring and Autumn Warring States period, the academic culture flourished. People were dazzled and dazzled, and people didn't know how to choose. However, Meng Mu had firm claims, ignoring the mystery of Lao Zhuang, and disdainful of Yang Zhu's utilitarianism. She was only fascinated by Confucius' way of loyalty. She personally sought and searched, and finally found an enlightened teacher for Mencius among the Kong Men.
At the time, Confucius' grandson Kong Ji, Zi Si, was setting up a museum in Qufu to teach Liu Yi. Students from near and far were busy studying. When Mencius was 15, he came to Qufu from Zoucheng with encouragement from Meng Meng. Since then, he has officially accepted the essence of Confucianism. Zisi has favored Mencius and said to the left and right, “Meng Ruzi enjoys benevolence and righteousness. Called Yao Shun, it is rare in the world.” Since then, Mencius studied under Zisi for five years, learned about morality, advanced by leaps and bounds, and finally inherited the mantle of Confucius.
Meng's mother's education for her son was meticulous. Even after becoming a family, the old mother had to worry about him. The ancient “Legend of Martyrs” records that Meng's wife, Yushi, walked naked in her bedroom, and Mencius was completely unhappy. She thought her husband was too aloof, so she asked her mother-in-law to explain it. For a long time, Mencius was always unhappy with his wife. He thought she was too proud and wanted to leave his wife. Mother Meng said a great meaning to her son: “Husband lie, I will get started and ask who is there, so I salute her. They will go to court and speak loudly, so they will also quit people. If you enter the house, it will be up to you, so I'm afraid to see people passing by. Today, you go to Yan's private place. There is no sound when entering the house, making people take sides and look at it. It's not that you are polite, nor is it disrespectful to a woman.” Mencius felt deeply about himself, Meng Lang, so he got rid of all his feelings and got along with his wife He Le as before.
Mother Meng worked hard all her life. She was very strong, and she did not relax in caring for and supervising Mencius. Except for the period when Mencius traveled all over the world, she spent most of her time serving her nursing mother, holding a leisure position as a professor in Qi, and was unwilling to stay away from her home country to seek greater development.
In Qi, Mencius explained his political proposition to those in power several times. Although Qi Xuan rewarded Mencius with 100,000 yuan, he was unwilling to actively implement his political proposition. In response, Mencius said, “Gentlemen are rewarded for not being proud.” Mencius is not hungry for fame and fortune; he wants to fulfill his political ambitions.
Mencius's ideal place was Song Guo, but she moved back and forth in order to serve her old mother. Thirty years passed by. Mother Meng was over ancient and rare, and Mencius was already in her famous years. She sighed and was bored and unhappy all day long. Meng Mu clarified the reason and said a famous quote from time immemorial to her son:
“A gift for a married couple. They are good at drinking alcohol, raising an uncle, sewing clothes, and are undefined, so they are treated by a girlfriend, and have no intention of going abroad. In the sense of being a woman, there is no law, and there are three ways; therefore, when young, follow your parents; when you get married, follow your husband; if you die, follow your son and be polite. I'm an adult now, but I'm old! A child's act is Ziyi; I act according to my courtesy.”
Three words and two words swept away the worries in Mencius's heart, so Mencius once again traveled all over the world and received unprecedented respect and welcome. Unfortunately, while her son raised her eyebrows and exhaled, Meng Mother simply ignored it. While returning to her hometown, Ma'anshan, the townspeople from the past scrambled to sacrifice on the side of the road, with great condolences. Today, at the foot of Ma'anshan Mountain, 20 miles north of Zoucheng County in Shandong Province, there are still stone carvings praising her steadfastness and motherly style, and Meng Mu Temple has been built throughout the ages.
As a woman, Meng Mu's greatness is more than “five flavors, putting wine syrup, raising an uncle, sewing clothes,” and “three ways”. More importantly, she can give her son different levels of education as he grows up. It is a candle that burns herself and illuminates her son's future.
