 In the Tang dynasty, which was rich in poets, Meng Haoran can be called a “freak.”
He is a “personable teenager” who pursues poetry, casualness, and freedom. He likes to wander between the landscape and the countryside, yet he has an extremely strong sense of utility, and his career path is difficult.
He was proud of his talents. He was not that peaceful, yet he became good friends with many famous writers: Li Bai, Du Fu, Wang Wei... Meng Haoran wrote poems for them, and they also wrote poems for Meng Haoran. The world once described it this way: Du Fu wrote many poems for Li Bai, but Li Bai rarely wrote to Du Fu because Li Bai wrote all his own poems to Meng Haoran.
How did a man whose career was a complete mess write those idyllic poems that were so light and natural?
Remain secluded in Lumen Mountain and write famous quotes from time immemorial
Meng Haoran's talent is well known to the world.
Born in Shuxiangmendi, he has been reading poetry since childhood. Even when many young people had been studying poetry in the cold and frost for ten years, he chose to be a “Li Ziqi of the Tang Dynasty,” and stayed in seclusion in Lumen Mountain with his friend Zhang Zirong. He toured the mountains and water, read books and write poems every day, which was not fast either.
Yamadera bell rang at noon, and Yuliang Dadao fought for the ferry.
People followed Sha Road to Jiangcun, and Yu also sailed back to Lumen.
Lumen Moonlight opened smoke trees and suddenly went to Pang Gong's hidden place.
The Iwado Pine Trail is quiet, and only a secluded place comes and goes at night.
——Meng Haoran's “Night Return to Lumen Song”
Most of Meng Haoran's poems are five-sentence sentences. The main expression is about his secluded life. The poetry style is light and natural. He loves the landscape. It is like the freehand style of landscape painting, and his pastoral life is vivid, fun, and full of charm.
The scenery falls on Yu Qinghui, and the river is in a light stream.
Hong Cheng loves water, so he doesn't want to be with him.
A white-headed fisherman, and a girl with fresh makeup.
They looked like acquaintances, and their heart was speechless.
——Meng Haoran's “Yexi River Rafting”
A picture like this appeared in front of me: the afterglow of the sunset, the fishing boat sings late, the blue water is clear, and the river is rafting. The bald old man sits on the shore and fishing. The young girl with fresh makeup is washing her shirt. They look at each other as if they have known each other before, but they can only look at each other's veins and have no chance to talk.
And Meng Haoran happened to pass by, so he recorded this beautiful moment.
Life in Lumen Mountain is beautiful. One morning, Meng Haoran woke up, pushed the door open and walked out. It was full of spring outside. Everything looked like he had just woken up, and he opened his eyes happily. Meng Haoran's literature sprung up and walked back to the house to write this famous poem from time immemorial:
I didn't wake up in the spring, and I heard birds chirping everywhere.
The sound of wind and rain at night, how much do you know when the flowers fall.
——Meng Haoran's “Spring Dawn”
What Meng Haoran didn't expect: Today, more than a thousand years later, as soon as we mention Tang poetry, the first thing children at any age memorize is still the song “Chun Xiao.”
We don't know how long Meng Haoran actually spent in Lumen Mountain, but it must have been the happiest time in his life.
The strongest circle of friends has spawned a series of astonishing “farewell poems”
Meng Haoran's talent has attracted many fans, and even the famous Li Bai, Du Fu, Zhang Jiuling, Wang Wei, and Wang Changling have become close friends with him. As a result, the most powerful “circle of friends” of the Tang dynasty was born. Because of these friendships, Meng Haoran also created a series of astonishing “farewell poems”.
The first person to say goodbye to seclusion was Meng Haoran's friend Zhang Zirong. In the fall of 711 AD, Zhang Zirong decided not to go hiking in the mountains and water with Meng Haoran. He went to Beijing to rush the exam to get his fame, and bid farewell to Lumen Mountain, where he lived in seclusion with Meng Haoran. “Why did you just leave? Why did you betray our ideals back then?” Meng Haoran, who was saddened, wrote this poem:
The sunset turned off the mountain, and the guests were sent out of Shibamen.
Melancholy lies in the middle, and kindly speaks at the crossroads.
The forest died down, and Joe Muer overturned.
There is no reason to let our friends survive.
——Meng Haoran “Sending Zhang Zirong to the event”
Ah, goodbye friend, ah, goodbye friend. I wish you great success and a title in the Golden List. I will continue to live in seclusion in Maolin. If you achieve fame and rise to prominence in the future, please don't forget that our friendship will last a long time.
Sure enough, Zhang Zirong got ahead in the exam, but the official situation didn't go smoothly. After a few twists and turns, he was demoted to the rank of Captain Lecheng, which is today in Yueqing City, Zhejiang Province. When Meng Haoran learned that his friend had been criticized, he immediately set out to visit. At this point, they had been separated for 15 years. This meeting was very emotional. Meng Haoran wrote several poems to commemorate their reunion.
Where the reverse trip met, Gangchon at dusk.
Zhong Shan Yao wrote a poem about wine and an isolated island.
Jiaoshu is next to Jiaoshang, and the crowds are everywhere.
More than 10,000 miles from the township, lost their way and felt sad.
——Meng Haoran “Yongjia Shangpu Hall Meets Zhang Bazirong”
Although they haven't seen each other for over 10 years, their friendship has not been separated. This is the friendship between Meng Haoran and Zhang Zirong. This is his lifelong best friend. While they were dating, they got away from each other and were alert and alert.
Meng Haoran's second best friend is the great poet Wang Wei.
After the age of 40, Meng Haoran finally lived the way he hates the most: saying goodbye to his secluded life and seeking fame by studying in Beijing. I spent the first half of my life at ease but had to fall into the mundane world. Naturally, it was difficult to adapt, and I ran into trouble everywhere.
In 729 AD, Meng Haoran entered Beijing for the first time. Famous for Sun Mountain, he became a “North Piao” for a year, saw no hope, and finally decided to return to Xiangyang. During his days in Beijing, he was accompanied by his friend Wang Wei.
Before leaving, he gave Wang Wei a poem:
Why wait for silence to return to the skies.
If you wanted to find herbs, it would backfire on the deceased.
It is rare in the world to know who is a liar.
You should just keep your loneliness, and cover up your hometown.
——Meng Haoran “Leave Wang Wei”
Wang Wei and Meng Haoran are both pastoral poets. Knowing each other, Wang Wei didn't persuade Meng Haoran to stay and continue his struggle. He knew that the landscape was Meng Haoran's destination.
As a result, Wang Weiti penned and wrote:
Duman hasn't come back; he hasn't been in touch with the world for a long time.
Use this as a good policy to persuade you to return to your old place.
Sing and drinking in the countryside and laughing at the books of the ancients.
Being good is a lifetime; there is no work to give for nothing.
——Wang Wei “Sending Meng Six Back to Xiangyang”
Wang Wei advised Meng Haoran: Go back to your hometown and stay secluded! The turmoil in the world is too complicated. Meanwhile, Meng Haoran accepted the advice of a good friend and went back to that landscape.
Wang Changling hosted a banquet and passed away without being afraid of a recurrence of his old illness
In the spring of 735 AD, Han Chao-jong, the assassin of Yangyang, was preparing to go on a trip to Beijing. He was preparing to take Meng Haoran, recommend him to his North Korean and Chinese colleagues, and get him a half job.
When it was time to leave, the left couldn't wait, the right couldn't wait. Han Si Shi was in a hurry and sent someone to the event. As a result, Meng Haoran replied, “I'm drinking with a good friend. My interest is intense, and I have no effort!” This is not Meng Haoran's willful act; it was Li Bai, the famous “fairy of poetry,” who drank with him. Throughout his life, Li Bai was unscrupulous and proud. Many people didn't pay attention to it. His predecessor Li Yi, his peers Wang Changling and Gao Shi, and his junior Du Fu hardly saw Li Bai praising these people's poems. Only Meng Haoran, who was 12 years older than him, instantly became a “little fan.” To this end, Li Bai also wrote a popular farewell poem for Meng Haoran:
The deceased said the Yellow Crane Tower, and fireworks fell in Yangzhou in March.
The lone sail ran out of blue in the distance, only to see the Yangtze River skyline.
——Li Bai “The Yellow Crane Tower Delivers Meng Haoran's Guangling Mausoleum”
My friend's deep feelings are all in this famous poem from time immemorial. Meng Haoran's poetry, which he loved the landscape and water, expressed his personal thoughts and feelings, brought a fresh atmosphere to the Tang dynasty literary scene at the time. Li Bai praised him for “being able to praise Takayama, let alone Qingfen,” and Du Fu praised him for “being able to pass on all the verses of the Qing dynasty.” Meng Haoran was famous even though he had no fame.
In 740, the great poet Wang Changling visited Meng Haoran in Xiangyang. At this point, Meng Haoran had a disease called “gangrene,” which is somewhat similar to current sepsis. The doctor told him never to eat fish or shrimp, otherwise it would endanger his life. Meng Haoran, who has an extravagant temperament, was very happy to see his friend arrive. They had a feast. The two had a great conversation. Before the dinner party, a famous dish from Yangyang was served at the table — Han River Chattou Gan. This is a delicious river dish.
Meng Haoran, who was willful, ate a lot of food, drank a lot of alcohol, and read many poems. A few days later, Meng Haoran's gangrene recurred, and he closed his eyes forever. He was 52 years old. Wang Wei cried very sad when he learned of the death of an old friend. He wrote his last poem for his friend:
The deceased were not visible; the Han River flowed eastward.
Inquire about old Xiangyang, Jiangshan, Kongcaizhou.
——Wang Wei's “Crying Meng Haoran”
For Wang Wei, alpine waters meet acquaintances. With Meng Haoran's journey, it is hard to find people who understand each other in the world. This is the best kind of friendship. It can be learned over time.
Li Bai, Du Fu, Wang Wei, Wang Changling, Zhang Jiuling... This is a super “circle of friends” created by the “talented teenager” Meng Haoran. It did not pave the way for Meng Haoran to have fame and fortune, yet it showed us the true friendship of people in the world, felt the love and pity of writers, and left behind a poem from time immemorial.
