 During the Qin dynasty, Wan Qiliang was drafted into military service to build the Great Wall on her wedding night. His wife, Meng Jiang, was unrelenting and extremely sad. Watching her husband Wan Qiliang get dragged out of the house by several government officials, disappeared in the middle of the night and cried loudly:
“My husband was separated on the wedding night,
Lonely geese are chirping in the lingering wind,
The candlelight moved in sorrow,
Ryotsuki Snow Ei is half seated on the bed.
Hateful! ...”
In a blink of an eye, it was November, and the autumn breeze blew, and the weather was getting cooler. When Meng Jiang Nu thought that her husband Wan Qiliang, who built the Great Wall in the north, was still wearing a single light shirt, she was extremely anxious. So I made up my mind to give my husband a warm coat. She has climbed dozens of mountains and dozens of rivers. I stayed overnight and walked for a few days and nights, and it took a lot of hard work to get to the vicinity of the Great Wall. I saw many people in service: some carried earth and rock; some held iron brazing to drill grooves; some used blue stone to build city walls; and a few others held whips and looked round and stared. ... The woman Meng Jiang walked all over the Great Wall, looking left and right in the crowd. She saw no sign of her husband Wan Qiliang. Her heart was devastated. Inquiring one by one from the veterans, some of them shook their heads; some waved their hands and said they didn't know Wan Qiliang's whereabouts. The woman Meng Jiang was so desperate that she sat near the Great Wall and cried loudly:
“The autumn wind is chilly and the weather is cool,
Meng Jiang stayed alone in his room.
Looking at Yuntian during the day, Si Qi Liang,
I hate long nights with bean lanterns.
I'm worried and the lone husband is cold.
Busy day and night knitting.
Please hide the thousand stitches and the thousand threads,
I hope the clothes are warm and protect against the cold.
Who knows everything about white anxiety,
I didn't see my husband for the clothes delivery.
Lang ah Lang! My dude!
My sweetheart Wan Qiliang!
Grudge! Grudge! Grudge! hate! hate! hate!
Resentment is all about building this city wall!”
“Boom—” The city wall completely collapsed. The body of her husband Wan Qiliang was revealed. Lady Meng Jiang thought: A married couple cannot be together when they are alive; their souls must also be accompanied after death. Suddenly he jumped up and hit the remnants of the city wall. He immediately fell next to the body of her husband Wan Qiliang, without standing up.
Author: Zhai Xin, Wangzhuang Middle School, Yicheng County, Shanxi
