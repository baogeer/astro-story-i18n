 Mi Yu never used anything other people had used in his whole life. He used to be Dr. Taichang and was responsible for sacrificial affairs at the royal temple. The official uniform he wore during the sacrifice was dirty, so he took his life to wash it, and even the pattern on his official uniform was washed off. It was for this reason that Mi Yu was demoted.
Song Huizong had a heavy rice bowl. In a windy and sunny weather, Huizong set up a long table in the inner court. The table was covered with two long silks, with agate ink, Li Ting ink, dental tube pens, gold ink boxes, jade paper, water drops, etc., then sent someone to bring in the rice bowl and ask him to wave his pen on the spot. Huizong himself hid it quietly behind the curtain. Mi Zong was in a hurry to pick up his pen. The pen was drawn freely. Huizong really liked it., even amazed! After Mi Yu finished writing, he softly asked Huizong, “Your Majesty, this inkstone has been stained by the minister. Your Majesty should not use it again, so give it to the minister!” Huizong loved his talents and immediately nodded for permission!
When rice was rewarded, I showed it to my great-grandfather at the end of the year: “I got a rare inkstone.” My great-grandfather said, “Now full of gifts, can I appreciate your treasure ink?” Mi Yu hurriedly got up to pick up the inkstone. Zeng Zu knew that Mi Yu had a cleaning habit. He only carefully held the Bao Yan in his hand, looked left and right, and praised him, saying, “This inkstone looks good. It's really fine, but I don't know how the ink is being released?” Mi Yi was busy asking his family to fetch water. The impatient great-grandson couldn't wait for the water to arrive, so he spit out a sip of water and began polishing the ink. Mi Yi was stunned and very unhappy and said, “This inkstone has been damaged and has lost its precious value.” After speaking, I gave this inkstone to my great-grandson. No matter how much my great-grandfather apologized, Mi Yi just wouldn't want this inkstone.
