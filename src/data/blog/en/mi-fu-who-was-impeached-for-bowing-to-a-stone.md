 What do you think of when you think of Zhenjiang, Jiangsu? Most of it is Zhenjiang aged vinegar, which is famous all over the country. Ancient Zhenjiang had more than just aged vinegar; it was also a popular place for writers and artists. Zhenjiang during the Song Dynasty was known as Runzhou, Song Huizong was promoted to Zhenjiang Province, and was also known as Danyang by literati people. Fan Zhongyuan was the governor of Runzhou during the Northern Song Dynasty, and the one who lived in Runzhou also had a rice cracker from the Northern Song Dynasty.
Live in Zhenjiang for a long time and return to Helin Temple
“Miyu still has strong roots in Zhenjiang.” Yang Hong from the marketing management office of Nanshan Scenic Area in Zhenjiang City told reporters.
It is said that Mi Yi is a descendant of the Xi ethnic group, and the fifth ancestor was Mi Xin, a hero in the early Song Dynasty. In the 3rd year of Song Renzong (1051), Mi Yi was born. She began practicing calligraphy at the age of seven or eight, and began copying calligraphy by Zhou Yue and Su Shi when she was in her teens. Unexpectedly, in the 5th year of Song Shenzong (1082), Mi Yi, who is in her 30s, visited Su Shi in Huangzhou. Since then, the two have become friends. Mi Yi is known as the “Song Family” together with Su Yi, Cai Xiang, and Huang Tingjian.
Mi Yi is 58 years old and has worked in many places throughout his life, but Zhenjiang has always been his home. “Mi Yi has been living in Zhenjiang for almost 40 years. Now the Nanshan Scenic Area in the southern suburbs of Zhenjiang is the place where Mi Yi lived mainly in the past.”
Miyu died in Lianshui, Huai'an, but was buried in Zhenjiang. There is also a story in this. According to Yang Hong, the abbot of Zhaoyin Temple is particularly fond of Miyu's landscape paintings. Mi Yu gifted the abbot a volume and requested that he be buried here after his death. Mi Yi passed away during her tenure in Huai'an. Her son Mi Youren followed his father's last wish and buried Mi Yi in Nanshan.
“Zhenjiang has deep roots with Mi Yi. In addition to Mi Yi's tomb, they have also built Mi Yi Calligraphy Park.” Now, when we come to Zhenjiang, we can also see a plaque titled “Urban Mountain Forest” next to Helin Temple in the southern suburbs. The clouds of the crane forest that Miyu felt a thousand years ago seem to have never disappeared between mountains and fields.
According to Yang Hong, the main attractions in the Nanshan Scenic Area in Zhenjiang include Zhaoyin Temple, Helin Temple, and Su Gong Zhu Yuan. The rice tomb is located on the southwest side of Helin Temple, and is also one of the many attractions in the Nanshan Scenic Area. The tomb of Miyu covers an area of about an acre. The tombstone is engraved with “Tomb of the Song Lai Clan, Wailang Miyu Yuan Zhang, a member of the Song Dynasty,” and there is a pair of stone columns in front of the tomb, inscribed with the inscription “A Thousand Tales from the Earth, Yangyang Wenshi, Shiyi, and Pen, the Jungle Nashu, Lai, Dao Lang and Mijia Mountain”.
In 2011, the tomb of Miyu was renovated again. Because Mi Yu is one of the best calligraphers in the Northern Song Dynasty, many visitors admired it.
He is an “artistic all-rounder”, full of anecdotes
Mi Qi is a very interesting artist. Not only is his artistic achievement extremely high, but he is also capable of piling up a basket of anecdotes.
First and foremost, Mi Yu is an artistic multi-faceted artist. She can write poetry, paint, calligraphy, and is also a collector. Mi Yi's calligraphy, especially his calligraphy, was highly respected by future generations. Among the Big Four of the Northern Song dynasty, Mi Yi's calligraphy was outstanding. Wang Wenzhi, a calligrapher from the Qing Dynasty, praised his calligraphy “No need to brag about it; you can always stand on your own feet in ancient times.” Mi Yi's calligraphy is the result of everyone. He was deeply influenced by Yan Zhenqing and other Tang dynasty calligraphers in his early years. He later followed Su Shi's suggestions and began studying Jin Shu. Wang Xianzhi's “Mid-Autumn Festival Post” had a great influence on him, eventually forming a personal style. His handwriting was beautiful and brilliant.
When it comes to painting, Mi Yu is also a master. According to Yang Hong, Mi Yi is particularly fond of the natural scenery of Zhenjiang and founded the “Mi Shi Yun Mountain” landscape painting school. “Mi Yi really likes the scenery of Huanghe Mountain. According to legend, he was inspired by seeing clouds and fog in the mountains during his hike. He splashed ink and painted the hazy feeling between the mountains.” Miyu's landscape painting method was quite novel at the time, and was highly respected. However, Mi Yi has always focused on calligraphy, and there are very few paintings handed down from generation to generation. His painting “Yunqi Lou” is representative of the Mi Shi Yunshan style.
In addition to this, Mi Yi is also a collector. He is particularly fond of inkstones and strange stones. The “36 Peak Inks” by Li Yu, the late emperor of the Southern Tang Dynasty, are all in Mi Yi's collection. The book “History of Inkstone Appreciation” is currently a must-read for studying ancient inkstones. Since Shishi can write this book, let alone theoretical works on calligraphy and painting, Mi Yi has written many theoretical works on calligraphy. “The History of Books” and “Famous Quotes of Haiyue” are all works by Mi Yi, and have been taken seriously by generations of calligraphers. Meanwhile, “The History of Painting” records in detail Mi Yu's collection, appreciation of paintings, and creative experiences. In Zhenjiang, Mi Yi wrote a collection of over 100 volumes of poetry, “Mountain and Forest Collection,” but unfortunately most of them were scattered.
Mi Yi has deep feelings for the city of Zhenjiang. He refers to Zhenjiang's Nanshan as an “urban mountain forest.” “Until today, when we promote the tourism industry in Zhenjiang to the outside world, we still talk about real mountains, real water, urban mountains and forests.” Yang Hong said.
Throughout Mi Yi's life, she has never been involved in a political vortex. Her life is stable, and she is also living whatever she wants in pursuit of her hobbies, and is relaxed and happy. “Liangxi Man Zhi” once recorded that he was addicted to strange stones and worshiped stones, and was impeached for being seen as being a misdemeanor, but Mi Yu didn't care. There is also a folklore that records that Mi Yi is a serious cleaning freak. When she married her daughter, she chose her son-in-law as Duan Qi. The reason was that this young man's name sounded so clean that it was really hard to laugh at. (Correspondent Shen Zhao)
