The old lie to pick the moon from the sky
The rich man invited the old liar to work for a long time. He knew that the old lie was a skipper. He disobeyed discipline, and often dealt with him by withholding his wages, in a vain attempt to make him subservient.
One day, the rich man had a problem with the old lie and asked him to pay for his work for 1,000 burdens a day; otherwise, he wouldn't be given a penny. Without saying anything about the old lie, carry a long bamboo pole, walk to the rice field, raise the bamboo pole, fight all over the field, and scatter grains all over the dam. Fill up a thousand tons of space and run back to pay the ticket to the rich owner.
When the rich man went to the rice field to take a look, his eyes were full of anger. He insisted on lying and picking up this thousand burdens of grains. If the grains didn't leak out, they would stop.
“Grandpa, did you just call me to fight, didn't you ask me to pick it up?”
“I'm just calling you to fight, not that you spoil the food?”
“Old man, can one person make a thousand loads of millet a day? You made a problem difficult for me; I can only use this method to do it for you, otherwise what do you say?!”
Wealthy owners don't win against old lies; they don't pay old lies by gambling hard.
It just so happened that this day was the Mid-Autumn Festival. The moon rose in the sky, and the rich owner saw it. They wanted the moon in the sky to play high and low. As soon as the rich woman heard it, she hurriedly said to the old lie, “Old lie, you can take the moon from the sky and play with my baby, and compromise the merits. The next day, Lao Lie sharpened a sharp axe and climbed the rich owner's back mountain to cut down straight fir trees in preparation for making a ladder to the sky. The rich man found out and immediately rushed to stop it: “Old lie, what are you cutting down fir trees for?”
“Take the stairs up to the sky to pick the moon?”
