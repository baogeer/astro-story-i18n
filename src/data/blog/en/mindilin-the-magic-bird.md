 Once upon a time, there was a king in Tibet, and he had a beautiful and wise concubine. When the princess was nine months pregnant, the king happened to go to the domain to do an important task. Before leaving, the king took the concubine's hands and ordered over and over again: “Princess, the grease in my heart! As soon as my child is born, you ask the maid to go to the roof, play the big drums that have never been hit, and hang up the colored flower that has never been hung before. No matter where I am, they will arrive.”
Shortly after the king left, the princess gave birth to three little dolls, two boys and one girl. They looked like they had been carved out of white jade. Each one was more beautiful and cuter than the other. The princess asked the maid to go upstairs and beat the drums; if she asked her to go upstairs and hang up the flag, she didn't go either. The princess had no choice but to climb upstairs step by step on her own. At this point, the maid put the three little dolls in a large casserole and threw them into the big river behind the palace; she picked up the three puppies and placed them on the princess's bed.
The princess knocked on the top of the building, hung up the flag, and happily went downstairs to breastfeed the baby. However, the beautiful female doll was gone, the cute male doll was gone, and she was crawling on a satin-covered bed with three barking puppies. She was in a hurry and her hair was half white.
After less than a cup of tea, the king arrived on horseback. He suspected that the princess was a banshee and imprisoned her indiscriminately. Give only one bowl of clean water and half a spoonful of pickles every day. This is how the black-hearted maid took the throne of the princess.
Not far from the palace, there is an old man fishing. On this day, he was paddling a small cowhide boat and fishing in the river. In the rolling waves, I saw a large casserole floating over and hurriedly picked it up. He never dreamed that the casserole contained three little dolls that looked like Heavenly Immortals. The old man was overjoyed. He cheerfully brought them home and fed them delicious fish soup every day. The little dolls grew up day by day. They were smart, and beautiful. People in and outside the city all boasted about the old man's good fortune.
Furthermore, the three brothers and sisters saw the old man fishing every day. It was really hard work, so they each made a basket, secretly went to Linkari to pick up firewood, and got some ghee and salt at the market. Once, they were seen by an abominable maid. She thought, “These three kids are exactly like the princess. I'm sure they are the monsters she left behind; I have to find a way to kill them.” As a result, she wrapped her head in a black apron, carried a basket of cookies made from ghee brown sugar, mixed with poison, and went to the place where the three brothers and sisters sold firewood. She made a big fuss and said, “Oh hey, what a poor little doll, you haven't had tea yet! Haven't caught the chopsticks yet at noon! Come, come, come! I have cakes here, they are fragrant and kneeling, so you can take as many as you like!”
At first, the three brothers and sisters didn't want it at all. Later, they couldn't stand the maid's advice from the left and right, and only took the smallest cake. They didn't eat this cake, and they didn't even lick their tongues; they had to keep the old man fishing and come back for snacks.
In the evening, grandpa came home. The three brothers and sisters happily handed over the cookies and said, “Dad, someone with a good heart gave us so many, many cookies today. We are all full. This is for you.” No matter how the old man shunned them, the children refused to agree. The old man had no choice but to take a bite. Suddenly, his eyes were straight, his face was blue, and he fell down on a broken mat, and he couldn't talk anymore.
At this point, the three brothers and sisters are really frightened. They have traveled to many, many places, and when they meet people, they ask if there are any ways to bring the elderly back to life. Once, they went to a high snowy mountain and found a hermit practicing in a cliff cave. I don't know how many years the hermit lived here, and his snow-white hair dragged to the ground. The hermit was moved by the actions of the three brothers and sisters, and he showed them a path. He said, “From here on, we have gone over nine more snowy mountains. There is a beautiful lotus canyon. There is a talking magic horse called Mindilin in the Lotus Valley. Just bring it out and you can revive your grandpa. However, if you have to ask Min Di Lin to get through the three city gates, you can either turn it into a block of earth or stone. Only the bravest person in the world can achieve the goal.”
After listening to the hermit's instructions, the three brothers and sisters were extremely happy. The older brother said, “You two go back; I'll go catch Mindilinbird soon”! After speaking, walk straight to the canyon where Mindilindao lives. When he went over the ninth snowy mountain, he saw a tall and large city gate. There was an old man sitting outside the city gate. His hair was whiter than a conch. He asked, “Young man, where are you going?” The older brother said, “I'll go to the canyon and ask for Mindilingao!” The old man sighed and exhorted, “Young man, I've been guarding the gate here for over 100 years. I've seen with my own eyes that many people have invited Mindi Linniao. They either turned to stone or dirt. None of them can make it back alive. I think you should go back early!” The older brother said, “I'm not afraid; I want to invite Mindilindao out!”
When the old man saw that he had a very firm attitude, he pulled out a large cable from his arms and handed it to him and asked him to walk and throw it away. When the bird was invited, he could return the same way. The older brother followed the old man's orders and walked all the way to the canyon. He walked and went. The more he walked, the more tired he walked, the more sleepy he walked. It was so easy to walk to the outside of the second city gate. His feet looked like stones, and he couldn't lift them at all. Big brother wanted to crouch on the floor for a while, and that's it! He's turned into a stone and he can't move anymore!
The second brother and younger sister waited and didn't see the older brother come back. The second brother said, “Little sister, stay at home. I'll go find the older brother, and bring back Mindilinniao at the same time.” Who knows, the second brother, like his older brother, can easily walk outside the third city gate. He was really sleepy and had no choice. He leaned on the side of the mountain, and that's it! He turned into a pile of dirt.
The little sister waited and waited at home, and the two older brothers didn't even have a shadow. She decided to go find her brother, and at the same time invite Mindilin's mythical bird. She was alone. I don't know how many days and nights she walked through. She entered the first city gate and then the second gate. When she arrived at the third gate, she met a very old, very old lady who didn't even have a pearl-sized tooth in her mouth. She asked, “Little girl, where are you going?” My sister said. “I'm going to go find my brother, and I also want to invite Mindilin!” The old lady sighed and said, “Little girl, I've been here for over 100 years, and I've seen with my own eyes that many people invited Mindilin and the divine bird, all died on the way. Your two older brothers are the same. One turned into a stone and the other turned into a pile of dirt. I recommend you go back!”
When the little sister heard about what happened to her two older brothers, she was even more courageous. When the old lady saw that she had a very firm attitude, she gave her a big cable group and asked her to walk and throw at the same time, so she could find her way back. Little sister, go, go. No matter how tired she is, she won't stop; no matter how tired she is, she won't rest her feet. Finally, she finally walked into that beautiful canyon. There is a sea full of lotus flowers in the canyon, and a small bird as bright as the sun stands on the lotus. This is a sensitive flint bird.
Seeing the girl, the bird was surprised and moved, and said, “Amazing girl, you were the first person to walk up to me! If anything happens, just order it!” The little sister originally talked about asking it to save her dad. Min Dilin spread her golden wings and flew down her little sister's shoulder and said. “Little sister, let's go!”
The little sister, carrying Mindilin's mythical bird, walked through one city gate and another, following the wool yarn she had just thrown away. Due to her little sister's request, Mindi Linniao flew down the stone for a while and shouted, “Wake up!” After a while, it flew down on top of the pile and shouted “Don't sleep!” Those mounds and stones miraculously turned into living people. The two older brothers wiped their eyes and saw that their sister had been invited to Kamitori, so needless to say how happy they were!
When the three brothers and sisters got home, the first major thing was to save the old dad. Like a moment ago, Mindi Linniao flew down on Grandpa and shouted, “Grandpa, it's time to get up!” From the broken mat, Grandpa slowly extended his hands and yawned for a long time, and said, “Oh my god, I've really overslept. It's time to go fishing!”
The story of the old man coming back to life was told to ten, ten to one hundred, and finally reached the king's ears. The king was a little unconvinced, and rumored the old man and his three children. Min Diling Bird said, “When I go, I go with you. When you eat, you eat in the same bowl with the king; when you sit, you sit on the same mat with the king.”
The three brothers and sisters listened to the mythical bird and entered the royal palace. While sitting, they huddled on a mat with the king; when they ate, they all scrambled to eat the food in the king's bowl. The king was very strange. He just wanted to get angry. Mindy Lindao stood on the little sister's shoulder and said, “King! king! Don't get angry! Don't get angry! They are your children, and of course they want to eat with you; they are your children and of course they want to sit with you!” Next, Kamitori originally told the king about the fate of the three brothers and sisters.
Now the king understood everything. He invited his concubine out of the dungeon and cleaned up her prison. The maid had her nose cut off and banished to a barren mountain infested with bears. The three brothers and sisters and the old fisherman moved into the royal palace and lived a happy and happy life.
As for Mindi Linniao, no matter how the king, his wife, and the three brothers and sisters were retained, they didn't want to stay in the bustling city. As soon as their golden wings spread, they flew back to their beautiful Lotus Canyon.
