 Totem culture is a cultural phenomenon common to all nationalities or tribes in the world at a certain stage in history, as did the original Mongolian people. Although the remnants of Mongolian totem culture are not as colorful and spread all over the world, they are not unique. There are many remains of totem culture in rock paintings, ancient books, epics, travel stories, and especially in folk oral works handed down from generation to generation. As mentioned before, the totems worshipped by Mongolians include wolves, deer, bears, mangos, eagles, swans, trees, etc.
