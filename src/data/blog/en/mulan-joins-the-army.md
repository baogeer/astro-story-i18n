 At the end of Northern Wei, ethnic minorities such as Rouran and Khitan became more and more powerful. They often sent troops to invade the Central Plains region and loot property. In order to deal with them, the Northern Wei dynasty often recruited large numbers of soldiers and strengthened its garrison on the northern border.
Mulan is telling the story of a female hero of the time when she joined the military. Mulan is said to have the last name Hua. A native of Shangqiu (present-day southern Shangqiu County, Henan), she has been studying and writing with her father since childhood, and has been doing housework on weekdays. She also likes horseback riding and archery, and is good at martial arts. One day, the police officer in Yamen sent a military recruitment notice to recruit Mulan's father to become a soldier. But my father is old, so how can he join the military and fight? Mulan doesn't have an older brother, and her younger brother is too young. She couldn't bear to let her old father suffer, so she decided to dress up as a man and join the military as a surrogate father. Although Mulan's parents couldn't bear their daughter's trip, they had no choice but to agree to her going.
Mulan followed the team to the northern border. She is worried that the secrets of her masculine attire will be discovered, so be extra careful here. Marching during the day, Mulan closely followed the team and never dared to be left behind. Camping at night, she never dared to take off her clothes. When fighting, she always stormed ahead with her good martial arts. After being in the military for 12 years, Mulan has made great achievements. Her peers greatly admired her and praised her for being a brave and good boy.
The war was over, and the emperor summoned meritorious generals to discuss meritorious deeds. But Mulan doesn't want to be an official, nor does she want money; she just wants to get a fast horse so she can go home right away. The emperor readily agreed and sent an emissary to escort Mulan back.
When Mulan's parents heard that Mulan had returned, they were very happy and immediately rushed outside the city to greet her. My younger brother also slaughters pigs and sheep at home to comfort my sister, who is a national honor. When Mulan got home, she took off her war robe, changed into women's clothes, brushed her hair, and went out to thank her companions who escorted her home. The companions were all very surprised to see that Mulan was originally a daughter. They didn't expect that the comrade they had fought together for 12 years was actually a beautiful woman.
The story of Mulan's godfather joining the military soon spread. Later generations even made up this song and spread it widely. These songs eventually became a long narrative poem “Mulan Poem,” which has spread to this day.
