 The Gaoshan people are an ethnic minority living in Taiwan Province of China. This ethnic group lived in a primitive society until the early years of the Qing dynasty.
At that time, since there were no written words and science was extremely underdeveloped, people could only use imagination to explain everything that happened around them. Therefore, there were quite a few myths and legends about the Takayama people, which boils down to four categories.
The first type is a legend about the origin of one's own nation. This type of legend also varies among alpine people in various regions. For example, legends from the Taiyar region say that in ancient times there were two gods, male and female. Later, the two of them got married together, and the children they bore were ancestors of humans. Saast legends say that ancient floods flooded the tribe, and only one man survived by chance; he was the ancestor of humans.
The second category is legends about natural phenomena, that is, moving stories about the sun, moon, stars, floods, earthquakes, landslides, wind, rain, thunder, electricity, etc. For example, legends about the Sun and the Moon from Ames and other places are that the earth was dark in ancient times, and a man and woman saw that the world was distressed by darkness, so they ascended to heaven and turned into the sun and the moon illuminated the earth.
The third category is legends about the origin of customs and technology, including legends about tattoos, teeth chiselling, hunting and use of fire, weaving, agriculture, craftsmanship, pottery, etc. For example, legends about fire say that in ancient times, people went to the mountains and suddenly there was a strong wind. They saw that trees in the forest rubbed against each other and caught fire. When this person saw it, they imitated it, so they got the technique of wiping wood to get rid of the fire.
The fourth category is legends about animals and plants, including legends about birds, apes, dogs, goats, pine, plantains, etc. For example, according to the legend about why the fur of the Zhang Zi was smooth, there were no fires in ancient times; there were only scattered fires on an isolated island in the sea, but people couldn't cross the water, and ordered bears and leopards to go, but it didn't work. Later, a weak Zhang Zi broke through the turbulent waves and brought back the fire. People praised its bravery and caressed its fur, and as a result, its fur became smooth and supple.
In addition, the Takayama people also have some unique legends about tribal changes and heroic deeds. However, all of these myths and legends of the Takayama people were spread orally and recorded in Chinese and other ethnic characters.
