 Author: Hou Hongbin
Nie Yin Niang: See “Nie Yin Niang” in “The Legend” by Tang Pei Yi. Nie Yinniang is the daughter of General Nie Feng of Wei Bo. When she was 10 years old, she was “stolen” by a female Nepheon, who taught her sword skills. She was able to stab people in the daytime, but she was not able to see anyone, and was sent back to her family. Meanwhile, Nie Yin Niang, who has amazing skills, chose a teenager who can only polish the mirror and has no other powers as her husband. After Father Nie's death, coach Wei Bo and Chen Xujiedu made Liu Changyi at odds. They wanted Nie Yinniang to assassinate him, but Nie chose Liu instead. Later, Liu Chang's descendants entered the Hajj, and Nie left. After Liu's death, Nie also cried in front of Liu Fei, the Beijing Master.
“Kill one person in ten steps, don't leave a thousand miles behind; leave your clothes in the blink of an eye; hide your merits and fame.” The image of a standard knight is nothing more than that. The Tang dynasty was a great time to be brave. There were knights all over the world, such as small mushrooms after the rain. Even Li Bai himself was a young ranger, a middle-aged traveler, and an old immortal, very cool. On this basis, the strangest knight, Nie Yin Niang, was born. She came without a trace, picked flowers and killed people, crossed the river in one fell swoop, and hidden her fame.
During the first years of the Tang dynasty, General Nie Feng had a daughter named Nie Yinniang. At the age of ten, Master Picuni came to Huayuan. He liked her intelligence and wanted to ask Nie Feng for her. Of course, Nie Feng did not agree. The nun left only one sentence: “Even if the general locks her daughter in an iron cabinet, she will eventually be taken by me.” In order to prevent accidents, Nie Feng sent many additional guards to guard her daughter. There wasn't much trouble at night. He thought it was safe. He quickly went to his daughter's room to check it out. As a result, the hermit girl actually disappeared!
The hermit girl was taken to a large stone cave overnight, swallowed a pill, held a sword, and learned rock climbing and walking along the wall with two girls of average age. In the first year, the hermit girl stabbed the ape without incident; in the third year, the hermit girl was able to stab an eagle in the sky, and the sword became shorter; in the fourth year, the hermit girl used a sheep horn dagger to kill wicked people without getting noticed; in the fifth year, Master made the hermit girl kill people, saying that a certain official had harmed people a lot, and ordered her to assassinate them in the middle of the night. Nie Yinniang is no longer a technical problem, but this time she has encountered a psychological obstacle. She saw the official making fun of the kid. The kid was so cute. She couldn't help but beat him until dark. The nun reprimanded her and taught her, “If you meet this kind of person in the future, you must first kill the person he loves and then kill him.” Master also opened her brain and hid the claw dagger inside. When she needed to use it, he pulled it directly out of the back of her head. It was more brilliant than Sun Wukong's gold hoop. The hardest thing to break through in murder is the psychological barrier and moral barrier. Philosophical education about justice and injustice guaranteed Nie Yinniang's mental health after the murder.
Like “Red Line,” this is the earliest Chinese martial arts novel, and the main characters are all young girls. This first paragraph establishes the usual paradigm in martial arts novels. A child of an aristocrat always started learning martial arts under the guidance of a world leader for some reason. Martial arts are progressing step by step and getting more sophisticated. It's like wearing a sandbag every day to practice jumping, and one day you will jump out. If you are interested, you might as well find a copy of the martial arts instruction manual and practice it.
In this way, a generation of great heroes, Nie Yin Niang, rose to prominence.
One day, a young man who specializes in polishing mirrors for people started a business in front of Nie Fu Men, and the hermit asked to marry a teenager who polishes mirrors. Although the door was wrong, Dad Nie Feng had no choice but to agree. Later, Grand Master Wei Bo hired Nie Yinniang as a staff member with a high salary, and invited the hermit woman to go to Chen Xu to assassinate Liu Changyi. At that time, there were talented killers all over the world, and Liu Changyi was probably one, with a bit of hooliganism. He guessed that Nie Yin Niang was coming, so he sent his subordinates to wait for the hermit couple at the entrance of the northern city. The hermit woman was surprised that her whereabouts were discovered. She thought Liu was an intelligent person; she simply acted as a bodyguard for Liu's men.
A month later, Wei Bo sent Jing Jing Er to kill Liu Changyi. Nie Yin Niang already calculated that in the middle of the night, two long red and white scarves fell in the air, wrapped around the foot of Liu Changyi's bed. The hermit maiden kicked off the wall and slashed the air. Soon, the hermit woman killed Jing Jing Er, and took out potions to destroy the corpse. Wei Bo also sent Kung Eun to assassinate Liu Changzi. Nie Yinniang knew she couldn't beat her, so she hid in Liu's intestines and waited for an opportunity to act, so Liu Dai slept with Yu Yi and Gu Yu Yu. At 3 o'clock, Liu Changyi heard a loud voice coming from her neck. The hermit woman popped out of Liu's mouth and said to Liu Hexi: “Master Liu, you are safe. You have always been very proud when you have empty hands. It's a shame for him to not be good at attacking after getting ready, so at the same time as we talked, he had already left his long distance!” Liu Changyi looked down and suddenly found a deep cut on the jade piece!
After a few more years, the hermit woman said goodbye to Liu Changyi and her husband, traveled all over the world, visited famous people all over the world, and never knew where it ended.
The story of Nie Yin Niang is based on Pei Yi's “The Legend”, which is the earliest martial arts novel. After all, they are ancestors; they aren't serious enough; if people were today, they would definitely stir up the hermit's love into a triangle or four-corner love, and then resent family hatred against the country. They would have to force people to die, force them to kill their father and brother, and force them to become ferocious. That's why it's called a martial arts novel.
