 Siber folk tales spread throughout the Chabchar Siber Autonomous County in Xinjiang.
Nimanji's mother died when she was a child, and soon she had a stepmother and brought along a daughter named Horning-chi.
Her stepmother harshly abused Nimanzi, but the longer she grew, the more beautiful she was; although Horning-chi was good at wearing red and green, she was ugly the longer she grew.
Her stepmother's jealousy made Niemanji go herding cows every day.
With the help of old talking cows, Nimanji's situation improved.
Stepmom knows the secret to killing cows and eating meat.
As ordered by the cow, Nimanji wrapped all of its bones in cowhide and buried it under a pile of teardrops in the wild.
At the spring of the following year, Nimanji wore bone-shaped clothing and headgear to the racecourse like a fairy. Zhang Jing's son, the handsome Xilin Ah, fell in love with him.
Her stepmother, mother, and daughter together defrauded Nimanji of her clothes and headgear, and pushed her down the well to death.
Her stepmother made another plan for Honingzhi to marry in place of Nimanji.
Later, with the help of a red-billed bird that had become the soul of Nimanji, Xilinah retrieved Nimanji's body from the well and buried.
Her stepmother was frightened and hanged to death, and her daughter was also frightened to death. Accompanied by a red-billed bird, Xilina never married in her life.
