This article is excerpted from People's Daily News Author: Liu Bo Yang Liu The original title: Liu Guanzhang is not “Taoyuan Three Kindings”
Although people regard it as an example of uniting love, the truth of history is that the three did not tie the knot. The Three Kingdoms were actually invented by the author of “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms” based on folklore and the plot requirements of the novel.
Reason 1: “The Chronicles of the Three Kingdoms” and “The General Guide to the Administration of Finance” have never mentioned this matter.
“Three Kingdoms: The Legend of Guan Yu” records that when Liu Bei gathered a crowd in his hometown, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei had already surrendered to him. Liu Beihou is Ping Yuan-sang. He “sleeps in the same bed with Guan and Zhang, like a brother. However, people sit widely (in crowded places), serve all day long, and revolve around with the Lord before them, without avoiding hardships and dangers”. Guan Yu also said, “I was blessed by General Liu and pledged to die together.”
From the above two sentences, it can be seen that the three people Liu, Guan, and Zhang have a close relationship, shared solidarity, and shared a partnership in suffering. Guan Yu and Zhang Fei are very loyal to Liu Bei. They stand by Liu Bei all day long in crowded places to protect him and protect him from hardships and dangers. However, this sentence only refers to the three “gracious brothers”; it is not necessarily a brotherly relationship. Guan Yu's statement further confirmed that the three did not marry because he called Liu Bei “General Liu.”
“Three Kingdoms: The Biography of Zhang Fei” also records that Zhang Fei served Liu Bei with Guan Yu when he was young, “Yu was a few years older, and Brother Fei had an affair.” Among them, it was only mentioned that Zhang Fei treated Guan Yu as his older brother; Liu Bei was not mentioned. However, treating someone older than yourself as an older brother, calling someone a “older brother” doesn't necessarily mean praying for a brother.
“Chronicles of the Three Kingdoms: The Legend of Liu Ye” records that Guan Yu lost Jingzhou. After being murdered by the Dongwu people, Wei Wendi asked whether Liu Bei would send troops to defeat Wu to avenge Guan Yu. Liu Ye answered, “Liu Bei and Guan Yu were 'righteous monarch, father and son'. Guan Yu was murdered. If Liu Bei couldn't avenge him, the kindness to Guan Yu wouldn't be complete.” Liu Ye described their personal relationship as “father and son,” without even using the word “brother.”
The above historical materials explain two problems: First, Liu Guanzhang had a close relationship, just like brother, father and son; second, there is no historical evidence that the three have ever worshiped a brother.
Reason 2: The age ranking is wrong. If they decide, it should be Guan Liu Zhang, not Liu Guanzhang.
In the first episode of “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms,” it is written that in the first year of Emperor Han Ling's Zhongping (184), the Yellow Scarf Uprising broke out. At the time, Liu Bei “was already 28 years old.” Afterwards, on the same day, he met Zhang Fei and Guan Yu, two great figures. They both met and hated each other late, so the next day, the three held a popular “Taoyuan Affair.” There seems to be a mistake in “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms” that Liu Bei was “28 years old” at the time. According to “The Chronicles of the Three Kingdoms: The Book of Shu,” Liu Bei died in the 3rd year of Zhang Wu (223) in Xia April, “63.” Since the ancients calculated ages as fictitious years, according to this inverse analogy, Liu Beiying was born in 161. Well, in the first year of Zhong Ping, he should have been less than 25 years old.
Let's take another look at Guan Yu. According to historical records, Guan Yusheng's year is unknown. He died in the 24th year of Jian'an (219). After research, Yuan Dynasty scholar Hu Qi believes that Guan Yu was probably born in 159, two years older than Liu Bei.
During the Kangxi period of the Qing Dynasty, while digging a well in Guan Yu's hometown, Jiezhou (Yuncheng, Shanxi), someone unearthed the tombstone of Guan Yu's ancestral tomb, which engraved the history of Yu's family. At the time, an official named Zhu Dan wrote the “Tomb of the Ancestors of Guan Hou” based on this. The article stated that Guan Yu was born three years after Han Yanxi (160). According to this statement, Guan Yu is also one year older than Liu Bei.
Zhang Fei's age is relatively easy to define. According to the “Guan Gong Chronicle”, Zhang Fei and Liu Bei are four years old.
Based on the above information, it can be concluded that of Liu Guanzhang's three, Guan Yu is the longest, followed by Liu Bei, and Zhang Fei the youngest. If Taoyuan were to have an agreement, it would have been “Guan Liu Zhang” rather than “Liu Guanzhang.”
Since the “Taoyuan Settlement” not only does not have much historical basis, but also has loopholes, it cannot be fully trusted. The reason why people have legends about Taoyuan ties is probably their imagination and attachment to the “Enruo Brothers” in history books. As for folklore and “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms,” they call Liu Bei the big brother because Liu Bei is a monarch and Guan Zhang Shichen. Judging from the administrative level, Liu has always been superior to Guan and Zhang.
The evolution of “Taoyuan Kizuyi” over the generations
The “Taoyuan Connection” section in “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms” was created by the author based on folklore to suit the plot of the novel. Before “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms” was written, the story of Liu, Guan, and Zhang Taoyuan's marriage had been circulating among the public for a long time.
Formed during the Song and Yuan dynasties
As early as the Song Dynasty, people began to circulate the story of the “Three Taoyuan Ties”. In the “Chronicles of the Reconstruction of the Temple” of the late Song Dynasty, there was a saying, “In the beginning, Wang (Guan Yu) and General Fei (Zhang Fei) and Chao Lie (Liu Bei) were friends and were about brothers”. In the Yuan Dynasty, the story became more and more complete, and different versions appeared. Here are two representative versions.
This is how the story is described in the original drama “Liu Guan, Zhang, and Taoyuan's Three Strings”:
The demon Yin Zang of Puzhou wants to stand on his own feet; he invited Guan Yu to be the leader. Guan Yu killed this person and then fled to Fanyang in Zhuozhou. At this point, Zhang Fei was opening a local butcher shop. On this day, Zhang Fei went out and deliberately held down a knife with a kilogram of boulders in front of the store, telling his buddy that if someone could remove the knife from under the boulder, they would give him meat without taking a penny, and make the buddy remember where he lived. Soon, Guan Yu passed by this store and heard about the incident. He easily removed the boulder and removed the knife from under the stone, but refused to ask for meat. Zhang Fei learned the news after returning to the store. He went to visit Guan Yu's hotel and treated Guan Yu as his older brother. The two later met Liu Bei again. Seeing that Liu Bei was so good-looking, they invited him to drink together. Liu Bei lay drunk and saw a red chain snake burrowing into Liu Bei's seven tricks. Guan Yu felt that “this person's blessing will be priceless in the future,” so he also worshipped Liu Bei as his older brother. The three slaughtered cows and horses in Taoyuan outside the city, sued heaven and earth, and vowed “not to be born on the same day; only to die on the same day.”
The “Three Kingdoms of Taoyuan” in the Yuan Dynasty's “Three Kingdoms Peace Tales” is more realistic:
Guan Yu is a native of Xie Liang from Puzhou, Pingyang. He was born with amazing phoenix eyes, a purple jade face, and is nine feet two inches tall. He enjoys watching “Chunqiu” and “Zuo Chuan”. Every time he sees Gangster Thieves, he gets angry. Later, because local officials were greedy for money and bribes, and maimed Li people, Guan Yu killed the county governor in one fell swoop, then fled into exile and fled to Zhuo County.
There is a local in Zhuo County. His name is Zhang Mingfei. He is over nine feet tall and sounds like a giant bell. He is a wealthy local family. On this day, he was standing in front of his house, just as Guan Gong was walking by. Zhang Fei saw that this man was born to look good, so he went ahead and greeted him. Guan Gong then told Zhang Fei about himself. After listening to Guan Gong's remarks, Zhang Fei felt that Guan Gong had the will to be fine, so he invited him to the hotel to discuss it together. The two enjoyed each other and spoke on the same page, as if they were old friends.
At this point, it just so happened that Liu Bei was selling sandals on the street, and after they were sold, he also came to the hotel to buy alcohol and eat. Guan and Zhang are indescribable blessed to see that Liu Bei was born in good shape. As a result, Guan Gong offered Liu Bei a glass of wine. Liu Bei was also unkind; he drank it all. After a few conversations, the three simply sat down at the same table. After drinking for a while, Zhang Fei said it was not convenient to talk here, so he invited Liu and Guan to go to his house for a break. In Taoyuan after Zhang Fei's family, the three of them set up a bar to drink. During the meeting, the three were ranked first: Liu Bei was the longest, Guan Gong second, and Zhang Fei was the youngest. As a result, the three became brothers. The older one was the older brother and the younger brother. They also slaughtered a white horse to sacrifice the sky and killed the black cow. They don't want to be born on the same day; they just want to die on the same day. The three sit and sleep together and swear to be brothers.
Reached its peak in “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms”
There is no doubt that “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms” carried forward the story of Taoyuan's transformation and brought it to the highest level of morality. Entering the Ming Dynasty, the Taoyuan Taoyuan Ties story has been circulating among the public for a long time, and there are many versions. Luo Guanzhong wrote the classic version of “Taoyuan Three Knots” by stripping down and refining the “Taoyuan Story”, removing some absurd ingredients. In “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms,” Liu, Guan, and Zhang all became brothers under the general premise that they had the same goals: to fight the army and destroy thieves, protect the country and the people, and carry out major events together. As a result, the story's intention is higher than other versions, and the friendship relationship is a bit higher. Plus, “Romance of the Three Kingdoms” itself is a great work, and its background is real, so “Taoyuan Ties” is even more convincing.
Why do people relish it
In the official history, which mainly focuses on “The Chronicles of the Three Kingdoms,” Cao Cao is actually a heroic figure with great talents. Liu, Guan, Zhang, and Zhu Geliang, led by Liu Bei, were not the epitome of justice or loyalty; they were only one party in the tripartite regime's seizure at the time. However, in the following dynasties, the image of these two parties underwent a dramatic reversal. Cao Cao gradually became an adulterer, and Liu Bei's side became the personification of justice. In particular, Guan Yu and Zhu Geliang were gradually deified and given the rank of emperor or even higher than the emperor. Until today, there are several thousand Guandi temples all over the country, more than the Confucius Temple. This dramatic reversal mainly occurred during the Tang and Song dynasties, and the Southern Song period was a major turning point.
At the end of the Northern Song Dynasty, after experiencing the “shame of Jingkang,” the Northern Song regime was destroyed by the Jin Soldiers. The remaining forces were unable to resist the war and were isolated, and established the capital in Lin'an (Hangzhou). At the time, the Southern Song people hated the Jin people who occupied the north. During the Three Kingdoms period, Cao Cao's Wei State occupied the North, and Shu Guo and Wu Guo occupied the South. Therefore, during the Southern Song period, whenever the story of the Three Kingdoms was mentioned, Cao Cao became people's imaginary enemy. The incompetent government and innocent people all unanimously transferred their hatred of the Jin people to Cao Cao. As a result, people have raised Liu, Guan, and Zhang's image of justice even more.
Furthermore, the Chinese have always promoted the ideals of loyalty and morality, and the story of Liu, Guan, and Zhang just satisfies this psychological need. Furthermore, in Chinese history, Liu Bei has always been regarded as an orthodox Han clan, while Cao Cao is seen as an adulterer who usurped power. Therefore, people are also willing to let Liu Bei have two brothers who are strong in martial arts and are fiercely loyal. However, “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms” clearly supports Liu Bei and belittles Cao Cao.
Knowing this historical background, it's not surprising that Liu, Guan, and Zhang Jieyi appeared in “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms.”
