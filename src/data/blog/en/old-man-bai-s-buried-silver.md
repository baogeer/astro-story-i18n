 Legend has it that there is a farmer, nicknamed Bai Weng. He is loyal, hardworking, honest and frugal. His family doesn't have many fields, but the barns are full of grain and pigs, so he doesn't worry about food and clothing. He has a son named Dan Yin, who is lazy because his family is rich. The neighbor next door had a bad heart. He was very infatuated with Bai Weng's wealth. He insisted on Dan Yin on his back and said, “Your father had a few tanks of silver buried in the ground, enough for you to eat for the rest of your life.” As a result, Dan Yin became more lazy to work hard. Bai Weng often taught his son, “If you work hard, you are rich; if you are lazy, you are poor; if you are not rich, you are full; if you are not lazy, you are hungry!” In her heart, Dan Yin didn't forget how many tanks of money she had underground. She took her father's words as a matter of ear, and still didn't work hard.
One day, Bai Weng was in critical condition in bed, worried that his son would not live long after his death, and his tears washed down. He said to his son, “After I die, you have to cultivate those few plots of land well before you have a good life.” Seeing that her father wasn't doing well, Dan Yin softly asked, “Dad, my neighbors all say how many pots of silver you have buried in the ground, where exactly?” Bai Weng understood his son's intentions. He slowly opened his eyelids and said powerlessly, “Yes, all my money is buried in the ground, and every plot of land has a cylinder. As long as you work hard every year and dig deep and fine, you'll find it one day.” When I finished speaking, I closed my eyes.
In order to find silver, Dan Yin often kneels in front of her father's spiritual position to pray for the appearance of spirits and tell him where the silver is buried. But it never passed the test. Seeing that the spring farming season had arrived, Danyin had no choice but to carry the plow down to the field. He kept in mind his father's last words and made every effort to plow the ground deeply, rake again after plow, and cut the soil finely. Every day I leave early and return late, fertilize the soil, catch insects and weed, and do all kinds of work very seriously. The neighbor was curious and asked, “Dan Yin, when your father was alive, you were so lazy. Why did you work hard again after your father died?” Dan Yin just kept quiet and worked hard. I hope that money will come out someday. He worked so hard, and even though he didn't find any money, this year's crops had a good harvest, and his life was still more than rich.
Danyin became a family when he was 20 years old. After his wife walked through the door, Dan Yin also told her about her father's burial of money. The wife is a devout person, and she admonished her husband, “After silver is buried underground, it will change itself. Only honest people who do what their father says can they find it.” Since then, the couple have been working hard, and their days are getting better.
“Where is the silver actually hidden?” Although Dan Yin's life is rich, he still misses it and never forgets it. He worked for most of his life, gave birth to three boys and two girls, and still couldn't find any money. Slowly, he felt pessimistic and discouraged. He didn't work hard anymore. He didn't cultivate the fields deeply, and the soil was not well cultivated. As a result, the crops grown in the fields are growing worse and the yield is decreasing year by year. Coupled with the increase in the family population, life is gradually becoming more difficult. The wife complained that her husband was not religious, and the husband argued for three days that his wife was not filial pious.
People are poor and have many nightmares. One night, Dan Yin had a dream: he was working in the field, and a cylinder of silver was lined up in the ground, leaving him dazzled. He reached for it, and the silver was taken away again; he chased past, and all the silver went into the hole. Just when he was going to go into the hole to find it, his father stopped him at the entrance and yelled, “You're too lazy, you can't get my money.”
As soon as she woke up, Dan Yin was lying in bed thinking about what she had dreamed about, thinking about her father's last words, and finally came to an understanding: When my father died, it meant a lot. As a result, he asked his children to come to bed and told them his father's last words, affectionately admonishing them: only when the fields are cultivated deeply can the grass grow well; millet is silver, and the rice bowl is a silver pot!
The children listened attentively to their father's lessons and couldn't help but nod.
