 I have an old husband, Zhang, who is from Shanxi. Since the daughter is about to get married, she brought her family to Jiangnan to personally buy a dowry for her. When the boat arrived at Jinshan, Mr. Zhang wanted to cross the river first, and ordered his family not to fry fishy fish on the boat. Because there is a giant mackerel in the river, when it smells, it will destroy boats and devour people. It has been harmful here for a long time.
After Grandpa Zhang left, the family forgot his instructions and grilled meat on the boat. Suddenly, huge waves in the river knocked over the boat, and Zhang's wife, daughter, and others all fell into the water and sank. Lao Xiang Gong took a small boat back to the place where the big boat docked. He didn't see his wife and daughter. He didn't want to live in pain; he couldn't help but take immediate revenge. He climbed to Jinshan, met the monk at Jinshan Temple, inquired about the harm caused by the whale monster, and wanted to take revenge by getting rid of the whale. The monk was very frightened when he heard it and was surprised to say, “We have been living close to it all year round, afraid of being harmed, so we had to worship it as a fairy and pray that it doesn't get angry. Pigs are often slaughtered to slaughter sheep. Half of the land is thrown into the river, and the whale immediately jumps out and devours it. Who dares to go against it!”
After hearing this, Grandpa Zhang immediately came up with a plan for revenge. He brought in a few blacksmiths and set up a stove in the middle of Jinshan to make a large block of iron weighing more than 100 kg. I asked about the places where big whales are often found. They called a few strong men who used large iron tongs to lift a block of iron and throw it into the river. The whistle leaped out and swallowed. After a while, the river surged like a mountain, and in an instant the waves were flat again. The dead body of the giant mackerel had already surfaced. The past merchants and the monks of Jinshan Temple were happy. They built the Zhang Lao Xiang Gong Temple, hung a statue of Zhang Lao Xiang Gong in the temple, and worshipped him as the god of water. People prayed to him, and they were very clever.
