Wang Zhaojun made permanent amends to the Han dynasty and the Huns, resolutely accepted the emperor's bounty, and married the Huns monarch as a princess, making for the beautiful legend of “Zhaojun leaving the country.” However, after the monarch, who was older than her, died of illness overnight, according to the tradition of the Huns, she had to marry the monarch's son — the newly ascended Singh. As a last resort, she wrote a copy of the song, sent someone back to Chang'an, and asked Emperor Dahan to allow her to return to her hometown for the rest of her life. Unexpectedly, Emperor Dahan was forced by the border situation and wanted her to marry and stay in the Huns alone. However, Wang Zhaojun had only one thought: he must go back to his hometown once.
Late that night, while Wang Zhaojun was having fun, someone suddenly reported that Pura Ba in the northern part of the Huns had invaded and that thousands of troops and horses were slaughtering Wangcheng. Shan was taken aback. After summoning ministers to discuss, he personally led the army against the enemy. At this point, the entire palace of Wangcheng was in disarray. After leaving with her soldiers, Wang Zhaojun knew that her chance to flee the Huns had arrived. She hurriedly dressed up with her handmaiden, Demei, and successfully attempted to leave the city at the time of the riots in Wangcheng. However, at dusk the next day, dozens of people alone were rushing to catch up. Wang Zhaojun didn't know what to do. He overheard a howling of wolves not far from behind him. At this point, it was dark, and the sound of wolves screaming in the wilderness was very scary. De Mei was happy to say, “We're saved, princess, let's go.” The German and American dynasties whipped Wang Zhaojun's pony tail harshly. The horse scattered all four hooves and ran off.
Furthermore, when the wolf's cry sounded from all sides, he had to order him to stop and prepare to fight against wild wolves. Wild wolves in the prairie are very ferocious. They often lay out of their nests; as few as few as dozens and as many as hundreds. At this moment, the howling of wolves one by one made those war horses frightened, and they all cried harshly. However, when they were fully prepared to face the wolf pack, those wolves didn't attack them at all; they just howled so hard that people were shocked.
The sound of the wolf continued for about an hour, and gradually became inaudible. Strangely enough, he hurriedly called a few bodyguards to check it out. The bodyguard came back soon after and said in a fit of anger that he hadn't even seen a single wolf. He was only puzzled and said, “This is strange. I said last night that Pura Ba was invading the country. I clearly heard the cries of thousands of troops and horses. I went up and took a look. I didn't see a single person. Is that really haunted?” A general bodyguard said, “Alone, I don't think this looks like a haunted ghost. We went out to face the enemy, but we didn't see any enemies, but the princess from the Han dynasty disappeared. We chased them here, and another wolf attacked, but after so long, there wasn't a single wolf. This must have something to do with her; did she use any magic tricks?”
Saying this, Shan Yu also felt that it made sense; he quickly got on the horse and wanted to go after Wang Zhaojun. But it was too late. I saw a gray haze in the distance, and the real wolf had arrived. In the dark of the night, wolves from all sides copied it from all sides. They only hurriedly ordered a battle against the wolf pack, but the wolf pack was ferocious and difficult to resist. Alone fled back to Wangcheng with his disabled soldiers. In one fell swoop, he immediately rushed 100,000 horses to the junction with the Han dynasty. He first sent someone to send a message to the Han dynasty border guard in the northwest, saying that Wang Zhaojun fled the Huns without permission and that she must be detained. Had she been released into the Han dynasty, the hungs' army of 100,000 would not be indifferent. I only know that Wang Zhaojun and her handmaiden are weak women. Each one is petite. They can't help but struggle to run day and night on the prairie, and no matter which path they take, they have to guard the city through the Han dynasty border, making it difficult for them to fly.
Wang Zhaojun certainly had a very difficult journey along the way, but with a sense of urgency to return to their hometown, they arrived at Yumen Pass with extraordinary perseverance. However, he Tianxiang, the guard of Yumen Pass, has already received a message that Wang Zhaojun's escape from the Huns was a major crime of annihilation. If she were let into the city, causing war at the border, his family, young and old, would turn into swords. As a result, while sending people to send the news back to Chang'an in an expedited manner using 800 miles, he also strictly prevented people from entering the city. Wang Zhaojun and others arrived outside Yumen and watched the tall and strong city wall in a dilemma. There are strict checks at the entrance of the city, and they don't have any memorabilia, let alone identify themselves. How can they get in? If you don't go in from here, you'll have to delay time and stay on someone else's land forever. If you die, aren't you still a visitor to another country?
Just when Wang Zhaojun couldn't do anything, Demei brought someone to her. This is a man in his 30s. He looks like he is Han, but his accent has a strong Hunnian flavor. Demi said his name is Yan Shi. Yan Shi said to Wang Zhaojun, “The princess doesn't need to worry about entering the city; I have a way for you to get in.” Although Wang Zhaojun saw that he had a strong heart, he was still a little uneasy. He asked, “Yan Zhuang, what can you do? Should we cooperate?” Pointing strictly at one's own mouth is considered an answer. Seeing that Wang Zhaojun didn't understand what he meant, De Mei said, “Yan Zhuangshi's mouth is extraordinary. If he is lucky, he can confuse others by making thousands of troops and horses. That night, he performed his stunts outside of Wangcheng, leading to Single Yu.” “Isn't this the ventriloquist technique we Han people are best at?” Wang Zhaojun suddenly realized, “Yan Zhuangshi, how can I be grateful to you when you helped me like this?” Yan Shi said, “The princess endured all her suffering in the Huns for the sake of Dahan, which is very respectable. Lao Dan died; the emperor should have welcomed you back. However, in order to strengthen the border, he also sacrificed you; those of us who walk the river and lake really can't stand it. As a result, I came to Hungary, and I stayed for several years in order to be able to save you. Not long ago, I finally met a German and American girl.” Wang Zhaojun immediately burst into tears and said, “It's not that I don't think about Dahan; I just wanted to go back to my hometown to see it, but I didn't even agree to this request. To get to this point now, it's a last resort.”
On the night of the same day, a terrible cry sounded outside the west gate of Yumen Pass. The cries were sharp and harsh, sad and angry. They cried here and there. The crying continued, as if tens of thousands of ghosts were pouring in. Among their cries, there were also screams from the frying pan, the cry from Mount Dao, the reprimand from the devil, and the cries of the judge, mixed with the horrible cries of wolves, tigers, and birds. They started one after another, and each wave was higher than the wave. The soldiers guarding the city couldn't see anything. They all shivered in horror and tried hard to hide their ears and not hear those sounds, yet those sounds were getting closer and closer, and it seemed like they were rushing to the entrance of the city. Next, the sound of knocking at the door, shouting at the door, and knocking on the door continued. The wind was whistling, and the little devils were shouting in a mischievous manner. It was really overcast. “Open the door --” “Open the door --” “We are generals who died outside of Guan, we need to go back to our hometown --” “Don't let us go into the city, our bad spirits have nowhere to go --” “Dad --” and “Ma'ah --” the screams were particularly scary and creepy on a quiet night. Soldiers guarding the castle invariably came to open the city gate, hoping that the wild ghosts would disperse sooner. At the moment, Yan Shi, outside the city gate, had a bulging face. All kinds of sounds in his mouth came out so naturally that even Wang Zhaojun, who was hiding next to him, was frightened to hear it. When the city gate opened “crackly,” he stopped his accent and turned around and said, “Princess, go in. Once inside, go straight out of the city through the East Gate, and I'll pick you up there. German and American girls, be careful to protect the princess.” Wang Zhaojun knew it was superfluous to say thank you at the moment. She nodded and went into the city with Germany and the United States.
The next day, when the sky was overcast, Wang Zhaojun was stopped 20 miles from the city by He Tianxiang with someone. Although Wang Zhao's two servants were dressed up in disguise, the way they tightly wrapped their faces attracted He Tianxiang's attention, and Wang Zhaojun was recognized. He Tianxiang knelt down in front of her and prayed, “Princess, do you know the consequences of your departure? The hundred-hundred-thousandth army has already arrived at Yumen Pass...” Of course, Wang Zhaojun believes that he had ambitions to invade the Han dynasty alone. His own escape this time is likely to be an excuse for him to use force. I wanted to go back, and I also felt that I had reached the land of the Han dynasty; not going back to my hometown would be a lifelong regret. Wang Zhaojun's left and right are difficult; it's better not to know what to do for a moment. Yan Shi always followed Wang Zhaojun and secretly protected her; at this point, she had to show up. He walked up to Wang Zhaojun and said, “Princess, I'll listen to your words. Go ahead, I'll pave the way for you. Back off, I'll escort you.” Wang Zhaojun wiped away his tears and sighed, “I can't kill innocent people for myself; I will go back to the Huns.” Strict nodded thoughtfully.
A few days later, they were solemnly greeted by the army of the Huns and asked to see Dan Yu. How could anyone see him, an unknown pawn? On this night, I was suddenly awakened from my sleep by the sound of fighting. A bodyguard hurriedly reported that something strange had happened. He clearly heard the sound of thousands of troops and horses charging into battle. However, when everyone was ready for battle, no one enemy could see it. Without finishing the conversation, another bodyguard came in in a panic and reported that someone from outside had broken in all the way and clearly saw that he was alone, but there seemed to be countless people behind him. Shan Yu hurriedly left the tent, only to see that he stood tall. The people in his mouth turned their backs on their horses, the earth-shaking sound of killing, and the roar of weapons intersected, causing chaos in the army of the Huns. How do you fight a battle when you can't find an enemy? Now that I can see it clearly, all the voices are coming from a strict mouth. What kind of magic is this?
“Heroes shut up, heroes shut up, dare to ask who the hero is a superior person?” I just hurriedly greeted them and shouted. “It doesn't matter who I am. I believe it would be difficult to win or lose a battle with 100,000 werewolves, tigers, and leopards just because they have no interest.” At this point, Shan Yu was amazed by his strict ventriloquism, and thought to himself why not take this opportunity to receive his account? He said, “OK, I'll allow Wang Zhaojun to go back to his hometown once, but on one condition, you have to work for our Huns.” Yan seriously thought that after Wang Zhaojun returned to the Huns, there might be other problems. Since I helped her, I had to help her to the end, and said, “I would like to work for the Huns, but you have to promise not to offend Dahan, and not prosecute the princess for escaping from the Huns without permission.”
With strict help, Wang Zhaojun finally returned to the hometown he thought about day and night. He was also a trustworthy person.
