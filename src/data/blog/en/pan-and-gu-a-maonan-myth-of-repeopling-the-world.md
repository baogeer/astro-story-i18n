 Pan and Guben are two brothers and sisters. They both grow gourds. They water and fertilize them every day. The gourds grow as big as a flower cellar. Later, there was a lot of water on the ground, and Pan Hegu got into the gourd and floated on the water. After the water receded, there were only two of them left in the world. How can we reinvent people and the world? The brother and sister discussed and always thought it would be difficult to get married. Later, they agreed that the two would each carry a stone mill to the top of the mountain and roll the stone mill down from the top of the mountain, such as putting the top and bottom of the rolling stone mill together, which proved that they had a marriage. Strangely enough, the stone mill rolled down the mountain and actually fit together. As a result, the brother and sister got married and had a small child. They both chopped the child into pieces and made crows and eagles peck and scatter them all over the place. Three days later, there were people everywhere.
Selected from “Selected Myths of Ethnic Minorities in China” by Gu Deming, 1984 edition, Northwest University for Nationalities Research
