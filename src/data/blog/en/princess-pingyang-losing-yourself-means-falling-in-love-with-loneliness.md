 Author: Hou Hongbin
Princess Pingyang: Sister of Emperor Liu Che of Han Wu. She first married Hou Cao Shou of Pingyang. She was widowed at age 40 and married General Wei Qing. Wei Zifu, the family diva, was recommended to Emperor Wu, and Wei Zifu later became an empress. Later, the diva Mrs. Li was recommended to Emperor Wu, and Mrs. Li was especially favored. It is very powerful and has influence on the political situation.
Ban Jieyu, a virtuous person, is outstanding, beautiful and amazing. Emperor Han Cheng once loved her very much. In order to be inseparable from her at all times, he specially ordered someone to make a larger tanker so he could travel with her. However, Ban Jieh-yu didn't accept this sentiment. She only wanted to help her husband become the master of the world, saying, “All sages have famous ministers on their side. Only Xia Yi, Shang Yi, and Zhou You have a lucky concubine in the seat. If I go in and out in the same car as you, then I'm very similar to them.” Emperor Han Cheng had no choice but to take it down. Ban Jieyu brought all of his loyal servant's to her boudoir. Unfortunately, Emperor Han Cheng has since snubbed Ban Jieyu and began to pamper Sister Zhao Feiyan.
She picked it all up herself. This beautiful woman made the mistake of not doing it with her promising concubine and treating the transformation of men as her ultimate job. Who wants you to train? Take care of yourself. Sister Zhao Feiyan is much more prominent. The two carefully combined and entered the palace one after another. Zhao Feiyan liked power, so she became the queen; Zhao Hede wanted to be loved, so she became the favorite concubine. The division of labor between the two was safe and happy.
Fortunately, Ban Jieyu still had insight. In the end, she invited herself to serve Empress Dowager Wang at Changxin Palace, stayed with an old lady for half her life, and safely became her talented girl. Since no one can count on it, people have become smarter.
Mo Wenwei quietly sang, “If I Never Loved You, I Won't Lose Myself.” Calm and bleak, yet true. It doesn't matter if others are sad or change their heart. Love always loses to time, and pain will pass as soon as the wind blows. I'm afraid I'll always be a Loser — both a lovelorn and a loser. I've lost my self, and I won't be able to climb back.
On this point, Ban Jieh-yu should have learned from Princess Binh Duong decades before her. Princess Pingyang is the sister of Emperor Han Wu. There are only two passages of her in the “Historical Chronicles”, but it is this long princess that is inextricably linked to the consolidation of the Dahan River and mountains. When she was 12 years old, she helped her mother take the position of empress and helped her younger brother Liu Che (Emperor Han Wu) become the heir; but while she was dominating the world, she didn't want to fall too much in love with fame and fortune and chose to retreat; once necessary, she quickly stood on the front line and took back her power. Wei Zifu, whom she dedicated to her younger brother, became an empress. For 38 years, her domestic slave, Wei Qing, her second husband, also made great contributions to the Han Empire's fight against the Huns. Even Wei Qing's nephew, Huo Gu, was able to help fight back the mountains. This is a painstaking, carefree woman; yet, she doesn't hate it.
Princess Binh Duong's amazing early wisdom is reflected in her keen observation and clear understanding of the balance between politics and love. Taiwanese novelist Chen Junqing fictitious a conversation between her and her mother Wang Yi in the novel “Princess Pingyang and Wei Qing”: “Ma'am! I just want to ask you one thing, would you rather be the Empress of Dahan or your father's favorite concubine?”
It's not just a question of whether fish and bear are compatible; it's not just a matter of choosing between power and love. In fact, it's the same in any age: either throw yourself into the pursuit of intense, sad and happy ideals, or simply choose mediocre happiness. Choosing A has the pleasure of choosing A, and choosing B has the comfort of choosing B; as long as you stick down, what are your complaints about asking for others? I'm just afraid both sides won't be able to do it thoroughly, and they'll have to be a bastard and have to set up an archway; this is not good. Princess Pingyang is smarter than her mother, Mrs. Wang, Empress Chen Ajiao of Emperor Han Wu, and Wei Zifu; she is not greedy.
However, any woman who can accept history is restrained, yet Princess Pingyang has not wronged herself. She has enjoyed singing and dancing every day; after the death of Princess Pingyang's husband, Cao Shou, she also discussed with her left and right waiters who could be her husband. The discussion turned out to be Wei Qing's most suitable, and she happily married her former bodyguard and domestic servant. At that time, Wei Qing had already officially worshipped the Grand General. Her younger sister was an empress, and her three sons were feudal marshals. ——According to legend, Princess Pingyang fell in love with Wei Qing for a long time. Wei Qing spared her orders to defeat the Huns seven times and make war exploits for Dahan. However, it is also very interesting to catch images like this. A lowly man has such an unarrogant ambition, that is, a very extravagant aristocrat spirit. Apart from external things of fame and fortune, such a man's inner world should also match Princess Pingyang.
A princess like this is really a baby: she doesn't lose anything during the big day, she has a clear mind, and she dares to love and hate. I'm just afraid of that kind of person: follow someone else's path and let yourself talk. Unfortunately, women like this have always been in the majority.
