 In Chinese history, there are quite a few examples of princesses or family members marrying the king of Fanbang. During the Tang Taizong period, Princess Wencheng married Tufan far away, which is an example of a close family situation. Under her influence, the friendship between the Chinese and Tibetan ethnic groups has developed greatly, so it is no exaggeration to praise Princess Wencheng as the most successful female diplomat.
Tuban is present-day Tibet, and there were no contacts between China and Turkey before the Tang Dynasty. It is said that the Tuban people were descendants of King Nanliang in the last years of the Eastern Jin Dynasty. They traveled to the Tibetan Plateau after losing their country. To commemorate their ancestors, they used “bald hair” as their country name, and later changed to “Tufan” due to their similar voice. People in Tuban lead a nomadic lifestyle, raising yaks, horses, pigs, and Dufeng camels, and some also grow barley and buckwheat. In the 7th century AD, he abandoned Zong Nongzan and succeeded Tufan Zanpu (King of Tuban). People also called him Songzan Ganbu. He was a brave and brave leader. He led the army to unify many tribes on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau and established a powerful kingdom centered around the city of Loge, which is today's Lhasa.
In the 12th year of Tang Taizong's government, Song Zanganbu led the Tufan army to attack the Datang border city of Songzhou, which is today's Songpan County in Sichuan. Under Tang Taizong's administration, the country was rich and strong at this time, so he sent Hou Jun to lead the army in the crackdown. Tufan was defeated by the city of Songzhou. Song Zanganbu had no choice but to bow down and admire Datang's strength. While he wrote a book to apologize for his sins, he also proposed marriage to Tang Ting.
After some consideration, Tang Taizong decided to accede to his request, so the palace chose a daughter from the family who is familiar with poetry, and named her Princess Wencheng; Princess Wencheng was originally the daughter of Tang Taizong's distant relative, Hou Wang, surnamed Li. She was handsome and plump, and had a lot of reading poetry since childhood. Although she had doubts about the distant Tuban, she was also full of novel yearnings, so she agreed. After more than two months of preparation, in the middle of winter in Zhenguan, a very impressive family donation team escorted Princess Wencheng to Tuban and her family under the leadership of King Li Daozong of Jiangxia County, the chief priest of the Ministry.
The reason to set out in the middle of winter is because the journey from Chang'an to Tibet via Longnan and Qinghai takes more than a month, and the river level is slow during the middle of winter, making it easy for families to pass through. In addition to carrying a rich dowry, this team also carried a large number of books, musical instruments, silk, and food seeds; in addition to the waitress that Princess Wencheng married, the members also had a group of scribes, musicians, and agricultural technicians, almost like a “cultural visiting team” and an “agricultural technical team.” What are these people going to do? Because Tushan had already defeated Tugu Hun at the time, it became an important power in the southwest; Tang Taizong was farsighted and felt that only strengthening ties to Tuban could guarantee the stability of the southwestern border of Datang, so he made every effort to assist them economically and culturally, so that Tuban could subtly appreciate and follow Datang. Princess Wencheng actually married far away, shouldering the political task of reconciling diplomatic relations, and this family donation team also went to help her complete this mission.
After more than a month of arduous trekking in the wind and snow, Princess Wencheng and her entourage arrived at the source of the first river, the birthplace of the Yellow River. There were many aquatic plants and herds of cows and sheep. They changed the desolate scene of wind and sand along the way, making people feel refreshed. Princess Wencheng, who was very worried about the poor situation along the way, also breathed a sigh of relief, so the family delivery team took a short break here for a few days.
At this point, Song Zan Ganbu personally led a brigade to welcome his loved one, Ma also arrived in Heyuan. Song Zan Ganbu and his entourage saw Datang envoy Li Daozong, King Li Daozong of Jiangxia County, prayed and held a grand marriage ceremony. He had already decided that Datang would go to the country of Tuban. Li Daozong invited Princess Wencheng to meet Songzan Ganbu. As soon as the King of Tuban, who is racing on the plateau, he immediately fell in love with her. He saw that Princess Wencheng was dressed in gorgeous costumes, had a dignified attitude, and a graceful demeanor, which was completely different from the original simple Tuban woman. However, the Songzan Ganbu that Princess Wencheng saw, although dark and rough, was shaped by the hot sun and wind of the plateau, but with his tall and strong figure and the extravagant air that came out between his eyebrows, she seemed very brave; Princess Wencheng was secretly happy in her heart; she was married to a great husband.
The group that sent and welcomed their family greeted each other in style. Under Li Daozong's presidency, Songzan Ganbu and Princess Wencheng held a grand wedding in accordance with Han etiquette. People all over the city sang and danced to celebrate their Zanpu and his wife. Song Zan Ganbule unsupposedly said to his subordinates, “My father in my family has never had a precedent of getting married to China. Today, I got a princess from Datang as my wife. I am really lucky. I want to build a gorgeous palace for the princess to be preserved for future generations.”
Soon, a beautiful palace was built, and the buildings inside were magnificent and gorgeous. The pavilions are beautiful and elegant. They have also excavated a pond with rippling blue waves, and planted all kinds of beautiful flowers and trees. All the structures imitate the pattern of the Datang Palace Garden and are used to settle down Princess Wencheng, thereby comforting her homesick feelings. In order to have more common language with Princess Wencheng, Song Zanganbu took off the fur coat he was used to, replaced it with the silk Tang costume that Princess Wencheng personally sewed for him, and also worked hard to learn Chinese from Princess Wencheng. The interracial couple had a good relationship, love and respect each other, and began a new life for them.
According to traditional customs, Tuban people use ochre-colored clay to coat their cheeks every day, saying it can drive away evil. Although it looks very ugly and uncomfortable, because of traditional customs, no one raised any objections; most Tubans just follow the rules. After arriving in Tuban, Princess Wencheng carefully understood and figured out this habit. She thought that it was unreasonable and harmful to hygiene. It was really a despicable habit, so she gently put forward her views to Songzan Ganbu. After listening to Song Zanganbu, she felt that her words made a lot of sense and immediately ordered the abolition of this custom. At first, some Tubans were very unaccustomed to it, but slowly they felt that they kept their true face, which was both convenient and good-looking, and everyone was happy to accept it. They were even very grateful to Princess Wencheng for breaking the rules for them.
After their lives settled down, the Han musicians brought by Princess Wencheng began to perform their duties. They worked very hard to play the most popular music in the Tang Palace for Songzan Ganbu and Princess Wencheng. The music was soothing and beautiful, making Songzan Ganbu feel like hearing a fairy. He admired the musicians and music, and selected a group of talented boys and girls to study with Han musicians, gradually spreading Han music all over the territory of Tuban and entering the hearts of the Tuban people.
Accompanying writers also began work. They helped sort out documents relating to Tuban, recorded important conversations with ministers announced by Song Zan, and brought about formalization of Tufan's politics. In addition to being happy, Song Zanganbu also ordered the minister and aristocrat children to sincerely worship the writers, study Han culture, and study the poetry books they brought; then he also sent batch after batch of aristocrat children to travel thousands of miles to Chang'an to enter the Tang dynasty country to study poetry and books, and bring Han culture back to Tufan.
Agricultural technicians don't promote anything; they only sow grain seeds brought from the Central Plains on the fertile soil of the plateau, then carefully irrigate, fertilize, and weed until the harvest season. The impressive high yield made Tuban people's eyes widen; because although the Tubans also grew some crops such as barley and buckwheat at the time, they often just grew nothing due to poor management, so the yield was extremely low. Under the direction of Songzan Ganbu and Princess Wencheng, agricultural technicians began to teach agricultural techniques to the Tubans in a planned manner, so that in addition to being nomadic, they were also able to harvest large amounts of food. In particular, after passing on the technique of cultivating mulberries and silkworms to them, Tuban also gradually developed homemade silk fabrics, which were delicate and colorful, greatly beautifying the lives of Tuban people and made them very happy. They were all very grateful to Princess Wencheng for the benefits she brought to them after entering Tuban.
Princess Wencheng treated Songzan Ganbu with tenderness, so that the King of Tuban, who grew up in a barbaric land, deeply experienced the cultivation and warmth of Han women. Not only did he cherish Princess Wencheng, but he also did his best to adopt some of her suggestions. Princess Wencheng, on the other hand, drew on her knowledge and insight to carefully examine the situation in Tuban, and then put forward all kinds of reasonable suggestions to help her husband manage this country with a vast area and rich folk customs. However, Princess Wencheng is not the type of woman who is extremely powerful. She participated in pregoverning the country, but she never asked Song Zanganbu to give her any official position. She only put forward her own opinions and did not forcibly interfere with the major political decisions of the Tuban country. Therefore, Song Zan Ganbu and his ministers were very pleased with her and often asked her to teach her about the Tang Palace's political system as a reference for their administration, and the vast majority of people in Tuban treated her more like a god.
In the 22nd year of Zhenguan, Wang Xuanze, the head of the Tang Taizong sect, sent an envoy to Tufan. On the one hand, he also negotiated relations between the two countries; on the other hand, he also visited Princess Wencheng, who was married far away. Wang Xuanze led an expedition to the road with a large number of silk artifacts. While passing through Tianzhu, they were unfortunately looted by the Tianzhu people. Except for Wang Xuanze, who escaped with a small number of people and horses, most of the people and items were robbed. Wang Xuanze arrived in Tufan in a hurry, met with Songzan Ganbu and explained the circumstances of the robbery. Songzan Ganbu believed that Tianzhu intended to cause provocation and damage his relationship with Datang, so he sent an army to attack Tianzhu, destroyed their capital, captured Prince Tianzhu, also seized a large number of livestock, and rescued the Tang dynasty envoy's entourage.
In the 23rd year of Zhenguan, Tang Taizong and Li Shimin died, and Tang Gaozong succeeded the throne. The newly appointed Emperor Tianzi of Datang awarded Song Zanganbu as Captain Fu Ma and became king of Xihai County, and sent a special envoy to send a large amount of gold, silver, silk, poetry, and grain, as well as special ornaments and cosmetics for Princess Wencheng to praise her and her family.
Song Zanganbuin wrote a letter of thanks and faithfully stated, “When Tianzi first took office, if any of his subordinates were unfaithful, they should go to the country to get rid of discussions.” They also presented 15 types of jewels. Please place Taizong Ling in your place to express your condolences. Tang Gaozong was very moved by Songzan Ganbu's loyalty. He was also promoted to King Bin, and he was also gifted with three thousand steps of color. After arriving in Chang'an, the Tufan envoy opened his eyes and took advantage of Tang Gaozong's happiness to ask him for techniques to make wine, mill rice, and make ink and paper, and ink, and Tang Gaozong agreed. The relationship between the Tang dynasty and Tuban, based on Princess Wencheng's contacts, has now reached the peak of a mixture of water and milk.
Under Song Zan Ganbu and Princess Wencheng's efforts to implement reforms, and the proper planning of Dalun (the prime minister of Tuban) Lu Dongzan, Tuban has developed by leaps and bounds in all aspects of military, politics, economy, and culture. As a result, it was able to dominate the western region and become a powerful barrier to the West in the Datang Dynasty.
Unfortunately, soon after, Song Zanganbu passed away, and his grandson succeeded Zampu. Since Zanpu was young, Lu Dongzan managed most of the state affairs, while Princess Wencheng handled most of the family affairs. At this point, everything was quite stable. However, Lu Dongzan also died soon after, and his son Qin Ling made great arguments. At this time, the relationship between Tuban and his neighbor Shigu Hun deteriorated. They all wrote to Tang Ting to judge right and wrong, yet Tang Gaozong was slow to decide. Qin Ling was unable to hold back down, and an army set up an army to defeat Tsugu Hun. Unexpectedly, this act violated Tang Ting's majesty. Tang Gaozong believed that before he handed down a verdict, Tuban used force and simply didn't take the Tang dynasty seriously. Therefore, in the first year of Xian Xiang, he sent his supervisor Xue Rengui to fight against Tufan.
Who knows Xue Rengui's army was completely defeated by the Tuban army in the Dafei River area. Since then, the Tuban people have stopped submitting to Datang's spirit and have been invading the Datang border for years. Tang Ting sent military chiefs to guard the town of Taohe to prevent harassment by the Tuban army. The two sides fell into a hostile situation, and Tuban became the greatest enemy that the Tang dynasty had never been able to resolve.
From the early spring of Tang Taizong in the early spring of the 15th year, Princess Wencheng married Song Zanganbu, to the time that Xue Rengui led an army to conquer Tuban in the first year of Tang Gaozong, a full 30 years. Due to Princess Wencheng's erudite abilities, it had a great influence on the development of the Tufan country. Not only did it consolidate the Tang dynasty's western border, but it also spread Han culture to the west. Unfortunately, Tang Gaozong was unable to make good use of it, easily provoked war, causing Wen to become unmanageable. The peaceful situation created by Princess Cheng's painstaking efforts came to an abrupt end. How unfortunate is that!
In the first year of Tang Gaozong Yonglong, Princess Wencheng died of illness in Luodaecheng. Tang Ting sent an envoy to pay a memorial service, but this did not improve diplomatic relations between the two countries. However, Princess Wencheng's admiration from the Tuvan public and public was not slightly reduced due to her estrangement from the Tang dynasty; her death caused pain to all Tubans.
After Princess Wencheng's death, Tuban people built temples and temples for her everywhere to commemorate her with magazines. Some of the Wentu craftsmen who came with her have also always been treated well. After their death, they were also buried on both sides of Princess Wencheng's tomb. To this day, Princess Wencheng and these friendly messengers are still regarded as gods by Tibetans.
