 Author: Hou Hongbin
Qin Xianglian: I saw the Qing drama “Sai Pipa”, which is the ancestor of “Qin Xianglian” and “The Case of Gan Mei”, which are popular on the modern stage. The story is about Qin Xianglian being hunted down by Chen Shimei's faction, imparted military law, and used military merit to make an official position. She personally interrogated Chen Shimei. There is also “Ming Gong Duan” by Hanabe, which changed the end to the Bao Gong Judgment Case, Bao Gong Mei. Various versions of the story have continued to flourish in various dramas across the country.
Recently, I was unlucky enough to read another novel like this: The two love each other violently. When they were in love, the sky was thunderous and the earth was on fire, and they finally parted ways due to various reasons. Men are on a different level; women are disheartened; news and communication are vague. Twenty years later, the man was already a director of Morgan Stanley. He had a net worth of tens of millions. His lovely wife loved his daughter. Suddenly, he discovered in his conscience that he was sitting in a helicopter to a small town with no weeds or poop, retrieved this middle-aged woman who was growing flowers, and tearfully took her hand and said, “Are you okay? Is our son okay? I've been thinking about you all these years.” Next, the flower pot in the woman's hand “shattered” to the ground, and her son, who is in his teens, ran over to the side: “Mom, mom, who is he?”
I know that many women who have been in love and have been abandoned have this dream. The end of the dream was like this: the man raved out a check worth one million, saying it was to pay her back. And when the woman took it, she tore it off, threw it on the man's face, and said, “We don't need it; we're doing great. Let's go.”
Isn't it true that being cold is the best revenge? In fact, Yuan Yu also did a similar thing. He abandoned Cui Yingying and got married again. Later, he went specifically to Ying Ying. He wanted to regain his old love. Of course, Ying Ying refused. You can imagine Ying Ying's impatience and disgust; it's just that the article was written by Yuan Yi, and he was too embarrassed to say it.
And the most famous abandoned woman in China is Qin Xianglian. This is the end she has been looking forward to day and night.
Chen Shimei's family is old and younger. She married a wife, Qin Xianglian, and kept her at home, then went to Beijing to rush to take the exam. As soon as possible, she became a county horse, leaving everything behind at home. Her parents were invigorated, and Qin Xianglian died. After her hard work, she brought her children to Beijing to find her husband. Of course Chen Shimei doesn't recognize it: if you do, you won't be able to become a county horse. Qin Xianglian had no choice but to live on the streets, playing pipa and selling songs for a living. Fortunately, there are always idle people in the world. Wang Chengxiang is one. His design made Chen Shimei see Qin Xianglian again, but Chen never recognized it again and again. Qin Xianglian was in a hurry and got down on one knee and said, “I don't care about you. Life and death are up to heaven, but kid, kid, you have to stay.” Chen Shimei turned her face down and disapproved, and also sent someone to assassinate Qin Xianglian.
The killer Han Qi had a kind heart. As soon as she heard Qin Xianglian's remarks, she was in trouble and had no choice but to commit suicide. Qin Xianglian, with the support of Chengxiang Wang, went to Bao Zheng to file a complaint. After hearing the statement and evidence, Bao Heizi went on fire and killed Chen Shimei despite the intervention of the princess and the Empress Dowager.
Since the Earth rotates, no one will always be in an unlucky position. As an abandoned woman, Qin Xianglian felt that she was very weak and pitiful. She needed protection from the forces of social justice. This is her fantasy plan 1 of revenge.
However, when she met such a ruthless and unscrupulous person, Qin Xianglian offended her: you are a first-year student, and I am a fifteenth. Her second fantasy revenge plan is — the scene of the assassination was at the Sanguantang Temple. When assassinations are carried out in this kind of place, it is easy to shock gods and ghosts; generally, missions cannot be completed. This is how Qin Xianglian was saved by the Three Gods and taught her the art of war. As a result, Western Xia used troops at the time, and Qin Xianglian sent troops and won a big victory because of military merit. Chen Shimei, on the other hand, went to jail because of Prime Minister Wang's participation. As soon as she came back from winning the battle, the emperor asked her to dismiss several cases, and Chen Shimei's case was also in her hands. Qin Xianglian heard the trial. She sat tall at the mirror table, while Chen Shimei, wearing a prison uniform, lay recklessly on her ground, and had no place for herself. Qin Xianglian loudly announced Chen Shimei's accusation on the stage. She also shook the media a lot of strong news about not washing his feet before bed and smoked the two cats in the family with smelly socks. Finally, he personally supervised the execution.
She walked up to Chen Shimei under the knife and laughed coldly: What else do you have to say? Terrified and desperate, Chen Shimei said, “Darling, I love you. Please spare me. Qin Xianglian slowly turned around, closed her eyes, and waved. Decisive.
Wow, that was the happiest ending. It would be better if I had one more teardrop slowly flowing down, that would be perfect. When Qin Xianglian was selling songs on the street, she sang while thinking and laughing.
However, Chen Shimei didn't send anyone to kill her; the two just broke up, making her unable to go in even if she wanted to file a complaint. Now, bad people are no longer the name of a rich person. Not to mention the law, they don't even care about unit leaders or old street aunts; they only persuade you to be strong, independent, make more money, be less foolish, and notarize your assets before marriage.
In fact, the actual outcome was that Qin Xianglian was singing a little song. The owner of the store selling musical instruments looked at her to make people feel sorry for her, so she married her back to her hometown, bought a big one and got the kid as a pet. Qin Xianglian lived a warm and satisfying life that every woman has lived. Even though she occasionally thinks of her former loathing people, her heart is still bleeding. You can't punish it; it's always OK to fantasize. One day, Chen Shimei will come and hold my leg and ask for forgiveness. Then imagine the thrill of ripping a check. Oops — that must be cool.
