 Qingming is one of the twenty-four solar terms in China. Since 24 solar terms objectively reflect changes in temperature, rainfall, and phenology throughout the year, ancient working people used it to arrange farming activities. “Huai Nan Zi? “Astronomy Training” Yun: “Fifteen days after the vernal equinox, when the finger is B, the clear and clear wind will arrive.” According to “One Hundred Questions Over Time,” “Everything grows clean and clear at this point. That's why it's called Qingming.” As soon as Qingming arrived, the temperature rose and rainfall increased. It was a great time for spring farming and spring planting. Therefore, there are agricultural practices such as “before and after Qingming, order melons and grow beans” and “plant trees and reforestation, no better than Qingming”. It can be seen that this season is closely related to agricultural production.
However, as a holiday, Qingming is different from the pure season. The seasons are a sign of our country's changing phenology and seasonal order, and festivals include certain customs and activities and some kind of commemorative significance.
The Ching Ming Festival is a traditional festival in our country. It is also the most important festival of sacrifices. It is a day to worship ancestors and sweep tombs. Tomb sweeping, commonly known as going to the grave, is an activity of sacrificing the dead. The Han people and some ethnic minorities mostly swept tombs during the Ching Ming Festival.
According to old customs, when sweeping tombs, people had to bring items such as alcohol, fruit, paper money, etc. to the graveyard, then incinerate the paper money, cultivate new soil for the graves, fold a few fresh green branches and put them on the grave, then prostrate to worship, and finally eat the food and drink and go home. Tang Dynasty poet Du Mu's poem “Qingming”: “There was a lot of rain during the Qingming period, and pedestrians on the road wanted to lose their soul. May I ask where are the restaurants? The shepherd refers to Apricot Village.” It describes the special atmosphere of Ching Ming Festival.
The Qingming Festival, also known as the Youth Outing Festival, according to the Gregorian calendar, is between April 4 and 6 every year. It is also a good time for people to go on a spring trip (called a green outing in ancient times), so the ancients had the custom of going out in Qingming and carrying out a series of sports activities.
To this day, the custom of worshipping ancestors and mourning deceased loved ones during the Ching Ming Festival is still very popular.
