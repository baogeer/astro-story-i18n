 Due to differences in regional culture, the same Tanabata Festival also has different festival activities all over the country, showing a variety of characteristics. Here is a brief explanation of the activities in each region.
In Gudoujing Village, Tanghui Township, Jiaxing, Zhejiang, there is a Tanabata Incense Bridge Festival. Every year on Tanabata, people come to participate and build an incense bridge. The so-called incense bridge is a bridge about four to five meters long and half a meter wide made of various thick incense sticks (incense sticks wrapped in paper). A railing is attached to it, and a flower decoration made of five colored threads is attached to the railing. At night, people sacrifice the two stars, beg for good fortune, and then burn the incense bridge, symbolizing that the two stars have walked through the incense bridge and met happily. This incense bridge is derived from the legendary Magpie Bridge legend.
In the Jiaodong region, it is more common to worship the Seven Sisters on Tanabata. Young women dressed in new clothes, gathered together, formed an alliance of seven sisters in court and sang the song: “Emperor Emperor, Emperor of Earth, I invite the seven sisters to heaven. If you don't want your needle, you don't want your line, you have 72 good methods.” Many places also make “smart flowers”. Girls use flour to make cake buns with flowers such as peonies, lotus, plum, orchids, and chrysanthemums, as well as ingenious dishes, that is, they cultivate malt in wine cups (this is the “seed” in the Song Dynasty), and use chocolate and smart vegetables to sacrifice weavers.
In Shaanxi, on Tanabata night, girls use straw to make a “Qiao Gu” shape (also known as Qiao Niang, or Weaving Girl) and have her wear the girl's green jacket and red dress and sit in the courtyard; the girls provide melons and fruits, and take out pre-planted bean sprouts and green onion shoots (called “seeds”, women call them Qiao Bud), cut a section of it, and float it on the surface of the water to watch the buds under the moon to make smart predictions; and use a needle to compete for quick guidance Slow; hold a flower cutting competition to compete for wisdom and ingenuity.
In Fujian, women and girls set up incense burners and various offerings: tea, wine, flower arrangements in vases, as well as sesame seeds (longans, red dates, hazelnuts, peanuts, and melon seeds) and fat powder for women. After worshiping the two stars, the fat powder dedicated to the weaver was divided in half, half put on the roof for the weaver, and half did her own makeup and beauty. Legend has it that if you share fat powder with a weaver, you can maintain your beautiful appearance. And the five children's decorations have the meaning of seeking fertility. The women also eat tea, eat melons and fruits, and play begging games.
In Guangdong, the Tanabata Festival was most valued during the Qing and Republic of China years, and many interesting customs were passed down. Qu Dajun's “Cantonese New Language” already records the grand event of the “Seven Women's Party” in the early Qing Dynasty. There were also many interesting customs spread during the Republic of China years. Qu Dajun's “Cantonese New Language” already records the grand event of the “Seven Ladies Party” in the early Qing dynasty. People often call it “Bai the Seven Sisters.” According to the recollections of the elderly who participated in the “Bai Qi Sister” event during the Republic of China, “Bai Qi Jie” was particularly popular in the Xiguan area of Guangzhou. Activities are generally held among young girls and young women (men and elderly women can only watch on the sidelines; they only pray at the same time). In advance, a dozen or more good sisters organize to prepare for the “Seven Sisters.” In June, they are required to soak some rice, wheat grains, mung beans, etc. in porcelain bowls to allow them to sprout. As Tanabata approaches, I'm even busier. To raise some money, ask for help from my family to use bamboo paper to build a magpie bridge and make all kinds of beautiful handicrafts. On the night of Tanabata, the Eight Immortals table was set up in the hall, and an embroidered table (table skirt) was placed around the hall, and various wonderful flower and fruit products and female red knick-knacks were displayed, showing off the girls' ingenuity. There is a rice and bean sprout plate surrounded by decoupage red flowers. The plate is illuminated with oil lanterns, and the light shows off the colorful paper lampshade; there are carefully arranged flower arrangements, fragrant white orchids, jasmine, vegan, and other flowers are placed in copper and porcelain vases; there is a tea spoon-sized load, rose, nightjar, and camellia placed in a small pot. There is also a fake flower, which is hard to tell; there is also a fruit plate where fresh fruits such as apples, peaches, and persimmons are cut and assembled into various bird and animal shapes.; Embroidered clothes, shoes, socks, and flower clogs of about an inch; small log, duvets, drapes, table skirts woven and embroidered with gold and silver colored thread; finger-sized fans and handkerchiefs; using small wood Itashiki, potatoes, and millet seedlings, and pavilions made of fine wood. Anyway, the more detailed, the more ingenious. Rice grains, sesame seeds, lanterns, and colored paper are also used to make various types of towers, tables and chairs, bottles, fruit, paperwork, and matzu with various patterns and characters; they also hang a glass or colored paper lantern, palace lantern, grapefruit, eggshell lantern (landscape flower and bird pattern on it), and animal-shaped lanterns. The most adorable thing is the beautiful puppet, or rag doll, made of colored silk by the girls. The baby doll has images of a cowherd, a weaver, and a pair of children. They are usually placed on the upper floor. The lower part is an image of a child playing the piano and dancing to celebrate the two stars meeting. There is also a complete set of theatrical figures such as “Xixiang,” “Red House,” and “Yang Men Girl” (also with porcelain dolls, which parents bought for their children as holiday gifts). Also, of course, it is essential to display cosmetic items, such as small rouge cases, mirrors, colored combs, velvet, grease powder, etc., which can be used not only by weavers, but also by daughters for their own use. There are also waxed melons, fruits, small animals, etc. In addition, there are foods such as sweet and salty snacks, tea, wine, melon seeds, peanuts, etc. What is essential is a candlestick, incense burner, and lighting with the best sandalwood.
On Tanabata night, the girls should dress up to their heart's content, bathe and wash their hair with water from the Tianhe River, then change into a silk dress, cheongsam, and wear floral ornaments such as white orchids and frangipani; paint their eyebrows, grease their lips, and print on their foreheads; and dye their nails with phoenix juice. After dressing up like a fairy, each of the girls sat around the Eight Immortals table and played all kinds of games: or entertain themselves, write correct poems, do puzzles, and worship begging with needles; and point to the Seven Stars in the Sky (Legend has it that Weaver is a member of the Seven Star Sisters) and double stars to tell the story of the cow girl, poetry, and literature; or invite singers to sing Cantonese music, play eight music, etc., and the daughters also play instruments such as the Hsiao Qin. At this point, people can go to various places to visit the Begging Table Furnishings. Despite the crowds, the owners were still happy to welcome them. The celebration ended at 12 o'clock in the middle of the night. At this time, all the lights and incense candles were lit, in all colors, and brilliant; the girls were in high spirits, wearing needles, welcoming the Seven Sisters, and there was a lot of joy everywhere. There was a party at the end, and then it all went away. As the Qing poet Wang Lun (legendary fish name) heard in “Yangcheng Tanabata Bamboo Branch Song”: “Embroidered Yaoyao Gate bloomed one after the other, and the flower became a barrier, and the jade became the platform. Little girl from Qingxi, Miss Lan Qiao, is begging for a date.”

In the Beijing bamboo branch, there is the phrase “five life pots are colorful”, and they also throw away ingenuity and seek ingenuity.
