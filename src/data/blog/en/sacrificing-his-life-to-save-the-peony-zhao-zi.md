 Legend has it that during the Guangxu years of the Qing Dynasty, Zhao Wu, a flower farmer in Zhaolou in Caozhou, cultivated a new peony with a thousand layers of petals. Zhao Wu is very sympathetic to this peony, treating it like her own child, so she named it Zhao Zi (harmonic consonant). Unexpectedly, Yuxian, the governor of Caozhou, learned about this. After the fall, he personally sat down to pick this peony tree. For Zhao Wu, this really seemed to hurt his heart and soul. While slowly picking peony roots, he thought of a plan to save Zhao Zi.
At this point, Li Zheng brought hot roast chicken and soju to Yu Xian. Taking advantage of Yu Xian's chance to see each other in a blink of an eye, Zhao Wu deliberately waved an ordinary peony in front of him, quietly hid it aside, and then held the real Zhao Zi in front of Yu Xian.
All of Zhao Wu's actions were seen by Yu Xian. He was furious: “Boldly harassing the people, how dare you trick the boss; you can't give the tree you hid to the old man!” Just talking about how far one foot would kick the real Zhao Zi out.
“Yes, yes”. Zhao Wu pretended to be the only one. Give the normal peony to Yu Xian. In my heart, I was secretly lucky: I saved Zhao Zi.
Whoever wanted Yu Xian to be old and cunning; he was afraid of fraud, so he picked up an ordinary peony and said to Zhao Wu, “Come on, set up a note for me. For example, this peony is not Zhao Zi. Next spring, when the flowers bloom, it will be your death date.”
Zhao Wu heard that lightning struck Yuxian as insidious and malicious; killing people is like killing a chicken. He knows that. But I think it would be worth it if they saved Zhao Zi's life. By the time they kill me next year, Zhao will have about ten trees after being divided. It won't be overrun by Yu Xian. Thinking of this, Zhao Wu took the lead and signed it with a pen.
During the valley rain season in the second year, Zhao Zi in the peony garden bloomed brightly. The townspeople know that this is the blood of the old flower farmer Zhao Wu.
Since then, the proverb that those who grow flowers will give up their lives spread in Caozhou.
