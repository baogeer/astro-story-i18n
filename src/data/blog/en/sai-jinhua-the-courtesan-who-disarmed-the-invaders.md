 Sai Jinhua (1864-1936), a famous prostitute in the late Qing dynasty, was a legendary and controversial historical figure in modern Chinese history. Sai Jinhua, a fireworks woman, happened to have an experience living in Europe. Unexpectedly, in the midst of the flames of war, she got along with the enemy's marshal and became the protagonist of a big drama in her home country.

That night, German soldiers broke into Stone Alley and knocked on the door of Sai Jinhua's house.
However, what made the German soldiers dream was that the famous fireworks woman in front of her was actually fluent in German. The soldiers looked at each other one and didn't know what to do.
