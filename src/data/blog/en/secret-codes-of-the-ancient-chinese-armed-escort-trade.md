Three rules of waterways
Walking the waterway darts and paddling without the hassle of riding a saddle carriage can be called a beautiful thing. So why are watercraft darts so beautiful? Isn't walking waterway darts like traveling; do whatever you want without any concerns?
Actually, water darts mostly travel along canals, and most of the regions they pass through are rich places. Although there are relatively few thieves who take risks compared to land, water darts also have unpredictable variables. Therefore, if you want to have a safe journey without trouble, you must abide by the “Three Rules” of waterways.
The first rule is “take a nap and wake up at night.” With the exception of the darts master on duty during the day, the rest of the darts went into the cabin and slept soundly. They didn't walk out of the cabin until the red sun was slanted and ready to work at night. Because river barricades and robberies almost never happen during the day, only thieves often come to sneak up, steal, or rob, and darts players have to be prepared.
The second rule is “people do not disembark”. Most of the areas along the canal are heavily populated. There are many cities, towns, villages, and markets. In some bustling areas, teahouses and wine shops abound. Not only are entertainers and singers present, but village meetings and social plays are also common. “Flower boats” and “Jiangshan boats” (these are all boats carrying prostitutes) are often full of songs and dances with prostitutes. However, the darts must never go ashore to watch, because walking away would mean losing the darts. Not only will the darts not leave the boat to watch the fun, but they will also not leave the boat to chase thieves, fearing that the Chinese thieves will take the tiger and leave the mountain. When the incident happened on the shore, the darts definitely ignored it. Even the villains bullied the boys and girls, and didn't ask. This is not to say that the darts don't have any sense of justice or public morality, but this kind of bullying against boys and girls is probably just a “trick,” because thieves often use the darts to help the weak, and get started after disembarking.
The third rule is “avoid women.” The boat family used the boat as their family, and the wife and daughter were the most open women in feudal society, and most of the darts were masculine and pompous men. According to the routine of heroic beauties and talented people in old novels, it would be a voyage along the way, and the love for a wife was strong. Actually, this is not the case. Darts have always attached importance to martial arts, and the women in the boat family also abide by women. They all know how to respect themselves and abide by the feudal code of ethics. The darts master never enter the back cabin after boarding the boat. The separation is like a curtain between the house and the house. If the employer is carrying treasure, the darts master even retreat. As for the Qinglou women and flower boat prostitutes along the way, the darts definitely disdain them. They are afraid of being “dazzled” and mistaken, and they are also afraid that the thieves will “release white pigeons.” Because of this, boatmen and employers can be reassured. If the dart master scares off the thieves and gets into the cabin himself, then wouldn't it be that the boatman and the employer must have “detailed weight” between gains and losses. Perhaps this game has destroyed their reputation and reputation because of these two darts masters.
No way to stay overland
We know that using land darts and escorting darts carts is the main business of a darts club. Since the Grand Canal was cut off, land darts have become the main industry in darts games. Naturally, the rules for running land darts have become more numerous and complicated, so what kind of rules do they all have?
Rationally speaking, land darts are all on the plains, and most darts ride horses to guard their darts carts along the way. Once the thieves show up, they can respond quickly and flexibly, and there's not much to worry about. Actually, the truth is not that simple. Especially when darts stay overnight in some larger villages and towns, you need to be careful about everything and be careful everywhere. Because these villages and towns generally have strong local power, once they get into trouble, they won't be able to keep walking around. Therefore, “three days without staying” is still the travel principle for darts masters.
I can't stop by the newly opened store. Because the darts usually follow a fixed darts path, they are all very familiar with the restaurants along the way, and have made good friends with the stores. Since they don't know the details, they always drive past the newly opened stores and never stay overnight. They are afraid of running into Zhang Qing and Zhu Gui-style stores in “Outlaws of the Water Marsh,” causing a shortage of personal money.

They are afraid that after the old store changes hands, it will become a thief shop. The thieves will be ambushed first, and just wait for the dart cart to arrive. Therefore, before the darts cart enters the village, the darts are always the first to go and inquire about it so that they can grasp the real situation.

Three things to enter the store
Arguably, the darts master drove a darts cart for a day and should have a good rest when he arrives at the hotel; in fact, this is not the case. However, if you don't have a good rest, you will definitely be tired when playing darts the next day. Wouldn't you lose your darts when playing fatigue in a vicious cycle?
But that's very unlikely. Because after entering the store, the darts are not in a hurry to rest; instead, they must first inspect the store to see if there are any “abnormalities” in order to prevent being “spotted” by thieves. Second, you should inspect the outside of the store to see if there are any “strange winds” to prevent people from “sticking” on them. Third, you need to go into the kitchen and inspect it to see if there are any “odors” to prevent people from secretly taking medicine.
The so-called “strange appearance” means there are suspicious people in the store. “Strange wind” is a suspicious sign outside the store, and “smell” means manipulating the food under the kitchen. If there is a strange appearance or strange wind, take effective preventive measures immediately. If there is an odor, it is claimed that everyone has “hit the head (eaten)” and wait until the door is closed at night to fill their hunger by eating dried food they carry with them.
After dinner, except for those on higher duty, darts must go to bed. Northerners usually sleep on a headrest bed to keep warm to avoid the cold wind blowing on their heads outside the window. However, all year round, the dart master sleeps close to the window and pedals, because this makes it easy to see the movement outside the window, and the other is that if there is a situation, there is no need to roll over and get down to the floor; he can land on the ground as soon as he slaps. The average person always wears shoes with their heels facing outward when getting out of the bed, but darts have developed a habit of putting their shoes upside down when they take them off and put them on, leaving their heels facing the floor. Once something happens, they wear shoes when they jump out of the couch. The darts master's habits are all taught in blood, because once a night attack occurs, life and death are decided in an instant.

Three will one no
If the “three inseparable” rule is a bit “ridiculous,” then this rule isn't even ridiculous. However, often these are rules that are not rules, or common sense things are rules for darts to protect themselves to the greatest extent possible on the dart path.
Everyone knows that darts are hard work, especially darts in the North Road, which is a very hard job. Coming out of Beijing and passing through Changping State, you will enter the mountainous area. The Great Wall winds between Yanshan Mountain and has many gates, such as Gubei Entrance, Renkou, Xifengkou, Xifengkou, etc., so as soon as you leave the Great Wall, it is collectively known as “outside the mouth.” There were few outsiders at the time. Whether it was on the East Road or West Road, it was very inconvenient to eat and stay. If something went wrong on the way, it would cause a situation where there were no villages in front or back, so they had to eat locally and sleep on the street. Therefore, when teaching their apprentices the skill of “three times and one no”, slowly became a rule. Because they didn't understand “three times and one no,” they really couldn't walk the darts in the northern province.
First of all, you have to be able to set up the stove. Whether it's mountainous or flat, when the road is windy, frosty, or snowy, the darts have to adapt to local conditions, set up a large stove and bury a pot to cook; the second is that they can repair shoes. If there is a problem with the shoes during the trip, it's very annoying, so people who go out have to bring new shoes, but the new shoes won't wobble for three days, so the darts all have to repair their shoes to keep their feet from getting hurt and have a good rest and preparation; third, they can get a haircut. Along the way, his beard was full of dust, and his beard was in a mess. When passing through towns and villages, you inevitably have to visit local forces. If you look shameless, it's easy to be looked down upon, so the darts have to get their hair done before going. After getting a haircut and shaving my face, I went to meet local leaders in high spirits. Both showed the style of a darts game.
In addition to the “three meetings,” there is also “one no.” This “no” means not washing your face. Outside of my mouth, the wind was cold and harsh in the spring and autumn, and the summer sun was hot like fire. After washing my face with earth and alkali, as soon as the strong wind blew, it cut my face like a knife. Therefore, in the process of darts in the North Province, “wash your face” and “get home” are synonymous. The young darts master gladly said, “It's time to wash your face tomorrow,” which means it's time to get home tomorrow.
Three taboos for guest darts
Darts sometimes protect not property, but travelers, so they are called “guest darts.” Wouldn't it be easy to get a bodyguard? You think, darts are alive. But in reality, bodyguards aren't as simple as you might think.
You need to know that travelers who can hire a darts escort are certainly not ordinary people. Most of these travelers are officials who have taken office or wealthy businessmen who have moved and returned to visit their families. They either have power, or money, or a combination of the two. After all, in three years, the Governor's Office was cleared, 100,000 snowflakes and silver. Hiring bodyguards to escort drivers are all large owners who carry family members and money. Bodyguards have a “three taboo” rule
The first thing to avoid is not to ask what you have in your bag; just ask what luggage you must insure in the event of an accident. Because wealth was concentrated and morality was lost, people who made money in the old society mostly made unjust wealth. They were most afraid of disclosure; in current terms, they were afraid of “exposure.” It is popular for merchants who are afraid of “exposure,” and for officials who are afraid of “exposure,” they are involved in history.
The second thing to avoid is to avoid contact with the employer's “loved ones.” Most of the senior officials and rich are three wives and four concubines. The third taboo is “admiration” in the middle. Because mid-way extortion is often viewed as extortion, even an unexpected offer to borrow money can be viewed as an attempt to “blackmail.” Generally speaking, after reaching the finish line safely, the employer will always reward the darts master for a dinner and give them some silver rewards. This is how quick the darts are.
If these three taboos are violated, it will often bring some discomfort and affect the business after the game. Furthermore, the employers are all rich and powerful people. Even the next officials can still show the darts master some colors with their three-inch official posts. Therefore, the Three Bad Laws are actually road rules for taking passenger darts.
The rules of the darts game were not set by anyone, but were formed over many years of practice. They represent not only the interests of the darts game, but also the interests of the employer. It can be said that it is an empirical summary of the lessons of dozens of generations of darts masters using blood. They were consciously implemented in the process of natural formation, and passed down from generation to generation. At the same time, it was further improved during the implementation process, and standardized and institutionalized standards were gradually reached. The ideology of maintaining these rules is based on the martial arts of the darts master. The external force behind implementing these systems is the cohesion of the group. The reason for persistence is that these rules are perfectly integrated with the darts master.

In the early Qing dynasty, the many rules of the Chinese Darts Board were mainly reflected in the operation of acting as a nurse, such as darts on a land route, darts on a waterway itinerary, leaving a hotel store on the way, and acting as a nursing home bodyguard.
The bodyguard industry is a special business with martial arts and Jianghu culture. Despite this, various industry rules unique to the industry have also been created and passed on naturally. Looking at the rules of the dart game, the prominent characteristic of the industry is that defense against winning darts is the core content. In other words, all dart rules are formed and operated around bodyguard activities.
