There is a person named Ke Si in Putian, Fujian. He has a kind heart, is loyal and kind, and is always polite. He opened a rice shop in a small town to spend his days.
Once, a local wealthy man invited a famous Feng Shui master from near and far to find a place for him. After months of painstaking searching, he finally found a beautiful cave on a local mountain. In his sleep, the Feng Shui practitioner slept in his cave, listening to God's call, and in his sleep, he saw the Earth God rebuke him, saying, “This acupuncture point is the ancestral tomb of the Ke Zhuangyuan family. You cannot induce others to invade and migrate quickly; otherwise, a major disaster will come.” The Feng Shui Master told the owner about the Night Dream Earth God and found another lucky cave for them to bury their relatives.
After the incident was over, the Feng Shui Master inquired from the owner about the way to Ke Si's rice shop. The Feng Shui Master opened up the door and asked, “Does Boss Ke's family have a burial place?” As soon as Ke heard it, she was shocked and asked, “How did you know, sir?” The Feng Shui master laughed, “As shown by the Earth,” Ke Si said, “My father died last year. As a result, Mr. Feng Shui told Ke Si about the location of the beautiful cave and chose a date for his burial. This place is shaped like a phoenix spreading its wings. There are two natural pools of water to protect Yin Dragon. The cave points on the Phoenix's mouth. The left and right wings are flat, and the mountains rise and fall. The mountain in front is shaped like three stars. The spacious and invisible water shines brightly. The Feng Shui master said, “It will be the winner within a few years after burial here.”
