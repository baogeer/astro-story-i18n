Wolves that don't eat sheep
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 One day, an old hunter asked Avanti:
 
 
 
 “Avanti, I've spared a lot of sheep, but quite a few have been bitten and eaten by wild animals! I want to ask you, are there any wolves in the world that don't eat sheep?”
 
 
 
 “Yes, yes!” Avanti said.
 
 
 
 “What kind of wolf is that?”
 
 
 
 “It's a dead wolf.” Avanti answered.
Sword and Fire Stick
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Avanti was going back and forth in the upper reaches of the bazaar and suddenly saw a man carrying a sword.
 
 
 
 “How much does this sword sell for?” Avan asked.
 
 
 
 “Ten ingots.” That person answered.
 
 
 
 “Too expensive! Too expensive!”
 
 
 
 “Yes, expensive is expensive, but my sword has a unique mystery: when you stab a wild wolf with it, it stretches ten steps forward.”
 
 
 
 Avanti touched his forehead and said, “It's no surprise that it stretches ten feet. When my wife gets angry and hits me, the fire stick in her hand stretches 20 feet and hits me in the head.”
Excerpt from “Blowing the Suona in the Middle of the Night” compiled by Zhao Shijie
