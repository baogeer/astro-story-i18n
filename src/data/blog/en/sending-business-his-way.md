 In the old days, there was a person in the village named Feng Daquan. He had a very small mind. The family clearly had a very busy life. On the one hand, he still couldn't see anyone else making money. As soon as he saw it, he was flustered, and they had to grab it if they found any way.
This morning, Feng Daquan sneaked out of the village. Seeing Zhang puppy from the same village pushing back a cart of reeds, he asked him why he had so many reeds. Zhang Puppy said that he wanted to make a few sets of reed fences and sell them to people who build houses, and earn some points.
Feng Daquan listened. His eyes lit up. Right, this is also a way to make money; why didn't he expect it? The reeds on the banks of the Yingshui River at the entrance of the village have no owner; whoever likes to cut them cuts them. If he cuts everything down, whoever wants to build a house can only go to his house to buy a reed fence, and he will be able to make a lot of money.
Feng Daquan said he could do it. He immediately went back to the village and hired ten or so short-term workers to harvest reeds by the Yingshui River. After working for more than half a month, all the reeds along this river were harvested by him and piled up like a hill in the yard. Next, he directed the short workers to build a fence, and went out to solicit business himself.
Soon, a customer came to the door. Feng Daquan brought that person to the courtyard to see the reed fence. He thought the lion would make a big opening and ask for a high price. Who knows that person saw the reed fence and left. Feng Daquan hurriedly stopped him and asked, “Aren't you buying it?”
The man sneered, “You call this a reed fence? Don't make people laugh out of their big teeth! Your reed fence is like a sieve; the mud must not 'buzz' down the floor. Can you use it?” As soon as Feng Daquan took a look, ah, poor families don't use tiles to build houses; instead, they lay reed fences on purges and then spread mud on them. His eyes on these reeds are so big that the mud from above will have to fall down. Feng Daquan stopped the short-term workers and angrily said, “What kind of reed fence did you make! If you make it up like this, I'll deduct your wages!” The short-term workers exclaimed that they really aren't very good at editing either.
After thinking about it for a while, Feng Daquan thought there must be a trick here, so he went to ask Zhang Puppy for advice. Little Dog Zhang shook his head like a rattle and said with vigor, “No, no, I really can't teach. I've taught you, and I'll starve to death!” Feng Daquan pulled out a piece of money or two and put it into his hands; only then did Zhang Puppy reluctantly agree.
I saw that Puppy Zhang picked two buckets of water and poured it on the reeds. After waiting for half an hour, the reeds softened. Zhang Puppy said, “Now you can make it; just pick the soft ones.” After speaking, he clapped his hands and was about to leave. Feng Daquan widened his eyes in astonishment: “Is this OK?” Puppy Zhang nodded. Sure enough, the reed fence that the short workers made again is very dense. Feng Daquan secretly humbled himself: Isn't this a free gift of money or two for Zhang Puppy? He was extremely distressed when he thought of those two pieces of money.
In a blink of an eye, it's almost the New Year. As soon as Feng Daquan settled his accounts, the money he earned from selling reed fences, minus the wages he paid to the short-term workers, he didn't even make any money! Moreover, he also gave Zhang the puppy a dollar or two. For most of this winter, I've been writing together to keep Zhang Puppy busy. Feng Daquan's eyes were dark, and he almost fainted.
Fortunately, Feng Daquan is still a bit clever. As soon as his eyes turned, he soon thought of a solution.
As soon as winter began next year, Feng Daquan hired a short-term labourer to harvest all the reeds along the Yingshui River and pile them up in his yard. Then he dismissed the short-term workers and wanted to wait for customers to come to the door, so they took the reeds and built their own reed fence. Who knows, after waiting for a few days, no one came to the door, they really rushed him to death.
On this day, Feng Daquan met Zhang Xiaogu again and asked, “What are you doing for a living this winter? Can I make money?” Little Zhang panicked and said, “I can't tell you anymore; otherwise, you should take it again.” Having said that, he turned around and left. Feng Daquan felt that Zhang Puppy must have found another way to make money, and his heart was itching again: Huh, if you don't tell me, I'd rather know!
Early the next morning, Feng Daquan hid outside Zhang Puppy's house early. Soon, Zhang Puppy pulled the car out. He walked all the way west. After walking seven or eight miles, he went up Xishan. Up to the middle of the mountainside, there are two forks. One is the path that continues up the mountain, and the other goes around the valley behind the mountain. Little Zhang put down the cart, carried a sickle and ropes, and ran to the valley.
The puppy arrived in the valley and cut off the thick thorns. After half a day's work, he cut down four large bundles of thorns and put them on the cart. He was pushing his car down the mountain. Feng Daquan suddenly jumped out to stop him and smiled and said, “Little Zhang, I'm with you!”
Little Zhang ignored him and pushed his car down the mountain. Feng Daquan followed him and asked, “Zhang Puppy, tell me, why would you use cutting thorns?” Puppy Zhang said coldly, “I won't tell you.” Feng Daquan pulled out a silver or two from his pocket and put it in Zhang's hands and said, “Puppy, just tell me.” Only then did Zhang Xiaogu laugh: “Don't hide it from you; this is the business you gave me.”
Feng Daquan's eyes almost fell in surprise and asked, “What, the business I gave you?” Zhang Puppy nodded and told him that in the dry weather this year, the reeds grew so thin and small that they couldn't build a reed fence at all. This made it difficult for people who built houses. The reeds along the Yingshui River were all cut off by Feng Daquan. Zhang puppy wandered all over the mountains. It turned out that these thorns were long enough to form a fence, and they could still be sold for money. Over the past few days, the people who built the house have come to order a fence with him one after another. He has been busy from morning to night.
Feng Daquan immediately understood. No wonder no one came to buy his family's reeds. Zhang Puppy can make money by weaving a fence, and he can do it himself. He patted the puppy on the shoulder and laughed, “Puppy, thank you for telling me the secret to making money. When I make money, please drink!” After speaking, he went underground with joy.
Feng Daquan immediately hired another part-time worker, cut down all the thorns in the valley, transported them to the yard, and piled them up into a hill. Feng Daquan also asked the short-term workers to make a jing-fence. As soon as the short-term workers got started, they were dumbfounded; they wouldn't do it.
Feng Daquan proudly instructed the short-term workers: splash water. However, water was spilled on it. After half an hour, the thorns were still hard, and they broke as soon as they were broken. The short-time workers worked hard for half a day. There were quite a few broken bars, but half of the fence wasn't made. Feng Daquan was helpless, so he had to go ask Zhang Puppy for advice.
Zhang Puppy asked him for another piece of money or two, and Feng Daquan had to gnaw his teeth to give it. Only then did Zhang Xiaogu smile and say to Feng Daquan, “Thank you, Big Brother Feng, give me another big deal.” Feng Daquan was so angry that he was speechless.
Afterwards, Zhang Puppy went to teach the short term workers to weave a fence. His trick is actually very simple. Soak the wingsticks first, then roast them on the fire, then leave the wisteria to the mercy of others. After only one hour of teaching, the short-time workers would weave a fence, and Zhang Puppy whistled and walked leisurely. The more Feng Daquan looked on the sidelines, the more angry he became, but there was nothing he could do about it.
In a blink of an eye, it's almost the New Year. Once Feng Daquan settled his accounts, he still hasn't made any money. For more than half of this winter, he only sold the fence, but he didn't sell the reeds. There was also a large pile of reeds in the yard, but that was all money. But now the question is, if the people who built the house used wigs, why do they need reeds? That bunch of reeds in his family is worth nothing!
Feng Daquan was getting annoyed; Zhang Puppy suddenly came. Feng Daquan got angry and said, “What are you here for?” Little Zhang laughed and said, “Big Brother Feng, I'm here to pick up the business!” Feng Daquan was half angry and yelled, “What kind of business will you take on? I can't make any money myself, how can I have a business for you!”
But Zhang Puppy wasn't mad. He still smiled and said, “It's almost the New Year. Every household has to set off firecrackers. Those firecrackers aren't easy to see. If they bounce onto your house's reed stack and light up the reeds, not to mention your family, the whole village will have to suffer. My wife brought the kids back to her mother's house. I'm fine on my own, so I'll take care of them for you. However, for the Lunar New Year, I'll have to pay a little more for the New Year.”
Feng Daquan originally wanted to scold him away, but on second thought, Zhang Puppy also had a point. If a pile of reeds actually caused a big fire, he would have to lose the houses and property of the entire village, and his family would have to go bankrupt. Seen in this way, a few dollars for Zhang Puppy is really a small amount of money. He clenched his teeth and said, “OK, I'll hire you until 15.” Zhang Puppy smiled and said, “Brother, thank you for giving me another business.”
Feng Daquan hated his teeth, but there was nothing he could do about it. He didn't understand. His family had a good life. Why did he go to work with Zhang Puppy to forge reeds? As a result, they didn't make any money, which made this whole mess!
