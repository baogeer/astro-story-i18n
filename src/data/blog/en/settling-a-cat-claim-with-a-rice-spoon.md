 Once upon a time, there was an old man in Bu Wenxiang, whose last name was Chen Mingqin. They are kind and united in their hometown. Their lifelong ambition is based on “peace is precious”, and they hang a “no matter card” in front of their homes. One day, there was an employee in the village who was well-off and saw this card and laughed out loud: “No one is good for a thousand days, no flower is good for a hundred days.” Little peasant, don't you dare to put up an unscrupulous card; I would rather say something is wrong with you. However, I saw him scratching his brain, patting the corner of his long shirt, and breaking through Chen's house in a big way. The old man Chen hurriedly greeted him and asked, “Outside the staff came to our house. I don't know what they are worth doing?”
“I didn't go to the Sanbao Temple without incident. Because your ancestor killed a black cat in the family before, I'm making a claim today.”
“If this is the case, why haven't I heard of it?” Chen Qin is a generous person. He immediately changed his mind and immediately said, “OK, OK, go to the market tomorrow and buy a black cat.”
“This cat is a black cat with a white belly. It is worth 2,500.” Lin Yuanwai added emphasis to the tone of “2005.”
Old Chen listened and heard thunder; he didn't know what to do. On the outside, he was hesitant and hurriedly said, “Let's do it, remember our love for our hometown, and we have an additional three days to refund. Otherwise, I'll have to sue the government for the crime!” Talk it over and go!
Old Chen couldn't stand this blatant injustice; he fell into bed. After learning about her father-in-law's heart disease, the wise and smart daughter-in-law comforted her. She served tea soup every day and thought about countermeasures.
After a few days, the county governor sent Chan Qin to the county government. The daughter-in-law decided to file a lawsuit for her father-in-law because the old man fell ill in bed. He went to court and ordered the officer to accuse the Chen family about what happened.
Lin Yuanwai answered, “Grandpa Lin heard it only because my ancestor Uehara had a good cat. His dark fur was very smooth, and his stomach was white, clean and brave. He was very smart, and he had it. There are no mice in the 100 Mile Family. They have made a fortune within 100 years. Commonly known as black cats and white bellies, worth 2,500. Unfortunately, they were killed by the Chen family. I hope the old man will decide!”
“Is this for real?” The old man from the county shouted at Chen's daughter-in-law.
Hyun's daughter-in-law replied, “It's true or false. I hope the old man is clear. However, the ancestors of the Lin family also owed the family a rice spoon. According to the child's opinion, food spoons should be used for the cat. It is better for the two families to rest.”
The county official laughed. “How can a rice spoon be worth thousands of dollars.”
Hyun's daughter-in-law went on to say, “Grandpa Yong Yu. My great-grandfather went to Luzon to do business to retrieve a section of black firewood from deep in the mountains, which smelled like incense, and was engraved by a famous craftsman. There is a picture of Dragon, Fei, and Phoenix dancing. There is a natural white eye in the middle of the spoon, which shines brightly. Rice spoons are filled with rice. One grain is ten for a person, ten grains for 100, and is priceless. People say, 'Black firewood is worth two thousand six, and the Lin family will have to pay another 120 dollars for the family.”
The staff were dumbfounded when they heard it. The old man from the county thought a bit and criticized: “The certificate is not real, the cat can get it right.” Eight characters. Since then, “take the food spoon and give it to the cat.” It has become a popular proverb all over Zhangzhou.
