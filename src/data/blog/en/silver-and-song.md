 Once upon a time, there was a rich owner. His land was as big as a blue sky; his cows and sheep could cover three mountains; his house was as gorgeous as a colorful painting; he couldn't count his money himself. However, from morning to night, he was calculating how to call others bankrupt, to make himself even richer, so that he couldn't eat during the day, slept well at night, and was as thin as an old monkey in Gongbu Forest.
Next door to him is a small water mill. There lives a girl who watches over the water mill. This girl's life is extremely difficult. Every morning she only has one spoon of chopsticks noodles, and at noon she only has one spoon of chopstick noodles. At night, she can only drink a bowl of chopsticks paste soup. What I wear on my body is not so much a piece of clothing as a rag to cover my shame. The padding on my shoes is attached to it. However, every day from morning to night, she kept singing, sometimes happy songs, sometimes melancholy songs, sometimes long narrative songs, sometimes dancing with her hands and feet, and dancing softly.
The rich owner watched it and thought it was very strange. He thought, “Oh my God! This girl doesn't eat anything, doesn't wear anything. Why does she keep singing like a cuckoo no matter the day or night, spring, summer, fall, or winter? And I, like a mountain of money, can't use it up for many years, yet I'm always sad and worried? Does money have anything to do with singing?”
One day, when he saw the girl go out to carry barley, he slipped lightly into the water mill, pulled out a bar of horseshoe silver from her arms and placed it on the grinding plate. Soon, the girl came in carrying barley. Seeing such a large piece of silver, she was both happy and scared. Seeing that no one was around, she quickly hid it in her arms.
From this point on, the girl was in a trance and restless all day long, and she couldn't hear her tirelessly singing any more.
Seeing this situation, the rich man sighed and said, “Alas, the sound of silver and the song seem to have a different temper!”
Narration; Yixi, Chengguan Town, Shigatse
