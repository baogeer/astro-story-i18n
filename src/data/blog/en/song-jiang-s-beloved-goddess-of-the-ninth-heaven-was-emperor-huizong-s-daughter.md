As can be seen from the analysis, the author's main intention in writing “The Outlaws of the Water Marsh” was: 1. Reveal that the conflict between Song Huizong and Song Jiang was irreconcilable, causing the foreign Jin Guo to take advantage of it and the fisherman prosper. In order to preserve his world, Song Huizong had to use Song Jiang, who is good at power planning. However, in his view, those with power must have no loyalty and no filial piety; they will not be willing to be son-in-law or courtiers; they must be promptly eliminated. However, the country has also lost its talents. On the part of Song Jiang, he did have rebellious intentions. He pretended to be loyal only to gain the trust and promotion of the emperor, but when he discovered that Song Huizong had repeatedly betrayed his words and his plans had failed, he began to passively neglect his war. As a result, the country lost a large number of talents. As can be seen from Song Jiang's deliberate fulfillment of his promise in the end, building a temple and a temple for Hsuan nun, he was very unhappy with Song Huizong and his daughter, and was very hateful (Curse the Princess died early). 2. Exhort the Chinese to change the concept of surnames and the hereditary system. The author believes that the emperor should start from the overall interests of the Chinese nation and relinquish the throne and Zen meditation to the person most capable of governing and defending the country. For example, Zhao Ji should relinquish the throne to Song Jiang. If Song Jiang succeeds the throne, the Northern Song Dynasty will not perish; it may even become stronger and stronger. For this reason, the author even went to great lengths to use Li Kui to use word games to persuade the world: “The emperor's name is Song, and my brother's last name is Song. If you are an emperor, you cannot be an emperor!” Meaning: Song Jiang is not from the Zhao family, but he is also from the Song family. Why is it wrong to take over the throne?
