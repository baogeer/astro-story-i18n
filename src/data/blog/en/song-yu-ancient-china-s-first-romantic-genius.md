So-called “merry talents” should generally have three elements: one is a good-looking man, and can attract people of the opposite sex, such as Pan An of the Western Jin Dynasty; the second is that he is talented; the second is that he is talented, and his works of charming poetry praising women have been passed down the world, such as Sima of Han, Song Zhiliuyong, Zhou Bangyan, etc.; and the three are famous and elegant, such as Ming Zhi Wenzheng, Tang Bohu, and Qing Zhi Xiao Ji Lan. If you measure these three things, you would think that the most “merry and talented person” in ancient China would be Song Yu, a Chu native at the end of the Warring States period!
Regarding Song Yu's life, “Historical Records: The Biography of Qu Yuan and Jia Sheng” says, “After Qu Yuan died, Chu had Song Yu, Tang Le, and the disciples of Jingshai, all famous for their good words. However, they all made the calm words of our ancestor Qu Yuan, so I never dared to speak out.” The notes are extremely brief. It is said in “Chu Ji Zhang Ju” that he was a disciple of Qu Yuan. Jin's Xi Che Chi's “The Ancient Legend of Xiangyang” said, “Song Yu's are also from Chu Zhi Yan. Therefore, Yicheng has Song Yu Kou. The incident started with Qu Yuan. Originally, they were banished and sought poor scenery from Chu friends.” From this, it is generally known that Song Yu is from the Zhongxiang and Yicheng areas of Hubei today, and is another famous poet after Qu Yuan.
In many ancient works of literature, people often describe men as beautiful as “as beautiful as Song Yu and look like Pan An.” Song Yu should be a handsome man comparable to Pan An, the most beautiful man in ancient times. There is no detailed description of how beautiful Song Yu actually is in history books, but we can still appreciate Song Yu's beauty from “Deng Tzu Zi's Good Talents.” According to “Deng Tzu Zi's Lustful Talents,” Dr. Chu served King Chu, and Song Yu said, “Jade is beautiful, quiet, and lustful. Please don't leave the harem with Wang.” This apprentice made a small report to Chu Wang, saying that Song Yu is a beautiful man with a “beautiful figure”. He also talks sweetly, but he is a good-natured woman, so don't let Song Yu run to the harem to come into contact with your concubine. King Chu told Song Yu what Deng Tuzi had said, and Song Yu countered:
There is no more beautiful woman in the world than Chu, and there is nothing more beautiful than my hometown. The most beautiful woman in my hometown is the girl from the east neighbor next door. This beautiful woman is a bit too long, while a fraction less is too short; pink is too white, and Shi Zhu is too red. The eyebrows are like bird feathers, the skin is like white snow, the waist is thin, and the teeth are like shells. A bright smile is enough to faint men in Yangcheng and Xiacai. This amazing woman has been lying on the wall and staring at me for three years, and I'm unmoved! On the other hand, Deng Tsuzi is a pervert. The wife in his family is very ugly. Her hair is messy, her ears are deformed, her lips are turned outward, her teeth are uneven, she is limping, or hunched, and has scabies all over her body. But Deng Duzi gave birth to five children in a row with her. Let Wang Shang decide, who is better, Song Yu or Deng Tuzi?
Song Yu used her extraordinary eloquence to deceive Chu Wang and actually made Chu Wang confused, so she decided that Deng Tuzi was a lustful person.
Song Yu can be described as an elegant and talented person. She was a grandmaster of a generation in ancient literature. His masterpiece “Jiu Bian” is comparable to Qu Yuan's “Lishao” in literary history, and “Qu Song” can be called the absurd “Gemini Constellation.” What we often call “The People of Xialiba,” “Yangchun White Snow,” and “Qu Gao and Widow” are all from Song Yu. In “Asking Chu Wang,” Song Yu said, “There are people who sang songs in Yingzhong.” They are members of the country and there are thousands of people who are members of the country... “Yang Chun” and “Snow White”. There are only dozens of people in the country.” This is the origin of three idioms such as “the person from the Lower Liba.”
Song Yu was the first in history to write sad autumn, and also the first to write a classic female “passionate” poem. The “Wushan Goddess” in his “Goddess of Goddess”, “is unrivaled, and extremely beautiful,” overshadowed Mao Qiang and Xi Shi, the two major beauties of Chunqiu. Song Yu did her best to depict every detail, posture, and expression of the “Goddess,” and regarded the “Goddess” as the most beautiful being created in nature. In fact, Song Yu's “Goddess” is nothing more than a typical “deification” of all kinds of beautiful women that he himself has admired. Some people think that the prototype for “Goddess of God” is a gorgeous and dignified woman in the Chu Palace that Song Yu once loved. If this were true, then Deng Tuzi's small report was not out of nowhere.
Another family pointed out that “Goddess of God” is actually a dancer or prostitute in the Chu Palace. Therefore, Song Yu should have been the first ancient poet to describe prostitutes, and it can be seen from this that her longing feelings for this “goddess.” Song Yu's previous work “Gao Tang Fu” was Song Yu telling a story to Chu Wang. Chu Wang was happy to hear it, so he asked Song Yu to “give” him once. However, in “Goddess Talents,” which was handed down from generation to generation, it became Chu Wang's dream. Chu Wang told Song Yu about his dream, and asked Song Yu to “give” him one more time after speaking. Actually, this is probably a confusion between “Wang” and “Jade.” Song Yu is the one who actually dreams of a “goddess.” This real-life model of “Goddess” is probably the “dream lover” he thinks about day and night!
Song Yu's “The Power of a Goddess” has had a profound impact on future generations. Poems praising beautiful women, such as Sima Xiang's “Beauty Talent” and Cao Zhi's “Luo Shenfu”, all clearly have traces of imitating “The Talent of a Goddess.” Song Yu's “Goddess of Tang” has accumulated into a prototype with permanent vitality. Whether it's “Jinbingmei” or “Dream of the Red Mansion,” every time a writer wants to express the sex and beauty of women, they always unconsciously and unconsciously go back to the prototype of a goddess shaped by the first “merry and talented person” in ancient times.
