 Author: Takaji
Yesterday, Gao Hua, professor of history and doctoral supervisor at Nanjing University, commented on Song Meiling's respectable places from five aspects in an interview with reporters. He believes that Song Meiling is an important woman who played a positive role in several key points in the 20th century Chinese political and historical stage with her special identity.
Resolving the Xi'an Incident Peacefully
After the incident in Xi'an, the Nanjing National Government was in turmoil. On the question of how to rescue Chiang Kai-shek and how to treat Zhang and Yang, there was a division between the main battle, the lord, and the two factions. Despite Song Meiling's objections, the main warring faction secretly mobilized troops to bomb Xi'an. With her determination and wisdom, Song Meiling made great efforts to ease the tension in all aspects of the Kuomintang, and finally resolved the Xi'an incident peacefully.
Seek aid for victory in the war of resistance
During the war of resistance, Song Meiling visited the US several times to lobby the US government to provide aid to the Chinese people. Even when she stayed in Nanjing, she continued to expose Japanese military atrocities to the world through news agencies, criticize Western countries' policies of condoning Japan, and at the same time show the determination of Chinese generals to resist heroically and seek support and sympathy for China from the US government and opposition. In one of her broadcasts to the US, she said, “Good morning, friends of the United States. I only spent a few minutes speaking this passage to ask all people who love freedom to know that China should immediately receive just aid; this is China's right. Everyone, you may be able to hear the sound of artillery on the radio, but I hope you can imagine the cries of pain from the wounded and the groans of the dying, but you can't hear them.”
Inspiring Soldiers by Going to the Front
On the days when the Japanese army took turns in air raids, Song Meiling didn't find a safe place to take care of her superiority, but instead fought with a large number of officers and soldiers on the front lines. She almost died in enemy bombings many times. Once on her way to the front line, Song Meiling's car was bombed. She broke several ribs, but she still insisted on giving a speech to the front-line generals and shouting, “Brothers, come with me!” The battle slogan then fainted amidst the cheers of the officers and soldiers.
Promoting the development of China's civil aviation
Very few people understand this. In the late 20s of the last century, Song Meiling was the chairman of the Aviation Commission at the time. Using her special status, she opened civil aviation routes for the first time in several major cities such as Beijing, Shanghai, Nanjing, and Wuhan. From the perspective of China's modernization, her approach is very positive.
Resolutely oppose “two Chinas”
Since Song Meiling and Chiang Kai-shek arrived in Taiwan, they have always advocated the position of national reunification and opposed “Taiwan independence” and “two Chinas.” This view has accompanied her throughout her life and has had a great influence on the Taiwanese government.
