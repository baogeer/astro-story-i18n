 Peonies are everywhere in the world, and peonies in Caozhou are the best in the world.
Before liberation, flower farmers in Peony Township in Caozhou often had to go to Guangzhou to sell peonies, called “Xia Guang.” On the one hand, because people in Guangzhou are particularly fond of Caozhou peonies, and on the other hand, they are also trying to increase income. However, due to long distances, inconvenient transportation, and restrictions due to natural conditions, it often backfires. Selling peonies in Guangzhou also made a fortune. One year, Gao Fengqi from Zhaolou Village made a fortune by selling flowers in Guangzhou. When he returned, he was afraid of being robbed on the road and that his hard-earned money would be taken. He thought and meditated, and finally sewed the ingots into a rotten and dirty cotton coat and dressed up as a flower. In this way, judging from his appearance, no one would have guessed that he would still have many gold ingots on his body. Along the way, he ate and slept in the open, and after much hard work, he finally returned home safely.
After returning to Zhaolou Village, as soon as the villagers saw Gao Fengqi's appearance, they all thought he must have lost money by selling flowers. They all came to persuade him one after another. They all said that he was lucky enough to be able to return to his hometown safely; they didn't think about anything else. Gao Fengqi smiled and didn't say anything. He only told his family the details and ordered them to invite everyone, and he wanted to set up a big dinner. The townspeople were all taken aback when they saw the banquet. They didn't know what medicine Gao Fengqi was selling in gourds. Gao Fengqi didn't explain it either; she just made a vigorous toast to the townspeople and thanked them for taking care of their families. After three rounds of drinking, Gao Fengqi took the yuanbao out of her dirty cotton coat to bask in the sun, saying that she had hidden it and covered it along the way, but it also became moldy and rusted. Only then did the people present understand that Gao Fengqi had made a fortune. They all praised and congratulated him for making a fortune.
In the afternoon, while cleaning up the ingots, Gao Fengqi's family discovered that a silver spoon was missing and told Gao Fengqi. Everyone said they wanted to find it to see who had lost the person in Zhaolou Village. Gao Fengqi laughed: “Forget it; anyway, you can't reach outsiders. If anyone runs out of money to spend, take it again, don't be embarrassed to speak up.”
The story of Gao Yuan sharing his ingots outside is still circulating around Zhaolou.
