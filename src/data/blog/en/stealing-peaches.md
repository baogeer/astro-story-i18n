 When I was a kid, I went to Jinan Province once to take an exam and happened to have experienced the Spring Festival. Following the old custom, the day before the Spring Festival, people from all walks of life doing business in the city had to carry the lanterns and beat the ground to the Chief Administration Office to celebrate the Spring Festival. This is called the “Spring Festival.” I also followed my friends there to watch the fun.
On that day, there were a lot of tourists. People surrounded it like a wall on all sides, and water couldn't drain through. There were four officials sitting in the lobby, all dressed in red robes and sitting facing each other. I was still young at the time, and I didn't know what kind of official was in the court. I only heard loud voices, loud drums, and deafening. Suddenly, a man, leading a boy with a bearded head, walked up to the hall. It seemed like he was saying something, but there was a lot of noise, and I couldn't hear what he said. I only saw the people in the lobby laughing. Next, there is a legend about government officials dressed in black, asking them to act. The man agreed. Just about to perform, he asked, “What trick?” The people in the hall had a few discussions with each other, and they saw that a government official walked out of the hall and asked him what good tricks he was good at. The man answered, “I can reverse the seasons of living things and grow all kinds of things.” After the government returned to the hall to report, he walked down again and asked him to perform to pick peaches.
The trick nods and agreed. He took off his clothes and covered it in a bamboo box, and deliberately made a complaint and said, “The rulers really don't understand the situation. Right now, the ice hasn't melted yet. Where can I get the peaches? Let's not go get it; I'm afraid it will make the chief angry. What can I do about this?” His son said, “My father has already agreed, so how can I deduce it?” The trick man was puzzled for a while and said, “I've seriously thought about it. Right now, the weather is still early spring, and the snow and ice haven't melted yet. Where can I find a pick in the world? There are only peaches in the peach garden where the mother and daughter of Wang Mu Niang are like spring in every season. But you have to go to heaven and steal them to get peaches.” My son said, “Hey! Can the sky walk up like there are steps?” The trick says, “I have my own way.” After speaking, I opened the bamboo box and took out a bunch of ropes. It was about tens of feet long. He pulled out a rope and threw it in the air. The rope was actually hanging in the middle of the air, as if something was holding it. As I watched the rope keep rising, getting higher and higher, vaguely rising to the clouds, I ran out of rope in my hand. At this point, he called his son and said, “Come here, kid, I'm old, tired, and clumsy, and can't go up. You can go for me.” Then I handed the head of the rope to my son and said, “If you hold this rope, you can climb up.”
When the son took the rope, his face looked very embarrassed and complained, “Dad was really confused. Such a thin rope made me climb tens of thousands of feet high above the sky. If the rope breaks in the middle, it falls to pieces.” The father coaxed and seriously said, “I've already gone out of my way to promise to others, and it's too late to regret it. It's still too late for my son to take a trip. Don't be afraid of suffering. If you steal a peach, you will definitely be rewarded with 100 gold. I will definitely marry you a beautiful daughter-in-law at that time.” My son was helpless. He held the rope with his hand and circled upward; his foot moved upward with his hand, living like a spider walking a wire net, and gradually fell into the clouds and couldn't see it. After a while, a peach fell from the sky, the size of a bowl. I was very happy to play tricks. I held a peach with both hands and presented it to the hall. The officials in the hall watched it for half a day, but they couldn't tell if it was real or false. At this point, the rope suddenly fell from the sky and shouted in horror, “Oops! Someone in the sky cut the rope, how can my son get down?” After a while, another thing fell. When I looked at it, it turned out to be his son's head. He held his son's head and cried, “This must have been when stealing peaches and was discovered by the caretaker. My son is finished.” When I was crying so sad, another foot fell from the sky; soon, my limbs and torso all fell down one after another.
The trick guy was very sad. He picked them up one by one and packed them in a box, then added a cover and said, “The old man only has this son. He walks north and south with me every day. Today, following the strict orders of the Chief Justice, I didn't expect such a disaster, so I had to take him back and bury him.” So he went up to the hall and knelt down and begged, “My son was murdered in order to steal peaches! The adults are poor little people. Please give them some money to clean up my son's dead body. In the future, when I die, I should repay the generals for their kindness.”
The officials in the hall were shocked and each offered him lots of money to reward him. He took the money and wrapped it around his waist, walked out of the hall, patted the box with his hand, and said, “Eight eight, don't come out quickly, thank you all for the money. How long are you waiting!” Suddenly, a child with a loose beak opened the lid of the box with the top of his head, walked out of the box, and prostrated his head in the hall. At first glance, it turned out to be his son.
Because this trick was so amazing, I still remember it very much until now. Later, I heard people say that Bai Lian can perform this spell. I think this is probably their descendant?
