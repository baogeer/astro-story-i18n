 During the Guangxu era (1877), there was a major drought in Jinyu, and men from many villages in western Henan either went out to ask for food or set up small businesses abroad to support their families.
There is a person named Stubborn in Runan Village in the south of Yicheng doing business in Luocheng. On this day, he was preparing to send money and goods to his family. Hu Laohan, who does business in the same place, wanted to ask him to send a letter to his wife. It turns out that Hu Laohan's son is about to get married to his daughter-in-law, but Hu Laohan just happened to have a business and it was difficult to get out of it, so he asked Stubborn to send a letter to his wife. He won't be able to return until two months late. Although Gou San said he is in the same hometown as Hu Laohan, he didn't know what village his family was in. He only learned when he asked that Hu Laohan actually lived in Big Poplar Village in the north of Yicheng.
The large poplar village in the north of Yicheng is quite famous in the Yicheng area. Of course, the large poplar tree surrounded by ten people shaded the sky and the sun. When Stubborn asked Hu Laohan's family where they lived in the village, Hu Laohan told Stubborn that his family lives in a large poplar tree. At that time, it wasn't unusual for people to live in trees, let alone such thick trees, so I didn't feel anything strange. But with such a thick tree, how can I go up on my own? Hu Laohan told him, “When you arrive, go around the poplar tree three times, reverse three times, then tap the trunk and shout 'sister-in-law opens the door' and someone will pick you up.”
Gou San wrote down Hu Laohan's words and arrived under the big poplar tree in Beida Poplar Village in Yicheng in less than three days. He circled the poplar tree three times and backwards three times, then slapped the tree trunk one after another and shouted, “My sister-in-law opens the door.” As soon as the conversation came to an end, the thick trunk suddenly opened the door, and an old woman came out from the inside and led him in.
After Gou San went in, he saw that it turned out to be a courtyard. The courtyard was clean and tidy. What surprised him was that there was a cage under the eaves on the left side of the yard, but there was a girl inside. He looked very familiar, but for a moment he couldn't remember who it was.
The old woman led Stubb 3 to meet Hu Laohan's wife. Stubb 3 repeated the letter Hu Laohan had sent her, then got up and said goodbye and went back home. He hasn't said a few words with his wife yet. His older sister-in-law came in: “Her third uncle, I heard you're back. Go and save your niece. Your brother isn't at home. I'm a woman, so what can I do!” I started crying when I said that.
Gou San quickly followed her older sister-in-law to her older brother's house. She was shocked when she saw her niece lying in bed. Isn't this the girl in Hu Ge Hu's cage? Stubborn understood all of a sudden. No wonder she felt familiar when she saw it; she turned out to be her own niece. He said to his sister, who was still crying, “Big sister, don't be sad; I will definitely be able to heal my niece if I go.” Having said that, I hurriedly rushed to Big Poplar Village.
The old woman brought Stubborn in the door again, and Hu Laohan's wife asked him what happened when she came back. He pointed to the girl in the cage under the roof and smiled and said, “My sister-in-law is really not hiding it. I saw that she was familiar when I first came in, and I couldn't remember who it was for a moment. I only learned that it was my niece when I got home, so I asked my sister-in-law to hold her hands high and let go of my niece.” Mrs. Hu's face immediately turned dark. It took a while to say, “My son married a wife and only visited this girl for many years. When Lao Hu comes back, I'll have to get married to them! If you keep sending me a letter, just let her go.” When the old woman who opened the door heard the news, she went over and opened the cage. The girl inside actually turned into a ray of blue smoke dripping out of the cage. When Stubborn returned to her older brother's house, her niece was sitting in bed talking and laughing with her mom. Stubborn grew in breath. At night, his wife asked him what methods he had used to save his niece, and he lied that he had gone to Chenghuang Temple to worship God.
The next day, stubbornly left his wife and went back to Luocheng. As soon as I arrived in Luocheng, the family sent an urgent letter to let him go home quickly. It turned out that his wife and niece also had the same illness as his niece after he left. His wife and her brother and sister went to Chenghuang Temple to worship gods, but there was no effect at all. Stubbornly unable to drink water, I rushed to Big Poplar Village all night. At 3 in the middle of the night, I knocked on Hu Laohan's house and vaguely saw another girl locked up in the cage under the roof. Hu Laohan's wife listened to her stubbornly intentions. She did not agree to release anyone, and said, “Your kindness in carrying the letter is over; you never want to let go again.” To no avail, he said all the good things. The final stubbornness came up and threatened to go to court. As a result, he turned around and wanted to leave. Suddenly, it fell to the ground as soon as it got dark in front of me. By the time he woke up, it was already bright, and he found himself hanging from a large poplar tree.
Good guys don't take advantage of immediate losses. Stubborn didn't dare to be any more stubborn. He kept saying nice things in his mouth, and finally got let go. Stubborn didn't dare to stay, so he quickly went home. Take a look at my wife and niece who are in a coma. Thinking about what happened to me overnight, I was so stubborn that I clenched my teeth. After briefly explaining the situation to everyone, he stated his plans.
That night, Gou San brought more than 30 people from the village, each carrying a large bundle of dried firewood to the poplar tree. While letting the invited mage do the trick, they piled up more than 30 bundles of dried firewood under the tree and set off a raging fire. For a while, there were miserable shouts from the tree, like people shouting, like animals. The fire is getting louder, but the shouting is getting weaker. When it was almost bright, the large poplars had already burnt to ashes. When they returned home exhausted, they learned that their wife and niece had died out of anger.
After the funeral of my wife and niece, I was afraid to go back to Luocheng. I stayed at home every day and didn't dare to go out, but the more afraid I was afraid of being intimidated. That night, as soon as Stubborn fell asleep, Hu Laohan stood in front of him and scolded him loudly, saying, “My wife doesn't agree to let go. You should look for me; you shouldn't have taken this harshly, burn dozens of members of my family alive, and burned to death your own wife and niece. People like you are to be condemned by God.” Speaking of which, I held out my hands to strangle 3's neck. When I woke up with a loud cry, I knew I was dreaming. After thinking about it over and over again, I felt that hiding at home was no solution; I wonder if it would hurt my family in the future.
“Good guys do good things. I've been stubborn and haven't been afraid of anyone in my whole life. Since I did something like this, whether right or wrong, ask Hu Laohan to deal with it.” Thinking about this, stubbornly packed up his luggage and wanted to go to Luocheng. When his wife heard it, she couldn't let go of her life or death, but her stubbornness was made up her mind. She would rather die clearly and secretly steal her life. Hiding and hiding is not a human duty.
Day and night, stubbornly arrived in Luocheng on the third day, but did not find Hu Laohan. Ask anyone, no one knows. Just when Stubborn didn't know how to do it, someone who had business dealings with Hu Laohan sent him a letter. In the letter, Hu Laohan wrote it to Dou San. The letter said that he already knows everything about his family. Although Stubborn has killed his whole family, young and old, his wife made a mistake first. Although his family are foxes, when he left home, he repeatedly told his wife to get along well with people, yet his wife still did things she shouldn't have done, and the family's extinction was due to her own fault. Of course, he also complained that he should not be impulsive and hurt his wife and niece. Hu Laohan told Stubborn 3 that if he says he doesn't hate Stubborn 3 is a lie, but when his grievances are rewarded, he won't go looking for trouble; he just wants to think twice after Stubborn 3; he doesn't want to use his enthusiasm to do anything foolish. At the same time, he also told Stubborn 3 that he had given up his business, cleaned up his family's ashes, hid himself deep in the mountains, overworked his family, and has since stopped getting involved in the world.
After stubbornly reading Hu Laohan's letter, he was deeply remorseful. He went home and had a serious illness, placed a fox spirit medal at home, and prayed in the morning and evening. At the same time, they spread good relationships and always did good deeds until they were 90 years old, and ended without incident.
