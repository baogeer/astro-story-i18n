 A talented woman from Shudi, and by the Song Dynasty, there was another Miss Su. It can be said that the spiritual spirit of Sichuan in the Song Dynasty came to an end with the Su clan. Xiaomei Su's father, Su Xun, and her older brothers, Su Yi, and Su Ze were all highly talented, so called “one father and son, three lyricists, and eight thousand autumn articles.”
At first glance, Xiaomei Su is not fat or thin. She has thin lips, a round face, big slouchy eyes, and a tall forehead and prominent jaws. Since she was a child, she has loved fighting with her two older brothers. In particular, her older brother Su Shi has a beard and a fat belly. He wears clothes with wide sleeves and is raunchy, unpretentious, and the object of her arguments, so they fight endlessly at home all day long. One day, Su Dongpo made fun of her sister's appearance, describing her sister's bulging forehead and concave eyes as:
If you don't attend class, take three or five steps; go to the front of the painting hall first;
It was so hard to wipe away my tears a few times; I was left with two big springs.
Xiaomei smiled and immediately turned back her lips:
A bunch of mourning grass comes out of my lips, and my hair is orange;
There was nowhere to be found during the altercation, and I heard that there was a rumor going around.
This poem ridicules Su Shi's unrepaired, unruly beard. The girl was most afraid that others would reveal her weaknesses in appearance. Xiaomei Su had a bulging forehead and eye sockets, and was ridiculed by Su Shi. Xiaomei said that Su Shi's beard didn't seem to have caught the pain. She felt that she didn't take advantage. Furthermore, she found that her brother had a flat forehead and no sense of exuberance. Another horse face, one foot long. The two eyes were far apart. The whole thing was that her facial features were out of proportion. Immediately, she worked hard to take up another poem:
The sky is 3,000 wide and the sky is three thousand, and you can see the double eyebrow of Yunhan from afar;
A drop of acacia last year, but I still can't shed a tear on my cheeks.
As soon as Su Shi heard it, she smiled so much that she patted her sister's head. As the brothers and sisters in the Su family proverb, it can be said that there are no taboos; they are often pun intended.
