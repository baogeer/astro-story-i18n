 Dingzhou City in Hebei Province not only preserves cultural relics and monuments such as Xuelang Stone and Dongpo Huai, but has also been singing the local opera “Dingzhou Yangge”. These are all related to the Northern Song writer, calligrapher and painter Su Dongpo.
Su Dongpo, the master Dongpo Ju, was born in Meishan County, Sichuan, and was promoted during the Jia You years. During the Song dynasty, he wrote a book against Wang Anshi's change of law and was criticized. Later, he dared to speak bluntly about advising the government because he was “stubborn in love, too bright in black and white,” and in the 8th year of Yu Yuanyou was also relegated from Shang Sho Shu of Dingzhou (the history of assassination). At that time, Dingzhou was bordered by Khitan in the north, and was a major border control center of the Song dynasty. After Su Dongpo took office, he first cleaned up military discipline and built an archery club to boost morale. Although he only worked in Dingzhou for eight months, he did a lot of good deeds to benefit the country and the people, and it is still a good story.
In Dingzhou, Su Dongpo is busy monitoring the public situation. One day, he visited Suquan, Xipo, Onisho, and Konisho villages in the northern part of Dingzhou City. He saw that these places were low-lying, swampy, and overgrown weeds covered the fertile soil. As a result, he thought that his hometown, Meishan, Sichuan, which is rich in rice production, would definitely turn Dingzhou into a prosperous place if it were to “move south to the north”. As a result, he ordered a special person to bring rice seeds to the south, and personally taught farmers techniques for planting, cultivating and managing rice, opening up a field with excellent rice grains in the past.
After a few days, Su Dongpo visited the countryside and passed by here again. He heard the farmers transplanting seedlings singing: “Jiaozi bit the mackerel and drilled; it was difficult to transplant the seedlings. Seeing that the scarce water had turned into a rice and grain river. I can't stop singing rice transplanting songs, and my heart is so happy and sweet.” The vivid and simple lyrics and lively and beautiful tunes attracted this great writer who is both good at poetry and painting, and also proficient in sound rhythm. As a result, he recorded the song, sorted it out a bit, and named it “Rice Transplantation Song.” Since then, the song of transplanting seedlings has continued to be sung among men, women, and children in northern Dingzhou. Later, Su Dongpo wrote a few more “Rice Transplanting Song” lyrics, depicting the popularity of Yangge in the Dingzhou area back then, using the phrase “Where White Cranes Fly Over the Water, Rice Paddies Go for Thousands of Miles”. “Rice Planting Song” began as a folk song and dance, and later developed into the “Dingzhou Yangge”, a local opera from Hebei with characters and plot.
In Dingzhou, in addition to encouraging farmers to improve soil and treat deserted beaches, Su Dongpo also planted trees and afforestation extensively. He is very fond of the large northern acacia trees. This tree is sturdy and has lush foliage. The yellow flowers are fragrant and pleasant in summer, and they are resistant to severe cold, wind, and snow. Su Dongpo once planted two trees in his “Xuelangzhai” yard in Dingzhou. Although this tree has been around for a thousand years, it is still full of leaves. The side branches of the tree on the east side stretch to the sides, like a phoenix spreading its wings; the tree on the west side is tall and tall, like a dragon traveling through the sky, so it is named “Dragon” and “Phoenix Dance”, making it one of the major sights in Dingzhou today. Now, after a thousand years of ups and downs, “Dragon Dragon” has a “dragon bell” and “dancing like a green onion.” A “dragon well” was also dug under the tree, just to place the wheel on the branch.
As for the “Xuelangzhai” built by Su Dongpo in Dingzhou, it is an even more famous sight. It is the former residence of Su Dongpo. It has the simplicity and depth of northern architecture; it also has the exquisite and elegant Jiangnan garden architecture, which is a representative work of the Northern Song dynasty.
