 Su Yi, a famous writer from the Northern Song Dynasty (960-1127), lives in Dongpo. A native of Meishan, Meizhou (present-day Meixian, Sichuan). Born in the 3rd year of Song Renzong Jiahu (1036), he died in the first year of Jingguo during the founding of Song Huizong (1101). He died at the age of 66. Su Shi is versatile and very famous in ancient China. In terms of prose, he was a member of the Tang and Song dynasties; in the lyric world, he was the leader of the liberal arts school. In terms of calligraphy, he was one of the four famous calligraphers of the Song Dynasty; in terms of painting, he was both a painter and the founder of literary painting theory. His literary painting theory had a profound influence on the development of Chinese painting after the Song Dynasty.
However, Su Shi's life was full of hardships. From when he was 26, he was bombarded, persecuted, imprisoned, and banished in the whirlpool of party disputes for 40 years, and finally relegated to present-day Hainan Island. Hardly, Song Huizong was pardoned when he came to power (1101), and ordered to return to the capital Bianliang (present-day Kaifeng) to prepare for heavy responsibilities. However, since it was in the middle of summer, he was on the road for several days. He was overworked. He was also a 66-year-old elderly man. He was frail. When he arrived in Changzhou, Jiangsu, he eventually fell ill due to heat stroke, and soon died in Changzhou.
During his time as an official, Su Shi did a lot of good deeds for the common people, and it has always been widely spread among the public. Writing and painting Bai Tuanfan's need to save people is a prominent example. According to He Yuan's “Chronicles of Chunzhu”, when Su Shi was demoted as an official in Hangzhou, a silk silk merchant went to court one day to sue a fan craftsman who owed him 20,000 yen for not paying back. Su Shi sent a public official to summon the fan maker to stand trial in court. The fan maker knelt down and said: My family has been making fans for generations. My father died not long ago. Since the beginning of spring this year, the ground in Hangzhou has been overcast and rainy for several days, and the weather has been cold. No one has bought fans. I haven't been able to pay back my bills for a while; it's not my intention to not pay back my debts. After listening to it, Su Shi felt worthy of sympathy. She felt that the case was difficult to handle, and it took half a day to figure out a solution. Say to the fan seller, “Get your fan quickly, I'll help you sell it. The man hurriedly went home and brought fans. Su Shi picked 20 white balls with silk fans, picked up a sharp-shaped brush, and painted dead wood, bamboo and stone with cursive inscriptions on the fan, and finished in a while. Then they handed it over to the fan maker and said, “Hurry up and sell it outside the government gate, and return it to others immediately after exchanging money. The fan maker took over the fans and proclaimed thanks. As soon as they left the house, they were surrounded by fans who bought them. You scrambled with me to grab them. The 20 fans were sold out quickly. Selling one thousand or twenty for just enough money to pay off the bill. Many people in Hangzhou were moved to tears by Su Shi's move, praising the county governor Su Shi for being a clean official. The fan maker's whole family is even more grateful.
Although Su Shi's paintings and calligraphy are very valuable, he never made money from them. At the time, people praised him for his good books and did not sell them. On the contrary, quite a few people asked him to write and draw. As long as he was happy, he waved on the spot and generously gifted them. When Su Shi was demoted to Guangzhou, there was a monk in the south named Qi Shun who walked a few months to Guangzhou to write in Dongpo. Su Shi was relegated to the border. Rarely did anyone dare to deal with him. Seeing Qi Shun's sincerity, they were very happy. They wrote several articles at once for a gift, and the two have become close friends ever since.
Not many of Su Shi's paintings and calligraphy works have been circulated because some were burnt down during partisan struggles. His paintings are even less common. The only real version of his painting that can be seen now is a wooden and stone drawing a large stone, pressing a dry curved tree. There is a bunch of grass growing next to the root of the tree, and a few bushes of tender bamboo growing in the crevices of the stones. This painting has inscriptions by contemporaries Liu Liangzuo and Mi Yi, so I know it was painted by Su Shi. Regarding the meaning of this painting, according to records relating to Su Shi's friends Huang Tingjian and Mi Yi, it is said that he had a sense of depression in his heart, that is, he was unhappy and deliberately painted dead wood strange and strange to vent his anger. This statement is probably credible.
