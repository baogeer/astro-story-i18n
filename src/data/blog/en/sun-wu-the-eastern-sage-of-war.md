 Sun Wu (c. 551—?) , famous general Wu Guo and great military theorist during the Spring and Autumn period, Zi Changqing, a native of Qi Guo Le'an (present-day Huimin, Shandong). His great-grandfather and grandfather were all famous generals from Qi and had great success in internal and foreign wars. Inspired by his family studies, Sun Wu loved the art of war since childhood, and was eager to explore ways to win the war in order to prepare for the future to take the stage, gather troops in the field, and carry out an earth-shattering career on the war stage. Unfortunately, civil unrest continued in the country at the time, and several large families were constantly fighting for power and profit. Sun Wu had no intention of getting involved in needless family struggles. His family moved to Wu Guo in the south, where he devoted himself to farming and seclusion, and sought new opportunities for development.
At that time, Wu Wang Helu had just taken the throne. He wanted to expand his military arsenal and establish a hegemonic power; he urgently needed the talent of a general to join the army. Sun Wusui was recommended to enter the palace by his friend Wu Zixu, who was Minister Wu Guo at the time, and presented 13 articles on the Art of War he wrote to Wu Wang. Wu Wang finished reading these 13 articles on Art of War one by one, but he didn't know if Sun Wu could apply these theories to actual combat, so he asked him, “I've read all 13 of your Art of War articles. Can you give commanding the team a try?” Sun Wu answered, “Yes.” Wu Wang also asked, “Can women try it?” Sun Wu replied, “Yes.” As a result, Wu Wang sent a total of 180 beautiful women from the palace to be performed by Sun Wu.
Sun Wu divided the 180 royal ladies into two teams, with Wu Wang's beloved concubines as captains of the two teams, and ordered everyone to carry spears. I asked them, “Do you all know your front heart, left and right hands, and back? The ladies answered, “I know.” Sun Wu also explained, “To move forward is to look in the direction of the previous heart; to the left; to the right, to the right; to the back, to the back.” The ladies answered, “Yes.” Sun Wu ordered people to set up axes and axes for law enforcement, repeatedly reaffirm military law, and then beat the drums and ordered to go to the left. However, when the palace ladies heard the sound of drums, they thought it was so fun that they all laughed. Sun Wu said, “My regulations are unclear. Your military orders are not familiar with military law; the general is wrong.” As a result, three orders were repeated and the drumming order was issued. The ladies were still laughing. Sun Wu said, “The regulations are unclear, and the military order is not familiar with military law; it is the general's fault; since it has been explained over and over again, they still don't carry out the order, then it is the fault of the lower-level non-commissioned officer.” He then ordered the beheading of the two captains. Wu Wang saw that Sun Wu wanted to kill his beloved wife and hurriedly sent someone to pass on his life, saying, “I already know that generals are good at using military forces. I don't have these two sweethearts, and I can't even eat a meal. Please ask the General not to kill them.” Sun Wu flatly refused, “Now that you have been ordered to be a general, your life is unbearable in the military.” Order an attack to be executed. Next, they appointed the court lady, who was in line for the two lines, and ordered to hit the drums again. Now, the palace ladies knelt back and forth, in accordance with the rules and requirements. Sun Wu sent someone to report to Wu Wang, “The team is well trained, please come and check it out! This kind of team, no matter how the king uses it, is OK even if they go to the soup and go to the fire.” Wu Wang lost his beloved wife. He was so sad that he didn't want to continue watching. Sun Wu said, “It seems that Wu Wang just loves the art of war phrase and doesn't really want to use it.” Wu Wang was not happy, but as a result, he learned that Sun Wu was a military genius who could both write and write stories, and finally appointed Sun Wu as general, making him train day and night to prepare for the battle.
In 506 BC, preparations for the Wu Guo-Chu War were completed. Wu Wang then worshipped Sun Wu as the lead general, led 30,000 elite soldiers, and advanced westward through Huai Shui. When he arrived in Huaiyuan (present-day northwest Huangzhou, Henan), Sun Wu suddenly landed in his boat. With 3,500 elite soldiers as his forerunner, he quickly passed through the three dangerous barriers of the Grand Tunnel, Direct Road, and Mingzhu in northern Chu (present-day south of Xinyang, Henan), and went straight deep into Chu. The Chu army was caught off guard and forced to respond hastily in Baiju (present-day Macheng, Hubei). The Chu army was defeated, and Wu Jun took advantage of the victory in the pursuit. Using tactics such as “half way to strike”, they marched 700 miles in 11 days, fought five battles, and conquered Chudu Yingdu in one fell swoop. King Chu Zhao abandoned the city and fled south in a hurry. The Battle of Baju became a glorious example of war history in the history of the Chinese War, where 30,000 people defeated 200,000 people quickly won. During the Warring States period, military chief Lieutenant Wei Zi praised, “There are 30,000 people, but who is the wrong person in the world? It's also called Takeko.”
In 484 BC, Sun Wu once again showed his outstanding military prowess, assisting Wang Fu Wu in defeating Qi in the Battle of Ailing, thus making Wu Guo strong and replacing Jin as hegemon in the Huangchi League two years later. Sima Qian pointed out in “Historical Records: The Biography of Sun Tzu Wu Qi”: “(Wu Guo) broke out strong in the west and entered the army; Northern Wei Qi and Jin are famous princes; Sun Tzu is powerful!”
The fate of Sun Wu in the future is still a mystery. One way of saying is that after Sun Wu helped Wu Wangfu establish his hegemony, he was well aware of the principle of “all the birds die, the good bow is hidden; the cunning rabbit dies, the dog cooks”, so he retreated to Lin Quan and worked as a writer for the rest of his life. Another explanation is that he returned to Qi and lived in seclusion in his hometown to teach disciples.
Sun Wu was revered by his descendants as the “Soldier of the Soldier,” the “Father of the Soldier Family,” and the “Master of the Soldier Family.” In addition to his outstanding military exploits, the main thing is that he left to future generations an immortal military masterpiece, “Sun Tzu's Art of War.” The book has 13 chapters and a total of over 5,000 words. But in just a few thousand words, the package contains a vast and profound theoretical system and very rich ideological content. It has had a huge and profound impact on the development of Chinese military academia. Successive military scientists and military figures have all drawn nourishment from it to guide warfare practices and develop military theory. Cao Cao, a famous politician and military man during the Three Kingdoms period, was the first to make a systematic commentary on “Sun Tzu's Art of War,” opening a convenient door for future generations to study and apply “Sun Tzu's Art of War.” “Sun Tzu's Art of War” has also been famous all over the world for a long time. It was introduced to Japan in the 8th century and Europe in the 18th century. It has now been translated into 29 languages and is widely distributed around the world. The famous British military theorist Liddell Hart revealed that the views expressed in his military works can actually be found in “Sun Tzu's Art of War” 2,500 years ago. He is also really interested in Sun Wu and his books. Not only did he preface the English translation of “Sun Tzu's Art of War,” but he also quoted Sun Wu's motto extensively in front of his famous work “The Theory of Strategy.” In the 1991 Gulf War, US Marine Corps officers were all ordered to carry a copy of “Sun Tzu's Art of War” to read on the battlefield.
