Zhao Yutian is a skilled craftsman from Mudan Township in Caozhou. He has a unique habit in his life, and he loves flowers like his life. I work in the garden during the day and often forget to eat. I worked tirelessly throughout the year, and my body gradually lost weight. My old partner feels sorry for him and sends him a bowl of egg soup every day to nourish his body. But he carried his old partner and poured egg soup on peony roots. After a while, the peonies that were watered grew very strong. Everyone felt strange when they saw it, and asked why. As soon as Tamada mentioned that they would pour egg soup, people suddenly realized it.
Tamada is particularly fond of these irrigated peonies, because it also includes the hard work of an old partner. Tamada was afraid of being stolen by idle scoundrels, so one day he invited them to drink at the flower parlor. After three rounds of drinking, Tamada said, asking everyone to help him to be optimistic about these peonies. Taking advantage of the booze, the guest said, “I can guarantee that your peonies are fine. With us here, no kid would dare touch a single hair!” Coincidentally, since Tamada wasn't thoughtful, two little scoundrels were left out. At this point, they were able to clearly hear from the window, saying that Tamada underestimated people, and the drinking guys were also too aggressive, so they decided to show them some colors.
On a dark night when they couldn't see their five fingers, these two scoundrels took a toxic hand and removed all 40 peonies that Tamada had poured with egg soup with an iron shovel.
Early the next morning, Tamada went to the garden and was taken aback. He shouted “ah-oh”. He passed out and vomited blood in his mouth. There were quite a few people involved. There was no soup for three days, and since then I have been sick.
The Peony Fairy was moved by Zhao Yutian's sincere love for peonies. Fairy Peony immediately made the fairy and fairy children join forces to save it. After watering with manna, the fairy medicine Miaodan was also widely applied. In the spring of the following year, the peonies that had been uprooted sprout again and bloomed as before. When Tamada heard about this incident, he was taken aback; he had no illnesses. People couldn't help but be amazed.
