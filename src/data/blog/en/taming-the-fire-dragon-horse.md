 Batu's horse has repeatedly won gold and won the championship in Nadamu, and his own horse training sutra has also been treasured by the Wranglers and is famous both inside and outside the prairie.
After the tea was over, the crowd sat down in groups. Batu “sucked” a sip of milk tea. Looking around a bronzed face, they shook their lips and applauded, and spoke in a loud voice.
Two-year-old cubs are fed in groups. The food trough needs to be raised two times a month, so horses can hold their heads high and keep their necks sharp. The horse ring is more particular. Horse manure cannot be removed during the day. Even if you let the horse stand on it to eat grass, the horseshoe can grow plump and round; otherwise, it will grow into a flaky horseshoe, and the horse will not be able to jump high and far. Rest at night and clean up horse manure so that the horse's fur is smooth, spotless, bright and odorless. Everyone nodded. Batu's loud voice added another layer to the onlookers. Some people have been drinking hot milk tea a long time ago, and Batu “sucks” it again, then goes on.
Horses must practice running until they are three years old. Choose to level the grassland, train the horse to control the reins, don't be slow, and let the horse find the right steps. After a month of training like this, you need to set up rafters, not far, not close, and keep your pace fixed. Horse trainers ride over horses and are afraid to kick their legs. Their front hooves are bent deep and close to their stomach, and their rear hooves naturally follow. After a while, horses fixed this kind of step. Horses domesticated in this way run smoothly and run in two straight lines with their four hooves. If riders look back at hoof flowers, Senrima must have thirteen hoof flowers.
Gee, gee! Batu smiles every time he talks about it. Thinking of me, Senri Prairie, there isn't a single good horse in the Thousand Mile Grassland! The crowd also shook their heads and sighed in unison. Batu has been training horses for decades, and he has never seen a thousand mile horses with thirteen hoof flowers. Nine flowers at most! Ahhh — nine flowers!
Who knows, in March of that year, Batu Zhen discovered a good horse from a thousand miles.
When Batu and the herders arrived at the ranch early in the morning to train horses, they saw the horses gushing out of the horizon. The sun had just broken free from the grass sea, and the golden light covered Gunma with a coat of coat. In the midst of thousands of horses, I saw fire dragons all over their bodies charcoal-red and long manes. They took the lead and rushed to the ranch. Batu was pleasantly surprised and applauded: a good horse, a great horse, truly a thousand miles away!
Batu learned from the horse population that Fire Dragon Foals are wild horses. After a night of storms, they mixed into the horse herd and took the lead after a few days.
The fire dragon foal is clearly past the age of domestication. Batu's heart beat faster, took a close look at the dragon foal, and was overjoyed. The Fire Dragon Horse has a straight waist, thin legs, soft muscles, and is unusually handsome. It really is a natural talisman! It seems that the appearance of the dragon foal can make up for many years of regret.
To train a horse, you must first hang a horse and be mature. First, put the horse in a two-foot high fence; let's get hungry. Horses are hungry for a day. Horse trainers carry carrots in one hand and cages in the other. Most horses are caged when they eat radishes, and are taken away after they are ripe. There are also those that don't allow people to wear hats, but due to hunger and lack of health, they were held tight by a few Mongolian Han and then tied the reins. Some riders leaped on horseback long ago and turned around in circles amidst the laughter of the crowd. In an instant, I ran out of horsepower and had to gently stick out my lips to the rider.
Batu approached the Fire Dragon Foal. The Fire Dragon Foal took small steps in an attempt to get close and eat the radish. He saw Batu hand over his horse's head. Suddenly, his ears were tilted, and the two front hooves also stood straight up, so frightened that Batu avoided it from afar. The riders tried to catch the dragon foal. The fire dragon was whirling, and its mane fluttered. It swirled and fell into the air for a few times. The field was dusty, and several riders retreated. For a moment, the crowd drank and wrestled outside the fence. Every time Batu falls down, he sings a challenge song and dances the hawk dance steps... Dragon Foal actually stopped his impatience, and Batu, who had won, gradually softened his gaze.
On the third day, Batu approached the fire dragon foal. After eating a few bites of radish, he took the initiative to stick his head into the cage, lick the back of Batu's hand, and rubbed Batu's bare face with his furry face... Batu jumped on the back of the horse and whistled. Several riders outside the fence agreed and sped fast. The fire dragon and foal chased furiously. However, after running for a few miles, I was out of breath, and apparently I was left behind by another rider. Ba Tu looked happy, and continued to whip the horse. The fire dragon raised its head and raised its long mane upside down like a flag. It seemed as if strength was injected into the body out of thin air. A few leaps of leaps actually rushed to the front of the horse team, and a huge column of smoke was thrown behind it. The hooves of a fire dragon are very rhythmic. If a strong man hits the drums, it's like a lady playing the piano. Batu bathed in the spring breeze, like a sailing boat. Looking back at hoof flowers, there were actually 13 flowers blooming...
Overjoyed, Batu reached the finish line, rolled off his horse, trembled and caressed the dragon's forehead. However, the dragon foal softened and fell to the ground. Bat Tu was shocked. When he saw blood on the horn of the dragon's mouth, the grass turned red in an instant, and he couldn't live when he saw it.
According to Batu Fangfang, the Fire Dragon was hungry for three days and had no physical strength. Only by cutting through his blood vessels did he breathe easily and compete for first place. Batu knelt down on both knees and tearfully communicated. Fire Dragon Foal treated Batu as a friend, but no one could have anticipated that this game was just Batu's arrogance to kill him.
Batu buried a fire dragon foal and will no longer train horses. Instead, his horse training sutras are still being handed down in the prairie.
