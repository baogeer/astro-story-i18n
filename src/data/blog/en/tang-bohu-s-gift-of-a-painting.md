 In the Ming Dynasty, Hushu Town in Jiangning had 13 doors. The doors all had wooden fence switches, and five additional buildings were built: one on the east, west, and north, and two on the south side, all built on top of the fence gate. Pedestrians come in and out during the day, and there are ground guards to watch at night. As soon as the second day passes, 13 doors are closed and locked, and they are not allowed to pass through.
The east gate is called Lion's Gate. One day, after two more knocks, a guest from outside the door called for the door. As soon as Lao Gengfu watched the moonlight, he was a literate scholar. He thought in his heart: Maybe they came from a long distance, so it's convenient for him, so he went downstairs and opened the fence door to let him in. Gengfu asked his husband, “Which one are you looking for?” Xiang Gong said back, “I visited this place, and I don't recognize anyone.” Gengfu is nice, and he said, “It's in the middle of the night. My husband doesn't like to be dirty, so just stay upstairs and take shelter from the wind.” My husband had nowhere else to go, so I had to go upstairs with him. At first glance, even though the building is small, there aren't many things. I slept on the floor, but there was a pot of fire. A copper teapot was hanging from the fire, and there was a smell of tea in the heater. After wiping the table and bench with his apron, Gengfu asked the students to sit down and take a break. Gengfu brought another cup, wiped it clean, and filled it with wine from the side of the fire. He also pulled out a handful of peanuts and laughed, “Cousin, let's take some alcohol to relieve the chill.” The scholar was already hungry and was unkind. He picked up the cup and drank it. He ate peanuts with great pleasure. He even said, “Good wine, good wine, good dish!”
The scholar had enough to eat and drink. He is in great spirits. He took out the four treasures he carried with him and said to Gengfu, “Old people, it's rare for you to take such good care. I'll send you a picture. Show your feelings!” Gengfu was very happy to hear it. He hurriedly turned on the oil lamp and watched his paintings. When I saw a sack of paper, a brush, and three and four strokes, I drew it into a big tree. The tree had five branches, a large nest in the middle of the tree, and five painted eyebrows sticking out of the nest. Xiang Gong said to Gengfu, “With this painting, you won't even need to use incense to time the day when you look at it in the future.” Gengfu couldn't understand it. The scholar pointed to the five thrush in the painting and said, “Every time I get to a change, one of the thrushes in the nest flies onto a branch, and it makes a noise as soon as it flies. After the five thrushes flew out, it was five more days. Afterwards, they will fly back to their nest. During the day, you can put your paintings away, hang them up at night, and sleep without worry; you can't go wrong!” Old Gengfu didn't talk about it; he was puzzled: “Young and light, it sounds better to speak than sing.” Masanobu took the painting with suspicion. Early the next morning, my father-in-law left, and only then did Gengfu remember to ask him about his last name. The scholar smiled and said, “My name is Tang Bohu.” By the time Lao Gengfu came back to his senses and went after him, Tang Bohu was out of his shadow.
That evening, after dinner, Lao Gengfu opened the painting. He had just hung it on the wall. There was already a painting with an eyebrow resting on a branch. The second one just arrived, and the second one crinkled up on the branch. Just like that, three changes, four changes; five times, the five eyebrows all flew out of the nest before and after, making people unbelievable. Old Gengfu couldn't help laughing.
