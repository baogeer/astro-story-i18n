 1. The marriage of Zheng Banqiao, a talented son in the Qing Dynasty:
Zheng Banqiao's literature prevailed. Unfortunately, he was poor. Walking to the front of the family one day, he was surprised that the couplets in front of the door were his own poems. Zheng Sheng asked the head of the household, Mrs. Rao, to find out the truth. Mrs. Rao said that she loved Zheng Banqiao's works. Mrs. Rao immediately called out her five daughters and assigned her to Zheng Banqiao. Zheng Banqiao later entered high school, and the couple also loved each other for the rest of their lives.
2. Mrs. Zheng Banqiao's “Everyone has their own body!”
In the Qing Dynasty, Zheng Banqiao was very diligent in copying the law posts of various families. One night, he had a dream where he was also posting, pointing east to west. In his dream, he mistakenly placed his finger on his wife's back.
The lady woke up. In her sleep, Zheng Banqiao heard the lady whisper, “Everyone has their own body!” Zheng Itabashi suddenly woke up, destroyed all the law books, and created one for himself.

4. Don't be casual about Zheng Banqiao's small places
Zheng Banqiao's characters are very valuable. Booksellers often steal his drafts, frame them, and sell them for money. Once, Zheng Banqiao wrote the words “You must not urinate anywhere” on purpose. He thought this time he wouldn't be sold for money. After a few days, Zheng Banqiao saw his words in a painting and calligraphy store, but the content was changed to “Don't be casual in small places.”
5. Bone-in warmth
When it comes to Itabashi's way of living in the world, the word “honest” can be summed up. He knows forgiveness and is very humane. In some details of his life, he can show a full sense of humanity. As a poem is written, he never reveals his love between the lines. He is the epitome of a Chinese book reader. He missed the grace of his nanny, the love of his mother-in-law, the love of his uncles and nephews, the morality of his friends, and his own hardships. He wrote the song “Seven Songs”. It was casually vernacular, with no fuss, and every word was deeply moving. Remembering his childhood companion (Sister Wang, a well-behaved little girl in the neighborhood), he gave him a gift with the words “Congratulations to the Bridegroom,” and made a few faint sketches. The kid's humble attitude, that is, it jumped on paper, leaving a sweet aftertaste.
Even though he is rich, he still hasn't forgotten his poor past. He has many questions and is extremely considerate. This is expressed more vividly in his family books. The idea of how to receive a poor petitioner: first invite them to eat a bowl of hot porridge and then ask them what they want; how to be kind to the child servant: think about other children, just like your own child; and even be willing to buy a wasteland that no one wants in order to preserve an orphan tomb where no one wants, so that he can be with the underground. This kind of idea is a great demonstration of kindness. No matter what he thinks, he can always show his honesty. In a society where hypocritical taxation is used, it is rare to be honest.
6. Gong Jie's Conduct

If fame and fortune go hand in hand, you will no longer think about the suffering of the people. The so-called “Demon County Order” can do less evil; it is already rare. However, Itabashi went deep into the public to understand suffering, contrary to the customs of the government, and went deep into the public; he was unpretentious. It's all sickening when you read it. These words of blood and tears pour out their feelings for the little people who are suffering without warning. Despite all his efforts, he is concerned about the citizens of the prefecture, but he is still fearful. 7. Itabashi Accidental Marriage Case
When he was in his hometown, he did some good things for the local people. For example, when he served as Zhi Xian in Wei County, there was a famine, so he started a big job, causing the big giants to cook porridge to save their stomachs. Many people survived. Rumor has it that Zheng Banqiao handled a marriage fraud case while serving as a county commissioner in Weixian County. Only then did Qiong Xiu accuse her father-in-law of not keeping his promise and relied on the marriage for no reason.
Itabashi knew in advance that Pou Xiucai's father-in-law was a large landowner. He ordered Pou Shucai to stay in the government office and sent someone to summon the landlord and his daughter to the church. So, Itabashi asked, “Why are you getting married?” The financial owner answered, “I can't support my daughter because I'm poor; they shouldn't live together.” Itabashi said, “I see, it seems like your daughter really shouldn't have married a poor talent. However, you also have to think about your son-in-law. Let's do it! You paid a thousand or two gold, and I'll help you solve this problem and guarantee your satisfaction.”
The financial owner was very happy to hear that Zheng Banqiao wanted to help him break his daughter's marriage contract with a poor talent. He paid his bills on time. Itabashi said, “Your daughter always has to get married sooner or later. Why don't I find you a son-in-law to ride the dragon. Even if this thousand and two gold is dowry money, what do you think?” After listening, the rich man smiled and even nodded and said, “Of course it's fine! Of course it's good!”
While they were talking, Itabashi had already spread the word about his poor talent, and immediately ordered him to have a family with the rich owner's daughter. Afterwards, they both went away with the money. The rich owner was astonished and overwhelmed. In the end, unable to cry and laugh, he left the hall.
8. Itabashi Myobi Battle Salt Shop
One day, a salt dealer took a hawker selling salt without permission to the county government office and ordered the prefectural governor Itabashi to be severely punished. When Itabashi saw the hawker, who was shabby and skinny, he knew that his life forced him to do so. As a result, he felt compassionate and wanted to make fun of this salt dealer. He said to the salt merchant, “This hawker should be punished more severely. I'm ready to put shackles on him and stand on the streets, OK?” The salt merchant smiled and said, “OK! OK!”
Itabashi immediately brought in some reeds, used fine pieces of wood to secure them, carefully secured them, and dug a large round hole in the middle. He also drew many bamboo and orchids, wrote inscriptions, and affixed them to reed shackles. Then put the hawker around his neck and stand in front of the salt merchant to show off. Itabashiko is famous all over the world for her paintings of bamboo and orchids, known for her poetry, books, and paintings. Many people want to witness it and are even more eager to get his ink treasure. Therefore, when hawkers wear shackles to the first stop in front of Yanshang Gate, they immediately attract many onlookers. The entire county town is full of excitement. The salt merchant's door is blocked and water cannot drain through, and the business cannot be carried out at all. The salt merchant had no choice but to go to the prefectural office to beg Itabashi to release the hawker.
9. Bamboo shadow wall
He is extremely talented, and his paintings are even more famous; many people ask for them but can't get them. It is said that Zheng Banqiao's paintings are very evocative and can make the things in the paintings come true. In other words, Zheng Banqiao has a friend who has built a new wall at home. He always asks Zheng Banqiao to paint a picture on the wall. Unfortunately, Zheng Banqiao is always busy and has no time.
At one point, this friend invited Zheng Banqiao and some friends to drink at home. Half of the drink was over at the dinner. The host was in front of everyone; he had to ask Zheng Banqiao to paint a picture on the wall. When Zheng Banqiao saw that he couldn't get rid of it, he said, “OK, polish the ink!” The owner hurriedly brought his son an ink. Zheng Banqiao took a look and said, “No, there are too few. At the end of the day, you have to polish half a small pot of ink.” As soon as everyone heard, with so much ink, would it be necessary to paint the entire wall black? While the owner had doubts, he still quickly asked his son to bring half a small pot of ink. At this point, Zheng Banqiao was already drunk and shaking. As soon as he walked to the front of the wall, applied his hand to the basin, applied a few strokes, and picked up the entire pot, spilled all the ink inside on the wall, leaving a black crow. The owner wasn't happy anymore. Originally, he only wanted Zheng Banqiao to paint on the wall. One was the scenery, and the other was beautiful. Who knows the black crow is drawing and doesn't know what it is? It wasn't easy to get rid of, so I had to leave it behind and get annoyed myself. It just started raining heavily one day, and there was no stopping thunder in the sky. Coupled with lightning, it was amazing. After the rain cleared up, hundreds of sparrows actually died in front of this wall.
After some time, an old man came to the owner's door and took a close look at this wall. When the owner saw it, he was curious and asked, “What are you watching?” “This painting must have been painted by a famous person, right?” The owner was still angry and said, “What kind of celebrity was that? It was just a friend who rubbed it with his hands.” The old man asked, “What strange things have happened since this painting was made?” The owner answered, “There was a strange thing. There was heavy rain, thunder, and lightning, and then hundreds of dead sparrows were found in front of the wall.” The old man nodded and said, “This painting is really nice! The average person can't see that he is painting a bamboo forest. It's only when it thunderstorms and lightning shines that they can see that it's a bamboo forest. Sparrows treated it as a real bamboo forest and flew to shelter from the rain, so they hit the wall and died.”
