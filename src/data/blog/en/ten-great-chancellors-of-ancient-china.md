3 Li Si in the Qin dynasty
Lee Si is a controversial figure in Chinese history. But no matter what, he's still a celebrity. Li Si was once ostracized as an official in Qin, but he wrote down an anti-eviction order, which laid the theoretical foundation for the Qin dynasty to attract foreign talents. He proposed a strategy of close attack from far and wide, so that the Qin King had one inch of soil and one inch of land to unify the six countries. He used various methods to divide and dismantle the six countries. He proposed unifying words and measures... Of course, he was also a sinner, and later generations made comments about him, “there is room for death.”

6 Fang Xuanling of the Tang dynasty
When it comes to the Tang dynasty, many people think of Wei Zheng. In fact, Wei Zheng never became a minister. He was an adviser. 7 Wang Anshi of the Song dynasty
The Song dynasty was a dynasty with both internal worries and external problems. In the middle of the Northern Song dynasty, Wang Anshi tried to change the law, but there was a lot of resistance, and he failed. But there is no denying that he is indeed an insightful celebrity.
8 Yelu Chu Cai of the Yuan Dynasty
Yelu Chu Cai (119 - 244 AD) is the character Jin Qing, known as Zhan Ran Ju. Since they live in the Yuquan Mountain area, they are also known as Yuquan Jushi, a Khitan native. Yelu Chucai is a famous politician in our country. He has written many books, learned about astronomy, geography, legal experience, medicine, and exegesis, and is good at poetry. After Yan Jing was defeated by Meng soldiers, he was called to meet with Genghis Khan and stayed in North Korea as an adviser. After Yuan Taizong came to power, he served as the chief minister (prime minister) to help Mongolians manage the Yuan dynasty and made contributions. It also made a positive contribution to the Chineseization and civilization of the Mongolian Yuan.
9 Liu Ji in the Ming dynasty
That is the famous Liu Bowen. An outstanding military strategist and politician of the Ming dynasty, an outstanding writer and philosopher. Born at the end of the Yuan Dynasty, he was smart at an early age, and grew up with a lot of books, so his talents and knowledge grew greatly. With the will to save time and help the world, he entered the exam at the age of 23 and dedicated himself to his career. He later left office and went home. He was invited by Zhu Yuanzhang to make a comeback. Liu Ji made great contributions in the process of unifying China and creating the Ming dynasty.

Zhang Juzheng is also a very controversial person. He was also criticized a lot; even the praise was greater than the negative. As you can imagine, in the dynasty that was coming to an end in the Ming dynasty, it was indeed very difficult to be an official, and even harder to be a good official. It was under such circumstances that Zhang Juzheng became an official. He really brought vitality to the politics of the late Ming dynasty.
