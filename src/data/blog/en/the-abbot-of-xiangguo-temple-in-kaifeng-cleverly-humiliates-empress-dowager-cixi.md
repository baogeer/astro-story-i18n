 Empress Dowager Cixi had an extremely dishonorable escape period. During that time, not only was she forced to suffer from hunger, but she was also teased by monks. Faced with such “great shame and humiliation,” why never mentioned “revenge”?
In August 1900, the Eight-Nation Allied Forces captured Beijing. After Beijing had temporarily calmed down, Cixi, who had fled the West, left and returned. On the way, she had a whim to drop incense at Xiangguo Temple, an ancient temple in Bianliang.
The intention spread to Kaifeng, and the Kaifeng provincial government was in a hurry and immediately posted a notice: Whether poor or rich, they will pay two or two pieces of silver per person in preparation for the Cixi gift. This has hurt the people of the whole city. Zhi Qing, the elder of Kaifeng's Shokoku-ji Temple, asked the governor to give away the treasure of the town so that the people of the city would not invest, and the governor agreed.
After two days, Cixi's brigade entered Kaifeng City and headed straight for Xiangguo Temple. After Cixi took a seat, she heard that Zhiqing wanted to give her a treasure, and her old face, which was full of wrinkles, immediately blossomed with joy. Seeing two strong monks carrying a red-lacquered wooden barrel, Cixi was wondering. Zhi Qing walked up to open Huang Ling. It turned out to be a bucket full of loess, and there were a bunch of ginger shoots growing in the soil. Cixi was furious and shouted loudly: “Zhi Qing, what is this?” Without hurrying, Zhi Qing said, “Dear Lord Buddha, this is a bucket of Jiang Shan!” The homonym of “one bucket of Jiang Shan” is “all the rivers and mountains.” Zhi Qing mocked Cixi for losing power and humiliating the country, and betraying the country to seek prosperity, causing the river and mountains to shatter, and the people don't talk about their lives. Although Cixi was worried, she could only wear a smiley face and say, “This treasure is too heavy and inconvenient to carry. Stay at the temple and send someone to take it back to Beijing later.”
Cixi came to Daxiong Treasure Hall and saw a “Ancient Bian Famous Blue” plaque hanging high above the main hall. She sank down and pointed to the plaque and asked, “Are you guilty of Zhi Qing?” Zhi Qing pretended to be ignorant. Li Lianying immediately stepped forward and shouted, “You bald donkey are so bold, you dare to break the law! Come on, take down this bald donkey and ask Grandpa Lafayette to drop it off!”
Why is it taboo to say the words on the plaque? It turns out that in the Buddhist sutras, the place where the monks live is called “Jialan,” or “Blue” for short. “Ancient Bian Ming Lan” means “the famous temple in the ancient city of Bianliang.” Unbeknownst to me, Cixi's maiden name is Lan (Lan). According to feudal rules, any name of an emperor is not allowed to be used without permission by the public. If it is the same as the name, it must be replaced with another character. This is called taboo.
At this point, Cixi walked up to Zhi Qing and pretended to say, “This place is a sacred place for Buddha, and there are no killings in this palace. There is now a release pool in front of the main hall, so just drop in!” Zhi Qing didn't answer a word. After praying for the sky, he leaned into the pool. Cixi smiled slightly and was about to get up. Suddenly she saw that Zhi Qing had climbed out again. Cixi couldn't help but be furious. Zhi Xi walked in front of Cixi drenched in clear water and said, “The poor monk just walked over Nai Ho Bridge and was stopped back by someone.” Cixi sneered and asked, “Who is so bold. How dare you disobey the fate of this palace?” Zhi Qing replied, “Empress Empress Dowager. This person is the former emperor Qianlong. The title of this plaque was handwritten by him. It has nothing to do with the poor monk, so he called the poor monk back to Yangjiang.” Cixi was puzzled: “Why didn't the first emperor sign the money?” Zhi Qing replied: “Back then, when the first emperor came to Honji Temple, he wrote a plaque with his own title. It's not convenient to stay famous just because I'm visiting on Weibo.” Cixi turned her head and asked the Kaifeng Governor: “Is this true?” The governor fearfully replied, “This plaque is indeed the subject of the ancient emperor; it is recorded in Kaifeng Province's annals.”
Zhi Qing went on to say, “The ancient emperor also said, I don't know which of his descendants actually made jokes with their ancestors; they didn't even know the widow's imperial pen.” Cixi knew that Zhi Qing abused herself by her ancestors, but she had no words to answer, leaving her in a daze for a moment. Zhi Qing thought to herself: Today I tore Cixi's face hard, calmed the hate in my heart, and it's time for the show to end. He also said, “Just now, the emperor also ordered the young monk. It was not easy for the ancestors of the emperor to start a business. Cixi knew there was something in Zhi Zhi's words, but she had no choice but to go down the steps and say, “This palace will never live up to the expectations of the previous emperor.
