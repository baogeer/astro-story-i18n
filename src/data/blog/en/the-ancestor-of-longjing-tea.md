 Earlier, Longjing was a small, desolate village. There are about ten families scattered all over the mountain. People grow bamboo and trees in the mountains, and they grow six grains in the nearby mountains, and live a difficult life at the end of the year.
There is an old aunt living in a broken, ventilated cottage on the side of the village. The old aunt had no children or women; she was alone. She's older. She can't go up the mountain or go down the ground; she can only take care of the 18 tea trees at the back of the house. These tea trees were planted when her old partner was still alive, and it's been decades since then. Old tea trees lack work and are low on fertilizer, and very few new leaves are produced; only a few catties of old tea leaves can be harvested every year.
The old aunt is a kind person. She would rather live a hard life on her own. Every year, she always leaves some tea leaves, burns wok tea every day, and sets up two benches under the pergola at the entrance to quench their thirst when they go up and down the mountain.
One year on New Year's Eve, it snowed a lot, and the neighbors on the left and right houses all sold some New Year's goods to prepare for the New Year. My aunt's family is really poor, and the rice bowl is almost empty. Other than a few old tea women left in the pot, there's nothing else left. However, she still followed the old rules. She woke up early in the morning, grabbed the tea leaves in the wok, burnt out the fire, and sat in front of the stove to make tea. At this point, I overheard a “whoa” sound, and the cottage door was pushed open. An old man came in and snowflakes fell all over his body. The old aunt hurriedly stood up and said, “Big brother, it's windy and snowy on this mountain. Hurry up and sit in the house.”
The old man brushed off the snowflakes on his body, walked into the house, burned the fire in the stove, and said to the old aunt, “Aunt, what did you burn in the wok?”
“Burn tea in a wok!”
“Today is New Year's Eve, tomorrow is New Year's Eve. Everyone is busy cooking three sacrifices, how do you make tea?”
The old aunt sighed and said, “Hmm, I'm poor, and I can't afford the Three Sacrifice Gifts. I have to cook tea wok every day to make it easier for passers-by.”
The old man listened and laughed: “Not poor, not poor, you still have a baby at your door.”

After New Year's Eve and New Year's Eve, spring has arrived soon. This year, the 18 old tea trees at the back of the old aunt's house actually produced dense, bright green shoots. The harvested tea leaves are fine, tender, and fragrant.

Year by year, more and more, more and more prosperous. Later, tea trees were planted all over the mountains in the Longjing area.
“Longjing tea” is famous all over the world because the tea leaves produced in this region are fine, tender, and fragrant, and delicious when eaten.
