 There is a high mountain on the North Sea coast. Snow and ice linger on the mountain all year round, and the wind is raging.
A young swordsman lives in a remote cave at the foot of the mountain.
The young swordsman has been living here for seven years. He practiced martial arts with his master for the first four years. Since he had some martial arts foundation before worshiping his teacher, plus hard work, he improved a lot in martial arts over the past four years, and became Master's most proud disciple.
Just in the summer of the fourth year, Master summoned all his apprentices and told everyone that he had decided to return to a foreign country and no longer ask about martial arts in the Central Plains; they had also all successfully practiced martial arts and could go down the mountain. After much discussion, the apprentices packed up their luggage separately, calculated their plans after going down the mountain, then said goodbye to their master and went their separate ways. Only this young swordsman has long established the ambition of being an immature fighter. Therefore, he feels that his martial arts are still insufficient, and he is still unable to face the world under the mountain. He thought left and right. One evening after the crowd had left, he knelt down at the entrance of the cave where his master lived and asked to stay with his master and continue his practice.
His master didn't answer. The night slowly crept into the stone cave, and the silence continued for more than half of the night. When the sky was bright, Master finally sighed and said:
“Alas, I have been an apprentice in the Central Plains for decades, and have never found any talents to create. I wanted to go abroad. I never thought I'd be able to meet someone with a heart before I left. Well, I don't even need to take my sword scores and secret maps with me. You come in.”
So Master led him to the deepest part of the cave and pulled out an old cloth bag from a stone crevice. Master placed the cloth bag on the table and prayed solemnly for three years before handing over the cloth bag to him and told him:
“From today on, you are a 26th generation descendant of this family. This is Benmon Zhenshan's sword score, and it's never easy to show people. I think you have this wisdom and can teach it, so I'll leave it to you.
After I leave, you practice and use your talents for as little as two years or as many as four years, and you will definitely achieve results. As for the opposite side of burdens, it's a secret map of this ancestral heritage, so you should read it carefully. If you have this life, you can find an ancient sword if you can read secrets that no one generation has seen. It was an ancient god soldier. He cut gold and jade, and cut iron like clay, worthy of being handed down from generation to generation.”
When Master said this, he looked up at the sky before continuing:
“The time has come. Let's say goodbye to you and me. Please do your own thing. The future of this region depends on you.”
When Master finished speaking, he stood up and walked out of the cave without turning back and forth. The young swordsman was stunned; Master had already walked to the entrance of the cave. The young swordsman cried out for a hold, but Master's determined figure finally disappeared into the deepest darkness before dawn, leaving only the wind of chaos as the night intersected, and continued to brag about it.
For the next three years, the young swordsman strictly kept his teacher's training and practiced hard on the mountain alone. He gets up early every day at 5, climbs alone into the snow at the top of the mountain, meditates his luck, and jumps. In the morning, he walked ten miles to work for others in a village down the mountain, earn a meager salary, and maintain a minimum standard of living. After a simple lunch, he went back to the mountain and spent the whole afternoon concentrating on practicing sword fighting. At night, under the faint oil lamp in the cave, he read sword scores over and over again to figure out secret maps. Sometimes it's extremely cold on winter nights, and when the skins he hunted couldn't keep warm, he often yelled, stepped out of the hole, and practiced his martial arts skills over and over again in the midst of the wind and heavy snow. Every time he reached the end of his practice, he gradually forgot about the cold and reached a state of unity between heaven and man in this harsh land.
After three years like this, the young swordsmen are proficient in rehearsing all the moves in the sword score. When he showed up, the recruitment was consistent and all at once. He is confident that if they face an enemy, this endless sword force like the ocean of the Yangtze River will only be forced to defend closely at first; when suppressing the enemy to the top, the last move in the sword score is also just right. This trick is to take the lead and use the power of falling to point directly at the opponent's critical point. The other party cannot avoid it, and there is no other way to do anything but go to a hard fight. Resistance from the bottom up naturally takes a lot of losses, so even if your opponent is more powerful than yourself, it won't be difficult to win with a single hit.
While learning his sword skills, the young swordsman also had an epiphany about the secret map he had seen thousands of times. His bold assumption is that the secret picture is four small paintings that are not continuous on the top, bottom, left, and right. In fact, he talks about four things. If you reduce it to four sentences, it would be:
Catch the Moon in the Sea
Flowery bird language
Three or five nights
What does rabbit ear mean
Although he wasn't able to solve the mystery of the hidden sword right away, he decided he should look everywhere and try his luck.
The young swordsman has gone down the mountain. He closed the cave, carried his sword and secret map, and began wandering alone in the nearby desert.
In half a year, the young swordsman traveled almost all over the desert, and finally wandered to the southwestern part of the desert. This is an extremely dry and deserted area. The ground is undulating, and the recesses are full of crystalline salt grains, and white flowers shine in the intense sunlight. Soon, the young swordsman ran out of drinking water. While he was thirsty, he had a fuzzy sense of his consciousness climbing over a hill with falling gravel. When he finally discovered that there was a spring in the recess in front of a hill, he just stumbled towards it and had forgotten to consider whether this was a mirage.
Fortunately, this spring water is real. It lies quietly in the yellow sand of bare rock, presenting a perfect crescent shape. The spring water is lined with poplars, and fragrant wildflowers bloom. When the young swordsman turned to the shore and drank it with their mouths, a group of waterfowl were frightened and spread their wings one after another, shouting and flying round and round around the small lake.
After the young swordsman drank enough water, his body temperature dropped, his consciousness slowly recovered, and fatigue followed. He lay contentedly in the shade of a few date palm trees and fell asleep.
He woke up already in the evening, and the blue sky without clouds was beginning to darken. Under the sky, in the desert, the young swordsman who had been thirsty for the rest of his life felt deeply alone as a swordsman. He stood up and walked back to the top of this concave outer edge.
The dark crescent-shaped surface of the water at the foot of twilight ripples with the evening breeze of the desert. Looking in every direction, it was a huge stretch of sand dunes. The western sand dunes are backlit by the sunset. The outline is blurry, layered, and even more vast. The undulating desert in front of them made the young swordsman think of his hometown on the Oriental Seaside and his childhood life at home by the undulating waves in the sea. He nodded and said to himself with infinite emotion:
“No wonder the desert is called the 'Han Sea'...”
Before he finished speaking, he suddenly stopped. He turned around to take another look at the crescent-shaped lake that was about to disappear into the night. He knelt down on the yellow sand that was still warm under the gradually shining stars. The bright light made the young swordsman think of the phrase “fishing for the moon in the sea” on the secret map. Perhaps it should not be interpreted literally as a moon-shaped island in the ocean or lake; just the opposite, it refers to a moon-shaped lake in the Hanhai Desert full of birds and flowers!
The young swordsman recited the last two sentences and suddenly looked up, only to discover that the stars were bright and the sky was almost completely dark. He wanted to immediately remember what day it was, but in vain, he went for so long that he even completely forgot about the days, but this must be the final key. “Three or five nights”, of course, refers to the night of October. So whether you can tell the meaning of the last two sentences on the secret map right away depends on what day it is today.
Under the pressure of fervent expectations, the young swordsman quietly returned to the lakeside. With his martial arts skills, it didn't take much effort to catch a grey goose living next to the water. He killed the bird, made dinner with it, then sat on the already cold sand and stared at the sky.
The moon had already come out at this point, and the young swordsman discovered that the moon was less than half bright this day. At first, he was a bit disappointed because it meant he had to wait at the lake for several days to unravel the mystery; but only when he bowed his head in boredom and caught his eye on the moonlit lake, he discovered that his luck might have improved surprisingly well.
It turns out that the young swordsman was on the northeast shore of the lake at this time. Judging from his location, the shape of the crescent moon and the lake was very similar, even the angles curved at both ends were the same. The dry and clear air in the desert has made the new moon appear slim, and a small portion of the jade rabbits in the Xanjing Palace have already appeared. From this, the young swordsman realized the meaning of the last sentence in the secret picture: doesn't “point by rabbit ears” mean that when the moon is full, the ears of a jade rabbit in the middle of the moon are used as the punctuation point and placed in a opposite place near a crescent-shaped lake? The young swordsman was happy and ran wild by the lake, startled the waterfowl sleeping in the reeds, and stirred up the scent of flowers in the moonlight.
Over the next few days, young swordsmen used stones to pile up an arc between the two sharp corners of the moon-shaped lake to make it look like a full moon, and also placed stones day by day at a position on the lakeshore equivalent to a jade rabbit in the middle of the moon as an indicator. During the day, he hunts and picks fruit to supplement food, and at night he fixes stones on the increasingly perfect moon and waits intently for the night of decision to come.
The moon is finally round. The lakeside scenery was particularly beautiful this evening. In the middle of the night, the breeze blew through the plants and made fine sounds. The shadows of the trees shook on the sand, and the silver light leaped on the surface of the water. After dark, the wind calmed down, the moonlight gently filled the lake area, the leaves stopped swaying, and everything was peaceful. I couldn't see at all that this was the only moisturized place in the desert, and beyond the edge of the lake basin, it was still an unreserved, arid and severe desert.
The young swordsman began excavating with the rudimentary tools he made in the past few days. He chose two locations, the outermost point in the moon-shaped area when the moon was full, pointed to by the two ears of the jade rabbit. He believes that compared to the profit and loss of the moon, these two points are the only places in the terrestrial lunar circle that are only included in the scope on the night of the full moon in March and May, and must have special significance.
The point indicated by Yutu's right ear is that the sand in the ground is wet, and water pops up when you dig it up again. Although the young swordsman continued to dig for a while, he found nothing, so he climbed out of the sandpit, spared no effort, and worked hard for another punctuation.
This time the situation is completely different. After removing much dry sand, a rock appeared underground. The young swordsman's tools were unable to break through the stone layer; they had to expand the bottom of the pit to find the edge of this stone. Suddenly, he discovered that the edge of the stone on the inner side of the lunar circle was facing vertically downward and was extremely flat. It looked like it had been cut and polished; it was by no means a natural occurrence. He was overjoyed. The hands that caressed the rock unwittingly pressed violently, and a long, right-angled stone edge fell. Underneath it was a groove, and a light colored silk cloth was revealed in the groove.
The young swordsman held out his trembling hand to unravel the silk, but as soon as his hand touched it, the satin shattered into fine powder and flew all over the sky in the night breeze that blew up again when I didn't know when. Young swordsmen know that this must be an ancient artifact that has been buried for thousands of years before it still remains in shape and completely destroyed. He waited with bated breath. After the silk ash was gone, in the dawn, the young swordsman saw a long sword in a strange form lying in the stone trough.
The young swordsman sincerely and respectfully knelt down and saluted the ancient sword, then carefully picked up the sword with both hands and pulled out the scabbard in the face of the rising sun in the desert.
The color of the ancient sword is gray-black, and it is simple and heavy, giving people the impression that they are big and humble. There are two ancient characters close to the handle that cannot be understood. It should be the name of this sword. The young swordsman stood up from the east. According to the mentality handed down from his teacher, he held his own strength, held his own strength, and then drank loudly, showed off his sword skills, and practiced one move at a time.
With this ancient magic soldier in hand, the young swordsman felt that the practice was particularly smooth. Every move was performed to the fullest. From beginning to end, it continued to flow like a river returning to the sea. When he got up and did his last move, he simply felt that the ancient sword had become one with himself. The essence of this field of martial arts was all there.
The next day, the young swordsman bid farewell to this little oasis. This is a secret shared only by him and his master father, who buried a sword many years ago. Although he stayed for less than half a month, he has developed feelings for every grass and tree here. The young swordsman said goodbye. He put the secret picture in the stone trough, carefully covered the lid, piled up the sand, and bathed clean in the crescent-shaped lake before setting out in full force.
Two months later, the young swordsman arrived in the Central Plains. Along the way, he went from an uninhabited desert to a civilized area full of castles and farmland; from a wilderness with only wind and thunder, passing through grazing land with howling horses to a loud city; and from being only governed by naked natural laws, he gradually stepped outside of the nature of being fed by the weak, strong, and superior to defeat. He is clothed in the dust of the Ten Red Dwarfs, clothed in an increasingly thick coat of hypocrisy.
The young swordsman felt that this was a place where he showed his ambitions and where the rangers fought their righteousness. At first, he decided on the principle of using more ears and speaking less, and while doing odd jobs for people everywhere, he listened to people's opinions.
Gradually, he discovered that a bully nicknamed “Zhongzhou Poison Dragon” is the most common source of pain in the mouths of oppressed kind people. Every time the young swordsman heard such grievances and sighs, he wrote it down in a book. One night, after reading the records of the Nakasu Poison Dragon's misdeeds under the oil lamp in the small hotel, he decided it was time to act. He pulled out his ancient sword, wiped it off with fine oil, and was ready to start holding this ancient artifact to exterminate the world's evils.
After a few more days, one night when the waning moon was about to set, at 4 o'clock, the young swordsmen found Feilong Mountain Villa, the home nest of the Zhongzhou Poison Dragon on time. He didn't notice the dark camellias surrounding the villa at all. After a few screams from far and near, a few ups and downs, he stood idle and proud in the center of the spacious courtyard in front of the villa. The many wind lanterns hanging high around the yard cast a light circle of radiating shadows around his body.
Murderous energy suddenly soared in the villa, and the shadows shook everywhere. Suddenly, a few daredevils flew in the direction behind the young swordsman and sprang out. Swords were thrown at him one after another without saying a word. The young swordsman waved a silver sword without hurrying, and suddenly turned around and stabbed several big men to the ground. The figure withdrew four times, but the murderous spirit grew stronger. Suddenly, the door to the main hall of the villa slowly opened. Twenty people walked out and stood on both sides. Finally, a short and fat middle-aged man wearing a cape appeared. You couldn't see his face clearly in the lights, but he had a frightening aura of power. The middle-aged man silently said:
“You left a book for provocation, and certainly went to the appointment alone. I admire you very much. But I don't know why they have to live with me or not?”
The young swordsman threw away an ordinary blue steel sword with his right hand, pulled out a book from his arms with his left hand, and held it in his hand. The minions of the Zhongzhou Poison Dragon evaded sideways one after another; only the Zhongzhou Poison Dragon stood still. The young swordsman said with disdain:
“If I don't do something, there must be a reason for what I do. I'll burn the things in the books to show you in front of your grave.”
As soon as Zhongzhou Poison Dragon raised his right hand, someone next to him immediately untied his cape and held a large sword. He pulled out his sword and suddenly scolded him in a series of swear words, then waved his sword. This is his typical style of play to distract his opponents. The young swordsmen, however, have long understood this. While the Poison Dragon in Zhongzhou drew his sword, Gu Sword came out of his scabbard and made moves a millimeter ahead of his opponent, seizing the opportunity.
Everything was as expected by the young swordsman. His speedy sword skills forced the Zhongzhou Poison Dragon to be on the defensive as soon as he came up. The young swordsmen are getting more and more smooth, and their sword is flowing in the air. The previous move hasn't been used yet, and the next move is handed out one after another. Zhongzhou Poison Dragon was busy responding. On two occasions, he had to use a sword and sword, a major offender to pass the taboo.
While the young swordsman was good at his meticulously planned strategy and Benmencio's sword skills, he also had a hint of doubt that the sword of the Zhongzhou Poison Dragon was still completely undamaged; however, as soon as the sword moved, the two sides fought fast again, and there was really no time to think carefully. Soon, the sword technique of the Poison Dragon of Zhongzhou gradually became dwarfed. The young swordsman took the plane and shouted loudly and suddenly took the last move.
Zhongzhou Poison Dragon was shocked and felt that the pressure on his head was falling from the sky. There was nothing to avoid around him. In desperation, they had to gather all their strength and fight against the trapped beasts with a single sword.
There was a huge sound of gold and iron, and the two figures suddenly separated. One fell to the ground and stopped struggling after twisting it twice; the other had blood stained most of his right sleeve. Although he couldn't hold the big sword anymore, they finally escaped the murder and stood firm.
There was a brief silence all around. The moon set and the stars were dark, and a frightened swallow flew up into the sky. The desolate cry made only the first sound, and it was overwhelmed by cheers from all over the courtyard at the same time. A few close friends of the Nakasu Poison Dragon woke up from their astonishment and immediately came to wrap his wounds and pick up the big knife. He was also immediately surrounded by a wave of rhetoric.
Amidst thunderous cheers, the young swordsman looked up to the sky and died on a dry yellow ground. There is a deep, horizontal wound on the forehead. In addition to blood, white skull and brain pulp can be seen. He still clasped the half-cut ancient sword tightly in his right hand. As for the tip of the sword that had been cut off, he had no idea where it had gone.
Other minions of the Nakasu Poison Dragon gradually gathered around the young swordsman's corpse. A white-bearded, thin, and short old man first bent down to check. He laboriously pulled out the young swordsman's right finger and pulled out the broken ancient sword. He was about to lay his broken sword on the ground, only to find two seal characters close to the handle of the sword. The old man squinted and saw these two words clearly. Unwittingly, he shouted “oops,” and immediately ran to the Zhongzhou Poison Dragon with his broken sword.
A few close friends relinquished one after another. The old man held a broken sword with both hands and said respectfully to the Zhongzhou Poison Dragon:
“Owner, please take a look. The two ancient characters on the handle of this sword are 'path'!”
“Oh?”
“The ancients, the Huns only had a sword. It was called the “Path Road,” which was later obtained by the Han people in the desert; that is, it disappeared. It was probably this sword. It's an antiquity between Qin and Han.”
Someone next to me immediately answered:
“Being a master has strong martial arts and natural strength. What kind of sword or ancient sword is not broken as soon as it is cut...”
Nakasu Poison Dragon interrupted him by waving his hand. He looked down for a while. Suddenly, he looked up at the sky and laughed furiously. The laughter penetrated the morning and stopped all other human languages. After laughing, the Nakasu Poison Dragon held out his left hand and patted the old man on the shoulder, pulled him over, and said in a voice only the two of them could hear:
“Master, it's fine. This kid is really good at martial arts. Being able to find this sword is even more like he is interested; it's just that he has forgotten how much the technology of iron smelting and steelmaking has improved over the past 1,000 years.”
