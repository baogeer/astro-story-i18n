 The last princess to be included in the “Qing History Manuscript” is Prince Gong's daughter, Prince Gong Yixin. Prince Gong Yixin is the half-brother of Emperor Xianfeng. After the Xinyou coup, Cixi Ci'an took charge of government, and Yi Xin became a major minister in charge of the power of the inner court of the foreign dynasty. The senior girl was born in Xianfeng in February 4th. Empress Dowager Cixi was kind to her at the time, declaring her daughter to live in the palace, and unusually gave her the title Princess Rongshou Gulun.
In September of Tongzhi 5, Princess Rongshou married Zhiduan as his wife, but soon after getting married, she lost her life to death. The young Princess Rongshou Gulun is arrogant and bullies others; everyone is afraid of her. There is a detailed record of the style of Princess Rongshou Gulun of the squadron in “Miscellaneous Notes from the Dynasty since Daoxian” by Sun Chongyi, a college student in the Daoguang/Xianfeng era. It is said that when the princess traveled, everyone avoided it. The horse-drawn carriage stopped to make way, and all the riders took refuge in the streets and lanes. At one point, her team met Xi Shangshu's team. Shang Shu's horse was not controlled, offending the princess's team. The princess imprisoned her driver and others, making it impossible to move forward. Later, after coordination, Xi Shang Shu prostrated himself in front of the princess and pleaded guilty before being released.
Princess Rongshou Gulun is crazy and has an eye on the opposition. A prominent official is still fearful of this “fan prince,” and the world is even more hesitant.
