 At the beginning of the Tang dynasty, Central Tianzhu, the most powerful in the Indian peninsula, unified northern India, established the Majietuo Empire, and soon established friendly relations with the Tang dynasty.
In 647 AD, Tang Ming and Wang Xuantse went on a trip to India with his deputy envoy Jiang Shiren. However, there was a coup in Tianzhu at this time. When the new king Alo Shun, who took the throne, heard that an envoy from Datang had entered the country, he actually sent 2,000 soldiers to ambush them. Wang Xuan Tse died from riding too many times, and Hyun Tse was captured and detained. Later, Wang Xuan Tse found an opportunity to escape. He traveled north from mainland India, crossed the Gandes River and the Sindustan Plain, targeting the Himalayas, and all the way to the Kingdom of Nepal. Here, Wang Xuanze negotiated with the Nepalese king to borrow 7,000 cavalry from Nepal in the name of King Zhong of Tuban, who married Princess Wencheng and has a friendly relationship with Tang. In addition to being furious, Xuan Tse continued to summon administrative envoys from various military capitals in the nearby Tang dynasty, and also gathered more than 10,000 soldiers to become his own superintendent. Jiang Shiren became a pioneer and went straight to the wheel. After learning about Zampusong Zanganbu in Tufan, 1,200 troops were also sent to help Xuan Ce.
Outside of Chabo and Luocheng in northern Tianzhu, Wang Xuantse defeated the Tianzhu Tens of Thousands of Elephant Troops in a single battle. King Tianzhu was shocked and couldn't defend the castle. Xuan Tse was bent on revenge and used all kinds of tactics by the Tang army to siege the city, using ladders, stone-throwing carts, fire attacks, and a fierce attack for more than a month. In 648, Cha Bo and Luo Chengbing destroyed the city. Xuan Tse went all the way to kill 3,000 Tianzhu soldiers. Tianzhu soldiers drowned more than 10,000 people and captured more than 10,000. Alo Shunna escaped back to Zhongtianzhu.
Wang Xuanze took advantage of the momentum to invade Zhongtianzhu and vowed to destroy Tianzhu. However, as soon as the Tianzhu soldiers fought against Tang Jun (considered a foreign army), they could not become an army. Alo Shun abandoned his country and went to Dongtianzhu, sought help from King Dongtianzhu's corpse, and then collected the remnants of the scattered soldiers to prepare a counterattack on the Tang army. Xuan Ce, Shi Ren bullied the Tianzhu people and didn't know how to use military law. They only knew about barbaric combat, set up an ambush to induce Alo Shun to take the lead, annihilated all the remnants of Alo Shun in one fell swoop, captured Alo Shuna alive, and killed all the rest. Finally, the Chaoqiantuo Acropolis, which was guarded by Alo Shun's wife with tens of thousands of soldiers, was also overrun by Shi Ren. The nearby town watched the wind fall, and Zhongtianzhu perished.
Due to Dongtianzhu's reinforcements, Alo Shunna, Wang Xuantse was preparing to follow the trend and die again. He was so frightened that he was busy sending thousands of heads of cows and horses, a bow and sword, and a number of treasures to apologize to Tang Shi in order to show that he obeyed Datang. Xuanze only went off to North Korea to debrief, and at the same time locked Alo Shun back to Chang'an. Emperor Taizong was overjoyed, and issued a royal decree to reward Xuanzhe, and he awarded the Daoist.
In the 3rd year of Gao Zong Xianqing (658), Wang Xuantse once again visited India. The main itinerary was to visit major temples and return to worship the Buddha. The Chinese humility was fully displayed, which was impressive. Since he hasn't done anything in China, there are no special promotions or appointments. Coupled with the fact that at the time, Datang led the way, Xuan Ce's exploits of “not doing business properly” (especially in countries that Tang had no intention of conquering) were not that remarkable. After coming back from India, it was just a matter of hard work, which was a bit pitiful. Xuan Tse wrote his memoir “Journey to Zhongtianzhu” in his later years, but he didn't pay attention to it, and it later went missing.
