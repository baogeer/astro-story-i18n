 Daishan Island has a coastal reef that curves and stretches out to the sea. Seen from afar, it looks like a stone dragon. Local fishermen call it the “Shi Qinglong”. It is said that this is the remains of a blue dragon located here in Hailongwang Town.
A long time ago, Qinglong served as a bodyguard general in the Dragon Palace in the East China Sea. This Qinglong general did what was ordered, and always responded to his requests. Sometimes they also become fishermen and go ashore to check people's conditions. If the fields on the island are cracked and the people are affected by the disaster, they secretly suck in water from the East China Sea and turn it into rain and dew to relieve the suffering of the people. Therefore, the local people respect him very much.
Once, Emperor Yu Huang ordered to select a competent general to serve in the Tianting Palace at the Crystal Palace in the East China Sea. King Hailong endured the pain and cut off, and sent the Qinglong General, who he was most proud of. On the day Qinglong left, King Hailong delivered it all the way to the surface of the sea and said to Qinglong:
“I specially selected General for promotion. I hope you will not forget the birthplace of the Dragon Palace in the East China Sea when you arrive in Heaven!” Qinglong thanked him again and again.
After the Blue Dragon ascended to heaven, Emperor Jade designated him as the general in charge of the Lingxiao Treasure Hall. For the first few years, Qinglong, like in the Dragon Palace in the East China Sea, was able to handle up, down, left, and right, and had a good life. Later, he met Bai Hu Xing, a royal lady close to his wife. They usually had contact with each other. Over time, the two gradually developed a feeling of love. As soon as they met, they always came back and forth with emotion. However, the rules of the Heavenly Court were strict, and they were unable to share their feelings together. Over the years, they both felt more and more that Heavenly Court was not free.
One day, Blue Dragon Star and White Tiger Star met again while serving Emperor Jade Emperor and Princess Mother in the Harem. This time, when they saw no one around, they had the courage to talk. The two really hit it off. They agreed that on the night of the royal mother and daughter's birthday party, when all the gods went to the Pantao Festival, they would elope together to stay married forever.
It was hard to wait until this day. They both sneaked out of Xitian Yaochi and hurriedly rushed to Nantianmen. While they were happy, the sound of bells and drums came unexpectedly from the Lingxiao Temple. The Nantian Gate slammed shut, and the Four Kings blocked their way.
Emperor Yu Huang learned that both of them had secretly eloped and had their beards crooked up in anger, so that Cao, who was on duty, invited Qinglong and Bai Hu for questioning. The Blue Dragon Star and the White Tiger are deeply affectionate. When they get to this kind of scene, they are not afraid of anything. In front of the Jade Emperor, they bluntly stated that they are in love and willing to be punished by the rules of heaven; they only ask for permission for them to become a couple. This infuriated Emperor Jade even more. He immediately sent the message that the Blue Dragon Star would be relegated back to the East China Sea, and the White Tiger Star would go to the ordinary. The two stars are permanently separated and must not meet! The Blue Dragon Star returned to the East China Sea Dragon Palace feeling sad and angry. I thought Dragon King treated him well. After returning to the East China Sea, I wanted to ask King Hailong to ask for love and find White Tiger Star. Unbeknownst to me, King Hailong is also aggressive and deceiving people. The last time Qinglong went to Tiangong, he gave each other a smiley face and was desperate to be flattering. This time, Qinglong returned to the East China Sea, but it was the opposite of normal; he was very indifferent. He sat tall in the crystal chair and said softly to Qinglong:
“You've been demoted this time for violating the rules of heaven, and you can't be reused as much as you used to. Remembering your past exploits, now I'm sending you as a trendsetter. This isn't bad at all, but it doesn't treat you badly either!”
Qinglong paid tribute to King Hailong and silently withdrew from Crystal Palace. Since then, he has been a trendsetter. After two waves of hard work in one day, there wasn't even a single mistake. But he couldn't get out of it; he couldn't find Baihu Xing; he only missed her bitterly.
Also, Bai Huxing. After being punished in a mortal room, she became a handmaiden in the family of an official and lieutenant in Zhenhai. Once, there was a huge flood of water in the Zhenhai Sea, houses collapsed, people and animals drowned, and the county was a vast white water world, many?? The body floated along the heavy water to the East China Sea. This official maid was also half drowned. She drifted along with the floods, and finally swept to a beach on Daishan Island by wind and waves. I don't know how many days of light it had passed. Bai Huxing woke up and saw an old man with white hair standing beside her. Only then did she learn that the local land had saved her. Bai Huxing recounted her miserable experience and also asked the land father-in-law to help her inquire about the whereabouts of Qinglong. Grandpa Di was very sympathetic to them and quietly told her that since Qinglong Star was demoted back to the East China Sea, he has been a trendsetter in this area. Bai Huxing thanked his father-in-law and immediately rushed to meet him...
On a night full of stars, the troubled couple Bai Hu Xing and the Blue Dragon Star met again after a long absence. They were both happy and sad, and shared their bitter feelings for each other. Who knows this was seen by King Hailong, who was sent by King Hailong to monitor the Blue Dragon Star, and hurriedly reported it to King Hailong. King Hailong was furious when he heard it:
“Great! This Blue Dragon Star and White Tiger Star violated the rules of heaven in the heavenly court, and then violated official rules again at my Dragon Palace. This is really an added crime!”
Fu immediately ordered Baihuxing Town to go to the bottom of a mountain on the northwest side of Daishan Island. This mountain was later called “Baihu Mountain”; Qinglongli Town was on a reef southeast of Daishan Island. This reef was later called Qinglong'an Reef. Blue Dragon Star and White Tiger Star went through a lot of hard work for freedom and love, and were finally crushed to the bottom of the mountain by King Hailong, and never became a couple. However, people don't sell Hailong Wang's accounts at all; on the contrary, they miss this affectionate lover even more.
Every year on the 30th night of the New Year, residents of the island have to put “blue dragon paper” with a dragon picture printed on them on their water tanks, rice bowls, and kitchen cupboards in their homes as a sign of commemoration.
