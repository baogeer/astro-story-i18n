 Author: Shinichi Hoshi
The bandits got together to discuss the next action plan.
“I really want to do this big deal that shocked the community.” One scoundrel said in a whimsical way. The group leader then readily agreed to his remarks: “Right, I've always calculated this way. Now I've come up with some ideas. Let's all get ready. Let's get to work.”
The robbers were taken aback by this remark. Everyone scrambled to ask, “How exactly?”
“In our business, we all chose the time to act at night. Since the surroundings are too quiet, it is inevitable that it will attract attention when we get started. I'm going to do the opposite this time, and to the surprise of people, they beat them all...”
“It makes sense. It's worthy of being our boss; the ideas we come up with are always superior. But how do you get started?”
“In broad daylight, they broke into a bank with a gun and robbed.”
“Don't be kidding, just do what you said. I'm afraid you'll be taken to jail before entering the bank gate.”
“Idiots, why do you always have so few roots in your head. OK, listen to me... Let's first write a script for a TV series and give it to the traffic police near the bank, then we all dress up as TV crew members and go to the bank to film an attack on the bank. In this way, the bank is defenseless, and we must be caught off guard. At that point, everyone will just grab the money. Even if they shoot as a last resort, the police will be indifferent and only use the sound effects specially arranged for the plot. In the end, everyone listened to my orders and retreated together...” The leader's words were still unresolved; his subordinates had already been talking loudly, and they were all impressed.
“High opinion, amazing, unspeakably wonderful.”
“You can get too addicted to this, guys, get to work.”
The robbers got a van and wrote the words “TV series production team” on the car body. Soon, they also found a camera. Naturally, there was no need to prepare a video tape. After the script was printed, the minions carefully dressed themselves up, some as vicious thugs, and others as workers maintaining order.
The robbers drove the car to the entrance of the bank. They had just walked out of the car door while holding a handgun. Sure enough, the traffic police on duty nearby all came to inquire. A robber hurriedly sent them a few TV series scripts and explained the reason. Soon, they understood and stopped asking questions.
The beginning went well. The robbers were full of energy. They stormed into the bank one after another and shouted, “Gentlemen of the bank, we are real robbers. Hurry up and hand over the money. Whoever dares to mess around will take his life soon.”
Who knows, the plan has gone awry.
A doorman suddenly came over with a smiling face, breaking the tense atmosphere here.
“Gentlemen, can we help? When you come to shoot TV, don't let them know first, so that the staff can get ready. You need to know how important publicity work is...”
Another young customer also rushed in and enthusiastically said, “I'm a writer. Your line just now isn't appropriate. What 'the lords of the bank' is just like delivering a campaign speech. Also, the statement “we are real robbers” is also unsubtle; it immediately illuminates the audience. Who wrote the script? Let me write it for you next time.”
He took out his business card and got tangled around, and the robbers had a hard time getting rid of him.
Arriving at the window, a girl working there hurriedly stood up and said, “When is it going to play, can I get on camera too?” The female bank employees left their seats one after another and hugged them over here, “Let's shoot us too; we are all TV fans.”
Faced with this chaotic scene, one of the robbers was impatient and couldn't help but raise his voice: “Enough, this isn't acting. Brothers, it's for real.” He then pulled the board machine, and the bullets howled to the ceiling, shattering the lights.
However, the move didn't work either. One boy jumped over and said, “Enough energy, just like the real thing.” Another person went on to say, “The electric light inside the ceiling was probably pre-loaded with gunpowder. It really fooled people who didn't know.”
At this point, the bank's governor showed up.
“Hey, gentlemen, can you add a shot of a glass shot. It's bulletproof special tempered glass. If you promote it for us from the side, it will increase the customer's trust in our bank...”
“Sir, let's play the honorable role of not succumbing to the threat of bandits and dying from drinking bullets, please.” The male staff also gathered around to make a request.
The robbers were helpless and had to explain everything, yet no one took their words seriously at this point.
The incident reached such a point that it was already difficult to end. The leader of the robbers stood up and shouted angrily, “Listen, stop filming today, go back and revise the script, and reshoot it another day.”
The bandits hastily withdrew from the scene, one by one full of complaints.
“How could it have ended up like this? There must be something wrong with today's society. We have never seen so many lawless people.”
