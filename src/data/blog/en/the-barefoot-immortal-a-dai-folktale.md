 Today, Zhuyuan Dam is a rich plot of land and is known as the beauty of Sugarland. However, in local Dai folklore, Zhuyuan Dam in ancient times was an arid and water-scarce land. Legend has it that a long time ago, the majestic Mount Eshan, located in the western part of Zhuyuan Dam, was a continuous mountain range with dangerous peaks. As a result, the mighty Dianxi River was blocked by Mount Eshan. As a result, Hongxi Dam in the west of Mount Eshan became an unshining area of water, while Zhuyuan Dam on the east side of Mount Eshan was burnt out, and no crops were harvested. Although the hardworking and kind people of the Dai people work hard all year round with their faces facing the loess and the earth, they are still unclothed and hungry.
One day, an immortal inspected the situation on earth and discovered the Bamboo Garden and Hongxi River in the east and west dams of Mount Eshan. One area was drought-free, and the other was flooded. These are two completely different situations, yet they are the same bleak scenery. A sense of compassion is born. As a result, he pulled the Golden Sword from his waist to the top of Mount Esan. I only heard a loud thunder. The mountain shook the ground, and Mount Asan was split. A big gap was formed in the middle. It was the mouth of Asan, one of the Eight Views of Bamboo Garden where “Eling is green” today. The raging Dianxi River gleamed from the gap to irrigate the fields that people have reclaimed for generations; Hongxi Dam also retreated into fields, and the hardworking Yi people reclaimed good fields and planted crops. The Dai and Yi people in Zhuyuan and Hongxi are all happy and encouraged. Considering that the sky would take a long time and that Yakou in Mount Ashan might once again merge and cause future problems, Daisen stuck the Golden Sword on the steep and steep peak at the northern end of Yakou, leaving it in Yongzhen. Also, considering that the rocks would flow into the riverbed with the water and block the mountain pass, they placed a stone ball at the southern end of Yakou to stop the rocks from filling the riverbed. As a result, it has formed a strange landscape where there are many strange stones in the river above Yakou, and there are actually no stones in the river below Yakou. The fairy's act of kindness infuriated Old Green Duck. As a result, Old Green Duck works his magic and uses dry smoke pot poles to fill the mouth of two hills. However, the unjust act of the old Green Duck was discovered by the Immortal, so he painstakingly discouraged him not to do anything that harms the people; instead, he should use charity as a foundation to save people in the midst of suffering. The old green duck did not listen to dissuasion and persisted. In order to save the people, Daisen took advantage of the late hours of the night to hit the dustpan, learn how to chirp the old green duck, and frightened the old green duck. At this point, Luk Ya Lao Dao was traveling to the edge of Xiaohaizi Village, which was five or six miles away from Yakou in Asan, and suddenly heard the sound of chickens. They thought the weather had leaked, so they left behind their burdens and fled at night. The two mountains he is responsible for are the two small mountains that now sit on the edge of Xiaohaizi Village. Daxian wanted to return to heaven. The honest, simple, and kind people of the Dai people bowed down to the merry Dianxi River in order to thank Daxian for their relief and relief, and shouted in unison, “I ask Daxian to leave her honorable name.” Daisen just laughed and didn't respond. There was no trace in an instant. Later, some people discovered that the place where the Daisen passed left deep footprints. Suddenly, they realized: Ah, it turned out to be a barefoot daisen! Since then, the local Dai people have adopted Barefoot Daxian as their ancestors. The Dai people have excellent water conservancy conditions, and are even more painstakingly cultivating land, regardless of spring, summer, autumn, and winter, not afraid of rain, snow, wind or frost. Day after day, year after year, they have carefully cultivated and worked silently from generation to generation to build Zhuyuan Bazi into a beautiful and rich fish and rice town without worries about water and drought.
