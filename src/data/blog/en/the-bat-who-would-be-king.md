 Even when full of talent,
Being scammed by people dressed in shabby clothes;
Bats fly high with a meter,
Depressed by the lack of feathers.
In the early days, birds had to choose a bird king. At that time, everyone agreed that whichever sees the sun rise first can be the king of birds. Although bats and pigeons can see the sun rise early at the same time, in order to become the king of birds, the bat tricked the pigeon into eating dried fried noodles and deliberately delayed it. As a result, although the bat saw the sun rise early, the birds did not recognize it as the king of birds.
Another time, everyone agreed: whoever flies the highest will be the king of birds. Bats advance into the gaps in the wings and feathers of the tallest flying giant dragon to compete with hundreds of birds. The birds are very tired from flying and flying. The other birds have to go back to the ground. Only Dapeng Bird takes itself for granted and proudly shouts, “I've reached the highest altitude!” Then it spread its wings and flew high into the air. At this point, the bat drilled out of the Dapeng bird's feathers and flew over the Dapeng bird again, shouting, “I've already flown to the highest sky!” Fly higher while shouting. It finally flew the highest, but all the birds still don't recognize it as the king of birds. He said, “Something like this, which has no feathers, only tentacles, and is hard to see, can't even be considered a bird, so how can it be our king of birds?” As a result, not only did bats not become king of birds, but they were also excluded from the normal bird ranks.
Although bats are intelligent, they have no feathers, so they are being bullied by many birds. As a person, clothing is certainly important, but what is more important is to have real talents and practical skills.
