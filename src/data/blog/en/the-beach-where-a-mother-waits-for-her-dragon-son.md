 There is a flat sandy beach at the junction of Shunyi River and Yangjiao River.
Legend has it that a long time ago, a poor Yi family lived on Mount Yi. In this Yi family, only mother and son are dependent on each other. Grandma is in her 50s, and son Aaron is 16 years old. Every day, Aaron's job is to cut grass. One winter, it snowed heavily in Mount Yi, and all the plants froze. Aron carried a scythe and on his back and looked everywhere for green grass. After searching all over Yishan, there were dry, fading weeds and fallen leaves. In desperation, he had no choice but to carry an empty basket back home. Having just turned to the top of a mountain, Ah Long suddenly saw a small patch of green grass near Qinggou. As if Aron got a treasure, he ran over and waved his scythe. He cut this green grass in one go. The cut grass just filled Aron's back basket. The next day, Aaron went up the mountain to cut grass again. When he walked to the place where he had cut grass yesterday in Qingbian, he found that another small patch of green grass had grown here. Aaron was very happy. He finished cutting the grass again, which just happened to be a basket. On the third day, Aaron came to Qingbian again. In the place where the grass was cut yesterday, another patch of green grass grew. After cutting grass on the green grass, the more he thought about it, the more strange he felt, so he picked up a scythe and dug up the green meadow, and a shining pearl of the night appeared in front of Aron's eyes. Ah Long happily picked up the grass and went home with the Pearl of the Night. Aron hid the Pearl of the Night in a bowl of Baogu.
As soon as it dawned, Alon was still going up the mountain to cut grass. When he walked to the place where he mowed grass yesterday in Qingbian, he saw that it was all dry and the grass was gone. Aron thought it must have been because he removed the Pearl of the Night himself, so he decided to rebury the Pearl of the Night here and grow the grass again. Aaron rushed home to get the Pearl of the Night. Running home to open the bowl of Baogu, Alon was dumbfounded: yesterday's half pot of Baogu turned into a full jar. Am I being dazzled? When I touched the Baogu, the grains were all very full. The next night, Aaron placed the Pearl of the Night in the rice pot again. In the morning, he opened the rice pot, which was also a pot full of white flowers. On the third day, Aaron hid the Pearl of the Night in the sheep pen again. When he woke up in the morning, the sheep were already full. Since then, the lives of Aaron's mother and son have become rich, and they don't worry about food and clothing. At night, Aron placed the Pearl of the Night in the house. The Pearl of the Night illuminated the entire house, and Aron no longer needed to light an Akiko torch.
The story of Aron's Pearl of the Night soon spread to Toast's ears. Toast thought, if I had the Pearl of the Night, I would be the richest person in the world; what would be great if I wanted anything. Toast called Aaron right in front of him and said, “Aaron, your family has been my baby for generations. This mountain belongs to Grandpa Toast. Even a small tree of grass cannot be cut without my consent. I heard you cut grass on my mountain and tore away the Pearl of the Night I had hidden underground. Is that OK? Hurry home and bring your cow and sheep and my family's ancestral Pearl of the Night to my house; otherwise, the lives of your mother and son will be unprotected.” When I finished speaking, I kicked Aron out the door.
Aaron went back to his home and told Grandma about the incident. After discussions, the mother and son decided to hide the Night Pearl in the stove. The next day, when Toast didn't see Aron hand over the Night Pearl, they went to Aron's house to search for it. The thugs looked through all the furniture in Aron's house, but they couldn't find any. I suddenly discovered that the light in the stove was flickering, so I opened the pot to inspect it. Aaron quickly got his hands up and ran to the side of the stove to pick up the Night Pearl in the stove. When the thief saw the Pearl of the Night in Aron's hands, he reached over to grab it. Aron held the Pearl of the Night in his mouth and accidentally swallowed the Pearl of the Night in his stomach. When Toast saw the Pearl of the Night being swallowed up by Aron, he had no choice but to leave with the beaters.
After a while, Aron felt that his mouth was burning, so he told Grandma that he wanted to drink water. Grandma handed Aron a bowl of water. Aaron finished drinking the water in one go. They shouted that they were thirsty again. Grandma handed Aron another gourd of water. After Alon took the water and drank it all in one go, he still shouted that he was thirsty. Grandma had to give him a whole tank of water to drink. After drinking a trough of water, Aaron was still thirsty. Grandma said, “Dragon, Grandma had to take you to the well outside the stockade to drink water.” Afterwards, Aron followed his grandmother to the edge of the well and drank the well water in one go, but he was still thirsty. Grandma said sadly, “Dragon, I've eaten all the water from this well, so Grandma will take you to the Yangjiangjiang River to drink water.” The mother and son came to the edge of the Yangjiao River. “When I went down to the river to drink water, Grandma held Alon's legs just in case I fell into the river.” Grandma said, “Dragon, you can eat it, I'll just hold your leg.” Grandma held Alon's legs. As soon as Aron touched the water, his head immediately became a sparkling faucet, binge-drinking in front of the river. At this point, the waters of the Yangjiangjiang River began to turn over. Grandma couldn't hold back. As soon as she let go, Aron slipped into the river and immediately became a golden yellow dragon.
The river swirled through the waves, and suddenly thunder roared again. A bad wave rushed to Toast's house. Instantly, the Toast family burst into the waves. Soon, Jiang Shuiping calmed down, and Grandma shouted at the riverside, “Dragon, where are you? Grandma misses you, come back.” At this point, I only heard a yellow dragon shouting in the middle of the river, “Grandma, the dragon is here. Grandma, go back. My son was originally a yellow dragon in the Yangjiao River. Today, my relationship with Grandma came to an end, and my son is leaving. Grandma, take care. Every year on this day, you go to the beach to meet your child.” After speaking, as soon as the dragon tail was lifted, a large beach dam appeared on the riverside. Aaron nodded and slowly swam away, not stopping to look back at Grandma on the shore.
After that, every time she arrived, Grandma had to visit Aron at the big beach, and Aron looked at Grandma when it sank in the water. The two mothers talk to each other. Every time it's quiet at night, if people lie quietly on the beach, they can also vaguely hear the conversation between Aaron's mother and son. Later, people called this place Wanger Beach.
