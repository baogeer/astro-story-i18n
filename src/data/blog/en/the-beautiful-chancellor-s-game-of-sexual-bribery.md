 Author: Zhang Jihe
The court in ancient China was a “big dyeing pot” full of talents. On the one hand, the Party fought against each other and sharpened Hoho; on the other hand, they also exercised people the most because of their feud. Even people like Shangguan Waner, who are holding on to power, are fearful and uneasy; they are afraid that overnight they will be completely stripped of their face by others. As a result, they are chasing power and seeking backers as if they were drinking white powder. Once you get addicted, you definitely won't be able to quit.
Mixed in the harem, the two things are the most powerful: one, mind; second, posture. Shangguan Waner is not lacking either. The woman's figure, like a flower, is too short and unreliable. How useful is a woman's mind, no matter how smart it is? A young lady who revolves around the cooking table has no use for thought at all. It seems that being smart and good-looking is far worse than being married well. Two years after Wan Er entered the palace, her mother, Zheng Shi, died. No one hurt the orphan, but how did she live? When first blooming, love is beautiful and charming. You can't just let the flowers fall with the water; grow old day by day. This smart girl began to look for someone whose life and death would depend on her. The first person she met was Wu Zetian's third son, Li Xian.
Tang Gaozong's eight sons, Wu Zetian, gave birth to four, namely Li Hong, Li Xian, and Li Dan. The first two kids died a long time ago, and the climate wasn't good. The latter two brothers became “political dolls” for their mother. They are established today and abandoned tomorrow. Lee Hsien and Lee Dan both ascended the throne twice; this is a special case of feudal emperors.
When Shangguan Waner saw it, she fell for Lee Hsien-huai with a sense of emotion. She had to combine “emotional bets” with “political investments.” Marriage is a gamble, and politics is also a gamble. Unfortunately, the emperor didn't want to, and Wan Er fell for a famous scoundrel. Lee Hsien is like Li Zhi; he is foolish and cowardly. In particular, an old woman like Wu Zetian can only act as a puppet. He himself is a mud Buddha, how can he protect the person in his arms? In 684, Li Xian, who had only been emperor for two months, was ousted and stationed in Junzhou and Fangzhou. Shangguan Waner's backer fell to the ground.
As soon as Lee Hsien left, Wan Er couldn't stay idle. The woman's “poplar nature” forced her to “follow the established policy.” After much deliberation, she could only bet the treasure on the queen's close family. Soon, she sat on Wu Sansi's lap again. This person is Wu Zetian's nephew. It is said that he is handsome and very popular. It is recorded in “The Old Tang Book: A Biography”: “Thinking twice about literature and history. He is a person who is sexually intimate and remote, and a person who does good deeds.” Waner is extremely careful about Wu Sansi. After all, she is not a man of steel; she is still flesh-and-blood. Excluding those worldly utilitarian purposes, she is willing to stand by her lover and help him rise step by step, just like all affectionate women.
Women in love are crazy and foolish without exception. The “lover” of Shangguan Waner also fainted. She actually braved the world and blatantly gave the green light for Wu Sansan, and even used her position of power to talk about the Wu family's good words. Li Tang's members of the royal family have been clearly belittled by her. This laid the groundwork for Shangguan Wan'er's murder.
In the twilight years of Wu Ze, Li Xian was recalled to Chang'an again. The “good old woman” Shangguan Waner quickly ran to give her a hug. She was immediately canonized as “Zhaorong.” Zhaorong is the emperor's little wife. According to the “Old Tang Book,” Zhaorong's status is ranked second among the “nine” after the Empress (one person) and the concubine (four people). Zhao Rong also has a rank and is paid. Shangguan Waner is not used to being well-respected and superior; her assistant is still the Secretary General of the Cabinet. After all, the master changed, and the way of thinking had to change. The emperor of this dynasty was easy to get away with, but the empress was not easy to deal with. In order to solicit and settle Empress Wei, Shangguan Waner paid a large sum of money — familiar with this woman's merry nature, she blatantly offered “sexual bribes.” Wu Sansi was branded a “erotic gift package” and promoted to the Inner Court. Empress Wei understood her heart and immediately smiled. She took all the photos.
“The Old Book of Tang Dynasty: First Legend” says, “The Story of Emperor Zhaorong, Sang Xuan (Wei), the Later Xing Zetian... (The Empress) was told by the superior officer Zhao Rong, leading three thoughts into the palace, ascended to Imperial bed, and later Shuanglu. The emperor thought he would be happy to save some money. Scandals are heard from the outside world...” What is “The Tale of the Sun”? It's nothing more than two things: 1. Being an emperor; 2. Raising a male pet. In order to preserve her status, Shangguan Waner did not hesitate to encourage rhetoric and insult stealing. In order to achieve her goals, she has completely degenerated into a lowly class politician at any cost. The emperor and empress are actually very popular. They hang out with Wan Er and Wu Sansi in the inner palace bedroom every day. How could they have any sense of royal prestige or shame? “Scandals are smelling from the outside every day”. The Tang Harem is filled with an unquenchable atmosphere of fox beauty.
