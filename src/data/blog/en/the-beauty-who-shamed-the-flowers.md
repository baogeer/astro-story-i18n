 “Embarrassing Flower” means Yang Guifei.
During the early years of the Tang dynasty, Emperor Tang Ming was extravagant and lewd, sending people and horses to search for beautiful women everywhere.
Yang Yuanyan, Hongnong from Shoudi County at the time, had a beautiful daughter named Yang Yuhuan, and was selected to enter the palace.
After Yang Yuhuan entered the palace, he missed his hometown. One day, she went to the garden to enjoy the flowers and relax. She saw peonies in full bloom, the moonshine...
I wanted to be imprisoned in the palace, wasting my youth. I couldn't help but sigh, and said to the blooming flower, “Flower, flower! At your age and when are you still in full bloom, when will I have my first birthday?” I burst into tears; she just touched a flower. The petals contract immediately, and the green leaves roll up low.
When I thought, she was touching a mimosa. At this point, I was seen by Mrs. Ichinomiya. Mrs. Gong said everywhere that Yang Yuhuan was more beautiful than the flower; the flowers all bowed their heads in shame.
When this incident came to the ears of Emperor Ming, he was overjoyed. Yang Yuhuan immediately came to see the driver. Yang Yuhuan was heavily dressed and dressed before entering. As soon as Emperor Ming saw him, she was certainly very beautiful, so he left Yang Yuhuan by his side to wait.
Since Yang is good at offering favors and favors, she was greatly delighted by the Ming emperor, and was soon promoted to a royal princess.
After Yang Guifei came to power, she colluded with her brother Yang Guozhong, playing with power techniques and framing Zhongliang.
After the Anshi Rebellion broke out, Emperor Ming fled west with his royal wife and Minister of Culture and Wu, and An Lushan led an army to chase them down. They needed not only Jiang Shan from the Tang dynasty, but also the beautiful Princess Yang Guifei.
On the way to flee to the west, the ministers questioned the emperor. The country broke down and the family record was difficult to survive. If you want a river or mountain, or if you want a royal princess, if the queen doesn't die, we all go west and east. Helpless, Emperor Ming gave the royal concubine her death and hanged herself under a pear blossom tree in Liyuan.
Later, the great poet Bai Juyi wrote the song “Song of Eternity Hate,” which narrated this period of history.
