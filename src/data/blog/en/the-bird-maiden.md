 On Guzhang Town Street, Xilin County, Baise City, Guangxi, there are three banyan trees that are over a thousand years old. They have taken root and are climbing branches. For thousands of years, the leaves have flourished and are lush all year round. Like a giant green umbrella, they have completely covered the ground with a radius of more than 100 meters. Under the tree is a place where people gather to relax and chat, and the tree is a paradise where birds live in nest. There is a folk legend about these three banyan trees:
In ancient times, there was a rich owner in Guzhang Village, who was very greedy. Although he is already very rich, wears silk and satin, and eats delicious mountain and seafood, he is still very unsatisfied. He heard people say that if anyone could eat bird meat every day, their soul could be transformed into a sacred bird flying to heaven to enjoy glory and wealth in the future. As a result, he ordered his domestic slaves to go up the mountain every day to hunt birds for him only.
The rich owner had a young and capable domestic servant named Ada, who was very good at shooting. Every day he goes up the mountain to hunt birds, and he always has a lot to gain. Gradually, in the mountains around this region, almost all birds were caught and exterminated by Ada.
One day, Ada climbed Mount Prince, the highest mountain in this area to hunt birds. Originally, the mountains and forests were dense, and there were many birds and animals, but now there aren't many left of them being hunted by Ada. Ada looked left and right, and had a hard time seeing a bird bouncing around a tree from afar in the middle of the mountainside until evening. He hurriedly pulled the bow and hit the bird under the tree with an arrow. However, when he ran under the tree to find the birds that had shot down, he unexpectedly found a beautiful young girl sitting under the tree crying sadly, but there was no trace of the birds at all. Ada strangely asked why did she come alone to this deep mountain, and why was she crying so sad? Seeing Ada's repeated calls, the girl slowly stopped crying and told him, “Her name is Miss Bird. Her parents and siblings have all been killed by evil people. Now, not only is she helpless, but she is also being hunted down by those wicked people who have had to flee to this deep mountain to find shelter, and cry bitterly when talking and crying bitterly. The girl's miserable experience aroused Ada's immense indignation at the wicked, and at the same time felt infinite sympathy, love and pity for her. “Who is so cruel and vicious? Where is he? I'll take revenge for you.” Ada said to her. Grandma opened her eyes in tears and glared at him. She shook her head and cried bitterly. The more she cried, the saddened she became. After a long time, Ada watched the sunset at the top of Mount West and advised her, “Don't cry! It's not early, I'll take you home.” “I don't have a home!” The bird girl said sadly. “If you don't mind, then go to my house first. I have a younger sister who is the same age as you, who just happens to be with you.” Ada said.
Just like that, Miss Bird followed Ada down the mountain. Along the way, the two had very speculative conversations, and they both felt that it was too late to see each other. Ada, on the other hand, is secretly in love with Birdgirl in her heart.
The next day, when Ada wanted to go up the mountain again to hunt birds for the rich man, but the bird stopped him with tears in his eyes. Ada told her, “I am a slave to a rich owner. If I don't listen to his call, I will be killed.” Grandma Bird said to her, “I know this. However, birds are lovely creatures in the world, and their existence actually has many benefits for humans. Furthermore, just like humans, birds have their own families, parents, and siblings. Don't you think it's harmful for you to shoot them so cruelly, break up their families, force their wives to separate, and their families break up and die?” “But you know I can't help myself!” Ada said with embarrassment. “Did you know? I just became a bird. The birds you shoot every day are my compatriots and my loved ones. Today, all of the surviving birds are cursing you, hoping that one day you will be rewarded as you deserve: either by fighting at all costs, or by throwing thunder in the sky to relieve their hate. But I know you're also one of the victims, so I'm here today, of course, to save the birds, and second, to save you.” The bird finally revealed her life to Ada. After listening to Mother Bird's words, Ada was surprised and said, “I see. But how should I deal with this damn rich man? He did all of this with his own hands!” The bird girl said, “If you don't go up the mountain to catch him birds, he will definitely come to you. At that time, I naturally had a way to punish him.”
The rich owner hadn't seen Ada pay his prey to him for two days. Sure enough, he came to the door in a menacing manner and wanted to settle accounts with Ada. When I arrived at Ada's doorstep, I saw a beautiful, gorgeous, and extremely beautiful girl walking out of the house. The rich owner's lustful and greedy eyes immediately looked to death at Grandma Bird and said, “Where are you from? Why haven't I seen it?” “She's my little sister's friend. She just arrived yesterday.” Hearing the voice of the rich owner, Ada immediately rushed to answer. “It's because this girl is here that you dare to disobey my orders and not go up the mountain to hunt birds for me, right?” The rich man spoke harshly to Ada. Ada just wanted to explain it to him, only to see that the rich man caught the bird girl and said, “I'll take her first, then I'll settle the accounts with you later!” When I said that, I dragged the bird away. “No! “Grandpa, she's innocent, you can't catch her.” While shouting, Ada ran out of the door in an attempt to snatch Lady Bird back. “It's none of your business, just go away!” The bird girl said to Ada. “No! I can't live without you.” Ada said and caught the bird girl to death. At this point, I saw that Bird Girl, Ada, and the three rich owners slowly turned into three banyan trees entwined with each other. Just like that, they finally stood in the village forever.
After losing that damn rich owner, birds were finally able to live and reproduce again. To thank Mother Bird and Ada for saving and protecting them, they flew in groups to live in knots in banyan trees and sing beautiful and beautiful hymns from time to time. Like this, year after year, it has continued, continued for thousands of years...
