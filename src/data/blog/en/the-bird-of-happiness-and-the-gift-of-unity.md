 A long time ago, there was a rich family. This family member, Ding Xingwang, was reunited and had a very prosperous life. Because it is said that Happy Torisu settled in his home, he hasn't left his house for a long time.
Happy Bird said to the parent, “I've lived in your house for many years, and after spending too much time in one place, I get bored. So I want to go all over the place. I'm leaving you now, so if you want me to leave you with anything, you can kindly ask for it; I will definitely meet your requirements.”
After hearing this, the parents answered, “If this is the case, please give me three days to discuss with my wife and children and grandchildren, and then answer you.”
As a result, the parent called his wife, son, and grandson together, told them what they had said to Happy Bird, and everyone discussed it.
At this point, one son said, “We want four kinds of livestock from it.”
Another son objected, “No, we should ask it to leave us the land, the mill, the mill, and the garden.”
But one daughter said, “We should leave it to us in a store full of goods.”
The little girl also went ahead and said, “The most precious things are gold and silver, pearls and agates, so let it leave these to us.”
Just like that, they fought back and forth, saying this or that, they haven't decided on a single thing for two full days.
That parent didn't come up with a good idea either. He asked his wife, “Hey, old lady, did anyone participate during the discussion?”
The wife replied, “My newly married daughter-in-law did not participate in the discussion.”
The parents immediately ordered, “You call her too.”
The new daughter-in-law came to her father-in-law. The father-in-law told the daughter-in-law everything that had happened and everyone's opinions to the daughter-in-law. I asked my daughter-in-law, “What good should we let Happy Bird leave for our family?”
After listening, the new daughter-in-law answered without hesitation, “We should let Happy Bird leave unity to our family. The reason is that unity can make all our businesses prosper, always be rich, and avoid disputes, so we can live in harmony and live a prosperous and prosperous life as we do now.”
After listening to the daughter-in-law's words, the father-in-law happily said, “Only then did we find the heirloom that Happy Bird left to our family.”
On the third day, Happy Bird came to the parents and asked, “Can you guys discuss it, what do you want me to leave to you?”
The parents said, “We have discussed it. Please leave the unity with my family before leaving.”
Happy Bird said, “When I left what you wanted to take away, it was unity. Since you want it, I'll give it to you so I won't leave your house.”
As a result, happiness never left this house and still stayed. The family is also still living in harmony and will always live a happy, happy, prosperous and prosperous life.
