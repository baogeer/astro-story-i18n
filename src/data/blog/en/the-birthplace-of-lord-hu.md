 “Birthplace”
——Where Emperor Hu was born
Enshrined in Fangyan, Yongkang, and once praised by the great man Mao Zedong as “being an official, benefiting one side” and “Qing official,” Hu Ze - Emperor Hu Gong of the Northern Song Dynasty (Jinyun people call him “Hu Xiang Gong”, hereinafter all “Hu Xianggong”) was born in Jingyue Hutadi in Jinyun County. He was originally from Jinyun. Can you believe it? Why don't you listen to me tell this story.
one
In Xiandu, a national scenic area, there is a majestic mountain called Ma'anshan. On the edge of Hao Creek in the north of Ma'anshan, there is a small mountain shaped like a turtle, so people call it Turtle Mountain. On the northwest side of Kameyama, the rock tower of the mountain is recessed and surrounded by one and a half moon-shaped clear water. It is called Rock Lake, also known as Moon Lake (around 1990, the rock at the northwest corner of Kameyama was blown up, and the rock lake was buried, which is really unfortunate and hateful). The ground near Guishan is a place name that people honored to commemorate Hu Xiang Gong's birth here — “Hu Birthplace.” It later became what people call “Huta Land” today because the homonym of the Jinyun dialect was falsified.
Legend has it that Hu Xianggong's father, Hu Chengshi, is from Yankeng Village in Rongjiang Township in this county. His mother, Ying Shi, married from Yongkang County. Shortly after her pregnancy, Ying's husband became ill and unfortunately passed away. As a result, Ying's family was poor and lacking in food and clothing. Before giving birth, I wanted to go begging along the way and go back to my mother's family in Yongkang to give birth. On the afternoon of August 12, when passing through Xiandu Mountain, where immortals gather, Ying Shi suddenly noticed that the fetus was kicking in his stomach, and there was a slight pain in his stomach. He insisted on moving forward... In the end, he felt that he was out of strength. Seeing a broken temple at the entrance of the village in front of the village, he hurriedly went inside to rest for a while and didn't sleep. By the time he woke up, it was already early the next morning. Ying Shi immediately got up, had a big stomach, endured his thirst, slowly climbed over Jianmailing, and arrived at the edge of Guishan. He saw that the lake was clear, washed his face, and then sat by the rock lake and rested his feet. It didn't matter when I sat down. Suddenly, I had severe pain that pierced my stomach, accompanied by “Ouch! Ouch! “...” With a terrible moan, Ying struggled with all her might at the lake, which was full of blood, and finally gave birth to a child. In this “no village before, no store after” wilderness, Ying had to do his best to cut off the umbilical cord with his teeth and put the child in a rock lake to wash his body. Strangely enough, the water in the entire rock lake suddenly washed bright red, and then every year on August 13, the water in the rock lake turned red. Ying Shi washed the child and wrapped the child in the broken wrapping cloth he brought. There was no strength left, so he was close to the child, hazy, and bowed down on the side of the road. I don't know how long it has been. A child rushed home from the mountain to herd cows. Passing by, they saw Ying's mother and son in such distress. They also saw a pool of red water in the lake. They hurriedly went ahead. After asking about the original committee, they immediately gently supported Ying's mother and son on the back of their cow and brought them home. This shepherd's home is in Jingyue Village, less than two miles from Rock Lake and Haoxi. Although the shepherd's family was not rich, the whole family was kind and kind, so they happily settled Ying's mother and son into their home and treated them well. Before I knew it, the full moon had arrived. The whole shepherd family and Ying's mother and son had fun drinking “full moon wine.” Thank you very much, Ying Shi. After drinking three times, Ying proposed to go back to her mother's house. The shepherd's parents have long known that she is serious about her motherhood, and they can't help but choose a lucky day for her mother and son, prepare enough dry food and clothing, and relentlessly send their mother and son on the road... Time went by like an arrow. One day after more than 20 years, two government cars and a few attendants arrived at Jingyue and walked into the shepherd's home... It turns out that those seated in this official sedan were Ying Shi and his son Hu Ze back then. At this point, Hu Ze had already been hired and became a senior official. They came to thank the shepherd's family. People in Jingyue Village have never seen such a big official. Furthermore, he was born on this site. In particular, they learned that he was an official and Qingzheng, and that he also tried to save money and help the people to solve their problems, so there was a lot of public discussion. They wanted to build the Hu Xianggong Hall next to Mount Kamiyama and sacrifice forever. After Hu Xianggong learned about this incident, he refused to approve it. Later, people in Jingyue Village had to choose a site on the mountainside near Guishan and build a small temple that still exists today, named the “Hu Xiang Gong Temple”. At the same time, the area around Guishan Rock Lake was named “Hu Birthplace” as a sign of permanent commemoration.
two
Coincidentally, the “birthplace of Hu” is said that Hu Xianggong was born not only in Jinyun, but also widely spread throughout Yongkang, and has been repeatedly written into relevant materials for studying Fang Yan and Hu Ze. For example, “Hu Gong Jing” recited by Yongkang folk women and children says at the beginning, “Nan Wu Buddha said “Hu Gong Jing.” Hu Gong was born in Jinyun. Hu Gong was a part of Huta, and both parents went to heaven.” (According to Yongkang scholar Hu Guojun's “The Faith of Emperor Hu and the Temple Fair in Fang Yan” - “Fang Yan's Poetry Commentary” published by Tianma Books Co., Ltd.)
In addition, “Fangyan Folk Tales” also collected a story very similar to the legends from all over Jinyun mentioned above. The story says that on August 13, Song Chu Jianlong, Hu Ze's mother set out from Rock Pit in Jinyun, and when passing through Dinghu Peak, the fairy spirit had an impulse of fetal gas and went to Jingyue Hutadi. Her stomach hurt, and the child “quack” fell to the ground. Ying Shi cut off her umbilical cord herself, held the child to wash her body in the rock lake, wash the pond water three times, and wash the child's face red. This child was Hu Ze. It is said that this rock lake pond is still half red and half mouth clear?
Also, according to “Hu Family Genealogy” in Yankeng Village, Rongjiang Township, there is a clear record: Hu Ze's father took his name and married Ying Shi (Yongkang Hu Ku's “Hu Family Genealogy” also records the names of Hu Ze's parents) as a Yongkang native. Ying is pregnant and is about to give birth. On the morning of August 13, she went to her mother's home in Yongkang to give birth. “When I didn't want to go to Jingyue Hutadi, I had a son, washed Liangtou Pang, and raised him by his mother.” Here is Hu Ze.
Fan Zhongquan, a famous Northern Song minister who was an official in the same dynasty with Hu Ze, had a close personal relationship with Hu Ze, and wrote an “Epitaph” for Hu Ze after his death. According to the title “Epitaph” in Jinyun Rock Pit, the title of the “Epitaph” is “The Epitaph of Dr. Zheng Feng, Shangzhu, Guo Guogong, Gives a Purple Goldfish Bag to the Ministry, Shang Shu Shu, Zuo Shirou Lang, and Hu Zhenghui's Epitaph”, and it is also written: “Public rules, the characters are correct. Lu Zhenghui is also a native of Jinyun.” The “Epitaph” included in “The Chronicles of Yongkang County” is entitled “Memoirs of the Imperial Tomb to Shi Shi Hu” and states “..., also from Yongkang of Wu.” Which is true and which is false? Unknown. Anyway, the people of Jinyun believe that Hu Xiang Gong's roots are in Jinyun and that he was born in Jingyue's “birthplace”. However, since he was a child, he grew up in Yongkang, and later went to the entrance exam under the name of Yongkang's student, so he is famous all about Hu Ze — Hu Xianggong is from Yongkang. For this reason, there is also a popular saying in the Jingyue area of Jinyun, “Muxilu flowers smell in the distance, and Hu Xianggong appears in the open sea.” However, even though “Hu Xianggong showed the outside world,” villagers in the Jinyun area have never forgotten that Hu Xianggong is a hometown citizen of the Jinyun people. Men, women, and children from various villages near Jingyue still maintain the tradition of visiting Hu Xianggong's temple during the New Year holidays to the “place where Hu was born” and the tradition of walking to Shangfang Rock to worship Hu Xianggong every year after entering autumn.
Postscript: The author of this story's hometown is in Jingyue Village, Dongfang Town. Since childhood, he has often played or worked in the Hutadi area. I have heard and seen the lush Kameyama, the clear rock lake, and the beautiful legend of the birth of Hu Xiang Gong. I have experienced it in my heart, so I wrote this article for the rest of my life.
(Ma Songgen, Jinyun County People's Congress Standing Committee, June 2005)
