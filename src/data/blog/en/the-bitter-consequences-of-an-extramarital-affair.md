 Once upon a time, there was a man who ignored his wife and children and ran around fooling around with a widow. Over time, it became an open secret. One day, more than 20 years later, as soon as the man came to the widow's house and sat down, the widow said, “If you want Aba to steam rice (Yi: Aba shou), take off your hat and scoop it out for you. After that, don't come back. The kid is grown up. It's ugly!” The man came out of the widow's house carrying Abadan, and when he got to the back of the house, the more angry he thought about it, the more sad he thought about it, and the more shy he thought about it. He lost Abadan, took his genitals, and scolded: “It's all your fault! It's all your fault!” Until he pulls himself to death.
