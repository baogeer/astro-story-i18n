 Since the Sui dynasty pioneered examinations, the Ming dynasty selected the best entrants and named them the top winners. There are countless champions in the algebra department. Throughout history, it's not unusual to be the champion. However, there are few female champions; previous generations have never heard of them. It wasn't until Taiping Tianguo pioneered the Women's Course, that China had a female champion, and the first female winner was Fu Shanxiang.
When it comes to Fu Shanxiang, the title of female champion, although she came up with her own enthusiasm, some of them are helpless. Therefore, after becoming the female champion, Rong was honored, but her suffering was difficult to talk about.
Fu Shanxiang was born into a Shuxiang family in the city of Nanjing. His father started a teaching career. Fu Shanxiang and her sister Fu Luanxiang had two flower-like daughters. Influenced by their father, the two sisters studied poetry at an early age, and can be called a talented pair of sister flowers. In the year of the Taiping Heavenly Lord in Nanjing, her sister Luan Xiang was 18 years old, and her younger sister Shanxiang was 16 years old. They were all incorporated into the “Women's House” by the Taiping Army.
The “Women's House” is a “women's camp” during the Taiping Army's combat period. Although it was renamed the “Women's House” after arriving in Nanjing, and no longer participated in the war, the structure was still militarized. The members of the museum often have to participate in work such as digging moat ditches and picking bricks. At night, they listen to the pastor's preaching, and their living arrangements are very tight. Fu Shanxiang's sisters are all weak, and they are really struggling with this kind of life. Fu Shanxiang used a poem to describe her feelings about this kind of life:
The constellation Toad hears the new law and recalls old pleasures in front of the cricket lamp;
The gap between coming to Japan is still under agreement, and the temporary arrangement will come to an end.
Soon after, the outstanding talents of the Fu sisters attracted the attention of those in power in Taiping. After several rounds of selection, the older sister was sent to Tennogu, while the younger sister was assigned to Dongwangfu. Fu Luanxiang was first in charge of court affairs at the Tianwang Palace. After a few twists and turns, she was favored by the very understanding Emperor Hong Xiu, and was taken as a favorite princess. While chatting with the Emperor, Fu Luanxiang inadvertently talked about her younger sister Fu Shanxiang, showing her feelings of concern. As soon as the Emperor heard it, he thought to himself why not take Fu Shanxiang into the palace too? You can not only reunite their sisters, but you can also enjoy a pair of sister flowers together.
At this time, Fu Shanxiang was working as the female secretary of Dongwangfu. I live in Zixawu, a place where flowers, trees, and birds sing and fish shows. Dong Wang Yang Xiuqing, who fell out in the shadow of the sword, was deeply moved by her brilliant talent and gentle attitude. Dong Wang is quietly pouring his love for her. Suddenly he heard that the Emperor wanted Fu Shanxiang. Yang Xiuqing was very displeased. The more he thought about it, the more annoyed he became, and decided to find a way to dispel the Emperor's thoughts.
Thinking back and forth, Yang Xiuqing thought about academic examinations. Taiping Heaven's academic examination system is modeled after the Ming dynasty; however, the examination period is uncertain. Every time there is a national ceremony or a major celebration for the kings, they take matters into their own hands. In the past, women were not eligible to take the exam. Since Taiping Tianguo promoted gender equality, why not start the initiative to open a female subject? Once she started the Women's Course, she learned with Fu Shanxiang's talents. There is no problem with getting a title on the Golden List; wait until she has achieved fame. If Tenno wanted to beat her up any more, he would have to be concerned!
This year just happened to be Yang Xiuqing's 40th birthday. He took the opportunity to propose to open two separate subjects for men and women to obtain permission from the Emperor. At the beginning of the scientific research, Tianwang Qin assigned her younger sister Hong Xuanjiao as the female examiner. There were two deputy examiners. One was Wang Zizhen from Anhui; the other was Zhang Wanru from Hubei. The title of the article was “Only Women and Little People Are Hard to Raise”, while the title of the poem was “Shout the Mountain Is Green.” Under the direction of Dongwang Yang Xiuqing, Fu Shanxiang took this exam. In her article, she cites classics, argues that women are difficult to raise, and recounts the domestic help of virtuous women throughout the ages. It was sprinkled in the ocean, and it was extremely powerful. His poetry is even more fresh and pleasant, and depicts the scene of sailing in the mountains and water vividly:
I can't hear the sound; the landscape sends lone sails;
The opposite side is picturesque, and the back is full of greenery.
There are clouds in the middle of the sky, and the area is full of water;
Funao Sumiryu and Minokoshi Asahi took the title.
Qingqing is suspicious of ancient banks, and Cui wants to conquer shirts;
The sound of flowing geese is astonishing, and the shade of Japanese cypress trees is rich.
After judging the paper, the examiners agreed that Fu Shanxiang's poetry was excellent. Even King Hong Xiuquan was greatly appreciated, so she was the winner. She, along with Mr. Zhong, in second place, and Mr. Lin, in third place, were wearing flower crowns, and wearing golden uniforms. Under the protection of Bing Yong, they toured the city of Nanjing for a while.
Since he is the top pick, it is inconvenient to admit Fu Shanxiang into the harem without understanding. Only then did Hong Soo Quan discover his own negligence and had to give up his original idea. Dongwang Yang Xiuqing's plan achieved the intended goal. The purpose of the academic examination is to select talents for the country. Since Fu Shanxiang won the female championship, she must logically be appointed as a member of the government. One of the first titles she received was the head of the Women's House's Central Regiment, and became the leader of the 10,000 dress troupe. Fu Shanxiang used to be a member of the Women's House and was very disgusted by that strict militarized lifestyle. After becoming the leader of the Central Regiment, he promoted reforms, relaxed the mandatory controls on the Women's Hall, and tried to avoid having the members of the museum do the heavy work of digging and picking bricks, and arranging for them to do some work such as needlework, cooking, etc.
The head of the women's house is Hong Xuanjiao. She is a strong woman who broke in from the flames of war. She still has deep feelings for the militarized system. As a result, the set of management measures implemented by Fu Shanxiang after taking office made her displeased, and a fierce conflict broke out between the two.
The conflict between these two women was actually not all caused by the management system of the women's house. Another focus of the conflict was buried in the hearts of the two. They didn't want to talk about it; they just competed for Yang Xiuqing's favor. Regarding the origin, Hong Xuanjiao and Yang Xiuqing can be said to have an old relationship. As early as Hong Xiuquan first formed God's congregation, Hong Xuanjiao and Yang Xiuqing had a lingering relationship; Yang Xiuqing joined the God Club; later, Hong Xiuquan took the lead, Hong Xuanjiao married Xi Wang Xiao Chaogui, and Yang Xiuqing had no choice but to retreat to Sanshe; after the death of Xiao Chaogui in battle, Nanjing, the capital of Tianping. Yang Xiuqing and Hong Xuanjiao also revived their old relationship for quite a while. But finally, a talented Fu Shanxiang, was inserted. Yang Xiuqing was greedy for the new and tired of the old, shattering Hong Xuanjiao's dream of reviving her old feelings. Why not make her hate Fu Shanxiang itch her teeth! Meanwhile, Fu Shanxiang also heard about Hong Xuanjiao's old relationship with Yang Xiuqing. What's more, the two are often linked. Hong Seon-jiao took offense at her, and she never showed weakness; the conflict between the two grew deeper and deeper.
