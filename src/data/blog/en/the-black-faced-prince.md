Poor Princess Conch was forced to take off her white silk robe, replace it with an old quba, remove the blue satin belt and tie a black yak rope; remove her pearl necklace and hang a string of bone rosaries. Every day, along with slaves in the royal palace, they do all kinds of hard work.

5. Return to Hometown
Prince Blackface and Princess Luoye, while begging, rushed over one snowy mountain after another, and walked through village after village. Finally, they arrived at the place of the Kingdom of Xewalen. Prince Black-faced said to Luoye, “Lovely princess, I have two more days to go to my hometown. I have to go one step further, discuss things with my sister, and find a place to settle down. Follow my stick and draw slowly!”
Princess Luoye felt that his words were reasonable and nodded in agreement; she didn't wait for the young man to walk a few steps, and she caught up to grab his belt and said, “Boy, now I have no hometown, no loved ones. In the middle of the blue sky and the earth, I only know you...”
Prince Black saw her concerns and hurriedly persuaded her: “My beloved girl, please don't worry! Even if I use gold equal to my body, I won't be able to repay your deep gratitude to me. Don't say I won't be separated from you in this life, and I'll be with you in the next life.” After speaking, I hugged the princess tightly and said many heart-warming words before dragging and asking for rice sticks and left in a hurry.
Prince Blackface returned to the royal city of Sevalen and met his sister Guisang Wangmu in Wang Guanli. They had not seen each other for three years and were so happy that they fainted. That is really our happiness. I see, you're still living in a stable and living a poor life. I'll check this princess again to see if she can be King Warren's queen.”
