 Legend has it that in Northern Song, after the defeat of the Liangshan peasant insurrection led by Song Jiang, the husband of Hu Sanniang and Zhang Qing died in battle. She went to the Huanglou in northeastern Caozhou alone (that is, where he is now in Mudan Township) and went to work in Huangyuan's garden outside Huanglou. With her ingenuity, she became a flower artist.
In the twilight of spring in March, peonies bloom in the garden, attracting many people to see them. The old man from the government office in the city came to see the flowers. Seeing that some of the young girls in the park were very handsome, he fluttered with lust, squashed their eyebrows, and made fun of them. Seeing this scene, Hu Sanniang went up in flames and got into a big fight, knocking the government old man to the ground, causing him to directly shout, “Please forgive Grandma. Soon, the old man from the government office immediately brought officers and soldiers to arrest him. Hu Sanniang couldn't help but be furious and revealed her identity and real name; “Grandma Hu San is here. If she dares to do anything wrong, let him move with her head and see how good I am!” As soon as the officers and soldiers heard that she was a young woman, they were all frightened, urinating and hugging their heads. Huang Yuan saw that Hu Sanniang had caused a major accident, and feared that the government would prosecute him for harboring the rebel leader, so he conspired with the government to devise drugs to poison Hu Sanniang; after Hu Sanniang's death, the girls who were rescued by Hu Sanniang happily buried her dead body in the garden at night and placed a black peony on top of her grave. This is a new variety cultivated by Hu Sanniang herself. To commemorate the benefactors who rescued them, the girls called this black peony “a green one.” Later, the “one green” black peony flourished in the Caozhou Peony Garden.
