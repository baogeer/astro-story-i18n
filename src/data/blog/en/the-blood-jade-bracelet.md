 Another year of the Shangyuan Lantern Festival is here. At this time in previous years, he would spend time with her to enjoy the love and warmth of the couple, enjoying the love and warmth of the couple, but this year she was alone. Walking alone on the street, the people around her laughed and filled with joy and happiness, but that joy did not belong to her because he wasn't with her.
Since he was a child, he and her have been going to the lantern festival every year. He once said that he would marry her as his bride, stay with her forever, and watch the lanterns with her. Later, they grew up, and he was the top pick in high school. He came to the door to propose a family, and she actually became his bride.
If you calculate it, I've been married to him for over ten years. Over the past ten years, the husband and wife have been kind, loving and respectful, and he has always loved her. However, I don't know since when. He doesn't come home very often. Once in a while, he also comes back in a hurry and leaves in a hurry. He can't say anything to her, let alone be kind.
His explanation was that the government office was busy with affairs and had no time to take care of the family. She didn't believe it, but she didn't say much. A month ago, he actually said to her that he wanted a concubine. In fact, she had heard that he was married outside, but he never explained it to her. This time, he was just saying hello to her.
She didn't make a big noise like a vixen; she just secretly complained in her heart, complained about his poor feelings, and lamented the pain of her own life.
The hawker on the side of the road greeted the guests warmly, and she unwittingly walked to a vendor's stall.
I couldn't help but sigh as I picked up a mirror and looked at myself in the mirror. No wonder he changed his mind. I've been married to him for over ten years. I think I'm already in my 30s. My age is gone, my face is faded, and my skin isn't as snowy as it used to be; looking down and looking at my figure, I'm already somewhat blessed. I can't find any willow waist in the past; it's really old.
“Madam, do you want to stay young and beautiful forever?” a voice asked.
She couldn't help but shudder. She looked up; it was the vendor. The vendor was wearing a cape, and the pressure was low. She couldn't see his face clearly; she just felt his eyes were strange.
“Youth is gone, can I get it back?” She said in a slightly bleak tone.
“Yes.” A sound comes from under the hood. “I have something that can restore your youth to the way you used to be.”
A jade bracelet with red dots appeared in front of her.
“The jade used to make this bracelet is called blood jade. If you see the red spots in the bracelet, it can continue to grow red blood lines until the whole bracelet turns red. Wearing it can recreate your beautiful appearance and fit when you were 28 years old, and it will definitely work within 10 days.”
She was a bit hesitant. Is it really OK to make a bracelet made of this jade alone? She is also a bit moved. After all, she loves him and hopes to win back his heart.
“How much?” She asked, “Don't you believe it?” The vendor seemed to have seen through her mind. “You can use it first and then pay me later.”
There's no harm in trying it out; after all, this temptation is too much for her. She took the bracelet. Looking at the size, it was a perfect fit for her wrist. When she looked up again, she found that the vendor had no idea where it had gone.
Back in Fuchu, she didn't see him, and her subordinates said he hadn't come back.
After taking off her clothes and washing up for a while, she was ready to rest, but she still didn't see him in sight. It seemed like tonight would be vacant again, and she couldn't help but feel sad.
On the dressing table, where is the bracelet I got from the mysterious vendor. Can this thing really help her? As she thought about it, she got up to get the bracelet. Sure enough, it's not made of ordinary jade. This bracelet looks crystal clear and transparent. If it's fine, it actually has a red dot the size of a bean. The red one looks like blood, and there are a few thin threads hidden around it. This is probably the kind of bloodline that can grow. No wonder it's called Blood Jade.
Gently put the bracelet on your hand. The bracelet seemed to have suction power, and you put it on all of a sudden, as if the bracelet was worn on your hand by itself.
I feel cold. It's like a chill feeling straight from my wrists and lungs. I don't know what it will be like when I wake up tomorrow morning. She thought about it and fell asleep before she knew it.
When I woke up the next day, the first thing I did was take a mirror to take a picture. There doesn't seem to be any change, my skin tone is still dull, my figure is still slightly bloated, or the same. I couldn't help but feel a bit lost. I only wore it for one night. How obvious would the results be? Let's wait a little longer. Didn't the vendor say that it must be effective within 10 days? I was also a little anxious.
After being busy cooking for the next few days, I forgot about this. If he doesn't go home, she has to take care of everything, big and small.
More than ten days later, I saw the bracelet on my hand, and only then did I remember this incident. Looking at that bracelet, the blood seems to have been a little longer than when I first put it on. I took a mirror to take a picture. Sure enough, my skin tone wasn't like the previous few days. It was a little fair, and I couldn't help but be overjoyed. This jade certainly had some effects.
After January, the bloodline had grown to about an inch long. The skin color gradually changed from dark yellow to white, and the lower back and legs were noticeably slimmer.
Three months later, half of the bracelet was covered with blood. Her skin was fair and lovely. I found the dress I had when I was a girl, but it was easy to put on.
This is simply a miracle.
Treasures like this are really hard to buy for a thousand dollars.
