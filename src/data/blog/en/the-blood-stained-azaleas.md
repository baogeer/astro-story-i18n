 Legend has it that once Emperor Shun toured the south and visited the Jiulong Mountain area; I heard that the girls here were all very beautiful, like red azaleas, but their fate was very disastrous, so Emperor Shun, who loved people like children, decided to find out.
On this day, the wind and the sun were beautiful. Emperor Shun walked up Mount Jiulong along the left bank of the Mother River. He only saw pink, plum and white, green willows, sparrows clear, and red rhododendrons blooming everywhere. Emperor Shun was very happy to see this scene.
However, when Emperor Shun passed Liangshan Pao and visited people's homes, he only saw that people were eating wheat bran cakes, drinking bitter vegetables and thirsty, living in thatched huts, and wearing broken clothes. The situation in the villages along the way was the same. He was very sad.
Emperor Shun also walked to Dasangtang Village next to Xiaosangtang Village and came to an old father's house. He saw that his father was silent, his wife was blind, and Emperor Shun was even saddened to ask Dad, “Why is there no one to take care of you? What about your children?” As soon as the two elders listened, they immediately burst into tears.
As soon as Emperor Shun saw it, he was even more puzzled, so after questioning the reason, Dad had no choice but to whine and say the beginning and end.
It turns out that they originally had six beautiful and intelligent daughters. They all woven and embroidered, raised pigs and fed sheep. However, three years ago, the chiefs of the tribe visited the countryside and saw that their daughters were all beautiful, so they forced them to get married, but they preferred to die and were sent to six mountaintops by the murderous chiefs to feed tigers. My grandmother cried sad for seven days and seven nights, and shed tears; the mountain god was so moved that she turned those six mountains into a spectacular show, just the way six girls had lived. When my wife saw those mountains, she cried out to her daughter: “You are Zhu Mingfeng, you are Shicheng Feng, you are Shilou Feng, you are Xiao Shaofeng, you are Qi Lin Feng, you are Guilin Peak.” After the call was over, she also lost her eyesight due to excessive tears. Later, these six mountains were always covered by clouds and fog, just because the six sisters didn't want to see the horror of their mother crying; these six mountains were also constantly shouting. It was the six sisters who complained about the chief's cruelty.
Emperor Shun was very sad. He always loved his people like a son. He knew that the chief was so cruel, that the people had such a difficult life, and that his heart was really cut like a knife. As a result, when he went down the mountain on the right bank of the Mother River, he couldn't help but cry bitterly. His tears fell on the azaleas, and the flowers turned white. Since then, azaleas have bloomed red and white, and people have called these rhododendrons crying and laughing flowers.
