 Bracken that grows in depressions is called “dragon claw” by locals. Legend has it that one year the best batik girl in the village got sick. The family invited a ghost master to the house a few times, but it wasn't effective. Seeing that the sick person wasn't as good as day by day, she was dying of breath. One day, her mother went up the mountain to collect bracken shoots. These bracken sprouts, which are rich in many vitamins and crude protein, are both vegetables and medicine. I couldn't imagine that the girl felt so much more comfortable after eating it. The mother saw that her daughter had eaten fern sprouts, which were effective in treating diseases, and went up the mountain to pick ferns. By eating fern shoots, the girl was cured of her illness. As soon as she was happy, she painted this life-saving dragon claw on batik cloth to show that she will never forget it. When others see it, dragon claw is both beautiful and seems to be full of endless vitality, and it also imitates it. As a result, dragon claw slowly became a popular pattern for Buyei batik.
