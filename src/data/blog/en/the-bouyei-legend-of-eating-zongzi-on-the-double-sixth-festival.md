 There is a rich dam in the upper reaches of the Baishui River in Guizhou. It is called Po Wutan, Han Ming Biantan Mountain. It is surrounded by mountains on all sides, and only a few Yamaguchi pass outside. The road is difficult to defend and attack. The people living in this fertile land are all Buyei people. Once upon a time, the industrious and brave Buyei people did not want to be cruelly exploited by their rulers, so they organized to resist oppression by the official family.
At the end of the Ming dynasty, Emperor Chong Zhen stepped down in his campaign and forced the people to fight back. In order to survive, the Buyei people in Poh Wu-tan took turns going to Yamaguchi to guard the gate day and night, and security guards and thieves entered the mountain to harass and loot. It is up to women to survive the crops in the fields.
In June, the sun bathed the earth like fire, and the Baishui River Valley became a big steamer. On the dam, the seedlings grew windy along with the weeds, and the earth was all exposed to cracks.
My sister woke up when Zhutian didn't shine. She couldn't even brush her head carefully, and couldn't even care about the meal, so she picked up the doll with a flower back fan, picked up the bucket and went out. While walking, she breastfed the child.
At this point, Buyei women from all villages also carried hoes and carried burdens up the mountain one after another. It hasn't rained for a long time. Baogu in the field is dry and overgrown with weeds. My sister spilled water on the ground, and when the sun was at its peak, she remembered that her husband Aron was guarding the mountain in Namsan Mountain. She hadn't eaten yet; she wanted to go home and cook for the man.
Meitake is a good daughter-in-law with a smart heart. She came back from the field and thought as she walked. Aron must have been hungry at this point. If only a little more food had been delivered yesterday, she wouldn't need to be hungry now. However, on such a hot day, if I couldn't finish cooking the first day, it went bad the next day, and I couldn't keep it long after delivering too much.
Thinking of Arun being hungry, Meitake forgot that she hadn't dripped since morning. Along the way, she picked many bamboo leaves along the way. When she came back, she cut a large piece of bacon and mixed it with a large pot of glutinous rice, and added many condiments. I wrapped three packs in bamboo leaves and delivered to Namsan. She handed over cooked glutinous rice wrapped in bamboo leaves to Aron and said, “The work in the fields is very busy, and Aron, you can't live without staying in the mountains. Keep these sticky rice with bamboo leaves until you're hungry to fill your stomach.”
Ajon put away the bamboo leaf wrap and put away his wife's warm stomach. Hungry, he took out the bamboo leaf sticky rice and shared it with everyone.
The sticky rice wrapped in bamboo leaves left by Meizhu is very fragrant. Everyone praises Meizhu for her good methods. As a result, the next day, June 6, the men in Moriyama all told their family to learn how they looked like a bamboo girl and wrapped glutinous rice in bamboo leaves and went to the mountain to eat. Since then, regardless of the era of war and chaos or the heyday of peace, the Buyei people in Biantan Mountain have picked bamboo leaves or Takahashi leaves, wrapped glutinous rice, bacon, crispy sesame, etc., cooked them and brought them to the mountains to eat.
