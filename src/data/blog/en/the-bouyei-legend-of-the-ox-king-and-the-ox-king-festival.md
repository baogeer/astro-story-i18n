 Once upon a time, villagers in this area were too poor to afford to farm cows. They used people to pull plows and rakes, which was tiring and slow. One day, an old man sighed, “I heard my ancestors say that there are sacred cows in the Heavenly Garden, which are extremely powerful. Alas, it would be great if there were a sacred cow to cultivate the fields.” As soon as the young man Ah Niu heard it seriously, he said to the old man, “I must find God Cow!” So he brought dry food and set out with a bow and arrow on his back. A cow walks and goes. Arriving at a stockade one day, he was tired and thirsty. Seeing a well by the village, he knelt down by the well to pick up water to drink. While drinking, he suddenly heard a voice in the well. It was so strange. He took a close look. There was still a bronze drum hanging on the well wall, so he sounded the bronze drum curiously. Who knows, when he hit the ninth knock, Tenno heard the sound of a bronze drum, and followed the sound in front of Ah Niu and asked, “Ah Niu, what do you want to do when you hit the bronze drum?” Ah Niu mentioned that there are no cows to cultivate fields in the village. He is determined to find a sacred cow and return to the village. “Alright, tonight I'll give you a magic cow.” Even though the king said it, there was no trace. In the evening, after thunder rang at the top of the mountain behind the village, a sacred cow certainly came to Ah Niu's side gently.
Local leaders heard that Agniu plows fields with Shiniu, which is fast and labor-saving, so they wanted to try out the truth or falsehood of Shiniu. He said, “If anyone can plant my 1,000 acres within 10 days before April 8, I will distribute my daughter's allowance.” As soon as Ah Niu heard about it, she came to take the test. With the help of a sacred cow, Ah Niu certainly plowed 1,000 acres of field and planted it for rice on the 7th day.
The head man was surprised and delighted, but he wanted to test the strength and courage of the devil again, so he announced that the bullfight would take place the next day: “If the devil were to win a hundred heads of water, I would marry one of my five daughters to Ah Niu.” Faced with a large, fat and strong head, and wide, and sharp horns, the more they fought, the braver the more they fought, the more brave they were, and they were defeated one by one.
The leader promised to marry his daughter Ah Niu. However, all five daughters love A Niu; which one did they actually marry? The boss had no choice but to let Ah Niu look for himself. The five sisters all wanted to show their ingenuity and craftsmanship in front of Ah Niu. As a result, the head man asked each of the five daughters to offer tea, toast, and deliver food to Shiniu. Whoever eats tea, wine, or food with the Shiniu will stay close to the cow.
The game has begun. In the first round of the competition, tea was presented to the sacred cow. The 5 sisters each brought their own black tea, green tea, tall tree tea, dwarf tree tea, and kudding tea. When the sacred cow smelled it through its nose, it drank kudingcha. The second round was a toast to the devil cow. The five sisters each brought their own glutinous rice wine, Takahashi sake, baogu wine, microphone wine, and purple spring wine. The sacred cow sniffed and ended up drinking only Shisen wine. The third round was to deliver food to the sacred cows. The five sisters each brought glutinous rice of five different colors, red, yellow, white, green, and black, which they each cooked. Kamito took a look, but in the end, they only ate black sticky rice dyed with maple leaves. The leader inquired one by one, and this kudding tea, Ziquan wine, and black glutinous rice were all made by the Five Sisters. As soon as the 4 sisters took a look, this is the medium written by Shiniu. The marriage was like this, and they all acquiesced in the marriage between A Niu and the 5th sister. As a result, A Niu and her 5th sister became friends. Descendants commemorate Shiniu, A Niu, and the 5th sister. Every day until the beginning of April 8, the family had to make kudding tea, make purple spring wine, steam black sticky rice to sacrifice the king of cows and feed their own farming cows, and hold bullfighting competitions in the village.
