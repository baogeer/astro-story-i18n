 In very ancient times, there were twelve suns in the sky, lined up side by side in the sky. They are like 12 fireballs that blow out flames day and night, burning the ground to smoke, and the crops are burnt on fire. Even Mount Ishiyama was almost melted by the sun. The raging river* has also been dried up by the sun. Cows and sheep are dying of thirst. People had to hide and live in caves, which is horrible!
At the time, some elderly couples in the Buyei tribe had one man and one woman. The two brothers and sisters are witty and brave, and they have the same extraordinary martial arts: they can shoot and shoot, and get out of nowhere. In this year of great drought. The brother and sister watched as most people died of hunger, so they hated the sun and decided to eliminate the spicy sun so that people could live a good life in peace and security.
One day, the two brothers and sisters summoned their parents and townspeople to discuss how to put out the sun. The parents and elders all said, “The sky is so high that I can't stab with a gun or hit with a stick. What should I do?” Everyone was silent for half a day and couldn't think of a solution. My younger sister came up with a wonderful method. She cut a piece of white cloth to look like a sun (today's Buyei batik, the sun-shaped pattern is drawn according to this) and coated it with a layer of gold powder and silver powder. After the sun was in shape, my sister asked the townspeople to place it on top of a high mountain. The white cloth-shaped sun shines in the sun, shining four*. At this point, the twelve suns in the sky felt as if a strong glimmer of light was shining from the ground, making it difficult for them to open their eyes, so they looked down and looked carefully. They saw that there was indeed a shiny golden thing on the ground. They thought that one of the suns in the sky had fallen to the ground, and they followed one underground to rescue them. At this point, the brother and sister were hiding in a dark place, and the time had come to see them. The older brother hurriedly took off the bow and set down the arrow. Immediately after that, the younger sister also pulled out the bow and “whistled” five magic arrows in a row, dropping ten suns*. The two suns saw the sun in front of them falling one after another. In the back, two suns saw the sun in front of them fall one after another, and were frightened and turned back to the sky. One hid in the clouds and didn't even dare to get out. One ran in a panic and fell into the Tianhe River and couldn't climb ashore at any cost. After soaking in the Tianhe River for more than ten days, it didn't shine, and then turned into the moon.
