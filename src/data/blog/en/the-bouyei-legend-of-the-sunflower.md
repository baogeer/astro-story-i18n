 Legend has it that in ancient times, the sun did not distinguish between day and night, and hung in the sky every day without falling. At that time, the seasons were as warm as spring, crops were harvested year after year, and people lived a happy life. However, there is an earthworm (Buyei, that is, the devil) called Kaka. I often climb up to the ground from the pool and hide in a dark corner to catch people to eat in the pool. I'm not afraid of anything at all; I'm just afraid of the sunlight in the sky. If the sun shines on it, it can't walk away like a blind person.
One day, Gaka climbed into the shadows on the ground and waited to catch people to eat, but after waiting for more than half a day, no one walked in front of it. At this point, it hated the sun and had to go back to the pool hungry.
After Gaka returned to the waterhole, he thought of a trick. Using iron, an iron rod with more than 900 bamboo poles stuck out of the stone cave in Lulang Mountain. Taking advantage of the northwest wind, it stabbed the sun. With a “click” sound, the sun couldn't hit, but the golden hook that lifted the sun was broken. As the sun falls from the sky to the ground, the sun goes out. At this point, it was so dark between heaven and earth that they couldn't see five fingers. People panicked in the darkness, and the earth became a terrifying hell.
Kaka's trick is amazing, so happy! It climbed up from the pool of water and made a strange “fine” laugh. There was strange laughter there, and people there cried and shouted “mom.” In this dark hell, Kaka ate quite a few people and killed quite a few people.
At the time, there was a young couple living in a cave under a mountain. Her husband's name was Guilong, a smart and capable man, and his wife was named Gui Feng, a beautiful, good-natured young woman. The couple were very sad to hear people crying and screaming. So the couple went dark to find people to find a way to find the sun, and they wanted to hang the sun in the sky and illuminate the earth again. But no one came up with a solution. Guilong only thought of a way: to attach bamboo poles to each other, build a bamboo ladder to extend straight to the sky, and let the men hold it under the bamboo ladder. But Guilong also said, “If a bamboo ladder were made, we climbed into the sky and found the golden hook chain that originally hangs the sun, but we don't have a gold chain, how can we connect the sun?” Gui Feng rushed to answer, “Isn't the twine we made ourselves very strong? We roll the twine into a twine and use twine to connect the golden hook chain. Everyone heard it and thought it made a lot of sense. In this way, women would rub twine to make twine. They're messing around, Minoru! It took 981 days to twist a very long twine. Guilong went to the sky to find the golden hook that originally held the sun, and Guifeng looked for the sun on the ground. When the couple agreed on who would find the Sun and the Golden Hook, they knocked on a bow (a type of Buyei percussion instrument) and told the people on the ground.
Guilong went dark in the sky looking for a golden chain. From the southern part of the world to the north of the world, he couldn't find anything. The sky was empty, and the cold wind, like a sharp sword, scraped Guilong's skin in pain like a sword. In order to find a golden chain to hang the sun and solve Li Min's disaster, he continued his search in the dark.
However, it was said that Guifeng was carrying a hat on his back and looking for the sun, climbing mountains and crossing rivers, looking everywhere, and had swollen feet, and couldn't even find it. She kept looking, looking! Finally, she was able to find the sun under Mount Lang. After she tied the sun with twine, she knocked on it a few times to report the news to everyone. The sound shook the valley and shook all the dust on the earth.
However, as soon as the sound of a whistle sounded, I was able to hear it. I quickly followed Guifeng's voice to find Guifeng. Using his long, sharp teeth and long nails, he grabbed Guifeng's chest and removed a bag of embroidery needles hanging from Guifeng's chest from the Sun. Guifeng was caught with blood flowing. Her heart fluttered out of her chest and fell to the ground. It got eaten by Kaka like this.
In the sky, when he heard the sound of “winter... winter...” coming from the ground, he knew that his wife had found the sun and was in a hurry to lift his spirits. He touched the west to the west, and finally found the original golden hook chain hanging the sun at the end of the west. After he tied the rope to the golden hook chain, it sounded and then slipped down the bamboo ladder along the head of the rope.
People were so happy to hear the sound of two beeps on the ground. When Guilong slipped to the ground with the rope head, everyone caught the rope and pulled it hard. The sun finally reached the sky, but it was really bad. The sun didn't shine. People spent the effort of the two tigers in Kowloon. Some people said, “It was a waste of effort,” and one by one became discouraged. However, Guilong was not discouraged; for the second time, he climbed up the bamboo ladder to see the sky.
When Guilong got to the side of the sun, he reached over and touched the sun. “Oops,” the sun was as cold as ice. It turned out the sun had fallen to the ground. It got cold because the light went out. Guilong estimated that the sun wasn't dead; it was probably cold. I had to find a way to heat up the sun, so I went back to the ground and brought the stove and sledgehammer to the sky. Putting the sun in the stove to refine it, hitting the sun with a big hammer and making a roaring sound, shook the sky for thousands of miles, and formed the thunder we heard today. He hit, hammer, and fire formed the lightning we saw today. The fire smokes Guillong's eyes, and tears all dripped out, forming the rain we saw today. He's still training and fighting! When sparks burst forth, they formed the stars we see today.
Under the refining of Guilong, the sun finally shines. People danced with joy when they saw the sun shining brightly. Kaka suddenly saw the sun and was blinded by the embroidery needle on the sun's body. He touched east and west and started crying. He couldn't find his way back to the waterhole, and was punched and kicked by people, which just knocked him to death.
Since the sun hangs from the sky with twine rope grown by the Buyei people themselves, although the twine is thick and strong, it only withstands the sun's flames and breaks for half a day, and the sun still sets. As a result, Guilong simply stayed in the sky to keep the sun, wait for the sun to burn off the twine and then fall, and he tied the sun back to the sky. As soon as the sun went down, the earth became dark. Guilong tied the sun to the sky, and the earth lit up again. This is today's day and night.
As soon as we look at the sun in the sky today, it seems like there are a lot of needles piercing our eyes. That is, when Gui Feng was grabbed by Kaka on the ground, an embroidery needle fell on the sun's body and turned into one. Guifeng's chest was torn apart by a card. After her heart fell to the ground, it grew into a flower. This flower is the sunshine flower we see now. It is Guifeng's heart and liver, so it always faces the sun. Once the sun gets there, the sunflower faces there, and I really can't bear to leave! No wonder today's Buyei people all call the sun and the sunflowers “Dan Wan” (Buyi voice).
