 From the beginning of July to the night of July 15 of the lunar calendar, it is a time for young men and women of the Buyei ethnic group to share their love ears. They sang in a love song: After dinner, the moon is bright, the sister-in-law and lover in front of the village share their feelings and sing in the night sky against each other.
Shunfeng Earbuyi says that it is made similarly to Huqin's piano, except that it has two handsets (can also be used as a microphone). When using a cotton thread about 40 meters long, both sides straighten the handset. One side speaks or sings with the mouth facing the tube, and the other side makes a weak sound, but you can still hear it clearly when you listen closely.
On the night when the moon stars were scarce, the young girls went to the village in groups of three or five to sing songs to express their love.
There is a story circulating among the Buyei people about Shunfeng Er:
Legend has it that a long time ago, there was a Bouyee girl named Sister Fang. When she was born, due to her poor family situation, the whole family made a living by selling songs, so her parents named her Sister (Buyee language, instant singing).
When my sister was * years old, she looked like a flower and had a good voice. Because of her good-looking appearance and sweet singing voice, young men came to see her almost every day (Buyi, that is, talk about love), but she said to every young man, “My parents won't let me go far. From the beginning of July to the night of July 15 in the ancient calendar, if anyone can make a handset to sing with me, I will become friends with whoever I make, but the handset I made must be a few dozen bamboo poles away from me.
Many young men couldn't fulfill the conditions mentioned by their sister. They all had to walk away in dismay, but they didn't stop. These two young men, one named Dayun, and the other called Da Ding, did not stop.
When Da Yun got home, he thought about it all night, and finally came up with a solution. He cut down more than 50 bamboo trees and connected them. It took a lot of effort for his sister to split a song with her sister, but her sister couldn't lift the bamboo stick, nor did she fail with Da Yun against Ge Da Yun.
But he said that on his way home, Da Ding saw an old father who was blind playing the huqin on the side of the road, and Da Ding watched... Suddenly, in his heart, he hurriedly went back home to find half a bamboo tube, cut it into two cylinders, then draped the snakeskin on the bamboo tube, kept the other end of the bamboo tube and made it like this. Then, on the night of July 15 in the ancient calendar, when the moon was more than one foot tall, Da Ding ordered lightly The fluttering handset came to pair with her sister, and the singer shared the handset. It was lightweight, compact, and full of praise. They both sang all night long, and later their sister split up I became close friends with the clever Da Ding Shing ever since then, people have called this handset Shun Da Dao (in Buyi, that is, Shunfeng Er).
After that fight, Shunfeng Er became a communication tool for young Buyei boys and girls. No wonder today's infatuated boys and girls sing this in a love song: Life must be connected to death, don't be afraid if parents are strict, if the two of us have trouble meeting, we can have a relationship (Shunfeng Er).
