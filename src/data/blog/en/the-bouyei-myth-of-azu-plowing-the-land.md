 It was a groundbreaking battle, and the floods were raging, and the whole world became a vast ocean. It took 7 749 days for the heavy water to disappear, yet large areas were still submerged in water, endless, like a large rice field with no end in sight.
The water in this large rice field is deep enough to grow crops. People only catch some fish and shrimp to eat, and barely survive.
Bu Yi's son Azu thought to herself that catching fish every day is not a long-term solution; you have to find a way to get rid of the water. This Azu is a hero in Buyi. He is as tall as a mountain, his eyes are like hoarding a basket, and he is so strong that he can move the mountain away. He brought his own buffalo. This buffalo is also different: it has a long back, and its horns live like a mountain peak. When it makes a sound, it's just a string of thunderstorms, and its breath is just a cloud of fog. Azu picked up the buffalo and “crackled” the whip, driving the cows to plow up the field.
As soon as his plow was inserted, it was at least a step deep. As soon as the plow passes, it becomes a deep ditch, and all of a sudden the water from the dam flows down the ditch. The ditch was filled with water, and the water in the dam quickly receded. The mud that was turned over by the plow was piled up, then it became a rolling mountain; the water covered by the mud turned into a dark river where the sky was invisible.
Azu plowed the west and ploughed the east, plowed round and forth, and finally plowed all the earth. He was very happy to see the mud rolling over the dam. He couldn't even breathe, and picked up wood to rake the dirt. As soon as the wooden rake passed, a flat plain appeared on the ground; where it had yet to be raked, the mountains still undulated. Azu kicked the cow and raked it in full force. Suddenly, there was a “click”, like the sky falling apart. It turned out that the cow's leash was broken. The buffalo, which was struggling to move forward, fell to the ground in one shot and knocked the ground all over. It fell on the east side. The earth suddenly crooked to the east, and the water accumulated in the furrow immediately flowed down the ditch towards the east. These ditches have become rushing rivers.
As soon as Azu saw that the cow's leash was broken, there were still many places where he hadn't raked, so he let the cow rest and went to Fujiko to make a cow rope himself. When I knew Azu had gone, I didn't know where Azu had gone. The buffalo turned to graze everywhere, and fell asleep on the ground when they were full. It slept for years and didn't wake up, and the wooden rake grew bacteria; the cows and cubs under it all had young, and it was still sleeping. I later heard that it wasn't sleeping; it had become the largest and tallest mountain.
There are now mountains, rivers, and the Yin River on the ground. Some places are flat, and some places have many mountains. This is what Azu left behind by plowing the fields.
Narrators: Wei Shaozhen, Wei Shaojing, etc.
Collector and organizer: Man of Accumulation Wan Ji Bang Jun
From “Buyei Folk Tales”, edited by the Institute of Literature, Guizhou Academy of Social Sciences, 1982 edition, Guizhou People's Publishing House
