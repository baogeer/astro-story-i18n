 In the old days, there were two brothers and sisters. The older brother's name was Di Jin, and the younger sister was Di Ying.
At that time, people all over the world were very diligent, picking up dung crops every day; brothers and sisters, Di Jin and Di Ying, were also very diligent, yet they only had a bunch of gourds.
This bunch of gourds only has one vine, and only one gourd has grown on this vine. The two brothers and sisters are picking up feces every day. After a period of 981 days, the gourds are ripe. What a big gourd. It looks like a small hoard basket! The two brothers and sisters were so happy that they hollowed out the gourd and sat inside. Hi, just two people sitting!
At this point, it suddenly started raining heavily. It continued to rain for 7, 7, 49 days. After all, the place was flooded. The two brothers and sisters sat down in the gourd. When the water rises, the gourd gets taller. The two brothers and sisters were sitting in a gourd swimming and floating. Seeing the ocean on all sides, there were no mountains, no houses, let alone no people, and they sighed with sadness.
I don't know when a rat got into the gourd. Hearing these two brothers and sisters sigh, the rat said, “Don't be sad. Let me go down and punch a few holes in the ground to let the water go away, and everything will be fine.” It digs into the bottom of the water to drill a hole, and soon all the water is gone. Now you can see large and small hydrants everywhere; that's when mice hit them.
After the water is gone, there will be only two brothers and sisters left in the world. The older brother said to the younger sister, “Little sister, now there are no people in the world; we are left with two brothers and sisters. We need to get married and get married, making people fuming.”
The younger sister refused and said, “We are brother and sister. There are only golden roosters and phoenixes; how can a brother or sister get married!”
My brother said, “Then let's just depend on God's will! Let's look for two mills. You roll down the mountain on this side, and I roll down the mountain on the other side. If the two mills go together, then heaven promised us a match; if they don't fit together, then they won't.”
My sister agreed to do this. My brother found a pair of grinders and handed one to my sister. They picked up one by themselves. The two of them each rolled down the top of the mountain on one side. After the mill rolled down, they definitely got together, and the two brothers and sisters had no choice but to get married.
Later, my younger sister gave birth to a meatball with no eyes, no nose, no ears, and no mouth. When the two brothers and sisters got angry, they cut it into over 100 pieces and scattered it in all directions. The next morning, smoke was popping up from all sides; it is said that human smoke was created in this way.
When cut into pieces of meat, the shell became the Buyei; the heart became Han, so the Han people had to be crafty; the intestines became the Miao people, so the Miao people should be more loyal.
The meatballs were cut into over 100 pieces, so they later became the family name of the people.
Narrator: Zhao Siyi (Buyei)
Selected from “Selected Myths of Ethnic Minorities in China” by Gu Deming, 1984 edition, Northwest University for Nationalities Research
