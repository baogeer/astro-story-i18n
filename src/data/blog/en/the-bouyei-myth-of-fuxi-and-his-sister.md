 Legend has it that in ancient times, there were twelve suns, and they bathed the earth at the same time. Oops, it was so hot back then that I was really annoyed. If you think about it, the rocks have all been sunburned. No matter how much anyone in the world can stand it, people hide in caves. It's only at night that they dare to come out and do something. However, the night was all covered in black paint, and people couldn't do anything they wanted to do. What should I do? If it continues like this, everyone will have to wait to die. While everyone was worried, someone suggested that Fuxi's brother and sister go shoot at the sun because they both have the greatest strength. They sent someone to talk to the Fuxi brothers and sisters. The Fuxi brothers and sisters agreed; however, they asked everyone to give them a plot of land. At that time, all the fields were in Thor's hands; if you wanted a field, you only wanted the same god of thunder. So everyone went to discuss it with Thor. In other words, Thor finally agreed.
Once the agreement was made, the Fuxi brothers and sisters went to build weapons. They made the best trees into bows and the hardest iron into arrows. Then I made a few more litres of glutinous rice, ready to eat when hungry on the way. Everything was almost ready. A hundred year-old told them, “There are two trees on the other side of the Yellow River. One is called the Marsan tree, and the other is the Huasang tree. There are two tree spikes at the top of the sky; you have to go up to the sky; you have to pass through there.”
Brother and sister Fu Xi memorized the old man's words in their hearts. Before the sun had come out, they ran across the river, quietly climbed a tree, and went to the sky.
They spread a bow in the sky, carried arrows, stood firm, and waited for the sun to come out. Three of them popped up all of a sudden. The Fuxi brothers and sisters saw it, released three arrows in a hurry, and shot down those three suns. The sun in the back continued to come out, and that was very fast. Fortunately, Brother Fu Xi was ready. He fired seven arrows in one fell swoop and shot seven suns down. There are still two suns left that are frightened and will not come out. It was at this point that Taibai Bai Xing came to persuade the Fuxi brothers and sisters, and told them to leave these two behind no matter what, saying that one light would grow the grain; the other would weave. Only then did the Fuxi brothers and sisters take advantage of Gong Li and turn things around. Those two suns left behind are today's sun and moon. The moon is frightening. Her face is always white, and she always doesn't come out until night.
According to the previous agreement, the Fuxi brothers and sisters went to Thor to get a field. I don't know if Thor turned his face down and didn't accept the account. He said that he had only promised them a piece of soil, so he pointed out a sandbag for the Fuxi brothers and sisters. The Fuxi brothers and sisters were furious and said to Thor, “If anyone falls into trouble in the future, we won't save them.”
Thor smiled and said, “The sun has all set. How do humans want you to save?”
Fu Xi's brother and sister are too angry and plan to take revenge on him. But they didn't expect that even the poor would suffer as a result of this retaliation. As a result, they both committed one crime.
This is how it happened because they both made up their mind to take revenge on Thor, and the next day they climbed up to the sky to find Rain God. They said to God of Rain, “Rain God, it's been so dry that there's no water left to raise the seedlings. Why don't you put some water down?” When the god of rain believed it to be true, he removed the water hyacinth from the wall and poured a drop into the world. Suddenly, it started raining heavily. At this point, Fuxi's sister took advantage of the rain's carelessness. She had snatched a water hyacinth in the past and fell downwards from the bottom of the sky.
The god of rain is busy and wants to compete for water hyacinth. But it was too late because the water ran out. Oh my gosh, this is no joke, because as soon as it fell, it rained heavily for seven days and seven nights. The floods filled the sky, and even the spikes of the horse sang trees and the Chinese sang trees were submerged in water. Everyone in the world has drowned.
The god of rain has reported this for too many platinum stars. Taibai Jinxing lost his temper. He ordered the Fuxi brothers and sisters to be tied up. Because it was a gourd robbed by Fuxi's sister, she was very annoyed that she was tied up. In addition to being strangled around his waist, he also has an iron chain around his neck. So today we see every Buyei woman with a belt around her waist and a necklace around her neck, from that time.
Taibai Jinxing decided to burn them to death and set off a blaze in front of them. Brother and sister Fu Xi were afraid. They knelt down and said hello to Taibai Baixing, and vowed to him, “We are willing to get married, have children and give back to the world.”
Taibai Jinxing brushed her beard. After thinking about it, she agreed. He sent down a few big gods and stabbed seven holes in the ground. When the water ran out, he called the Fu Xi brothers and sisters Xiafanlai.
Did you know that Fuxi's sister is an idiot. She coaxed over the Platinum Star. In fact, when she reached the world, she didn't want to get married, so she went to the Mount Emei Temple to practice the path. Brother Fu Xi couldn't help looking for her; he was in a hurry and worried. Later, Fuxi's brother also found Mount Emei. In front of Mount Emei Temple, he met a water ghost Taoist. Brother Fu Xi asked the Water Ghost Taoist, “Is there a girl coming to the temple?”
The Water Ghost Taoist nodded and didn't speak or look at him. Brother Fu Xi then explained to the Taoists why he was looking for a girl. When the water ghost Taoist listened, he said, “You wait outside.” Speaking of which, I became a giant python and dived into the temple. Soon, I only heard a scream coming from the temple. Looking up, Fuxi's sister was running out of the temple and was caught by Fuxi's brother.
The brother and sister went home, and Fu Xi's sister refused to get married at all. When they walked to the intersection and met a fir tree, Fu Xi's sister said to her brother, “If you say yes to this cedar tree, we'll get married.”
Brother Fu Xi couldn't do it; he had to go ahead and shout at the cedar tree, “Fir tree, fir tree.” The cedar tree did not respond. When Fuxi's brother shouted for the third time, the cedar tree suddenly answered. The voice was “Hike, Hike.” Turns out there was a nest of birds on the cedar tree. “Hike, chirp” was sent out by the birds being shocked. The brother and sister couldn't see the bird; they just thought it was Sugi, and the younger sister had nothing to say.
When she got home, Fu Xi's sister still refused to get married. She also said to her brother, “Tomorrow we will each carry a mill to the head of the slope. If the two flat mills roll to the foot of the slope and can fit together, we will get married.”
Brother Fu Xi was embarrassed. But he immediately thought of an idea. At night, I quietly carried the two grinding wheels together and set them up at the foot of the slope. The next day, he and his sister went brushing. As soon as they put one of their mills on the top of the slope, the two mills rolled along the slope all the way to the foot of the slope. The brother and sister ran over to take a look. The two mills fit well and were placed at the foot of the slope. When Fu Xi's sister died, she didn't know if her brother had set it up in advance; in fact, I didn't know where his two rolling grinds had gone. Sister Fu Xi had nothing to say.
The two of them got married. Soon, a baby was born. This cub is a mindless blob of meat. The Fuxi brothers and sisters were very angry when they saw it, so they cut up the pieces of meat, cut them up, and then scattered them. I don't know, where those rotten pieces of meat fall, fireworks are set up there, and where fireworks are set up, there are people. Since then, there have been people.
Narrator: Zhu Dengyong (Buyei)
Selected from “Selected Myths of Ethnic Minorities in China” by Gu Deming, 1984 edition, Northwest University for Nationalities Research
