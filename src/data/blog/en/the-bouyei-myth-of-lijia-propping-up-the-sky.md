 In very ancient times, heaven and earth were only three feet three inches and three minutes apart. In spring, the shell of the horse touches the sky; when digging the ground, as long as you use a light force, the hoe also touches the sky; you can only hold the flat load of water on your side; otherwise, you also have to touch the sky; when people go to work, they bow their bodies all day long, and they can't even stretch their hips.
Heaven and earth are so close that it's not convenient to do anything, so everyone complains. At that time, there was a descendant named Rikou. He had thick eyebrows and big eyes. He had thick waist and round arms, and was nine feet, nine inches long. He was very strong. Ninety-nine rhinos couldn't match him. Like everyone else, Lika has been struggling all day long. Not only does it cause foot pain and leg pain, but the skin on her back has been rubbed off by the sky. Seeing that everyone was complaining, he couldn't help it, so he pulled up his sleeves and said to everyone, “Stay away and let me hold the sky higher.”
When he finished speaking, he held up the sky a bit hard, but the sky and the ground were only shaken by him a few times; they didn't hold up. He also said to everyone, “I don't seem to be able to do it alone. I see it this way. You are all prepared with hoes and flat arms, and I am also working hard. When the time comes, everyone will support the sky together.”
After speaking, Ricard went to eat three stones, three buckets, three liters of glutinous rice, drank three pots and three bowls of glutinous rice wine, and slept for three days and three nights. On the fourth day, I got up and stretched out, and my muscles were strained all over my body, making a “loud” noise. Afterwards, he called everyone to help him get along. Everyone gathered, and they all held up the sky with their hoes. They cheered up and shouted “1 - 2 - 3.” The crowd shouted “Hi” and made concerted efforts to support them, so they held the sky up more than three feet. However, Li Ga felt that the sky wasn't strong enough, so he said to everyone, “It's not OK if a genius is this tall. You all work together to hold up just now, let me change my breath, and try my best to hold it up a little bit.”
When Ricard finished speaking, he took a hard breath. Banyan leaves, cotton leaves, camellias, and oleander were all sucked into his stomach. His eyes were bulging the size of a sea bowl, and his muscles swelled as thick as a bamboo shoot. With a “wake up!” He worked hard to hold the sky upward with both hands; the sky was held up to a height of 99,999 feet, and the ground was pedaled to a depth of 99,999 feet.
The sky is holding up, but unfortunately I can't stay there; it will collapse as soon as I let go. What should I do? After thinking about it, Rikar held the sky with her left hand, pulled out all of her teeth with her right hand, and used her teeth as nails to nail the sky. After that, the tooth that was nailed to the sky turned into a sky full of stars; when the blood shed when the tooth was pulled out, it became a rainbow.
Li Ga worked tirelessly. He was always working hard, and when he was tired, he was panting and sweaty again. When he gasped, it became wind; when he was careless, it became rain; as soon as he was careless, the flower pattern on his head fell, and it became a galaxy; when he blinks his eyes, it became lightning; when he coughed, it became thunder; when he took off his white sweatshirt, it became a cloud.
The sky has stabilized. Unfortunately, there is no sun or moon, there is no light in the world, and crops cannot grow. What should I do? When I thought about it, I didn't hesitate to cut out my right eye with my right hand. When I hung it on the east side of the sky, it became the sun; when I dug up my left eye with my left hand and hung it on the west side of the sky, it became the moon.
Ricard has been busy for 81 days, and everything was arranged before it jumped down with a “thump” sound. When he landed on the ground, the whole earth shook like a boat drifting on the water. The place where he fell was in the east. The terrain in the east tilted 9 feet 9 inches 9 minutes. Since then, the water has been flowing eastward.
While he was busy nailing the sky, Ricard hadn't eaten or drunk for a day in 1981. His teeth had been removed and his blood was exhausted. When he fell to the ground, he fell too heavy. Soon after, he died.
After Lijia died, his large intestine became a red water river, his small intestine turned into a river of flowers, his heart turned into a river of water, his arms and wrists, and a hillside; his bones turned into stones; his hair became woods; his eyebrows became thatch; his ears became flowers; his flesh became fields; his muscles became roads; his toes became all kinds of animals; his fingers became all kinds of flying birds.
Since then, the sky has been higher, the earth has been lower, and the sky and the earth have been far, far apart. The sky had the sun, the moon, the stars, the galaxy, rainbows, clouds, wind, lightning; the ground had mountains, rivers, fields, wells, roads, trees, grass, flowers, animals, birds, cows, and horses.
There are all kinds of things in the world. Everyone is unspeakably happy, and they are very energetic when picking up the crops. Generations have never forgotten the credit for their hard work.
Circulation area: The entire Buyei region
Narrator: Wang Yanchun, Fu, Grandpa Ban Gao, etc. (Buyei ethnic group)
