 In a very, very ancient time, King Pangu opened up the sky and changed the ground again. The world has hillsides, rivers, trees, people, and everything.
One year, there wasn't a drop of rain in a whole year. There was no water in the well, the river ditch and the water tap. The cracks on the ground were like a doll's mouth open. The trees, flowers and plants were dry, the crabs were lying at the bottom of a dry river, frogs were lying on the field, and water snakes were lying beside the ditch. They all died.
At that time, our Buyei had an ancestor named Bujie. His foot stem was bigger than a cotton tree, and his hand stick was as tall as a big banyan branch. He also has good sense of ear and clairvoyance, and is very capable. Bujie often goes to heaven to play, so he knows exactly what bad things the Emperor of Heaven and the gods talk about and do. On this day, Bujie had enough fun in the sky. After opening the Nantian Gate, he waved back to the earth. He looked at the ground and saw large cracks everywhere. He was so angry that disaster struck him everywhere. Bujie scolded in a loud voice: “Everyone in the sky is ignorant. They only know to eat, drink, and have fun all day long. No matter what kind of suffering, it's really abhorrent! huh! I want to go to the sky to find Lei Gong and ask him why it doesn't rain?”
When Buje finished speaking, he went to heaven again. When he walked to the Thor Temple, he saw that Lei Zheng was “snoring” and sleeping. Bujie looked directly at the star in anger. He hurriedly ran to Taishang Laojun's alchemy furnace, grabbed a fire clamp, first clutched Lei Gong's waist bar, then grabbed him by the ear and lifted him off the bed. Lei Gong's ears were sore. When he woke up, he saw Bujie. I wanted to get angry, but my waist bar got caught, so I had to change my voice and say, “Buje, you're here, you're not welcomed!” Seeing that Bujie was flustered and didn't speak, he timidly asked, “Huh! Buje! Why are you scratching my ear?”
Bujie was even more angry, saying, “You only ate and slept, slept and snorted and thundered all day long, but didn't rain on the earth. No wonder everyone said they only heard muffled thunder, and didn't see any rain falling.”
Lei Gong jokingly said, “I've only slept for one day.”
Buje said, “Don't you know? One day in the sky, just one year in the world.” After speaking, Bujie held the fire clamp tightly to Lei Gong's waist bar with his right hand, grabbed his ear with his left hand, and said, “Go! Take a look at the mortal world with me. What a disaster has become like everywhere!”
Bujie dragged Lei Gong everywhere. When they saw him, they all hated him for sleeping and not raining. They rounded him up one by one, grabbed him, pinched him, and wanted to kill him. Bujie said, “If you're not busy killing him, put him in a cage first, so that he can also taste the smell of aridity. Remember, don't give him water to drink.”
Bujie has a son and a girl. The son is Fu Ge. He is 9 years old. The girl is named Xi Mei. The two dolls are young and not very sensible. After Bujie put Lei Gong in a cage, he asked Fu Ge and Xi Mei to guard them and go out to pick wild animals for food.
On this day, Bujie went out to fight wild animals, and Lei Gong said to Fu Ge and Xi Mei, who were standing by the cage, “Little doll, I'm really thirsty. Your brother and sister are good to go. Scoop me some water to drink.” Brother Fu and Xi Mei said, “It's impossible! Grandpa said, “You're too lazy to sleep, don't let it rain, and you don't care about disasters. Right now they just won't give you water to drink, see if you're still sleeping or not!”
Lei Gong repeatedly demanded that Fu Ge and Xi Mei just didn't give him water to drink. Lei Gong was also very cunning. He tried hard to blink his dry eyes, and a small white light flashed out. Brother Fu and Xi Mei met. They thought it was fun and asked, “Duke Lei, what are you up to?” Lei Gong hurriedly said, “This is my baby. If you give me some water to drink, I'll be a better baby to show you.”
Brother Fu and Xi Mei just wanted fun. They forgot what Grandpa said and wanted to get water for Lei Gong to drink. Unfortunately, the weather is dry and the earth is arid. Where can I get water? Lei Gong was very smart. He was busy saying, “It doesn't matter if you don't need water; you can pee for me to drink.” Fu Ge wanted to see the baby's eagerness, so he spilled a bubble of urine for Lei Gong to drink. After Lei Gong drank it, his throat was lubricated, and his eyes weren't dry, so he yawned and the wind blew on the ground. Then he blinked hard a few more times, only to see that the golden light was really nice, and Fu Ge and Xi Mei liked it even more. Lei Gong also said, “This doesn't count. If you pour more pee on me to drink, I'll be a better and better baby to show you.”
Sister Xi also spat on pee for Lei Gong to drink. After Lei Gong drank it, he completely regained his energy. He yelled and heard a roaring thunder; as soon as his eyes twitched, he drew a bright, golden light. Then he tore the cage a few feet and claws and “whimpered” and fled back to the sky. Just after that, Lei Gong went back to the ground and took out a gourd seed from his purse to Fu Ge and Xi Mei and said, “Thank you, my two brothers and sisters, for saving my life. There is nothing I can do to repay you. I want to give you this amazing gourd seed. Remember, after planting it, it will only sprout in 3 years, bloom in 3 more years, and grow a big gourd in 3 more years. At that point, if there is a flood, you can just empty the gourd and lie down inside.” When I finished speaking, I went back to heaven.
When Lei Gong returned to the Leigong Temple in the sky, he slept for nine days. When he woke up, he told Emperor Jade Emperor and all the fairies about how powerful Bujie was, how he dragged him out of his cage, and how people beat him in a mortal world. Everyone listened. Some stuck out their tongues, and others glared. They were very afraid of ordinary people, and even more afraid of Buje's strength. Emperor Yu Huang said, “If we don't kill Bujie quickly, he will know clearly what kind of bad things we say and do, and he will come back and fix us. In this way, we will not be able to sit on the throne, and we will not be able to enjoy the blessings for long.” The fairies listened and all said, “Uh-huh, we have to kill Buje and these mortal people quickly.” Emperor Jade and the immortals thought about it for a long time, and they couldn't think of a good way to kill Bujie and these mortal people. At this point, Lei Gong said harshly, “Let me turn the Tianchi water into a flood and drown all these mortals.” Everyone listened. Qi said, “I want it! I want it!”
Furthermore, Bujie is leading everyone to the east on the ground and plans to go to the East China Sea to find water to drink. Suddenly, he saw Lei Gong dancing and roaring in the sky, thunder roaring, and his eyes blinking, emitting a dazzling flash of lightning; he opened his mouth in the air and became a tornado one by one; he also scooped water with a gourd scoop and poured it to the ground, turning it into rain of bamboo poles. Bujie thought that Lei Gong had changed his mind and that it was raining; he was very happy. However, for nine days and nine nights, the thunder continued, the flashes continued, the wind continued, the heavy rain did not stop, and the floods continued to rise. At this point, Bujie suddenly realized that he said to everyone, “No good, the damn Lei Gong must have taken revenge. Let's all quickly climb to the top of the mountain and hide.”
After another nine days and nine nights, the heavy rain continued. Bujie told everyone to take shelter at the top of the mountain, so he went to the sky to see for himself. When he came to heaven, he saw that Lei Gong only scooped up the water in the Tianchi pond and poured it to the ground one by one. Bujie was very angry. He hurriedly went to the alchemy furnace to pick up the fire pliers, clamp Lei Gong's waist bar, and shouted, “You damn animal! Having enough to eat, drink and do nothing will only hurt all living beings and see me get caught to death!” Lei Gong panicked and quickly knelt down to ask for forgiveness and said, “Grandpa Bujie, please spare me. I don't dare to sleep lazily this time; it will definitely rain on time. I'm no longer afraid to scoop up Tianchi's water and pour it down to harm mortal people.” Bujie clutched it even tighter and scolded, “You damn animal, you talk so nicely. Doing evil is the most poisonous! Do you know that? If three scoops of water from the sky pond were poured to the ground, it would turn into heavy rain for three days and three nights! I won't spare you today!” Seeing that Bujie actually wanted to kill Lord Lei, Emperor Jade was also frightened and shivered. He hurriedly led all the immortals to kneel down and ask for forgiveness, saying, “Hope Prince Bujie will spare Thunder. We all guarantee that in the future, we will definitely call him to throw thunder and rain every March; if you throw gentle thunder in June and July, there will be dragon rain.”
Bu Jie originally wanted to clip the abominable Lei Gong to death. Seeing that Emperor Jade Emperor and the immortals bowed their heads and pleaded for guarantees for him, he thought again: “If he were to be clipped to death, no one would rain in the future, and he wouldn't be able to live in a mortal world.” After thinking about it, I just let go of the fire pliers and point to the heart of Lei Gong's head and say, “OK, look at the face of Emperor Jade Emperor and the fairies. Please spare me this time. You must follow their guarantee in the future to blow the rain on time. If there is a delay, watch me catch you!” Prince Lei also quickly nodded and said, “Grandpa Shebujie is very kind. I will definitely follow your orders in the future, and I will definitely do it.” Therefore, since then, every year in March and April, when there is thunder and bamboo rain, people grow crops with water; in June and July, when there is gentle thunderstorm, the crops are moisturized by rain and dew.
Bujie let go of Lei Gong and plans to return to the mortal world. He opened Nantian Gate and took a look. Oops, the whole earth was covered with water; the flood had already flooded the entrance of Nantian Gate. Bujie went up in flames and went back to the Jinluanbao Temple to find Emperor Jade Emperor and said, “The flood has already flooded Nantianmen. You should quickly find a way to retreat from the flood and save the creatures of the world.”
Emperor Yu Huang led the immortals to take a look. He panicked and shook his faucet and pestle and said, “Let someone take my faucet and hit the pestle, boil the water to the foot of the east to poke a few holes, so that the flood can dry down the cave.” The immortals have listened. Look at me. I'm staring at you. None of them are born. Seeing raging floods, the wave head is bigger than a barn. It already scared the Three Souls and Seven Spirits out of their way. Who dared to go to the foot of heaven on the east side to poke a hole?
Bujie saw the ugly appearance of the immortals sifting bran one by one. He was angry and scolded, “Look at your well-dressed animals. They only know that they can take the Lotus Tower throne, enjoy blessings, and harm people, but they all have no skills, are greedy and afraid of death. They are not as useful as the pigs we feed in the mortal world!” After speaking, he took the dragon head from Emperor Jade and hit the pestle, pointing at Emperor Jade Emperor and the fairies and scolding again: “Animals, look at me!” Speaking of which, he jumped into the huge waves and raging floods and swam straight to the foot of heaven on the east side.
Bujie swam to the foot of the east side of the sky a few times, stabbing and stabbing him with a dragon. He stabbed 981 cave in a row. The more powerful he stabbed, the more powerful he was. He was determined to dry up the flood sooner and save every living thing. Because he used too much force, he couldn't pull it out even if he stuck it in a big rock crevice. As a result, he died in Tianyu on the east side. It is said that after Bujie's death, his hands and feet became four pillars to support the sky, standing on the edge of the East China Sea; his mouth became the Grand Palace of the Dragon King in the East China Sea; and his teeth became white jade pillars standing in the palace.
Although Bujie died, he stabbed 81 holes, but the flood was able to flow away smoothly. After nine days and nine nights, the flood had dried up, and the earth appeared. Unfortunately, the earth is already desolate, and there are no more people.
The big tree has two branches, one branch after another. After talking about the heavy floods, let's talk about brother and sister becoming a family.
Furthermore, since Fu Ge and Xi Mei peed for Lei Gong to drink that year, Lei Gong broke the cage and brought a giant gourd seed from the sky to teach their brother and sister to plant it, of course, it only took 3 years to germinate, 3 more before it blossomed, and in 3 more years they formed a huge gourd. At this point, Fu Ge was 18, and Xi Mei was 17. Also at this time, Lei Gong roared and bombarded the sky. His eyes flashed with flashes of fire in the blink of an eye, and his breath turned into a tornado, scooping up Tianchi's water and falling to the ground, causing huge floods. Brother Fu and Xi Mei followed Lei Gong's words and dug a hole in the gourd. After emptying it, they hurriedly went inside to hide. Gourds drifted along with the flood; unbeknownst to me, they floated for many days. When the flood was over, the gourds fell to the ground.
Brother Fu and Xi Mei felt that the gourd was no longer floating, so they drilled out of it. Once they took a look, oops! The world is deserted; all people have been drowned, and all the birds and animals have died. The brother and sister couldn't express their sorrow and burst into tears in a hurry.
When Fu Ge and Xi Mei were crying so sad, Taibai Xingjun in the sky and drove a colorful cloud with a pestle in front of them and said to them, “Fu Ge, Xi Mei, now the humans and birds in the world have all been drowned by the flood. In order to reproduce future generations, you two brothers and sisters should get married.”
When Fu Ge and Xi Mei listened, they cried so much that they refused to get married. They said to Taibai Xingjun, “Old people, our Buyei people don't want brother and sister to become a family.”
Taibai Xingjun said, “In order to have children and grandchildren, let's just do it this time. In the future, I will follow your Buyi rules. Brother and sister won't be close.”
Brother Fu and Xi Mei still refused; Taibai Xingjun thought of an idea. He took out a needle and a thread and said, “OK, Fuggo is holding this thread, and Xi Mei is holding this needle. I'll count 1, 2, 3. You two throw it in the air at the same time. If the thread can get into the needle's eye, then you'll become a couple.” Brother Fu and Xi Mei quietly discussed and said, “It's hard to get through the eye of a needle even if you have a needle and thread in your hands. If I throw it in the middle of the air, how can I wear it?” So I agreed. When Taibai Xingjun finished counting one, two, and three, the brother and sister all threw the needle in the middle of the air. At this point, Taibai Xingjun gently breathed a sigh of fairy spirit, only to see that the thread went straight into the needle's eye. Taibai Xingjun asked them to get married, but they still refused.
Taibai Xingjun came up with another solution and said to them, “Fuggona rolls down Nanshan Mountain with a stone mill, and Xi Mei rolls down one from Beishan Mountain. If the two mills are combined, you will become a family.” Brother Fu and Xi Mei quietly discussed again, “If we roll one towards the south of Nanshan Mountain and the other one is lost towards the north of Beishan, the mills won't roll together.” After the discussion was over, they agreed to Taibai Xingjun's approach. When they were rolling stones from the north and south, Taibai Xingjun gently used one finger, only to see the two grinders rolling smoothly from the two mountains together. Taibai Xingjun called them to get married again, but they still refused.
Taibai Xingjun shaved off his white beard and thought of another way. Pointing at the mountain in front of him, he said to Fu Ge and Xi Mei, “Let's do this, you two run a circle around this mountain. One is in front and one behind. If the one in the back catches up with the one in the front, you'll become a family.” Fu Ge also quietly discussed with Xi Mei, “Xi Mei, if you can't run fast, just run in the back; I run violently. Run ahead. In this way, you won't be able to catch up with me.” After the discussion was over, they agreed to Taibai Xingjun's approach. I started running around the mountain. Fu Ge ran briskly and violently in the front, and Xi Mei didn't move a single step in the back. At this point, Taibai Xingjun gently blew another breath of fairy spirit on Fu Ge's back. Fu Ge was driving like a cloud. His body fluttered lightly and couldn't stop. All of a sudden, he went around the mountain and ran behind Xi Mei. At this point, Taibai Xingjun shouted, “Xi Mei, please look back and see which one is here?” When Xi Mei heard the cry, she turned around violently and took a look. She just met Fu Ge. Taibai Xingjun laughed and said, “Look what else you guys have to say? If you talk, you have to count! Let's get married soon.”
The Fuxi brothers and sisters were no longer able to escape; they had to become relatives. Taibaek Seong-kun also went back to heaven.
Half a year after the Fuxi brother and sister became parents, they gave birth to a bunch of meat with no hands or feet. They were so angry that they cut the pieces of meat into 99 pieces with a wooden knife and threw them all over the place. As soon as I got up the next day and watched, Butuo Tuo all became people. Every person who changed to meat became a stockade, and each shaizi had the same surname; the person hanging from the peach tree had the surname Tao; the one hanging on the plum tree had the surname Li; and the one hanging on the poplar tree had the surname Yang. Ninety-nine blocks of meat became 99 shaizi, and the 99 shaizi became 99 surnames. With the addition of Fuxi's surname, it became the family name of the people.
When Fu Ge and Xi Mei gave birth to future generations, they said to future generations, “Taibai Xingjun has promised that our brother and sister will only become friends this time. From now on, you must remember not to have a family with the same surname, let alone open a family!” So until now, the Buyei have followed this rule.
Unfortunately, since Buje died, no mortal has been able to go to heaven.
