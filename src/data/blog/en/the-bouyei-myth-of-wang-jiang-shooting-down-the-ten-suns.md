 In ancient times, there were 12 suns in the sky. The climate was hot, and there was also a major drought. There was no rain for several years, rivers and seas dried up, fish became extinct, and even the stones quickly became sunburned. Humans at that time couldn't live at home; they all took refuge in caves, so the emperor issued an order ordering the luckiest person in the world to destroy the sun and save humans.
There was one named Wang Jiang, who was both smart and brave, and came forward. The people saw that Wang Jiang was going to destroy the sun, and everyone was happy, so they sent representatives to say to Wang Jiang, “If you actually knock the sun down and save us, we will give you half of all our assets as a reward.”
Wang Jiang then worked hard to set down all ten suns. There are only two suns left in the sky, the current sun and moon. Naturally, the weather was cool, and people moved back home to live.
However, the people all broke their promises. No one wanted to give Wang Jiang anything. Wang Jiang was extremely angry and came up with a plan of revenge. He traveled all over the world, and finally came to the south to find a gourd seed and take it home to grow.
Soon the gourds grew very big, and Wang Jiang secretly said, “This is my time to take revenge on you.” Speaking of which, he brought a dog to Tanaka to cultivate fields. Tenjin saw dogs cultivating the fields and thought that the people were asking for rain, so it started raining heavily. Floods flooded everywhere, drowning everyone in the world.
But Wang Jiang took his little sister in advance, hid in the big gourds he had planted, and let the water drift, so he didn't die.
At this point, Duke Lei took a stand and ordered their brother and sister to get married and pass on the race. The little sister thought that marriage between brother and sister was a custom that had never been seen before, and strongly refused.
Little sister fled to the north. Lei Gong asked Wang Jiang to chase to the north; Little Sister fled to the south, and Lei Gong also called Wang Jiang to chase to the south.
When little sister was helpless, she suddenly saw Jin Kui Taoist. Little Sister hurriedly asked him to do her fortune telling, and Jin Kui calculated: “Your destiny is to marry your older brother Wang Jiang.” My little sister was unable to do so; she had to become a couple with Wang Jiang.
Later, my little sister became pregnant and gave birth to a sarcoma. Wang Jiang cut that sarcoma into pieces and scattered it all over the world. After three days, all the crows had turned into humans; since then, people have smoked everywhere.
Wang Jiang also became an ancestor of humans, and her little sister became Our Lady. Today, all 120 people, whether Han or Zhong family, are descendants of Our Lady.
Selected from “Selected Myths of Ethnic Minorities in China” by Gu Deming, 1984 edition, Northwest University for Nationalities Research
