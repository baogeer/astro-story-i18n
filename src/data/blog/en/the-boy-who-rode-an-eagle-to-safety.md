 In the early days of liberation, there was a strange boy named Liu Yi in the mountains of Songzi County. Telling his story will definitely leave you amazed!
In the spring of this year, a few children had a great time looking for flowers and picking grass to catch butterflies in the mountains. While catching butterflies, a child in red accidentally fell on the edge of a cliff, and a red hat on his head flew unbiased onto a small tree growing on the edge of the cliff.
The kid in red got up and reached out to pick it up, but he couldn't get enough and started crying in a hurry.
At this point, a 12-year-old child, slightly older, comforted him and said, “Don't cry, I got it for you.” While talking, I bent down, carefully walked to the edge of the cliff, and walked close to the small tree.
Seeing his bold act, the children exclaimed in unison: “Liu Yi, it's too dangerous, don't take it!”
Liu Yu carefully stepped on a branch, stretched out her arms, and finally got a red hat. When the children were about to reach out and pull Liu Yu up, they didn't want the little branch to withstand the pressure. The “click” broke and fell down with Little Liu Yu.
This sudden change frightened the children. They looked down and looked down; they couldn't see the bottom. They even shouted “Liu Yi! Liu Yi!” I didn't hear back either. They thought Liu Yuzhun had fallen to death and went back to report the letter with a crying face.
Speaking of coincidence, it was a book. Liu Yu, along with a small tree, fell down more than 30 feet. It happened to be an eagle's nest. He fell in the middle of two little eagles. Because the eagle's nest was soft, he actually didn't get hurt at all.
After waking up in a daze, Liu Yu looked up and looked at the bottom. They were all straight walls cut like a knife. This eagle's nest is built on an oblique stone protruding from a cliff. If you climb up, you can climb without a rope, and there are no steps; if you want to go down, it's still more than 50 feet from the ground, and there's no place to set foot.
Liu Yu is in a desperate situation and is in a dilemma.
When the big eagle returned from foraging for food, he saw someone in his nest and was frightened and flew around the cliff. It also couldn't bear the two little eagles waiting to be fed. It circled a few laps or flew close to the hawk's nest, threw the animal meat in its mouth at the hawk's nest, and then flew away again.
Liu Yu received animal meat. Although it was raw, in order to survive, she had to resist swallowing it while tearing the animal meat to feed the kitty.
Time passed day by day. Eagles flew in every day to feed, and Liu Yu lived a wild life day and night with raw meat to fill his hunger. Under his careful care, Kitty grew slowly, and its wings grew longer and stiffer. Every day, it was able to make several turns in the air before flying back to its nest.
Liu Yu looked at the eagle flying in the sky. She was extremely envious. She thought to herself, if I had the ability for a kitty hawk to fly, that would be great!
One day, after feeding, Liu Yu saw a small eagle flying out of the nest. As soon as her claws were collected, she brought out a handful of grass, and she realized, “There is a way!”
He took off his shirt, tore it into a strip and tied it into a cloth rope. When the little eagle flew back to its nest, he tied the cloth rope to each of its feet close to a stronger eagle.
Kitty is familiar with Liu Yu, and doesn't mind him making small moves on himself.
Afterwards, he tied the other end of the rope to his left and right arms separately. Having done all of this, Liu Yu climbed out of the eagle's nest and leapt to the next level.
Kitty was taken aback and hurriedly spread its wings and soared into the air. After all, Kitty had limited strength, and was carrying a child weighing tens of pounds. It flew lower and slower. After a few circlings, it finally fell on a flat ground, out of breath.
Liu Yu was extremely happy and ran home safely.
The family looked for Liu Yu and hadn't seen Liu Yu's whereabouts for many days. They thought he had been buried in a ravine and had been eaten by wild animals. People were crying and crying one by one. Suddenly they saw Liu Yu return home, still holding a life-saving eagle in his hand. They were really overjoyed.
Neighbors and friends were even more surprised to hear that Liu Yu had returned safely and came to visit him. They were even more surprised when they learned that he had escaped death with Kitty Hawk.
