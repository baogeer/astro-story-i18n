 At Peking University in 1917, there was a group of professors. Liang Shuming, 25; Hu Shi, 27; Liu Bannong, 27; Liu Wendian, 27; Lin Shi, 27; Zhou Zuoren, 33; Chen Duxiu, 39; Zhu Xizu, 39... The principal is Cai Yuanpei, 50. The youngest is Xu Beihong, a mentor at the Drawing Research Association, 23 years old. At this age, many people are still “gnawing at the old people,” and their seniors have become masters.
