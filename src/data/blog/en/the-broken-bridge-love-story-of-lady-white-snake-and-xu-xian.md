In the morning, the flowers on the shores of the West Lake are red and green, and visitors rush over the broken bridge. It's really a beautiful picture of the bright spring light. Suddenly, two girls like flowers and jade rose quietly from the bottom of the West Lake. What happened? How can people rise out of the water? It turns out that they are two snake spirits who have been trained into human figures. Despite this, they are not harmful to anyone. Only because they envy the colorful lives in the world, they are under the pseudonym Bai Suzhen, and the other is called Xiaoqing, to visit the West Lake.
On the other hand, God suddenly started his temper. Suddenly, it was raining heavily. Baek Su-jeong and Xiao Qing had nowhere to hide. Suddenly, she only felt that she had an umbrella over her head. She turned around and took a look, only to see a gentle, elegant, and beautiful young scholar holding an umbrella to cover them from the rain. When Baek So-jeong met this young scholar, they both blushed and developed a sense of love for each other. Xiaoqing looked into her eyes and hurriedly said, “Thank you! Please ask the guest official's last name.” The young scholar said, “My name is Xu Xian, and I live by this broken bridge.” Baek Su-jeong and Xiaoqing also hurriedly introduced themselves. Since then, the three of them have often met, and the relationship between Baek So-jeong and Xu Xian is getting better. Soon after, they became a couple and opened a “Bohado” pharmacy. They had a wonderful little day!
Since “Bohetang” has cured many, many difficult conditions, and dispenses medicine to the poor, the pharmacy business is becoming more and more popular, and there are more and more people coming from near and far to seek treatment from Baek Su-jeong. People affectionately refer to Baek Su-jeong as White Lady. However, the prosperity of “Bohetang” and the happy life of Xu Xian and White Lady annoyed one person. Who? That is the Hokai monk at Kanayama-ji Temple. Because people have been cured of their illnesses by the White Lady, there are fewer people who go to Kanayama-ji Temple to burn incense and pray for Bodhisattva. If incense is not strong, the Fakai monk will naturally not be happy. On this day, he went to “Bohetang” again and saw that White Lady was treating people. He couldn't help but feel jealous in his heart. He took a closer look, oops! It turns out that this white lady is not a mortal, but a white snake turned into a white snake!
Although Fa Hai has some minor spells, his mentality isn't right. After discovering the identity of the White Lady, he spent the whole day trying to break up Xu Xian and the White Lady and destroy the “Bohetang.” As a result, he secretly summoned Xu Xian to the temple and said to him, “Your wife is a snake spirit. Break up with her quickly; otherwise, she will eat you!” As soon as Xu Xian heard it, she was very angry. He thought: My wife has a kind heart, and she has a deeper affection for me than the sea. Even if she were a snake spirit, it wouldn't hurt me, not to mention that now that she's pregnant, how can I leave her! When Hokai saw that Xu Xian couldn't be fooled by him, he became annoyed and became angry, so he imprisoned Xu Xian in the temple. In “Bohedo,” the white lady is anxiously waiting for Xu Xian to return. For a day or two, left back, right, etc., the white lady was distraught. Finally, I heard that Xu Xian had been “kept” by the Hokai monk from Jinshan Temple. Bai Niang Zi quickly brought Xiaoqing to Jinshan Temple, made a bitter plea, and asked the Fakai Sea to release Xu Xian. When Fa Hai saw White Lady, she sneered and said, “Bold Snake, I suggest you get out of the world quickly; otherwise, don't blame me for being unkind!” Bai Niang Zi saw that Fa Hai refused to let go. Helpless, she had no choice but to remove the golden chisel from her head, shake in the wind, set off huge waves, and push straight past Jinshan Temple. When Hokai saw the water fill Jinshan Temple, they quickly took off their shackles, turned it into a long embankment, and stopped it outside the temple gate. When the big water rises one foot, the long embankment is one foot higher; when the big water rises one foot, the long embankment is one step higher. No matter how big the waves are, they won't be able to overflow. In addition, Bai Niang Zi is pregnant, and it really is impossible to beat Fa Hai. Later, Fa Hai used fraudulent methods to put Bai Niangzi in a gold bowl and press it under the Leifeng Tower, to vividly separate the loving couple Xu Xian and Bai Niang Zi.
