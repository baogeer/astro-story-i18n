 The love story between Cui Yingying and Zhang Junrui is almost a household name in China due to the widespread popularity of the famous opera “The Book of Western Xiang”. Pursuing its roots, the earliest work describing the story of Cui Yingying and Zhang Sheng was the legend “The Legend of Ying Ying” by Yuan Yi, a writer from the Tang Dynasty. The male protagonist, Zhang Sheng, portrayed in the work is actually an incarnation of the author Yuan Yi. The story of Xixiang is also Yuan Yi's recollection of a poignant journey when she was young. The story of Xixiang by Yuan Yi was originally a romantic tragedy, but after continuous revisions and refinements by writers, it evolved into a romantic comedy where a young man and woman broke through the restraints of etiquette and became family members for the rest of their lives, with the ingenious help of Ya Huan Hong Niang. This ending is very familiar to everyone, but here, we want to go back to the beginning of the story and tell the story of a love tragedy where Cui Yingying's love dream ends in the West.
In the 16th year of Tang Desong's reign, Xiang Guo's Cui Peng died of illness in Beijing. He left behind his wife, Zheng Shi, and an orphan girl, Ying Ying. The mother and daughter were left without support in Beijing, but they had no choice but to help Xiang Guo Ling Shu return to her hometown. When I arrived in Puzhou, I heard people say that the thief Sun Feihu gathered and occupied the mountain on the way, and it was quite difficult to block the road and carry out robberies. The mother and daughter of the Cui family are weak and have weak family resources. There are bound to be many murders and few good luck when going here, so they had to stay at Fugu Temple in the east of the city for a while and slowly make plans again.
To put it bluntly, there is a young scholar named Zhang Junduan. He is 23 years old. He 'had a beautiful, elegant, talented person, read a lot of poetry, had a lonely temperament, and rarely interacted with people except for his poetry rewards. In the spring of this year, he went on a study tour to Puzhou. On an occasional excursion, he saw that it was far from the hustle and bustle of the market. It was quiet and pleasant. He quickly fell in love with it. He simply moved his luggage out of the city hotel, rented a room at Pugu Temple's Xixiangyuan, and decided to stay here for a while, study quietly, and prepare to go to Beijing next year to take the exam. Coincidentally, the house Zhang Sheng lives in is right next to the Cui family's mother and daughter, and there is a low wall in the middle of the courtyard.
On a gentle and pleasant evening, Zhang Sheng was a bit tired from studying, so he put down his books, walked out of his small courtyard, and went to the outer courtyard to enjoy the beautiful scenery. Walking outside the neighboring courtyard, he inadvertently looked into the courtyard, only to see that a world full of spring was still hidden inside. There are rows of willow trees in the courtyard. The fresh breeze blows, and the willow silk style flutters like a poem. A few large flower beds almost fill the small courtyard. The various flowers blooming in the flower beds are in full bloom, and the branches are uneven in height, making the entire courtyard look mixed with flowers. As Zhang Sheng was about to move his steps closer to the half-open courtyard door, he heard a string of female laughter echoing through the flowers. The voice was quiet, but gentle and beautiful. It was like a new warbler from Deya, and the cry was wafting through the flowers and trees. Do women still live here? Zhang Sheng was very surprised. He stopped, but he couldn't help but look around the hospital and wanted to find out.
Inside the hospital was Miss Xiang Guo, Cui Yingying, who was visiting the spring flowers with a red lady. Ying Ying came from a famous family and was educated in strict etiquette since she was a child, so she developed a quiet and elegant girl; however, the matchmaker is smart and lively. Because of her status as a subordinate woman, she doesn't have that many restrictions, and every move reveals her clever, playful, and straightforward personality. When the bride saw a pair of colorful butterflies fluttering up and down in a bunch of peony flowers, she said to Ying Ying, “The young lady looks at them. They are in pairs, left with flower stalks. How happy they are!” In fact, Ying Ying was also staring at the colorful butterfly. After the matchmaker said this, she also felt very embarrassed. She blushed and pretended to be angry: “You little girl are really not shy!” The matchmaker actually understood the young lady's mentality. It was because of her dark heart, which also interfered with all kinds of concerns, and was unwilling to reveal them. She knew that the young lady didn't mean to blame herself, so she didn't talk back; it was just that Miss La went back to the flower bed here.
Zhang Sheng from outside the hospital listened to the conversation between the two women, and his heart was also tickled. The matchmaker took Ying Ying as she walked right into his eye. The first thing that came out of the shadows was a young girl in a short red dress. Her gait was light and had a lively look. Looking at her costume, you can see that she was a servant. Following the girl in red, another girl stepped out. She was wearing a light yellow dress and had a long, slim figure. Under the sunset, she looked like tender willows in the wind, making people feel loved and pathetic. Looking closely, I saw that her face was smoky like apricot blossoms, her eyes were like autumn water, her eyebrows were slightly raised like mountains, and her lips were light like a tangerine. Her expression was faint, not happy, or worried. Looking at it this way, Zhang Sheng couldn't help himself. A few times he wanted to rush over and comfort that charming girl. But in the end, he was a scholar who knew how to read books. He always acted properly and didn't dare to be petty. He finally held back his impulse; he just stood there in a daze and was obsessed. Gradually, the two young girls were getting closer to the courtyard gate. Zhang Sheng was taken aback by surprise. Fearing that she had made a mistake, she left the courtyard in a hurry.
Back in his house, Zhang Sheng never felt comfortable with the young lady next door. He asked the little monk in the temple that it turned out that the girl in yellow was Cui Yingying, the only daughter of the late Xiang Guo.
An accidental surprise made it difficult for Zhang Sheng to calm down. He was forced to endure his thoughts and sit down to read, yet Ying Ying also showed a happy and sad expression in the book, which disturbed his mind. After a long period of time, Zhang Sheng gradually became familiar with the matchmaker who was often out of the hospital. Zhang Sheng was ambitious. The matchmaker saw that he was gentle and elegant, and was still popular, so the two slowly had a verbal exchange.
One day, Zhang Sheng couldn't hold back his inner turmoil and wrote a love poem. The poem expressed his love and longing for Ying Ying. The poem was written, but Ying Ying couldn't see it. After thinking about it left and right, he quietly found the matchmaker who had just entered the hospital and asked her to pass on her heart from generation to generation. The straightforward matchmaker was puzzled and said, “Since I adore my young lady so much, why can't I ask the matchmaker to agree?” This question made Zhang Sheng difficult. Since he was alone and had no parents to decide, he really didn't expect to find a matchmaker to ask for a family! After pausing for a while, he blankly said, “These days, because I miss my young lady, I don't know the taste of food, and I don't know where to do things, and I just can't live. If I were to get married in the middle of a few months and a half, it would be difficult to get results; why not make me a dead pond fish. It's hard to save a fire from far away water, can't you please girl?”
