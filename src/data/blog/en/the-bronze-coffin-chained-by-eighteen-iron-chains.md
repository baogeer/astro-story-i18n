 At the beginning, all the villagers thought it was a monument falling from heaven to bless both sides of the Strait with good weather and rain to ensure that the Yangtze River floods were not repeated. Therefore, since the stone monument was revealed on the riverbed in the center of the Yangtze River, believers from Ten Mile Eight Villages have come to pray every day to burn incense.
However, as the drying up worsened, the situation seemed to have reversed to some extent. There also seemed to be some engravings on the back of the stone monument. The handwriting on it was blurry, and no one knew it, but the engraving in the middle seemed to show that a person was cut to death by five horses... Although the villagers didn't know what that actually represented, they also knew that it wasn't a good sign, so they didn't dare to worship again one by one.
Sure enough, on the 7th day that the stone monument was revealed from the center of the Yangtze River, a giant bronze coffin locked deep in the riverbed by 18 iron chains gradually revealed 1 to 2 centimeters of water. The coffin was extremely huge. It was 45 meters tall and covered with sediment, so it didn't look very real. All the villagers thought it was an unknown sign, and they didn't dare to get too close one by one.
Until half a month later, the dry, dry day suddenly took a sharp turn. After three days of torrential rain, the water in the Yangtze River skyrocketed. The stone monument on the Yangtze River in Nayong Town, connected to the huge bronze coffin locked by a chain, once again sank to the bottom of the river, and never appeared again.
Later, interested people sneaked into the bottom of the river to search, and even hired fishing boats to salvage, without any harvest for several months. It seems that right after that torrential rain, in the same position as back then, a mysterious force has covered everything up, and it no longer exists on earth.
Even in those distant times, when young intellectuals like us were still overwhelmed by the secrets of the Yongzhen Stone Monument and the ancient bronze coffin at the bottom of the river, the old man sitting in the shade of a tree was smoking dry smoke, and it took a long time to say a word. He just said bluntly, “If it sinks, it sinks. The old objects in the Yangtze River originally only belong to the Yangtze River; no one can take them away...”
