 Once upon a time, there was a place far away, where the mountains and clear water were beautiful, quiet and beautiful. There was no smoke or war; therefore, the 100th Peach Festival was held there. After returning to Heaven, she discovered that she was pregnant, and was afraid to let the Jade Emperor know. By the time the Peach Festival came, she gave birth to a child. The gorgeous one named her Colorful Butterfly. Colorful butterflies will laugh for the rest of their lives; they are so cute. In a blink of an eye, the colorful butterfly was 15 years old, and she was so beautiful that she could not hide it, so she told the Jade Emperor about this incident. In one fell swoop, Emperor Jade banished the butterfly from the wonderland and despised it from the world.
After being evicted from Wonderland, the butterfly spent a year wandering outside. She couldn't eat enough, didn't wear warm clothes, and lived a difficult life. On this day, she was tired and sleepy, and suddenly passed out on the side of the road. When she woke up, she found that both of her legs had become fish tails, and she had become a mermaid herself. At this point, she saw a man fish watching her, so the butterfly asked, “Who are you? Where am I?” The man-fish said, “I am the most beautiful man in the mermaid world, and the only son of the mermaid king. Oh right, I forgot to tell you, this is the bottom of the sea, and it's also a mermaid world. You're now the most beautiful person in the mermaid world, so you must hide it!” The butterfly asked, “Why is that?” The man-fish said, “Because my father had a strange disease, which can only be cured by using the blood of the most beautiful mermaid, he has never been found, so he is crazy now, but now you have arrived at this time. Fortunately, you met me, otherwise you would have died a long time ago.” The butterfly said, “Since God made me appear at this moment, it means I can cure your father and king. Trust me!” The man-fish said, “Yes...” the butterfly said, “Trust me, I won't die.” As a result, she walked up to the man-fish father and cut a vein with a knife. Due to excessive blood loss, she passed out.
When she woke up, she found herself in an imperial palace, some handmaids were watching her, and she found that she had turned into a human again and was very happy. At this point, a woman came; she was the queen. The queen said to her, “You've woken up. You've been lying in bed for over a month. For over a month, I've been wondering the question: are you a fairy? Just tell me!” The butterfly said, “Yes! But why would you doubt that I'm a fairy?” The queen said, “A month ago, I was embroidering in front of my window. Suddenly I saw a bunch of colorful butterflies flying all the way to my window. I saw a butterfly holding a person. That person was you. I hurriedly opened the window and put you on the bed. At this point, the butterflies had already flown away.” The butterfly asked, “What does that have to do with whether I'm a fairy?” The empress said, “The emperor has an illness. It turned out like this, and I'd like to try it out.” As a result, the colorful butterfly injected blood into the emperor's body, and the colorful butterfly passed out again. When she woke up, she saw a group of lions. The lion said to her, “Our lion doesn't eat human flesh anymore, but our lion king is sick, and the medicine is a piece of human flesh, so please promise me to treat our lion king with a small piece of meat!” The butterfly said, “OK!” As a result, she cut a piece of meat with a knife, and the pain caused her to pass out again.
When she opened her eyes, she saw the Jade Emperor. “Since I banished you from Wonderland, your mother has been begging me, and she said that you are good and good, so I set up a triple test for you, and you have passed all of them, so I officially announced that in the future, you will be Fairy Butterfly, the leader of butterflies, and I will reward you with something.” The butterfly said, “Thank you, Emperor Jade.”
