 Once upon a time, there was a landlord with the surname Zhu, known as Zhu Yuan, and his daughter Zhu Yingtai was not only beautiful and generous, but also very smart and studious. However, since women were not allowed to study in academies in ancient times, Zhu Yingtai had to lean on the window fence every day and look at the readers who often came and went on the street carrying bookcases. I was extremely envious! Can women only embroider at home? Why can't I go to school? She suddenly asked herself: Yes! Why can't I go to school?
Thinking about this, Zhu Yingtai quickly returned to her room and mustered up the courage to ask her parents: “Dad, mother, I want to go to Hangzhou to study. I can wear men's clothes and look like a man. I won't let anyone recognize me; just promise me!” Zhu Yuanwai and his wife began to disagree, but they couldn't stand the temptations of Britain and Taiwan, so they had to agree.
Early the next morning, as soon as the sky was overshadowed, Zhu Yingtai dressed up as men's clothes with her mistress, said goodbye to her parents, brought a bookcase, and cheerfully set out for Hangzhou.
On the first day of school, Zhu Yingtai met a male classmate named Liang Shanbo. He was highly educated and had a very good personality. She thought: If such a nice person were to be together every day, they would definitely learn a lot and be very happy. Meanwhile, Liang Shanbo also felt very attached to her; he felt like he had seen it right at first sight. As a result, they often agreed on poetry and literature together. They cared and cared for each other coldly and warmly, and held each other shoulder to shoulder, without guessing. Later, the two became brothers and were inseparable all the time.
From spring to autumn, three years have passed in a blink of an eye, and the school year has come to an end. It's time to pack some clothes, say goodbye to teachers, and go back to your hometown. Being in the same circle for three years, Zhu Yingtai is already deeply in love with her brother Leung. Although Liang Shanbo doesn't know that Zhu Yingtai is a girl, he is also very fond of her. They had no choice but to break up in love, and when they got home, they both missed each other day and night. A few months later, Liang Shanbo visited Zhu's family. The results surprised and delighted him. It turns out that at this point, Zhu Yingtai he saw was no longer that beautiful young scholar, but a young and beautiful big girl. The moment they saw each other goodbye, they all understood their feelings for each other, and their hearts were already on the same page.
After that, Liang Shanbo invited someone to go to Zhu's house to ask for a family. But how can anyone else look at this poor scholar? He has already given his daughter Xu to the young grandpa Ma Gong from a rich family. Burton Liangshan felt that all his thoughts were in vain. He died not long after getting sick.
Hearing the news of Liang Shanbo's death, Zhu Yingtai, who had been fighting with her parents against arranged marriages, suddenly became unusually calm. She put on a red dress and walked into the flower stand to welcome her family. The welcoming team played drums and drums all the way, so it was really fun! Passing in front of Liang Shanbo's tomb, all of a sudden, sand and stones flew, and the flower car had to stop. I saw Zhu Yingtai walk out of the car, take off her red dress, slowly walk to the front of the tomb, kneel down and cry loudly. Suddenly, the wind and rain shook, thunder, and the sound of “boom,” the tomb cracked. Zhu Yingtai seemed to see her brother Leung's gentle face again. She smiled and leaped in. Then there was another loud noise, and the tomb closed. At this point, the wind dissipated, the rain cleared up, all kinds of wild flowers swayed softly in the wind, and a pair of beautiful butterflies flew out of the graves and danced freely in the sun.
