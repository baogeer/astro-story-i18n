 Xu Shaojun is a scholar. His family lives on the northern shore of Taihu Lake. He lost both parents when he was a child, and his family was poor, but he was smart, studious, and famous for decades. His uncle and aunt often help him, hoping that he will have a successful career in the future. Later, when his uncle got older and there was less support for him, he reclaimed a wasteland and grew some vegetables to feed himself.
Xu Shaojun often goes to Yingzui Rock by the lake to memorize poems. On this day, Xu Shaojun felt a bit hungry. He wanted to go back to the house to cook. He only heard a few shouts. A middle-aged fisherman shouted at him, “Little brother, book readers are nerve-wracking. Let's buy a fish to make up for ourselves. I had good luck today. I caught a red scaly carp and sold it to you at a bargain price. This is the first time I've caught such a good fish!”
Xu Shaojun didn't plan to buy fish, but I heard this middle-aged man say he had caught a rare fish and walked over to see it unusual. The middle-aged man leaned over the boat and picked up the big carp in the cabin. Strangely enough, this carp saw Xu Shaojun and kept nodding at him. Also, it also has a small half-cut fishing fork on its back. When Xu Shaojun felt compassionate, he paid only a penny to buy this fish. He removed the fishing fork from the fish's back, and when he got home, he took herbal powder, applied it to its wound, wrapped it with a cloth, and gently put it back in the lake.
One night, Xu Shaojun sat under a lamp to fix his clothes, stitching them. His fingertips were pierced with a needle, and the pain made him grin. He thought it would be great if he had a daughter-in-law! The clothes were torn, and others were hungry and brought delicious meals... Thinking about it, he couldn't help but sigh and said to himself, “Not a poor and kind girl, where are you...”
At this point, a knock on the door came suddenly. Xu Shaojun felt puzzled. Who else would come to my house at this late time? He put down his work and opened the door to take a look, only to see a slender and charming girl standing at the door. He stammered and said, “Girls come to my humble house late at night. What is it worth doing?” The girl shyly said, “My name is Hong Ling. I lost my way with my relatives. Can I stay overnight with you?” As soon as Xu Shaojun heard it, he immediately messed up: “This... this...” He looked at the dark paint outside, looked at the girl's poor expression, and finally invited the girl in.
The lone man and the widow shared the same room. It was so easy to get to the next day, but the girl Hong Ling had no intention of leaving; she still had no plans to leave on the third or fourth day. On the night of the fifth day, sleeping until the middle of the night, Xu Shaojun discovered that there were more people around him. He was excited and couldn't help but hug her tightly... Since then, Xu Shaojun and the girl Hongling have lived a sweet life as a couple. Hong Ling gave Xu Shaojun a dollar or two, and asked him to buy a textile machine. She woven the fabric at home, then had her husband sell it in the city to earn some money to pay back to subsidize household expenses. She had a happy and happy life.
In the fall of this year, Hong Ling became pregnant. When she was about to give birth, Hong Ling asked Xu Shaojun to set up two small huts in the back yard of the house, put a bed and a large water tank in the cottage. She also told him not to take a peek when the time came, and asked him to go next door to Cui Ji's small store in town and ask an old lady surnamed Liu to deliver the baby. Xu Shaojun did everything.
On the third night, Xu Shaojun only heard the sound of “fluttering” water coming from the hut, but he was afraid to get close. When it was almost bright, there was a baby crying in the cottage, then Mrs. Liu came out holding a baby. Xu Shaojun looked at his white and fat son and couldn't help but enjoy it. Mrs. Liu helped Xu Shaojun take care of Hong Ling after getting out of confinement before leaving.
However, after being 100 days old, the baby cried and cried all day long, so I couldn't coax him in any way. Hong Ling sighed and walked into the small cottage she had when she had children. After a while, Hong Ling came out with a bright green bead in her hands. She shook the bead in front of the child's eyes, and the child immediately stopped crying. Hong Ling had no choice but to sew a small sachet with a silk cloth and hang it around the child's neck, so that the child stopped crying and making noise. Since then, the child grew up day by day, and was able to eat, drink, and play quietly without having to hang a sachet with beads around his neck. Xu Shaojun wanted his wife to collect the beads, but Hong Ling said, hang them around the child's neck. This is a jewel that can cure all diseases.
Hongling still weaves cloth, and the fabric has been woven in more ways, sold at a higher price, and the days are getting better and better. Slowly, Xu Shaojun didn't study as hard as before. Sometimes he went to the city to sell cloth, but after selling cloth, he actually had to stay in the city for two days before returning. Hong Ling persuaded him to study hard. Xu Shaojun was very upset when he heard it. When he got tired of listening, he took out his alcohol and drank it.
One day after her son turned one year old, Xu Shaojun came back from the city and told his wife that the county official's wife didn't know what disease she had. Many of the physicians weren't good at treating her. The old county official was in a hurry like ants on a hot pot. He asked his master to write a notice and post it at the entrance of the county. The notice said, “Who can cure the county official's mother's illness and get twelve gold rewards. Xu Shaojun read the notice and remembered that his wife had said that the jewel on his son's neck could cure all diseases. Therefore, he came back in a hurry and wanted to get the orb to treat the county official's mother. In this way, he could exchange 12 pieces of gold for later travel expenses to go to Beijing to rush to the exam.
After listening to Xu Shaojun's remarks, his wife advised him not to covet that small fortune. As long as he can study with peace of mind, the travel expenses to Beijing to rush to the exam in the future will definitely be enough for him. When Xu Shaojun heard his wife say this, he sighed and casually picked up a book and read it in silence.
However, as soon as it dawned the next day, Hong Ling discovered that the orb on her son's neck was missing. She looked for Xu Shaojun everywhere, and saw no figure of him. When Hong Ling learned that Xu Shaojun had secretly taken jewels to treat the county official's old wife, she sighed softly.
At dinner time, Xu Shaojun finally limped back. His wife asked him with concern about his visit to the county government office. Xu Shaojun kept his head down and didn't say a word with shame. Asked in a hurry, Xu Shaojun sighed and said, “Maiden, I'm sorry for you...” It turns out that Xu Shaojun took the orb and soon cured the county official's illness. After the county official was very happy, he became greedy. He knew that Xu Shaojun's orb was priceless, so he gave him twelve pieces of gold. Of course, Xu Shaojun didn't agree to give him the orb. The county official made a plan and pretended that Xu Shaojun didn't agree, let him go.
Who knows, Xu Shaojun was taken back by two captors before he walked out of the county government gate. The two trappers knocked down Xu Shaojun to find the orb. Afterwards, the county official stole the jewels from the county government office under the guise of treating his old mother. He was confused for a while, and also treated the county official's mother, so they didn't investigate in depth; they only recovered the orb; they threw him out of the county office with a random bunch.
When the wife learned that Xu Shaojun had lost her jewels, she felt very sad. Xu Shaojun grabbed his wife's sleeve and said, “Ma'am, what happened to you? What's going on with this?”
Hong Ling complained and said, “As of now, I don't have to hide it from you anymore. I was originally the niece of Taihu Longjun, a red-scaled carp fairy in the lake. Xu Shaojun felt sad day and night without eating or drinking, but when the child disappeared, he always cried incessantly, making him even more worried. Xu Shaojun squatted on the ground and cried for a long time. He finally understood. Arriving at his doorstep, Xu Shaojun discovered that his cold house was actually wide open, and the smell of the food was still wafting through.
