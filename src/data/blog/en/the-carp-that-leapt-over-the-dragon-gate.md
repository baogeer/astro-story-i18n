 Miao Gorge, also known as Myokyo Gorge. The two majestic Phoenix Mountains, draining the water and standing in the middle of the river, are fascinating carp jumping dragon gates. They are lively and spectacular. Entering the canyon, the two mountains stand on top of each other. The cliffs are imposing, and the peaks are piercing the sky; strange rock flowers compete against the wall, making the canyon look like a wonderland. This magical and beautiful canyon tells a beautiful and moving story.
Long ago, the townspeople on the banks of the Longxi River, men farmers and women, lived a happy life living and working peacefully. One year, I don't know where a giant yellow dragon flew in and committed many evils. It either called for wind and rain to destroy crops, or swallowed clouds and fog to kill living beings, leaving the entire canyon smoky and not at peace. Every year on June 6, on its birthday, people are even forced to give a pair of boys and girls, ten large scalpers, and 100 pigs, sheep, etc. for it to enjoy. If this were not the case, it would become angry and act evil, and run up to the village to devour people and animals, destroy the countryside, and cause the people of Ninghe River to complain and suffer for a long time.
In Longxi Town, Xiakou, there is a smart and beautiful little girl named Yu Gu. She made up her mind to get rid of this evil dragon. On a few occasions, she went up to Yuntai to look for help from Yuntai Fairy, but she never found it. Still not discouraged, she kept looking. Early this morning, she climbed to Yuntai. The fairy was moved by Yu Gu's sincere spirit. She appeared in front of her and told her, “There is a carp cave thousands of miles away. You can go meet a carp fairy, and she will definitely be able to help you.”
Yu Gu said goodbye to Yuntai Fairy, trekked mountains and water, and went through a lot of hardships. She came to the Koi Cave and found the carp fairy, which explained her intention. Fairy Carp said to Yu Gu, “If you want to do harm for the people, this is a great thing, but you have to sacrifice yourself! Can you do that?” Yu Gu didn't hesitate to say, “As long as I kill the townspeople and exterminate that dragon, even if it goes up to Dao Mountain and the sea of flames, I'm happy to shatter my bones!” Seeing Yu Gu's sincerity and determination, the carp fairy nodded with great satisfaction. She spewed three white springs at Yu Gu. She instantly became a beautiful and strong red carp.
Little Red Carp went up the river and after 7 749 days, they went back to their hometown. On this day, in the early morning of June 6, she changed her appearance. She saw that the townspeople were ready: a pair of boys and girls, ten large oxen, and 100 fat sheep and pigs. The people beat the gongs and drums, like a long dragon walking towards the entrance of the Yellow Dragon. The pair of boys and girls dressed in red dresses in front of them had already cried to tears.
When Huang Long saw people delivering feast meals, he was already salivating and proudly opened his mouth. Just in the midst of a thousand fishing trips, Yu Gu rushed forward and stopped her parents and townspeople and said, “Everyone stop waiting here and let me go clean up this harmful to people.” As soon as I finished speaking, I saw Yu Gu leaping into the water and instantly became a big red carp. It flew straight into the dragon's mouth, and immediately jumped into the dragon's stomach, stabbed east and west, making the dragon's five organs thin. The evil dragon struggled desperately and rolled all over his body, but to no avail, she was finally killed by Yu Gu. However, Yu Gu herself was buried in Huanglong's belly.
Since then, the people of Ninghe have lived a peaceful and happy life. People built a carp temple in the middle of a mountainside in Xiakou to remember Yugu for the people. The story of carp jumping to the dragon gate is still widely spread around the Ninghe River.
