 Carpenter Nima is across the door from a rich man. The rich often bullied carpenters. One day, the carpenter quietly smashed the door of a rich man's house in revenge. The rich didn't see who smashed it; they had to ask the carpenter to redo the gate. The carpenter said, “Good wood is needed to make a large door.” The rich man took out a bunch of wood, just enough to make a door.
Wealthy people spend all day at home doing their accounts. The carpenter came in and asked, “The door is ready, what else do I need to do?” The rich man thought he had leftover wood and said, “Make a window.” The carpenter removed the door that had just been knocked down, made a window, and then asked the rich man what else he needed to do. The rich man thought there was still some wood left over and said, “Make another pot cover.” The carpenter removed the window and made a pot lid. I also asked the rich what else they wanted to do. The rich man said, “Make another casserole lid.” The carpenter removed the lid and made a casserole lid. Finally, the rich man asked what the leftover wood was enough to do. The carpenter said, “You can make a snuff cover.” He took apart the lid of the casserole and made a small snuff cap.
One day, when the sun was about to set, the carpenter held the lid of a snuff bottle and said to the rich, “We have used up all the wood. We are good neighbors, so I don't want any more money.” The rich man grinned and said, “I'll consider paying you my salary in the future. You're a really good carpenter, didn't waste a single bit of wood. Now, hand over all the doors, windows, pot lids, casserole lids, and snuff caps to my housekeeper.” The carpenter seemed very surprised. “Master, what did you say, I made the door out of that wood and asked you what else you wanted to do. You said to make a window, but they didn't give me any more wood. I had to remove the door as a window, then remove the window as a pot cover; remove the pot lid as a casserole cover; remove the pot lid as a snuff cover. Look, the snuff cover is here.” After speaking, the carpenter handed over the small snuff cap to the rich man.
