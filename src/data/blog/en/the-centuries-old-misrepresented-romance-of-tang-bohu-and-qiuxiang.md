Tang Bohu was born in 1470. He was named Tang Yin because his birth coincided with the year of Yin, Moon, and Yin Day, and also named Tang Bohu because Yin was the Tiger. Tang Bohu was smart and intelligent from an early age. He usually interacted with Shen Zhou, Wen Zhiming, and Qiu Ying. All four were talented and well-educated people. The pain of bereavement that followed left him with little energy to face the rest of his life.
Fortunately, there was comfort and encouragement from friends such as Wen Zhiming, and Tang Yin, who was extremely depressed, gradually stood up from the ruins of despair and tried to get stronger by picking up poetry and books again.
