 Once upon a time, there was a family where only two grandparents and grandchildren depended on each other and depended on cutting firewood for a living.
One day, my grandfather went up the mountain alone to cut firewood. He said, “Your grandparents come to my mountain every day to cut firewood. Today I'll let you guess a few words. If you guess correctly, you and your two can go to my mountain to cut firewood as usual in the future, but if you can't guess, I'll kill your head.”
The gentleman said, “What is tall is tall; what is growth; what is fat; what is bitterness.”
After listening, Grandpa answered, “You can't grow tall; you can't grow as long as Yunnan Province; you can't grow fat pigs that have been raised for three years; hard work is not enough for fish gall.”
The gentleman said, “No, I'll spare you today; you have to guess within tomorrow.”
Grandpa went home disappointed and told the grandson about it. The grandson said, “Don't be afraid of Grandpa, don't go tomorrow; I'll guess.”
The next day, my grandson went to see the gentleman. The gentleman asked, “Why isn't your grandpa coming today?”
The grandson said, “My grandpa couldn't come home from cutting firewood yesterday, so I'll guess for him today.”
After hearing it, the gentleman said, “If that's the case, then guess yesterday's anagrams for him!”
My grandson answered, “People who are taller than the sky; they don't live long; they are too fat and rain; poor people are afraid of suffering from old age.”
The gentleman said, “Even if you guessed right, you're still a genius, but I still have to ask you to help me do one more thing. Whether you complete this matter or not, the conditions I told your grandpa about before haven't changed.”
The grandson asked, “What happened?” The gentleman said, “I'll pay 50 bucks to buy a meal for me. I'll pick it up at your house tomorrow morning.”
Grandson said, “No problem.” I just went home.
On the way home, my grandson thought, how could horses that have eaten have young foals? After thinking about it for a long time, I finally came up with a solution.
The next day, the gentleman actually came to pull the horse. When he entered the door, he asked, “Is your grandpa there?”
The grandson answered, “Yes!”
The gentleman asked again, “Where?”
The grandson also answered, “My grandpa is giving birth to a doll in his bed in the house!”
As soon as the sir heard it, he turned his head and left. Since then, his grandpa and his two have gone up the mountain every day to cut firewood, and no one interfered.
Narrator: Luo Xuegao, a native of the Chicken Street ethnic group
