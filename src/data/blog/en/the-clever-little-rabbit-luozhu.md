 Once upon a time, along the Brahmaputra River, there was a green forest meadow. On the meadow, sparkling springs are flowing, colorful flowers are blooming, and all kinds of mushrooms are growing. There are so many birds and little beasts singing, dancing, and playing games here, so happy!
But one day, three wolves came running from a high snowy mountain: one was a gray giant wolf, and two were just blue female wolves. They said, “Whoa! This place is really not bad. There is food and drink, so let's set up our house here!” At this point, the quiet forest is not quiet, and the comfortable meadows are uncomfortable. Today, the mother-of-pearl lost her beloved son; tomorrow, Little Golden Deer lost her kind mother. The birds and animals that have lived here for a long time have been affected by a terrible disaster. They had to escape, move, and stay here. They all hid in very deep burrows, hidden in very tall, very tall treetops, and were afraid to go out easily all day long.
There is a clever little white rabbit named Luo Zhu in the meadow. It didn't run away or hide; it was figuring out ways to get rid of these rampant and overbearing demons.
Apply eye cream
One day, Luo Zhu, the little bunny, sat comfortably on the grass and basked in the sun. I caught a glimpse of the giant wolf and walked towards it full of spirit. It quickly grabbed a handful of dirt and rubbed it on its eyelids; it also held a block of ice and used it as a mirror to take pictures again and again.
The giant wolf knocked it on the head and strangely asked, “Hey! Speak up a little bit! What do you paint?” Only then did the little bunny turn back. It seemed as if he had just seen the giant wolf. He hurriedly raised two legs, bowed three times, and said, “Master Wolf, I'm applying eye cream!”
“Eye cream? What eye cream?” The male wolf was even more strange. His eyes widened, and his eyes almost popped out.
The little bunny put down the mirror made of ice cubes and bluntly said, “Mr. Tyrannosaurus in the forest, please listen to my little bunny three words: It's really pathetic for us to be rabbits. We are bullied almost every day; when the leaves fall, they think the sky has collapsed, and they even screamed for their lives. Luo Zhu invented eye cream, and I can't talk about the beauty of it! With my eyes painted, I can see for hundreds of miles, and no trees or rocks can stop me. When eagles come, I drill holes, and hunting dogs come and I climb trees. Since then, I haven't been afraid of anyone, and I've had a really comfortable life.”
The Grand Duke thought, “Wow, they all say rabbits in the forest are the smartest, and they don't look good at all. If I get some of this stuff and paint it, then I can clearly see all the birds and animals in the forest, no matter where they hide. They eat whatever they want, and eat whenever they want. They run east and west and eat when they want to. They run east and west and are exhausted. Sometimes they are hungry to the back, and sometimes they swell up and can't climb for days. “No, no, Lord Wolf, don't be kidding!” The rabbit pretended to be frightened and cried with trepidation: “Master Wolf, Master Wolf, please don't get angry. You are very caring for our little bunnies, and I remember this in my heart. Now that the eye cream is applied, I'll go back and make a box for you. When the sun rises tomorrow, apply the magic potion to the old man on time!”
When I said this, the wolf was happy. When we broke up, I hit the little bunny three more times to remind him not to forget it.
At night, the little bunny sneaked into a carpenter's house down the mountain and stole a piece of kraft glue. Early the next morning, it placed the cowhide glue on the stone and let the sun slowly roast it.
At noon, Grand Duke Wolf came proudly and said, “Hey, did you bring eye cream?” The little bunny quickly stood up and used his back legs to jump three times on the grass: “Grandpa! I'm here waiting for you! Please turn your lovely face to the sun, close your gentle eyes, and I'll apply ointment to you.”
The wolf sat on a rock and closed his eyes tightly. The little rabbit used a piece of bark, covered with cowhide gum, and put one layer and another layer on its eyes.
The wolf felt bad and shouted, “Oops! OUCH! “Damn bunny, are you lying to me?” The rabbit blew his breath in the wolf's eyes and said, “Alas, old man, it's too hard for you to wait! The old wolf used all his strength and hit the air with all his might. Before I opened my eyes, I fell into a very deep ravine and fell to my death.
Ride the Flying Tree
A young man followed a group of snow-white sheep to graze in this forest meadow. Suddenly, the big duke wolf's wife, a blue female wolf, came out and ran away carrying a lamb. The young man raised the “Urduo” in his hand and said, “Dazhu!” “Dazhu Ga!” The stone was like hail. It hit the female wolf's head and butt. The female wolf swims to the other side and slowly climbs a steep slope. Looking left and right, she wanted to find a place with the wind and sun to warm up. Suddenly, it saw a little rabbit riding on a “Yumo” tree on the edge of a cliff, shaking left and right, swinging up and down, like a swing. It was extremely proud.
The female wolf looked at it three times, and the more she looked at it, the more it looked like a little bunny that killed her husband. Then he jumped three and two, jumped over to the little bunny, put his claws on its shoulder, and said, “The bad guy who killed my husband, you're living a very good life! Tail fishing
