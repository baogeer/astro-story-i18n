On this morning during the Datang period, Li Li returned to his home after handling the case. A whirlwind suddenly blew up ahead, rolling up the dead grass and leaves on the ground and dancing wildly in the air, constantly making a “whoa -- whoa --” sound, “Grandpa, the previous whirlwind blocked the way.” Attendant A reported; Liao Li lifted up the curtain and looked outside, and thought in his heart that folklore had a whirlwind that changed people's souls after death; why did anyone have a major grievance! Then he shouted, “Stop the train! A whirlwind is a revelation of a person's soul after death. Let us not pass by today; there must be a major grievance here.”
“As soon as it took a whirlwind, did you know there was a major grievance?” Attendant B asked the old man;
“Right! Look how this whirlwind rotates.”
“Master, turn to the right.” Attendants A and B watched the whirlwind and heard the sounds made continuously screaming and talking like humans;
“Look if it's thick and tall or slim, thin, and delicate.”
“Old man, slim, small and delicate.” Attendant A said after careful observation;
“Let's see if it's strong or weak.”
“Master, weak.” Attendant B complained after reading it;
“A strong, powerful whirlwind that rotates to the left is a man's soul. The whirlwind of spinning to the right, slim and delicate, and weak is the soul of a woman.”
“Grandpa, is this whirlwind still divided between men and women?” Attendants A and B asked in unison;
“Aren't you listening to what people usually say, men left, women right! That's the truth; it can also be fake!”
“Grandpa, we only know that mountains have yin and yang, water has yin and yang, and flowers and trees have yin and yang. There is also a difference between yin and yang in this whirlwind.” A and B's entourage asked inexplicably;
“Yes! Everything has yin and yang. Cyclones can't be left out.” The law clerk stated unequivocally;
“Grandpa, this is a woman. Slim, thin and delicate, weak, and rotates to the right.” Attendants A and B carefully observed the report;
“Women, don't cry! Clear it and return it to its original state!” Lian Li shuddered, shaking the three mountains and mountains, and jumping in the five lakes and four waters.
“People's women are wronged!” A woman in cloth appeared standing and shouted in the middle of the road;
“If there is a grievance, the government will decide for you!” Lian Li Tieqing's face made the elder say;
“The surname of the folk woman is Ah, the name is a woman, a native name. Really wrong! ...”
“Can there be shaped paper?”
“Grandpa, please take a look.” A piece of paper in the shape of an old woman's head was handed in front of an independent official;
“It's simply lawless, reckless, and deeply sinful!” After reading the situation, Liao Li didn't write a word of paper;
“A and B followed quickly to the local area to apprehend the key criminal who murdered this woman.” The police officer went on to say; not often, offenders have to be brought before the crime.
“If you seek money to kill your life, can you blame her?” The local government shouted loudly;
“Acquittal! Conviction! Dwarves are guilty!” If the offender didn't dare to resist smashing garlic, he prostrated his head;
“The death penalty is unforgivable; the crime is deserved,” the local government said angrily; in the end, they were duly punished to avenge the whirlwind woman.
The whirlwind girl smiled and quickly turned into a gust of wind.
