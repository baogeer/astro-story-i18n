 Once upon a time, there was a long-time worker who helped a financial owner do the work. It was almost the end of the year, and this long-time worker went to pay the treasurer's salary for a year. The rich man said, “You can ask for money, but you have to do three things for me. If you do it, I'll give you one more year's work. If you can't do it, I won't give you a day's work.”
“What kind of three things?”
“First, I want you to buy a cow as heavy as a mountain; second, I want you to buy a cloth that can cover the sky; third, I want you to buy as much wine as water in a river.” After listening to it, Changgong had no choice but to go home sighing.
When Changgong got home, he didn't make a sound all day, but only sighed. His wife told him to eat, and he didn't eat either. The wife asked him, “You haven't talked or eaten all day long. What's going on in your mind? Let's discuss it well.” The long worker told his grandmother what was on his mind. His wife said, “It doesn't matter, you should eat first. Tomorrow, you'll take a scale, a ruler, and a wine jug. You have to use this scale to first measure how heavy this mountain is before you can buy such a heavy cow; use this jug to measure how many bottles of water there are before you can buy that much alcohol; use this ruler to measure how big this day is before you can buy such a wide cloth.” Changgong was very happy to hear it. The next day, I brought these three things to the rich owner and said to the rich man, “If you want me to buy a cow as heavy as a mountain, OK. But if you use this scale to measure how heavy this mountain is, I can only buy such a heavy cow.” As soon as the rich man heard it, his eyes widened, and it took half a day to say, “This is a no-brainer. But what about two more things?” Changgong also took out a ruler and a jug and said to him, “You can use this ruler to measure how big this day is before I can buy such a wide cloth. Using this bottle to measure how many bottles of water there are in the river, I was able to buy that much wine.” As soon as the rich owner heard it, he couldn't say a word; he had to give two years' wages to this long-term worker.
