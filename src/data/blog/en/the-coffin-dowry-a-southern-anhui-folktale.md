 August 8, the 12th of Guangxu was a happy wedding day for the son of Wang Yuheng, the owner of Dongmen Roast Shop in Xuancheng, southern Anhui. I saw the bright lights in front of the Wangjiamen, the red and green floors, the sound of firecrackers, and the constant sound of gongs and drums, filled with a lively and festive atmosphere. On this day, almost everyone in the city came to the door of the Wang Family to watch the strange thing. They wanted to see the bride's dowry. The dowry that Wang Yuheng's daughter-in-law accompanied her was really unique.
Turns out, Wang Yuheng's family name is Tan Jifa, and they are the owner of Liangtingtang Coffin Shop in North Gate of Chengbei. As early as a month ago, people heard that Boss Wang sent a special message to Boss Tan at the coffin shop, advising Boss Tan to send a few coffins as a dowry for his daughter to accompany him to the royal family. Are there any strange things under the world where a coffin is used as a dowry? Hearing is false; seeing is believing. Therefore, everyone should see for themselves whether the bride's dowry is as some people say.
At noon, people heard the sound of suona firecrackers coming from North Street. They knew the bride had arrived. Everyone took a look, and they were all dumbfounded! I saw that in the back of the flower car the bride was riding in, the person who sent it to her family actually carried a coffin, the size of five people. Is Wang Yuheng crazy?
Looking at Boss Wang again, I saw that his team arrived at the doorstep, and I couldn't wait to greet them. He patted and knocked in front of these coffins, laughed and examined them for half a minute, and only then ordered that the coffins be carefully carried into the house...
What's going on with this? We still have to start over with this. Tan Jifa's daughter, Tan Meiyun, is a famous beautiful woman around her. Last year, Tam Mei Wan visited Jingting Mountain and was bumped into Wong Yuhang's son Wong Cheuk-yan halfway along the way. Wong Cheuk-yan was instantly enchanted by Tam Mei Wan's amazing beauty. When I got home, I didn't want tea or food; my eyes were full of Tan Meiyun's shadow. He told his father about this. Unexpectedly, Wang Yuheng was furious after hearing it. He said that marrying the coffin shop owner's daughter as his wife was causing ghosts to enter the house and resolutely opposed it. Wong Cheuk-yan was not satisfied. A matchmaker secretly went to Tam's family, but it came true as soon as he said it. Since then, Wong Cheuk-yan has often dated Tam Mei-wan in private. Later, Wang Yuheng discovered this incident. In his anger, he locked his son in the inner room and sent someone to take care of him.
Soon, the father of a friend from Wang Yuheng's hometown died, and he was asked to buy a good coffin in the city. Wang Yuheng stubbornly came to Tan Kifa's house. I saw a group of carpenters busy plowing heaps of wood to make a coffin. He saw that the plastered wood showed a natural water pattern, revealing a fuchsia color and a pungent aroma. Wang Yuheng was shocked when he saw it. At this point, Boss Tan walked out of the room. Seeing Wang Yuheng coming, he hurriedly greeted him and said, “Rare guests, rare visitors, what gusts of wind blew my family?” At this point, when Wang Yuheng heard Boss Tan calling himself his own family, he was not at all displeased. His heart was held by that pile of wood, and he silently asked, “Boss Tan, where did you get this pile of wood?”
Boss Tan listened and laughed, “Why, my family likes this kind of wood? Unsurprisingly, this wood was given to me by a Western monk.”
“Is that true?” As soon as Wang Yuheng heard it, his heart jumped straight up and down, “Then why did the Western Monk send you wood?”
It turns out that half a year ago, Boss Tan went to Shizhou to buy wood. While passing through the sparsely populated mountains, he suddenly found a gang of criminals surrounding an old monk, trying to steal money and kill his life. Boss Tan has been practicing his fists since childhood. Seeing this scene, he rushed up and punched the gang with three punches and two feet, and beat the gang to escape. The old monk was very grateful to Boss Tan. I heard that Boss Tan opened a coffin shop and was about to go to Shizhou to buy wood. The old monk claimed to be from the Western region and would like to send Boss Tan a few sticks of good wood. Sure enough, not long ago, a few little monks from the western region came and sent him dozens of thick red-skinned lumber.
After listening to Boss Tan's remarks, Wang Yuheng nervously asked, “Did those little Western monks tell you what kind of wood this is?”
“I heard...” Boss Tan thought for a while and said, “I heard it's called 'sukangmu. '”
Hearing the three words “shouxiangmu,” Wang Yuheng almost didn't faint to the ground. Wang Yuheng usually writes a bunch of books. From an ancient book, he learned that the western region produces a type of wood called sujiangmu. This wood grows on deep mountain cliffs. It is extremely precious and rare for humans. What's even more peculiar is that they use shouxiang wood to make beds, and people don't get sick after sleeping; they are made into coffins, and corpses don't rot for thousands of years... As early as the Ming dynasty, the founding emperor Zhu Yuanzhang sent people to the western region many times to search for shouxiang wood, but they never found it. After hearing Tan Jifa talk about the origin of this pile of wood, Wang Yuheng was extremely happy and couldn't help but take possession of this pile of wood all of a sudden. So he thought about it and said to Boss Tan, “Family Tan, I love the water pattern on this wood. When your daughter gets married in the future, can you use this wood to make a coffin to be sent to my royal family as a dowry?”
Tan Jifa was originally a cheerful person. Plus, he didn't know the details of Shou Xiang Mu, so he agreed without hesitation. Just like that, the wooden coffin set was accompanied by the royal family.
After Wang Yuheng went to great lengths to get the Shouxiang wooden coffin, he invited a carpenter to remove all of those coffins and turn them into furniture such as beds, wooden boxes, and dressing tables. Just at this point, news broke that Master Bai of Anhui Province would soon visit Xuancheng in the future, so Wang Yuheng made another reckless plan and planned to dedicate this set of Shouxiang wood furniture to the governor to get rid of his addiction by taking a part-time job as an official.
After a while, the governor came to Xuancheng. Some local financial owners and leading figures appeared one after another, scrambling to set up a feast and settle the matter with the adults. Naturally, Wang Yuheng was not willing to be left behind. He also invited the constable to his home and treated him with delicious wine and food.
The seats were scattered, and Wang Yuheng invited Mr. Bai to the interior and proudly pointed at the newly made furniture set: “If adults like it, give this set of Shouxiang wooden furniture to adults.”
The white man had also heard of sujiangmu. When he learned that the set of furniture in front of him was actually made of sujiangwood, he was stunned.
Inspector Bai knows that an unscrupulous businessman like Wang Yuheng doesn't give anything for no reason, so he said, “If you give something so valuable to Bai, how can Bai help you?”
Wang Yuheng said with an old face: “Mr. Bai, the villain heard that the county governor of Jixi has just left office, and the seat is still vacant...”
Mr. Bai immediately understood Wang Yuheng's intentions and laughed, “Turns out you also wanted to quit business and become an official. This is easy to do; it's covered by Bai!”
The one with the surname Bai was also trustworthy. After leaving with the wooden furniture set, he recommended it to the imperial court soon after leaving. Wang Yuheng then led his family to Jixi and took office.
There are no impermeable walls in the world. Soon after, owner Tan of the coffin shop learned the truth and suddenly got sick in bed.
Boss Tan has not recovered from his illness. On this day, a few young monks from the western region came to the store. They said they wanted to meet Boss Tan.
When Boss Tan arrived at the entrance of the store, he recognized that those few were the little monks who had come to deliver Shoukangmu and were busy inviting them into the house.
Those few little monks from the Western region entered the house and knelt down in front of Boss Tan in unison, saying, “Now that Boss Tan is still alive, I feel at ease. We really deserve to die...”
As soon as he heard this, Boss Tan was stunned. He was busy supporting them one by one, and fearfully asked, “Little Masters, why are you saying this?”
Those few little monks told me the story in a nutshell. Boss Tan heard it. It really felt like a thunderstorm, and I was so frightened.
It turns out that there are two types of suka wood produced in the western region: one is red bark and the other is yellow bark. The aroma and tree pattern of the two types of sugarwood are exactly the same, but the red bark of sugarwood is highly toxic. It is said that people lie on a bed made of mahogany bark and in less than a month, their bodies will be covered with poison sores, and no medicine will cure them. In just half a year, these poison sores, along with the human flesh, will rot and turn into water. Therefore, people in the western region also call mangrove bark shukanmu a corpse tree. At the beginning, the little monk mistakenly gave mahogany bark to Boss Tan because he couldn't tell the difference between the two types of shouxiang wood. It also coincided with the old monk visiting the clouds, so the incident never went unnoticed. A few days ago, the old monk returned to the temple and was shocked to discover that a few of the red bark shouxiang trees in the temple were missing. He immediately asked the little monks to quickly come to Xuancheng to recover those few mahogany trees. At the same time, they also brought a few yellow bark sugarwood to Boss Tan.
After listening to the stories of the little monks, Boss Tan was so scared that his face was blank as paper. After sending away a few little monks, Boss Tan was too late to clean up his clothes. He hired cars and horses, and headed straight to Jixi.
Wang Yuheng listened to Boss Tan's remarks and was so scared that he almost peed on his pants. As a result, he rushed to the provincial city to find Inspector Bai.
Who just happened to happen? More than a month ago, Chief Paik married Lee Lian-ying's son-in-law Li Guer as a concubine. The furniture was taken as a dowry and accompanied to Li Fu.
When Bai Chuanfu listened to Wang Yuheng's narration, he was also shocked. He slapped Wang Yuheng hard and scolded, “You bastard, you have ruined me!”
Wang Yuheng touched his red and swollen face and said, “Dude, as long as you recover that set of furniture quickly, it's still too late!”
“Damn, you speak lightly; it's as easy as farting!” Bai Chuanfu angrily shouted, “My daughter has been married for over a month. Did she and Li Gou get venomous sores a long time ago? Do you know who Lee Gu Er is Lee Lian-ying? That is the root of his life! Well, Li Lian-ying is also a big celebrity at Galeries Lafayette today. Now that I have harmed his stepson, can he spare me? The only way to keep your head and me is by hiding it. God knows you know what I know, understand?”
“But... aren't you risking your daughter's life for nothing?”
“Damn, you haven't hurt me yet!” Inspector Bai raised his hand and slapped Wang Yuheng a few more times, “This is already the case. It's up to her to spill water from her married daughter. Life and death are up to her!”
Wang Yuheng returned to Jixi with a blue nose and a swollen face. Boss Tan hasn't left the Wang family yet. After listening to Wang Yuheng's story, he stayed like a chicken and went back to Xuancheng in a daze.
When he got home, Boss Tan thought about it the more he became afraid. Paper can't contain fire. Sooner or later, this will have to be revealed to the world. At that point, no one will escape the robbery!
After thinking about it for a few days, Boss Tan sent someone to take his daughter and son-in-law home from Jixi and tell them the truth. Finally, he said, “Thirty-six measures are taken as the top plan. It seems that we only have to go a long way and stay in seclusion.”
Wong Cheuk-yan hurriedly said, “Father-in-law, then what about my father?”
Boss Tan shook his head and smiled bitterly, “Your father is obsessed; will he leave office and run away? Besides, it was his own accusation.”
The next day, Wang Zhuoren and Tan Meiyun returned to Jixi. As soon as the couple arrived home, they had an argument for unknown reasons. Later, they committed suicide by taking drugs on both sides.
Boss Tan received a letter from the Wang family that his daughter and son-in-law had taken the “fake death medicine” he provided. They made people carry two coffins made of yellow bark and shouxiang wood, pretended to be sad, and cried all the way to their family's house. The “corpses” of the daughter and son-in-law were then collected using those two wooden wooden coffins. On the day of the burial, Boss Tan took advantage of people's carelessness to lift the coffin cover and secretly release the couple.
Since then, Boss Tan and his daughter and son-in-law have disappeared in Xuancheng...
Everything was as expected by Tan Jifa. Within a few months, Li Lianying's son-in-law and the daughter of Chief Consul Paik all died in a wooden bed. An old and knowledgeable doctor diagnosed that the cause of death of the two was related to this red cedar tree. Li Lian-ying cried to Empress Dowager Cixi, saying that Chief Paik intended to murder his son. Empress Dowager Cixi was furious and ordered the arrest of Consul Bai, and also implicated Wang Yuheng and executed these two to death. They also sent people to search for their families. Fortunately, Tan Jifar has already escaped and died.
Poor Wang Yuheng had no addiction yet, so he hurriedly walked to the guillotine.
