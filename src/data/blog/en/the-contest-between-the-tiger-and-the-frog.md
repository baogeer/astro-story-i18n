 One afternoon in the fall, at the foot of the mountain, in a rice field with endless views and the scent of rice, frogs were jumping around to catch bugs in the field.
A tiger rested with his left hand holding his chin in the grass on the side of the field and lazily placed his right hand next to his left hand. He thought it was a bit fun, so he smiled and said, “Brother Frog, can you only dance like this every time you jump?!”
The frog stopped to catch bugs and stood on the field. With their hands on their hips, they answered in a bad mood, “Who said that?! I've jumped so far! If you don't believe me, let's compare!”
When the strong tiger heard the frog say this, his whole body's muscles were stimulated. He yelled “miso” and jumped out of the grass and said, “Wow! Look who jumps that far!”
The tiger won the ground and the frog stood side by side to prepare for the take-off game.
But the frog quietly clenched the fur on the tiger's tail.
They shouted in unison, “Jump!”
This jump, the frog falls in front of the tiger
The tiger looked dumbfounded; he didn't believe it...
The tiger looked up at the crowds of giggling crowds sitting on the branches. They seemed very reluctant to lose.
The tiger walked two steps forward and stood in line with the frog. They shouted in unison, “Jump!”
With this jump, the frog fell in front of the tiger again.
The tiger looked dumbfounded, looked at the audience again, and rubbed Han. He didn't believe it even more...
The tiger walked two more steps forward and stood in line with the frog, and they shouted “Jump!”
The frog still falls in front of the tiger! The tigers are all dumbfounded!
He was so angry that he couldn't say anything...
The frog proudly said, “Brother Tiger, is it better?”
The tiger was panting and vomiting! I spit out the fur bones I had just eaten...
The tiger was not convinced and said, “Let's throw up our guts to see who eats what?!” His left hand stuck to his waist, held his right finger on the fur bone he had just spit out, and said loudly, “Look! I eat something with skin, hair, bones, and meat, so you can't throw it up!” he said with contempt.
The frog said, “OK.”
As soon as the frog crossed its hips and spit out tiger hair, it said, “Look, I'm the only one who ate your ancestors!”
The tiger came closer and took a closer look...
What the frog spits out is actually tiger hair!
I looked up again and looked at the frogs that didn't care. Without saying a word, I was so scared that they ran away without a trace as soon as they smoked. It turns out that the frog only bit the tiger's hair in his mouth when it clenches its tail during take-off.
The frog proudly jumped to the field with both hands and stood on the field. Looking at the back of the tiger fleeing in a hurry, he smiled and said, “Haha... haha! Brother Tiger, how can big be used to deceive younger people? Each size has its pros and cons... hahaha! ... just escaped...”
The little frog won with wisdom, defeating a tiger tens of times larger than him. Since then, the giant tiger was terrified of frogs to death, and when he saw the frog from afar, he immediately made a U-turn and fled.
This story tells us the principle that we should never look like people, bully young people with big things, and pressure people with power.
