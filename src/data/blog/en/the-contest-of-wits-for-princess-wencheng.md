This is a historical drama about Zampuson Zanganbu, who took control of the Tuban administration and sent Minister Gar Dong Zan to propose marriage in the Tang dynasty. Tang Taizong presented five difficult questions to test envoys from various countries to propose marriage. Whoever can win, the princess will marry the country's monarch.
The first question is to use silk thread to cut through nine pieces of jade. Other countries' envoys couldn't get through it. Gar Eldong recommended using a silk thread to tie the ant's waist, put it in the orifice of the pearl, blow slowly, and let it crawl past, and the thread was ready.
The second test was for messengers from each country to receive 100 sheep and 100 cans of wine; they had to kill the sheep, peel the skin, eat all the meat, knead the skin, and finish drinking. Other envoys got drunk when they didn't finish eating the meat, and some got tired without kneading the skin. Only Gar Eldong ordered his followers to slowly drink in small bowls, knead the skin while eating and drinking, and finally complete the “mission” assigned by the emperor.
For the third time, Emperor Tang also ordered the minister to recognize the mother-son relationships between 100 mares and 100 foals. Gar Eldong ordered the little pony to stay alone for the night, giving only forage and no water to drink. The next day, I put them at the mare's place, and the little ponies all acknowledged that their mother had pumped milk. In this way, the Tufan envoy won again.
The Tang emperor also ordered to take out 100 wooden sticks with a thick head and tail, so that the envoy could recognize the head. Gal Dongzan propelled the wooden stick into the water. The head and tail were light, sank heavily, and floated lightly on it. He clearly understood.
Finally, Tang Taizong mixed Princess Wencheng among 300 beautiful women dressed in exactly the same way, so that the ministers found out, and those who recognized the princess could follow along. Gal Dong Zang first asked the old lady who served Princess Wencheng about the princess's appearance and characteristics, and learned that the princess had a red cinnabar mole on her brow. Minister Tuban also followed the instructions of an old Han lady and finally recognized the princess.
As a result, Tang Taizong married the princess to Song Zan Ganbu and accompanied him with many gift dowries, and brought the princess to Tuban.
