 Zaxi Wangdui is the king's old chef. Not only did he have excellent cooking skills, and the meals he made were highly praised by the king and his guests, but he was also honest. He never said this or that in the king's ear. His behavior was unimaginable and aroused the hatred of Chief Minister Jin Mei Dao Dao.
One day, a grand ceremony was held in the palace. Leaders, shepherds, and merchants from all over lined up to take turns offering gold, silver, food, tea, rice, beef and sheep to the king. Chef Zaxi Wangdui came in and poured tea for the subjects and inadvertently let out a fart. The Chief Minister seized this opportunity and said to the king, “O king! Today, subjects from all over the country are happy to offer their gifts to His Majesty. Only Chef Zaxi Wangdui actually farted in the crowd to vent their dissatisfaction with His Majesty. You can't ignore it!”
Although the king liked Zhaxi Wangdui, he was unable to withstand the vicious provocation of Jin Mei Dao Dao Dao, so he called him to question. Zaxi Wangdui knelt down on the ground, knocked three heads, and said, “Dear King, what I just did was definitely not intentional. Please take care of me for so many years, Your Majesty, and forgive my rudeness!” As soon as the chef said this, the king's anger was reduced in half, and the subjects explained it to him one after another. Seeing that the storm was about to subside.
At this point, Jin Mei Dao Dang stood up from her seat and angrily said, “To fart in front of the king is to make the king eat poop. Such rebellious acts go unpunished. What kind of state is the country still in place? What other dignity does the king have? What else is the point of my Chief Minister? “King, please allow me to resign!”
Finally, the king agreed to Jin Mei Dao Dao Deng's claim and sentenced him to death in Wangdui, and immediately buried alive under the city's roof. Jin Mei Dao Dao realized a conspiracy that had been hidden in her stomach for many years, and she was so happy that she almost went crazy.
Next, the king and ministers climbed up to the city tower and saw that an earthen pit the size of a yak had been dug downstairs. Soon, Zhaxi Wangdui was taken to the edge of an earthen pit by 20 executioners carrying tens of pounds of shackles. After Jin Mei's sword made a gesture, the Zaxi Wangdui would be pushed to the bottom of the pit and buried. Suddenly, he looked back and sighed three times, and two pea-sized teardrops rolled out of his eyes. Strangely enough, the king asked, “Chef Zaxi Wangdui, what else would you like to say?” Zaxi Wangdui clasped his hands and answered loudly at the city tower; “King, I just shed tears and sigh; it's not that I have any nostalgia for life. It's just that I got a trick from elementary school, that is, I can grow pearls on the ground. I've always wanted to grow dozens of pearls to enrich His Majesty's treasure. Who knows I'm leaving the world suddenly today. My only trick was to take me to Xitian Elysium.”
When the king heard it, he was surprised and delighted, and asked, “You, can you really grow pearls?” Who said Zaxi Wang; “King, I've been serving you for 40 years, and I've never told a lie as small as a horse tail. As long as I scatter pearls in the ground, a pearl tree will grow back then, and the next year it will be full of pearls. If I tell a lie, ask the king to punish me more severely.”
As a result, the king ordered the executioner to let go of Zaxi Wangdui and ordered him to plant pearls on an auspicious day. On the designated day, Wangcheng was more lively than the Lunar New Year, and more jubilant than the holiday season. The king, queen, and ministers, as well as people from all over the city, arrived at the chosen place early. Zhaxi Wangdui is also full of energy. Wearing a brand-new robe, holding two farming cows decorated with satin and feathers, burning incense to worship the Buddha, and praying to the gods, everything is ready to stop, but they just refuse to sow it by hand. The king was very anxious. He came over and asked, “Zhaxi Wangdui, why hasn't it started yet?” The old cook hurriedly knelt down and said, “Dear King, I have everything ready; I just left someone to sow pearls. Back then, my master told me: This sower must have been born from her mother's womb before farting; otherwise, pearls won't grow. Please ask the king to find someone like this to sow for me!”
The king thought this request was very simple, so he loudly announced to his subjects, “Listen, everyone, if anyone born from a mother's womb hasn't let go of their farts, quickly stand up and help Zaxi Wangdui sow!” After a cup of tea and kung fu, none of them agreed.
“Then, let's ask Chief Minister Jin Mei Dao to come!” The old cook made a request to the king.
“I can't, I can't!” Jin Mei Dao Dao thought of the farts she had thrown. It was definitely not less than the number of farts she had, and it was very loud.
“I see, I have no choice but to work with His Majesty Lao...” Zaxi Wangdui bowed deeply to the king.
“OMG! I've let go of a lot of crap too!” The king shouted.
“That's right!” The old chef said sincerely, “When I was pouring tea a few days ago, I accidentally farted. The Chief Minister insisted that I was dissatisfied with the king and let His Majesty eat poop. Now it seems that even the Chief Minister has farted more than once; how should this be punished?”
The king felt that what Zaxi Wangdui said was very reasonable. He determined that Jin Mei Dao Dao Dao was a guy who mixed blood in milk, removed him from office on the spot, kicked him out of the palace with random sticks, and proposed that Zaxi Wangdui be the chief minister. Zaxi Wangdui continued to speculate, “King, I really don't have the ability to be the chief minister; let me do my own job as a chef! However, I hope that criminal laws that speak white as black and black as white will not be implemented anymore.”
