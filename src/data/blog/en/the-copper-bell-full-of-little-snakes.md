 In the sixties, when cleaning up the ancient road of the Yellow River, I found a 10-meter-high bronze bell under the silt. The bell entrance was sealed with iron juice. Open it and take a look. The bronze bell was full of dense skulls. There were all kinds of small yellow, orange and orange snakes on the skull, and they wouldn't come out at all. Experts did some research and couldn't figure out what this bell was for, and how snakes could survive in a closed bell for so many years; in the end, they were only able to sink the bell to the bottom of the river as it was. After the bell sank to the bottom of the water, every household heard the sound of the bronze bell ringing for seven days in a row.
