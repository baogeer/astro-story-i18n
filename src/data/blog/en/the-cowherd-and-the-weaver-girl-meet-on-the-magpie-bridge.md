tales
The Tanabata Festival has always been linked to the legend of the cowherd and weaver. This is a beautiful love story that has been passed down through the ages, and has become one of the four major folk love legends in our country.
Legend has it that a long time ago, there was a smart and loyal young man in Nanyang Chengxi Niujiazhuang. His parents died early, and he had to spend time with his older brother and sister-in-law. Ma's sister-in-law was very toxic and often abused him and forced him to do a lot of work. One fall, his sister-in-law forced him to herd and give him nine cows, but he was not able to return home. The cowherd had no choice but to rush out of the village.
The cowherd rushed the cow into the mountain alone. On the grassy mountain, he was sitting under a tree sad and didn't know when he would be able to catch ten cows home. At this point, an old man with white beards appeared in front of him and asked him why he was sad. When he learned about his experience, he smiled and said to him, “Don't be sad. There is an old cow that fell ill in Mount Funiu. You can feed it well. After the old cow gets well, you can rush it home.
The cowherd climbed over the mountain, walked a long distance, and finally found the sick old cow. When he saw that the old cow was seriously ill, he sent a bundle of grass to the old cow. He fed it for three days. Only then did he raise his head and tell him that he was a great gray cow in heaven. He was devalued to heaven. He broke his leg and was unable to move. It took a month to wash his injuries with a hundred flower dew to heal. The cowherd was not afraid of hard work. He carefully cared for the old cow for a month, picked flowers and treated the old cow with water during the day, and slept snuggled up next to the elderly at night. After the old cow got well, the cowherd happily rushed home with ten heads of cows.
When he got home, his sister-in-law still treated him badly. She tried to hurt him a few times, but they were all rescued by the old cow. The sister-in-law ended up being annoyed and angrily kicked the cowherd out of the house. The cowherd only needed that old cow to accompany him.
One day, Heavenly Weaver went to a game with the fairies. Bathing in the river, the cowherd met the weaver with the help of the old cow. The two had a relationship with each other. Later, the weaver secretly went down to the world, and became the cowherd's wife. The weaver also distributed silkworms brought from the sky to everyone, and taught everyone to raise silkworms, draw silk, and weave bright and bright satin.
After the cowherd and the weaver got married, the man and the woman were affectionate. They had two children, one man, one woman, and the family lived a very happy life. However, the good times did not last long. The Emperor soon learned that the royal mother and daughter personally came down to Earth and forcibly brought the weaver back to heaven, and the loving couple were separated.
The cowherd has no way to go to heaven. Still, the old cow told the cowherd that after his death, they can use their leather as shoes, and they can go to heaven when they wear them. The cowherd followed the old cow's words. Wearing shoes made of cowhide, he took his children and drove the fog to go after the weaver. When they saw that they were about to chase the weaver, they saw that the princess had pulled off the golden hairpin on her head, and a choppy Tianhe appeared. The cowherd and weaver were separated on both sides of the Strait, and only wept relatively. Magpies were moved by their loyal love. Tens of millions of magpies flew in and built a magpie bridge, so that the cowherd and weaver met on the Magpie Bridge. Mother Wang was also helpless about this; they had no choice but to allow the two to meet at Magpie Bridge every July 7.
Later, on July 7 of the lunar calendar, legend has it that the cowherd and weaver meet at Magpie Bridge, the girls will come under the Moon to look up at the starry sky and search for the cowherd star and the weaver on both sides of the galaxy, hoping to see them meet once a year, and ask God to make themselves as smart as a beggar girl, and pray that they can have a happy marriage.
According to research by folklore experts, the place where the interpretation of the ancient myth “The Cowherd and the Weaver” was performed has made the latest argument, that is, Tianhe Mountain, which borders Heshun County in Shanxi Province and Xingtai County in Hebei Province. Here, there is the legendary Nanden Pond, where the girl from Nakaori bathed, and Jiro Gorge, named after the cowherd.
