 The cowherd only had an old cow and a plow. He went down to the field every day when it was bright, and when he got home, he had to cook and wash his own food and laundry. He had a very hard time. Who expected a miracle to happen one day! The cowherd returned home after work. As soon as he entered the house, he saw that the house had been cleaned clean, his clothes had been washed fresh and fresh, and there were still hot, fragrant meals on the table. The cowherd widened his eyes in surprise and thought: What's going on? Have the immortals descended? Never mind, let's eat first.
After that, for several days, this was the case every day. The cowherd couldn't stand his patience; he had to get out of his way. On this day, as usual, the cowherd went out early in the morning. Actually, after walking a few steps, he turned back. Instead of entering the house, he found a hidden place to hide and secretly observe. Sure enough, it didn't take long for a girl as beautiful as a fairy to arrive. As soon as she entered the door, she was busy cleaning up the house and cooking, not to mention the hard work! The cowherd really couldn't help it. He stood up and said, “Girl, why are you here to help me with the housework?” The girl was taken aback, blushed, and whispered, “My name is Weaver. Seeing how hard your life has been, I'm here to help you.” The cowherd was so excited that he quickly went on to say, “Then you can stay. We will share our hardships and work together to build a happy life with our hands!” The weaver blushed and nodded. As a result, they became husband and wife, and lived a very happy life.
After a few years, they had two children, a boy, a girl, and the family had a great time. One day, all of a sudden, the sky was dark and the wind was raging, and the weaver disappeared. The two children kept crying, and the cowherd was in a hurry and didn't know what to do. While she was in a hurry, all the dark clouds suddenly disappeared, the weather became windy and sunny again, and the weaver returned home, but her face was full of sadness. I saw her gently holding the cowherd and holding the two children in her arms and saying, “Actually, I'm not an ordinary person, but the granddaughter of the Queen Mother. Now, people from Tiangong are taking me back. Take care of yourself a lot!” After speaking, tears flowed down, and the clouds went away.
The cowherd hugged his two young children and stood idly for half a day crying. No, I can't let my wife leave me like this; I can't let my child lose their mother just like this; I want to go find her; I must get the weaver back! At this point, the old cow suddenly said, “Don't be sad! If you kill me, put my skin on, and make two baskets with two kids, you can go to Tiangong to find a weaver.” The cowherd said nothing and didn't want to treat this partner who had been with him for decades, but he couldn't help but endure the pain and do what he said with tears in his eyes.
When she arrived in Tiangong, the Queen Mother and Mrs. Wang didn't want to recognize the cowherd, her son-in-law. Instead, she did not let the weaver come out to see him. Instead, she found seven masked, tall, fat, and thin women who were exactly the same. She said to the cowherd, “If you agree, let me meet you.” The cowherd was dumbfounded, but the two children in his arms jumped out to his mom. Turns out, nothing can stop the mother and child by blood!
Mother Wang couldn't help it, but she was still unwilling to return to the world, so she ordered the weaver to be taken away. The cowherd was in a hurry and quickly caught up with his two children. They ran and ran, but when they were tired, they couldn't stop. When they fell and climbed up, they quickly caught up. In a hurry, Mother Wang pulled out the golden hairpin from her head and drew a wide galaxy between them. Since then, the cowherd and the weaver can only stand at opposite ends of the galaxy and look at each other from a distance. And by the beginning of July 7 of the lunar calendar every year, tens of thousands of magpies fly in and build a long magpie bridge over the Milky Way to reunite the family of the cowherd and weaver.
