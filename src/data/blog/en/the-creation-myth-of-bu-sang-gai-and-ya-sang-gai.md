 Legend has it that when heaven and earth were first formed, there were only bare land and vast oceans, and no humans, animals, or plants.
Payayang, the greatest god in the sky, sent two gods, Busangai and Yasangai, to Earth with fairy gourds.
They both broke up the fairy gourd and scattered the gourd seeds on the sky and the earth. The sun, moon, and stars appeared in the sky, and the earth had forests and other plants.
However, there are still no people on Earth. Yu Busang Gai and Yasang instead used mud to crush adults in their own image. Busang changed their image to be men and women, and made them husband and wife, giving them life, soul, and vitality, and making them withstand the ups and downs of the sun. After a long period of time, the clay people became living people, yet they couldn't talk, so the couple taught them how to talk and think, and named them “people.” Since then, there have been people on Earth. Under the guidance of Busangai and Yasanggai, they have gradually learned to work, live, and recuperate.
The couple then used mud to invent tens of thousands of species of birds and animals, such as elephants, horses, cows, sheep, dogs, chickens, ducks, tigers, leopards, wolves, moose, deer, and birds, and put them into the ocean and forest.
Since then, there have been aquatic animals such as fish, shrimp, crab, and shellfish in the ocean, and various vertebrates, reptiles, and insects on land.
