 Once upon a time, there were two brothers, one named Pangu and the other named Pansheng. Every day they go to the mountains to cut firewood, cut it back and sell it on the street. One day, Pangu met a fortune teller named Miao Zhuang Wang on the street, and he asked Miao Zhuang Wang to do his fortune telling. King Miaozhuang said to him, “Judging from your life, it's better to cut firewood than go fishing.”
Pangu said, “I like fishing too, but where and what day should I go fishing?”
King Miaozhuang said, “I want to go fishing by the Sha River. On the third day of August, I will go when the sun comes out.”
King Miaozhuang also ordered him to “When fishing, don't fish the first one, don't fish the second one; you should focus on that red fish.”
Pangu asked again, “Can you cook it and eat it after fishing?”
King Miaozhuang said, “You must never cook it and eat it! If you want to sell it on the street, don't just sell it. If they want to buy it at zero, you say you want to sell the whole thing; if they want to buy the whole thing, you say you want to sell it at a fraction. Whoever pays the price to 360, you sell the fish to him!”
The red fish turned out to be Dragon King's three princes. After being caught in Pangu, Dragon King was very anxious. He went to the streets every day to look for it, and it was so easy to find someone selling fish. Dragon King was afraid of selling fish, so he cut up the red fish and kept adding money. Finally, he was able to buy this red fish to 316. Dragon King thought it was very strange. He thought: Why did the fish seller prefer to catch this red fish? Someone must have taught him! He then asked Pangu, “Who told you to fish for red fish?”
Pangu said, “This is the fortune teller Miao Zhuang Wang.”
Dragon King thought, how could King Miaozhuang have such ingenious ingenuity? I'll go look for him too. Dragon King found King Miaozhuang and asked, “Mister, please do the math. How do you fix this year's rainy season?”
King Miaozhuang bent his finger and said, “Two points inside the city, three points outside the city.”
Dragon King thought: How the rain will fall is entirely up to me. If you say 2 o'clock inside the city and 3 o'clock outside the city, I prefer to go down 3 o'clock inside the city and 2 o'clock outside the city, depending on how you are.
Who knows, Dragon King did this, and it continued to rain heavily for seven years. The sky is full of water, and the sky has caused a great disaster. The sky has collapsed, and the earth has split. Since then, there has been no heaven and earth, no humans, no sun or moon, and the world has become a black hole.
There is no sky, no land. It continued for several years. Legend has it that Pangu and Pansheng only came out again until the time of the previous dynasty. Pangu said to Pansheng, “Grandpa, I'm going to change the sky, who will change the land?”
Pansheng said, “Grandpa, you change the sky, I'll change the ground!”
As a result, the two brothers went to change the sky one by one, and the other to the earth. The sky changes from the northeast, and the earth from the southwest. Pangu became heaven in the Year of the Rat, and Pansheng became earth in the Year of the Ox.
The heaven and earth they have transformed are incomplete. The sky is not perfect in the southwest, and the ground is flawed in the northeast. Both Pangu and Pansheng are very worried. The daily calculation is how to fill the sky and how to fill the ground. Later, they finally came up with a solution: if the sky is dissatisfied, use clouds to make up for it; if the ground is uneven, fill it with water. Since then, the sky has been perfect, and the ground has been leveled. However, the place where Pansheng changed was larger than the sky that changed in Pangu. What should I do? Later, Pansheng came up with a solution. Pansheng said to Pangu, “My land doesn't match your sky; I'll shrink the ground a little bit.”
Pangu agreed very much with his brother's method, so Pansheng used the land reduction method to shrink his land. As a result of this contraction, many wrinkles appeared on the ground. These wrinkles are mountains on the earth.
After heaven and earth were completed, Pangu and Pansheng died. When Pangu died, he was one foot and eight feet tall. He is lying on his side in Kannon Temple with his head facing east and feet facing west. His eyes are big in a bowl and his mouth is big. Strangely enough, whatever Guanyin called him, he was just like that. Wherever Guanyin's finger goes, he becomes. His left eye became the sun, and his right eye turned into the moon. Open your eyes during the day; when you close your eyes, it's dark. Little teeth became stars, big teeth turned into stones, eyes turned into bamboo, mouths turned into villages, sweat hair turned into trees, small intestines became small rivers, lungs became sea, liver became lakes, nose turned into wind, oil became clouds, flesh turned into earth, bones became big rocks, fingers and toes turned into flying birds and beasts. Two hands and feet became four mountains: the left hand became Mount Jizu, the right hand became Mount Wuliang, the left foot became Cangshan, and the right foot became Laojun Mountain. The tendons became roads, and the fingernails became tiles on the roof. At this point, only the two brothers and sisters left behind by Guanyin were the floods, who were washed away by the flood. Guanyin hid them in a golden drum, and I don't know where they were washed away by the flood. Guanyin went four times down to find these two brothers and sisters. He walked for 99 days and 99 nights, and crossed 99 mountains and 99 rivers. I found Hanyangkou in the east, Hu Sanguo in the west, Leiyin Temple in the north, Putuo Rock in the south, and finally found them in the Erhai Sea. However, they hid in a golden drum. The golden drum floated on the surface of the water, and there was no way to salvage them.
At an unimaginable time, ducks are here, and so are eagles. They are all willing to help. As a result, the duck pushed on the surface of the water, and the eagle spread its wings to help. It took a lot of effort to catch the golden drum.
Although the golden drum has been caught, these two brothers and sisters are still unable to come out after hiding it inside the golden drum. Later, Guanyin went and invited the woodpecker again, gave it a big hat to wear, and asked it to peck off the golden drum. The woodpecker agreed. Unexpectedly, the woodpecker was very loud when it pecked. Guanyin was afraid that it would scare his brother and sister to death, and wouldn't let it peck any more. The big hat on the top was given to it in vain. Now, the red tassel on the woodpecker's head was given by Guanyin that time.
The golden drum couldn't be pecked, so Guanyin went and asked the mouse to help.
The mouse was invited, and it said, “I'd like to kick off the golden drum, but you have to give me 'Elo'!”
Guanyin said, “As long as you bite off the golden drum, I'll give you the grains.”
The mouse agreed, bit after bite, and finally bit off the golden drum. As a result, Guanyin distributed the whole grain to it. So now mice are eating human food everywhere.
The golden drum hit open, and the two brothers and sisters came out, but their bodies were together and couldn't be separated. Guanyin had no choice but to ask Swallow for help and said that as long as it separated the two brothers and sisters, they would allow it to live in people's houses. The swallow agreed, and it cut the two brothers and sisters apart with wings that were faster than a knife. Since then, swallows are different from other birds; they can live under people's roofs.
These two brothers and sisters, the man's name is Zhao Yupei, and the girl's name is Tai Sanmei. Guanyin wanted the two of them to become husband and wife. When the brother and sister heard Guanyin's words, they started crying in a hurry. The two said to Guanyin, “We are brother and sister, how can we be a couple!”
Guanyin said, “There are only two of you in the world now. If you become a couple, you can have children and reproduce.”
Guanyin insisted; the two just didn't rely on it. Guanyin asked them to burn incense in Higashiyama, and the other to burn incense in Nishiyama. Soon, cigarettes from the two mountains slowly converged. Guanyin pointed to the cigarette that had come together and said, “Look, this is a sign that you can become a couple.”
However, the two still refused. Guanyin also told them to take a small wooden stick each, and the two lost it in the river. As soon as the two small wooden sticks were thrown into the river, they soon became two beautiful goldfish swimming around in the water.
Guanyin said to them, “Of the two goldfish in the river, one is male and the other is female. I see what else you have to say.”
However, the two still refused. Guanyin also said, “Each of you carries a grinding wheel and rolls from the top of the mountain to the valley. If you put the two grinding discs together, you have to be husband and wife.” The brother and sister had no choice but to carry a grinding disc and roll from the top of the mountain to the bottom of the mountain. As a result, the grinding plate was put together. The two brothers and sister had nothing to say, but agreed to become a couple.
The two got married. If you don't have a house, use the leaves of a chestnut tree to make a happy house; if you don't have a matriarch, ask a pine tree to be the matriarch; if you don't have a matchmaker, ask a plum tree to hand over the cup; if you don't have a cup, ask a peach tree to hand over the cup.
So far, when the Bai people get married, they are used to hanging chestnut leaves in their happy rooms, planting pine trees in front of the door, and using peach blossoms when handing over cups. It is said that this is where they came from.
On their wedding day, they also invited many birds to help. Plum sparrows made arrangements, pigeons treated guests, crows brought water, magpies cooked, made a dish, made a dish by Zi Panzhao (a type of bird), and entertained tea.
Ten months after the brother and sister got married, they gave birth to a dogskin pocket. I have ten sons in my pocket. After that, the ten sons each gave birth to ten more grandchildren, and they became a hundred families. From then on, they each took their own name. This is the origin of the “People's Family Name.”
