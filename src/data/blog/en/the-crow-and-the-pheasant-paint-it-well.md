 Zuo Wenhan's oral description u2002u2002u2002u2002 collected and sorted by Zuo Dehui
A long, long time ago, crows and green chickens (white-bellied pheasants) were a very good pair of friends. They are all snow-white, foraging for food and play together at the foot of the field. The only thing that can be distinguished is that crows have short tails and green chickens have long tails.
One day, they met a group of beautiful pheasants (red-bellied pheasants) and wanted to socialize with them, and they almost got along. Unexpectedly, the pheasant said, “Ugly, scum, go away!” Crow and Qingji had very low self esteem after listening, and left with interest. The crow said, “What's so amazing? I just painted a little color on my body, so who wouldn't?” Qingji added, “Yes, if we were able to draw better than them all. What are they dragging!”
When the crow and Qingji talk, they can do it. They quickly bought colorful paints. First, the crow painted Qingji. The crows are well-painted. Look, the top, back, and chest of the crow are painted in metallic emerald green; the crown is painted in fuchsia; the back of the shawl is white; the lower back is brown; the lower back is vermilion; the flying feathers are dark brown; the tail is long, depicted with black and white cloud-like markings, and the tail is decorated with several orange feathers; the belly is white; the mouth and feet are blue-gray. Qingji ran to the waterside to take a picture and was very satisfied.
It was Qingji's turn to paint the crow. The crow was very worried that Qingji wouldn't be as good as it, so he ordered the crow to say, “I drew you very beautifully, and you should paint it well too.” Qingji said, “Okay, I will definitely be satisfied with your painting, so you can put your heart into it.” As I said, I started painting the crow's head. Without drawing a few strokes, the crow also ordered, “The head is very important. You have to paint it as beautiful as a phoenix's head; you have to paint it well.” Qingji patiently said, “OK, OK, I will definitely be satisfied with your paintings, so you can rest assured.” Qing Ji continued to paint her head in color while talking until she was satisfied. Then I started painting the crow's back. Without drawing a few strokes, the crow ordered, “The back is critical. You have to paint it as beautiful as the rainbow; you have to paint it well.” Qingji put up with her irritability, but still patiently said, “OK, OK, OK, I promise you'll be satisfied with the painting, so you can rest assured.” Qing Ji continued to paint her back in color while talking until she was satisfied. Then I started painting the tail of the crow. Without drawing a few strokes, the crow repeatedly said, “The tail is a very important position. You must be as detailed as embroidery, and you have to paint it well.” The chicken became very irritated, but she clenched her teeth and said, “OK, OK, OK, OK, I just want you to be satisfied!” While talking, Qingji continued painting the end until she was satisfied. Then I started painting the crow's neck. Without drawing a few strokes, the crow ordered: “The neck is a very important position. You have to be as careful as the finishing touch; you have to be as careful as the finishing touch; you have to draw it well! Good drawing!” Qingji was extremely irritated. He picked up the black paint bowl, split his head and fell down, then painted the crow's whole body with her hands and angrily said, “Let you shatter (talk over and over again, which is disgusting)!” When the crow reacted and was ready to beat the chicken, the chicken burrowed into a stabbed cage in the Qinggou.
To this day, dark crows stand in trees, waiting for the chicken to break out of its cage.
Nagging parents should beware of crows!
