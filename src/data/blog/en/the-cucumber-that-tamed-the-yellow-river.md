 Legend has it that a long, long time ago, the ancient Yellow River was a wild horse that was difficult to tame. It ran freely, like a grinning monster. It roared day and night, and continued to roar day and night, devouring 10,000 hectares of good fields. Biting tens of millions of mountains, the people returning to Han on both sides of the Yellow River can only live a life of slash-and-burn at the top of the mountain and the bottom of the ditch.
At that time, Ningxia was not a plain filled with flat rivers; instead, the green mountains were overlapping and the ravines were wide. There were no flat fields, and none of the fields could fill the Yellow River with water.
Legend has it that a few families lived on Mount Niushou and returned to Han. Their older generations fetched water from the bottom of the mountain and planted land on the top of the mountain. Young and old are busy, but they can't eat enough, can't wear clothes, and no one can stand this kind of torture for a long time.
One year, an old man from 70 years ago, named Erde, opened a melon orchard in the mountains and grew some cucumbers. Every day he gets up early and is greedy. He goes to the Yellow River to fetch water and pour cucumbers. His shoulders are swollen, the soles of his feet are peeling, and he carefully nurtures cucumbers. Huang grows tender and sweet.
Old German got tired this day and fell asleep lying on the vegetable garden door.
As soon as he fell asleep, he dreamed of a white cloud floating in the sky. Gradually, the white cloud became a white bearded imam, shaking his silver beard. He said to De Lao, “There are two strong winds today, so be careful. At noon, there was a yellow wind, which blew the cucumbers; in the afternoons, there was a dark wind that caused the cucumbers to fall. No matter how windy it is, don't pick cucumbers.
Old German woke up and saw that the white bearded imam was missing, but he saw that the yellow wind covered the ground in the north, and suddenly it blew up to Mount Niushou. Old German looked closely at the cucumbers. Sure enough, they were dead one by one. He was very sad. A year of hard work had been in vain, but as soon as he thought of Imam Whitebearded, he didn't move.
At the back of the day, a dark wind passed, and the mountain shook the ground, and the leaves fell layer by layer. As soon as Old German saw that the cucumber was about to land, he was so angry that he removed a thin, frayed cucumber and threw it hard into the Yellow River. The Yellow River immediately broke a line. As if an immortal had cut it with a knife, he clearly saw the bottom of the river. As De Lao Han swooped down, the river “wobbled” to the ground and merged again. The old man was tired and hungry. He sat on the riverbank, blinking his eyes, dancing wildly, and fainted and didn't know anything.
At this point, De Lao Han heard the white-bearded imam say, “This cucumber is the key to conquering the Yellow River. It can be called cutting off the Yellow River, or you can call the Yellow River listen to what people say. But now the Yellow River is called Yellow Wind and Black Wind, and Iberis are struggling. You can't be impatient; you have to work harder. Next year, you grow another garden of cucumbers. When the cucumbers are ripe, you pick the biggest one and throw it into the Yellow River. At that time, you walked into a cave at the bottom of the river, you picked the jewels, you took the grain, and there was also a sword that could slay dragons and kill demons and tame the Yellow River. Wherever you indicated, the water in the Yellow River flowed.”
The following year, the hardworking old German man planted another cucumber garden. He was not afraid of long distances; he did not hesitate to get sweaty, and drew water from the Yellow River to pour melons. It took a lot of hard work. In the end, a three-foot-long cucumber was formed in the garden, which looked like a key. The old man happily slept in the melon garden day and night until the melons were ripe.
On this day, the weather was so clear that there wasn't a single cloud. Old German took the three-foot-long cucumber, read “Tesmi,” and threw it into the Yellow River. At this point, I only heard the roar of the Yellow River, and I could clearly see all the stones that split a long gap at the bottom of the river. Old German went down to the bottom of the river and saw a hole along the riverbank. There were all kinds of pearl agates in the cave. The old man took some. When he first left, he heard a storm roar. At one point, waves rolled over the river, and each wave was higher than the other. Old German took up his sword and slashed dozens of swords at the Black Cyclone and Yellow Cyclone left and right. After a while, the black wind and yellow wind blew out of the sky.
At this point, the gaps in the Yellow River gradually became stricter. Old German was reminded of Imam Whitebearded's instructions. This sword can conquer the Yellow River. His heart was wide open and thought: I want the Yellow River to fill the ditches and lay the mountain beams. The old man, armed with two swords, slashed down the Yellow River. The water in the Yellow River stopped flowing right away. It seemed like the Great Wall had been blocked ahead, but it was rising steadily. Seen from afar, it was really scary.
Three days later, from Liupanshan in the south to Helan Mountain in the west, there was water everywhere, leaving only a few spikes. Only then did the old German man pull out his sword and let the water of the Yellow River flow forward.
Since then, Ningxia, which has deep mountains and ravines, has become a flat river. People back to Han who live on both sides of the Yellow River have relied on their own hard-working hands to open channels and build fields, and have lived a happy life.
