 When we talk about literati, we often have to start with his representative works. What is Su Dongpo's masterpiece? It is difficult to agree on this issue because his works cover various fields such as poetry, calligraphy, and painting, and are innumerable. Every Chinese person has a Su Dongpo in their heart. Su Dongpo turned his life into a work of art. Adhering to the Confucian spirit of entry into the world, he abandoned its utilitarianism, drawing on the Taoist view and comfort and abandoning its emptiness and negativity, drawing on the transcendent wisdom of the Buddha and abandoning its denial of the secular world. His thoughts and personality are not only an inheritance of the sages, but also a fusion and development of all of these traditions. He is not only an official, a scholar, and a literate man, but also a gentle elder who takes us to taste the taste of life and experience the realms of life.
Dongpo meat: the aroma that permeates the room
In Su Dongpo's poetry, there are fish and meat, tea and wine, vegetables and soup, radishes and cabbage, lychee and cherries, puffer fish, and abalone. It can be described as covering everything. His love for food is extraordinary. Under his name, he has a series of classic dishes: Dongpo meat, Dongpo fish, Dongpo tofu, Dongpo cake, Dongpo soup... The most famous of these is Dongpo meat. While being banished to Huangzhou, Hubei, he wrote “Ode to Pork”: “Clean the pan, use less water, and smoke in the firewood. Don't push him when he's used to maturity; when he's excited enough, he's beautiful. Good pork in Huangzhou, the price is as cheap as dirt. The rich won't eat it; the poor won't cook; they get up in the morning and make two bowls, so they can't help but feed themselves.” The pork, which is as cheap as earth, is fat but not greasy, and has a rich flavor due to his unique cooking techniques. After being banished to Huizhou, Guangdong, he had a difficult life. He was only able to buy some lamb ribs that others didn't want. After cooking, they were sprinkled with hot wine, sprinkled with fine salt, and charred slightly. The flavor was also amazing. In a letter to his younger brother Su Ze, he praised, “I am very happy. For example, eating crab sticks, and eating once every few days makes up for it.” After being banished to Danzhou, Hainan, he began studying how to maximize the flavor of raw oysters in a situation of “no meat, no medicine, and no room.” Therefore, what Su Dongpo pursues is not only delicious food, but also uses this to show an attitude towards life. No matter what circumstances fate puts him in, he will treat life with full enthusiasm, never be permissive, never sloppy, immerse himself in it, and do not change his joy.
Dongpo Ga: The Relaxation of Walking in the Countryside
Many of the images of Su Dongpo that we have seen in paintings from the past are wearing cloaks and wooden clogs. Famous artists such as Li Gonglin from the Northern Song, Zhao Mengjian from the Southern Song Dynasty, Zhao Mengjian from the Yuan Dynasty, Tang Yin from the Ming Dynasty, Huang Shen from the Qing Dynasty, and Zhang Daqian from the modern era have all drawn “Dongpo Clogs Map.” This is a story that happened when Su Dongpo lived in Danzhou, Hainan. Regarding this story, Li Gonglin said during his painting, “Dongpo visited Li Ziyun for a day. It rained on the way, so the farmhouse came back wearing fake hats and clogs. Women and children fight with each other, and dogs fight with each other. Dongpo said, “It's strange to laugh, and weird to bark.” Therefore, Dongpo Ga represents not only the chic spirit of Su Dongpo's “one drop of smoke and rain for life,” but also the warmth and strength brought to him by the local people. It's like a seed. Even if fate throws it into a barren stone crevice, it will still take root and grow and exude fragrance as long as there is a little bit of dirt. Poet Lu Li said in the poem “Dirt,” “I always think of myself as a pearl, and I always fear the pain of being buried. Treat yourself as dirt and let everyone step on a path for you.” The reason why Su Dongpo has such tenacious vitality is that he does not think of himself as a pearl, but is able to blend into the earth's soil. The ups and downs of the world and the thousands of changes in plants and trees have all been incorporated into Su Dongpo's life, making him stronger and more prosperous. Beginning in the 4th year of Yuanfeng (1081), he completed a change of mind from an official to a farmer. This place of peace of mind is my hometown. He talks with farmers, fishermen, woodcutters, and vendors in the fields, waterside, mountains, and bazaars, and gets along well. He said to his disciples, “My superiors can accompany Emperor Jade, and my subordinates can accompany Beitanyuan begging. I saw no one bad person in the world right now.” I know everything, and I still feel sorry for Cao Muqing. This is an understanding of life and love for people.
Dongpo Embankment: Writing on the West Lake
Su Dongpo's heart is full of warmth. He cares about people's livelihood, eradicating locusts, fighting floods, educating orphans, and doing whatever he can. “Dongpo builds embankments everywhere”. Su Dongpo built three long embankments in his lifetime, in Hangzhou, Yingzhou, and Huizhou, and is particularly famous for the Suzhou Embankment in Hangzhou. In the 4th year of Yuanyou in the Northern Song Dynasty (1089), Su Dongpo became the governor of Hangzhou. At this time, the West Lake “Fenghe Pinghu had been blooming for a long time, and people still withered after a long period of time”. Fengcao grew wild in the lake, and there was an urgent need for dredging and cleaning up. This matter involves water conservancy and affects people's livelihood. Su Dongpo attached great importance to it. He successively ordered the “Hangzhou Gedu Order to Open West Lake” and the “Six Rules Requesting the Opening of the Lake from the Three Provinces of Shen” to the imperial court to request the opening of the West Lake. After receiving approval from the imperial court, he immediately visited the construction site and mobilized tens of thousands of workers to dig up the silt and remove the grass. During the construction process, a difficult problem was encountered. Dredging the West Lake unearthed a large amount of silt, which had nowhere to be piled up. On the basis of soliciting comments from various parties, Su Dongpo has formulated an excellent solution. Using silt to build a long embankment that crosses the west, south and north, not only can it dispose of silt locally, but it can also facilitate people's travel. It can be described as getting two things in one stroke. In order to prevent Fengcao from growing again, he also thought of a way to rent the surface of the lake on the shore to farmers to grow rhombus horns. It can be said that dredging the West Lake this time has perfectly solved the problems of water conservancy, people's livelihood, and ecology, and has also created beautiful West Lake scenery such as Sudi Chunxiao and Santan Yinyue. Six bridges were built on the embankment, each named Yingbo, Suolan, Wangshan, Embankment, Dongpu, and Crossing Hong. The six bridges divided the embankment into seven sections, just like seven sentences. Su Dongpo left many beautiful verses for the West Lake, “It's better if the water is bright and clear, the sky is strange” and “The dark clouds cover the mountain, and the white rain drifts into the boat,” but perhaps the one that people remember the most is the silent poem Sudi.
Dongpo words: the sound of nature echoing in the earth
Lin Yutang said, “Knowing a person, or not knowing a person, has nothing to do with whether he is of the same generation. The main thing was to know if he had sympathy. At the end of the day, we only know people we really know; we can only fully understand people we really like.” Su Dongpo left many wonderful poems, which we can use to enter his inner emotional world. When I read Dongpo lyrics, I can really read many flavors because he is so three-dimensional and full of vitality. There is the ambition of “being able to carve a bow like a full moon, see the northwest and shoot a sky wolf”, and the endless despair of “I want to cry and be poor, and I can't stand to die”; there is also the sad and miserable feeling of “Xiaoxuan Window, dressing up, nothing but tears”, and the pomp of “going east to the east, the waves are gone, famous people from all over the ages”; there is also the delicate warmth of “Good luck, Smiley in front of the hall, miss me, Mo Jian Rouke” “No rain or wind” is transparent and open-minded; there are “like going through a reed, Ling Wan is at a loss”, and “good pork in Huangzhou, the price is as cheap as dirt” human fireworks. The reason we love Su Dongpo is probably because at some point when we read his poetry, we can vaguely discover our own shadow and have a deep resonance with it. As stated in “The Biography of Su Dongpo,” “His greatest charm is not to let his heart be swallowed up by the environment, but to surpass the environment and illuminate the path of life with his inner light.”
Like a gentle elder, Su Dongpo takes us to enjoy all the flavors of life. From his pen, we can hear the vibrations of the strings of emotions and see the joy of the water flowing; looking at the rushing river from afar, you can also scoop a clear stream; and look up at the shining star man and sit on the floor with his best friends. Today, Su Dongpo is loved by more and more people. With the deepening of civilizational exchanges and mutual appreciation, he is famous not only in ancient times, but also at home and abroad. Among the 12 “World Millennium Heroes” selected by foreign media, Su Dongpo is the only Chinese to be selected. “Meishan Su Shi” has become the “Dongpo of the World.” Su Dongpo is not only a name, but also a spirit. It evokes a kind of expansiveness in our hearts, making our lives wider, deeper, and warmer. We can enjoy ourselves in the closet, enjoy ourselves in the countryside, write chapters on the ground, and enjoy singing songs between heaven and earth. This is the most valuable cultural heritage he left us. (Jing Xiaoyan)
