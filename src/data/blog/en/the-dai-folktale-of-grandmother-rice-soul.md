 Legend has it that millet has a soul. In the Dai language of Xishuangbanna, it is called “Ya Huanhao,” meaning Grandma Gu Hun.
When Buddhism was first introduced to the Dai region of Xishuangbanna, the Buddha claimed everywhere how amazing he was; no one was superior to him.
At this point, Grandma Gu Hun stood up against him, and he drove her out. As a result, it caused a 3-year drought. There is no harvest of millet grains, people get out of the pot without rice, and countless people starved to death.
The Buddha was helpless and had to bring Grandma Gu Huan back.
