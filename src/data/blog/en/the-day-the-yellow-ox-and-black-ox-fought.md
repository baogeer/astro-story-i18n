Once upon a time, a rich kid wanted to go to Beijing to catch the exam. He brought a bookboy with him.
The bookboy is a person who has never seen the world. He went east and west along the way. The person rushing to take the exam was not bothersome; speak to him patiently.
On the first day, they passed by a pond. Two mandarin ducks were chasing and playing in the water. The bookboy asked, “Why did Ago help him? (Chinese meaning: Reading a book, what is that called?)” The reader told him, “This is called 'Mandarin ducks playing in the water'.” The bookboy was worried and forgot, and kept meditating on “Mandarin Ducks Playing in the Water.” Suddenly, he discovered that many dragonflies were flying around the surface of the water, and some even splashed their tails in the water. The bookboy strangely asked, “When did Argo help?” The book reader told him, “This is called 'Dragonflies drink water'.” The bookboy was worried and forgot, and kept meditating on “Dragonflies drink water.” After walking for a while, the dragonfly stood on the lotus flower that was about to open, and the bookboy strangely asked, “When Ago helped, it was a coincidence?” A book reader told him, “This is called 'Little He only showed sharp horns; dragonflies stood on their heads a long time ago. '” The bookboy was worried and forgot, and kept meditating “Little He only showed sharp horns; dragonflies stood on their heads a long time ago.”
The next day, they passed by a meadow, and the readers couldn't help but say, “The wildfires will not burn out, the spring breeze will blow again and again.” The bookboy asked the reader, “My brother helped me, and Adel Rabbit? (Chinese meaning: Reading a book, what does that mean?)” A book reader told him, “It means grass doesn't die easily. As soon as the rain hits in spring, grass roots that didn't burn last year grow again.” The bookboy was worried and forgot, and kept meditating “The wildfires will not stop burning, the spring breeze will blow again and again.” At dusk, they walked across a small bridge, and readers said with emotion, “As the sun sets, people are broken in the sky.” The bookboy asked the reader, “Did Ago help you, and Adel Rabbit?” The reader told him, “It means the sun is about to set. We still don't know where to stay tonight; it's not easy to go out!” The bookboy was worried and forgot, and kept meditating “When the sun sets, people are broken in the world.”
On the third day, they passed by a field where horses, cows, and sheep all ate grass and drink water. Suddenly, for some reason, a scalper and a black cow picked up the shelf, and the bookboy asked, “My brother helped me and got stuck with Ah? (Chinese meaning: Reading a book, what is this?)” The reader told him, “This is called a 'fight between scalpers and black cows. '” The bookboy was worried and forgot, and kept meditating on the “battle between scalpers and black cows.” As they walked, they arrived in Beijing. It was full of people, coming and going, and there was an endless flow of people. Suddenly, an eight-character gentleman stopped them and said, “This young man is here to catch the exam. Let me show you if you can pass the exam this year. I am a living fairy who 'knows half in heaven and knows everything on earth'.” The reader asked, “How much does it cost?” The living fairy held out 5 fingers, and the bookboy said, “Is it five dollars or five or two?” The living fairy said, “Of course it's five or two. Where can I do a five-dollar business as a living fairy?” The bookboy said, “You can do it right. Five or two is not expensive, but if you can't do that, five is not worth it. I'll test you first. Did scalpers fight the day before yesterday, yesterday, or today?” The living fairy was speechless. The bookboy also asked, “The young man said, 'When the sun sets, people are broken in the world. ' Where did you say that?” I still can't say living fairies. The bookboy also asked, “What does 'wildfires don't burn out, spring breeze blows and lives again'?” The living fairy still didn't dare to speak up. The bookboy said, “Living immortals, don't think our country is a cheater. I've seen people like you before.”
The living immortals don't talk; use gestures to signal them to walk.
