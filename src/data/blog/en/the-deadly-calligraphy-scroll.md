 [Author: Wang Renchang (Hubei)]
During the Republic of China, Wangjiangzhai was the number one cultural relics store on Hanzheng Street. This store's antique calligraphy, paintings, and jade carvings are worth a fortune. In particular, the “Lanting Prologue” post copied by a great calligrapher from the Tang Dynasty is the most famous, and is a treasure of the town. The owner, Treasurer Xu, never showed people easily. A Japanese merchant offered 3,000 yuan of gold and was rejected by Treasurer Xu. He said this is a Chinese national treasure; how can I buy it with gold? Newspapers in the three towns of Wuhan learned about the incident and wrote articles praising Treasurer Xu's Gao Fengliang Festival. As a result, Wangjiangzhai's business became more and more prosperous.
Early this morning, Treasurer Xu inspected the treasure house where important cultural relics were placed as usual. The bodyguard hung straight on the beam and lost air. A Ming Dynasty mahogany bench at his foot was knocked over, and the Western bell at the foot of the wall was knocked over, and the Western bell at the foot of the wall was also stopped. There were no obvious signs of flipping in the room, but the text was gone!
Sergeant Wang of the Hanzheng Street Police Station received the report and immediately rushed to the scene to investigate and discovered that the lower end of the iron fence on the window had been cut off more than one centimeter of round iron bars. Half a centimeter wooden stakes were inserted in the mortise where the iron bars were installed, but the saw edge of the iron strip was stained with rust. Obviously, it had already been cut off, making it easy for people to get in and out. Sergeant Wang discovered two long hairs on the bodyguard's bed and a lipstick mark on his collar. He picked up the Western bell that had fallen on the ground, pointed the clock at 5:30, then went to the back garden to inspect it. He saw a ladder with a special advantage leaning under the back window. There was only a broken Yuan Dynasty blue and white porcelain plate under the ladder... Soon, Sergeant Wang drew an outline of the case: the bodyguard hooked up a woman and cut off the iron fence to let the woman take the elevator from the window and enter the room for a private party. Last night, while the bodyguard was sleeping, the woman stole the postcard and carried a porcelain plate by the way. She accidentally broke it while going down the stairs, and woke up the bodyguard. The bodyguard discovered that he had been fooled and was unable to explain it to Treasurer Xu; he had to use a bench to stand on his feet and hang himself. Before he died, he flipped over a square bench and tripped over the Western Bell, which fell to the ground and stopped.
After receiving the notification of the case, the Hankou Police Department felt that the case was serious, and sent Detective Ouyang Changfeng to the Hanzheng Street Police Station to preside over the investigation work. After some investigation and inspection, Ouyang analyzed that the bodyguard's death was a homicide, not a suicide. If it was a suicide, the face would be pale or reddish black, but the deceased had obvious blue-purple swelling on his face and oval-shaped subcutaneous hemorrhage caused by two nails on his neck. It can be seen that the deceased was first strangled to death and then hung on a beam. Sergeant Wang was puzzled, “Can a woman stand up to such a strong man?” Ouyang nodded, “There must be another young man who is over 1 meter 75 tall...” Sergeant Wang is still puzzled: “However, only one person's fingerprints were found in the room!” Ouyang smiled and said, “Sergeant Wang, have you noticed that there aren't even a few fingerprints on the blue and white porcelain plate!” Seeing that Sergeant Wang was speechless, Ouyang went on to analyze, “This indicates that a person wearing gloves stole and accidentally broke a porcelain plate.” Speaking of which, Ouyang set the minute hand of the Western clock to 12 o'clock. The hour hand is exactly 6 o'clock. According to the normal report, it should have rang six times, but the clock only rang twice. Sergeant Wang and Treasurer Xu were once again shocked when they saw them. Treasurer Xu asked, “Did this clock break?” Ouyang smiled and said, “It wasn't that the bell was broken, but the culprit set the hour and minute hands directly to 5:30 in order to divert attention. In fact, the time of the incident was between 1 a.m. and 2 a.m. Judging from the many marks that the ladder has rubbed against the wall, the bodyguard has been in a relationship with the woman for a long time. It is certainly not easy for one person to move such a long ladder, but it is also remarkable to walk along the road. It can be seen that the murderer's residence is not far from here.”
Ouyang further analyzed that the two ladders made of fir looked very rough and were made by the criminal himself. The price of processing a ladder is not expensive; the main thing is that you don't want others to pay attention to it. “But they still showed telltale signs.” Ouyang pointed to a black fiber in his hand and said, “This is the criminal hanging on a ladder. It's a cotton thread on an ordinary Hunan person's black headscarf. The culprit is probably from Hunan, and this man and woman are from a winery or hotel.” He was holding a rectangular piece of glass, and several footprints were clearly visible on it. Sergeant Wang immediately recognized that they were newly left by two people. Ouyang immediately took a piece of clean glass to demonstrate, “This method of extracting footprints is most suitable for smooth surfaces and floors.” While talking, he rubbed the glass over and over again. Soon, many particles were glued to the glass plate. “Friction creates static electricity. The static electricity absorbs the particles, and the things left on the criminal's feet are collected. I've tested that the footprints of men and women are all smudges of alcohol that cannot be seen with the naked eye, so the perpetrator came from a place where alcohol was made and stayed there for at least a while... the ladder was placed there, and a lot of lees were glued to it. That house is not too deep; otherwise, you won't be able to put down the ladder; the house where the ladder is placed must be around the corner.” Treasurer Xu was astonished. What surprised him even more was that Ouyang determined that the mastermind of the murder was an antiques connoisseur. He even shouted, “The detective said no. The culprit must have been chasing the post. If you don't take so many gold and silver utensils in the treasure house, just pick blue and white porcelain plates. You're a true antique connoisseur!” Immediately, Sergeant Wang carried out a raid on Taibai Restaurant.
Taibai Restaurant is separated from Wangjiangzhai in an alley, and the house is not too deep; it takes up half the road. The other half is a home dyeing shop, with a facade facing the Han River. Taibai Restaurant also flourished for decades. It spread to this generation. Men got tuberculosis, got rickets all the time, and the disease was crooked. The business fell out right away. It was all supported by Yang Jinhua, the boss of the house. Yang Jinhua is flirtatious, charming, and lascivious. Seeing that men are useless, she flirts with and curses her customers and engages in business with color. They say it's a restaurant, open a teahouse, and set up a card board. Every midnight, they also let the Huangpi Xiaogan caodai team go out and sing a fluent flower drum show. When teased by a favorite character, Yang Jinhua lured him to the back room to get along. The sick man knew he was a waste, so he had to keep his eyes open and his eyes closed. Wang Jiangzhai's bodyguard beat Yang Jinhua's idea. She once scoffed, “They don't pee and take pictures of themselves; toads want to eat swan meat!” Yang Jinhua thought he was being crude. He made abusive remarks and scolded him, so angry that his bodyguard set the table off.
Sergeant Wang is unsuspected of committing the crime because he discovered that during the holiday season, and several people proved that Yang Jinhua played mahjong all night and only left the show at 7 a.m. the next morning. However, after some reasoning from Ouyang, Yang Jinhua became a major suspect. Sergeant Wang brought someone to Taibai Restaurant. There was no business, but the door was covered up. He went to the bedroom to take a look. The sick man was strangled to death in his bed. Arriving on the second floor, I saw Yang Jinhua struggling on the floor with a dagger stuck in his chest, covered in blood. Sergeant Wang quickly called for help. Yang Jinhua pointed to the southwest: “Yes... Yuan... the second brother...” and passed out without saying anything.
Sergeant Wang immediately took someone directly to Baoqing Hall East No. 1, but neither did they find any postings, let alone see Yuan Laoer's shadow. Ouyang smiled and said, “Would someone who is in a hurry to get their stolen goods sit at home and wait for you?” Sergeant Wang understood that. He immediately rushed to the Japanese concession, arrested Yuan Laoer, and carried the post in his arms.
It turns out that Yuan Laoer is from Shaoyang, Hunan. He makes a living by stealing tombs while also reselling antiques and making and selling counterfeit antiques. One day, he saw the news in the “Tai Kung Pao” that Treasurer Wang Jiang Zhai had this post and didn't pay for a few thousand dollars, and he couldn't help but think crooked. As a merchant, he stayed at the Baoqing Hall near Wangjiangzhai and began to think about how to get his hands on that baby thing.
Back then, teahouses and restaurants were the best places for information. Yuan Laoer often went to Taibai Restaurant to drink alone. Yang Jinhua saw that Yuan Laoer was young, handsome, and aggressive. When he spoke, he deliberately made waves and wanted to be close to him, but Yuan Laoer turned a blind eye and had no intention of getting close to him. One day, he saw a strong man being scolded by Yang Jinhua and lost his temper. He also heard that he was the nephew of Treasurer Wang Jiang Zaixu, a security guard at the treasure house, and immediately had an idea. Afterwards, he took the initiative to message Yang Jinhua and soon hooked up and became an rapist. Yuan Laoer tricked Yang Jinhua into saying that his wife had just died and wanted to marry her to revive the Taibai Restaurant family business. Yang Jinhua smiled with joy: “The sick man can only delay it for a year at most. When he dies, the restaurant will be renovated. With my connections, I will definitely be able to do business full of people, and the guests will be happy.” Yuan Laoer took the opportunity to suggest, “If you want to decorate the restaurant, you'll have to make a lot of money. I see that the bodyguard is interested in you. Why don't you coax him. Taking whatever you want from the treasure house is enough for us to spend the rest of our lives!”
Furthermore, even though this bodyguard was ridiculed by Yang Jinhua, I really couldn't bear her figure. He was addicted to going to the restaurant every day. He didn't want Yang Jinhua to take care of himself, so he was in a hurry to be affectionate with her in the restaurant. Yang Jinhua half-pushed and said, “No, sick people are watching all day long; they are fearful and unhappy! Why don't you go to your place at night...” The bodyguard shook his head and said, “No one will let me in my place!” Yang Jinhua said, “I can jump in with a ladder from the back window!” The bodyguard was infatuated and promised to cut off a round iron bar to let her in and out. In this way, the two delivered ladders from the back wall of the restaurant every night, and then climbed the ladders from the roadway into Wangjiangzhai's back garden, so that Yang Jinhua climbed the ladder into the treasure house. Without much effort, Yang Jinhua asked the bodyguard where the postings were collected.
This night, Yuan Laoer gave his bodyguard a bottle of liquor mixed with narcotics and asked Yang Jinhua to falsely claim that it was aphrodisiac medicinal wine. If you haven't finished drinking the bodyguard, you'll be unconscious. Yuan Laoer immediately entered the room and joined forces with Yang Jinhua to strangle the bodyguard and fake the hanging scene. In a hurry, the overturned bench hit the Western Bell. Yuan Laoer made a clever move and set the clock to 5:30 p.m. After stealing the post, he unwittingly glanced at the blue and white porcelain plate. Feeling like it was a treasure, he picked it up. Unexpectedly, because he was wearing gloves and couldn't move easily, he had just turned over and put the round iron bar on. The blue and white porcelain plate was particularly clear and harsh in the early morning. He was so scared that he couldn't even move the ladder. He pulled Yang Jinhua around the courtyard wall to the edge of a willow tree, then climbed over the wall and escaped. The bodyguard asked Yang Jinhua to go back to the restaurant to listen to the movie and play cards, and brought back to the club with his own handwriting. After a period of ecstasy, he was also worried that Yang Jinhua would spill out in such places where people came and went, so he took his heart and decided to kill and exterminate people. He first found Yang Jinhua and discussed killing the sick guy so they could get married quickly. After the two joined forces to strangle the sick person, while they were upstairs to have fun, Yuan Laoer couldn't help but pull out a dagger and insert it into Yang Jinhua's chest. She screamed and fell into a pool of blood. However, she only passed out and did not die on the spot. Yuan Laoer was too late to clean up the scene, so he hurriedly rushed to the Japanese rental sector, found the Japanese businessman, and said he was willing to write in 1,000 yuan of gold. Unexpectedly, the Japanese merchant waved his hand and said, “If you don't know your baby, don't give it to me!”
Yuan Laoer inevitably had a bit of a downturn. While on his way back in despair, he was caught at the intersection of Yamasaki Street in the Japanese Concession.
