 Author: Li Xiaomin
In the early years of the Republic of China, there was an antique store called “Xiao Yaxuan” in the west of Shaoyang, Hunan. The owner, Chen Yan, was a fat man in his forties. He usually laughed and laughed and treated people very kindly. However, in the antique industry in the Hunan region, he is a famous figure. Whether it is an antique from the Ren dynasty, ask him to appreciate it, and you can immediately say that this thing is inseparable. Even fakes cannot escape his eyes; he is known as “Divine Chen.”
In the winter of this year, heavy snow was raging against the wind. A young scholar named Liang Zifei appeared in Shaoyang City. Seeing him wearing a thin blue shirt, carrying a strangely shaped guqin, and walking lightly to the “Xiaoya Xuan” door, he took off the strange piano seat on his back and sat on a snow fan with a large margin. What's even more amazing is that every time he plays for a while, he takes out a black oil paper fold and brushes it on his body a few times. Instead of being in the cold and freezing wind and snow, the weather was the same as in June when the sun was hot.
Some time ago, Chen Yan was buying a copy of Tang Yin's masterpiece “A Beautiful Woman's Journey to Spring” at a high price from a disappointed merchant in Beijing. She was overjoyed. She took it out and enjoyed it in detail every day. On this day, he was watching a painting. Suddenly he heard someone playing the piano outside his house. The song was bland and, but the sound of the piano was round and smooth, and full of harmony. As soon as he heard it, he knew that this piano was definitely not an ordinary thing, so he packed up the paintings and walked out of the door. Liang Zifei ignored him and was still concentrating on playing the strange piano in his hand. Chen Yan stepped forward curiously and stared at the piano. It didn't matter; she was almost so excited that Chen Yan jumped. Turns out, that strange piano was an uncompromising Han Dynasty guqin. There aren't many remains of this kind of piano left in the world. When Chen Yan saw that Liang Zifei's shirt was vaguely shabby and pretentious, she guessed that he might be a frustrated child from a famous family. He tried to sell the piano himself, so she went straight ahead and said, “This brother, you play the piano in front of the small store; it's probably not just because of your elegance!” Liang Zifei still ignored him. Chen Yan also said, “Brother, this piano also looks a bit old, but he also loves antiques. I don't know if you want to sell the piano to me or not?”
At the end of the song, Liang Zifei coldly said, “Sir, seeing me sitting in the snow, he didn't ask me if I was cold or not, but he was playing my piano all the time. This is not the way to treat guests.” As soon as Chen Yan blushed and felt very ashamed, she invited Liang Zifei into the store. Liang Zifei was also unkind; he put away his piano and followed Chen Yan in the door.
The two sit down. Chen Yan asked the family to fry a few side dishes, heat a pot of wine, and chat after reporting each other's names. Unexpectedly, the two were very speculative and felt like they had met and hated each other late. Chen Yan saw Liang Zifei wandering alone in a foreign country, but when they talked loudly, they got to know each other: “Brother, I don't know if you came to Shaoyang for business or something else?” Liang Zifei took a sip of wine, and his face felt a bit bleak. “My little brother is from all over the world. Today, I just wanted to mix a bowl of rice to eat; I can't talk about work.” Chen Yan was delighted: “This is the best. I have a seven-year-old child who is usually very mischievous and capricious. I don't know if my brother would like to take him as a student and teach him how to read?” Without thinking about it, Liang Zifei readily agreed.
In this way, Liang Zifei stayed at “Xiao Yaxuan”. He usually taught Chen Yan's son how to learn calligraphy and read Tang poetry and Song lyrics. When he was fine, he chatted with Chen Yan about poetry, books, piano paintings, and antiques, and often had amazing insider opinions. One day, Chen Yan took out Tang Yin's “A Picture of Beautiful People Outing Spring” and showed it to Liang Zifei. Liang Zifei held the painting, studied it, and even cried out, then said, “This painting is worth a thousand two thousand dollars!”
It's been a few more days like this. On the 1st, Chen Yan was chatting with Liang Zifei in the store. Suddenly, two big men walked through the door and shouted that they wanted to buy “Beautiful People Outing Spring”. As soon as they entered, Chen Yan arched her hand and said, “Unfortunately for the two, “Beautiful People Outing the Spring Festival” is my own private collection and is not for takeout. However, there is no shortage of other famous calligraphy and paintings. The two want...” Unexpectedly, the two men didn't wait for Chen Yan to finish and took out a small gold knife weighing about one or two from his pocket. Poke hard at the counter, then arched his hand: “The deposit will be placed at the treasurer's place first. After three days, our owner will pick up the painting himself and look forward to the treasurer's Keep it safe first.” After speaking, the two walked out of the house in stride and left.
Chen Yan paused for half a minute before hurrying to see the little golden knife on the table. As soon as she saw a big change in her face, she blurted out: “Malaysian Golden Sword!” Turns out, this Malaysian Golden Slayer is one of the most famous and powerful bandits in the Shonan area. His subordinates had more than 200 guns. In his early years, he only fought against wealthy government officials and never bothered ordinary people; he was also considered a good man. Unexpectedly, in the past two years, the Golden Dao of Malaysia has suddenly become cruel and unkind. As long as he sees something, he will do everything in his power to get it, and not even ordinary people will be spared. That small golden knife, which weighed about one or two, was his mark and proof that he had come to retrieve the item.
Disaster fell from the sky, and Chen Yan couldn't help but be foolish. She walked around the house in a panic, uneasy. But Liang Zifei didn't panic. He smiled faintly and said, “Brother Chen doesn't need to be alarmed; little brother has his own way to help you return his Golden Sword.” After hearing it, Chen Yan looked at Liang Zifei with confusion; she was skeptical.
It was fine for two days in a row. On the night of the third day, Liang Zifei opened the courtyard door, imitating Kong Ming, and had a drink with Chen Yan in the courtyard if nothing had happened.
When Leng Yue was empty, Liang Zifei smiled at Chen Yan and said, “The moon is clear and the wind is high. Can Brother Chen enjoy listening to his little brother play a song?” Chen Yan nodded uncertainly. Great piano sound. It's just that this time the piano sounds very different from the past. Normally, Liang Zifei's piano sound is always flat and steady, and there is no surprise. But this time, the sound of the piano suddenly became like a thunder of lightning. The sound of the piano seemed like dark clouds were pressing against Shaoyang City... At its peak, someone suddenly shouted loudly at the entrance. Afterwards, more than a dozen masked men dressed in black walked into the courtyard. The first one must have been a Malaysian Golden Knife.
As soon as Chen Yan blushed, Liang Zifei was calm and at ease. She was still playing the piano like no one else was around. She stopped for half a day and smiled coldly at the Malaysian Golden Dao team and said, “At home, how do you think my songs are playing?” The golden sword of Malaysia stared dead on the strange piano in Liang Zifei's hand. His whole body shook like five thunder. As soon as the hand net was played behind him, he disappeared without a shadow. Liang Zifei laughed out loud; Chen Yan was also confused for a moment. The two sat down for a while, and there was a lot of people outside the door. The Malaysian Golden Sword went back and forth with dozens of big men holding fast guns. Liang Zifei's face was already a bit angry at this point: “If Leung wants to kill people in Japan today, you can't live or die; I think I'm afraid of you.”
Malaysia's golden sword laughed out loud: “I know you'll be looking for me sooner or later. I almost got fooled by you, so I don't believe you'll have thousands of troops and horses here tonight... Besides, no matter how good your martial arts are, how can you escape dozens of fast guns under my command? How would you kill me again?!” Liang Zifei tapped the strings lightly and sneered: “Will it take thousands of troops and horses to take your dog's life? Today, Mr. Liang will send you to the west with just one broken piano!” After speaking, my hands swam fast on the strings. The sound of the piano hits the shore, sometimes flying sand and stones, sometimes beautiful and moving... the crowd couldn't help but be fascinated. Halfway through, the sound of the piano stopped, and then looked at the Malaysian Golden Sword. It had already shed seven bits of blood and was violently killed on the spot. Liang Zifei didn't raise his head and said, “Listen, if anyone else takes the name of Malaysia's Golden Sword to commit crimes and beat up the treasurer in the future, the end will be the same as this person. Do you understand?” A group of bandits stood there for half a day, then nodded in unison, then picked up the corpse of a fake Malaysian Golden Knife and swarmed away.
Seeing all of this, Chen Yan actually felt like a dream. When she was in her dark, she heard Liang Zifei say, “Brother Chen doesn't need to be alarmed. I'm telling you now that I am the real Malaysian Golden Slayer. I'm also here for “A Beautiful Woman's Journey to Spring,” but she just couldn't bear to get started. A game between you and my friends is also considered fate. Paint, I don't want it anymore. Don't stop there; there will be a later date!” After speaking, I carried that murderous piano and jumped out of the house. By the time Chen Yan woke up and chased him outside the door, the cold street was empty.
