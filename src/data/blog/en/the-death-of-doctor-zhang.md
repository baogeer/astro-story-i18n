 An urgent phone call woke Detective Luo from his dream, “Hey, what's the matter?” Detective Luo asked, “Lao Luo, Dr. Zhang from the Changjiang Road Security Clinic was poisoned and died. Now most people are concentrating on cracking down on opium smuggling. Let's handle this case for you! Let my guard, Xiao Liu, go and help you!” The call was made by Director Li of the Public Security Bureau. Detective Luo is used to Director Li's commanding tone. Right now, his mission is to do his best to investigate this case.
Detective Luo and Guard Liu arrived at the scene of the crime. The deceased was a famous local traditional Chinese medicine practitioner. He was lying on a table. His right hand was red and swollen. There was a teapot and teacup next to him. Obviously, as Director Li said, he died from poisoning. Seeing Detective Luo's arrival, the old grandson, who claimed medicine in the medicine cabinet, came over. Detective Luo asked, “When did Dr. Zhang die?” , “I don't know, I slept early last night.” Detective Luo saw that Grandson was a very responsible person. “Well, didn't you hear anything last night?” , “I remember your reminder. His son Zhang Ming came back very late last night. The father and son seemed to have had a lot of arguments and both fell down! It woke me up!” , “Is Dr. Zhang's son home?” Detective Luo asked, “I haven't seen anyone since morning. I think I went out early.” Detective Luo then asked, “Has Dr. Zhang acted any differently than usual these days?” , “There doesn't seem to be any, but I think it's probably the poison under the small plug!” Detective Luo opened his eyes and asked, “Why did you say that?” , “Yesterday, Xiao Shuangzi was beaten after Dr. Zhang found out about stealing money from the cabinet!” , “Then why didn't you say it sooner!” Detective Luo asked, keeping his eyes on the old grandson, “Me... I don't dare to talk nonsense; I didn't see him this morning until I saw him this morning!” Detective Luo took a close look at the corpse again. Based on his experience, he discovered that it was a very rare type of poison in Dr. Zhang. It is very difficult to know the toxicity of this poison if you don't understand chemistry or pharmacy! “If that's true as my grandson said, then there is a big suspicion that Xiao Shuzi murdered people. Because he has been working in the medicine cabinet for so many years, he must know something about poison; he must quickly find Xiao Muzi!” , Detective Luo thought to himself. Detective Luo was about to say goodbye to his old Sun and went outside. Suddenly, he heard the old Sun cursing: “You bastard, you poison Dr. Zhang and still dare to come back! Detective Luo, he's just a little plug!” Detective Luo looked up and saw a teenager walking in. The teenager heard his grandson's curse and was about to run outside. Detective Luo stepped forward and knocked him to the ground with an arrow. “Did you poison it?” Detective Luo asked, “What poison? I don't know!” Little Shuangzi seemed at a loss, “So where were you last night?” , “I... me, I went to the smoking bar?” , “What? Your kid actually smoked a big cigarette!” Grandson said angrily on the sidelines. Detective Luo let go of his hand. Xiao Shuangzi quickly climbed up, patted the dirt on his body, and timidly watched Detective Luo and his grandson. “OK, don't go to the smokehouse again; I won't spare you if I catch it! Also, my grandson, let me know when Dr. Zhang's son will come back and I will be in the Public Security Bureau!” When Detective Luo finished speaking, he went outside. It seemed like Lao Sun had something else to say, but Detective Luo had already gone a long way.
“Detective Luo, why don't we arrest Xiao Shuangzi; he is an important suspect!” Guard Liu asked, “Yes, at first I thought Xiao Bozi was the murderer, but there were three things that ruled out my doubts about him.” Detective Luo seriously said, “First, Xiaobongzi is a kid, if he did local poison, he couldn't have thought so much about coming back; second, I haven't seen that kind of poison until now, but it's definitely very toxic and very rare. It's impossible for Xiao Shuzi to get it easily; third, if Xiaobangzi steals from the cabinet to buy this poison, it's even less reasonable for him. Money is definitely going to smoke opiates!” Guard Liu was in agreement with Detective Luo's analysis, “So who do you think the murderer is?” Detective Luo pondered for a while and said, “Maybe it's his son. Didn't the old grandson say their father and son had an argument last night? It's just that we don't have proof yet! What makes me wonder is that the teapot is not toxic in the teapot!” , “So what are we going to do now?” Guard Liu asked, “Now let's take a look at how Dr. Zhang's father and son are doing on weekdays!” Detective Luo and Security Guard Liu learned nearby that Dr. Zhang was a good person who saved the dead and helped the wounded, but his son did not do his job properly, and recently he often left early and left late and didn't know what he was doing.
Back at the Public Security Bureau, Director Li is in a meeting to arrange to arrest the opium smuggling incident at the pier this evening. The two of them just sat down, and the old grandson walked in under the guard at the entrance. “Detective Luo, Dr. Zhang's son is back and he is crying and living when he sees his father die!”
When I arrived at the security clinic, I could hear Dr. Zhang's son crying from afar. Seeing Detective Luo arrive, he stopped crying, “Detective Luo, we must find out the murderer to avenge my father!” At this point, Detective Luo discovered that Zhang Ming's wrist was bandaged and asked, “Zhang Ming, why were you arguing with your father last night?” Zhang Ming answered, “I came back very late last night, because when I was playing at the opposite clinic, Dr. Ma said I had a bad face, gave me a pulse diagnosis, and asked my father to quickly go home to give me a detailed diagnosis. When I got home, my father was still waiting for me. When he saw me, he first scolded me, then diagnosed me, saying I wasn't sick!” I was already in tears after saying, “Yes, Guard Liu, let's go back. Don't leave the house until Zhang Ming finds out about your father!”
Along the way, Detective Luo and Guard Liu have been thinking about this case. “Was it Zhang Ming's poison?” Guard Liu first asked, “It's not like that, but I'm thinking about it now. Why did Dr. Ma say Zhang Ming is sick, but his father says he doesn't?” Security Officer Liu smiled and said, “Detective Luo, you don't have to be too selfish! Maybe one of them made a mistake!”
In the evening, Detective Luo demonstrated in his mind Dr. Zhang's daily work: after breakfast, coming to the patient, diagnosing the veins..., diagnosing the veins! The problem is in the context of diagnosis, such a cunning murderer! “Guard Liu, we were tricked by Zhang Ming. It was Zhang Ming who killed his father! Let's go and catch Zhang Ming!” Detective Luo excitedly shouted that Guard Liu didn't understand the reason, but when he saw Detective Luo's full grasp, he followed him outside. The two just walked to the door. One broke in in a panic and knocked them down, “Detective Luo?” The person asked, “It's me, what's wrong?” , “The one who smuggled the opium was our Dr. Ma, the one opposite the security clinic.” The person said, “Detective Luo just wanted to go back and check, only to see that Director Lee led someone back. “How, have you caught them?” Detective Luo asked with concern. Director Li sighed: “The opium was seized but the main offender was not caught. He killed one of his helpers and fled. Yes, the deceased was Dr. Zhang's son!” , “Zhang Ming?” Detective Luo had his heart set for a while. Could Dr. Zhang's death be related to opium smuggling? Zhang Ming was involved in opium smuggling, but no matter what, check Zhang Ming's wrist first. Detective Luo removed the bandage on Zhang Ming's wrist before coming to Zhang Ming's body. Sure enough, just as he had guessed. Zhang Ming's wrist was red and swollen. The symptoms were the same as Dr. Zhang's finger! It was Dr. Zhang who was treating his son's heart with the highly toxic poison that Zhang Ming applied to his wrist, then Dr. Zhang drank the poison while drinking tea. Just a little bit of this poison was enough to poison people! “Director Lee, I just mentioned that the main culprit in smuggling opium at the pier is Dr. Ma. He is the pharmacist at Dr. Ma's clinic.” Detective Luo pointed to the visitor and said, “Really? If you lie, you'll get sued!” Director Li said to the visitors, “Yes, this afternoon I heard them say they would pick up the goods at the pier at night. Dr. Ma just ran back. He came to Jiangcheng just now to smuggle opium!” “Lead the way and catch people quickly!”
Dr. Ma was treating the patient. Seeing the police arrive, he very calmly drove away all the patients, “Arrest him!” Director Li yelled, “Dr. Ma, why are you instructing Zhang Ming to poison his father?” Seeing that Dr. Ma, who also diagnosed Zhang Ming, had no swelling in his hand, Detective Luo wanted to unravel the mystery, “Haha..., Zhang Ming, a fool, didn't understand when he died that he poisoned his father himself!” When Dr. Ma was about to die, he still had some opinions. Obviously, he was satisfied with his carefully planned plot. “On that day, Dr. Zhang came to me and told me not to take his son to commit a crime, so I thought I must get rid of this colleague. Of course, it was also because my medical skills were not as good as him! At night, I tricked Zhang Ming into saying that he was sick. After the pulse diagnosis, I put severe poison on his wrist and asked him to go home quickly to have his father diagnose him. Of course, traditional Chinese medicine requires diagnosis. Zhang Ming is afraid of death; of course his father loves his child and will be fooled!” Dr. Ma laughed creepy after speaking.
