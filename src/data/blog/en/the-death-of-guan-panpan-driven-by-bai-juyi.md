 Guan Panpan was born during the first years of Tang Dezongzhen. Because of his background in Shuxiangmendai, he is fluent in poetry, and also has singing and dancing skills. He has a beautiful appearance, making countless wealthy boys very fond of it. However, the good times didn't last long. Guan's family fell into disarray, and Guan Panpan was taken by Xuzhou manager Zhang Yu as a concubine. Although Zhang Yi is a martial artist, he likes elegant, literary and artistic songs and dances, so even though he has many wives, he only cares for one person.
Once, Bai Juyi came to Zhang Fu as a guest. Guan Panpan performed a song and dance for the guests. Bai Juyi was amazed, saying, “Drunken Jiao can't win, the wind is full of peonies.”
Two years later, Zhang Yi died of illness in Xuzhou. The Zhang family's wives and concubines found their own way, but Guan Panpan was determined to keep the festival. She lives with an elderly servant in a swallow house on the outskirts of Xuzhou and lives a clean and laid-back life. The Swallow House is a villa that Zhang Yi built for Guan Panpan before her birth. Because there is a small river in front of the building, willows are planted along the riverside. Every spring, pairs of swallows fly through willows, hence the name Swallow House. Now all I have to do is live alone in the room. Her unswerving character has won many praises, but one person raised a different opinion. This person is Bai Juyi.
Bai Juyi was deeply moved by Pan Pan's deep affection, but he thought that since Guan Panpan was so affectionate, so he wouldn't go down to Jiuquan to spend time with Zhang Yi, wouldn't that be more complete? So he wrote a poem:
Gold did not hesitate to buy a woman's mee, picking four or five pieces of flowers; the teaching of singing and dancing was exhausting, and it didn't go with her once.
Bai Juyi probably didn't mean to force Guan Panpan to die a martyr when she wrote this poem, but after reading it, Guan Panpan didn't think so. She cried: I spent ten years on Zhang Yu's holiday. I didn't risk my life and refuse to follow; I was just afraid that people would talk about my husband's bad name and force my concubine to die. Wouldn't that have tarnished my husband's good name?! Why is Bai Juyi so ruthless, forcing me to go to a dead end?!
In anger, Guan Panpan began a hunger strike, and no one's persuasion could change her mind. At a time when she was weak and frail, she wrote a poem about Bai Juyi: Children were ignorant of anything, and sweated all over the mud.
Bai Juyi was shocked when he heard the news of Guan Panpan's death. He felt that he had an unshirkable responsibility, and his heart was full of admiration and guilt. Probably because of this experience, he let Shi Ji Fan Su and Xiao Man go their separate ways when he was in his twilight years, so as not to cause a tragedy like Guan Panpan.
