 Li Shi Shi was originally the daughter of Wang Yin, who runs a dyeing house in Bianjing City. When she was three years old, her father named her a Buddhist temple. The old monk put a roof over her, and she suddenly cried. The old monk thought she was very much like a Buddhist disciple. Because everyone called a Buddhist disciple a “teacher,” she was called Master Li. After a year, my father died in prison for his crime. She was also raised by her neighbors, and gradually became beautiful and fair-skinned. Li Yi, who runs a brothel business, adopted her and taught her to play piano, chess, calligraphy, painting, and singing and dancing. Li Shi Shi became a famous prostitute in Bian Jing for a while. She was the target of competition between the literati Masashi and Gong Zi Wang Sun. In the end, even Song Huizong was famous, and he wanted to be friendly. Gao Yu and Yang Yi naturally encouraged Song Huizong and vowed not to miss the news.
As soon as he saw Master Li, Song Huizong felt that all these years had simply been in vain. Li Shi Shi's temperament, gentle and elegant, made Song Huizong like a dream. Shi Li and Gao Yi have known each other for a long time. Seeing that the high-ranking gentleman actually treated this unfamiliar guest with respect and was puzzled, but it is certain that this is also an unoffended expert and noble, so he served them very kindly.
