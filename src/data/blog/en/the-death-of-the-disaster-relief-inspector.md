 (Historical story)
Author: Zhu Ruimin
(Qing) At the turn of summer and autumn in Jiaqing in '13, heavy rain poured over the past few days in the Huanghuai basin, and the embankment broke down. Water from the Yellow River has engulfed fields, swallowed up villages, swept away countless cows and sheep, and people too late to escape. The Huai'an area was the hardest hit area. There were many refugees, hungry, and the scene was bleak.
An appeal rushed to the Forbidden City. The Jiaqing emperor was anxious and worried. As a last resort, the Jiaqing emperor disbursed 300,000 two relief funds from the empty treasury. At the same time, he ordered the Governor of Liangjiang Tiebao to appoint a group of reliable officials to monitor the distribution of the money relief and severely punish corrupt officials who took advantage of the floods.
After the Tiebao received the order, they didn't dare to slow down. They immediately disbursed the money and carefully selected the aid workers. Among them, the inspector sent to Shanyang County, the hardest hit area was Li Yuchang.
Li Yuchang, a native of Jimo, Shandong, is a newcomer in his career. He is only 23 years old. He is tall, thin, and has a clear face. He has quite a Confucian style. He acted decently, impartially and impartially. He was hailed as “Li Qingtian” by the public when he was the chief administrative secretary of Qipin in Chengyang.
Li Yuchang arrived at the Governor's Office in response to the decree.
Tiebao seriously ordered, “The money for disaster relief is a gift from the emperor to the victims. No one can withhold it halfway; you must verify it one by one. If you find out about embezzlement of money, don't be complacent.”
“Obey orders!” Although the answer was only two words, Li Yuchang also knows that these two words weigh a thousand pounds. In an age where corrupt officials are rampant and officials protect each other, how easy is it to enforce the law impartially.
Returning from the Governor's Office, Li Yuchang wrote a family book, telling his beloved wife Lin Ruolan, who is far away in Shandong, that due to the emergency of the disaster, she cannot return home to reunite. He asked his wife to take care of her health and take good care of her uncle Li Taiqing.
The next day, Li Yuchang continued to Shanyang County with his three companions Li Xiang, Gu Xiang, and Ma Liansheng. All the victims I saw along the way were unclothed, yellow-skinned and skinny victims. They huddled up on a high ground, with almost desperate eyes waiting for relief from the imperial court. The emperor sent 90,000 yuan in cash relief to Sanyo County, but when it was the disaster victims' turn, there was only three days' worth of porridge. Where did all this silver go? From the exhilarating complaints from the disaster victims, Li Yuchang learned that there was a big greed bug in Shanyang County. This is Wang Shenhan from Zhi County in Shanyang.
Wang Shenhan is a greedy person who has plucked his hair too much. He relied on bribes to take the seat of the local government. On weekdays, he exploited hollow ideas and stripped the public. In the event of a disaster, he also took advantage of the opportunity to make a fortune. There is a local jingle: “Wang Shenhan, Wang Shenhan, the big greedy man reaching out in the coffin.” He used his unjust wealth to build three luxury homes for himself, and Kanaya Zangjiao raised his second aunt and his third aunt. It can really be called “the meat in Zhumen wine stinks; there are frozen dead bones on the road.” Li Yuchang was filled with anger and was willing to go to death; he also wanted to bring this corrupt official to justice.
Li Yuchang traveled day and night to the places where the disaster victims were concentrated, carefully counted the number of victims, carefully calculated how much the relief supplies actually received by the victims were worth in silver, and obtained first-hand information on disaster relief in Sanyang.
Let's not talk about the Li Yuchang disaster area. Let's first take a look at how Wang Shenhan from Zhi County in Shanyang has been spending the past few days. The news that the imperial court has sent relief workers has already reached Wang Shenhan's ears; he is both worried and confident. What I'm worried about is that I heard that it was a new academic, and I don't know the details of this person. What I am confident about is that for many years, I haven't had a single inspector from above; I, Wang Shenhan, have been unsettled. But it's always right to be careful. He prepared an eight-lift cab and went outside the city gate to wait for the chasers to arrive. However, after three days of not seeing anyone, Wang Shenhan was wondering and reported, “Relief Officer Li Yuchang is in the countryside inspecting the disaster relief situation and listening to the cries of the victims.” It's true that good people don't come; those who come are not good. Wang Shenhan immediately returned home to discuss anything against Li Yuchang.
I couldn't have imagined that Wang Shenhan had just stepped foot in front of the county, and Li Yuchang had just arrived. They ordered the disaster relief accounts to be submitted for trial without first listening to Wang Shenhan's report. Wang Shenhan was not convinced by this. These accounts were all carefully faked by him; he couldn't see any flaws at all. He asked Bao Xiang, a government official, to pull out all the accounts. Next, Li Yuchang ordered all account inventories to be sent for inspection. Now, Wang Shenhan is in a state of panic. He knows that the account register has not had time to be falsified, and that the disaster relief accounts are not in line with the account register, so he revealed the truth. However, the reprimand's remarks are an order; they must not be counterproductive; they can only be sent over and then take countermeasures.
Li Yuchang ordered Li Xiang and his three other companions to load these pamphlets into the horse-drawn carriage. They immediately quit and left the county office and stayed at the Shanyang Yikan. They didn't go out for three full days.
In these three days, it was discovered that Wang Shenhan panicked the number of victims and fraudulently received money relief; cut the actual amount and deducted it; preliminary evidence of embezzling the imperial court's disaster relief funds of about 25,000 silver and 2, and wrote a revelation to be reported to the Governor General's Office.
That is, during these three days, Wang Shenhan was also extremely busy and happy. He sent people to monitor the movements of the chasers, and sent Bao Xiang, a government servant, to find an opportunity to get in touch with Li Yuchang's entourage. At the time, it was called “The Second Grandpa Speaks for Grandpa”. In order to avoid the embarrassment of direct conversations between the two elders, their respective followers first contacted each other to discuss some private issues.
Li Xiang is an old servant who has been working in the government office for many years. He has long been steeped in greed. He came out with Li Yuchang this time, and if he made up his mind, he wanted to take advantage of the opportunity to fake Tiger Wei and get some. Who knows, but I ate quite a bit. It was cheap but didn't stick at all, and I was always sad. So as soon as I met Bao Xiang, the smell was similar, and we hit it off right away. Li Xiang quietly told Bao Xiang, “My old man has taken control of your grandpa's fraudulent bank donation. If you report it, your old man will die. I think I should buy a safe one sooner.”
Bao Xiang understood very well. He just wanted to extort a few more dollars and said, “Then you can make a price.”
Li Xiang was very sluggish. He thought about it and wanted to say, “How am I qualified to say two things at this price? I think it's up to you to know the people in the prefecture to drive.” With this kind of promotion, the financial path is endless, and you don't take responsibility yourself. Wouldn't it be the best of both worlds?
Bao Xiang reported Wang Shenhan. Wang Shenhan laughed and said, “I have long anticipated that there are no cats that don't eat meat in the government office. You first bring these four bars of silver for a meet up ceremony, then thank you again later.”
Under Li Xiang's guidance, Bao Xiang presented Li Yuchang with a gift from Wangzhi County. However, I never expected that Li Yuchang would actually be scolded for being scolded as if they had fled in return for Wang Shenhan. Wang Shenhan was so restless that he scolded Li Yuchang for ignorance.
If one plan doesn't work, apply another plan. Wang Shenhan asked Bao Xiang to simply give the money to Li Xiang and asked Li Xiang to steal those evidentiary materials and destroy them.
Li Xiang, who received a lot of money, became an infidel to death. When people were quiet that night, he sneaked into the office room and unlocked the bronze lock on the briefcase, but he couldn't find any evidence in the closet. In fact, Li Yuchang is very careful. He took those files back to his bedroom that night and hid them in secret.
Only then did Wang Shenhan, who hit a wall twice, feel that the situation was serious, so another even more vicious plot is being planned.
On the morning of the fourth day, Wang Shenhan personally visited the Yikan, met Li Yuchang, and handed over a big red invitation: “My subordinate official met Lady Li. Gentlemen from all walks of life in this prefecture thanked the adults for their hard work day and night. They prepared a special dinner table, and invited the adults to enjoy their faces.”
Li Yuchang has always looked down on official business. He wanted to say no, but Li Xiang, who was next to him, first stepped forward to accept the invitation. How can a follower post the lead post without permission?! Li Yuchang was about to get angry. Li Xiang has already placed the invitation on the case and laughed and said, “The villain knows that Lady Li has always been clean and generally declined social gatherings, but today is the kindness of Sanyang's parents and elders, and they are also personally invited by Wang Zhi County. Of course, an exception must be made. What do you think, old man?” At this point, Li Yuchang really had no way back in the scene. If he insisted on not going, it seemed too unreasonable, so he had to nod against his will.
After Wang Shenhan left, Li Yuchang severely reprimanded Li Xiang and summoned the other two companions, Gu Xiang and Ma Liansheng, and announced three rules of discipline: “You are not allowed to meddle in business affairs without permission; you are not allowed to have any individual contact with Shanyang government officials; you are not allowed to accept any gifts; and if you violate them, never forgive them!” But where did Li Yuchang know that he was busy with his official duties and overlooked the inspection of his entourage. Now it is too late to sign three more chapters of the law.
At the banquet, Wang Zhi County and a few townspeople kindly advised alcohol. Li Yuchang was in a difficult situation. He actually drank a few more drinks and fell to bed when he returned to the post house. Li Xiang knew that the master must drink tea after drinking alcohol, so he put a pack of arsenic cream into the teacup. Sure enough, Li Yuchang felt a little thirsty and called for tea. Li Xiang handed over the teacup, and Li Yuchang drank a large sip. He felt that the taste was different. Wanting to put down the teacup, Li Xiang rushed forward and forcibly poured toxic tea into the owner's population. At this point, Gu Xiang and Ma Liansheng also broke into the house and held back the struggling owner. Li Yuchang rolled his eyes, tolerated the severe pain in his stomach, and scolded the three slaves for losing all their goodness. Li Xiang and others worked tirelessly. They strangled the owner with seven hands and eight feet and then hung it on a beam, creating a scene where he committed suicide.
The poor villain Fang Gang died so horribly in Wang Shenhan's drug plan, and died under the drug hands of his own evil slaves.
Arguably, the death of the chasers “self-inflicted” was not a trivial matter. How to intersect upward? This depends on Wang Shenhan's ability.
He first offered 2,200 yuan of silver to buy out Wanggu, the governor of Huai'an, his superior. Wang Gu promised to do his best to conceal the crime and forced Wu Zuo (equivalent to the current forensic doctor), who went to the autopsy, to sign the conclusion that he was “insane and committed suicide” on the autopsy slip. As a result, it was reported up layer by layer. Governor Liangjiang received the submission. Although he also felt that the matter was a bit strange, considering that Wang Gu is his uncle, why must he not be able to get along with his family because of the fact that Xiao Qi is his uncle? As a result, they also hastily finalized the case and reported it to the inspectorate. The Ombudsman's Office archived the submission, and the matter was settled.
Furthermore, the bad news spread to Lijiazhuang, Jimo, Shandong. Lin Ruolan was shocked by the thunder and instantly fainted. Uncle Lee Tae-qing went to Sanyo to pick up the coffin and relics. My uncle and nephew were both heartbroken and had no idea that Yu Chang would go insane and commit suicide. While sorting through the relics, I accidentally discovered that Li Yuchang had a handwritten manuscript in his pocket that read: “Zhi County in Shanyang was ruined to benefit Yuchang; Yuchang did not like it.” Looking through the clothes again, I found that there were dark brown spots of blood on one leather coat. Seeing this situation, Lin Ruolan hugged her leather coat and cried: “Fu Jun, ah, Fu Jun, you died so miserably!”
Lee Tae-qing took the opportunity and summoned his neighbors to open the coffin for an autopsy. Seeing Lin Ruolan kneeling in front of the coffin in plain clothes, Li Taiqing made a sad statement to the crowd: “My nephew, Li Yuchang, was ordered by the imperial court and suddenly died during a rescue operation in Shanyang. While we were cleaning up the relics, we discovered that there were many doubts. He definitely did not commit suicide. For this reason, we decided to open the coffin for inspection. Please ask all parents and townspeople to witness it.” So I asked the two young men for help and pry open the coffin cover. I saw that the corpse inside the coffin had not rotted yet. The fingers were blue and purple, and there were traces of blood in the mouth, nose, and ears. Lee Tae-qing also borrowed a silver hairpin and inserted it into the throat of the deceased. The silver hairpin turned black and couldn't be wiped off any way. The poisoning definitely caused death. Seeing this scene, Lin Ruolan jumped over the coffin and cried: “Officials are wrong! Officials are wronged!” It was a terrible feeling, and the audience was accompanied by tears.
Lee Tae-qing, who is over old, traveled thousands of miles to Beijing with complaints and evidence. It's already a cold winter season. The north wind is howling and snowflakes are fluttering. Li Taiqing came to the front of China Gate and filed a complaint with the Inspectorate General's Office.
The Inspectorate's Office also did everything in its power to speculate on such a difficult case; it actually dismissed it for the time being on the grounds that it was busy with official duties. How could Li Qingtai take a break? He stepped down and knelt down at the China Gate. There were more and more onlookers. The next day, hundreds of people knelt down with the elderly. The crowd shocked the imperial court. The inspectorate felt the seriousness of the case and relayed the complaint directly to the emperor. Emperor Jiaqing checked the case file, and Long Yan was furious: “The governance of Jiangnan is so bad, I was shocked!” As a result, he personally succeeded in the case, and ordered the Shandong police to re-examine Li Yuchang's body, and at the same time told the Criminal Department to quickly apprehend the suspect in question and escort him to Beijing for strict interrogation by the Military Aircraft Department and the Criminal Division.
After months of trial, the truth of the case was finally revealed. Wang Shenhan from Shanyang Zhi County embezzled huge sums of money, murdered imperial court officials, robbed his family property, and was beheaded; Wanggu, the governor of Huai'an, knowingly took bribes and was hanged; Governor Liangjiang Tiebao seriously violated his duties and was assigned to the Urumqi border. Wang Shenhan's henchman's henchman, Bao Xiang, and Li Yuchang's followers Li Xiang, Gu Xiang, and Ma Liansheng, aided and abused them. They were all punishable by death. Among them, the main culprit, Li Xiang, was taken to the front of Li Yuchang's tomb and executed to sacrifice the dead with his heart and soul.
At the same time, Emperor Jiaqing praised Li Yuchang's impartiality. He gave him the title of governor, reburied him, and personally wrote the song “The Thirty Rhymes of Min Zhong's Poems”, which was engraved on a stone monument and stood in front of Li Yuchang's tomb.
It can really be said that good has good rewards, and evil has bad rewards; corrupt and evil slaves curse thousands of people, and the wisdom of the Qing government has been around for a long time.
