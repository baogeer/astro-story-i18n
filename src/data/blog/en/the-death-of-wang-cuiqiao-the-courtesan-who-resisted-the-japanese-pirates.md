 During the Ming and Jiajing years, the southeast coast of our country began to be invaded by “Japanese invaders.” These Japanese colluded with local bandits and harmed the people. At the time, there was a famous prostitute on the Qin Huai River named Wang Cuiqiao. She was soft-spoken, extremely gentle, and had a bit of perseverance and outspoken personality. Since she didn't listen to her mother, she sold her to an old wealthy businessman as a concubine. On the surface, Wang Cuiqiao agreed well, but at night she packed up the softies and slipped away.
They fled to Jiaxing and were afraid to resume their old business in order to conceal their identity; they only wandered around the shores of the South Lake every day. One day at the lakeside, I met Luo Longwen, the son of a rich family in Tongcheng. Luo Longwen saw that Wang Cuiqiao was very good-looking and cheerful. He felt that this person had much use, so she stepped forward to chat. Wang Cuiqiao was also in a hurry to find someone to “rely on”. It didn't take long for her to get married to Luo Longwen as a concubine. Luo Longwen had her to help Zhou Xuan in the social arena. It was really like a fish. Luo Longwen's family also had a feudal customer — the Mingshan monk from Jingci Temple in West Lake. The monk Mingshan was impatient and was a very ambitious person, but soon Luo Longwen gave him the Green Pearl. He traveled far away from home with the Green Pearl, and there was no news.
The following year, Luo Longwen obtained an official position in Beijing and became the henchman Yan Song. In the 33rd year of Jiajing, Japanese pirates invaded the army, and Zhang Jing, the military chief, led an army to defeat the enemy army, but was killed because he offended Yan Song. The credit was placed on Yan Song's close friend Zhao Wenhua.
After two years, Zhao Wenhua and Luo Longwen fought against the invaders again because they didn't understand tactics at all, and Luo Longwen fled, and Wang Cuiqiao was arrested and imprisoned in a temple. Turns out there are a lot of women who have been arrested here. When a Japanese pirate head was currently visiting, Wang Cuiqiao was surprised to discover that this person was actually a Mingshan monk. It turns out that after Mingshan left that year, they had no plans for life. They befriended bandits and joined a group of Japanese pirates. He had a crush on Wang Cuiqiao for a long time, but he just didn't get a chance during France. Now that pie is falling from the sky, I immediately married Wang Cuiqiao as his wife. Wang Cuiqiao had no other plans at this point; she had no choice but to take action. Fortunately, Mingshan was very respectful and willing to listen to her. Every day, she saw that Mingshan helped Japanese pirates loot people and property and bully their wives and women, and felt uneasy. He often softly advised him not to help Japanese pirates harm his companions. Mingshan was gradually inspired by her, determined to abandon the dark and cast light into the light. So the two young leaders who killed the Japanese pirates went to Tongcheng and asked them to surrender. Unexpectedly, Zhao Wenhua didn't believe what he said. He killed Mingshan and suggested that he go to the city and ask for merit.
After Wang Cuiqiao learned about it, she regretted it. She thought she had harmed him. While grief and hate were mixed up, the remaining Japanese pirates dragged her aboard the boat. Wang Cuiqiao lost all her thoughts and jumped to death.
