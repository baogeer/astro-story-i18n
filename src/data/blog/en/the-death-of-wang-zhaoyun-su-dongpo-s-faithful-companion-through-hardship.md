 Wang Chaoyun, Zi Zixia, is a native of Qiantang. Due to her cold family, she has been in a singing and dancing class since childhood, yet she has a fresh and elegant temperament. In the fourth year of Song Shenzong Xining, Su Dongpo was demoted to the position of General Judge of Hangzhou. On one day, during a dinner, I saw Wang Chaoyun, who was very fond of him, and married her as his wife. At this point, Su Dongpo was 40 years old.
Su Dongpo is an extravagant person. He freely discussed his political opinions in his poems, offended the ruling dynasty, and was criticized several times. Among Su Dongpo's wives and concubines, Wang Chaoyun was the best at understanding Su Dongpo's feelings.
