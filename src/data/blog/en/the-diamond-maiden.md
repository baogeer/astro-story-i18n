 Once upon a time, in Pagasambu, there was a king who was insatiably greedy. He lives in a palace encrusted with gold and jade, eats a feast of mountain and seafood, and guards a treasure trove full of treasures, yet he is like a greedy bandit, scavenging every piece of money stained with sweat from the people.
Near the palace, there is a poor young man called Pantai. Because he looks so ugly, people call him an “ugly kid.” He lost his parents when he was a child, and his two older brothers were unwilling to raise him. Therefore, during the day, Pantai clapped his hands and sang begging songs at the market to get some chopsticks and food; at night, he went back to a broken wooden shed and slept overnight in grass scraps.
Pantai's two older brothers often go deep into the mountains to cut down trees and sell them at the market. They are getting richer day by day. Pantai asked his two older brothers to take him deep into the mountains to cut trees. One day, they arrived in a forest far, far from the city, cut down trees and cut branches. Afterwards, the two older brothers took out the food they were carrying and chewed on a rock. The poor ugly kid had nothing to eat and angrily said, “Huh, the richer people are, the more stingy they have on hand! One day, if I were to become king, I would give all my gold and silver jewels to the poor.” The two older brothers listened, patted their stomachs and laughed, and said, “Little brother, you can't get ghee out of the stone, so you can gnaw your fingers for a snack!”
The ugly boy was hungry and tired. Unbeknownst to him, he fell asleep. By the time he woke up, the sky was already dark, the heavy rain kept falling, and thunder was rolling through the forest. The two older brothers had already gone without a trace. He shivered in the cold and ran around the vast forest looking for his way back to the city. Suddenly, he saw a beautiful goldfinch being shot to the ground by a thunderstorm, and he saw that he was about to lose his breath. He felt sorry for this little bird. He picked it up gently and carefully carried it in his arms. Due to his warm body temperature, the bird woke up and made “Giga” and “Giga” chirping sounds. The teenager happily said, “Fly, fly, cute bird! Fly to your grandma's arms, fly to your grandpa's nest!” The bird spread its wings and flew three circles over the teenager's head before leaving reluctantly in love.
After a few days, he asked for food on the street, and suddenly he felt that someone was following him and sneaked back. Oh my gosh! It turned out to be an extremely beautiful girl, wearing a long gold shirt and a diamond necklace, and couldn't stop smiling at him. The teenager was frightened and thought, “She's not the king's princess, or the young lady of the aristocracy. If I answer her, it will cause disaster! Right! Let's run away!” So he sneaked through the crowd and fled back to his small wooden shed.
Since then, every time Pantai went out, the girl kept following him and kept smiling “eat and eat” in her mouth. He lost his soul. Sometimes he hid in a hotel, sometimes hidden in Linka, and couldn't get rid of the girl's entanglement. Once, Pantai boldly said, “Noble girl, if you came from heaven, go back to heaven! If it's from the sea, go back to the bottom of the sea! You follow me every day as a poor and ugly teenager, so I don't even have a way to beg!” The girl took a few steps forward and calmly said, “Boy, where do you live I want to see your house.” Pantai was taken aback. Lian Lian Yang complained, “The place where I live is about the same as a dog kennel; there really isn't a place to see at all. Besides, your powerful parents knew about this, and my head is about to move. Gu Ji, Gu Ji, please don't follow me again!”
The teenager took many turns. When she returned to her dilapidated wooden shed, the girl in the gold shirt had already cleaned up her things inside. She had been waiting for him for a long time. The girl smiled and said, “Among all men, you are the least active. I love you for ten minutes. I volunteered to be a couple with you, yet you treated me as a man-eating tiger and fled all over the city.”
The ugly kid cried and said, “I have nothing to wear, nothing to eat in my mouth, how can I support you, a fairy falling in the clouds. Besides, my appearance is so ugly...”
The girl said, “You look ugly, but you have a heart of gold! Let me tell you, my name is Palangmadi, and I'm an ordinary girl in the forest. As long as we both live together, there's no need for you to worry about eating and drinking.” After speaking, I shook off my thick hair and brushed it off a few times, only to hear a crisp, pleasant sound all over the floor. The dark wooden shed was shining brightly everywhere. It turns out that all of the diamonds that fell out of the girl's hair were extremely precious. Pantai stood by, but looked dazed.
Palangmadi picked up the diamonds, wrapped them in a piece of white silk, and handed them to the teenager and said, “You sell these diamonds to the merchants on the street! But remember, never talk about the origin of diamonds, or mention my fame. Because no one else can see me except you.”
A young man, Pan Tai, carried a diamond in his arms and walked into a store with four wooden doors. When the merchant saw these rare treasures, he was so surprised that he couldn't live for half a day before stuttering at the end, saying, “Boy, the value of your diamonds can buy the whole palace. I can't pay for the rest of my life. If you have to sell it to me, then I have a three-story house in my city. The house has nine warehouses full of ghee, tea, barley, barley, barley, and meat. I'll hand over this house from the warp on the roof to the broom at the back of the door to you!” As a result, the businessman accompanied the desk, inspected the house and warehouse, handed over all the locks and keys, and the two happily broke up.
Since then, in a dilapidated wooden shed, the girl and the teenager have lived a happy life together. They don't worry about clothes, don't worry about eating, sometimes drink and talk, and sometimes play the piano and sing. Gradually, Pantai's ugly face became neat and handsome; his thin monkey-like body was getting stronger day by day. Everyone who knows him is amazed at his changes. Some good-natured neighbors quietly went to the shack to eavesdrop and heard young girls singing and laughing, but looking out of the door, only the teenager was alone.
Furthermore, the fact that the merchant got the diamond reached the king's ears. He immediately dispatched a team of soldiers to retrieve all the treasures and drive the merchants into the royal palace. The king said, “Honest businessman, please tell me where did you get all these rare diamonds.” The merchant knelt down in front of the king, kept touching the ground with his forehead, and said with trepidation: “Lord of the report, I bought this from a wealthy foreign merchant because it was so expensive, I had to pay for it year by year.”
“Shut up!” The king shouted viciously: “Since it's a giant foreign merchant, how can you pay year after year! I need to use that red iron stick to stab you in your mouth and poke your footboard out of your heart before I can poke out the truth about you.” The merchant looked in the direction the king was pointing, only to see four ferocious warriors coming with a burnt iron bar. Terrified, he almost passed out and had to admit that he had bought the diamond from an ugly kid. The king ordered the merchant to find the young man within three days; if he couldn't find it, he had to kill him with a burnt iron bar.
The merchants looked for it. After seven days of searching, they couldn't find the young man's shadow. Because of what I just said, the appearance of a teenager has changed. The king said, “You can't do anything, let me mark you first!” He ordered the samurai under his command to burn the merchant's leg with an iron rod. The pain caused the merchant to die and live. He signed on the spot, arrested him, and continued to search for the teenager.
One day, the teenager Pantai was walking through the market and saw merchants wearing crutches, sad faces, and limping east and west. He ran off cheerfully and happily touched the merchant's long beard. The poor businessman looked left and right, and when he finally recognized him, he shed tears of pain and told the original story of his unlucky experience. The young man was very sympathetic to him and went to see the king with the businessman. The king said, “If you want food, where did you get so many treasures?” The boy answered, “King, it's really interesting to talk about the origin of these diamonds. Once. I was gathering firewood in the mountains far, far away. I saw a very tall, very tall tree with a big, very large bird's nest. I thought, take a few birds' eggs and cook lunch! I climbed, climbed to the top of the tree, saw a small stone in the bird's nest, sparkling, and quickly grabbed it home and sold it to this merchant, only to know what kind of diamond these stones were...” The king's sharp eyes turned a few times and pretentiously said, “I believe the people of Pagasambu won't deceive their king. Let's go, go home! Live a happy life with peace of mind.”
Coming out of the palace, the young man secretly rejoiced: because of the origin of diamonds, he finally hid the cruel king. Who knows, after three days, the young man was taken to the king by the samurai again. While walking, he exclaimed, “King, I have lost half of my diamonds. At this point, the king leaned down from the golden throne and cheerfully asked, “Boy, tell me where the girl who sings and jokes in your broken wooden shed comes from.” Turns out, on the day the boy left the palace, Guo Yu had already sent out secret agents to investigate his situation. Hearing what the king said, the young man was taken aback. However, he still smiled and said, “King, please don't look for happiness in the poor. I am a beggar who wants food, who will be with me other than my own shadow?” The king scolded angrily: “Shut up! I'm going to use a burnt iron bar to stab you in your mouth, poke it out of your footboard, and poke the truth out of your gut.”
After all, a group of devilish executioners knocked him to the ground, tied his hands and feet tightly with cowhide strips, and others held burnt iron bars, compared and sketched, and only waited for the king's order. Teenage Pantai closed his eyes in horror and silently prayed: “Beautiful girl Palangmadi, we can only meet in another world!”
However, the king's order to execute the teenager was interrupted by a burst of crisp laughter. I saw a pretty woman in a gold shirt, like a golden cloud blowing in the wind, floating in front of the king. Her bright, pure, silver bell laugh can really revive the dead and make the elderly young. She smiled and said to the king, “I am this teenager's wife. Don't kill him, just ask me if you have any questions!”
The king glared at his eyes, one older and one younger, and even shouted, “Too bad, such a beautiful woman married a poor man who wanted to eat. Come on, come on! Just stay and be my concubine!” After speaking, I reached out my fat sweaty hands and hugged the girl Palangmadi. But the girl was like a dexterous little bird, smiling “gracefully” and running fast. She was in the east for a while, in the west for a while, jumping on the throne for a while, and going around the steps for a while. The king was like a clumsy bear who either knocked down a chair or hit his forehead. Watching the king's scandal, not only was the teenager Pantai very happy; the samurai who stayed behind also covered their mouths and laughed more than once. Standing on the king's throne, Palangmadi smiled and said, “King, if you want to get diamonds, climb on the ground and pick them up!” After speaking, I shook off my thick, dense hair and kept combing it with a golden comb. A sparkling diamond splashed across the temple. The king exclaimed with joy, “Servant! Samurai! Please help me find diamonds!” They crawled up and down on the ground trying to catch those little babies that were jumping around. However, when they got a diamond, it was like a snowflake falling on the lake, and it disappeared silently. They were so tired that they fell back and forth, and they didn't get a single diamond. Meanwhile, Palangmadi and Junior Pantai have already escaped from the palace. The king jumped like thunder and ordered all the soldiers to be brought back to the boy and his wife.
Then Palangmadi went back to the small wooden shed and said to Pantai, “Tell you, I am the bird you saved in the forest. Now that he is being seen by the greedy king, he will definitely send troops to arrest people. Tonight, go to my sister Aizamadi and borrow that wonderful bellowcase!” Then, after handing him a gemstone ring and telling him various ways to find Aizamadi, the teenager hurriedly set off.
Following the girl's instructions, he walked all the way east and east, over snow-covered peaks, crossed jade-filled rivers, and walked into a very large forest. The forest is full of sandalwood, red pine, and white birch trees, as well as a variety of fruit trees, and dazzling flowers. There are countless birds he has seen or not seen. Some are dancing, some are playing in the woods, some are chasing each other, and some are circling in the clouds. People are dazzled by the colorful feathers; this undulating cry is as loud as the holiday season. The teenager walked through the forest and saw a palace made of coral. He walked up to the first floor, where many girls dressed in green were “chirping” and “chirping” and laughing. He followed Palangmadi's instructions, waved a gemstone ring in front of everyone, and climbed the jade staircase to the second floor. There were also many beautiful women in gold shirts, singing and dancing with joy. He also followed Palangmadi's instructions and waved a jewel ring in front of everyone and climbed to the third floor.
On the third floor, there is a gold chair with a small, gem-encrusted chest on the chair. He also followed orders and struck the gold chair three times. Suddenly, a cuckoo, the king of birds, popped out of the box and called “Jiga” and “Giga” three times, turning into a beautiful and majestic girl like Palangmadi.
Wearing a green jewel on her head and a long blue dress, she said to the teenager in a singing voice; “I just love Zamardi! I just love Zamardi! You have something to say! He was even more anxious. Aizamadi saw his mind. He put on him a feather-sewn shirt and gently blew it with his mouth. The boy had the wings of a fairy crane, flying through the forest, over the snowy mountains, and falling next to his own broken wooden shed.
At this point, the king's thousands of guards, armed with swords, spears, bows and arrows, screamed “Go One,” “Hi One,” and “Whistle One,” surrounding the small wooden shed and couldn't get through the water. Palangmadi was very happy to receive the wonderful bellows. She walked out of the small wooden shed and said to the king's guard, “Soldiers, go back quickly, go back! If you don't retreat, I'll ask you to show off your might and run away from east to west!” The king's guard commander did not listen to the girl's advice and ordered the soldiers to charge. Parangmadi was very angry, opened the marvelous bellows, and incessantly agitated the king's guard. I saw a strong hurricane blowing the soldiers from east to west. Some fell into a gutter, and some were pinned to the wall. Everyone didn't see the situation well. They used swords and spears as crutches, supported their bodies with bows and arrows, and turned around to escape for their lives.
Seeing the power of the marvelous bellows, the teenager was happy to throw his hat at the sky and said, “Girl! girl! Please lend me the bellows and let me teach that cruel king a lesson.” He brought the bellows and went to the bottom of the palace. The king, who was watching the battle from the roof, saw his guard falling to the ground like leaves blown down by the autumn breeze. I don't know what happened. The young man knelt his back and scolded the king with one finger; “You tyrant who doesn't know enough to eat the mountains and drink the sea. You've already stepped on a burial ground with one foot, and is still so powerful.” The king, seeing the teenager, clenched his teeth and ordered the samurai around him to quickly shoot him to death with random arrows. The young man said, “King, you want to go to heaven every day. Now, I'll send you to heaven!” He opened the bellows and stirred it up a few times. The king was like a kite, which quickly climbed into the sky, rising and falling in the market, all the time.
The people who came and went in the market all hated the cruel king and clapped their hands and applauded. The young man said, “King, since you don't want to go to the sky, you can go to hell!” As a result, the bellows were stopped, and the king fell from mid-air and lost air. People in the market said in unison, “Whoever is honest is the chief, and whoever is loving is a parent. “Young man, you got rid of the blood-sucking demon for us, and we are recommending you as the new king.”
Junior Pantai became the king of Pagasambu, and Parangmadi became the queen. They opened up the king's treasury and distributed the gold and silver treasures that the king had plundered in the past to the poor. One day, the teenager visited the distribution area and discovered that his two older brothers, carrying large cow fur pockets, were receiving their own copies. The teenager walked over and said, “Brother, what I said when logging has finally come true!” Only then did the two older brothers learn that the newly elected king was their own ugly brother. They were so ashamed that they couldn't help but turn into a mouse and hid in a burrow.
