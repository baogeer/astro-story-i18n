 A poignant love story is circulating in the Sanjiang Dong region of Liuzhou, Guangxi.
During the Kangxi period of the Qing dynasty, there was a girl from the Dong ethnic group called Niangmei in Shuikouzhai. She was smart and kind, and as beautiful as a fairy. In the neighboring Dopa Village, there is a descendant named Zhulang. He was an orphan since childhood. He is hardworking, brave, handsome and chic, and can play the pipa well. The fields of the two families are connected. While working, they often meet and sometimes even help each other in agricultural work. The seeds of love, like the crops in the field, slowly sprout in the hearts of the two young people.
1. Walking songs and sitting on the moon
“Singing the Moon”, also known as “singing at night,” is a form of communication between young Dong men and women talking about love. Every time night falls, a group of Dong guys bring homemade instruments and gather at a relatively fixed place in Dongzhai to sing songs with a group of girls gathered in the opera house. Sometimes they are invited to sit in the opera house and quietly exchange love tokens.
Jurang and Nao Mei are the most affectionate couple among these young singers. Others say they are a pair made in heaven. Under the faint moonlight, Jurang played the pipa and sang with emotion: “The moonlight is hazy, the spring is dinging, the girl upstairs, please listen to me sing a song...”
A beautiful nightingale song floated upstairs: “Magical land, Kyushu from all sides, beautiful piano sounds that move my heart...”
That's how you sing from place to place. The mountain breeze stopped calling, the birds stopped singing, and the silent woods all listened attentively to the beautiful singing of the girls and boys. They are immersed in endless joy and peace, making time flow slowly through the mountains as the creek flows through the mountains like moonlight.
2. Elopement at the cafeteria
The Dong family has an ancient custom that was passed down from her ancestors to take care of her cousins. Therefore, Niang Mei's mother gave her permission to her uncle's family in Dingdazhai. When my uncle's family learned the news that Jurang and Nangmei were in a relationship, they quickly sent someone to the door to meet their family. The mother couldn't resist her uncle's family's entanglement over and over again, so she had to agree to set the marriage period.
When she learned the news, the mother was so sad that she decided to go find Zhulang, but her mother locked her back in the cafeteria. Seeing that her uncle's family's marriage was getting closer, Miu Mei, who had lost her freedom, was in a hurry and finally decided to elope with Jurang.
Furthermore, Jurang doesn't see Nami for a day; for example, after three fall years, not for three days, for example, after nine years. Since Nao Mei was locked out by her mother, Jurang has been wandering outside the cafeteria day by day, and no one else has been able to get rid of it.
By April 18, just three days before her uncle's family came to welcome her family, Niang Mei took advantage of her mother's sleep at night to pry open the wooden window and meet up with Zhu Lang, who had already been waiting outside, and both left Dongzhai overnight.
They walked and walked all night long. Following the direction of the river, they didn't dare to stop for a moment, fearing that their uncle's family would catch up with them. They walked all the way through 99 miles and arrived at Halong Bay at dawn. This place was far from home, but they still didn't dare to stop. They kept on their way to eat some dried food brought by Niang Mei.
They went through village after village, village after village. They walked for three days and three nights. They really couldn't walk, so they rested on the side of the road. At this point, it was almost dark, and the sky was raining heavily again. Both of their clothes were wet. Seeing a stockade in front of them, they went to Gulou to shelter from the rain and set fire to bake their clothes.
This place is called Guan-dong, and there is a matchmaker in the village called Milk Flower. She saw the fire shining out of the drum tower from a distance from her window and came to inspect it. Seeing two young foreigners, one man and one woman, she understood something in her heart. Seeing that Niang Mei was a peugeot, as beautiful as a fairy, she immediately had a crooked mentality.
There is a rich man in the village named Yinji. The family is rich and powerful. He is not satisfied with marrying two beautiful wives. Seeing a young and beautiful girl is like being hungry for cats and smelling fishy. When he learned that a girl like a fairy had arrived in the village, he immediately followed Milk Flower to the Gulou and took the initiative to get close to Zhulang and call him Brother Dao, just like a family member, and invited them to stay at his own home. Jurang and Nao Mei still wanted to make excuses, but Yin Er picked up Nao Mei's baggage and dragged the two of them home with half a request.
The two young people, unfamiliar with the world, still thought that Yoshito was in good hands when they were taken in. When they were in trouble, they fell into a trap carefully set by the bad guys. As a result, Juro lost his life in his hometown.
3. Murder in another country
Gin Er arranged for Niang Mei to stay in her own warehouse, and let Juro live in the same room as herself. Jurang is grateful for Silver Er's care. He also worked very hard at the bank. He was busy carrying water, sweeping the floor, growing vegetables, and feeding the chickens and ducks. He was constantly busy inside and out. He never anticipated that the owner would conceal his misfortune.
Silver is coveted by a beautiful woman like Niang Mei every day, but she was unable to get started. One day, Gin-ji took advantage of an excuse and asked him to go abroad to collect the bill. Niang Mei knew that this was Yin Er's trick, and advised Jurang not to go. The Yin Er family has permanent workers, so you can ask someone else to go. However, Jurang didn't listen and said that the old man told him to collect the bill. This is his trust in him.
As soon as Jurang left, Yin Er walked into the warehouse. Seeing that Niang Mei was carrying and organizing the goods, she pretended to be concerned and asked her to take a break. While talking, she approached Niang Mei to help her wipe her sweat, and took the opportunity to manipulate Niang Mei. Niang Mei quickly dodged and loudly reprimanded, “You have your greens. I have my love for you. I'm tired of eating the greens in the garden, so don't pick them in my mountains.”
Even though Yin Er was rejected, she cheeky and said, “What can a homeless man give you, isn't it just part of suffering? If you follow me, keep eating spicy and wearing Ryara.”
Niang Mei said, “If you want to go out and find wild vegetables, don't come into my mountains. If you want to pick wild fruit with your own hands, you're only afraid of getting stabbed in the eye. You seduced my wife with gold and silver. I called my two sisters. Some people love thick tea and light meals; others don't like wealth by nature.”
Gin-ji had a gray nose, angry and annoyed. He found Dowan Song and devised a plan to kill Jurang by forcing Jurang to eat “meat on the tip of a gun,” and leave her mother, helpless, and at his mercy.
In ancient times, the Dong people had many rules governing their own people. Today we call them “customary laws.” These customary laws all stipulated the penalties for criminal acts such as gambling, theft, robbery, adultery, murder, arson, etc., and the person responsible for making punishment decisions based on these provisions in the village is called Zishou. In order to achieve the goal of seizing over Niang Mei, Gin Er did not hesitate to use heavy sums of money to bribe Shoumansong to the detriment of Jurang.
Under the pretext of having his family's belongings stolen, it is suspected that someone inside sent a report to the thief, then reported it to the owner of the village and arrested all the long and short workers working in Yin Er's family. After the interrogation, no one admitted that something was wrong, so they had to do it in the form of eating “meat on the tip of a gun,” and let the gods judge who was guilty. This is an ancient method of adjudicating cases handed down by the Dong people. It is said that an innocent person will be fine if they eat gunpoint meat, and a guilty person will be stabbed in the throat by a god and die.
Nang Mei knew that this was Gin-ji's trap, and there was no doubt that Jurang would die if she went, so she advised Jurang and her to escape as soon as possible. However, Jurang was deceived by Gin Er's superficial kindness, and he was convinced that the gods would keep him safe if he did nothing to lose his heart.
On the day she ate “Gun Tip Meat,” she watched Zhulang walk step by step into the abyss of death and couldn't help herself. Niang Mei had no choice but to bathe in plain clothes and give Jurang the last trip at home.
A ceremony to eat “guntip meat” was held in Jiangjianpo outside the village. The first one uses a sharp tip of a gun to poke cooked cubes of beef out of a wooden barrel and deliver them to the suspect's mouth one by one. When it was Juro's turn, Manmatsu poked at Juro's big mouth. When it was too late to hum, Juro fell to the ground.
After killing Juro, Manmatsu also asked people to abandon his body on a barren mountain.
4. Revenge for hate
When she heard the news of Juro's death, Niangmei was heartbroken. Juro knows who killed her, and is determined to avenge her husband.
Niang Mei stepped into the drum tower and sounded the drums and claimed to people that if someone helped her find Jurang's body, she would rather marry him. People from Guandongzhai went up the mountain to find it, but they didn't find it for three days and three nights.
Flowers bloom and fall on the mountain, and swallows come and go. One year has passed, and another year has passed. Every year during the Qingming season, Niang Mei visits Jiangjianpo to pay tribute and search for the dead body of Jurang.
This year, when Niangmei met a few cowgirls looking for bracken in the mountains, she asked them that the bracken on the opposite mountain looked very lush. Why didn't she pick them there? The cow herder told her that Gin Er said there were ghosts on the mountain over there and told them not to go there.
It's this silver two again! Niang Mei was thoughtful. When those few girls had gone far, she went to the opposite mountain alone to search for them. In a place full of ferns, I finally found a bunch of white bones. There was also half a copper coin next to the white bones. Nang Mei took out half of the pair of copper coins from her body, which fit together. It was a token of her love affair with Juro.
After feeling sad, Niang Meiqiang picked up her spirits and sounded the drums in the drum house again. People gathered to hear the sound. The owner of the village gathered to ask Niang Mei to play drums again. Niang Mei said that she had found Zhulang's dead body; whoever helped bury Juro will marry her. Yin Er, who was present, was the first to accept. The village owner said, “In the Gulou discussions, we must not regret anything promised by both parties.”
Gin Eryan is optimistic that something will soon come to fruition. She happily brought a few helpers up the mountain with Juro, but Nangmei only allowed Yin21 to go up the mountain alone. She said, “Who did I promise to get married to who helped me get married. With so many of you helping me, which one will I marry?” Gin-ji thought that made sense, so she invited those few people back.
Arriving at the top of the mountain, Silver Er asked, “Are the bones buried here?” Niang Mei said, “This place is too close to the stockade. I can hear the chicken barking; it's still a little further away.”
The two walked again and walked to a hillside. Silver Er asked again, “Let's bury it here.” Niang Mei said, “Facing north from here, the baby will be deaf later.”
The two walked to another high mountain, and Silver Er asked, “They were buried here, right?” Niang Mei said, “This side faces south, and babies will be paralyzed in the future.” The two went again and walked all the way to a mountain a long distance away. Niang Mei said, “Let's just bury it here.” When Yin Er thought, she was going to get a mother's beauty, and she was extremely happy. But he dug a foot deep and didn't want to dig anymore. Niang Mei said, “People who died from a knife and gun should be buried deep; dig three more feet down.” By the time it had dug more than three feet deep, Gin-ji was already tired and out of breath. Just when he was bending over and wanted to rest, Nang Mei quickly picked up the hoe, pointed at Yin Er's head, and hit him hard. Gin Yi was caught off guard and immediately tilted to the side. Niang Mei took advantage of the opportunity to make up a few more hoes. It wasn't until Yin Er never moved again that she plowed down the dirt on both sides of the pit and buried Silver Er in a deep pit he had dug herself to avenge Zhu Lang.
Niang Mei wiped away her tears, carried Zhulang's corpse, followed the mountain path where she first escaped from her marriage, crying all the way back to her hometown, and buried Zhulang's corpse next to Dopa Village, so that she could be with him all the time.
