 Legend has it that a long, long time ago, there was a young man named Chai Sheng in the Dong family who went to the well to fetch water one day. He had just scooped a scoop of water. A frog jumped from the bottom of the well into his water scoop. This is a big, very beautiful frog. It's blinking big eyes and screaming at him one by one with its mouth. Chai Sheng loved the frog. He held the frog in his hands and looked at it, then put the frog into the well and muttered in his mouth: “Frog frog, you still live in the well. I can't support you by being poor at gathering firewood.” But when he went to scoop the water again, the frog jumped into his water scoop and still croaked, like this three times. Chai Sheng shook his head and had no choice but to take the frog home and keep it in a water tank.
Speechless all night. The next day, Chai Sheng ate early, brought a flat and wooden knife, locked the door, and went up the mountain to cut firewood. The frog in this water tank saw that the house was quiet, and the surroundings were very calm. As soon as she jumped from the tank to the edge of the tank and went straight down to the ground, shook off the frog skin on her body, and transformed into an incredibly beautiful girl. As soon as the frog girl showed up, she cleaned the kitchen by hand: wipe the table, wash the chopsticks, and set the stove to cook. After a while, a delicious meal was ready. This frog girl put on the table cover and her body shook. The frog skin on the edge of the bowl put on her body, and she still turned into a frog and went back to the water tank.
In the evening, when Chai Sheng returned to his home, he had a strong smell of the meal. He thought it was his neighbor on the left and right who asked the guest to get the rusted lock, unlocked the swaying door, and unwrapped the table cover. Wow, what kind of braised fish, green stewed chicken, filled a large table. The dishes in these dishes were still steaming hot. Chai Sheng's heartfelt words: God bless, who has such a kind heart made such delicious food for me. Don't worry about him; let's talk about it after eating. Chai Sheng has lost his parents since childhood. He is alone. He lives by collecting firewood. He has never seen such a good meal since he came out of his mother's womb. Furthermore, after a day of playing firewood, he was extremely hungry, so he ate a fish head with his left hand and a chicken thigh with his right hand. I was full and went to bed and sobbed. The next day, I ate breakfast as usual, locked the door, and went up the mountain to cut firewood. But when he got home in the evening, another hot meal was waiting for him. After three days, Chai Sheng muttered again. Who is so distressed by me? There is a saying, “No merit, it's hard to sleep and eat well,” but this kid, Shibasheng, is uneasy. He went to ask Mr. Wu, who is an honest neighbor on the left. Uncle Wu said he didn't know, and Li Laohan, who had a relationship with Right Sheba, and Li Laohan couldn't make it clear. Then he asked Sister Yang of Kang Ling Ya, who also said that her sister knew, and then asked Aunt Zhang, who had Chai Sheng's ears attached to her: “It was made by a big frog raised in your water tank. I was able to read it clearly from the slit. After you went up the mountain, the frog jumped out of the water tank and became a beautiful girl like a fairy. After preparing a meal for you, it became a frog and went back to the water tank. It was your kid who had a good conscience, did a lot of good things, and helped so many people to accumulate blessings. If you don't believe me, take a look yourself; am I lying?”
How could such a thing happen under heaven? With half belief and half doubt, he made up his mind. The next day, he locked the door as usual, carried a flat wooden knife, and looked like he was going up the mountain, but he hid in the fence outside and watched the house intently. This Shibasheng house, ah, is covered with thatch, and the walls are lined with chai sticks. Seen from the outside, it's bald in the moonlight; it's clear. Take a look. Sure enough, the frog in the water tank jumped out, only to see her body shaking, losing her skin, and a young girl who came out alive. I saw her dressed in blue. Ting Ting's figure was like a hibiscus out of the water, and her sweet smile was more open than a peony. Her big eyes flashed a charming light. It really was 'Ten Mile Eight Hometown is difficult to see; it must have come from the Immortals. '
Take a look at this Chai Sheng; he's stupid. After coming back to my senses for half an hour, I quietly unlocked the door, and as soon as I grabbed the frog girl's hand, begged her not to go back to the water tank again, marry him, and live a good life. The frog girl said that she is still a few months away from her ascetic practice. If Chai Sheng waits patiently, the good days will come soon. After speaking, he broke off Chai Sheng's hand. As soon as he shook his body, he turned into a frog and went back into the water tank.
On a day like this, I arrived at the annual Dong Tanabata Festival. Men, women, and children in the village all dressed in beautiful Dong costumes and left the village. Shibao looked sullenly at the distant flow of people and sighed. Aunt Zhang came over and dragged him. He had no choice but to follow Aunt Zhang to the polder.
At the polder, people from ten miles and eight villages all came to rush to the polder, and it was full of people. They are singing and dancing, watching and juggling, and buying and selling. They are very popular and very lively. Shibao thought all about his beloved frog girl, mixed up in the crowd without a heart. Suddenly, someone said that there was a fairy-like girl over there handing out candy to everyone. Go quickly. Aunt Zhang took Chai Sheng's hand and squeezed it over there. Sure enough, there was a beautiful girl carrying a basket in her hand and was distributing candy one by one to the long line. When Chai Sheng saw it, they were at the back of the line. The girls were scattered all over. When they arrived in front of Chai Sheng, the candy in the Qiao basket was gone. Chai Sheng thought to himself, he didn't make it at the end of the line, so I'll take the first place. He ran to the front of the team and lined up, but this time the girl broke up at the end and went to Shibao's, and then all went missing. Chai Sheng thought, then I'll be in the middle of the line. He slipped into the middle of the line. He didn't expect the girls to scatter all over, and when it came to him, the deal was gone. Aunt Zhang, who has a sharp eye, saw it and whispered to Chai Sheng, that is your sweetheart, the frog girl, who is handing out candy; she is too embarrassed to give it to you in the face of so many people. Chai Sheng listened and blushed. It turned out that this was the case.
Just as Aunt Zhang was talking to Chai Sheng, Sister He from this village came over. She lowered her voice and told Aunt Zhang and Chai Sheng because she had something wrong and saw a big frog walking in front of her. When she got under the big tree at the entrance of the village, the frog shook its body, shed its frog skin, and became a big girl. Then the girl hid the frog skin in the tree cave. I think the girl who scattered the candy was the frog fairy. Aunt Zhang listened and pushed Chai Sheng and asked him to quickly go to the tree cave to get the frog skin and burn it. Chai Sheng woke up like a dream and quickly rushed back to the village. Sure enough, he found frog skin in a tree cave at the entrance of the village. He hurriedly went home and threw it in the stove.
The sun gradually moved west, and people in the village who went to catch the polders went home one after another. Chai Sheng sat in front of his house looking at the pedestrians on the road without blinking, but he just didn't see his favorite frog girl. The sun has set, and Aunt Zhang has arrived, but I still haven't seen the Frog Girl's shadow. In the evening, every household lit torches. The frog girl finally arrived, and Chai Sheng was overjoyed. She's still so charming during the day, but she just doesn't look a little less smiling. Chai Sheng greeted her and took the frog girl's hand as soon as she wanted to talk, but the frog girl spoke first:
“Chai Lang, did you take my frog coat? Where is it? Give it back to me soon”.
“Burned it, I threw it in the stove and burnt it”. Chai Sheng didn't notice any changes in the frog girl's face.
“Eh.” The frog girl sighed. “Chai Lang, you're too impatient. Didn't I tell you this? I'm still a little short of training, and after three months, I'll be able to live a happy life together. Now that you've burnt my frog coat, I have to practice for another 100 years to get real results. In other words, we'll have to wait another 100 years to become a couple.” The frog girl was talking. Her face gradually turned blue, her body gradually developed frog-like bumps, and finally she became a big, beautiful frog.
Shibasheng held this frog with both hands and burst into tears.
