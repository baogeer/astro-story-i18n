 The Dong people are a nation that is good at cultivating mountains and planting trees and growing rice. They are also a nation that is good at creating folk literature such as myths and legends, and inheriting beautiful customs.
Dong mythology, represented by “Save the Sun,” tells a fantastic story: the demon Shang Zhu broke off the golden hook hanging from the sun. The sun fell from the sky to the ground, and suddenly it was extremely dark. Shang Zhu drilled out from under the ground to eat people. There are brothers and sisters. The older brother led the men to build a long ladder, the younger sister led the women to roll the twine, the older brother looked for the golden hook in the sky, and the younger sister looked for the sun on the ground. When my sister found the sun, she tied her with a long twine, rang a bronze bell, and laughed happily. The devil ate my sister when she heard laughter. Her sister's blood dripped on the sun, and her heart jumped out of her chest. The older brother touched the golden hook in the sky. When he heard his sister laughing, he was busy using a twine to cover the golden hook, drop the head down to the ground, and pull the sun up to the sky. The demon Shang Zhu was beaten to death by the crowd. My brother left it in the sky, and when the sun went down, he pulled it up, so there was an alternation between day and night. My sister turned into a rising sun flower on the ground.
