 A long time ago, on the land of Shenzhou, there was a village called Peace Village. The village was surrounded by greenery. People worked at sunrise and rested at sunset, and lived an ordinary and happy life.
One day, a group of robbers came to the village. They robbed the villagers' food, confiscated the villagers' land, and used the villagers as their slaves, forcing the villagers to open up new land. After decades, all the trees around the village were turned into crops.
This year, Heping Village experienced a rare drought. There was no harvest of crop grains in the field, and all withered. In order to enslave this group of villagers in the future, the bandits also temporarily took out their previous food reserves and distributed them to the villagers. However, sitting here to eat in the mountains is not an option.
There is a boy in the village. Because of his poor health, people call him Little Boy. On this day, Xiao Yi came to the leader of the robber and said, “Dear Wang, seeing the drought, there was no harvest. He also asked Wang to leave my village and go to the East China Sea to find Dragon King to rain and save our Peace Village.
The bandit leader has been staring at Xiao Yi for a long time: Dragon King is just a legend, but I really admire your courage. Let's go. If you can find Dragon King to rain, we won't let you be slaves anymore.
Just like that, Xiao Yi embarked on a journey to find the Dragon King. You won't know how much the world is until you get out of the village. However, in Xiao Yi's opinion, there was no difference between the outside world and the village. It was all deserted. One day after two months, Xiao Yi was thirsty and hungry, yet Xiao Yi clenched her teeth and continued to move forward. Finally, she felt dizzy in front of her eyes and fell to the ground.
When Xiao Yi woke up, it was already three days later. When she opened her eyes, Xiao Yi was stunned by the scenery in front of her. I was lying on a soft bed, with crystal pearls hanging on the roof, some mountain delicacies and seafood on the octagonal table, and there seemed to be a black mountain outside the window...
“You're awake, kid.” Immediately after that, a coughing sound came from behind me. Xiao Yi couldn't help but flinch; it turned out that she also had an old woman behind her. My wife has wrinkles on her face. She looks old, yet her skin is very delicate, and there is a bit of elegance when she puts her hands and feet together. At first glance, they are from a noble background.
“It didn't scare you, kid.” Looking at Xiao Yi in a daze, the grandmother continued to tell Xiao Yi everything she knew. Following the words of her grandmother, Xiao Yi also understood a lot. It turns out that this old woman is the patriarch of the Haimafeng family in Xihai Fenggong. Every time she performs a mission, she combines with the Haimalong family in the East China Sea Dragon Palace to exert her proper mana. However, when a mission was carried out and passed through Mount Bou, his mana suddenly disappeared and it fell here. Fuzhoushan was born in a chaotic period. Fuzhoushan will attract everything on earth, increasingly, and eventually contain the entire universe. Back then, in order to save Grandma Haima Feng, the powerful members of the Haima Feng family wanted to destroy Mount Buzhou, but they all failed. Since then, no one dared to go around Mount Zhoushan. Grandma Haima also wanted to walk away, but as a divine tribe, Zhoushan was so attractive to the gods that she couldn't get out at all. The grandmother also asked about the origin of Xiao Yi. Xiao Yi had no choice but to tell her about the disaster situation in her hometown.
Grandma Haima kept quiet for a while and said, “Kid, if you want to find Dragon King, you have to go over Mount Buzhou to reach the Imperial Dragon Palace. There is a Haima Dragon God family there, but without our Haima Feng combination, the Haima Dragon Clan can't save your Peace Village even if you detour Mount Wuzhou. Only if you take me there will our Seahorse tribe be able to exert strong mana, summon wind and rain, and save your peaceful village, and even more people. I have a weak side here. After I hide in weak water, my mana will disappear. As soon as you get to the Imperial Dragon Palace in the East China Sea and release me, then there is hope for Peace Village.”
Xiao Yu heard it for a while and was nervous. It wasn't until she heard that only by bringing this weak water to the East China Sea could save the villagers, Xiao Yifang took a breath. As a result, Xiao Yi embarked on a new trip to the East China Sea with Grandma Haima Feng.
Kung Fu pays off. Fortunately, Mount Buzhoushan is only very attractive to the talents of the divine tribe. Only a young person can pass over Mount Bou Zhou in just one year, and it took another three months to finally reach the Imperial Dragon Palace in the East China Sea.
“Grandma, here we are,” said Xiao Yi while picking up Grandma Haima Feng from her weak water bag. I saw that Grandma Haima Feng's body was getting more and more. Her already slightly old body gradually showed a noble appearance. The wrinkles on her face had faded away, her white hair had turned black, and she had a gorgeous crown on her head. She was well-mannered and had a high temperament. With a burst of roar, Haima Dragon also appeared in the square of the Imperial Dragon Palace. The moment Haima Dragon God, who was already slightly old, saw Haima Feng also suddenly held up her head and chest. Haimalon and Haima Feng took off together. They overlapped with each other and radiated a golden glow on their bodies.
Xiao Yi stayed under this light for a long time. Suddenly, a loud voice came out: “Kid, thank you for saving our Haima family”, and saw that Xiao Yi was still stunned, “Without your help, we could only get weaker and weaker, and eventually grow old slowly. It was you who saved our Haima Clan. The restraining power of Mount Fushoushan is getting stronger, but our Seahorse Clan will also do our part to help you humans.”
Haimalong and Haima Feng brought their child to Heping Village. In a blink of an eye, there were dark clouds and lightning thunder. Immediately after that, it was pouring rain. The people in Heping Village all knelt down in front of their doors, looked at the sky, and bowed their heads and prayed for blessings. Even the robbers who took over Heping Village were shocked. They didn't expect Xiao Yi to actually invite Dragon King. A weak kid saved the entire Heping Village.
It took a total of two days for the rain to stop, and sections of the river that had already dried up have reflowed. At this point, Haimalong said: No need to be polite; if you want to thank, thank the little brother. It's not just Peace Village; wherever we can get, we'll help you humans. After speaking, Haimalon took Haima Feng into the air.
On the day the god Haima left, the robbers apologized to the people in Heping Village. Peace villagers were already kind and simple. In addition, during this period of drought, the bandits also took out the food they had saved over the years to feed their families. The suffering of decades faded away with this cry of apology. Peace Villagers accepted them and made them true Peace Villagers.
Later, Xiao Yi became the patriarch of Heping Village, and the area around Heping Village was also lush. To commemorate the Haima tribe, Heping villagers erected two statues in the village. The Haima Dragon held their heads high and exuberant; Haima Feng had a reserved demeanor and a high temperament. The two statues stand side by side. Every time during the New Year holidays, Heping Villagers are happy and sincerely grateful to the two Haima gods. People bring the best things to worship and pray for peace in Heping Village forever.
A few thousand years later, a peerless figure from the Haima tribe appeared, called Kyogong. With the rise of Kyogou, the threat of Mount Zhou was eliminated. Also, thousands of years have passed, and Sangtian in the Canghai Sea and Heping Village have gradually expanded. The patriarch has also been passed down from generation to generation, yet the legends of Haimalong and Haima Feng have always remained in people's hearts.
