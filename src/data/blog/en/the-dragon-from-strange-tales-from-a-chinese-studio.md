description: "“Liaozhai Zhi Zhi: Dragon” chronicles four strange stories: a dragon fell into the village, caught in mud, and flew away in a thunderstorm three days later; the little snake turned into a giant dragon in the temple and took off; in the eyes of a peasant woman, the thunderstorm flew out unscathed; and dragonclaws hanging down in the clouds grabbed people's heads and suddenly disappeared. The story is fantastic, reflecting the people's awe and imagination of dragons."

 A dragon on the border of Hebei Province fell into the village. The dragon crawled heavy and slow into a gentleman's home, and was able to fit his body at the door. The dragon crammed in, and everyone in the house was scared away. When they climbed upstairs, screamed loudly, and roared with muskets, the dragon crawled out of his house. There is a puddle of muddy water outside the gate, and the water is less than a foot shallow. The dragon climbed in, rolled in the water, covered all over with mud, and did its best to get excited, and fell just over a foot. The dragon wandered in the muddy water for three days, and flies covered its scaly armor. Suddenly it rained heavily, and the dragon flew away with a thunderbolt.
Fang Sheng climbed Mount Niu with a friend and visited a temple. Suddenly, the rafters broke down a yellow brick with a small snake on it, as small as an earthworm. The snake suddenly rotated for a week and became as thick as a finger; after another week, it was already like a strap. People were very surprised. They knew it was a dragon, so they were busy running down the mountain. As soon as I walked to the mountainside, I heard a thunderbolt from the temple shaking the valley. The dark clouds in the sky looked like a cover, and a big dragon fluttered in the clouds. It disappeared for a while.
At Xiaoxianggongzhuang in Zhangqiu County, a peasant woman went to the field. When she was faced with strong wind and sand, she felt that her eyes had been squinted, as if there were roots of wheat. I rubbed and blew, and my eyesight was always bad. Open your eyelids and take a close look. There is nothing wrong with your eyes. There is only a curved red line on the flesh. Some people say, “This is a dormant dragon.” The peasant woman heard that she was so worried that she would die. After more than three months, torrential rain suddenly fell. Suddenly there was a thunderbolt. Zhilong broke open her eyelids and flew away. The peasant woman was not hurt at all.
Yuan Xuansi said, “When I was in Suzhou, I had rainy weather all day long. Suddenly, a thunderbolt struck. The crowd saw a dragon hanging down from the clouds, its scaly armor was moving, clutching a person's head on the dragon's claws, and their eyebrows and whiskers were clearly visible. After a while, they disappeared through the clouds, and they hadn't heard of anyone dropping their heads.”
