 In the old days, there was a young man who died a long time ago. He cut and sold firewood for a living, and people called him Chailang. Although he is hardworking and fast, he is still rarely warm. He is almost 18 and 9 years old, and he still hasn't been able to marry his daughter-in-law.
One day Shiba Lang cut firewood and came home. On the way, he saw an old grandmother pass out to the ground. He hurriedly left his firearm and carried his grandmother down the mountain. He carried mountain after mountain, sweaty all over his body. He was still carrying back and forth, walking and walking, fearing that his grandmother would die halfway along the way. Carrying it on his back, the old man on his back suddenly disappeared. As soon as he looked up, his grandmother actually stood in front of him and smiled and said to him, “Good boy, I am a mountain fairy. I feel sorry for your loyalty and kindness. Your family is in a difficult situation, so I'm here to help you. The old dragon king in the Lancang River is most afraid of copper ware. If you put a few copper coins in the pot and bring the pot to the riverside to cook it over the fire, of course, Lao Dragon King will go to the riverside to beg for you. He doesn't want any gold or silver treasure from you; just bite his little flower cat squatting on a girder. When Grandma finished speaking, she disappeared.
The next day, Chai Lang set a fire on the riverside. Half of the firewood hadn't finished burning. I saw an old man with his beard stumbling from the river to the shore. He was carrying a dragon robe and rubbing his tearful eyes with the other hand, and said to Chai Lang, “Young man, don't cook anymore; it's almost burning my eyes! If you have any difficulties or want, I'll give it to you.” Chai Lang said, “I don't want anything, as long as your little flower cat crouching on a girder.” “No, I can't give this little flower cat to you!” Dragon King was very perplexed, “Aren't other things good too?” “Well, I'll have to cook for three more days and three nights!” Shiba Lang added a few more pieces of firewood to the pot. “You're really going to blind my whole family!” Dragon King couldn't help but say, “OK, wait, I'll bring you the little flower cat.”
Chai Lang took the little flower cat and happily brought him back home, and let the little flower cat eat and sleep with himself. He loved him very much. Every day before he goes up the mountain to cut firewood, he always says to the little flower cat, “Little flower cat, you are at home. Wait until I cut firewood to sell money to buy rice, then cook it for you to eat.” Little Flower Cat jumped from his arms to the ground floor, and “Mimimi” called him affectionately, as if saying to him, “Brother Chailang, go, I'm waiting for you.”
On this day, when Shiba Lang came home from cutting firewood, he discovered that the thatched house had disappeared, and it had become a new house compound with three walls and four in five patios. He walked through the door and took a look. Everything for use was available, and it was neatly arranged. He was astonished. Suddenly, the bamboo curtain was gently lifted and a little girl came out. She looked even brighter than red flowers in Yingshan, and more watery than dew beads. She looked at him with a smile. He was shocked, wondering if he had gone to the wrong person, and stepped back step by step. But the girl made a gentle move with her hand: “Come on in, my old brother Shibarang. You brought me here, why are you afraid to come in?” “So, Auntie, whose family are you from?” he asked timidly. “I am the young lady of the Lancang River Dragon King. I became a little flower cat, and the little flower cat became your daughter-in-law... When the girl finished speaking, she pulled her hand and held Jia Chailang's hand, “Come into the house and have dinner.” Since that day, Shiba Lang has had a happy and happy family.
One day, Mr. Tooji, riding a tall horse and leading his housekeeper and seven vicious bodyguards, returned from hunting in the woods. Coincidentally, Chai Lang's pretty daughter-in-law came to the mountain spring to fetch water. The Toastmaster's toad looked at it, and she was so hungry that she panicked: “What a flower, why put it on cow dung? What a handsome bird, how did it land on a barbed fence? Haha... people are here!” “Come on!” The housekeeper rushed to his face, “What is the old man's order?” The butler bowed down on horseback and whispered to the housekeeper for a while. The housekeeper nodded and bowed again. At the end of the day, he couldn't help but laugh at the end of the day.
The housekeeper found Chai Lang. After talking, Shiba Lang was in a hurry and his mouth was numb. He stumbled back home. He had a sad face and was anxious like tartary buckwheat. My daughter-in-law saw it, was she really distressed: “Chai Lang, how are you today?” Chai Lang shook his head silent. “What's a big deal, why should I be bored?” Shiba Lang burst into tears: “My days have only improved since you arrived. However, the Toastmaster sent a housekeeper to spread the word: Ming said, take a liter of new rice and leave it on the slope of the opposite gate mountain, and ask me to pick up the new rice crop one by one. Otherwise, my daughter-in-law wouldn't be mine. Looks like you and I are breaking up.” The daughter-in-law held up her sleeves and dried up her tears for Chai Lang, and smiled, “Chai Lang, don't worry. Tomorrow you can use a sack as a pillow and take a good nap on the slope of the opposite gate. When you wake up, you win.”
The next day, Chai Lang followed his daughter-in-law's orders. As soon as I woke up, the empty sack I slept on as a pillow was enough for a liter of new rice. The butler and butler were shocked and speechless for a moment. However, just as Shiba Lang walked home happy and happy, a ghost cry from the Turkish secretary came from behind him: “Shiba Lang, this time doesn't count.” “What counts?” “Will you be able to hand over 3,000 sparrows tomorrow?” “Can't hand it over.” “Then bring your daughter-in-law.” “This...” Chailang was in a hurry, stumbled back home, and sighed again. “Shiba Lang, didn't you win?” The daughter-in-law smiled and asked, “Why are you still sigh?” As a result, Chai Lang forced the Tusker to give his daughter-in-law everything he had gone through. “Don't worry, I have my own way.” The daughter-in-law comforted him.
The next day, my daughter-in-law called Chai Lang to go to a stone mill to grind a liter of wheat noodles. The daughter-in-law mixed the wheat noodles with water and softened them, kneaded them into a small bird, steamed them in a pot, then ordered Chai Lang to cut two long bamboo poles. She took a gentle breath, and the 3,000 birds “wow”, and flew to the bamboo pole to rest their feet. “Shiba Lang”'s daughter-in-law said, “Let's take it to the Secretary General.” Shiba Lang liked it very much. Carrying a two-pole bird, he swayed into Toast House. The butler and butler counted the rod one by one. They counted two times in a row. One was quite a few, enough for 3,000 small birds. The Toast Officer was so angry that his eyes fluttered. The butler hurriedly purred a few words in the ear, and the Toast Officer came up with a strange idea: “Chai Lang, limit it to tomorrow, cut down all the chestnut groves on the mountain behind the house.” “How did I finish cutting it?” “Then bring the daughter-in-law! Haha...” Chai Lang's face turned blue in a hurry and went back home in a crooked manner. The daughter-in-law hurriedly held him up to his seat and kindly asked him, “What is the trouble with the Tusker?” As a result, he also told his daughter-in-law how the Turkish Secretary turned his face down and didn't admit defeat. “This is easy,” the daughter-in-law comforted him. “Don't worry.”
The next day, the daughter-in-law handed Chai Lang an axe: “If you go to the top of the mountain under the shade of a tree, sleep sweetly, sleep sweetly, and in the afternoon, Mr. Tzu lost.” Chai Lang nodded and followed his daughter-in-law's instructions. When a mountain breeze blew him up, he got up and took a look. The entire mountain's chestnut grove had fallen to the ground. The butler and butler were shocked and exasperated. Like two dead tree stumps, they stood still in the sun. Chai Lang walked home happy. Who knows that Mr. Toast burned Basho — his heart wouldn't die; he sent the housekeeper to catch up with Shiba Lang: “Chai Lang, tomorrow you'll catch nine tigers for my old man.” “How could I get here?” Shiba Lang's face turned green with fear. “Then bring your daughter-in-law.” “This...” Chai Lang shook his head and shook his feet when he returned home. The daughter-in-law hurriedly gave him a bowl of fragrant tea and held Shiba Lang's shoulder and said, “Chai Lang, what kind of trick did the Toast Officer play again?” With tears in his eyes, Chai Lang told his daughter-in-law how he depended on the Turkish secretary. “This is not difficult to do,” the daughter-in-law said, “Don't worry.” She brought out a pot of wheat noodles, kneaded it into nine little tigers with water, steamed it in a pot, and took a gentle breath. In an instant, it became nine living big tigers, wagging their tails straight at Shiro and his wife. The daughter-in-law said, “Shiba Lang, these nine tigers are hungry. Let's go to Toast's house to feed them.”
The butler was sitting firmly at home and ordered the housekeeper to prepare an offer to pick up Chai Lang's daughter-in-law. The seven bodyguards are happy to play haha: “If that kid can get to nine tigers, then the doll that eats milk also has to catch nine wild cats, haha...” “The little ones must drink the old man's happy wine.” The toasts were having fun. Suddenly, there was a loud scream, shaking the door. I saw Shiba Lang riding on a giant tiger and yelling at the nine tigers running out. A giant tiger has colorful fur and dancing claws. The bodyguards and housekeepers, like Zhang Zi, scared the red deer. The mouse ran to the table of the Eight Immortals and scavenged bran all over their body. No matter how much, the Toast Officer even shouted “help,” and the huge Toast House turned into a mess. Like hungry cats hunting mice, the nine giant tigers swooped forward, opened their mouths, and ate all the fat clerk, the butler, and seven vicious guardians. Chai Lang laughed, and so did the slaves. Shiba Lang and Dragon Girl are living a happy and beautiful life again.
