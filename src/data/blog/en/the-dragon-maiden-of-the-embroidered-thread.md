 In the Ziwei region of Dinghai, there is a story about the “Female Dragon of the Golden Thread”.
A long, long time ago, there was a family living in Shamen Mountain. The family has a mother and daughter. Her mother is Han, and her daughter's name is Zheng Ming. Zheng Embroiders are kind hearted, hardworking and smart, embroidered with dragons, and ingenious. The phoenix she embroidered seemed to fly to the sky, the flowers she stung could attract swarms of bees, and the dragon she drew seemed to move faintly. Embroidery girls are famous near and far, and the mother and daughter spend their time struggling to help others embroider.
One summer, there were no drops of rain, crops died, and water wells dried up. The embroidery is in a hurry!
she thought! Everyone says dragons turn rain. Why don't I embroider dragons; maybe I can actually bring down Gan Lin to relieve the drought. So she found a white silk, put on a silver needle, colored thread, one needle, one thread, and carefully embroidered the dragon. Embroider, embroider, embroider during the day, embroider at night, don't drink tea, don't think about food, and embroider for a moment. After 7 749 days of embroidery, I finally embroidered a colorful golden dragon with orange horns, red whiskers, and yellow scaly gold claws. It was so lifelike, as beautiful as a living dragon!
The Jinlong was embroidered, and the embroidery had a hard time finding a pot of fresh water from the ravine, and respectfully placed the Jinlong in the basin to be used on the window sill of my embroidery house. Embroidered flowers guard it every day and night, praying that Jinlong will rain soon.
One day, the mother came to the embroidery room and saw her daughter lying exhausted on the window sill and wanted her to go to bed and rest. Han came close to the window sill and saw Jinlong swimming around in the pot. She was frightened and screamed. When the embroidery woke up, she saw that her mother was frightened and wanted to throw the basin out of the window and hurriedly reached over the sink. Mother said, “There's a monster in the pot!” Speaking of which, I'm going to grab the water basin again. Embroidering was not allowed, so I turned around and dodged. Unexpectedly, my hand slipped and the sink fell to the ground. I only heard a roar, and the place where I fell into the basin instantly became a puddle of water. This is the current “Dongdi Dragon Pool”.
As soon as the embroidery saw the pot smashed, the Jinlong was gone. There was a burst of heartache. She cried wow and jumped into the pool of water to catch the Jinlong. Strangely enough, the embroidery rolled around in the pool of water, and actually had two horns growing on its head. In the blink of an eye, a seven-colored Jinlong flew out of the pool. As soon as the Republic of Korea watched, she thought it was a monster that had taken her, so she tried her best to hold on to the dragon's claws. However, the dragon grew taller. As soon as she let go, she saw that something had fallen off the dragon's claw. After taking a close look, it turned out to be her daughter's embroidered shoes. The shoe fell unbiased and fell under a large tree. Han wanted to clean it up. They only heard a loud cry. A spring well gushes out from under the acacia tree. This is the current Acacia Well of Ziwei Zhan's family.
Jinlong took off and flew all the way to the sea. It was almost at the seaside. As soon as Jinlong rolled over, it rolled out of a river channel, and the river flowed into the field. The mother couldn't bear her daughter. She cried and ran to the beach, and while chasing her, she shouted, “The embroidery is back! The flower is back!” As soon as the mother shouted, as soon as Jinlong bowed back, the river took a turn. The mother shouted at the girl 13 times. Jinlong turned back 13 times, and the river turned 13 bends. This is now Dapu 13 Bay in Duntou.
The mother shouted 14 times, only to see Jinlong leap into the sea. The mother wanted to see her daughter. She always climbed the top of the mountain and looked blankly at the sea. This mountain is today's Ziwei Wanghai Ridge. Since then, Ziwei has pools, wells, springs, and rivers, and people have not been worried about droughts for a long time!
Because Jinlong was turned into an embroidered girl, the local people called her the “Lady Dragon of the Golden Thread.”
