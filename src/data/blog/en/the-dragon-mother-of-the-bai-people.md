 Legend has it that Longmu is a girl who cuts firewood. She lives with her mom in a field in Green Peach Village to grow crops. One year, the black dragon that took over Kanziwa took over the water outlet of the Erhai Sea, turning Dali into a vast ocean. Ordinary people were unable to make a living and fled abroad one after another. The mother and daughter were unhelped and were unable to escape; they had to go to Lan Feng in Cangshan to make a living by cutting grass and firewood. One day, my daughter was thirsty and saw a big, green peach growing on the tree. She took the peach and put it in her mouth, and the peach slipped to her stomach. Since then, she became pregnant and gave birth to a son. The daughter was afraid of other people's laughter and threw her son deep into the mountains. The giant snake on the mountain feeds children every day. Not only is it not starving to death, but it is also naive and lively. Dragon Mother hurt him very much. She took him home to raise him, and when she grew up, she went up the mountain to cut firewood and cut grass. Once, the mother and son went home to cut grass and rested at Longtan. They learned that Dragon King was sick. The child was invited to the Dragon Palace to cure Dragon King's illness with fairy herbs. While in the palace, the child was curious and put on Dragon King's common robe and became Huanglong, then defeated the Black Dragon, freed from flooding, and became a fertile field. The people in Green Peach Village built the Dragon King Temple for him, honoring Little Huanglong as the owner, and calling Little Huanglong's mother the mother of the dragon.
