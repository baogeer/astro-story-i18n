 Legend has it that this cliff boardwalk is a channel formed by dragons, so it is called “Dragon Geng Road.” Legend has it that Eastern Han
Liu Xiu
Before claiming to be the emperor, he was hunted down by Wang Mang's army. He faced an abyss and a cliff before, and later, there were troopers. In times of distress, the dragon descended from heaven to cultivate this path from between the rock walls, allowing Liu Xiu to escape from danger. The village opposite “Longgeng Road” is called Xiayang Village. Most of the villagers have the surname Liu and are said to be descendants of Liu Xiu. In the construction of a beautiful village in Xiayang Village, every household has become a fancy farmhouse that “push the window to see the green, open the door to see the trees”.
