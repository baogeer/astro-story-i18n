 On a hot day, lots of water shines brightly, and many feelings are always flying around the patio. The old man said, “Catching wax is too bad; it turned into the jasper hairpin of Buddha, the Great Mercy and Pessimistic Buddha.”
It is said that in the past, the Great Lake was a flat area. The county town above was called Shanyang County. There were people in the county who had no good conscience. Not only did they not cherish grains, they also did not honor grandparents, but they also robbed homes, murdered people and set fire to them! Emperor Jade Emperor in the sky sank several times when he wanted to take Sanyang County. Three times, the water was collected in a clean bottle by Lady Guanshiyin, saving ordinary people in Shanyang County.
The Jade Emperor was not happy, and he was in a hurry to offend Guanyin. He asked Guanyin to play chess at the Imperial Garden day and night. He said, “You are usually very busy day and night. Now you are putting a clean bottle at my place and waiting for a good game to return it to you.” After eating a few glasses of Kotobuki, Kannon agreed. As soon as Guanyin was seated, the Jade Emperor called Tenbingtian, who had already been arranged, to flood Shanyang County.
By Guan Yin's whim, General Tenbingtian had already left Nantianmen. Only then did I understand Jade Emperor's intention. I had another word just now; don't leave; let alone the clean bottle is in the hands of the Jade Emperor. As soon as Guanyin made a clever move, he secretly pulled out the jasper tube from his head, took the jade emperor and threw it underneath, turned into a dragonfly, and flew to Shanyang County to report the news: Heavy rain and water are about to fall. As soon as people with good intentions listen, they all help the elderly and take refuge in the mountains. Don't trust the wicked, and don't move. Soon, as soon as the Heavenly Soldier arrived, Shanyang Prefecture sank and became the current Taihu Lake.
Because dragonflies were about to get ahead of Tianbing and General at the time, two wings were not enough; dragonflies had four wings. Until now, dragonflies are still reporting news. As long as you see dragonflies flying in the sky in batches, the insurance will have to rain heavily, cause a lot of rain, and try everything.
