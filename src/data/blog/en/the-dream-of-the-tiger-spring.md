 Tiger Run Dream Spring is one of the ten sights of Xinxi Lake. It is located in the south of West Lake, in Daci Mountain Dinghui Temple.
There is a popular myth about beauty. Ming and Song Lim wrote in the preface to “The Tiger Runs Quan Ming”: “In the 14th year of Tang Yuan (819 AD), Master Shengkong visited Cishan to enjoy his energy and meditate. Seek out no water and take him. Suddenly, a man knelt down and said, “Since my teacher came, I've been waiting for a lot of beneficiaries, so why give up? The boy from Nanyue sent two tigers back and forth; his teacher had no worries. The next day, I saw Erhu Pao Mountain come out of the spring. It was sweet and mellow, pure and sterile. Known as “the third spring in the world”.
“Longjing Tea, Tiger, and Water”, is known as “the best of the West Lake”. Seeing, listening to, tasting, and testing springs here is a lot of fun. Tiger Run has been completely remodeled to restore the Jigong Pagoda and Luohan Hall, and to create lifelike reliefs of “Dream Tiger” and the legend of Ji Gong. In Song Su Dongpo's poem praising the Tiger and Pao Quan, there is a good phrase “Taoists don't hesitate to take steps forward and enjoy themselves.” Hupao Spring is one of the famous springs in Hangzhou. The water quality is pure, sweet and mellow. Soaking Longjing tea with Tiger Run Water is fragrant and refreshing, and is known as the best of the West Lake. Guo Moruo once wrote a poem praising, “Tigers go to the springs are still there, and the tea is very sweet for visitors.” Hupao Spring Water has a high molecular density and surface tension. If you put coins gently in a cup full of water, the coins can float on the surface of the water without sinking. Even if the surface of the water is 3 mm above the mouth of the cup, the water will not spill out.
Attractions near Hupao Spring include Di Cuixuan, Diecui Xuan, Luo Han Hall, Bell Tower, Monument, Ji Gong Temple, Ji Gong Pagoda, the Hupao Dream Fountain Statue, and Master Hongyi (Li Shutong) Tower for visitors to enjoy.
