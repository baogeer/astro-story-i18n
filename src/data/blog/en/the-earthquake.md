 Kangxi was struck on June 17, '7, when a major earthquake occurred. At the time, I was a guest in Jixia, and I was drinking under the light with my cousin Lee Du-chi. Suddenly, I heard a sound like thunder, coming from the southeast and rolling northwest. Everyone was so shocked and I don't know why. After a while, I saw the table shaking and the wine glass falling over; the roof beams and pillars made a crackling sound. The crowd was shocked and looked at each other. After a long time, I woke up that it was an earthquake, and I hurriedly stormed out of the house. I saw the paved house outside. It was tilted to the ground for a while and then stood upright; the sound of the wall falling down, mixed with the sound of a child crying, was overwhelming and deafening. People are so dizzy that they can't stand; they can only sit on the ground and bumps along with the ground. The river emptied more than a foot from the shore; chickens barked, and the whole city was in turmoil. After an hour, it settled down a bit. Also, looking at the street, men and women all gathered naked, scrambling to talk about what had just happened, and they all forgot that they had no clothes on.
Later, I heard that at the time of this earthquake, a well well had an inclined well somewhere, so no more water could be drawn; a certain building fell north-south; Qixia Mountain split a gap in the road; and Yishui fell into a cave a few acres in size. This is a rare and bizarre disaster!
