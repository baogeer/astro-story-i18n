 This story has been spread orally in the Xichang and Panzhihua regions of the Anning River basin. When I was a kid, I heard many stories from the elderly, but they had different opinions. So far, I've only been able to find a brief introduction to one statement on the Internet, but I've never seen any historical records. What is described in this article is not the same as many statements; it is compiled from memories of stories I heard when I was a kid. --Titles
A long, long time ago, there was Zitong County in southwest Sichuan. On the west side of the county town is the tall and beautiful Lushan Mountain. Li Jianchang's mother and son live in a thatched house at the foot of the mountain. When Li Jianchang was three years old, his father, Li Faye, was taken and beaten by the bully landlord Mo Xinren. When he got home, he had no money to get a doctor to treat his injuries, and soon died. The mother guarded a small plot of land and spent a difficult day with her son.
As her son Li Jianchang grew older, her mother gradually became unable to do her job again due to overwork. There is only a small plot of land in the back of the house, and only a few vegetables, sweet potatoes, can be grown, making it difficult to support the mother and child. Every day, Jianchang went up the mountain to find firewood, picked and sold at the bazaar, then bought rice to feed himself and his mother.
One day, Jianchang went up the mountain looking for firewood and saw a stink on the road, covered in mud and motionless. He bent down and pulled it softly, and the cockroach actually moved a bit. At this point, just in the middle of the day, the sun shone bitterly on the dying body of the cricket. Jianchang thought that if he didn't put it in a cool place with water, it would die right away. As a result, Jianchang hurriedly picked a leaf on the side of the road, carefully placed the crickets on the leaves, and carried his hands everywhere looking for places with water. Moving to the side of the mountain, he finally found a small pond the size of a pot and hurriedly put the crickets in. He took a long breath, sat on a rock next to him, and stared at the cricket with his eyes. He wants to see if his actions have any effect. After a while, the cockroach didn't move. Just when he was so disappointed that he was about to leave, the cockroach began to squirm. Jianchang immediately exclaimed with joy: “Hey boy, you're finally alive! Hee hee!” He remembered that it was time to go find firewood. After leaving, he looked at the cuckoo and said, “Just stay here; I'll come back to see you tomorrow.”
On the next day, Jianchang certainly went to that small pond on purpose to watch the cockroaches. I saw that the cockroach was leisurely crawling along the side of the pond. As soon as Jianchang arrived at the pond, the cockroach raised its head and swung up and down, apparently nodding at Jianchang. Jianchang was so happy. He immediately took out the chunks of rice he had on his body (when rural people go up the mountain to gather firewood or graze, they are used to kneading rice into fist-sized lumps and carrying them as dry food) and knocked off some and threw them in front of the crickets and said, “Be nice, can you find anything to eat? I'll give it to you.” Once again, the cockroach lifted its head and swayed up and down, as if to express gratitude. Jianchang was very happy. Since then, every day he has gone up the mountain to look for firewood, and he has to pour some rice on it. He saw that the cockroach was growing and getting thicker day by day. A sense of accomplishment he had never had made him forget all his suffering and tiredness. Every time he comes to see Koo, he has to talk about the joy or bitterness in his heart, regardless of whether he can understand it or not. Duckling has become his only close friend, and every time he goes up the mountain, he always has to check out the cockroach before looking for firewood.
A few years later. On that day, as usual, Jianchang came to watch the cockroach. He was about to take out a pile of food to feed the cockroach, only to see that the cockroach's body was bent, and the small pond had already been filled to the brim. The cat looked up and suddenly said, “Brother Jianchang, you saved my life, and he also comes to watch me feed me every day, which makes me so moved! Now that I've turned into a dragon, I'm sending you an orb to repay you.” Speaking of which, a golden bean-sized bean-sized bead popped out of the cocks' head.
“If you take this bead, put it in your mouth, and press it under the root of your tongue, it won't swallow it in your stomach when you eat or drink: when you need something, just say “help me” and you'll have what you want.” Yu Yulong said, “This place can't accommodate me anymore; I need to go somewhere else. Take care brother!”
Jianchang picked up the pearl, but had no time to answer. Suddenly, a large stream of water popped up in the pond. The fog filled the pond. He only heard a scream. A cloud of colorful clouds rose and drifted far away.
Jianchang stayed in shock. There was nothing in the small pond in front of him, and a sense of loss occupied his whole body. After sitting by the pond for a while, he found firewood in the mountains and returned home. The mother was distressed and said, “Hey, are you hungry? Take a break. I still have half a bowl of rice at home. I cooked a pot of porridge, eat it quickly.”
The mother and son were drinking vegetable porridge. The mother was worried and said, “There won't be any rice tomorrow, so I'll have to wait until you pick up the chai and sell it on the street to buy rice.”
His mother reminded Jianchang in one sentence, and only then did he remember what the dragon said when he gave him the ball. As a result, he told his mother the story of him and the dragon over the years. The mother was happy to say, “Hey, your kindness will definitely pay off, so try it out.”
“Uh-huh,” Jianchang answered, “Yuzhu helped me. I don't have rice to cook at home; I want rice.” As soon as Jianchang's speech was over, a bag full of white flowered rice appeared at the dinner table. Mother and son were amazed! After being happy for a while, the mother said, “It seems like the dragon really wants to repay you. “Hey, the family hasn't eaten meat in a few months, so why don't you want some more meat for a dental festival.”
Jianchang agreed. As soon as I finished speaking, fresh pork from Yiluo Box appeared on the table again. The mother was so surprised that she immediately picked up a piece of meat and looked over again. After making sure it was real pork, she went to the kitchen to cook the meat happily.
It rained heavily the next day. The broken cottage of the Jianchang family leaked everywhere, and heavy rain poured down, and it was impossible to repair it for a while. Jianchang said to her mother, “Mom, let's try it, can Yuzhu help us make the house better.” As soon as the conversation was over, his family's broken hut suddenly became a tall and spacious blue brick house. Mother and Jianchang were very surprised; were they in a dream? I was so excited that I rubbed my eyes and pinched my face. I was so excited. I quickly got down on my knees, put my hands together, and hoped the heavens would thank you. Afterwards, the mother took her son, and after running through every room upstairs and downstairs, he was even more surprised that each room was equipped with appropriate furniture and furnishings for different purposes: the bedroom had a wardrobe, brand-new clothes in the cabinet; the kitchen had pots, stoves, and water tanks; the hall had black-painted tables, chairs, coffee tables, etc., which was already the style of a local treasurer. After watching all of this, the mother and son stayed up all night excited.
The family situation has changed dramatically, and nothing is lacking. When the mother thought that her son should become a family, she said, “Oh, the family has everything now, you should have a daughter-in-law too. Just ask for a second time and find a daughter-in-law; I need someone to take care of me even when I'm old.” Jianchang thought that her mother had suffered and suffered over the years, and had a hard time pulling me over. Now it really is time for someone to do the housework for her, and she said, “Yuzhu, please help me find a daughter-in-law.” As soon as he finished speaking, a young and beautiful woman stood in front of her and immediately shyly prayed three times to her mother and said, “My daughter-in-law, Cui Gu, see Mom!” Her mother cheered up Cui with a happy smile.
“Hey, pour tea for Cui Gu soon.” Mother said, “My mother will clean up your new house.” The mother went to the cubicle with joy.
Since then, the family has lived a carefree life.
However, the good times didn't last long. The sudden appearance of the Jianchang family went unheeded. One spread to ten, ten to hundred, and all of a sudden spread to the ears of the bully landlord Mo Xinren. He brought four or five family members to Jianchang's house and said, “Li Jianchang, you poor man don't have any land, and you don't have any business. Where did you get the money to build a big blue brick house? Where did I get the money to marry a beautiful daughter-in-law?”
Jianchang didn't answer for a moment; he just smiled honestly and said, “Grandpa Mo, please sit down.” Mo Xinren ignored it. Jianchang had to say, “If you don't say it, then it must have been stolen money. I'll take you and treat you as a thief!”
The honest Jianchang was helpless; he had no choice but to tell the truth about the dragon. This shocked Mo Xinren: “Aren't you kidding? If it's real, then just bring Jeong Joo to me and take a look.”
“Yuzhu is in my mouth, I can't take it out.” Jianchang said.
Mo Xinren didn't believe it. He asked Jia Ding to hold Jianchang; they had to find Gu Zhu in his mouth. Jianchang was in a hurry and hurriedly said, “Yuzhu help me.” Suddenly, a lightning-like light flashed around Jianchang, and Mo Xinren and his four or five family members all fell to the ground a distance away. Mo Xinren slowly climbed up and shouted in horror, “Demon! Demons! You wait!” Good luck with the family.
Mo Xinren still didn't die when he got home. He thought, “It seems like the ball might be real; if I could get my hands on it, I'd like anything. However, after three days and three nights of contemplation, there was no good solution. In the end, he thought viciously that if Laozi didn't get the jewel, Li Jianchang wouldn't be able to enjoy it! As a result, Mo Xinren went to the Zitong County Government Office to sue Li Jianchang for stealing a large amount of money.
Wu Yicai, an official of Zitong County, is a corrupt official. He heard that it was a “large amount of money” case, and immediately sent someone to arrest Li Jianchang to the county government office for questioning. Li Jianchang flatly denied any theft and had no choice but to talk about Yu Zhu under severe torture. As soon as the county officials heard it, they got even more excited. Jianchang had to hand over Liu Zhu. He immediately asked the hitters to cut out Jianchang's mouth, but when the hitters caught Jianchang, Jianchang secretly called “Jeong Joo help me”, and the hitters were all knocked down and thrown off the street. When the county officials saw this situation, they thought to themselves that Eel Pearl was a real relic; only by killing Lee Kin-cheong could they get their hands on the eel. As a result, I was shocked and shouted angrily: “Put this idiot in jail and get the law right at 3 p.m. tomorrow!”
Jianchang was imprisoned on death row, and I always thought in my heart that I couldn't get away with this robbery. At 10 o'clock in the middle of the night, a voice softly said in my ear, “Don't be afraid, Brother Jianchang. I am a dragon. Now I'm a blue dragon. Tomorrow at the execution site, when you see two bamboo shoots popping up in front of you, you can quickly grab the bamboo shoots, don't let go, and don't be afraid of whatever happens: your mother and wife are also safe, don't worry.” After hearing this, Jianchang felt a little at ease.
The next day, Li Jianchang was taken to the Yamen execution site in Zitong County to kneel. The two executioners waited on both sides with sharp swords.
“Arrived at 3 p.m.!” The execution officer shouted. When Wu Yicai, the county official, raised a shock tree and was about to take a picture, an earthquake occurred. Two bamboo shoots suddenly popped up on the ground in front of Jianchang. Jianchang hurriedly held out his hands and grabbed the bamboo shoots. Then, the ground cracked, large water came out of the rift, and the water was getting bigger and bigger. Amidst the steaming water, a blue dragon camel soared into the air and flew west: the entire execution site and county government gate were quickly flooded by heavy water and sank to the bottom of the water, and this place became a vast ocean.
