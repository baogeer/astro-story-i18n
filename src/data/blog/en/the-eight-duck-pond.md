 On the west side of Yunlong Bridge in Yangjiao, between a green mountain and flat slope, there is a rectangular pool of water. Although I can't see the water flowing in and the spring flowing out, the grass at the pool is evergreen all year round, and the clear water in the pool is clear, and it never dries up. There is a shepherd who is around 13 years old. He has been rushing with his flock to graze by the pool all day long. One day, he suddenly saw a golden moon flashing out of this water tank, and he also swam out with eight snow-white silver ducks, patting the water while “quack quack” and cheering. The sheep herder was stunned: “Why is there still a moon during the day? Whose family kept the white ducks in the pool?” The more he thought about it, the weirder he rushed to the side of the pool and rushed to the edge of the pool. Unexpectedly, before getting close to the waterhole, the moon and ducks disappeared.
There is a merchant who takes on the task of transferring mountains, and often passes by the waterhole. He came back the other day, and the sheep herder told him about the strange events that had happened in this pool of water. The vendor was so happy to hear it: “There must be a treasure of heaven hidden in this pool. No wonder the water is clear and bright, and it never dries up in the four seasons.” Taking advantage of the baby sheep, he hurriedly took off his pants and dropped his clothes, like a water cat catching fish, and jumped into a pool of water and messed around. Touching and touching, a glimmer of light suddenly appeared in the water. He hurriedly dived into the water, and finally saw eight silver ducks with glittering silver lights lying around a golden moon. Overjoyed, he quickly popped out of his mouth, swooped at the golden moon, and held his hands tightly. The eight snow-white silver ducks “brushed” and flew in shock. One flapped its wings and flew into the air, drawing out eight lines of brilliant white light, and disappeared. I was so happy and excited. I was happy to catch this golden moon; I was angry that I flew away those eight silver ducks. The vendor hid the golden moon in the bottom, took a step, laughed three times, and went shopping in Kyushu to expand the deal. Since then, this waterhole has been known as Pa Ya Tan.
