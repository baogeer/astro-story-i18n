 Climb up to Zilai Cave in the middle of Mount Yuhuang and look down, and you can see that there is a Baguatian at the bottom of the mountain. Baguatian is neatly arranged with eight horns, and the field is divided into eight hills. Eight different types of crops are grown in the Hachioka field. Eight types of crops show different colors throughout the year. There is a round mound in the Hachigaoka field. It is a Taiji map of half yin and half yang.
Legend has it that this Baguatian is a “land of nationality” pioneered during the Southern Song dynasty.
That year, the emperor of Southern Song lost Bianliang Jingcheng and fled to Hangzhou with a large group of imperial relatives, relatives, and hundreds of military officials. When they saw the beautiful scenery of West Lake, they stayed and built a palace and flower garden at the foot of Fenghuang Mountain. They still eat, drink, play, have fun, and live a luxurious life. Ordinary people in Hangzhou, seeing the emperor's ignorance, were greatly dissatisfied with the many discussions in the streets and alleys. The sound of the wind spread once and twice and reached the emperor's ears. He was afraid that ordinary people would cause chaos, and he was a little panicked, so he summoned a hundred military officials to discuss it.
People and military officials went back and forth to discuss, and for a moment they couldn't think of a way to deal with it. Later, a civil servant came up with an idea. He said, “The emperor, the popular rhetoric is nothing more than a complaint about how comfortable life is in the court. As long as the emperor opens a field of land and cultivates it himself, the people will be satisfied.” When the emperor heard the truth, a sacred message immediately drifted down: “The widow deeply remembers the suffering of the people, pioneered the fields of cultivation, and experienced suffering with the common people...”
After a few days, under Mount Yuhuang, a field of registration was opened. Next to it, eight large piles were neatly laid down, and eight thick columns were raised. A thick cowhide curtain was placed between the columns. ——Because the emperor cultivated the land inside, ordinary people were not allowed to peek.
After a few days, the cowhide curtain was lifted. There are eight types of crops: rice, wheat, millet, millet, beans... there are a total of Hachioka fields. There is a round earthen mound in the Hachigaoka field. When ordinary people saw that the emperor was cultivating the land just like them, the discussion slowly declined.
The season for weeding and fertilizing crops was reached, and the emperor had to leave the palace again to “cultivate the fields.” On those eight thick pillars, another cowhide curtain was put up. On the ten-mile road, they were all held by the Imperial Forest Army, and ordinary people were not allowed to approach.
At the time, there was an old man who grew crops. He didn't believe the emperor would actually cultivate the land himself. On this day, he got up three in the middle of the night and quietly escaped the Imperial Forest Army in the dark. He fell three steps at a time, fell five steps at a time, climbed Mount Yuhuang, and hid in Zilai Cave in the middle of the mountainside.
Wait, wait, slowly, the sun came up, the sun rose, and the old Han dynasty looked down the mountain. Ordinary people all went down to the fields to work, yet the cowhide curtain was empty. I waited until the sun rose to the height of Mitake before I saw a group of people come out of the palace and walk into the cowhide curtain. The old man opened his eyes and took a closer look, hey! It turned out to be a few eunuchs weeding there, but the emperor and his concubines sat on the mound in the middle drinking and having fun!
The old man held back his belly and had a hard time enduring the dark, but he still quietly touched down the mountain. The next day, he told others what he had seen with his own eyes. One transmission to ten, ten transmission to hundred. All of a sudden, all the people in the city knew about it. The emperor saw that his trick had already been debunked. I won't go to the “farmland” again. However, this well-groomed “Baguatian” has been preserved until now.
