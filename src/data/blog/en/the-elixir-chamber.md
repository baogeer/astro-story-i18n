 Legend has it that the 10th year of Ming Wanli (AD)
1582
Year), the former prime minister Zhang Juzheng died and his home was ransacked by Emperor Wanli. Zhang Juzheng's eldest son, Zhang Jingxiu, committed suicide. The second son, Zhang Sixiu, was beaten and maimed. The third son, Zhang Maoxiu, was humiliated by political opponents. He decided to follow his father's brother underground, but he did not die in the well, going on hunger strike. In the end, he went incognito and secretly surrendered to his father's old subordinate, Zheng Rubi, who was filial at home. He disguised himself as a Taoist and called himself Master Ge Lianshi. He stayed here for 20 years. The beautiful scenery of Xiandu injected a hint of warmth into his bleak state of mind, and Zhang Maoxiu also added a bit of weight to the cultural landscape of Xiandu.
