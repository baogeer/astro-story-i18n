 In the early summer of the 9th year of Xianfeng, there was a glamorous event in Jingshi City. Cha Wan Xiang, the new widow of the Pan family, took advantage of the night to elope with her son. Since the Pan family is a famous family in Jingshi, this is particularly remarkable. It is said that the Pan family once sent five martial arts martial arts from the nursing home to catch up with an eloped man and woman riding the same horse near Yang Liuqing. Unexpectedly, the man's martial arts far surpassed that of the Pan family's martial arts masters. Using only a few moves, he spat the five warriors to pee, and the two rode away leisurely.
This incident was fascinating and amazing. It was enjoyed in the streets and alleys for a while, and all kinds of discussions broke out. Some people say, “A pair of dog men and women, make such an outrageous move!” Some people say, “What a pair of enemies. It's hard to have an evil relationship!” There are also people who say, “A pair of lovers are born for love!”
The actress, Cha Wan Xiang, is originally from Jiangnan Xiu, a giant salt merchant family from Zhejiang. She has lived with her family in Gusu City since she was a child. The man who eloped with her late at night was Yang Dinglai, the son of Cha Wan Xiang's neighbor. The Yang Family is the home of Shuxiang for the fifth generation of Huai'an Shanyang warriors. Yang Dinglai grew up with her father in Gusu. He studied and practiced martial arts since childhood. Not only was he good at reading, but he was also very good at his fist and foot skills. He only had about a dozen enemies, so let alone talk about it.
Both the Cha and Yang families are close neighbors, and they are also famous members of the local family, so they have a very good relationship and are extremely close. Adults are like that, as are children of similar age, Cha Wan Xiang and Yang Dinglai, who are like a brother and sister. If things were to go according to normal circumstances, they would have become a young couple, but they didn't have this relationship because the Cha family had been married to the Shijiao Pan family long before their daughter was born. Although Xiao Wanxiang is still a chaotic little girl, she is already a famous flower owner. When they were kids, they didn't guess, and they didn't feel any obstacles. At the age when they first started their relationship, the adults of the two families began to restrict their interaction since there was no possibility of marriage. Only then were these young boys and girls surprised that they were so hard to break up with each other, and that they were so helpless! But after all, living close to each other, the two can always find a chance to meet. At this point, they already know that they will spend less and less time together, so they cherish it. Every time they greet me, they are happy and worried.
When it comes to Cha Wan Xiang's future grandmother's family, it's not trivial. My grandfather, Pan Shien, was a college student in the Imperial Court for 30 years. My father-in-law Pan Guanbao has already held the rank of Gong Qing. Her fiancé is also a young and talented person. He has a strong character, and is regarded as having a great future. The Yang family is considered a famous family in Gusu, but compared to the famous Pan family of Beijing teachers, this is far worse.
At this point, Pan Guanbao was sent by the imperial court to serve in Gusu. Yang Ding became curious after getting the news. He thought, “My sweetheart, Cha Wanxiang, will enter Pan's family sooner or later. It would be better if I had a relationship with Grandpa Pan in advance, so I could enter and leave Panfu in the future. If I had the chance to meet Cha Wan Xiang, I would be able to comfort him.” Thinking about it this way, he looked for an opportunity to submit a famous post to Grandpa Pan, calling himself a disciple, and begged for advice. Yang Dinglai was already famous in the local community at the time. Pan Guanbao got a great position. Naturally, he was happy, so he actually formed a teacher-student relationship with Yang Dinglai.
Three years later, Cha Wanxiang married the Pan family and lived with her husband Pan Zu in Beijing and Shi. Soon after, Yang Dinglai married Pang Suk-ying, a beautiful woman from Gusu, as his wife. Father Peng was an official in Beijing at the time, and Yang Dinglai made an excuse to go to Jingshi Guangjie Road Gate in order to pave the way for Qingyun to go straight ahead and stay with his wife at his father's house in Beijing. At the same time, Pan Guanbao also returned to Beijing to recuperate due to illness, which just gave Yang Dinglai an opportunity to take advantage. Under the guise of visiting his teacher, he ran to Pan's house when everything was fine, hoping to meet Cha Wan Xiang in the hospital's hallway. It was no wonder that he followed along to Beijing Shi's painstaking efforts.
Having traveled a lot to the Pan family, I naturally had the chance to meet Cha Wanxiang alone. At a time like this, they both seem to have a lot to say, but they don't know where to start. Therefore, they often only talk about social affairs that are not painful or itchy; they seem like they are people I have met for the first time. As a result, it inevitably evoked Cha Wanxiang's fondness for her old feelings. However, she is now relatively speechless; it only adds an inexplicable kind of pain in vain. Therefore, she advised Yang Ding not to visit Pan's family after her arrival, simply never meet, and just keep everything from the past in her memory. Yang Dinglai didn't think so. He thought it would be better to be able to take a look than not to meet. He even fantasized about one day where the two would relive their old dreams and renew their relationship.
Cha Wanxiang's husband, Pan Zutong, is already working in Hanlin, so it can be described as a teenager. Who could have anticipated an unexpected turn of events. Intentional or unintentional, a disaster of extinction befell him. It was eight years since Xianfeng, which is the second year that Pan Zutong married Cha Wan Xiang. Jinqiu Xiang experienced a sensational exam room fraud case. Chief examiner Bai Jun came to the ground. Pan Zutong was also involved and convicted because he was also one of the readers of the paper. He could have also been convicted by death. The emperor thought he was the grandson of the famous minister Pan Shien, and only removed him from office and sent to the frontier. Shortly after Pan Zu left, bad news spread from the border, saying that he died on the way due to an infectious disease, and Cha Wan Xiang became a young widow.
After Cha Wan lost her husband, Yang Dinglai felt that hope was imminent, so he went to the Pan family more diligently. In addition to consoling the teacher's pain of losing his son, he also bought a girl ring and went to Cha Wan Xiang's house under the pretext of paying tribute to the newly-widowed person. Their passion was blatant. It was obvious that the two were having an affair quickly revealed to Grandpa Pan. His college student was furious, his son died, and his daughter-in-law actually hooked up with his protégé, which really ruined the situation! In order to take care of his face, Grandpa Pan made a big splash through a trifle incident. Since then, Yang Ding has not been allowed to enter the Pan family again. Naturally, the teacher-student relationship has also been completely broken.
Yang Dinglai knew in his heart the real reason why Grandpa Pan broke off his relationship with himself; he inevitably had some anger. However, the trial period for the Reibu conference was imminent. This was his mission when he came to Beijing. He had to show good grades and have an explanation for his family, so he had to put up with it for a while. Although Yang Dinglai forced himself to calm down and take the test, Cha Wan Xiang's beautiful image always went back and forth in his mind. He had no choice but to do anything with his heart. The list was released after the exam, but of course there was no name in the gold list.
