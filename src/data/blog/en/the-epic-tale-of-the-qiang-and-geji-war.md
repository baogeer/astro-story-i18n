 Legend has it that Mu Jie-ju and Dou An-joo had nine sons after getting married. The father's name is Gou, that is Aba Bai Gou. The name of the second child is Evil, the name of the third child is Zhi, the name of the fifth child is Ge Nan, the name of the sixth child is Black Bull, the name of the seventh child is Aries, the name of the eighth child is Ji, and the name of Jiu is Shang Dang. They led their tribe and rushed to a good grazing place with mountains, water, and plains, and decided to settle down on this prairie. As the years passed, Mu Jie-zhu and Dou An Zhu's nine sons proliferated and became nine people. This is the past life of the Qiang people.
However, there are tall cheekbones, short tails, and strong indigenous Gogi people living in the prairie. They are small but very ferocious. They compete with each other for pastures, and wars often occur. One night, the Jiuzhi Qiang people on the prairie were suddenly robbed by the Goki people, and the Jiuji Qiang people were forced to travel west to find a safe place to live. At one point, after the nine Qiang people were dispersed by the Goki people, they each escaped. The older brother Aba Baigou, who was taught the abilities of the Sun God, led the remnants to migrate to the Minjiang River. The elder brother, who originally knew about celestial events, human affairs, and Shinto rituals, was overwhelmed for a moment because Qiang Wen's classics were accidentally stolen by white-haired male goats. The local indigenous Gogi people were also relentless in their pursuit. They had no choice but to fight a bloody battle with the Gogi people in the Minshan area. Although Abba Baigou fired an arrow, they were still unable to stop the attack of the Gogi people. During the battle, the fighting cries of the Qiang people shocked the ancestral god Mu Jiezhu. Seeing the suffering of her children and grandchildren, Mu Jiezhu threw three white stones from the air, turning the white stones into three snowy mountains to block the Gogi people. In this way, the Qiang people were able to escape.
Guided by Mu Jiezhu, the ancestors of the Qiang people headed south from the northwest prairie in the face of the dark night and the stars, went over many mountains, and finally reached the wavy upper reaches of the Minjiang River and settled in the middle of the river valley. However, the good times didn't last long. Three years later, the Gokies attacked again. Aba and Baqqu led the crowd into battle. After several tough fights, they were still unable to win. Finally, Tenjin Kobita saw that his children and grandchildren were always unable to win and were unable to help them. This time, something else just happened --
One day, Tenjin Kobita's oldest son herds sacred cows at Kuroyama Ranch. In the morning, he rushed his herd to the grassland to raise them and had fun; in the afternoon, when he rushed home, he was haunted, and the horned cows were gone. These two cows are Mubita's heart; he wants to come and ask about this in person. Tenjin Mubita first asked the Qiang people, “Have you seen the lucky cows and young cows I have raised on Luoshan Ranch?” The Gogi people were the first to answer, “We haven't seen it!” Mubita also asked the Qiang people, “Did you steal the sacred cow?” The Qiang people said, “We don't dare to steal!” Mubita asked the Gogi people again, “Did you steal the sacred cow?” The Gogi people said, “We didn't steal!” Mubita got angry and summoned the Qiang people and the Goki people to check their mouths one by one. Tenjin Mubita pulled out dried vegetable tendons from the gaps between the teeth of the Qiang people, and beef tendon he pulled out of the gaps between the teeth of the Goki people. When Mubita knew the cow had been stolen by the Gogi people, she was very angry with them. Tenjin Mubita also asked the Qiang people, “Do you want gods, strong, or Gogi people?” The Qiang people said, “We want gods, we want strength, we don't want Gogi people!” Mubita also asked the Gogi people, “Do you want gods, strong, or Gogi people?” The Gogi people arrogantly answered, “We don't want gods, don't be aggressive, just the Gogi people.” When Mubita heard this, he was very unhappy. He wanted to think about cleaning up the Gogi people.
Another day, Tenjin Mubita shouted that his grandson Aba Baigou led the Qiang people and the Goki people up the mountain to cut firewood. Mubita called the Qiang people split the big tree in one hole with an axe, and asked the Gogi people to stick their hands in. The Qiang people took out the axe, and many Goki people got caught to death by tree clippings. Mubita called out that the Qiang people used wooden sticks as weapons, and the Gogi people used hemp sticks as weapons. The Goki people hit the Qiang people with hemp sticks, and a dozen hemp sticks blossomed; the Qiang people hit the Gogi people with wooden sticks, and many more people were killed. Mubita called out that the Qiang people used three white stones as weapons, and the Goki people used snow blocks as weapons. The Gogi people hit the Qiang people with snow cubes, causing snowflakes to fly everywhere; the Qiang people hit the Gogi people with white stones and stoned one to death, killing more than half of the Gogi people. Mubita called the Qiang people to kill nine grassroots and throw them under the stone rock. I deliberately asked Iwashita, “Hey, are you guys having fun at the foot of the rock?” The Qiang people who were originally ambushed under the rock answered, “It's fun.” Mubita called the Gogi people also jumped off the rock to play. As a result, tens of millions of people were knocked to death by the Gokies. Mubita also called the Qiang people to search the caves, put the Goki people they found in cowhide pockets, then hang them from a large tree with ropes. Firewood was piled up under the tree, and the fire was lit with fire and smoke. The entire Gogi army was almost destroyed, and Aba Baekgou won the war.
After defeating the Gogi people, Aba Baigou led everyone into Maowen, rebuilt their homes, and unified all the Qiqiang departments in the upper reaches of the Minjiang River. Aba Bai formed an alliance leader, built a village and built customs and sealed off the land. The nine sons of Aba Baigou became the leaders of nine regions. When Qiang Zhai was built, Aba Baigou called the Qiang people in the tribe to set up villages all over the place. Diahouses and tall white diaolou were built in the village. Qiang Min also built a “Lexi” on the roof of his house, with three white stones inside to commemorate Tenjin Mubita. As a result, various Qiang tribes were separated in the upper reaches of the Minjiang River valley. Every year on October 1 of the lunar calendar, the Qiang people kill pigs and slaughter sheep, smash alcohol, thank God, sacrifice their ancestors, sacrifice mountains and water, and repay God's grace. Gradually, they integrate with the rest of the Goki people into a large unified tribe — Ranqiang.
During the Tang dynasty, Tuban fought a protracted tug-of-war with the Tang army on the northwest plateau of Sichuan. At that time, Tuban's sphere of influence extended to the Songzhou (present-day Songpan) area, and quite a few Tuban soldiers were stationed in Songzhou. Since then, the Qiang people close to the Tibetan region have gradually integrated into the Tibetan people; the Qiang people close to the Han region have gradually been converted into Han people; the Qiang people in places where neither the Tuban nor Tang dynasty can reach, such as Maoxian County, Wenchuan, and Lixian County, evolved into today's Qiang people.
