 Author: Ling Yun
The war between Southern Song and Yuan dynasties has a very important place in Chinese history. Meanwhile, the Yangtze River basin, which is an important battleground between the two sides, played a decisive role in the war. Among these, the Battle of Xiangfan between Song and Yuan is particularly famous. The Battle of Xiangfan was a decisive battle between the Southern Song and Yuan dynasties, and decided the fate of the two countries. The Battle of Xiangfan ended in the defeat of the Southern Song Dynasty, which directly led to the final demise of the Southern Song Dynasty. The surrender faction within the Southern Song administration and some frontline generals always had a negative attitude towards Xiangfan throughout the campaign. Either you don't need reinforcements from the main force, or you can escape on the run. As a result, Southern Song has always been in an extremely unfavorable passive situation, and has always been passively beaten. Throughout the battle, the Southern Song Guards in the Xiangfan region remained under the strong blockade of the Yuan dynasty. They received almost no aid from outside, and were isolated and helpless. However, it is in this extremely difficult situation. Facing a strong blockade by the Yuan Army, in a situation where external reinforcements have become an impossible task. Southern Song's navy of only a few thousand people, made up of temporarily recruited militias, successfully broke through the strong blockade of the Yuan dynasty and brought supplies into Xiangyang. As a result, it became the only successful reinforcement operation for the Xiangfan Guojun from the outside world during the Battle of Xiangfan, which lasted 6 years. This epic voyage became a famous example of how few won more in water battles in Chinese history. So what kind of battle is this? How did this battle unfold, how did it go, and how did it end? Here, Ling Yun wants to talk about this with her own knowledge and netizens. Because of Ling Yun's limited abilities, there must have been shortcomings in what was written. Netizens are also invited to give more advice.
After the Mongols destroyed Jin, they immediately began a major Southern expedition in an attempt to destroy the Southern Song Dynasty. Since the Mongolian army, which comes from nomadic tribes, is not accustomed to water warfare, the Yangtze River Sky Insurance, which Southern Song relies on, is insurmountable. As a result, Mongolia changed its strategy. Change the previous strategy of going south and attacking directly from the Yangtze River. Implement a new strategy of extensive detour and copying of the Southern Song dynasty. The strategy's specific measures are to take defensive positions in the southeast Yangtze River basin and pretend to attack in order to attract the main forces of Southern Song. At the same time, the main attack was launched in the northwest. Concentrate all the elite main forces on the frontline in Shaanxi. Prepare to attack from Qinlong. First, conquer Sichuan, control the upper reaches of the Yangtze River, and bypass the Yangtze River Sky Insurance. Afterwards, they attacked Southern Song with a team that followed the river eastward. The main forces, on the other hand, continued to move south and formed a strategic encirclement circle with great detours after the fall of Dali's administration in the Yunnan region. Then, from south to north, the Southern Song dynasty was captured from the south. Meanwhile, the Mongolian army attacked simultaneously from western Sichuan and the northern frontal Jianghuai battlefield. Southern Song came to an end in one fell swoop. Ling Yun thinks it is necessary to explain a little bit. That is, the strategy was correct from a military point of view, and ultimately succeeded. In 1255 AD, Mongolia sent troops to Yunnan via Gansu and Sichuan, overthrew the Dali Duan administration, pacified the entire territory of Yunnan, and established the Yunnan Province. A strategic encirclement of Southern Song was achieved by imposing a major detour from far back. Subsequently, in 1258 AD. The Mongolian Empire sent his younger brother Kublai to attack Ezhou in Southern Song (note: present-day Wuchang, Hubei) and sent General Wu Liang to attack Tanzhou in Southern Song (note: present-day Changsha, Hunan). Meanwhile, he himself personally led the attack on Sichuan with the Mongolian Iron Riders as the core. The Mongolian army began to advance rapidly in Sichuan. The Southern Song Guards in the Sichuan region were almost immediately destroyed. However, when the Mongolian army advanced to Hezhou, it faced tenacious resistance from the Southern Song Patriotic military and civilians led by Wang Jian. The Guards relied on Diaoyu Mountain Insurance to continuously defeat the Mongolian army. In July 1259 AD, Mongkhan, who was on the front line to oversee the battle, was seriously injured by artillery fire from the Southern Song Guards and soon died in the army. This is the famous Battle of Hezhou in Chinese history. After Mongo's death, Kublai on the Southern Front temporarily made peace with Southern Song because he was in a hurry to return to his division and compete for Khan's position. As a result, the two sides entered a short and fragile period of peace.
After Mongkhan's death, the Mongolian Empire immediately fell into a civil war over Khan's rank. The end result was that Mongkhan's younger brother, Bo Er, only Jin Kublai took the position of Khan. After Kublai took the throne, he immediately resumed his attack on Southern Song. However, the previous attacks made Kublai realize that the main battleground between Song and Meng was the Yangtze River basin. The Yangtze River battlefield must first be resolved before the strategy of destroying the Song Dynasty can be realized. The turning point occurred in November 1267 AD. At this point, Liu Zheng, the former governor of Luzhou in the Southern Song Dynasty, who had surrendered to the Yuan dynasty for 6 years, suggested to Kublai: “If you want to destroy Song, you must first attack Xiangyang and remove its defenses. Song Wuxiang has no huai; without Huai, Jiangnan can do it at his fingertips”. Kublai realized that Xiangfan had a central position in the entire Jianghuai line of defense in Southern Song. Once Xiangfan loses, it will have a decisive impact on the battle against the South. As a result, he adopted Liu Zheng's proposal and decided to concentrate his efforts on seizing Xiangfan. Meanwhile, with Liu Zheng as the lead, the Yuan Army built more than 1,000 warships to recruit and train a large number of soldiers. Build a strong team of sailors. As a result, it broke the water advantage that Southern Song always had. In 1270 AD, after a series of operations, the Yuan Army completed a strategic siege of Xiangyang, and the Battle of Xiangfan immediately began.
After Yangyang was besieged, the Mongolian army imposed an extremely strict blockade on the waterway. To rescue Xiangyang, the Southern Song side tried to break the blockade through two large-scale waterways in 1269 and 1271 AD. However, due to the corruption and incompetence of the rulers and the frontline generals were greedy and afraid of death, they fled the battle. Rescue operations were successively defeated by the Mongolian army. The Mongolian army aimed to completely implement the blockade of Xiangyang. A large number of obstacles such as wooden stakes and iron cables have been placed on the surface of the river. At the same time, many military ports were built and warships were stationed. As a result, under the strong water blockade by the Mongolian army, the possibility of Song Army reinforcing Xiangyang by waterway is almost nil. Facing the dangerous situation on the front line in Xiangfan. Jia Sidao, an outlaw who controlled the actual power of Southern Song at the time and was also a “famous” traitor in Chinese history, always refused to reinforce Xiangfan with all his might. Furthermore, in order to block news against the then emperor Song Duzong, Zhao Yi, they wantonly persecuted ministers and frontline generals who dared to give advice, and nurture close friends. Due to Jia Sidao's unfavorable rescue, the military and civilians on the front line in Xiangfan are in an extremely difficult situation. In particular, due to the strong blockade of the Mongolian army over a long period of time, supplies were scarce in the city. There is a serious shortage of supplies such as grain, food, silver, and even table salt and cloth, and the Southern Song dynasty government turned a deaf ear to this. In November 1271 AD, Kublai changed the name of Mongolia to Dayuan, became emperor, and established the Yuan dynasty. Subsequently, the Yuan dynasty invaded the South with all its might. Xiangfan became the focus of the attack. Under the massive invasion of the Yuan dynasty, Xiangfan became an isolated island. It is in danger of being attacked at any time. For this reason, when the imperial court refused reinforcements, Southern Song frontline coach Li Tingzhi decided to organize his own forces to reinforce Xiangyang as much as possible. As a result, the story to be told in this article unfolded.
April 1271 AD. The supreme military chief of the Southern Song Dynasty in the Lianghu region, Li Tingzhi, the Beijing-Lake Construction Ambassador, led the army into Yingzhou (note: present-day Zhongxiang, Hubei) in order to strengthen command of the Yuan Army's battles. At this time, the situation on the front line in Xiangfan is extremely unfavorable. At this point, it had been 5 years since Yangyang had been trapped, and the city was running out of ammunition and food. For this reason, Yangyang Governor Lu Wenhuan sought help from the Imperial Court several times, but they all went into the sea like a muddy cow. Faced with death from the imperial court, Li Tingzhi, the supreme military chief of Southern Song in the Lianghu region, had to organize reinforcements on his own after repeatedly beating the imperial court to no avail. In April, Li Tingzhi began recruiting militias to rescue Yangyang. More than 3,000 people from Yingzhou (note: present-day Zhongxiang County, Hubei) and other places joined the military. Subsequently, Li Tingzhi directed Zhang Shun and Zhang Gui to lead an army of 3,000 reinforcements to Xiangyang. Both Zhang Shun and Zhang Gui are generals of local armed groups in Southern Song. They have long resisted the Mongol invasion and have rich practical experience. However, they and the 3,000 generals under their command know very well that they are going to do more harm than good. What they have to face is an almost insurmountable blockade made up of hundreds of thousands of ferocious veterans. Facing such a strong enemy, breaking through the blockade with only 3,000 people is tantamount to death. However, they are more aware that the military and civilians in Xiangyang, who have been under siege for 5 years, have the same desire for reinforcements to see through the fall. As a result, before the expedition, Zhang Shun, the captain, encouraged the generals of the entire army with a desperate determination. He made it clear to the general. In this expedition, faced with enemies that are tens of times stronger than myself, I will surely die nine times in my life. Those who don't want to be with them can leave. Inspired by the general's spirit of not being afraid of life or death, the 3000 generals of the Song Army boosted their morale and expressed their desire to fight to the death with the enemy. As a result, Zhang Shun began preparations for the battle.
Since the purpose of this nature is mainly to deliver logistics supplies urgently needed by the military and civilians in Xiangyang, rather than to annihilate the enemy. In order to break through the strata blockade of the Yuan Army's powerful navy as soon as possible, it is necessary for the troops to have the fastest possible speed and good flexibility. As a result, Zhang Shun and Zhang Gui did not use the large battleships previously used by the Southern Song Yangtze River Marines, but instead chose lightweight high-speed ships with excellent speed and maneuverability. Furthermore, in order to kill enemy ships from as far distance as possible, the Song Army also carried a large number of long-range weapons. These include strong single-handed crossbows, heavy three-bowbed crossbows, and sudden muskets and artillery that had just appeared at the time.
Here, Ling Yun believes it is necessary to introduce these types of weapons. A bed crossbow is a large long-range crossbow. This weapon appeared very early. There are related records as early as the Eastern Han Dynasty. By the Song Dynasty, since they wanted to fight against nomadic cavalry in the north and heavy armored war elephants from southern countries such as Vietnam, the Song army made great efforts to improve bed crossbows. The Song Dynasty was a period of high technological development in ancient China, so the performance of Song Jun's heavy bed crossbows was greatly improved compared to previous dynasties. According to the “General Collection of Martial Arts”. The Song Army's crossbow was smaller in size than the previous generation. The back of the bow is made of a variety of materials. It is a typical composite bow with extremely strong explosive power. Furthermore, in order to increase range, Song Jun also mounted three strong bows in a crossbow gun, the last of which was mounted in the opposite direction. Therefore, the Song bed crossbow is also called the three-bowbed crossbow. The bow string is an animal tendon that uses the combined force of multiple bows to fire arrows. The three-bowbed crossbow is very ingenious in construction. There are two wheels at the back, and pulleys are used to hold the bow string. The crossbow is made of solid sandalwood, which is light and strong. Use iron as a gun, steel as a crossbow machine, and fire with a wooden mallet. The heavy arrowhead used is nearly 2 meters long. It is recorded in historical records as “wooden drumsticks and iron rings”. Known as “one shot, three swords and arrows,” the shape is similar to a javelin; the three iron spikes are like three swords. The arrow is a steel triangular armor-piercing arrow. Also, you can fire multiple arrows at once. The Song Three bowbed crossbow gun had a maximum range of 1,600 meters. This was the limit humans could reach with direct fire weapons before artillery appeared! It was the most powerful heavy ranged weapon in the world at the time. Song Jun tied a gunpowder bag to the large arrowhead, ignited it, and fired it with a crossbow or bow, which acted as an arson, burning or exploding. The Song Shen-arm crossbow is a single-soldier light crossbow; it is a type of stepping crossbow. There is an iron ring on the front end of the crossbow body, which is specially used for pedaling. Compound bow technology is also used, and there are special organs on the fuselage. The maximum range of the crossbow is 370 meters, and it can still break through heavy iron armor at a distance of 300 meters. It was the longest range and most powerful single soldier weapon in the world before the advent of muskets. The Sudden Musket, on the other hand, is a new type of gunpowder-powered long-range infantry weapon invented in 1259 AD. The front part is a thick bamboo tube, and the center is a powder chamber containing gunpowder and projectiles. There is a small ignition hole in the outer wall, and a wooden handle on the back. After igniting the lead, gunpowder erupted and the projectile was ejected, with a maximum range of about 230 meters. This is the world's first body-barreled musket to fire bullets. The artillery mentioned here, on the other hand, is an iron-shell explosive firearm developed from a “thunderbolt.” The weapon was filled with a large amount of gunpowder in a spherical iron case, and a large amount of iron sand or gravel was added and a letter was installed. When fighting, ignite the letter and throw it at the opponent with heavy trebuchet or manpower. After the explosion, the iron sand or gravel filled inside is scattered all over the place, which can kill a large number of enemies, and also has the function of arson and combustion. It is mainly launched with large stone crushers. Its predecessor, the “Thunderbolt,” was used extensively in quarrying battles.
After everything was ready, in May 1271, Zhang Shun and Zhang Gui assembled a fleet of over 100 light ships at Gaotou Port. The ships were full of food, medicine, table salt, silver, and cloth. Zhang Shun and Zhang Gui connected their ships into a phalanx. Zhang Gui was in the front and Zhang Shun was in the back, leading the fleet to break into a heavy encirclement of the Yuan Army. The historic “Two Cards to Aid Xiang” voyage in the Battle of Xiangfan has begun!
After breaking through a blockade outside the Yuan Army, the Song Army's fleet arrived at Mohong Beach. It was met with the main fleet of the Yuan Army sailors, and Yuan warships were scattered all over the river. The Song Army's light ships were simply unable to operate under the encirclement of the huge Yuanjun fleet. The Yuan Army didn't even notice these small boats, which they saw as toys. At a critical juncture, Zhang Gui, who is the striker, led General Song Army to fight the same enemy and fight to the death. The Song Army first used three-bowbed crossbow guns to fire rockets at enemy ships. Dense rockets howled and flew towards the Yuan Army fleet. After hitting the target, an intensive explosion occurred and caused a fire. A large number of large battleships of the Yuan Army were quickly ignited and became a “volcano” floating on the water. The river was bursting with flames and heavy smoke billowing in, and it became a sea of flames! Afterwards, the Song Army bombarded the Yuan Army ships with muskets and artillery. There was a loud shelling of artillery on the face. A large number of battleships of the Yuan Army were destroyed by heavy artillery fire, and countless soldiers were killed. Many of the corpses of the Yuan Army were swept to the shore by waves and piled up like mountains! Meanwhile, the entire surface of the Yangtze River was filled with the corpses of the Yuan Army, and the river water was dyed dark red with blood! It became a real “mountain of dead blood”! Afterwards, the Song Army cut off an interception cable set up by the Yuan Army in the middle of the river with an axe prepared in advance. Thus, the Song Army's fleet broke through numerous blockades by the Yuan Army, moved for more than 120 miles, and finally arrived at Xiangyang Wharf on May 25, 1271 AD! This is the first time in the five years since Xiangyang was trapped that it has received reinforcements from outside. It has greatly boosted the morale of the military and civilians of Xiang and Fan. The supplies they carried mitigated the dangerous situation for the military and civilians in the city. All the generals supporting the Song Army used their lives to complete an epic voyage that was impossible for both warring parties to complete!
However, during the fierce battle, Zhang Shun, the captain of the fleet, unfortunately got four shots and six arrows in the battle, and was martyred in the country! A few days later, the military and civilians in Xiangyang found his body. Zhang Shun, who died in battle, was still wearing armor, holding a long bow, and maintained a fighting attitude. In memory of Zhang Shun, the military and civilians of Yangyang buried him and set up a temple to sacrifice him. Meanwhile, the other general, Zhang Gui, was besieged by the Yuan Army during the later battle to open the water traffic line between Xiangyang and Yingzhou due to the betrayal of the traitor, Fan Wenhu, a leader in Yingzhou. He was shot more than 10 times in the back of the battle and was seriously wounded and taken prisoner. He was brutally murdered after harshly refusing to persuade the Yuan Army to surrender, and martyred the country! The Yuan Army returned Zhang Gui's body to Xiangyang City. The military and civilians in Xiangyang will co-bury him with Zhang Shun and set up a temple for sacrifice. At this point, the front lines of Xiang and Fan cut ties with the outside world forever until the fall of Xiangyang in February 1273.
The voyage of Zhang Shun and Zhang Gui to aid Xiangyang in May 1271 AD was the only successful rescue operation from the outside world during the entire Battle of Xiangfan. Southern Song navy generals, led by Zhang Shun and Zhang Gui, used their blood and lives to break through the blockade of hundreds of thousands of yuan and complete this epic voyage. Despite the corruption and incompetence of the ruling group, the battle eventually ended with the fall of Xiangfan. However, the great patriotism shown by all the patriotic military and civilians of Southern Song during the campaign will go down in history and will always be worthy of being celebrated by future generations...
