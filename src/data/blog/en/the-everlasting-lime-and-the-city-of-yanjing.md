 Everyone says that it was the first Great Wall of China built by Emperor Qin Shi Huang. Actually, he didn't build the Great Wall at the beginning. Long before Qin Shi Huang, during the Spring and Autumn Warring States, there was King Yan. He had a small land, few soldiers, and weak strength, and was in danger of being eaten by neighboring countries at any time. In order to preserve the land, King Yan requisitioned people and built a tall wall on the top of the mountain on the border of his land to prevent foreign invaders.
Because there was no lime at the time, the city walls, stones, and bricks he built were all covered with clay. In order to save time and repair the city walls as soon as possible, he ordered that work not be stopped during the winter. It was cold and the mud had to use hot water, so the farmers carried the large iron pot to the construction site, supported it with three stones, and added firewood to boil water. Over time, the iron pot was burnt down with a big hole, and all the water in the pot leaked out; the fire under the pot was extinguished. However, the people also discovered by accident that the water was sprinkled on the stone of the pot, and when the hot stone met the water, it blew up and blew up a lot of white noodles. The peasants looked and thought, which was very strange. Some people mixed this white surface with water and felt that it was more moisturized and sticky than mud, so they applied it to the crevices of stones and bricks.
The next day, the peasants discovered that the stone and brick gaps covered with white surface were much stronger than those covered with clay. The people of Yan were inspired. Since then, they have burned lime to clean the gaps in the city walls.
Later, Qin Shi Huang unified China, and in order to preserve his throne, he also built the Great Wall of China following Yan Wang's method. When construction began, he issued an order to let the people of Yan Guodian take over the work of burning lime. As a result, all of the ashes used to build the Great Wall at that time were burnt by people from Yan Guo. Wherever the Great Wall was built, ash was burned on the hillside, and the quality of the burnt ash was very good. Later generations called it Ten Thousand Years Ash, which means it will not deteriorate for ten thousand years.
After the Great Wall was repaired, the other villagers went back to various places. Because of the success of the people of Yan, Qin Shi Huang also withdrew gold and silver and built a town for people from Yanyuan to live in. This town is now Beijing. Therefore, at that time, Beijing was called Yanjing, and the mountains where the people of Yan used stones to burn ash were collectively known as the Yanshan Mountains.
