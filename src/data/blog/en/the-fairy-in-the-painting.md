 Gao Mingfeng made a fortune today because he bought an antique from a stall. It was an antique painting. Although the painting was a light landscape, and the notes in some places have already begun to fall off, with his experience in the antiques industry, it can be seen at a glance that the history of this painting can be traced back at least to the Song Dynasty, so whether it's from a famous artist or not, it's an absolute antique, not to mention that he only spent 50 yuan!
As soon as he got home, he couldn't wait to open the ancient paintings and enjoy them carefully.
The painting depicts several cascading mountains. Nearby, a waterfall empties from the middle of the mountainside. The bottom of the mountain is a small river, and the riverside is adorned with a hut. The mood of the whole picture was pretty good, but there were no signatures or prints. This was a bit surprising to him, because every Chinese landscape painting leaves a special mark of its own, unless it's a work that hasn't been finished yet!
Gao Mingfeng was moved. Unfortunately, his research on painting is limited. Another day, I'll have to find an expert in this area to check it out. Maybe it's still a manuscript by some master. Haha, then I posted it!
The next day, early in the morning, after Gao Mingfeng got up and finished washing, he couldn't help but open the painting again, but in his mind, he was wondering which one of the people he knew was proficient in painting.
Suddenly, his whole eyes stopped at the painting, then he quickly rubbed his eyes, and after proving that he wasn't dazzled, blurted out: “MY GOD!”
It turned out that yesterday was just an ordinary landscape painting. There was one more person, and it was a girl, standing on the side of the hut, and a few blue threads floated backwards, just like they had just walked out of the hut!
How is that possible? A girl walking out of the house in the picture?
Gao Mingfeng threw his head down hard. Maybe the little ghost next door is making a fool out. He usually loves to paint all over the place. He thinks he is a big painter, so that no one upstairs or downstairs doesn't have a headache. It's just -- if that were true, the painting would be worth nothing!
“Are you saying this girl wasn't in the picture? It only appeared a day after you bought it back?” Gao Mingfeng's old friend couldn't help laughing, “Is your kid watching too many mythical movies? As a half-professional painter, I can definitely tell you that this is a complete landscape painting with no signs of new additions!”
Gao Mingfeng didn't care about the old friend's ridicule. That girl definitely only appeared in the painting the next day. There is no doubt about this. Her old friend's profession is also unquestionable, and he wouldn't have come to him. What shocked him was that his old friend actually said that no one had added any traces, that is to say that the kid next door is not making a ghost, but -- what is the point?
Is it really a fairy painting? or--
However, things didn't end there; the picture changes every day.
On the third day, the girls looked down and seemed to be looking for something.
On the fourth day, the girl bent down and picked up an item on her right hand.
On the fifth day, the girls looked up at the sky, as if thinking about something.
On the sixth day, the girls turned backwards.
On the 7th day, the girl began to retreat, and the creek was right behind her.
On the eighth day, the girl had already retreated to the river and flooded her calves.
On the ninth day,... Gao Mingfeng began to pay attention to the girl in the painting. Every morning after waking up, he unwittingly opened the picture to see what had changed. Something seemed to happen in the painting these days. That girl... Oops, Gao Mingfeng immediately climbed up from the bed. When he opened the painting, the girl actually disappeared, as if she had never appeared on the picture!
The first reaction in Gao Mingfeng's mind was that something happened to the girl. “What to do, what to do...” He actually had no idea what had happened, and even he himself couldn't explain why he was nervous about the person in the picture.
Over the past few days, he thought about a lot of reasonable explanations for this painting, and finally came to the conclusion that this picture is a magic painting. The girl in the picture is a fairy, but he just doesn't know why she was imprisoned in the painting. He also found many ways to save the girl, but unfortunately it was all in vain. But now, the girl seemed to be in trouble; she was in a hurry like an ant on a hot pot.
After a few more days, Gao Mingfeng almost never slept at night, and people became exhausted, yet the picture still didn't change much, as if nothing had happened.
Why is this happening? He just broke his head and couldn't figure out what actually happened. In the end, he couldn't bear the fatigue and fell asleep on the table...
Gao Mingfeng had a dream where he was in that painting, but there was nothing in it; in the end, he got lost in the painting and couldn't come out.
Gao Mingfeng stood up all of a sudden and found that his whole body was soaked, then he screamed. It turned out that the sweat he had just gotten wet from the painting on the table. Now that the ink has melted, the picture is already dark, and the painting is too dark to be painted.
Gao Mingfeng's hand has turned into a painting on a scrap of paper. He couldn't say anything for half a minute. Now he doesn't even have any paintings. Everything, such as landscapes, huts, girls, etc., is mixed into a cloud of ink!
After a period of silence, Gao Mingfeng put the painting aside and told himself that it was just a dream. No girl had ever seen any painting, but had just wasted ten days.
After Gao Mingfeng cleaned up the house, he discovered that the house turned out to be so messy. It actually took more than two hours to simply sort it out, and there were two big bags of garbage alone. He was carrying two large bags of garbage ready to be taken out and thrown into the trash bin. As soon as he opened the door, what caught his eye was not an aisle he saw on a weekday, but a small river. The river breeze blows, but it made him shiver.
Gao Ming saw that he hurriedly threw up the garbage bag and jumped out of the house. Yes, it was a small river, and there was a sound of water flowing down at the end of the river.
“A waterfall?!”
Gao Mingfeng subconsciously retreated but bumped into a person.
“Ahhh!”
It was a girl's voice. She was covering her feet at this point, and her tears were almost falling down. Gao Mingfeng suddenly felt that she was a bit familiar, and suddenly recalled that it was the girl in the painting. Is it true that she is already in the painting? But why didn't that girl do anything?
“It's rude to bump into someone who doesn't even say 'I'm sorry'!” The girl was slightly angry, “If you know for a long time, it won't save you from a sea of suffering!”
“Save me... from the sea of bitterness?!” Gao Mingfeng didn't know if he should trust her, because from the beginning to the end, he thought she was in danger and was trying to find a way to save her, but now the opposite is true.
The girl winked at him and looked a bit mysterious, “Guess where you are now?”
Gao Mingfeng originally wanted to say “in the picture,” but he also felt that everything was so unreasonable. He couldn't think of any other answers for a while, and was dumbfounded.
The girl smiled softly: “I think you should know that now you are in another world!”
Gao Mingfeng smiled bitterly and couldn't find a reason to refute it.
“So do you know the difference between reality and dreams?” The girls gave him no room to think, “What is reality? What is a dream again?”
Reality and dreams themselves are relatively abstract concepts. Gao Mingfeng has always had a headache about this kind of problem, but now he has to keep his head up and say, “Wouldn't you just tell me that I'm in a dream now?”
“Quite the opposite!” The girl shook her head and said, “You are now in reality, or in another dream!”
“Wait!” Gao Mingfeng was confused, “What is another dream?”
“Stupid! Another dream means that the place where you used to be is also a dream!”
“Impossible! Why is it a dream where I used to be?” Gao Mingfeng suddenly shouted, “Now is my dream. I must be dreaming, dreaming that I am in a painting!”
“I know it would be difficult for you to accept this reality all of a sudden!” The girl said sympathetically.
Gao Mingfeng slowly calmed down. At this time, running away was not a solution to the problem; the only way out was to face reality and figure out what happened as soon as possible.
“What kind of place is this? What kind of person are you?”
As soon as the girl was playing in the water by the river heard it, she immediately stopped, “Didn't I say that? This is another 'dream', or 'wonderland' is fine, after all, it's a constructed world!”
“Constructed world?!”
“That's right! The world was made to be! When the writer picks up a pen to write an article, they construct a 'world of words'; when the painter draws beautiful pictures on white paper, they also construct a 'world of pictures'... Everything in this world is built step by step!” The girl explained in as simple a language as possible, “As for me, I'm a traveler who has traveled all over the world!”
Gao Mingfeng was sitting on the ground with one butt in his original imagination. That is, the girl in the painting was a “fairy,” and who knows the result was that the girl told him a fact that was difficult for him to accept — it turns out that the world he has always relied on to live in is actually virtual, constructed by people like writers and painters, so what is he himself? Also virtual? Or was it constructed by so-called humans? If everything is virtual, where did the girl in the picture come from? ...
Too many questions flooded into the brain, causing Gao Mingfeng, who was already not good at thinking, to burst his head. “No way, how can people come out virtually? But how can all this be explained?”
“Ahhh! ——”
Gao Mingfeng finally couldn't help but shout in his heart's depression and jumped up.
“BANG!”
Gao Mingfeng fell to the ground and then jumped again. He didn't know when he would return to his house, but just knocked his chair over to the ground.
Suddenly a painting fell from the sky and fell on his face. It was that landscape painting. The painting was still in perfect condition, with no stains, and no girls. “What happened?!”
Gao Mingfeng opened the painting, closed it, and opened it again. The painting was just a painting. Nothing special. Is he really dreaming? Gao Mingfeng ran outside the door again. There was no creek, but a familiar aisle.
Isn't it a dream?! Or was it just a dream?
Gao Mingfeng went back to the house and was dazed at the painting. All of this was incredible; even if it was a dream, it was a bit real!
