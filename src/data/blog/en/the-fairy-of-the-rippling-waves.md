 On the banks of the Jiulong River in the southwest of Zhangzhou City, a round mountain stands high above ground. There are twelve sides of Maruyama when viewed from the front and back, and the scenery is not the same. As the saying goes, “Maruyama has twelve faces, and every face has treasure.” Speaking of the northeastern foothills of Maruyama here, there is a slope with lush woods, and is surrounded in the shape of a biwa. The place is called Biwazaka. A stream of clear spring water gushes out of Sakagami, nourishing the Yamashita countryside. This is a famous production area for middle-aged daffodils. However, do you know the origin of daffodils? It's kind of amazing to say.
Legend has it that during the Jing Tai years of the Ming dynasty, there was a man named Zhang Guanghui. He was an upright person and worked as a minor official in Ji County, Henan Province. During his career as Shi Huan, he saw through the darkness and corruption of the government, so he quit his job and went back to his hometown. His hometown is Yuanshan Biwazakashita, Funan Township, Zhangzhou. On this day, he took a boat past the vast Dongting Lake.
I was leisurely leaning on the side of the boat, enjoying the colors of the lake and mountains, looking at the clear blue sky, the green lake, and the green mountains. I couldn't help but feel refreshed. I forgot all the pampering, and unwittingly immersed myself in a quiet dream. Now, in a trance, he saw a beautiful fairy, wearing a golden feather coat, white jade dress and socks, standing on an ivory bow with a green silk sail behind him and flying through the waves. He stood up in surprise, rubbed his eyes, and watched the spectacle attentively. In an instant, the fairy and fairy boat disappeared. I saw a flower floating in the sparkling silver lake. An arrow stem, a white umbrella-shaped inflorescence, and about a dozen flower buds were still unbloomed. Only a small white flower, snow-white and crystal petals, formed into a six-sided jade plate. The flower heart held up a golden wine glass. It was very cute.
Zhang Guanghui loved this unknown flower so much that she reached out and wanted to pick it up from the water. Who knew this fairy flower was very well-behaved. She leisurely left the boat side and floated to the center of the lake far away. Zhang Guanghui was very sad and looked at the lake with dismay. At this point, this playful fairy flower quietly came close to the side of the boat, but it always kept a distance not far or close, and even swam along with the boat. Zhang Guanghui wanted to pick her up a few times, but she always couldn't get her hands. She was very anxious, yet there was nothing she could do. Slowly, his mood calmed down, and he blamed himself to think: Everything has its own goals. Since she is a fairy in the water, you can't treat her with an indecent attitude, 1
They must be greeted in a polite manner, huh! As a result, he shook his gun in awe, respectfully placed three incense sticks, and silently prayed to the Water Fairy:
The color fragrance of Limbo Fairy Country,
Whereabouts on the lake do you want to go?
Wouldn't you like to go back south with me,
Biwa Sakashita is Senkyo!
