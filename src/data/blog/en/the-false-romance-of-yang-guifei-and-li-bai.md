Yang Guifei, real name Yang Yuhuan (719-756 AD), was a Tang Dynasty court musician and dancer. Her musical talent was rare among successive concubines. She is naturally beautiful. She can be described as the most beautiful woman in Datang. She has the appearance of an “immortal flower” and has a natural appearance. Many future generations insisted that these two people be brought together in an ambiguous manner to slander this pair of talented people who have worked for a thousand years.
In You Xiaogang's new drama “The Secret History of Yang Guifei,” which is currently being broadcast on Hunan TV, “The Secret History of Yang Guifei,” this “Li Yang Love” is very emotional as one of the three major emotional threads in the drama. Yang Guifei's confusing relationship with Li Bai in the drama has become a major highlight and is also highly controversial. So what is the historical relationship between Yang Guifei and Li Bai? Looking at some historical materials, the author found that the age difference between the two was 18 years, which is similar to the age of the actors in the TV series. Therefore, it is not too exaggerated that the screenwriter of this secret history had a romantic history. So are the two really ambiguous in history, and have they had a history of love?
According to research, “Li Yang Love” is pure fiction; Li Bai and Yang Guifei never had a love dispute.
