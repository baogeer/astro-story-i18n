More than 100 meters south of Shimen Guanxia, there is Fukuo-ji Temple, a famous temple in western Yunnan. Surrounded by blue water, covered with walnut groves and flowers, it is as quiet as a hideaway.
Fukoku-ji Temple has an unusual history.
Legend has it that the late Ming and early Qing dynasties were full of risks, and mountains and rivers were shattered. Seeing that the rivers and mountains were about to change hands. Xu Wei of western Yunnan province is not a common man; he is worried about the country and the people; he is in turmoil. Mindful of the pain of beginning and death, he resolutely relinquished the crown and led his family away from the border crossing. Determined to emulate successive loyal ministers, return to their hometown, and live the rest of their lives.
As he passed through the border of Yangyi County, there was a burst of cranes and monkeys chirping from Shimen Mountain. I've already learned that in the foothills of the western foothills of Cangshan, “Shimen's Beauty in Southern Yunnan”, a generation of sassy people, and admirers are flowing along the way. Although Xu Wei was full of westerly breezes and his heart was cold, he had in mind the Daming River and mountains, one hill, one ravine, one grass, and one tree, so he led his family to take a look at it based on the feeling of the river and mountains. The light and color of Shimen Guan Mountain blended with the greenery of the surrounding smoky village. He and his family were amazed by the beautiful scenery in front of them. With such a beautiful mountain and water show, as beautiful as Tianfu in the world, he couldn't help but say to himself: Isn't this the peach blossom source drawn by Tao Yuanming? In terms of the number of views, I actually forgot to go back. He thought over and over: the world is impermanent, and there is a risk of being harmed by the Qing government. Since the general of the world is not the monarch of the past, and the minister is not the minister of the past, he immediately moved away from the earthly world to the idea of being a monk, and he can still stand still and wait for an opportunity to restore the Daming River and mountains. He shared his feelings with his family, and the government agreed to their ambition.
Xu Wei told the villagers near Shimenguan about the idea of rebuilding the temple building, and the villagers unanimously supported it. He then traveled east and west with his son, worked tirelessly to raise public morals in the surrounding villages, cut down wood and stone, buy bricks and tiles, and develop civil engineering in Shimen. After a long and busy winter and spring, a new temple stands at the stone gate of bamboo shoots and shuts down.
Xu Weicheng changed the name of the original “Yakushi-ji Temple” to “Fukukoku-ji Temple.” Speaking of Kai Kwang, Xu wrote two couplets and wrote a book with two couplets. One copy is “Father and son work together to bless the country, mentors and apprentices enjoy cigarettes for two generations”; the other is “Tiankai Shimen: Strange Flowers Through the Ages, and Terayun Fukukoku Forever Forever”. The first one expresses the feelings of Xu's father and son converting to Buddhism; the next one is beautiful and profound: Shangzhi refers to the magnificent “Tiankai Stone Gate” as a “strange flower from the ages,” and is in the same vein as Xu Xiake's different song of “revealing two pieces of green hibiscus.” Tiankai Shimen has a timeless charm. Of course, it is a “rare flower, green hibiscus,” which illuminates the ages and the present. The lower branch “Terayumofuku Province for a long time” suggests that a generation of late Ming patriots “had a desire to recover from the waves and return to the river”. We are also looking forward to the “eternal life” of the Daming River and Mountain. A strong off-the-string sound echoes through the beautiful waters of ancient Mt. Ryoyama. A generation of people in the late Ming dynasty shared their patriotism. Since then, the owner of Kecheng Temple has passed through the border, fell in love with the mountains, forests, and springs, and kept quiet in the turbulent world. Every day, it's either blue lights and yellow rolls, praying to the Buddha, or reading, tasting, and enjoying the past and present.
Although the Ming dynasty eventually fell to ashes, the good story of Xu's merits and demeanor for his father and son at the Fukukoku-ji Temple has been passed down from generation to generation.
Fukukoku-ji Temple has experienced many vicissitudes, monks come and go, and incense continues to burn. Although it is not the crown of the Yunyun Buddha's Jingzong Dojo, the temple has soared to become a famous temple in western Yunnan due to its name of the mountain display, Yamazuku-ji Temple, and its long history.
Postscript: Regarding the time when Fukuo Temple was founded, according to the “Night Tales of Huaicheng” “Baoding Emperor Duan Zhengming”, Emperor Duan Zhengming of Dali “ordered the construction of Buddha Temple in Diancanghou Mountain (that is, Fukuo Temple in Shimen, the foothills of Mount Cangshan). In one year, the temple gave way to the Takashi clan, fell to the commandment of Muweiji Temple, and joined Butkoku-ji in March to host for a generation. All five queens were ordained and lived at Gomeigetsu-an at Bulguku-ji Temple.” Description Fukukoku-ji Temple was founded in the Song Dynasty. It is only different from the original name Bulguku-ji Temple. “Xu Xia's Travels” claims that the temple was built by the monk Shi Guang at the beginning of the Ming Wanli era, and is called Yakushi Temple. However, according to popular legend, the temple was built by the patriot Xu Wei in the late Ming and early Qing dynasties. Inferring from this, Fukukoku-ji Temple has experienced the fate of repeated construction and destruction in the midst of historical turbulence since it was founded in the Song Dynasty.
