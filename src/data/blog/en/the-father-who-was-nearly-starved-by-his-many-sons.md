 There is a saying, “Too many children are hungry, dad.” This allusion comes from the banks of the Jiulong River.
In his early years, there was an old man with the surname Xiao. He had four sons: the boss, the second, and the fourth were biological children. The senior was raised by a medical examiner. Old man Xiao worked hard to raise his four sons and married his daughter-in-law to become a family. Old Man Shaw gave each of them a share of the property. He gave the field to the boss; he gave a store to his sophomore; let the older three learn to slaughter and gave him a burden to sell meat; the senior went to school but wasn't qualified as a talent, and later became someone's treasurer. The old couple got old, so they let their four sons take turns managing the meal.
In July of this year, it just happened to be the boss's turn to take charge of the meal. On the 29th day of New Year's Day, the north wind howled, and the weather was so cold that people shivered. The old couple took a few bites of porridge from sunrise in the morning until sunset, and hadn't seen anyone deliver the food.
At this point, in the east side of the West House, the family finished their firework. They ate New Year's Eve dinner, and the sound of firecrackers was constantly heard. The old two were cold and hungry; they huddled up on the couch and stared. The old woman said, “Old man, it looks like we're going to starve for the New Year today!” The old man said, “Old woman, I'm afraid you'll have to prepare to be hungry!” (The dialect “hungry” is synonymous with “fairy.” (“Positive” means New Year's)
Have their sons forgotten to take care of the meal? I haven't forgotten!
The boss went to the field to pick up the vegetables in the morning. By the time he came home with a full load of cauliflower, garlic, celery, and mustard, it was over. The wife brought water for her face, wiped her face, and washed her feet. Only then did she remember that it was time to deliver food to her dad, and asked, “Mom, did you bring a few bowls of hot and rotten goodies to give to your parents?” As soon as the boss's daughter-in-law heard it, she was unhappy and said, “Today is New Year's Eve.
