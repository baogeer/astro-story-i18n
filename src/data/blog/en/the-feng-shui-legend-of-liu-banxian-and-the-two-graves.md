 Dad is about to stop. Li Chong first spent a lot of money to invite Liu Banxian, a famous Feng Shui master from the hometown.
He led Liu Banxian around every corner of the village. After turning, Liu Banxian said to him, “The northern slope of Nanling is bordered by Julong Mountain in the south and Wenliang River in the north. It should be the best Feng Shui treasure in the village. After your father passes away, you can bury him there to protect your prosperity and prosperity for your children and grandchildren in the future.”
Li Chongxian was stubborn when he was a child. Although he had a flexible mind, he didn't like to study, and dropped out of school before graduating from elementary school. In the early 80s of the last century, when the village split up his fields, he stopped cultivating land and went into the city to start a small business. He is quite capable. From the flour mill to the winery, he drove a truck to pour wheat and earned two cents per catty. In more than a year, he became the first 10,000 yuan household in the village. At that time, there were only a few 10,000 yuan households in the area, and everyone in the village was extremely envious of him! After seizing his first pot of money, he gathered a group of people in the village, formed a construction team, and contracted work abroad. After more than 10 years of hard work, he became a millionaire and built the first small mansion in the village.
Many people in the village said that Li Chong was able to become a top person mainly because his father buried a good place.
Hearing this, Li Chongxian felt so happy.
Li Weixian is Li Chongxian's cousin. He has always been at the top of his class from elementary school to high school. When he graduated from high school, during the Cultural Revolution, he was unable to take the college entrance examination and had to go home to farm. Later, there was a shortage of one teacher in the village, so the village made him work as a private teacher. At that time, the salary of private teachers was very low. It was only about ten yuan in January. Li Weixian had a tight family life and always lived in the thatched house his father built. Li Chongxian said to him, “Let's go to our construction team and take care of the workers. The work is not tiring; the salary is still high. Li Wei first said, “I'm gone, what should I do about this bunch of kids in the village, not going. Li Chongxian bluntly scolded him in his heart. The eldest daughter wanted to eat — desperate!
Li Weixian's father was also furious. Li Chongxian said to Li Weixian, “Brother, you also have to look for a good Feng Shui sir, so that my uncle can bury him in a good place and change your destiny.” Li Weixian was very grumpy and said, “Where loess doesn't bury people, what Feng Shui is not Feng Shui, I don't believe that.” Li Chong first touched the ashes on his nose. He looked very embarrassed and stopped speaking.
Li Weixian has a responsible field. The soil is very poor. The bottom is a large slate. In the dry years, the crops they grow often dry and die. In the years when the rain floods, the crops they grow often drown. There is no harvest on this plot of land almost every year.
Li Wei saw this plot of land and it was of no use to keep it, so he buried his father there. On the street, many elderly people in the village had a lot of discussions about him. Li Chong first said in front of these people that Li Weixian buried his father in that haunted place, and later he would look good.
Li Chong had money first, and his family had excellent conditions. He spread his money to his son and neglected to discipline him. Over time, his son developed an arrogant personality. At school, fights are frequent, and absenteeism has become commonplace. Of course, it's a complete mess when it comes to studying, and I haven't been able to attend since I graduated from middle school. Li Chong first asked him to find a company. He often fooled around in society, didn't go to work, and was soon expelled from the company. Li Chong first wanted to discipline his son, but his son couldn't listen.
Later, Li Chongxian's son participated in a group fight and killed one person. Li Chong first spent a lot of money to “catch people,” and never got his son out. In the end, his son was sentenced to life imprisonment.
Li Chong first suspected something was wrong with his father's graveyard, so he invited Liu Banxian again. After visiting the graveyard, Liu Banxian said, “It's broken. The Panshan Highway has been built here, breaking the Feng Shui here.”
Liu Banxian found another place with good Feng Shui. Li Chong first had his father's ashes removed from his tomb and buried there.
After two years, Li Chongxian's son's sentence was reduced from life imprisonment to 20 years in prison. After two more years, my son's prison sentence was reduced to 18 years. Li Chong first saw hope and thought to himself that it was right to move back to the graveyard. After a few more days, my son's sentence was commuted to life imprisonment due to the prison escape incident. Hearing this news, Li Chongxian immediately fainted...
The country adjusted its policy, and Li Wei first switched from a private teacher to a public teacher. His salary was also like sesame blossoms — the days are high, and the days are like sugar cane climbing a ladder — step by step.
He deserved to be an educator. He was well-educated. At school, my son always had the lead in terms of performance. After graduating from high school, he became the first person to be admitted to Tsinghua University in the whole county. At this point, everyone in the village said that there was blue smoke on Li Weixian's ancestral tomb.
One day, Li Chong first went out. When he met Liu Banxian on the way, he asked, “I took you to see the burial place where Li Weixian's father was buried before. You said that the land was poor. It was a depression. Later generations were burdened and poor for the rest of their lives. However, Li Weixian's days became more and more prosperous, and his son also fought hard and was admitted to Tsinghua University, causing a stir throughout the county. Look at me again... Why the hell is that?”
Liu Banxian extended his right hand and spread his palm along the way and said, “If you want to know the mystery, take 200 yuan first.”
