At the time of the game, whoever spends well will get this money. However, for two years, he was relieved by his excuse of “not outstanding, difficult to award”. In the third year, he went up again. The winner of the flower competition received a prize of 10,000 yuan, a gold plaque, and was named “King Peony.”
On the day of the competition, people flocked to the field from all sides. The contestants held flower pots one by one; the flower pots were all covered with flower covers. At the beginning of the competition, people opened the flower covers one by one for everyone to enjoy. When a young man named Zhao Rui opened his flower cover; however, seeing a peony with lush foliage, bright red and transparent white, “brushing” people's eyes attracted the past. This peony, called Jin Dan, shines brightly in the sunshine, and exudes a fragrance along with the spring style. In an instant, the cheers were blazing. The prefectural government saw this scene and thought to himself: This time the prize money is about to fly away. He stood there motionless, helpless, and watched with a blink of an eye that Zhao Rui had taken the silver and gold plaques.
