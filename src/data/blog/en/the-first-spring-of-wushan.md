 Long ago, Hangzhou didn't have a single well... On that day's light, the rain in this area was very uniform, and there was no shortage of water for every household.
Unexpectedly, one year later, the weather suddenly changed: the sky was clear and there were no clouds, and not a drop of rain fell for several months in a row. The water in the West Lake is dry, and the fields are cracked, and it is difficult even for people to find drinking water. The government was afraid that the people would cause trouble, so they invited many monks and Taoists to set up an altar and practice their affairs; they also insisted that everyone prostrate and kneel and pray.
At that time, an old man was also brought in; however, he was reluctant to kneel down. The official was very angry when they saw him, arrested him, charged him with “disobeying the government,” and wanted to kill him on the head. The old man didn't panic at all. Instead, he laughed at the sky and said:
“I'm 80 years old; it's not worth dying! Unfortunately, people in Hangzhou won't have water to eat; even you will die of thirst! If you give me three days, I'll definitely be able to find a source of water.”
When the official heard the old man say this, he thought to himself: Is there really such a thing! Let him look for it: if you find it, it's all over; if you can't find it, it's not too late to kill him again. I agreed.
The old man came home and summoned his 50-year-old son and said, “Hurry up and tie up a bamboo sedan for me to sit on!”
“Dad, dad, what do you do in a car?”
“I'm old and can't move. I'm sitting in a bamboo car. I want to go outside to find water sources!”
My son tied up the bamboo car. The old man walked into the vegetable garden and summoned his 20-year-old grandson and said, “Hurry up and get two bamboo bars to carry me away!”
“Grandpa, grandpa, where are you going to carry it?”
“Stand tall, look far, you and your dad, lift me up to the city wall and go around!”
My grandson brought a bamboo stick.
The children and grandchildren carried the old man and circled around the Hangzhou city wall. Three laps in one day and nine laps in three days. Round and round, the bamboo car went around for three days; look, look, the old man watched nine laps. He discovered that at the foot of Chenghuang Mountain, there was a wave of smoke that wasn't like smoke or fog. It kept rolling upward and rising until it formed a white, sparkling cloud in the sky, and said to his children and grandchildren:
“Well, the place where the smoke comes from under the white clouds is the dragon's vein. There's a dragon breathing underground.”
The old man brought in many people to dig a well at the foot of Chenghuang Mountain. Dig, dig, dig three feet deep, yet there isn't a drop of water at the bottom of the well! If you are an official, take a look at what is being done in the well. They will kill the old man without saying anything!
The children and grandchildren cried a lot and buried the old man's body. The grandson, with tears in his eyes, helped his father, and still climbed up the city wall and circled. It took another three days to go round and round; look, look, watch nine more laps. They saw that the smoke was thicker in the area where they had dug a well at the foot of Chenghuang Mountain, and the white, sparkling clouds in the sky were even bigger. Grandpa pointed to the clouds and said to his son:
“Well, the place with smoke under the white clouds is really a dragon's blood. Your grandpa was right in looking for a place!”
They also brought in many people and continued to dig down the place where they had dug a well at the foot of Chenghuang Mountain. Dig, dig, dig three feet deep, but there's still not a drop of water under the well! When the government learned about this, they didn't ask any questions and killed the old man's son.
The grandson cried and buried Grandpa's body next to Grandpa's grave. He was left alone, alone and desolate. He climbed up the city wall and circled like Grandpa and Grandpa. It took another three days to go round and round; look, look, watch nine more laps. He saw that the smoke from the ground was getting thicker, and the white, sparkling clouds in the sky were getting bigger and bigger. The grandson pointed to the clouds and said to himself, “The place with smoke under the white clouds must have been dragon veins. Grandpa and Grandpa, you have died so wrongly!”
He went to find many more people. He is still digging down one after another in the old place where he had dug a well at the foot of Chenghuang Mountain. Dig it up, dig it down three feet and three feet deep, and the bottom is full of bulging rock, and you can't dig any more.
My grandson was at the bottom of a jiuzhang and nine foot deep well. Touching the east was dry, touching the west was dry. Every time he touched a large, round rock, it was like a dragon's eye. He waved his heart and shouted:
“Dragon, dragon, why don't you keep your eyes open! There is no rain in the sky, no water is released underground. How do you ask everyone to live their lives? I'm fighting with you today!” It just hit a round rock. I only heard a loud “thunderclap”. The rock cracked through a slit, and water gushed out of the gap. After a while, it filled the nine- and nine-foot-deep well to the brim.
The clean well water brought the grandson up to the wellhead; however, he died. The common people cried and buried their grandson's dead next to his grandpa's and grandpa's graves.
Since having this well, people have not worried about not having enough water to eat. Later, people continued to dig wells everywhere. Gradually, more and more water wells were being dug in Hangzhou. However, none of the wells that were dug later were larger or deeper than the one dug at the earliest. Therefore, people call it “Wushan First Spring,” and they also call the alley at the foot of Chenghuang Mountain “Dajing Alley.”
