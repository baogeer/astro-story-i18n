 A long time ago, at the foot of Gem Mountain by the West Lake, there was a small village. There is a young couple living in the village. The man's name is Liu Chun, and the woman's name is Hui Niang. The man is a farmer and woman. They are diligent and frugal, and have a very sweet life. The townspeople in the former village and back village all boasted that they were a good couple.
One morning, a morning glow appeared in the East, and the bright red sun grew. Liu Chun picked up her hoe and went down to work; Hui Niang also arranged the silk and sat on the plane to embroider. At this point, a gust of wind suddenly blew up, dark clouds rolling in the sky, and the sun, which had just risen, suddenly retracted.
Since then, the sun has stopped rising. There is no sun, it's dark and cold, the leaves aren't green, the flowers aren't red, and the crops don't grow. All the devils and ghosts take advantage of the darkness to commit crimes on earth. How did you live a day like this? Everyone is worried.
Where did the Sun go? Only my husband, who is 180 years old, knows. He said that there is a demon king under the East Sea. The demon king is leading many little demons. These demons and ghosts are most afraid of the sun; the sun must have been taken away by this demon king.
Liu Chun saw everyone living in the dark. He felt sad. He touched the dark every day, walked from his previous village to his back village, and went door to door to ask for warmth. He walked over to this house and said, “Liu Chun, without the sun, we're about to freeze to death!” He walked over to that house and said, “Liu Chun, without the sun, everyone would starve to death!” When Liu Chun heard these words, he felt as sad as being cut with a knife. When he got home, he said to Hui Niang, “There is no sun in the world. People are about to freeze to death and starve to death. I plan to get the sun back!” Hui-niang listened and thought about it and wanted to say, “If you want to go, go; I won't leave you. You don't have to worry about family matters; as long as you can get the sun back, everyone will have a good life!”
Hui Niang cut off a bunch of long hair from her head, made a pair of sandals in linen, and sewed a thick cotton jacket to bring to Liu Chun. She brought her husband to the door and saw a flash of gold in the sky. A golden phoenix flew in the distance and fell on Liu Chun's shoulder. Liu Chun caressed Jin Fenghuang and said, “Golden Phoenix, let's go find the Sun with me!” Golden Phoenix rolled her eyes and nodded. Liu Chun held Hui Niang again and said, “Huiniang, if I can't find the sun, I won't come back. Even if I die on my way, I'll be a bright star to guide the next generation of sun-seekers!”
Liu Chun left with Jin Fenghuang. Every day, Hui Niang touched the dark and climbed to the top of Gem Mountain to take a look. Hope, hope, I don't know how many days I've been looking forward to, but the world is still dark and dark, and there isn't a hint of sunshine. One night, Hui Niang suddenly saw a bright star flying up and hanging in the sky; after a while, Golden Phoenix flew back and stopped at her feet with her head hanging down. As soon as Hui Niang saw it, she knew that Liu Chun had died on the road. She felt sad for a while, and unwittingly passed out on the ground.
When Hui-niang woke up, the baby she was pregnant with had already been born. This baby grows up when it sees the wind. As soon as the wind blows, it can talk; when the second gust of wind blows, it runs away; when it blows three gusts of wind, it grows into a giant man eight feet tall! Hui Niang was very happy to see her. She named her child “Uncle Bao.”
Niang Hui brought Uncle Bao home. She looked forward to her. Thinking of her husband, she burst into tears. Uncle Bao saw him and asked, “Mom, why are you crying?” Hui-niang couldn't help but grieve, so she told him about her father's death in search of the Sun. When Uncle Bao heard it, he said, “Mom, let me go find the sun.” Miss Hui saw her son and couldn't bear to leave. But I also thought that there was no sun in the world; people were suffering and suffering, and they always had to fight for the first time. Thinking of this, I nodded in agreement.
Niang Hui also cut off a bunch of long hair from her head, made a pair of sandals with hemp silk, and sewed a thick padded jacket for Uncle Bao to wear. Bao Fu walked to the door, and the bright golden phoenix flew back and stopped on his shoulder. Niang Hui pointed to the bright star in the sky and said to Uncle Bao, “Oh, that star changed after your father died. As long as you walk in the direction it guided you, you won't take the wrong path.” Uncle Bao nodded, and Hui Niang pointed to Uncle Bao and said, “This Golden Phoenix once accompanied your grandpa to find the sun. You should go with it!” Uncle Bao nodded and said, “Mom, after I leave, no matter how long it's been, don't be sad! If you shed tears, my heart will tremble, and I won't be able to find the sun anymore!”
People in the former village and back village heard that Uncle Bao was looking for the sun, and they all rushed to see him off. Some delivered clothes, some delivered dry food, and kept sending Uncle Bao far and wide.
Uncle Bao took the Golden Phoenix and set out. Along the way, despite the darkness and cold, he moved vigorously towards the star of the East. Go and go. He turned and turned, over mountain after mountain, climbed 18 steep cliffs, and crossed 19 cliffs. The thorns ripped his padded jacket into cloth strips. The prickles cut a lot of blood on his body. The padded jacket was getting more and more broken, and the weather was getting colder and colder. One day, Uncle Bao walked into a village. People from the village met a distant visitor. They all rounded up and asked him, “Boy, where are you going!” Uncle Bao said, “I'm looking for the sun!” Everyone was very happy to hear that someone else was looking for the sun. They told him to be more careful along the way. People saw that the padded jacket on Uncle Bao's body was torn and couldn't stop the wind chill, so they each cut off a corner of their clothes and sewed a “hundred family clothes” for him. Uncle Bao felt warm from the bottom of his heart and felt warm from the bottom of his heart, and he was no longer afraid of the cold. He said goodbye to the townspeople and continued on his way.
Uncle Bao kept walking forward. Go and go. He swam river after river and beach after beach. One day, he came to the edge of a large river. You can't see the edge of this big river at a glance, even the eagle can't fly. The water in the river is swirling and rushing, and stones the size of a house can also be washed away! The brave Uncle Bao jumped into the river, clenched his teeth, held his breath, and swam hard to the other side. Xiaolang hit him without thinking, and the vortex rolled him around, but Uncle Bao still tried his best. Swim, swim, look like they're about to swim to the other side; suddenly a cold wind blew in. All the water in the river froze. Uncle Bao froze to live in the river, and the Golden Phoenix froze to death.
Uncle Bao wears a “hundred family clothes” on his body, so he can't freeze to death no matter how cold it is. Slowly, the heat on his body melted the ice around him. He first held Golden Phoenix tightly in his arms, then clenched his fist and smashed it hard on the ice. “Whistling,” the ice all over the river broke into pieces. The water at the bottom of the river rolled up ice cubes and rushed to Uncle Bao. Uncle Bao quickly jumped on a piece of ice, then jumped from this piece of ice to that ice, and finally jumped on the riverbank. The Golden Phoenix in Uncle Bao's arms received the energy from his body and survived.
Uncle Bao crossed the glacier and went to another village. People in the village surrounded Uncle Bao. Everyone was very happy to hear that he was looking for the sun; they also heard that he had experienced many difficulties along the way, and they respected him even more. An old man with a white beard said, “Kid, since we have lost the sun, our days have been difficult day by day; there really isn't anything I can give you. Let's each get you a handful of dirt! This is dirt that our ancestors sweated for generations; it is useful for you to bring it along.” The old man brought a cloth bag. Everyone in the village grabbed a taxi and put it in their pocket, filled the bag, and gave it to Uncle Bao.
Uncle Bao picked up this bag of dirt and continued to look for a bright star in the East. Go, go. He went over 99 mountains, swam through 99 major rivers, and reached a fork in three. Uncle Bao didn't know which path to take. He was worried. An old wife came by the side of the road. The wife asked Uncle Bao, “Boy, where are you going!” Uncle Bao said, “I'm going to find the sun!” My grandmother said, “There's still a long way to go to find the sun! I suggest you go home early!” Uncle Bao said, “No matter how far the road is, no matter how difficult the road is, I must find the sun back. Never go home if you can't find the sun!” When his wife heard about his work, she pointed to the road on the right and said, “If you follow this path, you can find the sun. There is a village not far ahead, so go in and take a break before you leave.” When Uncle Bao was talking to his grandmother, Jin Fenghuang tried his best to push at her, hit her head with his wings, grab her face with his claws, and peck her in the eye with his mouth. Uncle Bao thought it was because he saw a living person, so he drove Jin Fenghuang away, thanked his grandmother, and walked down the road on the right.
Uncle Bao hit the road, and Xin Fenghuang flew in front of him again to stop him and not let him go. Uncle Bao chased it away again and walked forward in stride. The road is getting flatter, with no wind or sand, no thorns, no steep walls, and no cliffs. Uncle Bao thought it was a bit strange. After walking for a short while, he walked into a village. The houses in this village are all very tall, the men are obese, and the women are all very slender. People in the village heard that he was looking for the sun. They were all happy to surround him and dance and laugh. They all gave him a thumbs up, boasted that he was a hero, and called him a good man. At one point, the owner brought wine, and the Western family brought food. The man pulled and the woman made a fuss, and they asked Uncle Bao to eat and drink. Uncle Bao was puzzled: along the way, the villages he visited were all dilapidated, and the townspeople he saw were all frozen and hungry. Why are the people here doing so well? He was freaking out while holding a wine bowl. He couldn't prevent the Golden Phoenix from flying above his head, making a “snap” sound, and threw a straw shoe into his wine bowl. Straws fell into the wine bowl and immediately ignited a fire “whistling”. Uncle Bao took a close look. These sandals were the same ones he wore on his feet, and they were also made of hair and linen. He immediately recognized that these were the sandals his father wore when looking for the sun. Uncle Bao understood all of a sudden. He knocked the wine bowl to the ground and yelled. Suddenly, the village disappeared, and the people in the village disappeared. I only saw so many blinking demons, and all of a sudden they escaped without a trace!
Uncle Bao was frightened by the monster, so he came back, walked to the three-way fork, and walked the road on the left.
Without killing Uncle Bao, the devil turned into many mountains and blocked his way. Uncle Bao ignored these and overturned them all. The devil has turned into countless rivers, and Uncle Bao has swam past them all. The devil tried to freeze Uncle Bao to death in the glacier, but it didn't work; they tried to trick him into Ecstasy Village to kill him, but it didn't work. They were so afraid that a gust of wind blew. They swarmed down Gem Mountain and entangled Hui-niang, tricking that Uncle Bao had fallen to death on a cliff; they also said that Uncle Bao had drowned in a big river. They used all methods to make Hui-niang sad and shed tears, so that Uncle Bao had no strength to search for the sun again. However, Hui-niang didn't listen. She clenched her teeth tightly and didn't let a single drop of tears fall from her own eyes.
Since Uncle Bao left, Niang Hui always hoped that her son would find the sun and come back soon. Every day, she looks east from the top of the mountain with her townspeople. Every time, she has to move a big rock to rest her feet so that she can stand a little higher and see a little further. I don't know how many months and years have passed. I don't know how many months, and I don't know how many years have passed. The big stone at the foot of Hui Niang has already piled up into a tall stone platform, yet the sky is still dark and dark.
Uncle Bao kept moving forward. He couldn't remember exactly how many mountains he had climbed; how many rivers he had crossed, he couldn't count. One day, he climbed over a half-day high mountain and heard “whoop, whoop!” in the distance The sound turned out to be that he had already arrived at the East Sea. When Uncle Bao approached the seaside, he saw a vast ocean, endless, and so big. Who knows where the sun is? How else can I get through it? Uncle Bao stood by the beach and thought, and suddenly remembered that the bag of dirt that the townspeople had given him along the way had arrived. So he unwrapped the bag and scattered the dirt into the sea. As a gust of wind blew through, many large and small islands immediately appeared in the sea. Uncle Bao was extremely happy. He jumped into the sea, swam island after island, and swam to the middle of the ocean. Swim, wander, seek. He swam to a place far, far away, to find the deepest seabed, and saw a big rock cave — the sun was hidden in this big rock cave by the devil.
Uncle Bao swam to the entrance of the rock cave and saw that the demon king was leading the big and small demons, and they had already set up a position waiting for him there. Uncle Bao went to war with the Demon King: he killed him from the bottom of the sea for a while, and then killed him from the surface to the bottom of the sea, making the sea turbulent, and the waves were chaotic. The devil gradually became unable to support him. If he was careless, he was blinded in the eye by the Golden Phoenix. After hurting it, he covered his face and screamed. Golden Phoenix took a flight and pecked it in the other eye. When the demon king lost his eyes, he collided randomly. One hit a rock and hit him to death. As soon as the demon king died, the little devil suddenly escaped clean.
Uncle Bao didn't even stop his energy, so he pushed the big rock blocked at the entrance of the cave and found the sun. He did his best to hold the sun upstream to the surface of the sea. Swim, swim, the sun had a hard time showing half of its head on the surface of the sea. Uncle Bao had run out of strength, and he couldn't hold the sun on the surface of the sea. At this point, the Golden Phoenix flew over, held the sun on its back, spread its wings and flew forcefully up the surface of the sea. As soon as the sun left the surface of the sea, it immediately rose into the sky.
On this day, Hui Niang and her townspeople were looking at the stone platform at the top of Gem Mountain. The demons that had escaped came to haunt her again. Suddenly, ten thousand golden rays of light appeared from the eastern sky, a colorful glow appeared, and the sun rose! Then, there was a long cry of phoenix above my head, and the Golden Phoenix of the news flew back first. Golden Phoenix stopped at the top of the mountain to dance.
“Huh, the Sun is back!” Hui Niang and the townspeople cheered with joy, and the sound of cheering shook the mountain. The demons surrounding Hye-niang shone in the sun and were shocked by the cheers, and they all turned to stone.
Since then, the sun has risen from the east and set in the west every day, and people have lived a bright and happy life again.
Until now, every day before the sun rises, a bright star shines brightly in the East. That is Liu Chunbian's. People call it “Enlightenment.” When the sun rises, the golden light always shines brightly. That's because the golden phoenix spreads its wings and holds the sun.
To commemorate Uncle Bao's exploits in recovering the sun, people built an exquisite pagoda at the top of Gem Mountain, and also built a small hexagonal pavilion at the place where the Golden Phoenix flew. These are the current “Baoshu Tower” and “Laifeng Pavilion.” Hui Niang and the townspeople look forward to the stone platform where the sun rises again. Because you can see the sun rising first there, it is called the “First Terrace.”
