 Once upon a time, there was a fisherman named Tofu. He lived alone. The fishing boat his parents left him rotted and his fishing net broke, but he had no way of getting a new fishing boat or a new fishing net.
One day, he went to the riverside to fish. Old fisherman Jin Gui, who just bought a new fishing boat, mocked him and said, “Tofu, your net is rotten like trash. Dragon King doesn't work when it hits the net, so you only need to wipe your butt -- I think you still don't stay by the river; look, you're scaring all my fish away! You can throw it on a mountain beam; I don't think you can net a fairy to go home. haha-haha-ha-”
Tofu was so ashamed that he couldn't lift his head. He left the riverbank with his broken fishing net and walked towards the mountain beam. He cried as he walked.
There is a waterhole on the hill called Taohuatama. The water in Taohuatan is very deep and clear, and a layer of peach petals has fallen on the surface of the water. Tofu wiped away his tears and wanted to try his luck, so he went into the shallow water and threw his fishing net into the water. Unexpectedly, by casting a net, I actually caught a beautiful blue fish.
Tofu was happy: “Haha, I'm just saying I'm not afraid the net will break, I'm just afraid the net won't burst! I'm hungry. This fish isn't big or small; it's just right for braised.”
“Tofu, don't eat me.” The blue fish said to Tofu, “You feed me with your house's crock pot of mountain spring water; I'll cook food for you to eat.”
Tofu brought the blue fish home, took the only large crock pot in the house from under the bed, washed it, filled it with mountain spring water, and kept the blue fish in the crock pot. When it was time to cook, the red fish came out of the crock pot and turned into a fish girl wearing a blue dress to cook the tofu.
Fish Girl's blue dress has two pockets. She pours rice out of her left pocket and pours out fish from her right pocket. After a while, the food is hot and fragrant. The fish girl filled the tofu and arranged the dishes: “Tofu, the rice is ready, you eat it slowly. I went back to the crock pot.”
Very satisfied with the tofu: “Let's eat it together!”
The fish girl shook her head and said, “I'm the daughter of the Dragon King of Tan Shui. I can't eat human fireworks — if I eat this meal, I'll never be able to turn back into a fish.”
“What's good about making fish? You might as well be my wife and live with me from now on.”
The fish girl stayed and ate with tofu. After eating, she burst into tears: “I'm afraid my father will get mad if I marry you without permission. Go to the pool and ask for my father! You want to say, 'Water Dragon King, I want to marry your fish daughter as my bride. '”
Tofu went to Taohua Lake and shouted, “Water Dragon King, Water Dragon King, I want to marry your fish daughter as a bride.”
There was a big wave of water in Taohuatan, and Water Dragon King's head showed off the surface of the water: “Tomorrow you will go up the mountain with me to hunt deer. You will hunt in the back, and I will block in the front. If I can successfully hunt golden deer, I'll marry you.”
Tofu came home and told the fish girl, “Water Dragon King will go up the mountain with me to hunt deer tomorrow. He will hunt in the front, I will block him in the back. He will only agree to this marriage if he hunts a golden deer.”
“My father is a good deer hunter. As long as he doesn't regret it, you will definitely be able to hunt deer. However, his temper is erratic. If you find out that he suddenly disappeared, you need to see if there are any buffaloes in the mud pond. If there are three buffaloes, don't be afraid; just run up bravely and hold them tightly together, and things will work out.”
The next day, Tofu went up the mountain with Tanshui Dragon King. Seven Golden Deer came out of the forest. Water Dragon King was in front, and Tofu was blocked in the back. Seeing that they were about to be hunted, Water Dragon King suddenly disappeared.
When Tofu saw three buffaloes in the muddy pond around him, he boldly ran down and held the three buffalo horns tightly together. The three cows immediately became the king of the water dragon in the pond. The two teamed up again to hunt deer, and soon captured all seven golden deer.
Tofu and Fish Girl got married, and at the beginning they had a very busy life. Fish girl is good at weaving and quickly earns enough money to buy new fishing boats and new fishing nets for Tofu. With new fishing boats and new fishing nets, tofu can catch a lot of fish every day, and they are slowly getting rich.
Besides that, Jin Gui. He has a daughter named Jin Hua, who looks very beautiful. Seeing that tofu is full of fish every day, and people are getting older and more handsome, she said to Tofu, “If you give me half of the fish you catch every day, I'll embroider a pair of mandarin ducks on your collar.”
Fish girls only weave; they don't embroider mandarin ducks. Tofu only wears plain clothes every day. Hearing Jinhua say this, Tofu is moved. Every day, he gave half of the fish he caught to Jinhua. Sure enough, Jinhua embroidered a mandarin duck on his collar.
The mandarin duck is now embroidered. Every day after fishing for tofu, I stay with Jinhua, I don't go home to eat, and I don't sleep at home.
The fish girl was very sad. One evening, she said to tofu, “Do you want blue fish or gold flowers?”
Tofu said, “I want golden flowers.”
The fish girl sadly stomped on the ground and stomped, and her foot became a fish tail. The fish's tail drilled hard into the dirt, drilling and drilling, getting deeper. After a while, all she had left was her head and neck to show off the ground.
“Tofu, do you think you want fish or flowers?”
Tofu still says, “I want flowers.”
All of a sudden, the fish girl burrowed into the dirt and disappeared.
Tofu and Jinhua have been together for three years. His fishing boat slowly rotted, his net slowly broke, and he caught fewer and fewer fish. Later, Jinhua ran away with someone else and never came back.
When Tofu thought of the fish girl, she picked up the rotten fishing net and ran to the Taohuatan side to cry.
He cast his net while crying, but every time he pulls it up, there is only an empty pool of water in the net.
There was a frog by the pool and asked him, “Tofu, why are you crying?”
Tofu talked about the whole thing from beginning to end. While talking, he shed tears.
The toad took pity on him and said to him, “I can soak up the pool water and help you get the fish girl back, but when I absorb the water, my belly flutters. That's really not very nice. Don't laugh no matter what.”
“I'm definitely not laughing.”
Tofu dried the tears, watched the toad absorb water, and only absorbed half of it. The toad's stomach bulged higher than tofu, trembling without stopping. It looked like a big, fat piece of tofu. It was extremely ugly and ridiculous.
Tofu couldn't help but laugh when he saw the toad. He laughed so much that he covered his stomach and rolled around the pool. He laughed so much that he couldn't breathe.
By the time he finished laughing, the toad was gone, and Taohuatan was full of water again.
Tofu can't find the fish girl anymore.
