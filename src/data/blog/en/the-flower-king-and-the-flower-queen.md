 The ancients called peonies the king of flowers, “Yao Huang” the king of peonies, and “Wei Zi” the queen of peonies. “Yao Huang Wei Zi” has almost become synonymous with peony. There is a folklore story about Kao and Kahou:
Legend has it that during the Tang dynasty, there was a little woodcutter in Peony Mountain. His parents died early. One made a living by gathering firewood. The little woodcutter loved peonies. He always had to prune and water peonies when cutting wood, etc., and never destroyed a peony.
Every day, a woodcutter goes up the mountain to cut firewood; he always has to pass by a stone person on the hillside. I don't know when Stone Man has been standing here. It looks like it, and it's very cute. When going up the mountain, the woodcutter hung the dry food bag around Shi Ren's wrist and said, “Brother Shi Ren, eat it!” When I went down the mountain, I took it down again and laughed and said, “Brother Shi Ren, if you don't eat it, I'll eat it!” Picking firewood in the mountains, it's rare to meet a living person. The woodcutter is also very affectionate with Shi Ren. Sometimes he talks to himself about his feelings to him. He feels that Brother Shi Ren can understand it.
In a blink of an eye, Little Qiao grew up to be a 178-year-old young heir. One day, he finished a load of firewood as usual. When he went down the mountain, he was ready to rest for a while in front of Shiren. Seeing a young woman flashing out from behind Stone Man, the woodcutter was extremely surprised. Woodboy turned around and wanted to leave, but the girl stopped him and told him that her name was Flower Girl. She was alone and wanted to be with him for 100 years. After hearing this, Qiao Tong shook his head and said, “Sister Flower, we both have known each other our whole lives. We have no media, and we have no documents. This is inappropriate. Besides, I'm poor, and if you follow me, you'll suffer.” The flower girl said, “Stone man is the medium. Peony Mountain testifies, isn't it OK? If we become a couple, with our hard work, we will get better.” Boy Qiao still refuses: “Can Shi Renhe and Peony Mountain act as mediators?” Still speechless, I didn't expect Shi Ren to actually speak out: “Dude, I'll be the matchmaker, just say yes!” At the end of the day, a bright bead appeared in Shi Ren's hand: “Take it. This is called the Eternity Pearl, which can be used as your medium.” Qiao Tong was surprised and delighted. He took the orb from Shi Ren and prayed to Shi Ren again and again: “Brother Shi Ren is willing to act as a mediator. This is God's intention; I agreed.” The flower girl smiled happily. The woodcutter and flower lady were leaving hand in hand, and Shi Ren also told them, “You husband and wife are changing this jewel, and you have to keep in your mouth for an hour every day. Otherwise, your husband and wife will break up.” The woodcutter hurriedly asked, “Why?” Shi Ren said, “Let's ask me again after 100 years.” When I said that, I didn't say anything.
From spring to winter, 100 years have passed. Both the woodcutter boy and the flower girl have pale white hair, but strangely enough, even though they are all over 100 years old, they are still strong. The woodcutter went up the mountain to cut firewood every day, and the flower lady handled household chores at home. The two lived in harmony and happiness. On this day, Qiao Tong came to Shi Man once again and said, “Brother Shi Ren, 100 years have passed. Can you open a gold mouth again and tell me why we keep that orb every day?” Stone man honestly said, “Dude, that jewel is originally a pill. If you keep it in your mouth for over 100 years, you can live in this Peony Mountain for a long time.” Qiao Tong said, “Seriously, I really don't want to leave Peony Mountain, but now my hair is all white, how long can I live?” Shi Ren told him, “When you get back, wait for him in half of that orb. You and the flower lady each eat half, and you'll understand everything.”
When the woodcutter returned home, he split the pill in half. He and the flower lady each ate half. In an instant, the woodcutter turned into a fairy boy, and the flower girl reverted to being a fairy. When the breeze blew in, they leisurely flew over the clouds, and the entire Peony Mountain had a panoramic view.
Turns out, Flower Lady is a purple fairy on Mount Botan. She is in love with Woodcutter, who is hardworking and honest, and loves peonies. She brought a fairy, and made Huaxian Weng act as a match. She would rather suffer for a hundred years on earth and become a fairy.
The woodcutter and the flower girl ascended into the clouds, and two yellow and purple handkerchiefs floated from the sky. They fell to the ground and immediately turned into two peonies. One plant has yellow flowers, and the other purple flowers. The flowers are beautiful. They all say they are incarnations of the Woodsman Boy and the Flower Girl. Yellow flowers are the most beautiful, known as the king of flowers; purple flowers are sometimes called after flowers. These are what posterity called Yao Huang and Wei Zi.
