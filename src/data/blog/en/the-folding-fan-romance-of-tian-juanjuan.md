 A marriage of thousands of miles goes hand in hand. This marriage was amazing. A couple of men and women who never knew each other competed to become a loving and affectionate couple just because of a fiddle. It's really hard to say whether it was a coincidence or a coincidence. The marriage between Tian Juanjuan and Mu Yuanjing's folding fan is one such story.
Kimoto Kyō is a talented son in Sanin. He is young, talented, and has a strong sense of style. In the spring of the year, he came to Mount Taishan alone to enjoy the majestic Dongyue “view the mountains and small mountains at a glance”. In order to see the sunrise from the top of Mount Taishan early the next morning, I stopped at Qin Guanfeng at night. The mountain breeze was refreshing, and I slept very sweetly at night, and I fell asleep with beautiful dreams. He dreamed that he was walking in a beautiful landscape. Suddenly, a beautiful woman floated by, left behind a fan of poetry, and turned around and hid in an elegant house. He picked up the fan and read it. He hadn't yet waited for him to read the inscription on the fan clearly. Suddenly, he was awakened by the roar of a crisp morning bell. After I woke up, even though I didn't remember the poem, I still remember the landscape house I saw in my dream.
The following year, Mu Yuan Jing was recommended to join Taixue in the imperial court by a local government school and went to Beijing via Wuqing County. When I arrived in Wuqing, the scenery was beautiful and pleasant during the twilight spring season, when the blossoms were everywhere. There is still some time until Taixue starts, so Benyuan decided to stop at Xiaozuo in Wuqing. I found an inn to stay at. Once the arrangements were right, when the sun was setting westerly, in the face of a warm evening breeze, Muyuan walked out of the hotel and unwittingly walked out of the hotel and unwittingly walked to the suburbs. After crossing a small stone bridge, not far in front is a verdant green mountain recess. A path leads to the depths and is surrounded by green grass. Mu Yuan Jing looked right and left in comfort, and suddenly her eyes lit up. I found a beautiful folding fan lying on the grass next to the road. I picked it up and took a look. It was a small, delicate, silk-faced fan with a fine sandalwood bone. A pair of small, exquisite crystal butterflies were tied to the fan with a red wire rope. The fan was unfolded, and a poem was titled on it:
Peony in the smoke sleeps dimly, and pear blossoms under the rain light makeup;
After the courtyard was settled at dusk, I could see the orchid and musk from the wall.
It is poetic and elegant, and has a strong sense of spring. Also, if you look at the handwriting, it's a beautiful small calligraphy, which looks like it came from a girl. Mu Yuanjing tasted the charm of poetry and looked around again, suddenly feeling that the scene in front of her was very familiar. When I thought about it carefully, it turned out to be the same dream I experienced on Mount Taishan last year, and I couldn't help but be amazed.
Further along the path, I saw an elderly girl from afar, taking two young maids to enjoy picking flowers among the flowers on the riverside. Kimoto Kyung couldn't help but walk forward and didn't wait for him to get close. The girl seemed to notice and summoned the two maids. After a moment of gentle movement, I followed the path into a courtyard in front of the mountain, leaving behind only light laughter all the way. Kimoto Jing didn't know whether to advance or retreat. Looking at the courtyard with a blink of an eye, it turned out to be exactly what he had seen in his dreams. He couldn't help but feel a bit dazed. He was both excited and disappointed. He pulled out his sword and engraved a seven-sentence poem on a roadside pole:
Across the river, you can see the green poplar slope from afar, and the girls sing and fall flowers;
I can't hear the quiet sound of the wind. Whose family did the red dress belong to?
Seeing that it was already dusk and dark, he couldn't stay here for long; he hurriedly turned back to the hotel. At night, he quietly asked the store what kind of people were living in the mountain recesses in the suburbs. The store said, “This is about three miles to General Arita's Garden. What the boy saw was probably General Tian Juanjuan, the beloved daughter of General Tian?”
Why is there a single-door general living in this mountain field? It turns out that General Tian was Captain Tian Tadayoshi in the light truck. While conquering the enemy invaders in the northwest, he was shot by an arrow. His wounds never healed after his triumph, so he brought his wife and daughter back to his hometown of Wu Qing to heal his wounds. Mrs. Tian, Qian, is the daughter of a family. She is proficient in literature and ink, and is gracious and quiet. General Tian and his wife have only one daughter, named Juanjuan. Under her mother's careful upbringing, she is familiar with the scriptures and history, and is good at vocals. Coupled with her natural beauty, they are the only best people in Wuqing County. Juanjuan lives in seclusion with her parents in the countryside where the mountains and water are beautiful. The main thing every day is to serve soup before her father's sick bed. In her spare time, she takes her two maids to play in the mountains and water. Although the days are quiet, they are not fun.
Also, Mu Yuanjing in the inn had many ups and downs this night, and it was hard to sleep. As soon as it dawned, he immediately got up and washed, and with great interest he followed the old road to the riverside in the suburbs and wandered in the shade of the flowers. I hope to meet his wife again by accident and comfort him with his longing. He waited until noon. Apart from the wind shaking and no sign of the beautiful woman, he had no choice but to return to the hotel for lunch. In the afternoon, Mu Yuanjing returned to the bridge and sat in the shade of the grass, waiting for a miracle to happen. The flowers fell along with the stream, and in a blink of an eye it was dusk again, but the miracle never happened. In desperation, the Benyuanjing engraved another poem on the trunk of another tree:
Petite birds are impatient, and Xiang Leng first rolled over the moon and sank;
There are no red leaves in March, but the flowers fall and flow in the water.
Due to his studies, Mu Yuanjing was unable to stay in Wuqing for a long time; he had to leave in a hurry and enter Beijing. In Taixue, he taught famous teachers and studied hard, but he couldn't help but take out the folding fan he found and play with it, treating it like a treasure.
