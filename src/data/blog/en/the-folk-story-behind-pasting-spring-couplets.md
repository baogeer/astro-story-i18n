 Every year after the Spring Festival, families must post Spring Festival couplets. Do you know how Spring Festival couplets come about? There's another story here.
That was a long, long time ago. At that time, there was a nice peach grove on the scenic Dushuo Mountain. In the peach grove, there is the biggest peach tree. There are two blue stone houses under the peach tree. Two brothers live in the stone house. The older brother's name is Shen Zhen, and the younger brother's name is Yu Lei. The brothers were working hard. The lion saw them bowing, the evil panther saw them paralyzed, and the tiger guarded the forest for them.
This is a wild peach grove, and the brothers were born here. My parents died a long time ago, and the two brothers are dependent on each other and are eating wild peaches. For this reason, they are very kind to this peach grove. When the weather was dry, they picked water from mountain springs; when insects were growing, they carefully caught them one by one; cultivated the soil and worked hard. Kung Fu pays off. The peaches grown in the wild peach grove are delicious and sweet to eat. In particular, the peach tree in the middle of the forest bears fruit, which is particularly sweet. People say that the peach that grows on this peach tree is a fairy peach. Eating this peach can prolong life and become a god.
In the northeast direction of Mount Saku, there is also Bison Ridge. There is a wild prince on Bison Ridge, and the wild prince also has clumsy strength. He has taken over this area thanks to his strength and strength. This wild prince's heart is more poisonous than a snake, and his hands are more ruthless than tigers and wolves. He often eats people's hearts and drinks human blood, but he has caused suffering to the people of this side. Prince Ye heard that there was a peach on Mount Dusaku. If he ate a peach, he could become a fairy. His mouth was three feet long, and he immediately sent someone to Mount Dusaku.
The visitors arrived at the peach grove and drank the Shenda brothers to offer peaches. The brothers smiled coldly and said, “This peach is only for the poor.” When I finished speaking, I drove the visitor down the mountain.
After listening to what his subordinates said, the wild prince was so angry that he immediately brought 300 people to Mount Dusaku. The Kamida brothers also brought Morin Tiger out of the Peach Grove. The two sides met and had a vicious battle, beating the Wild Prince to the end and running away in a hurry.
The Wild Prince escaped back to Bison Ridge, thought the tea wasn't tasty, and couldn't sleep day and night thinking about revenge. He thought, thought, got three layers of skin off his head, added three holes in his head, and finally came up with a bad idea.
On a dark, windy night, the Shanda brothers slept well in the stone house, overheard any movement outside. They got up and opened the door and looked out. They only saw dozens of ghosts coming from the northeast, one by one with blue fangs, red hair and green eyes, and strange shapes, screaming and rushing towards the stone house. The two brothers have been innocent all their lives and have never done anything bad, so they are not afraid at all when facing evil spirits. Shinda greeted him with a peach branch, and Yu Lei grabbed a bundle of reeds and followed behind. God caught it at the front, and Yu Lei was tied up at the back. After a while, dozens of ghosts were all tied up, and they all fed the tigers.
It turns out that these ghosts were all dressed up as the Wild Prince and his men, and they wanted to scare the Shinto Brothers away, but they lost their lives when the drug plan didn't work.
The next day, the incident spread all of a sudden. People thanked the Shindah brothers for killing the people, and the brothers' fame spread farther and farther. Later, the brothers died. Legend has it that they became immortals and went to the court of heaven. God ordered the two of them to take charge of punishing all ghosts, and when they met evil spirits, they tied them up with reeds to feed the tigers. People also say that since the peach grove is a breed of two Shinto brothers, it can also drive away ghosts and ward off evil. Since then, during the New Year holidays, people have cut down two pieces of mahogany boards with the names of Sheng Sheng and Yu Lei written on them and hung them on both sides of the door to express the intention of repelling disasters and protecting the safety of the family. This is the first Spring Festival couplets in our country, also known as the “Peach Charm.”
Year after year, people hang “peach charms” every year. By the time of the 5th generation, the monarch of later Shu was named Meng Chang, and on “Peach Charms,” he wrote the two sentences “Happy New Year, Happy New Year, Changchun” and hung them on both sides of the door. This is our country's first joint couplet.
Later, Zhu Yuanzhang, the ancestor of the Ming dynasty, ordered that on New Year's Eve, every Duke should post a Spring Festival couplet on the door. At this point, the Spring Festival couplets were already written on red paper. At first, Spring Festival couplets were limited to government offices; later, ordinary people also posted them at home. Since then, posting Spring Festival couplets during the Spring Festival has become a custom for our people, and it has continued to this day.
