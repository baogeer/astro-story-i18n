 In the old days, there was a child named Han Shu. His parents died early, herded cows for their family from an early age, and lived in a shed made of branches and thatch. There is a lotus pond next to the straw shed. Every summer at dusk, I am ashamed to return from herding cows, watching the lotus flowers blooming in the pond and smelling the scent of lotus, and I feel unspeakably happy. Using a bamboo flute he made himself, he played songs he wrote himself.
The beautiful sound of the flute moved the lotus fairy in the lotus pond. She not only loved the shy sound of the flute, but also sympathized with her humble life, so she turned into a village lady and arrived at the shy shed. Shy is a young man with a duty. Seeing a girl come to his shed, he blushes with shame and says, “Who are you and why are you here?”
The Lotus Fairy said, “My family lives a hundred miles away. Because my parents are greedy for money, they want to marry me to a strange rich old rich owner. I escaped from my home and left for half a month before I escaped here.”
“So where are you going?”
“I don't know... I haven't eaten in days.”
Shyly, he took out a small amount of food for the girl to eat. The girl asked about her shy life and shyly told him about it.
The girl said, “You and I are all hard workers. At least I took some silver when I escaped from home. Let's build a house as a family.”
Since I was a child, I was ashamed to see that the girl was so kind, so I agreed. He used money from the girl to buy materials to build a house, and also brought in craftsmen. Soon, he built a house by the lotus pond. The two became a family and lived together. People in the village were all happy when they saw that they were ashamed to have a family and married a daughter-in-law.
Shy used the remaining two pieces of silver to buy two acres of land. The husband and wife lived a life of man farming and women weaving. At leisure, the couple sat by the lotus pond and enjoyed the lotus flowers.
One year, the sky was very dry, and there was no harvest of crop grains. Shyly, he said to his wife, “It's not a good time. I want to go into the mountains to hunt. By the way, I'll dig up some herbs in exchange for some money to supplement my household expenses.”
The lotus fairy originally wanted to tell him that he didn't have to worry about food and clothing, but she was also worried that Shy would become a lazy person, and that would also make Shy worry that the couple's relationship would not last long, so she didn't stop her.
Shyly, I went into the mountains every day to hunt, and every day I was able to bring back some game, I was able to recover some herbs, and I was able to get on with my days.
One day, I was shy and went into the mountains for a day, and I never came home at night. The lotus fairy waited anxiously at home, but she was too shy to return home for three days. Early in the morning of the fourth day, the lotus fairy decided to go to the mountains to find him. She thought maybe she had lost her way in the mountains because she was ashamed.
The lotus fairy looked for a long time in the mountains, and only then discovered a shy hunting bow and an iron hoe for digging medicine at the entrance of a cave. She shouted a shy name at the entrance of the cave, shyly came out of the cave, and was followed by an enchanting woman. You can recognize the lotus fairy at a glance; she is an apricot flower essence from the mountains. The Lotus Fairy wanted to debunk her, but she was afraid that she would be shy and doubt her identity. As a result, she didn't say anything; she was shy and left.
Apricot Flower Essence followed behind and shouted, “Shame, as the saying goes, one hundred days for husband and wife in one day. You and I have also been a couple for a few days. If you want to go home, I won't stop you, but you always have to look back and take a look at me, right?”
The lotus fairy warned the shy one and said, “Never look back!” But I was so shy that I couldn't stand the cry of apricot flower essence over and over again, so I turned my head around. It doesn't matter this time around. Shyness is gone, and apricot flower essence is gone.
The lotus fairy spent three days and three nights searching in the mountains before finding a bunch of white bones next to a bunch of apricot blossoms under the cliff. She wrapped the white bones in her clothes and buried them next to her house.
The lotus fairy told the people in the village that Shy had been eaten by tigers. In fact, she understood in her heart that it was apricot flower essence that drank Shy Blood and ate Shy Meat. She regretted not stopping Shy from entering the mountain in the first place.
Every day the lotus fairy comes to Shy's grave and silently says, “Shy, shy, why don't you listen to me?”
Later, a grass that had never been seen before grew on the shy tomb. Every time the lotus fairy spoke to the grave in a reprimand tone, the leaves of that grass immediately hang down.
After a few more days, a few more girls came to the Lotus Fairy's house. The lotus fairy told people that it was a few of her younger sisters who came to take her back to her mother's house. Later, people didn't know where they had gone.
Strangely enough, by the second year, not a single lotus flower bloomed in the lotus pond next to Shy's house from summer to fall; the tomb of Shy and the side of the lotus pond were covered with unknown grass. As long as people poke the grass with their fingers, the grass branches and leaves will immediately hang down, and people name this grass “mimosa.”
