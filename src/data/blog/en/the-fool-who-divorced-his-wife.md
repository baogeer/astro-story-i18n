On this day, an idiot walked down the street and met a friend. Seeing his friend smiling and humming songs in his mouth, he curiously asked, “Why are you so excited?”
My friend smiled and said, “I took a break from my wife and now I am married to a beautiful young wife. She is gentle and considerate to me, and I am happy to death.” Looking at his face with envy, his friend knew he believed it. They couldn't help but make fun of him, “If you go back, take a break from your wife, and marry someone young and beautiful again.”
The idiot was certainly moved after listening to it. While walking home, he thought that his wife was old and stupid and nagged all day long. How great would it be if I took a break from her and married a young and beautiful one again!

The idiot laughed and said, “Ma'am! If you take a break, you can marry someone beautiful again!”
