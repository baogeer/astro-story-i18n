 In a city-state, the king's three brothers lived. They married a princess together and governed the country in an orderly manner. Unfortunately, this concubine died young.
Not far from here, there is a Raksha ghost. Seeing that the city-state of the three brothers flourished and developed day by day, he was so envious that he burst out of his eyes. I've always wanted to get rid of the king's three brothers and rule this city-state myself.
After the princess died, the ghost of Raksha became a beautiful woman, called a little Raksha turned into a yak, carrying many goods and selling them under the king's three brothers' castle.
At this point, the entire city-state was shocked. The maids greeted one by one: “Go check it out! Go check it out! Beautiful women are selling precious jewels!”
The king stuck his head out of the window, and his soul was immediately taken away by the beautiful woman. He called the girl to the castle and became his own concubine. Unexpectedly, after getting married, his body became thinner day by day, and eventually became skinny.
The beautiful woman who turned into a ghost from Raksha also became the concubine of the Second King. It didn't take long for King II to leave the world like his brother. Then, she married the little king again. Unfortunately, the little king also got sick and getting worse.
When ministers saw this situation, they got together and discussed: “Oops! Since this concubine entered the palace, two of the three kings have died, and the rest of the little kings can't see it. We need to quickly ask the gossip master to calculate the gossip, ask the lama to read the sutras, ask the Tibetan doctor to see the disease, and say nothing to let the little king die again.” The ministers agreed to invite a lama from Hirakawa to treat the little king.
This lama is a confused lama. His wife is sick, and his children are in a big group. He has no choice but to take care of the disease to support the whole family. I heard that he was being asked to treat the king the other day, and I was really scared and stunned. I thought, “If you don't go, you won't be able to save your life. Why don't you go for a run and try your luck!”
He entered the royal palace and sat next to the little king, pretending to read the sutras, but stared dead at the concubine with both eyes because she was so beautiful. A minister respectfully asked, “Lama, what disease did the little king have?” The lama didn't see it, so he said a mantra: “Bah! Damn it!”
The lama said whatever he wanted; Raksha's demon concubine was terrified. She thought, “Oh! This lama is really smart. He is staring at me and thinking about my fame in his mouth. Does he see my true form?”
Furthermore, the confused lama left at 3 in the middle of the night because he had no idea what illness the little king had and was afraid of being punished. He panicked and walked downstairs. He happened to have stepped on a young yak turned by Ronaldinho, and planted a big heel firmly. The lama climbed up and took a look. It turned out to be a yak. He was furious and said harshly, “Mom's corpse, come and mess with me too!” I picked up the sutras and hit the yak hard in the head.
At this point, there was another noise on the stairs. The lama hurriedly dived into the sheep's coop. When he saw the beautiful concubine, he came down the stairs step by step and walked in front of the yak. As soon as he rolled around, she became a terrible devil. The yak also suddenly became a little devil. The little devil said, “No good! That's not good! That lama discovered the truth about me. Just now, he beat me up in a daze with his scripture book.” The big devil also said, “I'm here for this. He stares at me during the day, and his eyes pop out fast! I think, let's do our best as soon as possible to kill the little king!”
The lama overheard Raksha ghost's conversation and got rid of the idea of running away. He quickly ran back to the king. While pretending to read the sutras, he patted the little king's body with his hands and said, “I want to wash the king with sacred water. No one is allowed in.” The lama brought the king into the bathhouse and told him what he had just seen and heard in person. The king was taken aback and was a bit sweaty, as if he had woken up from a nightmare.
The king hurriedly dispatched 50 guards to tie up the concubine upstairs with nine cowhide ropes. Another 50 guards were dispatched to tie up the yaks downstairs with nine ropes. When the guards brought them in front of the king and ministers, they were no longer beautiful women and yaks, but rather two Raksha devils. The fangs in their mouths were as long as fingers.
Surprised and happy, the king and ministers continued to criticize the confused Lama, calling him a master of Dharma. Later, the two demons were burned alive, and the little king's illness was completely eradicated. The confused lama became his master, and his wife and children no longer had to worry about eating and drinking.
