 A stupid turtle wanted to monopolize the world's wisdom, so he found a big, dry pumpkin and dug it into an empty shell. Then gather wisdom everywhere and hide it in it. After a while, he thought that the wisdom of the world had been completely collected. No matter where there would be smart people and intelligent birds and animals. He was very happy, so he tied the pumpkin with a rope, hung it on his chest, and wanted to hang it on the top of a big tree that no one could climb. He climbed and crawled. After climbing for more than half a day, his feet still hadn't left half an inch off the ground.
“Idiot turtle, the pumpkin hangs on his chest, and he can't even touch the trunk of a tree. How can he go up there?” A farmer saw it and laughed, “You put the pumpkin on the back, try it!”
The turtle did what the farmer said; it certainly did; it quickly climbed to the top of the tree. He wanted to hang pumpkins on the tallest branch, and suddenly remembered that the world still has a lot of wisdom; otherwise, this farmer wouldn't have had such a clever idea. I wanted to monopolize the wisdom of the world, but ended up being a big fool. The more he thought about it, the more annoyed he felt. He threw the pumpkin to the ground with force and shattered it. As a result, wisdom is spread all over the world, and anyone can find it if they are careful.
