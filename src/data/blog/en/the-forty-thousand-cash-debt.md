 King Sima of Xincheng has housekeepers and servants at home, and is very rich. One day, he suddenly had a dream where someone came in and said to him, “You owe me 40 thousand dollars, you should pay me back now.” He was surprised to ask why, but the person didn't answer; he went straight to the back room. He woke up all of a sudden, and his wife had just given birth to a boy. He knew that this kid had come to take account of his previous life, so he paid 40 thousand dollars to put it in a separate room. All of the child's clothing, food, and medical expenses are spent from these 40 thousand miles.
After three or four years of hard work, I see that only 700 is left of the 40 thousand dollars. On this day, the nurse was playing while holding the child. Sima Wang called the child and said to the child, “The 40,000 are almost used up. You should go.” As soon as I finished speaking, the kid's face changed. Then he looked back and glared, touched his nose, and was out of breath. So I spent the rest of the money to buy funeral items and buried the child.
People who are in arrears can take this as a warning. Once upon a time, an old, childless person asked a high priest why? The high priest answered, “You don't owe anyone else, and they don't owe you anything. How can you get children?” Therefore, to give birth to good children is to repay kindness; to give birth to bad children is to collect debts. Life and death are a matter of fate. Don't be too happy when you have a child, and don't be too sad when your child dies.
