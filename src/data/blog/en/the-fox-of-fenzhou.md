 There is a state judge in Fenzhou. His last name is Zhu. There are many foxes in the house where he lives. One day, Zhu Gong was sitting still in his room. Suddenly, a woman came and went under the lamp. At first, Zhu Gong thought that the wife of a servant in the family came to work, but he didn't care. After a while I looked up, I didn't know anyone. I also saw that she was very beautiful. I knew in my heart that this must be a fox girl, but I was still very happy with her, so I hurriedly invited her over. The woman stopped and smiled and said, “Your voice is so harsh, who is your maid?” Zhu Gong smiled and took her to a seat, apologized to her, and rejoiced with her. After a long time, they were as loving as a couple.
One day, the fox girl suddenly said to Zhu Gong, “Your official position is about to be transferred; we will be separated soon.” Zhu Gong asked her, “When?” He answered, “Right in front of me. But while the congratulatory person is in front of your door, the person who died is in your hometown; you can't be an official anymore!” Three days later, Zhu Gong's relocation order came; one more day, the hometown came to report her death, saying that Mrs. Tai had passed away. Gong Zhu had to quit his job right away and go home in desolation. He asked the fox girl to go home with him. The fox girl disagreed and sent Zhu Gong to the riverside. Zhu Gong asked her to board the boat once again. The fox girl said, “You don't know, our foxes can't cross the river.” Zhu Gong couldn't bear to be separated; he fell in love by the river. At this point, the fox girl suddenly went out and said she was visiting an old friend. After a moment, she came back. Then someone prayed back and forth, and the fox girl went to another place to talk to the visitors. After the guests had gone, the fox girl came to Zhu Gong and said, “Please get on the boat! I'll take you across the river.” Zhu Gong said, “You just said you couldn't cross the river, why can you do it now?” The woman said, “The person I just visited was not someone else; it was the god of the river. I went to see him for you. He limited me to come back for ten days, so I'm depending on you for the time being.” So they went home with the boat. At the end of the ten-day period, the fox girl certainly said goodbye to Gong Zhu and went back.
