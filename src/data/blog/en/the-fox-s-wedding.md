 Yin Shangshu from Licheng, Shandong, had a very poor family when he was young, but he was very brave and smart. There is a family home in the county. The area is tens of acres of land, and the buildings are connected together. Because strange phenomena often occur, it is abandoned and no one lives there anymore. It's been a long time, and the inside is gradually covered with artemisia, and no one dared to go in even during the day.
It just so happened that Yin Gong was drinking with his classmates. Some of them joked, “There is someone who can sleep in this courtyard. Let's all pay to have guests together.” Gong Yin jumped up and said, “How difficult is this!” So I brought a mat. The crowd took him to the front door and teased, “We're waiting for you here for a while. If you see a monster, just shout.” Gong Yin smiled and said, “If there is a ghost fox, I will definitely catch it as proof.” When I finished speaking, I went in the door.
Walking into the yard, I saw that the long sedge covered the path, and there were as many mugwort as hemp. This was the beginning of the month. Fortunately, there was a dusky moonlight, and the portal was recognizable. Gong Yin spent some time exploring the courtyard before arriving at the back building. When I got on the platform and saw that it was clean and cute, I stopped walking. After watching the western moon, it had already set behind the mountain, leaving only a glimmer of afterglow. After sitting for a long time and seeing that nothing strange had happened, I sneered at the absurdity of the rumor. I was resting on a rock, lying on my back and watching the cowherd and weaver stars in the sky.
At the end of the day, Yin Gong was confused and wanted to sleep. Suddenly, I heard footsteps downstairs, and they came up from below one after another. He then pretended to be asleep, squinted, and saw a man in a blue coat picking up a lotus lamp. Suddenly she discovered Gong Yin. Surprised, she retreated and said to the people behind her, “There is a living person above.” The person below asked, “Who is it?” The Tsing Yi man answered, “I don't know.” Instantly, an old man came up and took a close look at Yin Gong and said, “This is Yin Shangshu. He is already asleep. Just do our business. Yin Xiang Gong doesn't stick to the rules; he probably won't blame him.” As a result, they led people upstairs one after another and opened all the upstairs doors. After a while, there were more people coming and going. The upstairs are brightly lit, just like during the day. Gong Yin rolled over slightly and sneezed. When the old man heard him awake, he came out and knelt down and said, “The little man has a daughter and is getting married tonight. I didn't expect to offend a noble person; I hope you don't blame them.” Gong Yin got up and picked up the old man and said, “I don't know there was a big happy event in Guifu tonight. I'm very ashamed that no congratulatory gifts were offered.” The old man said, “I am very lucky to have a noble person here to suppress evil gods and evil spirits. Please sit with me for a while; the whole family of the little one is more honored.” Gong Yin was so happy that he agreed.
When Yin Gong entered the building and took a look, the interior was very ornate. At this point, a woman came out to visit her. She was about 40 years old. The old man said, “This is my wife.” Prince Yin bowed to her in return. In an instant, I heard the wind and drum music blaring in unison, and someone ran up and said, “Here it is!” The old man hurriedly went out to greet him, and Gong Yin also stood up and waited. Soon, many lanterns guided the groom in. The groom is about 17 or 8 years old and has a handsome appearance. The old man asked him to salute Gong Yin first. The groom looked at Gong Yin with both eyes. Gong Yin was like a wedding host and gave back half of the ceremony. Weng's son-in-law prayed to each other immediately, and after praying, they took their seats. After a while, young maids and maids one by one brought steaming food and wine, jade bowls and gold cups, illuminating the table. After a few drinks, the old man asked the maid to invite the young lady. The maid answered and left. It's been a long time since I saw him. The old man got up and lifted the curtain himself to urge.
After a while, a few maidens and servants came out surrounded by the bride. The ring bell rang, and the scent of orchid musk scattered all over. The old man asked his daughter to bow to him. As soon as she got up, she sat down next to her mother. Gong Yin took a look, only to see that she had a jadeite phoenix in her bun and pearl earrings. She looked gorgeous and was unrivaled in the world.
Afterwards, I switched to Jinjue for pouring wine. Jinjue is so big that it can hold many buckets. Gong Yin thought he could testify to his classmates, so he secretly put it in his sleeve. He pretended to be drunk and lying on the table, as if he were asleep. Everyone at the table said, “Yin Xiang Gong is drunk.” Not long ago, I heard the groom say he wanted to leave. The wind and drum music suddenly started blaring, and people left their seats one after another and went downstairs. Afterwards, the owner packed up the wine utensils and found that a golden jade was missing, and he couldn't find it no matter how he looked for it. Some people secretly argue that Jin Jue may be in the hands of a drunk Gong Yin. The old man heard a hurry to warn people not to talk nonsense, but I'm afraid Gong Yin will hear it. After a while, there was no movement inside or outside before Gong Yin got up. The surrounding area was dark and there were no lights, only the aroma of fat powder and the rich smell of alcohol filled the whole room. Seeing that the East had turned white, Gong Yin slowly went downstairs. Reaching out and touching his sleeve, Jin Jue is still inside.
When Gong Yin arrived at the entrance, his students waited there first, and they all suspected that he came out at night and went back in the morning. Prince Yin brought out the Golden Jade for everyone to see. The crowd was surprised to inquire about the origin, and Gong Yin recounted the situation at night. Everyone thought that such a valuable thing was not something poor readers could have, so they took his word for it.
Later, Yin Gong passed the exam and was sent to Feiqiu County in Guangping Province, Hebei to become a county governor. Zhu from the local government family hosted a dinner with Gong Yin and asked his family to go get a big wine glass. It's been a long time since he brought it. A young man covered his mouth and whispered something to the owner. The owner's face turned angry. Soon, Jin Jue was brought in and advised Gong Yin to drink. Gong Yin took a close look. The style of Jinjue and the image engraved on it were no different from the fox's Golden Jade. He was very surprised, and asked where it was made. Zhu replied, “There are eight such juniors in the family. When my father was a Beijing official, I was looking for exquisite craftsmanship to supervise them. This is a precious family heirloom item that has been stored in layers for a long time. There were only seven left when they were taken out of the bamboo box because of a visit from the prefectural government. I suspect that the family stole them, but the package had accumulated dust over the past ten years, and it's still untouched. It's really impossible to explain.” Gong Yin smiled and said, “Your golden lord has soared. However, the treasure handed down from generation to generation must not be lost; I also have one, which is very similar to your Golden Jade, and I will definitely give it to you.”
After leaving Xi Yin Gong, they returned to the government office to find Jin Jue and send someone to the Zhu family as soon as possible. Zhu was very surprised after holding it and looking at it over and over again. He personally went to the government office to thank Gong Yin and asked about Jin Jue's background. Gong Yin then recounted the beginning and end of the incident. Only then did foxes know that items from thousands of miles away can also be ingested, but they are afraid to keep them in their hands in the end.
