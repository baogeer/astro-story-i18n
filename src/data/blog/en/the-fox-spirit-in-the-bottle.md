 The daughter-in-law of the Mancun Shi family is being carried away by foxes. The family is very worried, but they are unable to let go of it. There was a bottle behind the woman's door. Every time she heard the woman's father-in-law come back, the fox essence was hidden in the bottle. The woman looked it in her eyes many times, then remembered it in her heart and didn't make a fuss.
Once, the fox got into the bottle again. The woman quickly sealed the mouth of the bottle with cotton wool and put the bottle in the pot to cook. After the bottle was hot, the fox shouted inside the bottle, “It's too hot, don't fuss!” The woman didn't answer and continued cooking. The fox essence shouted even more urgently in the bottle; after a long time, she couldn't hear any movement. When the woman unplugged the plug, there was only a pile of hair and a few drops of blood.
