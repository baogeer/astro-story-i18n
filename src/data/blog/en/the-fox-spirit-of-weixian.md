 Li in Weixian County has a vacant house that he wants to rent. One day, an old man suddenly came and wanted to rent out this house. He was willing to rent out 52 gold and silver every year. Li agreed. After the old man left, but there was no news, and Li ordered his family to rent the house to someone else. The next day, the old man came and said, “I've already decided to rent the house to me, why should I rent it to someone else?” Li told him that he suspected he wouldn't come back. The old man said, “I'm about to move for a long time. The reason I haven't moved here is because I chose the lucky day to relocate after ten days.” The old man paid Lee another year's rent and said, “This house has just been empty for a year, don't ask any more.” Li sent him out and asked him about the exact date of his move, and the old man said. After that date, several more days passed, and there was still no trace of the old man. Li went to check the movement. He saw that the gate was locked from the inside, cooking smoke billowing in the courtyard, and the crowd was loud. Li was very surprised and posted a visit on a famous post. The old man hurriedly greeted him, invited him inside, smiled, and spoke kindly.
After Li returned, he sent someone to give something to the old man's family. The old man warmly treated the person he sent, and also sent back many gifts. After a few more days, Li set up the table and invited the old man to a party. The two had a very speculative and cheerful conversation. When Li asked about the old man's hometown, the answer was Shaanxi. Li was surprised that Shaanxi was too far from here. The old man said, “You are blessed here. I can't stay in Shaanxi anymore; a major disaster is about to happen there.” The world was at peace at the time. Li listened to the old man and didn't care; he didn't ask any deep questions. After another day, the old man posted a post and invited Li back. The dishes and furnishings at the dinner party were all very luxurious and gorgeous. Li was even more surprised and suspected that the old man was a noble official. The old man claimed to be a fox fairy because he had a close relationship with him. Li was so shocked that he said it when he met people ever since. After hearing about it, officials and gentlemen from this prefecture saw that every day people rode horses to visit the old man, and they all wanted to be friends with him. The old man treated him respectfully, and gradually got in touch with the county officials. However, only the prefectural governor asked to see him; the old man always made excuses. The county superintendent also introduced him first, but the old man still didn't want to see him. Because of Li's inquiry, the old man left his seat to approach Li and quietly said, “You don't know, the county governor's past life was a donkey. Although people now rule ordinary people like dogs, they are a shameless person who can do anything when he sees money! Even though I'm not human, I'm ashamed to date him!” Li then looked for excuses and told the county commissioner that the fox was afraid of the gods of the county governor, so he was afraid to see him. The prefectural governor believed it to be true, so it was no longer reluctant. This is Kangxi's 11th year. Soon, Shaanxi was certainly besieged by military unrest. It seems to be true that foxes can see firsthand.
