 In the Ming dynasty, a Taoist came to a bachelor's village, turned into a dozen beautiful women, and married the bachelors as wives. The Taoists stayed in the village and drove them to work for themselves at will. Later, the Taoist made another evil request. After discussions with their wives, these men learned the details and decided to join forces to fight back.
During the Yongle years, there was an isolated village on the outskirts of Pingyang County. More than a dozen bachelors lived in the village. Because they can't marry a daughter-in-law, these people are consciously different from the world, so they gather here and live a comfortable life alone.
One day, a Huangpao Taoist came to Lonely Village carrying a marijuana bag. Watching a group of men sitting in a melon shed chatting, he came forward and laughed at the widows: “What are you all talking about?”
An old man looked down at the Taoist and said, “Naturally, we are talking about women; would you like to join the Taoist?” The Taoist laughed and said, “It's not an option to talk about women all day long; poor people can get married.” As soon as they heard it, their eyes were brilliant, but soon they booed again, and no one believed it.
When the Taoist saw that they didn't believe it, he bent down and threw the marijuana bag on the ground. After opening it, it turned out to be a weak old fox.
The Taoist grabbed a small handful of fox fur from the old fox. There were words in his mouth. His sword rushed to the sky. A bunch of fox fur in his hand instantly turned into a cloud of fire. After burning out, the Taoist gently blew the fur dust and turned into a weak little woman. When the Chinese saw this, they were surprised to make a burst of cries.
The Taoist took the little girl and shouted, “Who of you would like to marry her?”
These lonely guys have been single for decades, and now when they see beautiful women, they instantly feel like hungry tigers and slurping their mouths. They are all scrambling to get married to that woman.
The Taoist smiled cunningly: “Don't worry, come one by one, everyone has their share!”
After that, they grabbed a bunch of hair on the old fox, repeated the previous spell, and immediately turned into a second beautiful woman, and told everyone, “These are all fox women. They have a gentle temperament. They listen very much to me; whoever you want her to marry.”
In this way, the fur on the old fox's body was taken in large chunks by the Taoists one after another, turning out a total of 15 beautiful women, all of whom were distributed to lonely men as wives.
The wives of the Han and Han were grateful to Dade. They knelt down one after another to thank the Taoists for the blessings they brought them. Since then, they have treated the Taoists like reborn parents. They only followed their own lives and always served their side.
The Taoist became the speaker of the isolated village. He ordered the villagers to build a shrine for himself, worship with incense day and night, and stay in. The villagers took turns delivering three meals a day. Normally, he also instructed the villagers to do all kinds of chores for him, and also ordered the villagers to forge a big iron cage and lock up the old fox.
The lonely men also had no complaints. Since the Taoist gave them wives, their lives have begun to be nourished one by one, and they are more motivated to work in the field.
A year later, most of Fox Fairy's wives gave birth to men, and everyone was even more happy. They felt that the days were getting more delicious. For this reason, they loved their families very much, and usually couldn't bear to be a little bit aggrieved by their wives and children.
Soon after these days of harmony and happiness, the Taoists had trouble. He summoned men from all over the village to the student temple and said to everyone, “I am your great blessing; without me, you wouldn't have a happy family.” The crowd nodded.
The Taoist coughed a few times and suddenly had a stern face: “Now that I practice the Dharma, I need to drink a bowl of boy's blood every day, so everyone takes turns to worship.” The implication was for the villagers to cut their own child's blood for him to drink.
The crowd was astonished. A few villagers immediately refused; after all, their own flesh and blood!
When the Taoist heard it, he was furious and slapped the wooden table with a slap and said, “If you don't, I'll turn all the fox girls back to fox hair!” The men were worried that their wife would be taken away by him; they could only reluctantly agree.
Evil Dao drank the boy's blood, practiced in seclusion day and night, and laughed wildly at night from time to time.
The villagers watched their children grow weaker and were unwilling to continue like this, but when faced with the choice between their children and wives, many people were in trouble.
There was a man named Ganfu who couldn't stand it, so he cried and told his wife about the incident.
Although her wife has changed into fox hair, she has been in a relationship with Gan Fu Ri for a long time and is no different from a normal person. Sadly, she told her husband, “Fu Jun, I waited for a fox girl, all had alienated with an old fox. She is a thousand-year-old fox fairy. She was calculated by evil, and nailed into nine dog blood nails to block her mana before he was allowed to use it. If he were able to save the old fox, the Taoist would have nothing to do”.
On the same night, Ganfu found a few good lone villagers, and after discussions, decided to take advantage of the dark house to secretly save the old fox. A few people came to the large iron cage in the back house of the student's temple with torches, and it took a lot of effort to drag the old fox out.
At this point, the thousand-year-old fox, who had been imprisoned, was already dying.
The old fox said in a weak voice, “Throw me into the water soon, and the copper nails will come out. Remember to pull them all out in half a cup of incense,” and Ganfu and his team carried the old fox to the riverside overnight and left it behind.
The old fox screamed as soon as it got into the water. Seemingly, it was extremely painful. However, I don't know when the Taoist appeared. He shot two lonely men in one fell swoop and threw himself into the river, hoping to pick up the old fox.
Gump was willing to agree, and with a few punches of his own, he fought his life against Evil Dao. At the critical moment, the man and fox girl from the isolated village all arrived to help each other. The crowd joined forces to besiege the devil, and the fierce battle continued.
Gan Fu made no mistake, so he took the opportunity to get out of the water and discovered that the old fox had bronze nails sticking out of his limbs, chest, and forehead, so they removed them one by one.
Instantly, I saw that the old fox was like the Phoenix Nirvana. His eyes were bright and flew out of the river. As soon as the fox claws out of his heart, he shot Evil Road to death on the spot.
Afterwards, in order to thank Ganfu and the villagers for their rescue, the old fox fairy left all the fox women in the world and continued to be their wives. I, on the other hand, went into seclusion deep in the mountains and began my practice again.
This story is called “The Fox Girl in Singles' Village” and comes from “The Folk Tales of Jingyuezhai”. Author: Tang Youshi
Where there is evil, there is resistance. The man in Singles' Village got a fox fairy wife because of his evil intentions, but he had a very evil intention. Once the time was right, he wanted to hurt people.
“Poor parents in the world” Many parents are willing to suffer for themselves and protect their children. This Taoist's behavior was not humane; he deserved to be removed by a joint effort.
