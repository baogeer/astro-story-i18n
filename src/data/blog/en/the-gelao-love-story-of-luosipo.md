 There is a steel gold mountain in the Er Mountains, and at the foot of the mountain there is a village called Luojiepo.
Luojiepo was originally called Yilaopo. Speaking of Yilaopo, there is still a moving story circulating.
A long time ago, a young man from the Yilao ethnic group lived in Yilaoposhang. His name was Guo Lu. Guo Lu's grandmother died when she was three, and her dad died again when she was nine.
At that time, Yilaoposhang didn't have a second family home yet. As soon as Grandpa died, Guo Lu became a helpless orphan.
People who haven't been to Laer Mountain don't know how tall Mount Laer really is; people who haven't tasted Huanglian have no idea how bitter Huanglian really is; people raised by their parents know how sad the days of being an orphan are!
Nine-year-old Guo Lu can't pull the hard bow that Grandpa left behind, and can't dance the boldness that Grandpa left behind. It's really hard for Guo Lu to live a life hunting like when her dad was alive!
There is a hole on the half slope of Yilaopo. It's called Snail Cave. It's about half a mile away from Guo Lu's home. Water from the cave comes out from under the cave, accumulates in a small pond outside the cave, then flows loudly from the edge of the pond to the foot of the slope. It has never dried up. There are no fish in the water, no shrimp, only snails that can be caught.
One day, Guo Lu was worried about her future life. Suddenly she heard someone say, “Guo Lu Guo Lu has fruit!”
Guo Lu thought, “Grandma is dead, Grandpa is gone. Who is calling out in this wild mountain?” He hurriedly ran outside the door to take a look, only to see the big tree covering the small tree and the small tree covering the wild grass. Other than that, he didn't see anything, so he turned around and slowly returned home.
He was about to sit down and heard someone outside the door saying, “Guo Lu, Guo Lu, there is fruit!”
He ran outside the door again and still hadn't seen a single person. He just turned around and walked back and heard, “Guo Lu, Guo Lu, there is fruit!” The sound of speech.
Guo Lu looked for the sound. She saw a small bird with green feathers, a long beak, and fluff on its head like a snail shell standing on a peach tree, flapping its wings at him, and pecking a bright red peach with its long shell. It disappeared right away.
Guo Lu didn't take this to heart either. It's just that the red peach made him happy, so she picked one and sent it to his mouth. Hey, it's sweet. It really melts in your mouth.
Guo Lu thought: I can't worry about using it now; I'm afraid I won't be able to finish eating peaches and plums all over the mountains... for the rest of my life.
On a clear day, flowers bloom outside, butterflies fly, and birds sing happy songs on the branches. Guo Lu, who found food, was also like those lively and cute little birds. After eating wild fruits, she was chasing and playing with butterflies in the flowers.
Suddenly, another string of crisp sounds came from the branches: “Guo Lu Guo Lu, winter is no fruit!”
Guo Lu looked up and saw that little bird with green feathers, long beak, and fluff on its head was singing like a snail shell.
I saw it flapping its wings at Guolu and pecking wild fruit on the branch with its long shell, but it disappeared in a flash.
Guo Lu listened to the strange little bird with green feathers and had no intention of playing. He thought: The green feathered bird is right. The plants wither in the fall, and the mountains are closed by heavy snow in the winter, so there will be no more fruit to eat. Why don't you take advantage of the red fruits that are in full bloom now, pick more and keep them at home to save yourself from starving in the winter.
Once the idea was decided, Guo Lu picked fruit from the tree again and again and moved it home again and again. Pick, move, move, pick. After a few busy days, I left quite a few fruits at home, but the ones piled up underneath are rotten. It's been a long time since I came to Japan, how will I spend my days after that? Guo Lu was in a hurry to cry.
While Guo Lu was crying so sad, the cry of “Guo Lu Guo Lu” came from outside the door again.
Guo Lu wiped away her tears and walked out the door to take a look, and that little bird was talking to him again.
The bird said, “Guo Lu Guo Lu, Dong Zang Guo!”
Guo Lu was instructed by this little bird three times, and was very grateful. Just wanted to say a few words of thanks to it, only to see the bird flapping its wings at him and pecking the fruit on the tree with its long shell. It flashed and disappeared.
Guo Lu followed the instructions of the green feathered bird and hid the harvested fruit in a small cave behind the house. Sure enough, it wasn't moldy in the fall, and it didn't spoil in the winter. Until the second year, when the fruit was fresh and the branches of cinnamon were full, the old fruit was still fresh and fresh!
When Guo Lu grew up to the age of fourteen and five, she slowly pulled the hard bow that Grandpa left behind, and was able to dance with the vastness that Grandpa left behind. As a result, he stopped wearing an axe and began learning to hunt with a bow and arrow.
The beginning of everything is difficult. Guo Lu is a beginner in hunting. He can't shoot arrows or use an axe. When firing a bow, the place where the arrow falls is often far from the target; the axe doesn't have a point; sometimes an axe cuts it off and sticks firmly into the trunk of a tree, and can't pull it out for half a day.
However, Guo Lu was not discouraged. He practiced every day, one arrow in the east and one axe in the west, in the face of the scorching sun, braving wind and rain, and never stopped. Hungry, I climbed up the tree to pick a few wild fruits to satisfy my hunger; when I was thirsty, I went to the foot of the slope to drink two sips of spring water.
Kung Fu pays off. Guo Lu grew up to the age of 17 and was able to practice good hunting skills. It really was an arrow that didn't waste, and the axe didn't fall in the air; when the tit saw his soft wings, the beast saw him tremble.
Ten years of ups and downs have passed, and the small tree in front of the gate has grown into a big tree; Little Guolu has grown into a handsome and strong descendant.
As the saying goes, “People have big hearts.” Nineteen-year-old Guo Lu will think things through. He thought that in this vast forest sea, he was alone and had nothing to say. How lonely. It would be nice if someone had someone to talk to him. On this remote desolate slope, no one even asks when you're tired. How lonely, it would be great if you had someone to be with you!
Guo Lu went out hunting every day, and the mountains surrounding Yilaopo all sounded his moving song:
“Blue sky, no white clouds fluttering, it's not beautiful,
Aoyama is not beautiful without a clear spring flowing through it.
The moon is not beautiful without the stars,
Guolu! It's a bad day with no one to spend time with.”
One day, Guo Lu carried a bow and arrow, and went out to fight again without an axe. He went over Jiupiling, turned ten bends, and reached an unknown mountain.
Tall trees cover the sky, and old vines with thick legs cover the forest like dense cobwebs.
Feeling a little tired, he just lay down under an ancient tree. Remembering his situation, he unwittingly sang another song:
“The moon is missing. When it's round,
The flowers are thankful, and there will be a time for them to bloom again;
Year after year, I'm single with a lonely shadow partner!
When did Guo Lu have a happy time?”
Along with this moving song, a little bird appeared next to him again. Guo Lu took a close look. It turned out to be that little bird with green feathers again. He was overjoyed.
I saw that the bird jumped on the palm of his hand and pecked him gently; jumped on his chest and pecked him softly; then jumped on the open space beside his head and gently pecked him in the face.
Guo Lu sighed and said, “Green Bird, Green Bird, it would be great if you had someone to help me with you. Look, I'm single, I have nothing to talk about, and no one can help me. There is no warmth, no joy, but I am an individual! Green bird, green bird, if you were an individual, we would be together day and night, and we wouldn't be separated all the time.”
When the green bird heard Guo Lu's words, it only gently pecked Guo Lu's face, and disappeared after a while.
Guo Lu felt very sorry and lamented his life; not even a single bird wanted to be close to him.
Suddenly, I heard only a howl, and a strong wind rolled up, and a yellow-spotted white-fronted tiger with a 670 year old man in his mouth ran fast.
Saving people is important! Guo Lu turned over and stood up, and as soon as he pulled the string, the tiger was frightened and stopped immediately. It discovered that someone had Zhang Gong stopped them on their way, so they left the old man in their mouth and slammed at Guo Lu.
The hunter met the tiger and got excited. Guo Lu squatted down. The tiger swooped over his head, then immediately turned around and swooped at him.
Guo Lu bowed her body low in a hurry and ran behind the tiger with an arrow in front of the tiger.
Twice, the tiger lost its hair and turned around. I only heard it roar, shaking the mountain, and the dead branches on the tree crackled down. It supports its front claws with its back legs and pounces at Guolu with its mouth wide open.
Guo Lu's eyes were sharp and quick. An “arrow” hit it right in the tiger's throat.
With the sound of “whistling,” the tiger fell down. I just listened to it shout hehehehehe for a while, and after struggling a few more times, it stopped moving.
The tiger was shot to death. Guo Lu walked over to the old man and took a look, only to see him wearing green clothes and green pants, a snail shell on his head, being bitten through a hole in his leg, and bleeding out with blood. The old man has fainted.
Guo Lu quickly cut off a piece of tiger skin from his tiger coat, tightly wrapped his wound and blocked the blood; he also brought spring water from the ravine and poured it into the old population.
The old man slowly woke up.
When the old man saw the tiger being shot to death, he said to Guo Lu, “Kind-hearted young man, I'm already a person with loess buried all over my neck, so let me die here! Bring back the tiger soon. Tiger meat is enough for you to eat for ten days and a half months, and tiger skin can also make a beautiful and bright garment!”
Guo Lu said, “Old man, don't talk like that. I have no shortage of food and a lot to wear, so I have one less person to hang out with. Your old man will go and be with me. I'll serve you like your own son.” After speaking, don't wear an axe, take a bow and arrow, and walk back and forth carrying the old man.
As soon as Guo Lu went over a mountain with the old man on his back, he heard a breeze rushing through the ravine, and a giant python hurtling over the road. The giant python has a body the size of a water tank, a brain the size of a dustpan, and a 670 year old grandmother in its mouth.
Save people! Guo Lu hurriedly hid the old man in a roadside cave. Too late to say anything, he greeted the giant python without an axe or with a bow.
The giant python eats people without chewing; it can suck up hares a few feet away in one bite and swallow it vividly. At this point, as soon as I saw the giant python lift its head, the old grandmother was thrown a few feet away. The thick whiskers on the two handles and a bright red tongue stick out two or three feet long. It was so scary!
As long as the giant python ticked her brain, the old grandmother would die.
At a time of extreme urgency, Guo Lu “whoa whoa” fired three poisonous arrows in a row. The first shot the giant python's right eye, the second shot the giant python's throat, and the third shot the giant python's tongue.
The giant python was seriously injured. He immediately fell over his head and rushed towards Guo Lu as soon as he shouted.
Guo Lu hurriedly jumped behind a big rock and shot six poison arrows at the giant python in a row, but none of the arrows fell through.
The giant python only destroyed its power with nine arrows. It slowly slipped downward, and finally made a loud noise and rolled into the ditch.
The giant python rolled into the ditch, and a large area of wild grass and small trees was crushed; then it struggled, and all the old trees with thick buckets were swept away by it with its tail, and finally it slowly died.
The giant python was shot to death. Guo Lu took a look at the old grandmother, only to see her wearing green clothes and green pants, and a snail shell wrapped around her back of her head. Her eyes were closed, tears were flowing out in a flash, and she had already fainted.
Guo Lu carefully wiped away the old grandmother's tears and brought a cool, refreshing spring water into her mouth. Only then did the old grandmother slowly wake up.
Grandma knew that the giant python had died, but she couldn't see anything with her eyes because of the saliva that came out of the python's mouth, and she said, “Kind young man, I'm already a person with loess buried all over my neck, so let me die here! Get that big python back quickly. The python meat is enough for you to eat for a year and a half, and the python skin can also have a big, loud flower drum!”
Guo Lu said, “Old people, don't talk about it like that. I eat a lot. I don't need a flower drum; I won't need your old man's family to be my companion. I'll serve you like your own son!” When I finished speaking, I walked to the side of the giant python's head shell and pulled out the big python's left eye with an axe. The eyeball was big enough; I also tied a rattan and hung it around my neck, and ran to the cave.
He told the old man: Take the old grandmother who had just been rescued back home, then carry him back. After speaking, I came back and walked home with my old grandmother on my back.
From Guo Lu's home, there are 13 bends in the Jiuling Mountains, but he's not afraid to go; although the old grandmother was very thin, she weighed on Guo Lu's back like a mountain; he wasn't too heavy.
Go, go, Guo Lu was sweaty; go, go, go, Guo Lu was so tired that she couldn't catch her breath. There wasn't a single complaint. She thought to herself: Now there are two old people in the company. The more he thought about it, the more happy he was, and the more energetic he walked.
It got dark as I walked, but Guo Lu didn't think it at all. Turns out, the light from Guo Lu's python-eye hanging around his neck illuminated his path.
It was almost midnight before they got home.
Guo Lu carefully placed the old grandmother in bed, and was reluctant to sit down and take a break, so she turned around and ran to carry the old man back.
Guo Lu had these two elderly people in her family. She was extremely happy. She only listened to him go out singing and humming in the door:
“White clouds fluttering in the sky, and springs flowing in the mountains,
Daredevil man who brought down the dragon and the tiger, got a taste of being a human today.”
Every morning before going up the mountain to hunt and dig up medicine, Guo Lu always grills the animal meat to a crisp, raw, and place it next to the pillows of the two elderly people to eat when they are hungry; scoop out sweet, cool, and laid-back spring water and place them next to the pillows of the two elderly people, so they can drink it when they are thirsty.
I came back in the evening and was busy cooking medicine. Although I was very tired, I was very happy!
Time went by so fast. In a blink of an eye, seven seven seven forty-nine days had passed. The injuries of the two elderly people had healed and their health had improved.
One day, two old men called Guo Lu to him and said to him, “You are a kind and brave young man. May your days ahead be as sweet as the water in a snail cave!”
As soon as the conversation came to an end, the two old men disappeared in a blink of an eye.
Guo Lu was shocked. She was busy taking off the dangling python's eye. She searched inside and outside the house, but couldn't find it.
He had to go home and hang up his pythons, lie in his tiger bed and cry sadly.
Crying and crying, I fell asleep crying.
As soon as Guo Lu woke up, everything had changed. Instead of sleeping on the old hard tiger skin, I slept on a brand-new bed shelf.
The bed had flower covers, flower pillows, flower sheets, and a blue tiger cage.
Guo Lu was wondering if she was dreaming, and there was another pleasant singing voice from outside:
“Red flowers are meant to be green leaves; Guo Lu is paired with snails;
After ten years of loneliness, the days have been beautiful for a long time!”
Guo Lu got out of bed in a hurry and quietly looked for the song. As soon as I crossed the threshold, I saw a fairy-like girl preparing a hot, fragrant dish on the table!
Seeing Guo Lu come out, the girl stopped singing, smiled at Guo Lu, and warmly greeted her: “Sit down, it's time to eat breakfast!”
“Which one are you? Which house is this? Why am I here?”
Guo Lu didn't seem to hear the girl's greeting, but she stood there in a daze.
“Have you forgotten that little bird with green feathers, a long beak, and fluff on its head that looks like a snail shell?” The girl said.
Guo Lu didn't seem to understand, and asked, “What's your name? What are you bringing me here to do?”
The girl said, “My name is Snail Girl. I'm an aquatic person in a snail cave. Did you forget what you said to me the other day? You saved my parents and healed their wounds. They asked me to be with you. This building and small pond are my dowry; this is the reunion dinner my sister made for us.”
Guo Lu listened and was so happy that she took the girl's hand and said affectionately, “Sister Snail, you are so nice! Our days will definitely be sweeter than the water in the snail cave in the future!”
While eating, Guo Lu had such a delicious meal, and Sister Snail smiled so sweetly.
After Guo Lu and Sister Luo became husband and wife, they opened a plot of land at their doorstep, built a field, sowed seeds, harvested well year after year, and lived a happy life as a crop farmer.
Since then, people have grown crops as Guo Lu and the snail girl did.
Later, many people moved to live in Yilaopo. They all called Yilaopo. Very few people know the name Yilaopo.
Now, when people carry hoes to work, they always think of Guo Lu and Sister Luo, singing songs they've missed at the top of Tianba Mountain:
“Luojiepo, ah, nice place. Guo Lu married a nice girl.
My first mother is a snail girl. Hard work and beauty are unrivaled in the world.
She can do plowing fields and raking; order wheat to transplant seedlings, she opens...”
