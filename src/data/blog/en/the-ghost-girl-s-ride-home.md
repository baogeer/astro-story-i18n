 Since I was a kid, I grew up listening to the stories of my grandmother and my neighbors about Ox, Ghost, Snake, and God. Therefore, if you believe in strange things, there are no such things as believing or not believing.
After graduating from high school, I lost my job, and I didn't find any work. So I learned how to use a car book and became a car driver for long-distance transportation. This is a chore. It took three or four days to get to the route to Liaoning. I was alone on the road, and I couldn't feel anything other than the sound of the wind outside the window and the occasional car coming across from me.
After New Year's Day in 1999, I remember very clearly because on that day, I was in Fushun. I found a small ring in front of the motel. It's about silver, a little thicker than a ring. As soon as I wiped it off, it was quite bright, so I put it in my jacket pocket...
I drove to Tieling that day.
When the sky gets dark. Someone stopped the bus on the side of the road, so I had to take a ride. Normally, I don't care about this kind of thing. This is taboo when it comes to long-distance cars. You know what kind of people they are!
But that day, I stopped my car. Because the underground is a young girl, so beautiful, you can see that she is in good shape from afar. Everyone is a man, hehe, they don't reveal their feelings to each other anymore.
She got in the car and sat next to me. This girl has a pretty sweet mouth. One by one, I'm happy to take her home even if I take a detour.
After talking, I learned that she went out to work and worked outside as a waitress. This is less than old; she is going back to celebrate the New Year.
I don't know the place she said. It's a small place. I went down a big asphalt road and drove a small dirt road to get there. There was a big acacia tree at the entrance of the village, and I barely bumped into it at the time, so I remember it quite honestly.
She said that the third largest family in the village was hers, and they didn't have to let me go in and take a break. It's not early, and I don't want to delay in this small place, so I said no.
Seeing that I didn't want to, she pulled out a large 50 bill from her pocket and insisted that it be the fare. Where did such a good thing come from? I was disappointed. Later, she insisted on giving it, and when there was no choice, I took it.
“Big girl, this is too much. Let's just say, I'll get you 20 yuan, this is always it!”
She smiled sweetly: “Yes, let's just do it, then thank you!”
When I arrived in Tieling, I felt like I had a dream with a smile on my face.
When I woke up in the morning and ate breakfast, I took out the money. No, why are there... coins. It was 50 yuan she gave yesterday. Yes, I'm so stupid. I said there's no such good thing. At the end of the day, I gave them 20 yuan, which is really stupid!
The shipment arrived and I was fine. I was relaxed when I came back. I just passed the fork where I sent that girl last time. Thinking about how wrong you were to be deceived, just go check on her. After all, it only took three days to see if she could resist it!
I also saw a big tree at the entrance of the village. So from the village count, the third one...
Arrived. The one who opened the door was an old lady. The dark ones are thin, but the people are quite strong. As soon as I saw it, I fainted a bit, and still muttered in my mouth: “Why? Is that really true?” She went back and called the people in the house, and another young man and an old man came out.
Before God came back, they let me into the house.
It only became clear later. That young man is the son of two old men, and he also has a younger sister. I went out to work a year ago, and later I received a letter saying they would come back for the New Year. Everyone was very happy. However, it's been a week since the date was set, and I haven't seen her come back, and there's no news.
Three days ago, the old lady said she had a dream that her daughter had returned. They also told them that they had gone wrong. They also said that a big car guy will come to her and tell the family that she is back. Then the old lady woke up. My heart has never been broken.
I was a little convinced when I saw it today.
I took another picture and let me see if I could meet his daughter. I was terrified and sweaty as soon as I saw it. It's not who she is! Her figure is like a willow, her big beautiful eyes, and... she has a red rope around her neck and a silver white ring tied underneath...
The old lady said that the girl went to the temple to ask for it when she was a child. I've always carried it as an amulet.
When I shuddered and took that thing out, the old lady and the old man immediately cried: “Something must have happened to her; she never left!”
I drove them out to report the crime. Based on where this amulet was lost, the police felt that the incident should have happened in Fushun, so they contacted the police there again. I have also become a target of surveillance and cannot leave Fushun.
Actually, things soon came to an end.
Her body was identified in an unnamed corpse based on the photograph. Moreover, when it was discovered, it was determined to be a case of rape and murder. The murderer was a long-distance bus driver passing by, and the location of the incident was in the car hotel where I was staying.
If everything were as normal as I said, I might have treated it as a telepathy between loved ones and disregarded it, but when we went to find the corpse, I was surprised to find that she still held 20 yuan in her left hand...
