 I am a student at a medical school in a certain city. Our school is located in the suburbs of the city. It is said that this was a execution ground before liberation. Countless people were executed, died, and slept here for a long time. So people of the older generation always think this is the most unlucky place, which means there are... ghosts here.
Before this story happened, I was an atheist, and I never believed such ridiculous things could happen in the world. But my four-year college campus life taught me that there really is a world other than nature!
There is a school worker in our dorm room. She is an old lady who is 50 or 60 years old. Her face is full of wrinkles, triangular eyes, rickets, and an old fashioned head. The old man doesn't like to talk very much. She always works silently. She never comes home. She lives in our warehouse less than 10 square meters downstairs. When she's not cleaning, she rests in the house, and the small door is always closed. She never touched our dirty and messy dorm room; sometimes we greeted her, and she responded softly. After being in touch with her for a long time, we slowly learned from her mouth that she is a lonely old man. Her old partner died two years ago. Since she didn't have an old insurance, her son didn't want her, so she went to our school to clean up, eat and stay at school, so she didn't pay anything.
We all think the old man is very poor, but no one wants to have more contact with her! Can't say why.
One day in the second semester of our sophomore year, the students in our entire building felt that the dorm room always had a strange smell. It smelled really bad, and even covered up their noses when the college leaders came. The situation continued for almost a week until we -- suddenly discovered that the old lady cleaning had not been seen for a long time! We reported this situation to the dean, and he immediately sent someone to investigate. This smell did come from the old lady's hut, but her door was locked back as usual. The security office immediately knocked on the door. After opening the door, it was discovered that the old man had been dead in the house for a long time. She had a pungent smell all over her body, and there were still a few maggots crawling in her nostrils and ears. A few people in our room threw up at the time. She also holds a picture of a middle-aged man in her hand; presumably her filial son.
The old man had no family members, so our school decided without permission to hand over the old man's autopsy to the Department of Forensic Medicine so that it could be used for demonstration purposes during class. In this way, the old man's corpse was first pushed onto an autopsy table, then cooked at high temperature and pressure to peel off the flesh of the epidermis, and finally dismembered into bones piece by piece.
Soon, our dorm building was quiet again, and the person cleaning the dorm was replaced by a healthy old man, and the students gradually forgot about this incident. However, the good times didn't last long.
Once, the old man in our bedroom woke up in the middle of the night and went to the toilet. He kept his sleepy eyes open and walked in the direction of the toilet one step at a time. Suddenly, he felt cool behind him. Looking back violently, through the moonlight, he saw an old lady standing in the hallway, only four or five meters from him, with her legs bent and her head crossed. Her eyes were suddenly wide open and staring at him, and blood was still bleeding from the corners of her eyes... The old man immediately woke up. He used a trembling voice and said, “You, who are you?”
The old lady kept staring at him and didn't answer. The senior didn't think it was right. He didn't even dare to think about it, so he turned around and ran to his bedroom. At this point, the old lady trembled and got closer and closer, crying while walking.
“My old partner is... miserable... well, my old partner... is dead, my son... doesn't want me, and when I'm about to die, and even be steamed and cooked... and even leave all of the dead bodies behind, I hate ~ die ~ you ~ ah ~ ~!”
The older three were all freaked out. Their legs and stomachs all turned. Walking a few steps to the bedroom was like running for years. When they arrived in the bedroom, they woke us all up. As soon as they told us about this, we were all excited. There was still a power outage in the bedroom at night. We got out of bed. One person carried a chair in their hands and shouted “who, who——-” to bolster themselves while stepping out of the door, but how many old ladies are there! All that was left was a silent hallway and windows rattling in the wind. We never dared to sleep anymore. The two young men huddled in one bed and vacated the other four lower bunks. The eight of us were staring at the door, and our minds were tense all the time. When we looked at each other, we all saw the helpless and fearful eyes in each other's eyes. People who haven't experienced this kind of thing may never feel this kind of feeling. I don't know when this scary scene will suddenly appear.
At this point, I didn't know what happened and got a serious illness. Inexplicably, I hid under the covers and shivered all day long. Over the next few days, many more things happened in our building. There were always people who heard a woman crying in the building. In the middle of the night, sometimes the faucet was unscrewed and closed from time to time, and they could also hear loud sweeping sounds.
We were really afraid that it wouldn't work, so we reported this incident to the hospital leaders. The hospital leaders didn't believe it at first, but they couldn't help but believe what we said. Later, the hospital finally took action to recover the skull and torso of the old lady who had been cleaning, put them together again, and sent to the funeral parlor for cremation. As a result, we never heard the old lady crying at night~~~. My junior's illness also miraculously improved. However, when we walked downstairs and passed by her cottage, we always had a creepy feeling, just like the senior said, “It's cool behind...”
We don't dare to stay here anymore; we asked the school to change our beds. Soon we moved to another dorm building. This old dormitory building was left for new students to live in; anyway, they didn't know about it. After that, it seemed like we had an agreement, and no one dared to mention this again.
However, we moved to a new building. Will everything be fine? We--it's too simple to think.
