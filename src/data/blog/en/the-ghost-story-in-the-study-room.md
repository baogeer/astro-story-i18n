This is a true story.
China's politics and law is a very good university, so its students also enjoy studying. The main character of the story is a hardworking boy. He works really hard, and he buries his head in the study room on the third floor until late at night.
Another day when people were quiet, the boy was writing XYZ and ABC in the study room, and the sound of high heels tickled in the dark hallway. The boy was nervous, and he didn't pay attention. Suddenly, the back door of the study room creaked and opened, and a clear, beautiful female voice sounded in the classroom, “Can you ask me to dance?” The boy looked up, glared in four eyes, and looked at the back door in bewilderment. A beautiful, beautiful, but not blood-colored face appeared at the door. The boy was suddenly interrupted and very angry, then shouted: “Sorry, it's too late today. I'm going back to rest. Besides, you also have to dance in the ballroom. When you come to the dance hall on the weekend, I'll just invite you alone. OK, bye, I'm going back to rest!” Afterwards, he packed up his book, pinched it under his armpit, and went back to bed.
The next day, the boy was studying on his own in that study room again. Or yesterday, the girl's voice appeared on time, “Can you ask me to dance?” The boy was very angry and said, “Didn't I tell you that I invited you to the ballroom on the weekend!” At this point, the sound of the old man watching the building sounded downstairs: “Classmate, it's time to go, it's time to close the door!” As a result, the boy went downstairs with the book and said to Grandpa Lou downstairs, “Hello!” The old man watching the building said, “Classmate, you are alone; you are working too hard!” The boy said, “No, there's another girl!” Grandpa said, “No, I only saw you!” As soon as the boy thought about it, no, apparently there was another beautiful girl, why... well... Thinking about this, the boy couldn't help but beat his heart and hurriedly went back to his bedroom to sleep.
On the night of the third day, the boy got scared, so he found a boy in the same bedroom to study on his own and called him when he left. However, something happened to the boy. He went out alone and forgot to call him when he left. The boy was studying, and before he knew it, it was that time again. As soon as the boy saw the time, it was that time again. There was no one around. He was standing up and leaving, and the girl's voice appeared on time again: “Can you ask me to dance?” The boy just wanted to reject her. On second thought, he didn't dare, but she was a ghost; he didn't dare to mess with her. Then he shuddered and said, “OK, I'll ask you to dance!” When the girl walked into the classroom, dressed in white, the boy danced with the girl on the podium. Suddenly, a “knock knock” sound sounded at the door. It was the old man who was watching the door, “Classmate, it's so late, you haven't gone back. What are you doing?” “I'm dancing with a girl!” “Girl, where is the girl from?” “I was just dancing with her!” The boy spoke to himself. The old man asked what the girl looked like and said, “The girl you are talking about is like the one on the road next to this building who was hit and killed by a car the year before. A very beautiful girl. What a pity!” He looked down and his hands were full of blood.
One night a week later, a scream came from the boy upstairs studying on his own. People saw that it was the boy who jumped from the upper floor and committed suicide. I don't know the reason!
