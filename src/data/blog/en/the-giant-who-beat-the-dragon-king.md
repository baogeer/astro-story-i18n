 When the tide of the Qiantang River originally came, just like the tide in other parts of the world, there was no tide or sound.
One year, a giant came to the Qiantang River. This giant was really tall. It crossed from one side of the river to the other side of the river in one step. He lives on Shushan Mountain in Xiaoshan County and ignites salt. People don't know his name, because he lives by the Qiantang River, so they call him King Qian.
Qian Dawang is very strong. With his iron load, he often picks large boulders and places them by the river. Soon, he built up mountains one by one.
One day, he went to pick the salt he had been burning in Shushan for 3 years and 3 months. However, this salt was only enough for him, so he attached a large rock to the other end of his flat load, put it on his shoulder, and just picked it up and crossed over to the northern bank of the Jiangbei River.
At this point, the weather was hot, and Qian Dawang had lunch because he was a little tired, so he put down his burdens and took a break. Unexpectedly, he fell asleep.
Coincidentally, Dragon King of the East China Sea came out to cruise the river at this time, and the tide rose. What went up and went up actually rose ashore, slowly dissolving all of Qian Dawang's salt. Dragon King in the East China Sea heard that where did the saltiness come from in the water, and it is getting more salty and saltier. He couldn't stand it; he ran back and fled. He didn't want to escape to the ocean, making all the water in the ocean salty.
This Qian Dawang, who took a nap, saw that the stone on one side of Bian was still placed in Kip Shek (now known as Mount Kip Shek), while the salt on the other side was gone!
Qian Dawang looked for it. He couldn't find salt. As soon as he looked down, he smelled salty in the river. He thought: Oh, no wonder the salt is gone; it turned out it was stolen by Dragon King of the East China Sea. So he picked up his flat load and poured seawater.
Fish large and small were shocked to death when beaten with one flat load; beating the river bottom with two flat burdens turned over; the dragon king of the East China Sea came out of the water to ask for forgiveness.
Dragon King of the East China Sea asked King Qian with trepidation why he had such a big temper. Qian Dawang said, “Where did you steal my salt?” Only then did Dragon King of the East China Sea understand why seawater became salty. He quickly got his crime, and talked about how he cruised the river and how he inadvertently dissolved Qian Dawang's salt, making the ocean water salty.
Qian Dawang was so excited. He really wanted to lift the iron bar and smash Dragon King in the East China Sea before he was satisfied. Dragon King in the East China Sea panicked and even prostrated to ask for forgiveness, and promised to use seawater to pay compensation to King Qian; later, when the tide rises, he shouts so that King Qian falls asleep and is unable to hear.
When Qian Dawang heard that these two conditions were pretty good, he spared the East China Sea Dragon King, put his own flat on the bay of Hangzhou, and said, “When the tide comes, it will start from here!” Dragon King of the East China Sea repeatedly agreed; only then did Qian Dawang walk away happy.
From that point on, as soon as the tide entered Hangzhou Bay, it stretched out his neck and screamed “loudly”. It rose to the place where King Qian had been sitting, and his neck stretched high and screamed loudly. This place is present-day Haining. This is how the world-famous “Qianjiang Tide” came about.
