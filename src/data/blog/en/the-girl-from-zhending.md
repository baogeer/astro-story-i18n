 In the real world, there is an orphan girl who was 67 years old and became a child daughter-in-law. After a year or two, her husband lured her to share the same room. Since then, she became pregnant, and her stomach gradually grew bigger. I thought I was sick, so I told my mother-in-law. The mother-in-law asked, “Are you still moving?” The answer was, “Move.” The mother-in-law thought it was strange, but she was afraid to decide because the girl was too young. It didn't take long for me to have a boy. The mother-in-law sighed and said, “Unexpectedly, the little mother with big fists actually gave birth to a child the size of an awl!”
