 There is another more mythical version of the story about the Meng Jiang woman crying over the Great Wall. Few people know about it. The legend is like this:
Lady Lan Xiang is a nice girl who respects her mother-in-law. Her mother-in-law suddenly fell ill. She didn't touch her teeth, and she couldn't see it. Lady Lan Xiang is in a hurry. What should I do? The mother-in-law said, “If I want to cure my illness, I will go to the north to collect magic medicine.” Lady Lan Xiang set up a table in the hospital, burnt three incense, knelt down and knocked three heads. With tears in her eyes, Lan Xiang said, “Everything is above all. Lan Xiang really saved her wife. If I were sincere, don't let me get hurt when climbing mountains and rivers; if I had false intentions, let me die to see Hades.” Lady Lan Xiang left alone and brought only a wooden stick to be her companion. Overturning a mountain, crossing a hill, the sun sets, and the moon rises, day and night, and I went back and forth. In the evening, I came under a large mountain and looked around. There was no village, no one. Look at that mountain. The cliff was so old and steep, it was dizzying. There was only a small narrow path leading to the top of the mountain. In order to collect medicine for her mother-in-law, Lady Lan Xiang was not afraid of anything. She took a wooden stick and climbed the path with difficulty... Lady Lan Xiang finally climbed the most dangerous place. The road ahead was wide and the ground was flat, but she was still far from the top of the mountain. At this point, I heard a sneer laughter behind me. Looking back, they were three tall people, with blue clothes, long hair, dark, dark faces, and blue eyes. They were laughing heheheheh, showing white teeth like nails, and pouncing at Lady Lan Xiang. Lady Lan Xiang threw her legs and ran. The three black-faced and blue-eyed people followed closely... The sun was about to set, and the sky was about to get dark. Lady Lan Xiang ran up the mountain and stood on a large, egg-shaped white stone. Next to the white stone, it was an abyss, and she could hear the sound of the wind. Lady Lan Xiang thought: I must never let those three catch me; I can only jump off a cliff. Died, Lady Lan Xiang wasn't afraid at all, but she thought: I went out to get medicine for my grandmother. My grandmother is still sick. If I can't find medicine, what can I do? How can you be worthy of a mother-in-law? Lady Lan Xiang watched the three get closer and closer to herself. Knowing that she could no longer find medicine for her mother-in-law, she held the wooden stick that accompanied her, and cried sadly:
Poor me, Lan Xiang Xiang, who is alone. She can no longer take medicine and go home to save her wife. I just hate myself for not being able to grow wings. I can't save my wife anymore! My good wife... Lady Lan Xiang cried and burst into tears. She was about to jump off a cliff, and a voice sounded under her feet: “Good girl, don't be sad, I'll help you if you have any difficulties.” Lady Lan Xiang looked down. The white stone under her feet turned into a white horse. She was on horseback. Seeing that the three were only a few dozen steps away from themselves, they raised the wooden sticks in their hands and hit them hard at the horse's tail. Immediately, the horse took off carrying Lady Lan Xiang. However, the horse's tail was knocked off at the top of the mountain and turned into a stone wall, blocking the three black-faced and blue-eyed people. This section of the stone wall was the earliest section of the Great Wall.
Lady Lan Xiang is flying in the air on a white horse. The kind woman Lan Xiang is so tired that she sleeps on horseback. In her dream, Lan Xiang woman is still with White Horse. Bai Ma told Lan Xiang Nu, “If you don't knock off my tail, I can't fly. Now I can fly, but the blood on my body is about to run out.” As soon as Lady Lan Xiang heard it, she hurriedly touched her ponytail. “Ah, it's all blood. What can I do about this?” At this point, Hakuba also said, “Lan Xiangxiang, good girl, you really saved your wife. Your wife's illness is better.” As soon as Lan Xiang Nu heard that her mother-in-law's illness had improved, she felt a burst of joy. However, Hakuba shed tears. Hakuba said sadly, “Lady Lan Xiang, I can't fly away; I'm going to fall; if I fall, it turns into a stone. What can you do? I saved you, but now... I want to hurt you...” Lan Xiang said with tears in her eyes, “My sweet white horse, you will always be my friend. Now that my wife is well ill, my wish has come true, I would like to go with you and never be separated.” The sky was dark, the night was dark, the moon was hidden, the stars closed their eyes, and a fire flew up on a mountain. White Horse and Lady Lan Xiang fell down together. Hakuba turned into a rock, and Lan Xiang Nu became a melon seed and fell on the rock that Hakuba turned into.
Legend has it that wherever the Great Wall was built, white horse blood dripped. No matter how far the Great Wall was, the white horse flew as far as the Lady Lan Xiang. A hundred years later, the melon seeds, which had changed from the Lan Xiang woman, were taken by magpies to the courtyard wall shared by the Meng family and the Jiang family. These melon seeds sprout, grew long, blossomed, and produced a huge gourd. There was a little girl in that gourd; that kid was Lan Xiang Xiang. However, no one knows her name is Lan Xiangxiang; they all call her Meng Jiang Nu. Later, Meng Jiang's husband was taken to build the Great Wall. She never came back. Meng Jiang followed the Great Wall looking for it, crying while looking for it. I cried 800 miles to the Great Wall. Why did the Great Wall fall? Because the earliest Great Wall was changed to a white horse's tail, the Great Wall knows the sad past of Meng Jiang Nu, so as soon as Meng Jiang Nu cries, the Great Wall also falls down for a while...
