 There is a painter in the city of Sizhou named Du Feng'e. He doesn't paint birds or animals, nor flowers, fish, or bugs, but he loves to paint foxes. Du Feng'e often quietly goes to the mountains to observe foxes. Once, he spent a whole day in the mountain jungle and saw no sign of a fox. Seeing that it was almost dark, he had no choice but to go home. Walking alone on the Sheep Gut Trail, Du Feng'e was in a bit of a bad mood. Suddenly, he saw a hunter walking up the fork in front of him, carrying a fox with a leg wound on his shoulder. This is a young fox. The red fur shines brightly. Looking at the little fox's timid eyes and asking for help, Du Feng'e grew a hint of pity in his heart and harassed the hunter to buy the fox. After returning home, Du Feng'e carefully scrubbed the young fox's wound with salt water and applied herbal medicine to it. Under Du Feng'e's careful care, the little fox soon got well. This little fox is really clever. As soon as the wound was healed, it jumped up and down in the cage and made various positions to please Du Feng'e. Du Feng'e was overjoyed and took the opportunity to draw many sketches.
One night, Du Feng'e dreamed that a fox in a cage was talking to him. Remembering the legend of the fox fairy, he asked, “Is the legend that foxes can become humans true or false?” The fox said, “What you believe is true; if you don't believe, it's false.” Du Feng'e said, “Then become a beautiful woman to be my daughter-in-law!” The fox said, “Then let me go first.” Du Feng'e went to open the cage with half a belief and suddenly stumbled and woke up. Remembering the scene in my dream, it was a bit strange, so I subconsciously opened the cage. The little fox didn't turn into a beautiful woman, but “whistled” and disappeared into the night outside the door. The next night, as soon as Du Feng'e fell asleep, he was woken up by a sound coming from outside the door. He was taken aback when he opened the door. It turned out that there was a large group of foxes outside the door. Seeing him come out, a fox immediately ran over to grab his pants with its claws and lick his ankles with its tongue. It was the red fox he had let go the night before yesterday.
A few nights after another, the group of foxes came to Du Feng'e's yard to play, chase and roll around. Du Feng'e suddenly understood that it was the red fox who wanted to repay him, so he invited this group of foxes to paint. With so many cute models, Du Feng'e's fox paintings have been even more exciting since then. However, in the folk world, the fox is an ominous symbol. No one wants to buy this unlucky thing to hang at home, and Du Feng'e is still poor.
One day, Du Feng'e was selling paintings on the street. A woman in red walked up to his stall and bought two paintings of foxes at a high price. This is a rare occurrence. Du Feng'e looked at the woman with some strangeness. She only saw that she had bright eyes, a graceful appearance, and a faint hint of grievance between her eyebrows. The woman looked a little embarrassed, rolled up the painting in a hurry and left. The next day, Du Feng'e's art stand was just set up. The woman who bought the paintings yesterday came again and bought two more paintings at a high price. This was the case for a few days in a row.
Du Feng'e was puzzled. When the woman came back, he asked, “Who are you, and why do you have to buy so many paintings?” The woman smiled and didn't speak. Du Feng'e said, “If you don't tell me why, I won't sell this painting.” The woman said, “If you sell paintings, I'll pay, how can you not sell them?” Du Feng'e's temper came up and said, “If you don't talk, I won't sell.” The woman looked at him helplessly and left in silence.
Who knows, that woman actually came to the door at night. Du Feng'e was surprised and asked, “How did you find my house?” The woman smiled and said, “I'm just afraid I'll scare you if I say it.” Du Feng'e said, “What's there to be afraid of?” The woman said, “Actually, I'm a fox fairy. Because I love your paintings and I'm moved by your infatuation with foxes, I often buy one or two of your paintings and enjoy them. I didn't expect you to be a strange person. He went to the bottom of the street and asked me how to answer it?” The two had a conversation. When she left, the woman gave Du Feng'e some silver coins. Du Feng'e didn't accept the offer. The woman said, “Take it. I know spells. Money is easy to come by. Besides, I got this money from those bad guys.” Du Feng'e had no choice but to accept it. Later, the woman often sat at Du Feng'e's office to help him polish his ink and tidy up his desk. The two got along very well.
On this day, Du Feng'e was selling paintings on the street and heard people say that today the county government would kill a woman in a green house. It turns out that the son of the prefectural governor went wild in the famous Qinglou “Senkaku” and was unbearably killed by a Qinglou woman. The son of the county governor relied on his father's power and carried out many acts of evil in Sizhou. People dared to get angry at him but were afraid to speak up. This time, he was killed by a Qinglou woman. It was really a source of joy. People praised the woman for her strength, and they were also outraged that she couldn't escape her misfortune. Du Feng'e also crowded into the crowd to watch. Oh, isn't this the woman who bought his paintings, the fox fairy? Du Feng'e hated and couldn't help him right away, but he was a weak scholar, anxious but helpless. Du Feng'e suddenly remembered that she was a fox fairy and could do spells, so he was relieved.
Speaking of late, it was too late. I saw that the executioner cut it off with a knife, blood splashed all over, and the fox fairy's head split and fell to the ground. The crowd gradually dispersed, and Du Feng'e ran over to caress Fox Xian's gradually cold corpse. I couldn't guess if her real body had escaped. Suddenly, Du Feng'e heard someone nearby crying. It was a woman about the age of a fox fairy. While crying, the woman looked up and asked Du Feng'e, “Are you Du Caizi?” Du Feng'e said, “Exactly.” The woman caressed the corpse and said, “I often hear Sister Crimson talk about you.” Du Feng'e asked, “Is the fox fairy called Crimson? What kind of person are you?” The woman's voice choked and said, “Where is she a fox fairy! She, like me, is a woman from a poor family. She was forced to do nothing by the villains of the county ordinance before falling into red dust. Sister Crimson really likes your paintings. She adores your talent. When she learned that her life was difficult, she wanted to help you, and she was afraid that you would know the truth, so she only joked with you that she was a fox fairy.”
It was as if Du Feng'e had been hit with a stick. After staying for half a day, she held Crimson's corpse and cried.
