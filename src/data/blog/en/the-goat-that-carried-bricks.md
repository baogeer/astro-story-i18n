 In Jiayuguan City, the city wall is 9 meters high. Dozens of pavilions of various sizes and numerous stacked walls had to be built on top of the city wall. The amount of bricks used was astonishing. At the time, the construction conditions were very poor. There was no lifting equipment, and all were carried by hand. At the time, the bricks used to build the city were all fired 40 miles away. After the bricks are burnt, they are pulled under the castle with an ox cart, and then manually turned upwards. Due to the height of the city, the only road that can go up and down is very steep, and getting up and down is very difficult. Although many people were sent to put bricks on the city wall and were exhausted, the bricks on their backs were still in short supply, and the progress of the project was seriously affected. One day, a child herding sheep came here to herd sheep. Seeing this scene, he untied the belt, tied a brick at each end, placed it on the goat's body, and patted the sheep's back. The lightweight goat trotted with bricks and climbed up the city wall. People were surprised and delighted, and followed suit, and a large number of bricks were soon transported to the city wall.
