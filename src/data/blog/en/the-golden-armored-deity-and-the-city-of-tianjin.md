 In the public, people also call Tianjin City Abacus City because its walls are long east to west and short north to south, making it look like a big abacus. This is said to be related to the god Golden Armor.
In the early years of the Ming dynasty, after Liu Bowen built the capital city in Beijing, he discovered that Beijing was surrounded by mountains on three sides; only the south side was flat and the gateway was wide open; in addition, he also discovered that Tianjin was a land and water frontier, making it very easy to communicate between North and South. As a result, Liu Bowen decided to build a city pool here, one to protect Beijing, and the other to strengthen exchanges between North and South. However, he was still hesitant about where to build the castle, and the shortage of funds was also a major problem.
One night, Liu Bowen, who couldn't sleep, went out and walked around at will. Walking south of the Sancha estuary, I suddenly saw a giant with a golden helmet and gold armor standing in front of me, shining brightly. Liu Bowen knows that this is the legendary god of the Golden Armor. I saw the Golden Armor sitting there holding a golden drum with his left hand and an abacus in his right hand, without saying a word. While Liu Bowen was feeling Nahan, the Golden Armor turned into a light ray of smoke and disappeared.
The next day, Liu Bowen asked people to carry out excavations in the place where the god Golden Armor used to sit. Soon, they dug a two-foot square gold brick on each of the four corners of the east, west, north, and south. Liu Bowen was very happy, so he ordered the construction of a three-story drum tower in the place where Jin Jiashen once sat. The tower opened four doors, called Gongbei, Dingnan, Zhendong, and Anxi. Next, with the drum tower as the center, a rectangular castle wall was built around it according to the shape of the abacus taken by the Golden Armor god. The west of the city is 504 feet long, the north and south are 324 feet long, and the total circumference of the city is nine miles 18 steps long, like a huge abacus. Later, with the Gulou as the center, four streets were opened. The two ends of the street were the Gulou Tower and the city gate, respectively.
This is the prototype of the later city of Tianjin.
