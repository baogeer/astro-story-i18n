 Once upon a time, there was a crook living in a village. As soon as he sees something good in someone else, he has to find a way to trick him. Seeing the village leader, he nodded and bowed like a pug dog.
There is a very smart person in the village called Zaba. One day, he was fetching water at the well, saw the crook coming from afar and thought, “I want to teach him a good lesson today.” So I took off my clothes and felt like I was going to jump into the well. The scammer rushed over and asked, “Zabba, what are you doing down the well on such a cold day?” “Ahhh, when I was fetching water, I accidentally dropped the gold bar I was carrying in my arms into the well; I have to catch it!” Zaba pretended to be in a hurry and answered.
“Ahhh, you're such a big fool! On such a cold day, if you go down such a deep well, aren't you afraid to give up your little life as thin as a horse's tail?” The scammer hurriedly said. “Yeah, yeah, you're right!” Zaba seemed to suddenly realise, “If you lose your life, what's the use of gold bars? As long as I'm alive, I won't worry about not having gold bars in my hands.” After speaking, Zaba got dressed and went home.
Actually, Zaba never came home. He quietly hid behind a broken wall, staring at the crook without blinking his eyes. The scammer saw Zaba go a long distance, quickly took off her clothes, tied a rope around her waist, tied one end of the rope to a large rock near the well, and went down to the well. At this point, Zaba rushed to the edge of the well, cut off the rope, and lay down at the wellhead and shouted, “Hey, you scammer, slowly catch gold bars in the well!”
The scammer knew he had been fooled by Zaba. The well was cold and harsh, and the crook froze all over his body and shouted out in the well: “Please, friend! There are no gold bars at the bottom of the well, please save me!” Zabahha smiled and said, “Open your donkey's mouth and shout! Let those people who are bullied by you on weekdays come to your rescue!” After speaking, I built a stone slab at the wellhead and went home.
