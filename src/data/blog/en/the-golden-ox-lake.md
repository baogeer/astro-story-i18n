 Once upon a time, West Lake was called Jinniu Lake.
At that time, the lake was still a vast expanse of white water, and along the lakeshore there was black oil and fertile land. The surrounding people all grow crops there, use lake water to irrigate, and the ears of rice grow round like a string of pearls. Farmers are idle, so everyone is fishing for shrimp on the lake. People live in harmony and live a peaceful life.
At the bottom of this lake, there lives a Taurus. As soon as the weather clears up for a long time, the taurus in the lake appears: from afar, you can see its golden spine, raised head, and raised horns. It spits out a big mouth of water, and the lake immediately fills up again.
One summer, when calculated, it hasn't rained for 981 days. It was so dry that the bottom of the lake was so dry that the surrounding fields were as hard as stones. The cracks were a few inches wide, and the tender green seedlings all turned yellow. The common people were so thirsty that their eyes were holed in, and their bodies were exhausted. They look forward to the Golden Bull appearing every day.
One morning, just as everyone was standing by the lake looking forward to the Taurus, there was a sudden sound of “shouting”, only to see the Taurus breaking out of the bottom of the lake. It shook its head, swayed its tail, and spit out a lot of water, and in an instant the lake filled up again.
The common people shed tears of joy. They were thanking the Taurus. The Taurus looked up, flashed bright eyes, shouted “loudly,” and disappeared into the lake.
The matter spread very quickly. The land security was handed over to the government office, and the government office also reported it to the Qiantang County official. As soon as the county official heard it, he held his stomach and laughed hehe and said:
“This is really a living baby. If you give it to the emperor, you will definitely be able to advance to the lawsuit and make a fortune!” Order your subordinates now to quickly catch the Taurus.
Those government officials and land guards hurriedly ran to the lakeside and looked up. There was a vast white lake in front of them. Where are the golden bulls? Ask the people nearby. They all saw that they were from the government office. Either they didn't see it, but they quietly avoided it.
The government officials had no idea; they had to pay back to the county officials. The county official was angry and had an eight-character beard. He thought about it, and came up with a bad idea. He said to his men:
“Since this is the case, I will call all the ordinary people and dry the lake water truck. Kill whoever doesn't come!”
Ordinary people living by the lake, male, female, old, and young, were all evicted. Under pressure from the county officials, they had to set up a water wheel with tears in the lake.
The car, ah, car, car, had been driving for 999 days, so tired that everyone was exhausted. On the last day, they finally finished the lake water truck. Sure enough, the Taurus lay at the bottom of the lake, and the golden light on its body illuminated the sky and the earth.
The county official was so illuminated by the golden light that he couldn't even open his eyes, but he still called for the government officials and quickly went down to the lake to rob the golden bulls. Strangely enough, the golden cow seemed to have taken root; it couldn't be lifted, and it couldn't be lifted. Ordinary people are secretly happy in their hearts.
As soon as the county officials saw that they couldn't move, they said to the people:
“Who can lift a golden bull and enjoy 300 pieces of silver!”
However, the common people stood still and ignored him in a fit of breath.
When the county officials saw that ordinary people ignored him, they shouted loudly:
“If you don't lift the Taurus today, I'll kill you all!”
As soon as the county official finished speaking, the golden bull screamed, like a thunderbolt from the sky. I saw the sand walking down the stones and the earth shaking. The county officials were so scared that their faces looked like dirt, their legs were soft, and they wanted to escape, but they couldn't move even one step.
At this point, the golden cow turned round eyes, stood up, and shouted at the sky again, spitting a large white stream of water from its mouth, rushing straight at the county officials and government officials, and swept them all away in a big wave.
Instantly, the lake was full again.
Since then, there have been no more golden cows in the lake, and the lake has never dried up, so people can't forget the golden bulls. They built a tall tower on the city wall next to the lake and climbed up the city tower every day to hope for the golden bulls. This city tower is what later became the “Yongjin Gate”.
