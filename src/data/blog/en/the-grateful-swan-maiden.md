 In the old days, there was an old man named Zhang nearby. One spring, Zhang Laohan led his son Fu Gen to Swan Ping to cut grass and discovered that a large blue snake had bitten a swan that was hatching. The father and son slashed the giant blue snake with a knife and saved the swan.
The father and son worked hard and diligently. Gradually, the warehouses of rice were full, and the cows and sheep flocked, and the days were getting better day by day! One day a few years later, Fugan saw a group of swans flying through the sky and cheerfully blown the bamboo flute. Blowing and blowing, a little swan flew down from the sky and became a beautiful girl. It turns out this is a little swan that was hatched in Swan Ping back then; the swan's mother asked her to repay her favor. Swan and Fugan got married and had a pair of white, fat, and fat children the next year. Swan Ping still exists today, and people also call it “swans incubate eggs.”
