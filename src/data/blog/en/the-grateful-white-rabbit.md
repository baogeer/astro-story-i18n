 Ruan Zhen
This is a long story.
There is a firewood cutter named Li Wan in Shimen Village. He likes archery and often carries bows and arrows on his body to prevent accidents. One day, he went up the mountain to cut firewood. Suddenly, strong winds blew, making him unable to stand steady. Exactly, there was an old stone cave in front of him, so he went into the cave to take shelter from the wind. When he goes up the mountain on weekdays, he often goes to the cave to rest and even spend the night.
At this point, he stumbled and ran into the old stone cave. He looked up and was shocked. I only saw the big yard in front of me, with red lanterns hanging on the red lacquered doors. An open-minded girl dressed up as a girl greeted her and said, “Eun Gong, please come in.” He followed Ya Huan to the guest room with trepidation. He only saw a pretty young lady who said to him, “Thank you for your support. Please be praised by the little girl.”
He had never seen such a beautiful woman. She had nowhere to put her hands and feet for a moment, causing her to eat and laugh. The little lady reprimanded, “Don't be rude, serve tea to Eun Gong quickly.” He inexplicably asked, “Why is the little lady making claims that I am gracious?” The little lady said, “Did Eng Gong forget what he did?” He really can't remember how much favor he had for the little lady. The little lady smiled and said, “You really are a good person who doesn't have a picture.” I ordered the open mouth to take out a small round mirror and say, “I have recorded all of Eng Gong's great blessings and virtues in it. Please take a look at it.”
Li Wan took the small mirror and saw a scene like this: a wolf was about to tear apart the rabbit under his claws. He fired an arrow and hit the wolf in the back leg. The wolf let go of the rabbit and violently pounce on him. Too late to fire an arrow, he was knocked to the ground by an evil wolf. The wolf opened his mouth and bit him in the face. He stuck the bow straight into the wolf's throat. The evil wolf stepped back and threw his head off, threw the bow off, plucked it up again, jumped up, and tore his chest. He took advantage of the opportunity to pinch the wolf's throat with one hand, grab the evil wolf's tongue with the other hand, and pat it violently. The evil wolf screamed and fell to the ground, and never stood up again. And he has already been caught with scaly wounds all over his body.
The young lady said, “The rabbit you saved was me,” Li Wan said, “But that day, I didn't see a rabbit under the wolf's claws.” The little lady said, “Engong didn't notice the slave family when it was in crisis.” Li Wan didn't say anything anymore. The little lady picked up the small round mirror and went to the inner room. She opened her mouth and said, “In order to repay the brave warriors for saving their lives, I have long had the idea of making a mutual agreement. I specially went to Xiuyan Cave to practice for a year, repaired my mouth, and prepared to lay a bed cover for the warriors.” Li Wan thought to herself, “No wonder this little girl doesn't look like a rabbit; she has been trained. It's rare for her to be so infatuated. Being able to get married to her is a blessing from my past life.” The open-mouthed girl casually asked, “Do strong men have families?” Li Wanbao said, “I haven't married a wife yet.” She said, “I am the matchmaker for both of you. Today is the auspicious day of the zodiac, so you two will go to the cave.”
The two of them went into the cave that night, so there's no need to go into detail about how fun they were.
Seeing Li Wan's troubled appearance, Lady White Rabbit asked with concern, “Where is Lang Jun uncomfortable?” Li Wan said, “You and I have been together for 100 years; it is certainly a match made in heaven. However, my wife is very stubborn; she won't accept others. I'm wondering how your wife and daughter-in-law can live in harmony.” Lady White Rabbit said, “I will do my best to honor my mother-in-law. As long as you don't discredit my mother-in-law, it's fine.” Li Wan said, “My mother was widowed at an early age, so I was the only one, and I have endured all the hardships. My two wives are dependent on each other; you need to have a good life and be filial.”
The next day, Li Wan walked out of the red lacquered gate with the white rabbit girl and the open mouth. Looking back, the large courtyard was gone; it was still an old stone cave overgrown with wild vines.
Back home, Li Wan's mother was so happy to see her son take back a daughter-in-law who looked like a flower. Sure enough, Lady White Rabbit did her best and served her mother-in-law with a smile. Three months passed in a blink of an eye.
