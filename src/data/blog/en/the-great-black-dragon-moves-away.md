 Long ago, the big black dragon on Cangshan Mountain lived in a pond in the big willow tree nest at Dalang Dam. People nearby are very poor. Whenever you have a wedding or funeral, you have to go to the Big Willow Tree Nest to borrow utensils from Big Black Dragon. When you borrow it, you only need to order 3 incense sticks, 3 knives with paper, 3 cups of toast and tea, make 3 cups, and say how many pots, how many bowls, how many large bowls, and how many small bowls to borrow, then rent a bowl to a nearby farmhouse for a while. The borrowed utensils have already been moved to the side of the pond, and never returned empty.
The Big Black Dragon is very nice to people, but the people there aren't very good to the Big Black Dragon. One year, there was no rain for a long time, and it was impossible to plant. In order to get rain, the people there didn't ask the Big Black Dragon to rain a little bit, but instead hit the pond with stones. The Black Dragon was furious. Suddenly, dark clouds rolled in, and the chill was overwhelming. Hail the size of broad beans and pigeon eggs knocked down, killing one of the six people who hit the pond and injuring five others. Since then, people have never dared to throw stones in the pond.
By May of the lunar calendar, farmers were in a hurry and came to ask for rain one after another. People put up fruit offerings and pig heads on the incense case. They also knocked their heads and scolded again. The Big Black Dragon thought that if it doesn't rain anymore and the seedlings can't be planted, ordinary people won't be able to survive; there are only a very few people who work in reservoirs, and most people will still have to be rescued by rain.
By May 13, it was raining heavily, and many farmers were very busy and happy. Until now, there are still
The saying “May 13, the Great Black Dragon went up Cangshan Mountain” and “The big drought was no more than May 13.”
There was a sandalwood tree by the pond, and someone wanted to cut it down. One day, Big Black Dragon went to Heilongtan in Cangshan. Only Dragon King and his wife were at home. This person wore a brown coat upside down on his body, three horns on his head, and came to the side of the pond to chat with the Three Princes of Dragon King. During gossip, when he gossiped, he said that the yin and yang agreed to be cut; in the gossip, he said that the people in the yang room wanted to be cut; in the gossip, he said that both yin and yang agreed to be cut, so they cut it. Later, Big Black Dragon came back from home and was very angry when he saw that the sandalwood tree had been cut down. The Dragon King Trinity and his wife answered, “This is a rare strange person. He has a thousand eyes, three horns on the top of his head, and five inches long.” Of course, no matter where you look for this kind of person, you won't find them. The more I thought about Big Black Dragon, the more strange it felt. Thinking that this place wasn't a place to stay for a long time, I moved to Heilongtan in Cangshan.
