 There were originally no mountains on the East China Sea coast. One day, everyone was surprised and surprised to see the mountains overlapping in the sea for several miles.
Another day, the mountains suddenly moved to another place, and the seaside returned to its original appearance, and all of the mountains disappeared.
People say there are big fish in the sea. When it's Qingming season, they bring their families and go to the graves of their ancestors, so they are often seen during the cold season.
