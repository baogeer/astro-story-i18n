 The Qiang people originated in Guqiang. They are an ancient ethnic group in western China. They call themselves the “rimai” and “irma”, and are known as the “nation in the clouds.” The ancient Qiang people were famous for shepherding. They were not only an important part of the Chinese nation, but also had a broad and profound influence on the historical development of China and the formation of the Chinese nation.
The Qiang people, like other ethnic groups, are popular in folk epics that have been handed down by word of mouth for many generations. “The Battle of Qiangge” is one of the most famous heroic epics. It tells the story of the ancestors of the Qiang people who traveled from northwest to settled in the upper reaches of the Minjiang River in ancient times. Among them, the Qiang people fought frequently with the “strong, fierce and powerful” Gogi people. Later, with the help of Tenjin, they defeated the Gogi people, lived and worked peacefully, and thrived.
Here's a drama “The Battle of Qiangge” based on a mythical story.
Legend has it that in ancient times, after getting married, Mu Jiezhu, the daughter of the god Abamubita, gave birth to nine sons. As humans continued to multiply, they each had their own tribe and formed nine teams. They lived in a vast grassland, which became the past lives of the Qiang people.
The prairie is also home to the high-cheekbones, able-bodied indigenous Goki people. They are big and ferocious, compete with each other for pastures, and often go to war. One night, the nine Qiang people in the prairie were suddenly attacked by the Gogi people. Sheep were robbed, and all their possessions and food were looted. The Jiuzhi Qiang people were forced to travel west to find a safe place to live. In constant clashes with the Gogi people, nine teams of the Qiang tribe had to disperse and escape. Among them, the older brother Aba Baigou, who had been taught the skills by the Sun God, led the remnants to migrate to the Minjiang River. In order to compete for territory, the Qiang people and the Goki people have always been in a battle. Although the Qiang people were helped by Mu Jiechu, the good times didn't last long. The Qiang people fought and lost over and over again, and after all, they were no match for the invaders of the healthy Goki people. Tenjin Kobita was very anxious when she saw that her children and grandchildren were always failing and unable to help them. This time, something just happened.
Goki steals cows
One day, Abamubita's oldest son herded sacred cows at Heiluoshan Ranch. Due to his playful nature, when he came home in the afternoon, Bao Cai Horn Cow and Yang Bang Horn Cow disappeared, and I couldn't find them anywhere. These two cows were Abambita's heart, so he personally questioned the Qiang people and the Goki people. After inspection, he learned that the devil cows were stolen by the Gogi people, so he found a way to clean them up.
Tenjin Abamubita first asked the Qiang people, “Have you seen the lucky cows and young cows I have raised on Luoshan Ranch?”
The Gogi people were the first to answer, “We haven't seen it.”
Abambita asked the Qiang people again, “Did you steal the sacred cow?”
The Qiang people said, “We don't dare to steal!”
Abambita asked the Gogi people again, “Did you steal the sacred cow?”
The Gogi man sneakily said, “We didn't steal.”
Abambita was so angry that he summoned the Qiang people and the Goki people to check the mouths of the Qiang people and the Goki people next to each other. He saw that Qiang people had pickled vegetable tendon in their mouths, but Goki people had beef tendon in their mouths. Mubita was very angry when he learned that the god cow had been stolen by the Goki people.
Abambita Mubita also asked the Qiang people, “Do you want gods, or do you want Goki people?”
The Qiang people said, “We want gods, we want strength, we don't want Gogi people!”
Abambita also asked the Gogi people, “Do you want gods, forges, or Gogi people?”
The Gogi people arrogantly answered, “We don't want gods, don't be aggressive, just the Gogi people.”
Tenjin Abamubita was very unhappy. He thought he must find an opportunity to clean up the Gogi people.
Kamisuke Kōjin
Since Abamubita has always been stubborn about the Goji people stealing cows and wants to find an opportunity to deal with the Goki people. One day, he asked the Qiang people and the Goki people to come to Japan to fill the dam, putting the Qiang people and the Goki people on the battlefield.
Abambita said, “Gogi people, I'll give you a weapon against the Qiang people.” Each person was handed a twine stick to the Gogi people.
Abambita: “Qiang people, just use this weapon.” He handed each of the Qiang people a wooden stick.
The twine and wooden sticks look exactly the same from the outside.
Abambita issued an order saying, “Gogi people, go ahead and defeat the Qiang people.” Hearing an order from God, the Gogi people rushed to the Qiang people with evil spirits: “Guys, go ahead, defeat the Qiang people.”
On the battlefield, there was a sound of knocking on people with a hemp rod, breaking the stick, shouting and shouting.

Now it is the turn of the Qiang people to fight against the Gogi people.

The Qiang people killed one with one stick, two with two sticks...
After a few battles, the Gogi people fell apart.
Firewood Chopping Contest
After the first round between the Qiang people and the Goki people, the Qiang people beat the Goki people and won the victory. Abambita was very happy. In order for the Qiang people to win more victories, Tenjin decided to have the Qiang people and the Goki people face off in the second round.
Abamubita said to Aba Baigou, “Aba Baigou, today you and the Goki people will go up the mountain to cut firewood.”
Abba Baigou respectfully said, “Obey your orders, God. We Qiang people will definitely unite and work together to defeat the Goki people.”
Aba Baigou greeted the Qiang people: “Brothers, today we are going up the mountain to cut firewood. Let's carry an axe on our backs.”
The Gogi people mocked the Qiang people: “Hahaha, you see, these weak Qiang people still use axes to cut firewood? Look how strong we are. We can beat you guys if we beat them with our bare hands.”
Then they came to the mountain together and set up their positions.
Abambita: “To see if the Qiang people are strong or if Goki is strong, who is more powerful, let's play a firewood splitting competition today.”
“Begin!” Abamubita gave an order.
The Qiang people all used axes to cut firewood, and soon they cut a large pile of firewood. The Gogi people tore firewood with their bare hands, and they couldn't tear it apart no matter how they tore it.
The Gogi people watched a large pile of firewood split by the Qiang people. Goki was so popular that he burst to the ground with all his might.
The Qiang people said, “People of Gogi, you can't tear it apart no matter how you tear it up. Why don't we help you. We'll help you split the firewood in one way, so you can tear it apart.”
The Gogi people listened to what the Qiang people said and thought it was very reasonable, so they agreed. All the Gogi people were very happy to work with the Qiang people to cut firewood.
The Qiang people first cut the tree, then the Goki people reached in to prepare to tear firewood. When the Gogi people stuck their hands into the chai, the Qiang people immediately took out the axe. In this game, the Qiang people relied on wisdom to defeat the Gogi people.

Abambita said, “Today I'm bringing everyone together again. I hope you will compete again to see who you are the strongest.”

The Gogi people quickly ran to Abambita. Abambita brought three blocks of snow for each of them.

Battle of Red Rock
The Gogi people are still not convinced; they still continue to secretly attack the Qiang people from time to time. Tenjin Abamubita saw that they had no repentance and decided to get rid of the Gogi people.
From winter to spring for another year, the Goki people never stopped looting the Qiang people. The patience of the kind Qiang people over and over again not only did not result in a peaceful life; on the contrary, the Goki people, the Gogi people, were unscrupulous. Dragon Dam Celebration
