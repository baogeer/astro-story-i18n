 Legend has it that Yaochi, which came out of Kunlun, is the place where the mother and daughter of the Western King live. Little Qinglong, who guards Yaochi, is the 18th grandson of Dragon King of the East China Sea. Although Yaochi is a place with beautiful scenery, Little Qinglong felt extremely stuffy. On this 2,500-mile high mountain, there were no people even seen all day long, and deer and birds rarely came; it was so deserted!
After hearing “Caozhou Peony is the World”, Little Qinglong wanted to go to Caozhou to see Shedan. Little Qinglong became a young boy and arrived in Caozhou before the royal mother's maiden went to Tiangong.
I thought peonies were in full bloom in Caozhou, and it was very lively. Who knows, when I came to see them, the land was dry and desolate, the crops died, and the peonies were thin and yellow. Little Qinglong walked into a peony garden. Seeing an old man, he came forward and offered a gift and asked; “Old man, the valley rain has arrived, why are peonies not blooming?” The old man looked at Qinglong and said, “Young man, aren't you a local? There hasn't been a drop of rain in Caozhou in two years, and the peony trees are dying of drought. Can they still bloom?” The old man sighed and said, “People go to Longwang Temple every day to bow their heads and ask for rain; Grandpa Dragon is just not happy. Crops died, peonies died. The people of Caozhou have no way to survive!” The old man shed tears as he spoke.
Little Qinglong was very sad to see peonies that were about to wither. He wants to go to Dragon Palace in the East China Sea to ask for rain from the old dragon king. Little Qinglong came to Dragon Guan and knelt down in front of King Lao Dragon. As soon as he said, “The Great Drought in Caozhou,” Lao Dragon King started getting angry! It turns out that two years ago, Lao Long Wang ordered people to move ten peonies from Caozhou and plant them in the Dragon Palace. Until now, not a single peony has bloomed. The old dragon king was furious and ordered not to give Caozhou a drop of water for three years, so that all peonies were dried to death. Little Qinglong said, “Peonies are born in the soil. They grow on earth. If you move them into the East China Sea, how can they blossom? The people of Caozhou have rebuilt the Dragon King Temple. They go burn incense and knock their heads every day, just be merciful!” Old Dragon King didn't listen. Order Little Qinglong to return to Yaochi as soon as possible. Don't ask about this; otherwise, he will punish Little Qinglong! Little Qinglong was helpless and had to leave the Dragon Palace.
Little Qinglong never returned to Yaochi. He came back to Caozhou and saw the old man in the garden watering a red peony tree. The old man said that when the water in the well was dry, it was as difficult for the people of Caozhou to drink; he ran for more than 20 miles, brought half a can of mud from a gutter that was about to dry up, and if he didn't give it water to drink, this century-old red peony tree would die! Xiao Yulong looked at that peony. The leaves were yellow, the flowers were thin, and shook his head pitifully. It seemed like he was saying, “Brother Qinglong, save me!” Little Qinglong suddenly thought of the Yaochi Xianshui that he guards. Why don't I get some to water Caozhou peonies and save the people of Caozhou! Thinking of this, he pulled out and left. After walking two steps, he stopped again and thought to himself; “Stealing Yaochi Xianshui is a crime punishable by death. Saving Peony from Caozhou is just afraid my life won't be saved!” Little Qinglong looked back and saw the old man dripping a little bit of mud left in the can onto his dry, cracked, and white lips. Little Qinglong is in tears! The old man held back his thirst and watered the flowers. Why wouldn't I dare to take back some of the water from Yaochi, save the people of Caozhou, and save the peonies that are about to die?
When Little Qinglong returned to Yaochi, she opened her mouth wide and sucked the Yaochi fairy water into her stomach. Teng Yun also drove fog to Caozhou, opened her mouth in the air, and spewed out fairy water.
It rained in Caozhou, a life-saving rain! A thousand hectares of crops are green, 10,000 trees are alive, and hundreds of flowers are in full bloom! The red peony tree, which has been practiced for 100 years, was bathed in fairy water, and turned into a girl in red. She prayed three times to the sky and said, “Brother Qinglong, thank you for your life-saving thoughts, but you can't go back to Yaochi anymore, so come and hide!” Little Qinglong became a young boy again. He came to the Peony Fairy and said, “Mother Wang has a magic mirror. She can shine wherever I hide it. Now everything has been saved? “I'm willing to go back and take offense.” The Peony Fairy held Little Qinglong. She burst into tears and said, “I hid you in my heart, can they find it too?” Little Qinglong shook her head and said, “You're red; it's hard to hide, if it's black —” The Peony Fairy said, “Wait here for a while. I'll be back to writing a novel after a while, and it left like a gust of wind.
Little Qinglong doesn't know where the Peony Fairy went. I was anxious. While sitting and lying uneasy, the western sky made a noise. Wang Mu led the army to capture Little Qinglong. It turns out that soon after Little Qinglong took out the fairy water, Wang Mu arrived at Yaochi. When she saw that there was more than half of the Xianshui in the pond, she hurriedly sang the Jade Emperor. As soon as Emperor Jade heard it, she was very angry and sent General Tianbing to help the royal mother catch Little Qinglong. Little Qinglong is hiding nowhere, and the Peony Fairy has returned; her body is dark, dark and bright, and her face and hands have turned black. In order to save Little Qinglong, the Peony Fairy was not afraid to ruin her face and became an ugly girl. She flew to the ink pool in Mount Taishan and soaked her whole body black. The Peony Fairy flew in front of Little Qinglong and said, “Hide it in my heart!” It just turned into a peony. The peony flower is black. The black petals slowly unfold, waiting for the little blue dragon to hide; the little blue dragon nodded in gratitude and hid in the flower bud.
Wang Mu led Tianbing to search everywhere, but she didn't find the Little Blue Dragon. She took a picture with a demon mirror, nor was she able to take a picture of Little Blue Dragon. The royal mother was furious and angrily said, “It must have been hidden by some fairy!” As a result, she ordered people to go to Kinugen to fetch bad water and spill it in the air. No matter what kind of immortal you choose, if bad water is sprinkled on your body, you will never become a fairy again. After a whole day, the land of Caozhou was sprinkled all over, and only then did Wang Mu Niang return with her troops.
The black peonies were all covered by bad water, and the Little Blue Dragon's body was also soaked; none of them can become immortals! However, since then, a famous type of peony has been added to the Caozhou Peony Garden: the Qinglong Pond. Peony flowers are transparent red in black, bright in dark purple, and the blue flower heart curves, like a small blue dragon.
