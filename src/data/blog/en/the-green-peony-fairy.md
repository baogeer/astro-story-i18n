 According to legend, at the beginning of the Qing dynasty, there was a village called Liu Tun in Lushan, Caozhou. The village had a family of two, and the mother and child were dependent on each other. Her mother, Sang, can weave and spin; her son, Liu Junsheng, is 17 and 8 years old. He is smart, smart, and diligent in farming. The peonies he grows are extremely strong and bloom very brightly.
It was March of the spring season. The wind and sun were beautiful, and Jun made it on his birthday. I was working in the fields. Suddenly, the sky was dark and the wind was raging. Suddenly the sky was dark, the sky was full of haze, and heavy rain fell from the sky. It only takes crooked branches from trees, and seedlings take root, and Shun Sheng is like a chicken in soup, flowing all over his body. He ran desperately home one by one, only to see a peony on the side of the road being destroyed by wind and rain. Sadly, he turned to the peony field and lifted up the peony tree that had fallen to the ground. Soon, the wind stopped raining, and Junsheng dragged his tired body along the muddy path and returned home one by one. The peony, which was lifted up by Junsheng, saw the sun. The branches were strong, and the buds were blooming one by one. They were full of fragrance, and they were all purple and red.
The spirit of Junsheng braving the rain to save Peony moved the fairy Princess Peony. She used the elderly Apricot Shu as a medium and wanted to become a couple with her benefactor. He turned into an old man. When he came to Junsheng's house, he met Jun Sheng's mother, which explained his intention. Junsheng's mother only felt that the family was poor and made all kinds of speculations, while the old man made painstaking remarks and persuaded him over and over again. The mother was helpless and had to agree. The old man pulled out a silk sweat towel and handed it to Junsheng's mother as a memo. They also agreed on a good day to visit the church and become a family.
Time flies; the marriage period has arrived. Fairy Peony became an 18- or 9-year-old girl. She had a clear eye, passed the Shih Shi race, and came to Junsheng's home. When Junsheng's mother and son saw each other, they were overjoyed. The mother hurriedly asked, “Whose family lady are you, come to my poor family?” The fairy replied, “My name is Cui Peony. My family lives in Baiguoshu Wanxiangyuan. I have an old apricot as my mediator. I have given a slave to Shun Sheng. I invited my mother to take a seat and be worshiped by her children.” When I say that, I'll say goodbye. Junsheng's mother was overwhelmed, and the old man Apricot came by and loudly said, “Today is a lucky day. I'm here to host a church to become a family. Please get ready right away.” Soon, Zhang Luo was fully prepared and hosted by Xing Lao, and Junsheng became a family with Peony Fairy Gu Chapel.
After the marriage, the two respected and loved each other, and were very peaceful. Years passed in a blink of an eye. Green Peony gave birth to a boy and a girl. The boy's name was Tian Yu, and the girl's name was Hongshi. It is said that later, this man and woman also became peonies, the two varieties “blue field jade” and “ice covered with red stone.”
