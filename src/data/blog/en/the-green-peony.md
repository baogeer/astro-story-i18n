 A long, long time ago, there was a scholar who loved growing flowers, and people gave him the nickname “Huaxianggong.” Huaxianggong's garden was right on the road. People traveling from south to north saw it, and they all praised him for growing flowers well. Huaxianggong listened and nodded with joy in his heart.
One morning, Huaxianggong was watering flowers in the garden. Suddenly, an old man Bai Hu walked out of the garden. Huaxianggong smiled and asked, “Grandpa, what do you think of my garden?” The old man touched his beard and said, “A good return is good, there are few green peonies.” After listening, Huaxianggong asked, “Grandpa, where can you get green peonies?” The old man said, “If you have it, I'm afraid you won't be able to suffer and you won't be able to find it.” Huaxianggong said, “Grandpa, say it. As long as I can find green peonies, I'm not afraid to go up to Dao Mountain and go down to the sea of flames!” After listening, the old man with white beard said, “OK, since you have to look for it, I'll tell you.” So the white-bearded old man explained to him how to find green peonies in this way.
After listening, Huaxianggong went home, brought money, and rode right away. He followed the path indicated by the white-bearded old man and walked all the way along the road. Go, go, go, walk. I don't know how many days and how many roads they have walked. The silver has been spent, and the food has been eaten. Later, even horses were sold, but they still haven't found green peonies. While he was in trouble, he looked up and found an old man picking apricots in the orchard in front of him. He hurriedly walked over, hugged his hands, and slapped his hands, and said to the old man, “Grandpa, you're good at it. Tell me where to find green peonies?” After listening, the old man sighed and said, “Alas, I just told you, you can't go either!” As soon as Huaxianggong heard it, his knees were stuck on the ground. Again, he asked the old man to tell him where the green peony grew. When the old man saw Huaxianggong's eagerness to find green peonies, he said, “If you want to find green peonies, you have to go through Heishui Lake on the way. The water in that lake, ah, was as dark as the bottom of a pot; it was extremely cool. There are no bridges or boats on this lake. There is only a single tree lying on the surface of the lake. If you don't walk well, people fall into the lake and drown. Second, you have to go through Mount Tribulus. Each of the tribulus spines on that mountain is more than a foot long. There are no roads in the mountains; you can only pass through the air of tribulus terrestris. If people want to go through Mount Tribulus, if they don't get it right, I'm afraid even their feet will burn in a thin paste. Third, you have to go through the Stream of Fallen Souls. That stream is deep and steep. People stand at the bottom of the stream and look upwards; they can only see a glimpse of the sky. If you want to get over the stream, you can only grab a thin, long tree root and run past it. If you don't make it fall off, not only will you lose your life, but even the corpse will fall to pieces!”
After listening to the old man's words, Huaxianggong said, “Grandpa, thank you for your advice. I spared everything to find the green peony!” After speaking, I just followed the old man's instructions and went back on the road.
He was walking, walking, walking. I don't know how long he left. Sure enough, he saw a black water lake in front of him. Looking at it, the lake was really amazingly dark. The black waves were rolling, and I couldn't see the edge at a glance. On the surface of the lake, only a thin, round tree hangs on it. Her feet were on the wood, and she just shuddered. Huaxiang couldn't take care of this. She closed her eyes, lay down on the wood, and tried her best to swim forward. My body was submerged in the lake from time to time, and the lake was as cool as an awl stabbing me in my bones. He clenched his teeth, persevered, and swam for a long time, and it was so easy to get past Heishui Lake. Once on the lakeshore, he continued on his way. Go, go, go, go, I don't know how long it will be before I came to Mount Tribulus. Looking up, the tribulus spines on the mountain are actually a foot long. If you don't walk well and fall on Mount Tribulus, you'll be stabbed to death. However, in order to find the green peony, he kept his eyes wide open and chose tribulus terrestris on both feet. To walk through this tribulus air, you need to walk steadily, stand firm, and step on it accurately. As a result, walking is slow and slow. After walking for two days and two nights, he had a hard time walking past Mount Tribulus and found his way forward. Huaxianggong kept walking down the road again and again. On this day, he arrived in a deep mountain stream. Looking up, this large mountain stream is long, deep, and steep; only a thin, long tree root hangs from the mountain. In order to find the green peony, Huaxianggong endured a lot of pain, clasped the roots of the tree with both hands and stepped on the cliff with both feet, and climbed vigorously. He crawled, climbed, crawled, and his hands broke his skin; he didn't complain; his foot slit cut his mouth, and he didn't complain. I don't know how long it took to climb, and suddenly I saw a flash in front of me, and a bright road lay in front of me. Huaxianggong's heartfelt words: Now we can find a way. He hurriedly jumped off a tree root and walked down the road. After walking a short distance, I entered a small village and saw an old man at the head of the village sitting on a small bench basking in the sun. As soon as the old man saw Huaxianggong walking up, he stood up and said, “Your brother is probably looking for green peonies?” Huaxianggong was surprised and said, “Exactly. Grandpa, do you know where those green peonies are?” The old man said, “Don't worry, go to my house first to rest your feet and have some food. When you're full, I'll take you to find it.” As soon as Huaxianggong heard it, he jumped around and followed the old man. At the old man's house, he hurriedly caught fire to eat some food, so he went to the old man's house looking for green peonies.
The old man led Huaxianggong to his garden. I saw velvety peonies blooming in that garden. The color is charming and the fragrance is intense. Huaxianggong called this garden full of green peonies enchanted. He sued the old man to give him a tree for his left and right. The old man said, “Let's do it, I'll ask my seven daughters to take you home. Three years after arriving home, you'll have green peonies in your garden.” Huaxianggong was half convinced and half skeptical after hearing it. At this point, I saw that the old man made a move and called out his seven daughters. As soon as Huaxianggong took a look, the seven were all the same in size and appearance. The old man called the seven girls at the bottom of the line and said to Huaxianggong, “Let her carry it; keep your eyes closed; don't ask you to slouch; don't open it.” At first, Huaxianggong was too embarrassed to let his eldest daughter take his back. However, thinking that in order to get green peonies, I wouldn't be afraid of any hardship or danger, so I lay down on the back of the Seven Daughters, closed my eyes, and let her walk away. I just listened. The wind blew directly in my ears, and the sand flew away the stones. Soon, the wind stopped, the sound stopped, and I tried to land on my feet. The seven girls told him to open his eyes. He opened his eyes and took a look. Yo, he's already in his house. Huaxianggong's parents came back after seeing their son who had been gone for a long time, and they also brought back a handsome daughter. They were so happy that they couldn't help but be happy. When Grandpa saw it, he started crying loudly. He picked up his dead granddaughter and went home and didn't know what to say to his daughter-in-law. The daughter-in-law said, “People are dead; they can't live by crying.” As soon as Huaxianggong saw the child die, he started crying loudly. The daughter-in-law also said to Huaxianggong, who was crying, “When a child dies, they can't live crying even if they cry. You buried her in the middle of all the flowers in our garden. Then I went home crying again.
The next day, early in the morning, he woke up. As his daughter-in-law left behind, before sunrise, he came to the middle of the garden and surrounded his child's tomb. He turned left three times and three turns right. As soon as I finished turning it around, I saw that the tomb “huh la” suddenly cracked, and a peony flower with green poles and green leaves grew from under the tomb. In the blink of an eye, flower bones popped up one by one between the leaves, and then green peony flowers bloomed with green velvet and fragrance.
