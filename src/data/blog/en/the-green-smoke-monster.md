 Once upon a time, a sister and brother had their mother who died young and were dependent on their father. My father went out to work every day, and my sister took on the responsibility of taking care of my younger brother, so the sister and brother had a very good relationship. My sister and brother were digging wild vegetables in the mountains one day, and suddenly a strong wind hit and swept my sister away. My younger brother was very alarmed, and he couldn't find his sister anywhere. He was extremely sad.
 The father heard the news of her daughter's sudden disappearance and rushed back in a hurry. The father and son searched all the nearby villages, but they couldn't find the sister's whereabouts. The younger brother wouldn't give up, and secretly vowed to get his sister back. After a few years, the younger brother grew up to be a big boy, and he left home looking for his sister.
 One day he walked to a desolate place by the sea and unwittingly saw a cloud of blue smoke rising from a place in the distance, drifting farther and farther away. Strangely enough, he went ahead and looked at it, but the plot of land was very normal. There was nothing special about it, but instead he pulled out the shovel he was carrying and plowed it up and wanted to see what was underneath. Before digging a few times, I heard the voice of a young woman below, “Kind hearted man, please stop digging; the sand is in the child's eyes!” As soon as my younger brother sounded like my sister's, he worked even harder. Soon, a large, plain slate underneath appeared in front of the younger brother. There was also a large iron ring on it. As soon as the younger brother pulled it hard, the slate was pulled up. Below was a dark tunnel. The younger brother walked down the tunnel, and the wider and brighter he walked all the way to the house below. A young woman in the house watched the visitors outside in horror while holding her child. This isn't the sister who is it? The younger brother gladly jumped over and hugged her tightly, “Sister, I'm your little brother, I've finally found you!” My sister kept a close eye on the young man in front of her. After deciding that it was her younger brother, she cried with joy.
 The sister and brother talked to each other about their experiences over the years. After being swept away by wind resistance that day, my sister was brought here by monsters and has always lived here. At first, my sister resisted, but in the end she had to give in and gave birth to a child for the monster. The reason the sister endured humiliation and stealing her life was because she believed her younger brother would find her.
 The sister and brother discussed a plan to escape. Before the monster had come back, the sister quickly cooked to make the younger brother full, and ordered the younger brother over and over again that when the monster came back, he couldn't eat anything. After a while, the monster came back and was very angry when she saw a strange man in the house. The sister quickly explained that her younger brother had come to see her. Only then did the monster get angry and prepare drinks for the young people to eat. After the dinner was served, no matter how the monster persuaded the young man to refuse to eat a bite, saying that they were not hungry, the monster had to eat it himself. After eating and drinking, the monster sat down and chatted with the young man. As they chatted, the monster showed up, saying that if they had great skills, this would change and that would change. The young man deliberately pretended to be dismissive and let the monster show it to him. When the timing was about right, the young man pretended to accidentally see a small bottle on the table and said, “Brother-in-law, look at this small bottle. If you can actually get inside, I believe you have great skills; otherwise, you're just talking big words.” The monster hesitated over and over again, and the young man provoked him, “Aren't you afraid?” The monster wouldn't lose face, “Who says I can't do it, just wait and see!” Seeing that the monster turned into a cloud of blue smoke slowly penetrating the bottle. When the smoke was completely contained, the sister and brother immediately picked up the stopper and sealed the bottle tightly. The monster made them open the bottle again and again and they didn't want to open it. Only then did they know they had been fooled, but it was too late, and the sister and brother threw the bottle out of the window into the sea. Then he knocked the table down, only to see that the rice had turned into maggots, and the meal had turned into spiders, snakes, scorpions, etc.; the child threw it to the ground and turned into a monster. The sister and brother set the house on fire, then the younger brother brought his sister home.
