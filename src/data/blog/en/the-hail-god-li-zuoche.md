 Taishi Tang Jiwu went to Rizhao to bury a person surnamed An. Pass by the hail god “Li Zuo Che Temple” and go in and take a look. There is a pool in front of the temple. The water in the pool is crystal clear, and several red fish are swimming peacefully; one of them has an oblique tail swimming on the surface of the water to eat, so I'm not afraid to see people. Tang Jiwu then picked up a small stone and wanted to play with it. A Taoist hurriedly stopped it. Tang Ji Wu Xun asked why. The Taoist said, “The fish in the pond are all dragons; hitting them will cause wind and hail.” Tang Jiwu scoffed at the Taoist for being too aggressive; he didn't listen to him; he still fished.
After leaving the temple, Tang Jiwu continued to drive east. Soon, a dark, pressing cloud, like a lid, covered Tang Jiwu's head. As he moved forward, hail the size of cotton fell to the ground. After walking more than a mile, the sky cleared up. Tang Jiwu's younger brother Tang Liangwu walked behind and caught up with his older brother to inquire. Tang Jiwu didn't know that hail had fallen; he also asked the people in front of him, and they all said they didn't know. Tang Jiwu smiled and said, “Is this Guang Wu Jun doing a strange thing?” I haven't felt much of a surprise in my mind.
There is a Guansheng Temple outside Anjia Village in Rizhao. A small vendor was laying down his burden outside the temple gate. Suddenly, he threw two baskets, went straight into the temple, pulled off the big knife from the shelf, and waved it in his mouth, saying, “I am Li Zuoche. Tomorrow I will accompany Tang Jiwu from Zichuan to help settle down and be buried. Let the owner know first.” After speaking, I woke up. I didn't know what I had said, and I didn't know who Tang Jiwu was. Settling down and learning about it, I was very afraid. The village was more than 40 miles away from Guansheng Temple, hurriedly and respectfully prepared sacrifices, and prayed at the temple. They only asked for mercy from the Hail God; don't give in.
After Tang Jiwu arrived, strangely, he settled down to admire Li Zuoche and asked the owner. The owner said, “Hail God has always been the smartest. He often speaks in the mouth of a living person; he has never been unsuccessful. If undevout prayer stops him from coming, then tomorrow there will be strong wind and hail here.”
