 A legend about the search for light. There are many stories of this kind, such as “Cutting Down the Shade Tree”. The story describes a huge tree growing in the place where the ancestors of the Hani people lived. The large tree has thick branches and leaves, so the Hani and Brown people live in places where they can't see the sun. To drive away the darkness and seek light, the Hani and Browns united to cut down the big, shady tree in two directions. Through the efforts of the two nationalities, the big tree was finally cut down, the darkness was dispersed, and the people of Hani and Brown saw the sun and light again. This big tree has a total of 12 thick branches. Each branch has 30 giant leaves, for a total of 360 giant leaves. As a result, the Hani divided the year into 12 months, with 30 days in a month and 360 days in a year. This legend not only reflects the idea of the Hani people to transform nature and pursue light, but also reflects the historical facts of their accumulated experience and creative history.
