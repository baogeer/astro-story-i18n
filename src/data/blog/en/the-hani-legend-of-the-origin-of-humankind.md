 Legends about the spread of human race. The Nahani folk in Xishuangbanna have two opinions about this: the first one is that humans were born from heaven and earth. Heaven nurtured men, and the earth nurtured women, gave birth to a race in the world, and began to reproduce humans. The second one is that once, the Dragon King got angry and spewed endless water to flood the earth, and almost all people were drowned. At the time, a boy and a girl tied the buffalo's neck with a white cloth and asked the buffalo to take them both to the top of the mountain to escape the flood. When the water receded, only a pair of white-necked buffaloes and a pair of ignorant men and women were left in the world. At first, the man and woman didn't know how to love each other. Later, a stone mill popped up underground. The grinding wheel followed them all day long, and coincided up and down in front of them. Inspired by stone grinding, a man and a woman finally fell in love and became a couple. This is how humans began to reproduce.
