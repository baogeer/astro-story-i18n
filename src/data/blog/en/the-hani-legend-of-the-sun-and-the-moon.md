 Legends about the Sun and the Moon. Among them, there is a legend that the Moon and the Sun were transformed by a loving couple.
While burying his father, a young man named Lao Qi discovered a type of fairy herb that can recover from death. That fairy herb is guarded by evil tigers.
Lao Qi went through a lot of hard work to defeat the evil tiger, and finally got the fairy herb, and used it to save a beautiful young girl. The two became a loving couple.
Later, Lao Qi became ill, and his wife secretly boiled a branch of fairy herb soup for her husband to drink.
After Lao Qi drank the fairy grass soup, it turned into a glowing fireball flying high into the sky and turning into a round of the sun.
When the wife saw this scene, she also swallowed the fairy grass in her stomach, and immediately turned into a silver plate that flashed to the sky to chase her husband.
Since then, the sunset and the moon have risen year after year. Since the pace of the two is generally fast and slow, the moon has always been unable to catch up with the sun.
