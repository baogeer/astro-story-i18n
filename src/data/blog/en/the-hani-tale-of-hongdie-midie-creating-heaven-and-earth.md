 “Red Daddy Mi Daddy” (“Red Daddy Mi Daddy” is a Hani word, that is, made heaven and earth). This story uses rich imagination to describe the two deified characters, Labak Aya and Gabi Alang. At the time, there was neither heaven nor earth. The universe was just a hazy cloud of fog. Labai Aya separated the fog and flew high into the sky to create a blue sky; Gabi Alang pressed the fog to create the earth. Since then, the fog has dissipated. There is a blue sky at high altitude, land under your feet, wind in the world, water on the ground, and dragons in the water. The dragon in the water is a dragon. It uses magic to bring livestock, birds, and animals to the earth. Since dragons created livestock and poultry for the world, the Hani people have believed and worshipped dragons in the water since ancient times.
