 Legend has it that the ancestors of the Hani people didn't cultivate fields in the first place. Later, there was an immortal in charge of grains in the sky. He had a field in the sky where he planted 77 kinds of grains. To cultivate this hill field, he had to have 1,200 fairy children raking 1,200 heads of sacred cows and pears for a day before he can finish plowing the entire hill field. When planting, 12,000 fairies must be planted with 77 kinds of grains for a day to fully cultivate the entire field. When weeding, 12,000 fairies must work for a day to clean the entire field of weeds. At the time of harvesting, 1,200 fairy children and 12,000 fairies must be harvested for a day before 77 kinds of grains can be harvested. Of the 77 kinds of grains that have been taken back, 1,200 fairy children and 12,000 fairy knuckles must be dried and transported for a day before the 77 kinds of grains can be dried and stored in warehouses. Because people in the world didn't grow whole grains at that time, they had nothing to eat, so they had to go deep into the mountains and old forests to pick mountain fruits to fill their hunger, or hunt wild animals and wild birds. Without cotton and hemp, people use animal skins to protect themselves from the cold. People endure it day by day, year after year.
The poor lives of people everywhere were seen by a girl from heaven. One girl is a beautiful girl who is smart, kind, and has a kind heart. She saw that people in the mortal world were busy all day long, running from place to place. At the end of the day, they still couldn't eat or wear clothes; she really couldn't keep watching. When she got home, a girl said to the fairy with concern, “Ah Da, we have 77 kinds of grains in the sky. We don't worry about eating and dressing, but people in the mortal world live a life where they can't eat enough or wear warm clothes; even if they don't have enough to eat, they can't keep watching.” Speaking of this, a girl's tears are about to shed. Seeing that the immortals were silent, she tentatively asked, “Ah Da, if we send some of the heavenly grain seeds to the world, then the world wouldn't have suffered so much.” Who knows, after hearing the girl's words, the fairy immediately raised her face and coldly said, “Girl, people in the mortal world will have to wait another three years before they can get the seeds of these 77 kinds of grains.” “Three more years?” As soon as a girl listened, she blurted out a question. Tenjin was very unhappy with her impact. He glared hard at her, then said, “Girl, this kind of thing is not your girl's responsibility. I don't care if people in the world are fed or not; I just know that they obey the rules of heaven.” Having said that, I turned around and walked into the house. The girl looked at the fairy's back and couldn't help but frown.
A few days later, in order to understand the pain of saving people, a girl took the opportunity of the fairy asking her to dry the millet and quietly brought a bag of grain to the world. A girl came to the world and became a human grandpa, then distributed rice seeds to people, and also taught people how to grow rice. Following her instructions, people were busy digging fields and clearing trenches before the Spring Festival. The sparrows called, and people rushed to plow the fields and spread rice to sow. When swallows flew in, people got up early and got busy transplanting rice seedlings to fields carefully raked with pears. Kung Fu paid off. As the rainy season was about to pass, the weather gradually cooled down, fish and finches were about to call, and people began to sickle and harvest. As the swallows flew south, people also used their hands to plow plow fields and dig up new fields to prepare for next year's harvest. Later, the immortal saw a girl defying his will and stealing rice seeds to be planted by a mortal person, and she couldn't help but burst into flames. When the girl returned to heaven, the immortals arrested her, and after punching her, they sent her back to heaven. However, in order for everyone to be able to eat whole grains, a girl could wear clothes made of cotton or hemp yarn, so she found a way to escape from heaven. Without going back and forth, she simply stole the seeds of another 76 kinds of grains and brought them to an ordinary place to teach people to plant, as well as teach people to spin, weave, and make clothes. Since then, every human being has lived a good life where everyone has clothes and food. When the immortal discovered that a girl had stolen everything and violated the rules of heaven over and over again, she took the girl back to heaven, hanged her and beat her hard, and punished her never to return to heaven. Speaking of which, the fairy turned a girl into a female dog and devalued the world. After the girl became a female dog, she was no longer able to work with people, so she helped people guard the house. Since then, every time in early autumn, we Hani people have had to kill pigs and slaughter sheep, cook fresh rice, and host a new rice tasting festival after harvesting the first batch of rice. During the holidays, before eating, every family must first serve a bowl of rice for the dogs raised at home, which means that we Hani people will never forget a girl.
