 Once upon a time, people died; they could be seen and encountered on Yin Yang Street.
There is an old woman whose only son died. She was deeply saddened and missed.
One day, the mother went to Chase Yin Yang Street (a street where dead people and living people chased together) and met her son, whom she hadn't met again for a long time, and the mother grabbed her son. The son said that the 10,000 mother just wouldn't let go. In the end, the son couldn't help but said, “Mom, since you miss me this much, you'll be staying in an exclusive village on Mount Yin and Yang tonight. When that time comes, I'll come to you. Now, I'll go back and explain.” Only then did the mother reluctantly let go, advising: “Son, I must come to Nika tonight!”
The mother was very happy. She soon arrived at the exclusive village on Mount Yin and Yang, explaining her intention. The homeowner was very surprised and said, “This is a bad thing. Your son from the old man is coming to kill you tonight. You can't stay here.”
The old mother was very angry: “If you don't want to stay with me, just talk straight, why are you talking bad about my son? You don't know about my son. He was distressed by me when he was there. The whole village was famous for his filial piety. If you don't believe me, go and inquire; would a son like this kill his mother?”
Homeowner: “People are not the same when they are alive and when they die. After your son died, he went to the land of Yin Cao, became a fierce ghost, and no longer have a mother-son relationship on earth.”
“No, that's right!” The old mother rushed to say, “I saw it with my own eyes on the street today; it's not like you said.”
“It's all pretense. If you don't believe me, you'll be watching tonight. He only comes in the middle of the night.” The homeowner said.
The old mother was half-convinced and half-skeptical. The homeowner placed two beds in the house. The bed on the left placed a large gourd filled with water, and covered his head with a quilt to disguise himself as if an adult was asleep. The bed on the right sleeps the old mother. “Don't sleep tonight to see how your son comes to kill you.”
In the middle of the night, as was to be expected, there was a sound of horse bells coming from far and near, a wind blew through, and the sound of horseshoes in the yard: “Can a shameless old lady live here?”
“Living in one, she said she was your mother.” The homeowner said.
“I don't know where she is my mother, or where she is a crazy woman. She caught me on Yin Yang Street today, making me unable to keep up with my partner when I go back. If I don't kill her tonight, she'll haunt me every day. Where did she sleep?” the son in the underworld asked.
“She fell asleep in the bed on the left side of the hall.” The homeowner lied to him.
The dead son gently pushed the door open, and with a large knife in his hand, walked to the side of the bed on the left. He raised the large knife with both hands and cut it on the bed. Knife after knife, slashed the gourd to pieces, and the water spilled down the bed. “Hahaha! Why are you bothering me this time around?” Just talking, walk out and get on the horse. The sound of horseshoes and horse bells is getting farther and farther away.
“A dead son has no conscience; a dead son has no conscience.” The mother angrily said, “I can't get tired of thinking about him this time.”
