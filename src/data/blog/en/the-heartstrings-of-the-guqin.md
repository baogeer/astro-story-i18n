 In the early years of the Tang dynasty, there was a young man named Tao Mian in Cuishan. His parents both died. His family was very poor, but he had no intention of doing anything. He was obsessed with music all day long and lived on the family's pawn left by his ancestors, but he was also quite comfortable.
This night, Tao Mian was playing the piano in her room, and suddenly a knock on the door sounded. As soon as Tao Mian opened the door to take a look, she was a plain looking woman in red. Without waiting for Tao Mian to speak, the woman smiled and reported her home: “The little girl's name is Red Silk, just because she was attracted by the boy's piano sound, so she took the plunge!” Tao Mian is a temperamental person. Currently, she is hugging her fist: “I understand that the young lady misloved you, please come in and talk!”
Red Silk is also unkind; now I'm drinking with Tao Mian. While chatting, Red Silk couldn't help but sit down and play a song. The piano sounds like the sound of nature, and the faster you play it, it's as if several hands are playing at the same time. At the end of the song, Tao Mian was amazed: “I couldn't have imagined that the young lady had such excellent piano skills. I look forward to receiving more advice in the future.”
Since then, the two have had secret meetings every night. Red silk always brings delicious wine and food. Tao Mian is worried about rough tea and light meals every day, so she can't ask for it. However, Red Silk never talks about her birth. After lingering around, she quietly left when she was a child. Tao Mian didn't ask many questions about this either. Under Red Silk's guidance, Tao Mian's piano art advanced by leaps and bounds. Tao Mian thought it was just a pity that the red silk looked the same. Otherwise, it would be a beautiful thing to have a good relationship with her for 100 years.
Half a month later, Tao Mian saw a notice on the street. It turns out that the new emperor, who has just ascended to the throne, likes music, and is calling for musicians. Tao Mian was overjoyed: If you could be blessed by the emperor, wouldn't you be rich and noble?
On the same night, after hearing the news, Red Silk happily said, “Boy, it looks like you need time to run!” Tao Mian shook her head: “Alas, unfortunately my piano is too bad; I'm just afraid the sound won't match anyone else's.” Red Silk comforted him: “Don't be afraid, the slave will give you a good piano tomorrow, and you will definitely be able to take the lead. I'm just afraid the boy will forget the slave family after flying to Huangtengda!” Tao Mian swore by heaven: “If you lose red silk in this life, you will be punished by heaven...”
The next day, Red Silk certainly delivered a piano. It's just that the body of the piano is mottled, and not even painted. That string was also very strange; it was thin and tight. Tao Mian was a little disappointed: “Is this the good piano you said?” Red Silk looked a bit tired: “Boy, don't underestimate this piano. It's made of thousand-year-old fir, and the sound is as smooth as jeweled!” Tao Mian tried it out a few times and was amazed: “It sure is a great piano!”
Early the next morning, Tao Mian left for Beijing to participate in the selection of musicians for the Imperial Palace. Originally, he had extraordinary skills. Coupled with this great piano, he certainly excelled in the competition, was selected as the chief musician by the emperor, and stayed in the palace.
On the night of his canonization, the emperor gifted every musician a beautiful woman. Agar Jade was accompanied by a beautiful woman. Soon, Tao Mian drank like mud and went out to have fun with the beautiful woman. In the blink of an eye, a month passed. Tao Mian has long since thrown red silk out of the clouds. Slowly, Tao Mian felt uneasy, and her fingering became messy.
On this day, the marshal won the battle. The emperor was overjoyed. He specially hosted a feast for the marshal in the palace and made Tao Mian play the piano to cheer him up. When Tao Mian listened, she was terrified and sweaty. How did the emperor know? Over the past few days, Tao Mian's fingers have been shaking, and he can hardly play the piano. However, your life was irrevocable. At the Jinluan Palace, Tao Mian stuck his head and played the piano. As a result, the sound of the piano was shaking. Tao Mian was anxious and afraid, and actually cut off all seven strings. The emperor immediately became furious and took him to the heavenly prison.
That night, Tao Mianfan leaned blankly against the corner of the wall. Red Silk suddenly appeared in front of her, sighing and said, “My lover, how can you forget your slave family?” Immediately, Tao Mian cried and cried: “I'm sorry, I failed you!” Seeing him cry with sadness, Red Silk felt intolerable: “Forget it, I won't compare with you this time!” Tao Mian said, “However, I can't even touch the piano now; I'm already an idiot!” Red Silk angrily said, “This is all because you are having fun. It was Tao Mian's turn. I saw him concentrate for a moment and closed his eyes and started playing the piano. Soon, the birds in the palace were also attracted.
