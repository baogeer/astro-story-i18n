 Legend has it that in ancient times, there was a kind boy called “Mu Lian”. He was very filial, but he had a vicious mother. Mother ordered people to make dog meat steamed buns and go to the monastery to fast. They deceived people that they were vegetarian steamed buns and asked the monk to eat meat. Once they knew it, the persuasion didn't work; they only had someone notify the abbot of the monastery and prepared vegetarian steamed buns to hide in every monk's sleeve. Wait until Mu Lian's mother comes to fast and exchange the vegetarian steamed buns in her sleeve.
When Heavenly Jade knew about this incident, he was very angry. He turned Mu Lian's mother into an evil dog and thrown into hell.
 
 
 In order to be obedient to his mother, Takako also practiced to hell and let her mother out. Her mother, who turned into a vicious dog, was hated by the Jade Emperor and went to Heaven to seek revenge from the Jade Emperor. If she did not find the Jade Emperor, she had to eat the sun and the moon, leaving people without light. (Ancient people apparently thought the moon could shine) Evil dogs were afraid of gongs, drums, and firecrackers. After swallowing the sun and moon, they heard the noise of gongs and drums and threw up again. So they continued to catch up.
 
 
 
 
 
 The solar and lunar eclipses are caused by tengu swallowing the sun and the moon. Until now, people in some regions have maintained the custom of hitting drums and beating gongs and setting off firecrackers when a solar or lunar eclipse occurs.
