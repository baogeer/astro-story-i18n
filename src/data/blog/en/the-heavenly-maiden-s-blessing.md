 Legend has it that the Dulberts nomad, and there is a towering Mount Nader. The mountain top is covered with snow and fog all year round, and the mountain lake is as clear as a mirror. People say this is a place where the gods live in seclusion. One day, a young hunter suddenly discovered a group of celestial girls playing in the lake at the top of the mountain. The hunters were amazed by their graceful posture and moving laughter. He quietly watched incognito with a pair of leather horseshoes, and the celestial ladies chased and played to their heart's content without even noticing it. The hunter flew a leather satchel to cover one of the celestial girls, and the others panicked and hid in the clouds. The hunter courted the celestial maiden, and the celestial lady agreed. However, their love was short lived. Due to the huge differences between heaven and earth, the two broke up on the same day. After that, Tiannu became pregnant, and Zhong returned to the side of the mountain lake where she met the hunter and gave birth to a boy. However, she was unable to live permanently on earth. She placed her child in a self-made cradle and hung it on a tree, sent a yellow bird to guard it day and night, singing for it, and then went back to heaven in grief.
At this point, Durbert's ancestors didn't have their own chiefs; they were anxious to find an ideal leader. Guided by a “prophet,” they climbed Mount Nader and followed the birdsong direction to find this child on a branch next to the mountain lake. The people who greeted them said, “This is a gift from heaven.”
Durbert's ancestors were extremely happy and happy to bring the child back to the tribe. Later, the child soon grew up to be a great husband, founded a great career, and became the ancestor of the Ross family.
