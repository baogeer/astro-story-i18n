 When Genghis Khan conquered a tribe, he captured the son of Sagittaiji and gave him to Alagodai Bayan as a domestic slave to herd Bayan's hundreds of millions of sheep.
At dusk one day, Alagodaiba Yan went out to see the herdding flock and saw the shepherd boy holding a horse skull and crying while crying, “Does anyone know this is the skull of a thousand miles horse? Does anyone know that I'm the son of Soumaz Arugataji?” When Alagodai Bayan heard it, he was furious. He raised his leather whip and slapped the shepherd boy: “Why are you sitting here crying? What about the flock?” The shepherd replied, “I think my father used to be a Soma person. I saw this Zenrima skull and thought of my father, so I stayed here for a while, and the flock followed, and they won't be lost.”
After hearing what the shepherd said, Alagodaiba Yan was even more angry. He angrily said, “What a thousand mile skull! Isn't there a famous horse among my 100,000 horses, and a dead horse became a good horse for a thousand miles? If you really have the skills to recognize the Millionaire, go to the horse herd early tomorrow morning to see if there are any horses like this!” The next morning, after checking the horse herd three times, the shepherd said, “No Senrima was found, only Qianlima's feces.” Hearing this, Ba Yan beat the shepherd boy again with a leather whip. At this point, there was a hissing sound coming from the back of the yurt.” “Old man, don't hit me, there is a loud scream.” The shepherd pointed to the bald-eared horse in Bayan that was carrying water and had broken Leung sores and said, “This is a thousand miles horse.” Ba Yan became more angry after hearing it. While fighting, he said, “There are no good horses in my group of 100,000 horses; the flower horses carrying water have become millionaires!” The shepherd cried and asked, “Don't hit me, old man. If you don't believe me, please throw this flower away for three years, then you'll know what a thousand miles horse is.”
Alagoba Yan followed the shepherd's words and spread his bald ears. Three years later, the shepherd made him prepare three belly bands, three large pockets, and three bags of milk to carry right away. After getting ready to stop, the shepherd asked Ba Yan to watch him ride a thousand mile horse for three laps around a group of 100,000 horses. The shepherd began to revolve around the horse group. Every time he turned, he broke a pocket and poured out the dirt inside. After turning the last round, he broke through the last pocket and poured out the dirt inside. After turning the last round and breaking through the last pocket of dirt, he said, “People are good, strong, and strong.” After speaking, I headed for Mercedes-Benz in my hometown.
At this point, Aragobayan had no choice but to meet Genghis Khan and ask Dahan to come up with ideas and solutions for him. He said, “Please kindly ask the monarch for approval. My horse group has been kidnapped, and there is no way to report it to the Holy Lord. It turns out that the shepherd that Khan gave me knows Qianlima. I asked him to go to my group of 100,000 horses to see if there were any millipedes, but he said that my bald-eared flower horse that couldn't be carried was a millimeter horse. Then I prepared everything he wanted me to do as he asked, and I didn't want him to ride my Maxillion and ran away. “Prince, is there any way we can get him back?”
Genghis Khan ordered Sogudor Saihan to say, “Ah, my handsome Sogudor Sai Khan, you can quickly ride my little horse and set out with a golden hook that is five long. It would be best to catch the people and horses together; if not, arrest the fugitive.” On the same day, Sogudor Sai Khan caught up with the shepherd who had been on the run for three days. He stepped forward and hooked the shepherd a few times before finally hooking his collar and grabbed the shepherd. Unfortunately, when Sogudor Sai Khan returned with two shepherds riding a horse on top of each other, the little horse died on the way due to excessive fatigue. Genghis Khan buried the dead body of eight horses wrapped in brocade.
It has been several years since the little shepherd left home, and every day he climbs the Arugatai platform and looks forward to his son's return soon. One evening, he climbed up to look at his son and muttered to himself, “My son is a great companion. It's been a few years since he left, why hasn't he come back?” While he was looking uneasy, a cloud of dust suddenly appeared in the distance. He happily went back to his house and said to his old partner, “Oh, I have good news for you. I just saw the dust from Qianlima. This must have been our son's return. If he were the one running, no horse would have caught up with him.” After speaking, he quickly went to the blacksmith workshop to find a five-long iron hook, rushed outside, and waited for Hou's own son to arrive. However, what came running was an unrided millimeter horse. He was amazed and thought, “My son will never fall off the back of a horse! Maybe they were taken from heaven or Earth?” He went on his way to hook the running Qianlima and hooked Ma Lele, but he didn't hold it. After stopping for a while, he pulled it hard, and still didn't hold it. At last, Qianlima escaped.
Legend has it that since then, although Qianlima was born in a remote frontier, it has stayed in the Central Plains region forever.
