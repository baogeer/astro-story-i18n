 When Heyi came to power, the Qing dynasty was very corrupt. Local government officials were corrupt and rampant, and people were full of grievances. At the time, in the Hubei and Henan regions, the White Lotus religion was popular again. There is Liu Song, a native of Anhui, who went to Henan to preach and used the opportunity to treat people's illnesses to persuade people to enroll in education. He was later discovered by the government and banished to Gansu.
Liu Song's apprentices Liu Zhixie and Song Zhiqing fled to Hubei to continue their preaching. They propagandize that the Qing dynasty is about to fall, and a new world will emerge in the future, and all those who have joined the religion will be given land. Poor local farmers have suffered enough from being exploited by landlords and are eager to get land. After listening to this propaganda, they have joined the White Lotus religion one after another.
Emperor Qianlong was shocked by the news that more and more people were attending the White Lotus Church. Emperor Qianlong ordered all provincial palaces to apprehend believers. Some government officials were originally veterans of extortion. They took the opportunity to send missionaries and inquire door-to-door. Whether you are a religious or not, you have to pay a sum of money to “honor” them. Rich people pay for their lives; poor people with no money are taken to jail and tortured, or even killed. An official in Wuchang failed to extort extortion from the public. He was charged with several thousand people. Both believers and non-religious people have been persecuted and destroyed, and they hate the government even more.
Liu Zhixie, the leader of the Bai Lian Church, went to Xiangyang and summoned the believers to meet and discuss. Everyone said, “In this world, the government is really forcing the people against it! Why don't you just rebel.” After some discussion, it was decided to launch a mass uprising using the slogan “Officials force the people to fight back,” and to send religious groups to various places to make contacts.
In 1796 AD, the same year that Emperor Jiaqing took office, the Bai Lian people held uprisings in places such as Yidu and Zhijiang in Hubei. There is Qi Lin, the leader of the White Lotus Church in the Yangyang region. It was originally scheduled to rise during the Lantern Festival. Unexpectedly, the news was leaked. He was attacked by the government, and Qi Lin and more than 100 companions were killed.
Qi Lin has a young wife named Wang Conger. She used to be an art seller. She has practiced martial arts since childhood. Determined to avenge her husband and her fellow rebels, she worked with Qi Lin's apprentice Yao Zhifu to reorganize the uprising team. In less than a month, she formed a rebellion of 450,000 people. Wang Conger, along with other leaders, led the team to crack down on the government and punish corrupt officials everywhere.
When Wang Conger raised an uprising in Hubei, Bailian believers from Sichuan and Shaanxi also responded. The fire of the uprising spread across a large area of the three provinces. Some poor people and refugees all joined the uprising.
As soon as Emperor Jiaqing saw that the rebels were getting louder and louder, he hurriedly ordered local governors, governors, generals, general soldiers, and other large and small officials to dispatch a large number of people to suppress them. However, those senior officials and generals only know about embezzling military salaries; they don't know how to fight wars.
Wang Conger divided his forces and fought from Hubei to Henan. The rebels are not only brave, but also flexible in fighting. When they marched, they didn't have a full team. They saw that the government army faced off face to face, didn't take flat roads, and chose mountain trails to find opportunities to attack the government army. They also divided the soldiers into many teams of a few hundred people. They had their share and split between north and south, leaving the officers and army besieging them in a daze and running for their lives.
Wang Conger's insurgent army is on the move to fight in Hubei, Henan, and Shaanxi to attack the official army. The following year, he joined forces with the rebels there in Sichuan.
When Emperor Jiaqing saw the defeat of the government army, he was all infuriated. He scolded Wang Conger as the culprit, and issued another royal decree harshly reprimanding some of the generals, removing them from their posts, punishing them for crimes, and harshly urging local generals to concentrate their forces and encircle Wang Conger's rebels.
General Ming Liang of the Qing army proposed a vicious plan to the Jiaqing Emperor, asking local landlords to organize armed militias and build bunkers. As soon as the rebels arrived, they rushed the people to the bunker and told them that the rebels couldn't find help from the crowd, and that they would not be able to get food and grass supplies. This practice is called “Strong Wall Seino.” Emperor Jiaqing ordered all regions to adopt this strategy, and it is true that the activities of the rebels are becoming more and more difficult.
The Qing army besieged Wang Conger in northern Sichuan. Wang Conger escaped the siege of the Qing army and personally led 20,000 horses to attack Xi'an. Unexpectedly, in Xi'an, he was stopped by the government army and defeated; when they returned to Hubei, Ming Liang led the official army to closely pursue it. The rebels had an army behind them, and intercepted by an armed militia group of landowners in front, and finally fell into an enemy encirclement in the Sancha River area of Yunxi (in present-day Hubei Province, Yunyin County).
Wang Conger was not afraid in the face of danger. He directed the rebels to retreat to the Maoshan Forest and prepare to organize a breakthrough. The military discovered it and surrounded Maoshan again. They closely hugged Maoshan from the front and back of the mountain. After tenacious resistance, the rebels finally failed. Wang Conger and Yao Zhifu saw that they were unable to break through. They retreated to the top of the mountain, jumped off a steep cliff, and died heroically.
After the death of the heroine Wang Conger, rebels from all over the world continued to fight against the government. It took a total of nine years for the Qing dynasty to suppress this great uprising. However, the Qing dynasty went through this severe blow and fell flat ever since.
After the death of Emperor Jiaqing, his son Min Ning (Min Min) ascended the throne, that is, Emperor Xuanzong of the Qing Dynasty, also known as Emperor Daoguang. After Emperor Daoguang took office, the Qing dynasty gradually declined, Western capitalist countries took the opportunity to step up their aggression, and the national crisis became more and more serious. By 1840 AD, the 20th year of Emperor Daoguang's inauguration, the Opium War broke out. After that, China gradually changed from a feudal society to a semi-colonial and semi-feudal society. The heroic Chinese people fought an indomitable and arduous struggle to oppose capital imperialist aggression and feudal rule. Chinese history has entered a new period — the modern period of history.
