 During the rainy season, Caozhou peonies, which can be called the “national color and fragrance,” compete to bloom. The colorful flowers bloom all over the branches to welcome visitors from China and abroad with a smile. There is only one type of peony that is different from all other flowers. Its silver-red flowers do not bloom on branches, but are hidden in leafy foliage. People call them “Tibetan pearls.” Later, someone changed the name to it and called it Laughing in the Heart. Actually, she wasn't laughing, she was crying 2
It was during the Yongle years of the Ming Dynasty. There was a beautiful garden in northeastern Caozhou, and thousands of peonies were planted domestically.
The name of the flower farmer who runs this Shedan Garden is He Yuzhang. He Yuzhang's wife died when he was 40, leaving behind his daughter He Zhen, who had just turned four. Although the father and daughter don't worry about food or clothing, they always feel deserted and miserable. The townspeople all advised him to renew his wife's marriage. He always argues that he is older, but everyone in the village knows that he loves his daughter He Zhen and is afraid that his daughter-in-law will feel bad for the child. He Yuzhang is both a father and a wife. After being busy at home, he would rather suffer for the sake of his children.
One day, after dinner, Little Ho Chun learned to paint peony flowers under an oil lamp, and Ho Yuzhang made clothes for her daughter. He was clumsy and always unable to fix thick lines; his finger was pierced with a needle several times. Suddenly, he heard someone knocking on the door. He thought it was Gudan from Murakami who walked through the door again. There was a thin woman standing in front of the door. Before Ho Yuzhang asked, the woman sobbed and said, “Big brother, my name is Liu, and I'm from Henan. There have been major droughts in recent years, and the particles in the fields have been confiscated. They have come out to escape the desert, and I can't move!” He Yuzhang saw that she was pitiful. He hurriedly took out two wowotsu and put them to Liu: “Let's eat!” Then Liu took a look at Ho Yuzhang and whispered, “Give me some grass, I live here.” Before she finished speaking, He Yuzhang hurriedly waved his hand; “No! no! I'm a bachelor. It's not convenient, late at night, in the same room—...”
“Tell my aunt to sleep with me.” Little Ho Zhen ran over, took Liu's hand, and playfully said, “My dad's hand is broken, you fix my clothes.” Liu looked at the lovely Little Ho Zhen and smiled and walked into the house. She didn't make a sound. She picked up her clothes and sat on the bed to fix them. She was so careful and so good at sewing, that made little Ho Zhen enjoy jumping straight to the top. He Yuzhang was overwhelmed by the sidelines. He only looked out the door, fearing that others would run into him. At this point, the two dogs also arrived. As soon as he entered the door, he stopped: “Yo, when did my uncle become a family, and he hasn't said anything to his nephew, he hasn't given you a congratulatory speech yet.” He Yuzhang was about to argue. The two dogs laughed and ran away. He Yuzhang was in a hurry: “Tell you to go, if you don't go, look at this mess...” Liu put down her clothes. As soon as she stood up, he was held back by Ho Zhen. Liu bent over and picked up Little Ho Zhen and gave her a sweet kiss on the little face. He Zhen smiled. Her face was red, like a peony flower.
There was a burst of laughter, and the small house was full of people. This one wanted to eat candy. The one wanted to drink happy wine. “Boom Boom Boom” rang out in the yard, making him unable to argue or cry and laugh. The neighbor's aunt hugged Little Ho Zhen, and the two dogs drove away the crowd, ordered everyone to have a nice drink tomorrow, and turned around and locked the door of the house tightly.
What family had two members turned into a family of three. With one more woman in the family, the days have changed; people wash clothes when they are dirty, shoes and socks are broken, and others are sewn. When you get back from work in the fields, you can eat hot meals without doing anything yourself. What made Ho Yuzhang even more satisfied was that Liu treated Ho Zhen as close as his own.
The following year, Liu Shi gave birth to a girl named Wei Zhu. Liu Shi said to Liu Yuzhang, “I really want to give you a son!” “But Ho Yuzhang said, “How good is a son to have a daughter; a daughter is filial.” He Yuzhang is right in saying this. Fifteen years later, the two daughters have grown up. They respect their parents and never offend the elderly. Although the two sisters are five years apart, they are all the same height and look the same. People who come to see peonies say they are twins born from a mother. The younger sisters are very good. They are struggling with tiring work, have good food to eat, work in a field during the day, and sleep in one bed at night. They are as good as being alone. It's just that since Liu had a daughter, she hasn't been that close to Ho Zhen. He Zhen went down to the field to plow the field, left Ho Zhu at home to embroider, and secretly left delicious things to his biological daughter Ho Zhu. Ho Chu is mad at motherhood; they think she is eccentric. He Yuzhang didn't know anything about these things; he bluntly boasted that Liu taught women how to be smart.
Everyone said, “When my daughter is older, I miss my son-in-law.” This statement is absolutely true. He Zhen and He Zhu all had their interests; they just didn't talk about it to outsiders. They both whispered and said in a playful voice that if they don't marry a rich man, they don't marry an official; they want to marry a hardworking and loyal crop man. Her younger sister, He Zhu, is an affectionate person. She cultivated a peony tree in Qiyuan. The peony flowers have dense leaves, and the branches are hard and straight. She wants to wait for her sister to get married and give this magnolia to her sister for marriage. It's just that this stocking peony hasn't bloomed for three years. No one knows what breed it is, but He Zhu sees it as a baby.
The rain was good this spring, and the peonies in Qiyuan were so gorgeous. The peony that Ho Zhu nurtured also gave birth to flower buds. She was happy and wouldn't leave half a step. On this day, He Zhu was watering peonies in the garden. He saw a group of people walking in with an old man dressed in yellow. It turned out that Emperor Yongle had gone down to Jiangnan and passed through Caozhou to see the peonies in Qiyuan. Emperor Yongle was fascinated by the graceful, gorgeous, and beautiful peony. He saw the flower swaying in the wind, especially the smiling face of a beautiful woman. The emperor was happy for a moment and wrote two lines: “Three thousand beautiful beauties in the palace are less than a flower in a beautiful garden.”
The Governor of Caozhou held the letters in his hands, carefully watched the taste, and was full of praise. He looked up and saw He Zhu, looked at the letters in his hand, and laughed: it turns out that the emperor had an eye on Ho Yuzhang's daughter and wanted to choose her as his wife! He is thankful that he is smart and has not lost the opportunity to advance to the post and become rich. Actually, the emperor had no intention of choosing a concubine; he just praised peonies as being more beautiful than royal concubines. However, the Governor of Caozhou said that the emperor wrote “A Flower in Qiyuan” referring to He Yuzhang's daughter, and that he also personally escorted the “Lady Noble Lady” to Beijing.
When the news came, Qiyuan was messing around and crying. He Yuzhang didn't know which daughter the emperor liked, and he didn't know which one should be sent to the government. Liu couldn't bear to take her biological daughter Ho Zhu to the palace. She knew that entering the Imperial Palace was like being in a prison. There were thousands of beautiful women hidden in the Imperial Palace. The emperor was slightly unhappy; either he gave his concubine to death, or he was thrown into a cold palace. Yun Er, the eldest sister of the mother's family, was elected rich, and she never met the emperor until her death. She couldn't watch her own daughter jump into a fire pit. Sister Ho Zhen and Ho Zhu both cried to tears. They called the emperor a “merry ghost” and a “greedy person”! I don't want to go, and I don't want to ask my sister or sister to go.
The prefectural government has prepared all the large cars for the “Lady Guigi”. Not an hour passed, and visitors were urged three times. He Yuzhang summoned Liu and his two daughters in front of him. Ask which daughter would like to join the palace as a queen. Both girls didn't answer; they just cried. Liu said, “He Zhu is young, so let's call Ho Zhen.” As soon as he heard it, Ho Zhu stopped crying and said, “You can't let my sister go. What the emperor saw in the garden was me. How can I hurt my sister if I want to die or live, I will take my life!” “No, I'll go!” He Zhen hurriedly said, “We both look alike, and it's hard to tell the emperor apart; my younger sister practiced filial piety a lot at home!” As I said, I said goodbye to my parents and my sister. He Zhu took the lead and refused to give up. The two sisters continued to argue, and Ho Yuzhang had no idea. Liu said to Ho Yuzhang, “Both girls would like to go; it's better to draw a lottery to win.” He Yuzhang was helpless and had to order Liu to find two bamboo sticks. The couple went to the in-house room to make them.
The sisters encouraged each other and secretly prayed in their hearts to let disaster happen to themselves. At this point, Liu came out pretty from her room, pulled Ho Zhu aside, and whispered, “If your daughter wants to go to the palace as a queen, your mother will be all you. As long as you draw that red flag, you can make your wish come true.” He Zhu nodded tearfully.
As soon as He Yuzhang left the room with a bamboo signature in his hand, He Zhu grabbed the red signature and took a look. The word “stay” was written on it, then looked at the signature in his sister's hand, which had the word “go” written on it with a black pen. He Zhu knew that her mother had hurt her. She regretted being fooled by her mother. She stared at Liu's eyes. She was so angry that she couldn't say a word.
My sister Ho Chun is gone, but my younger sister Ho Joo fell ill! She couldn't listen to her father's comfort or her mother's persuasion. I didn't eat or drink, I just cried.
Three days later, some people reported that when the “Lady Noble Lady” was crossing the Yellow River, it was hit by wind and waves, and the boat overturned and people died. After hearing it, it was like a thunder. She climbed up from her bed, walked into the garden, and burst into tears at the magnificence tree she personally nurtured for her sister. She slowly knelt down on her knees...
When people came to the garden looking for Ho Zhu, they saw her die holding that peony in her arms. The green leaves of the peony cover her painful face. He Zhu held the peony so tightly that they couldn't separate them. When Ho Zhu was buried, they had no choice but to bring the peony into the soil one by one.
The following year, the peonies that were enlisted grew again. The flowers and leaves were dense, and the branches were hard and straight. Strangely enough, the flower didn't bloom on a branch, but was hidden under a green leaf, like a young girl's face hidden in a dense layer of green leaves. If you look closely, you'll see a few drops of water hanging on the silver-red flower. It's like sad tears. People named this type of peony “Tibetan Pearl,” and later generations called it “laughing in the middle of the bush.” Actually, she wasn't laughing, she was crying.
