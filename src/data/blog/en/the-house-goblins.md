 Li Gong from Changshan County is Li Gong, the nephew of Li Da Si Kou. He often had strange appearances in his family. Once, Li Gong saw a long bench in the room. It was flesh-red and very delicate. Since he hadn't seen this before, he came close and touched it. As soon as you touch it, the bench bends in your hand and is as soft as meat. Li Gong was shocked and pulled out and left. While walking, they looked at the same thing. The thing moved on all four legs and gradually hid into the wall. Once again, Li Gong saw a white slender wooden cane standing on the wall, which was very smooth and clean. As soon as he came close and held his hand, the wooden cane fell softly and bent like a snake into the wall. He couldn't see it for a while.
In the 17th year of Kangxi, a scholar, Wang Junsheng, taught at Li Gong's family. At dusk one day, as soon as the light was turned on, Mr. Wang was lying in bed with his shoes on. Suddenly, I saw a little man, over three inches tall, coming in from outside the door, and after a bit of twisting, he went out again. After a while, the kid brought two small benches and placed them in the middle of the house, like a child's toy bench made of sorghum straw. After a while, the two little people brought in a coffin, but it was more than four inches long and placed on two small benches. The arrangements were not ready yet. I saw another woman bring in a few female maids, all as small as the previous villains. The woman was dressed in a filial dress, tied with twine around her waist, covered her head with white cloth, covered her mouth with her sleeves, and cried softly. It sounded like a big fly's cry. Mr. Wang peeked for a long time and was terrified. He felt like frost all over his body. He screamed and pulled out his leg, but instead of being able to run, he fell under the bed, shivering all over his body, and couldn't stand up. When people in the museum heard shouting and hurriedly rushed to take a look, all the little people and small objects in the house disappeared.
