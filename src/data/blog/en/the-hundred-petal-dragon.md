 A long time ago, on the shores of Tiaoxi in northern Zhejiang, a hardworking and kind young couple lived. My wife was pregnant for one year and gave birth to a freak. Like a human being, like a snake, not a snake.
When the old patriarch found out, he was furious and bluntly determined that it was an ominous thing, forcing the couple to immediately get the baby stuck to death. The couple couldn't bear it, so they clenched their teeth and asked the man to pick up the footrest and throw the baby into the lotus pond before entering the entrance.
A few days later, the wife got out of bed and went to the lotus pond to pick rice. Suddenly, she saw her son swimming from the lotus bush to Shibu to ask for milk from his mother. My mother always loved her son, so she picked her up and breastfed her. Just like that, after picking rice three times a day, she fed her son three times. One day, two days, 360 days passed, and my son gradually grew up, but now he looks like a dragon.
The news reached the ears of the old patriarch again. He knew that the monster not only didn't get stuck, but rather grew up and was very angry, so he came up with a poison plan to kill it.
At noon one day, this old man hid a firewood chopper next to him and secretly hid in the woods by the pool. By the time the woman was looking for rice, she saw a little dragon swimming ashore to ask her for milk to eat.
The old man raised his sword when he saw it, and the little dragon hurriedly jumped into the lotus pond. But it was too late. This knife cut off the tail of the little dragon, and blood spilled instantly. There just happened to be a big beautiful butterfly parked in the pool. The butterfly saw the little dragon being harmed for no reason. It lost its tail, and was very sympathetic. It immediately flew over and attached it to its tail with its own body, so it became a beautiful dragon tail.
At this point, the wind blew, and the dark clouds rolled over, and the petals of the pond full of lotus flowers swirled. All of a sudden, the dragon grew to about ten feet long. It suddenly jumped out of the pool, filled its body with lotus petals, and flew straight into the sky.
When the old man saw this momentum, he was so frightened that he passed out and died. As soon as Xiaolong's mother saw her son flying through the clouds, she called Xiaolong three times with tears in her eyes, but the dragon had already flown without a trace.
Since then, whenever there is a drought on both sides of the Tiaoxi River, Xiaolong has flown in to bring clouds and rain, so that the townspeople have a good harvest. In order to thank it, ordinary people in the Changxing area use colored cloth to make lotus petal dragon scales and butterfly dragon tails to make the famous “Baiyerong”. Every year during the Spring Festival, people dance dragons to commemorate and celebrate.
