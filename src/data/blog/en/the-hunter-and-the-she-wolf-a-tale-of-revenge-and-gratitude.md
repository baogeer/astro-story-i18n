The majestic Qinling Mountains stretch for hundreds of miles. The mountains are undulating, vast and lush, and full of clouds and fog all year round. There is a small village below Chifeng, the main peak in the Qinling Mountains. The village is named after the peak, called Chifeng Village. 780 households all cultivate. There are many birds and animals on the peak, especially wolves. Perhaps because there are many small wild animals in the Fangyuan area, wolves never go down the mountain to harass animals. Wolves live in the mountains, and people live at the bottom of the peak, and have been safe and sound for many years.
In the fall of this year, Xiao Dafeng went to the city to buy a shotgun. Taking advantage of his leisure time, he wanted to go to Chifeng to fight some mountain chickens, rabbits, etc.
On this day, Xiao Dafeng was wandering around the mountain path carrying a shotgun. Suddenly, he discovered a bald-tailed wolf hiding in a short jungle. He was flashing a pair of green, quiet eyes, and was watching him with caution. Xiao Dafeng couldn't help but itch his hands: the old wolf's fluff was soft. He peeled off the wolf skin and made a blanket for his newborn son, Xiaofeng, and then picked up a gun to target the old wolf. However, the bald-tailed old wolf was extremely alert. Obviously, when he discovered Xiao Dafeng's intention, spread his four legs and flew deep into the jungle. He also deliberately rushed left and right, making a noise “brushing” against the bramble tree.
When Xiao Dafeng saw that the wolf had escaped, he was about to go after him. He overheard a “humming” sound. He followed the sound and took a look. He saw a young wolf lying on a field of hay. His body was shaking slightly. His eyes cleared of fear one by one, and looked at him timidly.
Xiao Dafeng understood. It turns out that the bald-tailed female wolf was to cover up the coyote's suffering, only to run away on purpose and try to lure herself away. As soon as his heart moved, he took the first step, picked up the little wolf's back leg and turned to the bottom of the peak. Sure enough, after a while, a sad cry came from behind Xiao Dafeng; it was the bald-tailed female wolf who followed him.
Xiao Dafeng hurriedly picked up his shotgun. How could he grab the baby wolf's back leg with one hand. The wolf was struggling in his hands. The shotgun's barrel trembled. He fired three shots in a row, and all the bullets flew off. When the female wolf flashes left and right, she just refuses to leave, making a long cry of mourning. Xiao Dafeng suddenly developed a hint of secrecy. Remembering his son who had just been born a few months ago, he stood still for a while, clenched his teeth, and drank: “Give it back to you!” Then he threw the wolf cub at the bald-tailed female wolf. How could anyone have guessed it? Little Wolf cub's head just hit a sharp, hard rock. The little wolf cub screamed and fluttered to the ground. After twitching a few times, it stopped moving. The bald-tailed female wolf screamed like crazy, swooped at the young wolf cub, licked blood stains in a circle, and kept whining...
Xiao Dafeng couldn't have imagined this fall. He actually knocked the little wolf cub to death. He couldn't help but feel guilty. As soon as he stomped his foot violently, he turned around and went down the mountain.
When people in the village learned that Xiao Dafeng had lost her hand and fell to death, they were all shocked and said, “If you fall to death the little wolf cub, the female wolf will definitely take revenge!”
When Xiao Dafeng listened, he couldn't help but feel afraid. Only then did he learn that the matter was serious. I'm in the light, and the wolf is in the dark, but it's really hard to deal with.
Sure enough, in the middle of the night, Xiao Dafeng was going back and forth and had trouble falling asleep. He overheard a few horrible sounds of wolves coming from outside. He jumped out of his bed, grabbed his shotgun leaning on the edge of the bed, touched the window □, and watched the outside with caution. Unexpectedly, the wolf screamed miserably in front of the house, then fell silent. However, the unexpectedly quiet made him unable to sleep at any rate this night. He comforted his wife Ling Ling a few words and sat down until dawn while holding a shotgun.
Just like that, the bald-tailed female wolf came to the front and back of Xiao Dafeng's house in the middle of the night and screamed, sometimes even grabbing doors and chopping windows with her claws, causing the Shaw family and his wife to shock. Xiao Dafeng tried to break out of the house with a gun several times, but they were all held back by Ling Ling's life.
Xiao Dafeng was also criticized by villagers from time to time, saying that he should not have broken the village rules, gone up the mountain to hunt, hurt lives, and cause female wolves to harass the village every day in the middle of the night, and the family must not be at peace. He also knew that he had to make up for his losses; he had no choice but to let the villagers complain. What made it most unbearable for him was that every night he had to boldly carry a shotgun, fearing that the vengeful bald-tailed she-wolf would break through the door and window.
A month passed in the blink of an eye, and Xiao Dafeng had already been tortured to lose weight by a circle.
On this day, Xiao Dafeng's brother-in-law got married. Naturally, the couple wanted to have a nice drink. To go over Chi Peak to his father-in-law's village, Xiao Dafeng immediately remembered the bald-tailed she-wolf that had bothered him for many days. Just in case, he carried that shotgun and had another dagger around his waist.
As soon as he got to Chi Feng, Xiao Dafeng knew by feeling that the bald-tailed female wolf had already caught up with them.
Xiao Dafeng pretended not to know, and let Ling Ling hold her son Xiaofeng in front and carry a shotgun herself after breaking. Walking to the right, he heard -- a slight “rustling” sound came; he suddenly turned back and raised his hand -- and saw the bald-tailed female wolf tumbling one by one, quickly climbed up, and limped away. He wouldn't let go of this opportunity and go after him with a gun, determined to get rid of this heartache.
Xiao Dafeng followed a mountain bag with a curse grin, and suddenly lost his goal. He had no choice but to slow down, pick up his shotgun, learn how Japanese devils enter the village in the movie, and search a hundred times more wary. Somehow, he stepped on a piece of moss. He slipped violently under his feet, and one by one fell into a trench more than ten meters deep. He fell into his head and his eyes blazed with gold; to make matters worse, his right leg was knocked on a prominent stone, causing heartbreaking pain. He gnawed his teeth and tried to climb up. Unfortunately, the deep wall of the ditch was steep and there was nowhere to climb. He never climbed up even after trying. He was in such a hurry that he patted and slapped his voice and shouted: “Ling Ling, come on!”
Ling Ling hugged Xiaofeng and followed the cry. She saw Xiao Dafeng falling in a trench and wanted to reach out and pull, but her hands couldn't reach it. Xiao Dafeng was in a hurry and said, “Hurry up, cut some wisteria strips!” He said he pulled out a short knife from his waist and threw it up.
As soon as Ling agreed, she put down Xiaofeng, picked up a short knife, and cut some wisteria strips at the edge of a cliff not far away. While rushing back, she conveniently tied a thick rope like a knot of bramble, then lowered the thick rope down the ditch, and finally pulled the man up.
Xiao Dafeng took a big breath and rubbed her right leg, and even called “I'm scared.” Suddenly, Ling Ling cried out of shame: “Xiaofeng, Xiaofeng is gone!” It turns out that when Xiao Dafeng and Ling Ling were busy, their son, Xiaofeng, had no idea where he had gone.
Komine can't walk yet. After more than ten minutes, he won't climb too far. However, the two shouted and didn't hear the slightest response; they looked nearby, and there were no people. Xiao Dafeng couldn't help but tremble, stomping his feet and shouting, “It must have been that bald-tailed she-wolf, it must have dragged our son away!” Ling Ling didn't have any other ideas; she just cried bitterly. Xiao Dafeng went crazy and screamed in front of and behind the mountain, searching every corner, and didn't find Xiaofeng until the sky turned dark, and there was no trace of the bald-tailed female wolf. He had to support his wife and stumbled down the mountain.
Second largest, third day... Xiao Dafeng is still hopeful to go up the mountain to search, but he is disappointed again and again. Ten days later, Xiao Dafeng was completely hopeless. Her son had no hope of being alive; even though he wasn't eaten by wolves, he starved to death even when hungry, because Xiaofeng hadn't been weaned yet! Despite this, he continued to carry a shotgun and search all over the mountains for the bald-tailed she-wolf; he had to get rid of it with his own hands. But regardless of whether he digs traps or sets up clips, the cunning bald-tailed female wolf just doesn't show up. He walked all over the pond and never found the wolf's den.
One afternoon, an exhausted Xiao Dafeng was sitting behind a big rock eating dry food and suddenly heard a loud noise not far away. He grabbed his shotgun and quietly turned around. Sure enough, the bald-tailed female wolf he hated was drinking water by the creek. Xiao Dafeng held his breath and picked up his shotgun... But as soon as he was about to pull the tablet, he suddenly changed his mind and put down his shotgun. When the female wolf had enough water to leave, he secretly followed the female wolf.
The bald-tailed she-wolf limped on one leg and walked in a hurry, not noticing that it was already being watched. Finally, the female wolf stopped on a steep slope. It looked back, looked wary for a while, and quickly penetrated into a bunch of thorns. It turns out that the hole in the wolf's den was covered by a bunch of thorns. Xiao Dafeng couldn't help but think abruptly: Isn't this where he fell into a trench the other day when he hunted down a bald-tailed female wolf? That trench was about ten feet away from the wolf's den. In an instant, he seemed to see the scene at the time: Komine saw that his mom was busy and couldn't take care of him. She climbed and climbed on the ground, climbed near the wolf's den, and was bitten off her throat in one bite by the bald-tailed female wolf! “Xiaofengfeng, Dad will definitely avenge you!” Xiao Dafeng whispered with hate, crept close to the wolf's den, and gently pulled open the twig strip. Sure enough, he found a hole. Xiao Dafeng leaped gently to the side and carried a few stones to firmly block the hole. Then I took a breath, sat on the side, lit a cigarette, and thought about how to get rid of this bunch of wolves. He snorted out a cigarette and let out a ray of smoke. Suddenly an idea came up: use smoke!
Xiao Dafeng found some hay branches, piled them up at the entrance of the hole, and sprinkled a small bottle of anti-venomous snake sulfur on it, and ignited it with a lighter. The flames began to roar, and the crackling became more and more intense. He took off his jacket and fanned it hard. The smoke had a pungent smell of sulfur, and went straight into the hole. Soon after, Xiao Dafeng heard a desperate cry and humming cry coming from the cave. I couldn't help but feel the thrill of revenge. The cave gradually calmed down. After wiping away his sweat and taking a heavy breath, he removed the wood ash, removed the stones, and waited for the smoke in the cave to dissipate. He brightened the lighter, slapped his waist, and drilled in. He wants to find the bald-tailed female wolf, peel its skin, and eat its flesh to avenge the loss of her son.
With the weak light of the lighter, he found that the hole was covered with dried leaves. The bald-tailed female wolf lay with stiff limbs in the hay, and died protecting her pups on her stomach. However, Ling Ling didn't notice Xiaofeng. The old wolf who lost her cub was holding back and feeling bad, so she pulled and dragged Xiaofeng under her stomach and stuffed his pacifier in his little mouth...
