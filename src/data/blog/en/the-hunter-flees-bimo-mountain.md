 Ma Zizhong
Bimo Mountain Liangzi, a branch on the western side of Shimen, was often infested with white-haired savages and brown-red bears during the Ming and Qing Dynasties.
Legend has it that in the late Ming and early Qing dynasties, a mountaineer brought three mountain climbing dogs to Mount Bimo to hunt (hunt). After two days of hunting in the jungle, I only caught two Huaqing chickens. On the third day there was continuous drizzling rain. When the rain turned into winter, suddenly there were lots of goose feather snowflakes. The mountain master and three hunting dogs surrounded a fire in a cliff cave to keep warm, while drinking hot tea while grilling chicken and soba to eat. I didn't hit the mountain animals (wild beasts), but I couldn't let the hunting dogs starve. They almost finished eating chicken and buckwheat noodles. There's only one breakfast left for tomorrow. After having lunch, he tried his luck in the jungle. Unexpectedly, the three hunting dogs suddenly raised their ears and looked east and west with alarm. He knew that the hunting dogs had already smelled the smell. Next, the hunting dog barked wildly out of the fire pond and flew into the woods behind the cliff. He was busy picking up his crossbow. The crossbow was crouching behind an old chestnut tree outside the cave, waiting for the hunting dog to kick his prey in front of him. If it was a big cat (tiger), he would go up a tree and shoot him with a crossbow to prevent accidents.
The dog barking was getting closer. Something was wrong with him. The dog barking trembled and trembled, and it was a fearful cry. If you meet a rabbit in Zhangji, the dog would never sound like this! It seemed that it was either a wild boar, an old bear, a leopard, or a big cat. He was staring ahead without blinking his eyes. Not good! The three hunting dogs howled and leaped back; instead, they were driven away by mountain cattle. He was left wondering, “What kind of beast is this? Hunting dogs are so afraid of it. Wait for them to be brought to the caves and ready to shoot! Amidst suspicion, the branches on the hillside in front of me swayed, and there was a rattling roar. It sounded both laughing and angry. Oops, it's a human bear! A human bear covered with long brownish red hair. A human bear that can climb trees and kill tigers! Chased down from the dense forest on the hillside. In the midst of wind, rain, and snow, it's like a torch. I ripped off the long hair in front of my face with my hands and swayed back quickly and slowly. It's like playing with dogs for fun, and it's also like chasing dogs to catch them; there's no fear. The three hunting dogs were afraid to run forward and bite. They stepped back step by step and alerted the owner to the police with a fearful howl.
His heart was beating so nervous that he didn't dare to draw a sword and fight hard with people or bears. He pulled out a poisonous arrow with crossbow medicine on it and placed it on an already stretched crossbow. His eyes were closely watching the bear. Closer, 70 steps; closer, 30 steps, he reached into his mouth and whistled, and the hunting dog quickly dodged. When people and bears were shocked, they stopped and looked east and west. The opportunity must not be missed. He raised his crossbow and aimed at the fatal point of the human bear, firing a string of arrows, and shooting the poisonous arrow into the white furry of the bear's chest. The man and Xiong Xia screamed and immediately fell to the ground. The crossbow medicine infiltrated blood and sealed the throat. Mountain craftsmen often apply this poison to arrows against fierce beasts. “Dangerous...” He was shocked and sweaty. He took a long sigh of relief. He had just left the roots of the old chestnut tree, only to see hunting dogs howling fearfully into the jungle on the hillside. “Strange!” He looked forward. The swinging branches vaguely revealed brownish red hairs. The back and forth was not good! Here are two more bears! One shot was shot, but the mountain taker didn't have enough time to deal with two bears. Escaped quickly. He flew into the cliff cave in three steps, picked up his backpack, blew a whistle, and ran downhill. The hunting dog also turned his head and went away with the owner. There was a lot of rain and snow, and the wet ground was slippery. He ran wild and fell all the way. After running for about 500 or 600 steps, he heard people roaring furiously, and probably saw his partner who had been killed... He didn't dare to think too much and ran all the way down. It doesn't matter if it falls and crawls; all I want is to escape from being chased by humans and bears. While barking boldly, the three hunting dogs followed their owner and defeated their protector. He heard heavy footsteps behind him, and no matter how tired he was, he didn't dare to stop to take a breath, and didn't dare to look back. After running back and forth, down slope after slope, he listened to the movement with his ears standing still. It wasn't until the sound of footsteps behind him gradually moved away, hanging in the heart of his voice, that he fell back to his stomach. He didn't dare to stop; he went on a steep slope before sitting down to rest. He rolled all over his body into a mud sow, and the pain felt like whipping. Finally, I took a long sigh of relief. Despite running away and falling all the way, they finally took their lives. The three hunting dogs sat affectionately next to him. He happily touched their heads and patted them on the back. These are partners in life and death.
Since then, this mountain master has never dared to go to Cangshan alone to hit the mountains.
