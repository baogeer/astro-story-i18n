 At the beginning, when Jiayuguan City was built, tens of thousands of stones 2 meters long, 0.5 meters wide, and 0.3 meters thick were needed. After the craftsmen cut the stones in Montenegro, people couldn't lift them, cars couldn't move, and the mountains were too high to transport. Everyone was worried while chiseling stones. Seeing that the middle of winter season is coming, not a single piece of stone has been shipped out of the mountain. If you want to delay the construction period, it's too small to save if you don't have a salary. Everyone was shouting and sighing. At this point, suddenly there was a thunder at the top of the mountain, and a picture of silk floated from the white clouds. The craftsmen quickly picked it up, only to see that there were a few lines on it. After reading it, everyone suddenly realized and acted according to it. After winter arrived, people built a road from the mountain to Guancheng, splashed water on the road surface to form an ice track, and then glided and transported the stones on the ice track. As a result, the stones were transported very smoothly to Jiayuguancheng. Not only did they not delay the construction period, but on the contrary, they saved a lot of construction time. In order to thank God for their blessings, the craftsmen built a temple near Guancheng to worship the throne, and became a place that craftsmen must visit after leaving their studies.
