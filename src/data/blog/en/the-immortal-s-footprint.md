 There is a giant stone in the stone shed south of Shimenkou in Hongmiao Township, Mingguang. The stone surface is relatively flat. In the center of the stone surface, there is a large pit about two feet long, eight inches wide, and about one inch deep. It looks like a footprint. The five toes of the footprints are clearly recognizable. This big footprint is called a “fairy foot” by the local people. Legend has it that Laozi Li Er lobbied in the East back then.
In spring and autumn, a famous scholar, Li Er, Zi Boyang, later known as “Laozi”, was a famous scholar in Ku County, Chu Guo (now known as “Laozi”). He was in charge of the Zhou dynasty's books, painstakingly studied “Dao” and “Dao Jing,” and was the author of “Dao Jing” and “De Jing,” the full name of which is “Tao Te Jing.” Legend has it that Confucius lobbied in Chu and went to the door to ask Laozi for advice. After the fall of the Zhou dynasty, Laozi lobbied everywhere to spread his “Tao Te Ching.” At the beginning, I brought a book boy with a bamboo basket on my shoulder. I only traveled 50 miles a day. Every time I went, I used a school to preach morality. It was very hard work. Later, I got a young man in Chuhuai. Not only can a book basket be placed on the back of a cow, but Laozi also rode on the back of a cow. In this way, I would travel hundreds of miles a day.
One day, Laozi crossed the Huai River, rode a green cow south, and walked to the foot of Fushan Mountain. When the blue cow saw the green grass on Fushan Mountain, it aroused his appetite. It took advantage of Laozi getting off the cow and asking for directions and quickly ran up the mountainside. As soon as Laozi saw it, it was amazing. The entire green mountain was shaking, and there was still water splashing along the mountain. Laozi hurriedly shouted at the blue cow and said, “This mountain is unreal; it floats on the surface of the water. Be careful!” Only then did the blue cow feel the mountain tremble slightly, as if standing on a big boat. Green cows don't dare to eat anything. They went down the mountain gently. Just when they were about to go down the mountain, the blue cow felt that its back hooves had sunk down, and the mountain had been trampled down, leaving a deep mark of the cow.
Laozi wanted to walk to Jingcheng (present-day Mingguang Shiba). Qingniu knew his way, and headed all the way southwest. There was no way to get through at that time; you had to go around the old river to get to Jingcheng. At first, the blue cow was carrying Laozi steadily southward, but just arriving in Gangji (present-day Hongmiao Township, Mingguang), the blue cow sped up its steps and headed west. As soon as Laozi saw that the direction was wrong, he desperately told Qingniu to stop. The more you stop it, the faster it runs. While going up the hillside, I accidentally knocked Laozi off the back of the cow, and the blue cow still ran up the mountain even though he didn't look at it. Laozi was so angry that I didn't know what kind of crazy thing the Blue Bull was doing today. Laozi couldn't help but hurt his butt and climbed off the ground to chase the blue cow. Laozi's shoes have also run away, and the gravel and dried artemisia poles are poking his foot again. Laozi had to find a rock and sit down and say to himself, “What happened to this cow now? You don't want to be offended by me, but don't carry away your books!”
Where did Laozi know, this green cow was once a Taoist in the alchemy house of this mountain. For stealing “Yindan” made from Taibai Bingxing for 7,749 days and “Jin Dan” produced in 981 days, he filled it with stones and filled it with stones, causing Taijin Xing to be ridiculed by the crowd at the Pantao meeting of the mother and mother of Wang. After returning to the alchemy room, Taibai Jinxing turned the Taoist who stole Xian Dan into a blue cow. How many years have passed, and the young man Laozi has visited this place again, and he can't help but feel the situation. How did Laozi know about this? I thought the blue cow had been going back and forth with him all day long, and couldn't bear the suffering and left him. What is a pity for Laozi is that two baskets of books that took half his life hard work.
Qingniu ran all the way to the temple at Shimenkou and the mountain house, and saw that the temple had only broken bricks and tiles, and that the Danfang had also become a pile of rubble. The master had ascended to heaven, and the brothers had gone back to the ground. The blue cow burst into tears. After shouting “” a few times, they slowly walked back along the original path.
Laozi saw the blue cow coming back from afar. He took a closer look, only to see the blue cow burst into tears, and he didn't know what actually happened to this cow. He was busy looking through the book. He found that one copy of the “Taojing” was missing. Lao Tzu was angry and anxious. As soon as he stomped his foot violently, he actually had a big footprint stamped out on a flat stone surface. Laozi angrily carried the book basket down his back and said, “You walk your Yangguan Road, I'll cross my wooden bridge; don't follow me from now on.” The blue cow just shouted “shouting” and was still in tears; it seemed like it had been greatly aggrieved. Laozi placed the old book basket on the stone and followed the path the green cow ran through to find the “Taoist Sutra” that had been turned upside down. Coincidentally, it didn't take long to find it. When Laozi came back, he put the two baskets back together and put them firmly on his shoulders. He ignored everything and went back on his way. Poor Qingniu couldn't tell how many things in his heart. It just silently followed Laozi and patted him gently on the tail from time to time. Lao Tzu couldn't stop, and so did the blue cow. Laozi thought, this young cow has been with me for over a year. This is the first time they have acted like today. There may be something strange. Seems like everything is over now. Laozi sighed and said, “Blue Bull, you don't want to leave me, right?” The blue cow nodded.” Then you can stop being aggressive and knock me down in the future.” The blue cow nodded again.” OK, we're still good friends.” The green cow shouted twice, and asked Laozi to put the book basket on his back. Laozi sat on the back of the cow and said, “I don't know where I lost my shoes; I only carried them on your back.” The blue cow shook its ears and walked steadily along its original path.
How many years have passed. The big footprints left by Laozi grew as the stones grew, and now they are nearly two feet long.
