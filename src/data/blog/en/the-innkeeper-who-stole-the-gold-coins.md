 Once upon a time, an old couple opened a roadside hotel. A small merchant staying at the store, wanted to go into the mountains and worship Buddha, so he kept a bag of barley in his hands with an old couple; the merchant also had three coins, which he feared would be lost with him, and they also packed them in a barley bag.
After a few days, the old man said, “Alas, should we make barley wine?” The old lady said, “Sorry, I've run out of barley.”
“Isn't that little merchant still storing a bag? He'll be fine when he comes back.” The old man was so hungry that he came up with this idea.
The old lady poured barley into the “Ga Dou” and prepared to make wine. Suddenly, the bell rang out, and three of Huang Chengcheng's gold coins were pulled out of the barley. The old people were staring at the flowers. The old man said, “Hurry up! fast! Hurry up and hide the coins, put the barley in the bag, and put it away in its original place!”
“If the small businessman gets into trouble, then what should I do?” The old lady is a little afraid of things.
“Idiot!” The old man scolded, “As long as the two of us don't acknowledge our accounts, even the devil can do nothing.”
After a few days, the little merchant came back and couldn't find three coins in the barley, so he went to ask the old couple. The old couple said, “Your barley is on the corner. Let's not say we haven't touched it, we haven't even looked at it; we don't know what gold or silver coins it is!”
The little businessman had no choice but to bring the case to “Chengkang.” In front of the judge, the little businessman said that the old couple stole his coins. The old couple said that he was insulting good people, and also said, “There are tens of thousands of people who have stayed in our store. This is the first time I've met an unreasonable customer like you!”
The judge said, “If you fight back and forth like this, I have no choice. You can only punish all of you a little.” After speaking, pull out two large wooden boxes from the inside, and ask the little merchant to carry one bite and go around the village from left to right; also called the old man to carry one bite and go around the circle from right to left.
The old man was carrying the suitcase and was so tired that he had to put it down for a break and say, “Alas, this box almost crushed all my bones! Grandma, come back for me!” The old lady said, “Shut up! It's all because you said you took the coins, and even the devil didn't know it, that ended up like this!”
Besides, the little businessman walked a while carrying a suitcase and was also very tired and sweaty. As I walked, I sighed and said, “Ahhh! I trusted these two shopkeepers to put the coins in the barley. I didn't expect them to be such ignorant people!”
The old couple and the little businessman were carrying suitcases, and they all went back to “Chengkang.” In front of them, the judge opened the box on the spot and walked out of the box two 17-year-old boys. The young man told what he heard on the way in the original. The owner of the hotel had no choice but to return the coins to the small merchant.
