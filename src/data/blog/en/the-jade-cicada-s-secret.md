 As the saying goes, Shengshi is a popular collector, and now there are more and more people playing with antiques. Yang Ping is one of them. He has collected a lot of jade and is an authoritative expert in the antiques industry. Since he is very famous, Yang Ping also often appears on TV treasure hunting programs to help people identify treasures.
On this day, someone came to the TV station with a jade cicada, claiming that he had accidentally dug it up from the field and wanted Yang Ping to inspect it. Yang Ping took a look and found that the jade cicada was red and bright all over. It was definitely just a bloody jade cicada. This kind of blood is very rare. It was made by soaking the blood from the dead person into the jade. After further research, Yang Ping discovered that the Jade Chan came from the Han Dynasty, which is even more valuable, so he quickly issued an inspection certificate.
A few days later, Yang Ping came to an antique auction site and saw that the blood cicada was being auctioned inside. There was a lot of people in the venue, and there were quite a few people who wanted to collect jade cicadas. When the host offered a reserve price of 100,000, the people present competed to bid. In the end, the price climbed all the way up to 800,000, and then the whole audience was quiet. Yang Ping nodded secretly. Yes, given his knowledge of the ancient jade market, this jade cicada is currently only worth this price.
The host saw the scene and was getting ready to clap. At this point, a voice suddenly shouted, “I'll pay 1 million!” Yang Ping was stunned. Following his popularity, he was taken aback. The person asking for the price was a thin middle-aged woman, who was his ex-wife Fang Hua. When Yang Ping became famous for his collection, he no longer looked down on the wife of the person close to him, Lao Zhuhuang. Afterwards, he met a young woman and divorced Fang Hua. Since then, they have never been in touch, and they never expected to meet here again today.
Seeing that Fanghua took a picture of Yu Chan, Yang Ping was very confused. Fang Hua has followed her for many years, is dazzled, and has a certain understanding of jade. She should know the value of Yu Chan. Why should she buy it at such a high price? Also, although Fanghua loves jade, she never likes to invest or collect. What is her intention in buying Yu Chan? Yang Ping really wanted to go ahead and ask for clarification, but he really didn't have the courage. He thought to himself that he had hurt Fanghua so much, and she would definitely hate herself in her heart.
However, when Yang Ping got home, the more he thought about it, the more strange he felt, so he called his friend Lao Yang and asked him to help inquire. Lao Yang also knows Fang Hua. When Yang Ping first got divorced, he still felt sorry for them. Now that he heard about this incident, he also felt very puzzled and promised to help him find out.
Soon, Lao Yang went back and forth. He brought amazing news: Fang Hua has sold his house in the city and is ready to go back to live in his hometown in the countryside. Yang Ping stayed all of a sudden. The house was given to her by Yang Ping as compensation when she first got divorced, and now it's worth exactly 1 million. It seems that Fanghua used all the money from selling the house to buy that jade cicada. Yang Ping hurriedly asked Lao Yang, “Do you know, why did Fanghua buy that jade cicada?” Lao Yang sighed, “I also asked Fang Hua, but she didn't say anything.” Yang Ping had to stop listening.
Yang Ping didn't hear any more news about Yu Chan for a long time after that. It seems that Fanghua collected it. At this point, Yang Ping had a more beautiful and busy life, so he gradually forgot about this incident.
Two years passed after a blink of an eye. One day Yang Ping came to the hospital for treatment because of a cold. Suddenly, she saw that Fanghua was taking medicine at the pharmacy. Her face was pale. She looked even thinner and emaciated than before. She appeared to be seriously ill. After Yang Ping waited for Fang Hua to leave, she hurriedly asked the doctor what kind of illness she had. The doctor sighed and told him that it was a heart patient and had to be treated with expensive medication. However, the patient's family was obviously very poor. Every time she took ordinary painkillers, she also said that if it continued like this, she was afraid it wouldn't last long.
Yang Ping finished listening and walked out of the hospital silently. Fang Hua looks like this. He is responsible, but it is too late to say anything now. Yang Ping found Lao Yang and talked about Fang Hua's situation. Lao Yang sighed, “Actually, I've heard about her a long time ago, but you've always been very busy, and I haven't had a chance to tell you.” Yang Ping asked Lao Yang, “What Fanghua lacks the most right now is money, but why doesn't she sell Yu Chan?” Lao Yang said, “I just don't understand. After the news of her illness came out, I heard that several people came to her house to buy Jade Cicadas, but she never wanted to sell them, or even take them out and see them.”
Yang Ping thought about it and thought, “Maybe the price has dropped. The antique market is currently sluggish. Plus, the price she originally bought is high, so surely no one can afford the ideal price.” Yang Ping said that he turned around and took out a card from the room and said to Lao Yang, “There's 1.5 million in here. I want you to do me a favor and buy Yu Chan from Fanghua.” Lao Yang listened and hurriedly took the card and said to Yang Ping, “Since you still care about her this way, I will definitely complete this task no matter what.”
Lao Yang arrived the next day. Yang Ping hurriedly asked him if he had bought Yu Chan back, but Lao Yang pulled out the card and shook his head and sighed: “Alas, I don't know what Fanghua actually thought. She never let go. She said that she was worried about her illness and didn't want to spend the wrong amount of money anymore.” Yang Ping felt sad after listening to it. He wanted to personally send cash to Fanghua, but he also thought that Fanghua had a stubborn personality and a deep hate for himself, so he definitely wouldn't accept his funding.
Soon after, news came that Fang Hua had passed away. Yang Ping felt a sense of grief. She thought that Fang Hua had no loved ones around her now, so she rushed to the countryside to cook for her. After the funeral, Yang Ping began cleaning up the house. The house was very dilapidated and the furnishings were very simple, yet Yang Ping never found the jade cicada. He felt very strange. Fanghua didn't sell Yu Chan, nor did he hear about giving it to anyone. Why did Yu Chan disappear?
At this point, Yang Ping suddenly saw something in the brick gap next to the wall. He walked over and took a look. He discovered that it was the Jade Cicada. He didn't expect Fanghua to hide it in such secrecy. At this point, after cleaning up the dust on the jade cicada, Yang Ping inadvertently looked at the light and suddenly felt that the blood color inside the cicada didn't look deep and heavy enough. After taking a closer look, he discovered that it was actually a piece of sheep jade. I heard that someone had sewn a piece of white jade into the leg of a sheep. After a few years, blood seeped into the jade.
Yang Ping had a fine sweat on his head. It seems that in the beginning it was because of the light, plus he was so excited at the time that he made a mistake in his judgment. Fortunately, this incident didn't come out; otherwise, he would have lost his name and ruined his future. At this moment, Yang Ping suddenly understood that Fanghua must have already seen that Yu Chan was a fake before deliberately buying it and not letting it go to the outside world, causing damage to her ex-husband's reputation. Turns out, not only did Fanghua not hate himself, but he also thought about him everywhere. Yang Ping couldn't help but burst into tears when he thought about this.
Soon after, Yang Ping made an amazing move. He sold all of his family's jade and refused to attend any antique-related programs and events. Lao Yang was surprised and asked him why he was doing this. Yang Ping laughed miserably: “All the best jade in the world has been thrown away by me. How else can I play with jade?”
