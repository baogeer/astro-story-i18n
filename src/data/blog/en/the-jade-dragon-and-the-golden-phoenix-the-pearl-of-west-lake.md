 The origin of West Lake has many beautiful mythical legends since ancient times, and the story of the Golden Phoenix, Jade Dragon, and Pearl is one of the most popular.
Legend has it that in ancient times, a jade dragon lived in a stone kiln on the east side of the Tianhe River, and a golden phoenix lived in the woods on the west side of the Tianhe River. Once they found a piece of puyu on Fairy Island in the Milky Way, they worked together for many years, and finally made Puyu into a brilliant pearl. Wherever this pearl shines, the trees are evergreen, and hundreds of flowers bloom.
This news later spread to Tiangong. In order to get this orb, the greedy Queen Mother and Niang sent the Sky Soldier to steal the pearl. Jade Dragon and Jin Feng discovered that the Pearl had disappeared and looked everywhere. I later learned that it was in the hands of the royal mother, so she rushed to Tiangong to claim it from the royal mother. The royal mother refused, and Yu Long and Jin Feng went ahead and robbed them, so you competed with me to take it, and you dragged me around, and the pearl rolled down the Tiangong steps to the world. To keep the Pearl from shattering, the Jade Dragon and the Golden Phoenix also flew down with the Pearl. As soon as this pearl falls to the ground, it instantly becomes a crystal turquoise West Lake. Yulong and Jin Feng couldn't bear to leave the pearl they had worked so hard to create, so they became two mountains to protect it. These two mountains, one is the majestic Mount Yulong (now known as Mount Yuhuang) and the other is the verdant Mount Jinfeng (now known as Mount Fenghuang).
Since then, they have always guarded the shores of West Lake. People in Hangzhou are still singing the song “The Pearl of the West Lake: From the Sky Falling, Dragons, Fei, and Phoenix Dance to Qiantang”.
