 At Yankan Rock Corner, there is a small bird with an emerald green body. It seems like it's always crying and looking for it in a hurry. Speaking of the origin of this kind of jade green bird, there is another sad story.
Father Lu Gan lost his wife in middle age. He is raising a pair of twin sisters. The older sister's name is Lulai, and the younger sister's name is Lu Cai. The sisters are 18 years old, and they are so adorable. At a glance, the two sisters have the exact same appearance and figure; it's hard to tell you and me apart; only by looking closely, can you see that sister Lu Lai has a layer of shallow pimples on her face, and her eyes also seem to reveal the slightest bit of ferocity.
In the summer of that year, my dad was sick in bed. My whole body was covered with sores and sores. It was very uncomfortable. He sought medical treatment everywhere, and never saw Dad's condition improve. The sisters were in a hurry, and the younger sister was particularly sad.
One day, my sister, Lu Cai, came home with a basket full of pork grass. As soon as she put down the basket, a frog jumped out of the basket. The frog jumped in front of Dad's bed and said to Dad in human terms, “Dad, dad, I'm here to ask for family. As long as you give me a girl as a daughter-in-law, your illness will soon get better.”
Dad said, “How can you be a son-in-law if you don't have two or two bodies. He looked at his oldest daughter, “Huh” and ran away; the father shook her head and looked at the second daughter. The daughter knelt down in front of her father and sincerely said, “As long as Dad's illness gets better, my daughter will marry Frog Lang.”
The frog nodded happily. It jumped onto Dad's bed and licked Dad's body with its tongue. Strangely enough, lick one spot at a time. Soon, the abscesses all over Dad's body disappeared, and his body recovered as before.
Dad rolled over and was about to thank Frog Lang. Suddenly, a well-dressed, handsome young man supported him and said, “My father-in-law, I am Frog Lang, a frog dragon living in a dragon pool on the other side of the mountain. I'm in love with your daughter, and I'm here to propose.”
Dad is happy to send his daughter and son-in-law on the road.
Sister Lulai sighed behind closed doors and couldn't help but regret it.
In the fall of the following year, my younger sister, Lu Cai, went back to her mother's house to visit her family. Looking at my little sister's radiant body and her face was spring-colored, showing her beauty and gentleness, I drooled over my sister with envy. She entertained them in a humble manner, and seemed so affectionate. The only thing I could do was pay close attention to Lulai's dad. He saw that her face was sometimes light red, sometimes blue, and her eyes were twitching. I saw that she had no good intentions, and I sighed in my heart. The kind Lu Cai didn't notice this; she didn't pay any attention to her sister.
On the third day, my sister is going back to Ryugu's house. The sister begged that the younger sister must follow her to the Dragon Palace to see and understand; Lu Cai had to agree.
They walked over a mountain. Lulai saw this place with high rocks, sharp cliffs, and no bottom of the road. She rolled her eyes in a daze and asked her sister to take a break here.
The younger sister pointed to the oasis at the bottom of the mountain and told her, “It will be here soon.” She also reached out to take a piece of jade from her intimate area and told her sister, “Follow this path to the Dragon Pool in the oasis. Just take out this piece of jade to shake it three times, and the Dragon Palace gate will open.” My sister was so happy that she screamed “Kiss your sister, good sister, kiss your sister” for a while, then sighed again and burst into tears. My honest sister, Lu Cai, couldn't guess that her sister was playing a ghost calculation and calculating herself!
The sister sighed and sighed and said, “Sister, my life is so miserable. As long as I was able to wear my sister's costume for a while, I was satisfied.” “It's not difficult. When I get home, I'll definitely give my sister a few more sets.” My sister said.
“No, I want to try it now.” The sister broke down in tears while the soft-hearted younger sister had no choice but to agree to her request. She took off her clothes and changed them for her sister, and took off her jewelry and bracelets to put them on. My sister smiled...
Suddenly, the sister pointed at Shenqing: “Sister, look, what is that?” The younger sister turned her head. Lulai pushed hard from behind, and Lu Cai's body emptied. With a scream, she fell into a bottomless deep blue...
Lulai dressed in her sister's costume, carried her sister's son Yu Lu, and boldly walked to the Dragon Palace...
Soon, in Shenqing, where Lu Cai was knocked to death, a small bird with an emerald green body flew out. It hurriedly flew out of Shenqing and landed on the rocky corner of Yankan on the side of the road. It jumped on and off, and screamed “jade green - green - little green - green - green - green - green” in its mouth. It cried and was in a hurry to find...
This kind of bird is the “jade green bird” that is common now. It became after Lu Cai died. It deeply misses her son, Yu Lu. It's calling, searching, and just like that, it's always crying, looking...
