One day, Emperor Yu Huang summoned the male rabbit to the Tiangong Palace. He reluctantly left his wife and children and walked on the clouds to the Tiangong Palace. Just when it arrived at Nantianmen Gate, it saw Taibai Jinxing leading Tianzheng to walk away with Chang'e. Unaware of what had happened, Usen asked a Tenjin next to him who was guarding the Tenmon Gate. After hearing what happened to her, Tuxian felt that Chang'e was innocent of the crime and was very sympathetic to her. But I have little strength, so what can I do to help? Thinking of how lonely and sad Chang'e was locked up in the Moon Palace alone, it would be great if someone accompanied her. Suddenly she thought of her four daughters, she immediately flew home.
Tu Xian told the female rabbit what happened to Chang'e and said she wanted to have a child with Chang'e. Although the female rabbit deeply sympathizes with Chang'e, she can't bear her baby daughter. This is tantamount to cutting the flesh off her heart! A few daughters couldn't bear to leave their parents and burst into tears one by one. The rabbit said with great emphasis, “If I were to be locked up alone, would you be willing to stay with me? Can we not sympathize with Chang'e in order to save the people? Kid, we can't just think about ourselves!”
The children understood their father's heart and all expressed their desire to go. The male rabbit and female rabbit had tears in their eyes and laughed. They decided to let their youngest daughter go.
Little Yu Tu said goodbye to her parents and sisters and flew to the Moon Palace to live with Chang'e!
