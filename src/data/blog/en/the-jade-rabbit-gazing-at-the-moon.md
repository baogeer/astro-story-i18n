 At the top of the Autumn Mountain in Yongxin Dragon Gate, there is an ingenious landscape “Yutu Mochizuki.” This giant stone, shaped like a jade rabbit, stands abruptly on a cliff. It lifts its head slightly, and seems to be watching the bright moon in the sky.
Legend has it that the Yutu in the Moon Palace accompanied the Chang'e fairy through the cold and lonely days one by one.
One day, on the occasion of the royal mother's birthday, she suddenly showed kindness and arranged for Taibai Jinxing to go to the Moon Palace to invite Chang'e to the palace to dance and cheer up. Despite her reluctance, Chang'e didn't dare to go against the law. She advised Yutu not to wander around, and rushed to the royal palace alone. Once there was, she went there for three days.
It turns out that before the three-day feast, Mrs. Wang had to dance with Chang'e at every banquet.
On the first day, Yutu kept his promise. The next day, she really couldn't stand the temptations of the world, so she secretly stepped down. As soon as she landed in the beautiful autumn mountains, she happened to fly over a giant, ferocious, and abnormal eagle that had become a monster. When she picked up a jade rabbit, she flew to the sky. At this critical moment, I only heard the eagle screaming and let go of its sharp claws, and the Yutu fell straight into the deep valley. As soon as it was about to touch the ground, a pair of powerful arms caught the desperate Jade Rabbit.
It turns out that a handsome and burly hunter shot the eagle and then ran in the direction where the Yutu fell. The moment was a perfect moment to save Yutu's life.
Returning to her home in the Hunter's Forest, the Yutu squatted quietly on the ground, her eyes filled with tenderness. The hunter's parents have both died. He is an orphan. His name is Ason.
He said to Yutu, “Bunny, I'm going out hunting. By the way, I'll pick some fresh wild grass for you. I can't take you with me; don't get mad!” When I finished speaking, I took it out the door.
When she came back, Ason was surprised to find the cooking smoke in the house and opened the door to take a look: the fragrant meal was ready, and there was actually a pure and beautiful young girl standing by the stove.
“Who are you? Why do you have to cook for me?”
“I'm the girl you saved and turned into a Yutu! Just call me Xiaoyu.”
Next, Yutu recounted her history.
As a result, Ason had a conversation with Xiaoyu. Both have a sense of familiarity and a sense that they have met before. Later, Ason married Xiaoyu and lived a free and carefree life in a vast forest.
However, the good times didn't last long. Ah Song and Xiaoyu spent less than a year of happiness. The eagle, who had been wounded by an arrow, stumbled upon their whereabouts in the forest and clenched their teeth and scolded, “This feud must be revenged; I must have an idea to break you up.”
While in the sky, Xiaoyu learned medicine from her fairy friend who secretly came to the Moon Palace to chat with Chang'e, and knew how to treat diseases and save people. As a result, she often goes down the mountain to pick herbs alone to help the village patients relieve the pain of their illness, and is loved by the local people.
On a late autumn afternoon, Xiaoyu carried a bamboo basket full of herbs down the mountain to help the villagers heal their illnesses. The eagle that followed swooped down, violently grabbed Xiaoyu's clothes with sharp claws, and flew into the air with Xiaoyu Bian while shouting, “Look who will save you today?! Not only do I want to throw you into a meatloaf, but I also want to set fire to your favorite village down the mountain and burn down the villagers who are close to praising you — I also need to put a viper on your lover's house and let the viper bite him to death. Only in this way can I truly avenge my revenge!”
Hearing the eagle make such a vicious vow, Xiao Yu was taken aback, but after a while she calmed down and decided to die with the eagle. So she quickly took a rope out of her clothes and wrapped her left hand around the eagle's left leg, then quietly pulled a knife out of her pocket with her right hand and stabbed the eagle's right leg hard.
All of this happened in an instant, and the eagles didn't respond, and the pain made them feel dizzy. Annoyed, the eagle crashed into a cliff while holding Xiaoyu. The eagle instantly fell to pieces, and Xiaoyu turned into a giant rock and stood quietly at the top of Mount Akiyama. Because I really wanted to see Chang'e in the Moon Palace, the stone shaped like a rabbit made from Xiaoyu looked at the place where the moon rises day after year.
After returning from hunting, Ah Song disappeared. He only saw a stone in the shape of a jade rabbit. The lifelike rabbit's eyes seemed to be filled with tears. Ason was heartbroken and soon passed away.
The people at the foot of Mount Akiyama specially built a temple next to the giant stone in the shape of a jade rabbit, and they worship every full moon day.
The fairy in the sky also knew about this. She was moved by Yutu's heroic devotion. At the same time, she longed for the beautiful scenery of Mount Akiyama that would be like a wonderland, so occasionally she secretly visited Yitian Lake at the top of Qiushan Mountain to bathe and have fun.
Poor Chang'e, on the other hand, didn't dare to take matters into his own hands. She only waited until the Mid-Autumn Festival, when the round moon slowly rose, and Chang'e stood at the closest place in the Moon Palace to the “Yutu Watch the Moon” attraction, looked affectionately at the Yutu, which had turned into a giant stone, and muttered to herself, sadly sad.
