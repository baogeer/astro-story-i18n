 Legend has it that the creator, Ning Guanwa, originally lived on the high Sun Mountain.
A very, very ancient time ago, the world originally had no sky, no land, nothing; it was chaotic and chaotic. Ning Guanwa wanted to create heaven, earth, and everything. He spent a lot of energy building the sky first, and then worked tirelessly to build the land. Armed with a sledgehammer, he hit on the east side and knocked on the west side. Sometimes he hit hard, sometimes he hit lightly. The place where he hit hard became a flat dam, and the place where he hit lightly became a mountain. Later, he also opened up the Kujo River in the middle of a mountain flat dam. The earth is ready, but apart from Ning Guanwa herself, there are no other people, birds, or animals on the earth. He feels very lonely. As a result, he followed his own example and crushed many, many small clay people with mud, both male and female. Strangely enough, these little clay people live as soon as they hit the ground, and they soon grew as tall as him, which made Ning Guan a happy baby. Ning Guanwa then happily paired them up as a male and a female, so they could live their lives as a family. Ning Guanwa then picked up many chickens, ducks, cows, horses, pigs, sheep, cats, dogs, rabbits, monkeys, deer, lizards, lions, elephants, tigers, leopards, fish, and flying birds. Since then, wild animals have played in the woods, fish have played in the water, and small birds have flown in the sky. The earth is full of life.
However, Ning Guanwa never expected that another vicious devil, Gauzolore, was born at the same time as him. He only likes chaos and chaos; he doesn't like the division between heaven and earth, let alone everything appearing in the world. When Ning Guanwa created everything in heaven, this guy didn't know where to hide and sleep soundly. Later, people woke him up from his sleep with lots of laughter. He looked down and unwittingly jumped for the appearance of everything in heaven and earth. He jumped to the ground and shouted, “How dare anyone be so bold to make my world look like this.” At this point, I just met Ning Guanwa. Ning Guanwa then said, “Oh, friend, are you asking this?”
“Did you make this world?”
“Yes! Look, isn't the scene so much better than the original scene, with the sky and white clouds, and the earth is green and full of energy?”
Ning Guanwa's excited words and proud demeanor made Gauzolore even more furious: “I want to destroy everything in heaven and earth!” Speaking of which, he “pulled out” the long knife, turned around, flashed his eyes slightly, and started the sword.
Gao Zolore's crazy behavior made the baby furious, and her steel teeth were crushed. How can he endure demons raging, poison life, and destroy the world! “Stop!” He shouted loudly, raised his long knife, and slashed hard at Gozolore. Godzolore was so frightened that he hurriedly raised his sword to greet her. So the two of you came and went, roared like thunder, and got into a fight. After three days and three nights, the battle was completely dark, and the sun and moon had no light. As a result, Ning Guanwa was slashed in the chest by Gao Zolore. He only blocked the bleeding wound, climbed the cloud, and went back to Taiyangshan; Gao Zolorei was cut in half by Ning Guanwa, but when he died, he did not forget to do evil. Although he lacked energy and was unable to destroy the world, he was able to cast spells, causing the Tianhe River to hang upside down and heavy rain pouring down. As a result, in an instant, the sky was vast, the earth was flooded, and everything was washed away by the flood. Humans only survived when a sister and brother herded cows in the mountains, saw heavy flooding, hurriedly killed four cows, made a big drum out of cowhide, and two hid inside and drifted along the water.
Sister and brother become married
I don't know how many days I've been drifting, I have no idea how many days I've been drifting, and then I just couldn't stop. The sister and brother took a look. The water had receded. They were extremely happy. They hurriedly tore off the cowhide, climbed out of the drum, discerned the direction, stepped in the mud, and walked towards their hometown. Go and go. I don't know how many days and nights they have gone, and they haven't met a single person along the way. The places they passed by were full of dead bodies. It was desolate and scary. The two felt very alone. At dusk one day, they were tired and hungry. They wanted to find a place to take a break. Suddenly, they saw a stone cave on the hillside in front of them. One arch should have stood at the entrance of the cave and looked around. The two were overjoyed and ran up and shouted “Grandma!” Then he said, “Grandma, can we take a night off at your house?” This arch was supposed to be a darumu ghost. It hasn't eaten human flesh in a long time, and now it's trying to salivate. As soon as they see the sister and brother, they are so happy that they take them and walk down the cave, and they can't stop saying, “OK! OK! Just stay with me, I love kids. You must be hungry, right? I'll go back to the water to cook for you, and you have a good rest at home.” Having said that, I picked up the bamboo tube and walked towards the mountain Qingli.
The clever sister saw that this arch should have been liked and that it had bad intentions, which aroused alarm. As a result, Darumegi had just gone out, and she quietly followed her. Along the way, I only heard Gong Ge walk and couldn't help but hum, “Go back to the water, go back to the water, I want to cook for those two kids to eat!” The sister was shocked when she heard it. She hurriedly ran back and took her brother and fled. By the time the Dalmu demon brought back the water, the two had already escaped.
The sister and brother worked so hard to walk day and night. They lost weight, their feet got swollen, and they couldn't move anymore. At this point, they really wanted to have a house to shelter from the wind and cold, get a good night's sleep, and nourish their spirits! My younger brother, in particular, doesn't want to leave anymore. Coincidentally, there was actually a house at the top of the mountain in front of me. The two climbed to the top of the mountain in one go and asked to open the door. Once they saw, they were the only ones who came out to open the door; they were the same ghost. “Grandma! We are homeless, can you take in our sister and brother?” The sister and brother pleaded in unison. Seeing the two exhausted orphans, they were very sympathetic and said, “Come in, poor kid. Grandma is also very sad on her own, so let's just live with me!” She welcomed them into the house, and after settling in, she said, “Rest at home; I'll carry water to cook for you.” As soon as she went out, her sister quietly followed and watched the movement. While picking up the water, I heard her sing softly: “Water is full, water is filling up. I want to go back and cook food for the two orphans. They are hungry.” The sister believed that Zhidong Ge wouldn't hurt them, so she happily lived with her younger brother.
Life went by so fast. In a blink of an eye, the sister and brother had grown up. Zhi Tongge is very worried about their marriage, and he always wants to resolve it for them as soon as possible. However, in the world, apart from the two of them, there is no other person to choose from. There is no way; they have to be persuaded to pair up and become a family. How can a sister and brother get married? But if they don't get married and don't give birth to offspring, after they die, aren't humans going to become extinct? It's not good to get married, and it's not good not to get married. Despite many persuasions from the same ghost, the sister and brother are still hesitating, and it is very difficult.
“Let's just ask God to see if God also agrees to you getting married? The two of you are in Higashiyama and the other in Nishiyama. If heaven agrees to you getting married, let the two of you roll together; if heaven doesn't allow you to get married, they won't let you roll together.” Later, Zhi Tongji came up with this idea. The sister and brother had no other choice, so they agreed to do it according to the will of heaven. They prayed heaven and invited heaven to decide, then the sister went up the Higashiyama mountain, and the younger brother went up the western mountain, and rolled down at the same time. They rolled three times in a row, all three times. Providence made sense, and the sister and brother happily became a family.
The mother who harnesses the sun
After becoming a family, they had a very happy time. During the day, the sister and brother worked together. At night, the younger brother played his voice and accompanied her sister in weaving and sewing clothes under the moonlight. Soon, they had a son named Wa Gang. The baby has just been born with clear eyes. She is white, fat, and very adorable. However, the baby just had a problem with crying. As soon as she started crying, she had to cry enough. If you don't cry enough, no one is nice. The more you make him cry, the more it just makes people upset and upset.
One day, the sister and brother went down to the ground, and for some reason the baby just started crying again. Zhitong carried him and hugged him, and he didn't stop; he didn't eat; made fun of him; ignored him in bed; he cried even more violently. He was so angry that he threw up the devil's fire, took him to a fork in nine, and lost it by cutting it into eight pieces with a knife. After this, there were no more cries, but no more children. Looking at the bloody chunks of meat, he felt a lot of regret. He deeply blamed himself in his heart, felt sorry for his sister and brother, and was too embarrassed to see them again. He escaped from afar. I can't imagine that soon after Zhitong Ge left the fork in the road, his eight bodies became four men, four women, and eight young people. They all had holes in their ears and beautiful earrings that sparkled in silver, and they all looked exactly like Little Baby Gang.
On the evening of the same day, the sister and brother returned from work and saw that Wa Gang and Zhitong were not at home, no food was cooked, and the fire pit was still cold. I was surprised: “Grandma never went to any skewers. Where would she go today? Why haven't she come back until now?” However, at first, I always thought I had gone out to have fun. When the food was ready, it was dark, and I never came back. Only then did the sister and brother get anxious. They quickly split up and searched everywhere, but there are still traces of Zhitong Ge and Little Wa Gang! The mother was in such a hurry that she went crazy. She stumbled, cried and screamed all night long. It wasn't until the next day, when the red sun rose east, that I saw four couples of young men and women snuggling up to each other and talking about love. She rushed to ask them about the whereabouts of Xiaowagang and Gongji, and they all answered that they hadn't seen it. But when she took a close look at them up and down, she couldn't help but scream: “Oops, you all look exactly like my little one!”
“Are you our mom?” Looking at people around their age claiming to be their mom, young people, look at me, I look at you; no one believes this is true. “Huh, we're not even named Wa Gang, and we don't know who our mom is.” One of them had a hard time speaking to her.
“What, aren't you my kids just now? Am I ignoring the truth?” She rubbed her eyes and looked closely one by one, but the more she looked at them, the more she felt that they looked like Wa Gang. Their voice and smile were exactly the same as Baba Gang in every move. Can a mother still not recognize her own child? No, that's right: “You're just my baby!”
“Huh, we can't be your baby just now. If you think about it, your baby was just a kid who could walk, but we are eight young people. How can a little doll grow into eight young people in one night?”
“Well, how can I explain this clearly? But you all look exactly like my little baby Gan. Can you say it's not my baby Gan? Kids, I'm really your mom. Go, go home with your mom!” She held the children's hands tightly and couldn't bear to put them down, and tears couldn't stop rolling down.
“Huh, you say we look like your little girl, but none of us have seen Little Wa Kong. I don't know what he has grown. Just by saying it, it doesn't make us believe that we are your baby Gong. What other proof do you have to prove that you really are our mom, please?”
The children's words left her speechless and unable to answer them. But yeah, what the kids say isn't unreasonable; if there isn't enough evidence to convince them, how can they easily believe this is true? But if I had to ask her to come up with evidence all of a sudden, she wouldn't be able to do it no matter what. “It would be great if Grandma Zhitong was there, but she...” She really didn't know what to do.
“Huh, do you think this is good?” A young man picked up an extinguished piece of charcoal from the fire next to her and said to her, “If you say it's our mom, then please wash this charcoal. If you can wash it white, it means you're our mom; if you can't wash it white, it means you're not our mom.”
The mother is serious about her child. Since the child said this, she did not hesitate to take the fire charcoal and go wash in the creek. Wash and wash, the river water is all black, yet charcoal doesn't wash it white at all. When the children saw that she couldn't wash the charcoal white, they thought she wasn't their mother, so when she was concentrating on cleaning the charcoal, she quietly left her, left the nine forks, walked into “Dingta Gate”, and arrived at “Dingta Ga”. They settled on a high mountain in Mongolia, and began a harmonious, friendly, happy and beautiful human life. Legend has it that they are the ancestors of ethnic groups such as Jingpo, Deang, Lisu, and Awa.
When the mother discovered that the children had left, they had gone without a trace. She hurriedly threw away the fire charcoal, which had not yet been washed white, and chased after her husband. No one knew, but she went in the wrong direction and arrived outside the Sun Gate. But the Sun Gate was closed, and it was impossible to get in. In order to find her child in Puerta del Sol, she sat in front of the Puerta del Sol, waited in a woven dress for the Sun Gate to open, and did not go anywhere. It is said that the beautiful rainbow is the skirt cloth she made.
Besides, one day in the sky and ten years underground. After Ning Guanwa returned to Taiyangshan to heal her wounds, she still thought about everything in heaven and earth he had made. When he bowed down to the earth, he felt desolate, and felt very sad. She regretted not being able to kill Gauzolore right away in the first place. When he wanted to go back to the earth to repair mountains and rivers and create humans, he saw the strange changes in Wagang and everything his mother was looking for children, and deeply sympathized with Wagang's mother. So he came outside Puerta del Sol, told her how it happened, and then took her back to the earth to meet the child.
Back on Earth, after Ning Guanwa's explanation, Wagang's mother and child met each other, making her smile and burst into tears. However, soon she was worried about who to live with, because now the four young couples who have just become babies have separated and have children. The kids are also arguing over who she should live with. You said you should do it with you; I said you have to do it with me; you will compete with me and not compromise with each other. Still, Ning Guanwa persuaded everyone and said, “Kids, listen to me, don't fight. Your mother is truly a loving mother and is totally worthy of all of your love and respect. But she can't live with you, because no one can control the sun yet, so I want to ask your mother to control the sun. In this way, she can not only make the sun better benefit everyone, but she can also meet everyone every day and get two things in one stroke. OK?”
Being able to meet her child was a satisfying experience for the mother. She was even more relieved to see that her children and grandchildren were able to live peacefully and work in peace and harmony. Now I listen to Ning Guanwa's arrangement and think to myself, “Yes, how can I sit back and enjoy the blessings of the kids? We should benefit our children so that they can have a more fulfilling life!” As a result, she refused to retain the children's enthusiasm, gladly accepted Ning Guanwa's gift, and followed Ning Guanwa back to the sun.
Since then, she has woken up every day and returned in the evening, driving the sun through the sky all day long, letting the bright sun shine on the earth and warming her children and grandchildren.
