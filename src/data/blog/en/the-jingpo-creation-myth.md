 In ancient times, the world was steaming foggy. There was no sky, no land, and the whole thing was chaotic. In this period, people in Jingpo called “Jiyingzha.” I don't know how many years have passed, and the fog has risen, and people in Jingpo have called the “Let's Compare” period. At this point, the world was hazy and hazy. There was some light, and it began to show a less obvious outline, just like when the sky was bright now. After a long, long time, it was time for the “Jane Eyza” period. In other words, we have entered the “Pangu” period. “Jane Yingzha” is a person who is being nurtured in something like an egg at this point. Caijao, an island in the northern sea, is the place where this person was born.
I don't know how many years have passed since Jane opened her eyes on this island. What he saw was just a world where heaven and earth were indistinguishable. It was always in a state of turmoil, which was very boring. Jane took a deep breath and decided to separate heaven and earth, and rose high into the sky from that egg-like thing. He worked hard to take the next step, and the tough one underneath became the ground; he pitched it up with both hands, and the sky was formed. Just like that, heaven and earth made it. But he feared that heaven and earth would come together again, so he put his hands on the sky and put his feet on the ground. They supported it for 360,000 years, and only then gradually became stronger. At this point, Jane Yingza couldn't help it. His hands were weak, and then he slowly died.
With heaven and earth, but heaven and earth were lacking, Longmu Geza sent two brothers, Pan Wa, Neng Sang, and Jian Wa, to come to the world. The older brother Panwa was able to make up for the day, and the younger brother, Jianwa, should make up for the ground. Heaven is made up from the east, and the earth is made up from the west. At this point, the eastern sky changed from chaotic gas to nine suns, six of which were relatively close to the ground. The sun was like fire, and the earth was torn, and none of the trees that my brother had planted underground came out. The younger brother went to the older brother to discuss: “Brother, your nine suns have tanned all of my land, and I can't make up for it. You have to find a way to help me.” My brother thought of many ways, and finally covered the bottom six suns with white clouds. Of the remaining three, one is far from the ground, two are in the middle, and the ground still cannot be repaired. The younger brother went to discuss it with his older brother again.
The younger brother said to his brother, “These two suns are still too hot. If you don't help me find a way, nothing will grow on the ground.” The older brother felt that his younger brother was right and decided to cover these two suns with dark clouds. As a result, one sun was covered and the other sun ran away. Since then, only one sun has been left hanging high in the sky, which is quite far from the ground. The sun that escaped was male and became the moon, so it wasn't warm. The rest of the Sun is a mother. Its light is composed of 7 gigabytes of fire ponds. These lights illuminate the earth and give light and heat to everything.
However, in this way, two more layers of heaven and one earth were formed. My younger brother, Jane Wa, said, “Don't have two layers of heaven; just one place a day.” The older brother Panwa Nengsang followed his younger brother's words, removed the first layer of heaven, and escaped to the middle of the sky and left the moon there, using it as a torch at night. The two then discussed again, thought the sky should be bigger than the earth, and decided to make up for the sky again. This time, I'll use clouds to make up for it. There are three types of clouds: gray, white, and black. The first two are used to make up the day. The black clouds make it flow in the sky, flow to the ocean in winter, rise again in autumn, and absorb water into the sky and rain when it falls into the ocean. The amount of rainfall is calculated as a basket. It cannot exceed six baskets per year; if it exceeds it, it becomes a disaster.
This makes up for the day. But the western side of the earth is still lacking. The two brothers asked the black clouds to fill them with water, so there is a lot of seawater in the west. Heaven and earth were made up, and the two brothers died. My brother died in a place no one knew. When my younger brother died, his head turned to the east, his feet to the west, and his head became the majestic Mount Kunlun. The large intestine becomes a large river that roars, and the small intestine becomes a small river that flows slowly. Hair and sweat have become forests and plants. The teeth and jaw turned into hard rocks. The right hand became the continuous rolling Mount Da Niang, and the left hand became the beautiful Jingdong Mountain. The eyes became twinkling stars in the sky, and the lungs turned into a lake with tens of blue waves.
The appearance of forests, rivers, stars, and plants has added vitality to the world. But at that time, there were no humans in the world, and there were no animals in the forest. Longmu Gesa sent another old man, the goddess Zhitongwa, to Earth to create humans and procreate life. When Zhitong Wa came to the ground, he pinched people with mud. Pinching one by one, these people gradually came to life. They were all bouncing around her, dancing and walking in pairs. Zhitong tiles are very strange, so I looked everywhere. In the endless forest, I had a hard time finding a pair of Zhitong tiles. When Zhitong Kawara brought them back, she found that one of her hairs had grown long. Strangely enough, she thought it might have been arranged by God. Men with short hair and women with long hair. Since then, humans have been divided into genders.
Zhitong tiles make people year after year, and many people. At the time, these people couldn't work, and they weren't hungry. Zhitong was tired, so he was ready to go back to heaven. However, Longmu Gesa disagreed. He said, “Humans are still living in chaos, and you must continue to help them.”
Zhitong tile has returned to the ground and continues to help humanity. At this point, a war broke out on earth. The Yellow Dragon in the sky and the Black Dragon on land fought a war over something. They call back and forth, and no one is convinced. In the end, Heavenly Dragon invited Thor to help. Thor spewed its blazing flames to the ground, causing a raging fire all over the earth. The fire continued for seven days and seven nights. Zhitong Kawara hurriedly asked the god of water to help put out the fire. At this point, the earth was burnt, and many people were burnt to death. Only a pair of brothers and sisters were placed in a golden drum by Zhitong tiles and not burnt to death. However, the god of water spilled too much water, and the earth became a vast ocean again. The golden drum floated and drifted away with a pair of brothers and sisters. I looked everywhere for Zhitong tiles. She first found the Jingjiang River in the east, then walked for 99 days, climbed 99 mountains, and waded through 99 rivers, and still never found it. She looked west for a long time. At this point, the water began to fall, the mountain peaks were already exposed, and I couldn't find a path. Zhitong tile had no choice but to ask Tenjin for help, and sent a group of animals to lead the way and led Zhitong tile to the southern sea. Sure enough, when I saw a golden drum floating there. Zhitong Kawara saw it, but there was no way to get it, so I asked the duck to help. The ducks swam past, but they can't push the golden drum. Zhidong Kawara also asked Otaka to help. They both pushed one by one, and finally pulled the drum to the shore. The two brothers and sisters were sealed in a drum. Zhitong was very anxious, so he invited the woodpecker to peck, but he couldn't peck; he invited the mouse, but it was too loud for fear of frightening the two brothers and sisters. Finally, Zhitong Kawara invited Swallow. Swallow's wings are very sharp, and they have drawn a golden drum (so now Jia Swallow's wings look like a sword). The two brothers and sisters have been rescued, and there is finally another human on Earth. At this point, Zhitong Wa wanted to go back to the sky. Longmu Gesha still wasn't sure. He said, “The two brothers and sisters haven't been passed down yet; you have to help them again.”
Zhitong Kawara stayed and taught the two brothers and sisters to form a couple and reproduce for future generations. However, my younger sister was too shy to agree, and so did my brother. Zhitong Kawara said a bunch of things, but they didn't listen. Zhitong Wa couldn't help it, so they brought out two incense sticks, and had them each bring one incense. The older brother went to the east side mountain, and the younger sister went to the western mountain to light the grass together. If the flames were connected, they would form a couple. After listening to it, the two brothers and sisters followed suit. As soon as the fire ignited a little, they connected together. However, the brother and sister still don't want to become a couple. Zhitong Kawara thought of another method. Divide a stone in half and ask the brother and sister to roll them down the mountain together. If the stones rolled down were combined into one piece, they would form a couple. The brother and sister agreed. Coincidentally, the stone that rolled down was merged into one more piece. But they still don't do it. Zhitong Kawara also led the brother and sister to the riverside, broke off a branch, and split it into two sections, so they each took a branch and threw it into the river together. As soon as the branch hit the river, it immediately became two fish, one male fish, and one female fish. The brother and sister saw it, only to listen to Tomotori Kawara's words and became a couple. The wedding date was scheduled in three days.
On the wedding day, the wedding ceremony was about to begin, but without a matriarch, matchmaker, or helper, they invited yellow pear leaves to build a new house, a bus to be the matchmaker, and an upright horsetail pine to act as the matriarch. Use the verdant green Gugongpo leaves to make pots.
Before the celebration, we also invited the birds from Muli Island to chow rice, invited the pigeons to cook, the old man to fetch water, the little sparrows to serve the meal, and the wild pigeons to sing. I invited the bus to make alcohol and medicine, Wu Ritao to cook the wine, and finally invited the beautiful peacock to dance.
After getting married, the two had a very happy life. My younger sister became pregnant three years later and gave birth to a dog skin pocket. This dog skin pocket was left at home for another three years before it came apart. It turned out there were nine children in the bag: five boys and four women. The four boys and four women naturally formed a pair and went to raise children. The remaining man, without a companion, went to the ghost house to be his son. After being abused by the devil, he finally ran away, tired and hungry, and fell into a pit to death. Therefore, people in Jingpo still throw out people who died unmarried.
Zhitong Wa wanted to go back to heaven, but Longmu Gesa still disagreed and asked her to help humans find grains and raise livestock.
Zhitong Wa also helps humans find grain seeds. After finding them, she first eats them, then selects seeds that are not toxic and scattered in the soil to allow them to germinate and blossom in spring and mature in fall. Some of these grains are grown in the sky, and some are planted underground, and they are harvested every year. Early one morning, a few wild animals came to steal millet, and people tried to catch them. These are the current cows, sheep, dogs, and pigs. Since then, humans have had domestic animals.
At that time, some animals that had stolen the elixir of immortality in the sky were also driven underground by Longmugesa, and more animals appeared in the forest. The poop they pulled out had seeds of elixir brought from the sky. After they grew, they became the Chinese herbal medicine of today.
At last, Zhidong Kawara never returned to heaven; she died on the earth that nurtured endless lives. To this day, the elderly of the Jingpo ethnic group still deeply miss her.
