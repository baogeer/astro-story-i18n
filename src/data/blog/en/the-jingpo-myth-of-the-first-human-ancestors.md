 Shortly after the formation of heaven and earth, there were no humans, no plants, no birds or animals, no crops, and only a few gods roamed around. There is a god named Nuoqiang and his wife who lives in the northern highlands; another god named Song Chang lives in the south with his nine sons.
One day, Song Chang and Nuo Qiang met. They all feel that the world is too desolate and lonely. Everything should be built to make the world full of life. Song Changman thought that the people in his family are powerful and have many ideas of his own, so he can definitely build better than Nuoqiang. He very arrogantly proposed, “Let's build our own place! But in the end, let's compare it to see who built it the best!” Nuo Qiang agreed, so they split up.
After Nuoqiang returned to the north, he discussed with his wife Su Qiong to start building everything. They worked while discussing, and because their families were small and weak, they rushed around the clock to do it. When Song Chang returned to his place of residence in the south, he ordered his sons to do it, and he only came up with his own ideas; he never wanted his sons to come up with ideas. He is so powerful because of his people. When he works, he does things at leisure, and at leisure and does things at leisure. Song Chang thinks every day: Nuo Qiang has no ideas, and there are few people, so he definitely can't build anything. We have to give him a good look this time.
Soon, Song Chang and Nuo Qiang arrived at the place where they had met. Songchang's head arrogantly said, “Go to me first. The place I built is huge. I've already built many, many trees and all kinds of grass, all kinds of birds, animals, and bugs, millet and all kinds of food, and a military camp...”
“I made these things too. I also made two people: an older sister and a younger brother.” Nuo Qiang spoke softly.
“Will you have that much skill and strength? I just don't believe it. Will you be able to create a full range of trees and bamboo? Will you make the best Shiratani? Can you create all kinds of winged birds? I know, you've only made one pheasant, and it's been giggling all day long, not making a sound.” Song Chang despised the numbers.
“Then please check out my place first!” After speaking, Nuo Qiang turned around and left. Song Chang decided to check it out for himself. He missed him once in person and let out this breath. As soon as he got home, he said to his sons, “I want to visit Nuoqiang's place myself and really shame him.” The nine sons said in unison, “Grandpa, you can't go to Nuoqiang's place. That place is small and cold. If you go, you won't freeze to death; you will also starve to death.” Song Chang answered, “No, I've decided not to stop me. His place is small and cold, so I'll wear a lot of clothes, wear a thick hat on my head, socks on my feet, fill my rice pockets, and carry my knife with leopard teeth on the scabbard, and I'm not afraid of anything.” Before leaving, he also ordered his sons: “If I don't come back after nine days and nine nights, the bees fly around the house again, it means I'm dead. At that time, all nine of you brothers had to pick up the corpse and say to Nuo Qiang, 'We want to drink the blood from the mountains and eat the valleys in the mountain fields. ' To destroy Nuo Qiang, we must destroy everything he has built.” Once everything was in place, Songchang was on his way.
Nine days and nine nights have passed, and the nine brothers are very anxious without seeing their father come back. One day, bees suddenly came to the roof and flew around. The nine brothers know that their father is dead. What should I do? The nine brothers discussed it for half a day. They discussed it. They thought it would be best to ask Brother Xiong to make Nuo Qiang lose his life first. The next day, Brother Jiu went to find Brother Xiong, asked him to go to Nuo Qiang to help talk about the loss of his life, and gave him the best collar after the incident happened.
When Xiong arrived at Nuo Qiang's place, Nuo Qiang told him, “Song Chang died by himself. The clothes he was wearing were too thin, and he had no rice in his stomach. There is no reason why he didn't freeze to death and starved to death; I'm not responsible for losing his life.” Brother Xiong turned back and told the nine brothers what Nuo Qiang had said. However, the nine brothers didn't believe it. They said, “Our father wore very thick clothes and went with a very full bag of rice; he wouldn't have frozen to death and starved to death. If Nuo Qiang doesn't admit it again, our brother will have to pull his cows and horses, get the fish in his pond, and finish harvesting the millet from his mountain.” Over and over again, they asked Brother Xiong to talk about it again.
Xiong came to the place where Nuo Qiang stayed again and told the words of the nine brothers truthfully to Nuo Qiang. Nuo Qiang repeatedly explained the reason why Songchang froze to death and starved to death. Finally, he angrily said, “If their nine brothers don't make sense and must rob them, I will cover them with darkness and drown them in floods.”
Brother Xiong came back and relayed Nuo Qiang's words to the nine brothers. After listening, the nine brothers made up their minds to fight. They built nine decker boats, one with firewood, the other with food... ready for the flood. Nuoqiang is also actively preparing to deal with this war.
On this day, Nuo Qiang remembered the sister and brother they created. He doesn't want them to be destroyed in this war; they should be preserved and let them reproduce as humans. As a result, he immediately dispatched a wild cat to tell the sister and brother how to avoid the disaster.
The wild cat found the sister and brother and said, “Songchang came to our north and froze to death. Now his nine sons are going to fight. The world will be dark, floods will flood the whole earth, and everything on earth will be destroyed. Nuo Qiang asked me to tell you that quickly prepare the next big wooden drum, put skin on both ends, and prepare nine rice balls, nine roosters, and nine needles. As soon as the flood hits, hide in the drum. Eat one rice ball every day, throw out a rooster and a needle every day, and wait until you hear the rooster's cry and the needle sounds, then you can come out.”
After the sister and brother sent away the wild cat, they were busy doing what Nuo Qiang ordered.
Soon, the nine brothers started a war with Nuo Qiang. Sure enough, it was dark, and floods poured all over the place from the north. The sister and brother quickly hid into the wooden drum and drifted with the water. They followed Nuo Qiang's orders. On the first day, they ate a rice ball and threw out a rooster and a needle. They didn't hear the rooster barking or the sound of a needle. I did this the next day, and the third day too... until the 8th day, I still couldn't hear the roosters or the sound of needles. In the end, all that's left is a rice ball, a rooster, and a needle. What should I do? The sister and brother are very anxious.
The flood had been flooding for a full eight days. The nine brothers were defeated and drowned, and everything on earth was about to be flooded. Su Qiong was very anxious. She soon came to her husband and said, “Nuo Qiang, you have already drowned their nine brothers, but you have also drowned everything in the world. Now, there is only one pig left in the mountain, only one beech left in the forest, only one “Dongxiang bird” left for all birds, only one bamboo pole left, and only one “real tube tree” left. Humans are left with only two orphans left, then submerged, and even we are finished. Let go of the flood and disperse the darkness!” Nuo Qiang was also very anxious when he heard it. He quickly went to find a porcupine that pierced the floodgates and let out light.
Besides, the sister and brother are shaking around in a wooden drum; they are very anxious. On this day, they finished eating the last rice ball, hesitated for half a day, and finally threw out the last rooster and the last needle. They calmed down and listened quietly to the movement. Suddenly, they heard the sound of a needle falling to the ground, as well as the rooster's cry, so they quickly opened the drum and walked out. At first glance, the earth has regained its light, and the floods have receded. However, there was nothing left on the earth, no plants could be seen, no birds were heard, and it was desolate everywhere. The sister and brother were so hungry that they looked east and west, but couldn't find anything. Suddenly, they discovered blue smoke coming out of the concave head, and walked to the place where the smoke was smoking.
It turns out that there is a leprosy ghost living in the head. He is also hungry and panicked; he is worried that he can't find anything to eat. The sister and brother walked up to him and respectfully said to him, “Thank you, Grandpa, we are very hungry, please give us some food!” The leprosy ghost happily agreed to their request and immediately took the water bottle and said to get some water to cook for them. At this point, the sister saw that the old man was a little uneasy, so she quietly followed him and watched his actions. The old grandpa followed the water in the recess and read to himself: “Water, water, fill up quickly. Today, my fortune came. Two young ladies and a brother, that's enough for me to eat.” My sister was shocked when she heard it. Instead of cooking for us, they wanted to eat our sister and brother! She quickly turned back and told her brother and led him away.
The sister and brother ran a few mountains and several dents, and saw a cloud of blue smoke coming out of a concave tail. They both arrived at Aozimao and saw another old grandpa cooking. They both respectfully said to him, “Thank you, Grandpa. We are very hungry, let's give us some food!” The old grandpa agreed to their request and immediately got up and went out to fetch water with a water bottle. The sister and brother followed quietly, only to hear the old man say to himself, “Water, water, fill up quickly. I need to quickly cook for the two children.” Only then did the sister and brother feel at ease.
It turns out that this old man's name is Dong Lashun. It was only in order to reproduce the human race that he specially came to host his sister and brother. After the sister and brother had eaten, the old grandpa asked them to stay with him and ask them to be brother and sister during the day and as a couple at night to reproduce as humans. However, the sister and brother are afraid to be a couple. Grandpa thought of a solution. He built two paths and asked his sister and brother to each walk the same path to find their own partner. The sister and brother have been gone for a day, and they are still seeing each other. Coming back at night, my sister said, “Grandpa, no matter how far or wherever you go, you only see people like my brother. That's not OK!” The younger brother also said that he only sees people like his sister, and that it's not OK to be a husband and wife. Grandpa had to come up with a different idea. They told them to take a stone mill and roll downhill from the top of the mountain on both sides. If the sister and brother could be a couple, the stone mill would roll all the way to the foot of the slope. The next day, the sister and brother went to the rolling stone mill. The stone mill actually rolled together. However, the sister and brother are still afraid to be a couple. Grandpa had to come up with another idea. On the third day, the old grandpa went to find lots of wild bananas and “tang-spicy,” and asked the sister and brother to go fishing in the river. The two knocked on wild plantains and “tang-spicy,” and the paste splashed on their feet, hands, and body, and it was itchy everywhere it splashed. They really couldn't stand it. The sister said, “Little brother, how are we doing today? Grandpa Dong Lashun wants us to be a couple, and Tenjin wants us to be a couple. Forget it, let's just cover our faces and be husband and wife.” That's how the sister and brother became a couple.
After a few months, my sister felt unwell and had many dreams. One day, she had a dream where she had a cigarette case stuck around her waist. She felt sad. How does she want to meet people in the future? I'm so ashamed! Once, my sister wanted to walk to the mountainside in the back to find her death, but halfway through, she saw many grass, such as “Gongla,” “River,” and “Lake Dig,” all in pairs. She thought again: Even grass is in pairs, so why can't people do it? She got rid of the idea of looking for death and went back. Later, a child was born. The sister and brother blessed the child and gave him a name. But this kid cries every day, and the more he cries, the worse. One day, the sister and brother went out to find food, and the old grandpa led the child at home. No matter how much grandpa teases him, the kid keeps crying. The old man hugged the child and coaxed him and said, “Ah! ah! Ahhh! Don't cry, grow up to be a 'Dong Sa' (priest) and a 'Qiangzhong' (sacrificial organizer)!” But the kid cried even harder. The old man coaxed again: “Ah! ah! Ahhh! Don't cry; grow up to be an official.” The kid was still crying. Grandpa was so angry that he scolded, “I'll chop you up and spread your meat at the nine crossroads!” Now, the kid really stopped crying. The old grandpa actually cut the kid down, chopped his meat, scattered it at the nine crossroads, and washed his stomach and cooked it into porridge.
The next day, the sister and brother came back. As soon as my sister entered the door, she said, “Grandpa, please feed my child. My milk is already sore.” Grandpa said, “The kid just fell asleep, you have to eat first.” The sister and brother took a look at the sleeping child and ate. After eating, my sister lifted the blanket and took a look. It turned out to be just a wooden pillow; where was the child! They asked the old grandpa. The old grandpa had to tell them both the truth, “Your child's stomach has just been eaten by you. Your child's meat has already been thrown at a fork in the road. If you don't believe me, go there and take a look!”
The sister and brother ran to the nine crossroads in one go. It turned out that every piece of meat had become one person, and everyone was sitting there!
“My kids, come back soon!” The sister called as soon as she saw the child.
“You are the one who eats our heart and liver. Where is our mother.” The kids scolded.
“I am your mother! “I haven't eaten...” the mother argued.
However, no matter what the mother said, the children still didn't believe it. Finally, the children suggested that if they could wash the charcoal white, they would consider her mother. At this point, a swallow flew over the sky and pulled down a bubble of white poop. It happened to fall on the charcoal, and the charcoal was dyed white. However, the children put forward another condition: they asked the mother to fill the bamboo tube with water. But after picking it up, the water didn't fill up for half a day. At this point, a small finch suddenly fell into the bamboo tube. The mother picked it up and took a look, and the little finch drilled out of the bottom of the bamboo tube. Only then did the mother learn that she had been fooled and went back to chase the children, but they all ran far away.
The children ran along nine paths. They walked a long, long distance, and each went to one place, and became the ancestors of all nationalities in the world.
My sister lost her child and was so sad that she finally came to life. After her death, one soul went to heaven and became a celestial ghost, and the other went to earth and became an earth ghost. People are always remembering her.
