 Legend has it that in ancient times, there were nine suns in the sky, and the nine suns illuminated the earth all at once, like nine fireballs. Dogs stick out their tongues in the sun, tigers have bald ears from sunburn, water is dried, and the ground is torn. People and all the flowers, trees, birds, and animals simply can't survive anymore. So everyone got up and scolded, poked, and drove the Sun away. After the Sun was driven away, the earth became dark, and there were black holes everywhere, making it impossible for people and everything to survive.
As a result, everyone discussed going back to Heaven and asking the husband to return to the Sun. Who's going? “I'm going!” The big cock standing on the sidelines spoke first. “Great!” Everyone was happy to give it a long tail, put wings in the best leaves, and give it eyes with small red beans. The big rooster is about to leave, and everyone kept waving to it to express their farewell and greetings to the big rooster. The big rooster “giggled” and shouted to fly towards the country of the Sun.
Arriving at the place where the Sun lives in the Land of the Sun, the big rooster drilled its head into its wing and sat outside the Sun Gate without making a sound. Sitting and sitting, the Sun Gate opened a little bit and saw the head of a little sun. Because Little Sun was timid, I saw this big furry cockerel. I was so scared that it turned around and ran back into the house. When something earth-shattering happened to the Sun, he looked frightened and asked, “What is it called?” Out of breath, Little Sun told Big Sun what he had just seen. After hearing the big sun, I came to the back of the door and looked through the slits in the door for a while, but I didn't see anything. It later boldly and gently opened the Sun Gate. The big rooster was extremely happy. For a moment, he held out his head and “giggled” in the face of the frightened sun, then pulled away. It was only after hearing the big rooster explain the intention that Big Sun slowly calmed down: “Oh! You're here to invite us!” The big rooster answered, “Yes, I have been specially invited by everyone, all the flowers and trees, all the birds and animals.”
Big Sun is happy: “We're going out again!” However, when it remembered the scene of being evicted in the past, it immediately changed its tone: “Ahhh! We've been scolded enough in the past, and poked enough.” The big rooster answered cheerfully, “As long as you don't break anything like before when you go out this time, everyone promises not to scold you, they won't poke at you, and they won't rush you.” At this point, the Sun timidly said, “If I want to go, I'll go alone.” The big cock blinked and said, “OK, OK, I'll pick you up as soon as it gets bright.” Because the big rooster was afraid that the big sun wouldn't believe what he said, he engraved a few deep marks on his crown, saying he was determined to do it. Big Sun didn't mind it either, and said, “No need to pick it up; just call me when it dawns.” It was white in the East, and the big rooster called out “ooh”. As soon as the sun heard it, it busily opened the door and walked out. Heaven and earth shone in an instant. All the flowers and trees, all the birds and animals cheered under the warm sun. Some clapped and others cheered. However, the next day, the big rooster almost broke his throat, and the sun still didn't come out. What happened to the Sun? There was a lot of discussion, and in the end, they invited Big Rooster to visit the country of the Sun. The big rooster has once again arrived at the place where the Sun lives in the Land of the Sun. This time, unlike the last time, the big rooster directly pushed open the Sun Gate and walked into the patio. When I saw the big sun, I was busy picking it up at the house. “What's wrong? Did everyone scold you, poke you, and kick you out again?” the big cock asked.
“No, no one scolded me, poked me, or kicked me out. Everyone looked at me; I'm shy.” The Sun answered. “Ahhh, I thought something happened again. OK, OK, I'll give you a pack of needles. If anyone looks at you, you'll stab him in the eye.” “You have to tell everyone first when you go back.” “I'll definitely stop them when I go back. If I'm negligent, let the wild cats eat me.” “OK, if I don't come out in the future, I'll also ask Tengu to eat me.” Since then, the rooster and the sun have become close partners. As soon as the East turns white, as soon as the rooster crows, the sun comes out. Of course, there are times when the sun is paralyzed, so sometimes tengues eat the sun; people and other animals also forget the rooster's warning, so sometimes wild cats eat chicken.
