 Mr. Wang Zengqi's essays are unique. This is the readers' consensus; Mr. Wang's articles often mention delicious food, but there is little mention of alcohol. I recently read Kim Si-chiu's book, and only learned that Mr. Wang's whole life was better than good wine. Mr. Wang is a foodie and an authentic wine fairy.
Mr. Wang Zengqi, a master of wine, had a habit of drinking good wine when he was young. While studying at Southwest Union University, Mr. Shen Congwen once saw a drunken person on the side of the road and took a close look. It turned out that Wang Zengqi was drunk. Mr. Shen is Wang Zengqi's teacher, so he quickly helped Mr. Wang back. This is also an empirical proof that Mr. Wang is a young man who is good at drinking. Her children are often mentioned in memoirs. In the eyes of his loved ones, Mr. Wang Zengqi has enjoyed drinking all his life. According to her daughter, Ms. Wang Chao, Mr. Wang was still drinking for two months before her death. Naturally, Mr. Wang's wife is the protector. Mr. Wang has liver disease due to alcohol, and his wife cannot help but forbid it. A writer is visiting Mr. Wang. Mr. Wang will take this opportunity to ask his wife to lift the ban. After getting acquiescence, visiting writers will also make all kinds of cover-ups when they get their likes to go the extra mile. Mrs. Wang had no choice but to let him go. With such alcohol restrictions, granddaughter Wang Hui is sometimes sent as a “spy,” in order to inform her grandmother. Just like that, while Mr. Wang was blazing around, he didn't drink less alcohol. Sometimes I also secretly go to the store downstairs to buy alcohol; when I really didn't drink alcohol, I drank the cooking wine at home. This is the first time I've seen from Kim Si-chiu's article that cooking wine can also be drunk, and alcoholic drinkers have imitated it.
In the eyes of the writer, Wang Zengqi is a writer who likes to drink. The reason why Wang Zengqi is known as “The Last Master of Literature” is related to his poetry, calligraphy, painting, and screenwriter, and is also closely related to his penchant for drinking. When drawing, Mr. Wang likes to put his left hand in his pants pocket and write with his right hand. He takes a sip of wine before meditating on crazy books. While drinking while reading, if no one is around, think of Mr. Wang's image, which is the style of a talented writer. Mr. Wang is a good smoker and enjoys the scent of a fairy in the clouds and mist. Mr. Wang usually wears it casually. He doesn't have anything to wear, but he has quite a few good drinks and cigarettes at home; most of them are gifts from friends, family, and friends. Mr. Lin Jinlan is a friend of Mr. Wang. Not only do they often discuss literature together, but they also enjoy drinking together for a long time. Mr. Lin went to the hospital near Mr. Wang's house to see a doctor, and he couldn't forget to invite Mr. Wang to a small gathering at a restaurant. It is said that the rabbit burnt down well there. The Beijing writer, who closely interacts with Mr. Wang, recalled that Mr. Wang never paid attention to superiority when drinking. I usually go out to enjoy myself, and I also like to bring my own small drinks. I paint a trip before meals, and get drunk before talking. Alcohol clears the mind. It is said that many of his articles are written right after drinking. After Mr. Wang had liver disease, his family restricted alcohol and his face turned gray. The writers saw that it was not as good as in the past. Mrs. Wang gave Mr. Wang a moderate amount of alcohol to drink. Mr. Wang usually likes to drink liquor, and it is highly alcoholic. As soon as the wine sticks to the lips, it is full of sparkle. When I'm outside painting, I often drink alcohol after meals, and the painting flourishes. Once in Tai'an, Mr. Wang painted until 2 o'clock at night. There was a record in his own article, but there was no mention of drinking. Witnesses at the scene said that Mr. Wang painted while drinking alcohol.
Mr. Wang Zengqi, known as a foodie, cooks excellent cuisine. He likes to cook, think and innovate, and he also likes to discuss all kinds of cuisines with people. His cooking was praised and loved by his friends. Overseas writer, Ms. Nie Hualing, once tasted delicious dishes made by Mr. Wang, and even drank the soup clean. Memories of food in his hometown will lock in his taste buds. Mr. Wang regards drunk crabs and shrimps sent by family and friends in his hometown as a good accompaniment to wine. He easily refuses to show it, which is also Mr. Wang's lovely hobby. Mr. Wang, who loved to prey on insects and cook alcohol at the Southwest Union University, later maintained this elegance when he worked as a teacher. Fry the insects in one hand and distribute rice wine in the other. If you drink alcohol, you don't have to worry about the food.
Mr. Wang can be at ease when he meets him. Being able to enjoy himself when he drinks, and he is crazy talented. It is also considered a blessing in life. Mr. Wang likes people who are drunk, especially when it comes to the literary world. After drinking, let's be proud and have fun talking. In his article, Mr. Kim Si-chiu found out that sometimes he drank too much alcohol, and he claimed that the article was comparable to his mentor Shen Congwen. There is confidence in the drunkenness, which is also considered a literary joke. It's rare for a writer to always be crazy. Some people blamed Mr. Wang for his death, stemming from the heavy drinking at the last winery pen meeting and the tiredness of continuous painting. Unbeknownst to me, alcohol is the power of painting. Mr. Wang's painting spirit is supported by wine; the beauty of wine is Mr. Wang's brilliance. Mr. Wang's long-term addiction caused Mr. Wang's physical illness, but how could Mr. Wang, who lacked alcohol, provide so many beautiful articles and paintings?! Writers who are close friends of Mr. Wang have commented a lot on this. Mr. Kim Si-qiu wrote a funny story about Mr. Wang's drinking into a book. It's unbearable and charming. It's as if Mr. Wang is actually still alive and standing right in front of your eyes.
Mr. Wang, who likes to drink, is the real Mr. Wang. His fireworks imbued with beautiful articles, calligraphy, and paintings. He is an excellent screenwriter for model shows, a detailed depiction of potato anatomy, and a fairy who likes to drink slowly. Wang Zengqi, who has had a troubled life, sees wine as joy. It is not simply a “how can you solve your worries, only Du Kang” mentality, but rather created a bold and happy life with wine. His writing, calligraphy, and painting had a relaxed mood and a relaxed mind. I haven't met Mr. Wang in this life, but I can clearly feel the aroma of wine from his books. That laid-back, not something a drunken person can enjoy writing! (Dai Rongli)
