 The king took Avanti to hunt, and at night the two slept beside a rock at the mouth of a canyon. In the middle of the night, the mountain breeze blew in, causing the king to curl up like a hedgehog into a bump. In order to ruin the king, Avanti took the cushion under the saddle and gently placed it on the king.
“Avanti, what did you put on me?” the king asked.
“Your Majesty, it's a saddle cushion.” Avanti said.
“Bastard! Who covered you with the dirty stuff from your horse?!” The king scolded and stepped the mat aside.
After a while, the mountain breeze blew even harder. The cold wind was harsh, the king froze his teeth and fighting, and his limbs trembled. As a result, Avanti put a saddle cushion on the king again.
“Avanti, what are you putting on me?”
“Your Majesty, that's the only thing I put on your body — an object that heats you.”
“Hey idiot!” The king said angrily, “When I asked you just now, you said 'something that can heat it', right?” After speaking, I huddled up under a saddle cushion and slept all night.
