One day, an old man came to rent his house and was willing to pay 120 yuan for rent. Li Sheng turned him down on the grounds that he didn't have a spare house. The old man said to Li Sheng, “Please feel free to accept the rent, don't worry.” Li Sheng didn't know what he meant, so he put down the rent and see what happened.
After a day, people in the village saw that a family of cars and horses had entered the Li family. There seemed to be a lot of people in the village. But the fortune teller solemnly insisted on saying this.
