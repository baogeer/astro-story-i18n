 Legend has it that a long time ago, there was a judge in the city who looked very religious and honest. People have a good impression of him. Some people also say that no mother in the world has ever given birth to an honest, honest, intelligent, and capable son like him. When the judge heard these arguments, it fluttered. He thought he was greater than God. One day he said to his wife:
“The position of judge is no longer suitable for me. People like me should be ministers in the king's place and take charge of national affairs.”
The wife replied, “There is no end to people's desires. You should fulfill God's plans for your fate, stop thinking nonchalantly, get rid of the idea of being a minister, or just do your job well.”
However, the judge was proud of himself and couldn't listen to his wife's advice at all. Instead, the desire to become a minister became stronger and stronger. He made every effort to find an opportunity to meet with the king in order to express his thoughts and implore the king to approve his request and appoint him as minister.
The judge later heard that the king often disguised himself as entering the city at night to investigate public conditions. He then decided to take advantage of this opportunity to meet the king. One night, the judge came to the palace gate and waited there. After the night patrol passed by, the king walked out of the palace, wearing crutches and a robe. The judge immediately greeted him, bowed deeply, blessed the king, and kissed the king's hand. The king said, “Hey, judge, why are you here in the middle of the night? Why don't you go home?”
The judge said, “Huh, King, Lord of the universe! I always come out late at night to pray, so I only get home at this time. Tonight, when I was reflecting on my sins to God, I was suddenly turned upside down and unconscious. At this point, I saw God, and he said to me, “Huh, the judge, the king needs a minister who is pious, honest, honest, and good at managing state affairs. You already have these characteristics. Go see the king right away, present your true feelings to him, and ask him to grant you the position of minister. As a result, the country is peaceful and the people are well. ' As soon as I woke up, I immediately walked to the palace and just met you—the king of the universe.”
The king is experienced. He stared at the judge's face and thought, “This person is a big liar. He wants to use lies to deceive me and steal power to manage the country's affairs. However, he doesn't know, and he can't see the characteristics and character of a minister at all in him.”
The king thought about it and said to the judge, “Judge, God is right; I really need ministers who know the affairs of the country and are considerate of the people. But before I award you this position, I must examine your wisdom and insight.”
The judge answered respectfully: “I would like to obey the king's will.”
The king looked at the judge and said, “Now that I have made this decision, let's go to the city and see how the subjects live, but you have to cover your face so people don't recognize you.”
The judge covered his face and followed the king. They walked through the streets and alleys. Everywhere they went, the king personally checked the night patrol to see if they were loyal to their duties, protect the safety of their subjects, and maintain the city's nighttime security. The king wrote down all the police officers who left their posts, sleeping, and drunken, one by one, and prepared to punish them the next day. When he heard the cry of a hungry baby, he put a mark on the door of the house so that the owner of the house could be brought into the palace the next day. When they encounter people living in poverty and have no jobs, the king gives them some money and sends them errands. When he sees old people and orphans, he allocates money to help them.
The king and judges visited the city for a long time, and finally arrived in front of a broken house, and the king walked in. On the floor near the corner of the house, a candle was burning. Under the faint candlelight, I could see a ragged-dressed praying. The king greeted him and sat down next to him. The Buddhist monk immediately stood up and sat down very respectfully. The king asked him, “How are you doing? You can tell from the look on your face that you are in a bad mood and very tired. Didn't you get three-ninths?”
The Buddhist monk took a deep breath and said, “No, why can't I get 3 out of 9. Not only that, I even got 9 out of 3.”
The king asked, “How did you get it?”
The Buddhist monk answered, “Yes, look...”
The king looked very sad, took some money out of his pocket and gave it to the Buddhist monk, and said, “Take the flowers, if... then...”
The monk immediately held the king's hand and kissed again, then said, “Am I a fool!”
The judge, standing outside the house, heard all of their conversations. But he couldn't understand what was meant. He thought, “Maybe the king is confused; otherwise, how could he say such foolish things to the Buddha.”
At this point, the king stood up and after saying goodbye to the prayers, he walked out of the house and said to the judge:
“I'm going back to the palace. You should go home and rest. I'm considering appointing you as minister.”
The judge happily walked home, thinking about the conversation between the king and the Buddhist monk on the way. What exactly were they talking about? What do these conversations mean, “I didn't get three-ninths,” “if... then...” and “Am I a fool?” There must be some mystery in it; the king must have said something important to the Buddhist monk. I have to go find the Buddha tomorrow morning and unravel this mystery.
When he got home, the judge lay down in bed and couldn't sleep at all. However, he couldn't understand the meaning of the conversation between the king and the monk. Later, he simply sat up and watched the stars in the night sky outside the window while counting, hoping that time would pass faster. The night is over at last. As soon as it dawned, the judge put on his coat, and couldn't help but eat breakfast, so he hurriedly went looking for the Buddhist monk. When he walked into the broken house, the worshiper was praying. He waited patiently for a while. When the prayer was over, he asked the praying monk, “I heard that someone came last night, and you had a mysterious conversation between you. For example, he said to you, 'Didn't you get three-ninths? ' And you answered, 'No, not only did you get 3 out of 9, but you also got 9 out of 3. ' He also said, 'If... then... 'you answered, 'Am I a fool?' What do these mysterious words really mean?”
The Buddhist monk said, “Judge, this is a big secret; you have no right to know it. This secret concerns the king himself.”
The judge said, “I am a judge in this city, and I have the right to understand all kinds of issues through various methods.”
The devil answered, “I don't understand what you're saying. But if you want to know what those words mean, you have to give me 1,000 coins. Otherwise, you just don't want to know.”
The judge understood that the threat was useless and said, “OK, tomorrow I'll bring you 1,000 coins.”
When he got home, he thought and meditated on how he could get 1,000 coins. On this day, he didn't go to work in court.
Suddenly, he had a clever move and became happy. He walked to the front of the box, opened the lid, removed a money bag from the inside, cut it with scissors, and poured out 1,000 coins. Then another 1,000 lead coins were tucked in. When he was done, he ordered his servant to find the best tailor. Half an hour later, the servant brought in a tailor. The judge said to the tailor, “If you sew this purse, you have to sew it as if it hadn't been cut.”
The tailor said “follow orders,” then sat down and sewed it. After his stitching, the purse was exactly the same as the original one. The judge was very satisfied, gave him a lot of money, and sent the tailor away. The judge put the money bag containing the lead coins back to its place and wrapped the 1,000 coins in a scarf. The next day, I went looking for the Buddhist monk. The monk took the money, put it on his bedside, coughed a bit, and said, “Judge, that's right. The monk wearing a robe that day was the king himself. He always comes to me once a year. This time, when he saw my sad face, he asked me, “Didn't you get 3 out of 9”, meaning didn't you take a break for three months and work for nine months this year? I answered, 'No, not only did you get 3 out of 9, but 9 out of 3. ' It means that not only did I take a break for three months, but I had nothing to do for the other nine months.
The king asked again, 'So how are you doing? ' I answered, 'Haven't you seen them all? ' Later, before leaving, the king looked in the direction you were standing and said, 'If... then... ', meaning that the judge wanted to ask him about the content of the conversation, he had to ask him for a large sum of money. Otherwise, nothing can be told to him. I answered, “Am I a fool!”
After listening to the Buddhist monk's explanation, the judge sighed deeply and walked home in dismay. Coincidentally, on this day, the businessman returned from a trip to retrieve the money he had stored at the judge's place a month ago. The judge removed the money bag from the box and handed it to the merchant.
The businessman returned home, opened his wallet, and found that the bag was not gold, but lead. The businessman was taken aback and immediately went to the judge.
“I kept gold coins with you, and why did you return mine to lead?”
The judge ignored it and said, “I haven't touched your wallet at all; I kept it in its original box. I'll give it back to you when you get back.”
The merchant understood that the judge embezzled the coins, and there was no use in saying anything to him. He made up his mind to go to the king and report the judge's embezzlement of coins to the king. The king said, “You don't have to worry; things will return to their original owners. However, there is one condition: you never go to a judge again, and don't tell anyone about this.”
The merchant kissed the king's hand and went home.
The king cut his robe with his dagger and said to his servant, “Go find the best tailor to sew my robe.”
Soon, the servant brought in a tailor. This person is the one who sews the money bags for the judges. The tailor sewed the robe very quickly. It's hard for anyone to find a robe that has been cut. At this point, the king took out the merchant's wallet, and the tailor looked at it. The king asked, “Did you sew this purse?” The tailor said, “Yes, I sewed it. The judge called me the other day and asked me to sew up this torn purse.”
At this point, the king called in two guards and ordered them to notify the judge and immediately come to the palace to accept the sacrament.
The judge was extremely happy. Wearing his best clothes, he accompanied the guard to the palace. Suddenly, he found the tailor and money bag. He was frightened, pale and unconscious. He understood that the secret had been debunked and that it was no use denying it. The judge sighed and came to the king and bitterly begged, “King of the universe, forgive me!
I have committed a crime!”
The king said to the judge, “I wanted to test you to see if you can fulfill the minister's mission.
Almighty God has provided such an opportunity, and now you know it for yourself. Not only are you unfit to be a minister, but you don't even have what it takes to be an official. Everyone knows that for judges, to know how to enforce the law, they must have the conditions of justice and honesty. Starting today, you don't have to be an official anymore. Use the rest of your life to atone for your sins!”
