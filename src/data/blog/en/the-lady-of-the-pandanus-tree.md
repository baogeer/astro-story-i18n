 The story takes place during the late Qing period. The male protagonist, Zhou Yasi, was originally from Shantou and did camphor business in Taiwan. Zhou Yasi met the widow Lee Chao-niang (later “Sister Lin Tou”). The two soon got married and had a child.
Later, Zhou Yasi said he wanted to do business in Hong Kong, but went back to his hometown of Shantou, married a wife again there, and had children. In other words, Zhou Yasi abandoned his wife and children in Taiwan.
Li Zhao-niang waited a long time for her husband to return, but she finally didn't see anyone. In the end, the family ran out of money, and the child starved to death. Meanwhile, Li Zhaoniang also learned the fact that she was abandoned by her husband Zhou Yasi. All her thoughts went to waste. Li Zhaoniang threw herself on a tree and hanged herself...
After Li Zhaoniang died, she often wandered around the forest tou tree when she was unwilling, and even said that some people saw her ghost using paper money to buy yakiniku rice dumplings to eat.
One day, Li Zhaoniang's ghost met a Taoist. Li Zhaoniang told the Taoists about her sad life. The Taoists took pity on her, so they decided to help her get revenge.
Taoists put Li Zhaoniang's soul in an umbrella, then went to Zhou Yasi's home in Shantou and opened the umbrella. Li Zhao-niang's spirit became a powerful ghost emanating from the Beetle. When Zhou Yasi saw this situation, she was immediately shocked to urinate and lost her mind. He picked up a knife, slashed his wife and two children, then strangled himself.
Li Zhao-niang took revenge.
