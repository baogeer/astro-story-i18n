 On the Lantern Festival in Taiwan, young girls often play this game: put a female doll on a small chair and serve with rouge, white powder, flowers, ruler, scissors, and some fruit. This is to miss a chair lady who was abused and died by her sister-in-law.
Miss Chair is a young girl in her teens. She lost her parents when she was three years old and raised by her older brother. However, my sister-in-law is underrated and has a bad heart. My older brother runs outside to live as a family. Normally, he rarely comes home, and his sister-in-law treats her sister in law even more leniency.
Lady Chair wears tattered clothes all year round and eats cold leftovers for three meals. At a young age, I was responsible for all the housework. When the fire was burning, because of the height of the stove, she was short and had to crouch on a chair to fill the stove with firewood. There used to be no matches, so starting a fire was very troublesome. My sister-in-law ordered her not to put out the fire, so the chair lady had to stay by the stove all day long. While working, take care of the fire. While sleeping, I was only able to take a nap in front of the stove. I was terrified that the fire would go out, and I would be beaten severely by my sister-in-law.
On the night of the Lantern Festival, my sister-in-law went out to see the fun. Grandma Chair had just finished her day's work. At this point, there was a burst of laughter and gongs and drums in the distance. She really wanted to go out and have fun with others, but her sister-in-law didn't let her leave the house, so she had to sit in front of the stove. Facing the burning flames, Miss Chair thought of the grief of losing her parents. She couldn't help but burst into tears and shouted at Mom, Mom! After a day of exhaustion, she finally couldn't help falling asleep in front of the stove.
Suddenly, Grandma Chair saw her kind mother and walked up to her carrying a small lantern. She affectionately called her mom and immediately threw her into her arms. Mom gave her a beautiful little lantern and held her daughter tightly. The mother and daughter haven't met again for a long time; it's really hard to figure it out. After a while, Mom took a chair and went to the bustling city to play. There were lots of fire trees and silver blossoms, people dancing dragons and lions, and there was also a makeup parade. It was very lively. Grandma Chair has never been so happy. She also followed a group of children with jacquard lanterns to watch a movie, and also to the temple to enjoy the lanterns. While she was having fun, she suddenly discovered that her mom close to her was gone. So she hurriedly looked everywhere, even though she shouted at her mom! But they were all swallowed up by the hustle and bustle of the festival.
The next morning, my sister-in-law called the chair gu to cook. Seeing that the fire had been extinguished, she was about to get angry. She also saw the chair sir lying in front of the stove and told her that she should not, push her. She was sorry; only the fire was still clasped in her little hands. Turns out, Miss Chair went to her mom and never came back.
To mourn the poor chair girl, the girls prepared a chair for the Lantern Festival, with a female doll made of cloth on it and presented some offerings. The two carried it and prayed for the chair sister to come to this chair while walking to accept the offerings and comfort her in the spirit of heaven.
