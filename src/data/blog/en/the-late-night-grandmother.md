 In other words, I went out to play with a group of friends one day. Since I was having too much fun at the time, and I stayed a long distance longer than the night bus time, I had to work myself to spend the night at one of my friends' houses. Once I got to his house, I recommended taking a bath before going to bed. My friend told me that the bathroom was in the back and handed me a towel, so I walked to the back of the house. It was a pretty small bathroom. There was a bathtub inside. I filled the bathtub with boiling water, then the whole person went into the water and enjoyed it.
Soon I heard a knock on the door and thought my friend was calling me, so I wrapped a towel and went to open the door. I opened the door but didn't see anyone. I suspected that I had misheard it, but strange things happened one after another, and I began to get angry, thinking that my friend was playing a prank. I washed myself, and while I was putting on my clothes, the knocking came again. I opened the door so quickly this time, and I was shocked because there was an old grandmother standing in front of the door this time. She said in a bad tone, “Don't waste water!” After I calmed down, I wasn't busy spending time with her. Originally, I thought this grandmother was a friend's family. When I first introduced myself, I didn't see her grandmother. I guess she must have taught me a lesson and then went back to her room. I was embarrassed to say sorry to my friend. He looked at me strangely, then asked me what actually happened, and I explained it to him again, but he was still stupid. I compared to drawing the old woman's face. Suddenly, his face sank and asked me not to talk anymore. He hurriedly urged me to sleep. Although I noticed something was wrong, I never asked again.
The next day, my friend told me that the old woman who knocked on my door last night was actually his grandmother. She also took me to see his black and white photo. Sure enough, it was the old woman who knocked on my door. My friend told me that this grandmother had been poor since childhood, was frugal, and never liked to waste anything. She just knocked on my door to tell me not to waste water. As soon as I heard it, my face changed. I was very sick when I got home, and I never dared to spend the night at this friend's house again.
