A long time ago, there was such a young couple. The male loved to brag, didn't do his job properly all day, and showed off how they were with people everywhere; women were delicious, lazy, and not good at housework. They are struggling to survive with a little bit of family left behind by their ancestors.
Later, they gave birth to several children, and because they had a few more mouths, the days of being overwhelmed were even more difficult.
On this day, after drinking the last bowl of porridge, they licked the rice soup in the bowl. The husband said to his wife, “If we continue like this, we will all have to starve to death. We have to find a way. Otherwise, how can we live our days from now on?”
The wife sighed and said, “You also know that we haven't cultivated our cultivated land for many years, we have finished eating the aged food at home, and no one wants to lend us food. I don't think even the immortals have come; we can only wait and see and starve to death.”
Hearing his wife mention immortals, the husband's eyes lit up. He hurriedly took his wife and child to the hospital and knelt down. At the same time, he made his wife and children all kneel down, put their hands together, and prayerfully prayed for the fairy's family to get through this difficult time.
After a long time, when they were hungry and dizzy, the fairy came and said to them, “I can give you a handful of rice, but as a punishment for being lazy, I want to drop the rice in the fields, on the side of the road, and in the grass. You should try to pick them up before the sun sets, otherwise they will fly to the sky, and you'll be hungry.”
After speaking, the fairy pulled out a handful of rice from her arms and spread it far away, then turned around and disappeared.
The couple hurriedly took the children to pick up rice grains one by one and put them in their pockets.
The sun set, and the grains of rice on the ground flew into the sky and became stars in the sky. The couple had no choice but to take their children home and collect the rice grains they had found and pour them into a pot to cook porridge and drink. They drank all the porridge in the pot, and didn't drink enough. They had to wait for the next day to arrive early, so they could pick up more grains of rice.
The husband was fed up with the taste of starvation. He was afraid that he would get up late the next day and delay picking up rice grains. Before it was bright, he started screaming, and Zhang Luo's family woke up. When they got dressed and took a look outside, the grains of rice were still hanging in the sky, looking at them in the blink of an eye. They had no choice but to return to the house in dismay.
After a while, the anxious husband started waking up the house again. When they went outside to take a look, it was still dark outside.
By the time my husband woke up the house for the third time, the sky was already blazing. The stars drop to the ground one by one and turn into grains of rice. The family began to be busy picking up rice grains. Sometimes they used their hands to pick up the stones and weeds and pick up the rice grains underneath. Despite the hard work, after a day, the grains of rice they picked were only enough to fill their stomachs. The next day, I had to leave early and return late to pick up rice grains. The family is in great pain because of this. My husband didn't fix his bragging problem. While picking up grains of rice, he said, “I picked up a lot, I picked up a lot...” It took a long time, and after getting used to listening, he only heard: ooh (me), ooh (me).
My wife thought it was really boring to pick rice grains all day long, so every time she picked up a grain of rice, she said, “One piece, one piece...” For a long time, people listened to her thinking: OK, OK (piece) all day long.
Because their children are too young to bear the pain, they cry and chirp behind their mom, and their mom doesn't have time to teach them to talk; they keep chirping like little birds.
Over time, my husband turned into a rooster, waking up every day with his voice shouting at home. He called his family three times every morning until he woke up his family from their sleep. His wife turned into an old hen and “giggled” with her young chickens looking for grains of rice to eat.
Since they became chickens, the grains of rice they found were no longer needed to be cooked and eaten. They put them directly into the bag, and their lives were much easier than before. They haven't gotten rid of their bad habit of being lazy. They don't want to take care of children, and don't understand family planning. They often ignore laying eggs. Sometimes they want children. In order to save trouble, they hatch lots of eggs together into chicks. Also since then, people have regarded people who are tasty and lazy to cook as their descendants, called “lazy eggs.”
