The development of Longjing tea has gone through a long historical process. From the poem “Two New Flags Under Baiyun Mountain” by Su Dongpo, can you think that during the Northern Song Dynasty, tea produced under Baiyun Peak in Tianzhu, Hangzhou had the prototype of Longjing tea; can the “two flags” indicate the appearance characteristics of tea when brewed. By the Qing Dynasty, the popularity of Longjing tea was growing day by day. In particular, during the Qianlong period, the Qianlong emperor visited Jiangnan many times, visited places such as Shangtianzhu, Xiatianzhu, Longjing, and Yunqi in Hangzhou, sealed the “Eighteen Imperial Tea Trees” in Longjing, and left many poems about Longjing tea, making Longjing tea more and more famous. The popularity of Longjing tea has promoted the development of Longjing tea, and villages around West Lake have begun to develop tea plantations. Around the end of the 19th century, temples near Hangzhou's West Lake, such as Hupao Temple and Longjing Temple, as well as some villagers, opened tea shops and began producing and selling their own Longjing tea. Later, when some merchants in the city of Hangzhou saw that it was profitable to operate Longjing tea, they successively visited the Longjing tea production area, invested in tea houses, and opened tea shops, such as Maoji Tea House (company) in Longjing Shifeng, Dashan Tea House (company) in Liji Longshan, and Hu Jingtai Company in Wuyunshan, Jiuxi. These companies are all relatively small, with only a few tens of acres to a few hundred acres of tea plantations. At the time, the largest Maoji Company had 3 branches near Jiuxi and Meijiawu, but only 300 acres of tea plantations.
After the founding of New China in 1949, the Party and state attached great importance to the development of Longjing tea. Prime Minister Zhou Enlai, Chairman Zhu De, and Field Marshal Chen Yi, and the older generation of revolutionaries visited the Longjing tea area many times to encourage the people in the tea area to produce more Longjing tea. After decades of development, the area of tea plantations has continued to expand, tea production is increasing, and major breakthroughs have been made in frying technology. In particular, the breeding and promotion of new varieties such as Longjing 43 and the development and application of Longjing tea frying machinery have played a positive role in improving the quality of Longjing tea, improving labor productivity, and improving economic efficiency. Among the many famous teas in China, Longjing tea has become a famous product with a large cultivation area, high yield, good economic benefits, and is famous at home and abroad, and has been selected as a national gift tea. A few years ago, the area of tea plantations in Xihu District of Hangzhou was about 9,000 mu, producing more than 500 tons of Longjing tea per year. In 1978, Xihu Township, Hangzhou covered an area of 4,597 acres of tea plantations; in 1997, the area of tea plantations was 3,300 acres, producing 185 tons of Longjing tea per year; in recent years, due to regional development, engineering construction, and attraction expansion, etc., the Longjing tea plantation has been reduced to about 2,700 acres at present.
The title of Longjing tea has also gone through a process of development, change, and even reaching standards in the future. At different times, it was also called differently. Before 1949, due to the origin of Longjing tea, there were slight differences in frying methods. Each product had its own characteristics and was called by the five characters “lion, dragon, cloud, tiger, plum”, etc., such as “lion character longjing” and “cloud character longjing”. After the founding of New China in 1949, it was changed to the three categories of “lion, dragon, and plum”. Produced in Longjing Village, Xihu Township, it has the best natural quality; the one produced in the Meijiawu, Fancun, Yunqi, and Waidaqiao area in Xihu Township is called “Mei Zi Longjing” or “Meijun Longjing”, which is exquisite; the rest of Xihu Township is called “Longji Longjing” or “West Lake Longjing”. In 1965, the country adopted a unified acquisition standard, collectively known as West Lake Longjing. In the 1980s and 90s, in the general environment of reform and opening-up, with the continuous development of famous teas and the gradual establishment of a market economy, as well as the vigorous promotion and application of Longjing tea varieties and training, dissemination and diffusion of Longjing tea varieties, especially the increasing improvement of Longjing tea machinery and mechanical technology, places other than the historical production area of Longjing tea, such as Yuhang, Xiaoshan, Fuyang in Hangzhou, and Xinchang and Shengzhou in Zhejiang, also successively produced flat tea according to the production method of Longjing tea, and “Zhejiang Longjing.”
Recently, according to EU countries' regulations on the protection of products from regions of origin, after implementing origin protection for their products, no other new products in the world can be involved in this new product brand, and other products with the same product name must be forcibly withdrawn from the market. Starting from protecting the interests of national specialty products in international trade, China listed Longjing tea as one of the first two ethnic products protected by origin. However, during the implementation process, there was considerable controversy over whether to use the name “Longjing tea” or “West Lake Longjing tea” to declare origin protection within a certain range. In response to this issue, relevant ministries and departments of the State specifically coordinated with Zhejiang Province, Hangzhou City, and Xihu District in Hangzhou, and organized expert discussions. Experts believe that it should be declared under the name “Longjing tea” and that the scope of origin should be determined by the relevant government departments. According to reports, national standards for the protection of the origin of Longjing tea will soon be issued and implemented.
