 I heard the older generation say that! The golden cow lives by a good demon and keeps a cow. He works for the common people every day. The common people worship incense for him every year. He lives the days when the sun rises and rests at sunset. One night, this ghost walked around the county. He was hungry late at night and wanted to find something to eat. He looked all over the county and didn't find anything to eat. How did he know there were refugees living in the city? They didn't believe in ghosts at all; no one gave it to him everywhere he went. When Jinniu was used to living a life without worry about food and clothing, I felt that the people in the county were so ruthless and decided to take revenge on the people in the county. He came home that night and turned his cow into a big mountain and pulled it between the two mountains. The sky immediately fell apart. The mountain was pulled out of a road. He was ready to pull the mountain to block the Yangjiao River, and he wanted to turn Yangyi County into a vast ocean. The noise made Wang Mama uncovered the devil's plot. Seeing that she was about to be blocked by the devil, Wang Mu got smart and learned to block the Yangjiangjiang River. The ghost had no choice but to run down the mountain. The next morning, people woke up and saw a mountain split open. Another 30 meters could block the mountain in the Yangjiangjiang River. People were afraid to come back since then. The mountain that was later pulled out was called Shimen Pass. The mountain that almost blocked the Yangjiangjiang River is now called Niutou Mountain. Now, when we look at it, it actually looks like Mount Niutou was pulled out of Shimen Guan.
An account of a Taurus collected by Yang Dengtang
