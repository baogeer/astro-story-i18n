 How did the first tribe in the snowy region appear? Folklore has beautiful, vivid legends.
Legend has it that people who live a nomadic lifestyle in the Yalong River Valley discovered a brave young man one day in Kuoxi, Zantang. This young man's speech and behavior are different from those of the native indigenous people. The herders couldn't discern what was going on; they sent someone back to the settlement to report on how to deal with this young man. The elder sent 12 very clever wizards up the mountain to question the young man where he had done it. This young man pointed his finger at the sky. This group of people thought that the young man came from heaven and was a “son of Tenjin,” and they were extremely happy. The first of the twelve had a long neck, used this “son of Tenjin” as a car, and hugged him down the mountain. People who live here came and saw this young man from heaven, who was smart and handsome, and publicly promoted him as the leader of the tribe. This is later called “Tufan,” and since there was a king, people started building royal palaces, or just houses. Now, more than 20 miles from Zedang, there is also a house called Yung Bulagang. This is the legendary first palace in Tibet. It was built for Nie Chizanpu, the “son of Tenjin.” The ruins of Yumbulagang are still clearly visible today.
