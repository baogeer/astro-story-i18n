 On the east side of Chiayi County in Taiwan, there is a mountain over 3,000 meters above sea level called Alishan. Large areas of pristine forest grow in the mountains, and most of the camphor trees, one of Taiwan's three treasures (rice, sugar cane, and camphor) grow here. Here, flowers and birds talk all year round, and it is a famous tourist attraction in Taiwan. What is particularly remarkable is a large cypress tree in the middle of the mountainside. It is said that this cypress tree is 3,000 years old, so people call it a “sacred tree.”
However, in the past, Alishan didn't have a single tree, grass, or flower all over its body, so it is also called Dushan. So, how did this bald mountain have trees, flowers, and plants? And why was the name changed to Alishan? There is such a local legend.
Once upon a time, on a ditch on the north side of Dushan Mountain, there lived a young man named Ali who hunted for a living. One day, Ali went hunting on the northern hillside. Suddenly, she saw an eye catching tiger at the bottom of the mountain, chasing two girls picking flowers. Ali hurriedly ran down the hillside and immediately jumped on the back of the tiger. He picked up his hand and only heard a “click”. The tiger's head was cut off to the ground and rolled down the hillside. The two flower pickers were saved.
When he was about to go back to the northern hillside to hunt, he also saw a white-bearded old man with a dragon cane falling from the sky. While laughing, the old man dragged the two girls' arms up the southern mountainside. Ali is a nice guy who acted courageously for justice. Seeing that these two girls had just left their mouths, they were also molested by this bad old man, which ignited a burst of anger in his heart. He shouted, “Stop it!” Then I rushed to the bad old man one by one, took down his dragon cane, and hit the old man hard on the forehead. The old head screamed so much that he let go of those two girls and saw that he had a huge pimp on his forehead. As soon as he threw his sleeve and flew into the air, in a blink of an eye, he disappeared.
It didn't take long for thunder to be heard on a clear day. The thunder was getting louder and louder from far and near. I saw the two flower picking girls tremble all over their bodies. They said anxiously, “This is terrible, this is bad!”
Ali asked strangely, “What's going on?”
The two girls said, “We are both originally fairies in the Tiangong Palace. We secretly came here when we heard that Taiwan Island had a beautiful scenery. I don't want to. I met an evil tiger, thanks to you who saved both of our lives. Who knows, I missed the right time to go back because I was greedy for the beautiful scenery here. The Jade Emperor sent an old star to arrest both of us and return them to the Tiangong Palace to punish them. We are afraid of Emperor Jade's criminal law, and we don't want to go back to Tiangong. While Lao Shou Xing was taking us, you just ran over and beat him away. He told Emperor Jade about this incident. Emperor Jade was furious and ordered Thor to burn all the creatures in this area with lightning.”
Ali was taken aback when they heard them both say this. He didn't expect that doing good deeds would cause a major disaster for the creatures in this area, so he anxiously said, “Isn't there any way to save the creatures in this area?”
The two fairies said, “Yes, but it is necessary for someone who is willing to sacrifice his life to run to the top of the bald mountain in the south to drive the thunderstorm away so that the thunderstorm cannot spread to preserve life in this area. “Go, take refuge in a safe place. We both will go to the top of Dushan Mountain to cause lightning.”
Ali shook her head and said, “No, I beat Laoshou Xing; I caused the trouble. How can I let you go? Let me start the thunderstorm!” After speaking, he picked up the dragon cane and hurriedly ran to the bald mountain on the south side. Soon after, he climbed the mountain slope of Dushan Mountain. He looked up and shouted at the sky, “Thor! Laoshou Xing was fought by me. Those two fairies were created by Ali, and the disaster was caused by me. All of this was done by me, and it had nothing to do with anyone else. Your thunderstorm hits me, Ali!”
At this point, Thor happened to be over Dushan Mountain. He held up a thunder drill and flashhammer, and only heard a “thunderclap”. A thunder struck Ali's body all of a sudden, and the thunderstorm flared up on Mount Dushan. When Thor saw that the fire was on fire, he turned to the Tiangong crossing. Because this mountain was bare, there were no trees, flowers, or plants, and the thunderstorm hadn't burned in the middle of the mountainside, so it went out on its own.
Although Ali was hit by thunderstorms, soon after his death, trees grew all over the mountain in Dushan. People say that these trees are the flesh and hair of Ali, crushed by thunderstorms. What about that sacred tree? It is said that it was turned into Lao Shou Xing's dragon cane. The two fairies were deeply moved when they saw this kind of scene. They checked and said, “Ali Ago died for both of us. After his death, his hair turned into trees for the benefit of people. The two of us became flowers and plants, making it a good partner for Ariago and for the benefit of the people.”
Since then, this empty mountain has had trees, flowers, and plants. The mountains are full of lush greenery and fragrant flowers. People changed the name of this mountain to Alishan in memory of this good descendant who gave up their lives.
