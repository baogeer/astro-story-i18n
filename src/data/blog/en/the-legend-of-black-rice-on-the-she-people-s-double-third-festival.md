 “March 3” for the Shes is really unique; it is Gumi's birthday.
On this day, every family in the She family ate traditional black rice, and the fragrance wafted through the front and back of the village.
Umihan is made by cooking soup from the leaves of wuhan leaf, then soaking the glutinous rice in this soup for half a day, scooping up the rice and steaming it in a container.
This kind of meal looked dark; it was really inconspicuous; however, it was fragrant, and I still wanted to eat it after eating it.
If you stir fry mountain game, shiitake mushrooms, fungus, etc., it will be even more delicious.
Why is Gumi's birthday on “March 3” for the Shes? And do you have to eat black rice? Speaking of which, there's another story.
It was March of a long, long time ago. Due to pests a few years ago, the harvest was not good. In addition, mountain owners raised taxes and raised taxes, and families of the She ethnic group cut off food.
The evil mountain owner stole even the grains.
The Shes people are suffering from hunger.
However, the lucky-hearted mountain owners were lucky and happy. Instead of lending grain, they released evil dogs and bitten the Shes people who came to ask for loans.
Everyone really couldn't stand it. One night, Lan Tianfeng, who was strong, climbed over the wall and entered the mountain owner's compound. They pry open the granary, carried bag by bag of grain crops back to the stockade, and planted overnight.
The next day, the owner of the mountain discovered that the granary had been stolen, and rushed like crazy to the village of the Shes people with a dozen thugs.
To save ordinary people from suffering, Lan Tianfeng stepped forward.
Just like that, he was imprisoned in a cell. This day was March 3.
In the dungeon, Lan Tianfeng was beaten all over his body. The mountain owner also complained to keep his mouth shut and not give Lan Tianfeng food to eat, hoping to starve him alive.
According to the news, the parents and elders of the She ethnic group went to visit the prison one after another. They used leftover grain seeds to make rice, cook it into rice, and knead it
Rice balls are sent to jail.
However, the rice balls fell into his mouth, and Lan Tianfeng didn't eat a single bite.
On this day, the person who went to the dungeon to deliver food was Chong Xiu, the best singer in Sheshan.
This smart girl thought of a way to deal with her abominable crooked remarks.
At noon, the girl Zhong Xiu was carrying a bamboo basket. The bamboo basket was filled with linen bags, and the food was delivered.
While looking at the girl with a crooked mouth, she opened the sackcloth bag and stuck her hand in.
At this point, I suddenly crooked my mouth and screamed, then my hands and feet jumped wildly.
However, the bamboo basket was small. After dancing for half a day, I still couldn't get my hands off it, and I was rolling all over my mouth in pain.
It turns out that the burlap bag contained dark, big, and poisonous mountain ants.
As soon as I was bitten by a mountain ant with a crooked mouth, I was poisoned the same day.
Since then, the She family has been picking black rice leaves from the mountain to cook for lunch. The cooked black rice looks like a bunch of mountain ants from afar. The prison guards, who were frightened by mountain ants, never dared to eat rice balls anymore.
Time passed day by day, and Lan Tianfeng ate black rice every day. Not only did the wound heal, but it also added a lot of strength. Therefore, in Sheshan, black rice was curable, but they talked about their illness.
By March 3 of the third year, Lan Tianfeng was finally rescued by the rebels and elected as the leader of the rebels.
In order for future generations to remember that the She family's rice was hard to come by, “March 3” was designated as Gummi's birthday.
From white flowers to dark and bright, it seems that they are wearing a flower costume. Therefore, Sheshan also says that they cook black rice to wear flower clothes for their birthday.
According to rumors, the dark blue burlap clothes that the Shes family usually likes to wear were learned from this.
Wearing this kind of clothing not only protects against sun poisoning, but mountain ants also don't dare to sting.
