 There is an area in the hinterland of Baektou Mountain called Herli. There are no graves after people died. After the coffin was buried, everything settled down, and a circle of ginseng was planted around the coffin at the same time. It wasn't until the day of the Ching Ming Festival three years later that a tall tomb was built at the graveyard and a grand funeral ceremony was held, and those carrots had already grown. Speaking of the origin of this custom, there is a strange story in it.
During the Kangxi era, this place was full of people. There was a local male owner named Erdun Guo Luo, and only one son named Han Qing. The family lived a happy and beautiful life.
On this day, Han Qing went into the mountains to hunt and accidentally harvested a wild Liupinye giant ginseng. This is a rare treasure! Han Qing immediately remembered that his dad was weak all year round. Having this baby ginseng will definitely prolong his life!
Han Qing flew home and Xing Chong chong shouted, “Dad, I found a large ginseng! Let's make you a sixty year old birthday present!”
When Guo Luo saw the ginseng, his eyes flashed brightly, and even shouted, “Baby! Baby!”
Han Qing handed over the ginseng to her father, went inside to meet her, and threw down a few chicken that had only been picked back. The mother hurriedly ordered the chef to cook Han Qing's favorite mountain chicken stew with fresh wild mushrooms.
At night, Han Qing suddenly had a stomach ache. I thought it was because he had eaten too many wild mushrooms. All fresh stuff was 3 percent toxic. When he woke up at night, he suddenly saw Dad's door creaking open. Dad bowed out to deliver the guests. Han Qing is very strange. It's already late at night, who will come? After taking a closer look, it turned out to be the county governor. I saw him smirking proudly and patted his father on the shoulder and said, “Guo Luo, you know current affairs. On your 30th birthday, you have given this treasure. Honghua and wealth are just around the corner!” Dad's face was full of smiles, and he kept on saying yes. Han Qing was so angry that it almost blew up! Dad actually wanted to give the Imperial Ginseng to the emperor!
It turns out that although Guo Luo is a flag person, Han Qing's mother is from Han. She is a young lady from a large family that Guo Luo sent troops to Jiangnan with Qing Ting back then and was rescued during the “Ten Days in Yangzhou.” Her whole family died in the massacre. This young lady has a strong personality. In addition to being grateful to be married to Guo Luo, she resolutely refused to let Guo Luo live in Beijing. She also gave her son a Chinese name that missed her hometown. Normally, she instilled in her son a lot of feelings for Jiangnan and hatred for Qing Ting. Han Qing has been influenced by her mother since childhood. Now hearing that her father wants to give his hard-earned baby to the imperial court to offer beauty and pampering, how can he stand it? At the time, I had to rush into theory. I also thought that my dad was old and sick. Making noise would hurt the elderly's heart. Han Qing thinks left and right; there it is! I'll just pick up my salary at the bottom of the pot.
Han Qing secretly took the giant ginseng and stayed at a friend's house for a month before returning home. Expecting that his father couldn't find the ginseng, he had to stop. He had military exploits back then, so the small county governor couldn't help him.
I didn't expect to see the house from afar. The main entrance was actually draped down, and there was a funeral at home! Han Qing was so frightened that he hurriedly ran into the room, only to see his mother in mourning. Seeing Han Qing, she pointed at him and said, “No filial son...” but passed out!
Han Qing and her sister shouted. After half a day, the lady woke up leisurely and had tears in her eyes and said, “You can kill your father!”
It turns out that after Han Qing fled with Bao Shen, his father couldn't find Han Qing everywhere. The mother made an idea to ask him to say that the treasure ginseng had been stolen by thieves, but his father said he would refuse to do anything, saying he was afraid that the county order would go on a big hunt and implicate the innocent, so he surrendered himself at the county government office, saying that he was old and had taken it by mistake. Never reported it to the imperial court.
When Emperor Kangxi received the news, Long Yan was furious, thinking that Guo Luo had merit in founding the country and gave him his death without harming his family. However, the emperor stated that the tombs of the Guoluo people cannot be erected within three years; people, horses, and livestock must trample on them before they can erect a monument.
Han Qing heard him cry bitterly. Only after crying did he say, “I've listened to my mother's teachings since I was a child, and I really didn't want to give this baby to the imperial court. Had my father not been greedy for wealth, he wouldn't have caused this big trouble!”
The mother sighed and said, “Is your dad greedy for wealth? You're wrong! The imperial court ordered that Manhan could not intermarry. My mother is from Southern Han. Even though she has been married to your father for many years, she can only keep your name private. I'm afraid you won't even get tired. Starting a family in the future will be a problem. Your dad only told me before he died that he offered his marriage to the imperial court to get an amnesty, so your mother and you can be decent people!”
Only then did Han Qing suddenly realise why his father, who always despised fame and fortune, was so interested in profit! Because they don't have a name, those eight flag children can't get any of the benefits they enjoyed as soon as they were born. This is one of the father's heartaches. He screamed loudly. The mother also said, “Your father also told us that you can't live a lifetime of hatred; otherwise, son, you will have a very difficult life! If you think about it, they're all obsessed with being a mother; it's your mother who mistook your father and son!”
When Han Qing arrived at the place where his father was buried, he saw a white field in a green valley. He buried his treasure ginseng there with tears. Strangely enough, that baby ginseng has been out of the soil for a long time. The ginseng seeds have dried up, but they are soon unearthed and germinated. By the day of Qingming three years later, a large patch of ginseng had already grown on that flat ground. They all grew very strong, and it was nice to look at.
Han Qing led the family to solemnly build a large tomb for Guo Luo. He also had no idea to establish his own name and enjoy a salary, and wait for his mother to grow old in the mountains with peace of mind.
Because of Emperor Kangxi's reign, his ban has never been lifted. Since then, people in the Hurley area have died, and their descendants have all planted a few ginseng seedlings in front of their graves. Three years later, on Qingming day, the tomb was raised again, and the ceremony was much more solemn than the first funeral.
