 Legend has it that Zhang Tianshi's niece is 27 or 8 years old and hasn't gone out yet.
At the time of the New Year, Zhang Tianshi walked to her sister's house. As soon as her niece saw her uncle coming, she hurriedly poured tea. After pouring tea, she went out. Zhang Tianshi asked his sister, “How old is my niece this year?” His sister said, “Not too young, 27 or 8 years old.” Zhang Tianshi asked, “Do you have a grandmother's house?” My sister said, “Yes, in Dongxiang.” Zhang Tianshi said, “I have a grandmother's house, then why don't you send her out”? My sister said, “There is a fairy in Higashiyama. Whichever family marries a daughter-in-law, the fairy goes to whichever house to make trouble. “The new daughter-in-law must sleep with the fairy for three nights, and the groom will have to eat at the end of the day.” As soon as Zhang Tianshi heard it, she said, “Sister, after a few days, you can find someone to look good about her life and send my niece out. I have my own way.” As a result, Zhang Tianshi's sister told the man that they were looking for someone to look forward to their life. After a few days, Zhang Tianshi bought many things and sneaked around to fill his niece's suitcase. Then I pulled out nine belts from the cloth bag and gave them to her sister, saying, “Sister, on the day your niece went out, you asked her to wear one pair of pants, tie a belt, wear nine pairs of pants (in total), and tie all nine belts together. The inside of the two red bundles and the outside of the seven blue bundles. During the day, the bridegroom used red paper to cut lots of money, mixed with bran, dates, and peanuts and sprinkled all over the ground. Don't close the door to your new house when it gets dark, don't be afraid when fairies come; everyone else just sleep behind closed doors.” Zhang Tianshi left after making his statement.
On this day, Zhang Tianshi's niece went out. Since I haven't had a daughter here for many years, and my daughter has married a daughter-in-law, there are a lot of people watching the activity during the day. As soon as it got dark, everyone closed their doors and closed their homes. I'm afraid the fairies are here to cause trouble. With even more effort, fairies actually came from Higashiyama. I didn't go to the second den; I went straight to Zhang Tianshi's niece's grandmother's house. As soon as I saw that the door to my new house was open, I was happy to go inside. When the fairy arrived at the new house to take a look, the house was still lit. The new daughter-in-law sat in front of the lamp and looked at the light in a daze, as if thinking about something. The fairy came to her new daughter-in-law and kissed her without asking to hold her horizontally or vertically. Zhang Tianshi's niece was frightened and screamed. I heard the new daughter-in-law shouting loudly in the house. They were all so frightened that they all shrunk into a circle. They didn't dare to squeak. When the fairy finished her mouth, she said to the new daughter-in-law, “Let's go to bed?” The new daughter-in-law was so frightened that she couldn't say she was sleeping, nor was she afraid to say she couldn't sleep. When the fairy saw that the new daughter-in-law didn't squeak, she took the new daughter-in-law to bed and clung to the fairy to loosen the new daughter-in-law's belt. The new daughter-in-law didn't dare to resist, so she fiddled with the fairy at will. The fairy unfastens a belt and takes off a pair of pants; then unfastens the belt and takes off another pair of pants. The first and second belts that were untied turned into two blue dragons flying to the door and stuck them on both sides of the door to block the door. Article 3 blocked the window, and section 4, 5, 6, and 7 blocked the eyes of the four windows. When rules 8 and 9 were removed, these two belts suddenly turned into two fire dragons, and they caught the fairy's cry and burned. The fairy was screamed by the fire dragon. Wherever it ran, there were blue dragons clenching their claws to grab it, frightening the new daughter-in-law to lie on the bed and shiver with a cover (quilt) covering her head. After about half an hour of touching, the two fire dragons burned the fairy to death. At this point, the new daughter-in-law opened the cover and took a look. She saw that the house had a bunch of fairy bones at the door.
At dawn, I called out my in-laws, little uncle, little girl, and groom. The family hurriedly grabbed the new daughter-in-law and asked for a brief question. The new daughter-in-law said to the crowd, “The fairy has been killed by my uncle's spell.” The crowd didn't believe it. When they arrived at the new house, the house really had a bunch of fairy bones. Asked what kind of spell it was, the new daughter-in-law said, “When I arrived, my uncle gave me nine belts. These nine belts were nine dragons. Seven were blue dragons, two dragons, six blue dragons held eyes on doors and windows, and two fire dragons caught the fairy burning, and just killed the fairies.” As soon as the crowd heard it, they hurriedly bought firecrackers to celebrate the victory of the Burning Fairy. From then on, every household dared to send their daughter out and marry a daughter-in-law for their son.
Since then, the tradition of marrying a daughter-in-law to set off firecrackers, stick green dragons, glue windows, and spread bran has spread to this day.
