Waniwu Village, Wachang Township, Yangyi County, has an inconspicuous small Yi village. The name of this village is Chou Keli. There is a small river called the Chou Keli River next to the village of Chou Keli. “Chou Keli” is a Yi translation and means “a place where ginger can be planted.” The creek originates from Baizhu Mountain. The dense vegetation of Baizhu Mountain makes the river clear all year round and never dries up. Every spring and summer season, the trees are buzzing, the water is covered with moss, small fish are shuttling through the pool, and tadpoles are gathered along the shore. This is a natural amusement park for dolls with bare butts. Both sides of the creek are shaded with trees, fertile land, and terraced fields. It is a storehouse for rice and grain. It can be seen that the Choukeli River Valley has always been a rich place and a place to live peacefully. At the entrance of the river flowing into the Yangjiang River, it crashed into a large sand dam called Hemenkou Dashaba. There is a ferry called Hemenkou Dashaba Ferry. It is the only ferry crossing for tens of miles up and down the Yangjiang River.
An old man in his seventies has been ferrying for over 10 years at the Dashaba ferry crossing. He lives alone in a riverside stone shed. He has no wife or children. He has a big beard all year round, his hair is disheveled, and his clothes are worn out. He is almost patched up, but he is very clean and tidy. It's been a long time, and people don't even mention his real name; they only know that his last name is Zuo, and they all call him Zuo Yu. The left shuttle bus rarely communicates with the outside world and rarely talks. Every day, it silently picks up guests, never cares about rewards, and lives on the oil, salt, and rice that pedestrians give them as they please.
On this day, Zuo Baifu was alone in a shiwo shed while watching the ever-flowing river water secretly hurt himself. Recently, I've often had back pain, numb hands and feet, chest tightness, shortness of breath, and a constant cough. I feel like I'm not as strong as before on the raft. Thought: I lost my old bones, but past pedestrians would have to cross the river to take a ferry! At this point, he thought of Yang Chengjiu, a young Yi guy from Chou Keli Village. What a nice young man. He has a strong body, a flexible mind, knows the cold and the heat, and has a kind heart. I've been following myself for a few years, and my raft skills have improved a lot, and I'm even better at fishing in the water. It would be great if he had him take over the ferry!
Just thinking about it, Yang Chengjiu broke in with a slice of bacon, put the meat on the small stone steps where oil, salt, and chai rice were stored in the shiwo shed. He jokingly said, “Dad, please order meat and teeth at the festival. He also asked the teacher to take me on a trip. I want to go across the river to Aketang to catch the street.”
Zuo Baifu gently patted Yang Chengjiu on the arm and pretended to be angry and said, “Lazy guy, the old man is full of energy; he can get by on his own! Do you want me to send it?”
“Just cross the river, but after crossing the river, you have to return the bamboo raft!”
“Oh yeah, yeah, I lost my head.”
The two got up and got on the raft. The left-hand ferry sat on the raft, and Yang Chengjiu held up the raft with a pole. When the raft reached the center of the river, the left shuttle suddenly had a severe cough. His face turned white, and then vomited a few sips of blood.
Yang Seung-jiu was frightened by the sudden change. He hurriedly passed by and hugged the left shuttle: “Dad, what did you say? What do you say?”
The left ferry held up strong and said, “Ah Jiu, I'm like a toad stepped on by an old buffalo — everything is wrong all over my body! It's been over half a year since I've been sick, and I haven't gotten any better after taking a few doses of medicine that Lang Zhong caught. It seems like I can't do my ferry work anymore. Unfortunately, I have no family and no one will replace me. Alas! It doesn't matter if my bamboo raft stops, but passers-by on both sides of the Strait don't know how many wrong ways to go around...” I haven't finished yet. Another severe cough, and a few bloody sputum.
Yang Chengjiu helped the left ferry lay flat on the bamboo raft and said, “Dad, you can take good care of your illness and feel free to hand over the raft pole to me. I will definitely not let down the pedestrians in the past, let alone let alone disappoint your old family!”
I was overjoyed when I heard about Zuo Baifu. He kept his eyes on Yang Seung-jiu without leaving for half a day. He had a happy smile on his face: watching him grow up and play with him on a bamboo raft. The kid is smart and knows the rules, and people brag about it. Furthermore, my raft pole was also handed down from his dad. He is my favorite pole taker for a long time, so handing over the ferry to him is the most appropriate and reassuring!
In this way, Yang Chengjiu became a ferry operator at the Dasaba ferry crossing at the entrance of the river.
A fixed ferry, a passenger flowing through the water. At the Dashaba ferry crossing, Yang Chengjiu met many people and experienced many strange things.
Just in the spring of last year, a Huideng Hui merchant went to the west bank of the Yangjiang River to buy a head of Huanggu veal and was ready to sell it in Menghua City. This cow had a grumpy temper when it was on the raft. No matter how you dragged, pulled, pumped, or knocked it, it didn't want to get on the raft. It held its head strong and stubborn. It had its front hooves stuck deep in the sand, and didn't move at all. Seeing that their business was about to come to nothing, the merchants were in a hurry and sweated, and begged Yang Chengjiu to find a solution. Seeing this battle, Yang Seung-jiu was also in a hurry. He put down his raft pole and rolled up his sleeves and said, “I'll try it out.” I saw him hugging the front of the scalper with his left hand and hooking the back of the scalper with his right hand. Aktang Street Day is the busiest day at the ferry crossing. On this day, there were a lot of people passing by at the ferry, and they all scrambled to get on the raft to cross the river. Early in the morning, a man who looked like a boss in a long shirt and a wool hat came to the riverside from the direction of Menghua with four or five companions. The first wave passed through the river. After crossing the river, a group of people sat and chatted under a large green tree by the river. Seemingly, they were in no hurry to rush. From time to time, they gave pointers and comments, which seemed like they were appreciating Yang Chengjiu's ability to sail a raft. Yang Seung-jiu noticed them a long time ago, but he had no time to take care of them. He helped the elderly, held children, carried goods, and continued to sail back and forth to deliver customers. After two hours of hard work, everyone came and went to the street. This group of people had to get on the raft again, saying they wanted to go back to Menghua City.
The “sheep felt hat” went on a bamboo raft with the help of his entourage. Only then did Yang Chengjiu take a moment to take a serious look at him. I saw that this person was medium in stature, with light eyebrows, and a round face. He showed kindness in his wealth, and wisdom was hidden in kindness. He sat on the raft speechless, always smiling, and closely watched Yang Seung-jiu's every move of his hand and every step. When the raft reached the center of the river, he had one look. His followers gathered at the same time to the left side of the bamboo raft. The raft tilted violently to the side, and the bamboo raft continued to sway along with a row of big waves, creating a dangerous situation. Yang Chengjiu almost fell down one by one. The “wool felt hat” got up on the plane and stuck out Yang Chengjiu's soft hole under the elbow, which is the key point of his right hand. I saw Yang Seung-jiu flashing his body to the side. Judging from every move of the “Felt Hat,” Yang Seung-jiu knew that he had no ill intentions; he just wanted to test his skills. At this point, “Sheepskin Hat” suddenly softened his smile and said with a dark face: “I've been traveling south and crossing the north for many years. I don't know how many ferries I've been to. I can count your raft being the slowest. At this speed, you just crossed over me, and I wouldn't pay for the raft!” Yang Seung-jiu looked at his unstrained and unflattering tone, and guessed that he must have revealed a leg in the clouds — an extraordinary foot (horn). Saying that the raft is slow is false; testing your own strength is real. I saw him hold up the raft rod in his hand and hit it deep into the bottom of the river. He only showed half a foot long pole, and immediately picked up double boxing: “Let a few of you pull this raft rod. If you can pull up this raft, I won't take a penny of the raft money. I still have to go to the biggest restaurant on Aketang Street to ask you for a drink. If they can't pull it up, just leave the money on the raft after disembarking. We old Yi people are straight poles and don't like to be curved!” “Felt Hat” laughed and ordered his entourage to pull the rod with force. No matter how the water rocked the raft, the rod kept floating. After half a day of twisting, they didn't pull the rod out, and they almost knocked over the raft. As soon as the “wool felt hat” saw it swing its hands, the followers stopped. Yang Chengjiu glanced at the raft, holding the tip of the pole vertically with one hand and gently lifting it. After disembarking, the “sheep felt hat” pulled Yang Chengjiu to a large rock and sat down, smiling and said, “We are Song Jiajie, the mayor of Menghua County. I've heard that the brave warriors are smart, extremely powerful, and kind and honest. Seeing them today certainly lives up to their name!”
Yang Chengjiu's eyes widened when he heard that the “wool felt hat” in front of him was actually the mayor of County Song. He couldn't blink for half a day. He is loyal and kind, helpful, suppresses evil and promotes good, and uses virtue as the treasure of his family. He likes fishing and ferrying. While fishing, he found dead bodies by the water.
