 In the Jiangnan region of our country, every time it is time for New Year's Eve dinner, every household must close their doors; they cannot speak loudly or knock on dishes. After eating New Year's Eve dinner, clean up the tableware and then open the door. This is called making money behind closed doors and opening doors.
Legend has it that this practice is to trick people into stealing luggage.
It is said that on the last day of every year, Emperor Yu Huang had to learn about the living conditions of the people, so he sent Li Xiafan to check the public situation. Tieguai Li is one of the Eight Immortals. He is a lame person called Huaxian. Therefore, when he was eating New Year's Eve dinner on earth, he went down the street with a basket to beg everywhere.
After asking for food, Tieguai Li brought what he had bought to Grandpa Jade to see. Whose family is poor. Whose family is rich, you'll know as soon as you see it. According to this, Grandpa Jade made the rich suffer disasters several times a year; don't get too rich; the poor let him get rich a few times and not be too poor.
The incident slowly spread to the world. After learning about the situation, a savvy businessman soon thought of a way to deal with it. When it was time to eat New Year's Eve dinner, this family kept their doors tightly closed, and no one in the family was allowed to speak loudly. When Tieguai Li came to ask for food, he opened the door, and there was nothing on the table. Tieguai Li took a look and thought that this family was too poor to even eat New Year's Eve dinner, so he was very compassionate and quietly placed a few gold ingots at the door of this family and left. Just like that, the family is getting richer.
There are no impermeable walls in the world. Other families also saw the reason why his family was rich, and they all learned along. Later, Tieguai Li saw that every household closed their doors to eat New Year's Eve dinner, and he knew that all his explorations had been observed by people, so they stopped coming to the world to ask for food to check the rich and the poor.
However, the custom of closing the door and eating New Year's Eve dinner has spread and continues to this day.
