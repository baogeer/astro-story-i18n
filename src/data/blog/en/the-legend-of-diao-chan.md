 Diao Chan, one of the four greatest beauties in ancient China, is not recorded in ancient books, but is often described in literary works, and is one of the important figures in the dispute between the Three Kingdoms in the late Han Dynasty. Luo Guanzhong's “The Romance of the Three Kingdoms” further described and portrayed Diao Chan, and had a great influence. Folklore is particularly moving, and it has become a well-known “Chinese and English woman” known to women and children. According to scholar Meng Fanren's research, the novel's character is Diao Chan, whose surname is Ren, in the small letters Hongchang, was born in Mu'er Village, Jiuyuan County, Bingzhou County. At the age of 15, he took charge of the Diao Chan (hat for a Han Dynasty servant official) worn by the courtier, and has since changed his name to Diao Chan. The court was in turmoil at the end of the Han Dynasty, and Diao Chan left the palace and was taken as a righteous daughter by Situ Wang Yun. Soon Dong Zhuo had exclusive power. Wang Yun used Dong and Lu to make Diao Chan carry out a “serial plan,” which finally prompted Lu Bu to kill Dong Zhuo and make a merit. After that, Diao Chan became Lu Bu's concubine. Lu Bu of Bai Men Lou died, and Cao Cao repeated the “serial plan” with the Taoyuan brothers, so he gave it to Guan Yu. In order not to harm the Taoyuan brothers, Diao Chan “led her neck and prayed for execution.” She was protected by Guan Yu and escaped and became a nun. After Cao Cao learned about it, he arrested Diao Chan, and Diao Chan resolutely killed himself by shooting a sword.
Diao Chan's hometown is in Muzhi Village, three kilometers southeast of Xinzhou City, on the way to Yuwang Cave from Taiyuan or Xinzhou. Kishiba Village was originally rich in fungus, hence the name Fungus Village. Later, it was renamed Mushiba wood because a thousand-year-old Ganoderma lucidum plant was discovered under an acacia tree in the village. Rumor has it in the village that the peach apricots in the village stopped blooming as early as three years before Diao Chan was born. It is still difficult for peach and apricot trees to survive. This is because Diao Chan looks shy. The village originally had a street archway, front hall, back hall, Wang Yun Street, Diao Chan Theatre, and Diao Chan's tomb. Time passed, and these buildings were all in ruins, and the tombs were razed to the ground during the havoc. Ancient bricks, stone components, copper coins, and ceramic fragments have often been unearthed in ruins, all of which are said to be related to Diao Chan.
The current tourist attraction is called Diao Chan Cemetery, which was recently restored and built by the townspeople on the original site of the cemetery. The cemetery is located in the southwest of the village. It covers an area of more than 4,000 square meters. It is surrounded by wavy dragon-shaped walls with yellow tiles on a red background. It is bright and dazzling in the vast expanses of the Mai Sea. The “Diao Chan Cemetery” plaque hangs on the eaves. On both sides, there are “Closing the Moon and Embracing Flowers; Endurance of humiliation and danger makes one look up to them” Golden Moon Airline. There is a Baiyue Pavilion and Fengyi Pavilion in the North Courtyard of Ling District. There is a bluestone tomb stand at the back, and there is a monument with a statue of Diao Chan in front of the stage. Under the dynamic streamer, Diao Chan walks casually and gracefully, and still has the appearance of a “closed moon.” The South Courtyard built 20 antique buildings and turned it into the “Diao Chan Color and Plastic Museum”, reflecting the earth-shattering life of Diao Chan “not hesitating for thousands of gold men, why be afraid of dangerous people”.
According to the townspeople legend, after the three Taoyuan brothers gained strength, they sent Diao Chan back to his hometown and buried him here after his death. It was also said that Diao Chan fought his own sword, and Guan Yu escorted the body back to her hometown for burial after learning about it. Therefore, there is a statue of Yu in the back hall, and there is a stage in front of the hall showing Diao Chan acting, all in return for Guan Yu's refusal to kill and escort him. Also, Zhonghuo Village in the southeast of Dingxiang County is Lu Bu's hometown. There are folklore legends such as “Huo Qingquan,” “The Smart Catch of Red Hare,” and “Crooked Neck Tree,” all of which are related to Lu Bu. Therefore, there is a popular saying, “There are no good girls in Xinzhou, there are no good men in Dingxiang”, which says that because of Diao Chan and Lu Bu, Xinzhou has never been able to give birth to good-looking women, nor can Dingxiang give birth to handsome men.
