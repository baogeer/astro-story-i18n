 The extraordinary quality of Longjing tea has always been praised by tea people. Gao Lian of the Ming Dynasty said, “The springs of the West Lake have the most tiger running, the largest of the two mountains, the tea of the two mountains, and the best is Longjing.” Longjing Tea Tiger Spring is famous as the best in Hangzhou. “I've always had good tea like a beautiful person”. Song dynasty tea master Su Dongpo also left famous quotes about Longjing tea from time immemorial. When Qianlong went down to Jiangnan, they drank Longjing at the Hugong Temple at the foot of Shifeng Mountain, and after drinking it, they were full of praise, so the 18 tea trees in front of the temple were sealed as “imperial tea,” and they are still full of life. Longjing is the name of the spring, known as Longhong in ancient times. It is located on the left side of the old Longjing Temple more than 1,700 years ago. Legend has it that when a well was dug during the Zhengde period in the Ming Dynasty, a large stone was dug out from the bottom of the well. It is shaped like a dragon, hence the name Longjing.
Over the past few thousand years, there have been many legends and stories about Longjing tea, making Longjing tea even more mysterious.
Once upon a time, Longjing was a small, desolate village. In the mountainside, there are about ten families scattered sparsely. There is an old aunt living in a broken, ventilated cottage on the side of the village. The old aunt had no children. She was alone. She was old. She couldn't go up the mountain or go down the ground; she could only take care of the 18 old tea trees at the back of the house.
My aunt is a nice person. Although she has had a very difficult life, she always leaves some tea leaves every year to cook pot tea every day, and set up two benches under the pergola at the entrance to quench their thirst when passing down the mountain.
One year on New Year's Eve, it snowed heavily, and the neighbors on the left and right houses all sold some New Year's goods to prepare for the New Year. My aunt's family is really poor, and the rice bowl is almost empty. Other than a few old tea women left in the pot, there's nothing else left. However, she still followed the old rules. She woke up early in the morning, grabbed the tea leaves and put it in the pot, heated up, and sat in front of the stove to make tea. Overhearing a “squeak,” the door of the cottage was pushed open. An old man came in, and snowflakes fell all over his body. The old aunt hurriedly stood up and said, “Big brother, it's windy and snowy on this mountain. Hurry up and sit in the house.”
The old man dusted off the snowflakes on his body, walked into the house, and while burning the fire in the oven, he said to the old aunt, “Aunt, what did you burn in the pot?”
“Cook tea in a pot!”
“Today is New Year's Eve, tomorrow is the Lunar New Year. Everyone is busy preparing the Three Sacrifice Gifts. How do you make tea?”
The old aunt sighed and said, “Alas, I'm poor, and I can't afford the Three Sacrifice Gifts. I have to cook pot tea every day to make it easier for passers-by. ”
The old man listened and laughed: “Not poor, not poor, you still have a baby at your door.”
The old aunt heard it very strange. She stuck out and looked outside. Except for the two old benches under the fence, a broken stone in the corner of the wall, and a pile of old garbage in the broken stone, everything was still the same. Where did the baby come from?
“Yo, how could I take your baby for nothing? If you want to, sell it to me, I'll go get someone to pick it up.” When the old man finished speaking, he braved the heavy snow and left. The old lady looked at the broken millstone and thought to herself, the millstone is so dirty, how can they move it! They then removed the old garbage in the dustpan and buried it in the roots of the 18 flower tea trees at the back of the house. I also went to Longjing to bring a bucket of clean water, brush the broken millstone clean, and pour the washed sewage on the roots of the old tea tree. She had just refreshed the millstone, and the old man brought someone along. When he looked at the door, he shouted, “Oops, what about baby? Oops, what about baby?”
The old aunt grew more confused, pointing at the broken millstone and saying, “This -- isn't it easy to place?”
“Oh, where did you get the stuff inside?”
“I dumped it on the root of the tea tree in the back of the house.”
When the old man walked around the house, he saw that was true. “Too bad, alas, this treasure broken millstone is in that pile of old garbage. Now that you have buried it under the roots of the tea tree, let's complete these 18 old tea trees.” When he finished speaking, he led the person away.
After New Year's Eve and New Year's Eve, spring will soon arrive. This year, the 18 old tea trees at the back of Mom's house actually produced dense, bright green shoots. The tea leaves that were harvested were fine, tender, and fragrant.
Neighbors saw that the old aunt's tea tree was growing so well, so everyone cut down bamboo and harvested grains. Using the seeds of these 18 tea trees, they planted tea trees on the top of a mountain near and far. Year by year, more and more, more and more prosperous. Later, tea trees were planted all over the mountains in the Longjing area.
“Longjing tea” is famous all over the world because the tea leaves produced in this area are fine, tender, and fragrant, and it tastes particularly beautiful when brewed.
Until now, tea farmers have said that the 18 dwarf tea trees behind the old aunt's house are the ancestors of “Longjing tea.”
