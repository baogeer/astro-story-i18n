 Feed your dog first when you try a new meal. Here's another old legend about why you should feed your dog first:
Since the birth of heaven and earth, people have relied only on wild fruits to fill their hunger; they have no food to eat, let alone fragrant white rice. A family raised a big white dog. Seeing that the owner was yellow, skinny, hungry and frozen, they felt very sympathetic, and always wanted to repay the owner for their feeding. One day, I had a dream where a god told it that a type of rice grows far, far away in the sky. It's very tasty to cook and eat after removing the shell, but people can't get to that place; only dogs can swim in the water and can get the seeds back. Ask if the big white dog wants to go? The dog woke up and told the owner about this dream. The dog said, “The road is so far, it's not convenient for me to carry around. I'm hungry to pick up some human poop to eat, and thirsty to lick some water from Qinggou to drink. I found rice seeds and came back; if I couldn't find rice seeds, I never came back.” After talking, the dog hit the road.
The big white dog runs, runs, regardless of the cold and freezing weather, and is even less afraid of the hot sun. One day, a large river blocked its path. Without hesitation, it jumped into the rough river, constantly moving its hands forward and slamming its feet backwards. It appeared all the time in the undulating peaks. In the end, a wave pushed the white dog to the shore. The white dog shook the water on his body and embarked on a new journey. He went through thousands of mountains and valleys, and he never saw the rice he had in his dream. But it didn't get discouraged; it also crossed 98 rivers, and couldn't find any trace of rice. One day, the big white dog came to the endless sea, and it hesitated. Can it swim past such a wide sea on its own? But I also remembered that my belief in death encouraged me, and I had no trouble jumping into the ocean. It swam, swam, and finally swam to the seaside. After taking a breather at the seaside, it dragged its tired body back on the path of searching for rice.
One day, in an oasis on the coast, it found a golden yellow plant with fruit spikes falling all over the branches. The big white dog tried it with its mouth. It was fragrant and sweet. It was so happy that it finally found rice. However, how to bring it back has become a problem. It looked at the rice for half a day and found that there was something plush on the rice husk that just stuck to dog hair. The big white dog was happy to roll around in the rice field. His body was covered with bright yellow rice, and returned to the seaside majestically and vigorously like a triumphant soldier. It swam across the sea, over mountains, over 99 rivers, 99 rivers, and 99 rivers, and finally returned to its owner's home.
At this point, the dog is exhausted and doesn't talk anymore; it just keeps wagging its tail. The owner finally found three golden rice seeds in the dog's tail and successfully planted them in a paddy field, making it an indispensable big white rice for two meals a day for humans. In order to thank the big white dog for bringing rice seeds to humans, every New Year's 30th night, you have to feed the dog first, and wait until the dog is full before eating.
