 In the southeast of Tengxian County, there is a mountain called Fushan. There is a group of blue stones at the top of the mountain. Seen from a distance, it looks like a dragon lying on its side. There are large rocks standing all around it. At first glance, they all look like foxes singing there. If I had to say, this is still a moving story.
In very ancient times, a gray-white fox was active along the East Sea. Over time, it became an expert. It wanted to ascend to heaven and become immortal. It broke through the South Tianmen Gate in the clouds. It was caught by the Heavenly Soldier and crushed it under a mountain by the East China Sea. After a short time, Dragon King wanted to expand his sea frontier, so he asked Jiro God to move this mountain to the southeast of Tengxian County.
The fox that came with the mountain settled in a ten-mile-long gourd house and continued to live its dream of becoming a fairy. I don't know how many years of practice, and not only can I summon wind and rain, but I can also transform into 18 transformations. It sometimes becomes a blank student, breaks into houses, and destroys young girls. Sometimes she becomes a beautiful woman and teases folk lads. It also brought in many foxes, formed a team, and went to the village down the mountain to steal chickens and herd sheep and trample on melons and fruits.
All of this, the Fox Demon Gan, was seen in the eyes of the Yulong, who lived in the sky above the mountains. It wanted to get rid of the fox demon. It flooded the gourd cover many times, but the fox demon had so much magic that it couldn't drown it.
One year, during a major drought, all crops and trees dried their leaves, and most of the living things on the ground died out. The crops over Teng County were barely able to survive because Yulong tried their best to save them. In the gourd holder, the fox demon performed spells. It rained on time, the mountains and forests were dense, and wild fruits bent the branches. Some wild animals run for gourds one after another. Some had nothing to eat, were hungry, and ventured to pick wild fruit, but most of them were eaten by wild animals, and people had to wait for the crops in the area to survive.
The fox demon also colluded with the ant spirit to eat all the crops in the field. At one point, ants flew all over the sky. It won't be a few days, let alone the crops in the field. There wasn't even any hay left in the house. Yulong was in a hurry. He drove to Dragon King in the East China Sea and invited Lao Dragon King to exterminate ants with rain. The old dragon king said, “No water can cure ants; only fairy water in the Heavenly Garden can do it. If you want to get rid of the Fox Demon, you can't do it without the Town Demon Orb. This town's Demon Orb is stored in the Imperial Treasury and is heavily guarded, so it's hard to get your hands on it. Besides, once the Demon Orb explodes, you won't be able to save yourself.” Yulong made up his mind to destroy his bones, break into the Heavenly Court, steal orbs, absorb fairy water, and save the people.
Yulong bid farewell to King Lao Long, and drove to Tianting. When I saw it in the sky, I was astonished: outside the treasure house, there were three layers of soldiers, three layers, each armed with weapons; all around the pool, there were three layers of water and three layers of fire, with no ventilation on all sides. When Yu Long saw that he couldn't get started, he had to use a trick to get out of the mountain and set fire to Nantianmen Gate. Taking advantage of the chaos in the sky, they stole the town's demon jewels, leaped into the pool to soak up fairy water, jumped into the clouds, and went straight to the gourd set.
The jade dragon spit fairy water to the earth, and ants turned into feces and fell one after another. The yellowed crops turned green, and the leaves rolled up in a tube unfurled. Yulong once again flooded the gourd set with fairy water, and the beast was drowned by fairy water. The fox demon saw it and drove out of the gourd set. Yulong was in a tight pursuit, and a slaughter unfolded in the air.
I saw a cloud of black clouds and a cloud of white clouds flying up and down in the air. After more than a dozen rounds of fighting, Yulong had many injuries, making it difficult to support, and used all his strength to shoot the Zhen Demon Pearl at the Fox Demon. The bead hit the fox demon's head. There was a loud noise and flames, and the fox demon turned into gravel and fell down. Yulong also felt that his body was sinking, lost consciousness, and fell to the top of the mountain.
Later, the fox demon turned into those big stones at the top of the mountain, and the jade dragon also became a stone dragon riding on top of the fox mountain.
