 Legend has it that more than 1,400 years ago, Liang Guo and Wei Guo competed for Shouyang (present-day Shou County), fought for the Chinese and Wei military Ma Lueqiang, and took the first place in Shouyang City. Shouyang is a stronghold between Huainan, Jingzhou, and Xiangyang. The moat around the city is more than three feet tall, and the moat outside the wall is also three feet wide and one foot deep, making it difficult to defend. Liang Guobing and horse siege to the city repeatedly failed, and there were countless deaths and injuries every time the siege was innumerable. Emperor Liang Wu and Xiao Yan continued to worry about this all day long.
At this point, Chen Wu, a native of Wei Guojiang, proposed to Xiao Yan to choose a narrow section along the Huai River, build a dam on the La River, use water as an army, flood Shouyang, and present a detailed map. The map shows that the dam construction site runs from Fushan in Xuyi County in Fengyang Province in the south (present-day Fushan in Mingguang City) to Sihong Mountain in the north. Xiao Yan accepted Chen Wu's suggestion. The road is 9 miles long, with a width of more than 140 feet, a width of 50 feet, and a height of 20 feet. More than 200,000 people were invested, and the construction of the dam, which took 18 months, began earth-shattering on October 10, 514. This is the famous “Fushan Weir” from ancient times.
Initially, Emperor Liang Wu sent two generals, Zu Heng, a civil servant, and Chen Chengbo, a water official, to arrange implementation. Through field inspections, Zu and Chen Erwei all believed that although the Huai River was narrow, the Huai River drifted rapidly and the sand along the coast was loose, making it difficult to fortify the weir. Building a tough weir was an act that hurt the people's wealth, and they came together for a long time, and urged Xiao Yan to abandon the dam construction plan. Emperor Liang Wu's Xiao Yan was furious when she heard it said, “Dripping water can be turned into a mound, and a shovel of earth can become a mountain. There is no reason why soldiers have come to stop it, and water comes to the dam. It's clear that the two have different intentions.” To show her determination to build a dam, Hsiao Yan prevented anyone from raising any objections, so the left and right guards tied Zu and Chen and beheaded them in public. After killing Zu and General Chen, no one dared to raise objections. Xiao Yan also sent his generals to recruit civilians from the front lines of Xuzhou and Yangzhou, drawing 5 people from every 20 households. In total, more than 150,000 people were recruited, plus more than 200,000 soldiers. Whether it's the heat or the cold, the peasants keep digging for earthen dams day and night. As Lei Dam approaches, the river becomes more and more ferocious. As soon as the peasants fell down the dirt, they were swept dozens of feet out of the raging river, and they couldn't get together for a while. At the time, some people proposed to use iron to make the world's big pot. The pot contains soil and sinks to the bottom of the water, which can block the river. As a result, Xiao Yan sent someone to collect 100,000 kg of pig iron and cast it into an iron pot and sink it into the water. The results still didn't help. Hsiao Yan was in such a hurry that she couldn't close her eyes, couldn't eat anything, and was irritable. She often took charge of the supervisors herself, and when she was slightly unhappy, she beheaded and thrown into the river. Coupled with the cold and freezing weather and exhaustion all day long, countless people froze to death, starved to death, and died of illness. At this point, there were two craftsmen, a pair of twin brothers. The older brother's name was Big Ryu Sang, and the younger brother's name was Koyanagi Sang. It's not a problem if it continues like this. In order to protect the other people, she took the initiative to suggest to Xiao Yan that they would jump into the confluence, nail it into a “well” shaped wooden stake, and quickly fill it with earth and rock, so that the dam could be brought together. Hsiao Yan sent people to cut down trees, build mountains, and quarry. Everything was properly prepared. Liu's twin brothers jumped arm in arm into the ferocious river. I only heard a loud “thump”. The water in the Huai River, which was as strong as a jiaolong, actually diverted the water's head and rushed back. Many villagers and soldiers on the shore took advantage of the gap to fill the dam with pre-prepared stumps, stones, and earthen bags all at the dam entrance. Soon, the dam was closed. It happened to be April 10, the 15th year of Leung Tien Kan (516 AD), and it took a full 18 months. To commemorate the twin brothers Da Liu Xiang and Xiao Liu Xiang, many willow trees were planted along the dam, and a town along the river in Sihong was called Da Liu Xiang, and a village and town along the river east from Fushan Mountain was called Little Liu Xiang. Later, when they shouted, they were called Daliu Alley and Xiaoliu Alley.
After the Fushan dam was built, the middle and upper reaches of the Huai River covered hundreds of miles of vast ocean. Suyang City was flooded, but only an empty city was flooded. As early as the Dye Army built the dam, Wei Junshou General Li Chong gradually evacuated the city's residents from the mountains. Coupled with the flood season of the Huai River, the water level skyrocketed. The dam collapsed in September of that year, and the water broke down like a fierce beast. The sound of high flood peaks pushed forward. The sound of thunder was heard hundreds of miles away. Villages along the Huai River were destroyed. Nearly 100,000 hectares of farmland were swept away by the floods.
