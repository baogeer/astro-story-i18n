 There is a mountain between Zhang Baling Town and Jiashan Ji Township. Now the mountain is called “Guanshan”. Legend has it that a long time ago, this mountain was called “Shuangmen Mountain”. It is said that Dongmenling and Ximenling are originally the same mountain. After about a few tens of meters, it was divided into east and west ridges. The two mountains are opposite each other. A mountain gate with a width of tens of feet is formed between the two walls. Looking from south to north, you can see Mount Laojia directly opposite the mountain gate. Later, the two walls came together, and the two mountains became one, and Laojiashan can no longer be seen at the foot of Nanshan Mountain. As a result, people changed the name of this mountain to “Guanshan”. Why did Shuangmen Mountain close the mountain gate? There's a legend here.
Legend has it that a long time ago, a family lived at the south gate of Shuangmen Mountain. The young couple have a son who is over four years old. The man's last name is Shan Liang. I don't know what the woman's last name is. People who know her from near and far call her breast name “Red Mountain.” Shan Liang and Shan Lihong are both loyal, kind, and honest mountain people. They have lived on mu mountainside for a few years by cultivating mountain passes. Spring planting and autumn harvesting; barely coping with the small days. There were major droughts a year. There was no rain for 100 days. The rice fields were cracked, and the dry fields were smoky. Fortunately, there is a two-foot mountain spring pool at the entrance of Nanshan Mountain. The pool is only enough to scoop two barrels of water at a time. Strangely enough, when you scoop enough for two buckets, the pool water will seep out automatically, filling the pool flat and full; if you don't scoop water out of the pool for a day, not a drop of spring water will spill out. Shan Liang and Shan Lihong relied on watering seedlings from this pool to preserve more than one mu of corn. However, on such a dry day, a large winter melon grew in the back vegetable field of Shan Liang's house. I didn't care about it at first; but the longer it grew, the longer it grew to be two feet thick and longer, which was particularly impressive when standing on the ground. Shan Liang had never heard of a winter melon growing this big. He went to Yamashita Village to ask the elderly, not even the 890 year old. People are curious, and many people come to see Strange every day.
The news spread quickly, and more and more people came to watch. On the evening of this day, Shan Liang and Shan Lihong were preparing to cook on the fire. A Taoist in black came outside the house. The Taoist in Black claims to be from the South. He has been a monk for decades. When he arrived in town yesterday, he heard something strange happened here. Every strange thing in the world has a cause, and a cause must have an effect. I'm here to see if we can pull out the cause and effect. Shan Liang and Shan Lihong heard the Taoist “Jiri Guru” talk about this section. I don't know what it means. Guess he also came to see melons, so they took them to the garden at the back of the house for him to watch enough, saving them “Jiri Guru” to say something they couldn't understand.
When Taoist in Black saw a melon, it circled seven or eight times in a row, and was shocked. After watching it, he looked around at a high place. At first, he saw a field of corn, thick, thick, and strong. It didn't look like drought-damaged water at all. The Taoist asked again that it was selfish; Shan Liang told him to pick spring water for watering. The Taoist wanted to see that mountain spring, and the Taoist in Black from the district did not get tired of coming to the side of the mountain spring. As soon as the Taoist saw the mountain spring, he looked at the dozens of feet wide mountain gate and exclaimed, “God helped me, it really paid off. I've been looking for this place for almost 30 years.” Shan Liang and Yamari Red are even more incomprehensible. The Taoist in Black said, “It's true that this mountain is a treasure mountain. There is an underground treasure house at the entrance gate. There are countless gold and silver treasures in the treasure house. That mountain spring is the keyhole for opening a treasure chest. The big winter melon in your house is the key to opening a treasure chest. Just lift the melon and stand in the mountain spring pond, and the treasure chest door will open automatically. This opportunity only comes once in 50 years. I missed an opportunity in the last 50 years because no one knew about this law, and no one grew melons at this mountain gate. We can't miss this opportunity again.” Shan Liang and Shan Lihong are like listening to a story. Half believing and half questioning: “How do you know?” The Taoist in Black said, “My master told me before he died. He was told by his master, but the master didn't find this place.” Red Yamanari still doesn't believe it: “Then why did you tell us?” The Taoist in Black said, “There are two reasons. One is that you have to be a melon grower to open this treasure chest, and the other is that I can't get any of them, because the North Dipper is facing the mountain gate at midnight, but you have to leave before the chicken calls; otherwise, this mountain gate will close, and the mountain will never open again when the mountains are merged.” Shan Liang and Shan Lihong heard the Black Taoist speak loudly, and believed most of them. The couple left the Taoist in Black for dinner. After the meal, the Taoist in Black asked, “Do you have any newsworthy roosters?” Shan Liang puzzled and said, “Yes! Why?” The Taoist said, “I want to tie the rooster's mouth with a rope and get some more treasure. I've been looking for 30 years, and I can't help but wear broken shoes. Besides, you can also get some more, go to the city to build a house and open a store, and donate money to your son when he's older. Isn't that better than if you've been busy all day?”
Shan Liang and Shan Lihong made their son go to bed early. They prepared a flat rope to pick the melons in the back of the house. Shan Liang picked it up by a Taoist in black, and Red in the mountains dragged it with a rope. After spending a lot of effort, he finally carried the winter melon to the edge of the mountain spring. The Taoist in black stormed out of the door and meditated facing the North; it was only midnight. Anxious, time was slow. Shan Liang felt that it had been a long time, but the North Dipper had just deviated from the mountain gate. After a while, she was in a hurry in the mountains, and she couldn't stand it because the mosquitoes were bitten by small bugs. She said she couldn't wait; she wanted to go back and see the kids. The Taoist in Black said in comfort, “Don't worry, it's almost time.” After waiting for a while, the North Dipper finally moved over. The Taoist in Black seemed to have been stabbed in the butt and popped up from the ground and said, “The time has come, hurry up!” The three of them did their best to put winter melons in the mountain spring pond. They only heard a thunderous “thunderclap”. At the entrance of Nanshan Gate, a flat and wide floor door was opened one by one. The doors of the fixed warehouse were knocked out, and a dazzling golden light shone through the door. The Taoist in Black first entered the treasure chest. He pulled out a large cloth pocket that he had prepared in advance and picked all the rare treasures in the treasure chest. Shan Liang and Shanrihong seem like the Seven Spirits are out of their way. They haven't seen so many gold and silver jewels when they come out of their mother's womb. I was stunned for a moment and was overwhelmed. Red Yamari thought it was in a dream. She pinched her ear hard and felt pain; it wasn't a dream. Take it! People in the mountains were ignorant and picked up everything. She easily took a few of the biggest ones, each containing 50 or so much gold, and carried them in her arms. The couple seemed to be stealing something from others; their hearts were beating “knocking”. Shan Liang took two more tablets and held them in his hand. Shanri Hong pulled Shan Liang and said, “That's enough! That's enough! How can I spend so much?” Shan Liang was also embarrassed to say, “Yes! Yes! Let's go out. If this door is closed and we can't get out, it's useless if all our gold and silver treasures are ours.” Shan Liang shouted again to Taoist in Black: “Master, that's enough, let's go out.” The Taoist in Black dragged half of his pocket treasure and said, “It's still early, so I'll get some more.” The Taoist in Black saw that Shan Liang and his couple had only taken a few bars of gold and mocked, “It's true that the townspeople are ignorant.” Speaking of picking up something the size of an egg that shines green, he said, “This is called the Pearl of the Night; one can buy a city.” Red Yamari said, “We want gold, why buy a city? Look, the old county official has been tired all day long.” The Taoist in Black smiled “hehe”. He chose his treasure and stopped paying attention to the couple Shan Liang. Yamarihong took her husband and said, “Let's go. This money is enough for a few lifetimes.” Shan Liang has a treasure trove of red in the mountains.
As soon as Shan Liang and his wife came out of the treasure house door, they heard a “giggle” rooster cry. Immediately after that, I heard another “roar” of thunder. The treasure house door closed, then a few loud “thunders”. The east and west mountains merged together, and the mountain gate closed. Red in the mountains was terrified: “Oops, oops, the Taoist in Black is still in the treasure house.” Shan Liang said, “No! At most, it's only an hour. What is this chicken called?”
It turns out that as soon as their son woke up and peed, he heard the big cock in the chicken cage “purr.” This kid usually likes to play with big cocks. He reached over to hold the big chicken in front of the chicken cage. As soon as he saw it, his mouth was stuck. He didn't know what happened. He untied the rope and put the chicken back in the cage, and angrily said to himself: “Who did this; it's more mischievous than me.” Who would have expected this big cock to be so uncomfortable when its mouth was pierced in the middle of the night; it would be loosened; it actually flapped its wings, straightened its neck, and “giggled”.
It dawned, and people rushed over to find out after a few bursts of noise. When people saw the east and west mountains merged into one mountain, they were all surprised to see that the mountain gate of the north-south channel was missing.
When Shan Liang and Shan Lihong arrived home, they took a total of 16 bars of gold, 52 each, for a total of 800. I really don't know how to use this much money. Later, in the village under the mountain, Shan Liang built several tall blue brick houses, bought ten acres of land, a cow, and donated one after another to do quite a few good things, built several dams, and two stone bridges. At first, people thought it was strange that the Shan Liang family suddenly became rich. Later, when people learned the story, they all laughed and said, “Be nice! People must not be greedy. If Shan Liang and his wife want to take a little more, they will probably be locked up in a treasure trove.” There is no more Shuangmen Mountain, there are no more East and West Mountains, and the north and south gates are tightly closed, so this mountain is called “Guanshan.” It is said that some people have also seen a stone gap in the middle of this mountain, saying it was a gate gap.
