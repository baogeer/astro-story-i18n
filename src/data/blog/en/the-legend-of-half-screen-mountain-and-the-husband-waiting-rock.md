 There is a mountain called Banping Mountain near Zuoying on the southwest coast of Taiwan Island. This mountain faces the Taiwan Strait, and there is also a mountain on the opposite mainland that looks like Banping Mountain, which is far opposite. This is Fuzhou's Nanping Mountain. They look like a mountain split in half across the sea. There are legends circulating on both sides of the strait about these two mountains being forced to be separated; there are several versions of the Taiwanese legend alone.
Mizune and Ishibana
This is a sad and moving legend. That was a long, long time ago. The island of Taiwan was connected to the motherland. Taiwan is a peninsula on the southeast side of the motherland. Banping Mountain on Taiwan Island and Banping Mountain on mainland China were originally a mountain called Nanping Mountain.
A young man named Shuigen lives on the northern slope of Nanping Mountain. He hunts for a living on weekdays; a sericulture girl named Shihua lives on the southern slope of the mountain. They often have feelings for singing mountain songs, and their feelings grow deeper and deeper over time. They agreed to become a couple in August this year.
August 15 finally arrived. The townspeople set up a small cottage on the top of Nanping Mountain and furnished a new house to host a happy event for Shui Gen and Shi Hua. At night, after drinking a glass of wine, everyone walked out of the cottage and sang and danced around the couple in the clear moonlight. They were really happy. However, this happy event was revealed to an evil tortoise god in the South China Sea. It flew out of the sea, carried an axe and slashed, drove away the townspeople who had enjoyed drinking alcohol, and kicked Mizugen down the northern hillside with one foot. It forced Shihua and forced her to go to the South China Sea to become a family with herself; Shihua refused at all. It picked up Shi Hua and left, and Shi Hua never died. She stood firmly on the top of Nanping Mountain and refused to move half a step. When the turtle god caught fire, it picked up a giant axe and violently slashed it at the peak. I only heard a loud click and the planet shook. Suddenly, the mountain shook to the ground, and Nanping Mountain was cut in half. Then there was another loud noise, and the earth split open, and the billowing seawater quickly filled the big mouth. The peninsula slowly drifted away from the mainland, and the large ditch in the middle became an insurmountable strait. A complete mountain was thus divided between the two sides of the Strait.
The god Kame dragged an exhausted stone flower off the mainland along with the drifting peninsula. When Shi Hua woke up and found herself far from home, she felt so sad. She knew it would be so difficult to see Mizugen, and she couldn't help but cry bitterly. Kame God didn't care about this; it forced Ishibana to become married to him, and Ishibana never died. In one fell swoop, Turtle God took her to Banping Mountain to do hard work. One night, Shi Hua had a dream. She dreamed that the separate earth was connected again, that the separate Banping Mountain had merged again, and that she was reunited with the water roots again. Shi Hua laughed with joy. But when I woke up, I saw the god Turtle who had come to force me to get married again. Shi Hua was furious. She picked up a big rock and hit it violently on the head of the god Turtle. Turtle God was defenseless and screamed in pain. Annoyed, he angrily ordered the fire dragon to spit fire and burn the stone flower, and ordered Thor to use lightning to split the stone flower. Ishibana doesn't give in at all. Kame died when he was sick with gas and vomited blood and fell to the ground. It became a turtle-shaped rock, which is Guifeng Rock next to Banping Mountain now.
The girl in the stone flower also died. She became a white, jade-like man-made stone called Wangfu Stone. Until now, this Wangfu stone still stands on the beach under Banping Mountain, like a loved one watching the mainland from afar.
The legend of Wangfu
On the tip of a mountain on the south side of Taiwan, there is a large rock called Wangfu Stone.
Once upon a time, the south side of the Central Mountains of Taiwan Island was not land, but a vast ocean. A young couple named Ah Long and Ah Feng live in a mountainside by the sea. During the day, Ah Long goes out to sea to fish, and Ah Feng weaves with home textile thread. In the evening, Ah Long sits on a stone bench under a coconut tree and plays the flute, while Ah Feng sings mountain songs to the sound of the flute. The younger couple had a very pleasant time.
One day, dark clouds suddenly rolled over the sky, strong winds, lightning thunder, and heavy rain all over the ground. At the same time, I don't know where a dragon popped out of nowhere. Turns out it made all of this. It tumbled up and down, stirring the sea as if it were boiling a pot.
On this day, Aaron didn't go out to sea; he was repairing his fishing net at home. Meanwhile, the fishermen who went out to sea had their boats overturned and died, and even the capital was eaten by evil dragons. For the next ten days, evil dragons continued to make waves. Fishermen can no longer go out to sea to fish. Fishermen will starve to death if they have no food source! A few times Aron wanted to risk fishing, but Ah Feng stopped them all. Aaron was distraught. This night, Aaron had a dream where Grandma Matsu came to him and said, “Although that dragon is powerful, as long as a brave person can fight with him for seven days and seven nights, it can cause it to die.” The next day, Aron told Ah Feng what he had dreamed of being told by Grandma Mazu, and made up his mind to go out to sea to fight dragons and kill the people. Ah Feng saw that his idea had already been decided, and did not stop him. He made seven large cookies for Ah Long as dry food. But Ah Long secretly left a large cake to fill Ah Feng's hunger.
Aaron came to the seaside and sailed to the depths of the sea in a small boat. At this point, the dragon hadn't eaten anyone for many days, and was lying on the bottom of the sea hungry. It saw someone rocking the boat and hurriedly vomited bad breath upward. In an instant, the sea twirled as if it had opened a pot, and the waves were raging. Aaron's boat is like a leaf. Sometimes it crosses the peak of the waves and sometimes falls into the valley of the waves. Aaron quickly ate a loaf of bread, accumulating strength. He tried to stand on the bow of a boat like a nail. At this point, the evil dragon thought Aron had already overturned and died; it wanted to get out of the water to eat Aaron. However, as soon as it got out of the water, it was immediately hit by Aron's fishing fork. The dragon swooped at Aaron in pain. Aaron was full of energy and faced the enemy calmly. Aron ate a flatbread every day when he was hungry, and his strength was immediately doubled. By the 7th day of fighting, Aaron had no flatbread to eat, and his strength had weakened. The dragon was exhausted, too. Finally, the bottom of the wooden boat was topped by the dragon's horn. The boat gradually sank to the bottom of the sea. Aaron wrestled with the evil dragon, and slowly sank to the bottom of the sea. The hull held both Aaron and the dragon firmly to the bottom of the sea.
After Aaron and the Evil Dragon were exhausted, the wind went away, the rain stopped, the sky cleared up, and the sea calmed down. Fishermen can go out to sea to fish again. Ah Feng didn't see Aron return. She stood on the top of the mountain and looked at the sea. She looked and watched, cried and cried. She didn't know how many days and nights she cried back to the sea, and never saw Aaron come back. But Aaron's little boat exposed the surface of the sea and became a small island shaped like a sail. Ah Feng also became a stone man, standing on the top of a mountain and kept looking at the sea.
This is the origin of Wangfu Stone.
It is said that seawater used to be sweet, but Ah Feng's tears made the seawater bitter and salty.
