 Near Kaohsiung, Taiwan, there is Banping Mountain, at the junction of Zuoying and Nanzi districts. There is a Lotus Pond on the south side of the mountain. The water color of the mountain is very beautiful. Seen in the evening, the top of the mountain is full of strange rocks, lush green forest, and the remnant sun shines brightly, making it one of Kaohsiung's “Cuifeng Sunset”.
Banping Mountain is more than 240 meters above sea level, and the mountain stretches for about a mile. At the top of the mountain on the west side, it looked like a large wall that had been cut through with a knife. Using great force, the entire mountain was cut in half. The stones, trees, and flowers on that side are all gone. Seen from afar, it also looks like half a large screen, so it's called Half Ping Mountain.
It is said that in ancient times, Mount Banping was not this strange; it was originally a very complete mountain, standing opposite Mount Yushan in the Central Mountains. When the clouds are low, this mountain rises high into the clouds, so it feels that it has a high status and looks down on other mountaintops.
In fact, its height is much lower than Mount Tamayama, but it wants to show its majesty and refuses to show weakness to Mount Tamayama. One day, it even loudly challenged Yushan and said, “Let's compare it to see if you're taller or am I?”
Of course, Tamayama didn't want to be weak, so he immediately answered, “OK!”
It despised Yushan and said, “I'm afraid you're not my opponent!”
When Tamayama listened, she was unkind enough to say, “Huh! Let's not talk big, let's ask Tenjin to judge and see who is superior and who is lower.”
Ban Ping Shan couldn't help but say, “Of course I'm worse than you. hey! You still don't know how tall I am. If I put three pieces of dried tofu on top of my head, it would blow up the sky.”
Tamayama stopped ringing because I didn't think I had anything to say about it. However, at this time, Tenjin happened to be passing by and heard the wild words of Banping Mountain, and was very angry; in particular, the phrase “if you add three pieces of dried tofu, it will break the sky” is even more unbearable for Tenjin. So he secretly ordered Lei Gong to punish this arrogant mountain.
Lei Gong was ordered to knock on his hammer lightly, and there was an immediate explosion. The arrogant mountaintop fell in half. The stones, dirt, trees, flowers, and plants all rolled down; only half of them stood there. In this way, it became the Banping Mountain that everyone sees now.
There is also a legend about the origin of Banping Mountain. It says that when immortals come down, they want to test whether people's hearts are content.
There is an immortal from heaven. Originally, he had an apprentice who was literate, capable of martial arts, and had a bright heart, so he was very loved by immortals. Unfortunately, this kid got a strange illness and died. In addition to being sorry, he really wanted to go anywhere and find another contented apprentice.
Immortal people went down to the common room, and when they saw a mountain. There was a big village underneath the mountain. They wanted to test people's hearts here first. They wanted to find someone who was not greedy and content to become his apprentice. As a result, the fairy held a fan in his right hand, directed a fan towards the mountain. A strong wind blew to the southwest of the mountain, and half of the mountain collapsed. He fanned it again, and the dirt that had fallen down the mountain instantly turned into wheat flour, and soon it became flatbread one by one. When piled up, these flatbreads are half a mountain high. There is a big sign in front of this pile of cookies that says, “Buy one for a penny. Two dollars, take whatever you want.”
This news soon spread ten times, ten to a hundred, and a thousand. Everyone knows that the top of the mountain has collapsed. What fell was not dirt or stones; it was all bread. So they rushed to say, “Go! Go! Go watch it!”
“Let's all go eat flatbread!”
The road was full of people and crowds. Everyone arrived in front of the big cake and saw the sign in front of the cake that said: Buy one for one penny. Everyone touched their pockets and wanted to find a piece of copper to buy a flatbread to eat. But after looking further, I saw “Two cents, take whatever you want”, and everyone touched their pockets one after another. Each one found two pieces of copper money and shouted, “I bought two cents!”
“I bought it for two cents too!”
There is no one who doesn't buy two cents. As soon as they put their money away, they took a loaf of bread to eat first. When you're full, use a bag if you have a bag; if you have a hat, use a hat to hold the flatbread; if you don't have a bag or hat, use the large pocket of your clothes to pack as many as you can, and take it home without fear of trouble. The fairy watched quietly from the sidelines, wondering: Why isn't there anyone satisfied?
The number of flatbreads piled up like mountains is also astonishing. Even though everyone takes whatever they want, they take as many as they like, and less than half of them move away.
Day by day like this, there is an endless flow of people crowded in front of the big cake pile, and they have to buy a flatbread that costs two cents. Until the 7th day, a young man with a beautiful face suddenly appeared in the crowd. As soon as the fairy saw it, they knew that he had come to buy cake for the first time. I also saw him look at the flatbread first, then take a close look at the brand next to it, then reach out to touch his pocket, touch five copper coins and pinch them in his left hand; then use his right hand to take out one and put one in the fairy's storage bowl, and at the same time respectfully say to the fairy, “Sir, I bought a flatbread that costs a penny.”
Speaking of which, pick up a piece of cake from the cake pile. At this point, there was a burst of laughter. Many people were laughing at the teenager, and many others criticized him: “How can there be such a fool in the world! He takes whatever two cents he wants; he would rather buy a pancake for one penny.”
“Haha! Let's watch idiots buy cake!”
“Ha! What a fool with Tenji no. 1!”
The teenager, who seemed unable to hear their ridicule and criticism, finished eating this flatbread while hungry, then took out another penny and sincerely said to the Immortal, “Mister, I want to buy another flatbread.”
Next, he put a penny in the fairy's money bowl and reached out to get the cake.
The fairy smiled and gave him back this copper coin, then used one of his fans. In an instant, only one large cake piled up in front of him was left; all the rest were scattered to ashes. The crowds also dispersed in surprise. The fairy kindly waved to the teenager, gave him his only flatbread, and earnestly said to him, “Kid, I love you. I want to take you as my apprentice. Let's go to Wonderland together.”
After listening, the teenager couldn't even speak for a moment. It took a long time to ask, “Where are you from, sir? Where is Wonderland?”
Immortals answered in detail, saying that they are one of the Eight Immortals. They are unique to the world, and they use experimental methods to seek apprentices. Now that I have found him, a contented and happy person, I am determined to take him as an apprentice.
Although the teenager wanted to go with him, he also had problems that were difficult to solve, so he had to be respectful and gentle and say, “Thank you for your kindness! However, if I have parents, I have to ask for their consent before I can go with you to Wonderland. Please give me three days to get back to you after I ask for instructions from my parents.”
The fairy nodded, and the two said goodbye.
Turns out this teenager lives close to the mountains. When I got home, I told my parents about everything that day when I met Immortals. After listening, the two old men thought this was a great opportunity. Unfortunately, they promised their son to worship the Immortals as his teacher and followed them.
The mountain, which was half overthrown by the immortals, stands near Kaohsiung, always reminding people to be content and not greedy.
