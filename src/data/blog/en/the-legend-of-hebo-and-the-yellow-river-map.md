In ancient times, there was a man named Feng Yi in Tongxiang, Huayin. He was uneasy about farming and wanted to become immortal. He heard that if people drink daffodil juice for 100 days, they can turn it into a fairy body. So I looked everywhere for daffodils.

Feng Yi crossed the river and when it reached the middle of the river, the river suddenly rose. As soon as he panicked, his feet slipped, fell in the Yellow River, and drowned alive.
After Feng Yi's death, she was full of grievances and bitterly hated the Yellow River, so she went to Emperor Jade to sue the Yellow River. Using the Yellow River's water conditions as a basis, managing the Yellow River would save much trouble.

The townspeople all hate him for his laid-back work; no one answered him. He found an old man in the village and talked about his ambition to manage the Yellow River.
