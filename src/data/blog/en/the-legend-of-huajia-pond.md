 Earlier, outside Qingchun Gate in Hangzhou was a large area of desolate government land. One year, DPRK's Huatai Master sent his housekeeper to Hangzhou with a smelly nose. I rode around the wasteland with a smelly nose on horseback. This large wasteland is even a Chinese family. Master Huatai also posted a list in the four cities of Hangzhou to solicit tenants and clear land, stating that as long as the field is turned into a mature field and crops are grown, it will not be possible to rent within ten years.
The poor have all wiggled their fingers and figured it out: no income in the first year, 50% income in the second year, 70% in the third year... well, how could anyone in the world have such a kind heart!
Coincidentally, a couple came from a foreign country at this time. They were in a hurry to find a place to settle down. When they heard that there was such a tenant, they put their fingerprints on Master Hua Taishi's contract regardless of 3721.
The couple arrived outside Qingchun Gate and found a deep pool of water. They set up a small straw shed by the pool and settled down. They woke up early and discovered the dark and worked hard to reclaim the wasteland.
In the middle of winter this year, it snowed heavily, and my wife was about to give birth. In this isolated hut, there were no relatives delivering brown sugar packages, and no neighbors handing out bowls of ginger soup. Only the husband guarded his wife and was in a hurry. Huh la! The north wind scraped open the thatched shed door, and the husband quickly got up to fasten it; whoa! The north wind blew open the thatched shed door again, and the husband got up and fastened it; whoa! The north wind blew open the thatched shed door for the third time, and when the husband locked the door for the third time, they screamed “wow,” and the baby was born.
The husband said to his wife, “If you knock on the door three times when you give birth, just call him 'three bucks'.”
My husband thought that three buckles would slow down and said, “Three buckles, grow up fast! Dad's hips are all broken!”
The three buckles quickly grew a bit taller.
My wife also thought that the three buckles were slow, and said, “Three buckles, grow up fast! My mother's eyes are blurry!”
The three buckles quickly grew a bit taller. Young children who have just been born are seven or eight years old. They are more sensible than those in their teens, helping them in their lives both at home and outside of home.
Three buckles helped his father farm, and there was always a dark cloud in the sky that followed him. The toxic days in June couldn't help him; three buckles carried water for his mom. As long as he breathed into the bucket, the two buckets were full! Everyone says that the three buckles are not an immortal; the three buckles were born from a dragon.
Since Sankou's father stamped his finger on Master Hua Taishi's contract, quite a few poor people have also become Huatai Shi's tenants and moved to live in this wasteland. Gradually, around that deep pool of water, a village of 20 or 30 families gathered. The wasteland quickly changed as soon as you pitched it: a green field in the east, a green field in the west, willow branches shook, and the flowers laughed in the wind. The scenery was so beautiful.
In the spring of the third year, the fields had just been cultivated, and the seedlings were only planted. Father Sanshou ran out of strength and died of exhaustion in the field. Mom Sankou was so sad that she even cried her eyes. Since then, three have taken over the herding of this family, mowing the grass, and the two wives have been struggling to live the day.
Soon, Master Huatai returned to his hometown and returned to Hangzhou. He saw that this wasteland had been turned into a fertile and beautiful garden, and he wanted to take it back to Gaitaishu to take care of his old age.
The stinky leader broke into the village and forced the tenants to move out immediately. When the tenants heard it, they all shouted, “Isn't it clear that you won't be able to rent within ten years?”
I laughed twice with a smelly nose: “Within 10 years, a year is also within 10 years, and half a year is also within 10 years. Now it's the 3rd year, and Taishi's good deeds have come to an end!” As soon as I shouted, my subordinates tore down the house. Demolish the house and disassemble it into a house with three detachments, and the three withholds are not supported. The three clasps swung up like crazy, scratching their smelly noses and biting around. The stinky nose was in a hurry. He ordered his subordinate to tie up the three buckles, hang a large rock, “flush” it, and throw it into the deep pool of water in front of the door; he set off another fire and burned the blind mother with the three buckles alive in the grass shed.
After the smelly noses left, the neighbors all rushed to the deep water pool to salvage the three claws. They fished for half a day and didn't catch anything. Someone dived into the water to explore. Turns out a bottomless hole appeared in the center of the pool!
Soon, a splendid Taishifu was built on this land. Taishifuli has golden brick floors, silver brick walls, pearl paneled walls, and white jade inlaid beams. It really is a “Heavenly Immortal House, the Prime Minister's Family on Earth”!
Taishifu has just been completed, and Master Hua Taishi's 60th birthday is just around the corner. On this day, Taishifu was brightly lit and very lively. Master Hua was sitting in Taishi's chair in the guest room, and the officials and gentlemen praying for Shou knelt down all over the place. After praying for Shou, I was about to take a seat to eat. I saw a smelly nose rolling in and climbing the ground, shouting: “Grandpa Taishi is not good! When two cypress trunks suddenly grew in the backyard, there must have been some kind of monster!”
Master Hua didn't believe it, so he went to the backyard with the officials and gentlemen to see. Sure enough, there were two bare cypress trunks standing there. An official was busy and jokingly said, “This is the fall of heaven. Grandpa Taishi lives better than Japanese cedar, forever green...”
I didn't finish talking; I saw a cypress trunk rising up, and a “boom” sounded, and a dragon flew out from under the ground. It turns out that this cypress trunk is the dragon's two horns. This dragon is just three buckles — three buckles for revenge!
The dragon turned down. As soon as the dragon shook its head and swept its tail, it sank the entire Taishifu underground and turned it into a pond with a radius of tens of acres. Master Hua Taishi and the officials and gentlemen all drowned in the ground.
The poor tenants who were evicted gathered from all sides and settled down by this pond. They cultivate fields and plant trees and flowers. Year by year, this place is slowly becoming more beautiful.
Because Master Hua Taishi's Taishifu sank to the bottom of the pond, people called that big pond “Huajia Pond”; others saw the beautiful scenery in this area and called it “Little West Lake.”
