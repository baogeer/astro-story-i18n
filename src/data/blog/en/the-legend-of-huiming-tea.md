 A long, long time ago, people in the Hexi area didn't know how to grow tea trees or use tea leaves to make tea.
Legend has it that a monk named Huiming from Mount Emei in Sichuan visited Hexi. Seeing that Mount Aomu was full of clouds and fog, it was a good place to build a temple, so he took the name Huiming Temple. After studying Buddhism, he took the name Huiming Temple. Later, every spring, he removed the tender tea leaves and made them into dried tea.
One year, the plague spread around Hexi, and countless people died of illness and fled. When Monk Hui Ming learned about this situation, he walked around the house with tea leaves and gave away tea when he was sick. Within a few days, the patient's condition improved after drinking tea soup. For a while, Huiming became famous, and the tea soup's fragrance and glycolic flavor, and its miraculous effects in treating diseases became known to everyone. People followed the example and started growing tea trees in front of and behind houses.
The Hexi area is grateful to the monk Huiming, so the tea leaves in the Huiming Temple area are called Huiming tea. The fame of Huiming tea also spread farther and farther.
