 At Seiryeon-ji Temple in the north of Mount Sengoyama, there is a rectangular pool called “Gyokusen.” The water from the jade spring is clear, the water from the jade spring is bright and crystal clear, how did the spring water come from?
There is such a legend.
Earlier, there was a bottomless trench at the mouth of the Qianluo River called the “Tiankai River.” One day, there was a grass dragon living in the Kai River. Cao Long can cross the river and drive through the fog. He has great ability, but he is crude, and doesn't understand the world. He has been drilling in the river all day, drilling out of the sea, and playing with big and small fish and shrimp.
One day, an official ship passed by the mouth of the Qiantang River accompanied by two barges. The official ship sounded the sound of a gong and was full of energy, but there was a cry on the barge. Cao Long saw it very strange. After inquiring left and right, he learned that there was an emperor on earth. The emperor had many officials under him. This is why the emperor sent officials to take ordinary people to Beijing to do hard work. When Cao Long learned about this reason, he was very angry. As soon as he flapped his tail, “whoa,” a big wave swung over, overturned the ship, and saved two boats of people. Caolong thought: The official ship was knocked over by me, but the emperor is still in Beijing! The emperor is so bad, I should get rid of him and get a good one. As a result, he picked up the dark clouds, broke out of the Tiankai River, and flew towards Beijing.
The emperor was drinking and having fun in the palace with his concubines. The women of Gong Ecai were blowing, bouncing, and singing. The sky changed in an instant; the wind blew down the trees, and the heavy rain kept falling on the ground, but when they saw Grass Dragon driving in with dark clouds, they rushed in menacingly. At this point, the Three Thousand Imperial Forest Army rushed in, and the emperor was even more stunned. Fortunately, a bold servant pulled out his slingshot and shot at Caolong. This slingshot happened to hit Zhongcaolong's right eye. It hurt him so much that he yelled and turned his head, “Whoa whoa!” The tail swept off a corner of the palace and flew away.
Caolong fled back to the Tiankai River at the mouth of the Qiantang River. The more he thought about it, the more annoyed he became. He gnawed his teeth and vowed to take revenge.
At that time, there was an old monk living in Seiryeon-ji Temple. He was highly skilled in medicine. When Chaolong heard that there was such a monk, he became a big dark man. He came to the house to seek medical treatment. The old monk saw that he had a strange shape. He was very skeptical, and asked, “Oops, how did you make your eyes look like this.” Caolong made up a lie, saying that he went up the mountain to cut stones and accidentally got bombarded by crushed stones.
The old monk used pliers to pick out what was in Cao Long's eyes. As soon as he saw it, it fell to the ground and said, “It's clearly a projectile. Why are you talking about crushed stone? What kind of person are you? If you don't understand, I can't cure it!”
The old monk lost his temper when he saw Cao Long, and was afraid that he would actually heal himself and honestly tell the story of how he was beaten with a projectile. At the end of the day, he also said, “When you heal my eyes, I'll take revenge.”
The old monk was taken aback when he heard Cao Long's words. I thought, “My temple was built by the emperor. I enjoyed the emperor's blessings. Now he wants to hurt the emperor, that's fine! Then I thought of the next plan and pretended to laugh at Cao Long and said, “It's not difficult to get my eyes healed, but what would you thank me with?”
“As long as you can heal my eyes, I'll give you whatever rare treasure you want.”
“I don't want exotic treasures. There's not enough water in this temple, so let's take a look at the springs for me first!”
Caolong didn't know what the plan was, so he fully agreed. The old monk chose a location, and Cao Long dived into the dirt one by one, and clear spring water gushed out of the hole he had dug into. As the grass dragon drilled deeper, the well grew larger and larger, and it soon became a big pool. At this point, the old monk hurriedly moved the exquisite stone pagoda enshrined in front of the Buddha, standing in the middle of the spring, and crushed the grass dragon to the ground.
Cao Long was duped by an old monk, and he was crushed underground and couldn't get out anymore. The spring that came out was called a “jade spring” because the spring water was as clear as jasper. There are also two pools near the Jade Spring. One is called “Pearl Spring” and the other is “Clear Sky Happy Rain Spring.” It is said that Grass Dragon's head was lying under Pearl Spring, so there was a vibration on the ground. When Grass Dragon woke up, he felt comfortable underground, and many small bubbles popped up on the surface of the water. Under the clear sky and rain spring, there is a grass dragon's tail, so on a clear day, the top is always foggy.
