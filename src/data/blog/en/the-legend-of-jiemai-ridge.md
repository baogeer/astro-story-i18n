 The sprawling Ma'anshan Mountain has a very long mountain range that winds down, spanning a beautiful stream directly into the Shibi Tan. It's like a giant dragon drinking water in the pool. Unfortunately, the dragon's neck was cut off by a deep gully that seemed to have been cut and broken to the end by an axe, and the dragon body and dragon were completely separated. The deep gorge is about 20 meters deep, and the widest point seems to be less than 2 meters. It is narrow at the top and bottom, and there is still a stone sandwiched between the waist. It is said that the fairy rolled down from above when cutting off the faucet with a flat load and embedded there. This is the place commonly known as Jiemailing in the Xiandu Scenic Area. Speaking of this, people can't help but ask: Then why did the fairy cut off this faucet?
Legend has it that in the ages, the Emperor Jade used to visit the world barefoot and exterminate violence. Daisen scaffolding with colorful clouds, waving fairy broom, and traveling through the three mountains and five mountains. When I arrived over Xiandu in one day, I saw a small village — especially the big courtyard in the village with bright clouds in the sky, which was full of energy. Hold down the clouds and take a look. At the back of this small village, there are black dragons (that is, Ma On Shan directly inserted into the mountain stream). The dragon is excellent; there are many flags in front, and soldiers and horses are all ready (that is, strange peaks and monsters lined with Iron City). Daisen was amazed: “True destiny Tenshi will come from this place!” In addition to admiration, he thought carefully: “Take on the heavy responsibility of inspecting the world. You must not let the immoral and incompetent Mu Jun take over the reign of the people. I have to go down and see if this family has a kind heart.” So I pressed the cloud, thought words in my mouth, and said, “Change!” The old fairy, who had a young face, instantly turned into a stinky beggar with a loose beak, dark face, and shaggy clothes. I saw him limping towards the village with a broken bowl and hitting a dog stick with his root in the other.
I arrived at the east gate of the large courtyard in the village. I was about to knock on the door. It just so happened that a young woman, dressed in style, was holding a child in one hand and carrying a flower basket in the other. She wanted to go out to the wild to pick mountain flowers. The fairy immediately begged, “Good sister-in-law, OK, OK, I haven't eaten in two days. Give me some leftovers. I'm almost starving to death.” “Huh, old bones, if you lose to me, I should have died a long time ago. I have no leftovers, go away!” The young woman said. “Well... that's fine for a bowl of porridge and soup! Please.” Daisen prayed bitterly. “It's not your turn to eat congee soup either. Move a little further away.” While talking, the young woman walked back to the door, shouted “whimpered” and closed the door tightly. Daisen thought to herself: I'm afraid a young woman who is stingy like this is also very rare...
Thinking about it and going back to the west gate of the large courtyard, he saw that the gate was closed, walked up the steps, and gently patted the door and said, “Have pity on me...” With a “squeak,” the door opened. It was a man in his 30s. Daisen hurriedly said, “Big brother, do good deeds. Give me something to eat. I'm so hungry.” The man listened coldly and said, “Why are you starving to death or me, let's go!” “Well, give me a bowl of water. I'm really hungry and thirsty, please.” The man pointed to the garbage ditch next to the house and harshly said, “There's water in that ditch, go drink it!” Speaking of which, he pushed Daisen down the steps with one hand, fell to the ground, and closed the door.
Daisen stood up and walked to the front of the Jeongnam Gate, only to see Juman Deva and Carved Beams, which was not impressive. As soon as Daisen's eyes lit up, she saw a piece of watermelon peel next to the big stone lion in front of the door. She quickly picked it up and gnawed it up with relish. Suddenly there was a barking sound of dogs in the yard, and the red lacquered door opened. A fat man in a splendid flower coat came out. He saw someone who wanted to eat eating watermelon peels and shouted, “Hey, don't die, if you dare to steal Grandpa Ben's watermelon to eat. What a crime.” “Grandpa, I'm not stealing your watermelon; I'm picking watermelon peels and nibbling them. Please let me eat it!” “What, to eat for you? I have to ask my dog if he won't!” Speaking of which, untied the dog's rope and shouted “up,” the dog fought for strength. All of a sudden, it jumped on the fairy's body and screamed and bitten him wildly. Daisen was bitten with blood flowing straight and shouted for help. When people in the village heard it, they saw that the rich owner, who had done nothing wrong, was doing everything, and no one dared to go to the rescue. The “stinky beggar” was bitten and scarred by a dog. His blood was full of blood. In the end, the rickets turned into a mass and never moved again. The fat guy called out a few family members and said, “My dog has eaten enough. Let's take it to the mountain to feed the wolves!” Daisen's heart was broken. He thought, “Your family is so arrogant and immoral. If you actually become an emperor, will the people still have a good life?” The more I thought about it, the more angry I got, the more I hated it. I pulled out the spirit fairy, brought Xiang Yun to the place of Dragon Neck, waved a flat shoulder at will, lifted it up high, and slashed it at Dragon Neck in anger. “Thunderous...!” There was a loud noise, the sky was dark, the earth shook, and the golden light flashed at the dragon's neck. Suddenly, the dragon head was cut off, and dragon blood stained the river red. Everyone in the village was stunned. They looked up and saw a “beggar” standing in the clouds. Knowing that they had offended the immortals, everyone quickly got down on their knees and asked for forgiveness from the immortals. Daisen said, “Y'all get up! Remember, to be a human being, you must have virtue; don't bully the weak and abuse the poor like this rich man; do more good deeds. Good has good rewards; evil has bad rewards!” Speaking of which, as soon as the fairy broom swung, he picked up the colorful Xiang Yun, and slowly left. Later, the wealthy family quickly fell into disrepair and begged abroad. Daisen not only left behind the pleasant scenery of Jiemailing, but also deeply taught people the virtue of helping the poor and doing good. As a result, people in Xiandu have had a good tradition of doing good deeds and accumulating virtue since ancient times. There is one less emperor in Xiandu, but there is a hint of fairy spirit.
It is said that this small village is the current village of Shangzhang.
