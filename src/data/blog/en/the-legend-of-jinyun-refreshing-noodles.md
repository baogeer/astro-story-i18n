 Handmade noodles drawn by Jinyun people are long and slender, so they are called “cable noodles.” Legend has it that the Huangdi Southern Tour spread the Central Plains civilization while stopping at Dinghu Lake and Jiujin Dan. The people of Jinyun thanked Dade, so they treated Huangdi's monarch with a homemade sack. The surface is appetizing, flexible and smooth, and full of fragrance... Huangdi even said, “Cool! cool! Cool!” The Jinyun people changed the name “suomian” to “cool noodles”, using Huangdi's golden words. Fresh noodles last a long time, with the synonym “long life span”. Huangdi food's dragon ascended to death, and people were happy and healthy. Since then, fresh noodles have become a delicious dish of choice for Jinyun ancestors to entertain guests and celebrate their birthdays.
The biggest characteristic of Jinyun Fresh Noodles is the word “soil”. It uses farm-grown wheat “908” as an ingredient. There are no additives, and it has to go through seven procedures of “mixing, firing, cutting, kneading, pulling, and grinding” during preparation. Some farmers strive for excellence in technology, and the fresh noodles are slender and even, even as beautiful as dragon whiskers in a ravine, so many people also call them dragon whiskers noodles. People in the countryside use it to cook eggs for birthdays or to entertain guests.
