 Legend has it that when Xuanyuan Huangdi forged magic elixir at Dinghu Peak in Xiandu, ascended to heaven, and flew into the sky, spiritual herbs were stained with the spirit of jindan fairy, and the green leaves turned golden branches into yellow tea. Every time spring warms up, the tea tree sprouts golden yellow shoots, which are as beautiful as if it were a strange bloom. Pick the yellow shoots, fry them and brew them in water. The soup is yellow and the leaves are yellow. The fragrance does not dissipate, and the aftertaste is glycolic. Drinkers are healthy and eyesight, and all diseases are eliminated. People appreciate the gift of Emperor Huangdi, called yellow tea.
Jinyun yellow tea is not traditional yellow tea among the six major tea categories. It is a rare product of green tea. It has the characteristics of early germination, high amino acid content, and high carotene and lutein. It shows the “three yellow transparent green” characteristics of “golden yellow and transparent green in appearance, goose yellow and green in the bottom of the leaf, and green in yellow and green in the bottom of the leaf”. It has high health and wellness value and drinking appreciation value. In November 2011, Jinyun yellow tea from Shenzhen was first displayed at the Zhejiang Agricultural Fair, and was praised by industry insiders and recognized by the general public. In recent years, Jinyun yellow tea has successively won the Zhejiang Agricultural Fair Gold Award, the Chinese Tea Cup Grand Prize, the International Famous Tea Competition Gold Award, and the Zhejiang Green Tea Fair Gold Award, and was selected for the National Famous Premium New Agricultural Products Catalogue. In 2014, Jinyun yellow tea successfully registered a geographical indication certification trademark, and in 2016, Jinyun yellow tea was selected as the product selected at the G20 Hangzhou Summit.
