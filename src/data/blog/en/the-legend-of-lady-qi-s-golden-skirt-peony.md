 Tulip-belted peonies are said to be named for their highly fragrant, golden, and ruffled dress like a beautiful woman. Meanwhile, in Peony Township, Caozhou, many people think that the tulip dress is the personification of a famous local historical figure.
Legend has it that in 206 BC, after the Hongmen feast, Xiang Yu was divided into 18 royal princes as the leader of the League. Liu Bang was crowned King of Han. Xiang Yu himself is known as the King of Western Chu. In order to compete for the world, there are frequent battles between them, and Xiang Yu often wins. In 205 BC, Xiang Yu led an army north to attack King Qi, and Liu Bang took a flight to seize the temporary capital of Xiang Yu (present-day Xuzhou). Xiang Yu was furious when he heard the news. He personally led 30,000 elite soldiers to throw back the mountain and the sea. Liu Bang led an army and fought hard to resist. In the end, Major General had no choice but to abandon the city and flee. Xiang Yu did not allow Liu Bang to take a break, and personally led an army to chase him down, so that Liu Bang was unable to break up and become an army, and it all came to nothing. When he single-handedly fled to Qijia Village, more than 20 miles southeast of Caozhou, Liu Bang was exhausted. After a few days and nights, frightened, unable to eat well, and unable to sleep well, Liu Bang felt that he really had no choice. It was getting late. Liu Bang got off his horse. He had just taken a break under an ancient tree at the top of the village. He heard the sound of horses again. The crowd became very noisy, and the chase caught up again. What should I do? Is it true that I, Liu Bang, should have lost my life here? He was thinking nervously and suddenly caught a glimpse of an old man and a young woman in the family's garden, working hard to repair the branches. Too late to think carefully, he hurriedly walked forward, saying that he was under Han King Liu Bang and was being chased. He hoped that the old man and girl would do their best to save them. The girl got smart and pointed her finger at a dead well in the garden. The old man agreed and led Liu Bang to hide inside. What about war horses? It was still that girl. She hit the war horse a few times with a wooden stick, and the war horse ran out of the village in smoke.
The chase went a long way, and Liu Bang was saved. In the evening, Liu Bang was shocked by the old man asking his daughter to buy wine and dishes. Only then did Liu Bang learn that the old man's last name was a flower farmer in Qijia Village. One daughter is the girl who saved Liu Bang. The girl is 18 years old and has a beautiful face. She is simply like a fairy. Liu Bang was affectionate, and also felt Qi Laoweng's life-saving grace, so it was revealed that his true identity was Han Wang. When Qi's father and daughter heard the news, they quickly fell to their knees: “Little people don't know Han Wang is here; they don't know Mount Taishan; they don't know how to entertain them; they still hope for forgiveness.” Liu Bang said, “Get up quickly. I want to thank you for saving my life. If I had the world, I would call my wife a royal consort.” Qi's father and daughter prostrated once again to say thank you. Qi Nu immediately changed her clothes, danced a fold dance, and enjoyed drinking with Liu Bang. The two became friends that night, saying that if they had a child named Ruyi, it would be Zhao Wang Ruyi later.
Later, Liu Bang prepared to abolish Prince Liu Ying, who was born after Lu, and made Zhao Wang Ruyi the prince because of his ingenuity. However, Liu Bang died before handling the incident; Qi Ji and Zhao Wangruyi were both brutally murdered by Lu Hou. It is said that Qi Ji wrote a poem when she was close to seeing her dead mother: “The son is the king, the mother is the prisoner. She is in twilight all day long, and often goes hand in hand with death. If you are three thousand miles apart, let anyone sue you!” I was very sentimental after reading it. The talented people in Qi Ji's hometown were very sympathetic to their mother and son, and specially built a temple called “Qi Ji Temple.” Another good person planted a “tulip dress” peony in the temple, saying that it was the personification of Mrs. Qi; it was nothing more than a memorial.
