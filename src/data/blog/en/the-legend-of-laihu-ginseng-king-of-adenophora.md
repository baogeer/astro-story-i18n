 “Raihu Ginseng” is the king of sand ginseng and is famous at home and abroad. Speaking of Raihu ginseng, there is another beautiful and moving legend
It is said that a long time ago, in Hucheng Village in Laiyang City South, there was a young man known as Big Brother Zhang. He lost his mother and father when he was 10 years old. He lived alone and lived alone. He was an honest and hardworking person. He left his parents with two acres of small fields to make the ground deep, and planted all of them with sand energy. He wants to use these two acres of sand ginseng to build three rooms and marry a wise and beautiful daughter-in-law. As a result, he stayed on the ground every day and wouldn't stay idle for a moment except for drawing, catching insects, and watering. The two mu sand ginseng is growing very well, several times better than the rich owner's “oblique eye” family.
The “oblique eye” thought that Big Brother Zhang had ginseng in his land; several times he sent someone to use two acres of good words for his two acres of sand ginseng land. Big Brother Zhang all said no. He hates the “oblique eye” and the root of his teeth is itchy.
The harvest season for sand ginseng is coming soon. Big Brother Zhang has set up a small shed on the ground to guard the ginseng field day and night. Big Brother Zhang saw that Sha Shen Ke grew as thick as a child's arms. Some also had arms and legs, and looked like an adorable fat doll. I was so happy that it was as sweet as eating wheat. This Sha Shen is his hope, his happiness, he wants to build a house, get a wife, and live a beautiful life. He loved the ginseng so much that he picked up the two largest ones, put them on a plate, and respectfully offered them in his small shed.
This night, Big Brother Zhang was lying in bed. As soon as he closed his eyes, a girl like a fairy stood in front of him. But as soon as she opened her eyes, she disappeared again. This was the case several times in a row. At the age of 17 and 8, the girl had a willow-like waist, apricot-like eyes, an added face, fine pink skin, and a pair of light dimples on her cheeks. She smiled slightly, showing two rows of white and neat teeth. A milky white dress was dragged to the floor. Big Brother Zhang has grown up, and he hasn't seen such a beautiful girl yet. It's been like this for a few nights.
On this day, Big Brother Zhang piled up the sand cucumbers he had collected, carefully packed them with tomatako, and prepared to sell them at the market tomorrow. However, after dawn, a bunch of sand ginseng trees disappeared. Big Brother Zhang immediately felt that the sky was spinning and his eyes were dark.” “Fluttering” fell down. When he woke up, he saw a girl sitting next to him. He took a close look. He looked exactly like the girl he had seen that night. Big Brother Zhang was excited and immediately grabbed her hand.
The girl told him that the sand ginseng had been stolen by the “oblique eye”. She is a Sha Shen girl. Seeing that Big Brother Zhang is hardworking and kind, she also cherishes ginseng so much, and would like to become a couple with Big Brother Zhang. As soon as Big Brother Zhang heard it, his excitement burst out of his chest. She hurriedly knelt down to salute the Sha Shen girl. The girl hurriedly supported him. On the same day, the two worshiped heaven and earth and became husband and wife.
This incident soon spread to “oblique eyes”. He immediately brought three dog legs to Big Brother Zhang's house, saying that Big Brother Zhang had kidnapped a woman from a good family and wanted to be sent to the government for punishment. As soon as he waved, his three legs swung at Big Brother Zhang.” The “oblique eye” himself hugged the Sha Shen girl violently. Big Brother Zhang was so angry that he immediately caught a stick and knocked down two dog legs, then raised the stick and hit it at the “oblique eye.” The “oblique eye” quickly loosened the young lady, and crawled away with three dogs.
After the “oblique eye” escaped, the Sha Shen girl took Big Brother Zhang's hand and said, “Let's run away quickly; they will come back.” As a result, Sha Shen Girl and Big Brother Zhang fled overnight to Changbai Mountain in Northeast China, where they still make a business growing ginseng. To commemorate Sha Shen Girl and Big Brother Zhang, people from Northeast China made a drama about their story, and even performed in Beijing.
