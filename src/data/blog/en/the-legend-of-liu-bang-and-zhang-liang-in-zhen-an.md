 Zhang Liang, a minister from the beginning of the Han Dynasty, is said to be the father of the city (southeast of present-day Boxian County, Anhui). He was admired by the world for not being greedy for power or pursuing fame and fortune after success. Legend has it that he once lived in seclusion in the Beiyangshan area of Zhen'an. As a result, snippets of legends about him and Liu Bang are circulating in many parts of Zhen'an. Although the legend has fictional components, the brilliance of his morals shines through in it.
Zhang Liang was an important adviser of Liu Bang and made extraordinary contributions to Liu Bang's establishment of the Han dynasty. Liu Bang praised Zhang Liang for “winning a decisive victory a thousand miles away in the midst of planning and success.” As a result, Zhang Liang was canonized as Liu Hou. However, Zhang Liang is a gentleman who is indifferent to fame and fortune. He declined to be granted a title and Li Lu, but he escaped alone to live in seclusion in the deep mountains and forests of Zhen'an. Liu Bang heard that his secluded place was called Beiyang Mountain, and he personally visited it. Zhang Liang was very moved. He knew that his secluded Beiyang Mountain system was a barbaric place, with difficult and dangerous roads, and that his holy car was unbearable. He sent people 15 kilometers east of Zhen'an County to stop the Saints in Zhuanghe Village, present-day Tiechang Township, to stop the Saints, so he called this place a “barricade river.”
Liu Bang wanted to meet Zhang Liang, implored Zhang Liang to return to the DPRK to assist the government, and promised a meritorious official, a money and beauty. But Zhang Liang sent a letter saying, “Civilian officials don't love money; military officers don't hesitate to die; it's enough for you to ban me from time to time. Life is short, why crave prosperity and wealth?” Insistent, Liu Bang insisted on seeing Zhang Liang and threatened that if he didn't see Zhang Liang, he wouldn't go back to Kyoto. Zhang Liang was helpless and had to meet Liu Bang under Jianfeng Pass in Yuexi Township. Since then, the small mountain beam under Jianfeng Pass has been called “Waiting for the Driver.”
The two monarchs accompanied each other. They talked while admiring the picturesque scenery of the rolling mountains. Unbeknownst to them, the two arrived at the riverside under Mount Nanyang. The scenery here is extremely beautiful. Dangerous rocks and steep rocks stand between rivers, forming deep gorges. The riverbed is rocky, uneven, and the river is steep and steep, forming one dangerous beach after another. The steep Nanyang Mountain stands on the riverbank and contrasts with the elevation of the creek. After enjoying the beautiful and spectacular landscape, the two monarchs said goodbye here. This is why this river is called the “Fallen River.”
