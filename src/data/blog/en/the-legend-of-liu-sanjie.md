 The legend of Sister Liu is a Zhuang folk legend from the Zhuang region of Guangxi. It is known as the “Song Fairy.” Sister Liu was born in the first year of Tang Zhong Zong Shenlong (705). Since childhood, she has been smart and able to sing well. When I was 12 years old, I became famous for my hometown by saying many words and singing as an endorsement. Since then, he has sung songs all over the neighborhood. There was an endless stream of admirers who sang with her, but as short as a day and as long as three or five days, they were all hungry and startled, had no songs to answer, and retreat in shame. However, her talent was envied by rogue bullies, and she was later murdered and died in Liuzhou.
Legend 1
Legend has it that in the Tang Dynasty, on the Tiandong coast at the junction of Luocheng and Yizhou, there was a beautiful small mountain village (now Landian Village, Luocheng Yilao Autonomous County). There is a Zhuang girl named Sister Liu in the village. Her parents both died when she was a child, and she was raised by her older brother Liu Er. The brother and sister make a living by fishing for firewood, and are dependent on each other. The three sisters are not only hardworking and smart; they are also skilled in spinning and weaving, and they also look like hibiscus coming out of the water, and they look amazing. She is particularly good at singing mountain songs. Her mountain songs are famous, so singers from near and far often gather in her village to compete and learn songs with her.
Sister Liu often sings about the voices of the poor and unhappy in the mountains, thus violating the interests of local lords and poor gentlemen. Local financier Mo Huairen coveted her beauty and wanted to take over as a concubine. She rejected and ridiculed her, so she held a grudge. Mo tried to ban songs, and was rejected by Liu Sanjie for being excruciating, and three talents were invited to play against Sister Liu. They were also made to play against Sister Liu and others, causing a huge defeat. Annoyed and furious, Mo Huairen did not hesitate to spend his family money to collude with the government, gnawed her teeth and put Sister Liu to her death. In order to avoid being drugged, the third sister and her brother, with the help of the townspeople, took advantage of the black sky to ride a bamboo raft, follow the Tianhe River straight down the Longjiang River, then enter the Liuzhou River, and live in a small rock cave at the east foot of Liyu Peak near Xiaolongtan Village.
It is said that after arriving in Liuzhou, Liu Erxin, the loyal and honest older brother of the third sister, had lingering feelings. He feared that the third sister would sing again and cause trouble, so he found ways to stop them. One day, he finally came up with a way to retrieve a round and thick pebble from the riverside and throw it to the third sister, saying, “Third sister, use the corner of your handkerchief to drill a hole in the middle of the stone and put the handkerchief through! If you can't wear them, you won't be allowed to go out and sing!” He then added word for word with a blue face: “No joke about talking about anything for my brother.”
First, it was the third sister with a sweet smile. Looking at her older brother's face, she still dared to argue rationally as usual, pick up the stone thrown in front of her, and secretly thought, “I'm not an immortal. How can I wear a handkerchief?” She subconsciously tried it on and sang: Brother is crazy, take a stone for your sister to wear; how can you get a soft cloth with a stone? Unless Mortal Girls become immortals!
“Whether you're a mortal or an immortal, say whatever you say for your brother, never change it!” My brother seems to have eaten a weigh—he has an iron heart. Thought: Is this trick good enough, is it still hard for you?
No wonder the Third Sister's poignant and gentle singing reached the ears of the Seven Fairies of Tiangong. The Seven Fairies were very moved. The Three Sisters were afraid that the song had gone out of circulation, so she performed a spell to remove a hairpin from her hair and throw a sleeve at the stone in the hands of Third Sister Liu in the world, unbiased, and pierced the stone through a round hole. The third sister unexpectedly saw a handkerchief pass through a stone. Her heart was overjoyed and her sweet voice opened:
Ah... wear it, wear it, Looneng Ke is just so happy. The song is like a raging river, and the flow never stops!
Since then, Sister Liu's singing voice has lingered back to the top of Yufeng Mountain and the treetops, and people who admired the song have continued to come to learn songs. Later, traces of the three sisters in Liuzhou were discovered by Mo Huairen. He also spent a lot of money to buy through the government office and sent a large number of officers and soldiers to surround the Liyu Feng Regiment. They are threatening to kill the three sisters. The townspeople in Xiaolongtan Village and nearby heard the news. Armed with hoes and sticks, they fought with the officers and soldiers to save the three sisters. The third sister couldn't bear to bleed and implicate the townspeople, and resolutely jumped into Xiaolongtan...
Just as Sister Liu took a leap forward, the wind was raging and the sky was dark. Following a red light, a large golden carp stormed out of Xiaolongtan, carried the three sisters, and flew into the clouds. Sister Liu just rode a fish to heaven and became a fairy in Tiangong. And her mountain songs are still being sung by people from generation to generation. To commemorate her singing achievements in Liuzhou, people sculpted a stone statue of her in Liyu Peak and worshiped it all the time.
Legend 2
According to rumors, Sister Liu was a Zhuang peasant girl from the Tang Dynasty. She was young and smart, and was regarded as a “goddess.” Being able to pass on the scriptures at the age of 12 means searching for things and being able to speak up. Self-written and self-sung, the song is beautiful and moving, and does not lose its rhythm, so it is known as a “Song Fairy”. He was hired by Lin when he was 15 years old. Tang Kaiyuan ran away with his lover Zhang Weiwang for 10 years (722) to resist Lin's forced marriage, but it didn't end up. People often think they're both immortal.
According to the legend of the Zhuang people in Yishan, Guangxi, Sister Liu was born in the first year of the Tang Zhong Dragon (703). Since childhood, she has been smart and able to sing well. When I was 12 years old, I became famous for my hometown by saying many words and singing as an endorsement. Since then, he has sung songs all over the neighborhood. There was an endless stream of admirers who sang with her, but as short as a day and as long as three or five days, they were all hungry and startled, had no songs to answer, and retreat in shame.
However, her talent was envied by rogue bullies, and she was later murdered and died in Liuzhou. Legend has it that after her death, she rode carp to heaven and became a fairy. There are also those who say that in Xishan in Guixian County, she sang a duet with a white crane boy and became a fairy on the 7th. Others say that the rich owner Mo Huairen wanted to marry her as his wife. The third sister resolutely resisted. Mo Maitong government persecuted the third sister, and the three sisters drifted away by boat.
“March 3” is the biggest song polder day in the Zhuang region, also known as the “Gexian Festival”. Legend has it that it is a folk memorial festival formed to commemorate Liu Sanjie.
As the legend of Sister Liu has been circulating for a long time, it has been continuously processed and enriched by the Zhuang people, and the storyline has been improved from simple to complex. This legend fully embodies the thoughts, wishes, and creative talents of the Zhuang people.
The legend of Sister Liu is also one of the regional legends in southern China. Similar stories circulate in Guangdong, Guangxi, and even among ethnic groups in Hunan, Yunnan, and Guizhou, but this legend has its own characteristics when combined with local customs and singing customs, so the legend of Sister Liu is very valuable for studying the history of social customs and folk literature and art of various ethnic groups in southern China. The popular song and dance drama “Liu Sanjie” and the film of the same name are based on this legend.
The Song of Sister Liu
“Now Guangxi Chenggehai is passed down by the Three Sisters.” People in the Zhuang ethnic group believe that “Ge Yu” was formed only when Liu Sanjie passed the song, and Liu Sanjie is an important symbol of the general formation of Ge Wei. Ge Wei's song is Liu Sanjie's song. Liu Sanjie is regarded by Guangxi people as a “fairy”. Yizhou is the most representative region for Liu Sanjie's songs, and is recognized as Liu Sanjie's hometown.
Liu Sanjie's songs are broadly divided into seven categories: life songs, production songs, love songs, ceremonial songs, riddle songs, story songs, and ancient creative songs. They have poetic characteristics and distinct ethnic characteristics expressed in songs. The heritage is relatively complete, the variety of songs is rich and diverse, and widely spread. Liu Sanjie's songs have had a profound impact across the country and the world, showing the charm of the vibrant traditional folk art culture of the Chinese nation. It not only has cultural history research value to witness national history and emotional expressions, but also has research value in ethnology, anthropology, sociology, and aesthetics.
However, with the disappearance of the traditional social foundation of the Zhuang people “relying on songs and choosing matches” and the impact of strong culture and new forms of entertainment, Liu Sanjie's songs have few successors and are in danger of being lost, and measures need to be taken to protect them as soon as possible.
