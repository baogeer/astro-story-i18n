 A long time ago, a family lived on the riverside of the Zijinshan Sha Ga Factory. The couple made a living by hunting. After three years of pregnancy, the woman finally gave birth to a son, but the child was blind and had six hands throughout her life. His parents personally regarded him as a monster, so they abandoned him by the river and moved away from home.
Orphans are suffering from human hardships and suffering. Growing up, he looked ugly, dark and extremely powerful. He was able to lick his nose with his tongue, and wandered around the riverside of the tile factory all day long in his torn clothes. People call him the name Leak Wan. I don't know where I learned this skill. When the sun rises and the moon sets, the sun must set when it gets dark; furthermore, when the sky gets dark, the sun must set; furthermore, when he has the ability to go over a wall and walk flat on the ground; in addition, he has the courage to rob the rich and help the poor, and poor people have all reaped the benefits of Lou Bei. He thought of a company that stole something. He didn't need to lock the door; he only needed to lift the eaves of the house with both hands to break into the house. He came and went freely, and never lost his hand. Lubai wandered along the river. Because they saw mountains covered with white bamboo, the trees were deep and the mountains were green, and the water had bamboo and water shows. The temple looked like a temple, but it was rarely visited, so they used mountain temples as shelters and shrines as places to stay. Occasionally, they went down the mountain to do chicken and dog robberies. It's half human and half astonished; some hate it, others are happy.
At one point, Lou Bei discovered that a family had bought three good sea horse horses from Weishan and wanted to steal one as a mount. When it was quiet in the middle of the night, Lou Bei came down the mountain, lifted the roof, sneaked into the stable, and held out six hands to mess around inside the stable. Unwilling to do this night, a hungry tiger also coveted the deliciousness of sea buckthorn horses. He sneaked into the stables step by step. While preparing a delicious meal, he was caught right by his three hands. Tigers are afraid of crowds and are afraid to move. Lou Bei touched the tiger's back. It was just that the seahorse had fat and thick fur. She was overjoyed, so she rode over the wall.
By the time I learned that I had ridden a tiger, Lou Bei wanted to get off the back of the tiger, so it wasn't easy. She said to the tiger, “I'm not afraid of the sky, I'm not afraid of the ground, so I'm not afraid of you.” The tiger said, “I'm not afraid of heaven, I'm not afraid of the earth; I'm afraid of the sky leaking (rain).” If I missed it, I told the tiger, “I just missed it.” As a result, the tiger was treated to the rules and regulations and used as the mount for omission.
Locals saw Lou Bei riding a tiger on the road and were afraid to take refuge. Lou Bei couldn't get anything to eat, yet there was no way. Fortunately, he has lived at Baizhushan Temple for a long time and is on good terms with the Buddha of Fortune in the temple. Every time he goes out on his way, he entrusts the tiger to Lord Caishen. Lou Bei rode a tiger and became famous. He died in the temple many years later, and legend has it that he has become a local mountain god. In order to remember him, people sculpted Lubai's golden body at Baizhushan Temple, revered him as the king of the mountain, and enjoyed incense forever. On the opposite side of the statue, the god of fortune, holds a sword with his right hand, a steel whip with his left hand, and treads on the tiger's spine with his right foot. The tiger at his foot is the mount of the mountain king Lubai.
