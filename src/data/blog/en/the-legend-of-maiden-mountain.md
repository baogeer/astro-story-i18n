At the end of the Tang dynasty, an old grandmother who was blind lived at the foot of Gushan Mountain and her only daughter, a beautiful lady. Ying Gu is 18 years old, and she hasn't found her grandmother's house yet. It's not that Ying Gu has a strong heart. First, her old lady is old, and she is blind, and she has been taken care of; second, Ying Gu has long been in love with someone; this is a poor man who lives in the mountains. Hanfu is also a strong, kind-hearted young man. Every morning, one goes up the mountain to dig for wood, and the other goes down the mountain to help plow fields. After going back and forth, the two hit it off, and they decided to live for life. The blind mother is also very happy; she is preparing to marry them on “Tanabata” this year.
The sky is full of ups and downs. One day, Ying Gu was washing clothes by the creek, but happened to be bumped into by a county official who came here to enjoy the mountains and water. When Yinggu saw that she was about to be overtaken, she leapt down the mountain stream. Han Fu got the letter and came to the place where Yinggu jumped a cliff. He cried day and night, climbed the top of the mountain on both sides, and also jumped off the cliff from the top of the mountain.
Ying Gu and Han Fu died, and the townspeople at the foot of the mountain were all very sad. They built a temple for them both in the middle of these two mountains.
