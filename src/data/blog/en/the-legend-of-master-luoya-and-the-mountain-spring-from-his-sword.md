 Legends about “Mr. Rokya” are circulating in southern Xiuning.
I don't know who this gentleman is from; I just know that he is a person with a great deal of wisdom.
The legend “Immortals Deliver Water” also happened to him.
Huangjianling is located at the southeast end of Xiuning County, at the junction of Anhui Province and Zhejiang Province.
The legend of Mr. Rokuya's “One Sword Plunches Yamanaka Izumi” is circulating here.
The main plot of this legend is not much different from the two legends above; it just adds a layer of educational meaning.
The immortals above all sent water because of everyone's hardships, and Mr. Luo Ya delivered water because while he was taking a nap on the mountain, two mountain people gave him the last bowl of water drawn out of a bamboo tube to drink.
Mr. Luya was very moved, so he used his sword to take a look at the clear springs to the benefit of the mountain people.
Also, there is another detail in the legend. Once someone becomes greedy and wants to find a sword in the spring to sell for money, the spring water stops; once the greed is removed, the spring water automatically gushes out again. Other texts in Huizhou legends often have similar plots, but the details are slightly different.
Author's comment: The difference in emotional colors is also an important characteristic of Huizhou legends.
