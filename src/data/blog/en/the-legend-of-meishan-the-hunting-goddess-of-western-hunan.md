description: "The girl from Meishan is a hunter from Tujia in Xiangxi. She is fluent in the language of birds and animals, and is like a god of arrows.  There is a “Meishan Hall” in Xinghu Chongyuan, Shangnanmen, Yuanling Town, Yuanling County, Xiangxi. “Meishan Niang”, the local Tujia hunting god, is enshrined there. It turns out that the religious beliefs of the Tujia are deeply influenced by the Han people. They are superstitious about mountain gods and worship their ancestors. The ancestors of Tujia are famous for being good at shooting. The god Meishan is a hunting god believed in by the Tujia family. Hunters had to hold a “Mount An” sacrifice ceremony before and after hunting to sacrifice the “Lady of Meishan.”
Legend has it that in ancient times, Yuanling Mountain in western Xiangxi had high forests, and there were a lot of tigers and leopards. Many years ago, Shanchongli between Heming Mountain and Wutong Mountain on the Yuanshui River in Wuling Mountain was famous as a result of the rise in groups of tigers and leopards. There is a hunter surnamed Mei in Xing Huchong. There is a girl in her family who is dependent on her father. That girl was born smart and intelligent. She had red eyes and red lips, and was extremely beautiful. Hunters loved it like a pearl in their pocket, and they wanted to train the girl to look good.
This girl has understood the language of birds and animals since childhood, and her personality is like a boy. She plays with her puppy during the day, sleeps with her baby rabbit at night, and is happy all day long hehe. Girls are also hardworking and studious. No matter what they learn, they always know a little bit, and they will do it as soon as they learn. The girl can embroider when she is five years old. The flowers she embroidered look vibrant. At the age of seven, he can make loud noises, making flowers bloom and birds chirp. The nine year old is already able to do housework, pick up firewood, fetch water, wash clothes, and cook. He is a good helper for Father Orion.
When the girl was 15 or 6 years old, she had to go hunting with her father in the mountains. Her father began to disagree. Later, the girl asked again and again, and her father had to agree to take her. The girl is very intelligent, and her father soon learned all of her hunting techniques; in addition, she has excellent archery skills, is accurate and far from side to side, and is also fluent in the language of birds and animals. Her strength is amazing, so she went into the mountains to hunt, making great strides. However, she doesn't breed foals in the winter, and she doesn't hatch nests in the spring.
The girl can fight more prey every day than the hunters at the 18-hole in Jiuxie, and every time there is always a big selection of prey. After the girls have caught their prey, they always follow the customs in the stockade, leaving the head of the beast and distributing the prey to every Tujia person on the mountain. People in the Wuling Mountain area really like her. Because she runs in the mountains all year round, everyone calls her “Meishan Girl.” In order to improve her hunting skills and efficiency, the girl from Meishan sought help from a blacksmith, invented an earthen gun, and wittily captured bears, so that Tujia's life was no longer harassed by bears. The girl from Meishan became famous ever since, and the Tujia loved her very much.
Girls from Meishan walked all over the mountains and mountains of Wuling Mountain. They killed 981 wild boars, 7,749 leopards, 5,525 tigers, as well as hundreds of goats and thousands of pheasants. The animal skins in the house were piled up into mountains, and animal heads were all over the walls. One day, there was a sudden rumor that seven tigers had appeared in Wuling Mountain. After only three days and two nights, they had bitten nine sheep to death, had taken nine cows, and bitten nine pedestrians, causing the house in the cottage to close its doors. No one dared to drive cows and sheep up the mountain, afraid to go out to do Yangchun, or go into the mountains to hunt; even the merchants in the past had disappeared. After hearing about it, the girl from Meishan made up her mind to get rid of these seven tigers for the townspeople.
The next morning, the girl from Meishan worshiped her ancestors, brought dry food and a steel fork, and went to say goodbye to the old hunter's dad. The old hunter wanted to go together. Unfortunately, she was too old, so she had to let the girl from Meishan go alone.
Girls from Meishan go up the mountain like racing horses and go downhill like flying arrows. She went over Jiudaoling in one go and walked over Jiujiao Creek. She sat under a big tree, took a breath, gnawed two rice crackers, drank a few sips of mountain stream water, climbed to the top of the mountain, and kept searching. After finding traces of a tiger, the girl from Meishan tried her best to fight a tiger.
On this day, the girl from Meishan killed six tigers one after another from morning to dusk. Just when fighting with the sixth tiger, the horn fork broke in two sections. The girl from Meishan was holding a half-prong fork and looked up. The sun had already set, the finch had returned to the forest, and there was another hanging rock cliff ahead, a deep valley. She was also tired, so she was ready to go down the mountain and go home. Tomorrow, she will look for the last tiger.
When the girl from Meishan was preparing to go down the mountain, suddenly, a strong wind blew in. With the sound of a tiger, the tiger appeared two feet away from her, opened its blood basin and glowed green light from her eyes. The markings on her forehead clearly showed the word “king.” Fiercely, the tiger swooped at the Meishan girl. The girl from Umeyama took a bow, and the tiger took advantage of the opportunity to throw half of the horned steel fork in her hand at the tiger's head when she saw that the tiger fell to the ground. Who knows, as soon as the Tiger makes a U-turn, the steel fork then falls down the Tiger's ear. The tiger turned around and once again attacked the girl in Meishan. The girl from Meishan saw the tiger coming ferocious. When the tiger was flying in the air, she flew past one step, hugged the tiger's neck tightly, pinched the tiger's stomach with both hands, pinched and pinched. The ten fingers fell into the tiger's flesh. The tiger's neck bleed. The girl from Meishan was still pinching the ground. The tiger's back claws grabbed the rotten Meishan girl's underwear, and caught Meishan girl's body scaly wounds all over. The pain was unbearable when she pinched the tiger. The tiger struggled and tumbled, determined to get rid of the Meishan girl. The Meishan girl worked hard, along with the tiger We rolled down Manjo Fukaya together. ... That night, the old hunter didn't see the girl from Meishan go home and couldn't sleep all night. As soon as it dawned the next day, I went up the mountain to find a girl from Meishan. Zhai Li's family heard about it, and they all went up the mountain to find it together. They each saw six tigers on the hillside and on the top of the mountain. At the bottom of the deep valley of the hanging rock in Meishan Mountain, they found the seventh tiger killed by the Meishan girl. However, there were no bones or traces of the Meishan girl.
Just as people were looking around, a few days later, the girl from Meishan came home by herself. It turns out that when the girl from Meishan and the last tiger fell down a hanging rock, they fell to mid-air and were caught by Tenjin. Tenjin saved the Umeyama girl from heaven and named her the god of hunting, and made her take charge of all the beasts in the mountains. But the girl from Meishan couldn't bear her dad, and only then went back to the mountain village.
The news spread to King Toast. Mr. Toast's matchmaker sent a few baskets of jewels and wanted to marry a girl from Meishan as a side house. The girl from Meishan didn't agree, so the toast king imprisoned the girl from Meishan. The girl from Meishan still did not comply.
The toast king saw that the girl from Meishan would not follow. Since he couldn't marry her, he wanted to kill the girl from Meishan. The housekeeper came up with an idea and said, “Killing her for no reason is likely to cause public outrage. I'll definitely call her a miserable life.”
So the housekeeper came to the cell and said to the girl from Meishan, “You can do if you don't marry King Toast, but there is one thing you have to abide by. There is a big tiger in Kitayama, and if you can get rid of it, I'll let you go.”
The girl from Umeyama believed it and went to Kitayama to fight tigers all night long. As soon as I went up the mountain, a colorful tiger suddenly flew out of the woods, yelling at the Meishan girl, trying to devour her in one fell swoop.
When the girl from Meishan saw the tiger pounce on herself, she was shocked, but soon woke up. She dodged the tiger's triple jump and began a battle of life and death with the tiger with a short wooden knife in her hand.
During the fight, the clothes on her body were torn apart by the tiger's claws and turned into pieces of butterflies that died with the wind. Her shoulders, back, wrists, and thighs were scarred and bloody, but the tiger didn't take advantage of the offer. Her forehead and body were cut open everywhere by the Meishan girl, and was also scarred. In the end, she gnawed her teeth and did her best to cut all the wood knives and handles into the tiger's den, and finally slashed the vicious tiger to death.
On her way home after the girl from Meishan slashed the tiger, she unfortunately stepped into a trap set up by Toast Wang a long time ago. Ding Lanjian, the director of Toast Wang, who was ambushed next to the trap, went in unison. The girl from Meishan died immediately after being hit by an arrow.
The bad news spread to the cottage. People were outraged. The Tujia immediately started an incident and yelled to avenge the Meishan girl. They all broke into Toast's palace and hacked the housekeeper to death with random knives. King Toast was also crushed to death. After the death of the Umeyama girl, she became the god Umeyama, who was in charge of people's hunting. God Meishan secretly gave prey to Tujia hunters to protect them. Since then, the Tujia worshiped the god Meishan, which the girl from Meishan turned into, and became the god of hunting. At the same time, the “Meishan Temple” was built in Xinghuchongli to forever keep the Tujia hunting safe and rich in prey.
Since tigers are being hunted down by Tujia hunters, Xing Hu Chong is located on the banks of the Yuanshui River. The ancients called the flat land on the shore a little far from the water. Apricot trees grew all over the place; the Meishan girls also had red apricot eyes and red lips to commemorate the Meishan girls, so people changed the name Xing Hu Chong to Xing Hu Chong.
Since then, when the Tujia go up the mountain to hunt and return from hunting, they have to worship the god Meishan. Before going up the mountain to hunt, burn incense and burn paper, and you can only set out after sacrificing the god Umeyama. It's called Kaimesan. If you don't respect the god Umeyama first, after going up the mountain, you won't be able to find any traces of your prey, or even fire a shotgun or kill your partner by mistaking them for prey.
It is said that all the beasts in Wuling Mountain listen to the orders of the god Meishan and go into the mountain to worship the god Meishan and ask the god Meishan to bring the beasts and give people prey; when they are hunted back to Jingmei Mountain, they say thanks to the god Meishan. When worshipping the god Umeyama, you must be well-dressed and have a pious attitude; otherwise, it will not be spiritual. Therefore, many hunters carry someone else on their backs and secretly set up an altar. When praising the god Meishan, they set up a Umeyama Shinto position in a secluded place on the side or back of the house, facing the direction of entering the mountain, and worshiping against the sky. It is to prevent others from making fun of others, and disrespecting the god Umeyama. Since then, Tujia people who have lived in the Wuling Mountain area for generations preferred group hunting. After hitting the prey, the principle of “going up the mountain to hunt and see others” is implemented in accordance with the practices of the Meishan girl. After the beasts were knocked down, the number of people present was counted, the hunted beasts were brought home, and the hunted beasts were first honored, then killed, and the meat was divided by shares. The dead beast is cut from the neck, including the head. No matter how many pieces, it goes to the hunter who fired the first shot or killed the first shot. The one who shot the second shot or assassinated the target of the second shuttle is called “Two Colors,” split the leg of a pig. Others are per capita. Anyone who sees a wild beast fall to the ground has a copy called “Catch meat along the mountain, and those who see it have a share.” When dividing the meat, cover each portion of meat with brown leaves, put it together in the dustpan, then cover a dustpan. When picking up the meat, shake the dustpan a few times, and no one can see the difference. Everyone holds the brown leaves on the skewers to pick up the difference; no one has any comments. Wild boar heart lungs are given to hunting dogs for food. Cook the other offal and four hooves in one pot. Everyone ate them together to share the joy of victory in the hunt and use this as proof of hunting and distribution.
This legend and custom has been passed down from generation to generation by the Tujia, and has left many mysterious and magical traces; the Tujia's worship of the goddess Meishan looks primitive and ancient, like Diana, the ancient Greek female hunter god.
