 There is a small mountain called Wangfushi Mountain in the southeast direction of Xindian Township in Ye County. The mountain is 455 meters high. The scenery here is beautiful and the scenery is amazing. What's even more fascinating is that it is known by locals as the birthplace of ancient love. And these all stem from the poignant and tangled legend of Wangfu Ishiyama.
A long time ago, two families, surnamed Jiang and Meng, lived at the foot of this mountain. They have an honest duty. Generations have relied on gathering firewood for a living. The relationship between the two families is harmonious and harmonious, and they have lived an ordinary and happy life. Sadly, none of them are childless. One spring, the Meng family planted a gourd at the foot of their house wall. Due to the good weather and rain, the gourds grew beautifully, and the branches extended all the way to the Jiang family's fence. In the fall, a huge gourd grows on the gourd seedlings that extend to the Jiang family's fence, which is really popular. Jiang Meng's family surrounded the gourd all day and prayed. Suddenly, a thunderbolt from the clear sky actually gave birth to a beautiful pink girl. The two families were very pleasantly surprised. The Meng family said that the gourds were grown by his family; the girl should be his family's; the Jiang family said that the gourds were on his wall, and the girl should have belonged to his family. As a result, the two families began to have an endless dispute. In the end, the two families came to a compromise and named the girl Meng Jiang Nu. Since then, they have both had daughters. Furthermore, this woman, Meng Jiang, is naturally well-behaved and cute, and she is also very good-looking. Unbeknownst to me, 18 years have passed, and the woman Meng Jiang has turned into a big girl, Ting Ting, and Yuli.
One day, when the sun was setting, the two families had just gone home gathering firewood. I saw a young man in shabby clothes hiding in front of the Meng family. It turns out that this person, whose name is Fan Xiliang, fled here to avoid the construction of the Great Wall. As a result, the well-intentioned Meng family took in Fan Xiliang. They saw that Fan Xiliang was loyal, honest, and talented. After discussions between the two families, they gave him Meng Jiang's daughter Xu. Unexpectedly, after some time, Fan Xiliang was taken to build the Great Wall by officers and soldiers of the Qin dynasty. The two elderly people wanted to cry without tears, and the woman Meng Jiang was even more heartbroken. She decided to look for her husband. When she came to the foot of the Great Wall, she heard that Fan Xiliang had died from exhaustion. The sad and angry woman Meng Jiang cried down on the Great Wall three times. On her way home, Meng Jiang Nu came to this small mountain, looked back at the crying Great Wall, and saw her home. She thought that Qin Wang would not let go of herself. She turned into a giant rock standing at the top of the mountain in anger. She looked like an infatuated woman facing northwest, forever watching over her husband who died by mistake. Since then, the mountain has had a nice name called Wangfu Stone Mountain.
Today, a giant rock still stands at the top of Wangfushi Mountain. Seen from afar, it looks like a passionate woman standing there looking back northwest. It's intoxicating and crying, making people feel sorry. And the thousand-year-old ginkgo tree not far from the foot of the mountain is known as the tree of love. The turquoise, barrier-like maozhu forest in the valley has also become a good place for young people today to talk about love. Mount Wangfu has also become a mountain of love. (Fan Xiaoying)
