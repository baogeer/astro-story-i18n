 During the Qin dynasty, there was a kind and beautiful woman named Meng Jiang Nu. One day, she was doing housework in her yard and suddenly discovered that there was someone hidden under the grape stand. She was about to shout. She was about to shout. She saw that person even waved his hand and begged, “Don't shout, save me! My name is Fan Xiliang, and I'm here to flee.” It turns out that at this time, Qin Shi Huang was arresting people everywhere to work as laborers in order to build the Great Wall. I don't know how many people have already starved to death and died of exhaustion! Lady Meng Jiang rescued Fan Xiliang. Seeing that he was well-educated and had a clear eye, she fell in love with him, and Fan Xiliang also fell in love with Meng Jiang. They have the same heart and are ready to become a couple after getting their parents' approval.
On the day they became a family, the Meng family was brightly lit and full of guests. It was a joyful scene. Seeing that it was almost dark, the people who enjoyed drinking gradually dispersed. The bride and groom were about to enter the cave room, and suddenly they only heard chickens and flying dogs barking. Afterwards, a team of vicious officers and soldiers broke in. It's hard to say that with a chain lock, Fan Xiliang was taken to the Great Wall to work. The happy event came to an end. The woman Meng Jiang was sad and angry, and missed her husband day and night. She thought: Instead of sitting at home and working in a hurry, I'd rather go to the Great Wall to find him myself. Right! Just do it! Lady Meng Jiang immediately packed up her bags and set out on her way.
Along the way, I don't know how many winds, rain, and snow, how many dangerous mountains and bad water she has trekked through. Meng Jiang Nu never shouted a single cry of bitterness or shed a single tear. Finally, with her tenacious perseverance and deep love for her husband, she reached the Great Wall. At this time, the Great Wall was already a long, long wall made up of construction sites one by one. Meng Jiang visited construction site by site, but she never saw a trace of her husband. Finally, she mustered up the courage and asked a team of migrant workers who were about to go to work, “Do you have a Fan Xiliang here?” The migrant worker said, “There is such a person, new here.” As soon as Meng Jiang Nu listened, I couldn't mention that she was so happy! She hurriedly asked, “Where is he?” The migrant worker said, “It's dead. The corpse capital has already filled the foot of the city!”
Hearing this bad news furiously, it was like a thunderbolt from a clear day. Lady Meng Jiang only felt dark in front of her eyes, felt sad for a while, and burst into tears. I cried for three days and three nights, crying so dark that even heaven and earth were moved. The sky was getting darker, and the wind was getting stronger. I only heard a “buzz”. The Great Wall fell down in tears. What was revealed was Fan Xiliang's corpse. Tears from Meng Jiang's eyes dripped onto his fuzzy face. She finally met her beloved husband, but he couldn't see her anymore because he had already been murdered by the cruel Qin Shi Huang.
