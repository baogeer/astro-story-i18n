 The sixth Dalai Lama, Kurayoshi Gatso, was an outstanding poet. He was an “unorthodox” lama in the eyes of “orthodox” people, so fate caused him to die prematurely. After his death, the 7th Dalai Lama was reincarnated in Litang, based on the sixth Dalai Lama poem: “A white crane, please lend me your wings. If you don't go far away, go back to Litang”. This almost lyrical poem became the secret to his reincarnation.
When he was reincarnated in Litang, he also added a hymn to Litang, “Don't see Litang as a poor beggar land; Buddha and Lama were born here.” Little Buddha, who has just been born, is naturally gifted. According to the fact that at the time, Lhasa Khan designated the reincarnation of the sixth Dalai Lama as his own legitimate child, but he sent people to harm the real Dalai. The young Dalai Lama was taken by his mother to escape the disaster and moved to Zhongdian.
The mother and son live at the foot of “Kari” Mountain, the source of the Duzi River. Every morning, when his mother started a fire, blue smoke drifted straight towards Songzanlin Temple. At this point, Little Spirit Boy said, “This is my temple over there.” While the mother and son of Ling Tong lived here, shepherds often worshipped fresh milk. Every time, the lama only drank half a bowl of milk and poured half a bowl of milk into the river. As a result, the entire river became milky white like a milk. “Milk River” is therefore named.
One night, Shozanrin-ji Temple received an order for the mother and son of a lama to be harmed. The abbot of the temple knew that the mother and child were extraordinary and should be protected, and sent a message to the spiritual child when entering at night. Early the next morning, Ling Tong said to his mother, “There will be a strong team of people coming today. If the leader asks you, is your son reincarnated as a living Buddha? If you answer them I am a beggar woman, how could I have given birth to a reincarnated living Buddha”.
Unsurprisingly, when the sun had just risen, the abbot brought a team of men and horses to the Ling Tong mother and son's home and asked in a menacing manner, “Is your son a reincarnated spirit?” Ling Tong's mother answered, “I'm a beggar woman, how could I give birth to a reincarnated living Buddha?” The abbot scolded: “Bah! Beggar woman, get out of here quickly!” At the same time, she threw away a bunch of chopsticks in the arms of Ling Tong's mother and grew up.
There were some silver dollars in this chopsticks. It was the abbot who secretly helped the Dalai Lama's spiritual boy and his mother get out of here. However, the powerful in Tibet said that no trace of reincarnated children has been found here, so let's deal with the past for the time being.
Later, the Ling Tong mother and son moved to Taar Temple in Qinghai with the help of the monks and customs of Songzanlin Temple in Zhongdian.
On this day, it was overcast and overcast. Kambu of Ta'er Temple was a well-practised monk, but he was irritated by the crying of a child outside the wall. He asked the housekeeper to take something out, discourage the child, and stop crying on the wall. After leaving, the housekeeper came back and complained that the kid didn't want anything; he had to come in. Kambu had no choice but to agree to the child entering the house. As soon as the child entered the house, Kanbu was surprised. Holding this child to see what he wanted. This kid wanted to take him into a sutra hall. After entering, the child had to be able to take him into a sutra hall. After entering, the child had to be able to take him to the sealed wooden bowl provided on his table. Kanbu only said, “Strange, unusual!” At the child's request, the sealed wooden bowl was removed and handed to the child. The child opened the mouth of the wooden bowl and drank the milk contained in it.
This milk was filled by the Dalai Lama himself in the past and sealed the wooden bowl as if it were a loaf of bread. Being opened and drunk by this kid today shows that this kid is extraordinary; he must have been reincarnated as the sixth Dalai Lama. As a result, after reporting to the Qing dynasty government, the Seventh Dalai Lama, under the protection of the Qing army and the monks of Tar Monastery, went to Lhasa to sit in his proper position.
The Seventh Dalai Lama couldn't forget the contributions of Zhongdian monks and customs to him. After he took control of government and education, the reward for Zhongdian was even greater. He built a new “Tsongkhapa Jinwa Hall” for Songzanlin Temple.
