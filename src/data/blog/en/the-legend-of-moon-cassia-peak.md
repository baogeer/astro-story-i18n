 During the Tang dynasty, on the night of the Mid-Autumn Festival one year, the round moon hung in the sky, shining brightly all over the world.
In the middle of the night, Shang Deming, the fire-burning monk at Lingyin Temple, got up and went to the kitchen to cook porridge. He heard a ticking sound. He thought it was strange. Looking out the window, the moon was shining brightly. Where did the sound of rain come from? So he opened the door and looked up, and saw countless small pearl-like particles falling from the moon one after another and falling on the mountain next to the temple. He watched, and when the small particles had finished falling, he went to the mountain to look for them. Find one, pick one. Find one, pick one... those small grains all bloom as large as a soybean. They are so colorful, so beautiful. He picked it up, picked it up, picked it up in the middle of the night until it dawned, and picked up a full pocket.
Early the next morning, Monk Deming showed the small particles he had picked up at night to the old monk Tomoichi and asked him what it was. The old monk Tomoichi took a close look and said, “There is a large laurel tree in the Moon Palace, and there is also a reckless man named Wu Gang. He cut down this laurel tree all year round, but he keeps cutting it down. Sometimes, if you try too hard, it will knock down the guisines. Maybe this is the persimmon tree that fell from Tsukimiya!”
Monk Deming was very happy to hear it and said, “Master, let's plant it so that everyone can see and see the laurel tree in the Moon Palace and smell the osmanthus in the Moon Palace.”
As a result, they planted those small, colorful particles on the hillside behind Teramaeji Temple. After a few days, they actually sprout; by the end of the month, the shoots grew a little saplings more than a foot tall, and the verdant leaves were extracted.
The laurel tree in the palace grew really fast this month. It grew one foot in January, one height a year, and by the Mid-Autumn Festival the following year, it grew tall and big. Every tree was full of small flowers. Some were orange, white, crimson, and all colors. According to the different colors of osmanthus flowers, Monk Deming called them golden cinnamon, silver laurel... Since then, there have been various types of osmanthus around West Lake.
Now, next to Lingyin Temple, there is a mountain called Yueguei Peak. Legend has it that the Moon Palace dropped the guysans back then.
