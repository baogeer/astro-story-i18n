 According to the elderly, many mountains were built when Pangu pioneered the world. On this day, after building Mount Tongbai, he was so tired that he lay down and fell asleep at the foot of Mount Bei.
Pangu slept and woke up to see a young girl in the distance. When the girl saw him awake, she called him, “Pangu! Pangu!” Strangely enough, Pangu asked, “Who are you?” The girl said, “I am the three daughters of Emperor Jade. My father saw you break new ground. He was too tired every day, alone and alone, and asked me to recognize you as my older brother. Would you like it or not?” Pangu smiled and said, “Yes.”
Since then, Pangu and Emperor Jade's three daughters have become brothers and sisters.
The two Pangu brothers and sisters live in their own hut made of branches and weeds, and are often bothered by monsters and wild animals. It took them 7,749 days of hard work to make a beautiful stone lion, put it on top of Tongbai Mountain, and also named this mountain Shishi Mountain. This one was guarded by a stone lion, so there were no monsters or wild animals to bother them, and the brother and sister had a better life.
Every day, Pangu picks wild fruits and grasps (luō) grass seeds on Shishi Mountain. He always has to play around the stone lion, touch the lion's body, and kiss the lion's face.
One day, when Pangu came to the mountains again, the stone lion suddenly said to Pangu, “Pangu! Starting today, you'll have to put a loaf in my mouth every day, don't forget it!” Pangu agreed.
The three daughters cook (lào) bread every day, and Pangu delivers bread every day. After 7 749 days, Pangu put 49 buns in Shi Zi's mouth.
On this day, Shi Lion spoke again: “Pangu, stop taking bread. As soon as my eyes blush, you can quickly call your sister to punch me in the stomach.”
The next day, when Pangu saw that Shi Lion's eyes were red, he immediately ran back and shouted for his sister. When the three daughters heard their older brother's urgent cry, they picked up the basket and ran in front of Shi Lion.
At this point, it was dark and dark clouds rolled over. Shi Lion's eyes glowed like a beacon, and Pangu's brother and sister ran ahead of him. They both ran and jogged. As soon as they ran in front of Shi Lion, it rained in the sky. The stone lion swallowed both of them in their stomachs as soon as they opened their mouths. In an instant, flames flashed in the sky, and the torrential rain fell down like a gust of wind. The more it rained, the sky broke down a long, long slack.
The rain kept “suddenly” flowing downwards. The trees were flooded, and the mountains collapsed. Only as the torrential rain grew longer, Shishi Mountain grew taller, and it was almost as high as the sky.
After 7 749 days of rain, the brother and sister finished eating 49 buns in Shi Lion's stomach. The stone lion opened his mouth and threw them both out.
Seeing the water all over Pangu, I asked Shi Lion: “Shishi, Shishi, did our brother and sister offend Emperor Jade and punish us?”
Shi Zi replied, “Your younger sister is the three daughters of Emperor Jade. After she came to Earth, there was a god with a kind and evil spirit in heaven. She also had to go down with the three daughters and claim king on earth. When Emperor Jade did not agree, he unlawfully colluded with Duke Lei, Prince Yu, and Feng Ba, and committed evil acts together. While Emperor Jade was not at home, he tore up the canopy and brought down heinous floods, hoping to drown your brothers and sisters.”
The Pangu brothers and sisters said, “Thanks to your help, otherwise, where would we be alive!”
Shi Lion said, “If you want to live forever, you still have to make up for the sky!”
The brother and sister asked, “What should I use to supplement it?”
Shi Zi said, “The axe handle that Pangu pioneered the world can be used as a golden needle to fill the sky. The battle at the top of this mountain is a golden thread that makes up the sky. Hurry up and make up for it; until late, the water will flood to the top of the mountain.”
After listening, Pangu and the three girls immediately stopped on Shishi's back, withstood the wind, got the needle alone, and made up for it. Make it up, make it up, make it up from here to there, and finally it's fixed. Everywhere they have been patched up, every needle is a star spot. The dense star spots on the Tianhe River are traces of Pangu and the three sisters making up the sky.
The sky made up, the rain stopped, and the water slowly receded. The Pangu brother and sister snuggled up to the lion and smiled. Shi Lion looked at the brother and sister's smiles and said, “I came down from heaven to do your work; it was all the intention of Emperor Jade. I'm going back to heaven soon. When I leave, I'll take care of my business again.”
The brother and sister said in unison, “What is it, just talk about it.”
Shi Zi said, “If you only have two brothers and sisters today, how can you continue for future generations if you don't become a couple?”
The brother and sister both twitched their faces and said, “No, no!” Shi Lion said, “Why didn't you win?”
“We are brother and sister, how can we become a couple?”
Shi Zi said, “The world is so good. If you don't let your children and grandchildren take charge of it, do you want monsters and monsters to run rampant forever?”
The three sisters listened and laughed. Pangu said, “Sister, don't say yes!” The third sister laughed again.
Pangu was angry. He turned around and was stumbled upon by a turtle that had just climbed ashore from the water. As soon as Pangu was furious, he picked up a stone and smashed the turtle's shell.
The three sisters crouched in pain in front of the turtle and cried. Pangu thought about it and wanted to say, “Well, if the turtle can be revived, brother and sister will become a family.” The third sister cried even more sad.
Shi Zi said, “Don't cry, the three girls, just face the turtles.” The three sisters then put together 48 small pieces of broken turtle shells. Then, as soon as the stone lion jumped, the soil splashed on the turtle shell, and the turtle shell immediately combined. Since then, the turtle shell has always had a neat pattern.
Arguably, if a turtle can be revived, brother and sister should become a family. But Pangu also said, “No, Stone Lion! I still have to do the rolling stones with my sister. The stone mill rolls down the mountain, and when they get together, brother and sister become a family.”
Shi Lion said, “Any crack in the sky can be repaired, and it is possible to roll a stone and become a parent. Let's start rolling!”
Pangu and her three daughters brought the stone mill from the mountain. Each one carried one and stood on the east and west mountains separately. The stone lion stood at the bottom of the mountain to hold its head a little, and the two stone mills rolled down the mountain one by one. The stone was rolling round and round, and a bright rainbow appeared in the sky. Strangely enough, this rainbow is like a ribbon, connecting the hearts of a brother and sister.
When the rainbow rose, the stone mill of the brother and sister rolled over and fell in front of the stone lion, making a “click” sound, making a strict and solid combination. As the grinding disc closes, the rainbow scattered, and flowers bloomed all over the ground.
As soon as they met, the Pangu brother and sister both ran down the mountain with joy. They knelt in front of a stone lion in the midst of a bunch of flowers, and they became husband and wife. Shi Zi said, “Your husband and wife will live a good life in the future, and future generations will always worship your husband and wife.”
As soon as Shi Lizi's speech fell, I saw that it spit white light, and it instantly turned into a blue smoke flying into the sky.
Pangu and her three sisters couldn't hear Shi Lion talking, so they started kneading clay people. There were tens of thousands of people kneading today, pinching tomorrow, and the courtyard was filled with sunshine.
As soon as Pangu fiddled with the clay man, the clay man could walk away or run away. When the three sisters blow their breath at the mud man, the mud man will talk. I called Dad again, and I called Mom again. Pangu and her three sisters were overjoyed.
On this day, Pangu discussed with her three sisters to give each clay person a name. At this point, I saw mud people running out of the yard. Some climbed onto a plum tree, some climbed onto a peach tree, some sat on a rock, and some stood by the river... Pangu and his three sisters said in unison, “OK! Everyone in the family is famous. That climbing on a plum tree is called Li; climbing on a peach tree is called a peach; sitting on a stone is called a rock; standing by the river is called a river...”
These clay people learned many skills along with Pangu and his three sisters. Pangu and his wife took the name of the clay man as their last name and sent them to various places to live their lives separately. Some of them go to flatland to cultivate, some go deep in the mountains to hunt, and some go fishing by the river...
As they grew older, future generations called Shishi Mountain, where Pangu and his wife lived, as Pangu Mountain. There is also a Pangu Temple on the mountain. The story of Grandpa Pangu and Pangu Milk has spread ever since.
