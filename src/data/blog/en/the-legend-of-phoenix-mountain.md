 A long, long time ago, two brothers and sisters lived at the foot of a mountain on the southern side of Xihu Lake. The older brother's name was Chunsheng, and the younger sister's name was Qiu Gu. When their parents died, they were left with three acres of land to rent and a broken hut. The brother and sister are cultivating this field in the middle of the night, and they always want to get more food. However, as soon as the autumn millet was ripe, the rich owner collected the rent. I paid the rent, and the rest wasn't enough to eat. There was nothing they could do. The brother and sister had to drink soup, eat porridge, and live a bitter life.
One year on New Year's 30th, a wealthy family, a table full of chicken, duck, and fish. At Haru Sheng's house, there is only one small cup of rice left. Qiu Gu then cooked a bowl of porridge for her brother to eat. Chunsheng said, “I'm not hungry, sister, have drunk it!” Qiu Gu said, “I'm not hungry, brother, drink it!” Brothers and sisters, if you push me for it, no one wants to drink it.
At this point, heavy snow was flying all over the sky, and the north wind was roaring fearfully. An old woman begging came to Fuyuki. Her hair was white, her clothes were ragged, and she was wearing crutches, step by step, and shouted, “North wind sky, white snow! Kind people, feel sorry for my old lady!” This hoarse voice came through the thatched room, and the brother and sister could hear it clearly. Qiu Gu said, “Brother, how pitiful you are to listen!” Chunsheng said, “Little sister, let's ask her to come inside.” Speaking of which, I hurriedly opened the door and brought this strange old woman into the house. Qiu Gu was busy dusting off the snowflakes on her body. Chunsheng picked up that bowl of porridge and gave her a drink for half a day.
The old woman stayed overnight at their house. The next day, the snow stopped, and the sky cleared up. She got up and said goodbye. When she left, her wife took out a piece of white linen and gave it to Qiu Gu and said, “Girl, embroider this piece of white silk with your dexterous hands. Happiness is bound to be for hardworking and kind people.” Gu Qiu took a look, only to see a faint picture of a phoenix on the white ring.
As a result, Qiu Gu embroidered this white canvas day and night. She embroidered phoenix heads with red silk thread, phoenix eyes with black silk thread, phoenix wings with golden silk thread, and phoenix tails with multicolored silk thread. Embroidered and embroidered. The embroidery needle pierced her finger. Blood was stained on white silk, and she embroidered the red sun and blooming clouds on it. From early spring embroidery to early summer, she finally embroidered a phoenix.
This picture of a phoenix is really beautiful. The phoenix looks back on its head and faces the blazing red sun in the sky, just like a living one. The brother and sister hung it in the house. The more they watched it, the happier they were, and the more they loved it. At night, something strange happened. When Qiu Gu woke up in the middle of the night, she saw a golden light in the house. After taking a close look, the phoenix came down from the picture. She woke her brother up, and the brother and sister watched quietly. I saw that the phoenix walked around the house for a few circles and went back to the white seal, and the golden light disappeared along with it.
The next morning, Qiu Gu got up and swept the floor and found a golden phoenix egg on the ground. The brother and sister sold it and bought a few acres of fields and a scalper. As the saying goes, “Good things spread far and wide. The phoenix picture incident spread like a gust of wind to the ears of the county officials. The county official thought: The phoenix in this picture can give birth to golden eggs. It's really a rare treasure; I must get my hands on it! As a result, he brought in Chunsheng and said, “The old man raised you and was willing to pay 32 silver to buy your phoenix picture.”
Chunsheng replied, “My sister put a lot of effort into embroidering the picture of the phoenix; we don't sell it!”
The county official listened, sank down, patted Shadangmu, and said, “This is clearly the emperor's treasure; how can a poor family embroider such a treasure map!” I couldn't help but add a charge of “stealing a national treasure” and imprisoned Chun. On the other hand, they ordered the government to go to Chunsheng's house to grab Phoenix pictures.
As soon as the Phoenix Map arrived, the county officials were really proud. He looked left and right, forgot to even eat, and laughed so much that he laughed. This night, he sat in Taishi's chair and waited for the phoenix to lay the golden egg. In the middle of the night, the picture of the phoenix suddenly shone dazzling light, shining all over the house. The phoenix certainly came down from the picture. He thought the phoenix was about to lay eggs, so he was busy crouching down to see it. No matter how aggressive the phoenix was, pecked without a head, and in pain, it made him roll around on the ground and shouted, “Hey, come here! Save my life!” When the government officials heard the sound, the phoenix had already made a “wow” sound and flew out of the window towards the mountain. The government office lifted the county official off the ground, only to see that his face was full of blood, and his left eye was pecked blindly.
The county officials have suffered this loss, but they have not given up their heart. He thought: If that girl could embroider this picture of a phoenix, she would definitely be able to embroider a second one. As a result, he handed over Qiu Gu and said to her, if he could re-embroider a picture of the phoenix, he would release Chunsheng. In order to save her brother, Qiu Gu agreed. She took back the piece of white silk from the county official and embroidered it stitch by stitch for three months, and the gorgeous phoenix was embroidered again. However, she left a pair of phoenix eyes that were not embroidered. She told the county official that she should let go of her brother and then embroider phoenix eyes.
The prefectural official called the government government to let spring students out. When Qiu Gu saw her brother, she immediately became a phoenix eye. Once this phoenix had eyes, it spread its wings and flew up to the top of the mountain carrying its brother and sister, and disappeared.
Later, people called this mountain “Fenghuang Mountain.”
