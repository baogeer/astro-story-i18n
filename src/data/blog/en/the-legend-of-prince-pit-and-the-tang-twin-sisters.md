 In the late years of the Northern Song Dynasty, Jin Soldiers attacked Lin'an in Kyoto in Southern Song and passed through the Tongling border crossing in the ancient Tea Horse Trail, which was infested by Huishang on the border between Zhejiang and Anhui in ancient times. At that time, there was a cottage built by the Shes people on the mountain. Locals called it Tang Zhai. Although Tang Zhai is said to be a local tycoon, he is good at helping the poor and the weak and doing good deeds. Therefore, he is highly respected. Locals call him Tang Gong. There is a pair of twins Qianjin under their lap. They look handsome. They have the beauty of a closed flower, a red fish, and a falling geese. However, he likes to be good at dancing with guns. He has excellent martial arts, wisdom and intelligence.
When the Golden Soldiers invaded, the Southern Song dynasty dispatched the Prince to fight the invasion around the Tongling Guan Ancient Battlefield on the periphery of Lin'an for the safety of Lin'an in Kyoto. At the same time, Tang Yuanwai, the enlightened owner of the village, was also a golden soldier who brought his beloved daughter, Jia Ding, and the officers and soldiers of Southern Song together to resist foreign invasions.
They have been in love with each other for a long time. Tang Yuan and others almost simultaneously fell in love with the prince sent by the Southern Song dynasty. When Jin Bing secretly attacked the Southern Song coach's tent, Tang Yuan's two beloved daughters were all shot dead by Jin Bing's random arrows in order to protect the prince. However, the prince was rescued by his guards. In the end, under the strong obstruction of Southern Song officers and soldiers and Tang Yuanwai's family, they won a partial victory, leaving Jin Bing to no avail.
The prince's class returned to North Korea and was crowned prince. However, the Crown Prince of Southern Song did not do what his father and emperor wanted. After returning to North Korea and handing over military power, he quietly returned to Tongling Pass, where the battlefield was located. With the help of local Shes mountain people, a Tang Clan Niang Niang Temple was renovated on the site of the Huangmiao Temple on Shiyanling, which is 1,130 meters above sea level, to express their love for the two Miss Qianjin outside Tang Yuan. Since then, the mountain located at Tang's Niangniang Temple is also known as “Mount Tanggong.”
Meanwhile, the Crown Prince is alone in the mountains between Shiyan Ridge and Lianhualing in Tanggong Mountain. Every day, she worships Lady Tang on Shiyan Ridge.
Year after year, yin and yang are separated. The Prince's deep obsession was also moved by the sister flower at Niangniang Temple on Shiyan Ridge. As a result, she washed her face with tears every day, and eventually became two clear springs, which slowly flowed into the mountains where the Crown Prince lived, and was in line with the Crown Prince's heart. Since the Crown Prince continues to wear incense every day, fragrance fog also forms. Incense ash also blocks another outlet surrounding the mountain on three sides, thus naturally forming the Longtan Tiger Cave in the middle of the mountain.
After the Crown Prince grew old in the mountains, villagers in the mountains built the “Prince's Mound” in the mountains where they lived to commemorate the Crown Prince of Southern Song. Also, since local mountain people avoid the word “mound,” it is commonly called “Taizikeng.”
As the years have changed, the two clear springs in Niangniang Temple have flooded the clear spring from the “Taizizuka” in the mountains, sprinkled all over the mountain, along with the incense ash that the Crown Prince had incense. As a result, every spring season, the entire mountain had bright Yingshan red, so it became the “Tanggong Mountain Thousand Mu Rhododendron Sea” attraction where influencers check in today.
