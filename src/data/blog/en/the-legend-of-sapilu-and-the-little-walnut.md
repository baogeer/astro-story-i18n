 Li Xiaobo
Legend has it that among the mountains on the west bank of the Yangjiangjiang River, there is a mountain called Puying Mountain, and there is a village called Sangmi's Card at the bottom of the mountain. I don't know what happened. In recent years, there have been a lot of people with lung disease in the village. Every year, several people die from lung disease, and many people have white hair when they are around 40 years old. A young man named Ah in the village was even more miserable. His hair was all white when he was under 30, and he became a “white teenager.” Ah Zi's parents died early, and they lived their lives in the East Family's house, asking for a meal at a Western family house. As an adult, he often helped others with their work, never considered pay, and was an honest and kind person. Sometimes they go up the mountain to hunt some prey to make ends meet, but they often distribute the prey they get to the poor. He never harms old, weak, pregnant, or young animals when he hunts.
One day, Ah Zi went hunting in the mountains again. There was nothing at home to satisfy his hunger. If there were no more harvests, he would be hungry. He had bad luck that day. He only saw a little white rabbit on his way home. He excitedly raised the arrow in his hand and carefully aimed at it. Only then did he discover that it was a pregnant female rabbit. He had to give up the idea of hunting rabbits and went home empty-handed.
That night, Ah Ji had a strange dream. She dreamed that the pregnant rabbit had turned into a fairy girl. The girl asked him to go to the Yangyi River to find walnuts to cure the illness of Sangmi's card person, but he couldn't let anyone know about this; otherwise, he would be faced with a disaster. I woke up the next day and the dream was still clear. Ah, I've never heard of walnuts, and I don't believe in dreams. Furthermore, the Yangjiangjiang River is more than 20 days away from Sangmi's card.
However, for four or five nights in a row, Ah Ji always had the same dream, so she decided to take a look at the Yangjiao River. He borrowed some bitter soba from the person next door, kneaded a few tartary buckwheat noodles, and hit the road. After 23 days of walking, Ah Ji finally arrived at the Yangjiao River. But where to find walnuts, he sat and rested on a stone beside the Yangjiang River.
Ah Shi's butt wasn't hot yet. He saw a thick bowl of wood rushing from the upper reaches of the Yangjiao River. If you take a closer look, the tree still holds a person. He jumped into the rolling Yangjiang River without even thinking about it. Strangely enough, Ah Zi, who had never been in the water, actually didn't sink down. As soon as he pulled both his feet and hands, he reached the front of the tree. As soon as he grabbed the wood and worked hard, the wood dockly leaned ashore, and then he dragged the man ashore. He only discovered when he was exposed to the surface of the water that he had saved was a young girl in her 20s. The girl's clothes have been washed away by the river, leaving only a few pieces of rags to cover her private parts. Ah Zi is almost 30 years old. I've never seen a woman without clothes; all of a sudden, her face turned red. Fortunately, the girl hasn't woken up; otherwise, he doesn't know what to do. Ah Ji wanted to leave, but she couldn't just leave this girl alone; she had to take off her patched coat and put it on for her, and found dry firewood to start a fire on the riverside, hoping she would be a little warmer.
Shortly after, the girl woke up, and Ah Ji gave her one of the tartary buckwheat noodles she had brought. With strength, she said to Ah Ji, “Thank you for saving me, uncle, I don't know how to repay your old man.” Seeing that she woke up, Ah Zi didn't move forward; she only said, “Don't thank me; how can anyone see death or be saved. Besides, I'm not an 'old man'.” Next, he told the girl how he had dreamed, how he had arrived at the Yangyi River, and his own situation. The girl sighed at his life and was both grateful and full of sympathy for him. Thinking about her own life, she couldn't help but cry. She and he were really suffering. The girl also told Ah Zi about herself.
It turns out that the girl's name is Sapilu. Her family lives in Rafzikou, opposite Shimen Pass in the upper reaches of the Yangjiao River. Her father died early and is dependent on her mother. Two days ago, she went to the mountain to cut firewood, but was caught by a group of outsiders, saying they wanted to rob her as a daughter-in-law. The villages near them all have the custom of robbing their families. Many women have been robbed to become their daughters-in-law, but they have no choice but to confess. She thought that her old mother in her family was helpless, so when those people let their guard down, she fled. She didn't expect to run to the edge of the Yangjiao River and be overtaken. She saw no way out and jumped into the Yangjiao River. Thanks to her good water supply, she floated in the river for two days and two nights when she grabbed a piece of wood and didn't let the river pour into her mouth. In the end, when she passed out, she died and held on to the wood until she was saved by an Arab word.
Sapiru wanted to go home to visit her mother. Ah Shi had to go with her. He returned the Sangmi card on his own. He never mentioned saving people by the Yangyi River.
Half a year later, however, Sapiru went to Sang Mi's card to ask people where her home was. When she met someone, she said that she was Ah Shi's daughter-in-law. It turns out that after Sapiru broke up with Ah Zi, it took more than half a month to return home. Her mother heard that she had been robbed, then jumped on the Yangjiangjiang River and fainted on the spot. Due to her original illness and excessive sadness, she left the world two days later, and her neighbors buried her. When Sapiru got home, all she saw was a bunch of new graves. She almost followed her mother and was stopped by her neighbors. It was only a few months later that Sapiru came out of the grief of losing her mother, thinking that Ah Zi's life-saving grace had not been repaid, so she came to him. She was afraid that Ah Shi wouldn't accept her, so when she met someone in the village, she said that she was Ah Shi's daughter-in-law, so he couldn't push him away. Ah Ji knew the truth of the matter, and he never forgot Sapiru, so he accepted her. The two have been living together ever since.
Sapiru actually brought something called a walnut. After getting married, Sapiru planted walnuts outside their door. Although the days were difficult, the two were also happy. Within a few years, the walnut tree in front of the gate came to fruition. Sapiru made Ah Ji eat walnuts every day, and also made walnuts into soup and distributed them to the weak people in the village. Two years later, Ah Zi's white hair turned black. The number of people who ate walnuts in the village also gradually decreased. No one died of lung disease that year. Previously, people with Sangmi's card had never seen walnuts; some people secretly called it a miracle fruit.
The walnuts that Sapiru brought to Sangmi's card blessed people, but they annoyed one person. This is Baru, the only drugstore owner of Sangmi's Card. In the past, people in the village were so sick that they wanted to go to Balu's drugstore to get medicine. Since Sapilu gave people the walnut soup, fewer people in the village got sick, and his income was low, so he held a grudge and thought of a toxic plan to mislead Sapiru. Balu found a pen pattern in the village and said that Sapiru is a fairy. After being with her, Ah Ji's gray hair turned black. This is something he had never heard of. Also, the walnut tree in front of Sapiru's house also has a magical aura; anyone who lies under the walnut tree will get sick. Bimo experienced this personally. That time, he went to Arji to help him with his work. He sat under a walnut tree in front of his house for two hours. He didn't expect to get seriously ill when he got home. As soon as Balu mentioned this, he believed it and decided to burn Sapiru to death along with her walnut tree.
If you want to deal with Sapiru, you have to pass the level of Aji, who will never burn his wife to death. They finally waited for their chance. One day, Balu heard the words in Arab to help others, and it took four or five days to return. They then treated Sapiru with poison. Although people in the village know that Sapiru is not a fairy, and the walnut tree doesn't have any magic, who dared not listen to what Bimo said, they had no choice but to watch Sapiru being tied to a walnut tree, and someone secretly went to inform Ah Si.
When Ah Ji hurried back, Sapiru and the walnut trees in front of the gate had already burnt to ashes. When he learned that all of this was a ghost behind Balu, he debunked his sinister intentions in front of the whole village, and then told everyone how he let go of the pregnant little white rabbit back then and how he met Sapiru by the Yangliang River. He also told people that Sapilu said that walnut trees have been planted along the Yangjiao River for many years. Walnuts have the effects of nourishing the skin, nourishing qi and blood, moisturizing phlegm, and warming the lungs and intestines. These have already been written into medical books; it's just that people with the Sangmi Card don't know. After saying these things, Ah Ji discovered that his eyes couldn't see anything. He knew that this was God's punishment for him. Now that Sapiru has left him, he doesn't want to live any longer, so he hit a stone in front of the door and followed Sapiru.
After listening to the words of the Arabic word, Bimo regretted it. I hurt the two young people by listening to the words, so that they could be buried well. They also buried Shappilu's clothes with him, so they could live together underground. Slowly, people gradually understood the circumstances of the incident. It must have been the fairy in heaven who saw the Sang Mi card people suffer, let the kind Ah ji go find Sappilu, and then pass on the walnuts to the people of Sangmi's card. I didn't expect the ignorant and ignorant Sangmi Card people to harm the two young people. I don't know what kind of disaster the Sanmi Card will have in the future.
In the spring of the following year, the burnt walnut tree germinated again. After a few years, the walnut tree bore fruit. However, the walnut fruits it grew smaller, and matured more than a month earlier than the original walnuts, and ripened after the summer season. Sangmi's card people call this kind of early ripe, small-fruited walnut Sappilu. “Sapilu” is a Yi dialect. Translated into Chinese, it means “little walnut.” This kind of walnut is also called small walnut.
Today, this kind of small walnut, which is known by many people, is widely cultivated throughout the county. Now there are walnuts that ripen early but are quite small; people in Yang Yi are still used to calling them small walnuts.
