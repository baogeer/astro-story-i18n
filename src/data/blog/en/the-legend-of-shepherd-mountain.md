 About 40 kilometers northeast of Mingguang, there is a mountain about 180 meters above sea level on the east shore of Lake Qili. This mountain winds ten miles north and south. Like a giant dragon prodding down on the shores of Qili Lake, it is now called Guanman Lake. In a square circle, the trumpet of ploughing fields, the sound of shepherds playing the flute, and fishing songs on the surface of the water can be called something different. The people live a peaceful and comfortable life. Here's a story about King Chu Huai, which has been passed down from generation to generation among the local people.
Legend has it that Xiong Xin, the grandson of King Chu Huai, went into hiding and herded sheep in a small village called Niegang on the east bank of Qili Lake in order to take refuge. There is plenty of pasture on the Shili Hillside, and children from many surrounding villages also herd cows and sheep here. My stomach is full of stories because the bear is older, Sven treats people well and is kind. Every day they graze, the children love to circle around the bear and tell stories to him. At the beginning, in order to let children know about human ancestors and the life situations of their ancestors, Xiong Xin often said, “In the past, there was no king. People gathered in groups; their mother didn't know their father; there was no difference between male and female relatives, siblings, and couples; there was no way to grow up and grow up.” The children couldn't understand, let alone the people who told the story to them, used the original period to criticize the criminal current events of war in the late Qin period, where everyone was equal and uncontested, and you fought and killed each other. The kids want him to talk nicely and playfully. As a result, he grazed every day, and Xiong Xin also told the children some stories of “Zhou Wu and Wang Destroyed Shang” and “Zhou Gongdan's March to the East”, told the historical events of the “Five Masters and Seven Heroes” during the Spring and Autumn period, the story of Zheng Zi born in 536 BC to “save the world”, and the story of “Confucius Missionary Travels Across the World”, etc. The children all love to listen, and some slightly older shepherds sometimes tell the villagers when they get home. The adults were very surprised to hear the children's stories. When they asked them exactly, they learned that the shepherd bear was telling it. People thought that Xiong Xin was extraordinary and laid-back, and gradually treated them differently.
On one day, several elders from the village met to discuss matters. They wanted Xiong Xin to set up a lecture hall in the village to teach the children to read, learn the sutras, and learn calligraphy. Xiong Xin gladly agreed. Since then, shepherds and village children have been studying every afternoon, and the shores of Qilihu have been reading loud every day. The news spread quickly, and villagers in the surrounding area also sent their children to study. There is a school on the shores of Qili Lake, but something new since the opening of the world in Pangu. The school is full of wisdom, and the omniscient gods have spread along with it.
The conversation is divided into two parts. Furthermore, in late Qin, Chen Sheng and Wu Guangzexiang ignited the uprising and suffered. The public, who were in deep heat, raised their forks and spears one after another. The rebels quickly grew from a few hundred people to have 700 military vehicles and more than a thousand horses, and tens of thousands of soldiers besieged the city and captured the town. During the conquest of Chen County (Huaiyang, Henan), he stood on his own feet and took the name “Zhang Chu”. Many old aristocrats from the six countries, such as Wei Yu, Zhang Er, Zhou Wen, and Wu Chen, joined the rebels. The old aristocrats of the Six Kingdoms each harboured ghosts, and each recruited troops and bought horses. They wanted to restore the situation in the Six Middle School split era, and then stand on their own feet as kings. There were two powerful armed forces at the time. One was Xiang Yu, representing the old aristocracy in Chu, and the other was Liu Bang, representing the middle and lower echelons. At the time, they all wanted to be king. In the name of deceiving the world, Xiang Yu's military master Fan Zeng and Liu Bang's sergeant Xiao He each made the same suggestion to their master: return Chu Huai and Wang Xiongxin and embrace him as your monarch.
Xiang Yu and Liu Bang each sent people to various places to inquire about Xiong's whereabouts. After some time, Xiang Yu and Liu Bang both heard the news that there was a shepherd on the east bank of Qili Lake. They knew everything about the world. Based on the legendary physical characteristics analysis, they all came to the conclusion that the shepherd was a bear heart. Xiang Yu was one step ahead and found Xiong Xin first, so he established a king, still calling Xiong Xin King Chu Huai. Later, Liu Bang also found King Chu Huai. Since Liu Bang also represented a certain influence of Chu, he worked with King Chu Huai with Xiang Yu. In the face of these two powerful forces, King Chu Huai was not good at favoring which side, so he agreed “Wang Zhi, the one who entered the customs first.” This led to the historical “Chu-Han War”. After Chu Huai Wang Xiong Xin left Guanshan, the local people learned that the shepherd was originally the king of a country. In order for future generations to remember this story, people named the mountain on the east shore of Qili Lake “Shepherd Mountain.” Until the “Cultural Revolution”, the school songs of some surrounding elementary schools had the lyrics “Muyang Mountain is always clear, the Huai River flows long”.
