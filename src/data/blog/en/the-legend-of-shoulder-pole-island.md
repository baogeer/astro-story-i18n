 “Cultivate a Family, Build a Business with Diligence”. On Biantan Island located between Kongtong Island and Zhifu Island, people love to post this kind of couplet during the Spring Festival. It is said that there is a beautiful legend behind this.
Every year on the night of Tanabata, the cowherd takes a pair of children and a pair of children to the riverside to meet the weaver. This sincere love has moved countless passionate men and women. On the night of July 7, they look up at the starry sky and bless them both. After learning about it, Taibai Baixing went to inspect. In a cottage in the countryside, he saw an honest cowherd leading the lives of the two children. It was very difficult and very sympathetic. He made the cowherd go to Tiangong University to further his studies, wait to obtain a diploma, arrange a job, and transfer him and the child to Tiangong and the Weaver to reunite.
After the cowherd arrived at the school, he saw that his classmates invited me to spend time drinking, eating, drinking, and having fun. At first, he was very unaccustomed to it. After a long time, I was envious, and gradually became flustered, and the more I looked at that portable burden, the more I was displeased: “Compare others, look at yourself, what kind of days we have lived before! Isn't it said that the book has its own Golden House, and the book has its own Yan Ruyu? By the time I get the title of the gold list, after putting on the black hat, the fairy in the sky can marry me, and the weaver should take a break. What use is this flat bag...” He just threw that flat bag out of the window. As a result, along with him for more than ten years, he fell right in the middle between Kongtong Island and Zhifu Island, and took on new uses and took charge of these two islands. After the cowherd lost his flat load, on July 7 of this year, of course, he didn't pick a pair of children to meet the weaver on Magpie Bridge.
After all, the cowherd is old, and he really can't read books. However, after being a cowboy for the rest of his life, he was not satisfied. To get over his official addiction, he tore up the book and folded it into a black hat made of exquisite paper. I also found scissors, cut two long black hat poles out of cardboard, put them on both sides of the hat, wear them on the head, and look in the mirror to appreciate myself.
When Taibai Jinxing learned that the cowherd hadn't arrived on schedule on the night of July 7 of that year, and the weaver was heartbroken, she went to the cowherd to reprimand. It just so happened that I saw the cowherd wearing a paper hat, walking around, and there were words in his mouth: “Be an official, be a good official, let me pick the fairy in the sky...” He was furious and slapped the cowherd hard and knocked down the feather on the left side of the black hat. I saw that Guanling drifted leisurely down and fell into the sea. It just happened to be stacked with the cowherd's flat load, and it became an island in the shape of a flat carrying.
Later, people on Biantan Island posted a couplet during the Spring Festival, and used “cultivate the family, work diligently and frugally” to admonish their children and grandchildren to study and read, gain fame, and not forget the duty of cultivating people. (Text: Liu Yansheng)
