 Long ago, Hangzhou was still a dry beach. I haven't seen a single creek in the surrounding area for tens of miles, nor can I find a single creek. Every day, ordinary people who live here have to carry buckets to go far and wide to fetch water. They are sad day and night just for this little bit of water; they have never had a happy life.
People in the older generation know that there is a clear spring in the back mountain of Lingyin because it is blocked by a very thick, very thick stone wall and can't flow out. Over the years, there have been many young people who have gone up the mountain and tried to cut through that stone wall, but they have had no success. Slowly, fewer people are getting to know it.
There is a young man in Houcun named Shui Er. He has had no parents since childhood. He grew up with his old grandpa. Since he was six years old, he has been picking water with his grandfather. It's been a full 15 years now. This day was Shui Er's 20th birthday. The old grandpa made him a big pot of noodles, and the two grandchildren had a happy and happy day. At night, Grandpa summoned Shui Er in front of him and told him everything about the clear spring in the back mountain of Ling Yin. Shui Er was happy to hear it. After tightening her belt and pulling up her sleeves, she called up a few good young people. Everyone discussed going up the mountain to cut a stone wall; they must let this clear spring flow into the village. Grandpa saw that Shui Er had such ambition. He was so happy that he shed tears. He spent the night preparing dry food, so that they could go up the mountain early tomorrow morning.
The next day, Shui Er, a group of 10 people, armed with hammers and chisels, prepared to go up the mountain. As they left, the old grandpa said to the boys, “If you go and cut the stone wall, you have to cut it down in one go. If you stop, it will grow back to its original appearance, then it will be in vain. Also, when the stone wall is cut through, a splash of stone is ejected from the inside, and when sprayed on the body, it will condense people into stones. You must remember that!” As soon as they agreed, Mizue went up the mountain.
When they got to the top of the mountain, they immediately began to cut the stone wall by hand. Chisel, chisel. From digging in Qingming in March to Duanyang in May, the chisel was a bit short, and blood bubbles formed on both hands; the stone wall still hasn't been cut through. Four young men said, “Maybe Grandpa misunderstood the place. How can there be any clear springs here! Let's go back and pick one out of the bucket!” Said he had gone home.
There are six of them left. From Duanyang in May to Mid-Autumn Festival in August, the chisel was a bit shorter, and their hands were covered with thick cocoons, yet the stone wall still hasn't been cut through. Two young men said, “Cut it to the end in one go. Who knows when it will be excavated, and the family should go back and check it out!” After talking about it, I went back.
Autumn is over, winter is here, and the snow falls more than half as deep on the mountain. The northwest wind is calling, stabbing people like sharp knives. Mizuer, the four of them didn't feel relieved. Chisel, chisel. It continued until the spring of the following year. When the rhododendrons were blooming red, the stone wall had already been dug deep, very deep.
This day was March 3. Shui Er suddenly heard a loud noise on the other side of the stone wall. He brought his ear close to the stone wall and couldn't help but scream: “Ah! spring water! This is the sound of spring water flowing!” Everyone jumped for joy, too. Shui looked back and said to her friends; “Hurry up and go, the stone slurry will be spewed out!” Seeing that the spring water is about to flow out, no one can bear to leave it. When Shui Er saw that everyone refused to leave, she screamed in a hurry, “If you don't run away again, I won't stop!” When everyone heard him say this, they were afraid that he would actually stop, lose all his previous achievements, and run away. At this point, Shuier hit the final blow, and only heard a loud “thumping” sound. The stone slurry spewed out, condensing the water into a stone man more than three feet tall! Next, a stream of clear spring water cascades down the valley, flows through the village, and fills a depression by the beach. The depression is filled to the brim — this is the current West Lake.
Since then, this place has never worried about running out of water. The mountain that was cut through the water was later called “Shiren Ridge.”
