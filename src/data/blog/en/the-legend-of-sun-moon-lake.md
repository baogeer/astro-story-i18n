 In the old days, a young couple lived along Daqingxi in Taiwan. The man's name is Da Zhan Ta, and the woman's name is Sister Mizuisha. They both lived fishing, and had a very sweet, very sweet life.
At noon one day, suddenly there was a loud noise. The sun disappeared, and Daqingxi became muddy. At night, it was another loud noise, and the moon was gone again. Since then, day and night at Daqingxi have been dark and dark. The seedlings have turned yellow, and the fruit has not borne fruit. Big fangs sighed, “How are you living this day?” When Sister Shuishe saw her husband sigh, she said, “The sun and the moon must have all fallen to the ground. Let's go find it, we want the light back.” So the couple held torches, turned over a mountain, through the jungle, and walked through many turbulent rivers...
On this day, they climbed a big mountain. I finally saw the pool bright and dark from afar. The couple immediately rushed to the edge of Tai Tam. Suddenly, I saw a pair of giant dragons swimming around the pool, swallowing the sun and moon and making strange noises, just like playing a big water polo.
The more the couple watched, the more annoyed they agreed to kill the dragon and take back the light. They walked forward and suddenly found a cave. The big fangs said, “This hole is probably the home of a dragon.” Speaking of which, they both drilled into the cave and walked for a long time, and finally saw the bright light and saw a caring and well-intentioned wife cooking. The couple explained their intention to their wife. The wife admired their bravery and told them that she had been swept into the cave by an evil tornado and cooked for the dragon every day. They also told them that evil dragons are afraid of the Golden Axe and Golden Scissors at the bottom of Alishan Mountain. After speaking, the wife gave them both an iron shovel and a fire fork. The couple quit their mother-in-law and traveled day and night to the foot of Alishan Mountain, trying desperately to dig and dig, cut and chisel. I don't know how long it took them, and they built a small mountain behind them. One day, a red light came out of the deep hole, and the Golden Axe and Golden Scissors appeared! They both picked up a golden axe and golden scissors, ran out of the hole, and went straight to the pool where Ssangyong lived.
At this point, the big fangs threw the golden axe down the pool and only heard a loud “roar”. The two evil dragons rolled over from the bottom of the pool, setting off waves several feet high. Sister Shuishe hurriedly threw the Golden Scissors down the pool. She only heard a loud “snap” sound. The two evil dragons were cut off, and the sun and moon were spewed out of her mouth.
Dajian Ya and the water company sister swallowed the dragon's eyes according to the method taught by her grandmother. Suddenly, they got taller and bigger. They picked up the sun and moon and threw them back into the sky with force. Since then, the sun and moon have taken turns shining brightly, and people have begun to live a normal life again. However, in order not to let the sun and moon fall back into the pool. The couple kept their hands on the edge of the pool. Year after year, Dajian Ya and Shui She became two majestic mountains.
Future generations called this pool Sun Moon Lake. These two mountains are called Dajian Mountain and Shuishe Mountain.
