 Here, every Ching Ming Festival, there is always a storm as scheduled. People talk about this natural weather phenomenon as a special excavation to sweep graves. Among the Zhuang people around Daming Mountain, this beautiful legend of filial piety and gratitude has been passed down from generation to generation since ancient times.
A long, long time ago, at the foot of Daming Mountain, there was a large small basin. Surrounded by mountains on all sides, the terrain is low in the southeast and high in the northwest, sloping like a chipped wooden box; the terrain is flat, the streams flowing down from the mountains are criss-crossed, the land is fertile and rich in products; the climate is spring-like, and the natural environment is as beautiful as a wonderland. The beautiful scenery of the mountains and clear water here is better than heaven. For thousands of years, the hardworking and simple Zhuang people have thrived here, like being in a hideaway.
Gu Nongzhuang has an old grandmother with no children. People call her “Ling Ying Grandma.” One winter, the old man came back from the mountain and saw a snake with a broken tail on the side of the road. It was dying; the old man looked and watched, and felt very sorry for it. Out of pity, he picked it up and put it in a bamboo basket and put some straw on it, then hurried back after the incident.
At night, the harsh cold wind was mixed with heavy drizzle. The next one continued, and the north wind blew loudly on the roof; the house was overcast and cold, and the long winter nights seemed even slower, longer, and more difficult...
In the low house, the old man sat alone in his bed. Maybe it was the snake waiting to be picked up, or maybe it was the habit of staying up late alone. Deeper and quieter, the old man's home is hazy and hazy in his dream———
The sun was shining brightly, and Mrs. Ling Ying and her son enjoyed lunch at home; after that, they had clear eyes and were handsome, and the house was full of joy and the sun was shining brightly; the magpies kept shouting on the roof, and the chickens in the courtyard were full of joy. What a happy and peaceful atmosphere...
Grandma Ling happily walked slowly and leisurely in the courtyard, sometimes looking at the surrounding environment of the village, and sometimes at the blue sky and white clouds of the mountains. But as I walked, my foot suddenly slipped, and it felt like I had fallen; now my dream was awakened. The old man raised his head and took a look. It turned out that the sky was already bright. When she got up, she first looked at the snake in the bamboo basket, but she looked again and again. The snake was gone; she only saw straw inside; then she looked inside and outside the house a few times, but there was no trace of the snake.
At 7 or 8 o'clock, Mrs. Ling Ying finished eating the porridge and thought to herself, maybe the snake hurt better and left on her own. The sun gradually rose, and the old man continued her work for a new day. A dull, lonely life day in and day out, where the sun rises and sets to rest. Spring comes and autumn comes, and in a blink of an eye, another four or five years have passed.
One day, as the sun sets, the sky will be dark. Lady Ling walked down the mountain at a steady pace and into the village. Entering my courtyard and opening the firewood door, the house was actually lit up. I saw that the meal had already been set up, and the aroma was pouring out. Seeing this scene, the old man was so strange and shocked, one of the students called, “Grandma, you can come back. You've worked a day. Are you tired? I've already cooked the meals, so let's eat.” Later generations greeted the old man respectfully. Grandma Ling Ying looked left and right. In her blurry eyes, there was indeed an older brother standing in front of her and calling her, and her voice was very kind.
At that time, Grandma Ling was surprised and happy; she didn't know what to do; later students went on to say, “Grandma, I have no parents or siblings in the world. I'd like to be a mother and son with your old family. What do you think of your old man's family?” “Ah, Moffito's dream come true?” The old man touched his head and talked to himself. I nodded subconsciously in a hurry.
At this point, the old man grunts his muscles and bowels. Let's say it's 3721, eat a full meal before talking. The two of them were restrained and overjoyed to have dinner with the CCP. Half an hour passed, and under the dim light, they sat opposite each other. The old man really wanted to say something and stopped; he didn't know how to speak up.
Later generations also saw the old man's mentality, and generously said to the old man, “Grandma, I'm not an ordinary person. I'm a snake of the Jade Emperor. I admire the world, so I secretly went down to the world and became a mother and son with your old man to share the joy of heaven; for example, if I could get your old man to cook something like spoons and chopsticks for me to eat at night. I'll be able to have bones and body, just like a normal person.” It was night, and when it was even quieter, Grandma Ling Ying did everything one by one and lit incense candles in the yard, as her later students said. At the end of the day, I said the next generation slept in the gazebo, and went back to bed in the room myself.
The moon hangs high in the sky, and the village is silent, as always, immersed in the vast night.
On the next day, Grandma Ling Ying woke up early. She was happy and happy. The old people had a great smile on their faces, and the people were much younger; she called up her son and ordered that today we invited the village's parents to eat at our house to celebrate. My son couldn't help it either, so he quickly nodded his head and said yes. At noon, Duke De Wanggong and the rest of the elderly and young descendants all came. One after another, they greeted Grandma Ling Ying. Dinner began in the evening, and there were endless voices of congratulations and glass claps; everyone was happy and raised a glass to celebrate together.
During the meeting, Duke Duwang, who is literate, said, “According to Zhuang dialect, my wise nephew is called Dig Er, and those of the same generation are called Te Diao.” At the moment, everyone spoke with one voice and unanimously agreed. “Dear parents, elders, brothers and sisters, I would like to thank you all. Please dig down and have a drink.” They raised their glass and drank it off, then they bowed down respectfully and prayed for Duke Duwang and Ling Ying, and profoundly prayed three times each. Gong Duwang supported Gu Er and said to Grandma Ling Ying, “With Dig Er, the elderly have been blessed by heaven. You will be supported by old age in the future, and you will not be worried that no one will take care of you; we all feel at ease. I hope you will be beautiful, happy and happy, and always be safe and happy in the future.”
At this point, it was late at night, and the feast was over. People said goodbye to Ying Ling's mother and son, and went home separately.
The silver moonlight shines on the quiet earth. The evening wind shakes the branches, and the shadows of the trees in the lake and the moonlight are intertwined and sparkling; the water color of the mountain is like a beautiful ink painting.
