Under Gulong Mountain in Taipingguan Town, Pengze County, there is a longxi that is tens of miles long, and there is a stone arch bridge from the Ming Dynasty on the creek. Beneath the bridge is the bottomless Dragon Pool. There are no waves of still water on the surface, and the dark current at the bottom of the pool is very dangerous.
Legend has it that a long time ago, a fisherman weaved a net by the pool and had a wooden comb on her head falling into the pool of water. Unexpectedly, this wooden comb with his name engraved on it was picked up by my father who was fishing in Taibo Lake tens of miles away. It turns out that Gulongtan crossed Gulong Mountain, and the exit was at Taibo Lake in Madang, the first town in northern Jiangxi. After a year of major droughts, the water in Longxi flooded. A confident, well-educated person went fishing in the pool, and his nose was blue and swollen when he came up. This man danced with his hands and said, “You know, under the Dragon Pool is the Dragon Palace. There are palaces and gardens. I saw the dragon lady picking flowers, and the Dragon Prince also gave me a Pearl of the Night!” This man was dumb when he finished speaking. Open his hands, and he certainly had a huge pearl of the night in his palm. Since then, people have been convinced that the Dragon Palace is underneath Gulongtan.
During the Kangxi period of the Qing dynasty, there was a village girl named Cuicui on the edge of Longxi. She was born beautiful, smart and intelligent, and loved to sing. Her voice was like a bell in the wind, and she sang for the spring larks.
After the flower dynasty, Cui Cui carried a basket to pick tea buds by the stream. Cuicui is 17 years old, and she is old enough to discuss marriage. The father was determined to climb high, so his daughter's time was greatly delayed. The weather in Gulong Mountain warms up in spring, and everything is green. The entire mountain rises and falls, like a jasper carving. The water in Longxi is dust-free, as if it were a green ribbon floating around. Pairs of koi carps chase cheerfully in the stream, making people feel enthralled. Cui Cui's heart blossomed for a moment and sang loudly: “In February, Baihuaxiang, and the carp go to the stream. Yu Mei was originally a woman from Longtan. Which river did Brother Yu live in?” The voice didn't go away. A young man selling oil on the other side went on to sing, “The scenery in Longxi was beautiful in February. The carp came in pairs and pairs. My sister asked where my brother lived, Doujiazhuang, Gulong Mountain.” Cui Cui took a close look. The person on the other side of Waku was a handsome young man, similar in age to herself. Her face was shy for a moment. She said, “People didn't ask you, so shameless!” “Who are you asking if you don't ask me?” “I asked about fish!” The young man laughed and said, “Can fish talk?” Cui Cui didn't show weakness either: “All I asked was fish. Anyway, you're shameless!” After saying that, just carry the basket and leave.
Cui Cui climbed up the wheat field, and the young man sang in the back: “My brother is a magpie flying in the sky; my sister is a plum tree in Longshan. The magpie depends on the plum tree, and the stones don't fly.” Tsui Chui discovered that the young man was not only handsome, but also sang well. She found her friend for a moment, turned around and shyly said, “What if a magpie hangs on a plum tree? Beware of people hitting you with pennies.” The guys shouted, “I'm not afraid; Pennies won't call or fly!”
Cui Cui later learned that the young man's name was Shuigen. He sells oil from Dou Jiazhuang next door. Known as the oil seller, he passes by the bridge every day picking oil. People are honest and smart, and love to sing. I fell in love with him and always made excuses to meet him by the bridge. Unexpectedly, my sister is affectionate. Lang Youyi, like many infatuated young men and women, Shanmenhai swore that one partner would not get married, and the other would not get married.
One day, Shui Gen Tuo's matriarch brought up her family. Cui Cui's father, He Mianjiang, couldn't look down on Shui Gen and said contemptuously, “The kid in the Dou family sells oil cheaply. He wants to marry Cuicui even if he can't earn a few copper dollars a year. The toad wants to eat swan meat. Unless I can get a dowry of ten buffaloes, I might consider it.” When the matchmaker returned, she told Mizugen the original story of Ho Mianzhuang. Mizugen was not discouraged. The next day, she threw up her fuel and went to the countryside to find a lucrative job. I vowed never to return home if I didn't earn ten buffaloes.
In other words, there is a second-generation wealthy person in Peng Li. His parents gave him the name Fu Yifang. Because I have money, I changed my name to the rich side. I'm not much younger than He Miao Jiang, but my heart has spent a lot of time, and I have coveted my beautiful appearance for a long time. I was overjoyed when I heard that Ho Mianzhuang wanted ten buffaloes as a wedding ceremony. He prepared ten buffaloes, plus silk, pearl agate, and sent someone to send them to where they wanted to be happy with Cui Cui for 100 years. How could a master craftsman give up this wealth when he knows that age is unequal? I was overjoyed for a moment. Without Cui Cui's knowledge, I immediately changed my wedding book, and set the wedding date for the next year's wheat harvest season.
Before Mizune went out, he asked someone to do the math. The person who calculated the gossip said that God of Fortune was in the south and wanted him to go all the way south. Mizune arrived in Hukou County and heard that a brick kiln hired people and mud. One day's work could deduct a month's salary from selling oil in Kushido in the village. When he arrived at the kiln, the master of the kiln saw that the person from Suigen was honest and told him the truth, “Young man, working with mud is hard work. You have to work barefoot to fight frost and falling snow; no one can do it for a few days. Originally, they were people who used cows to tread, and the owner only hired them when they cared for their cows. Did you suffer?” Mizune said, “As long as I can make money, I'm not afraid to suffer or get tired!” Mizugen prides itself on being strong. Every day, she wakes up early to dig up the soil and pick up the water to soak up the mud. The hardest part is to melt the mud; you have to keep stepping on the mud with both feet before it sticks. Within a few days, my feet were rotten, and my legs were so swollen that I couldn't even walk on the road. Suffering from wind chill again, I lay fully in bed for a month. Shuigen quit her job in a kiln and arrived in Huangmei County across the river. The family was recruiting people to dig a weir pond, and he caught up with him. Digging a weir pond isn't easy. The soil freezes as hard as iron in the cold winter, so I dug it down and braved Mars. As soon as he thought about Cui Cui, he encouraged himself to persevere. The owner was kind and entertained him with good food and drink. It wasn't completed until early spring. At last, Mizugen earned money from a buffalo. He continued south. After the summer season, farmers in Nankang Road were harvesting a type of early-maturing wheat called rice wheat. He passed through a mountain dock, and suddenly dark clouds were rolling in the sky. Seeing that a heavy rain was coming, an old man was snatching wheat. He rushed over to help. Mizugen helped the old man bring wheat home, and the heavy rain spread all over the ground. The old man was very grateful and sent him a bunch of wheat ears. It kept raining until late, and Mizune couldn't make it; she had to stay at the old man's house. Mizugen slept very well at night. I woke up early in the morning and found myself lying in a broken temple. I thought I had a dream. I thought for a moment that the old man had sent him a bunch of wheat ears yesterday and pressed them under his clothes. He opened his coat and instantly straightened his eyes. This bundle of wheat turned into bright yellow ears of wheat. Mizune knows that gods can help. This bouquet of golden wheat didn't just buy ten buffaloes. Thinking about going back and forth with tall buildings, buying fields, and living a loving life with Tsui Tsui, I couldn't help but rejoice for a moment. Despite being hungry, I rushed back and forth in a hurry.
The conversation split into two parts. In a blink of an eye, the rich side hired eight large carts and invited two classes of drummers to welcome his family along the way. He Mianzhuang had bright lights at home, and Cui Chui was still in the dark. The daughter needed to dress up when she got married. Her mother couldn't hide it and told her the truth. Cui Cui knew that she had been sold to a wealthy party by her father. She refused to follow suit. She packed her clothes and quietly slipped out the back door while her family was busy.
The rich side, who is old and cunning, is fine; Cui Cui must have fled to Gulong Mountain to fight her marriage. I sent my subordinates to squat there early. Cui Cui rushed forward in a hurry. Having just stepped on the Gulong Bridge, she saw two big men standing at the end. She knew that they had been calculated and turned back and forth. The rich side had just brought someone under the bridge. The front and back roads were blocked. Cui Cui angrily said, “On the rich side, don't waste your time; I won't marry you!” The rich side laughed and said, “Cui Cui, take your life and death for the rest of your life!” Speaking of moving forward, I went and hugged Cui Cui. Cui Cui knew she was in trouble. She said with all her heart, “Mizugen, I can't be a pair with you in this life; I hope to be a pair with you in the next life.” After speaking, I leaped into Gulongtan. The rich side didn't expect Cui Cui to jump off the bridge. It was very remorseful. They sent someone to tell the family to salvage the corpse, and fled in a hurry with her family.
It was the third day since Mizugen rushed back to Doujiazhuang day and night. Mizugen lit an incense by the dragon pool and said, “Cui Cui, I want those greedy people to pay the price. You wait for me!”
Shuigen disappeared. People found a pair of shoes at the edge of Gulongtan. Everyone in Dou Jiazhuang said that Shui Gen was martyred. A month later, a fisherman was fishing in Taibo Lake and found a wax-sealed bamboo tube with a letter written by Mizugen to Cui's parents. In her letter, Mizugen said that he met Cui Cui at the Dragon Palace. He was the dragon king's daughter. Cuicui said that she had a marriage book with the rich side and only waited for him to marry her. In this life, she was only compatible with her brother and sister. When Dragon King looked at Cui Cui, he let him manage the Imperial Garden, so he didn't have to worry about it. One transmission to ten, ten transmission to hundred, and the whole town of Taipingguan went viral. The news reached Peng Lizhen. The rich side personally visited the family to read this letter and was convinced that it was true. He thought that the daughter of Cui Cui who became King of Dragons is the princess. Isn't it just a horse when she married Cui Cui herself? When Cui Cui married Dragon King, she definitely didn't hesitate to give a dowry. Any stone in the Dragon Palace is worth a fortune. Thinking about this, the rich side feels itchy. If you don't go into the tiger's den, you'll get the tiger. He decided to go to Dragon Palace to marry Tsui Sui.
The rich side wants to welcome his family at Dragon Palace, and people from near and far from the village come to watch the bustle at Gulongtan. The elder from the Ho family also came. It's dangerous to go down to Gulongtan. Although the rich side had good water when young, he was still worried that he wouldn't be able to get on. He specially bought a hundred length rope, tied one end around his waist, and handed the other end to two friends named Pineapple and Winter Melon. The rich side said, “Listen up, you two. If Grandpa Ben pulls the rope three times from the bottom, it's dangerous. Pull the rope up right away.” When the order was over, he hugged his fists and slapped Ho's err and jumped into the Dragon Pool. People on the shore only saw the rope falling step by step. When it fell to 99 feet, it suddenly stopped moving. People all looked into the pool, and when the sun set, they still couldn't see any movement at the bottom of the pool. The butler of Fu Fu was relieved and said, “All the townspeople, everything went well. Tonight I'm afraid the candles will be in Dragon Palace and Princess Cui Cui Cave. Let's all disperse!” The two guys were already sick with their backs and legs. If they got an amnesty order and made concerted efforts to pull up the rope, they didn't expect to bring a corpse. Their bodies were rolled by a vortex and destroyed by stones. They carefully identified that it was actually the rich side that paid the old man.
Turns out the water root didn't drop into the pool at all. The letter was a bait he set up to catch this big fish from the rich side. From the beginning, I had no bottom; I had no idea that the rich side was too greedy and would certainly fall into the hook. As he stood on Mount Gurung to enjoy a scene directed by his own director, his heart was extremely excited. When people were quiet in the middle of the night, Mizune burned a thick pile of paper money for herself by the bridge, then walked up the Gulong Bridge and shouted, “Cui Cui, I'm here with you!” I leaped into the Gulong Pool, and since then there have been a pair of koi in the pool. They are snuggling up in the water, just like a loving couple.
