 There is a hot spring in Tex, called Ahrezen Hot Spring. Legend has it that it is a sacred spring where lovers shed tears. In Kazakh, it means “fairy water that can cure all diseases.”
In the Uzun tribe a long time ago, the handsome Kazakh teenager Anders fell in love with the beautiful Kazakh girl Mayna. They were hardworking and brave, supported themselves, and formed a happy family on the rich prairie on the west bank of the Grand Canyon of the Kuksu River.
During a hunt, Anders was unfortunately devoured by a giant beast. Unaware Mayna sat on a mountainside rock and waited day after day for her sweetheart to return home.
Day after day, year after year, Mayna's tears dripped into the rock crevices, causing her burning tears to flow out of the crevices. These affectionate tears became Ahresan Hot Spring.
