 After Pangu opened the world and the woman made people, the water and fire between heaven and earth were controlled by two celestial dragons. They were ordered by the Heavenly Court to benefit the world. It is only with water and fire in the world that all living things can prosper and live. It is also because of this that the dragon has become a totem for the Chinese nation and a symbol of life and power.
Qinglong is the water dragon in charge of rivers and lakes. It rains in a timely manner according to the wishes of Emperor Jade, and there is never the slightest deviation. The one in charge of the fire is Red Dragon, and the Blue Dragon is a brother and brother. At first, water and fire got along well together, but until one day, the earth dried up and bones were all over the place, and water and fire were no longer compatible.
Turns out, Red Dragon took advantage of Tianguan's negligence in supervision to evaporate large amounts of water from the lake and keep all the stolen water private in his own treasure house in Longfu. However, the Blue Dragon is still precipitation according to destiny. Gradually, there is less and less water in the world, and more and more birds and animals die of thirst. Humans who have grown up all have to donate children, boys, and girls every year in exchange for water to maintain the reproduction of the race due to the power of the Red Dragon.
Qinglong has advised Red Dragon many times, but Red Dragon still doesn't know how to repent. He still does his own thing and doesn't take Blue Dragon seriously.
One day, Qinglong came to Red Dragon Palace, which is located in the middle of the mountains, and persuaded, “Big Brother Red Dragon, you just have to stock up on lake water. The Jade Emperor now has too much time to take care of everyday trivia, and I can't beat you, but why should you use every situation where there is a shortage of water to force humans to feed you? By doing this, you have broken the rules of heaven, and you are bound to be struck by lightning!”
Red Dragon laughed when he heard it: “Brother Dragon, thunderstorms are used to punish mortal people, not our gods. What's more, humans hunt food animals every day. Those that fly in the sky, crawl underground, and swim in rivers. How can they not eat them? What's wrong with me eating a few of them in one Tenjin? If you think about it, our brothers in the mortal world, snakes, lizards, and crocodiles, were killed countless times by them. I also did this to help Emperor Jade punish them!”
“Then why don't you use fire to punish them?” Qinglong asked angrily.
Red Dragon still smiled and said, “Brother Long, if I use fire to punish them, the flames will surely burst into the sky, and the heavenly court will notice it. And when I hoarded up the lake, the Heavenly Court couldn't detect any abnormalities at all. Even if the Jade Emperor discovered it one day, they wouldn't punish us for killing a few people.”
“Don't get me involved; I won't go along with you; if I get the chance, I'll tell the Jade Emperor everything!” After Qinglong finished speaking, he left Red Dragon Palace in anger.
On the way back to Qinglong Palace, Qinglong spilled manna dew on the clouds. This was the first time it rained without permission. However, this rain did nothing to help the earthly world, which had been dried up for a long time. Suddenly, a burst of miserable crying went straight into the sky, hitting the Blue Dragon's heart a bit. As a result, Qinglong found a dragon in the sky and looked down. It turned out to be in the direction of the Red Dragon Palace, and a group of people were stumbling along the mountain path carrying several pairs of boys and girls.
Seeing this scene, Qinglong couldn't help but shed tears with sadness. It didn't matter if it cried. The world suddenly surged, and it rained heavily up and down the mountain road. It didn't take long for the mountain path to be blocked by mudslides. However, when Qinglong realized that he could no longer cry, it was too late. The contestants were blocked from the mountains, making it difficult to climb to the sky if they wanted to get close to Red Dragon Palace.
The cries in the crowd grew more and more miserable, and an old man at the head lamented, “We humans are running out of energy. It's just this abominable blue dragon. It's fine if it doesn't rain. I didn't expect it to kill us with the red dragon in the end. It's really despicable.”
When Qinglong heard this statement, he immediately turned the dragon and flew to Red Dragon's treasure trove of lake water. He wanted to steal that water and return it to all.
When it flew to the top of the Red Dragon Treasure Trove, the red dragon was already waiting there: “Brother Long, I knew you'd be back. Can't you stand the gossip of mortal people? How can I still be a Tenjin like this?”
Qinglong ignored Red Dragon's taunts: “Big Brother Red Dragon, from the moment the world first opened, water and fire have been managed separately by you and me. But today you are hoarding large amounts of lake water only for personal gain. It's just unforgivable. I suggest you take the initiative to open a reservoir to release water; otherwise, I would have to leave my brotherhood behind and fulfill my duties as the god of water!”
“What? Do you still want to grab it?” Red Dragon laughed, “I'm afraid you don't have that skill yet!”
When Qinglong saw that the persuasion didn't work, he had to spew out a water dragon and rushed to the Red Dragon Treasure House. The Red Dragon immediately confronted the Fire Dragon. Water and fire immediately became entangled and fought at the top of the treasure house. At this point, the ground shook the mountain, and the crying wolf couldn't describe the intensity of the fight.
After a few years of intense fighting, the Blue Dragon gradually ran out of strength, but the Red Dragon became more brave during the Vietnam War. Just when Qinglong wanted to retreat, it saw that the group of young women and children who came to Red Dragon Palace was carving out a new path, and several new graves had been added on both sides of the mountain road. The old man at the head had already slept underground for a long time, and the boys and girls had grown up. Their children had become new sacrifices. The harsh reality determined their miserable fate.
The Blue Dragon can no longer watch. It knows that if it fights against Red Dragon for one more day, everything will die of thirst. However, my current physical strength is far below that of the Red Dragon, and it is still unknown whether it can be defeated. As a result, Qinglong thought of the most helpless method, which was to use his body to break into the hard stone wall of the Red Dragon Treasure House and let the water flow down his body into the world, but if he did this, Qinglong would die, and he would no longer be able to become a god.
Immediately, Qinglong pretended to be defeated and retreated tens of miles from Red Dragon's treasure chest. Red Dragon didn't know what Qinglong meant by this move; he didn't dare to act recklessly; he only foolishly guarded the Red Dragon treasure house. Time passed day by day, and Red Dragon's alert became less and less alert. Just as Red Dragon turned around to prepare to return to Red Dragon Palace, the Blue Dragon suddenly spewed out a water dragon to raid Red Dragon, and Red Dragon hurriedly fought. The Blue Dragon takes a flight to the Red Dragon Treasure Vault, planning to return to the Red Dragon Treasure House.
Red Dragon was shocked. He wanted to stop it, but was knocked out a long distance by the water dragon. By the time it flew back, the blue dragon had already crashed into the red dragon treasure house. Its body was crossing the stone wall of the treasure house, and only one dragon was exposed. The water in the curry continued to flow out of the treasure house along its body. The red dragon loudly reprimanded, “Are you crazy? Can you do your best to kill a few lives? Don't worry, I'll get you out!”
Qinglong smiled bitterly: “If that's the case, why do we have to do it in the beginning? The Red Dragon Treasure House was built by Emperor Jade. If I destroyed the artifact, it's already a violation of the Tien Tiao, and you can't escape your relationship. You can't escape for your life. I won't live long, so don't worry about me anymore!”
Red Dragon stopped listening to this, sighed, and then fled the Red Dragon Palace.
Many years later, a large river was born under the steady flow of water from the Red Dragon Treasure. Its name is the Yellow River. They are the descendants of boys and girls who lived around the Yellow River. When people learned that it was the Blue Dragon in exchange for their new life, they all called themselves descendants of the dragon. Later, to commemorate the Blue Dragon, people called the source of water a faucet, and it continues to be used until now.
And after escaping from Red Dragon Palace, Red Dragon flew to the west. There, it is still a fire-breathing red dragon, except that in Western mythology it became the personification of the devil and evil, and was forever rejected by the world.
