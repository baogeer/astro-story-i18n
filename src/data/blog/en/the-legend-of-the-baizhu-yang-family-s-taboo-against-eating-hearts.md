 According to legend, among the six surnames Yang, Wang, Ma, Gu, and Xiong of the Baizhu Miao family, the ancestors lived in Yongchang. More than 200 years ago, the three brothers of Yang's ancestors had to leave their homes because they could not be bullied by the government and had a difficult life. All the way into exile, they went through a lot of hardships. When I arrived at Baizhu in Longtan, I was lucky enough to meet a family member of the same family as Xiong Wang, and stayed in Baizhu for a while. The three brothers had a crush on the Mikusu family's weighbridge flat beach treasure, and after settling with the Su family, they settled down in Baizhu, married a wife and had children.
After settling down in Baizhu, the three ancestors of the Yang family missed their hometown of Yongchang and their ancestors. They met on the 1st day to hold a ceremony to pay tribute to their ancestral spirits and to talk about their homesickness. The three brothers and their wife led their son to fast and bathe, slay pigs and cows, and pray devotedly. A pig's heart is required when paying homage to the god Kado. However, after turning over the earthenware pot with a large spoon, I still haven't been able to remove the pork heart that was cooked in the pot. The elders were furious and thought it was the three younger brothers who were waiting to burn the fire at the pot and stole pork hearts. The act of stealing the sacrifices angered everyone present, believing that it would not only annoy the gods and relieve the entire family, but according to Miao family customs, it was an outrageous act. Faced with questioning from the crowd, the young son was full of grievances, yet he was speechless. The little son's father was forced by the situation at the time, and couldn't help but say that out of shame, he took the pig's tip knife. The white knife went in, and the red sword came out. He killed his son with one sword, picked out his son's heart, sacrificed the gods, and completed the festival.
Afterwards, people discovered that because the meat was cooked in an earthenware pot, the younger son only had to add firewood and forgot to stir the meat in the pot. The pork heart was stuck to the bottom of the earthenware pot due to the high temperature. Everyone regretted blaming the younger son. The junior even lamented that he killed Aiko by mistake, but the big mistake had already been made, and all the regrets didn't help.
Since then, in order for descendants of the Yang family to always remember this painful lesson, they set a family motto: People of the same family will never eat any animal hearts in the future. He also cautioned the world to live in harmony, treat others with integrity, and not easily doubt anyone.
