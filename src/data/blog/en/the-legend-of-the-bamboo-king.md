 In ancient times, there was a woman at the foot of Shimen Mountain. One day, she went to the riverside to wash her clothes. Three sections of bamboo floated through the river, and the sound of a baby crying was heard in the bamboo shoot.
The woman felt afraid and reached over and pushed the bamboo away. Unexpectedly, it was fine if she didn't push it. As soon as she reached out and pushed, those three sections of bamboo followed her, flowing with the water to her feet, and stopped flowing away.
The woman broke open the big bamboo and saw a boy lying in the bamboo tube. He was good-looking, crying clearly and loud, and had bright eyes. At first sight, the woman loved it and took him home to raise him.
Because this child was born from a bamboo festival, the woman gave him the name “Chikushu.”
Bamboo grew up to the age of 12 and became the king of famous children near and far. Strangely enough, not only did the children in the village listen to him, but also the birds and sparrows in the mountains and fish and shrimp in the river. On his birthday, the fish called the bird. Too bad, everyone didn't call him “Bamboo” anymore; they all jokingly called him “Bamboo King.”
Early one morning, Chikuo went out fishing as usual. As usual, he didn't support a fishing boat. He only brought a fishing net and sat on a waterside stone. Seeing a group of fish swimming in the river from afar, Chikuo reached out and said, “Fish, fish, come here!”
Hearing his call, the flock of fish immediately changed direction and swam towards him.
When the fish swam to his feet and the bamboo shoots up the net, the flock of fish has caught his net.
At this point, an old beggar walked out of the riverside bamboo grove and asked Chikuo, “Who taught you how to do netfish?”
“No one taught me, I'll do it myself.”
“That's right, you're the one I'm looking for.” The old beggar said, “Come to me and worship me as your teacher, and I'll teach you to do a great job.”
The old beggar was shabby, old, and ugly. Zhu Wang looked sorry for him and just caught a fish and threw it over: “What sample do you dare to be my master?”
“You'll slowly find out how capable I am. I took your fish. When you go home and cook a bamboo bowl with fresh fish and bring it here, I'll teach you slowly.” After speaking, the old beggar stomped his foot, and a white cloud grew under his feet. The old beggar stepped on the white cloud and climbed above the bamboo forest.
Knowing that he had met a fairy, Chikuo immediately went home to cook a bamboo bowl fresh fish meal and brought him to the riverside bamboo grove to worship his teacher.
The old beggar ate the bamboo pot meal and taught the bamboo students to cut down two bamboo shoots, cut them into thin strips, and make two fewer bamboo baskets. The bamboo basket was made, and the old beggar asked the bamboo basket to go to the riverside to fetch water.
Put the two bamboo baskets down, and the two bamboo baskets lift the water. The bamboo basket made of bamboo only filled half of the basket with water, but the bamboo basket made by the old beggar filled a basket full of water, and not a single drop came out.
The old beggar kept quiet. He used that basket and a half of water and slurry, and dug up the mud from his feet. He pinched his left and right fingers, then quickly kneaded into three teams of soldiers and horses. The old beggar breathed a sigh at that team of soldiers and horses, and the mudmen immediately came back to life. The military training, the camp set up, and the cooking of the rice were lined up in a row and in order.
It was fun for Takeo to see the mud horses, and it was the same. He pinched the three teams of soldiers and horses with mud and breathed a “huh” at them. Sure enough, the soldiers and horses also survived. The military training, the camp set up, and the cooking of the rice — yet, the rice wasn't cooked yet, then the mud horse scattered and fell to the ground one by one, turning into a mud beach.
“You are extremely talented, but unfortunately you are too hasty.” The master said, “Takeo, you have to remember that if you want to get things done, you have to keep calm. You need to concentrate and slowly blow out the true energy in your chest. When you blow the truth, you can't make a 'cry' sound.”
The method taught by Master Takeo calms down, concentrates, slowly breathes out his true energy, and doesn't make a sound. Sure enough, the Mud Soldiers are no longer scattered once they have survived. Chikuo really likes to play with clay soldiers and horses. Every day, he goes to the riverside to knead clay people, and when he's active, he commands soldiers and horses to fight and play. Just like that, play every day until it gets dark. When the moon comes out, the old beggars blow the Stormtrooper and let those soldiers and horses run into the bamboo forest.
Just learn and play like this every day. After two or three years of playing, I'm tired of playing: “Master, do you have any other skills?”
“I won't be able to teach you the next sample thing until you're 16 years old.”
Well, Chikushou has always been impatient. He can't wait any longer and deceived his master by saying, “Teach me quickly; I turned 16 last month!”
Actually, in that year, Chikuo was just 15 years old.
Chikuo is tall and smart, so the master had no doubts. He brought Chikuo to the depths of the bamboo grove, fingered the oldest bamboo plant in the bamboo forest, and muttered a few spells.
“Bamboo, go home first today. Take out your kitchen knife early tomorrow morning and cut down this bamboo. You have to make a bow and three arrows. As soon as the bow and arrow are ready, you immediately take me to the stone cave next to Shimen.”
After speaking, the master left and went back to that cave.
Early the next morning, Chikushou cut down the old bamboo plant with a kitchen knife. It was just right to make a bow and three arrows. Takuo walked into the stone cave with a bow and arrow. Only the old beggar sat in the center of the cave to practice his energy, and placed a large copper pot in front of him.
“Bamboo, put your bow and arrow in the copper pot.”
The bamboo king put the bow and arrow in the copper pot. The old beggar opened his mouth and spit out three crows from his mouth. As soon as the crow entered the copper pot, it immediately turned into three golden flames. The bamboo bow and bamboo arrow burned in the copper pot.
Strangely enough, even though the bamboo bow and bamboo arrow were burnt, they didn't turn to ashes. Instead, the more they burned, the greener they became, and the brighter they burned.
The old beggar coughed three times, and his voice became very old because he threw up those three crows: “Bamboo, now I'm not hiding it from you. My family was originally a member of the royal family, but I should have taken the throne. Because of the murder of a traitor, I went incognito and hid in seclusion in Shimen Mountain. My grandfather practiced for 60 years, turning it into a treasure sword, capable of stopping water, fire, and rain; my father trained for 60 years and made a magic whip, capable of hitting 100,000 divine soldiers; I trained for 60 years and made seven eggs, which could smash through the stone gate of Kamiyama and release a sacred horse. The treasure has now been crafted, but I'm old and no longer have the strength to fulfill my ancestors' wishes. Now, all of these treasures are yours. As long as you do what I say, you will definitely be able to shoot the criminal emperor to death and get rid of the traitor.”
Takeo asked, “What should I do?”
The old beggar took Chikushou out of the cave and made Chikushou move stones to seal the entrance: “Chikushou, starting today, in seven, seven, seven, 49 days, the bow and arrow in the copper pot can be transformed into the magic power of the devil. At that point, you can walk to the top of the mountain, use a bow and arrow, and shoot towards the northern imperial city, and shoot to death. After firing the magic arrow, you immediately return to the stone cave and remove the copper basin. The three treasures are under the copper pot. If you hit the stone gate at the foot of the mountain with seven eggs, the stone gate will open, and the magic horse will jump out; if you ride a magic horse and hit the bamboo bamboo on the mountain, the bamboo will burst and bring out 100,000 gods; you only lead the gods against the army coming from the imperial city. They attack you with fire, and you stop it with a sword, and the fire will burn back to them; they attack you with water, you block it with a sword, and the water will rush back to them. However, you should keep in mind that the bamboo forest warrior is only powerful within 100 miles of Shimen Mountain. You can just keep Shimen Mountain and stand on your own feet. Never go out of your way; otherwise, all your previous achievements will be wasted.”
Takuo agreed one by one. The old beggar took off his sandals, was barefoot, loose his hair, and sneaked sideways into the stone gate of the sky, and disappeared.
Chikuo has always been impatient; where can he wait 7, 7, 49 days? In less than 20 days, he was in a hurry like an ant in a hot pot. He was uneasy to sit or sleep. He had a hard time surviving until the 47th day. He was so itchy that he went to the cave early to remove the stone that sealed the entrance.
As soon as a gust of wind blew in, the blue and quiet fire in the copper basin suddenly grabbed the top of the cave. Another gust of wind blew in, and the fire in the copper basin went out.
Refined by Sanmai True Fire for 47 days, the magic bow and magic arrow emit a turquoise light, like four sections of bamboo that are still alive.
Takeo picked up the magic bow. The magic bow was so cool that it seemed to have been washed away by ice and snow. He walked up to the top of the mountain and headed towards the northern imperial city. His bow was like a full moon, his arrow was like a meteor, and he “whoa — whoa” fired three arrows in a row.
As soon as the three magic arrows left the bow string, they disappeared.
Let's not talk about bamboo, just the emperor in the imperial city. Currently, the government and military officials are lined up on both sides, and the emperor sits in the center of the Golden Dragon throne. Suddenly, three green bamboo arrows flew into the air and hit the emperor's heart. The emperor was struck by three arrows in his mouth. Terrified, he rolled down from the dragon seat and passed out, leaving no one awake.
Hundreds of military and military officials made a mess, and the imperial court medics rushed in to lift up the emperor and examine his wounds. Fortunately, these three arrows reached the Jinluan Temple and had no strength when they hit the emperor's heart. The tip of the three green bamboo arrows shot through the dragon robe, only hurting the emperor a little bit of sarcasm.
The emperor was terrified and in a cold sweat. He immediately ordered the Emperor Taishi to thoroughly investigate this cold arrow incident and make sure to find the archers to avoid future problems.
Then, Master Chao also knew a little bit of magic. Using those three bamboo arrows, he deduced that he quickly locked down Shimen Mountain.
The emperor said, “I want to personally take the army to Shimen Mountain to catch the traitor, cut him into five horses, and relieve my deep hate!”
As a result, the emperor led an army of 200,000 and marched towards Shimen Mountain.
Also, after firing the three magic arrows, he immediately returned to the master's stone cave, removed the copper basin, and took out the three treasures left by the master. The moon came out that night, and the moonlight illuminated Shimen. He brought seven eggs to Shimen's cliff. The stone gate was steep and narrow, showing only a ray of sky. Chikuo took off his sandals, stood barefoot in front of Shimen, raised an egg, and struggled to hit the cliff of Shimen.
When the first egg was smashed, I only heard a “thunderous” sound, and a white smoke came out of Shimen;
Hit the second one, the third one, the fourth one... the sound got louder and louder, the smoke grew thicker, and fire broke out in the thick fog, hitting the seventh egg. The fire in Shimen went up and the cliff “thundered”. The stone gate opened on both sides. Immediately after that, a white light flashed through, and a big white horse with a silver glow jumped out of Shimen.
Chikuo shouted and summoned the horse to come to his side. He leaped over and rode the Shenma. He roared loudly, picked up Bamboo, drove the fog, and ran from the foot of the mountain to the top of the mountain, and then from the top of the mountain to the foot of the mountain. After running two turns like this, Kusunzhu, which is scattered all over the mountains of Shimen Mountain, heard the sound of shining horses and horseshoes. The branch stood upright and shook continuously in the wind, and the sound of thousands of troops and horses sizzled during the bamboo festival.
Bamboo pulled out his whip, and while running through the mountains, he “crackled” at Kusunzhu. Being whipped by that god, Kusunzhu was whipped like a monster. Bamboo swelled one by one, and then “bang bang” exploded. The explosions continued. Countless soldiers jumped out of the bamboo. The soldiers were all riding horses, wearing armor, holding long bamboo whips, and golden swords around their waist. They grew when they saw the wind. After only a while, they grew tall, strong, and extremely powerful.
Takeo whipped God all night and fought out a total of 100,000 divine soldiers.
With this hundred thousand divine soldiers, Chikuo became king on his own in the mountains and called himself the “Bamboo King.”
During the day, the divine soldiers hide deep in the bamboo forest, bask in the sun, drink wind dew, and stay energized.
At night, Chikuo brought his magic whip and sword up the mountain for military training. After training for 3721 days, the Baguabing team completed their training, and the officers and soldiers also reached the foot of the mountain.
The two armies played against each other. They saw the officers and soldiers approaching. The bamboo forest soldiers raised bamboo whips and slapped hard, but when they were whipped by bamboo, the officers and soldiers immediately fell to the ground and were unable to move. Soon, the officers and soldiers fell down a large area. As soon as the emperor saw this situation, he was taken aback and hurriedly ordered the officers and soldiers to retreat 30 miles.
Thirty miles after being discharged from the army, the officers and soldiers set up camp on the ground. Then, when Taishi began to calculate the mystery, he proposed a strategy to the emperor, “Don't worry, the emperor doesn't need to worry. Well, the soldiers and horses of King Shimen Mountain have five elements. When they follow him tomorrow, we will burn him on fire.”
Sure enough, on the next day, Takuo led the army to attack.
Taishi found 300 mules and wrapped pine branches and mangrove on the mules. He drove the mules to the front of the battle. When he saw the bamboo king's soldiers, they immediately ignited the pine branches tied to the mule's tail. The group of mules were driven away by the flames, which shook their courage, and ran desperately towards the 100,000 gods.
Seeing the fire mule rushing in, Chikuou was not in a hurry; he only pulled out the sword and “whistled”. The sword grew tens of times in the wind. The blade immediately became bright and bright, and it looked like a mirror.
When I looked at the image on the face of the 300 burning mules, I thought there were 300 fire mules on the other side, so scared they turned their heads and ran fast. They couldn't run away. They just ran to the officers' and soldier's camp, burning up all of the officers' and soldier's camp and food.
The officers and soldiers were defeated day by day, and the bamboo king won a landslide victory. At this point, Takeo won two battles in a row. When he was young and crazy, he stopped paying attention to the officers and soldiers. He forgot his master's advice and went after him to Beijing.
The emperor entered Beijing, locked the city gates, and ordered the officers and soldiers to stand guard against the city wall.

He ran in front, and the officers and soldiers chased him behind. He kept running for three days and three nights, and fled back to Shimen Mountain carrying bamboo shoots. Big White Horse came to Shimen and jumped in. I only heard a burst of “roar”. The stone gate got narrower and narrower, and finally closed. The officers and soldiers used axes to chisel, set fire to fire, and hit the rock. They exhausted their options and were unable to open the stone gate. In the end, Taishi couldn't think of a solution; in the end, Taishi had no choice but to bring troops back to Beijing to recover his life, saying that King Zhu had died on Mount Shimen.
However, legend has it that Zhu Wang didn't die; he became a god — until now, he still shows up from time to time. On moonlit nights, in the village under Shimen Mountain, you can hear the sizzling sounds of thousands of troops and horses — that is Bamboo King in military training!
