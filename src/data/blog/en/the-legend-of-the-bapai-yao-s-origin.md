 Our ancestors of the Yao ethnic group first lived in Huguang Daozhou. Why are they called Bapaiyao in southern China?
Legend has it this way: One year, the sky was very arid. There wasn't a drop of rain for three years, and the fields were too dry to cultivate. The Yao Han family fought over water. As a result, both parties were killed and injured. Things got worse later. The Han people sued the Yamen, and the government used this as an excuse to ask for money from the Yao people, forcing our ancestors to sell out all the houses in the fields.
Our ancestors had four brothers. Without fields, we couldn't live; we had to learn how to do business and wander everywhere. They split Daozhou Street into three routes to earn a living. The one who stayed in Huguang was their older brother, the second oldest who went to Jiangxi, and the older three and four who came to Guangdong. Our ancestors of Bapai Yao are old four. Their name is Dou Binguo.
After our ancestors left Daozhou, they saved their lives by eating mangroves and experienced an unknown amount of suffering. They stayed there for a while, and were unable to live there, “the fish were poor walking the pit, and the people walked the mountain”. They relied on a white dog to explore the path in front, and a yellow dog led the way in the back, along the Terrestrial Dragon (mountain), through the summit of Jingyang Mountain in Tonggan, and walked to Maping (some say it was Mangcaoping) in Lianzhou polder. They saw that the Feng Shui in this place was blocked, and no one was blocked. They then set up a draft house to live and start a business to earn a living.
I stayed in Dumaping for a month and 40 days. Due to poor business, I was unable to maintain my life, and walked along the Lianzhou River to Sanjiangwei Street. This day, I just met a kind Han person on the street and invited them to stay.
They lived in the Sanjiang River for eight or nine years, growing vegetables for a living. After that, they were still unable to live. They also walked up the Sanjiang River to Zhangguyuan, where they stayed for 18 days. One day, when they walked up Ziwei Mountain and saw that Zhanggudong Mountain was vast and the water was vast. It was not an ideal place to stay. They moved again and followed the river to find a good place to live. Later, I went to the place where Lianshan Mountain and the vortex meet, and saw that the river at the junction of Lianshan Mountain was clear, so I picked up a silver scale to weigh the water. The river that weighed Lianshan was 2 parts 5, and the river water in the vortex was 2 parts 9. That river followed that river, and walked 99 bends along the Woshui River. When I reached the Huangyuan Mountain Range, I saw that the nine dragons in Huangyuan were good, and the Ten Hima had a real trick (meaning good Feng Shui), cut down the mangroves, split the thorns, and set up a cottage to stay.
Soon after living in Huangyuan, the population grew larger and larger. 180 households lived on the left and 450 families lived on the right, making it a big cottage. Later, I saw that the Han people were having a lot of fun, so they built a temple, made 36 statues of gods, and started playing like Han people.
Unexpectedly, after a year of work, someone dressed in shabby clothes came. His name was Luo Xian Xiucai. When he walked to Nan Xiaoni's house, people didn't know that he was Mr. Geo. They thought he was a glutton, so they gave him a scoop of water to drink before giving him alcohol. Luo Xianxiu was very angry about this and wanted to corrupt Huangyuan Zhai's Feng Shui. He said to people in Huangyuan Village, “The name 'Huangyuan' is very unlucky. 'Huangyuan' means that people who have died of illness are carried out and buried. From then on, you'll get sick and yellow one by one, and die fine.”
Only then did Luo Xian-xiu say these things. After that, people in Huangyuan Zhai certainly died of illness one by one. Originally, it was a bustling village, but it soon became a barren mountain.
Huangyuan's dragon veins have run out of steam; only the Hachinohe people who live in Chabian have not died. When they decided to find a new place to live, they cut down the main bridge of the temple into eight sections, cut the thatch to build the temple into eight blocks, and each of the eight households went to each mountaintop to earn a living. Year after year, every mountaintop flourished, and became a big village (also known as Yao Pai). The military house on the north side of the Woshui River is the big brother, the Eighth Cave is the second brother, the Fire Pai is the third brother, the Dazhang is the fourth brother, and the Ma Jianpai is the fifth brother; on the east of the Woshui River, the Yuling Pai is the big brother, the Nangang Pai is the second brother, and the Hengkeng Pai is the third brother. After that, the population gradually increased, and the eight rows were unable to stay, and people scattered to live in 24 mountains.
After being scattered, it was difficult for people to meet because the mountains were high and the road was long. Later, they built a large temple in each row and decided to have a concert hall every three or five years, so that everyone could come and have a party. Just like that, our Yao people settled in the Liannan area and became the current Eight Paiyao.
