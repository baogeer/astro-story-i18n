 Dadu Rock is at the northernmost tip of Little Chibi. It rises high in the middle, and its stomach is as big as a drum. Legend has it that Xuanyuan Huangdi invented the rice bowl and placed the rice bowl next to Haoxian River on the opposite side of Dadu Rock when he was casting alchemy in Xiandu. There is a rice woman in the rice bowl, and rice is produced continuously every day for various fairies, ministers, and local people. One day, a monster with a big belly came. Because of his big appetite, he ate the meal. The rice woman kept growing rice in his stomach, and her stomach got bigger and bigger. By the time he barely walked past the good creek, he couldn't walk anymore. When Huang Di saw it, it was nice and funny. He ordered him to be stationed at Nantianmen Gate in Xiandu, across the river from Ichiban Rock.
