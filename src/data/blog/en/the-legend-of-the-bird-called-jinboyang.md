 Every year during the spring season, in the mountains, I hear one terrible bird cry after another: “Kim Bo-yang, Kim Bo-yang...” The ornithological name for making this kind of call is a horned sparrow. Along our western slope of Cangshan, people call this bird Jin Boyang based on the sound it makes. Every time they hear Jin Boyang's sudden and slow cry, people in the mountain village say that the season to order soybeans has arrived.
Speaking of the origin of Kim Bo-yang, there is another intriguing story. I heard the elderly say that a long, long time ago, there was a very mean and vicious stepmother in the village. She always treated the son left behind by her husband's ex-wife as a nail in her eye and a thorn in her flesh, and she always wanted to get rid of it quickly. However, they are also afraid of the rhetoric of the townspeople, and are afraid to blatantly poison themselves. For this reason, she sleeps and eats uneasy every day and makes careful calculations day and night. One day, she finally came up with a brilliant plan that she thought was smart.
This morning, the stepmother invited her stepson and her son together and asked them to go up the mountain to grow soybeans together. With bad intentions, she ordered them to say, “You each take the seeds and grow a piece of soybeans; you can only come back until the soybeans you have ordered grow sprouts. Whichever soybeans you pick will come back; whoever orders the soybeans you grow doesn't grow sprouts will not want to come back.” After speaking, I gave each of his two brothers a small basket of soybeans. She gave her biological son raw soybeans that can take root and germinate, while what she gave to her stepson was ripe soybeans that had been roasted in a pot.
Although the smart stepson uncovered his stepmother's machinations at a glance, he still quietly took over the roasted soybean seeds and climbed the eerie back mountain with his half-brother. Along the way, while walking, he ate the crispy, fresh, and fragrant fried soybeans in his basket in a delicious way. The younger brother, who was hungry, also learned from his older brother to eat the soybean seeds in his basket. However, since his soybeans had not been fried, he bitten them in his mouth, and they were stiff and smelly, so he couldn't eat them at all. The younger brother, who was clueless, proposed to exchange soybean varieties with his older brother. As soon as my older brother heard it, he pretended to be 10,000 reluctant. He just didn't agree to the exchange. In the end, the unsensible younger brother bothered him with grinding his left and right demands, and only then did the older brother show that he was very reluctant to exchange his cooked soybeans for his younger brother.
When the two brothers climbed to the back mountain, they each excavated a volcanic area with machetes and hoes, and planted the soybean seeds they each brought. They each kept their own soybean field, looked at the soybean field with a blink of an eye, and looked forward to sprouts appearing in their fields as soon as possible so that they could go down the mountain and return home as soon as possible. A few days later, a spring rain hit the ground, and the soybean sprouts in my brother's field sparsely broke through the ground overnight. So he happily ran down the mountain and went home. His poor brother, on the other hand, couldn't see the soybean sprouts unearthed in his own field, so he had to stay alone on the eerie back mountain and continue to wait, ah, ah. But I never saw the soybeans I grew sprout from the ground. In the end, this poor young, ignorant child died on the back mountain due to freezing and hunger.
This stepmother, who has a heart like a snake scorpion, couldn't have anticipated the “trick to harm people” she had painstakingly devised. As a result, she took a stone and hit her own foot. Not only did she not damage her stepson's hair, but instead risked her beloved child's life in vain. It's really hurting others and killing myself! I couldn't help but feel the excitement and sadness of losing my son for a while, and my stepmother never got sick. Within a few days, she followed her biological son. After the death of this vicious stepmother, who ate the fruits of her own life, she turned into a bird. Every time spring came to grow soybeans, she flew all over the mountains and the ground day and night, anxiously calling out the name of her beloved son: “Kim Bo-yang, Kim Bo-yang...”
