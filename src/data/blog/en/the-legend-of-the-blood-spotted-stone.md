 On the northern hillside of Badaling, there is a very peculiar stone. The surface of this stone is covered with blood-red spots. Not only that, but every time the midsummer season arrives, when the stones on the hillside are burnt hot, the only thing that is cool is this red spotted stone. Why is that? It is said that there is also a story in it.
Legend has it that back then, Meng Jiang went through all the hardships and came alone to find a husband at the construction site in Baguoling. She searched and found no sign of her husband. Someone later told him that her husband had died of exhaustion while building the city, and his bones were buried in the city walls. The bad news was like a thunderstorm. Her legs were soft and she sat on a rock next to her. It took a long time for Meng Jiang to wake up and cry out loud against her husband. She cried, cried, cried from dawn to dark, and from dark to dawn. This is true. Heaven has feelings, and the earth has feelings; the dead husband is even more affectionate. The Great Wall has been brought to tears, and the husband's bones have come out.
When Meng Jiang met her husband, she was so sad that she didn't want to live, so she went straight to the East and left to herself.
The death of the woman Meng Jiang moved the sky and the ground. Even the mosquitoes and bugs that had bitten her were moved to tears. They regret not having taken advantage of Meng Jiang's crying in the dark and white to eat her blood in the first place. Thinking of this place, these mosquitoes and bugs all climbed onto the stone that Meng Jiang was sitting on, spitting out Meng Jiang's blood little by little, and vowed not to bite anyone sitting on this stone.
Therefore, stones are also friendly; they are warm in winter and cool in summer. Legend has it that no matter who it is, as long as he (she) really misses his loved ones when they sit on this bloody speckles, summer is not hot, and mosquitoes don't bite. To this day, this stone remains on the slopes of Badaling.
