 Baoluo, the ancestor of the Zhuang people, helped the Zhuang family settle down on a mountain hill after creating heaven and earth. Let them be active in the bright sun during the day and sleep in the twinkling starlight at night.
However, there were more and more people on the ground, and there was almost no place to live; Paul had no choice but to make the world taller and wider. However, as a result, in the midst of the abrupt mountains and steep mountains, there were dark corners that could not be exposed to the sun.
In these dark and humid corners, many vicious insects and fierce beasts slowly breed. During the day, they honestly stay motionless in the mountains, but as soon as night falls, they run out under cover of darkness to hurt people.
After suffering enough, the Zhuang family implored Paul, hoping that he could remove the stars from the night sky and place them on the ground so that the bright starlight would illuminate those dark corners, so that poisonous insects and monsters would not dare to make trouble again.
Paul personally came to earth, and he wanted to teach everyone to create a kind of “earthly star.” He led the people to dig up the three-color clay, make molds one by one with a narrow circle in the middle, and then burn the most beautiful malachite with green steel firewood for three days and three nights. Malachite turned into a golden stone.
Baoluo led everyone to pour the stone paste into the three-color clay mold. After cooling, they got something with a narrow golden glitter in the middle of the two circles one by one. There is a pattern of people hunting and labor on it, and a huge star. Surrounding this big star, there are also many small stars.
Everyone thought: What's the use of this stuff? I saw Paul pick one up with a smile and knock hard at the big star above with his fist. Suddenly, a loud and deep “click click” sound rang in people's ears.
It sounded like thunder, shaking even the mountains. Poisonous insects and beasts hiding in dark corners were dazed by the sound, so frightened that they ran away lifeless. Seeing this scene, people couldn't help but cheer.
Paul told everyone that this is a “star on earth,” also called “Aran,” which means a bronze drum. If you beat it hard, the earth-shattering sound of drums can scare away all evil enemies.
Since then, bronze drums have been passed down from generation to generation, and have become an indispensable treasure in the lives of the Zhuang family.
