 Long ago, long ago, the Dragon Gate had not been cut open. The flow of Yishui water to here was blocked by Zilongmen Mountain, and a large lake was built up in southern Shannan.
The carp living in the Yellow River heard that Longmen is beautiful, and they all want to go sightseeing. They set out from the Yellow River in Mengjin, Henan, and passed through the Luo River and the Shunyi River to the Longmen Water Splash Point, but there was no waterway on Longmen Mountain, so they couldn't go up; they had to gather at the foot of North Mountain in Longmen. “I have an idea, why don't we skip this Longmen Mountain?” A beautiful and strong red carp spoke to everyone. “It's so high, how can I jump?” “If you don't jump well, you'll fall to death!” , the partners are unsure about their ideas. The big red carp volunteered to say, “I'll jump first and try it.” I saw that it used its full strength from half of the way, like an arrow that went off the string. It leaped and suddenly jumped into the clouds for half a day, driving the clouds and rain in the sky forward. A cloud of fire came from behind and burned its tail. It endured the pain, continued to leap forward, and finally crossed Mount Longmen and fell into the lake in southern Shannan. When the koi in Yamakita saw the big red carp's tail being burnt down by the fire, they were so frightened that they shrunk apart one by one, and were afraid to take that risk again. At this point, I saw a dragon fall from the sky and said, “Don't be afraid, I'm your partner, Big Red Carp. Because I jumped over the dragon gate, I became a dragon, and you have to jump bravely too!” When the carp heard these words, they were encouraged and began dancing next to Longmen Mountain one by one. However, with the exception of a few who jumped over and turned into dragons, most of them couldn't get through. Anyone who can't jump and falls from the air will have a dark scar on their forehead. To this day, this black scar still grows on the yellow river carp's forehead.
Later, Li Bai, the great poet of the Tang dynasty, wrote a poem dedicated to this incident: “The three-foot carp in the Yellow River was originally located in Mengjin. The amount was not worth the money; it came back with a fan fish.”
