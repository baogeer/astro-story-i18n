 Chuanmin Village in Longjiang Town has two carp stones that are more than two meters high and two meters long. There is a legend about these two carp stones.
Previously, these two carp stones were two carp living in the sky's Yaochi. One day, the two carps swam together. The two fell in love at first sight and are deeply in love with each other.
They both know that Heaven will not allow them to fall in love. As a result, the two of them secretly jumped out of Yaochi and arrived at the common room, and lived a happy life where they only envied mandarin ducks but not immortals.
Who knows, the two of them didn't have a long happy life. An immortal learned about it. This immortal went to Heaven to report it to Emperor Jade. Emperor Jade was furious and sent Jiro God to take them both to the heavenly court.
Although they both prayed bitterly in front of the Jade Emperor, they begged for the Jade Emperor to achieve perfection. Emperor Keyu had an iron heart. Not only did their plea not impress Emperor Jade, but instead infuriated Emperor Jade. Emperor Jade made people force them both to drink forgotten water, so that they both forgot everything in the past, forgot each other, and then banished them from heaven and devalue everything.
They were both relegated to the mundane, one at the end of the sky and the other at the corner of the sea.
After thousands of years of painstaking training, their practice finally came to fruition. At this point, the love they had buried at the bottom of their hearts was revived, and they struggled to find their own love. They both went through a lot of hard work and finally found each other. However, they both came to live in a hideaway because they were afraid that someone would report to the Jade Emperor again.
Later, Emperor Jade went to an ordinary place to enjoy the scenery and felt that they were both living in a hideaway. Then Jiro God was sent to arrest them both at Senbutsu-ji Temple in Yasutake. They both waited until late at night and escaped while the soldiers were dozing off.
Tianbing woke up to find that they were both missing, and immediately reported to Emperor Jade. The Jade Emperor was furious and sent Lei Gong Electric Mother to capture them, and asked Lei Gong Electric Mother to immediately correct the law as soon as they discovered these two carp spirits.
When they both fled from the Thousand Buddha Temple to Sichuan Minmin Village, they were overtaken by Lei Gong's electric mother. I saw a lightning bolt, a big thunder, that just hit them both. They both fell to the ground and became two carp. Soon these two carp turned into two carp stones. However, the two of them, who have turned into carp stones, are still closely attached to each other.
There is a well under the fish heads of the two carp stones. Every morning, when villagers come to fetch water, they find a well full of water during the day, but at this point there is only half of the well water. They all said, “This is a carp stone drinking water. Although they have turned to stone, their love and yearning for a better life will never turn to stone.”
Since Chuanmin Village had these two carp stones, the Fangyuan area has had smooth wind and rain, and plenty of grains. The villagers all know that this is a carp stone that uses their remaining mana to benefit everyone.
