 What people often call Xiangxi actually refers to the entire western region of Hunan. Since it is mostly mountainous, and there are many ethnic minorities, in addition to being remote and relatively underdeveloped, the Xiangxi region is also the birthplace of various legends, such as scavenging corpses, witchcraft, etc., which we often hear, but what we are going to talk about today is not a strange sounding myth, but a very poignant teenage dream.
The ancient city of Phoenix is now one of the most famous tourist attractions in western Xiangxi. The land area is only 10 square kilometers, yet it is home to 28 ethnic groups. Precisely because of this, Phoenix has unique customs, religious beliefs, myths and legends, and Luodong Flower Girl is one of them.
They all say that there are three major evils in Xiangxi. There is no need to say much about the first two evils. The first two evils all have a cold air, but this third evil seems more like a beautiful story. It's like a lost scholar who went into a cave by mistake and met a girl who lived in seclusion in the cave. Even if it wasn't a heartbreaking love story, it should have been a hermit saga like Taohuayuan. However, Luodong Flower Girl is not that beautiful. Mr. Shen Congwen, who was born in Phoenix, Hunan, once wrote in his own prose It has been described as follows, “Every woman who falls into a cave must have bright eyes, a perfect temperament, and be intelligent beautiful. You must be unmarried, have hobbies, be well-groomed, stay true to yourself in normal times, be passionate and unobtrusive, and have more fantasies. Occasionally, when she went out, she thought she had no intention of passing by a cave at some point, and was delighted to catch a glimpse of the cave god. As a result, she loves being alone, sitting still, and loves being clean. Sometimes she talks to herself, often thinking that the cave god has driven the rainbow to see her.”
In other words, Lokdong Flower Girl was actually looked up to by the god of the cave, and then fell in love with the fairy. This seems like an ancient sacrifice, choosing one or two wonderful girls to marry each year to pray for good weather and rain and disaster to go away. However, most of the women sacrificing did not do it voluntarily; however, most of the sacrificial women did not do it of their own free will, but the girl who sacrificed did not do it voluntarily, but she really wanted to be with the fairy. Immortals, of course, are impossible to live in a mortal world. If you want to be close to immortals, you have to give up your own body and body, which means that in the eyes of the world, the end of the flower girl in the cave is death. However, Mr. Shen Congwen further described, “At the time of death, the woman must have felt that Dong Shen had sent someone to greet her, or that Dong Shen personally picked her up on a white horse in a new suit. Her ears were racing, her eyes were glowing, her face was red, and sometimes she died laughing. Also, when he died, he looked clear and beautiful.” In other words, these women who fell into the cave seemed to have actually gone on a wonderful date. When they died, they were all bright, moving, and extremely satisfied.
There are many local stories about flower girls in the cave, but most of them are very similar to those described by Mr. Shen Congwen. They are generally girls in their teens and 20. Suddenly they go out one day like being fooled. They either talk to themselves or look in the mirror over and over to enjoy their beauty. In the end, all of them die. Some die of weakness, whether they don't eat or drink, and there are also those who commit suicide by throwing themselves into the well. The length of time is about 2 to 5 years..
Some people may think that a supernatural mystical force is guiding these girls. When they are loved by gods, they are willing to give up on life, go to the blissful, relatively closed region of Xiangxi, and see girls falling into the cave as something helpless or even a bit lucky, because it is impossible to explain why a good girl suddenly behaves abnormally and then laughs to death. They are already absolutely in awe of the ever-changing nature, and often bear the name “ghost god” for things they can't explain, so the phoenix in the cave becomes more and more Mysteriously, people who have fallen into the cave also think that their daughter has God's patronage consumed too much energy and passed away.
However, in a more rational way, these young girls are more like delusional. Mr. Shen Congwen further explained in his article, “An unmarried woman, especially an intelligent woman who loves beauty and cleanness, knows how to read, and is emotional, is naturally more depressed emotionally, or because of early maturity or late marriage, and it is easy to become morbid.” However, the best cure for a loch cave is a “normal and happy” marriage. Unfortunately, once you fall into the state of a loch cave flower girl, she becomes a spiritual sweetheart in the eyes of the locals. And who dares to rob God's daughter-in-law? So it's impossible for them to get married again.
