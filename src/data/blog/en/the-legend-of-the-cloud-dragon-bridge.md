 Legend has it that a long time ago, there was an evil dragon in the Yangjiao River, which often made waves. When happy, the river is calm and quiet; when angry, floods flood farmland houses on both sides of the Strait. A bridge built on the river was washed away. People had no choice but to cross the river, and evil dragons often opened their blood vessels to swallow passers-by. The suffering of people was unspeakable.
There is a village called Xiuling Village at the top of the western bank of the Yangjiangjiang River. They live in a family. The mother and son farm and cut firewood, and are dependent on each other. The son is named Brother Qing. After the age of 18, he grew up as a handsome, brave, and hardworking young man, proficient in all kinds of activities. One day, Brother Qing carried clear resin and animal skin to go to Yangyi Street to rush the streets. When I walked to the street, I saw a bunch of people around me. I took a look. It turned out to be an old man selling fish. He was shouting to sell fish, drawing crowds to watch. Brother Qing took a close look. There was a small white scaly fish with a silver glow in the fishing basket. When the little white fine-scaled fish saw Brother Qing, they blink at Brother Qing and shed tears, as if they were begging Brother Qing to save his life. When Brother Qing saw that the little white spiny fish monster was pitiful, he was moved with pity and asked the old fisherman how much would it cost to buy this fish? The old fisherman said, “Three dollars.” With no pennies in his pocket, Qing Ge blushed and said to the old fisherman, “I want to exchange these gelatin and animal skin for small white fish.” The old fisherman said, “It's rare for you to have this kind heart; there will definitely be good news in the future. I won't give you this fish for anything.” After speaking, I handed over the small white scaly fish in the fish basket to Brother Qing, and I didn't know where it had gone. Despite his fatigue, Brother Qing picked up the little white scaly fish and ran to the riverside in one go and put the fish into the water. Brother Qing came home and told his mother about what had just happened. Her mother said, “It doesn't matter. Even though we haven't earned money, we still have leftovers at home, so let's just eat the leftovers.”
It turns out that the little white scaly fish is Princess White Dragon, the daughter of the Yangjiangjiang Great Evil Dragon. Princess White Dragon is beautiful and charming, and she also has a kind heart.
One day, Princess White Dragon couldn't stay at the Dragon Palace, so she carried her father and king alone to the hillside along the Yangliang River. She picked mountain flowers, picked orchids, made a string of flowers and ran back and forth around her neck, enjoying a wonderful time on earth. When I ran to Shanqing, I suddenly heard the sound of chopping firewood in Shanqing. Alas, it was also accompanied by a pleasant mountain song. The princess gently opened the leaves and took a look. He was a handsome young man. The young man is Brother Qing. Princess White Dragon really didn't expect there to be such a handsome young man in the deep mountains, so she transformed into a little mountain rabbit and ran around in front of Brother Qing. Brother Qing saw the situation and said, “Little Mountain Rabbit, you run so dexterously. Thank you, Mountain Rabbit, for accompanying me during labor and making me not lonely.” The princess turned into a peacock and flew in front of Brother Qing to dance for him. Brother Qing was happy to see it and said, “Thank you, Mountain Peacock, danced for me while I was working.” After a while, the princess turned into a thrush bird and chirped in front of Brother Qing. Brother Qing said, “Shanhuamei, thank you. Sing for me when I cut firewood.” Soon, Brother Qing cut the firewood and went home after saying goodbye. After watching Brother Qing go down the mountain, the mountain god appeared in front of Princess White Dragon as soon as she stomped her foot gently. Yamagami arched, “Princess White Dragon is here. Little God is far away. Please forgive the princess. I don't know how valuable it is for a princess to go to this wild mountain?” Princess White Dragon asked, “What is this mountain called?” Mountain Shinto: “It's Mount Shuling.” The princess blushed and asked, “What is the name of the young man who just cut firewood? Let's listen to him in detail about his life.” Yamagami said, “Brother Qing is an unappreciated young man. Not only is he hardworking and brave, but he is also very filial to his mother. Does the princess love Brother Qing?” Princess White Dragon blushed and nodded. Yamagami said, “I'd like to arrange for the two of you to become the old man under the moon.” Princess White Dragon said, “I want to try if Brother Qing is really serious about me? Is it kind and unkind? Tomorrow you will become an old fish seller, and I will become a small white scallop. Qing Ge will definitely sell firewood to see if he is trustworthy Lang Jun.” The next day, Yamashin became an old fisherman and sold small white scallops on the street. Brother Qing felt compassion and bought small white scallops and put them back in the river. When Princess Bai Long saw that Brother Qing was really a kind-hearted young man, she decided to marry Brother Qing and grow old with Brother Qing.
Princess White Dragon told her father and king about her determination, and the big evil dragon was furious as soon as she heard it: “Huh, an imposing white dragon princess is mainly married to a scavenger, that's not OK!” The dragon flatly refused. As soon as Princess White heard that her father wouldn't agree to her marriage, she didn't think about tea for a few days, washed her face with tears, and finally fought to be cleansed. When the dragon watched his baby daughter cry to death, he had no choice but to agree to this family affair, but he made a request to the daughter: only Brother Zhao Qing came to the house and become her son-in-law. As soon as Princess Bai Long heard that her father had agreed to her marriage, she turned her worries to happy, and asked her father to take Brother Qing to the Dragon Palace early. The dragon had no choice but to obey the princess.
The next day, Evil Dragon waited early while Qingge was on his way to sell firewood. Soon, I saw Brother Qing crossing the river with clear resin on his back. When he arrived at the center of the river, the evil dragon took the opportunity to set off a wave of evil and engulf Qing Ge in the Dragon Palace. The dragon shouted, “My baby girl, come out and meet your lifesaver.” After a while, I only heard the sound of Huan Pei. Surrounded by women from the palace, Princess White Dragon walked up to Brother Qing and saluted Qing Ge: “Thank Brother Qing for saving his life. Please be prayed by the little girl.” Brother Qing hurriedly picked up Princess White Dragon and said, “I've never saved your life; I really don't dare to say a benefactor.” Princess White Dragon said, “The day you traded clear butter and animal skin for small white scallops on the street, and then put small white pellefish in the river. That little white scaly fish is me.” When I finished speaking, I would like to say a deep prayer to Brother Qing. The dragon said, “Brother Qing, my daughter is beautiful and kind. She hasn't left the cabinet yet. You are also her lifesaver. Old man, I'll marry her as your wife.” Brother Qing said, “Dragon King, I'm a woodcutter. My family is poor. How can I be worthy of the noble Princess White Dragon? What's more, my mother is still waiting for me to sell chai to buy rice and go home to cook.” The dragon said, “How difficult is this! Yasha, bring a bag of rice and a few pieces of meat to Brother Qing's house to give to his mother.” Yasha took the lead. The dragon also said, “Brother Qing, you can rest assured now. You and Princess White Dragon are getting married tonight. I'm happy today. I want to host your wedding myself.” The entire Dragon Palace was brightly lit and full of joy. Grandma Dragon dressed up her daughter. At night, the husband and wife both entered the cave room. After getting married, Qing Ge enjoyed all the glory and wealth at the Dragon Palace. After about a month, Brother Qing missed her mother and said to the princess, “Princess, my mother is alone at home. I want to go home and take a look.” Princess White Dragon said, “Then let's go as husband and wife.” Princess White Dragon immediately confessed to her father and Wang. The dragon said, “It's rare for a daughter to have such filial piety. You and your husband should go together, but the family visit lasts for three days, so go back quickly after watching.” Qing Ge and his wife are happy to return to Xiuling. The news that Qing Ge is married to a White Dragon girl quickly spread all over the village. Everyone in the village, young and old, visited Princess Bailong. Three days soon passed, and the Evil Dragon Slayer urged the two to quickly return to Dragon Palace. People had no choice but to say goodbye to Princess White Dragon. When she left, the princess asked everyone what they wanted. The villagers all said in unison, “I hope to build a suspension bridge over the Yangjiao River.” Princess White Dragon said, “OK, let's wait for tomorrow at a place full of white clouds on the Yangjiao River!”
The next morning, Princess Bailong arrived at the riverside and turned into a white rainbow, crossing both sides of the Yangjiao River. When the sun rises, the silver light shines brightly. People rushed to tell each other that it was Princess White Dragon who chose the bridge site for everyone. Soon, people built a suspension bridge in this area.
In honor of Princess White Dragon, people named it Yunlong Bridge.
