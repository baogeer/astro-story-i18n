 There is a strange natural landscape northwest of Shimenkou, about 40 miles northwest of the bright light. There is a crack in the rocky cliff on the mountainside. It is about one foot wide, seven feet long, and unfathomably deep. The stone walls on both sides of the crack look like smoke and fire. If you rub them with your hands, there will be a layer of dark gray on your hands. Dark smoke often comes out of this crack, and it looks like a cloud from a distance. Every time there is dark smoke, it rains. The concentration of black smoke, the size of the smoke cloud is directly proportional to the amount of rainfall. Once upon a time, a child herding a cow covered the hood in the crack, and the dark smoke that came out actually placed the hat above the clouds and swirled in the air. This is a natural phenomenon, but it has been passed down by ordinary people for thousands of years.
Long ago, Taijin Star Li Changgeng lived and practiced here. There was a stone alchemy room on the hillside. There are four Taoists in the Alchemy Room who take turns adding firewood to the tanker day and night. Since it takes 7,749 days and nights to make a kiln, there are also four Taoists who spend the whole day gathering firewood in the mountains. It is said that this pill can help people get their way and become immortal soon. This is called “silver pill.” Another type called “Golden Pill” requires 981 days of refinement. This “golden pill” enables people to live forever without getting old, and not invade all diseases.
Of the four Taoist children who set fire, the slightly older one is called a blue cow. This young cow has had a desire to become a fairy for a long time. Although he only works one shift a day, he runs to Danfang to urge his other teachers and brothers to cut firewood, hold firewood, add firewood, and sometimes help with the work. Li Changgeng was very happy to see that Tsing Niu was so diligent. He called him to take charge of Danfang affairs. Since then, Tsing Niu has worked even harder. All of his tiring work and activities have been taken care of. All of his younger teachers and younger siblings love him. Seeing that 49 days had already arrived, the Danfang cease-fire came out and the blue cow packed a hawthorn-sized Dan pill in the Dan Gourd in front of the master. 50 tablets at a time was quite a bit, covered the lid and offered it to Li Changgeng. I don't know if Qingniu had good intentions. When he was packing the last pill, he had already prepared to hold a stone about the size and shape of a Dan pill in his left hand and put it in the Dan Gourd, hiding it from his master.
When Qingniu secretly took Dan Pills, he felt a surge of warmth all over his body, a sharp rise in energy, and a surge in strength. He never felt tired after being busy all day long. Qingniu is happy at heart, but on the surface, it still says that if nothing happened, help the master fix the tan furnace and prepare to refine the “Golden Dan,” which can only be completed in 1998. Lee Chang-geng was also very satisfied with this apprentice. He made a wish and said, “When I stayed at the Princess Mother's Peach Festival, I offered Yin Dan and Jin Dan. When I came back, I officially taught you to practice sutras to help you become an immortal in the future.” The green cow was lying on the ground and prostrated to his master over and over again. Thank you very much.
Lee Chang-geng settled down and soon opened a fire to refine Jin Dan. Originally, there weren't many trees on Shimen Mountain. Refining Jin Dan required burning a large amount of wood. Once the trees nearby were cut down, they had to run a long distance to cut down. Gradually, the four Taoist children who cut firewood cut enough firewood every day for the day. If it were to rain in the sky, the Danbo had no spare firewood, and the Danbo would go out of style, then a big mistake would have been made. The four firewood choppers reported to their master at the same time that Li Changgeng was also worried about this issue, because the Danfang must prepare dry firewood for at least seven days to ensure that the fire continues. Li Changgeng asked if Qingniu needed to get help from Chai Woodman again. Qingniu said no use; he could help burn the fire every night and help cut firewood during the day. Since Qingniu has taken Yindan, he is no longer an ordinary person. He is able to use his energy to top ten people. Since then, he has been using firewood during the day and burning fires at night, and the Dan room has been filled with spare dry firewood. The four Taoists who cut firewood also treated the green cow like a big brother. Lee Chang-geng also has more trust in green cows. He knows that if they recommend green cows to their wife, they will definitely agree to practice green cows and go to heaven in the future. Therefore, in the morning and evening, I also intentionally started teaching young cows basic techniques such as meditating and chanting sutras. Qingniu has an extraordinary memory and never forgets anything. Lee Chang-geng is becoming more and more satisfied with this apprentice.
The 981st day has arrived, and the flames have been turned off, the furnace has been stopped, and the Dan has been taken. Qingniu still uses it again and again. After hiding it from his master, he took a pill of Golden Pill. After taking it, the power was even doubled. When one person secretly went into the mountains to test his skills, Teng Yun actually had the ability to drive fog.
At Mother Wang's Peach Festival, Taijin Star Li Changgeng brought refined Xian Dan and boasted about the wonderful uses and powers of Yin Dan and Jin Dan, which went out of the limelight. Mother Wang was very happy to hear it. She opened the lid of the Jindan Gourd and poured it out. Just about to put it in her mouth, the barefoot fairy next to her said, “Why is it like a stone?” Mother Wang took a look. It was a stone. She couldn't help but sink her face and asked Taibai Xingdao, “What's going on with this?” Lee Chang-geng took a look; it was indeed a stone. The fairies laughed out loud. Taijin Star Li Changgeng had no place for himself. Then he poured out the other Dan pills. It was true that they were rotten, and when Yin Dan was poured out, he also discovered a stone. He dedicated Yin Dan and Jin Dan to the royal mother. At this point, the royal mother's interest was over, and she handed them to the palace girl around her.
Taijin Star Li Changgeng was ridiculed by the immortals at the Peach Festival. He was furious. He pointed out that the blue cow did it; he couldn't get angry. When they returned to the alchemy room, they sent someone to call the blue cow. It is certain that the master will know sooner or later that the master was planning to escape, but he knew that the master could be caught and found wherever he escaped, he would definitely die. Why don't you kneel down and plead guilty; maybe the master will let go of his usual feelings. When Qingniu heard the master called him, he crawled up in front of the master and knelt down to the ground. Taijin Star Li Changgeng scolded while counting. The more he talked, the more angry he got. He raised the dust in his hands to kill the green cow. Standing on the sidelines, the three fire-burning children and the four firewood choppers all knelt down to plead for the blue cow. Lee Chang-geng changed his mind and thought: This young cow is also pretty good. It's also quite honest; it's just a matter of sincerity. Forget it, let's keep him alive! Li Changgeng stared at the dust in his hands and sighed, “Blue Bull, you should become a young man. From now on, you have to be diligent, honest and enlightened. I'll see you in Tiangong after a thousand years.” Just before we met, the young man actually turned into a cow, and left one by one with tears of regret in both eyes.
Later, this young man became Laozi's mount. One day, when Laozi was riding a green cow past Shimen Mountain, the green cow remembered stealing Xian Dan and being demeaned as a cow by his master. But after all, it was still a mentoring and apprenticeship game. The young man ran desperately to the mountain. Laozi really stomped his foot. This stomping actually left a big footprint on a big rock.
The 49 silver medals and 49 gold medals offered by Taijin Star Li Changgeng were later taken by Wang's mother. They were indeed Xian Dan. Mrs. Wang thought of Li Changgeng at the Pantao Party; it was a bit later. Later agents invited Li Changgeng to Tiangong to specially refine Xian Dan. After Lee Chang-geng left, the Danfang also collapsed. Since the flames were not extinguished, there was still a burst of black smoke. Afterwards, even the rubble disappeared, and the Danfang branch went out of the mountain, but the fire was still burning, and sometimes black smoke came out. From afar, I saw clusters of dark clouds gushing out of the cave. Therefore, local people call it “Izumo Cave.”
Many years have passed, and the hole has become smaller, leaving only a crack that is not wide. But people still call it “Izumo Cave.” Between Izumo Cave and the stone gate, the footprints Laozi left back then grew bigger and bigger with the stones.
