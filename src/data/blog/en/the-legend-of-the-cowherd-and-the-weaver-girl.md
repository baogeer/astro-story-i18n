 Tangram Festival
July 7 of the lunar calendar is our Chinese Valentine's Day. Some people also call it Beggar's Day Tanabata or Girls' Day. This day is the most important day for our daughter's family. The reason why early July 7 is called Begging is because of popular belief that cowherds and weavers will meet in the Tianhe River on this day. At night, the daughter's family prays melons and fruits to the sky, begging the goddess for good luck, hoping to get it.
Begging for luck, in addition to begging for the skills of famous knitters, they are also begging for a good match in marriage. Therefore, countless passionate men and women in the world will pray for a happy marriage against the starry sky this night, when people are quiet at night.
In Fujian Province, Guangdong Province, and Southeast Asia, it is most popular to worship the Seven Sisters on Tanabata. This is indeed a big festival at my daughter's house in the old days. Most provinces have the Tanabata Festival, which celebrates Tanabata, but most of them celebrate it as an ordinary celebration. Fujian and Guangzhou provinces, on the other hand, take quite a bit of attention, especially Guangdong Province. Everyone is in a lively setting, which is very solemn.
When it comes to the Tangram Festival, I have to mention the love story spread among the Chinese people - the cowherd and the weaver. Tanabata, where they meet once a year, is regarded by their passionate children as a symbol of love. The tragedy of their love left a deep impression on the hearts of every Chinese. Therefore, every night of Tanabata, many lovers pray to the dark starry sky for everlasting love.
Of course, this is just a legend. The ceremony of begging comes from the original belief of the ancient weaver, the god Mulberry. This belief, combined with the statement that the cowherd and weaver meet on July 7 every year, has become our current Tanabata 7 Kao folk belief.
The legend of Tanabata
A long time ago, there was an orphan who lived with his brother and sister-in-law. However, his sister-in-law still rejected him and rushed him up the mountain to herd cows when he was not bright. Everyone called him a cowherd. After getting older, my brother and sister split up with the cowherd. My sister-in-law with a wolf heart only gave him a broken house and an old cow. Since then, the cowherd herds cows during the day, cuts firewood, and sleeps with the old cow in that broken room at night.
One day, the cowherd rushed the cow into an unfamiliar forest. The mountains, water, and flowers were fragrant. The cowherd saw nine fairies falling on the meadow of the river with good clouds, then took off his colorful clothes and jumped into the crystal clear water. The cowherd stared at the youngest, a beautiful fairy. Ke took a look. At this point, Lao Niu suddenly said, “She is the weaver of heaven. As long as you take the colorful clothes, she will be your wife.” The cowherd quietly followed the tree and quietly took the weaver's multicolored costume. Close to noon, the other fairies dressed in colorful neon costumes and drove off to the clouds. The only woman who couldn't find the colorful neon pattern was left behind. At this point, the cowherd walked out of the tree and asked Orina to be his wife. Seeing that the cowherd was loyal and honest, hardworking, and hardworking, he nodded his head.
After the cowherd and the weaver tied the knot, men and women weaved, and respected each other and loved each other. Two years later, Ori gave birth to a boy and a girl. However, the Emperor was furious when he learned that a woman had married an old lady. At the beginning of July 7, Wang Mu took Tianbingtian and captured the weaver. The grieving cowherd, with the help of the old cow, picked up his children and went to heaven with the help of the old cow. Seeing that they had caught up, the royal mother dropped the golden hairpin, and a choppy Tianhe immediately appeared under the cowherd's feet.
The weaver, who had a broken heart, and the cowherd who picked her children, were moved by the sound of crying from afar. Countless magpies flew to the Tianhe River and built a magpie bridge. The cowherd and weaver were finally able to meet on the Magpie Bridge. The princess was helpless, so she had to allow the cowherd and weaver to meet on the bridge once every year on July 7.
