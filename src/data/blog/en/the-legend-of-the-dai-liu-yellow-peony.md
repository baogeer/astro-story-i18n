 In the old days, there was a descendant near Luoyang, whose name was Liu Mingdanting. Since childhood, he has loved flowers like his life, and has become addicted to growing flowers. Among all kinds of flowers, peonies are particularly good. They have planted many flowers and peonies in the front and back of the courtyard. However, precisely because he grows flowers well, he is often harassed by urchins. He was very angry. Every time he caught him, the lighter one was given a minute's hard work, and the heavier one was punched several times. As a result, local urchins nicknamed him “Bad Liu.” In this way, it gradually spread, and over time, it took over his name and became a “big name.”
Speaking of “Nao Liu,” his flower growing skills are truly remarkable. He grows more than 100 peonies, and there are many varieties with large and colorful flowers. One year, he cultivated a golden peony. Its color surpassed “Yao Huang”. Neighbors were amazed, and the rich people sought gold and silver. Yellow peonies were scarce at the time, so they multiplied them in large numbers and competed for a while to plant them near and far, becoming a fashion. Later, people named this flower “Bad Liu Huang,” and this product name was included in books and spread.
(Quoted from “Peony Grand View” Liu Xiang and Xu Xiaofan)
