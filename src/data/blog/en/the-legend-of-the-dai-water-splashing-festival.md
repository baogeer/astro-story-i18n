 A long, long time ago, there was a vicious demon king. He had magic. He couldn't float away when he fell into the water, didn't burn when he fell into the water, didn't break with a knife, couldn't get stabbed with a gun, and couldn't shoot a bow or arrow. He has superior mana, is arrogant and arrogant, and has been overbearing all day long, committing evil acts against evil. At that time, there were sixteen levels in the sky, and he became the hegemon of one of them. He bullied and looted the people; he did nothing evil. He already has six beautiful wives, but if any family has a beautiful daughter, he will take over as his wife. Once, he saw that one of the princesses on earth named Zongbu looked prettier than his six wives, so he snatched her and became his 7th wife.
One year in June, the day of the New Year on earth, the devil king invited demons and demons to drink and have fun in the palace for the Lunar New Year. After three rounds of drinking, all the guests were already drunk. Zongbu took the plane to praise the demon king: “My noble king, you have limitless mana and noble morals. With your prestige, you can completely conquer heaven, hell, and the world. You should be the master of the three realms.” The demon king listened with pleasure. After thinking for a while, he turned his face and said to his beloved wife, “I can conquer the Three Realms. No one knows my weaknesses.” Zongbu went on to ask, “If the king has such magic, how can he have weaknesses?” The devil whispered, “I'm afraid someone will pull my hair and hurt my neck. This will split my neck, so you have to watch a little more often.” Zongbu pretended to be surprised and asked, “Why is the king who can conquer the Three Realms afraid of hair hair?” The demon king whispered again, “My hair is small, but my hair will break my neck, so I won't be able to survive.”
