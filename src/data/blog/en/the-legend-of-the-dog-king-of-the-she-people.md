 Legend has it that in ancient times, there was a King Gao Xin in China who wanted to go to war with a people in the west called Inujung. They have fought many times in a row, and they have lost battles every time. So King Gao Xin summoned ministers to discuss it. There was nothing for the ministers. King Gao Xin was in a hurry to walk up and down the hall, frightening the ministers. King Gao Xin thought about it and wanted to say, “We can only recruit people from the list.” King Gao Xin wrote on the list, “If anyone can help me win the war, I'll give him a princess.” It's been a few days since the list was released, and Gao Xin Wang's heart became even more anxious.
One day, a dog unraveled the royal list, and the soldiers brought the dog to King Gao Xin's face. King Gao Xin was overjoyed to hear someone report: “Has anyone unveiled the list?” The soldier whispered, “Yes, it's just a dog.” The soldier brought the dog. King Gao Xin said, “How can you help me win this battle?” The dog proudly said, “Anyway, I can help you win, and you'll know when the time comes.” King Gao Xin had no choice but to wait and see.
After a few days, King Gao Xin sent troops to attack Inu Rong again, but unfortunately he still failed. While InuEbisu was having a feast, King Inu was already hiding in his military camp. King InuEbisu was very happy at the time, drinking and singing and dancing with the soldiers. InuEbisu and his soldiers drank too much alcohol and were too drunk. King Inu took advantage of this opportunity to cut off King Inujung's head and ran desperately. After the Inujung soldier in the back discovered it, they desperately chased him and ran for a while. King Gao Xin took over and beat Inujung to death.
Back at the Imperial Palace, Gao Xin Wang wanted to talk about merit. Of course, his greatest credit was Gu Wang. Gao Xin Wang smiled and said to Gou Wang, “Out of my three daughters, which one would you choose?” The dog king walked up to the three princesses and bit the three princesses' clothes with his mouth. The three princesses also love Dog King, but the queen doesn't want to marry her daughter to a dog.
King Gao Xin said, “There's nothing you can do about it! A gentleman's words are hard to catch up with.” Dog King thought about it and wanted to say, “Actually, I can become a human. If you take out the Golden Bell, I can become a human after seven days of drilling in.”
Seven days was a long time for the princess, and the three princesses were afraid that the dog king would be fine if they didn't eat or drink inside. After five days, the three princesses couldn't help but secretly open the golden bell shield. Oops, the dog's body had changed, but the dog's head hadn't changed, but once the lid was opened, they couldn't change anymore; they were just dog heads.
When King Gao Xin saw this situation, he asked Gou Wang to find a place to hide with his daughter, allocate them a plot of land, and take whatever they wanted. Dog King brought her princess to Nanling on the border between Guangdong and Hunan to begin a life as a husband and wife.
After more than ten years, Gou Wang and the Three Princesses gave birth to three sons and a daughter, but they still don't have a good last name. After discussions with the Three Princesses, Dog King decided to go back to the Imperial Palace and ask the emperor to give him a surname.
When Gou Wang's family returned to the palace, Gao Xin Wang asked, “We haven't seen you in a long time, are you doing well?” Gu Wang said, “Thank the emperor for his concern. I have something to discuss with you when I get back. My children are all in their teens and don't have a good last name, so I can't let them take my last name. I beg the emperor to give them a last name.”
The emperor asked, “What characteristics did your children have when they were born? ”
“The oldest son was born with a plate on it. The second son was born when there was a thunder. The third son loved to carry agricultural tools after birth, and the daughter heard a bell when she was born.” Dog King said.
Gao Xin Wang said, “Since this is the case, your oldest son's last name is Pan, your second son's last name is Lei, and your third son's last name is Yi, so your daughter's last name is Zhong.”
As a result, Shes Village now has four surnames: Pan, Lei, Dao, and Zhong.
Later, while hunting and chasing deer, Dog King accidentally fell down a cliff and died. His descendants continued to reproduce and migrate, and scattered to various southeastern provinces in China, forming a brave nation that lived deep in the mountains and hunted for a living.
