 Many regions of China celebrate eating rice dumplings during the Dragon Boat Festival on May 5 to commemorate the ancient patriotic poet Qu Yuan. However, there are exceptions. The Dong family in the Zhaimu area of Jingping County, Guizhou, ate rice dumplings on June Day to commemorate Lin Wang, the leader of a local Dong peasant uprising. During the holiday season, Zhaimu not only packed regular sized rice dumplings, but also large rice dumplings that were as thick as a big bowl and more than two feet long. Legend has it that back then, when Lin Wang led the army, he often used rice dumplings as dry food, so he wrapped rice dumplings and grew big.
Lin Wang's name is Lin Kuan. He was born in the Zhaimu region. His father, Lin Rang, participated in the Mian Wang Uprising. After the defeat, he was persecuted by officers and soldiers. After the defeat, he moved along the Langdong of Guzhou from Liping's Hongzhou area, through the Qingshan border, and across the Bashu River to his current place of residence. At the time, there were no people in the Podong area, and only then did they move to many Dong families one after another. Since this stockade was built the earliest, it is called Zhaimu by the surrounding stockade.
Lin Wang has a grandpa (he was only born after his father died). His family is poor. Since childhood, he has worked for rich owners, cut firewood and grass, herded cows and horses, raised fish and watched the water in the fields. He is tall and good-looking, and has an outstanding personality. He is extremely powerful, smart and capable. He is one of the best, and he loves helping people. The townspeople love him.
Legend has it that Lin Wang raised fish for the rich man in the mountain pond. The rich man told him to cut grass and feed the fish, so he rushed the fish uphill to eat grass. When he was full, he rushed back to the pond to put it away. As a result, the fish he fed were fat and big. He loves horses. During the day, he herded horses to graze, and practiced martial arts on horses. At night, he slept beside the horse and fed it tall and strong, and later became his war horse. Now, behind Zhaimu, he still has the stone horse ring he built back then. Both sides have been destroyed, and there are still two sides left. There are still horseshoe prints and his straw shoes on the bluestone slab. Once, Lin Wang herded a cow for a rich owner in the mountains. Seeing that many poor people had no food and was very unbearable, he knocked a cow to death and cooked for everyone to eat. Others were worried that he wouldn't be able to call him bad. He made an ingenious move and thought of a way to ask someone to stick their tail in a rock crevice and ask someone to hide in a cave and wait. He immediately ran to call the rich owner, saying that a cow stuck in the rock crevice and couldn't get out. The rich man hurriedly caught up to the mountain. At first glance, only the cow's tail was exposed in the rock crevice. The rich man then ripped off the cow's tail. The people in the cave learned how to call the cow; the more he pulled the cow, the more it barked. As a result, the cow's tail was broken, and the cow didn't come out, so they had no choice but to go back.
Lin Wang is enthusiastic about the position of everyone to do good deeds. There is a rock cave on the side of the road in Po-dong. There is a clear spring in the cave, but it doesn't flow out. Passers-by are thirsty and unable to drink it. After Lin Wang learned, he bit off one of his mouths, opened the rock slab with both hands, a stream of clear water flowed out, and the pedestrians had water to drink. If you don't believe me, you can check it out; now there are Lin Wang's finger prints and two knee prints on the slate beside the spring.
Zhai Mu's Houlong Mountain has two slopes facing each other. It is separated by a deep valley in the middle. Normally, when they go to Daimen slope to farm, it is very inconvenient for people to go around. Lin Wang then pulled out a long rock from the opposite side of the gate and made it a stone bridge. Her mother said, “The bridge you built looks dazzling; don't hurt others to fall.” Lin Wang broke the bridge with one punch. Until now, there are still broken rock beams several feet long on both sides of the slope.
There were many people and few fields in the Podong area, so King Lin wanted to cut off the Hachju River and divert water across Podong to clear the fields. Early in the morning, he drove three slopes to the Saihe River with a big whip. He happened to be seen by an old woman. She shouted, “Look at the three slopes, you can walk.” As a result, the three slopes stopped in the middle of Zhaimu Tianba and didn't go. The three slopes were called Yuanyang Slope, Dingbian Slope, and Baodu Slope, and were not connected to the other slopes. It has now been developed as a terraced field.
After Lin Wang grew up, he saw more and more people fleeing here from abroad, and the whole Po Cave was filled to the brim. There wasn't enough food to eat; the officers and soldiers came to grab food and rob people. People had no choice; the Dong family couldn't survive. As a result, 48 Dongzhai villagers (elders in the village) and Xia Fang (youth) from 48 Dongzhai, including Po Dong, Shangdong, and Yudong, gathered to discuss the affairs of the village mother. The villagers said, “Our Dong family has been bullied for generations. Can't we live a peaceful life?” Everyone decided that each of the 48 villages would elect one person to participate in the collective king election. Xuan Wang followed the Mian Wang Uprising, and each person planted a maple tree upside down. Whoever lived first was the king. Zhai's mother recommended Lin Kuan. At the beginning of the king election, 48 people planted a tree upside down in the zhai mother village. The one planted by Lin Kuan lived the fastest. Everyone chose him as king in one fell swoop, and Lin Wang got his name ever since. Currently, there is also a maple tree in the mother village of the village. It rises above the ground and has branches facing downwards. Unlike other trees, it is said that it was planted by King Lin.
Lin Wang joined forces with the poor everywhere, commanded all villages to fight and build arrows. The following year, he gathered more than 100,000 poor people to prepare for the uprising. For the sake of the uprising, Lin Wang also suspended his marriage. Legend has it that the girl he is good with is Mao Wang. She lives in Mu Wangzhai and has been cutting firewood, mowing grass, and singing mountain songs with Lin Wang since childhood. After the two grew up, the two elderly families are preparing to get married. However, the two young people are very ambitious. They said, “The world is so chaotic and the people are suffering so much, we have to wait until people's suffering is solved before we get married.” The old man couldn't make sense, so they both agreed: Lin Wang went from Kuliao, Maowang from Mu Wangzhai, and hit a stone at each of the two places at the same time to see if the two stones could touch each other. If they did, they would get married immediately; otherwise, they would postpone the marriage. As a result, Lin Wang's stone happened to hit Koujie Ao. Maowang had less strength and was unable to hit Koujie Ao. The two stones didn't touch each other, so they were in no hurry to get married; they all joined the uprising later.
King Lin of the uprising was very brave. Under his command, he defeated the city of Longli early in the morning. Everyone went back to Zhaimu Cave to have breakfast, and then captured places such as Xinhua and Pingcha. The plan was to attack Changsha, attack Nanjing, and overthrow the Ming dynasty. Legend has it that he built three arrows and almost shot King Chu to death. The first night, he woke him up when his mother called Wu Geng roe. After waking up, he attacked King Chu with his army. He hit King Chu's camp with three arrows in a row, and the three arrows hit King Chu's throne. However, he shot a little earlier; if he waited for King Wu Geng Chu to take his place, he could shoot King Chu to death. Later, Lin Wang was heavily surrounded by Chu Wang's 300,000 army. In the end, they ran out of ammunition and food, and the rebels were unable to break through anywhere. Lin Wang was so angry that he stuck his sword in the rock slab. As a result, Lin Wang was arrested. He also had his head cut off, but Lin Wang was not convinced. He held his head with both hands, went home and asked his mother to settle down, and fought against the officers and soldiers again. Later, he was beheaded twice and beheaded twice, until the third time, when his head never settled down, and the body fell down.
After Lin Wang's uprising failed, the imperial court feared another uprising by the local Dong family, and sent Zheng Zilong to take troops to dig up Lin Wang's ancestral tomb and dragon veins. Now, the deep, criss-crossed ditch on Longshan Mountain behind Zhaimu was dug back then. Although the dragon line has been cut, the peasant uprising continues. Sixteen years later, in the 11th year of Yongle, peasants in Podong and other places revolted again. In the 14th year of Orthodoxy, Li Ping and the peasants in Zhanqiao revolted, killing state officials, gathering 300,000 people and besieging the Bronze Drum Guard.
Lin Wang served everyone's welfare, and people will always remember him. Although he was falsely accused by history books as a “thug,” in the Dong family's mind, he is a hero. Every year in June of the lunar calendar, Zhaimu has to celebrate the festival. Other members of the common family also have to spend the holidays. This day is the most solemn holiday of the year other than the Spring Festival. It is said that the Feichen Festival was originally built for the village mother to first build a village and commemorate the ancestors of the Lin family. After Lin Wang left, Lin Wang took the lead. The surrounding Dong family put it off by one day in order to respect that Zhaimu is a mother village, and all have the last name Lin, and Lin Wang was born to Zhaimu again.
On the first day of the holiday season, Zhaimu slaughtered pigs and cows. The family released the fields to catch fish, grind tofu, and wrap rice dumplings. They also sent their children to the neighboring stockade to call for customers. On the day of the holiday, before eating, all families first arranged wine, meat, rice dumplings, and incense under the ancient maple tree to sacrifice their ancestors. When guests enter the village, they also have to pray under the ancient maple tree. Some elderly people also have to peel off a few pieces of bark from the ancient maple tree, wrap them in paper, and take them back to the children to pray for blessings and relief from the disaster. After noon, we started eating. The dinner was plentiful, singing while eating, and blessing each other. After eating, the host invited the guests, and the children sang “Lin Wang Ancient Song” under the ancient maple tree. After singing, the elderly told Lin Wang's story to the children. It was very lively all night long.
