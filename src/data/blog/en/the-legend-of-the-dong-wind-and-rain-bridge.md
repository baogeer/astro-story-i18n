 In ancient times, Pingping Dazhai had not been developed. The Dong family lived in the middle of a hillside. It was a small cottage with only about a dozen families. There is a descendant in a small cottage named Buka who married a wife named Pei Guan. The couple are very affectionate and almost inseparable. When the two came back from work, one picked firewood, one carried a hoe, and one morning sow, they always went back and forth. This Pei Guan is very beautiful. When the couple crossed the bridge, the fish in the river also jumped out of the water with envy to watch them.
One morning, the river suddenly soared. Buka and his wife were in a hurry to work in Nishiyama, but they couldn't care much, so they walked to the small wooden bridge in front of the village together. Just as they walked to the center of the bridge, a gust of wind suddenly blew, making Buka unable to open his eyes, and Peiguan fell into the river with a “oops” sound. Buka opened his eyes and saw that his wife was gone. Knowing that the river had come down, he jumped headlong into the water and dived into the river. However, after looking back and forth for a few laps, I couldn't find it. The townspeople found out, and they rushed to help him find it. After looking for a long time, he still couldn't find Pei Guan. What the hell is going on with this?
It turns out that there was a crab essence deep in the bay, which swept Pei Guan into a cave at the bottom of the river. All of a sudden, Crab Essence became a beautiful descendant. He wanted Pei Guan to be his wife. Pei Guan didn't support him, and he slapped him in the face. He immediately showed his virginity and threatened Bae Guan. Pei Guan cried loudly, and the sound of crying and cursing spread from the bottom of the river to the ears of a flower dragon upstream.
At this point, the wind and rain were mixed, and the waves were rolling. I saw a flower dragon in the wave, looking east and west with their heads held high. When the dragon looks to the left, the wave head hits the left. The landslide on the left, the dragon head looks to the right, the wave heads to the right, and the shore splits on the right. The Ogi Bridge has already been swept away by the waves. The crowd was fearful and shocked. However, when the dragon arrived at the beach in Buka, the dragon calmed down after a few clicks on the waves. Afterwards, Hualong made a circle on the surface of the water and rushed to the bottom of the river. Suddenly, the sound of “heavy and busy” at the bottom of the river continued to be heard, and the whirlpool kept spinning one by one.
Then, a cloud of black smoke came out of the water, rising to mid-air and turning into a cloud of dark clouds. The Hualong rushed to the middle of the sky, twirling its body, and crushed the dark cloud down, and finally crushed it to its original shape. It turned out to be a black crab the size of the top of a drum tower. The black crab ran away in a panic and climbed three feet above the cliff. The dragon flipped its head into the water, swung its tail, and swept the crab across the water. After a few rounds like this, the crabs were exhausted, swayed and climbed to the bamboo forest, hoping to use bamboo to stop the flower dragon. However, as soon as the flower dragon jumped up and spewed water from its mouth, the bamboo forest fell down one by one, and the crab fell into the river again. After Hualong closely followed the bottom of the water, the waves rolled over and down the river. At this point, the black crab was no longer visible. Later, not far from the river bay, a black crab-shaped stone was revealed, where Hualong settled the crab essence. This stone was later called a crab stone.
After the surface of the river calmed down, I heard the voice of a woman on the opposite beach calling. When Buka took a look, she was her own wife. Buka called a few people to swim right away. After disembarking, Peiguan said to Buka, “Thanks to Hualong for the rescue!” Only then did everyone know that it was Hualong who saved her. They are very grateful to Hualong. At this point, Hualong flew back upstream and nodded frequently at people from time to time.
This incident soon spread throughout Dongxiang. Everyone converted the small wooden bridge close to the surface of the water into a large wooden bridge that looks like a promenade in the sky, and also engraved the pattern of a flower dragon on the four columns of the bridge. I wish that the flower dragon will always be there. After the construction of the large wooden bridge with an aerial promenade, a grand celebration ceremony was held, which was very lively. At this point, colorful clouds floated in the sky, shaped like a long dragon, and there was plenty of light. When the crowd took a closer look, it was Hualong who came back to visit everyone. Therefore, later generations called this kind of bridge Huilong Bridge. Some places are also called flower bridges, and they are also called wind and rain bridges because the bridge can hide from wind and rain.
