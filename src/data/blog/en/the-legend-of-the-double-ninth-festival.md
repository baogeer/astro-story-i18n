 Legend has it that a long time ago, there was a god of plague. As soon as he appeared, people in the village fell ill, and ordinary people were tortured to the fullest. There is a teenager named Jing Heng. The plague took the lives of all his loved ones. He vowed to learn spells and defeat Plague. He searched for famous teachers everywhere, and was not afraid of the distance, hardships and dangers of the road. Under the guidance of a fairy crane, he finally found Xianchang, who has magical powers, on Mount Zhongnan. After studying for ten years, he finally developed an extraordinary martial art.
On this day, Xian Chang summoned Heng Jing to the front and said, “Tomorrow is September 9, and the god of plague will come out to do evil again. You have learned your skills; you should go back and exterminate harm for the people.” Xian Chang gave Heng Jing a pack of dogwood leaves, a cup of chrysanthemum wine, and a demon slayer, so that Heng Jing drove home on a fairy crane.
On the morning of September 9, Heng Jing took the townspeople to a nearby mountain according to Xian Changchang's advice and asked everyone to put cornflower leaves on their bodies. Each person drank a cup of chrysanthemum wine, and prepared to cure the devil himself. Take a quick look. The plague god came to this side of the village, but there was no one in the village. The god of plague was puzzled. Suddenly, the scent of dogwood and chrysanthemum wine fluttered in his nose, making him dizzy. When I looked up, the villagers were all hiding in the mountains, and they rushed up the mountain, crying out loud and angry. At this point, Heng Jing chased down the mountain with a demon sword, and stabbed the god of plague to death in a few rounds. The dark energy has receded, and ordinary people cheered and rejoiced, and celebrated victory at the top of the mountain. Since then, the September 9 climbing event has been passed down year after year.
