 Deep in Taihang Mountain in Xiuwu County, there is a small mountain village called Yidoushui. Although it has only about 200 people, it has a history of hundreds of years, and a large-scale Guansheng Temple has been built. Beneath the hillside of Guansheng Temple, there is a strange giant stone with a beautiful dragon shape. The local people call it Dragon Xianshi. Legend has it that the blue dragon on this stone is the avatar of the dragon on Master Guan Sheng's Qinglong Yueyue Sword.
It turns out that there are droughts every year, and there hasn't been a drop of rain for a few months. People in the village help the elderly and prepare to flee with their young children. At this point, an elderly man said, “It's a wasteland; if we can't leave, we won't leave. We respect the Holy Master in our village; let's go ask him!” Everyone prepared sacrifices and went to Guansheng Temple, so they asked Guan Sheng to save one side's life. The villagers have left, but it's hard to hurt Master Guan Sheng. In fact, Grandpa Guan Sheng has long seen the disaster situation in this area, only because he is not responsible for rainfall, so he is in a hurry. He suddenly saw the Blue Dragon Sword in his hand, so he shook the Blue Dragon Sword and asked, “Where is the Blue Dragon?” The blue dragon on the sword followed Grandpa Guan Sheng in the South and North War, and was also burnt by incense from all sides. He already had energy, but he never showed up. When I heard Grandpa Guan Sheng summoning today, he quickly appeared in the shape of a dragon and bowed down to the ground. Grandpa Guan Sheng said, “Qinglong, I have now seen this area affected by the disaster. As a dragon family, rainfall is your skill. Can you solve the mountain people from being overthrown?” Qinglong replied, “I would like to listen to the Emperor's instructions.” After talking, they soared up and cast spells. There were dark clouds, lightning thunder, and heavy rain. This long period of dry and happy rain lasted all night and saved the people here. Grandpa Guan Sheng didn't want to be greedy. On the night of the same day, he asked the villagers a dream, told everyone about the rain in Qinglong to relieve the drought, and said, “If you don't believe me, you can watch my sword like before. The blue dragon has already become a god and has left my sword.” In the morning, villagers went to Guan Sheng Temple to seek evidence. Sure enough, there was a vivid dragon shape on the boulder on the slope in front of the temple. They also went to the main hall to see that Grandpa Guan's sword had no blue dragon. Since then, although Guansheng Temple in Yidoushui Village has been renovated many times, the Blue Dragon has never been painted on Grandpa Guan's sword.
