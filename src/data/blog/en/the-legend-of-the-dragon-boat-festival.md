 A long, long time ago, in Fukada at the mouth of the Minamino River, there was an evil dragon. It is said that it often swallows people, spits it out, sleeps as a pillow, and rests as a cushion. I don't swallow it until I've played enough with people. There is an old man named Guya in Gadengzhai. He is loyal, honest, hardworking and brave. Not only is he an expert at growing crops, but he is also an expert fisherman. Everyone said, “So Ya is really a good man from the Miao family!” For hundreds of miles, everyone praised him and admired him.
As a result, the couple, who are close to half a hundred years old, have only given birth to a son, a family of three, and are still very affectionate.
At the beginning of May one year, it was already dark. So Ya and her son went fishing at the Minamino estuary. Under the hazy moonlight, the clear blue waves shone on the surface of the water. Gua Ya stood at the bow of the boat and cast a net, and his son supported him at the stern... the more fish he fought, so much so that he forgot about his son. One wave came, and the boat shook, so Ya thought of her son. Looking back, her poor son was sleeping as a pillow by an evil dragon, crushed flat, and he had lost his breath long ago.
Seeing this terrible situation, Anger started from the bottom of her heart, clenched his fists, and attacked the dragon. The evil dragon woke up from her dream and hurriedly rushed towards Guya. Guya jumped one by one and quickly removed the chain of fire. Clicking twice, the fire ignited, and set the Dragon Palace on fire.
The next day, Ma Ma Liang, people saw smoke rising from the mouth of the Minamino River. After another seven days and seven nights, an evil dragon emerged from the deep field, so Asia also floated out of the water. The evil dragon at the mouth of Minamino River was burnt to death, so Ya was also killed by the evil dragon. This news spread ten to ten, and at one point thousands of people gathered by the riverside... They mourned and buried the old man's body under Mount Cloak.
To remember this old man and celebrate peace in the place, people cut down oil cedars, built many dragon boats, and learned the style of the old Dragon Quest. Dozens of people flew like arrows on the surface of the water. They also cut the dragon meat and cooked a pot. Everyone who participated tasted a slice to relieve their heart and hate. They first spent three days and three nights at the mouth of the Minamino River, then cut the dragon meat into small pieces from the head of the river to the end of the river, so that the Miao family on both sides of the river knew how to fight dragons, and how the dragon killed the son of the deceased and himself... from the beginning of May until the end of May before they finished dividing dragon meat.
Since then, the Miao family in the Shimizu River area has had an annual dragon boat festival, accompanied by Lu Sheng, bronze drums, and flying songs. This has been the case year after year, until now.
