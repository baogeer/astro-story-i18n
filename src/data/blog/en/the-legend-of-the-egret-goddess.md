 Legend has it that, a long time ago, Xiamen was a beautiful island. Legend has it that one day, Mother Wang sent the Egret Goddess to visit the Taiwan Strait to Xianzhou - Kinmen Island. From afar, Kinmen Island looked like a beautiful butterfly flying in the ocean. The beautiful scenery deeply attracted the Goddess Egret. She was planning to stay on Kinmen Island for a few more days. One day, the melodious sound of the piano came from the direction of Datan Island, so the goddess climbed up to see it. As a result, the goddess Egret turned a tree into a boat and became a fisherman girl. She boarded a boat on Xiamen Island under the name Egret. She searched for sound and saw one called The young boy from Wan Shi played the piano, and many townspeople sang and danced to wish the old man a long life...
The beautiful scenery of Xiamen Island attracted the beautiful Goddess Egret, and the multi-talented Wan Shi Gong went on a blind date with each other. While the townspeople were preparing for their wedding, a vicious shark spirit came to Xiamen. The waves were choppy and huge waves raging through the sky. Seeing that many townspeople and her beloved Wan Shi Gong were maimed and fell into the mouth of a fish, Goddess Egret bravely fought with the evil Shark Essence. Under the joint efforts of Wan Shi Gong and the townspeople, after a few days and nights, they finally fought. Defeated by Shark Essence, Goddess Egret was seriously injured. At the cost of her precious life, Didi's blood seeped into her beloved Xiamen land. Her body is transformed into many egrets flying on Xiamen Island.
Mother Wang was moved by the “love” of the goddess Egret. Moved by her bravery, she sent white dolphins to guard the waters surrounding Xiamen. Since then, there have been no sharks in the waters of Xiamen. People can go out to work at sea without worry, and the children have fun playing in the water at the seaside.
The islanders also named Xiamen “Heron Island” to commemorate the goddess Egret. Later, they also had Lujiang River and Bailuzhou...
A few years later, many phoenix trees grew on the island, and beautiful phoenix flowers bloomed. They continued to shade the island's people and adorn the island even more beautifully.
Prince Wan Shi was extremely saddened by the loss of his beloved Goddess Egret. He missed it as a stone. He is still standing on Wanshi Mountain, hoping for the return of the goddess...
A few years later, the people of Xiamen and Kinmen also built a tall and beautiful “Egret Goddess Statue” on “Datan Island” to commemorate the beautiful and brave Goddess Egret. Since then, the “Egret Goddess Statue” has become an iconic building in both Xiamen and Jin, attracting people from all over the world to visit Kinmen and Xiamen.
This drama is based on folklore from Xiamen. It mainly tells the story of the holy and affectionate egret. Attracted by the beautiful sound of Prince Wan Shi, they became friends. The snake demon took the opportunity to make trouble and stole the colorful feathers that symbolize life from the egret's body. Egrets bleed more than once, and the specks of blood turned into fiery phoenix trees, dyeing the Lujiang River red... Heaven and earth were blessed and sealed as the “White Heron Goddess,” and the unnamed small island has since been named “Heron Island.”
