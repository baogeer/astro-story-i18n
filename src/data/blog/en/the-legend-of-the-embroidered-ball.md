 8,900 years ago, a poor family lived in a small mountain village under Jiuzhou Ancient Town in Jingxi City, Guangxi City.
A poor young man falls in love with Ah Xiu, a girl from a neighboring village.
Unfortunately, a rich and powerful villain in town also looked down on Ah Xiu and forced him to marry her.
Ah Xiu resolutely refused to do so, so he imprisoned Grandpa in his cell on unwarranted charges and waited for the fall to be executed.
Ah Xiu cried and went blind and began sewing hydrangeas one stitch at a time.
During this period, a sharp needle pierced Ah Xiu's hand. The blood-stained hydrangea flowers were redder, the leaves were greener, and the birds were more vibrant.
After 981 days, the hydrangea was ready.
Ah Xiu sells jewelry to buy a prison guard and sneaks into his cell with his family to visit his skinny old brother.
Ah Xiu hung a hydrangea around Grandpa's neck. Seeing a flash of light, Grandpa and Ah Xiu floated down at the foot of a beautiful and rich mountain far away from the young, and lived a happy life.
Later, hydrangeas became the mascot of the Zhuang people and a symbol of love for young men and women.
