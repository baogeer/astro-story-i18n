 There is a story about aquarium women who embroider lace on their pants and boat-shaped shoes: When the ancestors of aquariums moved from the south to the Sandu area of Guizhou, it was deep in the mountains and dense, overgrown with weeds, and there were many poisonous snakes. Women go out to work, how can we prevent many poisonous snakes. Women go out to work, how can they prevent poisonous snakes from attacking? There is a girl named Xiu. As soon as the viper saw a red, red, green, and green lace, she slipped away quietly. When Miss Xiu got home, she found red and green silk thread and embroidered all kinds of flowers on her cuffs, flaps, cuffs, and bow shoes. She put on this dress and went up the mountain to cut firewood, and the poisonous snake slipped away. She shared her experience with her sisters, and they all embroidered lace on their clothes, pants, and shoes.
