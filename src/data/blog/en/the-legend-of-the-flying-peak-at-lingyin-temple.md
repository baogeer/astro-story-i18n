 Lingyin Temple was built in the first year of Dongjinxian (326). Legend has it that the Indian monk Huili came to this point, believing that Feilai Peak is a place “hidden by fairies,” and built a temple on Mount Suimian and named it “Lingyin.”
Legend has it that on Mount Emei, there used to be a small mountain that could fly. Wherever it flew, it would collapse many houses and crush many people. At that time, there was a crazy monk in Lingyin Temple. He was crazy and insane all day long and didn't abide by the Buddha's rules.
One day, the mad monk learned that at noon, the strange flying mountain would fly down to the village in front of Lingyin Temple. He feared that the mountain would crush many people when it fell. He climbed up and rushed into the village and went door to door saying, “There is a mountain that is about to fly to Zhuangshang at noon today. Everyone move quickly; if it's too late, it's too late!”
He said his lips were broken and his saliva was dry, but no one believed him, let alone that the family was ready to move. The sun is getting higher and higher, and at noon, the crazy monk is in a hurry. At this point, he discovered that a family in the village had become a family. People came and went, and it was very lively. After thinking about it, the crazy monk pushed the crowd away, rushed to the front of the hall, put the bride on her shoulder, snuck out the gate and flew out of the village.
The crazy monk robbed the bride, it's worth it! Everyone caught up with their lives. While chasing them, they shouted, “Catch the crazy monk!” At this point, the whole village chased them out. Only the owner of the village east of the village didn't move. He stood in front of the door and watched the joke, saying, “Running out of the family to rob my new daughter-in-law is really new!”
The crazy monk carried the bride and ran forward vigorously. Everyone has been chasing him for more than ten miles, and they haven't caught up with him. When the sun came to his head, the crazy monk suddenly stood still. He put down the bride from his back and sat down on the ground himself.
People arrived in front of him and were about to grab and beat him, but suddenly it was dark. They couldn't reach for five fingers, and the wind blew loudly. There was a sudden “boom”, and everyone was shocked. When they climbed up and took a look, the wind had stopped and the clouds had stopped, yet a mountain peak had fallen on their village.
Only then did people understand that the crazy monk robbed the bride to save everyone's lives. The village was crushed to the bottom of the mountain, and everyone was left homeless. Some people were in a hurry and started crying. The crazy monk said, “Why cry! What you don't know is that the rich owner of the village has already been crushed to death under the mountain. If you grow your own fields in the future, you are afraid you won't be able to build a house!”
People were happy to be told, and they wanted to disperse with joy. The crazy monk spoke again: “Don't go, don't go, everyone listened to me. Since this mountain can fly from somewhere else, it will also fly away from here and to other places. If we cut 500 of its stone Luohan in the mountain, we can live in the mountain town and not let it fly to other places to harm people. Are you optimistic?”
Everyone listened, said it well, and got down to it right away. After a busy night, 500 pieces of stone Luohan were completely cut out, but it was too late to cut out the eyebrows and eyes. At this point, the mad monk used his long fingernails to stroke Shi Luohan's face. After half a day of hard work, he put all 500 pieces of Shi Luohan in his eyes. Since then, this small mountain peak has stayed in front of Lingyin Temple forever! Because it flew in from somewhere else, it's called “Feilai Peak.”
