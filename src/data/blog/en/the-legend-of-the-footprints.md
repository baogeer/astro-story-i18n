 Jingmei Mountain in the Jingmei District of Taipei City. There is a Senji Rock at the top of the mountain. There is a big footprint on the big rock. Legend has it that it is the footprints of an immortal descending to this place in ancient times. Recently, a temple was built in Iwashita to enshrine Lu Dongbin, known as Xianji Temple.
Legends of this kind of footprints are everywhere in every province in mainland China. As far as Fujian is concerned, in Gushan, on the outskirts of the provincial city of Fuzhou, there is an Luohan platform with two or three footprints on the big rock. Legend has it that the Eighteen Arhats visited Gushan in ancient times.
