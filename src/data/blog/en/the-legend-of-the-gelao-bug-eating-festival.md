 Every year on the second day of June of the lunar calendar, the Yilao people eat bugs.
Legend has it that in the olden days, the Yilao Mountain Pest was plagued year after year, and the grain harvest failed. People are helpless in the face of insect infestations. After discussions, the village elders were highly praised: who can get rid of the pests and reward the three fat pigs. As soon as the red list came out, the rooster came first. It said, “I woke up the earliest, and I can get rid of the pests.” Who knows, when the rooster arrived in the field, it didn't eat a few bugs, so it got wet with dew and shuddered. Next, the duck unveiled the list. It said, “My feathers are not afraid of water, and my mouth is big, so I can definitely get rid of pests.” However, when ducks swim in water, pests fly on seedlings, their necks stretch so long that they can't eat a few bugs. Finally, a Taoist unveiled the list. He said, “My magic can cure pests.” He tried hard to cast the spell, but when the bug understood the spell, the Taoist was defeated.
Seeing that one year's harvest is about to be eaten by pests, people are even more anxious. At this point, on the second day of June, a poor man named Jia Niang suddenly made an unexpected discovery. She came back to her mother's house from a foreign country without a present, and she was very sad. She thought as she walked, but couldn't think of a way. When she walked to her field, she was so worried that she couldn't move, so she sat on the field to rest. When a few kids saw that their mother wouldn't leave, they went to the field to catch bugs and had fun. All of a sudden, they caught several packs. When Jia Niang saw it, she suddenly thought, just use a bug as a present. As a result, she brought the bugs home and stir-fried them for everyone to eat. Everyone thought it was fragrant and delicious. The discovery spread all of a sudden. People are scrambling to catch bugs to eat. The number of pests was drastically reduced, and a big harvest was achieved that year. The villagers praised Jia Niang's three fat pigs; Jia Niang killed the pigs and distributed them to the people. Later, Jia Niang died, and people built a temple in the middle of the field to commemorate Jia Niang. This temple was later called the “Temple of Eating Bugs.”
Since then, every year on the 2nd day of June, every village in the Yilao ethnic group has had to kill pigs for the bug eating festival. On this day, Murakami's married girl had to go back to her mother's house and go all the way to catch bugs. After dinner, people gathered at the Temple to eat bugs and sing and dance. Afterwards, line up to the Tiandong parade. While walking, they also scattered small white flags with chicken blood to express demonstrations against pests and to commemorate the lady.
