 Guo Zhai, Madou Town, Tainan County, is known as one of the oldest existing residential buildings in the province. Legend has it that during the Kangxi period of the Qing Dynasty, someone in Madou was instructed by a geographer to build a house in a location with excellent Feng Shui, and became the richest man in this area. Soon, the geographer became blind, and the rich man, appreciating his old favor, invited him to stay at his home and promised to provide him with lamb of his choice at every meal.
The geographers lived in the rich family for a while and were at ease with each other. One day, while eating, the geographer discovered that the table was full of lamb, and invited others to eat together. But no one accepted it. A child labourer later claimed that the lamb of the day was a pair of sheep that lost their footing and fell into a cesspit. The rich man made him blind and cooked it for him to eat. The geographer was outraged when he learned of the incident.
A few days later, I told the rich man that the pond in front of his door was harmful to Feng Shui, and advised the rich man to turn it into an orchard. Unaware that this was an act of revenge from the geographer, and he followed his advice. Since then, the family has experienced a series of misfortunes and gradually declined. This is a typical Feng Shui legend.
