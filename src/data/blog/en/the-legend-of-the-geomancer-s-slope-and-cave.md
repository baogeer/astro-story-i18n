 Legend has it that there is a mountain by the small Liujiapo River in Tangjia Village, Linchong Township, Xinhuang Dong Autonomous County. The name is Teacher Po (teacher: Mr. Feng Shui in ancient times, not the current people's teacher). There's also an old legend here.
Liu Jiapo lives in Yizhai, a family with the surname Liu. My hometown is also here. Since childhood, I've often heard the elderly in the village talk about Teacher Po's history. Teacher's slope is located diagonally opposite Liujiapo. This is a cliff with dozens of feet high. There is a path leading to the top of the cliff. The top of the cliff is a one-person high cave. There is a small river in front of the cave. This river directly connects to the source of the mountain in front of the mountain and the water river outside the mountain. It is said that at that time, this cave was called Jiejia Cave (:). The evil family lived here. The cave was dark and had no idea where it led; no one dared to enter this cave. At that time, the people in the stockade were quite prosperous, living peacefully and happily here, but after three or five differences, the people in the stockade discovered that their homes weren't that rare; they just fell like that! People also feel very strange, but they don't know where to start.
One day, a woman in the village rushed two ducks to work in a field by the creek and let the two ducks forage for food in the upper reaches of the creek, but the two ducks looked straight down the river and went straight down into this evil house. At that time, the standard of living was not very good. If the family had ducks, they could lay eggs to subsidize household expenses. Seeing that ducks didn't come out of the cave, the women were in a hurry, and they went straight into the cave to find ducks. With the dark lights out, she went all the way into the cave and turned left and right in the cave, but she only heard the sound of ducks not far ahead; she could not find any trace of ducks.
After walking about two kilometers in the cave, she suddenly discovered that there seemed to be a bright light in front. The woman turned around a bay, and the front was bright and golden. The cave had stone beds, stone chairs, and stone pots and bowls. In another corner of the cave, the woman also discovered some items lost in the village: a fire stick (a tool used by the Dong people to blow fire with bamboo), ash sieve (ash sieve: a type of food used by the Dong people to sift firewood ash), rice pouches, etc. The couple were eating for the Lunar New Year (), I saw women come in and invited them to eat together. The women asked them what they had lost in the village Why were they here? The couple said they had borrowed rice from the village to make New Year's Eve, and that they would go back after the New Year, and also wanted to thank the villagers with their babs.
The women just sat down to eat snacks with them. After they had eaten, they took two of them and gave them back to the child. The woman thanked them and asked if they had seen her duck come in. Both husband and wife shook their heads and didn't acknowledge it. The woman said, “I clearly saw ducks coming in the hole. Please go well, I need to rely on these ducks to lay eggs for my doll!” At this point, I heard a few more ducks barking not far away. The woman said, “You still said ducks aren't here. Why are ducks barking here now?” When the couple heard the woman say this, they were also furious: “You don't want to let the ducks go in. We didn't let them in; they ran in to my cave house themselves, didn't talk about it. We kindly invited you to eat your meal. You're still here to blame us. You're not welcome here; you're not welcome here, you go out!” As a result, the couple cast a spell to attract a giant python to push the woman out of the cave step by step. As soon as the woman got out of the hole, the poop the couple gave her turned into a pile of cow poop.
The women were so angry that they went back to the village. Back in the village, the women said a lot of words that seemed like Buddha, and eventually grew to not know their in-laws, husbands, and sons! An experienced old man in the village said, “I'm afraid her soul has fallen into the wrong hands. If you want to save her, you'll have to ask a Taoist with deep knowledge nearby to take her soul!” As a result, the family paid for a highly skilled Taoist nearby. This Taoist's Daoist was excellent. He was able to see Feng Shui treasure houses, explore caves and find graves, and invite dragons to ask for rain.
He started fighting in the stockade. The Taoist first sent a “jumping” into the cave to catch demons. The Taoist had no choice but to go into battle in person. He brought a group of people shooting guns and drums from the stockade to Dongkou. He only saw him wearing sandals, a cloth wrapped around his feet, and a whip in his right hand, with a buffalo horn hanging on his chest. The sound of gongs and horns sounded in the cave. It was a somersault. At the end, the Taoist said, “I'm going to go into the cave to get my soul, I'll take off the sandals on my feet, and later you'll see these sandals fight, you guys Don't just watch two sandals fight outside of the hole and forget to beat the gongs and drums.”
The Taoist went into the cave to take his soul. People outside actually saw the two straw sandals fighting off. At first, they hadn't forgotten the instructions of the Taoist, knocked on the gong and drums, but later the two sandals became more intense, and they just laughed and forgot to knock on the gong and drums. At this point, they only heard a deafening sound on the cliff. A big slate fell off the cliff. It just happened that the entrance of the cave was blocked. Seeing that the Taoist University slate was sealed in the cave, there was no way for everyone. At this time, the Taoist held the large stone slab with a horn inside, because the horn was a magic weapon; the horn was so big that the mouth of the bowl on the large stone slab Here comes the cave. The Taoist relied on his wife to survive by delivering food to him every day. Originally, the Taoist was able to come out. He wanted his wife to help, but he was afraid that his wife would not be firm, so he wanted to test her will. Seeing that his wife insisted on delivering him meals every day for half a year, the Taoist knew that his wife was very determined. One day, he saw that his wife was very lucky to deliver food to him every day, so he took a pair of foot wraps and handed it to his wife, “You take these foot wraps and put them in the pot and cook the fire After wrapping my feet and cooking for 7, 7, 49 days, I was able to get out of this hole! ”
My wife listened very carefully. When she got home, she picked water to cook these foot wraps every day. When I cooked them until the afternoon of the 48th day, my wife thought: Just a pair of foot wraps, every day they want me to pick water to cook. Can it be that amazing? With it, I was able to save my husband, what the heck is this! The wife was very curious, so she uncovered the pot lid to see exactly. When she uncovered the pot, there were foot wraps. A pair of dragons lay in the pot and had already given birth to scaled armor, dragon claws, and dragon crowns, but she hadn't opened her eyes yet. As soon as she uncovered the pot, the two dragons that were about to become climate change back to foot wraps. Once you use the same spell, you can't use it a second time. Instead, she killed her husband.
Later, people named this mountain Teacher Po to commemorate this highly skilled Taoist. The cave where he hunted demons was called Teacher Cave. Now, under that cliff, there is still a hole in the stone wall where the teacher used a horn bucket back then. Whenever there is a major drought, people will go to the entrance of that cave to ask for rain; that is what you ask for.
