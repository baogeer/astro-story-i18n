 Legend has it that a long time ago, there was a small Zhuangzi on the east side of the Xiapi River. Shi and her daughter-in-law, Orchid, lived in three huts in Zhuangtou. Orchid is 18 years old. When she first got married, her father-in-law unfortunately died when she went up the mountain to cut firewood, then her husband died of illness. There were only two widows left in the family. Shi couldn't stand the blow and fell ill in bed. Just in time for the spring, dry and autumn floods this year, there was no harvest of pellets, and they sold all the food they only had at home, and they couldn't gather enough money to see a doctor for Shi. Orchids have to go out and beg every day; if they want to dry their mouths, they leave it to their mother-in-law; they only drink some lean soup to fill their hunger.
In the year of the disaster, food was difficult. On this day, from early morning until noon, orchids only needed half a bran cake and half a bowl of vegetable soup. She wanted to rush home and wait for her mother-in-law to take medicine and eat, but she was so hungry that she couldn't move, so she wanted to drink vegetable soup first. I just opened my mouth and overheard a faint groan. I saw an old woman with yellow skin leaning under a tree on the side of the road. Grandma shivered and said, “Daughter, I've been hungry for three days, can you...”
Orchid has a kind heart. She hurriedly came to her grandmother and brought vegetable soup to her mouth. Grandma took the bowl, drank up the vegetable soup in a few sips, and licked the bowl and said, “If I could eat some more dry ones, I might be able to walk energetically...” Touching the half of the bran cake in the orchid's arms, hesitated for a moment, and thought for a while before ordering something for my mother-in-law to eat. Right now, save people with this cake.
Orchid took the cake out of her arms and handed it to her grandmother. Grandma reached over and took it. After three bites, she swallowed her stomach and almost choked. Grandma took a break and said, “Daughter, thanks for saving my life today, but after eating this meal, I don't know what to do next. Alas, sooner or later you won't starve to death! Thank you, daughter, let's go now...” Before I finished talking, Grandma's face was filled with tears.
When Orchid heard her grandmother say this, she was so saddened that she burst into tears. After thinking about it for a moment, she came up with an idea and said to her grandmother, “Ma'am, my orchid has never had a mother. Now I live with my mother-in-law at home. Why don't I accept you as my wife. You go back with me and be a partner with my mother-in-law. Anyway, one of my wives is also a servant, and the two women are also servants. After that, two women with orchids will suffer!” Grandma took Orchid's hand and said, “Kid, aren't you afraid of many burdens in this year of disaster?” “If I want to eat later, I'll just run faster. Sister Gan, sit and wait for me, then I'll go to Dongzhuang to get something to eat for my mother-in-law.”
After a while, Orchid came back from asking for food and went home with Gan Niang Niang. Shi was very happy to see that Orchid brought her back a talking partner. But with more people, life became more difficult. Every day when it's not bright, Orchid leaves the village carrying a basket asking for food. Walking from Dongzhuang to Kushixizhuang, she has to run for tens of miles a day. When she gets home, she also has to wait for her mother-in-law and wife, and also have to wash and repair the seams.
The days passed day by day, and the two elderly people gradually got better under the care of Orchid. But Orchids are hungry, and their health is not as good as day by day.
On this night, after waiting for Orchid to put Shi Shi to sleep, Gan Niang beckoned Orchid to the hospital and said, “Orchid, it's been 3721 days since I arrived at your house, and my merits have been completed. To tell you the truth, I was originally a fairy crane handmaiden in front of the Kannon constellation. I was the person I was looking for. You were the person I was looking for. I'm handing you the 'On Request' stone.” As soon as she stretched out her hand, she saw a bluestone slap long and three fingers wide on her palm. She went on to say, “Don't look at it as small; as long as you say 'Please save the hardship' to it, it can instantly become wider and longer, whatever you want. Remember, you're the only one who knows about this slate; if you talk about it, you'll be dead soon!” When I finished speaking, it turned into a fairy crane flying away.
Orchid came to her room with the slate in her hand, put her hands together, and prayed three times to the slate, saying, “Please help and help...” The words didn't go away, only to see a flash of gold. The small slate “slab” grew to six feet long and three feet wide. Orchid also said, “Please give me food and cloth.” After seeing a golden glow, the slate was certainly filled with food, grain, and cloth.
The next day, when Orchid delivered fragrant meals and new clothes made overnight in front of Shi's eyes, Shi was puzzled and asked, “Where did all this come from? Also, what about my old sister?” Lan Hua said, “Last night, Gan Niang's brother came and took Gan Niang home overnight. Gan Niang's younger brother is very rich. He has only been doing business outside the home. When he saw that I was good to Gan Niang, he sent him a lot of money, food, and cloth, and said he wanted to help the townspeople more.”
To appease Mr. Shi, Orchid also delivered food, money, and other items door-to-door to the townspeople, and told the townspeople, “Look for me even if you have trouble. My uncle wants me to do his best.” This story was spread out to ten, and the poor people in the hundreds of miles heard about the orchid's loose food, and they all gathered at the door of the orchid house and lined up a long line. From morning to night, orchids are too busy to eat. If people want anything, she takes anything from the house. People say that orchids are living Bodhisattvas who help people.
Seeing that the poor neighbors had food and clothes, orchids felt even sweeter than eating honey. As soon as people are happy, and there is no need to go out to ask for food, orchids slowly become beautiful and beautiful. Their hair is like a dark cloud, their eyebrows are like a moon, their waist is like a willow, and their faces are peach blossoms.
There is a local rich man named Hu Lin. I've heard about orchids delivering food to poor people for a long time, and now seeing orchids become so beautiful again, they have asked a matchmaker to go to the door to meet her family and want Nalan to be her concubine, but they have all been harshly rejected by Orchid's daughter-in-law.
When Hu Lin saw that Orchid's wife and daughter-in-law were unmoved, he came up with a bad idea. He spent money to buy a Taoist and told people everywhere that orchids had turned into fairies, and that the food and money she sent were an eyesore. She wanted to wait for people to eat white, white, and fat, so they could drink human blood and eat people's hearts and hearts.
At the beginning, the townspeople didn't listen to that Taoist nonsense at all. Slowly, they began to listen more and became suspicious. One night, Shi Shi inadvertently saw an orchid kneeling in front of a large stone slab and saying something in her mouth. After a burst of golden light, the large stone slab was filled with food, grains, etc.
“Oh my gosh! This orchid really turned into a fairy!” My mother-in-law was so frightened that she didn't dare to meet her eyes overnight when she went back to her house.
The next day, Shi personally came to Hu Lin's house with crutches and said to him, “Mr. Hu, I would like to give you Bride Lan as your concubine. Hurry up and pick her up!” Hu Lin was so happy that he immediately took someone to the Orchid House to get married.
When Orchid saw Hu Lin coming, she followed Shi. She knew that her mother-in-law had doubts about her, but she couldn't explain the truth to her. She refused to get on the bus, so Hu Lin stole it. At this point, when the townspeople heard the cries of orchids, they all gathered around, but no one stopped them. They feared that Orchids were actually fairies.
Orchid had no choice but to violently push away the person holding her and cry out, “Ma'am! Folks! You've all been fooled by Hu Lin. Let me tell you the truth. The last time I brought home my grandmother was changed by a fairy crane. Guanyin saw that the people on our side were in trouble. She gave me a stone slab and asked me to ask for important food items from the slate and distribute them to my neighbors. They only told me not to talk about this. Once a second person knew, I would die! But I'm not afraid of death; I'm just afraid that as soon as I die, no one will share food with the townspeople, and no one will honor my mother-in-law...” she said, pulling out the small slate and showing it to everyone, “This is the slate that Guanyin Bodhisattva made a fairy crane give me, me...” Without finishing the words, the orchid fell to the ground and died.
At this point, suddenly the wind blew up and the sand flew away the stones. The small slate flew out of Orchid's hands and became 67 feet long and more than three feet wide. It hit Hu Lin and the Taoist hard. Both Hu Lin and the Taoist were stabbed to death. The wind blew louder and louder. In the place where the orchid fell, the wind scraped the earth and rocks into a large grave, and the large stone slab was firmly erected in front of the orchid's tomb. At this point, the wind stopped, and snowflakes floated in the sky. Not much; the whole world was white. Regretfully and hated, Shi went to Orchid's grave and cried, “My good daughter-in-law, I hurt you! It's just July 15, and the sky is snowing a lot; you died wrongly!” Neighbors all felt sorry at this point, and they all knelt down in front of the Orchid Tomb to cry and repent. At this point, there were a few cries of cranes in the sky. Everyone looked up, but they saw that the orchids in the clouds were falling on the crane's back and flying west.
To commemorate the orchid, every year on July 15, people come from all sides to worship her in front of the orchid tomb. Those who have received orchid food will use the food as pastries and place them in front of the orchid's tomb; those who have received money will use paper to burn gold or silver ingots to the orchid; others say that if orchids are alone, they use paper to burn boys, girls, cows, sheep, chicken, etc. to her.
Later, people designated July 15 as the “Ghost Festival.” The customs of commemorating orchids have been around until now, but over time, these customs have also been changing.
