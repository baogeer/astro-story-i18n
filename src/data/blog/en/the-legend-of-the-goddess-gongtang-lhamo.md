Note: About five kilometers from the eastern outskirts of Lhasa, there is an ancient temple built in the Song Dynasty called “Gongtang Temple”. The temple enshrines a goddess of protection called Gondangram. This story about the goddess breaking up a harmonious family and taking over the wife's husband has spread widely among the Tibetan people.
Judging from some of our records, this legend has three endings. One is that after Gadan's death, his wife Gongji recovered his bones and finally revived; second, Gadan was forced to marry the goddess. Gongji came to the temple, carried water for three years, burned fires for three years, and eventually became a god; the third type was that Gongji became a bucket shelf stone. When we sorted it out, we used this ending.
