 The old monk opened the door
On Cishan Red Mountain, there is an isolated piece of iron ore in the middle of the mountainside of the back mountain. It is about ten feet tall and more than two feet wide. It has a “head” on it, and “legs” and “feet” underneath. Seen from miles away, it looks like an old monk with a reddish-brown robe standing in the middle of the mountain. This is a magical legend well-known in Cishan — “The Old Monk Opens the Door.” Had it not been for Little Japan's iron ore mine in 1942 to steal the old monk as a “good son-in-law,” I'm afraid the old monk would still stand there and open the door until now!
Legend has it that a long, long time ago, the Red Mountain in Cishan was a treasure mountain. There was a cave on the mountain called Jinshi Cave. An old monk lived in the cave and fed a golden pony. The golden horse was able to eat golden urine. However, no one knows what the old monk's name is or how old he is. They only know that he often travels all over the world to give people medicine and help the poor.
