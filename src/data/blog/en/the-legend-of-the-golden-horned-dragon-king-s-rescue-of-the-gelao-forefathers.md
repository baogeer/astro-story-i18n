 Legend has it that a long time ago, the ancestors of the Yilao people lived in mountains and forests, built nests in large trees, and hunted small animals for a living. One day, when the ancestors were looking for prey in the mountains and forests, they unexpectedly met a group of tigers. After a brief confrontation, the ancestors began to flee. They fled to the highest point of the mountain, and the group of tigers also chased it to the highest point. When they climbed the tallest tree, the tiger group stopped under the tree and roared incessantly around the big tree, as if they had made up their mind; they had to eat their ancestors. Three days and three nights have passed, and the herds of tigers are still besieging the ancestors. Seeing that the people in the trees can't hold on. At this point, a colorful cloud floated in the sky, and the Golden Horned Dragon King of the East China Sea was standing in the clouds. He was dispatched by the Heavenly Court to spread clouds and rain in this direction. As soon as he took a look, he found people and tigers confronting each other. He thought that there are already few people on Earth; they have become food for tigers. Wouldn't that be pitiful; let me save them. As a result, Dragon King poured water from the East China Sea and poured down. Gradually, the water flooded the top of the mountain and flooded the tiger group.
The ancestors were saved. They were very grateful to the Golden Horned Dragon King for saving lives. At the same time, they were also in awe of the flood, leaving behind an eight-character mantra describing the flood: “The flood flooded the sky and flooded (read) a dead eagle.” Later, the Yilao people were a lifesaver for the Dragon King, and they ran through the mountains and forests. The shoes they had to wear on their feet were called the “Eight Golden Dragons.” Eight-piece shoes are sewn from eight pieces of animal skin. The Golden Dragon refers to the Golden Horned Dragon King. The intention is to hope that the Yilao people will wear the “Eight Golden Dragon” and walk in the mountains. With the support of King Golden Horned Dragon, they will be safe and smooth. In order to thank them for their big trees, they have sacrificed trees every year since then, and worshiped the big trees in the mountains as gods, so that the big, spiritual trees can keep the Yilao people safe. (This legend still circulates in Changjiao, Jiaoba in Wuchuan County, Guizhou Province, and Judu Yilao Village in Liuzhi, Guizhou. The Yilao people in Judu Village also go through a tree festival every March.)
