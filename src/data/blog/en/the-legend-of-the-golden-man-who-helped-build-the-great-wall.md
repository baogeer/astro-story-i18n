 While building the section of the Great Wall from Juyongwai Town to Badaling, the laborers were very exhausted.
One day, I don't know where a person with yellow hair, yellow eyes, yellow teeth, yellow face, and even his clothes are yellow. He rushed to a stout scalper, carrying nine small pots and nine heads that were yellow like a cow on his back. Everyone calls him “The Golden Man.” Jinren arrived at the construction site and had to work with the laborers to repair the border. Strangely enough, his scalpers are very energetic; they use his small pot to cook with water, no matter how many people eat and drink, they can't finish eating or drinking; using his head to shovel the mountain, they feel that they have doubled their energy, are very labor-saving, and can even break the ground. Now it's fine. The laborers drank hot water, ate a full meal, and were able to do their job without much effort. I was able to take a break in the middle of work. In this way, the secret of the small pot and pot spread all over the construction site, but no one claimed it; people secretly kept this secret. Since then, the progress of edge repair has invisibly accelerated a lot. By the time the Great Wall was nearing completion, the secret was finally discovered by the supervisors. He gave the report to the general manager, who was surprised and happy when he heard it. He thought that this person's scalper, pot, and ladle must be a baby, so he ordered the arrest of the person and ordered that the scalper, pot, and head all be surrendered. After hearing it, people sent messages to Jin Ren one after another, asking him how to do it. The golden man then buried the small pot and the head behind a large stone. When he left with the scalper, the military arrived and blocked his way. There are people on three sides, and only one side is a cliff. Jinren didn't panic. He only saw that he drew a door with his finger on the cliff, and saw a golden light. Jinren broke through the door with a scalper. The golden light flashed through, there was no trace, and the cliffs were still the same. Everyone present was astonished at the time. The government and military, in particular, were even more dumbfounded and out of ideas. But they didn't die; they didn't catch people or cows, so they found that small pot and ladle again. They inquired about the burial site; some said it was behind a large rock. But where to find it? There are big stones all over the mountains.
I don't know how many more years have passed. There is an oil seller. He got up early in the morning and went to sell oil. When he walked outside Badalingnan Cave, he found a household living under a cliff on the side of the road, lighting a blinking oil lamp. One person was following the scalper to grind beans. While busy, he said, “Add some oil, add some oil.” The oil seller saw that he was extremely busy, so he added some oil. The dim oil lamp came on, and it stopped flickering. Only then did he discover that people who grind beans are dressed in yellow and skinny. He appreciated the kindness of the oil seller, so he took soybeans as a reward. The oil seller took it and left. As he walked, he felt that the inside of his pocket was getting heavier. He pulled it out and took a look: “Ah!” He was stunned; the soybeans in his pocket turned into gold beans. Smoldering, yellow-orange. He then turned around and went back the same way and wanted to take another look. But when he arrived at the bottom of the cliff in the south of the town, the sky was already faint. How could anyone else be there? I only found a deep stone gate mark on the cliff. The oil sellers became more and more surprised. They looked at the stone wall and thought about everything that had just happened.
Just then, a young man came down from the mountain. He was naked, with a broken jacket around his waist. He didn't know what he was wearing in his pants. He was bulging on his shoulder, and the oil seller took a look. I recognize this person. Isn't this a good guy who plays firewood all year round in Chadaocheng! Tell me what's going on?” Second, I started refusing to say it. After a while, he remembered that the person selling oil had given him credit for oil, decided that he was a good person, and told him.
Turns out, he went up the mountain to get firewood. When he cut down a bunch of artemisia claws, he heard a thud, so he pulled the grass away with a small fork, revealing a round metal object, very much like the edge of a pot. He went outside again and found that there were others next to him, one next to the other, showing a total of nine. He was about to go deep and discovered that there were still nine shovels around. They were only covered by deciduous weeds and a thin layer of soil, and some were still showing barbs. He thought it was very strange. He wanted to go ahead and plow. From early in the morning until noon, he ate some dried food when he was hungry and then plowed. But all of this was welded into the stone pit, and no matter how you plow, you just couldn't take it out. Tired of digging, he lay down on a flat rock and fell asleep. He had a dream where he was plowing the ground. After coming from the mountain, there was a man dressed in yellow. The man smiled and said to him, “You don't need to cut it; it's mine. My stuff can only be discovered by virtuous people, congratulations! I have a word for you to keep in mind: if you find a golden pot, don't talk nonsense. But when a virtuous person comes, you'll get a golden pot of gold.” When I finished speaking, I went on and on. A strong wind blew through, but he woke up, but he didn't see the golden pot. Two small piles of yellow rice and yellow noodles appeared on the stone he was lying on. This unexpected harvest made him extremely happy. But how could he go back? He then took off his pants.
