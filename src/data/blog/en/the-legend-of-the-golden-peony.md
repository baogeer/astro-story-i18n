 On the shores of the Erhai Sea in Dali, Yunmian Province, there is a beautiful mountain called Dianchang Mountain. In this deep mountain, a world-famous type of golden peony grows. Not only is it as colored as gold, but it also looks like an ingot, which is very appealing. According to local Bai brothers, it was changed from gold. Here's a moving story.
At the end of the Yuan dynasty, there were often bandits in the mountains. An old local Bai man made a living by selling firewood. He was kidnapped by bandits one day to cut firewood, and let out the wind. His family was limited to three days, bringing 100 and two pieces of gold into the mountains to redeem it. Once the period was over, his life was not guaranteed.
The old man's family has only one daughter, Ah Qing, who is dependent on her. She was very sad after hearing the news. It's so cold at home, where can I find gold or silver? Extremely smart, she changed her mind and got an idea. On the third day, she went up the mountain with only a bag of gold-dyed stones and a sharp sword. When the bandits saw a young, beautiful, and weak woman, they all looked right. How prepared were they? She threw the golden stone on the ground, and the bandits flocked to grab it. She killed the bandit leader with a sword, and the rest scattered like birds and animals. Ah Qing rescued her father; the two went down the mountain untouched. Later, just where she threw “gold,” golden peonies grew.
(Quoted from “Peony Ceremony” by Liu Xiang and Xu Xiaofan)
