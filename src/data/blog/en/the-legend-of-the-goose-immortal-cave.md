The strange peak stands out for thousands of stones, and is very beautiful. It is the top of the eight scenic spots in Nanjing County. There is a Taoist temple on the mountainside. The plaque says “Jiu Li Fei Zhen”, indicating that the fairy tour Jiu Li Xian branch town is here. Legend has it that dreams have come true.
