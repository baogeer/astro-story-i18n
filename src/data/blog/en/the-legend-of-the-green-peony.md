 The Caozhou Peony Garden is the largest in the world with its large area and variety. In addition to black, white, red, yellow, pink, orchid, and purple, there is also a rare type of peony in the garden, which is the green peony. The name of this green peony is bean green. This flower is blue-green when it first blooms and gradually changes color. The color is like a green pea. It is delicate, sweet, and refreshing. Legend has it that the green peony was transformed into a jade hairpin on the head of a hundred flower fairies.
There is a local custom. Every day of the valley rain, nearby flower farmers have to hold a flower competition at Baiyuan Garden. Everyone moved the best peonies in their garden into pots, brought them to the flower fair, and placed them on the stone table. Whose peony flower is rated as the best flower. Then a gold plaque with the word “flower crown” on it is hung on the garden door. Flower farmers are cultivating new Shedan treasures every year to win the “Flower Master” gold plaque.

The news of the green peony blooming shocked Caozhou City. People who came to see the peonies crowded out the garden. Wang Er remembered the jasper hairpin and named the green peony Green Jade. He said, “Don't talk about your peony garden; if you give me 1,000 or 2 gold, I won't exchange it!” Until now, there is still a saying “green jade is worth a thousand dollars” in Caozhou.
