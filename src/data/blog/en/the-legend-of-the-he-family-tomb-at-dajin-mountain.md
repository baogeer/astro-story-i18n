 There is a mountain in the north of Sixiang in Mingguang City called Dajinshan (now in Wuhe County). There is a graveyard on the east side of Dajinshan Mountain. There are two large tombs. One is the tomb of Grandpa Ho, and the other is the tomb of the old lady of Ho's family. Grandma Ho was one of Emperor Guangxu's many teachers. Why did the imperial master send his coffin from Beijing thousands of miles away for burial after his death? There are lots of weird stories here.
It is said that after the Sino-Japanese War, a group of students who wrote books changed the ideology of defending the law and quickly formed a popular political movement to reform the law and restore the new. In 1888, Kang Youwei went to Beijing to participate in the Shuntian Township Test. At that time, when the Sino-French War had just ended, he was keenly aware of the deep national crisis and was full of patriotic enthusiasm. He wrote a 5,000 word book to the emperor, putting forward the three suggestions of “becoming law, sharing the situation, and being careful.” In May 1895, Kang Youwei also invited people from 18 provinces to a rally at Songchang Temple to promote the Shang Emperor Wan Yan Shu drafted by him. More than 1,300 people signed the Ten Thousand Words Book. Ten thousand words were handed over to the Metropolitan Public Prosecutor's Office. This is the famous “book on the bus”. On May 6 of the same year, Kang Youwei wrote to Emperor Guangxu twice in a row. Having read the book for the third time, Emperor Guangxu felt that Kang Youwei's transformative proposition was very useful in rescuing the Qing dynasty's ruling crisis. He attached great importance to it, and ordered the copying of Empress Dowager Cixi, the military aircraft department, and provincial superintendents. Since then, the Restoration faction began to receive support from the emperor; at the same time, they were boycotted and attacked by the conservatives in Qing Ting. The struggle between the Restoration faction and the old conservatives was intense. In the end, the old conservatives, led by Empress Dowager Cixi, imprisoned Guangxu in Yingtai, Zhongnanhai, to re-“ discipline the government” and ordered a search for the Restoration faction. The Wushu Restoration Campaign has failed. A large number of people involved in the Restoration Movement were arrested, killed, imprisoned, and exonerated. Among these people is Mr. He Lao. He has studied abroad in the UK and has some advanced ideas about reforming the monarchy system. At the beginning of the 100 Day Restoration, Emperor Guangxu recruited Mr. He into the palace and worshiped Ho as his mentor. The restoration failed in just 100 days. Mr. He Lao was implicated and imprisoned. At the time, he was imprisoned at the Wuhu government prison.
While Mr. Ho Lao was imprisoned, since Emperor Guangxu was only under house arrest, the head and prison guards of the Wuhu government prison didn't dare to offend Emperor Guangxu, because maybe one day they gained momentum again? This company was also a well-known wealthy merchant in Beijing at the time. It had several large factories, such as the Western Fire Factory (Match Factory) and the Pancreas Factory (Soap Factory), and several large foreign goods stores, and made huge sums of money every day. Mr. Ho was imprisoned in Wuhu, and his family's children had to spend a lot of money. They all received the benefits of his family while in prison, and they also paid special attention to Mr. Ho. While drinking alcohol with Mr. Ho for a day, he came up with an idea to let the family spend money to find someone who looked similar to the old gentleman and go to jail for him. Mr. Ho's son called it Ho Bian-dou, and he went to great lengths to search for it. On this day, He Biantou visited prison in Wuhu and returned to Beijing. He met an alcoholic in Linhuai Guan, Fengyang Province, and got into an argument with Mr. Diner because he had no money to pay his bills after drinking at a restaurant. He Bian's head looked a bit like his father when he saw that he was tall, fat, and thin. He then paid the money he owed for alcohol and took him to the side to inquire in detail. Originally, his name was Chen Ziheng, a native of Sixiang (present-day Mingguang Si Alley) in Wuhe County. He has been wandering for many days because he lost money by starting a small business and was afraid of being scolded by his family when he got home. He Biantou explained his thoughts in detail with Chen Ziheng; who knows, Chen Ziheng fully agreed. As a result, Chen Ziheng became Mr. Ho and imprisoned in Wuhu prison.
Three years passed in a blink of an eye. Apart from being unable to move freely in prison, Chen Ziheng had three meals and two meals a day with alcohol. Of course, his family paid for these. Since Mr. Ho was a sideline character in the Restoration Movement, he was released three years later. Chen Ziheng came out of jail and went back to his hometown in Si Xiang. As soon as he saw that the house and courtyard were brand new, he was able to figure it out. The family rejoiced when they saw Chen Ziheng's return. His wife blamed him, “You still know the devil comes back. For the past three years, you've sent someone to send money every six months. It's so strange that they won't say where to get rich. Based on your virtue, I think you must have raised a young child outside the home. Why don't you bring them back for me to look bigger now; she doesn't have two boobs longer than mine.” Chen Ziheng was bombarded with a barrage of bombardment, and he had to tell the truth as many times as he said. When she heard it, she even had a runny nose and tears, and she couldn't finish counting.
Mr. Ho from Beijing learned that Chen Ziheng came home after being released from prison. He thanked the special representative for sending 500 banknotes to make up for the suffering of three years in prison. Chen Ziheng was not at ease in the first place; when he had the money, he made a surprise; he was soon lured into a smoking house. In less than two years, there was not much left of the two pieces of silver that Mr. Ho gave away. One day Mr. Ho Biantou, son of Mr. Ho Biantou, went south to do a big business, passing through Fengyang County and made a special trip to visit Chen Ziheng. He saw that Chen Ziheng was addicted to drugs and even shook his head and sighed. He should not only give away two pieces of silver; he should be taught how to operate and make a fortune.
He Biantou thought again and again after returning to Beijing, and decided to set up a firework factory in the Fengyang area and give it to the Chen family. With such daily accounts, the Chen family would be spared from suffering. Second, the Chen family has done their best to take responsibility for the Chen family.
He Biantou chose Linhuaiguan, which has convenient land and water transportation and is a bit close to Sixiang, and invested in setting up a firework factory. At the beginning, a special person was sent to take the Chen family from purchasing goods to production, management, and sales. He only withdrew the people after they left. When they left, they handed over a letter to Chen Ziheng. Chen first saw that it was handwritten by Mr. Ho Lao. In addition to expressing gratitude, the entire content was to have Chen Ziheng follow the right path and manage the fireworks factory well, if they defeat that factory again. Chen Ziheng was moved to tears by the thousands of characters in the book, and every sentence was heartfelt. Since then, I have given up smoking and have dedicated myself to running a factory. A foreign fire factory was managed in a decent manner and gradually expanded. Since most of the buyers were in Bengbu, the land and water terminal, the Yangguang Fire Factory was later relocated to Bengbu.
Soon Chen Ziheng became a famous wealthy businessman on the banks of the Huai River. In order to thank his family, Chen Ziheng has to choose some souvenirs and send some gold and silver to Beijing every year during the Spring Festival. Every time, in addition to receiving some souvenirs, all the gold and silver they sent was returned, and they said, “Sending money and silver to any house is like pouring water into the river.” Chen Ziheng was sad. In the end, he came up with a crooked idea. “Zi Heng has been studying Feng Shui from a young age. Currently, Peking University Jinshan in Si Xiang has a Feng Shui treasure. He faces northwest and southeast. If it were a coffin, it would be a headrest for Dajinshan with his left hand, lift a mountain with his right hand, and step on the Chihe River.” Chen Ziheng originally wanted to give away his loyalty. It was expected that Mr. Ho Lao would not come from thousands of miles away to this remote mountain. I don't know if Mr. Ho is already old and has lived in Beijing for a long time. He has seen the changing situation. He is unfathomable. He originally wanted to go to a quiet place after 100 years and not let his bones get stained again. Therefore, after receiving the letter, he was very interested and sent someone to visit the place on a special trip.
Chen Ziheng panicked. His family doesn't have any cemeteries at all in Dajinshan, so Taishi's treasure was made up by him. But the visitors have arrived home, so if you don't want to watch it, you have to watch it. Chen Ziheng hired two cars to accompany the visitors to Dajinshan. Who knows that the visitors did have a style of Feng Shui. As soon as they went up to Dajinshan, they were interested in a place, exactly the same as what Chen Ziheng said in his letter. Visitors praised Chen Ziheng for being proficient in yin and yang techniques, and Chen also took the opportunity to make hypocritical jokes. The visitors were happy to return to Beijing to restore their lives.
After the visitors left, Chen Ziheng inquired that the gang was originally from his uncle's home far away. Chen Ziheng personally found the door and proposed to use 5 acres of land from Gangxia's house in exchange for the gravel in his gang where rabbits don't poop. At first, Chen Ziheng's distant uncle thought he, his rich nephew, was blaming him, but he thought Chen Ziheng was serious. It wasn't like a joke. Naturally, his heart was very happy. He just misrepresented him. Chen Ziheng gave him a piece of Feng Shui treasure in the shape of a “Master Chair.”
A few years later, Mr. Ho died, and his coffin was transported from Beijing to Dajinshan in the north of Sixiangbei for burial, according to his last words. A few years later, in 1911, Mrs. Ho passed away, and her coffin was also sent here for burial. This tomb was excavated in 1973.
