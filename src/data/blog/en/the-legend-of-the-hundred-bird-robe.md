 The story is about a young man named Zhang Yayuan. He was born into a poor family. His father died from hard work for toast before he was born. He was dependent on his mother since childhood, and mainly relied on hard work for toast and fetching firewood in exchange for rice to make ends meet. Zhang Yayuan has been going up the mountain to hunt firewood since she was a child, and has developed good skills and a strong body for firewood hunting and hunting. One day, on his way home from picking firewood, a big rooster jumped on the flat frame where he was picking firewood, and he brought it home. After raising this chicken for half a year, it actually became a beautiful girl like a fairy, and they became a couple with him. Zhang Yayuan is brave and capable. His wife is hardworking and kind. The two lived a beautiful life after getting married.
However, local toasts have coveted the beauty of Ahara's wife for a long time, and they wanted to do everything in their power to rob the girl as a concubine. As a result, Toast was in trouble everywhere, but they were all overcome by Yasuhara's wife with wisdom. Out of control, Toast forcibly robbed Ya Yuan's wife. When she left, her wife told Ya Yuan, “Go shoot 100 birds in the mountains and make a feather coat.” After 100 days, wear a 'hundred bird clothes' to find me.” The wife has been taken, the mother is angry, and the family is shattered. Ahara went deep into the mountains with hate and sorrow, but also with hope to shoot birds. The girl was robbed into the government office. There was no laughter; she was sad day by day, and the world was exhausted day by day. In order to get the girl's laugh, Toast was willing to pay 300 silver dollars, but nothing that sold the plaster or told jokes made the girl laugh. In order to save her wife, Ya Yuan went through a great deal of hard work, walked through 99 rivers, climbed 99 mountains, and shot down 100 birds.
The 100th day arrived. As her wife said, Ya Yuan finished wearing a “hundred bird clothes” and arrived at the government gate. The girl, who usually has a sad face, smiles when she sees Ahara dance “hundred bird clothes.” Toast was greedy, and in order to please her, she took off her official robe and changed it with Ahara. Yahara killed Toast when he helped him wear a “hundred bird coat.” However, they took the opportunity to escape from the government office. They both went far, far away, and lived a beautiful life together.
The story of Bird Coat profoundly reflects the strong will of the Zhuang people to resist rape and fight for freedom. In 20xx, the folk tale “Hundred Bird Clothes” was included in the first batch of intangible cultural heritage lists in Guangxi. In 1955, the famous Zhuang writer Wei Qilin (from Wencun, Xiaoqi Town, Hengxian County) wrote the long narrative poem “Hundred Bird Clothes” based on the local “Hundred Bird Clothes” story. The release of this excellent, long story poem brought the inheritance of the “Hundred Bird Clothes” story into an unprecedented period of prosperity.
