 Next to the Shimen Guan Yu Huang Ge, there is a famous fairy cave near and far. It is said that this fairy cave is one of the world's most famous 81 fairy cave houses. How can it become one of the 81 immortal cave houses that people in the world yearn for infinitely? It turns out that there was an ancient legendary story of “Master and apprentice working together in the mountains; the master didn't succeed, the apprentice succeeded”.
Listen to the old life. A long time ago, an old Taoist traveled all over the world with one of his apprentices. I don't know how many famous mountains and rivers the mentors and apprentices traveled from south to north, east to west, and chose a sacred place in Lingshan to practice peacefully and quietly, but they never fulfilled their wish. When they arrived at Shimenguan on the western slope of Cangshan, they were suddenly fascinated by the magnificent natural scenery of Shimenguan, which combines heroism, danger, wonder, beauty, and seclusion. They were pleasantly surprised to discover that not only was Zhong Ling Yu's show, but the place was full of blue and purple. As a result, the mentors and apprentices decided to practice this mountain. Since there were no temples such as Fukukoku-ji Temple, Jinan-ji Temple, or Jade Palace, the two mentors and apprentices were only able to live in a rock cave in the mountain—that is, in the Sennin Cave next to the Shimengguan Yuhuanggaku today.
In those years that had just begun, the mentors and apprentices all worked together seriously and earnestly to practice ascetic practices. During the day, they meditated and practiced on a sitting table hanging out of the sky in front of Xianren Cave, and at night they shared their experiences of practicing in the cave. However, although the scenery in the mountains is nice, the days away from the fireworks are still too bitter and lonely. Gradually, the “unfinished” master couldn't hold on. He took advantage of this to become friends, and often ran to the bottom of the mountain at the bottom of the mountain for three days. However, the apprentice remained single-minded and persevered in the mountains to practice without hesitation. After some time, the master gradually felt uncomfortable in his heart. He thought he was a master, and he was an apprentice, but he always only cares about his own practice, and also asks me to feed him when he is a master. Then one day, the master opened up to his apprentice and said, “Apprentice, the master is going to travel abroad for a few years, visiting his mentors and friends while asking and learning from his experiences. I don't know when I'll be back. There are three more bowls of rice here, so I'll leave them for you to eat.” The apprentice took a solemn position towards the master, and the master happily went underground to the mountain.
After the master left, the apprentice was left alone and left alone in the mountains to continue his training. Red flowers bloom over and over again in Eisan Mountain in the mountains, and the white snow at the top of the mountain piles up over and over again. Cold weather comes and summer, and the years are leisurely. So many years passed unwittingly. One day, the master, who had been traveling east and west for a few years and still visited many famous temples, went back to Shimenguanqian. He looked up at the misty Shimen Pass and suddenly remembered that he also had an apprentice practising in the mountains. He quickly climbed up the mountain, only to see that his apprentice was still quietly meditating and practicing on the sitting table hanging out in front of the cave, just like when he left. When I saw the master who had been away for many years and then suddenly came back, the apprentice just nodded slightly, which was considered a greeting. The master saw that his apprentice was still so dedicated and unrelenting in his practice. In addition to being moved, he couldn't help but feel a bit compassionate.
When the master bent over and walked into the cave, he could hardly believe his eyes: only to see that the three bowls of rice he left behind when he walked many years ago were still in the stone pot. The rice was covered with a thick layer of dust. Confusingly, he drilled a hole and asked his apprentice, “Apprentice, what have you been eating in recent years?” “Eat white taro!” The apprentice answered. “Where did you get the white taro?” The master hurriedly followed him. “It was the apprentice who jumped from here to the Shimenguan River to pick it up.” The apprentice simply answered softly. “Huh?” The master probes looked at the manjang cliffs that went down one step at a time. He was simply amazed and dumbfounded. Seeing the master's astonished appearance, the apprentice pointed to the hole and said, “Well, those aren't white taro that the apprentice just jumped down to pick up this morning. Also, the apprentice in the earthenware pot is still cooking an earthenware pot, so if the master is hungry, please go eat some yourself!” The master looked at the cave entrance, but only saw that there were some white pebbles the size of eggs and goose that had been washed clean by the river. The master took another look at the earthenware pot on the fire pond. What he saw was still white pebbles cooked in an earthenware pot. He couldn't help but shout, “Apprentice, is this the white taro you ate?” “Yes!” “But these are all stones!” Seeing that the master didn't believe it, the apprentice jumped off the bench and went to the fire pond to grab the white stone in the earthenware pot and said, “This is really a big white taro. If you don't believe me, my apprentice will eat it for you!” Strangely speaking, these white stones turned into Dongdong's white taro as soon as they reached the apprentice's hands, and when the master reached out to pick them up, they were still hard pebbles.
Seeing the situation, the master looked at the apprentice in astonishment and said, “Apprentice, haven't you already become an immortal?” Unbeknownst to the Master's words, the apprentice knelt down and nodded to the Master and loudly answered, “Thank you, Master!” The apprentice's body then took off, and Ran Ran flew into the sky. Turns out, after years of painstaking work, the apprentice had already completed his merits; he was only left with the final hurdle of his master's gift! I watched as my apprentices had all gone immortal. However, the one who became a master himself is still in the flesh, and he doesn't have the same fame. The master was simply jealous and extremely annoyed. He went crazy and suddenly removed all the white stones in the caves and clay pots from the Shimenguan River Valley. I lay on my back in a cold, dark cave with endless remorse. I didn't eat or drink, and within a few days I was invigorated to death.
However, since there were people who became immortals here, the fame of Shimen Pass and Xianren Cave is slowly spreading. Many monks, nuns, and Taoists came to practice Gudao, a treasure of sacred mountains. For a while, there were many Doganji hermitages on the top and bottom of the mountain in Shimenguan. According to the late local old man Zhu Duo, there were actually more than 100 temples here during the peak period.
