 There is a small lake at the top of Yuanzai Tangling in Fuxinggang Oriental, Beitou District, Taipei City. Legend has it that an immortal lived in ancient times. This fairy wanted to recruit an honest person as his apprentice, so he disguised himself as an old man selling rice dumplings and stood at the top of the mountain to sell them. They say it costs two dollars to eat one bowl, and it only costs one dollar to eat two bowls.
Most people on the road scrambled to eat two bowls of rice dumplings for a penny. Later, a young man saw this situation and felt that it was unfair, so he paid two yuan and ate a bowl. The old man who sold rice dumplings, then showed his original form as a fairy, told the young man his true intentions, praised him for being honest, and accepted him as an apprentice.
According to legend, this fairy rice dumpling was made from the dirt on the mountain. As a result, an earthen pit appeared on the mountain, and water accumulated through rain, resulting in the small lake described above. This legend was modeled after the legend of the Yellow Crane Tower in Hubei, slightly modified, and embellished.
