 During the Qianlong period, there were more than 100 large and small Huimo workshops in Huizhou City. Later, there were only two old brands left. One was an ancient family. The owner was Guyu Ting, and the other was the Yue Family. The owner was Yuehuatang.
Guyutei has an ancestral secret recipe for making ink, and the “kaibun pine tobacco” they burn is slightly superior to the ink at Tsukihado. It didn't take long for the blue smoke on the Yuejia ink kiln to gradually die out. Feeling faceless, Yuehuatang quietly left Huizhou with her family on a dark, windy night.
On the morning of the first day of the first month of that year, Guyu Ting opened the courtyard door and “popped”. A young man in a shabby coat fell in from outside the door.
Gu Yuting was shocked. Seeing that the teenager was still breathing, he quickly called someone to treat him. Turns out, the teenager was an orphan. His parents died a few years ago, and he has been wandering around over the years. Last night, he saw a big black door. I think it's a big family, so it's here...
Later, this orphan became a friend of Gujia Ink Kiln, and Guyu Ting gave him the name Dendrobium.
Dendrobium is quick and has a flexible mind. He burns Huimo, cuts trees up the mountain, burns the kiln out of the kiln, and stirs ink to make blanks. He has a good sense of craftsmanship. When he was idle, he went out to play outdoors with his boss's daughter, Hua Jiao. Under the green mountain, by the river, their laughter was everywhere. Qingdou's first blooming peppercorns soon fell in love with dendrobium. Gu Yuting and his wife also felt that these two children were a natural couple.
When spring blossoms bloom, the ancient family married peppercorns and dendrobium. Gu Yuting called dendrobium to his front and handed him a piece of rice paper full of words, saying, “Kid, I don't have a son. From now on, we expect our ancient family to pass on the magic of smoke and ink from now on.”
Dendrobium held the yellow rice paper with both hands and knelt in front of Guyu Ting: “Don't worry, my father-in-law says that my son-in-law has half of my son. I have no father or mother. My old family gave me this life, and even your name was taken by you. From now on, I will call it Gu Dendrobium.”
Gu Yuting's words about dendrobium made Gu Yuting feel enthusiastic, and the eyes of the old lady on the other hand were also wet. However, the next morning, peppercorns were still immersed in a beautiful dream, but dendrobium kept saying goodbye with that secret recipe.
What happened next was something Guyuting never expected: Yuehuatang, which had disappeared in Huizhou for many years, returned to his old business. Dendrobium turned out to be Yuehuatang's son. His real name was Moon Youguang. Only then did Gu Yuting learn that he had entered a trap carefully designed by Gekado's father and son.
Using his son's bitter strategy, Gekkado obtained the secret recipe from Guyuting, and returned to the river and lake, and the burnt Mojingdang in the imperial place used the “Kaiwen Pine Tobacco” brand handed down by the ancient family. How can Gu Yu Ting swallow this bad breath? He wrote a lawsuit and went to Huizhou Province to file a lawsuit. He didn't expect that Huizhou Futai had already been bought by Yuehuatang. Not only did they not win the lawsuit, but instead he was sentenced to 32 thousand silver, imprisoned for three years, and not allowed to burn Huimo for 100 years.
Gu Yuting is imprisoned. His friend Mei Huashi took a sum of money to clear the government office and keep him out of jail. At this point, his wife had died of illness, and his daughter, Huajiao, made a living by embroidering. Father and daughter met, hugged their heads and cried. Thinking that his ancestral stunts had reached his generation, he was so sad and angry that he went blind.
Although you can't burn ink, the good news is that Guyuting also has one skill: appreciating paintings. He can accurately determine the age and authenticity of a painting from the smell of ink. Famous paintings traded on the market can never be faked as long as he verifies that they are real. With this skill, the days have gradually improved.
In the spring of this year, Qianlong toured the South and arrived in Suzhou. Yueyouguang, the governor of Suzhou, knows that Qianlong likes calligraphy and painting. He has long prepared Tang Bohu's “Plum Blossom Snow Map” to be presented to the emperor.
How did Moonlight become the capital of Suzhou? Back then, after the ancient family went through the Huimo lawsuit, the ancient family suffered a fiasco, and the Moon family quickly disappeared. Yuekhuadang used a large sum of money to buy a Qipin County Order for her son Yue Youguang: He became a prefecturen in Aoyama, then cleared his joints and became the governor of Suzhou.
Qianlong held the “Mei Hua Ao Snow Map” presented by Qianlong, and couldn't help but be amazed: “Good painting, good painting!” He said to the minister close to him, “Pass on my message, ask Mr. Mei to come and enjoy the paintings with me.”
The Mr. Mei mentioned by Qianlong is a plum blossom stone. Every time Mei Huashi took the academic examination, she refused to be an official. Her extraordinary characters and splendid articles were praised by Qianlong, so she was summoned to the palace to accompany Qianlong to write poetry, enjoy painting, and play chess. Mei Huashi heard that Moon Youguang dedicated a painting by Tang Bohu to Qianlong. Her sharp gaze never left “Mei Huaao Snow Map”. He said, “The Emperor, IMHO, this painting is a fake.”
“What? Are you saying this picture is fake?” Qianlong was taken aback and called Lai Yue Guang to inquire exactly. Moon Youguang made false accusations.
When it comes to this painting, the two are telling the truth one by one; Qianlong didn't know how to judge for a moment.
Mei Huashi said, “Tell the emperor, there is a strange person in my hometown of Huizhou. He can read Mo and appreciate painting. He never made a mistake. Why didn't the emperor tell him to appreciate this painting and then decide?”
Qianlong asked, “The Moon has light. You and Mr. Mei are both from Huizhou. You must have heard of Huizhou Mo Shen too?”
Moon Youguang was full of excitement. Let Gu Yuting verify the authenticity of this painting, and is there any good fruit from him to eat? Gu Yuting knows that this painting was given to the emperor by him; even if it were real, he would say it was a fake. He said, “Emperor, Guyuting is a bit of a bug carving trick, but it's not as mysterious as people say.”
“Whether it's a master or a small bug carving skill, why don't you just let me see it?” Qianlong smiled. Legend has it that Gu Yuting came to Suzhou to see the driver.
After receiving the order, Guyu Ting didn't dare to slow down. Accompanied by Huajiao, he arrived in Suzhou day and night, and was taken to a luxurious painting house in Qianlong.
Qianlong wanted to see if Huizhou Mo Shen had real skills. He prepared many paintings, and also brought in many ministers of culture and martial arts. Plum Blossom Stone and Moonlight were also among them.
Qianlong asked, “Guyu Ting, I heard you can smell the truth or falsehood of a painting with your nose, right?” As soon as Qianlong's words came to an end, Mei Huashi came to Guyuting with an ancient painting: “Mr. Gu, you have to listen carefully this time!”
As soon as Gu Yuting heard it, it was the sound of a plum blossom stone, and his eyebrows furrowed. Mei Huashi was grateful to him back then, but I didn't expect to meet him here now. Too late to think about it, he took a close look at the painting and said to Qianlong, “This is a fake “Twilight Spring Map” imitating Zhang Zeduan of the Song dynasty. As can be seen from the ink, this small roll of less than two feet was completed in three steps. If I hadn't guessed wrong, this fake should have come from the Emperor's royal pen.”
Everyone present was taken aback. Qianlong laughed out loud: “Although Mr. Gu is blind, he can see the fall clearly. Not even the Beijing painter Liu Sankai could see this fake. How did Mr. Gu smell it?”
Gu Yuting said, “Actually, it's very simple. Zhang Zeduan used ink from the Song dynasty, and the emperor used today's ink. You'll know it as soon as you smell it. Everyone's ink is different in thickness, thickness, and thickness. Naturally, I know that the emperor used ink arbitrarily and unrestrained, such as a fresh breeze blowing in the willows, being chic and at ease.”
Plum Blossom Stone changed another painting. Gu Yuting heard it and said, “The first half of this painting is “Li Bai's Drunken Picture” by Chen Mazi, a landscape painter from southern Anhui in the Ming dynasty. Unfortunately, only half is left. The second half was added by another artist. I don't know her name, but I know she's a woman and she's pregnant.”
Qianlong was dumbfounded. This is one of his favorite paintings. The one recorded in “Palace Pictorial” is exactly what Gu Yuting said. The sequel is a palace girl from the previous dynasty.
Qianlong asked, “Let's take another look at this “Mei Huaao Snow Map”, is it real or fake?”
Mei Huashi unfolded the painting in front of Gu Yu Ting and said in a profound sense, “Mr. Gu, this is a painting presented to the emperor by Moonlight Moon. You must smell it.”
Gu Yu Ting was shocked. Seated on the side, Moon Youguang's head “exploded” and almost fell off his chair. He secretly complained: Plum Blossom Rock, Mei Hua Shi, didn't you want my life if you said this in front of Gu Yuting? Gu Yu Ting knows that I gave this painting to the emperor; even if it was real, he would say it was a fake!
Gu Yuting carefully smelled the painting. For half a day, he looked up and said to Qianlong, “Emperor, this is the real face of Tang Bohu back then.”
“Guyu Ting, don't look away!” While whispering softly, Plum Blossom Stone thought, what exactly is going on with this ancient rain pavilion? Moon Youguang's father and son have harmed him so badly. Now the chance for revenge is right around the corner. As long as he says “fake,” Moon Youguang is a “crime of bullying the king.” If it's light, he will go to jail, and if it's serious, kill his head.
When the light of the moon hung over his throat, his heart suddenly relaxed, and his eyes became moist.
When Gu Yuting heard the ink and judged the paintings, they tried and were unhappy. Qianlong was amazed.
The next day, Mei Huashi sent his father and daughter on the road and regretfully said, “Brother Yuting, yesterday's painting was a great opportunity for you to avenge Snow's hate. I knocked on the sidelines several times to prompt you, but why did you tell the truth?”
“When will the wrongs be repaid? I said that the painting is authentic, not because they don't dare to lie in front of the emperor, but because they abide by professional conduct. Of course, I know that as long as I say nothing, Moonlight may be destroyed, and I can take revenge on Snow Shame. But I can't say anything that reverses black and white and confuses right and wrong. My eyes can't see, but my heart understands it.”
Gu Yu Ting's act of righteousness to avenge grievances with virtue moved Moon Youguang's father and son. Moon Youguang resigned from the capital of Suzhou and returned to Huizhou. Yuehuatang knelt down in front of Guyuting with his son to apologize. Later, the two companies got back together, managed Huimo together, and combined their respective surnames to create the best-selling Huimo brand, Hu Kaiwen.
