 “The Legend of the Gyokusen Well” takes place at Mount Keyakune, which is a section of the mountain on Mount Senyu, but the clear spring given by a fairy turned into a well.
Legend has it that on a certain day of the year in ancient times, the sun was hot. A team of pickers stumbled over the mountain in sweat and sighed as they walked: “Left shoulder, right shoulder, not afraid of Bekken Ridge going up to the sky; there was no water and smoke on the mountain.
” It just so happened that Buddha Guanyin drove from the South China Sea to Jiuhua Mountain to attend Jizo Bodhisattva's birthday party. He was compassionate. He took a clean bottle of manna from the cloud, took water from the East China Sea, spilled it a few times on the hill, and read a few words.
Then, pressing down on Xiang Yun, she turned into a white-haired grandmother standing by the mountain to pay her respects.
When the husbands arrived at the top of the mountain, the wife pointed to the side of the road with crutches and said, “Isn't there water in the grass? The pickers hurriedly cleared the roadside grass, and when they saw a clear spring, they all scooped water to quench their thirst.
After drinking, the crowd wanted to thank the grandmother for her instructions, but there was no trace of them.
When I came back to my senses, I knew that Kannon Bodhisattva had arrived.
Later generations excavated the spring water and made a well area, named it “Gosen Well.”
