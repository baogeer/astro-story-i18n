 Legend has it that the art of making Jinyun biscuits was invented after being inspired by Xuanyuan Huangdi's alchemy and Jinyun Mountain. Legend has it that Xuanyuan Huangdi used to make medicine at Dinghu Peak in Jinyun Mountain. When he was hungry, he mixed noodles with a mountain spring, kneaded a ball and placed it on the inner wall of the alchemy furnace. The baked cake was golden in color, crispy, and delicious, and fragrant. After Xuanyuan Huangdi took the dragon to heaven, local people used clay to imitate Huangdi's Dan stove to make pottery stoves and grill the dough to eat. After continuous improvement, I finally created my own unique pancake bucket and the world-famous Jinyun biscuits.
Jinyun biscuits are the most famous traditional dish in Jinyun. They have been named “Zhejiang Famous Snack”, “Chinese Famous Snack”, and the first Chinese travel gold medal snack. Jinyun biscuits are also a traditional way for Jinyun farmers to start businesses and find employment. In 2017, 15,000 people in the county were engaged in the biscuit industry across the country, with an output value of 1.5 billion yuan.
