 “Ten pieces of gold leaf have their loops open, ten pieces of gold pot have their edges exposed, and a hundred kinds of herbs can be found everywhere.” This is a song spread on Badaling. What's going on? There's a story and legend. When Qin Shi Huang built the Great Wall, he recruited migrant workers and skilled craftsmen from all over the country. Northerners are fine. In particular, when people from the South came to the north, they didn't accept the soil and water. Coupled with not being able to eat enough, and the labor was heavy, many more people died.
The ghost of Hades took the soul that died on the Great Wall into custody and went to Chenghuang Temple to cancel the account. Grandpa Chenghuang opened the account book, checked them one by one, and said, “These people are under the age of death and cannot be cancelled.” The devil went back to Hades, and Hades came to Chenghuang to make sense. Said they were dead and filled up the Great Wall, why didn't they cancel their account? Chenghuang said, “They have not reached the end of their lives; they should not have detained their souls at all. The two said their own reasons; no one was convinced by anyone. If there was no choice, I went to the Jade Emperor to file a lawsuit. Emperor Yu Huang heard the reason for the dispute between the two. When the lawsuit didn't end for a while, he asked them both to go down first, then let Taibai Jinxing go down to the world to investigate. Taibai Jinxing turned into an old man. When he came to the Great Wall, he learned that there were indeed too many deaths. Thousands of people died a day in winter and tens of thousands a day in summer. The main cause of death was due to two problems: one was not being able to eat enough, and the other was living too hard. At the same time, I also learned that the Great Wall was built to defend against foreign invaders.
Taijin Xing returned to heaven to inform Emperor Jade. In order to save the migrant workers and speed up the Great Wall project, Emperor Yu sent the old monarch and Grandpa Yao Wang to take over and made ten golden pots and ten golden pans. These two are precious. After cooking those ten pieces of golden pot, no matter how many people ate them, they couldn't finish eating it. Migrant workers are no longer hungry to work; those ten gold paddles are even more powerful. No matter how big the stones are, if you draw a large stone with a square prism line, the migrant workers no longer struggle to cut through the stones. Grandpa Yao sowed the medicinal seeds. The migrant workers got sick, so they were fine as soon as they ate them. With these few babies, the project is progressing very fast. Soon, the Great Wall of China will be repaired. Those ten golden pots and ten golden pans were not taken back by the old gentleman. They were buried in Badaling the whole time. I only heard that they had open edges and loose loops, but no one could find them. But medicinal herbs are everywhere. There are dozens of varieties of chaihu, motherwort, windproof, etc.
Looking back, let's talk about the lawsuit between Grandpa Hwang and Grandpa Chenghuang. The migrant workers who built the Great Wall had no end of their lives; they should not have died in the first place; their souls should not have been detained. Emperor Yu Huang decided that Chenghuang was reasonable; Hades was unreasonable. Hades's lawsuit was lost. So later, people built a temple for Chenghuang, called Chenghuang Temple, and a temple for the Jade Emperor, called Yuhuang Temple. They also built Laojun Temple for Lao Jun, and Yaowang Temple for Yao Wang. However, no one built a temple for Hades. I don't know if there are any nearby. Anyway, there are no Hades temples around the Badaling area of the Great Wall.
