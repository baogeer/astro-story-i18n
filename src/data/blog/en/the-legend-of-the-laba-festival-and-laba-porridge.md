 December 8 of the lunar calendar is the Chinese Laba Festival. People have a tradition of eating labak porridge. Laba congee is also known as Babao Porridge. Legend has it that the Laba Festival is the day when the Buddha “became the path”. Buddhist temples want to follow the story of the shepherd offering rice and make porridge from eight kinds of fragrant grains and fruits for the Buddha. Hence the name Baba Porridge. The recipe for Laba Porridge is simple, nutritious, and sweet. In the Song Dynasty, people scrambled to follow suit, spread widely, and became a dish loved by the people.
