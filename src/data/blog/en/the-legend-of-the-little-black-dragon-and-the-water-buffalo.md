 Legend has it that the Dai people in Mengku moved from Dehong. After the Dai people arrived, they first developed Mengku Dam and settled in Mengku for several generations. One day, an old man herding cows discovered that one of his buffaloes did not follow the herd in the morning, but went down the river alone. When the herd returned in the evening, the herd was surrounded by aquatic plants. The next day, he followed the boss's orders to follow the buffalo to find out. As a result, we arrived in the Batou area of Shuangjiang River today. The herdsmen saw a large, vast lake in front of them. The blue waves rippling on the surface of the lake, and the scenery was beautiful.
The buffalo jumped more than once by the lake. Suddenly, a small black dragon appeared in the water, and a pair of golden horns flashed brightly on its head. Little black dragons and buffaloes play in a sheltered stand on the shallow lakeside beach. From noon until the sun sets in the west. The old shepherd drove the cow home and told the boss about the situation. The next day, everyone from Mengmeng came to watch. People then discovered today's Shuangjiang Dam.
