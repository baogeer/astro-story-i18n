 Story ④ The legend of Little Horaikaku
Yuhuangding Park is also known as Little Penglai. Little Penglai includes a small Penglai Pavilion. The Little Penglai Pavilion is also called Luzumiao. It was two years since Qingguangxu, and this building was created because of a serendipitous coincidence. In the spring of Guangxu's two years, there was no rain for a long time. People who relied on the weather came to Yuhuang Temple to burn incense and seek rain. Coincidentally, heavy rain fell in the sky. Gong Yitu, a Qing dynasty gentleman, moved the wooden statue of Lu Dong Binga from his family's collection and built a temple for worship. People then attributed the heavy rain to Lu Dongbin and built this building to commemorate his exploits. Legend has it that seven other immortals later saw Lu Dongbin come here, so they came together and got the name Little Horai Pavilion.
There is a mural painting on the wall of Luzu Temple. On the left is a real man from Fire Dragon, who specializes in teaching Lu Dongbin to fight; on the right is Mr. Wen Fang, who specializes in teaching him piano, chess, calligraphy, and painting. Lu Dongbin is a book boy on the left, and the black kid on the right is Lu Dongbin's apprentice Willow Shu-jing. Legend has it that when Lu Dongbin preached to him back then, he didn't pay attention. Even though he became a human later, he always had a willow branch on his head.
