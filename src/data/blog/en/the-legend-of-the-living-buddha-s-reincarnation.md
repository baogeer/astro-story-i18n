 The founder of the living Buddha reincarnation system is Karma Boshi. He is highly skilled in Buddhism, and is highly appreciated by the Yuan dynasty emperor. He was once gifted with a black brimmed hat from Kublai's brother Ali Bugo. This hat has been preserved to this day. Karma Hoshi died at Chupu Temple in 1283 and lived 80 years. At the time of silence, he told his disciple Wu Jianba, “After my death, there will definitely be a successor to the Black Hat faction, an heir to the Black Hat faction, a descendant of Esoteric Buddhism...” At the moment of Nirvana, Karma Dalhi went to Doushiten. This is where Maitreya Bodhisattva lives. Eight days later, he suddenly remembered his disciples and put his “soul” back into the world. He saw that the disciples were crying and crying over his passing. He felt very uneasy. He immediately felt compassionate and decided to use the “law of extermination” (reincarnation yoga) to be reincarnated, thus continuing to teach all beings. On this day, in Duilong Baochang Village in northwestern Lhasa, an old couple's 13-year-old son died suddenly. When Karma Bushi saw billowing mulberry smoke, they rushed there and moved their souls into the corpse. The dead corpse's eyes sparkled. The old couple were very afraid when they saw this strange phenomenon. They thought it was the devil's body. They hurriedly grabbed some soot from the stove and scattered it in their eyes, then pierced their eyes with a needle.
According to Tibetan customs, sprinkling soot on people is a traditional method of getting rid of trouble. The old couple thought their dead son was haunted by the devil and wanted to use the ashes, but he didn't expect it, but this act disrupted Karma Balhi's reincarnation. He had no choice but to remove his soul from the corpse and find another way to reincarnate. Finally, the soul was transferred into the mother body of the later Tibetan Dogi, and reincarnation was successful. Since then, the Tibetan living Buddha reincarnation system has been established.
