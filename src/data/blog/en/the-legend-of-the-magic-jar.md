 Legend has it that in the past, there was a family with only two children and a mother. The mother was old and frail, and she had been unable to do any work for many years. My son, who only has honest relationships all year round, sells horse-grass and barely survives. Fortunately, my son is very filial. Every day, he hands over a penny and a half of the money from selling horse grass to the old woman. The old woman uses it to buy some rice and noodles and buy some oil and salt to make ends meet. The two children are now dependent on each other year after year.
This morning, when my son was carrying a cage and carrying a scythe to cut horse grass in the field, he found a pond of green grass the size of a straw hat on the upper side of an old water well. He then climbed up and took out his scythe to cut. What surprised him was that the tender and fat horse grass in this pond was actually cut while cutting the length of the pond, and he couldn't finish cutting it. After a while of hard work, he had already cut a strong cage. However, if you stand up and take a look, the lush grass is still the same as it was, as if no one had cut it at all.
At night, when my son came home after selling horse-grass, he told the old woman about this strange and strange thing he had encountered. The old woman, who had some insight, couldn't help but move after hearing it. She thought there must be some treasure buried under the grass. She asked her son to bring a pickaxe, and the mother and son quietly came to the old water well. Under the mother's command, the son certainly quickly dug out a thick, half-man-tall water tank from under this pond. The two wives carefully carried the water tank home, and felt that it was of little use other than using it to store rice.
When they dumped the rice into the pot, an incredible miracle happened: just by pouring half a bag of rice, it became a full pot of rice in the blink of an eye. The mother and son were really surprised and delighted. Since then, the mother and daughter have not had a full day of suffering. My son didn't have to be called by his old woman to climb up and go out and cut horse-grass without getting bright. After the family's stomach problem was solved, the kind mother and son often helped the surrounding townspeople. Today, when they saw that the owner couldn't open the pot, they gave them rice. Tomorrow, when they know their western neighbors are hungry, they will deliver the meal.
However, the good times didn't last long. The news that the mother and son had dug a magical treasure tank next to an old water well somehow blew into the ears of a local local treasurer whose son was a foreign official. This local treasurer, who is always rich and uses his power to oppress his neighbors, personally led a gang of dogs and violently stormed into these two girls' homes, arguing that this treasure chest was buried in their family's ancestors and wanted them to return to their original owners immediately; otherwise, they would lure them into the spotlight for stealing. Where have these two ladies seen this kind of situation, so even though they couldn't bear it, they didn't dare to make a sound; they had to watch the local treasurer drink and make his subordinates carry the treasure pot away.
After the local treasurer rushed to his home, he placed it in the middle of the courtyard dam. The whole family, big and small, laughed and watched the bustle around the treasure pot. I heard that anything in a treasure pot can turn out a lot of things. The audience shouted out loud when they got on. This said let it hold rice and noodles; the one advocated using it to hold gold and silver; others even made a noise about using it to pack clothes, etc.
At a time when people were in trouble, the father of the local treasurer was also busy coming out of his back room to see the strange sight. I saw this old man, shaking his hands, stumbled and stumbled on a treasure chest with a crutch. I don't know if he was careless or what. Suddenly, his foot slipped, and an inverted clock stood inside the treasure pot. The person next to him quickly pulled him out with his hand. Unexpectedly, an old man came out of the tank, and another one exactly the same old man popped up in the tank, then pulled it out and caught it again. He pulled out 120 old men in a row, and there was still an old man in the pot. Faced with this sudden situation, I was so surprised that everyone on the scene was dazed and overwhelmed...
The local treasurer suddenly woke up and rushed into the stove to extract an axe. No matter how hard he hit the treasure pot on the 3721 floor, he only heard a “knock” sound. The treasure pot was shattered in half when it climbed. Staring at the fragments of treasure pots all over the place and looking at the exact same old man standing majestically with crutches in his yard, the local treasurer suddenly screamed “wow”. His legs were paralyzed and his eyes were dark and he died...
