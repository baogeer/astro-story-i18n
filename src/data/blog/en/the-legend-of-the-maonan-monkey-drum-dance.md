 Legend has it that they live in a family with dense trees and beautiful mountains and water. Her husband died early, leaving behind three sons, and his wife worked hard to make a living by collecting green okako in the mountains and brewing wine. One day, the three brothers went to the mountain to pick green potatoes. Unwittingly, the younger brother and two older brothers separated. They only discovered that the younger brother was missing when it was almost dark. They hurriedly ran home to call their mother and neighbors in the village and went up the mountain to find them, but they couldn't find them for three days. His mother washed her face with tears every day. After a long time, she cried her eyes blind.
This young son discovered that his brother was missing. He was very afraid. He unwittingly walked deep into the jungle and lost his way home. He finally fell into the cave. Turns out he fell into a cave where monkeys lived. There was a large group of monkeys living in the cave. The little son lived and played with the monkeys in the cave. When he was hungry, he ate wild fruits picked by the monkeys, and when he was thirsty, he went to drink mountain spring water with the monkeys. As he grew older, this kid gradually grew hairy, and he also learned the language of monkeys, and couldn't even speak the original words.
After many years, the child's mother ended up feeling melancholy because she missed her son too much. According to Maonan folk custom, the mage had to use bronze drums to sacrifice the dead old man on the day of burial. For a while, a low sound of bronze drums rang through the sky and spread to barren mountains thousands of miles away. The little son, who was playing in a group of monkeys, heard the sound of bronze drums familiar from his hometown. He followed the sound of the drum to find his way home. He looked closely at the sound of the village and discovered that the bronze drum came from afar. It came from my own home, and there was a white cloth hanging in front of the door. The two older brothers knelt in front of the coffin while crying while burning incense and paper, so Realizing that his mother had passed away, he ran to his house at any cost, but after running to the door, he discovered that the people in the family couldn't recognize him, didn't speak, and only screamed like a monkey. In order to express his thoughts and sorrow to his mother, he picked up an empty tree can and covered it with animal skin around his waist and made a beast skin drum. He shouted and shouted while singing “howling, howling” in his mouth The dance was to express how sad they had been away from their families for many years. At first, those present thought it was on the hillside The monkey was messing around, but when I saw tears in his eyes, he was always reluctant to leave. I only thought if he was a lost child more than 10 years ago. When the two older brothers recognized him, the three brothers knelt in front of their mother's spirit and couldn't stop crying.
Later, in order to commemorate their dead loved ones, the Maonan people used animal skins to make tambourines and evolved the legend of the three brothers into a form of folk dance to commemorate their deep feelings for their loved ones, which later became a cultural classic of the Maonan people — the “monkey encouragement dance.” When the dead elderly are buried, in addition to using bronze drums to sacrifice their dead loved ones, the Maonan people must also use the “monkey encouragement dance” to express their nostalgia for their loved ones. Because of its cheerful rhythm and funny movements, it gradually evolved into some festivals of their own nation, and became the traditional dance of the Maonan people.
