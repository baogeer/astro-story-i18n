 In the old days, the Miao family had a pharmacist named Shiyi. His skills were amazing, and he was able to cure any disease. Taking his medicine not only cures diseases, but also prolongs life. At that time, people could live to be thousands or tens of thousands of years old. No one in the world knows what he is capable of. Everyone calls him a master, even the heavens are famous for him.
Shi Siyi is a very studious person; he is not satisfied with his excellent medical skills. He saw people like Jade Mother Jade in the Sky, Taishang Laojun, and Taibai Xingjun. Not only did they not get sick, but they also didn't die. He thought there must be medicine in heaven to keep you from getting sick and dying.
As a result, Shi Siyi went to heaven to pick medicine! Once more than 30 days have passed, a day in the sky is a year on earth. By the time he came back, his wife, children, and quite a few others had already died. How did he die? Killed by the plague. There are a few people, so thin as firewood, that they are about to die. He saved these people. There is no way to save a wife, children, or others who have already died. Because they have no muscles or internal organs, only a skeleton is left.
Acolyte Siyi looked up and said, “Oh! It turns out that doctors can only heal people who are alive, not people who have died. What kind of ability is this!”
Samurai Master Yi learned that Taishang Laojun has medicine that revives death, and can even save dead bones. Then I went to heaven and asked him to teach me again.
Taishang Lao Jun taught Jade as Mother Jade, and was not allowed to teach Jade Mother. He also said, “Everyone in the world is going to die. If they are all like us, never die, and can't live on Earth for two days, how can they do it?”
When Taishang Lao Jun listened, he came up with a bad idea and inspired him to say, “For the eternal peace of heaven, there is no need to let go back to the world.”
Mother Yu Dang asked what the reason was.
Taishang Laojun said, “He went back to heaven and taught me the medicine of immortality; this time, he wants me to pass on the recipe for regeneration. If we let him go back, wouldn't the world just be the same as us? They just live and don't die; one day they will fight against heaven.”
Mother Yu Dang heard it and was shocked. She hurriedly asked, “So what should I do? We have to find a reason to detain him!”
Taishang Laojun laughed, “This is easy; they say he can't blame anyone!”
When he got home, he said to Shi Siyi, “Aren't you here to take medicine from death?”
Shi Siyi happily said, “Yes, my husband, please pass it on to me, so I can go back and save those who died.”
Taishang Laojun said, “The medicine is in the sandalwood tree on the moon.”
“How can I get it?” .
“Cut down the tree and you get it.”
Shijiyi then went to the moon to cut down sandalwood trees. Who knows the axe was cut down. When I picked it up, the cut hole in the sandalwood tree grew long again. He cut it to the point where it was dark in one day, and even cut down a piece of bark. He went and asked Taishang Laojun, “What's the point of this?”
Taishang Laojun said, “This is rebirth from death! You cut it off, and the place you cut it will come back to life.”
“How can I cut it down?”
“You cut an axe, compare it with your neck, cut another axe, compare. In this way, the cut off area won't grow back together.”
The boss Siyi did as the boss said. Indeed, after cutting it down, his mouth didn't get along, so he cut down and compared the ground and continued to work. Who knows, it was cut in half. When she saw the heart of the tree, Shi Siyi had just compared it with her neck. Suddenly, her mouth grew long and stuck in his neck! ·
Since then, the magic doctor Shiyi has been stuck on a sandalwood trunk in the moon. If you don't believe it, take a look at the night when there is a big moon. There are individuals stuck in the sandalwood trunk. That is our Miao family's magic doctor servant Si Yi! Look, he's still lying around. One day, when he comes back from getting rid of it, the world will have medicine for immortality and recovery from death, huh!
