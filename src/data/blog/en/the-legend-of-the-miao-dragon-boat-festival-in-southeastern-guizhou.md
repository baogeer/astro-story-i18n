 The Dragon Boat Festival is a festival held in Miaojia Village. Each village specially made one or two dragon boats, parked in a covered promenade boat shed, and waited until the festival was launched for a paddling competition.
The Hmong people's Dragon Boat Festival has a long history. “Miaojiang Stories” by Xu Jiagan from Qianlong, Qing Dynasty, records, “The Miao people fought dragon boats. Starting May 20, they raced in the wide and deep waters of the Qingshui River. The boat is made of large wood and is five or six feet long. It has a faucet in front and a phoenix tail in the back, and can accommodate more than 20 people. It's like walking in the water for a short time.”
Regarding the Dragon Boat Festival, the Hmong people tell a moving story, passed down in the distant past. A Miao fisherman named Bao lives by the river. He has an only son named Jiubao. One day, he took his only son down the river to fish. Suddenly, the sky was overshadowed, the wind was strong, and the river waves hit the sky. An evil dragon leaped out of the deep pool and dragged Jiubao into Dragon Cave, saving him a lot of grief, so he searched for Diver's Cave. Jianlong has killed his son and slept soundly using the body as a pillow. Bao was extremely angry and determined to avenge his son. He went ashore and took the sickle, and once again sneaked into the dragon cave, setting fire to death while the dragon was sleeping. The smoke from Dragon Caves filled the entire area. At this time, it was raining heavily, and the sky was dark for nine days and nine nights. Everyone was very worried. At the time, a woman took her child to the riverside to wash her clothes. The naive kid stroked her mom's sleeper in the water and played, saying, “'There's a lot of knocking! Too many knocks!” I don't know when he shouted, and the clouds cleared up in the sky. The East shines brightly, and a bright red sun rises high above the ground. Everyone is happy and cheering for the province. Afterwards, when people saw the burnt dragon floating on the surface of the water, they rushed to the riverside one after another to eat dragon meat. Sheng Bingzhai was the first to discover that the dragon was given a share of the dragon (there is also a saying that the dragon head was given first to protect the dragon by not being afraid of dragons). Shi Dong went one step later and got to the dragon's tail. Yang Jiazhai also went late; he only got dragon intestines. As a result, Yangjiazhai's dragon boats are all painted dark green, which is considered a blue dragon. When people took away the dragon meat, the evil dragon Bento Meng said to everyone, “I lost the old man's only son, and I have already lost my life. I hope you, young and old, do well. Use cedar trees to imitate my body, paddling around the Qingshui River and the Little River for a few days, and enjoy the rivers just like when I was alive, so I can enjoy the clouds and rain to keep you rich.” After this dream spread, there was a lot of discussion. They all said that dragons had been surrendered and drawn like it, so they could get rain and preserve a good harvest. Why not have the two at the same time? At first, they tried out a few boats, but of course there was heavy rain, so they built dragon boats in every village. It was agreed that it will be the fifth or sixth day of the beginning of May in the lunar calendar... This was a busy farming season, which hindered production, so the villages also negotiated with each other. Apart from Shengping and Pingping paddling boating at the beginning of May 5, Pingzhai changed to May 24, Tanglong 25, Rongshan 16, and Shidong designated the 26th day of the “division of the dragon.” Since then, Dragon Boat Festival activities have been handed down in this order.
