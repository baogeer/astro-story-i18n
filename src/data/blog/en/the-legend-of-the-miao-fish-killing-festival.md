 Every year on March 9, it is a “fish killing festival” for Miao people living in Longli, Guiding, Fuquan and other places in Guizhou. On this day, people came to the riverside, picked up a fresh fish from the river, set up an iron pot, set up a bonfire, cooked fresh fish in river water, drank rice wine, prayed for rain, and wished the wind and rain would be good, and there would be plenty of grains. There is another legend about this day.
A long time ago, Grandpa Tenno had a very beautiful princess. She was smart, smart, and super talented. Grandpa Tenno loved it like a pearl in his pocket. One day, the princess suddenly became ill. Grandpa Tianwang was very anxious. He sent the Heavenly Soldier to deliver the magic elixir from Tiangong, collected Ganoderma lucidum grass from Kunlun Mountain, dug up ginseng dolls from Changbai Mountain, and fetched pearls from the sea. But none of these medicines cured the princess's illness. Seeing that the princess's condition was getting worse day by day, Grandpa Tenno was in a hurry and didn't think about dinner. Later, when Grandpa Tenno heard that “a hundred fish in human rivers can cure all diseases,” he immediately sent Tenjin down to the world, caught 100 kinds of fish from rivers, lakes, and seas, boiled them into soup, and gave them to the princess. After the princess ate it, she got well within a few days of getting sick. In order to thank the fish for saving lives, Lord Heavenly ordered the protection of fish in rivers, lakes, and seas, and not to harm them at will. Grandpa Tenno also ordered the god of rain to drop all the rain into rivers, lakes, and the sea, and not give a drop to humans.
At this time, there was a major drought, and the Miao family was very worried. As a result, the townspeople killed pigs, slaughtered cows, and sacrificed the sky for rain. However, Grandpa Tenno still doesn't give a drop of water to humans. Finally, all the pigs and cows were slaughtered. On March 9, the townspeople took some carp, mackerel, salmon, crucian carp, and dollfish from rivers, lakes, and seas to replace pigs and cows and pray for rain. Who knows, when Grandpa Tenno saw that humans killed his good fish in order to live, he couldn't help but cry bitterly when he thought about the fish's life-saving blessing to his daughter. He cried for three days and three nights, with dark clouds rolling in, the wind raging, and lightning thunder. Grandpa Tenno's tears turned into heavy downpours, falling from the sky. It rained for three days and three nights, and water flowed everywhere, filling the farmland and moisturizing the plants.
Until now, the Miao family in Pingpo, Guiding Guangming, and Fuquan Xinanzhai had to go to the riverside to kill fish every year on March 9.
