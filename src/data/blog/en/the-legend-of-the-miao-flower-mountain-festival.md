 Every year on the 3rd, 4th, and 5th day of the Lunar New Year, it is a grand festival for the Miao people — stepping on Huashan. Over the past three days, men, women, and children of the Miao family all wore gold and silver, and rushed from all sides to the foot of Huagan Mountain, blowing Lu Sheng, playing, kicking, slapping, bullfighting, and drawing eyebrows... In particular, the inverted climbing pole was even more remarkable: a mido waist ribbon, headband, boopa, back applique, extended both hands, raised above the head, and held the flower pole in reverse. They tilted their heads upwards, leaping one by one to the top of the flower pole, one climbed onto the other. Suddenly, cheers started pouring in. This is the Miao family's unique trekking on a mountain of flowers.
How did this show come about?
Legend has it that in ancient times, the Miao family lived in the plains. The land there was so wide that it was as flat as a playground. It had good weather and rain year after year, and the family had food and clothing.
At that time, the Miao family had a king named Meng Ziyou. He was very nice. He had nine sons and eight girls. Every night, he leads his son and girl to play Lu Sheng, play cowhide drums, sing, and dance.
One night, the sound of cowhide drums reached the Imperial Palace, shaking the palace and shocking the civilization and martial arts of the dynasty. As a result, the emperor sent people to inspect everywhere.
One day, the emperor learned that the Miao family had a large plot of good land. The family had money and food. His eyes were red, and he was mischievous, so he sent troops to fight Meng Ziyou. Every time the imperial soldiers arrived, they wantonly occupied the Miao family and did nothing to cause the peace of the Miao family. At this point, Meng Ziyou became infuriated. He led the Miao family to resist the imperial soldiers together. However, due to Meng Ziyou's poor weapons, as a result of repeated battles and defeats, he was driven to the deep mountains and old forests.
In this deep mountain forest, one peak is larger than one peak, and one peak is taller than one peak, and it continues. The Miao family fled here, shouting no matter how many times they shouted, and they couldn't find it. Meng Ziyou cut down a tall, straight cedar tree, pruned it, peeled it, dyed it red and white, untied the red cloth belt around his waist, tied it to one end of the flower pole, and stood on the highest mountain to summon the children of the Miao family. When the Miao family saw it, they gathered together. Meng Ziyou then set up a camp under the flower pole, with nine sons and eight girls each leading some of the Miao people and stationed at the top of each mountain. They hunted while cultivating the land to weave, building houses and making weapons at the same time. Within a few years, the Miao family became rich again. As a result, the Miao family played cowhide drums, played Lu Sheng, and sang and danced again.
The sound of cowhide drums spread to the imperial palace, and the emperor sent soldiers to attack Meng Ziyou along with the sound of the drums. When Meng Ziyou heard the news, she summoned her son and girl and asked, “The Imperial Soldier is fighting again. Are there enough weapons?” They answered in unison, “That's enough.” Meng Ziyou also asked, “Are all the levels strict?” With the exception of her son Nong Ju-mi, everyone else answered, “Strict.” Seeing that Nong Shoumi didn't squeak, Meng Ziyou angrily asked, “What about you?” Nong Shoumi laughed and answered, “I didn't block the level.” When Meng Ziyou heard it, he was furious and yelled, “If you don't block the level, are you going to send the imperial soldiers into the mountain, kill your brothers and sisters, and cut off my head?” Nong Ju-mi still smiled and answered, “Dad, I want to open the door, lead the imperial soldiers to the foot of the flower pole, and play behind closed doors. The Imperial Soldiers are well-armed and full of people. If you don't do this, you won't be able to beat them.” After hearing this remark, Meng Ziyou's anger abated slightly and asked, “How are you going to introduce the law?” Nong Shoumi watched the eight younger sisters and answered, “A few years ago, we fought against the Imperial Soldiers, and they went back to victory. This time, one of them would be careless. We could choose some girls to dress up, and at the same time select some boys and girls with high martial arts to dance under flower poles to lure the Imperial Soldiers up to Huashan, and lay down heavy soldiers around them until the imperial soldiers reached the middle of the mountainside. “Light...” After listening, Meng Ziyou turned angry and turned happy: “OK, OK, so who will hang the flag?” Nong Shoumi answered, “The key to this battle is the person who hangs the flag. You must hang the flag in a timely manner. You must have both courage, energy, and flexibility...” Meng Ziyou heard this, turned happy and worried. Instead of waiting for Nong Shumi to finish, she also hurriedly asked, “Well, who will hang the flag?” The eight sons rushed to the front and scrambled to hang up the flag. After watching a few sons over and over again, Meng Ziyou still couldn't decide who would hang the flag. Nong Shoumi said to her eight younger brothers, “When climbing the pole in the competition, who wins and who hangs the flag.” Meng Ziyou listened and shouted: “OK, OK, let's do it!”
At the beginning of the game, the Miao family flocked to the bottom of the flower pole, and Meng Ziyou's nine sons surrounded three floors inside and three outside. The nine sons surrounded the flower pole again. Meng Ziyou stood under a flower pole and held a red flag. Suddenly, the entire mountaintop was filled with people. Meng Ziyou looked at the nine sons and asked, “Which one climbs first?”
The conversation went down. The younger son Zhisuduo walked under the flower pole, hugged the flower pole, rustled up like a squirrel, and then slipped down again. The crowd saw it and cheered. While shouting loudly, Zhisuduo said to his father, “How? Give me the red flag!”
Meng Ziyou hasn't spoken yet. His second son, Nong Paigong, shouted, “Slow down, watch me.” He grabbed the pole with both hands and stepped on the pole with both legs. His body didn't stick to the pole. He brushed up and down. The crowd saw it, and the applause was thunderous. Nong Pai Gong smiled and said to his father, “Dad, give me the red flag!”
As soon as Nong Shoumi leaped forward, took the red flag one by one, bit the red flag, and grabbed the pole. With only two hands, she climbed to the top of the pole in the blink of an eye, then slipped back and forth several times, up and down, going back and forth several times, one by one. The crowd was dumbfounded, and it took half a minute to cheer in unison. A few younger brothers met and said in unison, “Dad, Grandpa's martial arts are superior to ours, so let Grandpa hang up the flag!” That's it, the election is over.
Next, according to Nong Shoumi's advice, some beautiful girls who can sing and dance well and some handsome young men were selected to stay under the flower pole to lure the imperial soldiers, and the rest were ambushed at the foot of the mountain.
The people at the top of the mountain whistled, and it was very lively. Around the foot of the mountain, the Miao family prepared weapons and waited for the imperial soldiers to arrive.
Soon, the imperial soldiers swayed into the encirclement circle. Nong Shoumi stood under the flower pole and saw that the imperial soldiers were not far from him. He asked everyone to stop singing and dancing and kill them down the mountain. He immediately turned around, used his best ability, and climbed to the top of the pole.
When the Imperial Soldier knew Nong Ju-mi's intention, they fended off the attacking Miao Soldiers while facing Nong Ju-mi to archery. In order to dodge the bow and arrow, Nong Shoumi circled around the pole, and one arrow flew empty. Halfway up the climb, several arrows hit Nong Shou-mi's back. He shouted “ahhh,” and the red flag in his mouth fell to the ground. He immediately slipped down the pole, picked up the flag, bit it, and hovered towards the top of the pole. Having just climbed to the top of the pole, a few arrows hit him in the head. He screamed, and the red flag fell again. At this point, the imperial soldiers were screaming; they were almost at the bottom of the flower pole. Nong Ju-mi saw it, pulled out a big sword, and struggled to kill the Imperial Soldier. The Imperial Soldier was finally temporarily repulsed. Taking advantage of this opportunity, after thinking for a moment, Nong Shoumi picked up the red flag, rushed to the bottom of the flower pole, carried the decal pole with her back, held the pole with both hands, and lifted her feet up, head down, feet up, leaping one by one, and climbing to the top of the pole. The imperial soldier saw it and fired another arrow. An arrow was placed on the flower pole and put it on Nong Shoumi's leg. Blood flowed down the pole on his back. He moaned, cursed, and used all his strength to climb to the top of the pole and hang a red flag.
Miao soldiers ambushed all around them and stormed out one after another, struggling to slay the imperial soldiers. Meng Ziyou rushed to the front, killing the imperial soldiers, causing the devil and the wolf to roar, and scattered and fled. Nong Shoumi watched it and laughed with joy, but he also closed his eyes because the injury was so severe. Meng Ziyou won this battle, but nine of his sons and eight daughters died in the battle. Meng Ziyou and the Miao family carried them to the foot of the flower pole and watched and cried. It wasn't until the 3rd, 4th, and 5th of the Lunar New Year in the following year that they cooked alcohol to kill cows and buried them at the foot of the flower pole. Since then, the Miao family has held memorial ceremonies every year on the 16th lunar day and on the 3rd, 4th, and 5th of the second year of the Lunar New Year. At the same time, they have practiced martial arts such as climbing upside down, dagger, pole, and wooden sticks for their nine sons to inspire the Miao family to resist oppression.
