 Legend has it that there is an old woman with no children living in a place called “Gu Hong Gu Liu” in the north. One day, the old man washed clothes by the riverside in front of her house and saw a colorful bird covering it with two wings. When the old man salvaged it, it turned out to be just a copper pot. There was a child lying in the pot with thick eyebrows and big eyes, a big nose and square lips, which is very cute. The old man took him home and raised him and named the child “Ade.” “Germany,” the Miao word is dragon. The child salvaged it from the river. The colorful bird also followed the old man home and made a nest in a big tree next to the house. Every day the sun comes out, it cries. Everyone says that the sun changes, calling it a “sunbird.” Day by day, Ade has grown into a strong and strong young man. He works on the ground with the old man during the day. As soon as he has free time, he knocks on the copper pot on the floor. The sunbird on the tree flies down when it hears the sound of the copper pot and jumps on the ground, attracting a group of young men and women to gather on the front floor. The girl's door imitates a sunbird to start and jump.
One day of the year, suddenly the sky was covered with dark clouds, heavy rain pouring down, and floods skyrocketed. Sunbirds fly on branches, peck off fallen leaves, become white clouds, and float in the air. White clouds carried people to the northwest. The northwest was full of ice and snow, and Ade had to fly southeast with the sunbirds. Fly! Fly! When I flew south, I saw a large mountain showing the surface of the water. Soon, floods fell, and forests, mountains, and valleys appeared. They began to settle down, cut down tree houses, and cultivate mountains and fields. Following the arrival of Adnan, 500 men and women became couples with each other.
After Adnan arrived, he established the Three Commandments to teach future generations: First, to prosper the clan; second, never to go down the hillside; and three to wear clothes that are not easy. Therefore, today, many Miao people live in mountainous areas. They love Miao clothes, respect their ancestors, and love their clan.
After Ade's death, his grandchildren called him “King” and built a temple for him at the foot of “Ga Liang” Xishan. In the past, every Miao year and February 2, Miao people gathered in front of Miao Wang Temple to play bronze drums, play Lu Sheng, sing, and dance to commemorate “King Miao.”
