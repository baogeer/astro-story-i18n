 The young man never leaves his mouth; the girl doesn't take her hands off her needle; it's not Miao's Da Heng (young man) who can't embroider; it's not Miao's Da Pei (girl) who can't embroider. Lu Sheng, called “Gix” (Gix) in the Miao dialect of Qiandong and “Ghenb” (Ghenb) in the Miao dialect of Sichuan, Qiandong and Yunnan, is made of bamboo. Each Lu Sheng's six-root tube is described in the Lu Sheng song as “Lu Sheng's three-section cylinder, empty inside; a ladle with six poles, blowing and bombing”. Along with dialects and costumes, it is an important symbol that distinguishes the Miao branch. According to Miao legends, in ancient times, when there was no smoking in the world, the Emperor sent his little daughter Gou Su to the earth to create everything. After they had everything, one day the brothers Jia and Xue (that is, fir and bamboo) went to the heavenly court to ask the Emperor to ask for a few pieces of entertainment. Gou Su, the daughter of the Emperor, cut off 6 of her fingers and 1 arm to make a Lu Sheng. She herself died due to excessive bleeding. Emperor Tiandi was very sad and handed over Lu Sheng to the brothers Jia and Ze and said, “This is your mother. Everything must go through her (permission) in the future, and she will bring you happiness”. Since the Miao family had Lu Sheng, they have lived a stable, happy, and happy life.
Almost every village of the Miao people usually has a group (team) of Lu Sheng. This is a sign of whether life in the village is full and happy. In Miaozhai, in places such as Danzhai, Sandu, Rongjiang, and Leishan in Guizhou, the Lu Sheng team consists of a combination of the six types of Lu Sheng: Mangtong, medium, large, medium, small, and extra small. Unlike other Lusheng trees, Mangtong doesn't have six tubes; it's just a three- or four-foot long garden tube. Each small bamboo tube is inserted in the tube and played with a bamboo tube in its mouth. The sound is low and rich. The large Lu Sheng is the mother of a sheng. It is about two feet tall. It just can't blow; it needs two people to lift it.
