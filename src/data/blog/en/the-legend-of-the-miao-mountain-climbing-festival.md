 Every year in March of Yangchun, young Miao men and women enjoy the annual Mountain Climbing Festival with an extremely happy mood. The “Mountain Climbing Festival” is a festival where young Miao men and women “travel”, that is, talk about love as the main content. Through mountain climbing activities, the goal of marriage autonomy and a happy lifelong partner is achieved. In the Leishan County area, they are held on the day and afternoon of March in March of the lunar calendar in places such as Sanjiaofeng commune, Niujiapo of Huangli commune, Gypsophila of Wujiang commune, and Senbianfu of Dagou commune. The scene at Wangfeng Triangle Field was the biggest, with over 10,000 people each time, because young men and women from neighboring counties such as Danzhai, Kaili, Majiang, and Taijiang also participated. In addition to singing songs between young men and women, mountain climbing activities have also been added in recent years, such as sparrow fighting, horse racing, basketball games, and tug-of-war.
There is also a legend about the origin of the Mountain Climbing Festival.
Behind the stockade in Dalong Ao, Huangli Commune, under Mount Leigong, there is a Niujiao slope. Legend has it that in ancient times, in the place of Baoganfu (the former site of Dalong Ao), a poor family lived with only two people, a mother and child. Her mother is old and frail, and her son is Wang Jian. He is over ten years old. He is strong, hardworking and simple, and can do all kinds of work. The old mom saw Wang Jian grow up day by day, and she really wanted to find him a daughter-in-law. However, the family had no possessions; they were poor. The central media asked how many companies, but they all hated being poor; no one agreed. There is a wealthy family in the village. Her daughter is called “Meifu”. She is very good-looking. She is famous in this area. Many people came to her to propose marriage, but all of them were rejected, yet the girl secretly fell in love with a young student, Wang Jian. Sometimes when the two meet on the side of the road, they have to share a few heartfelt words. When they have the chance, they also sing a few love songs for each other. As they grew older, love grew deep in each other's hearts. Maifu secretly vowed that he wouldn't get married; he would be willing to pair up with Wang Jian and work together until old age. The situation of the two young people falling in love with each other went unruly in the village and spread to Mafu's father's ears. The old man was so angry that he locked Mei Fu in the cellar and prevented her from meeting Wang Jian.
From winter to spring, it's time to get busy with spring farming. Wang Jian works hard as always and works hard in the fields every day. Mafu was locked all alone in a cellar. Looking at Wang Jian, his heart almost flew out. However, she understood in her heart that her parents had strict regulations, and even the “Yu Fang” wouldn't let her go. It's really harder than heaven to get along with Wang Jiancheng! One day, Wang Jian came to work as a helper at Meifu's house and just went down to the ground to plow fields. Mafoe thought this would be a great opportunity to talk to his sweetheart about his feelings. It just so happened that her family had died a Huang Gu cow. She came up with a great idea. She killed the cow in the dark, draped the cowhide over her body, put on the horn, and pretended to be a big Gu cow. When the sky was bright, Wang Jian rushed the cow to work. When they arrived in the field, they prepared to set up a plow. No matter how much the cow didn't listen to the call, they pulled out their legs and ran. Wang Jian was very strange; he followed closely. Chase, chase, come to a hillside, and the cow stopped. Wang Jian took a closer look. Oops, where is the cow? It's actually my sweetheart, Meifu. The two met, and it was really hard to figure it out. Wang Jian had mixed feelings and was extremely excited. They walked hand in hand on the hillside and sang their first heartfelt love song, singing from the early morning until sunset, and from night to dawn. The melodious song spread to the village, and everyone was moved by the lover's singing voice. Gradually, young men and women in and outside the village all rounded up this hillside. On that day, the mountains and mountains were full of people, all searching for songs from their sweethearts. When Mafu's parents found out, they were very angry. He rushed to the hillside with a bunch of family members, and beat Mafu and Wang to death. This day is the first day of March of the lunar calendar.
The unfortunate couple died, leaving a pair of horns on the hillside. The mountain grows taller year by year and grows into a small mountain bag in the shape of an ox horn, hence the name “Gyu-Kaku Po”. Every year when spring arrives, the grass on Ngau Kok Slope is greener than anywhere else, and the red in Yingshan grows before anywhere else. When people saw this, they naturally thought of this legendary couple. Every time in the twilight of spring in March, young men and women are required to wear costumes, hold parasols, and carry folding fans, and gather from everywhere to “travel” to Niujiakpo. It expressed nostalgia for this faithful couple, and it was also a big gathering to fight for independence in marriage.
