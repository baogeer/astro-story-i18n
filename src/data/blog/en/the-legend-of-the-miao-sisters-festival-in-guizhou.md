 The Guizhou Miao Sisters Festival mainly refers to a festival where Miao people from Taijiang County, Shibing County, and parts of Jianhe County in Guizhou Province eat sister meals every year during the spring season. The time varies from place to place, and is generally scheduled for February 15 or March 15 of the lunar calendar. The festival etiquette is simple and unique. The most typical and spectacular one is the Miao Sister Festival on the banks of the Qingshui River in the Shidong area of Taijiang County.
The legend of the origin of Little Sister's Day
The Sister Festival covers a wide range of topics. The 100,000 Miao people in hundreds of villages have simplified or similar customs, and are also slightly different in terms of legend. The origin of the legend is the following two.
1. Legend has it that in the Geyi district of Taijiang County, there is a Miao village. The men in the shaizi have to go far, far away to hunt every year, and don't come back for a long time. There are also many women and children in the stockade, and 70 sisters who look like flowers. When women return from work, they raise children, and live a life they will never finish. The sisters worked up the mountain to pick fruit and work in the field. At home, the sisters were still spinning, weaving, brocades, and picking flowers to rust. The colored brocade they made was more beautiful than the color of the sky; the flowers, bees, and butterflies they embroidered all flew to stay, yet the seventy sisters still felt that something was missing, and their hearts were always unhappy, because they had reached the age of cardamom. As a result, the sisters were invited to open fields again. They chose a place rich in water resources, and turned their minds to reclaim land and cultivate fields. Open up, open up, open a very large field. From this side of the field, they couldn't see the field winning on the other side. As a result, they kept many, many fish in the fields. Spring came, they sowed glutinous rice seeds in the field. Autumn arrived, and the glutinous grains they sowed filled one warehouse after another, producing cylinder after bottle of glutinous rice wine. They had everything to eat, drink, and dress, but the sisters always felt that something was missing; they rarely sang or laughed. The old people saw the sisters' mentality and came up with a good method. They suggested that they preserve the glutinous rice they couldn't finish eating and the glutinous rice wine they couldn't finish, use polished rice to make glutinous rice when spring blossoms bloom next year, then go to the fields to catch fish, shrimp, and snails and set up a feast, and welcome young men from afar to eat the sisters' meals, dance, socialize, and choose a partner. The following year, it was true that through this format, some younger sisters also chose Ruyi couples and happily married. After that, eating the sister's meal formed a specific festival.
2. Legend has it that the Miao people used to live in the Far East. Later, due to population growth, living difficulties, disasters and wars, they were forced to continuously migrate to the West. Every time they lived in a place for a while, some of the sisters had to get married, and the sisters who migrated and got married again were unable to return home. It was hard to say goodbye when they met, so they decided to go to the field to catch fish and shrimp in the spring season, and invite their married sisters to meet up with the unmarried sisters, have a sisters' meal and then say goodbye. In this way, they became an annual sister festival. As the saying goes: After eating a sister's meal, they fell in love for a year.
A few days before the Sister Spider Festival, the sisters went to the mountains to gather southern candelabra leaves. Various types of flowers and herbs, such as sister top and honey, are soaked in cold water and boiled in water to make various types of black, red, yellow, and green flower juices. Then soak the glutinous rice separately with this juice. After a day or two, the juice completely penetrated the glutinous rice, then removed and filtered separately, then rinsed with clean water to remove more than 100 of the colored juice, then soaked them separately, mixed the various types of glutinous rice, and steamed, making them a bright and fragrant multicolored sister meal. This sister meal, made using a variety of wild flowers and herbs, is not only colorful, aromatic and delicious, but it's also not bad if you keep it for a long time. It also has visceral fitness, nourishing and strengthening effects, and is a special food shared and gifted with family and friends during the holiday season.
