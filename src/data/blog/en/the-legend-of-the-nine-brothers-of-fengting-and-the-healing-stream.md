 Legend has it that during the time of Emperor Han Wu, there was a person named He Renxia in Lujiang, Anhui who gave birth to nine sons. Except for the boss, who had an eye in the middle of his forehead, the other eight were blind.
One year, the nine brothers and their father visited Wang Liu An's home in Linchuan, Jiangxi, and Huainan.
After dinner that day, when they heard their father and Liu An whispering in the hall, they hid behind a screen to eavesdrop and learned that Liu An wanted to fight back.
They painstakingly advised their father not to conspire with Liu An, but the father didn't listen to the advice, and the nine brothers had to quietly walk away overnight.
They went through many hardships and risks, climbed over the majestic Mount Wuyi, waded through the torrential waters of the Minjiang River, and finally arrived at Xianyou Beach. At this point, from autumn to winter, the maple leaves are flowing. They picked maple leaves, folded maple branches, built a pavilion on the hillside, and stayed here that night. Later, this place was called “Maple Pavilion.”
The next day, at dawn, the nine brothers left Fengting and headed north.
In the evening, they reached a hill.
Here, ancient trees are towering, bamboo groves are lush, clear streams are winding, and the sound of water murmuring.
The brothers came to the riverside, picked up the cool water, and drank a few sips. They felt that the flavor was sweet and refreshing.
Then they washed their faces with a scoop of water.
When the drops hit my eyes, I felt a little bright; after a few more washes, my eyes were all open.
I can clearly see the mountains, the landscape, the water, the flowers and the grass.
Later, this place was called “Xianshui Creek.”
In ancient times, people also built a “washing house” by the creek.
