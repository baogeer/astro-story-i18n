 The Jingpo people are an ethnic minority that can sing and dance well. Dance plays many roles in Jingpo culture, not only art. Their dance is unique, and among them, Mu Nao Zengge is the largest and most significant. There is also a beautiful legend about the origin of Zengge Mu Nao, so let's take a look at it.

Mu Nao Zong Ge is meant to dance for everyone. It is held every year on the 15th of the first month in various parts of Dehong Prefecture in Yunnan Province. At that time, people gathered in costume to Menao Zengge Square, accompanied by drums, gongs, and Western instruments. Under the leadership of Nao Shuang, wearing a bird's head and feather crown, they walked into the dance hall and danced along the ancestral migration route map guided by Mu Nao Shidong, and enjoyed dancing with joy and loud drums and music, but they saw the dance team flickering, and the flowers were rolling around. The people were delivering water and wine, roaring to the sky, and everyone danced without making a fuss.
There is a beautiful and ancient legend: in ancient times, only children of the Sun would dance “Menao Zenge.” At one point, the Sun King sent messengers to invite everything from Earth to the Sun Palace to attend their “Menau Zenge” event, and all the birds on Earth went to participate.
After the “Menau Song” ended, the birds said goodbye to the Sun King and the children of the Sun. On their way back, when they arrived to rest in the vast pristine forest of “Kang Xing Yang Yu”, they saw that the yellow fruit trees were full of ripe fruit. The birds were very happy. They held the first “Menau Song” dance party for birds on Earth to share the fruit.
“Sun Wamudu” and “Gan Zhan Kennu” heard this news and hurriedly went to watch. They were immediately enchanted by the warm and beautiful songs and dances of Hundred Birds, and were immersed in joy with them. Sun Wamudu and Gan Zhan Kennu decided to transplant “Menao Zengge” into the world, use a spacious and flat area as a venue for “Menao Zenge”, and held the first “Menao Zenge” event on the first day of the year.
On this day, people dressed up in festive attire, played wooden drums, gongs, bamboo flute, and “dongba”, gathered at the stage, lined up in a long line, imitated the bird-sparrow dance, and sang and danced to their heart's content. After people learned to dance the “Menau Song”, everyone became rich day by day, living a happy and happy life.
Later, in order to drive away evil and promote good, the people of Jingpo prayed for good fortune and good fortune in the coming year, wishing good fortune and happiness, and celebrating a good harvest and victory. They linked the “Menao Song” with their ideals and wishes, using the middle of the first month of every year as a holiday period, and passed it down from generation to generation.
With the development of the times, the Menao Song Festival has become a folk festival for the people of Jingpo to celebrate a good harvest. Known as the “Dance of Ten Thousand People”, “Menao Song” not only has a long historical tradition and broad mass character, but also focuses on the historical origin, religious beliefs, moral ideas, music, dance art, and cultural artistic characteristics of the Jingpo people. It is the best living material for studying the social history of the Jingpo people, as well as ethnology and folklore.
The Jingpo people are a nation that lives across borders. There are nearly one million Jingpo people living in northern Myanmar and northern India, and they sing songs at home and abroad. Meanwhile, Longchuan County is the region with the largest population of the Jingpo ethnic group in the country, the most complete and standardized heritage of the Menao Zengge, and the most representative region of the Jingpo ethnic group. Mu Nao Zong Ge integrates Jingpo culture, covers all aspects of social production and life, concentrates on showing the traditional culture of the Jingpo people, and is of great value to the historical and cultural research of the Jingpo people.
