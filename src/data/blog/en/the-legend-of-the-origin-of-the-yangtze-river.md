 Legend has it that tens of thousands of years ago, people still lived a life covered in leaves and ate wild fruits. Many people live together; they don't have a house; they just build a nest in a tree. At that time, I didn't know that people who cultivated the land were running east and west all day long, hunting and fishing. Later, I don't know where a giant crow flew in and fell down Mount Elephant. Its legs alone were 120 feet tall. It flew up and spread its wings, like it was overcast. Tigers, leopards, elephants, and snakes eat them all; if they can't find wild animals, they eat people. The mouth holds the individual, and as soon as the head and neck stretch, it swallows.
Since the birth of this big bird, people have been unable to live safely; they have always moved around to hide from the big bird. Big birds are getting more and more aggressive and eat people every day. Many, many more years have passed, and people have learned to drill wood to make fires. On this day, everyone was surrounded by fires burning wild animals to eat. After a while, it was overcast, big birds flew in. People were shocked and knew they would suffer again. Who knows, the big bird saw the fire. As soon as it shook its body, it flew away in a panic. People found that big birds are afraid of fire.
Now, there is a way; people are determined to drive away this big bird. As a result, many people gathered and lit torches to catch up. When the big bird saw a sea of flames, it was so scared that it flew up and ran away. People rushed quickly and got closer. The Big Bird Wings Road rushed hard, and the wind blew away the sand and stones, and the fire was extinguished. The big bird was happy for a while and ate many more people. After suffering losses, people came up with another method. They found many red things and put them on their heads. Seen from afar, they looked like fire, to scare the big birds.
The big bird flapped its wings vigorously, and the “fire” couldn't be extinguished; it was afraid, so it flew for life. People shouted and chased, from the mountains to the seaside, and from the seaside to the Central Plains. I don't know how many days they chased, exhausting the big birds. On this day, the big bird flew to present-day Qinghai. It really couldn't move. It fell to take a breath, but people caught up with it. As soon as the big bird spread its wings, it was so sore that it couldn't fly. Seeing that they were about to be caught, the big bird hurriedly laid an egg, and when the egg saw the wind, it instantly looked like a mountain, blocking people's path.
People brought axes, chisels, and hammers to chisel black eggs without stopping for three days and three nights. The bird's egg finally cracked. The big bird was angry, tired, and scared, and died as soon as it stretched out its legs. When the big bird reached its legs and died, it slapped the bird's egg. People on this side were slaughtering their lives again. Using force on both sides, they only heard an earth-shattering loud noise, and the bird egg fell apart. As soon as the big bird's egg falls apart, the egg white spills out and rolls west; then the yolk gushes out and flows east. Egg white flowing west became the Yangtze River; egg yolk flowing east became the Yellow River.
