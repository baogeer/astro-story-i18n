 A long time ago, still in ancient times, there was silence between heaven and earth. There were no birds or animals in the sky, and there were no wild animal groves on the ground. No singing during the day, no fire at night.
 
 
 
 Among thousands of mountains and valleys, there are two mountains. One is called Mount Robus, and the other is Mount Minoda.
 
 
 
 Mount Boros looks like a powerful man, while Mount Minoda looks like a beautiful young woman in a dress.
 
 
 
 One year, on May 29th in the lunar calendar, an earth-shattering thunder suddenly sounded in the sky.
 
 
 
 Following this thunder, Mount Boras and Mount Minota were split open for two days at the same time.
 
 
 
 A powerful man came out of Mount Boros, and a beautiful and gentle woman came out of Mount Minoda.
 
 
 
 Later, the two formed a happy and loving couple. The man's name was Boros, and the woman's name was Minotah. They are the first hardworking couple in heaven and earth.
 
 
 
 At that time, the mountains and rivers of the world were complicated. Every day, Robbs worked tirelessly to pick up the mountains and rearrange the mountains and rivers in the world.
 
 
 
 One day, when he got home, he said to his wife, “The sky is endless, the water has no end. I need to sort all the mountains, landscapes, and water in the world before I can go home. You have to do the housework, take care of the three children, and wait until they grow up, so that they can earn their own lives.”
 
 
 
 After speaking, I hugged every child and kissed them, so I set out to manage the rivers and mountains.
 
 
 
 Remembering her husband's advice before he left, Minodore worked hard at home to carefully raise her three daughters.
 
 
 
 She went to the fairy's place to collect all kinds of seeds and sprinkled them on the slope, growing dense woods; sprinkled them in the wasteland; and grew colorful flowers. When sprinkled on the ground, abundant grains grew.
 
 
 
 She also kneaded the animals out of dirt and placed them in six pots. Today, people call them the “Six Animals.”
 
 
 
 After 21 days, chickens, rabbits, and birds appeared in the tank.
 
 
 
 Cats and dogs appeared in the seventy day tank.
 
 
 
 Pigs and sheep appeared in the tank for 120 days.
 
 
 
 And in 270 days, cows appeared in the tank.
 
 
 
 Horses appeared in the tank for 360 days.
 
 
 
 A total of 42 kinds of creatures were nurtured in the six tanks, and the world has been bustling ever since.
 
 
 
 Time passed day by day, and Minoto's hair gradually became white, her face was wrinkled, and her back was humped.
 
 
 
 One night, Minotaur called his three daughters in front of them and said to them, “Children, now that you have grown up, it is time for you to earn a living alone.”
 
 
 
 The children listened to their mother and agreed.

Early the next morning, the eldest daughter woke up, carried a hoe, and went to the plains to cultivate fields. Later, she had children and procreate, and became the current Han people. Therefore, all Han people are hardworking, families are rich, and people are tall and strong.
After the oldest daughter left, the second daughter also woke up. She carried a load of books and went to study. This later became the Zhuang family. Therefore, everyone in the Zhuang family is good-looking, good-natured and clever, and proficient in piano, chess, calligraphy, and drawing.
There are only three daughters who slept until noon before waking up. As soon as I saw that all my possessions were gone, I cried and said to my mother, “Mom! My sisters took everything from home, what should I do for a living”
Minoto said to her little daughter, “I still have a bucket of rice at home, so take it to the mountains to sow it!”
So the three daughters brought that bucket of millet into the mountains. She cleared the wasteland and grew millet.
The millet germinated, the cat came out to plow; the millet grew, and the sheep came down; when the millet matured, the tit flew to peck. After a year of hard work, they didn't even get back the seeds they had planted.
The three daughters ran to the house and cried and said to Minodore, “Mother, the mountains are too boring. The birds and animals have eaten up all the crops. How can I make a living in the mountains?”
Minoto said, “We still have a bronze drum at home, so you can use it.”
The third daughter brought the bronze drums to the mountains. When she was bored, she was happy as soon as she knocked on the bronze drums. When wild animals hurt domestic animals, knocking on the bronze drum scares them away; when birds want to eat the crop, knock on the bronze drum, and the birds are afraid to hurt the crops.
Every New Year's holiday, prepare millet wine, and play bronze drums. Brothers and sisters from the Zhuang and Han ethnic groups come to the mountains to sing and dance together.
In the event of a natural or man-made disaster, the bronze drums were sounded. Brothers and sisters from the Han and Zhuang ethnic groups immediately came to help.
Since then, the three daughters have lived and worked peacefully in the mountains, and have become the current Yao ethnic group.
Therefore, the Yao people have lived in the mountains for generations, cultivated millet, and bronze drums became their family treasure.
