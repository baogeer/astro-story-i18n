 Legend has it that long ago, there were no acacia trees in the courtyard of Tianhou Palace.
Once, Tieguai Li and Zhang Guolao played against each other. The blazing sun shone in the face, and there was nothing to hide. Iron Guy performed a spell, poured out a seed from his fairy gourd, buried it in the ground, and carried the spell. In a blink of an eye, green shoots grew on the ground. The green shoots grew bigger and bigger, and soon grew into a large acacia tree with green leaves. The two immortals continued to play against each other in the shade of this tree.
It is said that this acacia tree hasn't changed since it grew. The old people say that as far as I can remember, it looks like this, because it was spawned by immortals, and they can't continue to grow or die; it always looks like this. A fire broke out at Tianhou Palace during the Qingdaoguang years, burning down more than 30 temple buildings overnight. Only this acacia tree survived; the mystery of it is also here.
