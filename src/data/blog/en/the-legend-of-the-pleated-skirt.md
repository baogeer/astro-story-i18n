 Our Miao women wear dresses that are small and large at the top. They have dozens of folds that can be stretched and retracted. They look like umbrellas; when folded, they look like umbrellas closed. What kind of umbrella would it look like? The old people told this story -
In the old days, our Miao family didn't have dresses. Women wore pants sewn with thick cloth. The legs were so big that almost one pair of pants could hold rice. At that time, there was a large forest where you couldn't see the edge at a glance. It was covered with green leaves all year round, like a green sea. You can't see the sun during the day or the moon at night in the forest; it's dark all year round. Footprints of jackals and old leopards are densely covered in the forest, so no hunter dared to go in and hunt.
There is a dark Chaotian Cave in the big forest. The cave has stone pots, stone bowls, stone benches, and stone beds. All the utensils at home are available. There is an eight-year-old monkey spirit living in the cave. He is the prince of the wild animals in the big forest. Its mouth is like a cave, its ears are like two small dustpans, and its four legs are as thick as difficult bars. It often comes out to snatch the good-looking girl from the stockade near the big forest, take it to the Chaotian Cave and eat it after torturing them to death. People just can't handle it!
Close to a large forest, I live in a stockade. There is an old family in the village. They have given birth to a girl named “Dianhua.” Lady Douhua looks very beautiful. Her face looks like the moon in the sky, her eyes like two clear wells, and her lips live near and far. The descendants carried bows and arrows and carried sharp steel knives into the forest. For a day or two... the old couple held hands and counted. Look at the seven or eight days that have passed, and the next students have all turned around one by one in dismay. Some of them had scars on their faces from fighting with ferocious beasts, while others went and never came back. No need to ask if they weren't looking for a girl.
The old man is so sad! They had to send someone to ask for Mi-Gong's wife, and they planned to do a full day for the girl before they came back in a month. At this point, the gate was caught rattling, followed by a loud dog barking. The old couple were surprised and hurriedly opened the door to take a look. I saw the yellow dog that followed the girl out that day standing in front of the door holding the girl's bright silver collar in its mouth. It was covered with blood all over its body, leaving marks of being scratched by a fierce beast all over its body, head, and legs. All of its yellow fur was stained red with blood. When the old couple saw the silver collar, they understood everything: the girl must have been taken by Monkey King. This is calling the yellow dog to send it back! The old two cried with sadness.
It turns out that Lady Dianhua was taken by the monkey spirit. She was picking up pigweed that day, and suddenly the sky was so dark that it looked like a hillside had fallen. The monkey grinned at her and took her to Chaotian Cave.
Lady Dianhua was tortured to the fullest in Chaotian Cave. Every day, the monkey asks her to fetch water for it to drink. Every day, it has to drink a lot of water, and the heavy stone barrel makes her unable to straighten her back. Monkey King also told her to roast a wild boar it caught in the forest every day to eat, and at night she stood by the bedside and gnawed it on its back. It had a lot of lice. If Lady Dou Hua were to slow down a little bit, Monkey Essence would beat her with a tree stick dipped in water. Every time, she was bloodshot, and she was not allowed to cry. Girl Dou Hua was tortured to the bone, and almost fell ill. She really couldn't stand this kind of torture. Every day until late at night, she secretly cries and misses her parents. One day, the yellow dog smelled her smell and arrived at Chaotian Cave. She then took off the silver collar and asked the yellow dog to take it back and send a message, so that her parents could come and save her quickly.
In the middle of the night, Lady Dianhua secretly cried while sleeping while serving the monkey. She cried and cried. Her tears rolled down in big pieces, soaked her clothes, and I don't know when she cried and fell asleep. She had a dream about a white-bearded husband. Her bearded husband felt sorry for her and said to her, “Girl, if you want to escape, go to the forest to find turpentine. Every day at noon, the monkey essence sits on a large rock outside Chaotian Cave and basks in the sun to find lice. If you put a lot of pine resin on that big rock, the monkey will sit on it and won't be able to get up. Then you can go home and be reunited with your loved ones.”
Lady Dou Hua kept in mind the words of her white-bearded husband. Every day after the monkey spirit came out, she walked out of Chaotian Cave to find turpentine, cut the pine resin drop by drop from the pine tree, and slowly gathered. This is the case every day. How many times she fainted and almost fell off a tall tree trunk, but she endured it. She kept cutting her nails. Her nails were all worn off, and her fingers were bleeding. She bit her teeth again... '· Day by day, January passed, and the Dianhua girl endured all the pain and finally gathered a lot of turpentine. She applied pine resin to the big stone, and Monkey King sat down and it stuck. It made a violent effort to earn money. It roared like thunder, and the mountain shook the ground, but it couldn't get up at all; it just sat and couldn't move. It is said that the monkey now has a bright red patch on its butt, which was when a piece of skin was glued off.
Lady Dianhua took her umbrella and escaped from Chaotian Cave. She walked and walked in the forest. She ate wild fruit when hungry, lick dew when thirsty, and slept in trees when tired to prevent attacks by old dogs and panthers. She walked in the big forest for a whole month, and after suffering, she finally walked out of the big forest. Her clothes and pants were all torn, and her muscles were exposed. Shyly, she went back to see her parents like this, and was worried about her appearance while standing by a well. She suddenly remembered the umbrella in her hand and hurriedly removed the umbrella handle to cover her lower body. As a result, she discovered that she had become so beautiful. The brightly colored umbrella looked like a Russian flower blooming in the well. It was so beautiful: she walked home happy and happy.
Lady Dianhua got together with her parents. When she got home, she showed off her flower skills and sewed a pleated skirt with cloth to look like an umbrella. The girls felt very novel when they saw it; they all imitated it with cloth. In this way, it spread ten to ten, ten to one hundred, and slowly spread all over the nearby village.
Since then, our Miao family has had dresses, which are still popular today.
