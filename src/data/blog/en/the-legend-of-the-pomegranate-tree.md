 Story ③ The legend of the pomegranate tree
Yuhuang Temple has a long history. There are many ancient trees, including boxwood and holly that are over 200 years old. The 600-year-old pomegranate tree in the backyard is even more remarkable.
As the saying goes, summer pomegranates bloom like fire, but this old pomegranate tree has white flowers and bears white fruit. In contrast to this old pomegranate tree, it is a small, strong pomegranate tree. It blooms with red flowers and bears red fruits and red seeds. They are one old and one young. They have become a scene in the Yuhuang Temple and are known as the “Wonders of Children.” Every year in June and July, pomegranate flowers bloom. Red and white, they are very enchanting and beautiful. When the pomegranate fruits are all over the branches, they greet the visitors with a smile, and there is a different view.
Speaking of this old, 600-year-old pomegranate tree, there is also a moving story: the revolutionary elders were concerned not only about the safety of the people of the motherland, but also about the survival of natural plants and trees. In 1964, someone wanted to cut down this old pomegranate tree. This coincided with Vice Chairman Dong Biwu in Yantai. After learning that someone wanted to cut down this old tree, he said with emotion, “This old pomegranate tree, which has gone through many vicissitudes, cannot be killed. Let people see how it survived generation after generation, as a witness to history.” Just like that, in Dong Biwu's words, the 600-year-old pomegranate tree has been revived. Presumably, trees are also affectionate. The old pomegranate tree repaid Dong Biwu's life-saving grace, and has since blossomed to welcome guests at home and abroad. So it's not easy for us to be able to enjoy this “Wonder of Childhood” again today.
