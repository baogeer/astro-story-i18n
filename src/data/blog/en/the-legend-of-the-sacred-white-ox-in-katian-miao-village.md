 Legend has it that in ancient times, a white fairy cow caused trouble and violated the rules of the Immortal Order. Emperor Jade punished everyone to cultivate 100,000 fields in the desolate border city of Xianggui. On a sunny day, fairy cows plow in 100,000 ancient fields. The weather was hot, their mouths were dry, and they were hungry, so they ran out of the old fields to find food and water to drink. It took Li Hua out of the mountain path for tens of miles, along the way to the upper reaches of the Wushui River. It was discovered by mountain people who were cultivating fields. They liked it very much. They wanted to keep it, so they let go of their efforts and set up a card to stop the cow, with the intention of leaving it behind. However, the card didn't get stuck even when it went, so I let it escape to the bottom of the river. This place was later called “Kada.” The fairy cow continued to run down the river and arrived at a long, narrow Daping. A group of villagers who were cultivating in Daping discovered the fairy cow and were also in love with it. They came up and held it together, but they were unable to stop it either left or right, front and back; they only snatched a few white hairs on the fairy cow's body. Later, people called this large pond “White Mao Ping.” The fairy cow struggled to turn over Baitouao Mountain, and escaped into a large large flat — a barricade.
Some old people also said that in ancient times, Bainiutuo (Qilong Village, Bumaoping Town, present-day city) walked out of a big white cow, entered Qingxiping, which was rich in aquatic plants, and flowed up along the Qingxi River. It walked to Nanping. Someone stopped it at the front, but they didn't stop it. Later, this place was called the “bullpen.” Bai Xian Niu turned around and came to Tang Jiachong. I met a girl grinding a pot of rice milk and a burst of fragrance dripping out. Bai Xian beef was unbearably hungry and thirsty. Taking the opportunity of her daughter turning around, she ate the sweet pot of rice milk. The only thing left was the syrup pot on the ground. Later, people called this place a “pulp pit.” After eating and drinking, the White Fairy Cow was full of energy. In one go, they went over Hawthorn Pond and rushed towards the mountain on the south side. On the way downhill, I bumped into a village girl fetching water. The white fairy cow was afraid of being held by the girl and hit it hard. Unexpectedly, it hit the shackles on a flat load and pulled off a few hairs. Later, this mountain run was named “Ushige Chong.” The White Fairy Cow hurriedly ran up the mountain and rushed to the Wushui River. People intercepted it in the upper and lower reaches of the river. They couldn't bear to let this cute white fairy cow slip away, but they couldn't keep it. Later, the upper and lower reaches of the Wushui River were called “stopping cows up” and “stopping cows down.” Frightened all day, the White Fairy cow rushed to choose a path. Unaware, he escaped into a sparsely populated mountain chong, and inadvertently fell into a muddy field with deep mud feet and got stuck. Later, this mountain run was called “Kada”...
Because of this beautiful legend, since Tang and Song, the government has named “Bai Maoping” and “Cow Stall” together as “Cow Stop” as a township-level administrative agency for ethnic minorities. After the founding of New China, the county government named “Lanrong Township” according to its homonym, and then set up Baimaoping Township separately.
