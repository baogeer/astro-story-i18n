 Are scaly peony synonymous with dead peony? Compared to ordinary peonies, what are the characteristics of varieties? This is a question often asked by flower farmers. Ancient writers and artists have rated peonies extremely highly, believing that “they are not only beautiful, but strong bones are superior to Wan Hui.” Ouyang Xiu also said in “Peony Foreword,” “The world is a real flower, only peony ear.” The book notes, “So-called truth is also true, the opposite of evil.” Song people praised the inner beauty of peonies, that is, their intense spirit. Liao Cheng Guan's new drama from Guangdong Province includes the story of the Scorched Peony because of his poem “Sentimental”. At the end of the poem, it says, “If you give up your life, you can't give up on flowers. Deep in love with the homeland, and a high level of patriotism.” I also wrote “Praise for the Scorched Peony”: “Patriotism and Love Peonies, the King of Flowers with a Thousand-Year Tradition. Dare to go against the Holy Will and will fight for his life; Ning Hua is desperate and refuses to spit. Dryness was a source of strength, and compassion was exchanged for children and grandchildren. Luoyang Ancient Garden portrays fairies, and it is the most fragrant song in the world.” The boned peony is regarded as a symbol of national spirit, and the impression left in the hearts of the public is very deep.
There has been a lot of talk about dead peonies in the press in recent years, and there are different legends about them. When Yancheng built the “Dead Branch Peony Garden” in Bancang, some people based it on “Mirror Flower Garden.” According to the story in Part 5, the peonies couldn't stand Wu Zetian's fire; when they all bloomed, Wu Zetian removed the fire. (Bian) Barn's dead peony. This variant is Wu Zetian's legacy of Gantang. The author of “Jinghuayuan” was Li Ruzhen (1765-1830) in the Qing dynasty. She was poor in her later years and sent to write “Mirror Flower Garden”. In his book, he complained about women, rewrote the legend of Peony, and affirmed Wu Zetian. This pair of ancient writers said that there is nothing wrong with it. However, there are stories that are completely fictional and unfounded. Hundreds of years before “Mirror Flower Garden,” there is a historical record of peonies rather than legends. Biancang Peony Garden was originally the Bian Family Ancestral Temple. The founder Bian Jizhi brought back two red and white peonies from Luoyang and planted them in the temple. According to “The Genealogy of the Bian Family,” he “planted flowers and took the red one to show his loyalty to serve the country; he took the white one to show that he was an official and clean.” His descendant, Bian Yuanheng, joined Zhang Shicheng's rebellion and opposed the alien oppression of the Yuan dynasty. When he was in the military, he used peony dried as a horse whip and encouraged himself with peony's bitter spirit. Later, Zhang Shicheng established himself as King Cheng and named his country Zhou. Legend has it that Bian Yuanheng came back from the army and still planted the horse-whip in the garden, but revived and blossomed again. Later, the ancestors of the Ming dynasty destroyed Yuan, and Zhang Shicheng also destroyed. Bian Yuanheng was resigned to the Liaodong Army. The peonies in the garden have not bloomed since then. After being pardoned for ten years, the peonies have bloomed again. Bian Yuanheng's peony poem has been passed down in society. Here's one: “The pine chrysanthemums in the thatched cottage died out late, and there are still old peonies from Nanyuan. “Jiao Zhi made the most of his life; he only loved his deceased.” All of the above situations were before Li Ruzhen wrote “Mirror Flower Garden”. Biancang Peony Garden was already over 700 years old, so it seems that dead peonies have nothing to do with “Mirror Flower Garden.” The park has now been expanded and has become one of the scenic spots in northern Jiangsu.
There is an ancient legend about the scorched peony and the dead peony. Legends are not historical facts, but the role of traditional flower culture in building spiritual civilization cannot be ignored. Burnt bones and dead branches were originally portrayed by writers using a characteristic of peonies; they are not a special type of peony. In floral terms, there is a saying “peony grows one foot and shrinks eight inches”, which means that every year, 20% of peonies grow new branches. 20% lignify, and 80% of unlignified branches wither like grass in winter. In fact, due to the different growing environments, there are few lignified parts. The legendary scorch bone and dead branches all refer to this phenomenon. None of them are the names of peony varieties, so there are no varietal characteristics or characteristics to speak of. To be precise, they are all literary images of peonies, and bone-chested peonies are written as heroic images of rebelling against tyranny. However, the dead peony in “Jinghuayuan” is written as a despicable image of surrender. Of course, this is two different things from the literary evaluation of “Jinghuayuan” and the historical evaluation of Wu Zetian.
