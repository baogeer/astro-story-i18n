Sure enough, it wasn't long before the ancestral tomb, surnamed Liao, was buried, and Lin Banxian's eyes gradually became blind.

Lin Banxian said, “It doesn't matter! I'll just lock up the dragon!”
There is no doubt that he is the surname Liao, so he followed Lin Banxian's instructions and chose a date to break ground and prepare for the renovation.

The surname Liao is strange, but I'm just asking how to deal with it?
