 The character model of the Seven Fairies is Zhang Qijie from Sendai, Xiafeng Village, Wuzhi County. She pursues marriage autonomy, breaks through heavy resistance, and has a self-formed marriage with Dong Yong, the great son of Xiaodong Village, under the daacai tree in Dafan Village in Wuzhi County. This true love story has received unanimous praise from future generations, and many beautiful and moving myths and legends have been interpreted as a result.

As a result, a “prize ditch” with water flowing all year round appeared eight miles north of Xiaodong Village in Dong Yong's hometown, and a “Qin River” flowing for thousands of miles appeared on the south side of Xiaodong Village.
The Shanggou River and the Qin River have been flowing for 2,000 years, singing songs day and night, and constantly telling people such beautiful and beautiful myths and legends.
Mooncakes and Reunion Festival
The Seven Fairies were forced back to Tiangong, leaving Dong Yong and her son alone to live a difficult life. When my son Dong Zhuoer was 7 years old, it was the Mid-Autumn Festival. The children on the street threw torches, played dragon lanterns, ran around, and concentrated on having fun. The more he thought about it, the saddened, the more he cried, the saddened. Wu Gang from the Heavenly Court heard it. He was very sympathetic to Zhuo Er's experience, so he went back to heaven to meet the Seven Fairies. When the mother and son met, they were extremely affectionate. There were endless heartfelt words, and endless tears of love. The mother of the Seven Fairies was full of love, so she made a bunch of delicious things for her son to enjoy. She also mixed some fairy fruits such as walnuts, raisins, peanuts, and red date paste with osmanthus honey to make round sweet cakes, ready for Zhuo Er to take home. When Emperor Jade discovered it, Long Yan was furious and punished Wu Gang for not stopping to cut down the osmanthus tree, which could never be cut down, and drove Zhuo Er back to the world. Picking up round cakes, I will never forget my mother for a long time. Later, every year during the Mid-Autumn Festival, Zhuo Er had to hold her own round cake, face the bright moon, and look forward to a reunion with her mother. People in the public saw this and followed suit. Every family served round cakes to celebrate the family's reunion. Because the round cake is shaped like a bright moon, people have changed their name to mooncake, and the day of August 15 is called Reunion Day.
Nine Treasures and a Treasure Chest

There were so many times, and seven bright paths were left on the embankment slope. The larger road in the middle was taken by the Queen Mother.
