 Once upon a time, we had a family with only two people, a father and daughter. The daughter is not only beautiful, but also very smart.
On this day, the father had something to go far away, leaving behind his daughter and a white horse. This horse is very strong. It runs for thousands of miles a day and is as fast as the wind. What's even more peculiar is that this horse knows human nature very well; it knows people's words. Everyone says it's a “magic horse.”
When the father went out, he told his daughter to carefully feed and care for this horse, and he would be back soon. After the father left, the only thing left in the family was this horse to accompany his daughter.
Whenever she feels alone, she talks to the horse. Although the horse is speechless, it nods and twists its tail, showing a very affectionate appearance.
The days went by, but my father never came back. The daughter misses her father very much and is even more worried about what will happen to her father when he is outside.
One day, half seriously and half jokingly, she said to Ma Er, “Ma, do you understand what I'm saying? If you can get my dad back, I'll marry you as my wife.”
As soon as the girl's voice had come to an end, the horse got out of control and disappeared as soon as there was a smoke.
It turns out that my father got sick far away, and he was worried about how to go back, but suddenly he discovered that his horse was running away, and I was very pleasantly surprised. My father was anxious about women and couldn't think much about anything, so he stepped on the back of a horse and rode to the house.
After arriving home, the father and daughter, who had been together for a long time, were naturally very happy. The father felt that this horse was a great source of credit, so he specially added herbs and fed it carefully. What surprised the father, however, was that there were so many fine herbs, yet the horse wouldn't eat a single bite.
Every time I see my daughter come and go, the horse not only makes a long chirp, but it also jumps and makes sounds of joy or anger. The father secretly asked his daughter what was going on. As a result, the daughter told her father what she had said to horses.
After hearing it, the father felt very uneasy. After meditating for a while, he whispered to his daughter and said, “Remember, never exaggerate this matter. If people knew I married my daughter to a horse, what would that be like? Don't go out of the house for a while, and don't go to the horse.”
The next day, the father was around the stable, loaded with crossbows and shot the horse to death while it was defenseless. Afterwards, he peeled off the horse skin and hung it on a large rock in the yard.
One day, my daughter was playing in the yard with her neighbor's girlfriend. She was very uneasy when she saw horse skin on stones, especially when she thought of the days when her father left his hometown... She felt very sad.
Thinking about it, she reached over and caressed the horse's mane, and then the accident happened. The horse skin suddenly lifted and swept her away like a gust of wind. The female companions on the sidelines were terrified and hurriedly went to tell her father.
When the father arrived, there was no trace of the daughter and the horse skin on the stone, and the whereabouts were unknown.
Turns out, Shenma Skin swept away the lovely girl and flew all the way southwest. In the far southwest, there is a place called Dachu. It is an uninhabited wilderness, full of mulberry trees all over the mountain.
However, after getting there, the girl in horse clothes lost her original form and became a silkworm in the shape of a horse's head climbing on a tree. She doesn't eat anything else; she only eats mulberry leaves. Later, she became the owner of this mulberry forest, and Emperor made her a silkworm god.
Faced with this experience, the girl had unspeakable suffering. Day and night, she missed her hometown and her father and girlfriend. Every now and then, she will keep spitting filaments out of her mouth, holding her back for a long time.
Every spring, I can see a beautiful young woman kneeling on a branch of a mulberry tree and constantly spitting out white and bright filaments in her mouth. People affectionately call her the Silkworm Girl.
