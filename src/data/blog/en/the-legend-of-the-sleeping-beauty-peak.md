I thought about it day and night, washed my face with tears all day long, and my thoughts passed away. A few years later, her husband was promoted to the rank of general due to his bravery in combat and repeated military exploits. What I saw when I came back was a sleeping beauty who turned into a mountain beacon after her death. The general was sad and desperate. He spent the whole day waiting for his lovely wife.
