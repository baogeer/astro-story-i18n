 Shehong County in Sichuan is Taihe Town, known as one of the four famous towns in central Sichuan. On the east bank of the Fujiang River, about 8 miles north of Taihe Town, there is a place called Mukongba. The scenery on the dam is beautiful, the air is fresh, and there is an endless flow of visitors. The Fujiang River under the dam is full of smoke, and the waterfowl gather. The blue waves are rippling, and the beauty is overwhelming. Many snails have settled here, so it is known as Luo Lake. This section of the Fujiang River is shaped like a tai chi, surrounded by a jade belt, encircling Mukongba as a peninsula. Locals call it the Luohu Peninsula. There is a place on the island called Miaozigou. According to an elderly local person, in the early years, this place had temples stretching over a mile from Mizoguchi to the ditch. The pavilions overlapped, and incense flourished. Later, due to the changing times, there were many fires, and most of the temples were destroyed. There is only one back temple left on the hill at the end of the ditch. The name of this temple was Cheng Quan Temple, which was later changed to “Chengquan Temple”. Jozenji Temple has now been renovated. The temple has a Guanyin Hall and a Snail Temple, which have images of Master Kannon and Snail Girl respectively. The temple also has a 15 story pavilion. If you take a look at the pavilion, you can enjoy a panoramic view of both sides of the Fujiang River, making people feel refreshed and relieved of their worries. Hence the name Forgotten Worry Pavilion. Here, Buddha can show strength, water can exercise, trees can sense, energy nourish the mind, herbs can cure diseases, flowers can make you happy... It is a Feng Shui mecca for home health. An ancient yellow jew tree in front of the temple bears witness to the history of Mukongba.
Why did Joquan Temple change to “Seizen-ji Temple”? Why do temples worship Master Kannon and Snail Girl? Why is the five-story high-rise pavilion also named “Forgotten Worry Hall”? What story did the snail people in Luohu play out? The water of the Fujiang River, which runs continuously under Mukongba all year round, tells people about an ancient legend that occurred on the Luohu Peninsula.
Woman with a Kind Heart, a Stone Woman, and a Snail Guard
Many years ago, there was a hardworking and able-bodied young man on the banks of the Fujiang River. People didn't know his name; they only knew he could use stone tools, so they all called him Shi Wa. Shi Wa's father died when he was very young. He was the only one in the family who lived with a blind woman. Since blind women can't work outside, life at home is hectic. Although Shi Wa knows the art of stonemason, since people around him don't have enough time and there are very few people who build houses, he can't get much work. He has to go to the Fujiang River every three to five minutes to catch some fish and shrimp, and sell them to subsidize household expenses. In July of this year, the Fujiang River was flooded. Shi Wa hadn't been fishing for a few days. Seeing that her family was about to finish her food rations, she had no money to buy them. Both of them were distraught and didn't know what to do.
On this day, he went fishing in Luo Lake again. He has a rule for fishing. If he can't fish three times in a row, he takes the net and goes home, and never cast a fourth net. This time, he threw two nets in a row; let alone fish, he didn't hit a single shrimp. He was a little anxious. He wanted to throw another net and not be able to catch fish. Today was for nothing. So he prayed to heaven: “Heaven is above; bless me with a big fish in this net. If I can't catch fish in this net, I don't have money to buy rice, and my family only has to lift the pot and knock on the clock. I really wouldn't be able to live this day.” After saying this, he squinted and cast a third net. After a while, he began to shut down the internet. As he prayed, he slowly pulled the net up. Pull up this net and take a look. There are still no fish in the net, but there is a golden snail.
Shi Wa gently picked up this golden snail. She thought it was so cute, and she even couldn't help but love it. At this point, Zhang Gui, the first son with a fortune of one million, walked over and saw this golden snail, and they all really liked it. Zhang Gui immediately offered 5 yuan of silver to buy this golden snail for fun. Shi Wa is a bit tempted. She thinks that she won't be able to earn 5 yuan even if she works on her own for a month. If she had 5 yuan of silver, not only would the mother and son be able to eat enough for a few days, but even the blind woman would have money to heal her eyes. He held the golden snails in his hands, but was a bit hesitant. At this point, a strange thing happened. I saw the snail sticking its head out of its shell and pointing at Shiwa a few times, as if it were begging for something. Shi Wa felt that snails were a bit pitiful. Jiang Gui only bought them for fun. If she tossed snails to death, wouldn't it be a pity. He finally shook his head and confiscated Zhang Gui's silver.
Good luck rebuts the poor
Shi Wa brought the golden snails home and explained the situation to her mother. The mother touched and touched the golden snail, and loved it so much that she even boasted that her son had done the right thing. Shi Wa kept the golden snails in one of her own stone water tanks and went outside to get a living.
Unfortunately, Shi Wa hasn't taken care of a job for a long time. She doesn't have any money to take home. Both of her daughters lack food and clothing, and often end up sad after eating. The blind woman became ill and had no money to hire a doctor. She soon passed away, leaving Shi Wa alone in her family.
Shi Wa leaves early and returns late every day. She is busy and busy. She has had a very hard time, and her life is nothing to be desired. Some people advised him to marry a daughter-in-law. Every day he comes home, he also eats a bowl of hot rice and drinks hot soup. But he thought that his life was so difficult, how could he make others suffer from poverty. So he didn't even think about marrying his daughter-in-law.
One day, Shi Wa went out and came home, and when she pushed the door open, she suddenly smelled the scent of the meal. He glared his eyes at the table, only to see that the table was full of cooked dishes. Walking further to the stove, I felt that the stove was steaming hot. Open the pot and take a look. Fragrant dried white rice was steamed in the pot. He didn't know what was going on; he was afraid to eat it. But after a while, when he was hungry, he really couldn't help but be tempted to sit down and eat. I was so hungry, and I had never eaten such a delicious meal before, so soon it was all over the clouds and I ate the whole table clean.
After eating, he thought that there was no one else in the family. It must have been my neighbor, Sister Zhang, who saw that I hadn't had time to cook for a day to help. I had to go and thank them. He then went to Sister Zhang's house, met Sister Zhang, and thanked her for helping to cook. Who knows, Sister Zhang actually said, “I didn't help you cook? I just heard someone walking around in your house and the sound of cooking in your stove; I thought it was a guest in your house.”
Shi Wa was dumbfounded when she heard it. Who has such a good heart? He thought about it over and over again for a while, and felt that he wouldn't be able to ask for a result for a while, so he stopped worrying about it. Who knows, when I came back at noon the next day, just like the day before, someone had prepared the meal a long time ago. He was also too lazy to ask Zhang Erzao any more. Anyway, he asked in vain, no matter if he ate 3721.
After a few days like this, Shi Wa still couldn't help it. What the hell is going on, I have to figure it out. One day, he pretended to go back to work as usual. Shortly after he left, he turned around and walked back, but he didn't go inside and quietly hid outside the window to watch the movement. It was almost noon, and something unexpected happened. I saw a beautiful girl suddenly come out of the water tank, wash the dishes, cook, and fry the food with her hands and feet. After doing this, I drilled it back into the water tank. Shi Wa immediately opened the door and entered the house and quickly walked to the side of the water tank to take a look. There is only one golden snail in the water tank, so how many girls! After observing for a few days, this was the case every day, and he immediately understood something in his heart.
One day, Shi Wa broke into the house while the girl was cooking again. Before the girl had recovered, she rushed to the side of the water tank, grabbed the snail shell from the water tank, and locked it in the box. The girl couldn't leave, so she had to bow shyly and say, “You can't have a single woman in your house. If you don't dislike it, I'll be your wife.”
As soon as Shi Wa heard this, she couldn't help but be overjoyed. He couldn't believe it was real and asked the girl over and over again what was going on. The girl had no choice but to talk about her history. It turns out that she is a snail extract from the nearby Fujiang River. She has been practicing for 500 years, so she can change her character. Jiang Zhong also has an old snail essence. This is her father. He has been practicing for 1,000 years and has a lot of mana. The old snail master controls her very strictly, and she is not allowed to walk around freely during normal times. On that day, she took advantage of the old snail essence and went to the riverside to play. Somehow, she was taken by Shi Wa as a fish and shrimp net...
After talking about this, the girl asked Shi Wa, “Now you know my origin. If you have any concerns, give me back the snail shell and I'll leave right away.”
Shi Wa couldn't bear to let her go and said, “I don't care if you're a fairy; I just want you to be my wife.”
After listening, the girl was moved to tears and said, “I've seen for a long time that you are a good-hearted person. Your family needs money so much, but Zhang Gui paid $5 or two to buy me, but you didn't do it. If you had sold me that day and had money, maybe your mother wouldn't die. I owe it to you, so I'm here to help you and be your wife.”
Since then, Shi Wa has been a couple with the snail girl. The two have lived a loving life. Unfortunately, good things have come and gone, and some people won't let them live a peaceful life.
