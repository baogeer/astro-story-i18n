 People in Taiqinggong Town, Luyi County know that there is an unearthed stone box in Xiaowuzhuang, not far west of the Taiqing Palace. There are ten large characters engraved on it: “If you want to open the stone box, you have to come to Tang Wang.” These ten words lead to a piece of historical legend.
Li Yuan of the Tang dynasty followed Li Laojun as his ancestor. Later, when Li Yuan's son Li Shimin became emperor, he deliberately rushed from Chang'an in Beijing to worship his ancestors and burn incense.
Li Shimin came to Laozi Temple, and Lao Dao Gu sent a 17-year-old Xiao Dao Gu to serve the marquess. This little girl wears a Dao robe and has a white, white, tender face, and black silk hair. She needs to be very well-groomed. Little Dao Gu brought Li Shimin a pot of face wash, put a hand towel on the basin, and Li Shimin washed her hands and secretly said, “This little girl looks so good, how can she stop being at home? Don't look at them as if they are from the family; look, there aren't any women in the Sannomiya Rokuin.” While thinking, he cut his fingernails, and hit his finger to drop a drop of water on the little girl's face. Miss Xiaodao blushed, pouted her mouth, and walked away.
This little girl is an intelligent woman. When she returned to her house, the more she thought about it, the more she felt that Tang Wang cared about her. Three years later, I got up from bed in the middle of the night and quietly walked to the living room where Li Shimin was staying, and “knelt” in front of Li Shimin's bed: “Long live, the little girl asked for a seal.” Li Shimin asked her why she was asking for a blockade. The old lady recounted the incident about his explosions and what he thought in his heart after returning home. Li Shimin pretended to say, “Being widowed is really unintentional. You are out of your family; you should not have this kind of thinking.”
The little girl was ashamed and hated. She turned around and left. She went back to her house, and the more she thought about it, the more sad she was, so she used a three-foot white lining to hang herself up.
Furthermore, as soon as Li Shimin closed his eyes and fell asleep in the living room, he saw an old man with a white beard coming to his bedside and pointing at him, “I am Laozi, what kind of people! This is your fault. My aunt didn't mean it. It's clear that you fell in love with her at first sight and smashed her on the face. Instead, they should not be blocked. The two of you had a relationship in a past life, should have become a family today, and you should not look down on people with low status because of who you are.” When Lee Se-min woke up and thought about it, he knew it was a dream. He is very sorry about what he just said. He was about to send someone to call Gu Xiao Dao. He heard that Gu Xiao Dao had already been hanged.
Li Shimin thought that he had despised Xiaodao to death. His heart was broken and he regretted it a lot. In order to redeem his faults, he sealed the palace after Xiaodaogu died and buried Xiaodaogu Jin Dingyu in a large stone box. Ten large characters were engraved on the cover of the stone box: “If you want to open the stone box, you have to come from Tang Wang.” Currently, this stone box is still placed next to the Taiqing Palace.
