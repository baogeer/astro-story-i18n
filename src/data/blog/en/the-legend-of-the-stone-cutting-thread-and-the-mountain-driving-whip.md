 It is said that when the Great Wall was first built, even though millions of migrant workers were involved, it was not possible to repair the entire length of Longgou in a day. The emperor was in a hurry. He ordered a limit on the amount; if he didn't reach it, he would kill himself. The supervisory officials were frightened and forced the migrant workers to work day and night. If they couldn't finish the amount, they wouldn't let them go to the construction site, and they wouldn't give them food. Migrant workers are hungry, and they die every day. Stonemasons, in particular, hit the stone with a sledgehammer and then break it into small stones. No one can do much in a day. The supply is always in short supply; the less supply, the more workers are forced to do it. Therefore, they are always unable to eat or drink, and it doesn't take long for most of them to die. The more people die, the less they can do their job; this is really useless. Master Guan Mason was sad. He thought: I can't do my job; I don't know when my head will fall!
On this night, the head stonemason walked around the house and was so sad that he couldn't sleep. Suddenly, the door of the house “creaked”, and an old lady came in, holding a large red thread in her hand, and handed it to the head of the mason. After a few words of command, they disappeared in an instant. Master Guan Mason was first surprised, then delighted. He clasped the big red line tightly and stared at it. He didn't let go all night, and didn't see eye to eye.
The next day, he arrived at the construction site, sent this red line, one for each person, to the working stonemasons, and learned what the old lady said again with the stonemasons. hey! True enough. A mason holds a red thread. It takes as many large stones, or large stones, to drive from. As soon as you pull the red wire, the big stone “hits” the ground and it breaks. There is no need to throw a sledgehammer anymore. It's much less laborious, and it's much faster. The craftsmen in Forbidden City want how big, how many pieces; how many, how many. In this way, the whole process is much faster. Migrant workers are able to work and eat on time; almost none have died from exhaustion or hunger. This difficult hurdle was broken, but it didn't take long for another difficult situation. That is, some mountains have usable stones, but some mountains are full of wind fossils or crushed sand. They cannot be used; they have to be transported here from other mountains. This is also very expensive, and the supply is in short supply. The master mason is sad again, and he is in danger of losing his head again.
One night, the old lady gave Chief Stone Master another dream. The master stonemason woke up and was extremely happy. The next day, he came to the construction site and ordered that the red line in the mason's hand be taken back in half, then put the red wire together and twisted into a big whip. The mason's head waved a whip and drank. Strangely enough, the stones in the mountains all ran with the sound of the whip. If there are no usable stones in this mountain, go to other mountains to catch useful stones. Stonemasons rush for stones during the day. People don't look like stones; they are a flock of white sheep. Actually, it was a stone; they used the power of that whip to run after them. One day, a rich man saw a man chasing a large herd of sheep and passing through his field. He didn't agree; he had to leave him a few. The sheep catcher had no choice but to agree. As a result, he was left with a big rock. Since then, legends of stone cutting lines (later called stone cutting swords) and mountain whips have spread among the people.
