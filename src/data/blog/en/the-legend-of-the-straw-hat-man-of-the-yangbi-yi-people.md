Legend has it that a long, long time ago, there was a beautiful woman who was kind and gentle on the banks of the Yangtze River in Yunnan. At noon one day during the planting season, she was standing in the water washing her long dark, shiny hair. Suddenly she was hit by something. She immediately felt a little strange. She quickly stood up, pulled the loose hair off her face, and looked around, but there was nothing.
A loud voice echoed through the valley: I am a dragon, and we are related...
Her heart was beating wildly, and she didn't know if it was a blessing or a curse.
Later, she gave birth to nine dragons.
She named dragons from trees in the landscape or objects around her. One of them was a dragon. She really couldn't think of a name for him. She looked up and saw a straw hat hanging on the wall and thought to herself, so let's call him “Straw Hat.”
When Straw Hat grew up, like the dragons, they all became tribal leaders.
Straw Hat calls his tribe the Straw Hat Tribe.
Straw Hat is a thoughtful person. He wants to depict scenes of the tribe's production and life for future generations to see.
The people of the ethnic group are united, make concerted efforts, and use their talents; they must do this thing well.
Some people used branches to draw pictures on the ground and sand, but as soon as they were washed away by the rain, they were gone.
The Straw Hat Man said that we can paint pictures on stones, so we won't be afraid of being washed away by rain.
However, they don't have big enough stones to paint here. What should I do? The people of the tribe are silent,
The days passed day by day, and the people of the tribe discovered that the Straw Hat Man had disappeared; no one knows exactly what day he disappeared.
A huge stone suddenly appeared on the mountain. Seen from afar, it looked like their Straw Hat Man.
The people understood that the Straw Hat Man sacrificed his body and turned it into a giant stone for the people to record the life of the tribe.
The tribe tearfully painted the scenes of the animals, hunting, planting, harvesting, celebrating, and sacrificing on the boulders. The images were realistic and vivid.
The leaders of other tribes also imitated painting their own tribes on stones. However, it didn't take long for the color to fade, and there was nothing on the stone.
The leaders paid a high price in exchange for paint from the Straw Hat Tribe. Unexpectedly, after a while, there was still not a trace left on the stone.
The leaders believed that the Straw Hat Tribe had falsified it, so they used the same kind of paint, randomly applied it on the straw hat stone, and carefully painted it on their own stone.
After a few years, the color on their stone completely faded, and the paint that was randomly applied to the straw hat stone was very bright, as if it had just been applied.
The leaders think it's probably okay to just apply it at will.
As a result, they were randomly applied to each stone, and some animal and plant patterns were carefully drawn on the straw hat stone.
A few years have passed, and the situation is still the same. What is the reason?
Thinking left and right, I finally thought that the problem was with the stone.
As a result, they took a close look at Straw Hat Stone and went to great lengths to find the exact same stone as Straw Hat Stone.
They finally breathed a sigh of relief after repainting.
Unexpectedly, after a few years, there was no color at all.
The leaders really couldn't figure out the reason, so they had to stop doing it.
The Straw Hat Cliff painting stands proudly on the banks of the Yangliang River after being destroyed by wind and rain, lightning and lightning, and encroaches over time. It stands fresh for a long time and never disappears.
The Straw Hat Man, the descendant of the dragon, uses his unyielding backbone to carry the tribe's past.
How can a picture scrolls carried by flesh and blood fade?
People who have seen the Yangyi Cliff Paintings know that standing in the distance and looking at the cliffs is like seeing a person wearing a straw hat waving to us.
