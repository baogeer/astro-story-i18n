 Under the beautiful Mount Xianyu, there is a village called Oyama Village. Legend has it that the “fairy descends” once appeared here. Is this true? It's true or not true. Why say that, because the one below is not the Seven Fairies, but the legendary Tea Lady.
There is a young man named Awang in Oyama Village. He is hardworking, capable, and helpful. They make a living by hunting snakes.
On this day, Awang went up the mountain to catch snakes. While passing through a forest, he suddenly heard a sharp cry: “Help!” Awang hurriedly flew past. As soon as I saw it, I immediately breathed a sigh of air. I saw four or five brightly colored vipers haunting a girl. Nobuko stuck out to her elder, and it seemed like she was about to approach the girl. Awang is an expert snake catcher. He ran to the girl, pinched a seven-inch venomous snake, then threw it in a cloth bag behind him. After a few seconds of hard work, all the snakes became Awan's loot.
As soon as the girl saw that all the vipers had been caught, she patted her chest with her hands and took a breath. Ah Wang only had time to look at that girl. She only saw a bright red flower in her hair, a small green jacket with a perfect fit on her upper body, and green pants on her lower body. Awang felt that his eyes were fresh. Intuitively, he felt that this girl was not a local. However, the girl looks very smart. When Awang grew up, she had never seen such a beautiful woman. He stammered, “What's your name? Where to stay?” The girl smiled and said, “My name is Wu Liqing. I live on the side of Xianyu Mountain.” Awang wanted to ask a few more questions. The tea lady said, “It's almost dark. I should go back. I'll see you tomorrow.” After speaking, I turned around and ran away.
The next day, Awang went up the mountain to catch snakes again. As soon as he was halfway there, he saw Wu Liqing running in a panic with a bag. As soon as she saw Awang, she said, “Awango, save me! My parents gave me a promise to the villagers; I sneaked out. They're chasing after them.” Awang hurriedly dragged Wu Liqing back home.
Wu Liqing stayed at Awang's house for a while. After a long period of time, the two developed a relationship and it was as good as glue. When Niang Ah Wang saw that Wu Liqing was beautiful and sensible, and that she was in love with her son, she chose a good day and became a family for them. Wu Liqing treated people well and was very hardworking. Neighbors all boasted that Awang had married a good daughter-in-law.
Half a year passed in a blink of an eye. On this day, Awang went out to deliver goods. On the way, he met an old Taoist wearing a black hat and a black robe. As soon as this Taoist saw Awang, he kept looking up and down. Awang glared at Lao Dao in disgust: “Look at what you see, what's beautiful.” That person, Yin and Yang, said in a strange manner, “Young man, you are dark in the hall and your eyebrows are frowned upon; I'm afraid you are being haunted by demons and ghosts! This is all your daughter-in-law's fault; you should kick her out quickly.” Ah Wang angrily said, “Nonsense. My daughter-in-law is very kind. If you chew your tongue any more, I'm not being nice to you!”
When I finished speaking, I ignored that Taoist and left on his own. The Taoist chased behind his back: “Young man, if you don't listen to the poor, something will happen to your village, but it will be too late.”
After two days, the plague began to spread in the village, and even Awan's mother was infected. Ah Wang's mom extended a thin hand and dragged Awan's hand and said, “Boy, mom, this disease doesn't seem to get better... Everyone in the village said that this disease was brought by your daughter-in-law...”
“Huh?” Ah Wang was stunned. When I thought about it carefully, Wu Liqing's origin was indeed a bit unknown. Since passing through the door, she hasn't even mentioned going back to her mother's house. Is she really a monster? No good! Wu Liqing must be driven away. He must not lose the lives of the whole village because of her. He stood up, turned his head and walked outside, not even hearing his mother's cry.
He walked to the hospital in a hurry. Seeing that Wu Liqing was busy in the hospital, he didn't feel like saying, “Wu Liqing, get out of this house soon!” Wu Liqing was taken aback and cried with sadness: “Awango, what did I do wrong?” As soon as Wu Liqing cried, Ah Wang's heart softened. He told Wu Liqing about his encounter with Hailao Dao and all the rumors his mother had heard.
Wu Liqing smiled bitterly and said, “That black old road is a fairy; it was transformed into a thousand year old poisonous snake.”
“Ah!” Awang couldn't help but scream. Wu Liqing went on to say, “The people in Tunli got the plague. It was a ghost carried out by the Black Lord. They wanted to take over me as my wife in the mountains and forests. If I refused, they sent their disciples and grandchildren to hurt me. Thankfully, you saved me that day. Now, it also spits venom into the Shishui River, poisoning everyone while drinking water, and spreading rumors that I'm a monster.”
Only then did Awang understand the cause of everyone's poisoning, but he was worried: “Everyone in the village is sick, but what should I do?” Wu Liqing pulled the dazzling red flower from her head and said, “This flower contains 100 thousand-year-old tea seeds. It is boiled into soup. People who are sick will be fine as soon as they drink it. But...” Before Wu Liqing finished speaking, Awang took a handful of tea seeds and said, “I can't imagine there are any treasures this good. I'll treat my mom first.” When you're done, turn around and run. Wu Liqing hurriedly shouted at the back: “Awang Ge, listen to me when I'm done...” But Awang is desperate to save people and has long since gone missing.
Awang ran to the kitchen and immediately set the fire to cook soup. The thousand-year-old tea seed fruit was amazing. After drinking only half a bowl, my mom “wow” threw up a big beach of sticky water, and her face became rosy. Awang was very happy. Couldn't talk to her mom, so she went door to door to deliver tea to everyone. Everyone in the village drank the tea and it was fine. Ah Wang jumped back home, only to see Wu Liqing lying unconscious on the ground, and his face was as white as paper. Awang was in a hurry and shouted, “Wu Liqing, Wu Liqing!” After a while, Wu Liqing opened her eyes and was powerless and said, “I am a tea fairy. Seeing your honesty and kindness, I married you. Tea seeds are my lifeblood. You can save the lives of the whole village by taking only 50 tablets. I can still live with the remaining 50 tablets. Now that the tea seeds are gone, I'm about to die.” “Wu Liqing, you can't die; I can't live without you!” Awang hugged Wu Liqing, and tears continued to flow down. Out of breath, Wu Liqing said, “I'm about to stop. If you miss me in the future, go find an old man with a white beard under the big acacia tree on the mountain forest.” When Wu Liqing finished speaking, a red light disappeared. Ah Wang felt sorry for Wu Liqing and stomped his feet in regret.
Ah Wang lived in pain for a year, and his feelings of missing Wu Liqing became more and more urgent. He remembered Wu Liqing's farewell message and decided to find the mysterious white-bearded old man in the mountains and woods.
Awang climbed the mountain and crossed the mountain. I don't know how many days he has walked, and he hasn't found the white-bearded old man in the woods. He asked the big tree, and the tree shook his head in ignorance. He asked, and the tea sparrow whimpered and flew away.
One night, Awang slept tirelessly leaning against an old acacia tree. I saw an old white-bearded man walking from behind a tree and asked him if he was looking for Wu Liqing. Awang hurriedly nodded and said yes. The old man rubbed his white whiskers on his forehead and said, “Actually, that girl's name is Wu Liqing, but Wu Liqing. If you want to find her, you have to go through dangerous risks, are you afraid or not?” Awang resolutely said, “Even if I were to shatter my bones, I would love to!” The white-bearded old man nodded. Only then did he explain in detail the whereabouts of Qing Mu.
Turns out, Wuli Qing is a sacred tea plant at Hei Snake Cave in Xianyu Mountain. When Awang took all of her tea seeds, she was taken back to the Black Snake Cave by the Black Snake Spirit and turned into an ordinary tea tree. Black Snake Jing knows that after 500 years, Wu Liqing will be able to regenerate tea seeds and become a beautiful tea fairy. At that time, she will still be able to take over her as a wife, so she is closely guarding that tea tree. During the day, Black Serpent keeps a group of thousand-year-old poisonous bees around and stings anyone who approaches. At night, the Black Serpent stays by itself, making it difficult for anyone to get close.
Awang asked the white-bearded old man to give instructions on how to rescue Green in the Mist. The old man said, “Viper Essence is a black cock-crowned snake. All his actions are on a chicken crown; that is, the black hat he wore when he became an old man. If you can take the hat and put it on your own head, you can command the poisonous bee and sting the black snake spirit to death.” Speaking of this, the old man handed Awan a wooden stick: “This is called a pick-stick. Take it; it will help you.”
As soon as Awang woke up, the sun was already old. He didn't see the white bearded old man's shadow, but he clearly remembered the scene in his dream. He still had that five-foot long pickaxe next to him, so he picked it up and walked to the other side of Xianyu Mountain.
He turned half a circle along the mountainside. Sure enough, he saw a big black hole. There was an aspen tree at the entrance. A group of poisonous bees the size of a small hawk were flying back and forth around a small tea tree as tall as a person.
Ah Wang hid behind a tree to observe the movement. It was just dark. He heard a burst of “brush brush” noises. A black cock-crowned snake, which was more than five feet long and the size of a bucket, climbed up. Its eyes are green and like lanterns, and Nobuko sticks out more than five feet long. It climbed to the entrance of the cave and rolled around, and it became an old Taoist dressed in black all over his body. The old man shouted, “Kids, is anyone here today?” The poisonous bee hummed and answered, “No!” Lao Dao laughed, “Haha, no one dared to come. You guys take a break.” The poisonous bee then flew over the big poplar tree and lay motionless. Hei Lao Dao circled around the tea tree, held out his hands to touch the tea leaves, and said to himself, “Green in the fog, grow fast. When you grow tea seeds, we can become husband and wife. I'm much stronger than Ah Wang, the mortal person.”
By midnight, Hei Lao Dao was sitting by the tea tree to take a nap, and Ah Wang quietly walked behind him and picked up the black hat on Hei Lao Dao's head with a pickaxe. When Lao Dao heard the movement, he rolled over and showed his original shape. He opened his blood basin and swung at Awang. Ah Wang quickly jumped to the side and held out his stick to face the enemy. The stick turned into a sharp sword. As soon as Awang made a clever move, he cut off Black Snake's tongue with the sword.
The poisonous snake made a “squeak” in pain and became an old Taoist running into the cave. When entering the cave, the tea tree was blown by the wind, and the branches carried the black flag to the ground. Awang knew that Mu Liqing was helping himself. He immediately picked up his hat and put it on his head and shouted at the poplar tree, “Poison bees, sting the underworld soon.” A large group of poisonous bees, the size of a kitty hawk, immediately flew out and swooped out and stabbed him. Black Lao Dao screamed and died after a while. Awang threw the black hat violently down the mountain, and the group of poisonous bees all turned into black smoke and followed the black hat without knowing where.
At this point, the sky was already bright. Awang walked in front of the fog, only to see that the fog was green and yellow, and there were a few drops of dew flashing on the weak leaves, like her tears. Awang felt sad. He shouted, “Green in the fog, come back with me!” The tea tree didn't move at all. Awang was so sad that he knelt down on the ground to plead. At this point, Wu Liqing's voice sounded in his ears: “Awang Ge, I can't become a man anymore. However, I am a sacred tea tree. If you pick my leaves, you can sell them for hundreds or two of silver, which is enough for you to marry a few beautiful daughters-in-law.” Awang cried: “I don't want to be rich, and I don't want another woman. If you can't become a human, I'll stay here for the rest of my life.” Seeing that Awang in the fog was sincere, she said, “Use a pestle stick to knock on that big poplar tree. The old white-bearded man and my eight sisters will help.”
Awang turned sad to happy. He immediately went to the poplar tree and knocked a few times. Soon, the white-bearded old man and his sisters fell from the top of the mountain. Ah Wang hurriedly knelt down for the white-bearded old man and asked him to save the tea lady. The white-bearded old man took a look at the Eight Sisters and smiled and said, “Please don't do it; you have to ask the sisters and sisters for help.” Ah Wang hurriedly bowed deeply to the Eight Sisters. They were in a hurry to return the gift, but they didn't know how to save Misty Liqing. The old man with white beard said, “If each of you gave me ten tea seeds, I could save her.” The eight sisters said together, “As long as you can save the green in the fog, anything will do.” Speaking of which, they each took off the safflower on the top of their heads and took out ten tea seeds and gave them to the white-bearded old man. The white-bearded old man sprinkled 80 tea seeds on the tea tree. A red light flashed through. The yellow tea leaves turned green, and a cluster of flaming red tea seeds continued to shine on the top. Then the tea tree disappeared, and Misty Green appeared in front of Awang.
Awang held Wu Liqing tightly in her arms. When she turned her head around, the white-bearded old man and his sisters had long since disappeared. Awang brought Wuli Qingling back home. Since then, the couple have lived by growing tea and selling tea. Wu Liqing has unique tea making skills. The tea leaves she makes not only have a special fragrance, but also a white mist that rises for a long time. The brown color is blue and transparent, and a full and beautiful tea bud floats up and down in the cup, like a group of fairies dancing in the clouds and fog. This spectacle has attracted many tea customers. Awang's small teahouse is full of customers every day, and the business is excellent.
Later, Wu Liqing passed on his ability to make tea to the villagers in Oyama Village, and they gradually lived a prosperous life by growing tea.
