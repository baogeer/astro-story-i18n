 In the Ming Dynasty, there was a scholar in Bozhou, Anhui. His name was Ouyang Boyun, and the character was a hard writer. He was originally born into a family of eunuchs. Later, due to the family's situation, it gradually fell into decline and was very poor. However, scholar students are unwilling to accept fateful arrangements, and are even less willing to be placed under the guise of others. They are determined to win “merit” and shine their ancestors. How can they fall off the list year after year. A kind-hearted gentleman told him, “My post-generation skills are so poor. I still need to read 10,000 books to move the gods of heaven and earth.”
As a result, he spent the whole day copying books and studying literature. However, my family was poor, and paper was too expensive, so I had to copy an article on the wall and door panel. One day he felt bored in his room and came to the backyard to relax. I was pleasantly surprised to see the peony bush in the backyard that hadn't bloomed for many years, so I suddenly had a whim. I went back to the room, fetched ink, and copied the article on peony petals, and used flowers instead of paper. The kind gentleman passed by the Lord and saw this scene, calling this peony “ten thousand volumes of books.” “Flower God” was probably moved by this. The following year, Ouyang Boyun actually “raised people.”
(Quoted from “Peony Grand View” Liu Xiang and Xu Xiaofan)
