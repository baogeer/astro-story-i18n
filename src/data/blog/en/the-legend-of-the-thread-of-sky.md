 I don't know how many years ago, there was a young man in Zhuji named Shi Wa. He is both smart and strong. Not only is he an expert farmer, but he is also a skilled stonemason. Every morning and evening, he always brings hammers and chisels, and greets the young people in the village to go to the mountains to cut rocks. Whether it's spring, summer, autumn, or winter, no matter the wind, frost, rain, or snow, there is no break for a day. Slowly, he made the hilltops on the side of the village beautiful, and the scenery was very beautiful.
In the village where Shi Wa lives, there is a girl named Flower Girl. The flower looks like a peony that has just opened. She has a clever mind and is very good at embroidering. No girl can catch up with her. Every day, Huamei and the girls in the village sit at the window to embroider and embroider. They embroider all the beautiful flowers on the canvas, and then wear these beautiful splendid embellishments as costumes. Everyone is dressed up beautifully.
Flower girls can also sing. As soon as she starts singing, the flowers will bloom, the birds will fly, and everyone in the village will laugh. Shi Wa was chiseling stones in the mountain. Hearing her singing, her neck grew louder.
Shi Wa loves Flower Girl, and Flower Girl loves Shi Wa. They both have a common wish; they must wait until Shi Wa has cut all the stones on the mountain. It wasn't until Flower Girl finished embroidering the flowers of the four seasons. At this point, the two of them became married. Everyone in the village loved Flower Girl and Shi Wa, saying they were a natural couple.
We are finally looking forward to a good day. This day is the Mid-Autumn Festival on August 15. Shi Wa cut out the last stone, and Flower Girl also finished embroidering the last thread. They both ran to the side of a creek outside the village. Flower girl spread her guts and let the fresh spring water wash her long hair. Huamei's long hair was soaked in the water, and the stream swirled, “Whoa, whoa” and laughed incessantly; when I saw the beautiful flower girl on the peach tree by the creek, a particularly bright peach blossom bloomed on the top of the tree. Shi Wa climbed up, took off the peach blossoms, and placed them in the hair curls that Huamei had pulled up for the first time. The moon shines in the sky, the stars blink, and the fairies in the sky all look out. Who wouldn't envy this pair on earth!
Thor standing at the top of the clouds also saw it; Shi Wa and Flower Girl were so happy and so close. The more he looked at it, the more he blushed and hated to think: There are people who are living a happier life than me; that's impossible; such a beautiful girl should belong to me! So he wiped off his black face, turned into a thick, black-faced man, and went underground.
Hua Mei got dressed up and came back to the village with Shi Wa. The villagers hugged them both to the most beautiful hill. There, everyone had already built a new house for them. Men, women, and children from all over the village spent a joyful night in the mountains, only to gradually disperse back when it was almost dawn.
The guests were exhausted, and Shi Wa and Hua Mei were about to go in the house. Suddenly, the moon disappeared, and the dark clouds held their heads. All they could hear was a scary sound rolling in from afar. Thor appeared on the top of the mountain. Thor smiled and said, “Beauty, follow me to the sky,” Huamei was in a hurry and angry. As soon as she reached out and “knocked back”, she slapped two times. Shi Wa came over. One shoulder hit him far and wide. Thor has been beaten, how dare they stop! As soon as his eyes turned, there were two flashes of lightning, and one of his mouths was a thunder; as soon as he swung his robe, the wind blew in, and the torrential rain fell. Lightning pierced people's eyes, thunderstorms and deafness, the wind made people unable to stand up, and those hit by the torrential rain couldn't lift their heads. Flower Girl and Shi Wa are snuggling up, and they promise not to be separated! Thor hit the middle of Shi Wa and Flower Girl with his hands, and a blue light “suddenly” flashed through, and the top of the mountain split in half; Thor took a breath. Half of the mountain where Huamei was standing, immediately flew up. Shi Wa cleverly jumped forward and climbed the vines on this half of the mountain. Fly, fly, fly faster, fly higher. Shi Wa only heard the sound of the wind in his ears. Gradually, he became dizzy and dazzled. Unwittingly, his hands became two wings, and he couldn't hold back. He clenched the tree vine tightly with his teeth; however, his mouth was getting longer, sharper, and harder... In the end, the top half of the mountain flew over Lingyin in Hangzhou, and a “thunderous” sound fell to the ground, leaving Shiwa stunned.
Shi Wa woke up and saw that she had become a bird with a long mouth. She felt very sad. At this point, he vaguely heard a burst of singing, so familiar! As a result, he spread his wings to the top of the mountain and looked around: the stone carvings on the mountain were carved by himself, and the trees on the mountain were planted by himself. He recognized it below. This is the half of the mountain that flew from his hometown! He stuck his ear to the rock and listened to it. The song was even more clear. Even the thick rock wall kept blocking the singing of the flower girl. Huamei must be at the bottom of this mountain! After listening to a position, Shi Wa pecked the rock. She pecked the rock for one day, then pecked another day, and pecked the rock directly at Mars.
The flower girl was singing, and suddenly she vaguely heard a “knock, knock” sound above his head. He held his breath and listened quietly, which was not bad at all! The sound is sharper than the sound, and the sound is louder than the sound. This must be a stone baby chiseling a stone! Flower girl is so happy! He removed the silver chain from his head and dug it up at the top of the cave where it made a noise. He wanted Shi Wa to cut through the rock sooner with less effort. In this way, Shi Wa went up to the top of the mountain to peck, and Hua Mei dug in the cave. Shi Wa's sharp beak was pierced, and Hua Mei's silver hairpin was only a short burnout. I don't know how long it took for Shi Wa to peck to a silver chevron, and the rock finally pecked through! A warm ray of sunshine shone into the black hole. Shi Wa flew into the cave through this gap and flew three laps around Huamei. Huamei also immediately became a beautiful bird. They both flew into the sky in the face of the sun.
Fly, fly, Shi Wa and Hua Mei break through the blue sky, pass through white clouds, and fly all the way to the sky. They found Thor and patted their wings together and rushed past, shouting “Dick, Dick,” and pecked Thor's two bronze bells in the eye. Since then, Thor has become blind. He can only yell in the sky, and no longer dare to go underground to commit crimes.
There is still a “Half Mountain” outside Zhuji City; the other half of the mountain that flew away from there is now “Feilai Peak” at Lingyin Temple in Hangzhou. There is a small hole on Feilai Peak called “First Line of Heaven,” which is where Shi Wa rescued Flower Girl back then.
