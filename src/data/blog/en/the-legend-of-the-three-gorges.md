 Legend has it that Guanyin, Luban, and Qin Shi Huang all fight for positive energy. Guanyin wore 100 pairs of cloth shoes overnight, Luban dug 100 stone caves overnight, and Qin Shi Huang drove the mountains into the East China Sea overnight. They agreed that the chicken would only call the sky bright.
Guanyin pulls his shoes, Luban makes a cave, and Qin Shi Huang drives the mountain, and the sky rotates around the earth. When the Golden Rooster sounded, Guanyin only wore 99 pairs of cloth shoes; Luban only hit 99 caves; Qin Shi Huang only pushed the mountain to Nanjin Pass.
The three tied the knot and came to Wuyuan Cave to boil tea and play with the landlord. Guanyin turned cloth shoes into wooden boats on the river, sails hanging down the river, and pulling the fibers of Xiling against the water, so it's as complicated as a woman pulling the sole of a shoe, and you can't pull a boat even if you don't wear a red stripe (naked)! Or just use a penny to hold up the boat. Penny is like Guanyin pulling a needle at the bottom of her shoe. Guanyin said that the wooden boat on the water was her 100th pair of cloth shoes. Master Luban built a stone arch bridge (Luban Bridge flooded under water) at the mouth of the Wuyuan Cave canyon. He said that this bridge was his 100th stone cave. Qin Shi Huang used Wuyuan Cave as the center point to point the Three Gorges, making the Three Gorges water drift a little more; making the Three Gorges mountain a little more dangerous, making Luban's bridge taller; Emperor Shi Huang also turned Luhan Mountain into Wuyuan Cave. The whip that chased the mountain waved to Pyramid Mountain and Lianhua Peak, and the mountain he chased moved to the Three Gorges. Kannon also lives in seclusion in Wuyuan Cave, among Purdue people. The grateful Badong people later dedicated a Guanyin Pavilion (already flooded) on the west bank of Luban Bridge. Therefore, in the Three Gorges region, there are many strange mountains, ancient mountains, high bridges, and ships, as well as many emperors, generals and talented people in the past!
