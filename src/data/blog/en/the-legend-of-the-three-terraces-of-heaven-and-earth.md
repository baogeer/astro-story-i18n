 Legend has it that the family named Chen, who lives in Migu, moved from Yingtianfu in Nanjing. At the time, a woman from the Su family accompanied her to Miku. The Chen family bought a property in reclaimed land in Michigan, and the Su woman also got married and had children. As for the two families, the Su Chen family are still relatives with her nephew. After many years, after years of painstaking management, the Chen family has flourished, and his nephew has grown into a smart Peugeot boy.
As the Chen family grew older, it was difficult to fulfill their wish to return to their roots, so they wanted to choose a lucky place on Mount Miku as their destination after 100 years. The Feng Shui Master changed hands over and over again, and Chen Yuanwei still hasn't decided on his ideal graveyard. One day, a local teacher from Hunan rode the White Road through Migu and was nurtured by the Chen family because he felt the wind and cold. A few days later, when the district master learned about Chen Yuan's foreign affairs, he recommended his own location and thanked the Chen family for their extensive care.
As a result, the local teacher spent the whole day riding horses and touring the mountains, leaving early and returning to Kankichi in the evening. More than 20 days have passed without saying a word. The Chen family's father and son were busy with the family business; second, they saw that the teacher was acting strangely. They suspected that his mentor was a liar. After a few decades, they were a bit impatient, so they simply commissioned their nephew to take care of the district teacher. After dozens of days like this, the district master still didn't say a word, and the staff were a bit unhappy. Only my nephew had no complaints. As always, she meticulously took care of the land master's clothing, food, lodging, and travel.
Finally, one day, the district master was leaving. The day before he left, the district master took his nephew up the mountain again to explore the good land. The district master said to his nephew, “There are three mountains in this mountainous area. It is a lucky place given to Miku by God. I can't look at the ground myself; I just sit down on this white horse. Thanks to your extensive care, I chose a lucky plot of land for your family today. Wait for your mother to be buried here after 100 years, and keep your family prosperous and your children prosperous. If you go up the mountain today, if a white horse shovels the ground three times and doesn't lie down, you can put in a good spot as a sign; if this pile is full of foliage in the coming year, the prophecy will come true; if a white horse shovels three times and shouts three times, it is Nakayoshi, which can be used by your uncle; if Hakuba only plows the ground three times, then it's a good place to go down.” When the two walked to the first platform, the white horse certainly lay down on the ground three times. My nephew was overjoyed and picked a pine branch as a sign; when they reached the second platform, the white horse sowed the ground three more times and made three loud noises, and my nephew secretly memorized it; when they reached the third platform at the top of the mountain, Hakuba only plowed the ground three times.
Earth Master left the next day, and there was no news as soon as he left. My nephew told Chen Yuan about the second platform. After Chen Yuanwai made many considerations, he had nothing to say.
In the spring of next year, the pine branches that were planted on the first platform will certainly have green leaves. My nephew knows everything. He only pretends to be deaf and dumb, keeping his mouth shut.
A few years later, my nephew's mother died and she was buried on the first site. Strangely enough, my nephew's family's fortune has been smooth sailing since then, and all his wishes have come true. The family business is like day and sky, riding mules and horses, and the family has a fortune of millions. The old uncle was jealous. He suspected that his nephew had manipulated the site and ordered his nephew to move his tomb. My nephew had no choice but to agree. On the day the tomb was moved, when the tomb had just been torn apart, a pair of white pigeons suddenly flew out of the tomb and landed at Weishan Zijin Mountain. Seeing this, the Chen family knows that the ground has been leaked, that it cannot be repaired, and that there are many regrets.
Since then, the family fortune of the Su Chen family has been no different from that of ordinary people in the village; they can only rely on hard work to revitalize the family business; they have no luck. This is how the story of Tiandi Santai spread.
