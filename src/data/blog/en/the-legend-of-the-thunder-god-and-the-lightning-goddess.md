 Lei Gong's electric mother are two immortals who were released by thunder in ancient Chinese mythology and legends. They belong to a couple gods. They are mainly responsible for weather changes and cheer when it rains; they also have rain gods, wind gods, and cloud gods as colleagues; they can punish demons and ghosts with the power of electric shocks and even the sun.
Lei Gong is the god of thunder, who is in charge of the sun, so he is called Gong, also known as Master Lei and Thor. The magic weapon is a hammer drill chiseled by Lei Gong. Legend has it that it looks like a bird's face and Thunder Gong's beak, and has wings behind it.
The Electric Mother is the god in charge of lightning. It is yin, so it is called Mother, also known as Our Lady of the Golden Light and the Lady of Lightning; the magic weapon is a two-sided lightning mirror, and in legend, it looks quite majestic.
Lei Gong is the god of thunder. He belongs to the Sun, so he is called Gong, also known as Master Lei and God of Thor. Electric Mother is the god of lightning. It is yin, so it is called Mother, also known as Madonna of the Golden Light and the Lady of Lightning. The cult of thunder has been around since ancient times. There is a saying in the Sengoku “Journey” section of Qu Yuan: “Master Zuo Yu treated Xi, and left Lei Gong acted as his defense”; in “Li Shao,” he also said, “Emperor Luan was Yu Xianjiao, and Master Lei was unarmed. We ask for Fenglong to take advantage of the clouds and seek the place of Princess Mi”. Feng Long, according to Han Wang Yizhi, “is called Master Lei.” There are many records relating to the god Thor in the “Shanhai Sutra,” saying that the god Thor “has a dragon body and a human head, and bulges its stomach,” and is considered a beast. However, the Han Dynasty Thor God described in Han Dynasty Wang Chong's “On Heng,” “If you look like a sumo wrestler, you would call him Lord Lei. Let the left hand lead the drum and the right hand push the spine, as if hitting it. It meant thunder and loud, even the sound of drums clashing”. Lei Gongdang is a doll. During Wei Jin's Northern and Southern Dynasties, Lei Gong became a beast again. “Search for God” called Thor “as colored as a silver, eyes like a mirror, three feet long with horns, and shaped like six animals, like a monkey.” The job of lightning was also performed by Lord Thunder in ancient times. It wasn't until the Han Dynasty that there was talk of the father of electricity. Around the Tang and Song dynasties, the god of lightning became an electric mother. Tang Cui Zhiyuan has a saying, “Make the electric mother Lei Gong and carve a path to the sky in outer regions.” Song Su-shi also wrote a poem called “Captain Thunder Gong Ho Electric Mother.” After Song Yuan, the electronic mother became more famous. “Zu Ding Yu Wen” is called “the messenger of Dianmu Xiu, known as Wen Ying.” “The History of Public Opinion Service” in “Yuan Shi” says that the military has an “electric flag”. The flag depicts a god person in the form of a woman, “Zhu Chang in white pants, carrying light with both hands.” The Ming Dynasty novels “Journey to the West” and “Journey to the North” were both written as “Our Lady of the Golden Light” or “Lady Zhu Pei”.
