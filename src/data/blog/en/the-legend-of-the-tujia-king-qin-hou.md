 Legend has it that one year, the imperial court killed quite a few Tujia mountain people, and Qin Yi's wife was also murdered.
After her death, she gave him a dream. She said that there were three large bamboo shoots on her grave. She wanted Qin Yi to cut them into a bow and arrow, and practice martial arts for 3 years and 6 months. When the chicken and flying dog came to the house, she opened the gate, fired arrows at Beijing, killed the emperor, avenged the people of Tujia, and avenged her mother.
Qin Yi told her sister about this. The sister and brother then cut bamboo to make bows and arrows. Qin Yi closed the door and practiced martial arts in the hall every day.
Practice, practice. I've been practicing for three years. Kung Fu had learned almost everything. At this point, my sister couldn't stand it, so she chased the chicken and the dog and made the chicken fly dog jump, then panicked and asked Zhang Qin to open the bow and arrow. As soon as Qin Yi took a look, it was true that the chicken flew up the house, so he took out his bow and arrow and headed for Beijing, “Fuck! Fuck! Fuck!” That's three arrows.
However, due to the lack of fire, the first arrow fell in the Lishui River 30 miles away; the second arrow landed in Lizhou, 300 miles away, and the place is now called Arrow Gate; and the third arrow, on the emperor's dragon chair, was in the early morning, and Qin Yi's arrow did not hit.
After Qin Yi fired the arrow, all the bamboo shoots in the back garden blew up. One person and one horse ran out of each bamboo festival, but they fell to the ground and died after they came out. The rice industry used to be a few mountains of rice. It was Qin Yi's military food, but it also became a heap of loess because time hadn't arrived.
The emperor was taken aback and hurriedly removed the arrow from the dragon chair. At first glance, the arrow pole had the words “King Qin Wei of Maogang” engraved on it, so he sent four people to seize him, and finally caught Qin Yi to Beijing.
Before leaving, my sister told Qin Yi, “If the emperor asks you if the nine dragons on your body were spawned or painted, you said they were painted.”
When Qin Yi arrived in Beijing, he questioned him in the emperor's lobby. Pointing at Qin Yi's back, he asked, “Was the dragon on your body spawned or painted?” Qin Yi said, “It's a painting.”
As a result, the emperor ordered that Qin Yi be stripped and killed. Who knows, once the skin is peeled off, only a few golden flashes of light. All nine dragons on Qin Yi's body burst into the sky. The sky suddenly became dark, and the sun and moon in Beijing were unclear.
The emperor knew that he had killed his real life, Tenshi, and had to let Qin Yi sit on the throne for three times every year.
