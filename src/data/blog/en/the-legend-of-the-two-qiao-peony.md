 In the Caozhou Peony Garden, there is a type of peony called “Huaerqiao”. The flowers come in two colors, are bright and beautiful, and are very popular among visitors. Speaking of this flower, there is also a moving legend.
A long time ago, Cao Zhou had two siblings. Her sister's name was Da Qiao, and her younger sister was Xiao Qiao. They were inseparable all day long and got along — always very well. They both love peonies. As soon as they have free time, they run to the peony garden to loosen soil, fertilize, and water the peonies.
When the sisters were 18 years old, one day, a ferocious and stout giant black dragon suddenly flew in from the north. It circled over Peony Country and made great efforts. Suddenly, there were dark clouds over the peony garden, and the sky was gone. “Ash” the black dragon sneezed deafening. Immediately after that, it started raining in the sky. The light rain was not heavy, but strangely enough, the rain was dark. When dark water like charcoal water falls on the peony, the peony flowers wither, and the tall flowers hang their heads weakly.
After an hour, the Black Dragon flew away, the rain stopped, and the sun came out. At this point, the townspeople all ran to the peony garden one after another, and everyone was amazed by the withered peonies. Peonies are the root of life. Without flowers, it means no food; the townspeople don't know what to do for a moment.
Just then, a white cloud floated from the western sky. A fairy with white hair and a silver beard was standing on the white cloud. He loudly said to the people, “I want to save Meitan. Only by killing the Black Dragon and dripping its blood on the peony flower can the peony be revived. Remember, peonies that have been bathed in black water only live for seven days.” After speaking, Xianweng disappeared. How easy is it to kill a black dragon. Not even knowing where Black Dragon lives, the townspeople once again fell into hopelessness. Suddenly, someone in the crowd said, “It's not difficult. Leave it to us to kill the Black Dragon.” As soon as everyone took a look, it turned out to be Big Joe and Little Joe. “Two little girls can't even kill a dog. How can they kill the big black dragon?” But people didn't have any other good options; they had to look at the sisters.
One day has passed, no sister in action; two days have passed. I didn't see the sisters acting, so some people urged them, but the two sisters stayed in the house and couldn't get out. It went on like this for six days. On the morning of the 7th day, 999 fresh and beautiful Shedan flowers suddenly grew in the withered peony garden. At noon, dark clouds began rolling over the peony garden again, and the big black dragon appeared in the sky. It circled and flew. Seeing that there was still a large area of peonies on the ground that had not died, it sneezed viciously, and dark rain suddenly fell from the sky. Strangely enough, the 999 new peony flowers were covered in black water but did not wither at all. Black Dragon was so surprised that he left and watched the competition. When it fell five feet above the ground, it suddenly drilled Big Qiao and Xiao Qiao out of the peony leaf. Each holding a bright sword in their hands, the sisters used their full strength to stab the Black Dragon's left and right eyes violently.
“Ah!” The Black Dragon screamed. Its eyes were stabbed blind, and the Black Dragon tumbled in pain. The sisters fearlessly wrestled with the blind Black Dragon. At one hour, two hours, and at the third hour, the two sisters killed the Black Dragon. Big Qiao and Xiao Qiao looked at the black dragon that died on the ground, and exhaled for a long time and said, “Now the peony is saved.” When I finished speaking, I was exhausted next to Black Dragon.
It turns out that the nine hundred and ninety-nine peonies were made of paper by the sisters over six days and six nights. Because they knew that the Black Dragon would come on the 7th day, they lay down under the peony and attacked the Black Dragon.
Hearing the movement, people came in one after another. They all cried with heartache when they saw Big Joe and Little Joe die on the ground. People buried two sisters who love peonies together in a peony garden.
People drew out the black dragon's blood and died on Dudan's body one by one. Soon, the peonies all came back to life and regained their former beauty and fragrance.
In the spring of the following year, a peony tree grew on the sisters' graves. By the time of the rain, the peony bloomed in two colors. People said it was an incarnation of the two sisters, so they called this type of peony “Flower Erqiao.”
