 The most famous attraction in Keelung City is the Fairy Cave. In addition to the enshrined Buddha of Guanshiyin, there are many stone carvings from the Tongzhi period in the Qing Dynasty, which has a mysterious feel. According to folklore, immortals used to live in the cave, and legends say that this cave has a tunnel leading directly to Tamsui Port.
The most famous monuments in Tainan City are Gelanche City (also known as Hongmaocheng, Red Wall, and Taiwan Town) and Promincha City (also known as Red Building), which were built by the Dutch in the late Ming Dynasty. The two cities are four kilometers apart, and according to legend, there is an undersea tunnel connecting them. Every nation in the world has this kind of authentic legend.
