 Xianyuling is located at the junction of the three counties of Qimen, Dongzhi and Shitai. It is 1,200 meters above sea level.
Due to inconvenient transportation in the old days, this was the main transportation route for villagers on the border of the three counties.
There are no families on this long mountain road, not even the springs in the cave.
Legend has it that after a year of major droughts, a farmer slowly picked up a load of rice.
Passing by the foot of a high rock cliff, I was thirsty and smoky, and wanted to moisturize my throat.
Lu Dongbin, who happened to meet one of the Eight Immortals, came here to take a break.
Lu Dongbin asked, “Old Uncle, isn't it difficult for you to pick when you go up the mountain? “But the farmer smiled and said, “The left shoulder comes to the right shoulder. Drink the Immortals with saliva.
” Lu Dongbin immediately pulled the sword out of his body and slapped it on the stone. A clear spring immediately popped out of the sword's eye, continuing all year round, for people coming and going to rest and quench their thirst.
Legend has it that when people rest here, they only need to put a small wooden stick in the crevices of the cliff and the lower back won't hurt, so people call it a “lumbar support stone.”
