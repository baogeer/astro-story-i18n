 Legend has it that a long time ago, an old woman and her only son lived in a cave.
This only son is very docile. Every day he boils water for his mother and bakes cookies for her to eat.
One day, my only son went out to collect medicine and was unfortunately murdered.
The old woman was heartbroken and cried every day in the cave. The stone next to her was soaked with tears and gradually became a pair of eyes, forming a characteristic landscape of Half Day Rock.
For this reason, Half-Day Rock is also known as the “Weeping Stone.”
This story has spread to this day, and it has become a unique legend of Half Sky Rock.
Half Sky Rock is a mountain peak located in Linxiang City, Yueyang City, Hunan Province, China. It is named after its shape like a crouching giant turtle. This mountain has many local legends and folklore; here are some of them:
1. The origin of Half-day Rock: Legend has it that a long time ago, this place used to be a wilderness, without a single tree, not a drop of water, or even a single bird. One day, an immortal passed by. He knocked on the ground with his scepter, and a golden light immediately flashed through, and a mountain peaked out of the ground. This is today's Half Day Rock.
2. Legend of Half the Sky Rock: Legend has it that a beautiful fairy once lived here in ancient times. She practiced in the mountains every day until one day she met a young farmer. The two fell in love and became a couple, but after the fairy's identity was discovered by other fairies, they sent someone to kill the farmer and turn the fairy into stone. Since then, every night, this stone will turn into a beautiful fairy, guarding this mountain forest.
3. The myth of Half Sky Rock: Legend has it that in ancient times, this place used to be the home of a fairy.
This fairy loves to drink and entertains guests here every day.
But one day he got drunk and spilled a glass of wine on the floor.
This glass of wine turned into a dragon, and it began to devour the trees, flowers and plants on the mountain.
To save this land, the gods and dragons fought a fierce battle.
In the end, the fairy slaughtered the dragon with the sword in his hand, but his arm was also bitten by the dragon.
Since then, he has left a scar on his arm, and this scar has also become a scar on the rock for half a day.

Half Tianyan is a folk tale handed down in southwestern China.
Half Day Rock refers to a rock in Chongsheng Temple in Dali. There is a folk story about this rock that tells of three human marks on this rock, each representing three great monks, so it is revered by the temple as “Half Sky Rock.”
The story of Half Tianyan has been circulating in the Dali region for hundreds of years. People's heritage and interpretation of it continue to evolve and enrich, and it is worth understanding and exploring.
