 In the Ming Dynasty, Caozhou peonies were already famous in Beijing.
Legend has it that after a year of rain, Emperor Zhu Yuanzhang of the Ming dynasty brought military commander Liu Bowen and a team of ministers of culture and martial arts to Caozhou to see flowers.
When they arrived at a peony garden in Caozhou, they were immediately amazed by what they saw in front of them. I saw the peony garden. The eight colors of red, yellow, black, white, orchid, green, and pink peonies were in full splendor, dazzling, and majestic. The fragrance is invigorating and invigorating. It is truly heavenly, far superior to the Royal Gardens. Zhu Yuanzhang is extremely jealous and will never allow the people to win over the royal family. As a result, I immediately decided to move all the peonies in the garden to the Imperial Garden.
Liu Bowen is a person who can figure things out. After going around the garden a few times, he saw that the peony garden had such a prosperous doorway. As a result, he went up to Zhu Yuanzhang and whispered a few words. Zhu Yuanzhang hurriedly ordered not to dig up other peonies; just dig up the one in front of the garden's owner, Zhao Yi. The flower farmers were delighted. Although Zhao Yi usually likes this peony, she had no choice but to endure the pain and cut love when thinking that this would preserve the peonies of her flower farmers. Zhao Yi's wife, Wei Hua, is the only one with more pain devices. Zhao Yi was unsure and hurriedly inquired. Wei Hua was in great pain, but she knew that the Holy Spirit was not counterproductive, so she had to confess the truth: “As of now, I can't help but tell you the truth. I was originally a peony flower decoration here. Seeing your hard work, innocence, and kindness, I married you. I didn't expect to be raised by Liu Bowen today. I told him to take my people, leave my heart behind, take my body, and leave my roots behind. I had no choice but to leave my six-month-old baby under the root...” After all, as soon as the wind blew up, Wei Hua was gone. At this point, Zhu Yuanzhang got on the bus and took Ye Zhonghualie's peony.
Zhao Yi knows that her wife was originally a peony flower fairy, but when she thought about the couple's kindness on weekdays, she was so sad that she fell in the flower bed in front of the door and cried to life... Unexpectedly, she came to see spring. In the peony flower bed in front of Zhao Yi's gate, a bright purple peony grew. The flower bloomed in thousands of layers, big like a plate, and more than 700 petals. It has a rich fragrance, and it smells for ten miles. The villagers know that this is a child who admires Wei flowers, so they named it “Wei Zi” (homonym: child). Since then, peonies have changed from seed to root.
(Li Baoguang)
