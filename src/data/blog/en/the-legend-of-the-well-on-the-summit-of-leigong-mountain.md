 At the peak of Leigong Mountain in Guizhou, there is a well. The square one is about the size of a basket. It is unhappy when it rains; it doesn't dry for a long time. Legend has it that this strange well was dug by Zhang Xiumei and Yang Daliu. During the Qing dynasty, Zhang Xiumei and Yang Daliu led the Miao family against the tyranny of the dynasty. They fought for more than 10 years, conquered the state and occupied the capital, killing the Qing soldiers to death and shouting their father and mother.
When the Qing government saw that the situation was not going well, it sent General Xi Baotian to lead the officers and soldiers to suppress it. Xi Baotian had many soldiers, and there were few soldiers in his senior year. They both thought they couldn't get along, so they led the Miao family's younger brother up to Mount Lei Gong. However, the top of Mount Leigong was full of weeds on the deserted slopes, and there was no water to drink. What should I do? Let's go down the mountain to Peach. The bottom of the mountain is full of enemy soldiers. The Miao brothers were extremely anxious because of this. Xiumei and Dai-six are even more anxious than their brothers. One day, Xiumei and her senior two went to find water themselves. I've searched all over the mountains, big and small, and visited the Popoling Ridge of Mount Leigong, but I still can't find it. In the end, they both climbed to the highest peak. Yang Daliu looked at the sky and the ground. Mr. Jiao was in a hurry and shouted, “There are mountains and water everywhere! Leigong Mountain, why don't you have water?!” Speaking of which, I slipped violently to the ground with my foot, only to see a clear spring like a silver pillar popping out loud. Zhang Xiu saw it and was extremely happy. He pulled Yang Daliu apart and said, “Brother, let go quickly and wait for me!” Then he pulled out the machete “Knock! Knock!” As soon as the ground was chiseled, a barn-like pond appeared, and the water flowed all over the hillside like vaginal discharge. The brothers saw it and swarmed to the top of the mountain, “There's water! There's water!” The ground kept shouting, and the sound continued to the sky.
Since then, Zhang Xiumei, Yang Daliu, and the Miao family brothers have not only had water to drink; they have also used water from that well to cultivate fields, grow crops, and keep fighting. The Qing army simply had no choice. Later, there were too many Qing soldiers, and only then did Zhang Xiumei and Yang Daliu fail. That group slowly shrunk down and became what it is now.
