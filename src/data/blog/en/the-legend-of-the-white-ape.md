 Note: “The Legend of the White Apes in Fujiang” is one of the representative works of Tang legend. It shapes the three typical images of Ouyang, White Ape, and Monkey, and creates an artistic image of a white ape that combines the three sexes of divinity, humanity, and apes.
At the end of the Liang dynasty in Datong, the imperial court sent Pingnan general Lin Qin to Guilin and defeated Li Shigu and Chen Che. Don't siege Ouyang to Changle, completely pacify all the holes, and then go deep into remote and dangerous places.
Ouyang Yi's wife is slim, white, and very beautiful. Some of his subordinates said to him, “Why did the general bring beautiful women here? There are monsters in this place. They are good at stealing young girls, but it's even more inevitable that they are beautiful, so care should be taken to protect them.” Ouyang was very frightened when she heard it. At night, soldiers were arranged to guard the house, hid his wife in a secret room, carefully closed doors and windows, and guarded her with more than a dozen maids.
That night, it was overcast and the sky was dark. At 5 o'clock, it was still quiet and silent. The guardians all began to doze off due to burnout. Suddenly, something seemed to wake everyone up. When they went to see it, they discovered that Ouyang Yi's wife had disappeared. The doors and windows are still tight, and I don't know where they are coming out. The mountains outside the gate are steep, and the maze is hard to discern within easy reach, and impossible to track down. By dawn, no trace was found.
Ou Yang was very sad and angry, and vowed not to go back if she couldn't find a wife. So they speculated that they were sick, stationed the military, and went to far places every day to search for dangerous places. After more than a month, I suddenly found one of his wife's embroidered shoes in the bamboo grove a hundred miles away. Although they had been soaked in rain, they were still recognizable. Ouyang Wei was even saddened, and her desire to find a wife became more and more determined. Thirty strong soldiers were selected to join him in the search, armed with weapons, carrying dry food, and eating and sleeping in the mountains.
After more than ten days, about 200 miles away from the station, I saw a mountain facing south. It was green and beautiful, taller than the surrounding mountains. When I arrived at the bottom of the mountain, a deep stream surrounded it, so I made a raft to cross it. Between the cliffs and green bamboo groves, they saw red colored silk from time to time, and heard laughing and talking sounds, and the group clutched the vines, grabbed the ropes, and climbed up. Beautiful trees are planted in rows on it, and famous flowers are also planted in between. Underneath the trees is a turquoise meadow, thick and soft like a blanket. The silence of Qingyuan is truly a different place. There are dozens of women in front of the cave on the east side. Their clothes are bright and bright, and they are playful, singing and laughing, and entering and leaving the cave. After seeing them, they all slowly stood and watched. When the group passed by, they asked, “What's coming here?” Ouyang Yi told them the details of the incident. While looking at each other, the women sighed, “The lady has been here for over a month and is now sick in bed, so let him go in and take a look.”
Ouyang Yu entered the cave. The door was made of wood. There are three spacious rooms in the middle that look like a hall. There are beds by the four walls, all covered with brocade mattresses. His wife lay on a stone bed, covered several layers of mattresses, and placed in front of him delicious food. Ouyang Yi walked over to see her. She took a quick look and immediately waved to him to walk away quickly. The women said, “We've been here with your wife for ten years. There is a monster living in this place. It has great power to kill people. Even if you avoid 100 people with weapons, you can't subdue it. Fortunately it hasn't come back yet, so you should leave right away. If you prepare two dendrobiums of wine, ten sticks of meat dogs, and tens of pounds of hemp, we'll work with you to try to kill it. It must be noon when it comes back. Be careful later and don't come too early. We agreed on a ten-day period.” Then I urged him to leave. Ouyang Yi also retreated in a hurry.
When Ouyang Yi returned, he prepared alcoholic beverages, hemp, and dogs, and left on the agreed day. One woman said, “That monster likes to drink and is often drunk. When they're drunk, they must show that they are strong. They ask us to tie his hands and feet to the bed with colored silk, leap forward, and all of the colored silk will be cut off. We sewed three sheets of cloth to tie it, and it ran out of strength and was not relieved. Now hide the horse in Buri and bundle it up, hoping it won't be able to get rid of it. It is as hard as iron all over the body. It is only a few inches below the navel and often conceals protection; this part must not resist weapons.” She pointed to a cave next to it and said, “This is its granary, so you just hide inside and wait quietly. Put the wine under the flowers and the dog let them stay in the woods. Wait until my plan works, I greet you, and you come out right away.” As she said, Ouyang Yi hid in the granary and waited patiently.
When the sun is westerly, something like a piece of white silk comes down from another mountain, flies through the forest, and straight into a stone cave. Soon, a six-foot-tall man, wearing a white coat and walking sticks, came out surrounded by a group of women. He watched in surprise when he saw the dog, then caught the dog. After tearing it, he ate blood and ate meat until he was full. The women all scrambled to drink with jade glasses, chatted and laughed, and were very happy. After drinking a few buckets of alcohol, the women helped them in. Then I heard the sound of playful laughter coming from inside. After a long time, the woman came out to greet Ouyang Yi, and he went into the cave with a weapon. He saw a great white ape with four legs tied to the bedside. He raised his forehead and shrunk down when he saw the person. He wanted to break free but couldn't do it. His eyes flashed like lightning. The crowd competed to stab it with swords, just like stabbing it on an iron stone. So I stabbed it under its navel, and the sword immediately stabbed it in, and blood spewed out. White Monkey sighed loudly and said in surprise, “This is God trying to kill me; it's not your ability. However, your wife is pregnant. Don't kill this son; he will meet the Holy Emperor in the future, and he will definitely be able to expand your family's ancestral career.” When I finished saying these things, I died.
Searching the White Ape's storage, they discovered that there were many treasures. The same was true of rare food. There were several large tables full of them. Every rare item in the world is ubiquitous. Famous spices include several dendrobium, as well as a pair of treasure swords. There are more than 30 women, all of them stunning. It's been ten years since it's been a long time. They said that if they have color loss in old age, they must be taken away by white apes, and they don't know how to treat them.
Also, white apes are all alone in catching and enjoying women; no one is in the same party. Every morning, it takes up the shower, puts on a hat, a white robe, and a white robe, and never knows how the heat changes.
It has white hairs all over its body and is several inches long. At its place of residence, it often reads wooden abbreviations, and the characters on it are like runes or seals, and they don't know it at all. After reading it, I put the wooden version under the stone steps. On clear days, it sometimes dances with double swords. The sword flies around the body like lightning, forming a circle of light like a full moon. It doesn't have a fixed amount of time to eat the fruit chestnuts. It especially loves dogs, chewing dog meat and drinking dog blood. After noon, it just flew away. It can travel thousands of miles in half a day, and is bound to return in the evening. This is its usual habit. Nothing it wants is the same thing it doesn't get right away. At night, it went to those beds to have fun with women. It played all night long and never slept. It is knowledgeable, detailed, interesting, colorful, and fluent. However, it is shaped like an ape like a mackerel. When the tree began to fall its leaves this fall, it suddenly said sadly, “I am being sued by the mountain gods. I am about to be punished by the gods, and I have sought protection from the gods. I hope to survive.” At the beginning of last month, flames broke out on the stone steps and burned its wooden booklet. Disappointed, it said, “I've lived a thousand years without a son. Now that I have a son, the time to die is coming.” As a result, I looked around at all the women and shed tears for a long time, and also said, “This mountain overlaps and is steep to the extreme. No one has ever been there. No one has ever been there. Even a woodcutter can't see it, and there are many tigers and wolves under the mountain.
Now someone is here. Isn't that God lent his hand to kill me?” After the incident, Ouyang Yi brought back the rare jewels and those women. Some of these women still remember where their homes are.
Ouyang Yi's wife gave birth to a son a year later. He looked like that great white ape. Later, Ouyang Wei was slaughtered by Emperor Chen Wu. He has always been on good terms with Jiang Zong. Mr. Jiang likes that his son is smart and has kept him at home, so he was spared trouble. When that kid grew up, he was certainly famous for a while because he was educated and good at calligraphy.
