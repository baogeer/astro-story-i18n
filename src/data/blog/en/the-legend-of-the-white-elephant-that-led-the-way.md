 Legend has it that the Dai people in several villages in the urban area of Shuangjiang County today migrated from Jinggu.
At the time, they worshiped heaven and earth, prayed for gods, and used a white elephant to guide their way. This white elephant walked in front, and behind it was a vast army of migrants.
People walked for several months, and the elephants didn't stop. Finally, they arrived at the place where Shuangjiang County is located today. The elephants didn't leave. Everyone knows this is a new home where they will take root and thrive.
