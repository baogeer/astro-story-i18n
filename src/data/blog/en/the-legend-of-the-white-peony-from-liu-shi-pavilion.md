 At the end of the Sui dynasty, in the east of Miaoxia Town, Ruzhou, Henan, there was a place where the Liu family lived — the Liu Family House. There is a beautiful and naive girl here. She was born in Shuxiangmendi. She has understood everything since childhood, playing chess, calligraphy, and painting, and is loved by her relatives and neighbors. After that, her parents passed away one after another, and the girl came to live in Chang'an with her brother and sister-in-law, who worked as an official in Chang'an. After the fall of the Sui dynasty, her brother and sister passed away one after another, leaving her alone, with nowhere else to go. They also saw through the red dust, and went out of their own home to become a nun.
When she left home, the girl brought white peonies that she had grown with her own hands in her home to the hermitage as a sign of devotion to Buddhism and cleansing herself. Under her careful management, white peonies have grown very lush and beautiful. One plant has a thousand flowers. The flowers are large, and the petals are heavy. The flowers are white with a hint of redness, and crystal and moisturize, such as beautiful skin, boy's jade face. Viewers all praise and praise their virtues, so every April, many believers visit this temple to worship the Buddha and enjoy offering the Buddha, and the incense is more and more intense.
Therefore, the flower came from “His Excellency Liu's Residence,” hence the name “Liu's House”, also known as “Liu Shi Ge.” Later, this peony variety spread to places such as Tianpeng in Sichuan and Heze in Shandong, where the fragrance spread far and wide.
