 The legend of Baisha Well starts with the ancient Tianxin village. Legend has it that a long, long time ago, outside the wall of the Yang family, where they lived under the large acacia tree above the stockade, a clear spring that was as thick as a bucket of water flowed “loudly”. The clear spring flows from the head of the village to the end of the village, nourishing all the fields in the village, forming one waterfall after another that runs straight to the Turu River. The people who live here rely on this sweet spring given by heaven to transform all of the steep slopes into good fields to preserve the harvest from droughts and floods. What is unusual is that the food and vegetables that are watered and grown with this clear spring are extremely sweet. If you make a pot of white rice, use a pot of pickles and taro soup. It's so tasty that it can support the lazy people in the village. What's even more amazing is that if people in the village want to do a red and white happy event, as long as they go to the water source to burn incense and pray on the first night of work, the cooking utensils and dishes they need will be neatly piled up next to the water source. There is only one rule when returning things: they can only leave the washed dishes in their place; people turn around and can't look back. But curious people want to know how the dishes were taken back; they can't help but look back and take a peek. Although I didn't see anything, I wasn't that clever when I borrowed something again, and slowly I couldn't borrow dishes anymore. The water is also flowing less and less day by day. Later, people who wanted convenience went to the water source to slaughter dogs and slaughter pigs. The area around the water source was full of sewage and smelly. At noon on the first day of June 6, torrential rain, lightning thunder, and loud sounds like beating gongs and drums sounded at the source of the water. It made noise all the way to the highest peak in Heimatan in Houshan, Lhasa, opposite Tianxin Village. Since then, the clear spring under the acacia tree has dried up, and the 100 mu paddy fields in Tianxin Village have to be completely converted to drylands.
After a long time, a hunter discovered by chance that a three-foot-wide, bottomless well had appeared at the summit of Heimatan Peak, which had no water source for five miles in the past few decades. News of the appearance of a well at the summit of Heimatan spread 10 to 10, 10 to 100, making climbing over Heimatan Mountain a shortcut between Menghua and Yongchang. The horse gang and hunters rested at the well to fetch water and cook, which was very convenient. Surprisingly, the spring water “suddenly” surges up from the bottom of the well, and the fine white sand in the well also swirls in the water to the rhythm of the spring. The brush makes a sound as beautiful and beautiful as fairy music. Regardless of the year's droughts and floods, the well is always full without overflowing out of the wellhead. A pair of red sparrows crouched and waited on tree branches all day long, picking out leaves falling from the tree from the well. If you look at a well during the day, the sun is at the bottom of the well; at night, the moon is in the water. The water is clear and white, as sweet as manna, and the fame of Baisha Well spreads around the world like it has grown wings.
One day, the mules and horses were restless when a group of horses were driving by the well for a while. The alert Ma Guotou looked around and found a giant python with a thick bowl hanging above the well. Horrified, Ma Guodou couldn't think much, so he took out his musket and shot at the monkey. Monty Python rolled over for a few times and disappeared. After a few days, when Ma Bang returned to his original path, he found a giant python dead in the well. Just when the horse gang had just crossed the hill, there was a sound of a mountain crashing on the left side of the Lhasa stockade. A white column of water soared from the well, and mudslides surrounded the trees and roared down the back mountain in Lhasa. Straight down the Turu River. Everyone in the stockade was stunned by this sudden change. Just like that, the Baisha Water Well disappeared from the summit of Heimatan Peak.
Within a few days, at the foot of Hetian Xinzhai in Lhasa, on the edge of the Turu River, where debris flows into the river, another strange clear spring appeared. In winter, the spring water is steaming hot, so people can take water to wash their faces and feet to get rid of fatigue; in summer, the well water is cool to the bone, and workers take water to drink, and the taste is better than ice cream in the city. The spring water is densely lined with cabbage and wild celery. Once you get home, the whole family can enjoy a vegetable soup that clears the heat and relieves fatigue. To this day, the old people in the stockade are still saying, “Our Miku is a blessed place; this water is real dragon water!”
