 Legend has it that in the early years of the Southern Song Dynasty, my family lived in Zhuer Alley (near present-day Gulou), a youth store partner named Xu Xian (also named Xu Xuan), and went to his tomb near Lingyin during the Qingming Festival of the year. When I came back, there was a heavy rain, so I hired a boat to go home from a broken bridge. At this point, Baek Su-jeong, who had been practiced by white snakes for thousands of years, and Little Young, who had been trained by blue snakes, became the master and maiden, and the two met Xu Xian. Baek Su-jeong fell for Xu Xian and asked to take a boat home. Xu Xian agreed to her. It was still raining when they arrived ashore at Chung Kinmen. Xu Xian lent her umbrellas to her husband and wife and braved the rain to return to the store herself. The next day, Xu Xian went to the Bai family to pick up an umbrella and was warmly treated by Baek Su-jeong. At last, Xiao Qinger agreed, and the two got married. Soon after their marriage, they moved to Zhenjiang and opened a pharmacy. The couple had a very happy life.
Unexpectedly, Jinshan Temple in Zhenjiang presides over Fa Hai. He learned about Bai Su-jeong's origin. He thought that Jiangnan Buddha would not allow demons to take refuge, so he provoked the relationship between Xu Xian and Baek Su-jeong in the name of Dengmen Huayuan. Seduced by Fa Hai, Xu Xian made Baek Su-jeong drink male yellow wine during the Dragon Boat Festival and discovered that Bai Su-jeong revealed the shape of a white snake after being drunk, and was shocked and passed out. Paik woke up and saved Xu Xian by stealing fairy herbs from Xianshan despite all odds and risks, but after Xu Xian's illness was cured, he was prompted by the French Sea to return his wish at Kanayama-ji Temple. Fa Hai wanted him out of the house and placed him under house arrest. When Baek So-jeong went to Sufu, she made all kinds of prayers, but instead was abused. Helpless, she and Xiao Qinger mobilized the Shrimp-and-Crab Army to pour water from the Yangtze River and flood the golden mountains; the French Sea invaded the Heavenly Heavenly Generals to resist. After a bitter battle between the two sides, Baek Su-jeong lost to Hangzhou. At this point, Xu Xian took advantage of the chaos and fled back to Hangzhou to “meet Bai and Qing at a broken bridge.” Soon after, Baek Su-jeong gave birth to a boy named Xu Shilin. While celebrating the full moon, Fa Hai arrived. He sacrificed a golden bowl and suppressed Baek Su-jeong under the West Lake Leifeng Tower, claiming that she would have to wait until the tower fell to allow her to see the sun again. Little Qinger, who escaped while “being in a bowl,” later practiced deep in the mountains for many years, learned spells, and finally overthrew the Leifeng Tower, rescued the white snake, and ascended to heaven together; Fa Hai, who was defeated, had no place to hide, and finally had to hide in a crab shell. Today, when people eat crabs, there is a “crab monk” in the shell, which is said to be a monk from the French Sea.
