“The Blue Snake”, which Xu Ke filmed in her early years, was created using the essence of the novel by Hong Kong female writer Li Bihua, and talks about the tangled grievances of a ladyboy. Now, after “The Ghost of Qian Nu,” Cheng Xiaodong has filmed “The Legend of the White Snake”, stripping away the “Forbidden Love” in mythology and reinterpreting the story of “The Legend of the White Snake.”
“The Legend of the White Snake” deliberately blurs the line between ladyboys. Perhaps they are all taking the fresh route now, and the charming and seductive image of a fairy is out of date. Wong Sing Yee and Choi Cheuk-yeon can be said to have no demons at all. Instead, they are clean and elegant, and go well with Sven Hsu Sin, played by Lam Fung. In the film, Master Fa Hai, who originally loved to fight mandarin ducks, is in a dilemma.
The most interesting thing is the relationship between Xiaoqing and Neng. The two originally had a crush on each other. In addition, after being able to endure being hurt by the demon, his physique was between humans and demons. He began to experience some of the demon's grievances, so he felt even more sympathetic with Xiaoqing. At the same time, as a proud disciple of Fa Hai, he suddenly became a monster, which also left this master in trouble. Kill or not kill? That's a problem.
Fa Hai, played by Jet Li, and Fa Hai, played by Zhao Wenzhuo in “The Blue Snake,” are very similar. They aren't as harsh on monsters as you might think; although they have principles, they also know how to reflect. The difference is that Jet Li's version of Fa Hai is due to having witnessed the suffering of Baek Su-jeong and Xu Xian's separation from life and death, and the embarrassing situation they were able to endure, only to ask themselves the real significance of conquering demons; while Zhao Wenzhuo's version of Fa Hai is lustful, devious, and more cruel and realistic. Therefore, “The Legend of the White Snake” followed the orthodox path, but there was no shortage of thought, and the actors worked very hard.
Although there are popular elements in this drama that cannot be abandoned, and the drama is not particularly fresh, it is important that the scene is beautiful. “White Snake” took the lead, gave the audience enough visual enjoyment, and the screenplay was weakened by the lack of internet effects.
