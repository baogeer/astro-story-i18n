 Things are rare and precious. Most yaks in the world are black and variegated, and white yaks that survive on the amazing plateau of Tianzhu have naturally become a rare breed of yak in the world. “There are only white yaks in the world, only God bless them”. As a result, the white yak advertisement is famous all over the world. It has become a rare treasure dedicated to humanity in the Tenzhu Plateau.
There was a folk legend about Tianzhu (also known as Huarui) white yaks.
It is said that in ancient times, Huaxiu, the ancestor of the Huarui people, lived under the snowy mountains of Bayankara in the far west. There were many cows and sheep, so the prairie seemed insufficient. Huaxiu discussed with her brother Ah Xiu to find a new grassland. So Huaxiu said goodbye to her brother and prayed that the mountain god would show him a path for him and his tribe.
At this point, a god wearing a war robe and riding a white horse appeared in the sky. He fluttered eastward with a multicolored cloud in the middle of the air. Hua Xiu then bid farewell to her hometown and led the tribe's men, women, and children to set out in the direction that large herds of cows and sheep drifted to the clouds. When the tribe and the cows were about to walk out of a stone gorge, the black yaks made a very painful and sad voice. Everyone knows that these animals, like humans, are difficult to share with their native land. At the time, the whole herd was all in one place, and no one wanted to move forward. When the herders saw this scene, they couldn't help but burst into tears and cry loudly.
At this point, a white yak appeared from the depths of the majestic snowy mountain behind it. It was as white as snow. It was very beautiful and powerful, like a white cloud. The white yak yelled and ran towards the entrance of Shixia. Strangely enough, when they saw the white yaks, the other cows stopped crying and rushed to the Xiakou with the white yaks. The whole tribe started moving forward again.
When people followed the herd out of the Xiakou, there was a bleak scene in front of them. All the other yaks have fallen. The white yak was fighting against a black monster, causing the earth to fly sand and rocks, and the sky was dark. People are terrified and nervous, and they are all worried about white yaks. Suddenly, the black monster screamed and didn't know where it was going. The white yak defeated the monster with its bravery and sharp horns.
An injured calf kept screaming. The white yak saw it. It walked over and used its tongue to lick the poor calf again and again, lick and lick, and the black calf suddenly became white all over.
At this point, horses howled in the sky, and when everyone looked up, the god in the white robe reappeared above their heads. Huaxiu, who was in pain and despair, and the herders of the tribe continued. Let's go, I don't know how many hardships and obstacles we have gone through, and I don't know how far or how long it has been. One day, the mountain god in the sky and the white horse suddenly fell to the ground, and a majestic snowy mountain sprung up in front of people. This is Magya Snow Mountain. Huaxiu told everyone that under this snowy mountain is our hometown! Everyone stopped moving forward and settled here forever. This place has vast grassland, lush grass, murmuring streams, and twinkling mountain springs. It really is a great place to stay for grazing. Since then, Huaxiu and his tribe have been living here very happily. The yaks that drank the water from Ma Ya Snow Mountain are whiter, group after group, like white clouds floating in the sky.
Tianzhu has become the hometown of white yaks, and the white yaks are even more proud of Tianzhu (gorgeous) people. White yak is famous all over the world. It is officially named “Tianzhu White Yak”. As a rare breed of yak in the world, the Ministry of Animal Husbandry Research maintains and selects breeding.
