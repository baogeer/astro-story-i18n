 Legend has it that there is a girl from the Yi ethnic group in Yang Yi. (Her name is Saami, soak walnuts in Yi) looked at the iron walnut trees full of mountains (iron walnuts are “savage fruit” in Yi) and thought, how good would it be if the shell of these walnuts were thin and thick and crushed, it would bring infinite happiness to the people of the family. Since then, the girl began her journey of searching for walnuts. She traveled all over the mountains, mountains and water, drank a sip of mountain spring water when she was thirsty, picked wild fruit to eat when hungry, and wore dozens of pairs of sandals. One day she was tired of walking and fell asleep under an iron walnut tree. In her dream, she saw a tiger talking to her. The tiger said, “If you keep searching like this, you won't be able to find soaked walnuts. Unless you integrate with the iron walnut (savage fruit) tree and sacrifice yourself, you can grow a walnut tree.” After she woke up, she thought this was a hint from the gods. She attached the walnut tree she was sleeping on for the benefit of her family. The family organized the family to search everywhere after seeing the girl not return for a few days. They found large walnuts falling all over the ground. People picked them up and pinched them with their hands. They showed white walnut rice in front of her eyes. The people moved this walnut tree back and planted it, and grafted the “Sami Mother” branch onto the iron walnut (Sami)) In the trees, the mountains are full of wild places, and since then, the ancestors of the Yangyi Yi ethnic group have had large sauerkraut walnuts. She created unlimited wealth for the Yi ancestors. Later, to commemorate this Yi girl, the Yi ancestors called her Saami Mother (“soak a walnut in Yi) and worshiped her as the god of yangyu walnuts. Every year when walnuts are ripe and open, they first sacrificed the walnut god, and the elders of the family were the first to harvest.
Yangyi Yi Autonomous County also designates the walnut maturation season — September 1 of every year as the walnut festival, and a walnut god sacrifice ceremony is held during the walnut festival. After the worship ceremony, the elders or leaders must take the initiative in order to generally reap the benefits.
