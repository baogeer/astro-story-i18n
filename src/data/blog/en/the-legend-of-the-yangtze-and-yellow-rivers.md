One year, there was a great drought on earth, and the land of the mountain gods was in a hurry to the celestial world. For example, Buddha knew that the two dragons were familiar with the world, so Blue and Yellow Dragons arrived on Earth. Soon, the Blue and Yellow Dragon discovered that it turned out to be two “demons” and monsters on earth. It scattered the devil and grandson around the world and set fires everywhere. It also uses magic to make everyone hide a kind of “fire” in their hearts, mischief each other, and disrupt the cool world, so many people adore it and follow it.
Qinghuang Erlong is determined to rid humans of demons and restore the right path on earth. They disguised themselves as two members of the family to get rid of people's nausea and heart disease in the name of treating diseases and evil. Legend has it that “demons” poison people, causing people to suffer from a strange disease. Only by doing something that harms others and oneself can temporarily relieve the pain caused by the illness. When the Blue and Yellow Dragons of the Celestial Realm saw the suffering of the world, they came to the world in disguise as monks to send people medicine containing cinnabar, pearls, and seaweed. After treatment, many people regained their conscience and relieved their pain. From one to ten, to ten to one hundred, people rushed to tell each other. Three months later, tens of thousands of patients had recovered and lived a normal life, and people also had legends about the arrival of two living immortals.
“Demons” heard that someone had broken their spells, so they sent two generals, Crocodile Essence and Toad Elf, to investigate the truth. The toad had a bad stomach and lots of tricks. It dressed up as two patients with a simple yet ferocious crocodile and arrived at the place where the monk lived. When Master Toad saw that the two monks sitting under the shade of the tree had two blue and yellow lights on their heads, they made a plan. It told Crocodile Essence that these were two little monsters. There was no need to worry about it, and let the crocodile sit and rest; it alone could take back the two little woolly gods. As soon as Alligator Essence heard it, Toad Essence couldn't be chosen by itself. As a result, it took its original form and punched the two monks straight with their big, knife-like mouths open.
The two monks knew that the two fairies had arrived. They didn't move. Seeing that the crocodile spirit suddenly fluttered in its original shape, one of them hit a dragon ball the size of a marble at the same time, knocking it over to the ground. When the crocodile saw a bad situation, he turned around and wanted to run away. As soon as the other monk flipped his hand, his brain burst into pieces. But Master Toad took advantage of this opportunity to escape and told the “ghost” that the Blue and Yellow Dragons were saving people.
“Demons” were furious and personally brought 50,000 demon soldiers to challenge. Qinghuang Erlong ordered all healed people to hide and never come out, so they set up forces in the clouds to fight against “demons” in the air. The Blue and Yellow Dragons each cast their mana and fought against demon soldiers for seven days and seven nights. Seeing that there were fewer and fewer demons of their own, they were unwilling to fail, so they discussed a vicious idea. The “Demon” demons divided their demons from north to south into two long snake-like fire dragons and slowly squeezed them to the middle. Wherever they went, everything turned to scorched earth. They tried to burn the blue and yellow dragons and people to ashes.
The Blue and Yellow Dragon had been struggling with the Demon Soldier for a few days and nights, and were exhausted. When they saw the sinister intentions of the “Demons,” they disregarded safety and turned into two cold rivers, blue and yellow, and each left in the face of the two dragons. When they come into contact with the “Fire Dragon,” water and fire meet, stirring up a huge vortex and engulfing the demon soldiers in the river one after another. After three days and three nights, the two “big dragons” gradually crushed the “fire dragon” under their bodies with their huge bodies. The devil was destroyed, and the two dragons also ran out of energy and gradually became embedded underground, forming the current Yangtze River and Yellow River.
