 Once upon a time, there was a mother who sat by the river and embroidered insoles.
Although the insole is small, it can reflect the mother's mood.
Along with the picture in your mind:
Sometimes a magpie falls on a plum branch — “happy on the top of the eyebrow.”
Sometimes it's just a pair of mandarin ducks, bald to old.
Right now, my mother was sitting by the Yangtze River.
Embroider a few small goldfish on the insole, stitch by stitch.
In the hands of the mother, the fish gradually grew and came to life.
In the morning, I planted in the field, and at noon I went home to cook and do laundry,
Mother was tired, and the afternoon sun made her drowsy.
Also, look at that insole. The little goldfish only has two and a half.
The tail of the half of the little fish is connected to the long gold and silver thread in the mother's hand.
That silk thread looks no different from the afternoon sun.
My mother is asleep, and she is still wearing needles and leads in her dreams.
And the child in the dream, holding her mother's silk thread, was toddling on the river. The waves under her feet were like lotus leaves.
At this point, a group of little goldfish came up against the current and saw a sleeping mother sitting by the river with the silk thread shining in the sun's light.
“Oops, look how hard this mother is!”
The little goldfish exchanged their heads and whispered.
“Yes, she is so tired that she is asleep.”
“Look at her hands, they're still shaking.”
“Yes. How can we help her?”
“Mom is so tired, how can we make her sleep a little longer?”
The little goldfish swam and thought. On the surface of the river, golden bubbles of water appeared one by one.
Then, a miracle happened. I saw little goldfish in the river, lined up, wandering and leisurely, one by one, swimming onto the goldfish insole in the mother's hand.
My mother was sleepy. When she woke up from her dream, she saw a few more little goldfish on the insole in her hand.
These little goldfish are swinging their heads and tails one by one, throwing up bubbles and saying hello to their mother.
