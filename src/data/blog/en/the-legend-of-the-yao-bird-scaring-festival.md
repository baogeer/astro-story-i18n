 Every year on the first day of February, it is a bird catching festival for the Yao people. Legend has it that a long, long time ago, the Jianghua Mountain area was densely wooded and very suitable for birds to thrive. Chickens, pheasants, turtles, etc. that eat whole grains have survived the harsh winter. When they saw peaches blooming, they spread their wings, soared into the sky, stood on a high branch, and sang happy songs when they saw people in the mountains; when they saw the golden brown rice seeds in my sister's hands, scattered one by one into the mountains of Hei Wowo, they enjoyed singing even more. Invite your friends and come and have a “dinner”! In this way, they often form groups and fly like dark clouds to cover the sun. As soon as the mountain farmers walk, they cleverly test the “scarecrow,” flying down the slope, and peck incessantly with their sharp mouths. Soon, the mountain soil is ruined. Bird damage has become a serious problem for mountain farmers. There was no harvest in the mountains. Farmers only ate vegetables, and the government's money, water, and food supplies were exhausted. The emperor panicked and hurriedly ordered: “Who stopped bird damage. Enjoy the forest for nine years.”
After the sacraments were handed down, the mountain owners and mountain farmers all thought of a solution. There is an old lady Pan Ying in Panyunzhai who loves to sing. Gengshan people listened to her song, and there was a piece of honey in her mouth; she sang to the mountain springs, and the springs all stopped flowing; she sang to the mountain forest, and the birds were too shy to speak up. Pan Yinggu's song stopped, and the birds didn't leave for a long time. Farmers in the mountains thought: If you want to catch birds, Pan Yinggu must have a way. As a result, men and women from Panyunzhai all came to Ying Gu's wooden house, learned to sing with her, and discussed spreading the song to the farmers in Jiujiuzhai. They agreed that on the last day of the first month of next year, before planting, they would drive the birds away from Jiushan to Baitou Mountain, where there were no spring crops. In Panyunzhai, there is a mountain owner named Pan Abou who has a pair of thrushes. Every morning, he hangs his birdcage on the beams of the wooden house and sings amused. Strangely enough, they also brought in some tits. They stopped at the beach and stayed overnight on the eaves. The mountain owner was very happy: “Haha, Jiujialing is under my name again.” As a result, he hurriedly fixed the book and reported it to the emperor. Emperor Zhu Bijiao ordered all cottage owners to raise their eyebrows. On the last day of the New Year, use birds to attract birds and bring birds from Jiushan and Jiuling to Shiyan Ridge in Baitou Mountain.
When the last day of the New Year arrives, it will be time to plant barley. Early in the morning, the farmers of Jiujiuzhai gathered to sing in Zhaitou. The mountain owner of Kujukuzhai watched a group of birds fly out of the forest and to the cottage. He was very upset. He raised the birdcages, grabbed the mountain farmers, and gathered at Baitou Mountain. The birds also really chased the song and flew to Baekdu Mountain with the people.
On this day, there were many people and lots of birds on Baekdu Mountain. On a clear day, birds fly in rows to shade the singers. On rainy days, birds fly in groups to shield the singers from wind and rain. Mountain farmers sang from early morning until dusk. Birds really forgot to fly back to the forest. When they got tired, they fell on the rocks, and people left quietly. By the next day, the birds flew to the clouds to find songs. They flew around Baitou Mountain, and it seemed like there was an inexhaustible song at the top of the mountain. In this way, the birds stayed in Baitou Mountain for half a year. By the time they woke up, there were only rice poles left on the slope, and mountain farmers had already collected food in Zhaimen.
