 Legend has it that a long, long time ago, Wang Jing ruled the world in Beijing, and the world was peaceful. There is a large dog with a spotted coat in the palace. The name is Dragon Dog, and the king of the review loves it very much. Later, Ziyu rebelled against the world from far away, and sent troops to criticize Wang from time to time. The war has been going on for many years, and Zi Wang has not been defeated. Critic Wang then posted a list to announce to the world: if anyone can defeat Zi Wang, in addition to being rewarded by the feudal government, they will also marry his three most beautiful daughters. None of the Manchu dynasty's junior ministers dared to make the list.
One day, the dragon dog saw Pangwen, jumped up, unraveled Pangwen, and met the critic with his mouth leading up to the temple. When the critic saw that it revealed the list, he asked, “Do you have the skills to fight Zi Wang?” The dragon dog nodded a few times. The commentator was surprised and delighted, so he set up a rich feast and made a feast for the dragon dog. After the Dragon Dog ate the King's feast, they immediately set off and flew away like Teng Yun; it took 7 days and 7 nights to swim through the vast ocean, and finally arrived in Zi Wang's country and continued to break through the Golden Palace. Zi Wang was sitting in the morning. He saw a dragon dog. He was very happy to know that it was Wang Yu's pet dog. He said to the ministers, “Look, the dragon dogs raised by Jang Wang have now all fled to our country. It can be seen that King Gong has lost the hearts of the people at home, and victory is not far off for us.” Since then, Ziyu has kept dragon dogs in the palace and kept them close to them, inseparable.
One day, when Zi Wang went to the toilet, the dragon dog took advantage of his lack of preparation and rushed up. He immediately bit off Lord Zi's head and flew back home to meet the critic king in the morning. The critic summoned ministers to identify this head. When I learned that this was indeed the head of the Purple King, I reviewed Wang Daxi as if he had taken away a problem from his heart, and arranged a big feast to reward the dragon dog, and gave the dragon dog many gold and silver treasures. However, dragon dogs don't see these gold and silver treasures; they think they want to get married to a princess. The critic knows this, but it's also very difficult: how can a princess marry a dog? As a result, I had the intention of repenting of my marriage, and I was bored and unhappy all day long. When the wise princess learned about this, she sincerely advised her father: “You should act according to the previous example; don't break your words. “Deception will be laughed at by the world, to the detriment of the country.” Also, they said they would like to marry a dragon dog. The critic accepted the princess's advice and personally arranged the marriage between the princess and the dragon dog.
Since getting married, the couple have loved each other, and their relationship has always been very good, and everyone thought it was strange. One day, the princess's mother talked with the princess about this, and only then did the princess reveal the dragon dog's secret. She said to her mother, “The dragon dog is a dog during the day, but at night he is a beautiful man; the speckled hairs on his body turn into a colorful dragon robe at night, so the two of us have a very good relationship.” Mother said, “Since dragon dogs can become humans at night, they should also become adults during the day. Then someone will inherit your father's career.” The queen told the critic about this. The critic said, “Can dragon dogs become humans? That's great! I sent him to Nanjing to become king.” The queen told the princess what she meant, and the princess discussed it with the dragon dog. Dragon Dog Called Princess and steamed him in a steamer for seven days and seven nights, then he was able to remove the hair from his body and become an adult. If the princess says the dragon dog — do the same. However, after steaming for six days and six nights, the princess couldn't hold back her anxiety and was afraid that her husband would be steamed to death, so she opened the lid. Ahhh! I saw that the dragon dog had turned into a handsome and powerful young man. Legend has it that this young man was the original ancestor of the Yao people. However, due to insufficient days of steaming, the hair on the head, underarms, and feet never came off. However, the lid was removed, so I couldn't steam it anymore; I had to wrap the hairy head and shin with a cloth. As a result, people of the Yao ethnic group have wrapped their heads in cloth for generations, and it continues to this day. After the dragon dog became a man, the critic made good on his promise and named him the king of Panjiu. They also have lots of gold and silver jewels, silk and satin, for the princess's dowry.
