 A long, long time ago, humans, like animals, couldn't talk; they were all stupid and ignorant. You didn't agree with me, I didn't agree with you. They fought and killed each other at every turn. The whole world was in chaos. To change this situation, separate humans and animals. Separating cleverness from foolishness, Tenjin placed four bowls on the highest peak in Liangshan, Sichuan: a gold bowl, a silver bowl, a bronze bowl, and a wooden bowl. The inside is filled with stupid water, bad water, good water, and smart water, respectively. Both animals and humans have to drink, but they can only drink one type of water. As to which bowl contains what kind of water, no one knows, but everyone wants to drink Zhishui.
There is an orphan Yi ethnic group in Liangshan, Sichuan. He heard that there is a giant frog in Qionghai Lake and knows the secret of those bowls of water. As a result, he left his hometown and walked towards Qionghai Lake. Coincidentally, the orphan had just run to the edge of Qionghai Lake and was meeting a black crow bullying the god frog. He hurriedly rushed to drive away the black crow and save the wounded giant frog. The orphan explained his intention to the frog and asked for advice. The frog held back the pain and whispered, “You just came. The battle just happened just now because I didn't want to tell the Black Crow the secret of the water. To thank you for saving my life, I'll tell you the secret. The wooden bowl contains Zhishui, and if you drink it, you will become the smartest person in the world, and you can dominate everything. That water can also cure diseases and save the wounded, so please bring back a sip.” “I'll go with you on my back. Heal your wounds first, then I'll drink.” “No, black crows will find out. Go ahead, I'm here waiting for you.”
The orphan said goodbye to the giant frog and continued to run to the highest peak in Liangshan. Seeing all kinds of animals and people mixed together, pressing a large sheet in black, it looked like it was surrounding those four water bowls, but no one dared to drink it first. The ferocious black crow circled at a low altitude, spying on the motion, and was always ready to dive down and grab Zhi Shui to drink. The orphan looked up at the black crow, and couldn't wait to squeeze it through the crevice, pick up the wooden bowl, and drink it all at once. “Ah, I'm smart!” The orphan was overjoyed and excited, jumping up and shouting loudly. But when he realized the empty wooden bowl in his hand, he stopped all of a sudden. After a while, he turned over the wooden bowl and slapped it back and forth, hoping to shoot magic water to heal the monster frog. After being patted for half a day, I couldn't pour out a drop of water other than the “gurgling” sound made by the wooden bowl.
Oh my god, the monster frog is still waiting for me to apply Zhishui to it. What can I do about this? The orphan was very sad. He sobbed, held an empty wooden bowl, and walked to Qionghai Lake one by one in pain. Halfway through, tears filled the wooden bowl and spilled out. Ah, so many tears! They all say that tears come from the heart. It is the most precious water, and it may heal the trauma of giant frogs. With a glimmer of hope and a feeling of being made up, the orphans sped up their steps. When the orphans arrived at the edge of Qionghai Lake, it was already dusk. They had searched all over the lake, and there were no shadows of giant frogs. Suddenly, I heard a crow pecking in my head. He looked up and saw that on a dead tree next to him, the black crow was maiming and tearing apart the poor giant frog. The orphan opened his mouth in astonishment and had no idea what to do. Suddenly, with his ingenuity, he smashed the wooden bowl against the tree. The tears in the wooden bowl were like flames sprinkling on the black crow's body. In an instant, the black crow was covered in fire, like a ball of fire, flying away from the dead tree. It flashed a few times in the air, and “whistled” and fell into Qionghai Lake. At this point, the wooden bowl and the giant frog fell into the orphan's arms at the same time. Looking down, I couldn't help but be amazed. The five organs and six organs of a monster frog. It has already been eaten by a black crow; there is only one turquoise giant frog skin left. “Tenjin, save God Frog!” The orphan carried the skin of a giant frog in his hand and cried like a rain of tears.
It turns out that the black crow saw that the orphan snatched Zhishui, and knew it was God's frog's instructions. As a result, it flew to the edge of Qionghai Lake and vented the giant frog. Because they were seriously injured and unable to resist for a long time, the giant frog easily became the food of the Black Crow's mouth. To mourn the god frog, the orphan put the god frog skin on a wooden bowl and made a moonlike two-string piano, named the “Russian Moon Piano.” (Russian bar - meaning god frog in Yi) After that, every time he missed the god frog, he picked up the Russian moon piano and stirred the strings hard to talk about the pain, remorse, and sorrow in his heart.
Until now, young Yi guys have played the moon piano to the point of intensity. The hand style, posture, and rhythm of playing the strings are the same as when people are in pain and remorse, hitting their chest and tearing their hearts out. Therefore, some people also call the moon piano a sad piano.
