 There is a legend about the origin of the Dragon Boat Festival. Legend has it that in ancient times, the world was very chaotic, and frequent banditry caused unspeakable suffering to the people. One day, a group of bandits saw an old lady on the road, carrying an older child but walking with a child. They thought it was very strange. After asking, they learned that the old lady was carrying an orphan from another family and holding her own grandson. The bandit leader, who is also an orphan, was moved by the old lady's kindness and told the old lady that they were going to raid the cottage on early May 5. They wanted her to hang an iris leaf on the door when she got home, and tie a multicolored thread to the children in the family so that they could avoid a disaster. The kind old lady went back to the village and told the townspeople to hang irises on the door. All the children had five-color strings attached to their hands. On the 5th day of the 5th day, the bandits actually arrived at the cottage. They saw that the Yi family all had five-color strings attached to their hands. There was no way to get started, so the Yi people escaped a major disaster. To commemorate this incident, every time in early May of the lunar calendar, the Wuyi family wanted to kill a chicken to kill a chicken and lay an iris at the gate to hang irises to tie the children with a five-color line.
