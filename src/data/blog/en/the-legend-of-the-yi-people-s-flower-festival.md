 Every year on February 8, it is the Yi Flower Arrangement Festival, also known as the Lantana Flower Festival. How did it come about?
A long time ago, the entire world was flooded and the land was flooded. There were only two brothers and sisters left in the world. They hid in a big gourd, and the mouth of the gourd was sealed with wax. This gourd has been drifting in the flood for many days, and I don't know where it has floated. One day, the flood began to recede, revealing the top of the mountain and the treetops. This gourd fell on the branch of a big tree. The eagle is flying at the top of the mountain. It hears the sound in the gourd; it's a human race inside! The eagle caught the gourd and placed it on the top of the mountain. The gourd is now slender; it was caught by an eagle back then. The rat drilled out of the ground, and he heard the sound inside the gourd; it was a human race inside. The rat gnawed through the wax, gnawed through the wax, and a glow appeared in the gourd. The brother and sister climbed out in line with the light. In a desolate world, there are only them left.
The elder Golden Turtle came to his brother and sister and said, “In order to spread the race, only you became husband and wife.” The brother and sister answered, “How can one parent become a family?” The elder Golden Turtle ordered, “There are only two of you; you must become a family.” The brother and sister are both very embarrassed. My brother thought about it and said, “We both burn an incense on the other side and ask the gods of heaven and earth. If cigarettes are raised and wrapped around each other, we can only become a family.” As a result, the cigarette went up, and they actually went around together, but the younger sister insisted on not getting married, and the older brother said, “The two of us are each grinding at the top of the mountain. If we grind the two dishes together, we can only become a family.” They followed suit again; as a result, the two plates were brought together in an unbiased manner. That is, my younger sister still refuses to get married. My sister said, “We roll the dustpan once. If the two dustpans are not biased and put together face to face, we can only become a family.” As a result, the two dustpans fit together. Old Golden Turtle said, “If you have anything else to say, let's get married.” There was no point in trying again, and my sister also said, “How can we become a family without parents' lives or media as proof.” The old man Golden Turtle replied, “This burst of human smoke has become extinct. Where can I find someone? Look, that pine tree is your father, that Evergreen tree is your mother, and that plum tree over there is your matchmaker. Let's just get married.” The brother and sister couldn't hold on anymore. In order to pass on the race, they finally became a couple.
After 12 months, my younger sister became pregnant and had a baby. Unfortunately, she didn't take human form; it was a meatball. What to do? The younger sister complained about the older brother, and the older brother complained about the Elder Golden Turtle. At this point, the golden turtle showed up again. He smiled and came to congratulate the brothers and sisters: “You have already given birth to a race.” After all, pull out the sword, split the meat mass with one sword, and 50 young boys and 50 virgins appeared. There was still blood on the skin, which was wrapped in a race. The old golden turtle picked a sword and threw it on a small tree next to it. Since then, the tree has bloomed red lanceolate flowers. This day is February 8 of the lunar calendar.
After 50 boys and girls are born, how can a younger sister nurture these children alone? At this point, birds and animals all came to help; they took the children one by one. We, the Yi people, are fed wild horses, so we Yi people don't eat horse meat now. Fifty boys and girls married each other when they grew up. Today, there are dozens of ethnic groups including Yi, Han, Miao, Hui, Tibetans, Bai, and Dai. All nationalities are descendants of the same parents. After 50 boys and girls grew up, they couldn't find their parents, so they set out to find one.
The first thing they came across was an earthen bee. They asked, “Brother Earthworm, have you seen our parents?” The earthen bee was flying around buzzing and ignoring anyone. One person knocked it down with a branch, and his back was broken. Another person picked it up and tied it up with a ponytail to let it fly away. Since then, the earthen bee has become a thin waistband; it stings when it sees people. The second time I met Pines, they asked, “Grandpa Pine, where did our parents go?” Pine answered impatiently, “I didn't see it. My parents fell on my shoulder and crushed them to death.” People say, “OK, when Ding prospers up, cut down one tree when you grow one.” Therefore, pine trees are used to build houses today.
The third time I met was a palm tree. People asked, “Brother Palm Tree, have you seen our parents?” The palm tree answered, “I didn't see it. If I saw it, I would peel their skin.” People say, “Huh, one day when there will be more people, I'll definitely have to peel your skin.” So now I have to peel a layer of brown tree bark every year.
People continued to walk forward. When they met a Luohanshan tree, people asked, “Big Brother Luohanshan, can you see our parents?” Luo Hanshan answered, “I was just here to cool off!” People were happy. The parents found it. People thanked Luo Hanshan and said, “Thank you. If you break a branch and get cut down in the future, the roots will grow again.” As a result, now even if there is only a little bit left, the Luohansa will sprout again.
People continued to walk forward and met a little bee. People asked, “Little bee, can you see our parents?” The bee said, “I just passed through that girl.” People are happy to say, “Kind bees, if people grow in the future, you can live with them.” The bee answered, “That's great. If I could live with people, I'd have to feed people a little bit every year.” So now people have to cut honey once a year.
People chased to the side of the pond, met a willow tree, and asked, “Sister Willow, can you see our parents?” Willow answered, “I saw it; I was just draining water.” People excitedly say, “With good willows, you will definitely have good results and take root everywhere.” Since then, willow trees have been very easy to plant, and they can survive no matter how they are planted. People kept rushing forward. Unfortunately, they never met their parents. They only saw red lantus flowers all over the hillside, so they settled down in a place full of lantus flowers. Every February 8 of the lunar calendar is our Yi national holiday. On this day, men, women, and children danced together, sang mountain songs, killed chicken and cooked meat to celebrate, and planted lantus flowers everywhere. This is the origin of the Lantana Festival.
