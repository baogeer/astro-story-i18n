 A long time ago, there was a man named Wei Dagui in Zhuangxiang who was minister under a Turkish emperor. Da Gui is not very old, but he is very knowledgeable. Moreover, he is very concerned about the suffering of the Zhuang people. The emperor gave him all his salary back to his hometown and distributed it to the people. He had nothing left. One year, there was a great drought in Zhuangxiang. The townspeople asked Dagui to give the Turkish emperor free of royal food. Da Gui knelt down and said, “The people of Zhuangxiang had no harvest. I visited with him for a thousand years.” Da Gui accompanied him to Zhuangxiang. He saw that the fields were cracked, the ears were burnt, and a group of people with yellow skin and thin skin knelt on the mountain path to suffer. Da Gui knelt down and said, “See you in a thousand years, hopefully free of food.” The Turkish emperor had no choice but to get rid of the emperor in his hometown. After the attack, he hated Da Gui to the bone, but due to Da Gui's popularity, he couldn't find any trouble to get rid of him, so he devised a toxic plan and plotted to frame Da Gui.
One day, he summoned Da Gui to his front: “Da Gui, you've always been very capable, and now I want you to do something for me.” After listening to Da Gui, he knew that the Turkish emperor had no good intentions, but he calmly said, “Please come over a thousand years old.” Emperor Tu squinted and said, “I'm building a pavilion. There is a shortage of tiles. I heard that the skin can protect against the cold and heat, waterproof and fireproof, and is durable. I want you to get me 900 zigzag skins to use as tiles within two months. I would greatly appreciate it. If it doesn't work, it must be severely punished.” Da Gui easily answered, “When that happens, please ask Chitose to personally order and inspect the goods under the city gate.”
A month has passed, and Da Gui hasn't moved. Fifty-nine days have passed, and Da Gui still hasn't moved. When the deadline was reached, Da Gui summoned 900 powerful men. Each brought a catty of glutinous rice, one catty of rice wine, and one catty of pepper noodles to the foot of the Imperial City, put the pot on a stand to cook glutinous rice, cook chili vegetable soup, take off their coats one by one, sit shirtless, and sit shirtless under the city gate drinking, drinking chili soup, and eating glutinous rice. Just when they were full of wine and full of sweat, the Turkish emperor arrived in a large sedan in eight. Da Gui hurriedly stepped forward and said, “The goods have already been delivered to Chitose, but all of these poor bones are leaking water. Can they be used?” When the Turkish Emperor got out of the car and took a look, their dark and red skin was all wet. It looked like it had been taken out of water, and the smell was pungent. The Turkish emperor covered his nose with his hands, frowned, and stepped back a few steps: “It's leaking and smelly. You can't use it, you can't use it.” After speaking, I transferred the car back to the palace.
One plan didn't work, and two more were born. It's almost the Ching Ming Festival, and the Turkish emperor replaced Da Gui with a very sincere expression: “Da Gui, you've always been very smart. Now I have to do one thing you have to do.” As soon as Da Gui heard it, he knew that the Turkish emperor had bad intentions, but Da Gui was not afraid to say, “Please speak clearly to the emperor.” Emperor Tu squinted and said, “I need a pig that is as heavy as the mountain behind the palace to sacrifice my ancestors during the Ching Ming Festival. You can get it for me within a month. It will be very rewarding. If it expires, it will be severely punished.” Da Gui laughed very easily: “Easy to do, easy to do.”
Twenty days have passed, and Da Gui has not moved. Thirty days have passed, and Da Gui still hasn't moved at all. The deadline has passed. The Turkish emperor sent troops to catch Da Gui. Da Gui carried a large scale and soldiers to visit the Turkish emperor: “The emperor has many pig heads that are bigger than the top of the mountain, but I don't know how heavy the mountain behind the palace is. Please ask the emperor to use this scale to name it. I can go back and carry the pig head.” “This...” the Turkish Emperor was speechless.
The Turkish emperor saw that Dagui was smart and that staying in the palace was the root cause; he had to get rid of him, so he came up with a poison plan. One day, he called Da Gui to his front: “Da Gui, you've always been smart and capable. In a month, the empress will be on the moon. I heard that Zhuangjia's male eggs are very nutritious. You brought me 490 male eggs in 20 days, which is very rewarding.” Da Gui agreed in full. After the deadline was reached, the Turkish Emperor sent troops to arrest Da Gui. Da Gui hurriedly said, “I'm very sorry. I was in confinement. According to the Zhuang family's rules, I have to take care of myself for 7, 7, 49 days before I can go out. I will send male eggs to the emperor at that time.” Soldier shouted: “How can men in the world have children?” “Why do roosters lay eggs when men don't give birth to children?” Soldiers are speechless. I had to go back and report it to the emperor. The emperor was furious and ordered the arrest of Dagui.
When the people of the Zhuang family heard the news, they immediately took it to Gui, and the maple forest on the mountain was hidden. The imperial army went up the mountain to search and was heavily surrounded. They saw that there was sticky rice left in the forest, but no one saw anyone. The emperor ordered the mountain to be set on fire. This day happened to be on March 3. After the imperial soldiers left, the townspeople went up the mountain to find Da Gui's body in a maple tree cave where they couldn't get along. Everyone buried him in tears. Men, women, and children cried in front of the grave. Cry! Cry! Tears were shed on the tomb, and a small green maple tree and a bunch of red orchids grew on the tomb. To commemorate Da Gui, the townspeople also built a temple next to the tomb, called “Da Gui Tang.” Because Da Gui liked to drink alcohol and eat glutinous rice when she was born, she defeated the emperor by drinking and eating glutinous rice, and was later murdered because she couldn't take out male eggs. Therefore, by March 3, every household in Zhuangxiang brought rice wine, glutinous rice, and cooked eggs to sacrifice in front of Da Gui's tomb. Just as people were falling into mourning, the sky suddenly thundered, and a large five-colored snake burst out of the temple. This giant snake nodded to the townspeople and went straight to the palace to bite the emperor to death.
Since then, to commemorate Dagui, the Zhuang family has set up greenhouses in every village and village since the 3rd day of March, because legend has it that undead people who died outside cannot enter their homes, so people have had to put up sacrifices such as five-color glutinous rice under the shed to sacrifice Dagui's undead, and sing praises and thanks for Da Gui around the shed. Passed down from generation to generation, the current custom of catching the March 3rd Song has formed.
