 “Skeet dance” originated from the wooden shackle military training method of toast in Guangxi in the Ming dynasty. During the Jiajing years of the Ming dynasty, Japanese pirates invaded the coast of Jiangsu and Zhejiang. The Ming dynasty wanted Guangxi to send troops, and Guangxi sent 300 soldiers from Tuzhou to destroy the Japanese invaders. Toast's Luo Wujie immediately recruited 300 people for training. The first time the recruits were training, it was difficult to keep the same pace. Toast ordered to use wooden shackles to clamp the recruit's feet. The three formed a team, and the nine formed a team. The steps were unified; they could only move forward; they were not allowed to retreat. After strict training, this team had unique tactics, bravery and tenacious, and contributed to defeating the Japanese pirates. Since then, the three-person wooden military training law has become popular among the public, and it evolved into a “three people wear sneakers” competition. Three people wear sneakers, a sports competition program that has undergone artistic processing and developed into a “sneaker dance.”
Today, the Zhuang people's “sneaker competition” consists of several people in a team, and everyone wears a pair of long sneakers. Participants must keep the same pace and do their best. If any one in the team makes a mistake or doesn't cooperate with the other team members, the whole team will lose the game. “Sneaker Dance”, on the other hand, is an upgraded version of “Sneaker Competition”. Using the beating of props and the accompaniment of music, the performers must display their beautiful dance posture in a coordinated manner.
The “sneaker dance” generally consists of a group of three men and women from the Zhuang ethnic group, wearing six nearly two-meter-long sneakers and walking along a beautiful melody. The walking process is dangerous and funny. As the music changes, Zhuang girls and guys can also cheerfully dance in modern three-step dancing in long sneakers. The audience was happy, while the dancers received thorough training. With the widespread development of the “sneaker dance”, the clever and ingenious Zhuang people also decorated the two shoeboards of the sneaker dance, greatly increasing the level of viewing during the performance. During the Asian Games, “Skeet Dance” participated in 14 shows in Beijing and was warmly welcomed by people from all walks of life. The novel and unique performance and wonderful fun made the audience laugh, and applause and laughter erupted.
As a culture, “sneaker dancing” not only enriches the cultural and recreational life of the people of Zhuangxiang, but also has had positive and far-reaching social effects by taking on the heavy responsibility of promoting the Zhuang national culture and boosting the Zhuang economy. It uses a microcosm of history to show the image of the Zhuang people's high level of organization, discipline, and heroism, and good fighting, and invigorates people's sense of social responsibility, patriotism, and nationality. With the improvement of social material and spiritual life and the influence of ethnic cultural integration, “sneaker dancing” will be further developed, and its social function and value will surely receive more and more attention.
