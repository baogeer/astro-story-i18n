 In Shexiang, they also have a folk festival — Please God Festival. This is every year on September 15, in the Fuhu area of Luoyuan County, the Shemin have to set up a stage at the head of the village and happily invite the three immortals home to watch the movie.
So why did they just invite the three gods and not the other gods? Who are the Three Great Gods? Why do you prefer September 15?
Speaking of which, it's a really sad and moving story. It was still in ancient times. There was a place called Shenyan in the middle reaches of the Daijiang River in Luoyuan Fuhu Village. There are three ghost gates: the upper reaches are cliffs, and the water flow is rapid. A large reef with a height of 100 feet and a width of 10 feet stands in the center of the river. When the river flows here, it causes huge waves. Locals call this place “fairy sorrow,” which means that fairies also attack this place. In the middle reaches of the sunken rock, there are many caves at the bottom of the river. The water that comes out of it forms a vortex one by one on the surface of the river. The boat has been stuck here and can't get out for a few days. When it reached the lower reaches of the sunken rock, it was also heavily foggy, and the white area was vast. There was no smoke, no sunlight penetrating, and the wind didn't dissipate, making it hard to tell the difference between east, west, north, and south. These three ghost gates have caused great inconvenience to the lives and travel of the people of Shexiang.
According to rumors, there were three brothers from the Gao family who lived in Sheshan Village at the time. The oldest was called Gao Xin, the second was Gao De, and the third was Gao Lu. These three brothers have been smart and intelligent since childhood; what they learn will do. It is said that their brother learned the art of stonemasoning from the immortals. According to the fairy, the cow carved out by the boss Gao Xin would cultivate the land, the dog that the older brother Gao De chiseled would hunt, and the chicken carved by the older three could tell the news.
When these three brothers saw the inconvenience caused by Shen Yan to the people of Shexiang, they decided to solve the problems for the people of Shexiang. They knew that wooden boats and bamboo rows couldn't pass through the three hurdles, so they chose three boulders and carved out three stone boats. The boat of the boss is like a barn, the boat of the second is like a cylinder, and the boat of the third is like a pot.
On September 15, the three brothers prepared tools and dry food, and set out in their own boats. They first reached the first level. The three brothers jumped on the big reef and made concerted efforts. It took more than a month to finally remove the huge reef. Just as they filled the three stone boats with gravel and were preparing to fill the vortex at the second gate, a huge bad wave suddenly came rushing towards the big brother's boat. They only heard a loud noise. The older brother and his boat turned to gravel and sank to the bottom of the river.
With grief, the two brothers continued to sail to the second crossing. When they arrived at the vortex zone, despite fatigue, the brothers filled the hole with gravel brought from the boat. Ten days passed, half a month passed, and they finally filled the holes one by one, and the vortex disappeared one by one. Just as they were sailing to the last gate, they discovered that there was another hole that had not been filled. At this point, they had already used up the gravel they had brought. What should I do? They thought of their older brother; if his boat were there, the gravel on the boat would be enough. Too bad my brother is gone. What to do? The water that had broken into the cave rumbled outward, and a large vortex on the river kept spinning. What should I do? At this point, the older brother Gao De immediately took the opportunity and raised the hammer in his hand and cut through the bottom of his stone boat. The stone boat and Gaode sank to the bottom of the river as the vortex rotated, and crushed it strictly and firmly to the last broken hole. The vortex was gone, and the older brother Gaode was gone. Mr. Gao Lu was deeply saddened.
There is one last hurdle left, advance or retreat? Come on, can you solve the problem by being alone? If I don't get in, can I be worthy of my two older brothers? In order to fulfill the original wishes of the three brothers, the elder chose to move on. When the old three arrived downstream, it was shrouded in smoke, and he couldn't reach for five fingers. I couldn't tell the southeast from the north to the west, and I had a hard time finding a way to go out to sea. Gao Lu thought to himself, if a lighthouse were to be erected here, wouldn't it be possible to indicate the direction of the incoming and outgoing ships? But it takes stone to build a lighthouse. Where can I find so many stones on this vast river? He thought to himself that there was only one way, and that was to turn his stone boat into a lighthouse. As a result, he erected his stone boat, drilled it with a thousand hammer, and finally dug out a lighthouse. The old three were swallowed up by the river in the end because they didn't have a boat to return home...
The three brothers from the Gao family have given their precious lives to the problems of the people of Shexiang leaving the river. To commemorate the work of the three brothers, local residents of Shexiang built a “Dawang Temple” at the head of the village to worship the three brothers in the Gao family, and designated September 15 as the “Please God Festival.” Every day, women, and children of Shexiang have to come to worship in front of the temple and invite the three brothers to “return” to the world to “watch a movie” to express the admiration of the people of Shexiang.
