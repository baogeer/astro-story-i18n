 The Wuhan Yangtze River Bridge lies between Turtle Mountain and Snake Mountain, and is extremely majestic and spectacular. Kameyama Snake Mountain, which is the north-south base of the bridge, also has a magical legend.
A long time ago, the two Turtles and Snakes under Dragon King in the East China Sea were at odds. They looked down on each other; they all boasted about their great abilities, and often quarreled. One day, the two of them got into a fight again. The turtle would be holding a sword, and the snake would hold up a spear and kill them, causing the waves in the Dragon Palace to stir. Even the old dragon king's throne was shaken, and some fish and shrimp were injured by mistake. The old dragon king was furious and sent two princes to tie them up and go to the edge of the mortal Yangtze River. One town was on the banks of Hanyang and the other on the banks of Wuchang, so they both thought about it here. Since then, they have turned into two mountains, turtles, snakes, and confronted each other across the river.
Even though they were punished, they still disagreed with each other. Across the river, they still gambled with each other to see who grew the mountain quickly. As a result, Mount Turtle and Snake grew bigger day and night. The top of the two mountains did its best to extend into the river, and they all wanted to quickly reach across the river to bite each other. This is a bad thing. The waterway of the Yangtze River is getting narrower and narrower. After less than half a year of hard work, it is already almost as narrow as Hanshui. There is poor ventilation in the upper reaches. Many villages in good fields have been flooded by the river, and people are full of complaints.
As soon as Lu Dongbin woke up in Lui Zu's house, he learned about this. I thought to myself, the two of them have done such a good job. If they want to touch their heads, why don't they just cut off the big river, and all the 10,000 hectares of land upstream become the country of Thailand! He turned into an old drug picker, carrying a silver hoe on his shoulder, walked out of Lu Zu Ge, reached the middle of Snake Mountain, held a silver hoe in his hand, and dug a hoe at the snake's waist. The snake spread all over its body. The snake bone in its lower back was cut off. The pain was unbearable. He had to quickly shrink his head and retreat all the way to the shore of Wuchang, where he could no longer move. So in the middle of Snake Mountain, it's still much lower, and it looks like it's broken.
Lu Dongbin also crossed the river to Hanyang. He invited skilled craftsmen to build a “Yu Wang Temple” overnight at the top of Guishan Mountain, and invited Dayu to live in the temple to calm down Turtle General. The turtle will be crushed and numb all over its body and slowly shrink to the shore of Hanyang. When it first shrinks to the shore, there is no strength to shrink, so far Kameyama has left a rock head in the river, which is the turtle head.
Turtles and snakes will let go of the waterway. The Yangtze River rolls and flows eastward for thousands of miles, and will no longer be hindered.
