 A long, long time ago, Iron Coffin Gorge was exported to the broad valley area. Dozens of townspeople worked hard to cultivate here and lived a stable life without worrying about eating or drinking.
In a cave on the east bank of the canyon, there is a yellow dragon. After more than 300 years of training, it gets along well with nearby townspeople, helps each other in case of trouble, and always responds when saved. The local people greatly admire him. Every time there is a drought, groups of people come to the cave to seek rain. In order to meet the needs of the townspeople, Huang Long spewed out a wave of purple fog from the cave. The purple fog rose to the sky and quickly condensed into a cloud of dark clouds. Tilting, thunder rumbling, and heavy rain poured down, making the canyon's crops rich in grains. In order to appreciate Huanglong's grace, the townspeople set up a temple and sacrificed in front of Huanglong Cave.
I don't know how many more years have passed since I lived in an oolong in a West Bank cave. After more than 100 years of practice, it began to have some teachings. Seeing that the incense was very popular in front of Huanglong Cave, there were many sacrifices, and I couldn't help but be fascinated. I thought to myself: Huh! How talented Huang Long is. You respect it so much that you don't look me in the eye. OK, let me show you my spells. Then you will come and beg me. As a result, Oolong began to gain strength. At this point, when it happened to be in a drought, it took the opportunity to refuel the fire and spit the flames, causing the fields to break, the crops to turn yellow, and many people and animals became sick due to the heat. When Huang Long learned about this incident, he was busy spreading rain to relieve the danger, and Oolong's plot failed. The contest was repeated a few times, and Huanglong turned the risk into relief. Oolong saw Huanglong opposing it everywhere, and was very annoyed.
One day, Oolong came outside Huanglong Cave and threatened Huang Long and said, “Let go of the cave, or else you will be buried in the cave. You will regret it!” Faced with the unreasonable threat of Oolong, Huang Long helped with good words: 'Practice and Facilitate the People' is our true essence; it is wrong for you to do evil and harm people like this. The arrogant Oolong shouted: “Stop talking nonsense, let me show you how good I am today!” As soon as the speech fell off, Oolong spewed a red light. Following a loud noise, the temple immediately collapsed, and even the entire cave shook. Enraged, Huang Long stormed out of the cave and fought with Oolong. The two dragons took to the air and extended their claws to each other. You caught me and bit me, and each performed a trick. It was a vicious battle, but the killing was dark. After 981 round, Oolong gradually became less able to do as much as Huanglong's training and kung fu, and the battle went back and forth, and his scaly armor was knocked down all over the canyon. So far, people have seen the multicolored stones in the Ning River. Legend has it that they were transformed into scaled armor that fell when two dragons fought.
Just as Oolong was defeated and captured, and it just so happened that Master Guanyin was there, inquiring about the original situation, reprimanded Oolong, and dissuaded Huanglong from showing mercy to his men. He wanted the two dragons to get along well, live in the same cave, practice fairy ways together, and exterminate harm for the people.
The townspeople saw that Huanglong dedicated himself to spreading the rain for the people. Oolong also changed the evil spirit, and raised funds to rebuild the temple and named it “Double Dragon Temple”. The two dragons cast rain during droughts; when flooded, the weather cleared up. The townspeople have cultivated the canyon to the verdant, the crops are beautiful, and their lives are getting better day by day.
Ming dynasty writer Yang Shen (Hao Sheng An), when passing through the Three Gorges, heard the townspeople praising Ssangyong for their achievements in benefiting the people, and gladly left behind the four words “protect the gorge and protect the people.” People made these four characters into a golden plaque and hung it in Shuanglong Temple to honor the two dragons. For this reason, this section of the canyon is also called “Double Dragon Gorge.”
——From “The Legend of the Three Gorges”
