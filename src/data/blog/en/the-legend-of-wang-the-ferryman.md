description: "At the end of the Tang dynasty, Wang Yanzhang, a young man from Shandong, went into exile at the Nushan Lake ferry crossing to replace the seriously ill old boatman Wei Fangdu. After Wei died, he took over the ferry and was famous all over the world for his strength and ability to hold cows aboard the boat. Houliang Wang Zhu Quanzhong visited with admiration and saw that he was loyal, kind, and interested in recruiting. The story praises the folk wisdom of being kind to help others find opportunities at last."

 There is a ferry crossing in the southwestern part of Mingguang Mountain Lake. The south bank is the northern village of Shaogang Mountain, the north bank is the harvest polder, and a key point for merchants from the Huaibei River to go to Liuhe and Pukou. This ferry crossing is very famous from ancient times to the present, and is known as the “King Ferry”. This “Wang Ferry” is named after a person and has a history of more than 1,000 years.
Legend has it that in 906 AD, Shandong started water, and a young man named Wang Yan came into exile here to seek a living by transitioning south. The person ferrying on the lake was a lonely old man. He was sixty to eight years old. He is known as the Wei ferry. Wang Yanzhang arrived at the ferry crossing this day. Since no one was coming, Wei Shuyu was already resting in the cabin to take a nap. Wang Yanzhang politely called out, “Grandpa, I want to make a transition.” Wei Pangdu got up to help and sent Wang Yanzhang for the transition.
As the boat reached the center of the lake, Wei Shuang had a severe cough and then vomited blood in his mouth. Wang Yanzhang hurriedly went over to support Wei's ferry: “What happened to the old man?” Wei Baidu had a yellowish face and was powerless and said, “Little brother, I've been sick for over half a year, and after taking medicine a few times, I haven't gotten better. It seems like I haven't been able to do this ferry trade for a long time. Unfortunately, I have no one to replace me. Hi there! As soon as my boat stopped, passers-by on both sides of the Strait didn't know how many detours it would take...” I had another severe cough and vomited a few sips of blood. Wang Yanzhang hurriedly supported the old man to the dock and asked, “Does the old man live on the south bank or the north bank?” Wei Baidu said, “I live in Weidu Village on the North Shore.
“Wang Yanzhang said, “Old man, I'll take you home to rest and recuperate. I'll take a break on this ferry for a few days. When you get well, I'll leave. What do you think?” Wei Baidu was overjoyed to hear it. He took a look at the young man in front of him. He was very happy to see Wang Yanzhang's height, round waist, and thick shoulders. He was very happy, but I don't know if this person is knowledgeable? Wang Yanzhang explained, “I am from Liangshan County in Shandong Province. I grew up by the lake. I know how to play with water, can fiddle with paddles, and have practiced martial arts since childhood. I can do a few tricks with all 18 weapons. My parents and siblings were affected by the floods in my hometown. I heard that my mentor and brother have a martial arts gym in Jinling. They want to run there and find a place to stay.” As soon as Wei Ferry heard it, he was even more happy. He said OK in the cabin. Wang Yanzhang stayed to take over the ferry for the time being.
The next day Wei was bedridden. Wang Yanzhang asked Lang Zhong to grab the medicine, and his neighbor's sister-in-law helped make the medicine. Wang Yanzhang has officially taken over the ferry service. This has been around for more than three months. Not only did Wei Lengdu's condition not improve, but it worsened day by day. A few days later, Wei Fangdu died of illness. Since Wei Ferry's family had no savings, Wang Yanzhang borrowed some money from a village neighbor to bury Wei Ferry. Since then, this Wei ferry has become the king ferry.
One day, a cow dealer sold half a scalper on the southern shore of Lake Lake to transition. When it boarded the boat, this cow had a grumpy temper; no matter how you dragged, pulled, or knocked it, it wouldn't want to get on the boat. The cow dealer was in a hurry and sweaty. Wang Yanzhang put down her penny and rolled up her sleeves while saying, “I'll try it.” I saw him hugging the front of the scalper with his left hand and the back of the scalper with his right hand. With both arms, the half of the scalper was picked up by Wang Yanzhang. As the saying goes, “I'd rather lift a kilo of stone than hold 200 animals.” Strangely enough, the scalper seemed willing to cooperate with Wang Yanzhang and was taken to the boat in response to the post. The cow seller and a few transitioners looked dumbfounded. Since then, Wang has been extremely capable of ferrying, and the incident of hugging a cow on a boat has been spread, and it has spread farther and farther, more divine.
Wang Yanzhang had excellent arm strength, and the legend of his superb martial arts spread hundreds of miles away to Xuzhou. At the time, King Zhu Quanzhong of Houliang abolished Emperor Tang Ai and became emperor on his own. The country name was Liang, known as “Houliang” in history. In order to consolidate, Zhu Quanzhong is recruiting troops everywhere and recruiting talents. In 908 AD, Zhu Quanzhong heard that there was a secluded half-immortal in Xuzhou who knew about 500 years ago. Zhu Quanzhong personally brought someone from the north to Xuzhou. He stayed for a few days without hearing any trace of the Half Immortals. While searching for Half Immortals, I heard rumors about Wang Yanzhang. Zhu Quanzhong was eager to seek wisdom, so he took four or five companions day and night to Wang Ferry.
On this day, King Zhu Quanzhong of Houliang arrived at the ferry crossing. At the time, it was Pan Cun's meeting. There were many people passing by. Zhu Quanzhong and a few of his companions were not busy getting on the ferry. Instead, they sat under a tree at the ferry crossing and watched Wang Yanzhang's every move. Wang Yanzhang also saw a few people under the tree. The boat went back and forth on the lake, but these few people still sat under the trees and couldn't board the boat. Wang Yanzhang was a bit puzzled, but in the past there were so many people that he was so busy that he never cared about it. The sky gradually fell into the sky. There were few pedestrians in the past, and a few people under the trees on the shore began to transition aboard the boat. Since Zhu Quanzhong has been watching Wang Yanzhang for a while, watching him help the elderly, hold children, and carry goods, the more he likes it. The more he sees it, the more he likes it, and thinks to himself: this person is kind and has a kind heart. If he really is as powerful as in legends and has excellent martial arts, he will set the world for him, and his actions are set above the generals.
