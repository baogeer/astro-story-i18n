 Zhaojun's tomb is located on the banks of the Dahei River, 9 km south of the old city of Hohhot. Every year, due to “cool autumn and September, the grass declines outside of the country” and the earth withers. Only the tall Zhaojun tomb is blue-green. People call Zhaojun's tomb a “blue mound.” “Aotsuka Yudai” is known as one of Hohhot's Eight Scenic Spots. Throughout the ages, it has attracted many Chinese and foreign tourists, visiting for sightseeing, and honoring Wang Zhaojun, the empress of Han Ming, who has made a great contribution to national unity through the ages.
Wang Zhaojun, Ming Qiang, Zi Zhaojun. During the Western Jin dynasty, the name was changed to “Ming Jun” in order to avoid Sima Zhao's taboo. He was born in the late Western Han Dynasty in Xingshan County (present-day Zigui County, Hubei Province). Because of his intelligence and beauty, he was “awaiting death” when Emperor Han Yuan entered the palace.
In the late Western Han period, the Han dynasty and the Huns stopped fighting for a long time and resumed a “friendly” relationship. In the first year of Emperor Jingning of Han Yuan (33 BC), the Western Han dynasty agreed to the Huns' request to call Han Xie Danyu and agreed to Wang Zhaojun's escape to his family. Since then, there has been a situation of harmony between China and Hungary and the nation living in harmony, which has been praised by people from generation to generation.
Wang Zhaojun voluntarily left the country and married a different race, showing the extraordinary courage and courage of a weak woman despite the desolation of the border and not afraid of the wind and suffering of the North. Regarding the departure of Zhaojun, there is a vivid record in “The Book of the Later Han Dynasty: The Legend of the Southern Huns”. Wang Zhaojun “asked for action. The Emperor summoned five women as an example for the Korean Ecclesiastes Conference. Zhaojun is rich and beautiful, and the Han Palace is bright, and Gu Ying wanders and moves from side to side”. Huhan Xie Dan Yu Wei is very happy to have such a beautiful wife.” Shangshu wants to protect Shanggu west to Dunhuang, and spread endlessly. Please stop and prepare the police to take a break from the people of Tianzi”.
The departure of Emperor Zhaojun and his relatives was a major political event for both China and Hungary at the time. Emperor Han Yuan changed the yuan to “Jing Ning” to commemorate this peace and quiet. Hu Han Xie only crowned Zhaojun as Ning Hu Yi, the friendly empress between Hu and Han. The Huns called on Han Xie to return to China and leave the country alone, which also reflected the common wishes and demands of the working people of all ethnic groups at the time. The “Single with Heqin”, “Long Live a Thousand Years”, and “Single with Heqin” four-character bricks unearthed in late Western Han tombs near Hohhot City and “Only Heqin”, “Only Heqin”, “Long live a thousand years”, and “An Le Weiyang” indicate that the people of all ethnic groups along the Great Wall warmly praised Heqin and Zhaojun's departure. Zhaojun went out of the country and sowed the seeds of peace and good neighborliness between China and Hungary. This seed took root, germinated, blossomed, and came to fruition, and had a profound impact on the unity and friendship between the Han people and the northern fraternal nations in the future.
Unfortunately, after only two years of marriage, Wang Zhaojun and Huhan Xiedan passed away. She and Huhan Xedan gave birth to a son named Yitu Zhi Dentist. After the death of Hu Han Xedan, Zhaojun also followed the Huns' custom of “father's dead wife and stepmother”, and remarried Hu Han Xedan, the eldest son born to the First Clan, and returned to Dao Mo Gao. Zhaojun and Diao Tao Mo Gaosheng have two daughters. The eldest daughter's name is Subu Juji, that is, Princess Suboku.
When they got married, Zhaojun and Huhan Xedan were around 20 years old. It is estimated that they died during the Xinmang years (9 to 23 BC). According to the Tang Dynasty's “Wang Zhaojun in Literature” discovered in Dunhuang, after Zhaojun's death, the burial ceremony was carried out according to the customs of the Huns, which was very solemn.” “The coffin is a hollow, not even round”. Brew 500 pots of wine, kill 100,000 sheep, get rid of their calves, eat Yingchuan, and be as popular as a sea of people. A hundred miles long? A blanket, get down to earth. There are five hundred miles of gold and silver bottles, and there is no place under the foot. When they surrendered alone, all the tribes came, and the country became a feast, and Zhaojun (Jun) was buried.” Emperor Han Xiaoai (6 to 1 BC) also sent Yang Shaozheng to pay his condolences to Shan Yu. The solemn funeral reflects the Huns' nostalgia for Zhaojun and their positive attitude towards the Chinese and Hungarian family.
The 60 years since Zhaojun left the military, were 60 years of peaceful coexistence between China and Hungary, and 60 years of peaceful development in the entire southern part of the country, including the Hohhot region. There was a prosperous scene where “the people of Niu Mabuno flourished”. The people of all ethnic groups in China and Hungary, who have enjoyed 60 years of peaceful life after suffering from war and turmoil, deeply love Wang Zhaojun. According to folklore, Zhaojun was originally a fairy in the sky and married Han Xie Shanyu. When she left the battlefield, Hehu Han Xie only walked to the edge of the Heihe River, only to see Shuo Feng yelling, flying sand and stones, and people and horses couldn't move forward. Zhaojun's style of playing the pipa she was carrying, and as soon as the wind stopped calling, the sky was filled with colorful clouds, the ice and snow melted underground, and everything recovered. After a while, fresh grass grew everywhere, and gorgeous wildflowers bloomed all over. The dark mountains in the distance turned green, and the dark water nearby cleared up. Countless larks, cuckoos, and magpies also flew in, hovering and singing above their heads. He was so happy with the people of the Huns that he settled down by the Black Water. Later, Wang Zhaojun and Shan Yu traveled all over the foothills of Mount Yinshan and the north and south of the desert. Wherever Zhaojun went, aquatic plants were abundant, and both people and animals flourished. In places where water is scarce, as soon as Zhaojun uses a pipa, a jade belt-like river and green grass will appear on the ground. Zhaojun also took out the seeds of whole grains from a beautiful basket and scattered them in the ground, so whole grains grew. When Zhaojun died, farmers and herders from near and far rushed to be buried. They covered the placket with earth and built up Zhaojun's tomb one by one. Legend has it that Zhaojun's tomb changes three times a day, “like a front in the morning, like a bell in the afternoon, and you like a conch”. In other words, the tomb of Zhaojun is like a mountain peak in the morning, like a bell at noon, and like a chicken tree (conical plant in the family Aspergillaceae) at dusk.
Wang Zhaojun is a symbol of national unity and friendship, and her exploits have been respected by future generations. Archaeologists infer from artifacts obtained from Zhaojun's tomb that during the Jin Yuan period, there were buildings such as Kyodo in front of the tomb. During the Kangxi years of the Qing Dynasty, Zhang Pengyi and Qian Liangduo passed through Hohhot, and they also saw piles of glazed tiles in the south of Aotsuka, a black stone tiger, a stone horse, a white stone lion, and a stone house. The small stone house also had a flag written in Mongolian writing on top of the stone house, and a small square pavilion with earthen walls at the top of Aotsuka. There is also an old willow tree next to Aotsuka. The ground is heavily shaded and full of greenery. Those stone tigers, stone lions, stone horses, stone houses, and small square pavilions supported the deep love and condolences of the people of all ethnic groups in the Hohhot region at the time for Wang Zhaojun, and faithfully accompanied this long-married Han girl.
Writers and artists of all time have commented on Zhaojun's departure, saying that the most pertinent one is the Tang Dynasty poet Zhang Zhongsu: “Now that the fairy is married, we all know that she is proud of her son. The sword returned to the field, and the cows and sheep revolved around Saido.”
In addition to Aotsuka, there are more than a dozen tombs of Shogun at the southern foot of Mount Daqing. Historian Jian Bozan said it well: “Where is Wang Zhaojun buried? It's not important; what matters is why so many tombs of Zhaojun have appeared. Obviously, the appearance of these tombs of Zhaojun reflects that people of all ethnic groups in Inner Mongolia have a crush on the character Wang Zhaojun; they all want Wang Zhaojun to be buried in their hometown.
After liberation, the municipal people's government carried out numerous repairs to Zhaojun's tomb. In 1964, the Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region People's Committee listed Zhaojun Tomb as a key cultural relics protection unit in the autonomous region. Today's Zhaojun Tomb is a large-scale cemetery. It is 300 meters long from north to south, 162 meters wide from east to west, and 33 meters high. It covers an area of about 73 acres. When I walked to the tomb, the first thing I saw was a large 3.95-meter-high, 5-ton bronze statue of Huhan Xedan standing side by side with Wang Zhaojun and his clan. Further north, Chaoyang stands a tall stone monument. The poem “Visiting the Tomb of Zhaojun” by the late Vice President Dong Biwu is engraved in both Mongolian and Han characters: “Zhaojun has had a long history, and Hu Han and his family are highly respected. The lyricists all express their grievances; it's always futile to paint the dance text.” On both sides of the back of the stone monument, seven stone monuments from various generations can be seen.
