 Legend has it that a long time ago, the area of Weishan Lake was originally a large mountain that was tens of miles long and hundreds of feet tall. People named it Weishan because of the Weizi tomb on the mountain. There is only one family living on the mountainside of Weishan Mountain, with only two grandchildren. My grandparents reclaimed a few acres of hillside land and grew some crops to make ends meet. Although my life was poor, I was still comfortable and stable.
One fall year, the beans were almost ripe, and the beans were fat and big. The grandparents were happy to think that this year was a good harvest, and they didn't worry about not having any food to eat. One evening, my grandson went to the bean field to see beans. He saw an old man with a white beard and rushed a flock of sheep to put them in the bean field. The little grandson ran over and asked, “Grandpa, how did you herd sheep in my bean field?” The old man said, “You won't be able to harvest these beans. Within 10 days, this mountain will collapse. This place will become a large ocean and lake. Can you still harvest beans?! Why don't you feed my sheep.” After stopping, he said, “Your grandparents are nice people. Hurry up and run away in the direction of the sky, but you can't stay here for long.” After all, a gust of fresh wind disappeared, and the flock disappeared without a trace.
The little grandson quickly ran home and told Grandpa about what had just happened. Grandpa heard it and said it was a fairy lighting, so he spent the night packing up his luggage and clothes. While preparing to lead my little grandson to escape to the east, I told the townspeople to flee with them. Just like this, 10 to 10 to 10 to 100. Within a few days, all the people up and down the mountain had escaped.
Just in the middle of the night of the 9th day, I only heard the sound of a mountain falling apart, then Weishan sank.
Weishan collapsed and became a vast ocean. Originally, the highest peak of Weishan became a small island. Since then, people have called this vast ocean Weishan Lake, and the small island in the ocean has been called Weishan Lake Island.
