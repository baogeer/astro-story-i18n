 West Lake vinegared fish is the most famous dish from West Lake in Hangzhou. It was first made in Gaozong in the Southern Song Dynasty. According to ancient records, the West Lake vinegared fish dish comes from “the legend of the uncle and sister.”
Legend has it that during the Southern Song dynasty, there were two Song brothers. They were quite educated, but they didn't want to be officials, so they stayed in seclusion and fished for a living. There is a local bully, the famous official Zhao. When he saw that Sister Song was young and beautiful, he plotted to kill Brother Song in order to take over Sister Song. At this point, disaster befell the Song family's uncle and sister-in-law from the sky, and grief was overwhelming. In order to avenge their brother's revenge, the uncle and sister went to the government office to file a complaint. No one knew that the government office and the evil forces at the time went out of their noses. Instead, they were severely beaten, and they were kicked out of the government. When I got home, my sister-in-law had no choice but to let my younger brother flee his hometown. When my uncle and sister broke up, Sister Song specially cooked a bowl of mackerel with sugar and vinegar and said to her brother, “This dish is sweet and sour. I hope you have a good day, don't forget the bitterness of today.” Later, Song Di went out and fought back against Jin and the country. He went back to Hangzhou to punish the villain, but he never found out the whereabouts of his sister-in-law. Once I went out to a dinner, I learned about this dish during the dinner. After questioning, Fang Zhi's sister-in-law went incognito to work as a cook here, so they started to reunite. As a result, the beautiful dish “Uncle and Sister Chuan Zhen” spread like a legend among the people.
Fang Hengtai, a native of the Qing dynasty, wrote the poem “West Lake”, saying, “Wuliuju by Xiaopo Lake, get fresh fish when you have a feast. Miso loves silver saury the most, and Kawari is not as good as Kawagai.” It shows the cooking and deliciousness of West Lake vinegared fish. West Lake vinegared fish until the end of the Qing Dynasty, was most famous for being cooked in restaurants outside the West Lake Tower, and it is still today.
