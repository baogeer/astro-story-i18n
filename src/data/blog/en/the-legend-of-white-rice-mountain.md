 At the junction of Shahe Town in Chuzhou and Zhangbaling Town in Mingguang, there is a tall, upright mountain. The mountain is full of trees and evergreen in all seasons. This mountain has a beautiful and magical name called “White Rice Mountain.” There is such a wonderful local legend.
Legend has it that a long time ago, Mt. Hakumone was called Ransekiyama. The mountain is not as high, upright, and beautiful as it is now. Overgrown with weeds, piles of rocks, and scattered, there are a few mixed trees. Around the mountain, there is a clear stream that is not dry all year round and flows through the four seasons. Both sides of the stream are earthen, pine, and herds graze here every day in the surrounding villages. There is a larger village in the northern tip of Shandong. The name of the village is “Dazhang”. At the south end of the village, there is a family surnamed Zhang, the two of them. Grandpa was born in early July, so Grandpa's father gave him the name Zhang Qi'er. He is almost 70 years old. When his grandson was born, he was also on the 2nd day of July, so he can't call him Zhang Qier anymore. Because there is a curved jujube tree at the door of the house, Grandpa made a funny name Zhang Juo. Zhang Zao is 13 years old. He herds cows for Zhang Laotie's family, a large village owner, in order to make ends meet. According to family generation, Zhang Laotie should also be called Uncle Zhang Zao. However, the rich generation has grown. This old iron is only called Zaozi. He said that his family is ten generations apart from Zhang Zao, so regardless of age, Juzi should be called Grandpa. Zhang Laotie's family keeps three buffaloes. They talked when hiring jujube to herd cows. They eat and stay at Zhang Laotie's house all year round, and also subsidize three buckets of jujube corn for household use every year. Although the salary was small, Zhang Qier agreed because his family lost one mouth and was still able to buy three buckets of corn a year.
In other words, one day in the fall of this year, Zhang Zao herded cows by the Qingshui River, and his little stomach was so hungry that he “grunted.” In the past, there was a saying along the Huai River called “no lunch for dogs, no dinner for cats, no breakfast for young cows.” These were all fueled by big families and trying to overcome them. Zhang Zao was hungry and drank cool water “knocking” by the stream. I kept looking up at the sun; the sun just didn't move when it was hanging. When Zhang Zao looked at it, she felt like she had turned her head around and fell to the ground, hungry and passed out. I don't know when, Zhang Zao woke up and was sitting cross-legged with a grey-haired Taoist. The white-haired Taoist saw Zhang Zao woke up, pulled out a small wooden bowl from his arms, and put two grains of rice in the bowl, then scooped up and down the river, shook both hands up and down, and “pursed” in his mouth. Suddenly, a bowl of snow-white rice was delivered to Zhang Zao. Zhang Zao watched the whole process without blinking a blink of an eye. Oh my god! After picking up the bowl, I couldn't take much care of it. I ate the larger the smaller one, and the more I ate it, the more delicious it was. Strange! Zhang Zao's little stomach is growing tight, but there is still no shortage of rice in the small wooden bowl. Zhang Zao is eating and thinking, can I take back the leftover meal and give Grandpa some to eat? I haven't seen Grandpa eat a single meal of white rice since I can remember. The white-haired person looked like a savvy person. He caressed Zhang Zao's head with one hand and said, “He was really a filial kid. He was hungry and passed out. Once he ate some, I immediately thought of Grandpa.” Zhang Zao looked at the white-haired Taoist and saw that he was a good person, so he boldly said, “Old immortals, can I have this leftover meal? I've never seen Grandpa eat white rice. I don't know how happy his old man would be if he ate the fragrant white rice I left him.” He saw the white-haired Taoist nodding, stood up and ran to an outcrop pond to pick a large lotus leaf, and ran back. Zhang Zao poured the white rice from the small wooden bowl, wrapped it in a lotus leaf, and carried it in his arms. He knelt down to knock at the white-haired Taoist. The white-haired Taoist picked up Zhang Zao, removed the whip from Zhang Zao's waist, then wiped off a jade finger from his thumb and put it on the bullwhip stick and said, “Kid, there is inexhaustible white rice under the rocky mountain ahead. This is the key. The keyhole is the stone gap in the middle of the three sandalwood trees on the mountainside. Every day, when the sun shines on the stone crevice, the tip with the jade finger must be inserted into the stone gap. “Rice God, Rice God, wake up quickly, give some white rice to save the poor. After three times in a row, the white rice will automatically flow out. Remember?” Zhang Zao looked at the whip in his hand and nodded, “I remember everything.” The white-haired Taoist also said, “Don't let people know about this, lest greedy people be unscrupulous about you.” Zhang Zao nodded. The white-haired Taoist said, “Go catch the cow; the cow has gone a long way.” As soon as Zhang Zao looked back at the cow, the grey-haired Taoist disappeared. Zhang Zao pinched her little face and felt pain. She knew it wasn't a dream; she had met a fairy.
Zhang Zao drove the cow back to the village, deliberately circled the south side of the village, and handed the pack of rice he was carrying in his arms to Grandpa. As soon as Grandpa saw his grandson bring white rice, the aroma was pungent, and he devoured it without asking about the origin. After eating, use your finger to pinch the rice grains stuck to the lotus leaves and deliver them to your mouth. Zhang Zao was extremely happy to see that Grandpa ate it so deliciously.
“Where did you get white rice?” Grandpa asked after kneading the last grain of rice on the lotus leaf.
Zhang Zao said, “It was given to me by a Taoist passing by. I ate half of it and left half for you.” He touched the bullwhip in his lower back and said, “Grandpa, give me a pocket.”
“Why?” grandpa asked.
“I'm useful. Don't ask why; I'll herd the cows tomorrow morning and pick them up.” After talking, Zhang Zao rushed the cow back to Zhang Laotie's house.
The next day, when Zhang Zao was herding cows, he went home to the south of the village and picked up a cloth pocket. Drive the cows to the riverside and look for lock eyes on the mountainside. Since there are few trees on the mountain, I saw those three fist-thick sandalwood trees at a glance. There is a huge stone in the middle of the three trees, leaning on the hillside, and there is a stone gap in the middle of the big stone. In order to be able to see cows grazing by the stream, Zhang Zao sat on a stone and waited for the sun to shine. After a while, the sun moved over, and the sun was shining directly on the crevice. Zhang Zao hurriedly inserted the tip of the cow whip with the jade finger in the crevice and thought, “Rice God, wake up soon, give some white rice to help the poor.” After three days of worship, I saw a loud noise of white rice flowing down the stone gap, and soon my pocket was filled to the brim. Zhang Zao pulled out his whip and carried his pocket down the mountain. When he went back to herding the cows, Zhang Zao took a detour to give a bag of rice to Grandpa. Grandpa was shocked to see Zhang Zao carrying a bag full of snow-white rice. He thought it had been stolen by Zhang Zao. He followed Zhang Zao to tell the truth. Zhang Zao quickly became sweaty and glared his eyes widened and said, “Don't worry, Grandpa. I have kept your teachings in mind since childhood. Gentleman died in cold weather. If you don't cook it, starve to death. This white rice is definitely not crooked, so don't worry.” Grandpa saw Zhang Zao speaking earnestly, so he didn't ask any more questions. Since then, every day Zhang Zao comes back from herding cows, carrying a bag full of rice. When Zhang Qier couldn't finish eating it, he carried it on the street and sold it. Over time, Zhang Qier had something to eat, drink, and wear, and also saved quite a bit of money to build a new house next year, waiting to take his granddaughter with him.
Zhang Qi is out. People in the village are very curious. An old man who is nearly 70 years old and doesn't have any rice paddies at home, how can he go to the streets every day to sell rice? People are arguing with great fuss. This old iron was careful. At first, he suspected that Zhang Zao was stealing rice from his house, but he kept watching quietly for a while, and every day he left the house empty-handed. Besides, his family didn't have rice for a while, and Zhang Qier still carried a bag of rice to sell on the street.
On this day, Zhang Laotie followed Zhang Zao from afar to see what kind of ghost this cow herder is playing. After he saw Zhang Zao driving the cow to Jiangou, he carried an empty pocket up the mountain. Zhang Laotie still quietly followed him, lying very close to Zhang Zao. As soon as Zhang Zao looked up at the sun, then looked at the stone crevices, only to see that when the sun was shining directly on the stone crevices, Zhang Zao inserted the whip with his jade finger in the stone gap and heard the thought: “Rice God, Rice God, wake up soon, give some white rice to help the poor.” Snow white rice flowed out of the crevices. Zhang Laotie's eyes were all right. When he got home, he told his wife everything he saw. Together, the two wanted to get their hands on a bullwhip. Zhang Laotie's woman strained her face and said, “Jujube is my family's hired cow herder. Now we don't hire them. Tell him to drop the cow whip and go away, that's fine.” Zhang Laotie shook his head after thinking about it for a while: “Improper, inappropriate, what if he doesn't want to hand over his bullwhip?” The woman glared her eyes and clasped her hips and said, “Come on hard, grab it, what else?” Zhang Laotie still shook his head and said, “No, no, what if he goes to the county government to sue?” The woman was in a hurry to jump up: “What about the complaint? Will the prefectural lord help herd a little cow?” Zhang Laotie nodded, “I wouldn't help that little cow herder. Since ancient times: the poor don't fight against the rich, the rich don't fight the official; how can a poor herder beat me?” After hearing this, the woman relaxed her tone and said, “Hey, isn't this just the picture?” Zhang Laotie squinted and said, “Then we have to fight with the county grandpa. Who do you think will lose in the end?” The woman was in a hurry and scolded: “You sly pig, why don't you just pull something out of your stomach and finish it?” Zhang Laotie seriously said, “I don't want to be tough; I'm afraid this incident will spread. If the news gets to the county grandpa, can I still have my share of this treasure?” The woman was surprised and said, “Have you heard the story of Tanuki being replaced by a prince?” The woman said, “I'll memorize it. Do you have any new passages, just this one all day long.” Zhang Laotie said, “I'm not telling you a story. I wanted to go to the jade store and buy one that was about the same color as that jade spot; I changed it to a jujube one. This jujube uses a fake jade finger to ask for rice, and the rice won't come out. This kid still thinks the rice is gone; doesn't he recognize that? Didn't God unwittingly return this treasure to me then?” The woman laughed: “Baby, you're a ghost.”
While sleeping on Zhang Zao, Zhang Laotie secretly pulled off his whip, removed his jade finger, and replaced it with a fake jade finger.
The next day, Zhang Zao followed the old method and asked for rice again. However, after reading the recipe seven or eight times, the grain of rice was gone. Zhang Zao thought to himself: Maybe there was no more rice, or the rice god saw me asking for rice every day and got angry and annoyed. Hi there! Looks like Grandpa was right; this person can't be insatiably greedy.
Furthermore, when Zhang Lao Tie saw that Zhang Zao didn't notice that it was a fake, there wasn't much reaction if they didn't come out. The two were so happy. Another day later, Zhang Laotie secretly came to the stone crevice, holding a cow whip with a real jade finger, and while the sun was shining directly into the stone crevice, he hurriedly inserted the cow whip into the stone crevice. “God of rice, God of rice, wake up soon, give some white rice to save the poor.” I saw an overflow of snow-white rice flowing through the crevices of the stones. Zhang Laotie grabbed a handful of rice and smelled it. It was extremely fragrant; when he looked at it, the snow was white and dazzling; when he chewed it, it was sweet and refreshing; he was so happy that he didn't know how to do it. The rice flowed faster, and in the blink of an eye, it spread over Zhang Laotie's waist. At this point, he always remembered putting the rice in the bag. When he got up to pick up the bag, he couldn't move his feet and was struggling to crawl out of the rice pile. It was difficult. Soon, the rice spread over his shoulders and over his head. Because the bullwhip was still stuck in the crevices of the stones, the rice was still flowing out. More and more, the entire rocky mountain was buried underneath before the sun set.
Just as white rice was walking through the mountains, people around them discovered that many people picked it in baskets and carried it in their pockets, but when some wealthy people went to carry rice, the rice became white sand. Zhang Laotie's woman saw rice spread over the top of the mountain. She didn't see Zhang Laotie come out. She knew it was bad; Lao Tie must have been buried underneath. While crying and desperately chopping on a pile of rice, she broke her voice and shouted, “Lao Tie, Lao Tie, where are you?” The next day, as soon as the sky brightened, a tall mountain made of white rice replaced the original rocky mountain. Later, people called it “White Rice Mountain.” There is a large rock on the northern slope of the mountain, half cut in the mud. Legend has it that Zhang Laotie's wife is the wife of Zhang Laotie. The local cattle herder likes to whip them a few times.
