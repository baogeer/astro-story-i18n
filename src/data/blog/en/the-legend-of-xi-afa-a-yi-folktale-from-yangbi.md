Xi Afa is a native of Xi Village in Ihe Village, Wachang Township. What's amazing about him is that he can use the fixed root method, the path reduction method, and the change method. Let me get into the details below.
1. Fixed root method
At one point, Xi Afa wanted to go to the secret mission (now in the Okura area of Mount Weishan) to meet up (lover, girlfriend). He rode a big white horse to the crossroads of Zijin Mountain. Suddenly, a group of masked people popped up on the side of the road and stopped him on his way: “Stop, the mountain is my mountain, my dad drove the road, and my grandpa planted the tree. If you want to cross this path, save money to buy the road!” Xi Afa got off right away without hurrying, holding his fists and said, “Let's talk about it. Come on, everyone sit down and smoke. After smoking, you just unpack your bags and walk away.” After speaking, take out a pack of special cigarettes and give each person one. The robbers forgot they were robbing, and thought it was a mackerel in the urn, so why not smoke first. Xi Afa handed the horse rope to a robber: “Please tie it to a tree on the side of the road and relax. Don't be scared. I'm timid.” The robbers completely let their guard down and chatted with Xi Afa. After smoking a cigarette, the robbers sat and couldn't stand up. Xi Afa stood up and patted each bandit on the shoulder and said, “Everyone is idle. My good looks are that the chicken has been cooked to the limit. When I eat, I'm leaving!” Xi Ah waved, made a funny face, and walked off on the big white horse. Bandits, look at me; I look at you; I just can't say anything.
After five or six days, Xi Afa came back riding a big white horse. The robbers were still sitting there, and all the surrounding grass roots were eaten up by them. Xi Afa patted each of them lightly on the shoulder and said, “Oops, why are you so sentimental? A few days have passed and you are still waiting for me. Come on, everyone is thirsty, drink water.” The robbers still dared to drink water there and fled in a hurry.
II. Road reduction method
I don't know when Xi Afa actually started studying a way to cut back the road. The so-called way to reduce the road is to hold the handle of a broom with both hands, sit on the tip of the broom, read a spell, and say “Get up!” The man and the broom took off, and in the blink of an eye, they reached their destination. One morning, my wife just cooked, and a group of guests came. The wife quietly said in his ear, “No side dishes. Are you afraid to go to the secret secretary to buy some side dishes?” Xi Afa said, “Why is this difficult; just go and come.” Xi Ah gave each of the guests a glass of wine, handed over a cigarette, picked up a broom, and flew away after thinking in their mouths. The guests were dumbfounded, and the old man only reacted. He hasn't finished smoking yet, and he hasn't finished drinking alcohol. Xi Afa has already ridden a broom carrying small dishes such as dried beef and ham from Okura. The guests were amazed, and each gave a thumbs up for praise.
However, it is said that the spell of the Shut Down Road Law is very difficult to remember, and it is impossible to reverse it if you are a little careless. At one point, Xi Afa was already sitting on a broom and was reciting a spell. The secret envoy had to kiss him goodbye. After being distracted, Xi Ah was sent to Baoshan City. After arriving in Baoshan, Xi Afa bought a new broom, and even after trying it dozens of times, it couldn't fly. Helpless, I had to walk back. After being away for over a month, Xi Afa returned home.
III. The law of change
One day, Xi Afa's wife wanted to go up the mountain to cut leaves. She told Xi Afa to guard her home and not go to her neighbor's house to have fun. Xi Ah nodded in agreement.
After a while, Xi Afa got bored. No one punches when drinking, no one brags when drinking tea, and no one talks when smoking. To catch the chicken, the door is closed, and the chicken can't get in; picking honey, I only picked it yesterday; pulling and grinding it; it's too much work alone. He finally frowned and thought it through.
He opened the door, locked it, took off his headscarf, hung it horizontally on the door, and went out to play at his neighbor's house.
When Xi Afa's wife came back from cutting the leaves, she saw that the door was locked. What was even scarier was that a big black snake climbed on the front of the gate, so frightened that she lost the leaf and ran to her neighbor's house. Seeing that Xi Afa was also here, he couldn't help but say, “His father told you to guard your home. The black snake all climbed up to the front door and scared me to death.” Xi Afa said, “No way, where there are black snakes; that's just my headscarf. If you don't believe me, let's go home and check it out.”
When I went home, it was really just Xi Afa's headscarf. Since then, people have said that Xi Afa can change; he turned his headscarf into a black snake; others have seen that it is actually a big black snake that can move and eat.
Today, when people talk about Xi Afa, the people in Xi Village feel very proud and proud.
