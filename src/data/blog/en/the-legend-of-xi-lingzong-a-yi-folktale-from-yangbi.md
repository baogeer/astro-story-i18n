 Xi Ling has always been a member of the Xi family in Shema Village, Wachang Township, Yangyi County. He lived around the end of the Qing dynasty, and it has been over 100 years since then. He was a tax collector at the time for Snake Ma and the surrounding west bank of the Yangjiangjiang River, and was also the owner of a large amount of land.
One day, he led a servant on horseback and prepared to go to Menghua (present-day Mount Weishan. In the Qing dynasty, Yangyi did not have a county system; the tile factory belonged to Menghua County) to pay taxes. When I first arrived at Nick, I met a group of Hui people chasing horses. After the meeting, someone knew President Xi, greeted each other, and rubbed shoulders. Just a few steps apart, a Hui man who was chasing horses asked, “Huh, how do you know him?” Another person answered, “Oh well, he's the famous Captain Xi of the Snake.” “Wow, how could a master be black, short and thin, and still ride a bench horse!” These conversations are quite loud; the speaker has no intention of listening. Xi Ling always felt that he had been greatly harassed and ironized. He didn't get angry at all; he wanted horse hunters to taste his awesomeness. He asked his servants who were holding horses to go to Hexiazhai, and asked them to send someone to inform the Hakka family who lived in Dashuiqing that they would kill all the Hui horse hunters tonight, and be careful to throw away the corpses to the west side of Baizhu West (at the time, Baizhu Mountain was the outside world; the east side was converted back to Yongping), so that they could plant the living paddy fields along the Aller River.
The horse-hunters that night were called out by the Hakka family one by one in Dashuiqingkai in Baizhushan, and never came back. The last one looked wrong and ran away. Ran back to Menghua to report the crime. At the time, the mayor of Menghua County had the surname Song; people called him Song County. After Song County received the case, people were quickly dispatched to investigate the case. Xi Ling always knows that the case has been revealed, accusing the Hakka family of “not doing things well. If they don't want to get into a lawsuit or land on their head, they immediately move away overnight.” They also gave the Hakka family a large sum of money. People investigating the case know that the Hakka family killed them, but they have already escaped and are unable to be apprehended and brought to justice. Since people died in Yongping, the Yongping people should solve the case and be responsible for handling the aftermath. People in Yongping believe that the dead were Meng Hua people, and the murderers were also Meng Hua people; they only moved the corpses to their borders; they were only blamed on them, so they should not be responsible. Gong says that justice is reasonable, and mother-in-law says that grandma is reasonable. In the end, even the two county governors got into a dispute and were unable to step down. No way, the two county governors met and squeezed each other out. The rules are: the two county governors stand on the left and right sides, draw a line in the middle, and the two winners in three games win.
At the beginning of the game, the two county governors did their best. Everyone had the belief that they would win. The person on the left used the gas on the right, the person on the left pedaled his left foot and bent his right foot, and the person on the right pedaled his right foot and bent his left foot.
As a result of the match, Song County won.
The mayor of Yongping County ordered the Miku people to be responsible for specific compensation and disposal of the corpse. There was a wealthy man named Su Yuwen in Miku. He was still angry. He said two things. Song County couldn't listen to him. Su Yuwen said, “You're only the mayor of Menghua County in Song County. Can you handle me?”
Song County returned to Menghua and was adamant about Su Yuwen's words. When I went to Wuhua Mountain in Kunming, I spent a lot of money to get a Dali government office. On the second day after taking office, a notice was issued accusing the writer Su Yu of winning the prize. Shortly after the notice was posted, a person selling porcelain bowls from other provinces said there was a way to sue Su Yuwen. After saying this, Song County made him do it.
The porcelain seller went to the house next to Su Yuwen's house and called and sang again, leading the dog to chase him. He took the opportunity to smash all the porcelain, and then went to Su Yuwen's family to make a claim at a high price. He said, “You're just extorting. If the dog chases you and you fall, it will indeed break a bit, but why is it all? Besides, porcelain isn't that expensive.”
The porcelain seller said, “You don't count what I said; dare you talk to the county governor?”
“Dare!” Su Yuwen said, “Don't say that the county governor is in front of the government office, I dare say that it is difficult to walk all over the world rationally.”
“Then let's ask Dali Fu Taiwan to evaluate it!”
“If you go, go, whoever is afraid of which.”
So Su Yuwen rode a tall horse and walked for three days to Dali Futai.
Su Yuwen clapped the drums and complained and entered the lobby. “Su Yuwen, can I take care of you today?” Su Yuwen looked up. Seated on it, it turned out to be the mayor of Menghua County. The enemy's path is narrow. He admits that he is unlucky, and while knocking his head and asking for forgiveness, “I can take care of it, I can manage it. Adults don't remember the lives of the little people; the old man forgives his life.”
Song County said, “Lock up Su Yuwen!”
The next day, Song County asked again, “Su Yuwen, can I take care of you today?”
“I can handle it, I can manage it. Adults don't remember the lives of little people; the old man forgives his life.”
Song County said, “Lock up Su Yuwen!”
Just like that, Song County only asked this question every day. Half a year passed, and Su Yuwen only had a bone stand left. The family carried five piles of silver before putting him back.
