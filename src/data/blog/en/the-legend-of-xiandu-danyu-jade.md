 “Xiandu Danjade” is a new type of jade discovered in 2011 in Shuhong, Jinyun. Legend has it that Xuanyuan Huangdi visited Jiangnan after settling the world. He took male root trees, female yin springs, and five-color stones from Dinghu Peak in Xiandu to use for alchemy. After Dancheng, he climbed on the back of the dragon. When the dragon held its head high and swung its tail, the dragon tail was dipped in the elixir in Danding. As soon as the dragon flutted gently, the dragon threw the solution on its tail onto a mountain in Shuhong, turning it into today's “Xiandu Danjyu.”
In May 2012, the Zhejiang Institute of Geology and Mineral Resources and the Zhejiang Jewelry and Jade Jewelry Appraisal Center carried out mineralogical and gemmological tests on Xiandu Danjade, which determined that Xiandu Danjou is a type of jade with chalcedony as the main component. The hardness is between 6.5-7 Mohs, the density is between 2,6-2,70, and the refractive index is 1.53-1.55; the main component silicon dioxide content is around 92-96%. Compared with the “Name of Jewelry and Jade” national standard (GB/T16552-2011), the gemstone characteristics of Xiandu jade and Huanglong jade are basically the same. Since then, Xiandu Danyu has begun to enter ordinary people.
Xiandu Danyu includes mountain water, turf, and mountain materials. It has yellow, red, and white as the main colors, and is accompanied by various colors such as black, purple, and gray, as well as forms such as gold-clad silver, silver-wrapped, and gold-clad iron. The jade is pure, the color is beautiful, the water head is warm, the jade is crystal, does not fluff, does not crack, has good toughness and is easy to carve. Therefore, both the rough stones and the carvings are beautiful, and are loved by jade experts, especially yellow dragon jade collectors. Experts believe that the color of Xian Dudan jade, the warmth of Hetian jade, the hardness of jade, and the quality of Huanglong jade can be said to combine the excellent quality of contemporary Chinese jade. It is the best quality Huanglong jade mountain material discovered in China so far.
