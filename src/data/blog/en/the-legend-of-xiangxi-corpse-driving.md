 The legend of the extermination of corpses in Xiangxi is inseparable from the Miao people. Legend has it that thousands of years ago, Lu You, an ancestor of the Miao people, led an army to fight the enemy along the Yellow River. After the fighting ended, the troops needed to be evacuated to the rear. After all the wounded were removed, many dead soldiers were left on the battlefield. Lu You couldn't bear to leave the dead bodies of his compatriots in the wilderness, but there wasn't enough manpower to carry all the corpses away, so he asked the soldiers who died in the war to return to their hometowns. The military master made a plan to let Lu You lead the way forward with runes. He cast his own magic to make all the corpses on the battlefield stand up, follow Chi You Gao Qing's runes, and return the rules to their hometown.
