 In ancient times, in the middle of a valley, there was a deep green pool with green trees and beautiful flowers and plants on both sides of the mountain. The spring water in the well is quiet, and you can see the clouds and birds in the sky, as well as the trees and flowers next to the well. One day, a group of herons flew in from the east, and a group of ducks flew in from the west. As soon as they saw this beautiful well, they wanted to look for fish and shrimp inside. The earthgod guarding the well jumped up and shouted:
“Bah! What are you guys doing here? This is not a fish pond; this is where Yang Asa was born. She's about to be born tomorrow, so don't peck! Whoever dares to touch her hair, I'll call her dead bones and not return home!”
Herons and water ducks had no choice but to pick up their tails and fly away.
The next day, suddenly it was dark, the lights were flashing, thunder was loud, and heavy rain fell. The well was bubbling and making a “wave-wave-wave” sound. Soon after, the rain stopped, and the sky cleared up. The colorful clouds, like the strange flowers embroidered by the Miao family girls, floated in the clear sky. At this point, Yang Asa came out of the well, crowds of butterflies fluttered around her, and countless birds sang around her... Everyone was happy to celebrate the birth of Yang Asa.
Yang Asha laughed the first day she was born, talked the next day, sang on the third day, and woven and embroidered on the fourth day. Yang Asa's singing voice was so gentle and beautiful. It flew all over the mountains, mountains, and mountains, and echoed through the villages and villages. On cloudy days, her singing can drive away clouds and fog and evoke the sun; in winter, her singing drives away the cold, and flowers bloom all over the mountains. The bees heard her singing, huh, and forgot to pick honey; the birds heard her singing, huh, and forgot to sing; the young guys heard her singing, huh, and forgot how to live; the girls heard her singing, huh, learned to sing word for word.
The days have passed day by day, and the year has passed. Yingshanhong drove again, thank you and open again. Yingshanhong has driven 18 times, thanked 18 times. The girl Yang Asha is already 18 years old. Yang Asha, who is 18 years old, looks even more beautiful! The beautiful Yang Asha has eyes like water grapes, a white and tender face like a white camellia, two thin and long eyebrows like a brocrow's hair, and dark and bright hair like black silk. When she wears her own embroidered flower clothes, bees will fly to gather nectar; she wears a 100% discount dress made by herself, and the crepe folds are more beautiful than the crepe lines on bacteria. Of the 99 girls in Jiang Luli, which one is comparable to her; of the 99 Jiang Yueli young guys, which one doesn't love her.
Yang Asha grew up, and her fame spread all over the world. There are a lot of people who propose marriage, like bees picking flowers. A group is here today, and a gang is coming tomorrow. They broke down the road to Yang Asha's house and broke through the big threshold of Yang Asa's house. Wu Yun saw that Asa was smart and beautiful. In order to please the Sun, she wanted to tell the Sun about being her wife. One day, dark clouds floated over to the Sun's house. He said to the Sun:
“Under the sky, there is a beautiful woman named Yang Asha, the Sun. You are a rich person in heaven. Marry her and start a family soon!”
The sun was eating lunch. After hearing the dark clouds said, she hurriedly dropped the tableware on the table and hurriedly ran to the foot of the building to take a look. I saw Yang Asha washing her hair in the quiet water of the river. Huh! Yang Asha is so beautiful! She is like a flower with dew. Her white, tender face, dark hair, and sparkling eyes, the more she looks at the sun, the more fascinated she is, and she is determined to marry Yang Asha. He happily returned to his arms and urged Wu Yun to say:
“Dark clouds, dark clouds, let me paint as a medium!”
The dark clouds that speak for themselves soon drifted over to Yang Asha's house. He said to Yang Asha in clever words:
“When millet is ripe, you have to beat it; when girls grow up, you have to get married. The sun is given by a hardworking, brave, smart, and beautiful guy; the richest person in the sky is his family. Look up to Asha and praise Asha. If you were married to the Sun, being rich and rich would be enough for you to enjoy for the rest of your life!”
Yang Asha originally didn't love the Sun family's wealth, but as soon as she heard that he was hardworking, brave, smart, and beautiful, she was a little tempted, and later decided to marry the Sun family.
The day of marriage arrived, and Wu Yun led Yang Asha to the Sun's house. They walked to a valley and met a cherry tree. Cherry Blossoms asked where did Asa go? Yang Asha answered:
“I married to the Sun family.”
Cherry Blossom shook her head and said:
“The sun is so ugly, my nostrils are long, and my face is full of bumps. Don't go soon, marry me!”
Yang Asha was a little hesitant. Dark clouds deceived her with clever words:
“The Sun is a beautiful man famous in the sky. Yang Asha is smart, don't listen to the cherry blossom rumor!”
Yang Asa and Wu Yun continued to walk forward, walked to a hillside, and met a thrush bird. Where did the thrush go? Yang Asha still answered:
“I married to the Sun family.”
The cuckoo waved its tail and said:
“The Sun is a big lazy man. He sleeps all day and doesn't want to do anything; Sun is a bad person. He is a big villain. He stands up and scolds people when he scolds people when they say anything that doesn't suit his mind. Look up to Asha and praise Asha. Don't go soon, come join me as a family.”
Yang Asha was even more hesitant; she didn't want to marry the Sun. The dark clouds deceived her with clever words:
“The sun is a hardworking young man. He goes out to work every day before it's bright, and only comes home when it's dark; the sun is gentle and kind to people; he smiles before talking, and talks after laughing. Wise Yang Asha, don't take the thrush's ghost story.”
Despite the dark cloud's ingenious words, Yang Asha is determined not to marry the Sun. The dark clouds got angry, extended their arms, held Yang Asha, and flew to the Sun's house.
After Yang Asa married the Sun, I learned that Sun is really lazy and sleeps all day long; Sun is really ugly, has long teeth in his nostrils, and big pimples on his face; the Sun is really evil; he often hits people and scolds people. How can such a lazy, ugly, and vicious person be worthy of admiration for Asha? Even her neighbors angrily said to her:
“Look up to Asha and admire Asha. You are married to the lazy, ugly, and vicious Sun. How will you live in the future?!”
When I heard it, Yang Asha felt a burst of pain, and a moment of sadness.
After the Sun married Yang Asa, it was like a tree had roots and a knife; a hardworking wife took care of the family, and the Sun was even more lazy than before: One day, Yang Asha persuaded the Sun:
“Why don't you work and stay at home every day? Even if your family's wealth is as big as a mountain, you can sit back and eat it!”
The Sun answered arrogantly:
“It's so hard to work. Every day I go in the wind and rain. huh! I wouldn't do it because of such a heavy lifestyle! As long as I go out to take care of the elderly, go out and be reasonable, and be both famous and make money, isn't this much better than being active?”
The Sun decided to go out and work as a caretaker, but who is going to plant the large fields at home? With so many cows and horses in the family, who are they looking for to feed them? When the sun saw that the moon was hardworking and honest, I invited him to work as a permanent worker, ask him to cultivate fields, and ask him to cut beef grass.
It's been three years since the sun went out, and I haven't been home once in three years. Yang Asha was quiet at home. Seeing someone coming from the East, she asked them anxiously:
“Have you seen the sun? When will he be home?”
This one says:
“The Sun is fascinated by money and fascinated by fame. Who knows when it will take him to turn around?”
That one said:
“Sun works as a caretaker on the East China Sea, filing lawsuits and justification for others. He thinks he has a lot of money and has had a beautiful life. The sun has long forgotten you. If you wait another 3 or 5 years, I'm afraid he won't come home. I think you should remarry someone else!”
After listening to Asha, she felt sad and sad for a while...
Moon is a strong, peaky young man. He is very loyal and diligent in his work. Every day, before it was bright, he woke up to work. It was dark before he came home. After returning home, he also helped Yang Asa pick up water and help Yang Asa spring rice.
Yang Asha graciously said to the moon:
“You've been busy from dawn to dark, tired enough. Rice, let me do good; water, let me pick it, sit back and have a good rest!”
Moon, who doesn't like to talk a lot, only said, “I'm not tired, I'm not going to stop.” He's still beating him.
They have been getting along for a long time. The Moon is in love with the beautiful Yang Asha, and Yang Asha is also in love with the hardworking and loyal Moon. After a discussion, they decided to escape and settle down in a faraway place.
Yang Asha and the Moon escaped. The cow had no grass to eat and was hungry and screamed; the cat had no food and was so hungry that they called Mimi...
When Tong Sang found out, he went to the East China Sea to get a letter from the Sun. He saw that the Sun was there making a sound of reason. The sun “clicked” and the dust raised a few feet high. For the sake of fame and fortune, he kept talking “wow wow” without stopping. Tong Sang hurriedly said to the Sun:
“The moon has run away from your wife, go home soon!”
When Tong Sang said it the first time, the Sun didn't believe it at all; when Tong Sang said it the second time, the Sun became suspicious and fiercely asked him:
“Are you telling the truth?”
As soon as Tong Sang saw the Sun and didn't believe himself, he gambled and said:
“If I deceive you, I'll be struck by lightning...”
When the Sun heard that the Moon had kidnapped his wife, she was so angry that she threw the piece of paper in her hand on the ground. She even forgot to take the money on the desk, and forgot to take the beef leg under the table, so she hurriedly ran home! While running, the Sun furiously thundered and scolded:
“Moon huh moon, I thought you were honest; how dare you run away from my wife. If I met you, I would cut you in two; if I looked up to Asha, how dare you run away with the moon. If I met you, I would have crushed you into mud. huh! Wait and see how good Laozi is!”
