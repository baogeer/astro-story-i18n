 On the night of the 15th lunar month, if you take a close look at the moon, you will see dark shadows inside. There is a saying about the dark shadows of the moon that have been passed down from generation to generation:
A long time ago, there was a man named Zhang Guan who lived in the moon and worked hard every day to see a large camphor tree with food and a pair of axes. Cut it down... When the tree that was cut down was about to fall, I don't know where a dog came from and took away Zhang Gu's packed lunch when he wasn't paying attention. Because he had lost his food and had no food to eat, Zhang Gu had no strength to cut down the tree. Zhang Gu then put down his axe and went after the dog. The dog ran very fast. Zhang Jiu followed and passed, but he couldn't catch up. But he wasn't mad. He kept chasing after him, and finally caught the dog and snatched the lunch box. From putting down the axe and chasing the dog to retrieving the lunch box, it was only a short time in the moon, but hundreds of years have passed on earth. When Zhang Gu returned to the bottom of the camphor tree, he saw that all the places he had cut down before had been restored, and there was no sign of it being cut down at all. Not only that, the tree had grown taller and larger than before.
Zhang Gu is a very patient person. Instead, he has cut down this tree as his lifelong career and is determined to continue cutting down the tree after a few days. At this time, the dog stole the lunch box again, he put down his axe and went after the dog, waiting for him to take back the lunch box and go back under the camphor tree. The places he cut down before have now been restored. There is no trace. Until now, Zhang Gu has not been able to cut down the big camphor tree. I don't know when he will be able to fulfill his wish.
Because Zhang Gu didn't cut down that big tree, until now, there are still dark shadows in the moon, so there have been no natural disasters in the world.
