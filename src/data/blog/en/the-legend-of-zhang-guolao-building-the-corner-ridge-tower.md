 Between Guancheng in Badaling and Bangshuiyu inclined tower, there is a majestic ridge tower. People found a large handprint on the city brick. The palm was the size of a fan, and the finger was as thick as a hammer. Whose hands are this big?
The old people say that during the construction of the corner building, Zhang Guolao was invited by Wang's mother to the Pantao Club. I'm afraid he was rude and didn't dare to ride a donkey. After that meeting, he went back to the fairy cave in Zhongnan Mountain and saw that his beloved monster donkey was so thin that it was covered with bones, and asked the boy, “Why don't you worry about feeding it to drink?” The boy said, “Emperor Qin Shi Huang of the Lower Realm built the Great Wall. There were not enough mortals, and he also sought Ding from the Immortal Realm. Other caves have already sent immortals. We don't have enough manpower, so we have to let the magic donkey carry the bricks.” Zhang Guolao was shocked and furious when he heard it. He was amazed that Qin Shi Huang, an evil dragon, actually carried out a huge undertaking; he was so angry, how did the magic donkey get so tired?
The next day, the donkey went back to work, and Zhang Guolao quietly followed him in the cloud. As soon as he arrived at Badaling, he heard people on the Shuiyu side shouting their horses. It shocked the world. He hurriedly broke through the clouds and looked down, only to see that a ridge-bending tower was being built on a high mountain peak. It was full of people, carrying stones and bricks, and picking soil, just like ants moving house. His donkey, carrying a mountain of bricks, was struggling to walk up the mountain. The fur all over his body was soaked, and he was beaten from time to time. Zhang Guolao was extremely distressed. He swept up and down the mountain again, only to see quite a few dead bones piled up in the ravine between the cliffs. He couldn't help but scold: “Tyrant!” He immediately turned into a tall, thin old man and flew down the clouds.
A kind foreman said to him, “The ridge tower project is arduous and the terrain is dangerous. Go to a flat place and clean it.” Zhang Guolao looked at him and smiled: “Do you know the story of veteran Lian Po?” Speaking of taking the rope out of my arms, carrying eight city bricks, holding four more blocks under my armpits, and holding two more pieces tightly with each hand, like running up a hill in an enclave...
 The bricks and stones in the ridge tower have been brought in, and Zhang Guolao climbed his magic donkey back to the mountain. The bricklayers used the bricks they had brought to the wall and discovered that there were very deep and very large handprints on them. They were all very surprised and thought in their hearts: “The old man who helped us fix the edge must have been an immortal. What kind of fairy? Zhang Guolao, only he has a good head.” In fact, there are many popular opinions about how Zhang Guolao became a fairy.
