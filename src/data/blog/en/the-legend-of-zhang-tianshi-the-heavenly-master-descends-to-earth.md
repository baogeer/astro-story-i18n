 Zhang Tianshi became a heavenly immortal. One day, he took his nephew to the sky to play. When he came to a garden, he saw that all the flowers in the garden were dry and dry, and the ground was all dry. His nephew felt very sorry, so he poured three shells of water on the glass well. Who knows this could have caused a major disaster. It is because of these three buckets of water that the earth flooded the ten prefectures, one hundred, and eight prefectures in Kyushu. Yu Huang was extremely angry. He said that Zhang Tianshi took mortals to heaven without permission and harmed many creatures on earth, then punished him.
After Zhang Tianshi stepped down, one day, he came to the door of the house dressed up to want to eat and shouted, “Grandma, let's eat something.” A woman in the house said, “Nothing to eat for you! There's also an oil cake to keep for the kids.” When Zhang Tianshi saw that people were so bad at food, he asked the Jade Emperor to harvest less food. Legend has it that wheat, sorghum, and millet have spikes from the bottom to the top. After Zhang Tianshi used his hands, there were only a few spikelets left. He then went to stir the sesame seeds and beans, so he stopped wobbling. So now sesame seeds and beans are crusted from bottom to top.
On this day, Zhang Tianshi pretended to be a gossip man and showed a family married with a daughter-in-law a good day. After a few days, another rumor came around saying that it wasn't good to have a happy event that day; the Five Ghosts were wrong. The one who married his daughter-in-law got worried. Everything was planned, and it wasn't easy to make any changes, so I found Zhang Tianshi again. Tianshi said, “It doesn't matter. I also know that the Five Ghosts were committed this day, but I have my own method of breaking it, and I will definitely be able to turn the evil into good. When the time comes, I will have my own generals to protect, and the second champion in literature and martial arts will take control. Naturally, the Five Ghosts are afraid and don't dare to commit crimes. You can rest assured.” On the day of welcoming the new daughter-in-law, when the new daughter-in-law entered the door, someone happened to pass by, bought an iron pot, and crowded into the crowd to see the daughter-in-law.
