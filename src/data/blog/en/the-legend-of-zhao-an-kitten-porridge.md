 Cat porridge is freshly cooked and eaten by guests. It is characterized by being quick and easy to prepare, and delicious. When the diners were seated at the table, the stall owner hurriedly placed fresh meat soup in a small pot, then put in pre-steamed white rice, topped with fish fillets, meat slices, liver, shrimp, oysters, and shredded shiitake mushrooms, and stirred the fire. After less than five minutes, it was cooked into a bowl of hot, fragrant “cat porridge.” Why is it called “cat porridge”?
It is said that during the Qing dynasty, there were household officials and families in Zhaoan City. Her grandmother, Mrs. Tae, had a strict family. The three pairs of sons, daughter-in-law, and about a dozen grandchildren and granddaughters all followed the family's teachings and did not dare to go beyond the rules; they seemed to have a family style. After Chang Sun got married, the new daughter-in-law left the kitchen for three days and cooked meals for the whole family herself, but she was only able to eat leftovers from the family's meals under the kitchen.
Chang Sun couldn't bear to let his beloved wife be offended for a long time. He ate all three meals left over, so he came up with a brilliant idea: he bought a few cats to raise. Every day after meals, I always use “cat porridge” to use fish, shrimp, meat and other dishes in the kitchen to quickly prepare a fresh and tasty meal for the newlywed daughter-in-law.
Once, when Chang Sun just prepared a fresh and delicious porridge and placed it on the table, the old lady walked into the kitchen to check and asked her grandson in surprise what she was busy with in the kitchen. Chang Sun cleverly answered, “Make cat porridge and mix leftovers with leftovers for the cat to eat.” Because the family does have a few cats, the old lady believes it to be true. Since it takes time to make “cat porridge,” the new daughter-in-law likes to eat fresh ingredients and rice with boiled soup. The new daughter-in-law likes to eat it. The little couple is even more affectionate and extremely affectionate.
Time went by so fast. Thirty years later, my daughter-in-law became a mother-in-law. After the elderly passed away, the granddaughter also took charge of the family, and her grandchildren formed a group. When the wife was in her 50th birthday, the husband asked her wife what she wanted to eat. She recalled the scene and meditated for a long time before saying, “I haven't eaten 'cat porridge' for a long time, and 'cat porridge' is still delicious.” As a husband, Huan Tianxie said, “This is easy. Now I don't need to cook secretly, so I'll go to the kitchen to do it.” The “cat porridge” is filled with the love of a young couple. It is naturally delicious and has an endless aftertaste. Neighbors left and right heard that “cat porridge” was delicious, so they all asked how to make it. That's how “cat porridge” spread.
Since then, folk cuisine from southern Fujian has spread to this kind of “cat porridge,” which uses a variety of fresh ingredients to quickly cook porridge. This traditional snack is loved by young and old in southern Fujian.
