 Zhaoyin Cave is tall and spacious, and can accommodate more than 100 people. The stone wall at the back of the cave has statues of eight ancient gods, namely: Qin Yue people (that is, Bian Magpie), Canggong, Zhang Zhongjing, Hua Tuo, Sun Simiao, Song Ci, Zhu Zhenheng, and Li Shizhen. Our country's medicine has a history of thousands of years. Many medical pharmacists have been worshipped as gods for generations to protect everyone's safety, health, and happiness!
The upper floor of Zhaoyin Cave is small. Legend has it that a woodcutter here watched the arguments of two old people. It was just a game of chess. Looking back, the handle of the knife was broken. The fairy family played a game of chess. It has been around for 100 years. It turned out that woodcutter met the fairy.
Legend has it that these two immortals are Huangdi and Qibo. In addition to alchemy at Dinghu Peak, Huang Di felt that he was already old. When he came here, he talked with Qi Bo, the most famous doctor in ancient times, about ways to prolong life, stay healthy, and get rid of diseases. “Huangdi Neijing,” the first of the four classics of traditional Chinese medicine and the foundation work of traditional Chinese medicine, was compiled by posterity based on discussions between Huangdi and Qibo.
