However, for a long time, Zhong Kui's true origin has remained an unsolved mystery.
What kind of legendary experience did Zhong Kui have? How did it become the most famous god in ancient China?
Most of what we know about Zhong Kui comes from drama and folklore stories.
In the story “Zhong Kui Marries His Sister,” it is mentioned that he was a poor student from a poor background in the Tang dynasty. He studied poetry since childhood and was extremely talented. However, he is extremely ugly, and he doesn't have the kind of elegant temperament of a book reader. Zhong Kui relied on his outstanding talents to pass the level and was finally admitted to the palace exam. However, Emperor Tang Ming couldn't stand Zhong Kui's ugliness. After spending a lot of money, he was disqualified from being accepted as a child's play. The suffering of ten years of cold windows instantly came to naught. Zhong Kui, who has a strong personality, chose to die...
The earliest version of this story was found in “Supplementary Notes” by naturalist Shen Kuo's “Written Tales of Mengxi,” which is slightly different from today's story.
