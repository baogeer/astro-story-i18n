 “If you are sick, don't ask for a doctor - take a look (bridge)”, I often hear people say the afterword of the place name Jiraibashi, but few people can clearly explain the origin of the name Jiraibashi. After a double holiday, I went back to my hometown, Mingguang, and visited some elderly people. Only then did I learn that it had quite an amazing origin.
The tap bridge is located in a group of mountains about 40 km southeast of Mingguang City. It is bordered by Chuzhou City, Lai'an County, and Xuyi County in Jiangsu. It is a famous historic mountainous town. It was called Taipingian before the Qing Dynasty. Legend has it that Yang Liulang and Li Kaifang of Yang Jiajun in the Northern Song dynasty were stationed here during the Northern Expedition. After the defeat of the Taiping Army, Hong Xiuquan, one of Hong Xiuquan's men, also set up camp here and took up the village and Zhanshan as king. In the spring of 1940, the Chinese Communist Party established the Jiashan County Anti-Japanese Democratic Government. The county government was located in Zilaiqiao Town, and Wang Daohan was the first county mayor. Although Jiraibashi is a small market town in the mountains, its geographical location determines its thousand-year history.
Right next to the town, there is a small but not too narrow Qingshui River. During the dry water season, the river is shallow, and pedestrians on both sides of the Strait have to take off their shoes and roll pants to wade through the water; during the rainy season, the water is deep, so it is very inconvenient for past pedestrians and businessmen to take a long detour. In the early years, a forest bridge was also built over the river. It was not used. There was a lot of water, and when the flood came down, it was washed away. Once again, the next year the flood came and rushed again.
In the Northern Song Dynasty, there was a year when an autumn general, Hu Yanqing, wanted to visit his uncle in Wangcun (now Baisha Wang). Seeing that people on both sides of the Strait used to be very troublesome, he stayed at Taipingan, summoned rich local households to pay money, and the people went to work to build a stone bridge. When the bridge foundation was built, there were no coalescence stones on the bridge deck. Although there are many mountains in the surrounding area, the big stones are all round grey marble, or dust stones that are not resistant to pressure, and must be mined far away. Whether it's to Mount Laojia, Mount Zhongjia, Mount Lushan, or Mount Longshan, the nearest one was more than 40 miles, but there was no local transportation capacity at the time. Hu Yanqing was helpless; in the end, he used Lin Qiaomian. After studying for two years, he was swept away by a flash flood. Since then, two stone bridge piers have remained.
In the summer of 1853, Hong Xiu, under two powerful generals Lin Fengxiang and Li Kaifang, set out from Yangzhou and carried out Hong Xiuquan's Northern Expedition plan, passing through Taipingan. He inadvertently heard ordinary people talk about Huyanqing helping to build a bridge back then. After discussions, General Lin and Li stationed their troops and decided to repair the bridge. On the afternoon of this day, General Lin and Li led several former bricklayers, carpenters, and stonemasons, and some generals to the pier, and later sent quick horses around to search for folding stones. The next morning, someone returned to report that the Longshan stone east of Jianxi was hard and large. It was granite, but it just couldn't be transported back. Generals Lin and Li are worried. One general in the military is skeptical. He said that a few recent torrential rains will definitely cause flash floods. When the time comes, the stone bridge can be brought together by people due to the sharp rise in flash floods. Lin and Li Erjiang took the advice of the outsider, sent people to mine huge granites upstream, and sent many others to prepare thick twine. However, the sun was burning for several days, and the Northern Expedition mission was also urgent. I didn't know how to do it. Suddenly, dark clouds rolling in the morning of the 6th day after dinner, lightning thunder, and heavy rain continued. The opportunity came. General Lin and Li immediately dispatched personnel to take action in the rain according to the original plan, and ordered the entire army to move out overnight after closing Ishibashi.
The next day, after the rain cleared up, the river gradually receded, and people discovered a huge piece of granite, which was carried on the bridge base. The news spread quickly throughout the town. Onlookers were all amazed. People were talking loudly. Some said that the Taiping Army did a great good thing for the people of Taiping, and praised the generals of the Taiping Army. A rich living dragon in the town came to life and said that he had been burning incense at Feilai Temple for three days, which moved the Jade Emperor. The Jade Emperor sent a giant turtle to carry the granite, and said that he saw with his own eyes that the turtle had a big fence on its head. This is an auspicious sign of Taiheian. Encouraged by him, many people came to Feilai Temple again to burn incense and nod their heads, thanking the gods. Later, people took the initiative to donate money to build stone railings on both sides of the bridge, and there are also stone lions standing at both ends of the bridge. Since then, the Zulai Bridge has been famous near and far. Later, people changed the name of Taiheian to “Jiraibashi.”
