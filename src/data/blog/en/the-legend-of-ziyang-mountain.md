 More than ten miles from Mount Women, there is a mountain with three peaks extending north for more than ten miles. Seen from afar, it looks like the word “mountain”. It is majestic, rich in trees, and vast grassland. The surrounding villagers graze here all year round.
Legend has it that Yang Erlang drove the sun off the mountain back then, and the last lame sun hid under this mountain because it couldn't run, so it is called the “Sun Shelter Mountain.” A type of Chinese herbal medicine called “zichaihu” is abundant in the mountains. Every summer and fall, some medicinal farmers around the world call it “Ziyang Mountain.” It is easy to remember. Over time, Ziyang Mountain is called this way.
This Ziyang Mountain also has another moving story that has been passed down to this day.
Farmers on both sides of the Huai River have a habit of picking up manure early in the morning. Early in the early winter of this year, the sky was just overcast. Zhu Laohan from Xiaozhuzhuang under Ziyang Mountain was carrying a dung pan and holding a dung shovel to pick up manure from Zhuangqian to Zhuang as usual. Suddenly, I saw the golden light shining continuously in the middle of Ziyang Mountain. From a distance, it looked like a woman was chasing a donkey and grinding it. Zhu Laohan wondered, who was the woman doing brushing in the middle of the mountainside this morning? I couldn't help but walk to the flashpoint. It's been a while since I left before I got close. It turned out to be a young woman who looked like a fairy, rushing to a dazed horse mill, grinding her eyes on a small pile of soybeans. Zhu Laohan was dumbfounded. The woman looked dumbfounded when she saw Zhu Laohan staring at the grinding plate. There was no squeak. She just smiled at the old man. It was considered a greeting, and she scooped a scoop of soybeans for Zhu Laohan. Zhu Laohan hurriedly pulled up his robe and tucked one corner of the robe into his belt. That woman continued to work and didn't pay attention to Zhu Laohan. Zhu Laohan thanked him and went back to the village.
When he got home, he told his family about the amazing events he had just seen, but no one believed them; they thought he was making up a story to amuse everyone. Zhu Laohan was in a hurry and angrily said, “If you don't believe me, look at the soybeans in my pocket.” Zhu Laohan pulled down his robe tucked into his belt and took a look. There were still a few grains left in it. Because Zhu Laohan didn't think of soybeans as a thing at first, they basically fell out along the way. The daughter-in-law quickly reached over and grabbed one. As soon as she saw the soybeans shining golden, she screamed: “Mom, these are golden beans!” The whole family gathered around to look at it. Qi said, “It's a golden bean.” So they all bent down and looked for beans. After half a day looking for beans, there were only eight rotten beans in total. The son asked the old man, “How many beans did the fairy give you?” Zhu Laohan said, “I gave you a scoop,” and “Oh my gosh, they all fell on the run.” My old partner and daughter-in-law shouted at the same time: “Go back and find it.”
Zhu Laohan's whole family went out together. They bent their eyes wide and followed Zhu Laohan's way home to find Golden Beans. People in the village didn't know what kind of mental illness Zhu Laohan's family had. They only knew they were looking for Golden Beans once they asked, and they all followed suit. However, so many people never found a single golden bean on the mountain from the entrance of the village, nor did they see the fairy pull the golden horse grinding the golden beans. Everyone suspected that what Zhu Laohan said was false, but Zhu Laohan did have eight golden beans. With Zhu Laohan's family background, how can there be any golden beans?
The story of Zhu Laohan, who met the fairy and got the golden beans, went viral in ten towns and eight towns. Since then, many people have been running to Ziyang Mountain every day when the sky is dark, but no one has ever experienced the beauty of the fairy Ra Golden Horse grinding golden beans.
There is an old wealthy man surnamed Fan in Ziyang Town under Ziyang Mountain. He is insatiably greedy. When he heard about this golden bean, he just wanted to get a golden horse, a gold mill, and a golden bean. He sent people to guard the mountains day and night. No one was allowed to go up the mountain, not even herding shepherds were allowed to go up the mountain, and he often personally guarded the mountain.
One day, just as Fan Laocai personally guarded the mountain, in the middle of the night, suddenly dark clouds rolled over, lightning mixed, and heavy rain. He hid under a mountain cliff. His eyes were watching his surroundings. Suddenly, a flash of lightning illuminated the night sky. A large, square hole appeared in the middle of the mountainside. The golden light was shining in the cave. A beautiful woman was sitting in front of a gold mill, grinding the golden beans. Fan Laocai came to his senses and rushed desperately to the cave despite everything. Run into the hole and pull the golden horse. At this point, there was a loud noise, the mountain shook, and a huge stone fell from the sky, blocking the opening of the cave. Since then, people have never seen Fan Laocai again.
