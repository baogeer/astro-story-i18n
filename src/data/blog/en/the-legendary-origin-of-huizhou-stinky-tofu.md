 Legend has it that in the 8th year of Kangxi in the Qing dynasty, Wang Zhihe, who came to Beijing from Huangshan in Anhui Province to take the exam, stayed idle in a conference hall and wanted to return to his hometown. Transportation was inconvenient, and there were no problems. He wanted to study in Beijing and was ready to take the exam again, and it was far from the lower academic exam period. Helpless, I had to make a living in Beijing for a while.
Wang Zhihe's family wasn't originally rich. His father set up a tofu shop in his hometown. Wang Zhihe learned how to make tofu when he was a child, so he rented a few rooms near the Anhui Hall, bought some simple utensils, ground a few liters of tofu every day, and sold it along the street.
It's summer, and sometimes the tofu left over from sale quickly becomes moldy and unedible, but I'm not willing to throw it away.
He thought hard about countermeasures. He cut this tofu into small pieces, dried it a little, found a small pot, salted it, then stopped the business and devoted himself to studying it, and gradually forgot about it.
The autumn breeze was refreshing, and Wang Zhihe resumed his old business and then made tofu to sell. I suddenly remembered the pot of marinated tofu. I quickly opened the lid, and a smell came out. When I took it out, the tofu was already gray. When I tried it with my mouth, it had a rich aroma. Although it wasn't a tasty dish, it was also intriguing. It was given to the neighbors to taste, and they all praised it.
Wang Zhihe failed in his trial and had to abandon his studies to do business and process smelly tofu according to the method he tried in the past.
The price of this product is low. It can be used as an accompaniment to meals, and is suitable for low-income workers, so it gradually opens up sales channels, and the business is becoming more and more prosperous.
After a series of fund-raising efforts, they purchased a noodle shop on the west side of Middle Road of Yanshou Street, produced and sold it themselves, and sold it themselves, and sold it at the same time.
According to their contract for the purchase of the house, it was the winter of Kangxi '17.
Since Wang Zhihe created the unique stinky tofu, after many improvements, he gradually discovered a production process for stinky tofu. The scale of production has continued to expand, the quality is better, and the reputation is higher.
At the end of the Qing dynasty, it was introduced into the palace.
Legend has it that Empress Dowager Cixi also liked to eat it in late fall and early winter, and also listed it as a side dish for a royal meal, but she thought the name was indecent and named “Qingfang” based on its characteristic blue square.
Huizhou's stinky tofu, commonly known as “Big Stinky,” was created by Wang Zhihe during the Kangxi era of the Qing Dynasty. Emperor Kangxi praised it after tasting it. He specially wrote his own book “Qingfang: Two Characters Give Wang Zhihe,” and since then it has become very famous. Along with Shaoxing, Zhejiang, and Stinky Dry in Huainan, Anhui, it is a third of the ranking, and is famous at home and abroad.
