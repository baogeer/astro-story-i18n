 This was a spring day during the Wanli era of the Myojin sect. A strange test was being conducted in a mountain pavilion on the outskirts of Tsuneyama. The main examiners were two old men with good intentions. The candidates were two young boys, one named Xu Tiaolang and the other Liu Hanlao. This exam was not for merit; it was for the purpose of marrying a beautiful woman.
What kind of girl can be honored like this? That was the chief executive — Wang Runzhen, the daughter of Shen Bigui. Since she is the daughter of the Shen family, why is her last name Wang? There's a slight twist here. Wong Yun-ching's mother is Tong Yuen-xiu, known as the “Beauty of Changshan”. She married the Shuxiangmendi Wang family. Her husband and wife loved and happy. Who knows a disaster struck. When her daughter Wang Runzhen was two years old, her son-in-law fell ill and died, leaving an orphan and widow with nothing to rely on, so she had to remarry to the Shen family to fill her house.
Her husband, Shen Bigui, was a kind businessman. His family was well-off. His ex-wife died young, and he had no children. After remarrying Tong, Tong did not have children either. Wang Runzhen, the only child in the family, was Wang Runzhen. Shen Bigui treated her as if she were her own daughter, and she was very careful. Her mother is a beautiful woman. The stepfather is concerned about his daughter. Since childhood, he has invited Master Wang to teach Wang Runzhen. He is familiar with the four books, the five sutras, and the rhythms of poetry.
Her girlfriend had a long list of good girls. When Wang Runzhen grew old, the matchmaker who came to the door to raise her family broke through the Shen family. Shen Bigui and Tong went through a lot of thought, and finally selected two candidates for her son-in-law. One was Xu Tiaolang and the other was Liu Hanlao. Just in March of Yangchun, the flowers were in full bloom. Shen Bigui specially set up a dinner party in a quiet place surrounded by mountains and water in the suburbs. For the sake of prudence, they also specially invited Shen Gengyun, an old man from the family, who is well-informed and highly respected, to act as a staff officer. When the two boys, Xu and Liu, received the invitation, they all only thought that the Shen family had already met them. They had a special banquet to celebrate their family, and secretly rejoiced in their hearts. When they arrived at the mountain pavilion, they discovered that they also had another enemy present. They soon understood the intention of this dinner party, and inevitably became a bit nervous.

The next step was the written test. After a moment of silence, Shen Gengyun spoke four poetry questions. These four topics are interesting. They are the names of the four stripes hanging in the local Suiyun-ji Temple. They are: “Save Flowers, Get Up Early,” “Love the Day and Night, Sleep Late,” “Scrub Water in Your Hand,” and “Get Your Clothes Full of Fragrance,” which are just right for the current season. On the stone table in the pavilion, the Shen family had already arranged paper, pen and ink, and the two boys sat separately.
The remaining two elderly people, not wanting to bother the young people looking for poems and phrases, quietly raised a glass to each other.
