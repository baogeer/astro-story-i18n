 There is a Taoist in Chaotianguan who likes spitting spells. An old man lived in his house, which happened to have the same interests as him, so they both became Taoist friends. After staying for a few years, every time the incense festival sacrificed gods, the old man walked away for the first ten days; he only came back after the sacrifice was over. The Taoist asked him skeptically, and the old man said, “The two of us are already irrevocable; let me tell you the truth. I'm a fox. At the time of the sacrifice, all the immortals clean up the filth. I had no place to go, so I had to hide somewhere else.”
Another year, when it was time for the sacrifice, the old man left again. He hasn't been back for a long time. The Taoist is very skeptical. He came back suddenly one day. The Taoist asked him why, and the old man said, “I almost never saw you. The last time I sacrificed, I was supposed to stay away from it, but I was too lazy to walk. Seeing that the gutter was very hidden, I hid it under a rolling urn for a while. Unexpectedly, the spirit had cleared up here. When they saw me, they were so angry that they wanted to whip me. I was so scared. I ran away in a hurry, and the spiritual officer was in a hurry to chase me. When I got to the banks of the Yellow River, I saw that they chased me to the edge of the water. I had no choice but to plunge headlong into a large toilet pit. The spirit was dirty, and only then went back away. I climbed out, got smelly, and couldn't travel around the world anymore, so I washed it in water and hid in a cave. After a few hundred days, all the dirty stuff was clean. Today I'm saying goodbye and telling you that you should also hide elsewhere; the day of the heist is coming; this is not a blessed place.” When I finished speaking, I said goodbye.
According to the old man's words, the Taoist also moved elsewhere. It didn't take long for the transformation of Jiashen to occur.
