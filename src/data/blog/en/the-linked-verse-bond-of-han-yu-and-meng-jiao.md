 A synonym is a beautiful landscape in the creation of ancient poetry. Two or more people each sing a sentence, link, or multiple sentences together to create a composition. Legend has it that it began with “Bai Liang's Poems” and was very popular in the Tang Dynasty. Han Yu and Meng Jiao collaborated on a large number of joint sentences, innovated joint sentence rules, promoted the development of joint sentences, and left behind a wonderful story in the literary world.
Meng Jiao is 17 years older than Han. In 792, the two were admitted as contestants together. Han Yu is famous on the list, and Sun Mountain is famous in the suburbs of Meng. Han Yu wrote a poem to comfort him: “People in Chang'an are rich and poor. Family and friends are out of date, and they also have their own ways to entertain themselves. There is literature and history in the bad room, and the high gate is rich and noble. How can we discern between good and bad, and want to divide the good and the bad.” Meng Jiao sadly left Chang'an, and Han Yu wrote “Meng Sheng's Poems” to send them. At the beginning, he said, “Meng Shengjiang Sailor is ancient and ancient. It is said that reading the books of the ancients is ancient and still present.” Expectations are high at the end: “If you get it, you think back; there is no point in staying engaged for a long time. Bian He tried three times; his future was in the fall.” His purpose in writing this poem was not only to comfort Meng Jiao, but also to make Meng Jiao famous in Beijing.
Han Yu (768-824), a native of Heyang, Henan, is known as “Han Changli” and “Mr. Changli,” a Tang dynasty writer, philosopher, and proponent of the “Ancient Literature Movement”. He was revered by later generations as the “Eight Great Masters of Tang and Song,” and is known as the “Great Duke of Writers” and “100 Dynasties of Literature.”
Meng Jiao (751-814), a native of Huzhou Wukang (present-day Deqing). He was not the best in both trials. He was 46 years old when he joined the middle school in 796, and was later appointed Liyang County Liyang County Liyang County Captain. Dissatisfied with this position, he left Lin Quan. The county commissioner asked someone else to act on his behalf, calling him a “false lieutenant,” and divided half of Meng Jiao's salary to the “false lieutenant.” Meng Jiao was even more disappointed. Later, recommended by Henan Yin Zheng and Yu Qing, he worked in land and water transport in Henan. He cooperated with Lulang, and spent much of his later years in Luoyang. In 814, Zheng Yuqing acted as Xing Yuanyin and Zuo Mengjiao as a staff officer for the Xing Yuan Army. Meng Jiao was happy to go there, but he became ill and died during the journey. He died at the age of 64. Meng Jiao is known as a “Poetry Prisoner,” and along with Jia Dao, he is also known as “Suburban Cold Island.” His masterpiece “Yu Ziyin” is a household name and has moved countless people.
Han Yu is deeply affectionate with Meng Jiao. His poetry ideas and style are close. They appreciate each other, have close relationships, and sing a lot. He became the founder of the “Han and Meng Poetry School” and is known as “Meng Shi Han Pen.”
Meng Jiao had no ambition throughout his life and lived in poverty. Han Yu always gave him meticulous material and spiritual care. Every time Meng Jiao had setbacks, Han Yu always encouraged each other in poetry and writing. In February 798, Han Yu wrote “Drunk in the East Field”. The entire poem was moving: “In the past, I hated the two for not being the same because I read Li Bai and Du Fu's poems. When I live in the wild side of the East, how can I get back on track. Higashino can't be an official. He has a white head and a black dragon bell. Han Zi is a bit aggressive and is ashamed to lean on Nagasong. Keep your head down and pray for Dongye, and I hope it will always be like a blessing. If Higashino doesn't look back, it's like hitting a giant bell. I want to be a cloud, and Higashino becomes a dragon. They chased Higashino from all sides, but there was no reason to part ways.” Han Yu is also a proud person. He never praises others easily, but when it comes to Meng Jiao, he is willing to “bow down and worship Dongye.” He positioned his relationship with Meng Jiao as the relationship between Li Bai and Du Fu. It can be seen that Han Yu had a desire to become famous in poetry with Meng Jiao for a long time.
Han Yu also wrote “A Book with Meng Dongye” and “Foreword to Meng Dongye”, which have been widely publicized. The core idea is “when something is unsettled, it will blur”. Han Yu “cried” for Meng Jiao and also “cried” for himself.
The “Han and Meng” synonym can be called a “perfect match”. 806 was the peak period of the two's joint poems, and there are as many as 14 songs that have been handed down to this day. This year, Han Yu just recalled Chang'an from being derogatory. The two met again after a long absence and often gathered to sing poems.
“Han Meng”'s longest “Seongnam Joint Sentence” (September 806) reached 154 times, 77 times each. “Seongnam Joint Law” is iconic in the history of joint sentences because they pioneered the “cross-sentence joint law.” Han Yu wrote the first sentence, Meng Jiao wrote the second and third sentences, then Han Yu wrote the fourth and fifth sentences. In this cycle, Han Yu finally ended with a sentence. The previous joint sentences were all linked separately for each person. Regardless of whether they were two or four sentences, each person structured the duality themselves. Han Yu changed to starting with the second sentence. The other person must first read the upper sentence, then write the next sentence, and leave it up to the other person to find the right sentence. This has the advantage of “inheriting from above,” making the joint sentence more coherent and natural.
Of course, “Han Meng” also had quite a few joint works, such as “Sending Xing Joint Sentence” (806), where Meng Jiao premiered: “My Heart Follows the Moonlight and Writes the Central Court”, and Han Yu continued with the second link: “Moonlight is sometimes obscure, I forget it.” It was then alternately performed. The entire poem consists of 12 parts. The final two parts are: “Yin Jianli is not far away, Perrin will always be fragrant” (suburb). “If I don't listen to my husband, who will make the sound better” (Heal). The same goes for “Giving the Swordsman to Li Yuanlian” (806). It has a 10-part series of poems. Meng Jiaosheng started, and Han became successful. What is quite wonderful is the two-part in the middle: “Taiyi is packed with treasure, the Immortal and Shanqifen (suburbs). You can use it to deter all gods, but only strengthen the three armies (heal)”.

After “Han Meng,” “intersentence syndication” became a common syntactic rule used by future generations. Similar “variants” include: 1 person starts with 3 sentences, then 2 sentences per person, and ends with 1 sentence; 1 person starts 1 sentence, then 4 sentences per person, and ends with 3 sentences at the end. The principle is the same. Open the question in the singular and then match it with an even number to ensure that the joint sentence both “takes” and “starts”. It doesn't matter how many sentences or combinations of singular and even numbers are there.
The “Han and Meng Joint Sentence” also sometimes attracted others to participate. For example, “Meeting Joint Sentence” (June 806), three people from Han Yu, Meng Jiao, and Zhang participated, and 34 joint sentences. A “joint sentence” requires not only a high level of skill, but also the interlocutor to have a sense of mind, grasp of the subject, and create charm. The ancients often enjoyed joint sentences when they had a feast in the month before the flower. Occasionally, they might have a chance encounter, but if “Han Meng” became a fixed “joint sentence partner” and created a large number of masterpieces, it must have been the crystallization of like-minded and shared hearts. “Han and Meng Joint Sentence” is a model in the history of Chinese poetry.
