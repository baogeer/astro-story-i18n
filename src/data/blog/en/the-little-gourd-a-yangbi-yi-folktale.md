 Ruan Zhen
Wang Jin, a bachelor, rode around the village on a motorbike and specialized in buying antique treasures. After a few years, his ability to discern antiques became very good, and he also made some money.
On this day, he rode back to the county from the rural bazaar. Seeing that it was about to rain, he increased his throttle and drove at full speed. While passing by Cuizhuzhai Health Center, he suddenly ran out of the health center and stopped his car on the road. Scared, he braked urgently, and his motorcycle hit the side, almost hitting that person. He shouted, “You don't want to die!”
It was a beautiful young woman holding a little girl and cried, “Big brother, please take me to the county hospital.” It turns out that her daughter was bitten by a poisonous snake, and the health center was unable to treat her, so she had to seek medical treatment at the county hospital. There are still more than 20 kilometers from here to the county hospital. No wonder she is so anxious.
The little girl looked like she was five or six years old. The snake bit her calf and belly, and her teeth were still fresh. Wang Jin said to the woman, “Don't worry; I have medicine for snake damage.” I saw Wang Jin use his mouth to suck the wound vigorously, vomit bleeding fluid, pull out a small bottle from his body, pour out the medicine powder, and apply the wound. He said, “Thanks to the bite that didn't last long, my snake wound medicine still works. However, I also got a bit of treatment from the county hospital.
Wang Jin took her mother and daughter, and before they went very far, it started raining heavily. Wang Jin gave her raincoat to her mother and daughter. The rain never stopped, and Wang Jin became a chicken in the soup. When she arrived at the county hospital, the woman didn't have enough money, so Wang Jin took the initiative to pay for it. Wang Jin asked her if she had any relatives in the city. She said, “No.” Wang Jin said, “I'll go home and change my clothes, and I'll see you later.
Wang Jin has excellent cooking skills. He prepared the meal and took it to the hospital. The woman ate it and just praised her sister-in-law's good craftsmanship. Wang Jin smiled and said, “I'm a bachelor. Where did my sister-in-law come from?” The woman blushed and looked down.
Wang Jin suffered from wind chill and was not comfortable, so he stopped going to Taobao. He was bored and panicked at home, so he went to the hospital to chat with the woman. The woman told Wang Jin that her name was Huang Youfeng, her daughter's name was Xiao Rou, and that her husband had died of illness for three years.
Three days later, Xiao Rou was discharged from the hospital, but Wang Jin fell ill, and Huang Youfeng stayed to take care of Wang Jin. Huang Youfeng bought groceries, cooked meals and did housework. Like a housewife, Wang Jin felt very warm.
After Wang Jin got well, he took Huang Youfeng and Xiaorou for a day of sightseeing and took many photos.
Huang Youfeng is going home. She said that she has pigs and chickens at home, and she is afraid that Xiaorou Milk will be affected. Wang Jin sent Huang Youfeng's mother and daughter back to their home in Cuizhuzhai. Wang Jin was amazed by the bamboo crafts placed in the house. Huang Youfeng told him that these were all hand-made by Xiao Rou's father. Xiao Rou's dad sold bamboo crafts in exchange for pocket money. After Xiao Rou's dad died, she kept these bamboo crafts as a memorial. Originally, she was also skilled in bamboo weaving. However, Xiao Rou's father had been ill for three years and had many debts. As a result, her illness was not cured, her personal finances were empty, and her life was tight. How could she have any spare money to invest in bamboo weaving?
Huang Youfeng said that this time, if he hadn't met Wang Jin, he didn't know what to do. The money owed to Wang Jin can only be paid back gradually. Wang Jin said, “If it's so difficult for you, I won't let you pay back a penny. If there are any difficulties in the future, feel free to come to me. Huang Youfeng tearfully said, “I remember your great thoughts and virtues in my heart.
As soon as Wang Jin heard this, he knew that Huang Youfeng was under psychological pressure. He does good deeds sincerely, and he doesn't want people to be burdened with any ideological burden. He made a clever move and pointed to a small gourd hanging from the beam and said, “Look, isn't that just a treasure? It's worth a lot of money.
Baffled by Huang Youfeng, handing over the gourd to Wang Jin, said, “You can really talk. This is a treasure that Xiaorou's father took from the field three years ago and hung it on the roof. Where is it?
Wang Jin took a close look at the gourd. To put it bluntly, based on my years of Taobao experience, this gourd can be worth at least 10,000 yuan. Can you sell it to me?
Huang Youfeng is an intelligent woman. Her heart is like a mirror. She knows that Wang Jin is changing her methods to help herself. She couldn't refute Wang Jin's kindness, and promised to sell the gourd to Wang Jin as a surprise.
Wang Jin also understood that Huang Youfeng pretended to follow his own intentions. He couldn't help but secretly praise Huang Youfeng as a thoughtful woman.
Huang Youfeng said that before her birth, Xiao Rou's father said that she has a treasure in her family. It is a priceless treasure that can't be exchanged for gold or silver, yet he never saw any object. Is this the gourd?
Wang Jin said that the priceless treasure Xiao Rou's father said is definitely not this gourd; you should search for it slowly. Huang Youfeng said that as long as I have this treasure at home, I will definitely be able to find it. Wang Jin laughed loudly; I believe it.
They both laughed heartily.
Wang Jin's wife divorced Wang Jin. The main reason was that Wang Jin's wife thought Wang Jin had no fertility. However, after her divorce and remarriage with Wang Jin, she still did not have a baby. After an examination, I learned that she would not have children herself. When Wang Jin learned that he had been wronged, he was happy to sing the song all night long.
Wang Jin spread out a picture of the day he visited. He chose a photo taken with Huang Youfeng's mother and daughter, and asked someone to carve it on a gourd and hang it on the bedside. You can see it as soon as you open your eyes. He always dreams that Huang Youfeng is giving birth to a son for him, and he often wakes up laughing from his dreams.
Xiao Rouyu is an orphan old woman from her neighbor. It was she who helped Huang Youfeng get through difficult times during the three years when Xiao Rou's father was in bed. Wong Yau Fung is a kind person, so she moved to her home to live together and treat her as a mother. Xiaorou Milk told Wang Jin that Huang Youfeng lost her fertility after a major illness. Xiaorou picked up an abandoned baby on the side of the road.
Only then did Wang Jin understand why Huang Youfeng always avoided him. However, Huang Youfeng can't give birth to a son for him. What should I do? He thought about giving up.
Xiao Rouyu had the sequelae of a cerebral thrombosis. Wang Jin bought a wheelchair and often helped Huang Youfeng do this and that, sharing Huang Youfeng's burden as much as possible. Wang Jin saw that Huang Youfeng was busy and had no complaints; even Xiao Rou clutched her hands and feet.
Wang Jin also had a new understanding of Huang Youfeng. Xiaorou had no children, but someone she could rely on to end her old age. Wang Jin understood that changing people's hearts is more important than blood lineage. He is happy that he has met the best woman in the world. No wonder Father Xiao Rou said that the family has a priceless treasure.
A year later, Huang Youfeng used Wang Jin's 10,000 yuan money to expand the bamboo weaving process and became the village's leader in getting well off. When Wang Jin proposed to her again, Huang Youfeng asked, “I can't have a son for you; don't you regret it?” Wang Jin said that it is enough for us to have Xiaorou.
On their wedding night, they both looked at the gourds hanging from their wedding bed and all said, “This is our priceless treasure.
Under the soft light, the gourd shook, and the three people engraved on it laughed very sweetly.
