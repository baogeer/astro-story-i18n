 Somebody's history, I forgot his name. One day, he was lying in his study during the day. Suddenly, a small guard of honor walked out of a corner of the house. Horses are as big as frogs, and humans as thin as fingers. The small guard of honor consists of dozens of people. An official, wearing a woolen hat and an embroidered robe, sat in a car carried by the two, and went out one after another. Taishi felt strange in his heart; he suspected that he was drowsy and dazzled. But then I saw another kid. He came back to the house, walked all the way to the bed with a bag the size of a fist in his hand, and introduced himself, “The owner of my family has a small gift. I am dedicated to Taishi.” After speaking, I stood facing Taishi, but didn't open the bag and take out anything. After waiting for a while, she smiled and said, “It's a small gift; it's useless even if you think of it too much; why not give it to a little person.” Taishi nodded, and the villain happily carried the bag away. Haven't seen him since. Unfortunately, Taishi was a little afraid at the time, and he never asked where the villain came from.
