 When she was still in her mom Sophia's womb, Little Rostar was bound to be no ordinary baby girl. It was February of last year. In southern Mozambique, the sky was always overcast and rainy.
On this day, Sophia was hungry and rushed home from the hospital. The doctor clearly told her that she would be about to give birth in a few days, and recommended that she stay in the hospital for observation. However, Sophia thought that due to the rain over the past few days, the rain had already leaked in several places in her home. The two children and the two elderly had not settled down yet, and she still had to prepare some laundry and baby products, so she decided to take a trip home first.
As soon as Sophia got home, there was certainly water everywhere in the house. The two children, their grandmother, and grandma were all tired and stuck in bed, and seemed helpless. When her daughter Lina saw her mom come back, she shouted, “Mom, I'm hungry!”
Sophia was so busy walking into the kitchen that she couldn't help but be shocked. The kitchen was in a low position, and water had already flooded in. The floor is already full of water, and the pots and dishes are all floating and floating on the water. Seeing this, Sophia immediately realized that if this heavy rain did not stop, it wouldn't take long, and the house would be flooded.
However, at this time, it was already dark, and everything was shrouded in this pouring rain. Where could they move? It seems that she can only wait until dawn to find a solution. Sophia locked the door with two nukes, raised the big bed, and let the children and the elderly sleep, and sat by the bedside and waited for dawn. She kept praying in her heart: Oh my God, be merciful and stop raining.
The next day, when it was almost bright, Sophia was awakened by Lina's cry as soon as she fell asleep. She opened her eyes and took a closer look. It was amazing. The water was about to spread to the edge of the bed, so she had to be evacuated quickly!
Where can I evacuate to? The old family was old, the little one, and she had a big stomach. Sophia was distressed.
“Mom, let's climb up the big tree outside the door!” My seven-year-old son Neto said.
Sophia looked at the mavola tree outside the door. It was a tall fruit tree with thick branches and strong enough to withstand the weight of several of them. In the past, every time my son climbed up the tree, he would always be scolded by her, but now it seems that this tree has become their family's only hope!
Too late to think about it. Sophia prepared dry food, found plastic sheets to protect from the wind and rain, and scrambled to lift the two children and the elderly to the tree. Sophia knew it was impossible for her to go back to the hospital. Shino looked out, except water, but water! The family, young and old, also need her. Now they can only hide in this life-saving tree. Everyone depends on each other. I hope the rescuers can arrive soon! At this point, Little Rostar in Sophia's stomach slipped uncontrollably, “Little baby, let's lie down on Mom's stomach for a while and stop messing up the mess with her already strained mom!” Sophia patted herself on the stomach, like saying hello to Little Rostar.
It was raining heavily, and there was no intention of stopping at all. The family just sat on a tree branch and held hands with each other.
The day seemed particularly long. The flood was getting higher and higher, and the family watched their home slowly being flooded. There was also a corpse floating on the surface of the water from time to time, which made the two frightened children cry out loud in horror. When will the rain stop, and when can we expect rescuers to come? Sophia's heart was empty, and her fear grew stronger and stronger.
At night, something terrible finally happened.
The floods destroyed the entire electricity supply system, and there was no trace of the moon in the sky for a long time. It was as dark as if the whole Earth had been engulfed by a giant object, and everything was shrouded in deep darkness. No one in the family could see anyone. After a day of ups and downs, everyone didn't even have the strength to shout. The elderly grandma wanted to close her eyes and take a nap. Who knows, relax a bit. If she was careless, she slipped off a slipping branch. Before she had time to say “ah,” she was swallowed up by the flood in an instant...
At dawn on the third day, Sophia suddenly felt unbearable abdominal pain. She knew she was about to give birth, but how was it possible for Little Rostar to be born safely on this branch of a tree that couldn't sit still? She clenched her teeth, held back a period of severe pain, and reminded the two children and the elderly from time to time to grab the tree trunk and not to doze off.
Suddenly, “suddenly,” the sound of the helicopter's roaring motor woke Sophia's family from extreme fear and disappointment, and rescuers finally arrived.
“We are here! Come save us!” Neto first discovered the helicopter. He waved his little hand, and the family shouted with all their might, but he couldn't make a sound.
Rescuers didn't find them; helicopters hovered above their heads to fly somewhere else. At this point, Little Rostar seemed unable to wait. A loud cry not only announced her birth in the storm, but also summoned rescuers aboard the helicopter.
Only then did the rescuers discover that there were survivors among the thick leaves. What surprised them even more was that Sophia was lying on the branch holding the trunk with one hand and holding a baby girl with her umbilical cord hard with the other hand. She was moaning in pain. They immediately called for emergency help to the headquarters.
The helicopter from the headquarters arrived in time. The midwife quickly got off the helicopter and helped the newborn Rosta cut her umbilical cord from a tree branch. Afterwards, several rescuers worked together, and it took a lot of effort to finally rescue the mother, daughter, and family from the helicopter.
All of this was captured on his camera by the reporter who arrived with the helicopter! On the night of the same day, the news that Little Rostar had been born in a tree and was saved spread to all sides through TV, newspapers, and other media reports, and the whole world was shocked. People have learned how much disaster the floods in Mozambique have caused to the local people. Large amounts of relief supplies and donations have poured into Mozambique, enabling this African country to receive timely relief.
In this way, Rosta Jr., became a famous “national hero” throughout Mozambique. When the Mozambican President met Sophia's family, he happily held Little Rosta in his arms and gently touched her face and said, “You are our little hero. You made the world aware of our disaster situation and welcomed their help.”
