 There is a long waterfall on the steep mountainside, like a woman lying on a cliff and hanging her long, white hair down the mountain. Locals call this waterfall white hair water.
There's a story about a girl with long hair going around here!
A long time ago, there was no water near steep mountains. The water that people here eat and the water they use in the fields depends on the rain falling from the sky; if it doesn't rain, they can get water from a small river seven miles away. The water here is as precious as oil.
There was a girl in a village near a steep mountain. Her hair was dark blue and dragged from the top of her head to her heels. She wears her hair around her head on weekdays, and wraps it around her neck and shoulders when she can't finish wrapping it around her head.
Everyone called this girl a long-haired girl.
There is only one crazy paralyzed mom in the family. She can't move while lying in bed. The whole family only depends on Changfa Mei raising pigs alone to make ends meet.
Every day, the long-haired girl wraps her hair around her head and neck, fetches water in a small river seven miles away, and goes to steep mountains to pull pork vegetables back to feed the pigs. She's busy every day.
One day, the girl with long hair carried a bamboo basket to a steep mountain to pull pork dishes. She climbed the mountainside, climbed over a large cliff, and saw a radish vegetable growing on a large stone wall. The leaves were verdant and green, which was very cute.
She thought: If you take this radish home and cook it to eat, it must be sweet and delicious.
She pulled out the radish dish vigorously with both hands, pulled out a round hole, and a stream of clear spring water flowed out of the hole. After a while, with the sound of a “brush,” the radish flew out of her hand; with another “bub” sound, the radish was still stuck in the hole in the stone wall, and the water couldn't flow out.
The long-haired girl was very thirsty and wanted to drink water. She pulled out the radish again, and the spring water flowed out of the hole. She brought her mouth close to her eyes and took a few sips of water to his heart's content. This water is cool and sweet, like pear juice. As soon as her mouth left the stone hole, the radish flew out of her hand with a “brush” sound; with another “bub” sound, the radish was still stuck in the hole in the stone wall, and the water couldn't flow out.
The long-haired girl looked blankly on the cliff.
Suddenly, a gust of wind blew in and blew the long-haired girl into a cave.
A man covered in yellow hair sits on a stone pier in the cave. He spoke ill of the long-haired girl and said, “I discovered the secret of this mountain spring for you; you can't tell anyone else. If you tell anyone else that they are here to get water, I'll kill you. I'm a mountain god, remember!”
A gust of wind blew and swept the long-haired girl to the bottom of the mountain.
The long-haired girl walked home feeling exhausted.
She was afraid to tell her mother about the springs, let alone the people in the village. As soon as she thought of the vicious Yellow Hairy Man, she immediately felt goosebumps all over her body.
The girl with long hair is a good-hearted girl. How could she not tell the people in the village the news about Spring Water? However, how dare she tell the people in the village about the spring water news?
She's in so much pain!
The girl with long hair turned out to be a lively and lively girl, but recently she has become a dumb kid.
She saw the dirt in the field dry and the crops yellow and yellow.
She saw men, women, and children in the village picking buckets every day to a small river seven miles away to fetch water. Everyone was so sweaty and out of breath.
She wanted to tell the people in the village that there is spring water on a steep mountain. As long as you tear off the radish, cut the radish, and cut a big hole, the spring water will flow down the mountain in a loud voice. As soon as she opened her mouth, she said, “There is... on a steep mountain,” but as soon as she thought about the vicious Yellow Mao Man, her words swallowed up her stomach.
She's in so much pain!
She can't eat, she can't sleep, she's dumb, she's like a fool.
Her eyes are no longer watery; they are dark and dark. Her face was no longer red and crimson, but yellow wax-waxed. Her long hair is no longer dull, but burnt.
Mom grabbed the thin hand of the long-haired girl and said, “Kid, what's wrong with you?”
However, the long-haired girl bit her lip and didn't speak.
Day by day, January and January passed.
The girl with long hair changed from swarthy to snow-white. She had no energy to sort through, and she had no energy to pull up, leaving Snow White's long hair scattered all over her body, like a white haired person.
“Ahhh! That's weird! Young girl with snow-white hair!”
This story is being spread all over the place.
The long-haired girl leaned blankly at the front door and watched the people coming and going. She murmured, “There is... on a steep mountain.” Speaking of this, she clenched her lips with her teeth and bit out blood marks one by one.
One day, the girl with long hair leaned at the door and saw a white-bearded old man carrying a load of water from a small river seven miles away and walking majestically on the road. I didn't pay attention, hit a stone and fell to the ground. The water spilled out, the bucket was broken, the old man's leg was broken, and blood was flowing all the time.
The long-haired girl ran over to lift up the old man. She tore off a piece of clothing from her body and crouched down to tie the old man's wound. She heard the old man humming huh. She looked at the old man's closed eyes, and the wrinkles on her face twitched.
The girl with long hair said to herself, “Long haired girl, you are so afraid to die! Because you're afraid of death, the mud on the field is just stupid! Because you're afraid of death, the crops on the field are dying! Because you're afraid of death, the talents in the whole village are sweaty and out of breath! Dad broke his foot because you were afraid of death! You, you, you! ...”
She hit herself on the head.
She couldn't help it anymore. She suddenly shouted to the old man, “Dad, there's a spring on a steep mountain! As soon as you remove the radish, cut the radish, and cut a large stone hole, the spring water will flow down the mountain in a loud voice. Really, really! I've seen it with my own eyes!”
She didn't wait for the old man to answer. She stood up, wore long white hair, ran back and forth around the village like a lunatic, and shouted:
“There's a spring on a steep mountain! As soon as you remove the radish, cut the radish, and cut a large stone hole, the spring water will flow down the mountain in a loud voice. Really, really! I've seen it with my own eyes! Let's all go!”
Then she talked about how she discovered the spring water, but she just didn't say the words of the mountain god.
People in Murakami always thought that the girl with long hair was a good-hearted kid, and everyone believed her words.
Some of the people in Murakami carried kitchen knives and others carried steel chisels, and followed the girl with long hair up a steep mountain and over a large cliff. The girl with long hair pulled out the radish from the stone wall with her hands and threw it on the stone, and said, “Everyone cut this radish, hurry up! Shred it up, quick!”
Chop up the carrots with a few kitchen knives and cut them into shreds.
The cavernous spring water came out brushing out of the ground. However, the stone cave is only the size of a teacup, and there isn't much water flowing out of the spring.
Changfa Mei also said, “Everyone use steel chisels to cut hard! Cut out the stone hole wide. Dig it fast! Quick chisel!”
A few chisels, “punch the bottom”, chisel, chisel, etc. After a while, the stone cave was as big as a big bowl! One more time, Sekidongan is as big as a bucket! After a while, the stone cave is as big as a big water tank!
The spring water rushed down the mountain in a loud noise.
The people in the village laughed hehehehe.
At this point, a gust of wind blew in, and the long-haired girl disappeared.
Everyone looked at the spring water and laughed, and they didn't notice that the girl with long hair wasn't around.
Someone later said, “What about long-haired girls?” As someone answered, “She almost went home first. Let's go home and report the good news to my mom who is sick in bed!”
Everyone was happy to climb over the cliff and walk down the mountain.
However, instead of going home first, the girl with long hair was taken by the mountain god.
