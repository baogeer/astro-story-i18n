 [Author: Chen Jinshun (Anhui)]
This year, a flying thief named Bu Tiandiao came out of Xuancheng in southern Anhui. He had excellent martial arts, killed and set fire, picked flowers and slept willows, and did almost nothing evil. Every time he committed a crime, he had to draw a white skull on the wall. People in Jianghu called the “Skeleton Thief.”
The Skeleton Thief once worshipped a stranger from the West and developed a mixed spirit. He was able to change his shape and appearance at will. Although the government posted a list many times and invited martial arts experts to catch the Skeleton Thief, in the end, the Skeleton Thief went haunted, and they didn't know anything about his appearance or whereabouts, and the results came to an end.
On this day, a mysterious woman named Mei Niang rented a front door and opened a hotel in the east of the city. She always covered her face with a light veil in front of others, and no one could see her real face clearly. The wine she sells is made by her own hands, and the name of the wine is also very appealing; it's called “Rose Red.”
On the first day of opening, Mei Niang Hotel had a good start. I don't know where she learned the perfect “rose red” wine. No matter how much you drink, you won't get drunk. After a few bowls, it can be quickly excreted from the body. It looks like sweat, but that sweat is red and has a rose-like scent. After drinking this kind of wine and taking another bath, the whole person will feel refreshed and otherworldly.
For a while, Mei Niang and her “Rose Red” wine became famous. Guests traveling from south to north were honored to be able to enjoy Mei Niang's delicious wine. Some rich people, no matter how far they go, have to run three or five places to the Mei Niang Hotel. However, it didn't take long for Mei Niang to set a strange rule to open the store every three days. No matter who got addicted to alcohol, how much money they were willing to spend. If they wanted to ask her to open the store for another day, she wouldn't agree. Later, she only opened the store for two days a month. Later, she only opened the store once every three months. At one point, County Governor Huangfu Yiyi wanted to give her mother a birthday and personally visited Mei Niang to buy a few copies of “Rose Red”. Since she hadn't arrived on the day the hotel opened, she said nothing and wouldn't sell them. The pressure was tight. She actually pulled a knife from her waist and lay it on her neck and said, “Adults, little girls set this rule. Naturally, there is a reason for this. If you push me any more, the little girl will die in front of you!” It made Huangfu Yi blush for a while, then shook her head and hesitated.
Mei Niang Hotel has hired quite a few guys. Someone calculated an account for her. Since she made this strange rule, not only did she not make any money, but instead had to put up quite a bit of silver every day to pay her partner's salary. If she doesn't do such a booming business, what is her plan? Everyone was puzzled. Despite much discussion from others, Mei Niang continued to do her own thing. In order to prevent someone apparently not being able to get “rose red” wine but secretly steals it, she specially arranged for her partner to patrol the area around the hotel day and night.
One night, her hotel was met by thieves, and none of the patrolling companions actually found out. While checking the warehouse the next morning, Mei Niang discovered that the wine had been stolen. The thieves stole a total of ten “Rose Red” altars, and three more are empty. As can be seen, the thief was quite timid. After entering the storeroom, he actually sat down and drank three bottles of wine before leaving. On one side of the wall, Mei Niang suddenly found a large skull painted on it. She laughed secretly: it seems that the skeleton thief has finally appeared, and the drama is yet to come.
As a result, the Skeleton Thief couldn't have imagined it. After drinking those three cups of “Rose Red,” he accidentally left a clear line of red footprints on the ground because the alcohol was quickly excreted from his body. Mei Niang immediately summoned all the friends in the store and followed the footprints to the foot of a mountain more than ten miles west of the city, only to discover that the footprints had suddenly disappeared next to an inconspicuous tomb. A stone monument was erected in the weeds in front of the tomb. It is vaguely visible that the monument has this line engraved on it: The tomb of Bu Tiandiao. Oh my God! Mei Niang and her friends were dumbfounded. Is it true that the Skeleton Thief is a dead person? Was it his ghost who stole it? Impossible! Mei Niang definitely doesn't believe that people can commit crimes even after death; there must be a reason for this. She took a picture on the tombstone. Suddenly, with a “squeak,” the tombstone moved to the side, revealing a dark hole. Immediately, a strong scent of roses floated out of the hole.
“Hurry up and open!” Mei Niang shouted and quickly flashed to the side. Two of the guys ran slowly and fell to the ground with a “flapping” sound, and were instantly unconscious. Mei Niang took out the antidote she had prepared in advance and put it in their mouths. Soon, they both woke up. No one knew what had happened, and she didn't explain it. After the scent of roses and alcohol in the cave had dissipated, she rushed in to take a look and found a fleshy person lying there motionless. There happened to be ten cans of wine next to him. Mei Niang guessed that this person must be a skeleton thief, but she just couldn't imagine that he would hide in an unknown tomb. She asked her buddy to tie him up, lift him out of the cave, and quickly deliver him to the government office. When I arrived at the county government office, after a surprise investigation by Huangfu, this person was the skeleton thief that people were talking about!
A few days later, the skeleton thief was taken to the execution site. The news spread, and people were very happy. It's just that people don't understand: Mei Niang's “Rose Red” is 1,000 cups undrunk. Why did the Skeleton Thief drink her alcohol, get drunk like a dead pig, and lie tied up in a grave? There were also those two guys, who actually fainted by the scent of roses. This wine was really amazing.
Mei Niang's “Rose Red” wine and the arrest of the Skeleton Thief soon spread to Beijing. When Emperor Li Yu of the dynasty learned about it, he immediately handed down the Holy Promise, causing Huangfu Yiyi to take Mei Niang and go to Beijing immediately.
When Huangfu Yiyi arrived at the Mei Niang Hotel, she discovered that after dismissing all the friends in the store, she blocked the door from the inside. As soon as Huangfu Yi and others broke through the door, they saw that someone had taken the lead. He took a close look, and couldn't help but move with his heart. The woman who felt that she had given up herself was actually the same as Ye, who disappeared in Panma Town a few years ago, to cut her eyebrows! There was also a letter placed on the table next to it, but it was actually left for Huangfu Yi Yi. When Huangfu took the letter and unpacked it, he couldn't help but be taken aback. Turns out, Mei Niang was Ye Xianmei. She and her father, Ye Heting, were Qin offenders wanted by the imperial court many years ago.
Ye He-ting's real name square cabinet was originally a winemaker in Yang Guozhong's estate during Tang Xuanzong's period. All the wine Yang Guozhong drank was brewed by him. Fang Qiangzi is obsessed with the winemaking business. While making wine, he constantly draws on the experiences of his predecessors and innovates. After years of hard work, he developed a “rose red” wine with roses as the main ingredient. This type of alcohol is also known as the “Thousand Day Drunk,” and even after a few sips, you will stay drunk in March without waking up. If you add a special formula to this kind of wine, you won't get drunk after a thousand cups, and your whole body will exude a rose scent, which has effects such as prolonging life and revitalizing blood. It is said that Yang Guozhong offered “rose red” wine to Tang Xuanzong, and quickly became a big celebrity close to him, and then monopolized the power of the DPRK government and did whatever he wanted.
Unexpectedly, during the “Anshi Rebellion,” Yang Guozhong's head was cut off by mutineous soldiers. Fang Jianzi knew she was in trouble. Taking advantage of the chaos, she escaped with her daughter and changed her name to live in seclusion in Xuancheng. Sure enough, not many years have passed. After Tang Xuanzong's son Li Heng took over the throne, he ordered the arrest of Fang Ke's father and daughter. Fortunately, Ye Heting changed his name and became a tailor, and no one recognized their father and daughter. After experiencing that horrible scene, Ye Heting established a family rule: the descendants of the Ye family will no longer be allowed to make wine; if anyone violates it, they should die for their ancestors.
However, in her dream, Ye cut her eyebrows and didn't expect that many years after her father established this family rule, she died a horrible death at the hands of a skeleton thief. One year in spring, Ye Jianmei and her neighbor went to Jingting Mountain to have fun. They didn't come back that night and stayed at the home of a female partner. As soon as she stepped into the house the next morning, she discovered that her father had fallen in a pool of blood. He had a sharp knife inserted into his chest, and a large skull was painted on the side wall. All the valuable items in the house have been swept away.
The government received the report, and Huangfu Yiyi rushed to Ye Jianmei's home with a government official to investigate the scene. Undoubtedly, this is another case committed by the Skeleton Thief. Huang Fu Yiyi and Ye Heting were once good friends. He comforted Ye to cut her eyebrows and said he would find a way to arrest the criminal as soon as possible and give her an explanation. Seeing as time passed day by day, Ye Shimei lost confidence in the government. She secretly made up her mind to personally capture the skeleton and bring it to justice, avenge her father, and exterminate the damage for the local people.
After Ye Shimei left Panma Town, after many inquiries, he learned that Skeleton steals are addicted to alcohol. Every time, he uses most of his stolen property in exchange for alcohol. In order to get revenge, Ye Cuimei had to break the family rules set by her father, hide in a deep mountain, and spend a few years redeveloping the “rose red” wine. Afterwards, Ye Shimei came to Xuancheng under the pseudonym Mei Niang. She was afraid that someone familiar would recognize her and block her plan, so she covered her face lightly to conceal her identity. She made “Rose Red” wine popular in Xuancheng to attract the attention of skeleton thieves. In order to attract fish as soon as possible, she deliberately opened the store only once every three months so that skeleton thieves could come and steal alcohol. Ye's plan to cut her eyebrows certainly worked. It's just that the Skeleton Thief never imagined it. The alcohol he stole from the treasury was a “thousand day drunk” without a special recipe. He didn't even expect that he actually left a string of red footprints along the way, and Ye cut his eyebrows easily tied him to the government office.
Unfortunately, soon after the Skeleton Thief was decapitated, Ye cut her eyebrows on her own, for nothing else, just for the family rules her father had set during her lifetime. Since then, the once amazing “rose red” wine has been lost...
