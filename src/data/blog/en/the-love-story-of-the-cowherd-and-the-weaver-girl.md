 There is a Weaver in the sky, and there is also a Morning Star. The weaver is in love with the cow, and she wants to print it with all her heart. However, the law of heaven does not allow men and women to fall in love or fall in love without permission. The weaver is the granddaughter of the royal mother, so the queen devalued the morning cow to dust, causing the weaver to keep weaving clouds and gold as punishment.
A weaver's job is to use a magical type of silk to weave layers of beautiful clouds on a loom, changing their color over time and season. This is a “heaven suit.” Since Morning Glory was demeaned, Weaver often washed her face with tears, and missed Morning Glory without showing any sense of sadness. She sat by the loom and kept weaving beautiful clouds and gold, hoping to win the royal mother's kindness and allow the morning cows to return to the celestial world as soon as possible.
One day, a few fairies begged the Queen Mother to visit the Green Lotus Pond. The princess was in a good mood today, so she agreed to them. When they saw that Weaver was in distress all day long, they begged Wang Mu to go with her. Wang Mu was also distressed by her granddaughter after being punished, so they quickly returned.
In other words, after being devalued, they were born to a peasant family and named Cowherd. Later, when his parents passed away, he spent his days with his brother and sister. My brother and sister treated the cowherd very harshly. When they wanted to break up with him, they only gave him an old cow and called the cowherd a portal to stand on his own feet.
Since then, cowherds and old cows have depended on each other. They have gone through thorns in wasteland, cultivated fields, and built houses. After a year or two, they built a small family where they could barely make ends meet. However, apart from that old cow who couldn't talk, the quiet family had only one cowherd, and he lived a very lonely life. The cowherd didn't know; that old cow was originally a golden bull star in the sky.
On this day, Lao Niu suddenly spoke up. It said to the cowherd, “Cowherd, take a trip to Bilian Pond today. Some fairies are bathing there. If you hide that red fairy suit, the fairy in the red fairy will become your wife.” When the cowherd saw the old cow talk, it was strange and happy, and asked, “Big Brother Niu, can you really talk? Are you telling the truth?” The old cow nodded, and the cowherd quietly hid in the reeds next to the green lotus pond, waiting for the fairies to arrive.
Soon after, the fairies floated around, took off their light clothes, and leaped into the clear stream. The cowherd then ran out of the reed and took the red fairy suit. When the fairies saw someone coming, they hurriedly put on their own clothes and flew away like a bird. There was only a fairy who couldn't escape without clothes. She was a weaver. Weaver saw that her fairy dress had been taken from her by a young man. She was shy and anxious, but she was helpless. At this point, the cowherd came up and said to her that he wanted her to promise to be his wife before he could return her clothes. After taking a close look, the weaver learned that the cowherd was the morning cow she thought about day and night, so she shyly agreed to him. In this way, the weaver became the wife of a cowherd.
After they got married, men and women went on blind dates and fell in love, and had a very happy life. Soon, they gave birth to a son and a daughter, very cute. The cowherd and the weaver thought they could stick with each other for the rest of their lives and grow old.
However, when the Queen Mother learned about this incident, she was furious and immediately sent the Tenjin Fairy, the Weaver, back to the Heavenly Court to plead the crime.
On this day, Weaver was cooking. The cowherd who had gone down to the ground hurriedly rushed back and told the weaver: “Big Brother Niu died. Before his death, he said he wanted me to peel off his cowhide and put it away. One day, if I put it on, I could fly to the sky.” As soon as Weaver heard it, she was puzzled. She understood that the old cow was the golden bull star in the sky; only because he said a few fair words for the devalued morning owl, she also belittled the heavenly court. How could it die all of a sudden? The weaver then made the cowherd peel off the cowhide and properly bury the old cow.
At this point, the sky was full of wind, and the Heavenly Soldier will fall from the sky. It's impossible to say for sure; they escorted the weaver and flew up into the sky.
It was flying, and the weaver heard the cowherd's voice: “Weaver, wait for me!” When the weaver looked back, she saw that the cowherd arrived in cowhide with a pair of baskets, picking two children. Slowly, the distance between them became closer. The weaver was able to clearly see the lovely appearance of the children. The children all opened their arms and called “mom” loudly. Seeing, the cowherd and the weaver were about to meet. However, at this point, Wang Mu arrived in Xiang Yun. She pulled off the golden hairpin on her head and stroked between them. Suddenly, waves of the Tianhe River rolled between the weaver and the cowherd, and were unable to cross.
The weaver looked at the cowherd and children on the other side of the Tianhe River and cried to death. Their cries, the children's cry of “mom,” were so frightening and tear-jerking. Even the fairies and angels watching felt sad and unbearable. When Wang Mu saw this scene, she was also slightly moved by the strong love between the cowherd and the weaver. She agreed to let the cowherd and children stay in the sky and let them meet once every July 7.
Since then, the cowherd and his children have lived in the sky, across the Tianhe River, and are far away from the weaver. Among the stars in the autumn night sky, we can still see two larger stars on both sides of the Milky Way, shining brightly. These are the Weaver Star and the Morning Star. Along with Morning Glory, there are also two little stars; they are the son and daughter of the cowherd and weaver.
On July 7, when the cowherd and weaver met, countless groups of magpies flew in to build a bridge for them. On the Magpie Bridge, the cowherds and weavers are reunited! The weaver and the cowherd are affectionate. They are hugging their children. They have countless things to say and endless feelings to confide in!
Legend has it that on July 7 every year, if people listen quietly among the vines under the grape stand, they can vaguely hear the sound of fairy music, and the weaver and the cowherd talk affectionately. Always: It was hard to say goodbye when we met. Day by day, they are looking forward to meeting again on July 7 of the following year.
