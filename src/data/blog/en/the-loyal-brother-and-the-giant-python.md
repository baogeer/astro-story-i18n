 There is a family named Hu in Hutian Village. The two brothers went to the mountain to cut firewood and inadvertently walked into the deep mountain canyon. Suddenly, I met a giant python. The eldest brother walked in front and was bitten by the giant python. The younger brother met him at the back. At first, he was so frightened that he wanted to run away. Seeing that his brother was being bitten and swallowed by a python, he struggled to pull out the axe to cut the firewood and cut it at the head of the giant python. Although the giant python was injured, he still couldn't help but bite. My elder brother's head was swallowed, but luckily he couldn't swallow his shoulder. The younger brother was in an emergency, and there was no other way to do it. He held his brother's feet with both hands and fought hard against the python, and actually dragged the brother out of the python's mouth. The giant python also left in pain due to the injury. If you take a closer look at my elder brother, his nose and ears have all melted, his breath is dead, and it's very dangerous. He carried his elder brother on his shoulder and walked back. He rested more than ten times along the way before carrying him back home. Seek medical treatment from a doctor. It's best to keep them at home for half a year. Until now, my face was full of scars, and the only place where my nose and ears were long was a hole. Ah, among farmers, there is a younger brother like this! Some say, “Big Python didn't kill his elder brother; it was inspired by his younger brother's virtue and righteousness.” That's true!
