 It is said that Sun and Moon are brothers and sisters. Brother Sun is passionate and unrestrained, and Sister Moon is beautiful and shy. Brother Sun rides a gold car. There is a bright headlight in front of the car. He takes a trip from east to west in the sky every day to bring light to the world; Sister Moon rides a silver car, and there is also an extremely bright headlight in front of the car. Every night, he takes a trip from east to west in the sky and shines silver light on the earth.
Because Brother Sun is passionate and bold, his brightness is the same every day. Sister Moon is different. She is young, beautiful, and shy. She always dodges and doesn't want to be seen by people. Every month, it's only the 14th, 15th, and 16th that she is shy and shows her face. People can only take a good look at her during these few days.
There is a frog in Tiangong. He has been in love with Sister Moon for a long time and wants to talk to Sister Moon, but he looks ugly and is afraid of being ridiculed by Brother Sun. He is even more afraid of being scolded by Brother Sun. If he wants to go back and forth, he can never find a way to get close to Sister Moon. Later, Frog finally got an idea. On the day Sister Moon fully showed up, he covered the lights in front of her car with his claws. In this way, not only could people see them, but even Brother Sun couldn't see them. He used this method to find opportunities to get close to Sister Moon a few times. Therefore, sometimes on the 14th, 15th, and 16th, Sister Moon should shine brightly. Even though there was no wind or clouds, the Moon hid and slowly showed its face again for a while. At this point, it was just a frog that came looking for Sister Moon.
In order to let the silver light of Sister Moon shine on the earth, every time it reached this point, they knocked on gongs, drums, and set off firecrackers, making all kinds of noises until the frog was driven away and the moon was illuminated again. They knock on anything they encounter, from the bell and bell at the Temple of Myanmar, to the copper pots and pot lids at home, as well as new roof racks, pig food troughs, and beef ring railings that haven't been tiled yet...
People say that when the frog meets Sister Moon, anywhere that made a noise, grains will be plentiful, the six animals will prosper, and everyone will be well.
