 Kim Se-sung is a native of Changshan Prefecture. He was unscrupulous in his usual behavior. He suddenly became a pedantic monk. He looked insane. He loved to eat dirty food, and ate it like eating delicious food. Some dogs and sheep pooped in front of them, so he ran over and lay down on the ground to eat them with relish. Also claiming to be a “Buddha,” those stupid ordinary women were amazed at his unusual behavior, and tens of thousands of people volunteered to worship him as their mentor. Kim Se-sung scolded them for making them eat poop; none of them dared to disobey. He built a palace for himself and spent countless amounts of money, all of which were donated voluntarily by people. County governor Nam Gong hated Kim Se-sung's bizarre behavior. He arrested him at the county office, beat him up, and asked him to pay money to build a temple of literature. Kim Se-sung's apprentices rushed to tell each other, “Buddha is in distress!” They are all trying to raise money to save him. As a result, the Temple of Literature was repaired in less than a month. Raising the money is far faster than being chased by the agents.
