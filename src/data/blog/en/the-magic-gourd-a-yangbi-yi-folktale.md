 On the west side of Yangliang City, in the green mountains and water about ten kilometers away, there is a flat dam with hundreds of square feet. The surrounding area is full of mixed trees, wild flowers, and green grass. People in the mountains call it Big Bad. The southwestern part of this beautiful alpine plain is full of mountain willows. The shade of the willows reflects a bay of blue water. Not a single leaf can be seen in the water. It is said that birds in the tree have taken it away. This blue water is Huanglongtan.
Legend has it that thousands of years ago, a yellow dragon lived inside. Every time there is a dry year, local crop farmers must choose an auspicious day to pray for the dragon and rain. They are all successful. People in the nearby mountains need the dishes needed to host a red and white celebration. As long as they go to Huanglongtan to burn incense and borrow snow-white tableware, they will float out one by one and be delivered to you. Once you've used it up, it's not difficult to borrow it again. People in the mountains appreciate the benefits of Huanglong and invite each other to bring delicious food to Huanglongtan during the New Year Festival. On the south side of Dalanba, there are more than a dozen Yi family cottages. Among them, the mother and son are living. The son is named Alon, who is good-looking and has a kind heart. Originally, there were four members of the Alon family, but since the tough old man, Ah Hu, killed his father and maimed his grandmother and fled the house, Ah Long, at a young age, took up the burden of raising grandma. They went to the mountains to pick mushrooms, pick fungus, and grow buckwheat on the volcano. This year, Ah Long grew up to be 18 years old and was more savvy and capable. Amatuo gave him a pretty daughter-in-law named Ah Hua. Ah Long usually likes to help others. Now everyone has heard that Alan wants to get married and donate money, get food for food, rent dishes, and go to Huanglong Lake to make Alan's happy events lively and fun.
Since Ah Hu left home, he has been walking around villages and villages, stealing chickens and touching dogs, making the family uneasy. The household cursed. It really was “one mother raised nine children; nine children are not like mothers.” I heard that Aaron had a happy event, and he rushed home. As soon as he heard that the bride was an Ah Hua girl, he clenched his teeth with hate. It turned out that Ah Hu had already gone to pick the camellia flower like a weasel stealing a chicken, but was slapped in the face twice by Ah Hua. Now Ah Hua is willing to marry Aaron. How can he hold back this tone?
The next day, Ah Long invited Ah Hu to return his dishes. Ah Hu agreed with a smiley face. The two brothers came to Huanglongtan and knocked forward with Alon burning incense and pushed his hands forward. Alon only felt dark in front of his eyes, cold all over his body, and drowsy and falling down... Suddenly, his eyes lit up, there was no water. He was dumbfounded, illuminating the house as cool as snow. He was dumbfounded, and suddenly he laughed a few times. I watched, but I saw an old man, dressed in a yellow robe, with yellow beard and yellow hair, antlers on his head. His eyes were like electricity, and the sound was like thunder: “Aaron, you are a filial son of the world. Now that I'm here, I'm sending you a treasure gourd. Just shake it, whatever you want, and keep you rich and healthy.” Aaron hurriedly bowed to say thank you: “Thank you, Master Huang Long!” Huang Long held out a long sleeve: “Put your head in your sleeve, I'll send you ashore.” As soon as Ah Long stretched his head and heard only a “wow” sound in his ear, he went back to Huanglong Lake. Aaron's family was anxious and shook the treasure gourd: “Give me a horse.” I heard a scream, and a tall horse immediately appeared in front of me. Aaron loved it so much that he turned over and ran home.
When I arrived at the doorstep, I only heard Grandma hoarse and shouting Alon's name. The neighbors advised Grandma while shedding tears. There was a sound of horseshoes, and Aaron suddenly appeared in front of them. Everyone was surprised and delighted. Surprised, didn't Ah Hu say Ah Long fell into Huanglong Tan? Gladly, Aaron came back alive. In an instant, crying turned into laughter. Aaron told Grandma and the townspeople about the whole process. Everyone was both angry and happy.
At this point, Ah Hua's crying and Ah Tiger's strange laughter came from the cave room. Ah Long quickly flew away and kicked open the door of the cave house. He only saw Ah Hu molesting Ah Hua with a smiling face, but Ah Hua became a tearful person. Aaron was furious and shook the treasure gourd: “Give me a knife.” Xueliang's big sword is steady in his hands. Ah Hu was terrified and dismayed, “Ah, ghost!” Kneel on the ground and nod like a green onion. As soon as Ah Long's heart softened, he put down the knife he had raised. He reached over and lifted Ah Hu, and yelled: “Roll” and threw Ah Hu out of the door. A tiger ran away as soon as he smoked. The townspeople all clapped their hands and applauded. Aaron shook the gourd again and asked for two tables of steaming, fragrant wine and food to entertain everyone, and they had a great time until midnight.
Since then, Ah Long, his wife, and Grandma have continued to work hard, and they have had a sweet and sweet life. A year later, Ah Hua gave birth to a white, fat, and fat doll for Alon, and the whole family was even more happy. But Aaron couldn't forget his poor townspeople. He often alleviates poverty and helps the poor. The whole cottage is as close as one family. However, Ah Hu is still acting recklessly and stealing for a living. Aaron personally took him home, advised him to change evil and return to justice, and live a reunited life together. However, Ah Hu set fire to Basho's heart; it is difficult to change his stealth. The better the Aaron couple is to him, the worse he feels for Mr. and Mrs. Aron. As soon as he saw Ah Hua's camellia-like appearance, and then saw what kind of treasure gourds he wanted, his eyes kept turning and turning to the thief again.
On this day, I drove Aron down the mountain before returning home. It was quiet in the middle of the night, and Ah Hu slipped through the window into his younger sister-in-law's room. Ah Hua woke up from her sleep. While resisting, she shouted loudly, and was shocked that the five neighbors rushed over to save each other. Ah Hu hurriedly let go of Ah Hua, snatched a treasure gourd and fled. After hiding in the deep mountains and old woods, Ah Hu was busy shaking his gourd and wanted a table of wine and dishes to quench his thirst. Who knows nothing. He waved back and forth. He wanted this or that, but he still wanted nothing. The treasure gourd, which he had made every effort to get his hands on, turned into a useless fake gourd. Ah Hu was angry and tired, and fell asleep on the root of the tree.
Suddenly, an old man dressed in a yellow robe, yellow beard and yellow hair, and antlers on his head came. His eyes were like lightning and his voice was thunder: “Wouldn't you use a treasure gourd? Let's go to Huanglongtan and ask for tips.” As soon as I spoke, I turned around and left. Ah Hu quickly grabbed the old man's sleeve. The old man tucked the sleeve gently, and Ah Hu fell backwards. When I opened my eyes, it turned out to be a dream. He was busy picking up the treasure gourd and hiding to Huanglong Tan Bian. While knocking his head, he read words and prayed to Huang Long for the secret of the treasure gourd. There were a few sounds of “whoa,” and Huanglong and Tan Bo surged. Grandpa Huang Long extended his two giant hands, took back the treasure gourd with one hand, grabbed Ah Hu with the other hand, threw him dozens of miles away, and stoned him to death on the opposite side of the hill.
Aaron rushed home. Hearing Ah Hua's cry, his lungs exploded. At this point, the old man, dressed in a yellow robe, yellow beard and yellow hair, and antlers on his head, suddenly appeared in front of his eyes holding a treasure gourd and smiled and said to him, “Things have returned to their original owners.” When I finished speaking, I disappeared. I only saw a treasure gourd placed on the table, shining brightly all over the house.
