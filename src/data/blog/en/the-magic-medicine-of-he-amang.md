The elders said that according to legend, there is a magic medicine that can cure strange diseases, but this medicine can only be found in mountains with dragon palm blood trees and red cohosh grass. Along the way, you have to climb mountains and cross rivers. Poisonous snakes stand in the way, and evil eagles attack people on their heads, which is very dangerous. There is a young man named He Abu who is determined to take the risk to find the magic medicine.
One day, He Aman hurriedly arrived in front of a cliff and suddenly saw an old white-haired, white-bearded old man digging for herbs being besieged by several evil eagles. Despite the danger, He Aman raised his bow and shot down the evil eagle. The white-haired and white-bearded old man even thanked He Ah for being busy and gave him a black gourd.
One day, He A hurriedly came to a deep ditch and saw a middle-aged man gathering firewood. A poisonous snake was quietly attacking behind him. He hurriedly shouted and rushed past, cutting off the snake's head with a knife. The middle-aged man even thanked He Ah for being busy and gave him a yellow gourd.
One day, He was in front of a big river and suddenly discovered that a girl was struggling in the rapids. He plunged into the river and worked hard to save the girl. To express her gratitude, the girl gave He Ah-han a white gourd.
He Ahu finally arrived at the mountain, which had flying dragon palm blood trees and red hemp grass. The white-haired, white-bearded old man smiled and said, “Good boy, the medicine you want is in the gourd.” Then I told him how to make pharmaceuticals.
He Ah hurriedly rushed back to the cottage. As the white-bearded old man said, he soaked the medicine in the gourd with Miaojia rice wine and asked the sick people to scoop off the sore spot. The townspeople were certainly well sick. To thank He A Busy for not being afraid of hardship and danger, the Miao family called this magic potion “He Ah Busy.”
