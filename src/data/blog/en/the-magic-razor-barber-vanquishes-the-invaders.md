 Tianzi Town has never had an emperor, not even a decent official. Instead, it was a famous shaver during the Republic of China.
This shaver's last name is Wang. He is over fifty years old. Not only is he excellent at shaving his head, he also knows how to massage his head. What's even more interesting is that every time his razor touches his hair or scalp, it makes an amazing noise. Therefore, people don't call him Master Wang, but rather “King of the Magic Sword.”
Although “King of Magic Swords” is a head shaver, he is quite talented. He is very fond of music. He is good at playing the guqin and playing erhu, and he also writes folk songs himself, which is quite a half-time artist. However, in order to make a living, his love for music is only occasional. Since his only son was killed in the Lugouqiao Incident, and since his old partner died, every day it got dark, he didn't do any work, either guqin, or erhu, and played songs he was familiar with over and over... that leisurely sound of music was like crying.
Just like that, he shaves and massages people during the day, and plays Guqin and erhu alone at night. Day in and day out, several years have passed.
On this day, after a burst of gunfire, a group of Japanese devils broke into Tianzi Town in a daze. The Japanese devil murdered people, set fire and robbed things, and made a big fuss, and used this as a stronghold to settle down. The town of Tianzi, which was originally calm and harmonious, suddenly became hell on earth.
As a result, the “Magic Sword King” shaving shop business has obviously cooled down a lot. However, “Magic Sword King” opens the door every day as usual, carefully shaves and massages people's heads, and plays guqin and erhu as usual from the beginning to the night.
One morning, “Magic Sword King” stayed in the store. Suddenly, he burst into a girl and knelt down in front of him and said, “Master! Help!” Looking at “King of the Magic Sword,” isn't this the little girl who sings on the street every day with her blind father? He settled in and quickly picked her up and asked, “How are you? What about your father?” The girl said, “My dad made the devil kill him. The devils are chasing me. You're good to go, save me!” “Magic Sword King” gnawed her teeth, and without saying a word, dragged the girl into the back room and hid her.
“King Sword” settled in a good girl. As soon as she came out of the back room, she saw a Japanese military officer carrying an interpreter and two soldiers rushing into the head-shaving shop in a menacing manner. The military officer spoke Japanese for a while, but he couldn't understand the phrase “Magic Sword King,” but he just stood still.
The interpreter said to “King Sword,” “This is Captain Yamamoto.” He asked if you saw a girl running over?”
“Magic Sword King” shook his head and said, “No, I haven't seen a girl. I'm a head shaving shop. If Imperial Army wants to shave or get a massage, I can help.”
The interpreter was in a hurry to translate for Yamamoto. As soon as Yamamoto heard that he was a head shaver, he also massaged. After shouting “oh hey,” he sat down on the chair. The two guards immediately stepped forward and stood on the side of the mountain itself, one left and one right.
The interpreter patted “King of the Magic Sword” on the shoulder and said, “Old man, Taijun just wanted to shave his head. By the way, you have to weigh your craftsmanship. If you make Taijun angry, then...”
“Magic Sword King” nodded and said, “Don't worry about that.” Speaking of which, I took off Yamamoto's military cap, shook the white cloth around his chest, picked up a knife and pasted it on a piece of leather a few times, then held it high.
“King of the Magic Swords” thought: if this sword goes down, the devil of Dog Day will die for the rest of his life! This is also considered revenge for my son who died so horribly... but instead he asked, “Taijun, would you like to listen to some music while shaving your head?” The interpreter hurriedly translated for Yamamoto.
Yamamoto giggled for a while, and the interpreter said, “As long as it sounds good, anything goes.”
“That's good.” When “King of the Sacred Swords” finished, he clasped his razor, and only heard a “click”. Yamamoto's hair floated down, and a pleasant sound of music immediately sounded. This sound made Yamamoto close his eyes comfortably...
The shiny razor quickly floats back and forth around Yamamoto's head, and flips up and down rhythmically as the razor plays. The two Japanese soldiers next to the mountain itself looked nervous, staring dead at the sharp razor with four eyes.
Not long after, the music stopped abruptly, and “Magic Sword King” said, “OK, I've finished shaving my head.” Yamamoto enjoyed opening his eyes and asked the interpreter to ask what song “King of the Sacred Sword” was. “King of the Sacred Sword” laughed: “Aren't you Taigun? I know this is the famous song “Ambush on Ten Faces”!”
The interpreter carefully translated the words “King of the Sacred Sword” to Yamamoto. Suddenly, Yamamoto widened his eyes, jumped out of his chair, and shouted, “Eight grid teeth!”
Hearing only a “roar,” Yamamoto pulled out his saber, picked up his hand, and dropped, and red blood splashed on the snow-white wall.
“King Sword” slowly fell backwards. His eyes were round and he stared harshly at Yamamoto, still holding the razor tightly in his hand.
Yamamoto put up the sword and said “open the way,” then took the interpreter and soldiers with him.
Ten days later, news suddenly spread in Tianzi Town: Captain Yamamoto, the head of the devil soldier in the stronghold, died suddenly and unexpectedly. Doctors couldn't find out the cause of his death; they only found ten blood stains on top of Yamamoto's head from the inside out.
