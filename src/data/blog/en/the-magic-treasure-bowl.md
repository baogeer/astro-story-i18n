 At the beginning of the Ming Dynasty, Pancun Street (present-day Pancun Town, Mingguang City) came to an escaped house. The man was driving a bicycle with a bed quilt, a few pottery pots and dark thick porcelain bowls. The woman had a tall stomach, and she looked like she was six or seven months old. There were also two children in the car. One was four years old and the other was 2 years old. They were all boys. The male surname was Hua Liang, and the female surname was Liang Hua. They fled the desert in Shandong. The couple settled down at a Fortune Temple on the east side of the street. This Temple of Fortune is just a broken temple with no windows. It is a half-man-tall wooden statue of the god of fortune. No one has served it for many years, and the thick floating ash has covered the Buddha's nose.
Hua Liang and Liang Hua spent two days sweeping, washing, and cleaning the floor and walls of Little Broken Temple. At the top of the gable, there was also a fire in the open air to cook. Adults called and children made noise, hey! It became a home. The woman's family is careful. She repaired the shrine, washed the statue, stood the god of fortune on the stage, and placed it properly. She also bought two incense sticks, and prayed while arching her hands. The family was accompanied by Grandpa Fortune.
This Hualiang is a crop man. He cultivates rakes and harvests, forks, brooms and shovels. He also has an inexhaustible amount of energy. Within a few days, he was hired as a partner by Pan Lao Insurance Company on the street. Although Liang Hua is a woman with small feet, she makes good pasta. The steamed bun that comes out can be used as a racket, and the noodles rolled out are brushed thick, long, and wide. The noodles I cut tonight won't stick together tomorrow morning. I only showed up at Grandpa Pan's house; no one knows about Pancun Street from south to north. Whoever wanted to eat Liang Hua's hand-rolled noodles at the beginning went to the door and asked for it. The noodles were ready, and most of the owners sent her a bowl and a half bowl of flour to make money for work, so she made up for the little days. However, Liang Hua is a woman with a body. Watching her stomach get bigger day by day, it's not very convenient to walk east or west. Mrs. Pan came up with an idea. She borrowed Leung Hua's ten kilos of flour to make Liang Hua roll noodles at home every day and set up a stand at the door. Whoever wants to eat noodles will come to the door to buy them. Just like that, Pancun Street has had its own noodle shop ever since. Since they moved into their home at the Temple of Fortune, people started coming to the shrine every three to five places to enjoy incense, and then the incense gradually continued to burn.
One night, Hua Liang suddenly woke up from her sleep and couldn't sleep anymore. He climbed up and woke up Liang Hua, who was sleeping, and said, “Wake up soon. I had a dream just now. I had a dream where I was cultivating a large tile pot. I hadn't waited until I picked it up, and an old man with a white beard came over. The old man said that this is a treasure pot. If you put grains of rice in the pot, it will soon turn into a pot. If you use it well, it will bring happiness; if you don't use it properly, it will ruin your family and cause death. Before I spoke, the old man turned to a burst of smoke and drifted away. I don't know if this dream is bad or good?” After a tiring day, Liang Hua had a good night's sleep. Her husband woke her up. She was a little annoyed and said, “Never mind him. It's a blessing or not; a curse is inevitable.” After speaking, I turned over and fell asleep. After dawn the next day, the owner called Hualiangtao Cow Farming Field. The old cow never finished his trip, and the iron plow pulled out a large tile pot. Hua Liang hurriedly picked up the tile pot, removed the mud, and took a look. There was nothing special about an ordinary tile pot. After taking a break, Hua Liang took back the pot and handed it over to Liang Hua. She happily said, “Last night's dream came true. I actually cultivated a treasure pot on my cultivated land.” Liang Hua saw her husband holding back a pot and rolled her eyes: “You are leading backwards while not walking. I discussed with you the previous two to buy a pot back. You also said that the pot at home only has a crack, so you can use it even if you use a rope hoop. Who made you buy it today? Did you pick up a cheap one?” Hua Liang said, “I told you about my dream last night, have you forgotten? It really came from cultivating fields; maybe it really was a cornucopia.” Liang Hua took the crock pot and didn't see anything special. She thought it was her husband who made up a story to coax people and knocked it over the table. She heard a “dang bang” sound and made a metal impact sound. Both were shocked at the same time: “Oops! It's really not the same as a normal pot.” Hua Liang grabbed a handful of soybeans and threw it in the pot. She saw a layer of fog in the pot, and soon the pot was full of soybeans. Hualiang jumped up in joy. He poured the soybeans into a pocket, grabbed another handful of soybeans and put it in the pot, and soon the pot was full; he also poured the soybeans from the pot into his pocket, and still grabbed a handful of soybeans and placed it in the pot, which was full again. After three pots in a row like this, Hualiang still needs to change. Liang Hua calmed down and stopped Hua Liang from saying, “Please revisit last night's dream.” After repeating last night's dream in detail, Hua Liang looked at Liang Hua and asked, “Why?” Liang Hua said, “This pot should be used for noodles. We have to work hard to maintain our family; we cannot make money by taking advantage of chance; we can make money without effort. Otherwise, this family will be in trouble.” Liang Hua has established a rule. Other than mixing noodles every day, you cannot put anything else in this pot. Hua Liang always listens to her wife, and of course this time is no exception.
Since then, Hua Liang has been working with his partner, and Liang Hua has continued to sell her noodles. But now there's no need to buy flour anymore. Liang Hua finished mixing one pot of noodles every day, and after rolling the noodles, put the flour in the pot. The next day, she was able to make a pot of noodles out of the pot every day.
One night, Hua Liang took advantage of Liang Hua's sleep and quietly placed a copper coin in the pot. Second, Tianliang Hua was doing business with noodles and found that the pot was full of copper coins. She knew it was Hualiang's work. She got angry, ran to the house and dragged Hua Liang out of her bed, and started her temper: “Do you want money, or do you want the safety of this family, young and old?” Hua Liang smiled honestly: “How good is money to have a wife and children.” Liang Hua remained invigorated: “Then why are you carrying me to make this dark money?” Hua Liang said, “I don't want it at all, but I'm just curious to try it out.” Liang Hua said, “That's good. If you go find a craftsman, we'll use this money to restore the Temple of Fortune and then gold plated Grandpa Fortuna.”
Another year passed in a blink of an eye, and Hua Liang and Liang Hua saved quite a bit of money. They then built three decent houses east of the Temple of Fortune. Hua Liang still works as a friend at Grandpa Pan's house, and Liang Hua still sells noodles every day with noodles. This year, summer locusts became a plague, and the sky was covered by flying locusts. Wherever they went, there was no wheat. Five of the ten families asked for food, and the elm bark on the roadside and hillside were all peeled off to fill their hunger. If this continues, many people will surely starve to death. Liang Hua and Hua Liang discussed handing out steamed buns to disaster victims starting tomorrow, two per person a day. The next day, when it wasn't bright, Liang Hua steamed a pot of steamed buns, poured it into a large pot, then took a steamed bun and placed it in a cornucopia. Soon it would turn into a pot of steamed buns. Over and over again, it was almost bright, and the snow-white steamed buns were already piled up. Hua Liang took her oldest son and told the past food seekers and poor people at the intersection that Liang Hua from the Temple of Fortune distributed steamed buns to the victims. People spread the word 10, 10 to 100, and there was a long line in front of the Temple of Fortune. The victims were holding snow-white steamed buns in their hands and were extremely grateful to Liang Hua. Just like this, thousands of disaster victims eat steamed buns every day in front of Liang Hua's house, and no one in Pan Village starved to death. Poor people all say that Hua Liang and Liang Hua are Bodhisattvas sent by immortals to help people in every world. Among those wealthy families, people are beginning to pay attention to the Hualiang family. They feel strange about Liang Hua handing out steamed buns every day. There was almost no wheat harvest within 100 miles. Where did Hualiang's family get so much wheat noodles? Tens of thousands of steamed buns are distributed every day; how many pots need to be steamed, but why can't we see the smoke in Liang Huajia's chimney? People were wondering, curious, and increasingly suspicious. Some of the elders asked Hualiang where he got so much flour; the old lady and young daughter-in-law asked Liang Hua how long it would take to steam tens of thousands of steamed buns. Every time people ask Hualiang, Hua Liang always smiles bluntly and says, “Our daughter-in-law is capable.” Every time people ask Liang Hua, Liang Hua also smiles and says, “Even steaming in a pot saves time.” Grandpa Pan works from his heart. One day, he went to Hualiang's house to deliver his wages. As soon as he entered the door, he said, “His older sister-in-law, this year was a disaster year. Your family also used their own meals and helped the victims every day. I estimate that you have already spent almost all of your savings. This year's money will be calculated for you in advance.” They said they took three coins out of the basket and placed them on the table; at the same time, they looked like they had installed a shaft and swirled around. One didn't see the grain pile, two didn't see the big pot, and three didn't see the big steamer. After leaving Hualiang's house, Grandpa Pan amazingly said that Liang Hua is the god of salvation in the world. He waved a pot of big steamed buns. They didn't use flour, no cages, and no fire at all.
People spread the word more and more, and many people came from far away to see the respect of the living Buddha, and the other was to buy a few catties of noodles that Liang Hua personally made to take home, saying that they had eaten this noodle without getting sick. Liang Hua starts a potted noodle business not too many times a day. Liang Hua saved up the money she earned. Having saved a lot, it was enough to do a decent job, so she took it all out. Pan Cun has a decent Meng Museum, which was donated by Hualiang and Liang Hua; Pan Cun has a decent stone arch bridge, which is also a donation from both of them.
More than ten years have passed in a blink of an eye. Hualiang and Liang Hua are all old. Their three sons, Hualong, Huahu, and Huabao, have all become families one after another. Along the old house to the east, there are three rooms in each family. The boss runs a restaurant on the street, and the third runs a cloth shop on the street. Only the second-generation Huahu inherited his father's business and cultivated more than ten acres of his family's fields. Because people from hundreds of miles nearby have great respect for Liang Hua, Sanfang's daughter-in-law also respects her mother-in-law. Although the three sons each have their own businesses, they are still sharing the same pot and spoon. The three daughters-in-law don't burn or mess with it, and they are also at ease. One day, Liang Hua suddenly became bedridden due to a strange illness. She couldn't say anything; she was in a hurry. Liang Hua has been directing this family all his life. He can only do stupid things. When Liang Hua wants to give up, he has to follow along. What Liang Hua was most uneasy about was that cornucopia. She feared that her three sons and three daughter-in-law would get this and be insatiably greedy. She called Hualiang, and used her fingers to explain the aftermath by gesturing. What she wanted to say was that the boss got the restaurant industry, the old three had the field, and the older two had fields, and all three could get by. It wouldn't be beneficial to keep this pot; it would be better to bury it in the ground, so as not to cause trouble. But this gorgeous mind is spinning slowly. He mistakenly understood Liang Hua's words to mean that the old big restaurant and the old Sanbu store required money and turnover. The old two farms only needed seeds every year; no one gave them this pot; he took care of it.
After Liang Hua's death, Hua Liang gave 50 bars of silver each to the boss and the elder three, respectively, and gave the older brother a bag of wheat seeds. The three sons split up and went their separate ways. The elder Huahu worked hard to cultivate more than ten acres of land he earned and served Laozi. The boss and elder opened their store. They each got 50 bars of silver. Seeing the old man live with the old man, and the old guy only got a bag of wheat seeds. Why are they so happy and not making a noise? The more I think about it, the more I wonder if the old man left another family fortune for his sophomore. The three brothers, the boss and older, forced the old man to ask how much money they had left for the sophomore? Hualiang was forced to do nothing and told the truth. Three sons only learned that their father had a cornucopia. The boss thought, if this cornucopia were to me, who else in the world would be richer than me? My older brother thought, how can there be any beautiful thing in the world without effort? You won without effort today; you still don't know what you'll lose tomorrow! I don't want any cornucopia. The old man thought to himself that the boss cannot go alone in this cornucopia. He proposed that the three brothers should take turns to use this cornucopia before it is reasonable.
After some controversy, it was decided that boss Hualong would use the cornucopia first, and then every household would use it for one day. Boss Hualong happily brought home the treasure pot and busily put a bar of silver into the basin. In less than an instant, it became a pot; it fell to the ground, and then turned into another pot. Just like this, the two people kept busy, pouring silver over and over again. The silver became more and more and more piled up. By the middle of the night, all three rooms were full. These two people were still changing, falling, and piling up. Suddenly, with a “bang”, the four walls were crushed and collapsed, the roof collapsed, and both were buried in a pile of silver. The old trio didn't sleep in the middle of the night. They only waited until dawn to pass for a cornucopia. Suddenly they heard a “boom” and hurriedly ran out to watch, only to see that the old man's house had collapsed. At this point, both the old man and the old man heard the noise. The two families came over and got their hands dirty in the ruins of the downed house. What the junior wanted was a treasure pot; what the elder and old man wanted was to save people. In this way, until dawn, the older three didn't find the pot, and the old man and the old man didn't find anyone. Other than breaking bricks and tiles, I couldn't remove any silver in the pile; it was just a piece of rock. At this point, Hua Liang remembered his dream back then. In the dream, the old man with white beard said, “Improper use can ruin people.” This statement has all come true.
