There is a village in Qinghai Minhe Zongbao Township called Yuanpo. The village is called Ramayu by a mountain. Today, Ramayuan's terraced fields are patchwork. The mountains are kind and solemn, and the plateau is like a leader. A “blue dragon” channel flows straight down from Ramayuan, like a column of water spewing out of a dragon's mouth. This canal, which was built spontaneously by villagers, still plays an important role and has made significant contributions to local agriculture. The field on the plateau is a masterpiece of the United Nations 2708 Project. The arid land in the past is now an endless water-filled small plain. It is said that a large number of artifacts were unearthed when leveling the land. According to legend, when sitting on a plane, you can see a golden sow running on the plateau with 12 little golden pigs, but when you actually go looking for it, you can't find a specific location. There is also a legend that an oil field in China that Li Siguang was exploring on an airplane at the time. The geological exploration team explored several times a few years ago but did not find a location for the oil. It can be seen that this place has a long and splendid history, and it is also one of the birthplaces that people have forgotten!
There is a cemetery on the hillside opposite Lamayuan. Legend has it that it is the tomb of King Li Jin ①. Rumor has it that when Li Jin Wang passed by here and saw this Feng Shui treasure, he ordered his subordinates to belong here after his death. When his descendants went to the grave to worship the dead, they went up the mountain from Xiang Tang to Bazhou. When they saw his graveyard from afar, they got down and walked to the graveyard on horseback. This is the origin of “Ramayuan,” and now people call it Ramayuan. Unfortunately, when the barren mountains were opened and superstitions were dispelled, good graves were leveled into cultivated land, and those stone monuments, stones, and monkeys were already destroyed. The general outline of the cemetery is still vaguely recognizable. Seen from a distance, the graveyard looks a bit like a blue dragon on the left, a white tiger on the right, a backer on the back, and a front garden. Unfortunately, this monument has rubbed shoulders with history like this...
There is a Dragon King Temple at the foot of Lama Yuen. It is famous all over the world. When people stand at Lama Yuen or from a high place, the entire Dragon King Temple looks like a big boat riding the waves. It's quite a spectacle. It looks like a big goldfish swimming independently on the surface of the sea, so there is a saying that carp jumps at the dragon gate. What's even more interesting is that on the right side of the temple is the Xigou River flowing down from Gushan Reservoir, and on the left is a creek flowing out of Demon Fairy Pool ② after Lamayuan. There is a saying that there are two dragons playing in front of the temple, so the temple is called the “Dragon King Temple”. Today, two legends relating to the Dragon King Temple make people think they are both true and magical.
Bazhou probably experienced a thunderstorm for three days and three nights during the Guangxu years of the Qing dynasty. At that time, it flooded the entire streets of Bazhou. At that time, ordinary people prayed for the blessings of the gods and threw in a live sheep. Every time the flood threw a foot, it was later rumored that it was Prince Dai of Quan Longwang and Prince Dai of Bazhou ③ during the battle. When he arrived at Longwang Temple, Dai Wang sent troops rolling in. When he arrived at Longwang Temple, the Quanlong flag blew in and blocked the army. Back, it was only a pity that ordinary people in Bazhou were affected by hailstorms and floods, while three days and three nights of fresh wind and drizzle fell a hundred miles around Longwang Temple, saving this place A large area of dried up crops. After the flood, ordinary people in Bazhou discovered that the head of their statue of Grandpa Dai had been broken. According to Grandpa Dai's appearance, it was broken by Zaiquan Dragon King. Later, the statue they made could only show its spirit by breaking the head, so it is called “King Dai the Head.”
It also happened during the late Ming and early Qing dynasties. Back then, the disciples of the Three Living Buddhas ④ took his mounts to drink water. When sitting on the Dragon King, they felt that he had taken the plunge and infringed his rights and interests, so they cut off the horse's back leg and forced the three living Buddhas to work as mages in the temple for 120 days. One day, he ordered his disciples, “Don't make a sound, even if the dark clouds press down”. As soon as the voice fell, the dark clouds crushed the earth. The entire temple was shrouded under the dark clouds. The disciples were frightened when they saw it. Suddenly, a click in the monastery made a sound, and the atrium beams were crushed. It is said that the three living Buddhas captured Zazen Dragon King at this time, and the moment the disciples spoke out, Zazen Dragon King crushed the bridge. The three living Buddhas were also hit hard. They were very sweaty, like a ball of vengeance. Afterwards, the entire hail fell at the junction of Sanjia Village and Yuanpo Village. The hail, ah, flowed for three days and three nights. Later, the border between Yuanpo Village and Sanjia Village was clearly distinguished, and the junction formed a natural divide. Ordinary people now call it the “Sanjiasi Ditch.”
Every year on April 8 of the lunar calendar, villagers go to the monster pond behind Lamayuan to go to the mountain. It is said that King Quanlong is in a position to prevent Grandpa Dai from sneaking up and preventing ordinary people's crops from being attacked by hail. I heard the old man say that in the dark night of heavy thunderstorms, there was a golden rooster crowing at Ramayuan. The flames erupted from their mouths melted the dark clouds in the sky, and the torrential rain turned into fresh wind and drizzle, and moisturized the crops!
The demon fairy pond also has a vivid origin. A caidong had a dream one day, and someone borrowed eight mules from his circle. When he woke up, he found that the eight mules were lying on the ground sweating, but it didn't return to normal until dawn. The next night, Caidong had another dream about that person. He also said that eight mules had borrowed things and that they had already carried them behind Ramayuan. In order to say thank you, they looked for rewards under the horse trough after dawn, but when he finished speaking, they disappeared. After dawn, Caidong found a gold ingot under the manger. Exactly on the night of Caidong's dream, another Caidong also dreamed that someone had borrowed the plot of land behind Ramayuan at your doorstep. He also found gold ingots in the morning. When I went to see the cropland behind Ramayuan the next day, he discovered that it had become a large natural lake. This is the source of the monster pond behind Ramayuan. It is said that when the weather is dry, villagers come here with incense frames to ask for rain. If the pool owner agrees, there will be fresh wind and drizzle the next day. On the eve of liberation, there was a big drought in Shimokawaguchi, and the villagers there also came to ask for rain. They asked for a full month, and the pond owner didn't agree, so they dug the pond to release water to save the crops. Unexpectedly, the hole they dug during the day closed again at night. At the same time, they also heard a voice shouting “Merge, Merge, Join” and they dug together. One night more than two months later, they heard a voice shouting “I'm gone”, then a strong wind blew through the village and happened to go southwest to the southwest. An old willow tree at the entrance of Sanjia-ji Temple was blown off. According to the old man, the tree was a tree where the owner of the Demon Pool welcomed the wind and rain. Whenever there was a flood here, the demon king Come to this tree to see the scene here. Now there are also people who say that the monster fairy pond has settled down in the Pinggou area of Gansu.
There is a mountain on the opposite side of Ramayuan. People in the village call it the “Incense Burner Stand,” and there is a plot of land next to it called the “Buddha Case.” Seen from afar, it looks like a volume of “scroll painting” unfolded, and that mountain peak also looks like an incense burner. It's snowing. Every time people in the village go down to the field to farm, they look at the snow on the “incense stand”. If the snow melts anywhere, they can work; if the snow doesn't melt anywhere, it's either the ground is too wet or the snow hasn't completely melted away when you go. ... There is also an even more amazing place on Lamayuan called “Cheung Di Bay”. Whenever there is a thunderstorm, “Cheung Chi Bay” is not “easy to get damaged”, and the surrounding crops are hit by hail. Whenever there is a drought, “Xiangdi Bay” is windy and drizzly, so ordinary people call it “Lucky Bay,” but unfortunately “Xiangdi Bay” has been forgotten over the years.
When I heard the old man say that Liu Bowen (Liu Ji) was watching Feng Shui in the west, he discovered that Lama Yuan had signs of dragon veins, so he sent someone to dig deep into Lama Yuantou and cut off the mountains. Afterwards, the steamed soil built a small city wall facing Lamayuan a hundred miles away. The area around the city wall was called “knocking on the head” and “dragon outline”. The meaning was to stop the dragon, knock on the dragon head, and make the dragon contoured. The meaning of using an arrow on the city wall to cut off the dragon vein; now it is in Qiaotou Village in Longzhi Township.
This is the obscure Ramat, the charming yet legendary Ramah. The ancients said, “If the mountain is not high; if the water is not deep, it is called; if the water is not deep, the dragon means spirit”. Ramayuan has mountains, water, and stories, but people have forgotten it, probably because no one went to tell them?
