 Wu County has a county magistrate. I have forgotten his name; he is stubborn and stubborn. Wu County folklore respects the god Chenghuang the most. Locals use wood to carve statues of gods, then dress them in brocade clothes to make them look lifelike. Every time it is the birthday of the god Chenghuang, the residents have to raise money and parade through the streets with gorgeous cars; wearing colorful flags and various ceremonies, they line up in a tidy line, whistling and whistling all the way. Whoa whoa whoa, the streets and alleys are crowded with people looking forward to watching. It's been a long time, and being a god has become a custom, and I don't dare to slack off a bit every year.
Once, the prefectural governor went out and happened to run into a connoisseur. The parade stopped and asked exactly, and people told him. The county commissioner also learned that it would take a lot of human and material resources to become a god. He was furious, pointing at the statue and reprimanded the statue, saying, “You are the god of the city god in charge of a county. If you are stubborn, you are a dumb ghost and not worthy of people's worship; if you have a spirit, you should know how to cherish people's power and use these useless expenses to consume people's fat!” After scolding, they ordered someone to knock the statue to the ground and beat them for 20. It was only then that old habit was broken.
The prefectural commissioner is a clean and selfless official; he is only young and playful. More than a year later, he happened to go up a ladder in the government office to remove a bird's nest under the roof. He lost his foot and fell, broke his thigh, and died soon after. People heard angry shouts from the county governor in Chenghuang Temple. They seemed to be arguing with Chenghuang God, and it didn't stop for several days. People in Wu County did not forget the mercy of the County Order and gathered at Chenghuang Temple to mediate for them. Another temple was built to enshrine the prefectural order, and only then did the controversy disappear. The prefectural shrine is also known as Chenghuang Temple. Regular sacrifices are performed in spring and autumn, which is more spiritual than the original Chenghuang god. Wu County still has two cities.
