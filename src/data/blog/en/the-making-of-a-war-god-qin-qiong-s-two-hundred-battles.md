The year of birth is unknown; he died in 638 AD. Zi Shubao is from Licheng, Qizhou (present-day Jinan, Shandong). The founding general of the Tang dynasty and one of the 24 heroes of Ling Yan Ge. According to legend, Qin Qiong and Wei Chi Jingde are traditional gatekeepers.
Qin Qiong's fame appears to be a coincidence, but in fact, it is inevitable.
Many times, people call Qin Qiong Qin Shubao, and legendary novels also use Qin Shubao as an alias for Qin Qiong. Actually, Uncle Bao is Qin Qiong's character, but his handwriting is so loud that it suppresses his name.
Qin Qiong is a native of Licheng, Qizhou. His family is a very ordinary family. His father was nothing more than an unknown record to join the military during the Northern Qi period. At a time when Qin Qiong had almost no memory, Northern Qi was destroyed by Northern Zhou, and Qin Qiong's father died in battle. Since then, Qin Qiong and her mother have been dependent on each other and have lived a lonely and poor life. However, Qin Qiong was very remarkable; he was known for his courage when he was a teenager. It can also be considered a secret help from God. Qin Qiong, who loves to speak righteousness, grew up tall, powerful and fierce. The courageous and aggressive manner gave Qin Qiong a certain popularity in his hometown.
Qin Qiong didn't have a high starting point. In the beginning, he was just an inconspicuous soldier in Sui's future nursing home. Despite his low status, Qin Qiong is the type that pursues progress. Coupled with his chivalry and courage, he quickly won the attention of those who came to take care of children. When the world was in turmoil at the end of the Sui dynasty, Qin Qiong relied on Sui General Zhang Sutuo. At the time, Zhang Xutuo was confronting Lu Mingyue, leader of the peasant army in Xiayuan (northwest of present-day Suining, Jiangsu). Compared to Lu Mingyue, Zhang Xutuo is clearly at a disadvantage. Although Lu Mingyue's claim that he has an army of 500,000 is a bit exaggerated, everyone who knows the details knows that Lu Mingyue has at least 100,000 soldiers. However, Zhang Xutuo, who claims to be a regular army, is far behind. His army, along with the elderly, the weak, the sick, and the disabled, had no more than 10,000 people. The actual effective combat strength was 7,000.
After careful thought and meditation, Zhang Xutuo decided to take a dangerous step in chess. As a result, he said to the battalion general, “Due to the scarcity of military supplies, we have reached a point where the military has no food left over, which determines that we can no longer continue to confront the enemy like this. However, if we retreat and the enemy takes advantage of the opportunity to hunt them down, our military will surely be destroyed. Thinking back and forth, I think we should break our usual mindset and do the opposite. Take a risk, and maybe we'll have a glimmer of life.” Having said that, he took a look at the people around him. Those generals, who had just been listless, had a glimmer of life when they heard about it. Who doesn't know that the top priority right now is to retreat first? Looking at everyone's expressions, Zhang Xutuo cleared his voice and continued, “Yes, maybe everyone thought about withdrawing, but at the same time, everyone must have thought. If the troops were withdrawn, the enemy would definitely hunt them down. Do people think differently about this?” Seeing that no one expressed an opinion, Zhang Xutuo added, “Since no one disagrees, let's start the analysis from here, because all of my plans are based on this starting point.” Hearing this, everyone suddenly got excited again. Zhang Xutuo loudly said, “Yes, the enemy will definitely hunt them down. If they hunt them down, we will face a crisis, right? But that's only one side of the problem. If we go after them in turn, we can definitely win more with less, and we can completely resolve the impending crisis!”
At this point, everyone's emotions were completely mobilized by Zhang Xutuo's portrayal of the future. However, the key point of this plan is that someone must secretly attack the enemy battalion with soldiers — this is a hard bone. If you don't do it, you will not only lose your own net worth, but it will also affect the entire army, leading to its complete demise. Therefore, although the coach had a brilliant idea, no one was willing to take such a big risk to take on this mission.
Zhang Xutuo waited for half a day, and actually none of the generals came out to take the lead. He hurriedly shouted, “Who is my traveler? Who is my traveler?”
“Me!” At last, someone agreed!
Zhang Xutuo looked excitedly at the one who promised. He recognized him; this person was Qin Qiong. I only heard Qin Qiong say, “My status is low. It wasn't my turn to speak, but since the generals didn't speak, I had to stand up. Please forgive me!”
Zhang Xutuo is now happy. At the moment of the war, he couldn't care less of the red tape. He immediately appointed Qin Qiong as the captain of the Daredevil Team, and handed over nearly 1,000 elite generals to Qin Qiong. Before Qin Qiong left, Zhang Xutuo took him dead by the hand and said, “The life and death of our entire army is in your hands!”
The situation was similar to what Zhang Xutuo had anticipated.
First, Zhang Xutuo's troops retreated, then Lu Mingyue's soldiers came to catch up.
On the other front, Qin Qiong and his 1,000 soldiers beat Ma Rufei and stormed the enemy's rear battalion as fast as possible.

Lu Mingyue, who was slaughtered in front of him, only learned at this point that his home in the back had already been secretly attacked. His first reaction was to fight back!
By the time Lu Mingyue changed direction and returned to the military for emergency aid, Zhang Xutuo, who was still hugging his head and mouse, suddenly seemed like a different person. He picked up a weapon at his fingertips and rushed past viciously...
Zhang Xutuo won a big victory, and the crisis was successfully resolved. Qin Qiong's fame spread all over the military camp overnight, and he became the object of worship for everyone.
In the battles that followed, Qin Qiong's brave personality was fully exploited, and he was repeatedly promoted due to his outstanding combat exploits. Slowly, Qin Qiong has developed into a general in Zhang Xutuo's army. However, everyone knows that Qin Qiong is actually a genius, not a handsome talent. If he were a handsome talent, he would have gone it alone and set up a separate mountain for a long time.
Time flew by, and when Zhang Xutuo met Li Mi, his life came to an end.
Li Mi was the leader of the Wa Gang Army in the later stages. In 613 AD, he and Yang Xuangan set up an army against Sui in Liyang (present-day northeast of Junxian County, Henan Province). It didn't work. Yang Xuan felt like a failure, and Li Mi fled. Later, Li Mi joined the Wa Gang Army. Li Mi excelled at scheming and gradually formed his own power within the Wa Gang Army. Coupled with his strict military management and good rewards, all the soldiers were happy to serve him.
In fact, Pei Renji is also Zhang Xutuo's subordinate; it's just that in Zhang Xutuo's army, he is much more famous than Qin Qiong. Later, Qin Qiong surrendered to Li Mi along with Bae In-ki.
Qin Qiong was very loyal and was deeply trusted by Li Mi. He was soon appointed General Hussars by Li Mi.
Qin Qiong lived up to Li Mi's love.
During an operation, Li Mi was hit by an arrow, fell to his horse, and fell unconscious. At this point, Li Mi's left and right followers had already been beaten by the enemy and fled everywhere. Li Mi, who was unconscious, was unguarded. The situation was very critical. Thanks to Qin Qiong desperately trying to save each other and gathering scattered soldiers Yu Yong, this was able to repel the fierce pursuit and save Li Mi's life.
Li Mi later failed, and Qin Qiong took over Wang Shichong for Sui and was appointed General Long Yi. Among the heroes of the late Sui period, Wang Shichong was unquestionably an outstanding person. He had good arguments, knew the law, wrote books, and was good at war, but his fatal flaw was that he was cunning and sinister at heart. Obviously, his personality is completely different from Qin Qiong. Therefore, although he favors Qin Qiong, Qin Qiong has always had differences. Later, Qin Qiong seized the opportunity and left Wang Shichong along with Cheng Yaojin and others to rely on the talented Li Shimin.
We know that Qin Qiong's personality is that as long as he is convinced of any person, then he will definitely lose heart and do his best for this person. Li Shimin is recruiting talents at the startup stage. Qin Qiong, who is loyal and has extraordinary abilities, was greatly appreciated by Li Shimin. As a result, soon after returning to Tang Camp, Qin Qiong was appointed as Ma Jun's general manager.
Since then, Qin Qiong has followed Qin Wang and Li Shimin successively defeated many rebels such as Wang Shichong, Dou Jiande, and Liu Heyi, and contributed greatly to the founding of the Tang dynasty. Li Yuan once sent messengers to give gold bottles as a reward. Since then, Qin Qiong has been rewarded many times for his combat achievements. He was successively appointed as the leader of the Qin Wangyou army, awarded Shangzhu Guo, and later promoted to the rank of Prince Yi. Among the many military generals, Qin Qiong was deeply trusted by Li Shimin.
At the time of the Xuanwu Gate change, Qin Qiong had a clear flag and resolutely stood by Li Shimin. Together with him, he killed Prince Li Jiancheng and Qi Wang Li Yuanji, clearing the way for Li Shimin to take the throne. In June of the 9th year of Tang Wude (626), Qin King Li Shimin was established as Crown Prince. In August, Li Shimin officially ascended the throne, Qin Qiong was appointed General Zuo Wuwei, and was granted a feudal government of 700 households.
Later, Qin Qiong summed up the first half of his life this way: since childhood, he went to war between North and South Korea, experienced more than 200 battles, and shed dozens of battles.
In the 12th year of Zhenguan (638), Qin Qiong died due to illness. In the fifth year after Qin Qiong's death, his portrait was also displayed on the Ling Yan Ge, which honored the great hero. Qin Qiong became one of the 24 heroes of the Tang Dynasty.
At this point, Qin Qiong, a loyal person, was also considered a good person and had good luck.
