 Tan Jinxuan is one of the best talents in this prefecture. He really believes in a kind of qigong technique. He practices it every day, and doesn't stop in winter and summer. After a few months of practice, I feel like I've gained something. One day, he was sitting cross-legged and heard a very small voice in his ear, like a fly calling, and said, “Can I see you?” As soon as he opened his eyes, he couldn't hear it anymore. He closed his eyes again, held his breath, and heard Fang Cai's voice again. He thought: This is probably because he has learned his skills, and he is secretly happy.
Since then, he has sat down to listen every day, thinking in his heart that when he talks again in his ear, he should say yes and open his eyes to see what it is. One day, of course, I heard that “Can I see you?” In the words of the novel, he quietly agreed: “You can see it.” I soon felt a rumbling sound in my ears, like something crawling out. He slowly opened his eyes and took a peek. Sure enough, he saw a villain who was over three inches tall, looked hideous, and was as ugly as Yasha, walking around the ground. He was secretly surprised. He thought no matter what, let's see what changes he had made before talking about it. I was watching and overheard that someone from my neighbor was borrowing something to call the door. When the little one heard it, they looked very panicked. They wandered around the house, as if the mice couldn't find a nest. Tan Xiucai also felt unconscious. It felt like he had lost his soul and didn't know where the villain had gone. Afterwards, he became insane and cried nonstop. The family requested medical treatment for him, and after being treated for half a year, he gradually got better.
