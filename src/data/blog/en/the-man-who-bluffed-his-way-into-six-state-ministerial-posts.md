 Today, the term “fool” is quite fashionable, and is often used as a joke. However, in Chinese history, there was a person who relied on superior deception skills and was on par with the six countries, but little is known.
Throughout history, we have to associate Su Qin in the late Warring States period with the person who was the most foolish person in Chinese history, saying that he was the most foolish person in history, or his grandfather who “fooled” people. It's no exaggeration at all, because he not only relied on ingenious deception strategies to put himself on the rank of the Six Kingdoms, but also “avenged life and death.”
Su Qin (337-284 BC), a native of Xuanli in Luoyang, Eastern Zhou (present-day Taipingzhuang area in the eastern suburbs of Luoyang), a Korean during the Warring States period. Su Qin was born in a farmhouse. He has five brothers, including the four elders of the Su Dynasty, Su Li, Su Yi, and Su Hu. Since childhood, they have had big ambitions. He studied the art of vertical and horizontal separation from his teacher Kiyuko for many years, and painstakingly read vertical and horizontal techniques such as “Taigong's Conspiracy”, and became a famous vertical and horizontal family during the Warring States period. He advocated cooperation (cooperating with other countries to deal with the Qin State) as “being angry and fearful, living in peace and extinguishing the world.”
After studying Guizi from her teacher, Su Qin really wanted to make a difference. She sought advice from Zhou Tianzi, but had no introduction. In one fell swoop, she sold her family property and found a way out in another country. However, he has been running around for several years without becoming an official. After that, I ran out of money, and my clothes were torn, so I had to go home. , resulting in “My wife doesn't work, my sister-in-law doesn't cook, and my parents don't talk.” Su Qin lamented, “My wife doesn't use me as her husband, my sister-in-law doesn't use me as my uncle, and my parents don't use me as their son; it's all Qin's fault!” I couldn't get out of my room; I read his books everywhere. Su Qin painstakingly read Taigong's “The Shadow Sign” and studied “Zhou Shu's Yin Sign.” Every time he was sleepy and wanted to sleep, he stabbed himself in the back with a cone. Although it was very painful, he felt refreshed, so he continued reading it. This is the origin of the word “prickly strung” in the idiom “hanging beam prickle”. Just like this, Su Qin spent more than a year of hard work, and his knowledge is much richer than before. Once you've had some harvest, go back on a trip.
After King Yan Zhao (reigned from 311 to 278 BC) took office, the former country was destroyed, and efforts were made to recruit wise people in order to gain strength and shame. From Su Qin to Qin, they were not used. At a time when they were at the end of their lives, when they met Yan Zhao and Wang Guang recruited the world's wise men. Su Qin was overjoyed and immediately joined Yan.
Su Qin came to Yan, and King Zhao sent him to Qi to negotiate land in Yan Guo that was still occupied by Qi. Su Qin arrived in Qi and said to Qi Xuanwang, “King Yan Zhaowen is the grandson of King Qin Huiwen and the son-in-law of Qin Guo. With strong Qin as your backing, you must have taken over Yan and Qin are dissatisfied, and brought in the elite soldiers of the Qin Kingdom! If King were able to return the land he occupied in the Ten Cities of Yan Guo to Yan Guo, then Yan and Qin would instead be grateful to the King for his kindness. The king can then command the world with Qin Yan's support. No one in the world dares to follow suit, then Qi can become hegemonic.” Xuan Wang was overjoyed and returned to the ten former cities of Yan Guo. In 295 BC, Su Qin sent protons to Qi for Yan's envoy. Su Qin was in Qi. He was very kind and friendly, and later returned to Yan.
In 292 BC, the three kingdoms of Qi, Zhao, and Qin came to power. When they tried their best to seize Song Kingdom land, Su Qin saw Yan Zhaowang's intention to conquer and made a plan for Zhaowang Dao: “Even though we took back the land that had been occupied by Qi, Zhao, and Qin. However, the hatred of the country that died back then cannot go unreported. If Yan Guo wants to avenge Qi's revenge, he must first show submission and submission to Qi, cover up his desire for revenge, and gain the time needed to revitalize Yan Guo. Second, it is necessary to encourage Qi to continuously attack other countries in order to prevent Qi from attacking Yan and depleting its national strength. If we make Qi Xilao in Song and the South exhausted, we can take the opportunity to launch an attack and destroy them all in one fell swoop. I asked Qi to say that King Xuan attacked Song.” Su Qin assured Wang Yan Zhaowang that his “faith is like the end of his life,” and that he would act in accordance with secret agreements and keep his trust until death. As a saying goes, Yan Zhao rewarded Su Qin for promoting “the art of collaboration” by waking up to his dream, and giving him 100 luxury cars, 100 pairs of white bikes, a golden manyi, and countless lilacs and satin. Su Qin began a majestic tour around the world, “using his three-inch tongue as the emperor's master.” The princes on all sides were thrilled, fervent, and obeyed their words. King Yan Zhao treated Su Qin as Shangqing and went on a trip to Qi.
When Su Qin arrived in Qi in 285 BC, he tried his best to stir up the relationship between Qi and Zhao, gain the trust of King Qi, and promote Qi Xiang. However, Su Qin was still secretly planning for Yan Guo while Cao Yingxin was in Han.
Qin Guo always had good relations with Song Guo; Qi Fa and Song had to break ties with Qin. It just so happened that Qin sent people to Qi to discuss becoming emperor together. Su Qin took the opportunity to persuade Qi Wang: “Qi and Qin stand side by side as emperor. Do people in the world respect or respect Qin?” Qi Wang said, “Of course I respect Qin!” “So if Qi abandons the imperial title, does the world love Qi or Qin?” “Of course it's Aizi!” “The two emperors stand side by side, agree to conquer Zhao, or attack Song alone with the Qi army. Which one is more advantageous?” Qi Wang answered, “Of course it is beneficial to defeat Song!” Su Qin then advised Qi Wang to say, “If we claim emperor like Qin, the world will only respect Qin. If we give up our imperial title, the world will love and call Qin a strong Qin. It would be better to conquer Zhao together and conquer Song alone. Therefore, I am in favor of abandoning the imperial title to suit the world.”
Qi Wang followed Su Qin's suggestions and joined forces with Zhao Guo in the Arab region to agree to fight against Qin. The relationship between Qin and Qi deteriorated. Su Qin took the opportunity to persuade Qi Wang to attack Song: “The monarch of Song Guo was wild, and the whole world was outraged. If we attack Song in the West, it was a feat of punching the crimes of Fongtian. The king would surely be famous against the princes, and he would also be able to obtain practical benefits, making Qi stand tall in the East and become a leader of the princes of the Central Plains.” So they attacked Song. In order to gain Qi's trust, Yan sent troops to assist Qi. Under attack by the allied forces, Song divided Huaibei to seek peace, and Qi's strength was weakened by the war.
In 285 BC, King Qi did not know the truth about Su Qin's plans, and still appointed Su Qin to lead an army against the Yan army. When Qi Yan's army fought, Su Qin intended to defeat Qi's army, and 50,000 people died. He caused discord between Qi and his government, distracted the people, and laid the foundation for Le Yi's five coalition forces to defeat Qi. Afterwards, Su Qin also persuaded Zhao Guo to join forces with Korea, Wei, Qi, Chu, and Yan to attack Qin. Zhao Guojun was very happy and gave Su Qin many treasures. With help from Zhao Guo, Su Qin also went to Korea to lobby Han Xuan Wang; to Wei to lobby Wei Xiang; to Qi, to lobby King Qi Xuan; and to Chu to lobby King Chu Wei. The princes all agreed to Su Qin's plan, so the six countries reached a joint pact. After Su Qin returned to Zhao Guo, Zhao Wang named him Wu Anjun. Qin was taken aback when he learned this news. After that, for 15 years, Qin Bing did not dare to plan an attack on Hakogu Guannai.
In Qi, Su Qin continued to work to weaken Qi. He advised Qi Wang to develop civil engineering and indulge in fun, while waging war with the outside world and building up enemies. The relationship between Qi and Qin deteriorated. Coupled with the concerted attack on Song Guo, the Qin King was very angry. Su Qin advised Qi Wang to take military action first to attack and contain the development of Qin's forces. At the same time, they also want Qi's strength to be further consumed.
Wang Qi had concerns about Yan Huai. Su Qin defended Yan, “Yan Guo is weak and has always been attached to Qiang Qi, and the reason Qi was able to command the world is precisely because of Yan's support. This kind of friendly relationship is what the people of Yan Guo want; how can they be aligned and have different intentions?” Qi Wang was relieved. As a result, Su Qin sent out to run for Qi Wang to invade Qin.
Su Qin lobbied Han, Zhao, and Wei, and the rulers of the four kingdoms separately. They each sent troops to attack the Qin State and chose Feng Yangjun, the prime minister of the Zhao dynasty, as co-leader, yet Qi was actually the real organizer and commander of the coalition. Qi nominally invaded Qin; in fact, they only used the Qin system so that they could not step out to save Song, and Qi Hao attacked Song again. This actually left the coalition forces of the other four countries in a situation of advancing and withdrawing. They were unable to join forces to attack Qin, nor were they able to immediately withdraw their troops back to China. As a result, all countries are secretly dissatisfied. Su Qin strongly advocated a strong attack on the Qin State, yet Qi neither worked hard. Naturally, Zhao Han and Wei Yan all made concessions to each other and were unable to advance. Therefore, the coalition forces never had a large-scale war with Qin. Despite this, the advance of Qi Lao and Shi still greatly undermined the country's strength. Originally, Su Qin went south at this time, apparently to attack Qin together, but secretly they were actively linking up anti-Qi allied forces for Yan. When Su Qin saw that countries were centred, he secretly advised Meng Shaojun in Wei's country: “In the past, when you were in Qi, you established worldly achievements for Qi, and Qi Wang's dark knowledge not only didn't reuse it, but it also made you leave your hometown and return far away to Wei. Now they are abandoning their faith and making fun of the coalition countries. Yan Jun is planning to attack, and Zhao Guo has long been in agreement and hate. If they join forces to attack Qi in the east, the Central Plains will be powerful, and you, Mr. Wei He, will definitely be famous all over the world.” Meng Zhaojun agreed to Su Qin's proposal.
Su Qin also persuaded Qi Wang to seek peace with Qin: “Wei Zhao is close to Qin and far from Qin Yuan. If our five countries join forces to defeat Qin, Wei Zhao will definitely have to seek peace with Qin in order to preserve the country. Once Qin is united with other countries, they will definitely fight back and forth. I hope King will prepare early and make peace with Qin first to avoid passivity in the situation.” Qi Wang thought what Su Qin said was very reasonable, so he first made a friendly statement with Qin, and planned to use Han Nie, who is pro-Qin, as prime minister.
Zhao Guo and Feng Yang Jun were busy invading Qin. Seeing that Qi Wang had a good relationship with Qin without discussion, they were very annoyed, and joined Wei Yan to go to war against Qi, which was busy attacking Song. Qi Wang hurriedly withdrew his army from Song and promised to give Fengyang Jun the land. Only then did Fengyang Jun stop attacking.
Fengyang Jun received a promise of land from Qi Wang, and his relationship with Qi improved again, and Su Qin's secret alienation activities to reconcile Zhao's relationship were also detected by Fengyang Jun. As a result, Feng Yangjun detained Su Qin in Zhao Guo and restricted his movements. Su Qin asked Yan Wang for help and planned to continue her separation activities. Wang Yan made a serious protest against Zhao Fengyang. Feng Yangjun released Su Qin, yet Su Qin was no longer able to find an opportunity to work in Zhao. He wanted to meet the envoys sent by Qi to Zhao; Zhao wouldn't allow it. Su Qin was helpless and left Zhao Ruqi.
After Su Qin joined forces, Yan Zhaowang became suspicious of him because he discouraged Yan Zhaowang's confrontational attacks several times, using the excuse that the time had not come, so Zhao Wang planned to have someone else replace Su Qin and return home. Su Qin felt very aggrieved and wrote a letter of defense to Yan Wang. This letter can be described as a review of his life's accomplishments. He said, “Yan and Qi have been feuding for a long time. I am working hard for Yanqi's diplomatic relations; it is already difficult to gain the trust of all parties. Qi is Yan Guo's deepest affliction. Being in Qi can make Qi not plan to attack Yan, and small can cause the relationship between Qi and Zhao to break up, so as to prepare for the great king's big event. Although the Five Kingdoms defeated Qin and Yan sent troops to provide food, the first removed the humiliation of being called Emperor and Yan, and the other, there was no harm from Qi Zhao attacking Yan. Later, Fengyang Jun accepted Qi's fiefdom and detained me in Zhao Guo. The great king saved the minister out of water and fire. Now Qi and Zhao have no plans to attack Yan, and Yan has been able to save his national strength. Although I am unsuccessful, I think I can be exonerated. As Yan Chen, my activities in Qi have always been rumored. If I were Qi Xiangui, Dr. Yan Guo wouldn't trust me; if I was being humiliated by Qi, the world would look down on me. If I am reused by Qi Wang, Dr. Yan will have hopes for me; if my hopes are not met, my grievances will increase. If Qi is not good at Yan, they put all the responsibility on me. If people in the world don't attack, they say I am good at planning together. My situation can also be described as dangerous enough. I'm not afraid to die to retaliate against the king, but the king doubts blaming his vassals. I am really afraid. Even though I think I can be listed among the world's rulers, I have nothing to feel guilty about. For example, Dawang only re-uses talented wise people, and I would like to cooperate seriously with him; if the king doesn't feel safe with me, I will return to Yan to serve the king in order to ease the king's concerns. At last, King Yan Zhaowang did not replace Su Qin.
The six countries coalition originally started from their own interests, so the foundation is not deep. The Qin faction made their first mission to Qi Guo and Wei Guo to conquer Zhao Guo in order to disrupt the six countries' coalition. Su Qin left Zhao Guo, and the merger fell apart.
Yan Wenhou died, Yi Wangli, and Su Qin had an affair with Yan Yi Wang's mother (Mrs. Wen Hou). Yan Yi Wang knew about this, but treated Su Qin better. Su Qin was afraid of being defeated, so she persuaded Yan Yi Wang: “If I stay in Yan Guo, I can't improve Yan Guo's status. If I were in Qi, I would definitely be able to improve Yan Guo's status.” Yan Yi Wang said, “Let the gentleman do everything.” As a result, Su Qin pretended to offend Yan Wang and fled to Qi. King Qi Xuan then appointed him as a guest minister. After that, King Xuan died, and after King Qi took over the throne, many of Qi's doctors and Su Qin and the doctor who competed with Qi Wang Chongxin assassinated Su Qin. Su Qin was seriously injured. Qi Wang sent people to apprehend the thieves, but they didn't catch them. When Su Qin was about to die, he said to Qi Wang, “I'm about to die. Please show my dead body to the crowd in a crowded market, and say, 'Su Qin attempted a rebellion in Qi for Yan Guo. 'By doing this, the murderer who assassinated me will definitely be caught.” Qi Wang followed his plan. Indeed, the murderer of Su Qin was assassinated. In order to solicit merit, Qi Wang took the opportunity to arrest the murderer and kill him.
Su Qin would rather humiliate his corpse and have Qi Wang apprehend the thief who murdered him, and the “life and death revenge” plan offered to King Qi became a sensational event in the late Warring States period. Su Qin was reflected in many of the writings of people at the time, such as the bamboo version “Sun Tzu's Art of War: Use of the Sun Tzu” unearthed in Yinqueshan, Shandong, which contains the words “Yan Zhi Xing is also, Su Qin is in Qi”, and “Lu's Chunqiu · Zhidu”: “Use Su Qin together and the world knows his death.” These records show that during the Warring States period, everyone knew that Su Qin served Yan and eventually made Qi die and prosper, but he also sacrificed himself for Yan. Su Qin was very famous in the late Warring States period. “Xun Zi: Chen Dao” compared “Qi's Su Qin” to “Prince Chu Zhi” and “Zhang Yi of Qin.” During the Western Han period, Su Qin was still praised by people, such as “Historical Records: The Biography of Zou Yang”, praising him for being a loyal servant of Yan. “Huai Nanzi” also mentions him in many places, and affirms that he has the advantage of intellectual power planning. Sima Qian thought that Su Qin showed his ingenuity in the process of “joining the six countries.”
