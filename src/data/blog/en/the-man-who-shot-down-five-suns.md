 Legend has it that in ancient times, there were 6 suns, and the earth evaporated with flames. People couldn't stand the sun. They used bows and arrows together to shoot at the sun, but no one could shoot a bow or arrow where the sun is. Later, there was a man named Gabeache Merlin. He overcame many difficulties. He borrowed divine bows and arrows from the dragon king Xiang Mai Street, and also borrowed arrows that could make the divine arrow fly high, and finally shot 5 of the 6 suns in the sky, sparing the extinction of the human race.
