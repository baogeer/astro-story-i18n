 In the early 80s of the last century, I was placed in a small town to specialize in writing at a cultural institution in the provincial city. I learned about Li Yu at the Drama Research Institute within it.
I was very impressed by Li Yu's earliest name. The original name was Immortal Couple, the character Yufan, called Tiantu — as if it were a prophecy about his life.
In the slightly exaggerated account, Li Yu is simply strange: he was literate when he was swaddled; he wrote an essay with a thousand words; the boy tried his hand at the “Five Sutra Boy” and was famous for a while; however, he went back and forth to his hometown to no avail. At one time, she was a state samurai, and wrote songs by Shen Yue and Li Qingzhao. Since then, no one dared to write easily, Ba Yonglou wrote “It's a difficult sentence after Shen Lang goes; Wu Nu takes the lead”, which is highly acclaimed. Qing soldiers entered the city, returned to their hometown Lanxi, built Yi Garden, and became “literate farmers.”
“Even chickens and dogs are welcome, and we met in the mountains and rivers. Farmers have enjoyed themselves since they dropped out of fishing, throwing their fishing rods together...” “Homecoming's Tales” seems like an escape from Tao Yuanming's “Homecoming.” If that were true, there was at most one more hermit in the history of literature.
However, Li Yu is not “Mr. Wu Liu.” He advocated the construction of a gazebo and named it “stop the pavilion”, titled “It's good for the road to run and be quiet and stop; those who come and go are quiet and stop”; he advocated water conservancy, excavating weirs in the fields, so that Tianhe would be shaded. Shipingba was later known as “Li Yu Ba”; he made a pact with the Prime Minister's Ancestral Temple, signed a temple agreement, and built a family tree, which was highly respected by the villagers.
The reason for the change was a lawsuit with a neighboring village. Li Yu apparently doesn't have the genes to fight, and her family moved to Hangzhou.
A bustling city, full of traffic, it should have a relatively open foothold; it should be more than enough to support the family; it should be more than enough to support a family; it has no relatives, and is blessed to have strengths; it can be used by proud gentlemen and people with a strong interest in drama novels.
The first professional writer in Chinese history to “sell his talents to make a living” was born.
Like all his humble disciples, Li Yu once decided that Guang Zong Yaozu of the Shitu Economy was the right path in life. Unexpectedly, the situation changed; if you learn better, your dream will be shattered. Although “people's tears are all blood, and paper money is all gray,” he did not lie back down, but instead took a different path that no one had taken before.
Fate doomed him to wander and deviate. Li Yu chose the “last skill” of “selling words,” which orthodox writers disliked, and was viewed as a “cheap business.” In terms of self-indulgence and humility, it is similar to Guan Hanqing, the founder of the original miscellaneous drama.
Guan Hanqing used “copper peas” to express his rebellion against traditional norms. To some extent, it was a form of protest. However, Li Yu had no such pride, no such cynicism. His choice was simply a matter of course. He doesn't care about the secular hierarchy of “Seven Masters, Eight Prostitutes, Nine Confucians, and Ten Prostitutes,” and doesn't care about how the world views him. He admits that it is more than true that he is praised as “King Tang.” If Izumishita knew, he would only laugh at later generations who despised him as a “popular writer.” He has never written a blood-wrenching tragedy like Guan Hanqing's “Dou Ewen”, nor did he write famous quotes such as “Blue Clouds, Yellow Flowers, Strong West Wind, North Geese Fly South” and Tang Xianzu's “Peony Pavilion”, “Morning and Twilight Scrolls, Clouds, Wind, Wind, Wind, Wind, Smoke and Waves.” He conceals the sharpness of punishing evil and promoting good and denouncing Daoism in gentleness and smoothness. He is empathetic and invests in what is good, and only seeks to understand and understand. People of all ages appreciate elegance and customs. “Among Kitasato Nanjo's songs, there are none who don't know Rishuro.”
Looking at ancient and modern times, it seems that many people have written books that are deeply rooted, and their position in the literary world is so high that it is impossible to hope that it is imminent. However, what Li Yu gave to the world was a kind of ease and kindness.
Li Yu has never been an official in his entire life. He has an outstanding attitude. He has innovative opinions, does not rely on others, does not repeat himself, strives to discover “things that have never been seen before,” “copying unfinished feelings, and drawing imperfect images”. He has written an astonishing number and is unique, and has led the trend of fashion culture for a long time.
His novel is a “book of fresh eyes,” which was sold out as soon as it came out; as soon as he changed the screenplay, it became a flaw, “expensive but not too expensive”; his “Relaxation and Dispatch” founded the first systematic theory of drama in Chinese history, which actually also included creation, decoration, medicine, health, cooking, beauty, dressing, etc.; his garden was exquisite and unique. “Mustard Nasuya” has gone back and forth; when it comes to publishing, from title selection and engraving to distribution, he went back and forth. Proficient in mustard garden books; he is not a painter, but he advocated the compilation of “The Legend of Mustard Garden Paintings”, so that “everyone who paints landscapes in the world can paint landscapes “Joy doesn't have to be in the name of a painter; he is well-liked on all sides; he is well-respected in all directions, and is well-versed in all aspects. He works well in the three schools, earns money, and is careful and easy to navigate step by step; he “imprisoned his life in smoke, bamboo and stone”, “Famous Mountains, Daikawa, Ten Sutras 67,” “after a time, you can see the people of one place, and see the victory of one side through the others. Also, he never ate, never tasted it”; his opera family is popular all over the country. They get along in the morning and evening, where kindness, friendship, art, and love blend well with each other.
After becoming famous, Li Yu also gave himself many names: “Ga Hong,” “Ga Shi Wei Guan,” “Ga Daojin,” “Master of Sui An,” and “Kasaou on the Lake.” All of this flaunts a meaning — a self-proclaimed small person, as if deliberately contrasting with his extraordinary, extraordinary, and very bright life. The titles his descendants gave him were dazzling: novelist, poet, playwright, drama theorist, publisher, book operator, social activist, garden artist, inventor, aesthetician, foodie, fashion culture advocate, cultural industry pioneer... and I think the title he is most qualified to enjoy should be Life Home — a unique lifestyle. He lives artfully and artfully puts the art of life into words, so he finds his way in art and life. He is an artist for life — first for survival, then for enjoyment.
Precisely because of this, he is far superior to countless pretentious and merry talents.
Li Yu's body has gathered almost all the ingenuity, talents, dreams, personalities, strengths, fetishes, and flaws of traditional Chinese writers. However, compared to his old and reserved peers, he has a high-profile style of behavior and a broad lifestyle. He is sensitive and fascinated by Hanaya Kien, delicious wine, and beautiful sounds. He is passionate and full of desires. He spent his whole life in the midst of fireworks, leisurely, and lifted weights as lightly. In his opinion, a masterpiece of living without asking for a living is nothing more than the “idleness” of “Occasional Memories.” He is a man's talkative, and women appreciate it. He represents a different culture with a fresh air.
He has been a book reader, but he is not bound by solemn classics; he has fantasized about being an official, but his dry order cannot tolerate his vivacity. Li Yu is too ahead of the curve, at least hundreds of years ahead of schedule. He abandoned his fame as a mentor, went beyond the normal path, and lived through the succession of legendary operas. He reviewed “The Chronicles of the Three Kingdoms” and revised “Golden Lotus”. His specialty during his heyday was a game, like an outstanding joke. He only writes comedies, “I only write lyrics and don't worry; I'm worried if I don't laugh.” He enjoyed society and lived like an immortal himself. Or the other way around, he lived like an immortal and delighted the whole society at the same time. As a result, he was quite comfortable: “Even though many mentors have talents from heaven and earth, such as not being able to ascend the sky, why did they think?” He himself is a legend, a comedy, and an enviable garden. He is like an immortal couple. How long has he ever been a lion?
However, in the end, Li Yu was unable to avoid a bleak ending.
He's so successful. His influence was far-reaching, and so was his jealousy. When you reach the peak of your life and career, you fall into the trap of human nature. Defamation and rumors followed, and Li Yu, who relied on pen and ink, always held back her breath. He only betrays his talent and doesn't hurt others. He always wanted to live well for himself and had no intention of opposing anyone. The human nature he drew was a beautiful landscape, and he didn't want to get dirty.
Li Yu has both fame and fortune, but he is no ordinary person. At the end of his life, he quietly embarked on the path of return. A friend of many years wrote eight small letters on his hand: “To be clever is extremely counterproductive.”
The early hours of this morning have finally arrived — in the rare years of life, winter days, heavy snowfall, and the luck of the owners of a generation of wind and moon came to an end.
His last resort was a tiered garden he worked so hard to build. The garden is built around the mountains, and the “Sun on the Lake” can enjoy the views of the lake and mountains from their sitting and sleeping. “The hustle and bustle drives people, and all my old work dusts the city; Hushan invites me, and the whole family moves into the picture.”
Layyuan lost its original owner, and eventually lost its original life due to decay. And the person who gave it life won't go away with it. The impressive number of intangible buildings he has created in his lifetime will last much longer than all the tangible buildings he has ever owned.
