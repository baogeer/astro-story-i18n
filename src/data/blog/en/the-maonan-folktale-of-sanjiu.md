 The Maonan mountains have few water sources and rivers, and have often been threatened by droughts since ancient times. There is a Maonan native named Qin Sanjiu who wants to turn Maonan Mountain into a seaside. So he waved a “bamboo shell knife” day and night to cut the top of the mountain and asked his wife to deliver food to him every day. Once my wife came to the bottom of the mountain, I saw him cutting a mountain with a “bamboo shell knife” and angrily said that he was a “fool.” How can a “bamboo shell knife” level a stone mountain? 39 was shocked, and the “bamboo shell knife” broke.
When they were unable to cut down the mountain, they tried to drive the mountain away. One day, as he was walking forward with a large group of stones, he ran into a rich woman and asked her: Have you seen a herd of cows walking ahead? The treasurer colluded with the government's wife to answer that he had not seen any cows, but only saw many stones rolling forward. As soon as she said this, all of these stones couldn't move. However, 39 was not convinced, and had to invite the 12 dragons to gather at Sien and agree to come out when the three are screaming. However, this secret was revealed to the rich woman again.
Before reaching the 3rd quarter, Grandma Zaifan slapped her butt. As a result, a group of chickens screamed. Only two of the 12 dragons came out and became the Huanjiang River and the Dongjiang River, flowing through the outlying areas of the Maonan ethnic group. Qin Sanjiu moved mountains to create the sea and was relentless. The Maonan people saw him as the embodiment of their ancestors conquering nature, so it has been passed down from generation to generation. It is the glorious history of the Maonan people to resist class oppression and ethnic oppression, and to oppose the dark forces of evil.
