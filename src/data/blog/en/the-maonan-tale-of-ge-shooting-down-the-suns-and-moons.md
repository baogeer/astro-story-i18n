 In ancient times, there was a man named “Ge”. He was very tall, skilled in archery, and had a great fortune.
There were 12 suns and moons in the sky at the time. The sun was burning day and night, rivers dried up, trees and seedlings were killed by the sun, and people were also very uncomfortable in the heat. So the “emperor” invited “grid” to shoot the sun and moon. “Ge” took the bow and shot down ten in a row, leaving only one sun and one moon. The sun shines on the grains during the day, and the moon illuminates the path at night. Since then, the wind and rain have been smooth, and the grains have been harvested well. People are very grateful to “Ge” for his work, and they have praised him from generation to generation.
