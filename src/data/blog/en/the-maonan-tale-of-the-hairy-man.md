 Legend has it that the “Mao” family was poor, and his father fell down a mountain and died. He became an adult when he was five years old. He has dark hair, round ears, and is extremely powerful. The “Maoists” hate the bullying and oppression of the rich owners and beat the lackeys of the rich who arrested people at their homes. The treasurer colluded with the government and made every effort to kill him, but he united the people of Zhuang, Yao, and Miao to overcome many difficulties, and finally achieved victory.
