 During the Qianlong period of the Qing dynasty, there was a martial arts champion in Tengxian County. His name was Man Dekun.
At that time, the country was still too peaceful. Although Man Dekun was the champion, he did not lead the military, nor did he guard the town for a period of time. It's just guarding the palace gate, that is, a low-level errand of being on guard duty.
As the saying goes, dogs are about character. The palace maiden and eunuch both looked down on Man Champion. Every time the emperor, wife, and concubine enter and leave the palace gate, Man Dekun stands by and is often scolded by eunuchs and palace maidens. Man Dekun has excellent martial arts and is also an imposing martial arts champion. He really doesn't want to do this kind of missionary work. However, I couldn't talk to anyone, let alone suggest it to the emperor; I had to hide it in my heart.
On one day, when he met Minister Lianli and Liu Yong, who is also a fellow countryman, Man Dekun secretly told him that he was unwilling to guard the palace gate. Liu Yong meditated for a long time, but he couldn't think of a good solution. Then there was some gossip. When Man Dekun had no intention of talking about Qianlong, he said, “Long live today. You can enter the palace at night without entering or leaving the palace gate. Since he is well-versed in literature and martial arts, he often flies to steal over palace walls, so I can just pretend I haven't seen it.” According to the rules of the imperial court, the emperor must inform the eunuch every night in which palace, and the eunuch will make arrangements. However, Qianlong loved not only his concubines, but also Nishinomiya. I stayed overnight in two palaces. Even though he was an emperor, I always felt that it wasn't good when things came to light. To this end, Qianlong adopted the method of stealing over the palace wall.
Liu Yong heard about Qianlong crossing the palace wall late at night and said to Man Zhuangyuan, “You don't want to use the palace gate method anymore.” Then I said this and again to Man Dekun, and Man Dekun repeatedly nodded and said yes.
A few days later, Man Dekun was on duty late at night. Seeing that the Qianlong Light Vehicle had to cross the palace wall again. Man Dekun caught up with an arrow, took a step, and knocked him to the ground. He held his shoulder with his left hand, and had to fight with an iron fist with his right hand. Speaking of being late, it was fast. Qianlong hurriedly said, “Don't fight; it's a widow.” Man Dekun immediately grabbed him with his hands and knelt down on the ground: “Hooray! Damn the minions!” At this point, Qianlong had to say, “You are a good boy. Don't speak up; I apologize for not being guilty.”
Man Dekun kicked Qianlong with flying feet. Qianlong thought he did the right thing. However, it was inconvenient for him. Finally, a sacred mission was ordered to take care of the border.
