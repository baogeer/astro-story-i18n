 In the eyes of the world, Grandpa Cat can be considered the most blessed and most envied animal. This isn't just because legends say it has “nine lives,” and it won't fall to death no matter how high a roof it falls. Furthermore, due to how relaxed and enjoyable its life is, it is free and at ease. It basks in the sun on the wall during the day, and sleeps peacefully in the hot stove at night. If you're hungry, open your mouth to eat in the owner's well-prepared bowl; when you're happy, go hunting — catching mice.
Therefore, in the past, there was a popular saying that if you want to become a cat in the next life, you must accumulate enough virtue in this life: that is, to connect 99 pairs of men and women, that is, to be a popular media in 99 Taiwan. It can be seen from this that it is not easy to have a “cat's life”. However, Grandpa Cat himself is not very satisfied with his current situation. Every time it curls up to rest, it always keeps talking and “snoring” and complaining, “Yes, no delivery, no delivery...”
According to people in old age, Grandpa Cat's complaint certainly has its own reason and origin. Legend has it that Grandpa Cat was not originally our earthly animal; he was a pet nurtured by Emperor Jade. At that time, what it ate, drank and drank was natural fairy food from heaven, and what it visited was also a wonderland full of beauty in the sky. Later, due to the plague of rats on earth, the crops that people had painstakingly harvested were all destroyed by mice in large quantities. I saw how hungry people were one by one, yellow, thin, and bone-covered. The Jade Emperor then sent his darling, Grandpa Cat, to personally clean up these bad guys who acted recklessly and rampant for a while. At the beginning, Grandpa Cat naturally didn't want to leave Heavenly Garden, where he was at ease. The Jade Emperor personally made a wish to him and said, “As soon as you go down and catch the mice, I'll let them send you back to Heaven.”
Grandpa Cat was extremely reluctant to go down to the earth. In order to be able to complete the mission as soon as possible and return to the heavenly court, he went around and caught mice day and night. After many days of fighting, there were only five mice left. Grandpa Neko, who is in a hurry to get ahead, saw the right moment. He jumped over and stabbed one with his mouth, then used his two front claws to catch two of them each. Unexpectedly, at this critical moment, an old black dog sneaked up from behind. Suddenly frightened by this accident, Grandpa Cat couldn't help but loosen his claws. The four mice, which had already been tied down to death, took the opportunity to escape. Grandpa Cat failed because of this. Since then, the cunning mouse and Grandpa Cat have played the hide-and-seek game of “You go in and I retreat, you come and I hide, you walk out, you sleep and I make trouble”, and are desperately trying to reproduce many children and grandchildren to go all the way with Grandpa Cat. As a result, mice in the world are always caught, and they can't be caught endlessly. As a result, Grandpa Neko has been too late to return to the heavenly court.
Grandpa Cat only descended from all over the world to help humans eliminate the scourge of mice. Naturally, he contributed greatly to humans, so it has always been treated particularly well by humans. But no matter how good the world is, it can't be compared to heaven! Therefore, Grandpa Cat is always stubborn about not being able to return to the Heavenly Court. As a result, when it was idle, it kept on complaining, “Yes, no delivery, no delivery...”
