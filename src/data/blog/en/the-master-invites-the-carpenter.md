 In September 1929, Chinese painting master Xu Beihong became the dean of the School of Art at Beiping University under the recommendation of Cai Yuanpei. He immediately appointed Qi Baishi as professor.
The Beiping art scene at the time was lifeless. It was capable of imitating the ancients, and the conservative forces were quite stubborn. Qi Baishi, a carpenter, is bold and innovative, and lives very isolated in the midst of cynicism and sarcasm in the Beiping painting scene.
When Xu Beihong first arrived in front of Qi Baishi Gate in a four-wheeled carriage, Qi Baishi choked so much that he couldn't say anything: “I'm a carpenter with an axe in an old house in Xingtang. How dare I become a professor at an institution of higher learning?”
“Not only can you teach me Xu Beihong's students, but you can teach me Xu Beihong himself!” Xu Beihong said, “Mr. Qi, I, Xu, want to use your axe to cut down dead branches and trees on the Beiping painting platform!”
