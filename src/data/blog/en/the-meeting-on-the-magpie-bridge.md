In other words, when Mrs. Wang saw the cowherd chasing the weaver, she pulled off a hairpin to draw out a wide Tianhe River, separating the weaver's cowherd, so that this loving couple could only look forward to each other day and not be dependent on each other. The weaver misses the cowherd. No matter the day or night, wind or rain, she always stands on the other side of the galaxy and watches from afar.
The cowherd also really missed the weaver, looking through the autumn water. On this day, the weaver saw a few magpies flying to the other side and wrote a few words to the cowherd:
“Moarang on the other side of the Oriya,
Silver and Han are vast and the ocean is full of water,
Looking forward to the twilight without hesitation,
Stay calm and wait for Magpie's good fortune.”
After reading Orionna's letter, the cowherd wrote:
“Husband and wife are closely connected.
The cowherd is sad like the waves,
It's really hard to sleep thinking day and night,
Until when is the reunion.”
After writing, let the magpie take the other side to the weaver. After reading it, Weaver burst into tears and wrote a letter to the cowherd to express her determination to love each other to the death. Just write to each other like this. You come and go, and keep on writing with each other. On this day, the magpie was about to fly to the other side carrying a letter from the weaver, and was intercepted by the royal mother.
When Mrs. Wang opened the letter, she read:
“The Tianhe River is endless and vast,
The husband and wife look forward to each other and are sad,
I shed tears and wet my clothes,
I hate Mother Wang for one mistake.”
Looking at it, Mother Wang felt that the weaver and the cowherd already had a deep relationship and were extremely affectionate, and difficult to break up. This is a matter of fate and purpose. Although the common man on earth is no match for the fairy Chang'e in the sky, they already have an iron heart for each other. I shouldn't have gotten in the way and broke up this happy marriage.
After weighing things over and over again, Mother Wang wrote a letter asking Magpie to bring her to Weaver:
“Magpies helped to the end,
The Milky Way Tanabata Bridge was built,
We are happy to meet once a year,
No wonder the old lady doesn't understand!”
The cowherd and the weaver were very happy after watching it. Since then, every year on the night of July 7 of the lunar calendar, the weaver crossed the galaxy and met the cowherd, and magpies built a bridge. People call it Magpie Bridge Encounter, and it continues to spread to this day.
