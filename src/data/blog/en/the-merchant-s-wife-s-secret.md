 The businessman came back from trading in northern Tibet and discovered that his wife had no rules at home. He was so upset that he told a friend the details of his doubts. A friend said, “Brother, don't be suspicious. I'll dress up as a lama to help you explore the truth!”
After three days, the businessman went out to do business again. My wife is busy at home, waiting for a secret meeting with someone who is good with her. When it was almost dark, an old lama stumbled in from outside, clasped his hands, and kept crying; “Master, I'm an ascetic monk wandering everywhere. Seeing that it's getting dark, please stay with me overnight!” At the beginning, the merchant's wife didn't agree at all. Couldn't stand the old lama's bowing and bitter pleas, so she let him stay in a small house with firewood piled up in the yard.
After a while, the businessman's wife opened the back door and welcomed her. The two killed a black goat, made a pot of gnocchi, and took out a can of barley wine from the bar, sat next to each other, and drank in a relaxed manner. Needless to say, it was very comfortable. Suddenly, the sound of ping pong pong on the door came from the yard. As soon as the merchant's wife heard it, she immediately blushed her face. Her husband had come back because she heard the sound.
However, she did not panic. While agreeing, she threw goatskin in the corner of the door, poured the gnocchi into the ashes, and spilled the wine in a small ditch. She also called herself good looks and hid it in a big wooden box before opening the door.
The businessman walked in and said to his wife, “Oops, today I'm very unlucky. I just walked to the ferry crossing, and suddenly my brain felt dizzy and my chest hurt. Go ask for a lama and help me see if you're sick!” The wife said, “Brother, don't worry. There just happened to be a guru staying at my house and he lives in the yard.”
The old lama was woken up by the merchant's wife and invited to see a doctor for the merchant. He spent half a day detailed and said, “Boss, you've been fooled! I just slept in your yard, and suddenly saw a dark cloud flying in the sky. The dark cloud was as big as goat skin in the corner of the door; then, another hail fell, and the hail was as big as the gnocchi in the furnace ash; at the same time, heavy rain fell, and the rain was as much as alcohol poured into the gutter. I also saw a dark headed devil burrowing into that big wooden box.”
After hearing these words, the businessman's wife was really afraid. The merchant saw a piece of black goat skin in the corner of the door, and found a bunch of gnocchi in the furnace ash. In the gutter, he found that barley wine that had just been thrown out was still flowing. Finally, holding a large wooden stick, knocked a few knocks on the big wooden box, and shouted, “Come out, devil!” After a while, the lid slowly opened, and a man's head poked out of it. The businessman was so angry that he had to fight when he raised his stick, but he was stopped by the old lama.
The old lama said, “Boss, don't do anything, let's figure things out!” The businessman's wife cried and said, “You're running a business outside. I'm very quiet at home, so I secretly deal with my neighbors. These are all my faults.” The man also said, “I'm your neighbor. Not only can I not take care of your household chores, but I also do such humiliating things. I really don't see anyone. From now on, let alone playing drums, I don't even dare to touch drumsticks.” Under the advice of the old lama, the businessman forgave his wife and neighbors. The two were very grateful to the lama and went back and forth, saying that they had changed their past mistakes.
Narration: Duoji, the Pearl of the Xiangyang Commune, Chengguan District, Lhasa
