 Once upon a time, an old man named Yang Luo lived in a Miaojia village in Yanlou Township today. He has two girls, the oldest named Alec and the younger Algo. When the two girls were still very young, their mother died. The old man from Yangluo worked so hard to pull the two girls up, and they became the two most beautiful girls in the village. In particular, the eldest daughter Alec looks like a fairy from all over the world. Everyone praises her as the golden phoenix in Miaozhai. The old man from Yangluo loves the two girls, and he also loves the cucumbers he grew himself. The garden at the front of his house is full of big, fresh cucumbers every year. The cucumber sprinkled with water on the shelf makes people drool when they see it. However, a strange thing happened that year. The entire cucumber garden only grew one seedling, bloomed two flowers, and produced two melons. The old man didn't give up either. He carefully cared for these two melons, just like two girls. The two melons also grew larger, several times larger than a normal cucumber. One day, the old man from Yangluo had a dream. A white-bearded fairy said to Yang Luo in her dream, “Two cucumbers are treasures. You can't pick them until 981 day. You can't pick one more day, or one less day. The cucumber I picked when the time came was just two golden keys. If you use it to go to Nanshan and open the stone gate, you can get the Tiger Slayer and Dragon Slayer Sword. When Alec and Al Gou get married, they can use swords and swords as companions and give them to their lover. Using these two treasures, they can protect the Miao family's peaceful life. However, the cucumbers weren't ripe, so the time didn't come. You can't say this to anyone, not even your two girls.” After the old man from Yangluo had his dream, he cherished these two big cucumbers even more. During the day, they set up a shed and slept next to the big cucumbers. After a day, a knife stamp was engraved on the pillar in the melon shed. I can see that 77 knife marks have already been engraved.
Early this morning, a group of people came to Yilaozhai and wanted to invite Lao Yang Luo to set up a festival. The old man Yang Luo is a famous enthusiastic person near and far. He is highly respected. Everyone must invite him to be the chief priest. Yang Luo really couldn't get away with it; he had to agree. On his way out, the old man went back and forth and told the sisters Alec and Allego. They must take good care of the two large cucumbers in the garden to prevent others from stealing them and stopping sparrows from pecking them. Allegao said, “Dad, don't worry about doing business. My sister and I moved a chair to the melon stand to embroider. Don't let anyone walk close.” The old man Yang Luo nodded and left with peace of mind.
The old man is gone. The two sisters actually each carried a chair to the side of the melon stand while embroidering while watching out for people and sparrows. We ate noon ② next to each other. The two sisters were very sweaty in the hot sun. The two sisters looked up and saw two large cucumbers dripping with water and were moved. Alec said, “Dad is really strange. It's not unusual to have a full garden of cucumbers in previous years. Big baskets ask for gifts. This year, these two old cucumbers are just that expensive; they keep their ground late during the day.”
Algo said, “I'm afraid my dad wants to keep the seeds; I'm afraid the melon seeds will be cut off.”
Alec said, “No, there are so many varieties of melons. Dad kept them in a bamboo basket upstairs.”
Allegao said, “This is strange. It's cold outside later, and my dad stays in a melon shed. If he gets sick, he'll be unlucky.”
Alec thought about it and smiled and said, “Just eat the melons. We both ate the melons so that our dad wouldn't have to worry about these two melons every day. Can you say OK?”
Allegao said, “My dad likes these two melons so much ③. If he doesn't pour melons when he comes back to watch, he's afraid he'll be scolded?”
Alec said, “We are Dad's heart and soul. How can he bear to give us? How unusual are the two cucumbers; it's not worth nagging for a few words.” As a result, the two sisters picked two cucumbers and ate them.
Before the sun had set, the old people from Yangluo rushed home. Before they had to enter the house, the old man went to the garden to take a look. After going back and forth, he saw only melon leaves but no cucumbers. The old man was angry and rushed to the door and shouted, “Where did the cucumbers in the garden go?!” The sound sounded like a thunder, which shocked the two girls. They secretly came over and took a look. Seeing that Dad's face was blue and his mouth was crooked, they didn't dare say it if they wanted to. Lao Yang Luo rushed to Alek and held his daughter and said, “Are you deaf? Dumb? I asked where the cucumbers had gone!”
The two sisters had never seen their dad get so angry; they just shook their heads.
“Did you steal it?” The old man from Yangluo asked.
The two sisters still couldn't stop shaking their heads, and tears welled down.
The old man from Yang Luo shouted “ah” and walked to the garden, motionless, and stood for a long, long time. The two girls hid in the house and didn't dare to get out of the atmosphere.
The moon rose from behind the slope. The old man Yang Luo raised his hands to the falling moon and muttered a spell in his mouth: “Whoever steals my cucumber will let the tiger eat him...”
When Alec and Allego heard their dad cast a spell on the moon, they were so frightened that they quickly knelt down in front of the old man Yangluo and cried: “Dad! Don't ask tigers to eat us; we won't dare to steal cucumbers again. Dad, you have to save us!”
The old man from Yangluo was taken aback and couldn't regret it. He also cried, “What should I do? My spells have all gone out. If I spit on a stone, the stone will blossom; if I spit on a dead branch, the dead branch will sprout. People who have passed through the gate have to go back; if they get out, they won't be able to take it back!”
The old man from Yang Luo couldn't sleep until dawn. As soon as the sky dawned, he came up with a solution. He built a wooden house in the middle of a large pond not far from his home and moved the two girls to live in the wooden house. There is deep water on all sides of the wooden house, and tigers can't get through. He also built a suspension bridge in Tangkou. He usually hangs the suspension bridge; no one can get through it. The wooden house is beautifully built. Several corners of the house are raised high, just like swallow tails. Therefore, people call this wooden house Yan Lou.
Alec has a lover named Nord Zhong, a brave and beautiful young man. He is on good terms with Alec. Yang Luo's dad doesn't know; the two just met secretly. After Alec moved into a wooden house in the pond to live, Norderjung also often met her. However, the two always met at a later date. They agreed on a code. When Nord arrived, he was wearing cleats and lighting a lantern. If you can't stop hearing the sound and what you see are two lanterns, don't put up a suspension bridge; that is, the tiger is here.
Did you know the tiger had quietly heard the code for their date. The tiger put a pair of bamboo tubes on its claws and went to the edge of the pond with another blindfolded eye. When a tiger holds a bamboo tube on its foot, it makes a “click click” sound when walking, just like a nail shoe.
Alec sat alone on the wooden floor and missed Nord Zhong. There was no moon in the sky, and there were no lights on the ground. She was about to get up. Suddenly, she saw a lantern coming up on the other side. Hearing it again, the “click click” footsteps were getting closer and closer. Alec was overjoyed, so he quickly untied the rope, put down the suspension bridge and greeted him. By the time she discovered it wasn't her Nordjong, it was too late, and the towering monster bit Alec's throat. Just like that, the tiger harmed Alec and carried her away.
Norderjung arrived at the side of the pond. It was dark everywhere, and the suspension bridge had already been lowered. He knew in his heart what was important or bad, so he hurriedly ran across the bridge. As soon as I crossed the bridge, I fell down, and my nails and scabbards came off. My hands were sticky. At first glance, it was blood. He stormed into the house and saw Algo falling on his bed, and he couldn't wake up even though he shouted. Norderjung pointed at the blood stain with the tip of a knife and said, “It's Alec's blood, and it just flows down the sword.” Before I had to say anything, blood just flowed down the sword. Nord Zhong was deeply saddened and screamed like crazy, following the trail of blood and chased him up the mountain.
Norderjung followed the mountain beam and saw that there were two green lights in the bottom of the mountain, flashing one by one. He knew it was a tiger. Nordjung yelled like a thunder in the middle of the air. He picked up a steel knife and slashed it at the tiger. Tigers are ferocious, but they can't resist Norderjung's magic power. Not much, Nordjung killed the tiger and cut it to pieces. He exhausted all his strength and fell down beside Alec, and had no strength to even cry.
As soon as the sky turned white, Norderjung buried Alec. He sat motionless by Alec's grave and sat for a long time. His hair was messy like a thatched roof. He had a dragon nest ④, and his clothes were torn like rotten leaves; he looked like a rock. Suddenly, Norderjung remembered that there will still be tigers in the mountains, and Alec's younger sister, Alleko, will still be in danger. He immediately stood up and walked deep into the mountains to search for tigers again and kill them all. Furthermore, the old man from Yangluo went to see his daughter in Yanlou the next day and found blood stains on the side of the suspension bridge. It was like falling into a bottomless cave. He ran into the building to take a look. Alec didn't know where he was going. Allegao was so sick that he couldn't wake up even when screaming.
Where did Alec go? The old man returned to the side of the suspension bridge and found another empty scabbard and a pair of nails. The old man thought, was Alec eaten by a tiger, or was he harmed? The old people of Yangluo invited many townspeople to help them search everywhere. Finally, they discovered the dead bodies of tigers cut to pieces in a remote mountain, and there was also a bunch of new graves next to it. Everyone dug up the dirt and took a look. It was Alec's dead body. The old man from Yangluo cried bitterly and took his daughter's bones back to the stockade and buried them again. The old man thought that there must be a hero. Seeing Alec being eaten by a tiger, he went after the tiger. Who is this hero? I looked around and couldn't find it.
Since Alek, the most beautiful girl in the mountains, died, the sky seemed to be very sad. The dark clouds in the sky were getting thicker and thicker, and the sky and earth were dull and foggy; the crops in the fields were about to die, and everyone was hungry, so everyone gathered at the old house in Yangluo to find a way. The townspeople believe that only by sacrificing heaven and asking for the sun to shine on the earth can crops be brought back to life, and people will not starve to death.
As a result, villages and villages all selected the best singers and Lu Sheng hand and held a grand ceremony to worship the gods. Everyone danced, played Lu Sheng, and sang carols to ask for God's blessings. The old people in Yangluo were looking everywhere in the crowd, holding scabbards and nail shoes, hoping to find the hero who killed the tiger. However, none of the knives can throw on the scabbard, and not a single pair of feet can close the shoes.
Furthermore, Norderjung searched for tigers everywhere in the deep mountains and old forests. After going over 77 mountains and crossing 77 rivers, he couldn't find any trace of the tiger. He was exhausted and fell asleep in a cave. He slept for seven seven seven forty-nine days. In his sleep, Norderjung saw an old white-bearded man saying to him, “Hurry up, go save Miao's hometown. The Miao family is in distress.” The white-bearded old man also handed down a spell to him, asking him to go to Nanshan to remove the magic weapon in order to save the Miao family's loved ones.
Nord Zhong woke up to find that his body had been washed clean, his hair had been brushed, dark and bright, he was in great spirits, and he seemed to have inexhaustible energy all over his body. Following the instructions of the old man in his dream, he ran to the edge of a large rock wall under Nanshan Mountain and patted the smooth and hard rock with his hand and said, “Open the stone gate, open the stone gate, give me my clothes!” After shouting three times in a row, the big rock “thundered”, and with a loud noise, a large open stone gate appeared. Norderjung walked in and saw a brand-new set of clothes on the stone table in the cave, and another Lu Sheng hanging on the wall shining brightly. Norderjung put his costume on and it fit very well; he also removed Lu Sheng from the stone wall, walked out of the cave, and walked towards the stockade.
Those who jumped on the field in the village have been dancing for seven days and seven nights, and the sky still hasn't brightened up. People are becoming more and more anxious. Men, women, and children kneel down to pray for the sun to rise from the sky.
Suddenly, a clear sound of Lu Sheng came from the top of the mountain in the distance. The sound reached people's ears. The spirit was immediately lifted, and it felt unspeakably comfortable. At this point, a big bright hole opened out of the dark clouds, and a ray of sunlight shone from the sky and shone on the body of a young Miao family shining all over his body at the top of the mountain. He was blowing Lu Sheng while dancing Lu Sheng dance.
“Nord Zhong!” Many people shouted out the name of the young man at the top of the mountain. Everyone cheered and ran to him. At this point, the sky is getting brighter. The dark clouds soon dissipated following Norderjung's magical Lu Sheng tunes. The sky was full of golden sunshine. Little birds that haven't sung for a long time flew out of the woods in groups, circled over the heads of Nordjom and the townspeople, and changed their voices.
The old man Yang Luo walked in front of Norderjung with a sword scabbard and nails, and asked him to try it out. Of course, just try it. As a result, the elder Yang Luo assigned his second daughter, Al Gaoxu, to Nord Zhong. The townspeople organized a lively wedding for the two of them. The boys also sang songs for seven days and seven nights, danced for seven days and seven nights, and sang Lu Sheng for seven days and seven nights. This time, no one felt tired, and some heirs even jumped through the soles of their shoes.
From this point on, the Miao family had to blow Lu Sheng and sing and dance, whether it was a holiday or a wedding, or traveling and rocking the horse. In particular, every year on the 9th day of January, Miao families around Huaxi gather at Huaxi Tongmuling to hold a grand celebration for several days, and they always have to sing a Lu Sheng song. This Lu Sheng song is called “Nord Zhong.”
