Officials wanted to attack them at any time. Due to the high road insurance in Mount Geba, the government had no way to get in. In this way, the Miao people of Geba settled down.
Year after year, generation after generation, the Miao people in the Geba region soon became nine villages and eighteen villages.
One year, in a Miao family in 18 villages in Jiucun, there was a young daughter-in-law who was pregnant with twins. She was pregnant for ten years, and no dolls were born. The two dolls are in the mother's womb, can eat, talk to their parents, and set up Dragon Gate ③.
Their parents didn't see the birth of their two babies. They were very anxious. Every day, they asked God to honor their ancestors, and hoped that the two babies would be born soon.
One day, their father went uphill to work. Their mother had two babies in her arms and said, “Baby! You guys should come up with a quick idea. Mom will be very sad if it continues like this!”
The two babies answered in unison from their mother's bellies, “Mom, don't worry about your old man; we haven't even been born yet! On the day we're born, we'll be out.” While talking, the two babies squatted and reached out on their mother's stomach, making a “thump” sound in their mother's belly.
Mom was in so much pain that she angrily said to the two children, “Are you humans or ghosts!? You won't be born soon, so why will your mom give birth to you in the future?”
“Mom, we're human, not ghosts. Don't worry about your old man; when the time comes, we'll leave our house.” To put it bluntly, he reached over and kicked again.
Mom asked the two babies again and said, “Baby! Are you guys making a name like that again? Don't kick it! But after a few days, they stuck their legs and kicked Mom in the stomach again.
Mom got angry again and asked the two babies, “What are you guys doing again? The kick made my stomach ache!”
The two babies answered loudly in their mother's stomach, “We're training our hands and feet! Mom, let's put up with it, the old man. Let's practice and we won't practice.”
Mom endured the pain and hugged the two babies and said, “Baby! You guys should be born soon, it's so much fun outside!”
The two children said in unison, “Mom, don't worry about your old man; we will come out the day you are born.”
Another year passed in a blink of an eye. Mom said to them again, “Baby! You've been in your mother's belly for 11 years, and it's time to be born. Come out soon! 'At the end of the house*, your dad worked all by himself, so hard work!”

She was happy and anxious, and asked the two babies, “What would you like to eat? Good doll.”
The two babies were happy to hear it and said to their mom, “Mom, we love to eat raw bamboo shoots! Please tell Dad to pick more bamboo shoots and eat them for us.”
When it was dark, the baby's father came back from work on the slope. Mom said to the father, “The two babies want you to pick more bamboo shoots to eat for them.” Dad listened, patted his wife's big stomach, and said to the two babies, “Do you like to eat bamboo shoots? From now on, Dad will pick bamboo shoots for you to eat every day.”
When the two children heard their father's words, they were happy to say, “Thank you, Dad.” After speaking, I fell asleep peacefully.
From winter to spring, the moon is missing and round, round and lacking. A year has passed. By the time they turned 15, the two dolls said to their mother in unison, “Mom, we're going to be born!”
My mom heard that the two dolls were about to be born. She was also anxious and happy, and hurriedly asked, “How did you come out?”

On the day the two dolls were born, the two big bamboo sticks in their mouths also “clicked” and two little ponies jumped out of them. They grow when they see the wind; when they see the light, they grow into two tall, fat horses. In the dam in the courtyard at the entrance, they “boo boo” their noses, holding their heads up and barking on the floor of the house where the doll was born. The two young Miao boys, who had just landed on the ground, heard a horse barking and ran outside the door, and each jumped on a horse to train their feet. As soon as the two dolls were born, the two horses ran and screamed. The Imperial Palace, which was shaken thousands of miles away, was also unstable. The tiles on the palace “made a noise” and fell down, frightening the maiden to keep screaming. As soon as the emperor arrived, he hurriedly ordered the prime minister to take out the mirror. The prime minister put down his mirror and quietly said to the emperor, these two bare-ass teenagers are training soldiers and horses. Looks like they are about to attack Gesang's military camp. What happened to Gesang? The faces of the 100 military officials were so frightened that they all changed color one by one. Look at me, I look at you; I don't know what to do. They want to attack Gesang and ask the emperor to quickly send a leader, send additional elite soldiers, and rush to Gesang to defend.
When the emperor read the secret letter, he was secretly surprised. A few days later, they arrived at the Gesang military camp, and the general immediately sent troops to attack the Geba Miao people.
After the Miao family learned the news that Gesang officers and soldiers had arrived to attack Geba, they immediately turned over and got on the horse, each standing at the top of a hill.
Using horse-whip, they beat bamboo all over the mountains. A forest of bamboo immediately “crackled”.
