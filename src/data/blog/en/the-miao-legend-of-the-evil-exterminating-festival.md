 Every year on July 13 of the lunar calendar, the Miao family in the two counties of Huangping and Shibing in Guizhou celebrate the “Eradication Festival” as usual. Why is this holiday celebrated?
Legend has it that once upon a time there was a demon king. He was very tall and very powerful. The most abominable thing is that he only steals people's cows and horses, causing people to have no fields to cultivate, no goods, and no horses. Everyone is cursing: “If someone kills this devil, that would be great.” While people were looking forward to it, a little girl named Ani said, “I can get rid of demons.”
When people heard it, they were amazed: “How can you kill demons when you're so young?”
Ani said, “If you find ten baskets of broken iron, please go to the smartest ironsmith, and I will have a way to get rid of the devil.”
People were very happy to listen to it and found these things right away. Annie immediately asked the ironsmiths to cast the broken pot into three types of iron balls. One was as big as a hoard, one was as big as a water tank, and one was as big as a can.
Annie also asked everyone to make a pair of sandals the size of a ferry and place them on a large iron ball. Finally, she pulled a cow next to her sandals to graze.
One day, the devil saw a cow and was so upset that he immediately ran over and wanted to eat the cow. Who knows, when I took a look nearby, I found three iron balls neatly placed on the ground. Seeing a little girl sitting on the ground, she asked loudly, “Whose iron ball is this?”
Annie answered, “It's my dad's.”
“Why is your dad playing iron ball?”
“Let's play!”
“Can your dad play with these things?”
Ani pointed to the sandals and said, “What do you think this is? Are you saying my dad is strong or not? Can he play with these iron balls?”
The devil also asked, “How can your dad play with these iron balls?”
Annie said, “It's as easy for my dad to play with these iron balls as eggs and ducks. He threw a small iron ball into the sky and held it with his thumb; he threw the iron ball to the sky and held it with one of his knees; he threw the big iron ball to the sky and held it with one head. Can you do that?”
The devil was not convinced and said, “What's so amazing about your dad? Look at me.” Then, as Ani said, he threw the small iron ball to the sky and put his thumb on it. Unfortunately, he didn't hold it; instead, blood was thrown out. Ani smiled and said, “Haha, you didn't catch it.” The devil wasn't convinced, and threw the iron ball to the sky again. He hit the top with his knee. He didn't hold it, and he lost a bone in his knee. Ani smiled and said, “Haha, you hurt your knee.”
At this point, the devil was so angry that he couldn't say anything. He grabbed the biggest iron ball and threw it up. Only when the tree was so tall that it fell. He hurriedly used his brain shell to reach the top. He knew it, and with a snap, his brain blossomed.
People saw that the devil had died, and they all ran around Ani, praising that she was a brave girl. This day is July 13 of the lunar calendar. To commemorate the ingenuity and bravery of this little girl, this day was designated as “Eradicate Evil Day.”
