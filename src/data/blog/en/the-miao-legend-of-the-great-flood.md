 In ancient times, there were two elderly people who raised two sons and a girl. It didn't take long for the two elderly people to die one after another, leaving behind three children. The two sons often gamble with heaven. When the weather was very cold and raining, they said it was too hot, so they used fans to fan up the mountain; when the weather was very hot, they had to go to the riverside to burn a big fire again. After seven years of drying, I finished eating the grass roots. After seven years of cold weather, cows, horses, and livestock were all slaughtered, then floods flooded the entire land. The boss and the elder made two drums, ready to let them flutter on them. The boss only made an iron drum that could sit alone, and the second one made a big wooden drum. He called for his sister to sit inside with him.
The floods got higher and higher, and the boss's drum disappeared for a while. The wooden drum that the old man and his sister were sitting on got higher and higher as the water level rose until the sky was above the sky, making the sound of winter and winter. When Grandpa Tien heard the sound, he sent a soldier from the sky to inspect it. At this point, he knew that the flood had already filled the sky. As a result, he commanded God to release dragons and pigs to the ground and arch out many large grooves, cavities, and holes to allow water to escape. The older brother and younger sister's wooden drums also fell to the ground. All the people on the ground died out, leaving only their brother and sister.
Under these circumstances, the elder brother said to his younger sister, “Little sister, everyone on the ground is dead. It seems like only the two of us are married.” My younger sister said, “It's OK to become a family; I'm afraid heaven and earth will not allow it.” The second brother said, “God will agree. There are no more people in the world, what can be done.” My sister said, “Since this is the case, let's try it with the grinding disc in front of us. You roll down the opposite mountain with the one on your back. I rolled down this mountain with the one underneath on my back. “If you put your heart in the middle of grinding your eyes, you become a family.” The older brother rolled down the opposite mountain with the upper one on his back, and the younger sister rolled down this mountain with the lower one on her back. As a result, the heartache just hit her eyes. The younger sister was still uneasy, and she said to her brother, “I threw it off this side with a needle. If you throw it over the needle, you can get married.” One of them was holding a needle and the other was holding a thread, and they each threw it off the side. As soon as they looked for it, the thread was put on the needle's eye, and they became parents.
After the brother and sister became married, they gave birth to three sons in three years and couldn't talk. I really wanted to know what to do. One night, my little sister met a Tenjin in her sleep. Tenjin told him the situation. Tenjin told him that the two of you picked up a large stone to burn hot wood, and if it were to burn hot, you just brought the three dolls to burn their butts, depending on the reason.
The next day, the younger sister told her brother about her dream, and the two did the same. After burning the big stone, they first picked up the big one and shouted “hey” as soon as the butt fell on the stone. The brother and sister only knew it was Miao when they heard the shouting; then they hugged the second one, and as soon as their butt fell on the stone, they shouted “hey hey”. The brother and sister heard the shouting and knew it was Yi; finally, they hugged the third one and shouted “hey hey,” and the brother and sister only knew that this was Han nationality. In the fourth year, they gave birth to another thing that didn't look like a human; it had no feet and no head; it was a paw. It rolls back and forth when placed on the ground. One night, my little sister had a dream where she saw Tenjin again and taught them how to do it. Early the next morning, the old man found a large knife to sharpen the left and right on the grinding stone. The knife sharpened very sharp, so he chopped the big meat ball into a large pot, and the two scattered it all over the mountains. The name that falls on the peach tree is (Tao), the surname that falls on the plum tree is (Li), the last name that falls on anything, and the last thing is surname (Wang). After two days, there were people everywhere they scattered, houses were everywhere, and they were cooking and eating on a fire.
Since then, the numbers have slowly grown.
