 During the Republic of China, there were three “military gate gardens” in Peony Township in Caozhou. One is in Wangli Zhuang, one is in Nanyang Zhuang, and the other is in Zhao Lou. Although the “Military Gate Garden” in Wanglizhuang is small in size and has a full range of colors, it retains many rare varieties, such as palm flowers, pear blossoms, snow, etc. Nanyangzhuang's “Military Gate Garden” is eight acres long. The plants are all big red, and are sold exclusively for “Xia Guang”. The “Junmen Garden” in the north of Zhaolou Village covers an area of nine acres, with peonies, plum, peony, magnolia, etc., with a full range of colors. There are five flower halls in the park. It is a small villa. An old flower farmer named Zhao Guangming cultivated a flower tree, which is more than two meters tall and has more than 400 flowers. One hawthorn tree in the garden is over 5 meters tall. 4,500 kg of hawthorn are harvested every year. Who are the owners of these “military gardens”? This is Zhang Peirong, Han Fu Yi's close friend in Chaozhou Province.
Zhang Peirong, a native of Yizhou, Shandong, worked as a “military officer” (military attendant) in Caozhou when Han Fuyi became the chairman of Shandong Province. This person is good at making a big fuss, so many people give out the “big splash” sign.
Zhang Dapeng looked at Caozhou's large and small gardens. The black donkey was also followed by a few bodyguards armed with short guns and swords. Wherever they went, peonies suffered. When they saw which tree was growing well and was brightly colored, they pulled it out and left. Flower farmers often dare to get angry and dare not speak up if they dare to speak up. As such, Zhang Peirong was not satisfied. Later, when he saw which garden was good, he put up the “Military Gate Garden” brand. Take the garden as your own and claim to be the owner of the garden.
