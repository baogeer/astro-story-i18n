He has been working for the Wanlixing Construction Team for almost a year, and apart from the food expenses, he hasn't received a single cent of his wages. What worries Liu Sanfu even more is that now that the new year is coming soon, he promised his girlfriend Xiaomei early that she would give her a watch as a birthday present for her on her birthday during the new year. Yes, they've been in a relationship for over two years, and Liu Sanfu hasn't given Xiaomei a decent present yet. Liu Sanfu made up his mind that this gift would be redeemed for Xiaomei no matter what, but now... Liu Sanfu couldn't help but be filled with anger and went to the foreman to bargain with several of his colleagues. But like the previous few times, there were no results.
“Grandma's, the contract payment has already been paid. Isn't this clearly due to our salary arrears?” Worker Lai Xiaoqiang angrily scolded, “If you don't give him some color, I still think our country people are being bullied.” Lai Xiaoqiang said he wanted to blackmail the foreman to pay his work. Liu Sanfu also patted his chest when he heard it and said, “This is a great idea. Let's go dancing together tomorrow, so I don't believe the foreman really dares to take human life for nothing?”
However, Liu Sanfu didn't expect that Lai Xiaoqiang would go alone first, and before he went, he drank alcohol and was drunk. He climbed to the roof before being discovered, so he went downstairs and died one by one. I lost my life in vain because it was a drunken accident. The death of a colleague made Liu Sanfu see a little clearly. Jumping from a building to blackmail this path is impassable, and if you don't get it right, it will cost your life.
On this day, Liu Sanfu wandered around aimlessly alone. Seeing watch stores on the street, he also remembered his promise to his girlfriend Xiaomei. He couldn't help but walk into the store, look at a shiny and exquisite watch through the glass cabinet, touch the only five cents in his pocket over and over again, and refused to leave for a long time.
It was almost noon, when Liu Sanfu lost his way out of the watch store and walked down the street. Having just turned a street, Liu Sanfu's eyes suddenly lit up. He saw a post office in front of him. There were also many people carrying cards in the mail box at the entrance of the post office. Liu Sanfu knows those people are sending New Year's cards. “I can't afford a watch myself now, so it would be nice to buy a greeting card for Xiaomei.”
Liu Sanfu immediately felt refreshed. He went to the post office and asked if a greeting card was two cents. Liu Sanfu picked back and forth. He finally picked the one with a red heart, paid the money, and took the pen from the counter. First, he neatly wrote the four words “Chen Xiaomei received” on the greeting card. Then write the eight words “Happy Birthday and Happy New Year” in the space next to it. After thinking about it, Liu Sanfu felt that there was still something missing. By the way, I haven't been in touch with Xiaomei in a few months, so feel free to write a few words here to express my feelings for her.
After all, I was writing heartfelt words for my girlfriend, so Liu Sanfu found a corner and hid there and wrote word for word: Xiaomei, how are you? Brother Abao is sorry for you. He said he bought a watch for you this time, but the wages left the foreman in arrears. When I get my salary, he will buy you a nice watch and bring it back. “Don't be mad at Brother Abao, OK?”
There aren't many blank spaces on the greeting card. Liu Sanfu was only able to simply write these few sentences. After writing, he carefully meditated two or three more times. I was about to sign my name, and suddenly someone knocked on the table and yelled, “Hey, how did you do that? After taking the pen and writing for so long, nobody else wanted to write?” As soon as Liu Sanfu heard it, right. Liu Sanfu read the message he left to his girlfriend on the greeting card twice, and suddenly regretted it again. If the card was blank, anyone could see the text on the greeting card. She revealed her personal feelings about the two of them here. Maybe Xiaomei would get angry when she read it. It's impossible to buy another greeting card. After thinking about it, Liu Sanfu went looking for another small piece of paper and glued it to it, just enough to cover up the text part. After doing this, Liu Sanfu was at last relieved. He carefully threw the greeting card into the mailbox. Only then did he leave the post office, go to the street, and use the remaining three cents to buy a steamed bun for lunch.
A few unsuccessful salary claims already aroused Liu Sanfu's anger. Coupled with the death of his colleague Lai Xiaoqiang, Liu Sanfu and his colleagues finally became angry. On this day, a workmate finally heard the news that the contractor would go home for his son's birthday the first night. Liu Sanfu and they made up their mind and decided to do it tonight. Others were also afraid, and the violent wage-collection action plan came to an end halfway. Liu Sanfu followed the police to an office building uneasy.
