 On the banks of the Chaolu River in Has, there is a herding girl. This girl sings songs like Huang Peng and looks like a lily. Anyone on the way has to stop if they see her; the young “enemy” herders don't want to go to other ranches to graze if they see her. People call her a lily.
There are three members in Lily's family: Grandpa Bao Yin Tu, Grandma loves root flowers, and they live for the rest of their lives
Just give birth to a girl like this. Their family lives a very poor life. The tent is too dilapidated to cover the wind and rain, and “Hana” is so dilapidated that it is about to break down. Other than a cow and an old mule and horse, the family had nothing else. This old horse's stomach is always quite big. As soon as the herder saw Baoyintu, he said, “Hey! Baoyin Tuaza, Huang Piao Ma is about to step down.” Everyone who saw it said this, but I never saw it give birth to a foal.
Many more days have passed. Early one morning, the old mule and horse in Baoyintu finally gave birth to a little pony. The yellow mane and yellow coat looked like a golden pony. I can't make myself happy with a Bao Yin Tu; lily is happy to jump straight, and Aigen flowers make me happy with the wrinkles on my face. It's all unfolded. They used ripped sheepskin robes to carry the little pony into the tent. However, the old mule and horse died after giving birth to the foal. The family was so distressed that they had to take care of it sadly.
The longer the little yellow horse, the more beautiful it is. As soon as the sun comes out during the day, the yellow mane and yellow fur sparkle with gold; as soon as the moon comes out at night, the yellow mane and yellow fur flash silver; when you run for the road, you can simply beat the meteors in the sky. When the Little Yellow Mule Horse was one year old, Lily Qige rode it to the Haas Chaolu River to drink water; when the Little Yellow Mule Horse was two years old, Lily Qige rode it to graze; when the Little Yellow Mule Horse was three years old, Lily Qige rode it to horse races with male herders, winning first place every time. Lily Qige is very fond of Little Yellow Piao Ma, and Little Yellow Piao Ma always loves to turn around in front of and behind Lily Qige's body.
One night, there wasn't a single star in the sky; it was dark like a dark cowhide. The northwest wind blared at Baomen, and the mountains sounded “drumming,” and I heard hungry wolves screaming nearby, frightening Aigenhua for a night without even daring to catch his eye. When it was almost bright, the northwest wind blew even harder. Baomen made a few “drumming, drumming,” and then “when” the door was kicked open, only to see Grandpa Wang's Baidai rushing in with Fu Ding. Baidai crossed his waist in one hand, held Grandpa Wang's black whip in the other hand, glared at a pair of calves and shouted, “It's been two days since Grandpa Wang paid the head tax, so why haven't they sent it! If you don't pay for five sheep, you have to take the lead!”
As soon as Aigenhua heard that she wanted to take the lead, “Ah-tong” knelt down and begged Baidai, “Grandpa, grandpa! Save us! There aren't even half of the croissants in the family. How about getting five sheep to pay Grandpa Wang's head tax!” While talking, the old lady tore off Baidai's coat. Baidai raised her hand with two whips, then “tented” her feet and knocked Aigenhua to the ground. Baoyintu wanted to collect Aigenhua. Baidai's leather whip fell on his head and body like a drop of rain. Soon, both of them were beaten and passed out. At this point, as soon as Baidai waved, more than a dozen people came up and took the lily out in a bundle. When the older couple woke up and took a look, their daughter was gone; when they went outside to take a look, the yellow horse in the stable was also taken! The sika deer is the heart and soul of a group of deer; lily is a father. Mom's heart and soul. The old couple looked at the empty tent and quiet stable, and their tears turned into a river.
Lily was robbed into the royal palace and became Grandpa Wang's dancer. She misses her old grandpa and grandma all day long, 'and her beloved little yellow piaoma, how dare she practice dancing. As a result, she was beaten and scolded every day, leaving her suffering with new wounds and old scars. She couldn't eat during the day, couldn't sleep at night, and lost weight day by day.
One day, Prince Zas, came to discuss the exchange of herders and slaves, and the prince hosted a big reception. Grilled meat, horse milk wine, mountain and seafood were placed on the jade table... The two kings were seated face to face with marmalade beads on their bodies. Kobu Jin personally poured wine for Prince Zasto. Knowing that she had humiliated Grandpa Wang, Merlin hurriedly paid him a gift. While viciously leaving the lily out of the hall, she beat Lily to death. There was no good place all over her body, and then locked her in a cowshed.
The lily lay in its own position for three days and three nights without growing a grain of rice. On the night of the fourth day, she fell asleep. As soon as she fell asleep, she had a dream that Yellow Hussars had rescued her. Lilies woke up as soon as they enjoyed it. She opened her eyes and saw that Huang Piao Ma was actually standing in front of her and gently licked her wound with her tongue. Lily had tears streaming out of her eyes. She held the yellow horse by the neck and even called its name in her mouth. The yellow mule horse woke up and opened her mouth and said to her, “Quick, quick: I'm here to save you. You don't need to take anything; just pick up your flute, wooden comb, and mirror.” Lily couldn't help but ask for the truth; she picked up the flute, wooden comb, and mirror as it said. All of a sudden, the yellow mule horse carried her and ran away.
Grandpa Wang was sitting idle in the main hall. He overheard Tieqing Horses using their hooves to plow dirt in the area, biting and screaming. Merlin took Fu Ding and chased Lily in the direction where she ran. Huang Piao Ma ran in front, and Fu Ding chased closely behind. Poison arrows were scattered around the left and right sides of the yellow mule horse. Lily's body was scaly and weak, and the yellow mule horse was afraid to raise its hooves and run; Wang Yefu Ding shouted and killed, and the chase got closer. Lily was so frightened that she leaped straight into her heart. She lay down tightly on the back of the horse and said to Huang Piao Ma, “Huang Piao Ma! Try to save your master to escape!”
The yellow horse nodded. At this point, seeing Fu Ding as soon as he was in front of him, the yellow mule horse screamed for a long time and said to Lily, “Hurry, fast, fast! My master, please put the flute in my mouth!” Lily once reached over and took the flute out of her robe. As soon as Huang Piao Ma turned around, she bit the flute with her mouth. With a “whistling” sound, she threw it outward. A large pond immediately appeared in the back. As soon as Huang Piao Ma turned around, she bit the wooden comb with her mouth and “popped” and threw it out. As soon as the wooden brush fell to the ground, it immediately became a large forest. The running horses were able to stop their four hooves right away. Some of the Chasing Fu Ding hit a big tree and even fell down with the horse. The people led by Merlin looked like they were about to catch up with Lily again. At this point, the yellow mule horse made three sounds and said, “Hurry, fast, fast! The water was too deep to see the bottom of the lake. The fuddlers, who were running away on horseback, couldn't hold the horse's reins; all those that “popped through” fell into the lake. Merlin was floated by the crowd, and he made a “grunt” with his mouth open; he “grunted” when he opened his mouth, and sank to the bottom of the lake without making a single sound.
Since then, the lake has gotten deeper and deeper. Every time Grandpa Wang wanted to pass by, the lake would boil, just like the tide in the sea, so frightened that Grandpa Wang wouldn't dare to act recklessly. Lily Qigge has returned to her home and has been living a stable life with her father, grandmother, and her beloved Huang Piao Ma.
