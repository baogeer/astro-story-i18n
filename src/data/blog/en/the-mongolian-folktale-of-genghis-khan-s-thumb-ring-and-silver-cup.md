 Legend has it that Genghis Khan had two beloved treasures. One is a board finger made of ivory, which is placed on the thumb of the right hand to use as a crochet string during archery. The other is an engraved silver wine glass, which I use every time I drink. Unexpectedly, these two treasures of Genghis Khan saved his life during a party with his self-determined brother Zamu. This led to a little-known legend.
After Genghis Khan rose up in the Hulunbuir prairie, his brother Zamuhe was very jealous and always wanted to harm him. Zamuhe secretly won over Genghis Khan's enemy. Irago, the great-uncle of the Tatars, invited Genghis Khan to Alatanbolag in the lower reaches of the Krulun River in the name of marrying Genghis Khan and poisoning him when he drank horse wine.
Genghis Khan discussed with the general public after receiving a letter from someone sent by Zamuhe. Everyone said that Genghis Khan's father was killed by the Tatars using poison alcohol. This “marriage alliance” was fake; it was real that they wanted to take the opportunity to kill Genghis Khan, so everyone objected to him going to the dinner.
Genghis Khan, who has a huge heart, has his own plans. He sat on the tiger chair in the big tent and said, “The cunning Zamuhe has teamed up several times and failed to attack me. If he wants to use fraud this time around, I'll have to see the opportunity and act. There used to be a saying: if you want to catch a tiger, you have to feed him; if you want to ride a dragon, you should risk your life.” He held out the ivory board finger on his right hand and gently tapped the engraved silver wine glass on the table. With a smile, he said, “They can't use force. They really want to poison me. With my finger and this silver wine glass, are they still afraid that they will poison the alcohol?” Genghis Khan arrived at Alatambolag as scheduled. As soon as he got off the horse, he saw Zamuhe take a glass of wine from Ilagu, and handed it to Genghis Khan with his hands in front of Genghis Khan and said, “Before, my little brother was ignorant of current affairs, blamed you, and made a big mess between my two families. Today, my little brother is hosting a feast here. I hope you don't remember your previous feud.” Genghis Khan took the glass with a very generous smile, and stuck his finger on the ivory plate in the wine. Who knew the snow-white ivory plate finger immediately changed color. Genghis Khan was shocked: “Alcohol is definitely toxic!” But he still looked very relaxed. With a clever move, he held the cup with his left hand, raised his right hand in the air, and gently bounced it in the sky with his middle finger, saying, “Thanks to God's blessings, you and I have formed an alliance. Respect God with this glass of wine!” His hand held the glass too high and threw it at the sky.
As soon as Zamu saw it, he was a little panicked, and quickly signaled to Ilagu to pour another cup. Ilagu handed the glass in front of Genghis Khan with trembling hands and stammered, “Sweat, please drink this glass and glass of wine to get rid of previous feuds, so that we can always be united and united.” Genghis Khan had seen through their plot for a long time, but if nothing had happened, he stuck his finger in the wine, and his finger turned dark. Genghis Khan looked at him coldly, raised his hand in the air, and softly said, “You and I are all originally sons of this earth. This glass of wine should be dedicated to the earth that gave birth to us and nurtured us.” While talking, spread it gently on the ground. Zamuhe and Ilagu really panicked at this point. You snatched the fern and poured the third cup, and stuck it in Genghis Khan's hand and said, “Heaven is honored, and so is the earth. Please drink this cup with great sweat!” Genghis Khan sneered and showed a dismissive look on his face and said, “Although we are blessed by heaven, we are nurtured by the earth. How can today's alliance go dark and forget that we are the same ancestors!” While talking, I gently dipped a drop of alcohol on Mu's finger. At this point, after soaking in alcohol three times, it had already turned black. He held back his anger and bounced heavily in the sky, saying, “Ancestors of the Heavenly Spirit! Open your eyes and see clearly! Don't blame the kids for being disrespectful if they don't have filial piety and take dirty wine!” After speaking, I looked north from afar and spilled alcohol hard. When he said it was done, Gis Khan smiled, secretly carried his finger in his arms, and said to those who followed him, “Bring me a glass. At this point, there was nothing they could do. I had to replace it with good wine and pour it into Genghis Khan's silver glass.
Genghis Khan escaped the dark calculation of Zamuhe and discovered the false reality of the Tatar Ministry. Later, he destroyed the feud of the Begging Ministry on the shores of Dalai Lake in one fell swoop.
