 As for the origin of the Mongolian people, the legend of the “Blue Wolf and White Deer” has been the most widely spread for a long time. In fact, this is nothing special about most ethnic groups believing in a totem mentality to identify themselves as belonging. Because wolves and deer live in mountains, forests, and grasslands. Since they are totems, blue wolves and white deer are revered by Mongolians as gods, and at the same time give them a mysterious and beautiful legend.
There is a very beautiful legend about the origin of the Mongolian nation. There is a famous river called the Foran River on the Mongolian Plateau, which is regarded by Mongolians as a sacred river.
It is said that in the distant past, a blue wolf and a white deer came here and fell in love at first sight. Therefore, the Mongols think they are descendants of a combination of a blue wolf and a white deer. In fact, in the north, there are beautiful legends about the origin of many ethnic groups. For example, the legend of the origin of the Khitan people is a combination of white horses and green cows - fairies that ride white horses and fairies that ride blue cows, gave birth to the Khitan people.
Legends are all legends. According to historians, it is likely that a man from a clan with a blue wolf as a totem was combined with a woman who used a white deer as a totem, and their descendants formed the Mongolian nation.
According to “The Secret History of Mongolia” compiled by the Mongolians themselves, the Blue Wolf and the White Deer are the ancestors of Genghis Khan, and they were born to earth by order of heaven. They then crossed Tengji Si together, and began to thrive at the source of the Foran River, in front of Buhan Mountain, and gave birth to Batachikan. The Bhata Chihan mentioned here is the ancestor of Genghis Khan.
In fact, blue wolf and white deer are read separately in Mongolian as: Boertiechna and Huemalanle, but they are only translated in Chinese as blue wolf and white deer. As the translator of “The Secret History of Mongolia” said, Blue Wolf and White Deer are just two legendary names, just like we have the words “dragon” and “tiger” in our names.
Also, according to the famous historical book “Historical Collection” of the Persian Mongol dynasty:
An earth-shattering war broke out in the northern steppes. As a result, the Mongolian tribe was defeated, leaving only two men and two women after the massacre. They fled to a dangerous valley called Ergunye-Kun, where they formed two couples, and the population gradually increased over the generations. Legend has it that one of the two families is called Nagusi, and the other is called Kihiko. Later, after several generations, they finally returned to the Hulunbuir grassland where their ancestors lived. Genghis Khan is said to be a descendant of the Beggar Clan, a later Mongolian tribe.
So how did the Golden Family come about?
Since the beginning of the legend of the Blue Wolf and White Deer, it has basically been a legend. Doben Meergan was the first Mongolian figure with a clear record in history. He married Alan Hua, a goddess that the Mongols would later revere. After they had two sons together, Doben Meer Ganbian died.
However, an amazing thing happened. After the death of Duoben Meergan, Alan Hua gave birth to three more sons out of thin air. Regarding how these three sons were born, Alan Huaa said this: One night, a fairy with blond hair and white light flew in. After flying in, her peritoneum penetrated my stomach, then a white light penetrated my stomach, so she gave birth to these three sons.
As a result, there were two Mongols, one called the Niluven Mongols, referring to the three sons born to Alanhuah. Their descendants were the most noble Mongols, the “Golden Family” to which Genghis Khan belongs.
The other one is a Du'er Lujin Mongolian. It is a descendant of Alan Hua and Duoben Meergan, meaning an ordinary Mongolian. Among these five sons, the youngest son, Bo Duanchar, is the tenth ancestor of Genghis Khan, according to the records of “Yuan Shi: The Original Chronicles of Taizu”. Thus, the Mongolian Golden Family was born, and Genghis Khan founded a world-wide empire.
