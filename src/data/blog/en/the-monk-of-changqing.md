 In the Changqing region of Shandong, there is an old monk with a deep sense of Taoism and pure conduct. He is in his 80s and is still very healthy. One day, he suddenly fell and couldn't get up. The monks from the temple rushed over to rescue him. At first glance, he saw that it was completely dead; yet he didn't know he was dead, and his soul drifted away to the border of Henan.
There is a son from an old government in Henan. On this day, he led more than a dozen horse-riding attendants to fight rabbits with falcons. Suddenly, the horse was so frightened that the boy fell from his horse and fell to his death. At this point, the old monk's soul just happened to meet the boy's corpse and suddenly merged. The boy gradually woke up. The slaves surrounded him and questioned him, and he opened his eyes and said, “How did you get here!” The crowd supported him back home.
The boy entered the door, and the concubines, who were wearing pink, gathered one after another to visit and express their condolences. He was shocked, “I'm a monk, how did I get here!” The family thought it was ridiculous, and they all sobered his ears and earnestly enlightened him, making him awake. He didn't defend himself; he just kept his eyes closed and stopped talking. He was given coarse rice to eat, but he refused any alcohol or meat. He sleeps alone at night, not with his wife and concubines. After a few days, he suddenly wanted to walk around a bit. Everyone in the family is happy. After leaving the house, as soon as he had settled down, a few servants arrived and asked him to review the income and expenditure situation one after another, with money and food books. The boy insisted that they all refused to do it because they were sick and burnt out, but he only asked, “Do you know where Changqing County in Shandong is?” The servants all answered, “I know.” The boy said, “I'm bored and bored. I need to go there and get ready to pack.” People said that he had only recovered from his illness and should not go far, but he didn't listen and went out on his way the next day.
Arriving in Changqing, he saw the local scenery just like yesterday. I didn't have to worry about asking for directions; I actually arrived at a Buddhist temple. When several of the old monk's disciples saw the guests, they all came very respectfully to see them. The boy then asked, “Where did the old monk go?” They answered, “Our master passed away a while ago.” The boy also asked about the old monk's graveyard. The monks guided him to see the three-foot solitary tomb. The wild grass wasn't full yet. The monks didn't know what this boy meant. The boy was preparing to leave soon, and ordered, “Your master is a monk who abides by the precepts. The handprints he left behind should be carefully guarded and not damaged.” The monks respectfully agreed, and only then did the boy leave. When he got home, he didn't sit around and did nothing but ask about household chores.
After a few months, the boy went out and walked on his own until the old temple in Changqing. He said to his disciples, “I am your master.” The monks doubted his absurdity and looked at each other and laughed. The old monk then described how he recovered his soul, and also talked about what he did during his lifetime, all of which were true. Only then did the monks believe it, let him sleep in his original bed and still serve him as before.
Later, the boy's family repeatedly sent cars and horses and painstakingly invited him home; he ignored it at all. After more than a year, the boy's wife sent a housekeeper to Changqing Temple and gave away a lot of things. He doesn't want any gold, silver, or satin; he just takes a cloth robe. Some of the boy's friends visited him at the temple in Changqing. Seeing him stand in silence, he is determined; even though he is only in his 30s, he always talks about what he has experienced for over 80 years.
