 Once upon a time, there was a monk who didn't think well in a Buddhist temple, so he carried a nun and fled. After escaping, he looked everywhere to find a place to escape until one day...
When the nun learned that the monk had escaped and traveled all over the sky, she was never able to find a monk. However, while the nun was looking for it, the monk was hiding in the Yaochi pond, so the nun left Yaochi and asked. The nun said, “Excuse me, have any monks here escaped here?” The ministers all recalled, “If a nun came and asked me when I wasn't there, you would say no. Can you hear me?” The ministers all nodded. As a result, they said, “We've never had a monk pass by here. You're a nun, how did you come back to our Yaochi?”
The nun said, “I came here under the direction of Lady Guanyin to find a monk, but since the monk didn't study well at the Buddhist temple, she quietly escaped. So, I'm going to see if the monk escaped here and didn't, then forget it, retreat!”
After speaking, the nun flew away.
When the monk saw the nun flying away, he quickly walked out and said, “Thank you, for your great kindness, I will repay you later, Amitabha!” After speaking, the monk also flew away...
The two of them are in opposite directions, and the nun thought, “Hey, I haven't caught a monk today, what should I do?” The monk also thought, “I want to enjoy happiness elsewhere as soon as possible, so I don't carry it with me in a boring Buddha hall. The whole day, Amitabha Buddha's boredom is dead.”
It's been a while...
When the monk came to the world and saw how he looked, others would definitely say, “Why is this person so strange? It doesn't seem like a human, could it be a ghost?” I was so scared that I panicked. But the monk thought about it again. Even if the nun found the world, she wouldn't recognize me, so as soon as the monk swooped around, he became an ordinary person.
After about a year...
The nun learned that the monk had a good time on earth. He took action when he was defenseless. Finally, when she saw the monk in a dilapidated house, she rushed down the world and grabbed the monk's hand and said, “Monk, go back with me. If you don't go back, you'll be severely punished.” The monk at this time didn't listen to anything. She thought about how to eat and drink. The nun got angry, pulled back and forth, and finally, brought back to the monk. Hall.
