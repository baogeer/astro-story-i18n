He deliberately used the name of touring the mountains and playing in the water to come to Jiangnan to listen to the news and check the facts.
At that time, there was a monk at Jingci Temple in Nanping Mountain, Hangzhou, called Slander. This monk doesn't focus on chanting sutras and meditating; he just likes to discuss major events in the world. If you want to talk, say it; if you want to scold, scold; there is no doubt about it. Just because he was reasonable and scolded in a funny way, ordinary people like to get close to him.
When Emperor Qianlong arrived in Hangzhou, when he heard that there was such a monk, he raised a pimp on his brow and thought to himself: This old monk took such a strange name; he must have been a Ming dynasty survivor in a hidden mountain forest. He didn't keep his duty. I'd rather listen to what exactly he destroyed. As a result, he changed into a blue shirt, wore a gold-painted folding fan, dressed up as a talent, and waved around Jingci Temple, where he would slander the monk.
The slandering monk came out of the temple. When the Qianlong Emperor saw him, he asked:
“Is the teacher just slandering the monk?”
The slandering monk answered, “Yes, I just slandered the monk; it was me who slandered the monk.”
Emperor Qianlong also asked, “Did the teacher and father come out of the house since childhood, or did they leave home halfway?”
He slandered the monk and said, “Am I? I'm a half-way out of my family. Xiucai, why are you asking me to do this?”
Emperor Qianlong had nothing to say. As soon as he swept his eyes, he saw the monk's unruly dress, and said, “I heard that my teacher was a virtuous monk. Why did he wear this broken shirt like a loofah?”
The slandering monk laughed: “When I was young, I also wore splendid clothes! Later, the splendid shirt was torn apart by wild dogs, so I became a monk and came wearing this ripped linen robe! However, even though my clothes are shabby, I have the right intentions. It's not like those old men in official uniforms. They look so pompous. Secretly men steal women and prostitutes.”
Emperor Qianlong was beaten at the head, and he couldn't get out of his head. He thought with hate: this slandering the monk certainly lives up to its name! You always have to find a way out of it, and punish him so harshly for his crime. He had an evil plan in his belly, and smirks piled up on his face, asking the slandering monk to take him to the temple for fun.
They entered the gate of Joji-ji Temple and saw the people next to them cutting bamboo to make incense baskets. As soon as Emperor Qianlong's eyes turned, he picked up a split piece of bamboo at his fingertips, slandered the monk on his side and asked, “Teacher, what is your name of this?”
The slandering monk said, “This is called bamboo skin.”
Emperor Qianlong turned the Maozhu sheet around, slandered the monk with the white side, and asked, “Teacher, what is this called?”
The slandering monk said, “Is this? Let's call it bamboo meat.”
Emperor Qianlong frowned and smiled bitterly: “What a fresh name!”
The slandering monk called haha and said, “Old guests, now the world has changed, and the name has to change with it!”
Emperor Qianlong was exhausted, so he had no choice but to make no noise. It turns out that they were in Daxing prison at the time, trying to find a way out of their way to kill people. When Emperor Qianlong saw it in his eyes, he asked, “Teacher, is this bean sprout considered a clean thing?” The slandering monk said, “Bean sprouts are raw in water and grow in water.
