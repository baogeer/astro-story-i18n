When it reached the center of the river, they deliberately threw the monkey into the water, and the monkey was swept away by the rapid waves. The story warns that those who betray their faith will eventually reap their own evil consequences."

 There is a little monkey on the west bank of the Moon River. One day, the little monkey saw peaches in the family's orchard across the street and drooled so hungry, but it couldn't get watery; it was in a hurry and startled.
At this point, an elephant swayed out. As soon as the little monkey saw the elephant, he had an idea. It knew that elephants liked sugar cane the most, so they hurriedly settled in with it, “Brother Elephant, do you like sugar cane?” The elephant said, “Of course, my favorite food is sugar cane,” the monkey said, “I just picked fruit from the tree and saw a slice of sugar cane across the street. Your ability to pass the water is greater than anyone else, so why not enjoy eating it?” As soon as the elephant heard it, he hurriedly said, “Thank you, little monkey. I just ate and I'm not hungry now, so I'll go eat tomorrow! Little monkeys will be able to eat fresh peaches as soon as they hear it. I think in my heart: I'll sleep in the cave and go eat enough tomorrow.
The next day, the monkeys arrived at the riverside and waited for the elephants early in the morning. Soon after, the elephant came. As soon as it saw the monkey, it said hello to it: “Brother the monkey, you're good morning,” the monkey hurriedly said, “Brother Elephant, be a friend to get to the bottom of it. I want to go and give you hope and have fun, but I can't drink water. As soon as the elephant makes sense, it says, “I'll carry you over!” In the middle of the monkey's mind, it quickly jumped on the elephant's back, and the elephant slowly walked into the water carrying the monkey.
As soon as they reached the east coast, the monkeys hurriedly ran to the theater garden to eat peaches. The elephant couldn't find sugar cane. There were a few corn trees in the garden. I had to go into the garden and eat corn.
The monkey's stomach is small, and it eats fast. It fills its stomach after being a while old. As soon as it sees the elephant eating big mouths, it jumps down the peach tree and shouts, “The elephant is eating corn!” As soon as the owner of the garden heard it, he hurriedly came out, only to see that the elephant was coming out of the garden and smashed it with stones. The elephant was beaten and said to the monkey, “How can you treat your friends this way?” The cunning monkey's eyes turned and hurriedly said, “Sorry, sorry, I just ate too many peaches, had a headache, and didn't know what to say. Now I feel a little more sober. Let's go back soon and jump on the elephant's back again.
The elephant was carrying the monkey, and thought to myself: This monkey is also too ruthless. It won't be beneficial to make friends with it. The elephant said, “Monkey, I'm sorry, I haven't eaten enough. Now my stomach is so hungry that I can't walk. I need to rest here for a while and let the river take you to meet Hades.” As soon as the monkey heard it, he hurriedly knocked his head on the elephant's back and couldn't stop asking for it: “Brother Elephant, I asked you to carry me Go to the river. I just made a mistake, so please spare me this time.” No matter what the monkey asks, the elephant straightens its head. A sharp wave sweeps the little monkey into the rapids, and it disappeared without much effort.
