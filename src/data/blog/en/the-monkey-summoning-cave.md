 One year was clear, the willows were green, and the peach blossoms were red. It was the beautiful daylight of the West Lake. People were visiting the lake and going on a green outing, and the area in front of Lingyin Temple was bustling. On the same day, the governor of Hangzhou also came out to play around. He sounded a gong and went to Lingyin Temple. He saw a large crowd of people at the foot of Feilai Peak, so he asked the sergeant to drive out a road from the crowd and take a look around himself, haha! It turns out there was an old monk who was playing Go with a Golden Retriever monkey there. This prefectural government also likes to play chess. At the time, those who shot idiots held him as high as the sky, calling him an invincible national player in the world. At this point, he saw the pieces on the chessboard and unaware that his hands were itchy, so he kicked the monkey away and sat down to compete with the old monk, showing off his skills in front of the crowd.
The old monk knew that people who worked as officials wanted to be honored, so he kept his mercy and intended to give up a few kids and confess defeat. The governor won chess and was so proud that he stood up, looked back on his head and laughed out loud, and scoffed at the old monk.
The old monk thought to himself: I'm willing to save face for you, but you're shameless! I laughed too. Seeing him laugh, the governor said, “You're crazy; you're still laughing when you lose!” The old monk said, “Dude, you don't know. Strong people have their own midfielders; I also have teachers and fathers on top of me!”
The governor asked, “Where is your master? Dare you come out and walk with me?”
The old monk pointed his hand at the mountain: “Well, that's my master.” The prefectural government looked up and saw the golden retriever he had just kicked off. It was flying on the top of the mountain climbing the branches and swinging. Just say, “Bah! Who am I, I turned out to be a furry monkey! You just call it down, and I'll go with it.”
The old monk clapped his palm at the top of the mountain, and the monkey jumped to the old monk's side one by one. The eyes of the two elves flashed and looked at the prefectures. The old monk made a gesture to the monk, and it sat down in the old monk's seat and went to play chess with the prefectures. Where in Chifu is the enemy of the monkeys! He blushed and blushed when he couldn't leave for a few kids. Look at the crowd, they all pouted their mouths and laughed. He coughed a few times and said, “The devil monkey is furry. This game doesn't count; come again, come again!”
Chifu still lost in the second set. He was in a hurry, and a string of white, soy-bean-sized sweat beads hung from his forehead. Surrounded by the lively crowd, the governor of the prefecture actually lost to the furry monkey, and they all laughed. The governor was humiliated in front of the crowd. His face suddenly became iron-blue. Huo stood up, dropped the chessboard to the ground, and yelled, “Grab this animal and hit me hard!”
The sergeants swarmed up. The old monk saw something wrong, so he patted the monkey on top of the head and shouted, “Go!” As soon as I heard the monkey whistling, the archery just flew up and down the peak.
The sergeants chased up the mountain, only to see the monkey jumping from the top of this tree to the treetop, and from that tree to the treetop. A while east and a little west, it was more flexible than a squirrel. The sergeants ran for half a day and were so tired that they couldn't catch it. Yamashita in Chifuji was shivering all over her body and shouted, “Set fire to me, set fire to the mountain!”
The agents had just lit the torches, but they saw the monkey “pop” down the tree and scream for a long time, then went into the stone cave next to it. The officers hurriedly chased into the cave. As soon as they saw, the cave had stone walls on all sides. There were no doors at the front, no way back, and the monkeys were gone. The sergeant paid back to the provincial government. The governor didn't believe it. It was as if he saw the monkey hiding behind a wall. He hurriedly jumped past and didn't want to use too much force; instead, he flattened his nose. The prefectures couldn't do it; they had to cover their noses and go back to the government office in a daze.
Since the golden retriever hid in a stone cave, people haven't seen it. However, as long as the old monk slaps the stone cave and shouts, it will also drill through the hole. Later, the old monk died, and the monkeys in the cave never came out again. Because the old monk called this cave for monkeys back then, posterity always called it “Call the Monkey Cave.”
