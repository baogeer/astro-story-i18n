 It is said that Tenku Monk is the most knowledgeable monk in the temple. People come to ask him questions about incredible things, and he often talks about his own experiences. One day, he told a story with the young monks in the temple and said that in Qingzhou, there are two foreign monks. Their appearance is very strange and different from the Central Plains region.
They have two rings on their ears and yellow cloth on their bodies, which look like yellow coats. Their long hair is actually curled up, and their beards are also very long. Unlike local monks who are taught in the region, they have to shave their hair. They claimed to be from the western region. They heard that Taishou in Qingzhou really admired the Buddha, so they made a special visit.
Taishou sent two powerful assistants to take them to the Taishou Monk's home. At the time, Taishou also had several companions. One of them was called Monk Ling Yi. He wasn't very polite to these two foreign monks. He thought they had no real skills; they were just begging for food. However, the empty monk felt that these two were unusual, so he hosted his own banquet to entertain them and invited them to stay.
While chatting, some people asked those two monks. There are many powerful people in the West, and they all have some magic. Master, do you also have some magic tricks? One of the Western monks smiled and took a small tower out of his sleeve and placed it in the palm of his hand like King Tota. This small pagoda was only one foot tall. It looked exquisite and compact, and was very cute.
The monk looked around and found a small box at the highest point on the wall, and he threw it away. The small tower just fell directly above the box. The small tower suddenly gave off a sheriji light, illuminating the whole house, and everyone clapped their hands and praised it. Soon, the monk raised his hand, and the small tower returned to his palm.
The other monk opened his arms and extended his left arm, and actually extended his right arm by 67 feet. Only then did people learn that these two Western monks had great respect for their abilities, and no one dared to slack off.
