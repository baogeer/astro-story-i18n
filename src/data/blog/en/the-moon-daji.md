 Another missing baby has occurred in Nigatake Village. The baby was later found in a small hill not far from the village, but it still died mysteriously. As in previous disappearances, the baby's mouth was covered with white liquid, and his stomach was bulging, as if he had eaten a full meal. It was verified that the white liquid was a woman's milk and was not toxic. It can be seen that the child was not poisoned, so how did they die? The forensic doctor looked closely and discovered that both sides of the child's nose were blue. It is speculated that during breastfeeding, the child was held so tightly that he was unable to breathe and suffocated and died. Who was so ruthless to kill these ignorant babies? There was a lot of discussion, but no one could say why.
This is the sixth time a baby has disappeared. People in the village are anxious. In particular, the family has a baby who is still in their infancy. They also guard the child day and night, fearing that they will fall asleep at night, and the child has disappeared inexplicably. The prefecture also sent people to investigate time and time again, but they always had no clue, and the baby was still missing.
There is a young fisherman named Zhang Qing in Kuzhu Village who carries fishing nets and fish baskets all year round to fish in nearby villages or in reservoirs further away. Sometimes the places I went were far away, and when I came back it was completely dark. One day, Zhang Qing returned late again. Because he had a lot to gain this time, he proudly sang uncoordinated songs as he walked. At the time, a bright moon was hanging high in the sky, illuminating the way home. Suddenly, he heard footsteps behind him. Looking back, Mom! It really doesn't matter; I was shocked when I saw it: I saw a woman naked all over her body, with her beard on her head. Strangely enough, her breasts were bulging, big, and seemed to take up more than half of her body! Whose woman is this strange and has no shame? Zhang Qing was about to criticize this woman out loud. Suddenly, she saw the woman's bloodless and expressionless face. In particular, the woman was walking towards herself step by step... Zhang Qing screamed in horror and fainted.
When he woke up, he found himself lying in his bed at home, and his wife was anxiously waiting by the bedside. Turns out he had been in a coma for several days. Uncle Liu, who got up early in the village to pick up feces that day, found him unconscious by the river. At the time, he still had milk flowing from his mouth and his stomach was bulging... After being cared for at home for more than ten days, Zhang Qing's body gradually recovered, and he also gradually remembered the scene that night, the woman with big bulging boobs, and her face so white under the moonlight. He remembers that woman approached him step by step and put her tits in his mouth...
Since then, Zhang Qing never dared to return late from fishing; he was afraid he would run into that strange and scary woman again. One day, he went to the nearby village of Fenglai to fish, and because it was hot, he went to a family to get water to drink. A white-haired old woman enthusiastically scooped him a large cup of cool water and asked him to sit and take a break in the hall. Despite the good intentions of the old man, he went into the house. Suddenly he saw a portrait of a young woman hanging in the house, and he suddenly remembered the woman's face that night. It was clearly a portrait of that woman! Zhang Qing was immediately frightened and softened in her legs, yet it was still difficult to open up and ask the old man who this woman was and what really happened. The wife sighed and said with a bitter look, that this is her hard-working daughter-in-law. Her son works outside the home all year round, leaving only two of her mother-in-law and daughter-in-law to farm at home. Just one night three years ago, my pregnant daughter-in-law was suddenly about to give birth. The mother-in-law quickly called the midwife in the village, but the midwife had been busy for half a day and still hadn't given birth. It turned out to be a difficult delivery! The mother-in-law had no choice but to ask a few young men in the village to help carry her daughter-in-law to the hospital ten miles away. But I didn't expect it to be too late, and my daughter-in-law would die of pain on the way... “It really is a scoundrel”! The old man was already in tears. “If I had taken her to the hospital early in the first place, she wouldn't have died, and the child wouldn't die. She loved children so much, but...” The old man choked and couldn't go on. Zhang Qing was soft-hearted and even persuaded the elderly. This woman turned out to be a bitter person, but since she died, why was she able to stand alive in front of him, then what she saw that day must have been her ghost?
The more Zhang Qing thought about it, the more afraid he quickly rushed to Qingshan Temple carrying a fish basket to find the Abbot of the Tokai Sea. Aoyama Temple is about 30 km from Nigatake Village, where incense is very popular. The abbot of the Tokai Sea is highly respected. Not only does he have the heart of a Bodhisattva, but he can also pass through the ages and the present, and has the art of catching demons. When he found the abbot, Zhang Qing voiced all the doubts in his heart. After listening, the abbot shrugged his beard and said, “This is the Moon Maiden acting as a monster. Because she loved children during her birth, she had a difficult delivery and died, so she was very unwilling and had a sense of resentment in her heart, so she was unable to successfully conceive a child. As a result, she always steals other people's children to breastfeed to satisfy her deformed motherly love. If she actually doesn't find a child, she'll also take action on adults. She always shows up in October of every month when the light is just right. Although she wanted to satisfy her mother's love, she killed quite a few babies as a result, so she is called Moon Daji along with the ruthless Daji.” Hearing this, Zhang Qing thought about it. It is true that every time a baby in the village disappeared, it was 15, and when he returned that night, it happened to be 15.
On another night when the moon was shining brightly, Moon Daji stole another child from a family in the village. The abbot of the Tokai Sea, who had already been buried in a dark place, woven straw and placed a child on the bed. By the time Moon was ready to leave, the Abbot of the Dehai Sea knocked on the wooden fish and said, “Let's go, your child has already been given to you; it's time for you to get pregnant. If you reinvent evil, I'll never let go of you; I'm just afraid you'll only be a lone ghost forever.”
When Moon Daji heard the abbot's words, she shed two lines of tears on her bleak face. She hugged the grass child in her arms and quickly disappeared into the twilight. Since then, there have been no cases of lost babies in the village.
