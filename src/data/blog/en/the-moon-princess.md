 In all the world, when there is only the sun during the day and no moon at night, there is a beautiful royal palace on the banks of the Aomulun River. Neighbors Lehan and his empress and princess live in the palace. Hardworking and brave herders and hunters live in the grasslands and forests on both sides of the Aomulun River.
Neighbor Lehan had no sons; only two princesses. However, his two princesses were born lame, so ugly that even Nighthawks were afraid to take a look at them in the dark of the night. Neighbors who defected were so sad that their hair was all white.
One day, his neighbor Lehan went out hunting. He met a beautiful and kind hunter who hated him on the Ao Mulun River and robbed her to carry her on horseback and forced her to become his concubine.
A year later, this beautiful and kind concubine gave birth to a little hate and became the Three Princesses who defected from neighboring Lehan.
As soon as the Three Princesses were born, they are as beautiful as a treasure. Lehan, a neighbor who likes pearls, likes the Three Princesses even more, and is even more fond of the beautiful concubines.
This infuriated the Grand Princess and the Second Princess, and even more infuriated, the toxic and cruel Empress. Less than three months after the birth of the Three Princesses, the beautiful concubines were secretly murdered by the vicious Empress. The poor Three Princesses all depended on their nursing mother to survive.
The nursing mother is a poor herder, kind hearted and open-minded. When the three princesses were young, the nursing mother led the three princesses to pick flowers in the prairie and listen to birds in the forest. The three princesses distributed the wild flowers they picked to play with the herdsmen's children. The three princesses grew up. The nursing mother taught the three princesses to practice horseback riding on the prairie and learn to hunt in the forest. The three princesses distributed the prey they caught to the hunters to eat. By the age of seventeen, the beauty of the Three Princesses surpassed all the flowers on the prairie. The Three Princesses' arrows passed the most amazing hunters in the forest, and the hearts of the Three Princesses were purer than the transparent springs on the mountain.
The father of the Three Princesses defected from his neighbor Lehan was a very violent person. The herders were often killed in groups because they couldn't pay enough tribute to horses and sheep. Hunters are often bitten to death by hunting dogs bite to death in large forests because they don't have enough prey. Seeing the herdsmen's blood stained all the water in the Aomulun River red, and seeing that the tigers and leopards in the forest didn't want to eat the hunter's meat, the three princesses' sad hearts were all broken. story card
Folk Tales of Eastern Mongolia from Kazuo were selected as the first batch of national intangible cultural heritage lists in 2006, and “The Moon Princess” is one of Kazuo's Eastern Mongolian folk stories. It mainly tells the story of the three royal princesses on the banks of the Aomulun River who brought light to herders and hunters from being poor and turned into the moon.
According to Tong Tao, director of the Kazuo County Cultural Center in Liaoning Province, the Kazuo East Mongolian folk tale consists of 24 volumes translated by Meng and Han, mainly distributed in oral form. Currently, many heirs who have told the “100 Stories” have passed away, and there are only 67 inheritors left who can tell these stories. The inheritance and protection of these stories is one of the issues that need to be solved urgently.
In order to better solve the problems of inheritance and dissemination, Tong Tao also combined the folk stories of Eastern Mongolia from Kazuo and the art of decoupage to present relevant story content; recorded some stories into reviews and conveyed the wonderful content to the audience in the form of commentaries; in addition, they also tried to adapt these stories into stage plays and shadow plays to attract more people's attention and interest in Kazuo's Eastern Mongolian folk stories. “It not only describes the history of interaction between the Mongolian people and the Mongolian people over the past 300 years in the Eastern Mongolian region, but also shows the history of the Mongolian and Han people cooperated in farming and development in western Liaoning, and the integration of the cultures of the Mongolian, Han, and Manchu nationalities from various angles.” Tong Tao said.
