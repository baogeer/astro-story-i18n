 [The most knowledgeable person in modern China] Gu Hongming: The first person in China who learned from China and the West, and is now familiar with China. He has successively studied at prestigious universities such as the University of Edinburgh in the UK, the University of Berlin, the University of Leipzig, and the University of Paris in France; obtained 13 doctorates in literature, philosophy, science, engineering, and theology; proficient in English, German, French, Italian, Latin, Malay, and Greek, Japanese, and Russian languages, and was the first to translate China's “Four Books” into the West.
