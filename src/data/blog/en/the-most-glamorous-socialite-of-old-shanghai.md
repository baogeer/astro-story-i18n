 The most beautiful social flower in old Shanghai
The gathering of beautiful women made old Shanghai from the 20s and 30s look extremely beautiful.
Sixty years ago, the Paramount Ballroom (Paramount) in Shanghai was known as “the first amusement hall in the Far East.” The dreamy lighting, rose-patterned floors, romantic jazz music, and a smooth, mirror-like flexible dance floor all speak of the splendor and luxury of Shanghai. There is a beautiful woman who often comes here to dance, relax, and enjoy her youth. She is Tang Ying. At the time, she and Lu Xiaoman, who had the most prominence in the social scene, were also known as “Southern Tang Beilu.”
But now, Lu Xiaoman is well known because of the poet Xu Zhimo, and Tang Ying's face is gradually hiding from the clouds. An important reason for this outcome is that although Tang Ying is beautiful, after all, she doesn't have a very sensational marriage history.
However, in terms of the name “social flower” alone, she was actually much more “competent” than Lu Xiaoman at the beginning, and was also far “worthy of her name.”
Now, novels or movies about life on the beach in the past are in fashion. Among these works, “Flower of Communication” is one of the essential main characters. They are both beautiful and sociable. They spend a lot of time hanging out with rich men. They are supported by these men, and have a very good material life.
In fact, such a group of women did exist in old Shanghai at the time.
At the time, some Class A hotels in Shanghai, such as “Dadong,” “East Asia,” and “Greater China,” had such female tenants. However, this type of woman who has been renting for a long time in premium hotels such as “International,” “Golden Gate,” and Chinachem Apartments is even more “classy.”
Among these women, some are red dancers in the major dance halls in Shanghai; some are red characters in the old school house and senior year middle school, and come out of the hall to attract bees and butterflies; others have left their families to live outside to make “friends” and be supported by others... These women live spacious lives, have a fair amount of space, and even travel back and forth between high ups and down to the underworld, but at best they can only be considered “social nerds.”
To be eligible to be called a “flower of friendship,” the first requirement is a recognized “celebrity” at the time. At that time, the flower of friendship was an imported compliment, and it was mostly monopolized by famous people with big names. After a systematic and orderly training, they combine talent and beauty, which is truly enviable. Communication flowers are actually like agents. The latter aims to steal intelligence, while the former mainly “steals feelings,” and they all have the same clear goals. In a nutshell, it's a lubricant, body powder for social situations, and a tassel that embellishes peace in a troubled world.
So, which women were the most famous socializing flowers on the beach in the 30s to 40s of the last century?
Half a century ago, there was an “Old News of Chunshen” which talks about “socialites” in Shanghai back then and wrote, “Famous people in Shanghai are known for their social relationships, starting with Tang Ying and Lu Xiaoman. They were followed by Zhou Shuping and Chen Haoming.” These socialites are well-mannered, gracious and graceful, like a group of beautiful butterfly elves. Among this group of beautiful butterfly elves, the one that has attracted the world's attention the most and is the brightest person is Tang Ying, who has beautiful looks and has a Western style of facial features.
Tang Ying was born in 1910. Her father, Tang Naian, was one of the first students studying abroad to receive compensation from the Qing government and the first Western doctor to study abroad in China. After returning home, he worked as a doctor in the Beiyang Fleet, then opened a private clinic in Shanghai to see a doctor for the Shanghai family at the time, so the Tang family was well-off.
Tang Ying is not only slender, has a sweet voice, and is avant-garde, but also versatile. She has excellent looks and is good at Kunqu. She graduated from the Shanghai Church School, the Chinese and Western Women's School. Her proficiency in Chinese and English is outstanding, and she also has a high level of artistic achievement.
In addition to her beautiful appearance, the reason why Tang Ying was able to become a leading socialite in old Shanghai is also inseparable from her strict parenting since childhood. In addition to being highly educated, Tang Ying is well-dressed and avant-garde, and has always been a trendsetter leading the Shanghai Beach fashion trend. ChannelNo5 perfume, Ferregamo heels, CD lipstick, Celine costume, channel perfume bags, LV handbags... everything a French lady owned at the time also had it.
According to rumor buyers' accounts at the time, Tang Ying had ten gold-painted boxes, all of which were clothes, and the leather coat alone hung a large closet full of walls. The Tang family raised a tailor and made clothes for her alone. Therefore, Tang Ying's attire always represented the most fashionable girl on the beach at the time.
A gorgeous life, unlimited scenery
Even if you've been sharpening your sword for more than ten years, you still have to have an opportunity to enter the social world. Lu Xiaoman's reason for entering the social scene was very impressive. She was hired as a part-time diplomatic interpreter by Beiyang Foreign Minister Gu Weijun because she is fluent in both English and French. In this way, Lu Xiaoman rightly made a splash in the limelight at conferences and prestigious dance parties. At the time, Tang Ying was still a young 13-year-old girl at the old Shanghai Chinese and Western Girls' School (the predecessor of St. Mary's Girls' School where Zhang Ailing attended). She was at an age where she had no idea about the future.
Tang Ying's father was deeply influenced by Western civilization. In addition, the Tang family was also a Christian family, so there was some “emphasis on women over boys.” Girls have a high status, but that doesn't mean they can just go out to socialize; they have to wait until a man invites them to come to the door or get married before they start socializing. Tang Ying officially entered the social circle around 1926, which fully complies with the Western rules of starting socializing at age 16.
At the time, “socializing” was introduced to China as a new thing. There was a magazine called “Ling Long,” which encouraged women to learn to socialize all day long, and used Tang Ying as a role model for a “socialite celebrity.” Once, the British royal family visited China. Tang Ying used to perform piano and Kunqu, which was very dazzling. Large jade photos of her were published in major newspapers at the time, which overshadowed the royal family, and it was also the most prominent period of her social life.
There was a saying at the time: “Southern Tang Northern Lu,” which probably meant that southern China had a beautiful lady Tang Ying, and the north had a stunning Lu Xiaoman. Later, after Lu Xiaoman went to Shanghai, she shared a sympathy with Tang Ying and soon became good friends.
In 1927, Lu Xiaoman and Tang Ying performed the Kunqu opera “Collecting Paintings” and “Called Paintings” at the Shanghai Women's Circle Comfort Theatre Festival held at the Central Theatre. There is a picture of Lu Xiaoman and Tang Ying in a movie. In it, Lu Xiaoman gently swings a folding fan, and Tang Ying steps on the stage. Both of them are in the same scene.
In the fall of 1935, Tang Ying made another amazing move — performing the entire “Wang Baochuan” in English at the Carlton Theatre. At the time, Wang Yun was played by Fang Bofen, one of the directors of “Wen Wei Po” when it was first published in Shanghai; Xue Pinggui was played by Ling Xianyang, president of Hujiang University; and Tang Ying played Wang Baochuan. Since this was the first time a Peking opera was performed in English, it attracted great interest from the audience. In addition, Tang Ying is not only fluent in English, but she is also very good at acting. As a result, this performance caused a stir in China at the time.
 
 
 An embarrassing dinner
Tang Ying is young and beautiful, and loves to be in the limelight. Naturally, there are quite a few famous men around her who fell under her pomegranate dress, and Yang Xingfo, who was Sun Yat-sen's secretary at the time, was one of them.
Yang Xingfo is friends with Xu Zhimo and Lu Xiaoman, and Xu Lu are regular guests of Tang Ying's family, so it was only natural for Yang Xingfu to meet Tang Ying, who is beautiful and has all kinds of style.
Lu Xiaoman had not yet divorced Wang Geng at the time, and her romance with Xu Zhimo had already gone viral. On this side of the house, Yang Xingfu is also bitterly in love with the beautiful Tang Ying, yet the Tang family has already decided on a family affair for Tang Ying. Tang Ying's future husband, Li Zufa, is a member of the Li family in Xiaogang, Ningbo, and has returned from studying at Yale. Meanwhile, Yang Xingfo happened to know Li Zufa, and is also a brother with Li Zufa, so he went back and forth and described himself as embittered.
At the time, Tang Ying, Yang Xingfu, Li Zufa, Lu Xiaoman, Xu Zhimo, and Wang Geng became the two most famous “love triangles” on the beach.
In the second half of 1925, in order to solve this problem, the famous painter Liu Haisu decided to open a restaurant in response to Xu Zhimo's request. This is the famous “one of the most embarrassing dinners” in modern history.
On that day, Liu Haisu invited guests at Gongdelin Restaurant in Shanghai, including Lu Xiaoman, Xu Zhimo, Wang Geng, Yang Xingfu, Tang Ying, Li Zufa, Tang Qulu, Xu Zhimo's ex-wife Zhang Youyi's brother Zhang Xinhai, etc., all of whom are linked to these six love rivals, to have a meal.
During the meeting, Liu Haisu spoke extensively. During the toast, he first discussed the relationship between life and love on the topic of anti-feudalism, and also discussed that the relationship between husband and wife is based on mutual harmony and mutual interests. Marriage without love is unethical.
Just after this dinner, Wang Geng later agreed to divorce Lu Xiaoman, and they became the perfect couple. On the other hand, Tang Ying's side “flowing water is intentional; falling flowers are ruthless,” and she also clearly rejected Yang Xingfu. Yang Xingfu returned disappointed.
On June 18, 1933, Yang Xingfo went out by car and was shot dead on Shanghai's Yalpei Road by secret agents. This is really “how proud is the old days; flowers blossom and fall for two reasons.”
 
 
 
 Clouds want costumes, flowers think
In 1927, Tang Ying, Lu Xiaoman, and others, who are very familiar with fashion, founded “Yunchang Clothing Company”. Yunchang Clothing Co., Ltd. is located in a small three-story building on Jing'an Temple Road in Shanghai. It is the first clothing company in China exclusively for women.
The image representatives of Yunchang Clothing Company are Tang Ying and Lu Xiaoman. Stars gathered at the company's opening ceremony, which attracted the attention of the Shanghai media, and major media outlets scrambled to report.
Tang Ying claims that her company is an art clothing company. The company uses the most popular costumes in the world, and its purpose is to be “new” rather than “expensive.” Furthermore, Tang Ying also hired Jiang Xiaoyi, who had returned from studying art in France and Japan, as the designer for Yunchang Clothing Company. Jiang Xiaoyi's addition gave Yunchang Clothing Company clothing the guarantee of master and high quality fashion.
Furthermore, at the time of opening, Tang Ying and Lu Xiaoman personally tried on new clothes for customers in the store, which was equivalent to professional models in a Parisian haute couture store. Of course, this kind of service was very appealing.
In the winter of 1927, wherever fashionable women appeared on the streets of Shanghai and nearby cities such as Nanjing, Suzhou, and Wuxi, there would be a beautiful landscape composed of Yunchang brand coats.
Soon, Yunchang brand coats went to places such as Beijing and Tianjin, and became an indispensable winter outfit for fashionable women.
The following year, Yunchang also designed and produced spring and autumn jackets, single coats, and decorative silk outerwear worn on midsummer nights, and quickly became popular on the beach...
The success of Yunchang Clothing Company has led to the development and prosperity of Shanghai Beach Clothing. As a result, Shanghai also became the fashion capital of the Far East and even Asia in the 30s. Wealthy businessmen from Japan, the Philippines, Singapore, India, and other countries all rush to Shanghai to buy fashion—some people have made statistics that show up on the streets of Shanghai after 10 days.
Tang Ying's first commercial attempt was an absolute victory. This made her, who already has endless beauty, a celebrity and the focus of attention among the famous people on the beach. Naturally, such a brilliant woman is incomparably attractive. At this moment, she is as dazzling and charming as a radiant light, and has finally attracted Song Ziwen, “Diamond King Laowu,” who was wealthy and most admired by women in all of China at the time.

A relationship with Song Ziwen
Today's world is familiar with the love and hate between Miss Sheng Jiaqi and Song Ziwen, but they don't know that after Miss Sheng Qi, Tang Ying became Song Ziwen's favorite.
The Tang and Song families have been in a relationship for a long time. Tang Qulu, son of the Tang family, and Song Ziwen are good friends. Not only did they study together in the US, but after returning home, Tang Chulu also became Song Ziwen's secretary. In the eyes of the Tang family, working in politics is not a good career, so Tang Naian and his wife are not optimistic about their son's career. However, Tang Chulu did not take his parents' words to heart. Not only did he have close relationships with Song Ziwen, but he also often brought Song Ziwen home. After going back and forth, Song Ziwen finally met Tang Ying.
At first sight, Song Ziwen was strongly attracted to Tang Ying, who was in the best years of her life. Song Ziwen began to chase Tang Ying, and a fiery love letter was presented to Tang Ying's dressing table. Although Song Ziwen was 16 years old, Song Ziwen was considered the richest, mature, and handsome man in the world at the time. Naturally, this also had considerable appeal to Tang Ying. However, Tang Ying's father is extremely opposed to her dealings with Song Ziwen. Tang Nai'an believes that “one dynasty is one king, one minister.” In his view, it is not an honorable thing involving politics; on the contrary, it may cause unpredictable trouble for the family. Unexpectedly, his concerns actually came true.
In 1931, Tang Chulu was assassinated.
On the same day, Tang Chulu and Song Ziwen came to Shanghai's train station and left on the morning train. Unfortunately, Tang Chulu was dressed in a very similar way to his companion Song Ziwen on the same day. After the two arrived at the train station by car, Tang Qulu first got off the bus and walked towards the train. At this point, someone suddenly released a smoke bomb, and the smoke immediately dispersed. At this point, Song Ziwen had already gotten out of the car, but when he saw the smoke bomb, he immediately alerted himself to dive under the train.
As a result, while Song Ziwen was hiding, there was a burst of gunfire in the smoke screen, and Tang Chulu fell to the ground in response.
After the incident happened, the driver and Song Ziwen immediately took Tang Chulu to the nearest German hospital. Tang Chulu had many shots in his bladder at the time, and his condition was very critical. However, the doctor at the hospital was slow to arrive. Finally, when the German doctor was ready to go to the operating table, Tang Chulu had already passed away.
The assassination shocked the beach. There were many rumors at the time that Tang Chulu was killed because he helped Song Ziwen block bullets. In fact, everyone familiar with the matter knows that Tang Chulu was mistakenly killed because he was dressed very similar to Song Ziwen.
Tang Naian originally opposed his children's dealings with Song Ziwen. With the death of Tang Chulu, Tang Naian became even more disgusted with politics and people involved in politics. And Tang Ying's relationship with Song Ziwen is bound to have no further results.
However, on Song Ziwen's side, after being lucky enough to recover a life, he naturally felt guilty and grateful to the Tang family. Also because of this, he didn't want to disrupt the Tang family's life any more, so he dismissed the idea of continuing his relationship with Tang Ying.
There is no way to know whether Song Ziwen's departure caused emotional harm to Tang Ying. It's just that the 20 love letters Song Ziwen wrote to her have always been locked deep in Tang Ying's small drawer and kept in good hands. This also shows her true feelings for Song Ziwen.
Golden Ornate, Ukiyoshin
Soon after breaking up with Song Ziwen, Tang Ying soon married Li Zufa, the son of the “Xiaogang Li Family” in Ningbo and the son of Li Yunshu, a wealthy businessman in Shanghai. The Li family is a social celebrity or wealthy businessman; Li Zufa's father, Li Yunshu, is also a giant businessman in Shanghai. Marrying into such a powerful royal family, Tang Ying's life was pleasant at first, but she still maintained a social scene and continued to live a beautiful life.
Contrary to Tang Ying's “overly lively” lifestyle, her husband, Li Zufa, is a quiet person. He doesn't like to socialize, and naturally doesn't like his wife to always live a “flower butterfly” lifestyle.
Naturally, all of this is out of place with Tang Ying, who sees communication as life. In 1937, Tang Ying and Li Zufa finally divorced due to a mismatch in personality. After her divorce, Tang Ying was not disappointed; on the contrary, she was even more fearful in social settings.
“The colorful sleeve graciously holds the jade bell. I worked hard back then, but I was dazzled. Dance low in the willow tower and sing the peach blossom fan base.” This poem is the most appropriate way to describe Tang Ying's life back then.
Later, Tang Ying married Xiong Qi Gong, the nephew of Xiong Xiling, who was the general agent in China for America's Meiya Insurance Company at the time.
Xiong Qi Gong is not tall and doesn't look good, but he has a lively personality, and is “in the same league” as Tang Ying. They both have a strong sense of fun, so the combination of the two complements each other. In 1948, Tang Ying accompanied Seven Xiong to Hong Kong and then immigrated to America. In the 70s, Tang Ying went back to Shanghai to visit her family. Her green cheongsam made people think she was still a green girl. In fact, she is already an old woman in her sixties, but the beauty always seems to be worry-free with her late twilight.
Following Tang Ying, several famous socializing flowers have sprung up in old Shanghai. Such as Zhou Shuping, Chen Haoming, etc. However, compared to Tang Ying, they always seem to have a little less. In a nutshell, it's probably: “People who are prettier than me are not smart; people who are smarter than me are not beautiful.” This is Tang Ying's specialty, and it is also the foundation of the old Shanghai Queen of Communication that is hard to forget.
