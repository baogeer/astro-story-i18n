Because my “Three Thousand Years of Feng Shui” was published and made for the sake of shouting, I've been talking about what was written in the book these days. This blog post is no exception. It continues to talk about the legend of the tomb of famous people in Nanjing, the tomb of Dai Ga, the tomb of the first Kuomintang agent.
Although there are various opinions about the truth about the Dai Ga crash, the real reason has not been determined. According to superstition, this incident is also related to a sword. This sword is called the Dragon Sword. It is named because it has nine dragons engraved on it. It was stolen from Yuling in Qianlong. Anyone who gets caught with this sword dies at risk — this sword can be said to be the most legendary sword in the history of tomb raiding in ancient and modern China, an uncompromising weapon.
After Dai Ga died, this sword, which had been destroyed by the fire, became his burial item and went into the tomb. So, how did the outside world know that the sword in Dai Gi's tomb was buried by Emperor Qianlong of the Qing dynasty? There are a lot of stories here; I want to mention Sun Dianying, the thief of the Eastern Tomb of the Qing Dynasty.
After Sun Dianying stole the Qianlong Yuling Mausoleum and Cixi's Qingdong Mausoleum, all sectors of society were shocked, making people all over the world aware. Emperor Pu Yi of the late Qing dynasty, who had already descended from the throne, swore an oath to heaven. If he did not take revenge, he would not love the descendants of Xinjue Luo. In order to exonerate the crime, Sun Dian-ying used stolen treasures to bribe various important figures, mainly Chiang Kai-shek, Song Meiling, Ho Ying-qin, Yan Xishan, Dai Li, Xu Yuan-quan, Kong Xiangxi, Song Ziwen, Shang Zhen, and Wang Zhonglian.
Sun Dianying once told Wen Qiang, another Kuomintang secret agent and cousin of Mao Zedong's first president of the Republic, about the circumstances of the theft of the tomb and the delivery of the treasure. Here, “Wen Qiang's Oral Autobiography” and other materials are all explained. Like the pillow under Cixi's head — a big emerald watermelon — was given to Song Meiling.
The case of Sun Dianying using stolen treasures to bribe prominent people in the Republic of China has now been mentioned in many books. What did Dai Ga get? Maybe many people don't necessarily know. According to Sun Dianying, these are the two largest vermilion beads in the string of imperial beads Qianlong wore around his neck. Qianlong is a famous string of imperial pearls. You can also see images of Qianlong with the Chao Pearl in modern movies and TV productions. There are 108 imperial beads, representing the Eighteen Arhats. At the time, Sun Dianying gave it to Dai Ga in person in Tianjin as a first meeting gift.
Sun Dianying also mentioned in particular to Wen Qiang that Dai Yunong (Dai Li), a Jiulong treasure master who was buried at Qianlong, brought to Chiang Kai-shek, and I don't know if he received it. It is true that Chiang Kai-shek never received this sword, but had it not been for the plane crash, it would have been almost in Chiang Kai-shek's hands. As a result, Dai Ga was cheap and buried in the tomb.
Why didn't Chiang Kai-shek get the sword sooner? There was another accident here.
In spring and autumn, there is the legendary story of “One Peach Kills Three Masters”. Qi Xiang Guo and Yan Ying used only one peach to get rid of the “Three Great Masters” of Tian Kaijiang, Gong Sun Jie, and Gu Yezi. The “Jiulong Sword” buried in the Qianlong tomb is even more powerful; it is “One sword kills four people”. Anyone who comes into contact with this sword has died abnormally one after another —
Sun Dianying died in the People's Liberation Army prison after being taken prisoner;
Dai Li died when the plane crashed into a mountain;
Third, Han and Ma were shot dead;
Kawashima Yoshiko was executed after being sentenced to death.
The story here is quite twisty, yet legendary, and it's amazing. After Dai Li received the Jiulong Sword from Sun Dianying, he temporarily called Ma Han, the director of the Pingjin Office of the Military Statistics Bureau, for the third generation to keep it and give it to “Principal Jiang” at a convenient time, but in the end, an accident occurred.
Ma Hansan made up his mind and wanted to misappropriate the sword and took it as his own, but the treasure should not be owned by Ma Hansan. At one point, Ma Hansan, under the pseudonym “Long Wen,” went to work in the occupied area of the Japanese army near Zhangjiakou and was caught by the Japanese special services agency that received intelligence. In order to survive, Ma Hansan not only gave away many secrets of the military system, but also sacrificed the valuable “Jiulong Sword,” which saved his life. Dai Ga, who has always been known for her sensitive sense of smell and is the head secret agent, was completely unaware of this.
In the end, the sword fell to Japan's top secret agent, Kawashima Yoshiko. Kawashima Yoshiko is a famous Japanese female spy who spies “Ichigana.” She was originally the 14th princess of Prince Su in the late Qing Dynasty and the cousin of the last emperor, Pu Yi. In addition to Kawashima Yoshii, she also has many aliases such as Jin Seizo, Kim Mengzhi, Kinbeki, Kawashima Yoshiko, and Kawashima Ryosuke. After the 1911 Revolution, she was adopted by the Japanese ronin Kawashima Naniwa as an adopted daughter, so she had a strange and strange Japanese name.
Kawashima Yoshiko served as a pseudo-Manchukuo “female chief” and “commander-in-chief of the Angoku Army,” and later mainly engaged in espionage activities. At the time of Japan's defeat in 1945, Ma Hanzan arrested Kawashima Yoshiko in Beiping on October 11, 1945 because of a “history of change”, lest it be revealed by Kawashima Yoshiko. In 1948, Kawashima Yoshiko was sentenced to death and executed in a prison in Beiping.
No matter how you think, Ma Hansan got it wrong. Ma Hansan invited credit and reported that he had caught Kawashima Yoshiko, a Japanese spy. After hearing that they had been caught, Dai Gi personally flew to Beiping for arraignment. During the interrogation, Kawashima Yoshiko revealed that Ma Han-san had changed his mind, and Dai Ga immediately sent someone to Kawashima Yoshiko's home to find the Dragon Sword.
Then the sword passed to Dai Ga again, and it was the turn of this big secret agent who had bad luck...
