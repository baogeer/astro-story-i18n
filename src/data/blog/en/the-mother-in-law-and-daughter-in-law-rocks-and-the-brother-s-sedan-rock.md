 People who have been here for thousands of years have a legend: a long time ago, there was a family nearby. The daughter-in-law was very aggressive and often abused the elderly mother-in-law. There was torrential rain one day, and my mother-in-law was too late to harvest the rice, so she got wet from the rain. When the daughter-in-law found out, she beat and scolded her mother-in-law. When the mother-in-law fled to the opposite mountain, she couldn't run anymore and shouted, “Oh my God, is there any reason!” Lightning thundered in an instant, and the daughter-in-law's head was cut off.
On this day, the daughter-in-law's brother brought a car to pick up his sister home. He happened to see her sister's head being torn off by lightning. He was so scared and sad that he couldn't walk anymore, and the car stopped. Let's take a look at Shibao Mountain across from Grandma's Wife Rock. There are also two stones there. The smaller one in front looks like a man carrying a burden, and the larger one in the back looks like a car. These two stones are called “Grandpa Jiao Rock”. They are across the stream from Grandma's Daughter Rock and form a fantastic set of landscapes. Precisely because of this set of natural landscapes and legends, the local daughters-in-law are very respectful to their mother-in-law.
