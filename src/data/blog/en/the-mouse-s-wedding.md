 A long time ago, there was a mouse couple. They were all very old, and their daughter had reached the age to discuss marriage.
Seeing that the New Year is just around the corner, Mouse Dad and Mouse Mom are in a hurry to find the greatest and most talented husband in the world for their daughter. As a result, early the next morning, Mouse's father and Mouse's mother walked out of the house and began searching for Ruyi Lang Jun for their daughter.
At this point, Prince Sun rose from the East, bringing a ray of light to the earth. Father Mouse and Mother Mouse looked at each other and smiled, and invariably said that Taiyangong was the ideal target we were looking for! When Grandpa Sun learned about their intentions, he couldn't help but smile and tell them that although I can shine brightly and bring warmth to the family, when dark clouds come, I become bleak and dull. Therefore, dark clouds are the greatest in the world. Moreover, I'm already at this age, so I'm really unfit to be your son-in-law.
Father Mouse and Mother Mouse felt that Grandpa Sun's words made a lot of sense, so they planned to find Dark Clouds.
As they were about to leave to find the dark clouds, the sky suddenly darkened. It turns out that Wu Yun just happened to worship Prince Sun. When he learned about the arrival of Father Mouse and Mother Mouse, he hurriedly said, “Ah! No, even though I can block Prince Sun's light, I'm not the best; the wind is your ideal target, because as soon as he comes, I'll be blown all over and out of my head; he's the greatest in the world.”
When I said it was too late, then suddenly, as soon as I cried out, the wind waved his cape, and the energy flew over.
At the time, the house was being blown east and west, and I felt that the wind was indeed as powerful as the dark clouds said, very strong.
Prince Sun and Wu Yun tried their best to select the son-in-laws of Fengzuo's father and mouse's mother.
The wind was a bit embarrassed by what they said, “Don't look at me, I'm very powerful sometimes, but as long as I have a wall, I can throw it on the ground, and I'm hurt all over my body! So, in my opinion, the wall is the most talented and greatest in the world; you should go find the wall to be your son-in-law.
The mouse's dad and mouse's mom heard this, looked at the vast grassland all around, and said to the wind, “This place is empty. Where can you go find a wall?”
The wind said, “You walk all the way in this direction. Once you reach a village, you can find a wall.”
Mouse's dad and mouse mom had no choice but to keep walking. After walking for several days, they finally arrived at that village. As soon as Mama Mouse's eyes lit up, she said, “Sure enough, there's a wall there!
They hurriedly ran past. While they were preparing to ask Wall to marry their daughter, they saw Wall saying with a sad face:
“Look! You mice just love to punch holes in my body; there's really nothing I can do about you.”
Turns out, a young and strong mouse was digging a hole under the wall at this point!
It wasn't until this point that Dad Mouse and Mother Mouse suddenly realized that they also had the ability to make others envious and helpless. So I married my daughter to this young, strong mouse. This day coincides with the 3rd day of the Lunar New Year, so people call it the “Mouse Wedding Day.”
