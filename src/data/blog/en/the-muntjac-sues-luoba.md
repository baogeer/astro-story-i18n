Once upon a time, a chamois ate wheat in a wheat field. Robana was carrying an old musket and aimed at Suede. With one shot, Suede fell after running a few steps.
After Zhizi died, it was very unconvinced. He asked someone to write a sheet of paper and go to King Yan Luo to complain: “Dear Lord Yan Luo, you have worked hard! Now, with a feeling of great sadness and indignation, I am suing a person named Luoba from the Yi ethnic group who illegally possessed a gun and killed innocent people indiscriminately. Without any warning, while I was hungry and had no choice but to eat a few human grains, he hid on the ground and shot me to death. Lord Hades, is stealing a few wheat trees a capital crime? You know, it's not easy for us jays to live. Tigers and leopards hunt us down, but we run faster than them, and most of them can save lives; in the past, when people got knocked down, we didn't walk on the road and rarely caught us; people also invented crossbows, but they didn't have a long range. When people get close, we still have time to run away. Not to mention that Rob was shot so fast that it was too late to escape. If adults don't care, our chamois will face extinction. Adults, you must uphold justice, punish evil and promote good, and save our Jizi family!” King Yan heard that the fire was raging here and ordered Rob to be arrested as soon as possible.
After listening to the words on the paper in the shape of Sui Zi, Luo Ba retorted: “Lord Yan Luo, Zi is talking nonsense here and falsely accusing him of being a good person. He said I didn't warn him; this is complete nonsense. Lord Yan Luo, you know best how loud the shots are. Not only can you hear any number of mountains and recesses nearby, unless your ears are deaf; second, Suizi says that crossbows aren't powerful, then why do people say “open guns are good for hiding dark arrows and hard to defend”? Are you more powerful than humans; third, Zizi said that eating a few grains of wheat is not a crime of death. In your opinion, it's not a capital crime; it's a trivial matter. But did you know how easy it is for people to grow a grain tree on 'Harvest Day in the afternoon, sweat drips down the soil; who knows how hard it is to cook a Chinese meal? ' Suede is good for drinking and laziness. When people work under the hot sun, you hide and sleep in the shade of trees. People are tired. When you get home to eat and sleep, you stretch out and slack the crops to death. If you don't agree, alright, you guys go grow food. We are here to eat only crops, and if we want to kill them, we want to beat those who follow you. Fourth, Jizi said that our people are responsible for whether you can survive. This is even more ridiculous. Haven't you heard of it? “Natural selection, survival of the fittest”. You can run past tigers and leopards; you have survived; if you can dodge crossbows, you have survived; you can only find a way to avoid being shot; this is your only hope for survival. Without me in the world, people would invent more advanced guns and ammunition. Can you finish it? King Hades is also under no obligation to keep heaven by your side every day to protect you.”
Speaking of this, King Hades and the others present couldn't help but applaud. King Yan Luo handed down the verdict: “Roba was acquitted, peeled off the skin of the chamois, ate the meat of the chamois as compensation for eating crops and false accusations against Roba. Suizi knocked down 18 layers of hell to see the aftereffects.”
