Old White has two daughters who look like flowers. Her older sister is named Jenny, and her younger sister is Lily. White has publicly announced that her Wanguan family estate will be split equally between her two daughters. Soon Jeanne got married and married to John, a police officer. Jack also recognized Jenny, but he also smiled and politely said, “I'm very happy to meet you, Mrs. John.”
Since then, Jack has often gone to John's house to eat. John had a lot of trust in his best friend, and he didn't notice anything wrong between Jack and Jenny. On a night full of thunderstorms, Jack was playing at John's house. John suddenly received an assignment to rush to the police station immediately. “Jack, the weather is terrible tonight, please stay and take care of Jenny for me.” John made up his mind and left in a hurry. The only thing left in the huge room was Jenny and Jack. They all had a bit of a sense of emptiness. The outside was windy and rainy, and the house was warm and comfortable. An old pair of lovers started talking about the past, and their old love was reborn. They hugged each other tightly...
