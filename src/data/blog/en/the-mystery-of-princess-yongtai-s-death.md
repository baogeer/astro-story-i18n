 Princess Yongtai, Xiang Huan Yi; there are many opinions and confusion. Born in the imperial family, the daughter of Tang Zhongzong, Li Xian, and the granddaughter of Wu Zetian; Xi Ronghua happened to be only 7 out of 10; she hated impotence and fell early, causing Xiao Qiang to return to the West. Life is honorable, burial is prominent, and the cause of death is mysterious.
It is said that Princess Yongtai was executed by Wu Zetian. “The New Tang Book: The Chronicles of Empress Shengwu” says, “In September of the first year of Dazu, Jinshen killed Shao Wang Chongrun and Wu Yanji, the lord and son-in-law of Yongtai County.” “Empress Dowager Zechen: Empress Zetian Shunsheng” says, “Empress Empress Dowager Chunqiu, the brothers Zhang Yizhi, and Shao Wang Chongrun, and his sister Wei Wangwu Yanji, the lord and son-in-law of Yongtai County. Yi Zhi sued the Empress Dowager. In September, the Empress Dowager Renshen all forced them to commit suicide.” According to historical records, they are all Lee Chung-yun, Prince Yide, and Princess Yongtai, who were gifted to death by Wu Zetian for discussing Wu Zetian's private life.
It is said that Princess Yongtai died of a difficult delivery. In the 1960s, the tomb of Princess Yongtai was excavated, and the tomb was given the monument “Zhiming of Yongtai Princess in the Past of Datang”. Some people have recovered Princess Yongtai's pelvis based on 11 pelvic fragments unearthed from Princess Yongtai's tomb. After scientific measurements and assessments, it was determined that “all parts of Princess Yongtai's pelvis appeared to be narrower than that of women of the same age, and combined with the epitaph “Pearl Destroyed the Moon”, it was determined that “Princess Yongtai died of difficult delivery” rather than her grandmother Wu Zetian.
It is said that Princess Yongtai was poisoned by Wu Zetian. The epitaph of Princess Yongtai includes the phrase “since Jiao lost her majesty, Luan is alone; the fire has not moved, and the boat is empty”. Some people have judged that “since Jiao lost her supremacy, Luan is alone”, indicating that Princess Yongtai's husband Wu Yanji died from a sharp blade, and that Princess Yongtai still lives alone. “The acacia fire hasn't moved, the boat is empty”, which shows that the fire that burned the big acacia tree killed Wu Yanji. Although it did not affect the princess, she died soon after. The epitaph also includes the phrase “When the Pearl Emblem Destroys the Moon, Ten Mile Grudge Has No Fragrance”. “Pearl Te” is a pregnancy, and “Pearl Child Destroyed Moon” is the author of Zhi Wen's metaphor that the princess was destroyed by Wu Zetian. Due to her pregnancy, she was forced to commit suicide by taking medication due to not being executed or stabbed. This is “the death of her majesty from Jiao, and Luan is alone”, the so-called “widow life” of Princess Yongtai.
