 The millionaire has three daughters with completely different personalities. The eldest daughter is very beautiful and likes to show off; the second daughter is frugal and inherits her father's virtues of hard work; and the younger daughter is ugly and tasty and lazy all day long. Even so, the rich don't have a particular preference for any of them.
The rich man grew older and became seriously ill. The old man understood in his heart that he didn't have much time left, so he found someone to write his will and dictate his will. Soon after, he passed away peacefully in his sleep.
The lawyer officially announced the will after the old man's death. I didn't expect the will to be very strange: “I appointed my wife Amy as the agent for my three daughters. She evenly distributes all of my assets to the three daughters, but they are not allowed to own or enjoy their share of the property.” The old man said this in his will.
Furthermore, he said, “If the inherited property is removed, each person must pay 1,000 pounds to their mother.”
The wife couldn't understand the intentions of the elderly. None of the lawyers in the city were able to find a perfect answer to this mysterious will, so “the rich man's will” became a topic of mutual debate between people in and outside the city.
Since she couldn't find an answer, the mother decided to distribute according to her own ideas, and the eldest daughter was given some gorgeous clothes, women's ornaments, bath supplies, and servants to serve. The second daughter received a flock of sheep, land, farmhouse, cattle, donkeys, workers, and all agricultural tools. The little girl also has a cellar with a large collection of fine wines, as well as a high-class mansion and a garden full of all kinds of flowers and plants.
The wife uses this method of distribution, mainly based on the personalities of the three daughters, to give them what they like and want the most.
Just as the mother was preparing to hand over the distributed wealth to her daughter, suddenly some people in the crowd loudly lamented, “If this rich man knew that his assets were handled this way, I think he would never die in his tomb. In such a big city, no one has found an answer to this riddle, which is really surprising.” At this point, there was an uproar and a lot of public discussion.
“Since you say that, sir, you must have come up with an answer.”
The man finally spoke up and said, “Actually, the reason is very simple. If the house, furniture, garden, and aged cellar were given to the second daughter, clothes, jewelry, and servants to the three daughters who love to drink, and the land, agricultural tools, and workers to the vain eldest daughter, the three of them must be very dissatisfied with this distribution method, so the older daughter sells those agricultural tools and land in exchange for gorgeous clothes and ornaments; the third daughter will also sell off those jewels to buy alcohol; the second daughter is not used to living in a building, and she will also sell it.
First, it doesn't meet the rules for wealthy people; they are not allowed to own or enjoy their share of the property. Since all of their assets are sold out of hand, they have money to pay their mother 1,000 pounds.”
After listening to it, everyone suddenly realized that they admired that person's wit.
