 There are different opinions about Shih Tzu's death, both among people and historians. Some people think that Shih Tzu eloped with Fan Li; others think that Shih Tzu died when he lost his footing and fell into the water; others think that Shih Tzu was killed by his adulterous wife; even more so, there are those who believe that Shih Tzu was killed by Fan Li. So how did Shih Tzu die?
Shih Tzu is one of the few most beautiful women in Chinese history, and one of the few most widely popular women. Xi Shi, the name Yiguang, is a native of Vietnam during the spring and autumn period. She was born in Ziluoshan Village, Zhuji, Zhejiang. Shih Tzu is one of the four greatest beauties in ancient China, also known as Xizi.
Naturally beautiful. At that time, Vietnam claimed sovereignty over Wu Guo, and Yue Wang went to court and tried to restore the country. At the height of the national disaster, Shih Tzu endured the burden of humiliation and saved the country. Together with Zheng Dan, she was dedicated to Wu Wangfu by Yue Wang Goujian. She became Wu Wang's favorite concubine and ruined Wu Palace to dominate.
She was born in Ziluo Village, Zhuji, Zhejiang, Vietnam during the Spring and Autumn Warring States period. Her real name is not Shih Tzu, but since she is naturally beautiful and beautiful, and was born in Nishimura, her neighbor on the left and right side called her “Shih Tzu,” meaning a very beautiful woman. This woman, who is passionate about her country, is bound to be a victim of political strife from the time she died. When she had just turned 15, Wu Guo's monarch and husband destroyed Vietnam in the name of avenging her father's death and trampled Yue Wang Gou to Wu Guo as a slave.
After all the efforts of Yue Wang Goujian, after three years, the husband finally agreed to let Goujian return to his hometown. After returning home, the first thing Goujian did was select a group of beautiful women from Vietnam to give to Wu Guo in order to paralyze the poor. Under these circumstances, Shih Tzu was admired by Fan Lixiang for her beautiful figure. At Fan Li's suggestion, Gou Jian dedicated Shih Tzu to Wu Wangfu. After being dedicated to her husband, Shih Tzu did not disappoint Gou Jian. With her charming face and superb piano, chess and dance, she quickly became Wu Wang's favorite concubine. Confused by her, her husband relaxed his guard over and over again, thus creating favorable external conditions for Vietnam's revival. Ten years later, under her guardianship, Vietnam raised an army to destroy Wu, and her husband was eventually forced to commit suicide by drawing a sword.
Shih Tzu was also honored to return to his hometown. According to common sense, a person like her, who has made such great sacrifices and contributions to the country, should be respected by the king after returning home. However, her end was not proportional to her efforts.
There are different opinions about the final outcome of Shih Tzu, both in historiography and in folklore. There is an opinion that after returning home, Shih Tzu and Fan Li went into hiding in the Five Lakes. As early as the Goujian-style Fan Li held beauty pageants all over the country, Fan Li and Shih Tzu had already met and developed into lovers. However, in the face of the national disaster, they gave up their love for a while. After the defeat of Wu Guo, Fan Li already had a desire to retreat, because he saw through that Gou Jian was a monarch who could only share suffering and not share wealth. After Xi Tzu returned to China, he successfully fled with her and returned to the Yin River Lake, where it didn't end up.
There is some rationality in this statement. According to historical records, Gou Jian was indeed a monarch who could only share suffering and not become rich. With Fan Li's cleverness, he worked with Gou Jian for more than ten years, and it is impossible not to see the dark side of Gou Jian, so it is plausible that Fan Li resolutely chose to go back to seclusion with his lover after becoming famous. Folklore's claim that Fan Li went boating in the Five Lakes further confirms this.
There is also an opinion that Shih Tzu was pushed into the water by her husband's wife and drowned. According to Feng Menglong's “The Chronicles of Eastern Zhou” and Mr. Bai Yang's “The Death of the Empress”, on the night Shih Tzu returned home, Yue Wang Goujian asked her to sleep. In his opinion, Shih Tzu can sleep in the same bed with Wu Wang, and of course he can also spend a good night with him. However, the husband's behavior greatly irritated his wife, who shared his suffering, so she took advantage of her husband's lack of preparation and threw Shih Tzu to the bottom of the water, and a generation of beautiful women were inexplicably buried at the bottom of the lake.
This statement also has a certain degree of credibility. It is important to know that at the time, Shih Tzu was used as a tool to be used, and after achieving the goal, Shih Tzu had no political use value. However, Gou Qian was still thrilled with her enchanting face, and it was not impossible to call Shih Tzu a nap under such circumstances. It is also reasonable for his wife to sink Shih Tzu to the bottom of the water in order not to repeat Wu Wang's mistakes.
There are also people who think that it is true that Shih Tzu sank to the bottom of the water, but that it was not the fault of the original wife, but rather her lover Fan Yu, but this view is less credible. Fan Li is not a ruthless and unscrupulous person. Since he has decided to leave Vietnam, he has not treated his lover like this.
Others believe that Shih Tzu was not pushed to the bottom of the water, but that he accidentally fell into the water and died. This view is based on a poem by the early Tang poet Song Zhiwen, “Once the Old Capital, Looking for Beauty; Birds Dream Amazing, Fish Fear Lotus Flowers” and Li Shangyin's poem “Jingyang Miyai Remains Sad; Long Luan Says to Die; If the Water Outside Wu Wanggong Palace is broken, the muddy mud still buries Xishi.” It is difficult to determine whether its credibility is geometric.
However, the poem was originally processed by the poet; it is only a literary genre, and it is difficult to use it as historical evidence. Seen from this point of view, it is likely that the claim that Shih Tzu fell into the water was just a reasonable imagination of the poets at the time, and future generations mistook it for real based on these poets' imaginations.
According to legend, after Wu died, he drove a flat boat into Taihu Lake with his beloved Fan Li, and ended up unsure; another story is that Wu was drowned in the middle of the river after his death. In fact, since there are no clear records in history books, Shih Tzu's fate has been questionable throughout the ages. However, the popular version favors the former, so Shih Tzu is undoubtedly the happiest of the Big Four.
Since ancient times, many people have lost their lives; this is a fact that has been proven countless times in history. It is logical that Shih Tzu, who was born in a rural area, became a victim of a political struggle due to her charming appearance. After completing her mission, she also became a victim of a court struggle. As to whether she retreated from the river and lake with Fan Li or was sunk to the bottom of the water, historians have yet to investigate further.
