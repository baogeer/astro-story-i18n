 Once upon a time, the earth was chaotic, with no flat ground, no rivers, no oceans, no grass, no trees, no wind, only stones mixed with water.
There is a sun in the sky, and the Sun God has a pair of children. The male name is Ning Guan Zhi Yi, and the female name is Ma Zhang Weishun. They are the ancestors of the Jingpo people. They had a son named Ning Guanwa.
Ning Guanwa gradually grew up and was determined to tidy up the earth. He said goodbye to his parents and went to the earth to work. The Sun God gave him strength. He hammered the ground with a large stone, cut a trench with a conical stone, and used a hemp snake as a ruler to measure the ground.
Ning Guanwa used a stone cone to cut out many deep trenches, and the water flowed out along these deep trenches — there were rivers on the ground. The water flowed to its lowest point and gathered where Ning Guanwa hit the heaviest — it became an ocean. Ning Guanwa hit the earth with a stone hammer, and the earth became uneven. The place where it was recessed became a dam and a flat ground, and the place that protrudes became large, small, tall and uneven mountains. The area that was too low was filled with water, and Ning Guanwa wanted to dry it out and turn it into a flat ground.
Ning Guanwa is busy. His father sent Xinwabao to call Ning Guanwa. Xinwaniao flew to Ning Guanwa's side and said to Ning Guanwa, “Go back soon! Your grandma is dead!” Ning Guanwa was only dumbfounded when she heard it, then answered, “I'm busy. Grandma will die when she dies. Her mother's name can be changed to her father's name!” After speaking, I buried my head and got busy with work. Originally, Ning Guanwa was her mother's last name, but she changed to her father's last name later. Ning Guan Zhi Yi and Ma Zhang Weishun sent Xinwabao to drive Ning Guanwa home. This time, as soon as Xinwabao flew to Ning Guanwa's side, they said, “Go back soon! Your dad died too!” Ning Guanwa cried when she heard the news of his dad's death: “You can't change your father's name!” I had not dried up all the accumulated water, so I rushed home. Later, the accumulated water that was not drained became a lake.
Ning Guanwa came home crying. Seeing that both his parents were still alive, she was surprised to ask, “Yo, grandma, grandma, what the hell is going on with this?” Grandpa and Grandma told him, “We have raised you as an adult and have done our best. Now you are old and frail, and you will die soon. There are still many things I need to tell you before I send Xinwaniao to call you back.” Ning Guanwa cried sadly and asked, “You two are gone, what should I do?” Ning Guan Zhi Yi said, “After we die, our flesh and blood will turn to dirt, sweat and hair will turn into grass and trees, and our intestines and lungs will become all kinds of treasures. It's up to you to prosper this world. From now on, dirt will be your parents. To make a living as a crop, we must listen to the cries of the birds. The ground is cut down in January, planted in March, planted in May, and harvested in October. That way future generations won't go hungry.” Ning Guanwa sobbed and said, “Well, which one do you want to give grandpa and grandma vibes?” Ning Guan Zhi Yi replied, “I want to leave the feeling of Grandpa and Grandma to Qingfeng.” Ma Zhang Weishun went on to say, “The dance of the Sun God — wood brain is a dance celebrated by heaven and earth, and the birds will teach you about it.”
Later, Ning Guan Zhi Yi and Ma Zhang Wei Shun died. Their flesh and blood became dirt, their sweat and hair turned into grass and trees, their internal organs became all kinds of treasures, and the fresh breeze was their scent.
Ning Guanwa met Nong Ruo Mazabang, the daughter of Dragon King, and they plan to become husband and wife. However, Nong Ruo Mazabong is a dragon girl, and her body smells fishy like a dragon. Ning Guanwa asked the Sun God. Following the Sun God's instructions, Nong Ruo Mazabang passed through in front of Bang Bang Caobang. Passing through the thickets of Bang Bang, the fishy smell of Nong Jo Mazabon disappeared. From that time until now, when people get married, Jingpo people have to lead the bride through the colorful gate made of Bang Bang grass, calling the bride a “fishy woman” — Mu Nan Nong.
Ningguanwa Mogan and Nong Ruo Mazabang gave birth to many children, and they have become all nationalities today. The Jingpo people are Madu — senior. Jingpo people call the place where Ningguanwa Mogan and Nongjomazabong live as the center. All ethnic groups split up from that place and spread all over the world.
Therefore, the Jingpo people think that the ancestors of the Jingpo people were Ningguanwamogan and Nongromajabang. Ning Guanwa's parents are the earth covered with earth; they are children of the god Sun. All nations on earth are brothers and sisters.
