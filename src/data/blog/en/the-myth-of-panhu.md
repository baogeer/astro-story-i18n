 The steps of the elders of time slowly entered the end of the Yuan Dynasty. At this time, the Yuan dynasty was like an ancient autumn tree. The leaves were falling and falling, society was in turmoil and uneasy, and the people were in deep heat. The Luo people living in Katian were afraid and helpless. They had no choice but to ask for the blessings of the gods and spirits. In February in Yangchun, they went to the temple to make a wish and pray for the safety of King Wugu and the Lord Chenghuang. If the heart is sincere, then there is a good harvest in the fall. As a result, on October 10th, all householders gathered at the temple to hold a grand celebration to celebrate good weather, rain, and a good harvest of grains. Since then, this event has slowly been passed down, taking its name “Qinggutang”, called “Qinggutuo” in the Miao language. Every year, once every year, one day and one night in Xiaoqing, and three days and three nights in Daqing.
The Keikodo event is divided into three stages: “Invite God,” “Celebrate God,” and “Send God.” The gods invited mainly invited ancestors and gods, such as the ancestors of humans, the emperor of the earth, emperor of the people, and Pangu; the ancestors of the Miao tribe, Lu You and Dou, the ancestors of the Miao people, shegong, shegong, shengong, zhongjun, and general Xiayong. The purpose of the event was to educate people not to forget the ancestors such as Huan Xuan, Lu You, Miao Father, Sui Clan, and King Wutong. They created human society, invented Wugu medicine, and started human civilization. As it is sung in “Please Gods Song”:
The sky is sacred, and the Holy Spirit has a sense of descending from the earth.

There is a shrine on the back wall of the main hall of the temple, and various gods are enshrined. The one enshrined in the center is the three-headed and six-armed King Pangu (that is, Panjiao). The Miaoyao compatriots in Chengbu revere him as the pioneering human ancestor. Therefore, the first god of sacrifice and the god of first request in the Keikodo drama is Panjiao. When we were kids, we often heard the old people “talk about money,” tell stories about “Pangu” and “Bian Gu,” and we know the ancient logic of “Pangu opens up the world.” Chengbu Miao District still maintains customs of respecting dogs and loving dogs, such as children wearing dog hats, scooping the first bowl of rice for the dog to eat, putting meat and tofu to respect dogs during the New Year holidays, etc. The Yao people, in particular, celebrate the “Wine Bar Festival” and “Panwang Festival” every September. The Pu family of the Yao ethnic group in Qishutian, Huilong Village, Lanrong Township, in Chengbu has kept a precious historical book, “Judging the King”, which is commonly known as the “Roller Mountain List.” It is said that a total of 5 copies were distributed, one to Golden Yao Township in Xinning County, Hunan, one to Huangshan Old Village in Qingyuan Township, Chengbuyuan, one to Zhaizixi, Tingping Township, Chengbu, and one unknown.
According to the “Judgment of the King”, among the descendants of Emperor Huangdi, Emperor Yu is very famous. He was the first to become a human king, laying the foundation for the country. People at the time called him King Gao Xin, and Miao Yao called him the King of Judges.
When Wang Jing came to power, one year, her big-eared queen suddenly had earache. There was no cure after many treatments. It wasn't until one day three years later, when a Taiji pulled a gold bug about three inches long from the queen's ear, and the earache disappeared. The queen covered this bug with a fence, covered it with a plate, and fed it every day. Before I knew it, this golden bug turned into a dragon dog. The whole body was colorful, the golden light was shining, and it was three feet tall. The critic named it Panjiao and accompanied it every day.
Suddenly it was reported that King Guofang (known as King Gao by Miao Yao) made a mess, commended Wang's anger, and announced to the group of ministers that whoever can slay King Gao's head to offer me will marry the princess. There was no response in the court because they feared that Gao, Wang Bing would be strong, and Ma Zhuang would be ferocious. At this point, Pan Yu jumped out of the left temple to meet the critic, and said loudly, “I have my own plan to repay the Lord's grace, prosper, strong rhetoric, and kill Gao Wang; there is no need to use thousands of troops and horses.” The critic Wang is happy. He wants spiritual help. King Gao will die. He is only worried that he will go to a foreign country, cross the sea for thousands of miles, and flood waves. It can be reached in less than a day. How can food rations be solved on the way? Pan Yi answered that humans can only endure one day of hunger, and I can endure seven days of hunger. Please be carefree. Commenting on Wang Daxi, the public said that Panyu has a human spirit. If successful, I will definitely give you a palace daughter. It was ordered to be given Baiwei Food. After the meal, they quit, and Baiguan sent it outside the court. After Panyu left the palace, he sprinted and floated through the sea. After seven days and seven nights, he finally arrived in a foreign country and headed straight to King Gao's military camp. It coincided with King Gao sitting in the dynasty. He was extremely happy. He couldn't help but laugh out loud, “Gong Wang is afraid he will die out soon. Even dogs with such spirituality can't be fed. They actually run for me. It seems that God of heaven must have helped me get rid of Jing Wang. As the saying goes, pigs come to be poor, dogs get rich, and foreign objects come to North Korea, our country will win!” As a result, Gao Wang set up a large banquet to celebrate the arrival of Panjiao, and entertain the plate with good wine and good meat. Every day, bring a plate into the inner palace and arrange to be accompanied by a palace lady when sitting in the morning, giving them delicious taste and love like jewels. Unwittingly, for a few days, Gao Wang forgot about state affairs, loved flowers and enjoyed the moon, played with the palace maiden, drank and had fun, drunk, unconscious, and slept in a Chinese military account. Pan Yu took advantage of this opportunity to violently bite off King Gao's head, put his back on his shoulder, run out of his military account, get rid of his pursuers, run wild to the seaside, fly over the rough sea, and return to the front of the judging king's palace. The commentator was sitting in the DPRK. Seeing that his dog had taken the title of King Gao and ran back to the palace, he couldn't help but be overjoyed. He asked the minister to inspect people's heads, ascend to the palace to celebrate, and praise the hereditary government. Pan Yi said, “I was originally an animal; why would I want to be honored and prosperous; I hope my king doesn't lose sight of his foreword.” The critic Wang said, “Pan Yi is in love with Gong Eh, and she will choose a date to get married. You're just a dog, why get married?” Pan Yi said, “Please don't worry; you only need to cover me in a golden bell. After seven days and seven nights, I can become a man.” After hearing this remark and believing it to be true, he wrapped the plate inside the Golden Bell. One, two, or three days passed, and nothing had happened. Until the sixth day, the affectionate princess feared that it would starve to death, so she quietly opened the bell and watched. Pan-chan's body had all been transformed into a human, leaving only one dog head. Unfortunately, the bell had been opened and could no longer be transformed into a human. So the fan jumped out of the bell. The critic immediately ordered a five-color patchwork, embroidered belt, embroidered towel, embroidered pants, and embroidered cloth to cover his entire body. On the morning of the next day, commenting on Wang's orders, and now it's time for good fortune. The court ladies dressed up the princess with a new look, met the princess, got married, and praised Wang Guang for setting up Wang Yan to entertain the ministers.
