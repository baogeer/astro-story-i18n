 The nine older brothers go out hunting every day. Before going out, the older brothers ordered their younger sister to never pick the flowers in the flower pond.
One day, my sister prepared a meal, picked the flowers in the flower pond, and placed them in a vase on the table.
Since then, the nine older brothers have turned into nine crows hovering more than once on the roof. At night, the older brothers gave their sister a dream. As long as they didn't cry or laugh during the day, they would be able to restore their shape after 100 days.
The younger sister persisted for 100 days without saying a word, so that the older brothers took their original form and started a new life.
