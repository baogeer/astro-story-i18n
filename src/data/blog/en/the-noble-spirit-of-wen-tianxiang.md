 Mencius said, “I am good at nurturing my sense of grandeur. After Mencius, he was famous for his magnanimity and promoted Wen Tianxiang, the famous minister at the end of the Song Dynasty. Wen Tianxiang was born in Luling, known as the “Land of Article Festival”. When he was young, he saw portraits of Xiang Xiang Xian Ouyang Xiu, Yang Bangping, Hu Quan, etc. in the Xuegong Temple. They all had the word “loyalty,” so he bluntly swore, “There is nothing wrong with that; he's not my husband!” Wen Tianxiang's life, just like his vow, was loyal to the Song dynasty, strong and unyielding, and generous and righteous, reflecting the majesty and integrity of being alright.
The right path is difficult; retreat from seclusion and keep one's will. Wen Tianxiang and Wen Cai were astonished. He was the top pick in high school at the age of 21. In November 1259, there was an emergency at the Southern Song Border Gate, and Dong Song Chen, an internal servant, advocated moving the capital to Siming. After learning about it, Wen Tianxiang decided to risk his life. At the time, Wen Tianxiang had completed his father's funeral. As soon as he returned to North Korea to serve as a supplementary official, he presented the much-talked-about “The Book I Have Not Done to the Emperor” in the name of a royal devotion to persuade Song Li to protect his country, cut off personal love, and punish his opponent Dong Chen Song Ming Zheng to reassure the army. At the same time, they also put forward four propositions to fight against foreign enemies and protect the people, namely, simple grammar to establish an act, imitate a town for construction, unity to draw troops, and break qualifications to hire people. However, Song Lijong did not respond to this book. Wen Tianxiang felt that the right path would be difficult, and resolutely quit his job and returned home. Later, the imperial court summoned him several times. Since the traitor took charge, his words did not make sense, and his ambition to save the country and people could not be carried out. Wen Tianxiang chose to retreat from seclusion in order to preserve his temperament.
Relief from a ruined home is a source of pride. At the beginning of 1275, the Yuan Army approached Lin'an, and the imperial court ordered them to raise troops from all over the world to serve the king, and conveyed the message to Wen Tianxiang, asking him to “set up duty quickly and go to work.” Under pressure from a strong enemy, the country will fall, and Wen Tianxiang stepped up to take over without hesitation. “The country is suffering from a major disaster; we must defend it from scratch!” Wen Tianxiang has already shown his determination to defend the country in “The Chronicles of Ba Peng Shuying”. At this time, he is even more responsible and irrevocable. However, for a scribe who has long since retired, it is not an easy task to recruit troops and work hard. Other than a royal decree from the imperial court and a Jiangxi ruling title over a decade ago, Wen Tianxiang has no other backing. In order to recruit volunteers, he ran up and down, hired Chen Jizhou and Chen Fengfu as military captains, and joined forces with Ganzhou Haojie. In order to raise military expenses, Wen Tianxiang took the lead in rescuing families and set off a wave of donations. This not only solved the military spending problem, but also greatly boosted morale and strengthened the military spirit. At the beginning of 1276, the 3rd army of the Yuan Army was stationed less than 30 miles away in the northern suburbs of Lin'an. Yuan You Prime Minister Bo Yan ordered Prime Minister Song Ting to go and surrender. The then Southern Song Right Prime Minister Chen Yizhong absconded overnight after hearing about it. In desperation, Empress Dowager Xie had no choice but to be promoted to Prime Minister Wen Tianxiang, who went to Yuanying to discuss surrender matters. Bo Yan originally thought that those who came should bow down and bow down to the court. He didn't expect Wen Tianxiang to be humble or to argue based on reason, so he was furious and threatened to execute Wen Tianxiang. Wen Tianxiang was resolute and fearless. He said, “We are the top prime minister in the southern dynasty, but we owe the death to serve the country and cut through the wall; we are not afraid!” Boyan was helpless and placed under house arrest and taken hostage to Dadu. Halfway through, Wen Tianxiang took advantage of the Yuan Army's inability to escape. After nine deaths, he went south to the imperial court. The famous phrase “Trust is a magnetic needle stone; if you don't guide, you won't rest” comes from the poem “Yangzi River” he wrote when he returned to the mouth of the Yangtze River, which expresses his magnetic loyalty to the Song dynasty.
Imprisoned by Zhi Jian, he has great integrity. At the end of 1278, Wen Tianxiang committed suicide after his defeat and was unfortunately taken prisoner. He wrote the shocking “Crossing Zero Ding Yang” to express his desire to die as a martyr for the country: “After all the hard work, the past has passed, and there are few stars around us. The mountains and rivers were shattered by wind and debris, and the ups and downs of life settled in the rain. Fearful on the beach said fear, and Ling Ding Yangli sighed. Since ancient times, no one has died, leaving Dan's heart in the dark.” When General Zhang Hongfan asked him to write a letter to Zhang Shijie persuading him to surrender, Wen Tianxiang transcribed this poem and sternly stated, “I can't save my parents myself; I just teach people to turn their backs on their parents, okay?” In the dark dungeon, Wen Tianxiang wrote another song “Song of Integrity” to show his intention: “Heaven and earth have righteousness, and they are in a state of chaos. Below is Kawadake, and above is Sunstar. People say they are majestic and full of darkness.” In the face of death threats, Wen Tianxiang was adamant and resolute. In 1282, Kublai personally persuaded him to surrender and tempted him by appointing him as prime minister. Wen Tianxiang was unmoved at all, stating that he would never become a minister with the surname of the second surname; he only wanted to die. He had already prepared his last words: “Kong says Cheng Ren, and Meng takes justice. They only do their best, so be benevolent. What did you learn by reading the Book of Saints? Now and then, how many people deserve it!” Before serving his sentence, Wen Tianxiang went south to bid farewell to his native country and calmly practiced justice.
Wen Tianxiang's majesty and integrity are outstanding in the annals of history. After the Song dynasty, whether feudal rulers, doctors, or the public, they all praised Wen Tianxiang's heroism. Famous generals of the Ming dynasty praised him: “Martyrdom and forgetting, sacrificing one's life for justice. The world is engulfed, and the world is sincere.” Emperor Qianlong of the Qing dynasty commented on him: “Loyalty is not meant to inspire for a moment, to inspire over time, to win glory with the Sun and the Moon.” Wen Tianxiang's majesty inspired countless patriots in future generations. At a time when the country was in distress, they lost their lives and died, succumbed to succession, and served the country faithfully! (Xu Xiaofang)
