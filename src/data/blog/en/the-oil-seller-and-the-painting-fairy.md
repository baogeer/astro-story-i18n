 Once upon a time, there was a young man who carried burdens and walked the streets to sell oil for a living. People called him a “oil seller.” Yurang has not married a wife because his family is poor. On the way one day, he saw someone selling paintings. The painting was a very beautiful young woman. The oil seller was fascinated and immediately bought the painting, then sold no oil. He carefully took the painting home, stuck it right on the wall, then looked at the painting and watched the woman in the painting come alive and laughed at him. For a few days, the oil seller didn't go out to sell oil; he just looked at the painting, but it wasn't possible to continue like this. Sitting on the mountain air, a few days later, he took the burden and went out to sell oil. It's just that every day before he left, he had to take a look at that painting a few times.
When I came back from selling oil at noon, I smelled the aroma of the food in the house. I opened the pot and took a look. Inside was a hot bun. Yuran was hungry, so he devoured it without much thought. It's been like this for days. Whether it's buns or dumplings, or ravioli, noodles, they are all delicious, and hot. When I first started selling oil, I thought my neighbor's lady saw that he had no one to take care of him, so she thanked them. However, the neighbor lady said that she didn't do it; it was strange that Yurang Lang was strange; he made up his mind that he must find this person.
Early the next morning, Yurang left with his burdens as usual, but before he had gone far, he quietly retracted back and hid behind the door of the house. It was almost noon, and I saw a beautiful girl walking down from the painting in the house and starting to cook in the kitchen. Her hands and feet were numb, and the meal was ready after a while. After that, when the girl walked into the house, she had to go back to the painting. The oil seller ran out from behind the door and hugged the girl, begging her not to go back to the painting; the girl agreed.
The girl in the painting became the wife of an oil seller. Later, the oil seller went out to sell oil, and the girl handled household chores at home. The girl is very good at managing a family. The house is well-organized, and her little life is thriving. It's just that the girl always looks at the painting. The oil seller was worried that she would go back to the painting, so she put that painting away.
A few years later, they had an adorable pair of children. Slowly, the children grew up, and that picture was forgotten. One day, while packing up her stuff, the girl in the painting found the picture pressed on the bottom of the box. She unfolded the painting, then went back to the painting and disappeared. When the oil seller came back from selling oil, he looked around and couldn't find it. He suddenly remembered the painting pressed on the bottom of the box, then opened the box. As soon as he saw that the painting was gone, he knew that his wife had gone back to the painting.
Originally, the girl was a painting fairy. Because of her past life, she came to repay her kindness. She was a couple with Yuran for over ten years, and had a pair of children for him. Now that the relationship is over, she is gone.
