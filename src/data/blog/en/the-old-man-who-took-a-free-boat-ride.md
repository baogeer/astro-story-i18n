 There is Prince Li in Jiahe County. He has a fortune, but he is at an age where there are few things to do. On this day, Prince Li had the idea of visiting a foreign friend, so he put on bright and gorgeous clothes, brought a lot of entanglements, packed a large ship of extraordinary style, and sailed down the Yangtze River with his two servants.
On this day, the boat arrived at Xunyang Wharf. While the boat was stopping to cook, an old man asked his servant to take a ride on the boat. The servant Li Fu flatly refused: “Old man, our boy doesn't like to be bothered by outsiders, so go somewhere else!” However, the old man refused and was still struggling there. Prince Li was watching the scenery from the boat. He couldn't help but look at the old man who wanted to take a ride on the boat: the old man was in his sixties, in good spirits, slightly white on his head, and only five feet tall, but his eyes were bright and powerful. Clothes are usually simple but clean and tidy. Gongzi Li thought to himself, “This old man must not have enough money to take a free boat.” As a result, Gong Li sympathetically pointed out: “There is a place on the boat, so take him with you!” The old man even thanked him. But the boatman also heard the news. Seeing that Prince Lee had agreed, the boatman immediately blocked him: “Boy, as the saying goes, 'People have their heart in their bellies. 'How can you just let outsiders you don't know board the boat? Should anything happen, we can't wait! Please think twice, boy!” However, Gong Li was very sympathetic to the elderly and insisted, “In such an old family, we don't help him; it's really unspeakable.” So he turned his head and said to the old man, “Old man, bring your luggage and get on the boat!”
After getting on the boat, the old man saluted Prince Lee again, thanked him over and over again, and inquired about his whereabouts with concern. Gongzi Li laughed: “I have a few friends who all live in the lower reaches of the Yangtze River. I'm idle and uneventful. I want to visit them, and I have no plans. Old man, it's you. Wherever you go, I'll give it to you!” The old man said, “My name is Jin. I am from Jiahe, Hunan, but the teenager left home and haven't been back in decades. Now, maybe where to go, that is, just walk along the way, boy, don't change your itinerary for me. I really don't want to bother you like this.” Prince Li was very happy: “Well, we are 'known in other countries'; I am a native of Jiahe.” As a result, Gong Li was more respectful to Jin Lao. Every day when eating, he always invited Jin Lao to eat together, and the time spent more and more time chatting after the meal. However, Li Gongzi's servant, Li Fu, and Li Gui looked down on this shabby old man. He always showed it in words. After being detected by Gong Li, he harshly reprimanded them.
On this day, the ship is about to enter the territory of Anqing, Anhui. Jin Lao quietly came to Li Gongzi's cabin and said to Gong Li, “Boy, along the way, I have carefully observed you and found that you are a nice person. I'll tell you the truth today! I learned martial arts when I was a kid. I started being a bodyguard when I was young. I am also quite famous in the rivers and lakes. People give me the nickname '100 Shengjin'. After more than 30 years of accumulation, it is already burdened with a lot of money. Because of my age, I started a boat trip in Xunyang after washing my hands five years ago. On that day, I was cooling off at the pier. I saw that your boat was parked at the pier. The helmsman on the boat had a dark crescent-shaped spot on his cheek. I knew that he was the “poison loach,” an Anhui water thief known as a “poison loach” in Jianghu, who only robbed guests to make a fortune. I'm sorry that you were so young that you lost your life in a state of confusion. I couldn't bear it. I wanted to save you before boarding your boat as if you were a guest. You treated me with such sincerity, and I won't hide it from you.” Prince Li was shocked when he heard it: “Oh my gosh! I never thought I'd be targeted by thieves! What should I do now? Old people, there are seven people in their group! I'm so bothered by you, I just can't help it!” Old Jin laughed: “There is an old man here, seven or eight people, the dish is a piece of cake! However, Anqing is their home, and if they don't stop tonight, they'll have to do it. We have to keep an eye on it. You and I have to sleep in a different bed tonight, I see what kind of trick they have!” Prince Lee was skeptical, and his heart “fluttered through”.
Sure enough, in the evening, the boat passed through a bustling town and didn't stop; instead, it pulled the sails forward. Prince Li asked the helmsman why he didn't stop to rest. The helmsman answered, “Of course we have to rush to the road in such a good wind; it doesn't matter if we don't take a break.” Prince Li was even more afraid, so after dark, he quietly changed his bed with Jin Lao, but he didn't dare to sleep at all; instead, he raised his ears and listened to the surrounding movements.
Two flowers bloom, one on each side. Jin Lao certainly didn't look away. This ship was indeed a ship of thieves; the first one was the “Poison Loach.” In the middle of the night, “Poison Loach” invited his teammates to discuss how to act together. “Poison Loach” frowned and said, “Originally, seven of us would have dealt with three of them, which was more than enough, but I didn't expect more bad old men halfway through. It was really troublesome.” One of the robbers said, “It doesn't matter. Even though the old man looked strong, he actually got older. He didn't have to be afraid at all; it just took a little bit more effort.” “Poison Loach” nodded: “Let's get rid of Lord Li first.”
“Poison Loach” brought a few accomplices and quietly arrived at the cabin where Prince Li was staying, carrying bright axes. “Poison Loach” took a deep breath, exhausted the energy of eating milk, and slashed it violently on the head of the person sleeping there. Unexpectedly, the “Poison Loach” only felt a shock in the arm and numb in the mouth. It didn't seem like it had cut a human head, but a tough stone. He quietly said to his partner, “Strange! Did I cut my pillow?” A bandit graciously said, “Just aim the moonlight at his head!” At this point, they heard the sleeping person making a slight, sweet snoring sound. As a result, the “Poison Loach” raised the axe again and cut it over with a “huh” sound. As soon as the axe shook, it quickly bounced back, and hit the “Poison Loach” violently on the head. Old Jin immediately called, “There are thieves!” Prince Lee and his servant quickly came in with the lanterns lit. Old Jin smiled and said to the blood-ridden “Poison Loach,” “Old and immature. It has the false name '100 Victory Gold' in the river and lake, but I've also been able to get in and out of front of hundreds of bandits. Just because of you little thieves, you also want to play with me? Be careful about your dog's life!” As soon as the “poison loaches” “fluttered”, they knelt down and knocked their heads like pounding garlic: “Your old lords and little ones are just as loud as ears. I didn't expect to meet you here. It's really a blessing for us! Needless to say, a few of us have changed our minds, sent the boy to his destination, and never dared to be different again! Please forgive us!” Old Kim smiled and said, “Okay. Forgive you guys that fearless dog. Let's go sailing!”
After the crowd had left, the boy bowed deeply to the old man Jin: “The saving of life will never be forgotten. But when the axe was cut down just now, it was definitely a sweat for you!” Old Jin laughed out loud again: “The qigong I practice is called the 'Golden Bell Cover', also known as the 'Iron Cloth Shirt'. Even if a 10,000 kg stone is pressed against me, it doesn't matter, let alone an axe!”
The next morning, the old man Jin flew away.
