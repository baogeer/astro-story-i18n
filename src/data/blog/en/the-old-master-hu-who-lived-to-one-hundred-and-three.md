 During the Republic of China, the most famous craftsman in the gold mining industry was Hu Santaibao. His original name was Hu Taiyuan. It was only because he was profoundly familiar with the ancient gold mining method, did a few big deals, and he gathered a bunch of local thugs and thieves, and became one of the most popular and thieves of the gold mining faction. He was revered by the rivers and lakes as Hu Santaibao.
Legend has it that in his early years, he took people to mine for gold in Zishui, Hunan, and found an unknown gold mine nearby. After three days of searching, I found a three-kilogram dog head gold underneath a Buddha statue at the site of a temple at the bottom of the river. The image of this dog head gold is surprisingly strange. It looks like a token. In the middle is a woman who has been punished by capital punishment. Two lines of red rust have slipped down her cheeks. Her expression is painful, and she looks full of resentment. In particular, underneath the token, a big 'dead' word was naturally formed, which was frightening.
At the time, an old man in bad clothes from Hubei came. He was horrified at the time, saying that the origin of this thing was unknown. It was originally located under the Great Xiong Treasure Hall in the ancient temple, then later diverted to the Yangtze River and buried in the silt at the bottom of the river. I didn't expect it to be dug up by Hu Santaibao. Unless you throw it back at the bottom of the river and never see the sun, there will be a big disaster.
However, no matter where Hu San Taibao was convinced, he asked people to kick this Taoist out. Who knows, it wasn't long before some people in the group Hu San Taibao went out drinking at night and didn't return overnight. He was found dead on the street the next day. His body was already cold, half of his head had been bitten off by something, and he couldn't die any longer. Immediately after that, I got out of the water again. There is a veteran gold miner with a lot of experience. He was born as a river soldier for generations. In the water, it's like white bars in the waves, which is comparable to swimming fish. However, for no reason, they were caught in the ankle by aquatic plants, and drowned at the bottom of the Yangtze River. When they were discovered, their skin was already white and wrinkled with bubbles.
At first, everyone thought it was a coincidence. Who knows that since then, things have always happened at three or five intervals, and then people have died one after another. Otherwise, they drowned in the water, otherwise they were stabbed to death, and even died in a woman's belly while shopping in the kiln. All in all, the appearance of death wasn't very good. Paper can't hold fire; this story gradually spread.
Everyone was shocked. They all felt that, just like the old saying, the dog head gold they had touched from under the wreckage of the Jiangdi Daxiong Treasure Hall a few days ago and the old objects from the bottom of the river were infuriated. There is a word 'dead' on the token; this thing is very evil when you look at it. Others said that since Grandpa Hu got this piece of money, when he went to the toilet in the middle of the night, he could always vaguely hear women crying in the dark, but they couldn't find anyone.
This incident became more and more sinister. Later, there was really no way to do it. Hu San Taibao was also afraid, so he sent someone to re-invite the old man to help change Feng Shui. Lao Dao ordered Hu San Taibao to sacrifice three sacrifices and led a group of people to bow down and apologize to the king of the Yangtze River. They also ordered that the word “dead” be put back under the Buddha statue at the bottom of the river before it was over.
Strangely enough, since Hu Santaibao followed Lao Dao's instructions and put the dog head gold back to the bottom of the river, all the strange things stopped, and no one suddenly died. This incident has been enjoyed by people in the old Yangtze River, and it has been spread all the time. Later, the famous Grandma Hu suddenly announced that the golden pot had washed his hands and lived until the age of 103 before dying.
