 There is a bay called Cengang on the west coast of Zhoushan Island. There is a high mountain in Cenggang, and the landscape falls down like a hundred feet of white cloth hanging from the cliff. It's really spectacular. Year after year, a deep rock pond called “Dragon Pool” breaks out at the foot of the mountain.
Legend has it that a long time ago, there was a white dragon in the pool. Every time there is a dry and dry year, the white dragon sucks in water from the East China Sea and turns it into Gan Lin, so that villages with a radius of tens of miles can adjust the wind and rain smoothly year by year and be rich in grains. People appreciate White Dragon, and they all call him the “Cengang White Dragon.”
One year, there was another drought, and Bai Laolong was about to absorb water and rain. Unexpectedly, Emperor Yu Huang heard the East China Sea Dragon King's greed and gave up his will, and not allow him to go to the East China Sea to absorb water again. Bai Laolong had no choice but to swallow his voice and return to Longtan. Along the way, I saw that the flowers were dry and burnt, and there were marriages all over the place. I was so upset. While walking, I suddenly heard a cry in my ears. I took a close look. A young woman covered in linen was kneeling on a hot beach and crying in the face of the sea. Bai Laolong couldn't help but feel sad when he came forward and asked, “Why is my sister crying here?”
As soon as the woman took a look, she saw an old man with white beards, white hair, and a friendly face. She looked at herself with concern. As soon as her heart warmed, she cried. It turns out that the woman's name is Qinglian. Her parents and daughters died in childhood. Her brother and sister made the decision to marry a lone kid. The couple were very affectionate. Who knows, the good times didn't last long. Less than half a year after the two became parents, they hit this dry season. There was no hope in the field, so the husband invited the townspeople to go fishing. Unexpectedly, when she first set out to sea, she was overthrown by wind and waves. Her husband fell into the water and died, leaving behind her as a young widow, so helpless, so miserable.
Bai Laolong was very sympathetic when listening and sighed and said, “Don't be too sad, sister-in-law. Since the ancients died, they have not been able to recover. If you don't give up, I'd like to help you catch fish for 3 years.”
Qinglian frowned and burst into tears: “This... how can this be used! As the saying goes, “The sea has no wind and three foot waves,” how can you stand it at such an old age!”
Bai Laolong rubbed his white whiskers and said, “My sister-in-law doesn't worry; the old man has his own reasons.”
Seeing that he was sincere, Qinglian quietly muttered in her heart: “Look at what he looks like, 80% of them were also deserted and affected by disaster. Why don't I build up some bad character and take him in.” So he said, “I've never had a father or mother since I was a kid, so let me recognize you as my dad!” If you say that, you have to prostrate.
Bai Laolong really liked it or not. He hurriedly reached over to lift her up and smiled and said, “Qinger doesn't have to be polite; the old man should be ashamed!”
On the night of the same day, Bai Laolong used Starlight Dingdingdang to build a boat by hand. When Qinglian got home, she took out a dustpan of glutinous rice and made a basket full of glutinous rice cubes according to Bai Laolong's instructions. At dawn the next day, Qinglian arrived at the seaside with her basket. She looked left and right, but couldn't find the broken boat. She was anxious. She overheard someone calling her and looked closely. She saw Bai Laolong crawling out of a brand-new boat sweaty, only to learn that her broken boat had been repaired. She was happy and grateful, and hurriedly greeted him and said, “Dad worked hard! Let's eat soon!”
Bai Laolong ate glutinous rice nuggets, brought a few fishermen, and went out to sea to fish the same day. The fishing boat left the harbor like an arrow and soon reached the East China Sea. Bai Laolong ordered the fisherman to cast a net, but he “snorted” on the deck and dozed off. I saw him snoring while sweating, and huge drops of sweat continued to pour out of his forehead and wet the cabin. The fisherman was so surprised that he didn't dare to wake him up. Soon, the snoring stopped, and I heard him cry like a dream: “Get up on the net! Hurry up and get online!” The fishermen heard the sound and hurriedly rushed to the side of the boat to pull up the net.
Strangely enough, a few people were pulling a huge fishing net, yet it was as light as if they were pulling a wire. Pull it out of the sea. It's actually a net full of rhubarb fish. The golden strips are shiny, and the tail is lively. The fisherman was so surprised that they hurriedly dumped the yellow fish into the cabin. Who knows, uh, ah, the three cabins are full, and the fish in the net haven't finished. Fishermen, look at me; I look at you; they're all stuck.
In the evening, the fishing boat returned home. Seeing that Qinglian caught so many rhubarb fish, she was extremely happy! Everyone heard that this white-bearded, white-haired old man had such great fishing skills, and they all came and asked him to take the lead. Bai Laolong hugged his white whiskers and laughed, and agreed.
Since then, Ambassador Bai Laolong has led everyone to catch fish in the dark. Every time they go out to sea, they return home full of loads. The fish they catch are big and fat, and the lives of the townspeople are getting better day by day.
Half a year has passed in a blink of an eye. One day, Bai Laolong led everyone out to sea again. Qinglian prepared the glutinous rice nuggets that Bai Laolong loves at home, and as usual, she went to the seaside and waited for the boat to return. Wait, wait, until the sun sets and the moon rises, we haven't seen the fishing boats return to sail. Qinglian was hungry and tired, so she leaned on the reef to take a nap...
Suddenly, she saw her emaciated father-in-law hurrying over and said to her with grief and anger:
“Young Er, I'm leaving, take care! If you miss me, go down to Cen Gangling and look for it. There is a nine- and six-foot white cloth hanging in front of my house.” Just talk about it and drift away.”
Qinglian went up to pull it, but it was empty. I immediately woke up, only to learn that it was a dream, and my heart was puzzled. Suddenly, I saw the strong wind on the surface of the sea pouring big waves like small mountains all over the place. How windy is this!
It blew away all the boat piles on the shore and shattered all the hardest reefs. Qinglian looked at the turbulent sea in silence. Thinking about her husband's miserable experience, her face immediately blew up. She shivered all over her body, and lost her voice and cried bitterly at the vast sea.
Day after day, until the morning of the eighth day, the sea was calm, yet Bai Laolong, who had a good heart, never came back. When Qinglian remembered what Bai Laolong said to her in her dream, she decided to go down to Cengangling to find her father-in-law. She made a basket of glutinous rice nuggets that Bai Laolong loves to eat to say goodbye to the townspeople and hit the road.
After walking day by day, after village after village, I finally arrived at the bottom of Cengang Ridge. Looking at it, I saw that the four fields were vast. How many families are there? I couldn't help but feel a little scared. I'm anxious. There is a rock pool at the foot of Mengjian Ridge. A hundred foot waterfall hangs over it, like a white cloth floating on it. Qinglian quickly put down the basket and took out the glutinous rice cubes and threw them in the pool. Throw away a block of glutinous rice and call Dad. After losing for a while and calling for a while, waves suddenly erupted in the pool, and a pair of dragon horns slowly appeared on the surface of the water.
Qinglian was shocked, but she heard a voice from Tan Zhong saying, “Don't be afraid, I am your godfather.”
Qinglian listened and cried sadly, “Dad, why are you like this?”
Bai Laolong said, “I was originally a white dragon in this area. I couldn't imagine helping the people to fish this time. I also offended the Dragon King in the East China Sea. I went to court in front of the Jade Emperor, saying that I had maimed the water people and disrupted the Dragon Palace. Emperor Jade condemned me and banned me from bronze in this Longtan, and I was not allowed to be free.”
Qinglian heard: I'm so excited! He immediately dried up in tears and sighed angrily:
“Alas! Instead, people who work hard for it suffer; what about those with good intentions get sinned instead? What's the point of being alive in such an unfair world!” After speaking, she poured all the pieces of glutinous rice into the pool and jumped down with her. When Qinglian jumped into the Dragon Pool, it became a blue dragon and disappeared with Bai Laolong in the clear pool water.
Later, local people set up two companies to commemorate them: one called the White Dragon Club and the other called the Blue Dragon Club. Once every three years, one general meeting every 12 years. At the time of the meeting, people played gongs and drums, carried beautiful blue and white dragons, and toured the village. It was very lively.
