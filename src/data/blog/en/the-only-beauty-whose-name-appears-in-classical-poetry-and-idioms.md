There are not a few characters involved in the background and allusions of ancient Chinese poetry and idioms. However, it is extremely rare for a person's name to be written both in ancient poetry and widely spread in idioms. Since written records have been recorded in history, only one person has enjoyed this honor. She is Liu Biyu, the home of blogger Liu Jixing and a famous beautiful woman from ancient China.
She is the protagonist of the well-known idiom “Little Family Jasper.” The Chinese Idioms Dictionary explains “Little Family Jasper” like this: “Jasper: Liu Biyu, Queen of Runan. Jin Sun Yu's “Lover's Jasper Song”: “Little Jasper Family Girls Don't Dare to Climb Guide. I was impressed by Lang's strong spirit, so I was able to win over with Jin Lan.” It was later used to refer to the daughter of a small family.”
According to “Jin Shu: The Legend of Runan Wang”, the name of King Runan was Sima Yi, and he was a regular servant of Jin Zong's family, Guan Zhi Sheng. Jasper is the concubine of the Runan king, and the king of Runan cared for her too much. Among Lefu's poems, six poems directly describing jasper have been passed down from generation to generation (the name of jasper is indicated in the text of each poem):
one
When the jasper broke the melon, Lang Wei's feelings were reversed.
Hibiscus Mausoleum is frosty, and the autumn scenery is still good.
two
The girl from the Jasper Family doesn't dare to compete with Guide.
I am impressed and have no sense of shame.
three
The girl from the jasper family doesn't dare to take the lead.
Thanks to Lang's strong spirit, he was able to win over Jin Lan.
fours
When jasper melons are broken, feelings are reversed.
Impressed and unashamed, I went back and hugged Lang.
——Jin Sun Xun
(“Broken melon” is not what modern people call “broken,” but what the ancients said was 28 years old. Because the word “melon” overlaps with two eight characters, broken melon means being 28 years old.)
five
Xing Liang shone at the beginning of the day, and the Cymbidium was very happy.
Jasper is served in a golden cup, and qalu wine helps to add color.
——Liang Xiao Yan
six
The jasper in the palace entered and left the Thousand Flower Grove.
The beads are beaded by tortoise shells, and I am deeply moved.
——Tang Li Jia
Since then, jasper has almost become a generic term for a young and beautiful woman in ancient literature. Jasper is mentioned in many works, and it is highly praised. such as:
Tang He Zhizhang's “Wing Liu”:
The jasper is made up as a tall tree, and ten thousand strips of green silk hang down.
I don't know who cut the fine leaves; the February spring breeze is like scissors.
(Tall willow trees, dressed like jasper, have green threads hanging from all the willow branches. (I don't know who cut these thin willow leaves; the warm yet cold February spring breeze looks like scissors.)
Wu Rong of the 5th Tang Dynasty “Moonlight Chase”:
I once listened to the jasper song from the Maojia, and the snow fell on the autumn river in the clouds.
There is no shadow on the Moon Rin Takako Curtain, and there are waves in the wind passing through the Corridor curtain.
Just a few scattered with the clouds and rain, winning a lot of snow and frost.
I saw a cool shadow in this time and space, and I was accompanied by loud noises.
