 If you want to pick the stars in the sky,
I need the wings of colorful clouds,
If you want to find an orange girl,
You need a heart of gold.
Once upon a time, a song like this was played along the banks of the Brahmaputra River. Many people have heard it, sang it, and then forgot about it. There was only one little prince named Dawa who treasured this song in the most sacred place in his heart. The little prince is long, long, old enough to get married. Kings near and far wanted to give him their own princess, and Prince Kedava always repeated, “I don't love any princess; I only love a beautiful and kind orange girl.”
Actually, what does an orange girl look like? Where does she actually live? They are all just oral legends; for Prince Dawa, this is also a mystery. In front of the royal palace, there is a sweet water well. Half of the city's residents go here to fetch water. Prince Dawa thought; there is gold in the old man's mouth; as long as I go to the well every day to ask, I can always find a way to find the orange girl. As a result, the prince appeared every day on a well made of white stone. He arrived earlier than everyone who fetched the water, and went back later than everyone else. At noon, I didn't leave; I brought a large piece of green-oil chopsticks as a snack. He always repeats the same question to every old man with his back: “Old man, please tell me, are there any orange girls in the world? Where does she live?”
Prince Dawa, wait, ask. It's been a full seven, seven, forty-nine days, but no one has been able to answer his question. He got really angry. He picked up a stone and threw it at the sky. Who knows, the stone fell and smashed an old lady's jug. This is a very old, very old lady. Her hair is white like a conch, and she doesn't even have pearl-sized teeth in her mouth. This water jug is half of her family's property, and now it's broken, so how can she not be sad? Prince Dawa saw his old lady cry more than once. He quickly sent her a gold coin as compensation, and gave her half of the green oil chopsticks he had eaten. The old lady was very grateful. She clasped her hands and murmured, “Bodhisattva, this prince's heart is really as kind as that of an orange girl.”
Prince Dawa heard the orange girl's four waves, and his favorite elephant found a baby the size of a sheep's head. He hurriedly paid tribute to the old lady again and asked, “Old Mom, where exactly does the orange girl you just mentioned live? Can you show me a path so I can meet her?”
The old lady shook her head and said, “They all say snow mountain lion milk is sweet, and you can't get any; they all say that the orange girl is beautiful, and you can't see any of them because the place where she lives is too hard to leave.”
The prince patted his chest and said, “Old mom, please tell me! She just lives on the moon, and I dared to go to Caiyun to find her; she just lives in the ocean, and I dare to search for her in Halong Palace.”
Seeing the prince's affection, the old lady was as strong as a diamond, and she gave detailed instructions on the path to find the orange girl.
Prince Dawa, brave, runs during the day and at night. During the day, the golden sun accompanies him; at night, the silver moon lights him up. He climbed the snowy peak next to the blue sky, conquered the brave snow lion, and rode it over the mountains. He jumped into the choppy river, defeated the vicious dragon, and dragged it through the rapids; he went into the vast forest, and many fierce beasts rushed at him. In the distance, he shoots with a golden arrow; in the distance, he cuts with a sword. After walking the arduous path of 7,749 days, I finally saw a canyon full of flowers. Here, the dense layers of orange trees, tens of millions of golden oranges, shine in a strange light. It gives off an enticing aroma.
The prince danced and sang. He ran into the orange grove in one go. Many orange trees held out their green palms and held his clothes; many, many oranges pleaded with him in a sweet voice:
“Prince! prince! Take me away!”
There were kumquat smiles everywhere, and sweet noises everywhere, making Prince Dawa dizzy. I don't know what to do. At this point, he remembered his grandmother's admonition: “Orange girls live in the tallest orange tree and hide in the biggest and biggest oranges.” The prince looked for three circles from east to west, and three circles from west to east, and finally saw a very tall orange tree. There was a very, very large orange hidden in thick leaves. He didn't say anything or lift his head; he just pat his lips and smiled shyly. The prince reached over to pick it, but the orange ran from one branch to the other, and finally climbed to the top of a tall tree and hid in the middle of a few golden clouds.
Now, the prince is really upset. He wanted to shoot with an arrow and was afraid of hurting it; he wanted to shake the tree and was afraid of touching it, so he stood under the tree and sang:
Beautiful orange girl,
live in tall treetops;
Girl, if you want to,
Please fall into my arms.
Sure enough, as soon as Prince finished singing, the orange fell softly and fell into his arms. The prince was so happy that he held his hands on his chest, turned his head and ran for his hometown.
The prince runs and runs. As soon as he blinks through the forest, he crosses rivers as soon as he jumps, and crosses snowy mountains as soon as he lifts his legs. He came to the foot of a snowy mountain, sat by a moonlike lake, and rested by the Dharma flowers. Here, very close to my hometown, I can see the golden roof of the royal palace in the clouds. He picked up the golden orange from his arms. The more he looked at it, the more happy he was; the more he touched the model, the more he liked it. He forgot his old lady's advice and couldn't help but peel it off. Suddenly, along with the wonderful music and dazzling golden light, a very pretty girl came out of the orange with a smile. She wears a crystal turquoise jewel on her head, and wears a shirt woven with gold thread. Her face is bright red and as tender as an orange; her body is slender and as gentle as an orange tree. Slowly falling on the grass, she gave off a wonderful fragrance all over her body. The prince was so surprised that he didn't even dare to take a breath. He was afraid to blow this fairy-like woman far away again. He quickly took a step forward, grabbed the Orange Girl's streamer, and told her how much he loved her. The orange girl didn't look back or answer; she just pouted her little mouth and smiled softly.
On the edge of the moon lake, the sun was bright and bright, and the lake sang cheerfully. Grandpa Snow Mountain, who had white hair, also had a red smile, because Prince Dawa and the Orange Girl formed a marriage here. They came with lots, lots of flowers and sang lots of love songs. Finally, the prince lay down on a carpet of aromatic herbs, and fell sweetly into a dream, resting on the girl's lap.
There is a witch living in a lakeside rock cave. Seeing that they are in love with each other on a blind date like this, spring water and milk are inseparable, she came up with a vicious idea to frame an orange girl who is as kind as Baek Ta-mu. She became a woman, twistled and walked to the orange girl, glared at her three times, squinted and looked at it three times, and made a fuss and said, “Ah heh! The most beautiful orange girl in the world turned out to be much worse than me!” The orange girl didn't answer; she just smiled. The witch grabbed the orange girl and asked her to take a picture by the lake. She looked better than anyone else. The orange girl even shouted, “No! “No, this will wake the prince up.” The witch said, “Yes, I know you can't compare! If not, wouldn't it be the same as putting the prince on the ground?” The orange girl then held up the prince's head, moved to the flowers she had just picked, and then lay her brightly colored waistband. When they arrived at the lakeside, there were two reflections in the lake: the orange girl was like a golden peacock, and the witch was almost like a black crow.
The witch was defeated, and she still didn't give up. “If you have a gorgeous gold shirt, of course you're better; if I wear your clothes, I'll definitely be prettier than you.” The poor orange girl fell for the witch's trick, changed her clothes to the witch, and was thrown into a very deep lake by the witch while she was illuminated.
The witch jumped to the prince's side in three steps and held the prince's head in her arms one by one. The prince felt as soft as sleeping on wool before, but now he feels as uncomfortable as sleeping on a horn. He soon woke up. He opened his eyes and took a close look, and he felt that the orange girl had changed, ugly, dark, and couldn't help but frown slightly. The witch saw his mind and hurriedly said, “Noble Prince! You slept here for three days, and I stayed motionless for three days. The snowy mountain sun tanned me, and the cool breeze on the lake blew me away!” Prince Dawa thought, “Orange Girl's heart is so kind. Even though she's uglier than before, I can't dismiss her.”
As a result, the prince and the witch had a grand wedding in the palace. The king, who was near and far, brought precious gifts. Residents from all over the city came to dance and have fun in front of the palace. Everyone who has seen this princess has not felt sorry for Prince Dawa, because there really is no place where she can be as beautiful as a young and handsome prince.
After seven days, the Wrangler by Moon Lake came and reported, “Dear Prince, please allow me to report the happy news. A golden lotus flower suddenly grew in the blue waves of Moon Lake!” The prince was so surprised that he quickly followed the Wrangler to the lake. Sure enough, I saw a lovely lotus flower shaking alone on the surface of the lake, and the dew rolling on the petals. It seemed like endless tears. The prince was so compassionate that he asked the Wrangler to take it back and put it in the Buddha hall.
Wonderful lotus, lovely lotus. Its fragrance fills the entire palace, and the brilliance of Jin Huanghuang can be seen from afar. Many, many people came to watch it and couldn't stop praising it. Only the witch with a dark heart knows the origin of the lotus flower. She touched the temple in the middle of the night, crushed it, and scattered it in the back garden.
After another seven days, the old man looking at the flowers came and reported, “Dear Prince, allow me to report a good omen. A tall peach village has grown in the back garden, and the trees are full of sweet fruit.” The prince was even more surprised. When he came to the back garden with his subjects, he saw a tall peach tree with many huge fresh peaches. They had slightly pink lips. The prince was silent, thinking about strange things that occurred one after another. The witch happily said, “O prince, prince! Please give these fresh peaches to your subjects and people to taste, so they can remember your merits!” Prince Dawa felt that her words were reasonable, so he distributed them to his subjects. Everyone came from all sides, one by one, and they ate up after a while.
In the small ravine opposite Wangcheng, there is a very old lady who herds sheep for the king from morning to night. When she arrived with her young son, the peaches had already split. She spent half a day searching in the bush to find a very small, tiny peach. The mother and son treated it as a baby and were happy to bring it back to their small stone house. Grandma let her son eat it, and the son let Grandma eat it. The two of them pushed it back and forth, and finally put it in a sheepskin pocket. Since then, strange things have happened in the little stone house. Every time the old grandmother and little boy came back from herding sheep, they found that the house was well-cleaned, the ghee tea was strong, and the lamb was cooked fragrant. One day, the old grandmother asked her son to drive the sheep to the mountain. She hid outside the stone wall to take a peek, only to see a beautiful girl in a gold shirt inside a sheepskin pocket, busy in the house. The old man ran in with a gust of wind, called for the fairy, and knelt in front of her.
The girl hurriedly picked up the old man and said, “Grandma, I'm not a fairy; I'm the orange girl everyone is familiar with.” Next, she shed sad tears and told the elderly about her experience of being framed by a witch. Grandma kept her in a small stone house, and the family of three lived in harmony.
One day, the orange girl was washing her hair outside the door and was seen from afar by a witch. She screamed and pretended to pass out. The court invited so many doctors and took so many good medicines, but none of them had any effect; it seemed like I was about to lose my breath. One day, the prince went to the witch and cried like a witch. He held the prince's hand and said, “Prince, there is a way to save my life. I don't know if you want to do it or not?” The prince said, “What method? Speak quickly!” The witch said, “In the opposite valley, there is an old lady herding sheep. Her daughter is a demon girl. She brought my illness. I can only recover from death if I use her heart and liver to make soup to drink.” When Prince Dawa heard this, he had no taste in his heart. He felt that the orange girl not only didn't look beautiful, but she was also not kind hearted. How can a kind and pure woman eat someone else's heart?
In order not to make the concubine suspicious, the prince sent three samurai to take heart from the daughter of the old lady shepherd. Following that, he pulled out the fastest chasing horse. “Denaga” and “Denaga” soon surpassed the three samurai. The first one came next to the shepherd's small stone house and heard a parrot shouting in the tree: “Girl! girl! The loathing prince is here! The relentless prince is here!”
Prince Dawa was so annoyed that he tried to shoot the parrot with a golden arrow. Suddenly, someone came out of the little stone house and said, “Prince, please don't shoot innocent birds, or kill the poor orange girl. The witch is trying to eat my heart!” The prince looked back and saw a pretty girl, standing in front of him, wearing a green jewel and a gold coat. Her face was as tender as an orange petal, and her body was as gentle as an orange tree. Oh my god, this isn't the most beautiful orange girl in the world, and who could she be? But if this is the real Orange Girl, then who is the one in the palace?
Grandma, who herded sheep, came over and told the story of the murder of the Orange Girl. Prince Dawa seemed to have woken up from a nightmare. I vowed to kill that vicious witch. It just so happened that when the witch saw the matter unraveled, she showed her vicious form, opened her mouth like a female wolf, and rushed straight from the palace to where the orange girl lived! The prince pulled the bow and put on the golden arrow, “Fuck! Fuck! Fuck!” One arrow at a time sends the witch to hell.
At this point, the snowy mountain rose a rainbow of seven colors, and the meadow bloomed with gorgeous flowers. The small, humble stone house suddenly became a magnificent palace. The palace walls were covered with green orange trees like jasper. The orange trees were covered with bright red fruits like agate, and a burst of wind blew in from the valley. The orange touched the orange and made a pleasant sound. The leaves stretch gently and exude a pleasant aroma.
Prince Dawa and Orange Girl have gone through this suffering and have never been separated since then. They have been affectionate and loving until they have gray hair for a long time.
