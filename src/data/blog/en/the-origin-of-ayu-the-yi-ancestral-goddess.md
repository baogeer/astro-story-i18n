 In the Yangguang Bimo Sutra, “Bi Jun doubles up as Big Abi, the ancient father of Ayu,” the god Ayu said that Ayu, the ancient leader of the Yi people, was a big Bimo who held both the positions of monarch and Bimo. Legend has it that in ancient times, when heaven and earth were cloudy, everything on earth was on the verge of extinction. Yuzu Ayu covered the heat with a “ayu maka” (apron) woven from leaves and saved humans. Legend has it that the beginning of February 8 is Ah Yu's birthday. Yi people enshrine this goddess in the corner. Every birthday, they worship the goddess Ayu, who is both the goddess of the Yi people and the originator of Bimo. On the Bimo altar of Yang Yao and Zuo Shangxiang, Bimo enshrines the god Ayu and a leaf apron. Every time Bimo is invited to die at home and recite the sutras, he prayerfully asks his ancestors to protect him.
