 “Ingenious extortion” is generally referred to as “skilful extortion.” “Clever capture” uses all kinds of tricks to defraud; “extortion” uses outrageous methods to forcibly rob. This idiom is a metaphor for using despicable means to take possession of someone else's property or rights. Actually, this turned out to be an elegant and interesting story in the history of calligraphy and painting.
Mi Yi, a famous painter and calligrapher in the Song dynasty, had elegant calligraphy and was more strict than the law. Su Dongpo praised his “true, grass, slave, and feisty, like a wild horse, calm and happy.” Rice crackers are used in the certified collection of calligraphy and painting. However, what he lacks as an outsider is that he often uses deceptive methods such as “package transfer” to “steal” famous calligraphy and paintings. On weekdays, as long as he hears about someone's famous calligraphy and painting, he makes every effort to borrow it, say it's for viewing; in fact, it's a copy. He can copy it to look exactly like the original, fake it, and then return the copy to others and leave a real impression for himself. Sometimes he even selects the original work and the copy for the original owner at the same time. The original owner is often unable to discern and is fooled. Mi Yu loved the calligraphy of Master Shen of the Tang dynasty. When he was working as a small official in Changsha, he heard that a temple had real works of Shen Chuanshi, so he asked to visit the temple. Unbeknownst to me, when I borrowed it, I actually took the temple and sailed away with this post. The monastery sued him at the government office, and Miyu still refused to pay him back. On the 1st, Mi Yi had a boat trip with Cai Jing. Cai Jing took out a picture of Xie An's “August 5 Post” and let Mi Yi look at it. Mi Yi was very excited to see this post, because he had seen this post 14 years ago, but he was too shy at the time and always had a lot of remorse. He didn't want to see it again today. He couldn't help but love it. He immediately asked Cai Jing to give him this collection or exchange it with him. He prayed so hard, and Cai Jing was in trouble. Unexpectedly, Mi Yi suddenly jumped onto the side of the boat and shouted, “If you don't give it to me, I might as well jump to death.” When Cai Jing saw the situation, I had to give this post to him. This is how Miyu “gets” his favorite calligraphy.
“Qingbo Magazine”, written by Zhou Yu (a native of the Song dynasty), refers to the trick of Mi Yu as a “clever extravagance.” Even the great writer, Su Shi, scoffed at him in “Second Yun, Mi Yu, Er Wang Shu Ba Wei” for “cleverly taking advantage of what has been around since ancient times; when laughed, it would seem like an idiot.” .” (Jin dynasty painter Gu Kaizhi, short name Tiger Head.) Mi Wei has a strange personality. He likes strange clothes, has a penchant for being clean, likes stones, calls Shi his brother, sleeps while bouldering, and is known as “Mi Ba.” There are so many things like this, and it's no wonder that everyone has seen it.
