 There is an event called Zaoduanpo horse racing during the Aqua Crossing Festival. There is a legend about the origin of Duanpo horse racing.
Once upon a time, there was a village king. He has been well-respected for a long time, and he is too lazy to even go downstairs. Over time, his health became extremely weak. After a year, he was so excited that the family helped him get on the horse and slowly climbed the back hillside. Although he was sweaty, climbing the hill made him feel physically and mentally relaxed and invigorated. Since then, his health has slowly improved. By the end festival in the second year, Zhaiwang made the villagers have to ride to the top. He said that they had climbed the mountain slope, were able to eliminate disasters and diseases, prosper people and animals, and bring good fortune to the village. It is said that since then, aquariums have had traditional activities such as climbing and horse racing.
