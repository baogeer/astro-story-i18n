 “” means Yi Hmm, and the Chinese word means “son of a tiger.” Legend has it that a long time ago, Fuchang was heavily forested, and the land was vast and sparsely populated. There is a family living by the Abu Road River. The old wife died at an early age, and the old man dragged his son up alone. Later, my son married a wife and had children. The old man grew old and frail. After a major illness, he was bedridden for a long time. He ate, drank, and lasa all in bed, and his life was very inconvenient. For the first two years, the son and daughter-in-law were able to serve the elderly with all their heart; seven or eight years passed, and the old man was still lying in bed, and the daughter-in-law complained and was very impatient. The old man was hungry and full, pooped in his bed, and the house was smelly.
Soon, something strange happened. The old man, who is panting, often makes strange noises in his mouth, and slowly grows his tail behind him. The longer it gets, the harder it is, like a chestnut stick. The tail penetrated the bed board, swung back and forth under the bed, screaming loudly, and swept away all the floor under the bed. My son and daughter-in-law were very scared when they saw them; only my grandson brought food and water to my grandfather.
The old man had been ill in bed for a long time. Seeing that he was being disliked, he said to his son, “Tomorrow will be a good day, and everything you want will come true. Since you're afraid of me, take me to the crossroads of Tiger Street!” When the son saw the old man say this, he couldn't stand the instigation of his daughter-in-law. The next morning, he took the young son and carried the old man to the back of the Four Ancient Moshaizi, Houshan Tiger Street. When the father and son put down the old man and were getting ready to go home, they only heard the old man whistle twice. They saw a white tiger running out of the jungle and swam straight over. The father and son were so scared that their legs trembled and climbed into a big tree to hide. I saw the white tiger approach the old man and didn't bite; he just stuck out his tongue and licked the old man very affectionately. Wherever the tiger's tongue is licked, the tiger hair on the old man's body grows everywhere. Soon, the old man's body was covered with tiger hair all over his body, leaving only his head and face, and he became a human-faced tiger monster.
At this point, the grandson, who was hiding in a big tree, accidentally broke a branch, and the tiger was frightened and fled back into the jungle. After a long time, there was no movement before the father and son trembled down the tree and got ready to go home. The grandson couldn't bear to throw his grandfather away on the mountain, so he thought of a way to ask his father to carry the diabolo basket back home. The father was puzzled and said, “What's the use of taking a broken bamboo basket home? Just throw it on the mountain.”
My grandson said, “Your father is old and has a strange illness, you carry him here; when you get old or have a strange illness, and don't have a bamboo basket, what do I use to carry you up the mountain?” When my son heard this, he was taken aback. He realized that it was not easy for his father to nurture his own growth. Thinking about the day he grew old, he was very remorseful. Only then did he learn that his actions were really rebellious and disrespectful.
The father and son once again carried the half-human, half-tiger old man back home to take care of him with care. Strangely enough, after a few days, the old man's hair and tail were gone, and his illness got better. The family lived in harmony for more than ten years before the old man passed away.
In order not to forget to be filial to the elderly, posterity held a ceremony every three years at Ziyakou in Hujie on the day they carried the elderly up the mountain to kill sheep and slaughter pigs to offer the mountain gods, called “throwing food and praying.” The village where this family lives got its name.
