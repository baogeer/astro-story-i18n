 Legend has it that a long time ago, this place in Tengxian County was very beautiful and prosperous. It had a prosperous and prosperous scene.
Suddenly one day, it became dark, thunder and lightning struck, and the heavy rain continued. What's going on with this? Turns out it was Emperor Jade who demoted a white dragon that had broken the rules of heaven to mortal space. But it is obsessive and unaware; it still doesn't abide by the rules and harms the people.
Ordinary people don't know why God is angry; they all burn incense at home and knock their heads one after another, asking God not to stop raining. Just when people were crying out, the evil dragon appeared in the rain. I saw it fluttering its claws and viciously saying to the common people, “If people don't send pigs, cows, and sheep to the place it says, it will have to thrive and drown out crops. The people were afraid, so they had to agree to it. When the evil dragon saw that it agreed, it accepted the spell and waited for a nice meal.
After the dragon left, the people had no choice but to send their livestock away. This is the case year after year. It hasn't been a few years since all the livestock called Evil Dragons have eaten up. Evil dragons still need more and more food every day. The common people sigh and lock in sorrow, but there is no way to do it.
One day, an old man with a white beard came here, heard people talk about this, and said to the people, “This is an evil dragon. You can't call it evil anymore; get rid of it.” When people heard him say this, they were very happy, but they immediately hesitated again: Can such an old man get rid of the dragon? But don't hurt him. The old man didn't wait for people to say it. As if he had seen through their thoughts, he said resolutely, “I have a way to get rid of it. As long as you send more livestock and good wine tonight, don't worry about me.”
When it got dark, that is, it was time to deliver food to the dragon, people did as the old man said. The dragon was hungry. When he saw so many things, he ate and drank when he opened his mouth. That really left a cloud of wind. Soon, he had enough to drink and eat, and fell asleep crying. At this point, the old man walked out of the crowd, reached out to pull out a tower that looked like a little finger from the clothes drawer, and covered it down towards the dragon. Strangely enough, when the tower saw the wind, it became very big. Suddenly, the dragon was still dreaming.
When people saw this scene, they were all stunned. By the time they remembered to thank the old man, the old man had disappeared. They shouted everywhere, and only heard someone talking on their heads. When they looked up, it was that old man, and they all got down on their knees and knocked their heads. I heard the old man say in the middle of the air, “I was sent by Emperor Yuhuang to get rid of the dragon. The dragon has been crushed by the tower, and it will never do evil. You can feel free to grow crops in the future, and have a good life.” When I finished speaking, I disappeared.
This is the origin of the Dragon Spring Tower.
