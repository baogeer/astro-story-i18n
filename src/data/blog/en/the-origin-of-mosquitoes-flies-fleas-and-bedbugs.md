 Once upon a time, there were two couples who had one son, one daughter, and two babies, but the two couples didn't want this baby and were ready to live far away. In order to be able to leave after their two babies, the couple arranged for their two children to lift water one day. The son brought an iron pot, and the girl brought an earthenware pot, but both utensils worked. When the two little ones went to the ditch to fill the water, they were not satisfied at all. At this point, an eagle flew in and said to them, “Fill the bottom of the iron pot with mud, fill the bottom of the clay pot, then slowly fill in the water and bring the water home. Your parents are gone.” They both refilled the pot as instructed by the eagle. After filling it with water, they quickly returned home, but the parents in the family didn't know where they were. The two brothers and sisters looked around and found a cow herder on the hillside. After asking, the herder said, “I saw you, but I'll tell you where your two brothers and sisters have to help me drive the cow home before I can tell you where your parents are going.” Their two brothers and sisters had no choice but to help the herders drive the herd back. But when they put the herd in the cow ring, the herder said, “I haven't seen your parents. Go fast, otherwise I'll herd the cows on top of you.” In desperation, the two brothers and sisters had no choice but to keep searching. On the way, I met another sheep herder who was rushing past the sheep. They also hurriedly asked, “Big Sheep Herder, have you seen my parents?” The shepherd answered, “I saw it, but I won't tell you until you help me drive the sheep home.” The brother and sister helped drive the flock home. Only when they got home did they herd the sheep and said, “I haven't seen your parents. You can leave soon, otherwise my sheep will support you.” Their brother and sister had no choice but to leave in frustration. The two brothers and sisters walked a distance and saw a family. An old mother was sitting around twine in the courtyard. The two brothers and sisters were busy asking, “Old aunt, have you seen my parents?” The old aunt looked up and looked at the brothers and sisters, and picked up a string and handed it to them and said, “You roll this wire. Wherever the wire group rolls, your parents will be there.” The brother and sister took over the old aunt's line-up and did as she ordered. When the thread rolled to the edge of the pond, the line rolled over. They closed their eyes and walked into the pond. Finally, they found their parents in the pond. Their parents lived in the pond! However, their parents didn't recognize them, and they also took out two sticks to them and said, “You ate the needle; if you eat it, you are my children; otherwise, it wouldn't be.” The daughter was dressed all over her clothes, tied around her waist, and dropped the needle, so she threw it into her clothes and hid it around her waist. My son was wearing a pair of clothes. When he dropped the needle on top, the needle slipped out of his clothes. Their parents admitted that the girl was their daughter and didn't acknowledge that the boy was their son, and they used a needle to seal the boy's eyes and put him in a pig pen to feed them. His mom fed all the bran when she fed the “pig,” and her sister packed all the rice under the bran when she fed the “pig.” Slowly, the “pigs” are getting fat, and they are getting ready to be killed and eaten. Her parents told the girl to go find pine feathers. The girl cried all day long. She stole some glutinous rice from her home and brought it up the mountain, then sprinkled it on pine trees. When she got home, she said to her parents, “I can't find pine wool. You can see for yourself. There's all the poop under the pine tree.” When Mom and Dad didn't believe it, they said, “You can keep this pig at home, let's go find it.” After her parents left, she killed a mouse, put blood on her hands and the pig pen door, and let her brother run away quickly. Her brother said to her, “If you wash clothes by the river after three years and see wood residue floating off the water, then I'm building a new house. If wool goose feather floats off the water, then it means I'm having a great time.” When I finished speaking, I ran away. When her parents came back and asked her where the “pig” had gone, she lied and said, “When I went to feed the pigs, he bit my hand and ran away.” Her parents immediately went out to look for it, but no matter how they looked, they couldn't find it.
Three years later, my sister went to the riverside to wash her clothes. Seeing wood debris, wool, and goose feathers floating down the river, she knew that her younger brother had a great time after escaping. Another day, my sister went to the riverside to wash her clothes, and suddenly saw a box floating in the water. When I took a look, it contained gold and silver jewels, silk and satin, and a note with a note “I've already become Dragon King's son-in-law. I asked my parents to go to the riverside at some point, and my son will pick them up and play.” After reading it, she put on the satin clothes inside and went home with the suitcase. Her parents were astonished when they saw the girl full of jewels. Her parents envied and asked her, “Where did your baby come from?” Thinking that her parents were ruthless to her brothers in the past, she was unwilling to tell them the truth and made up a lie to punish them. She said, “This baby thing just rolled out of Tsuen Ma Tei.” As soon as my parents heard it, they found a piece of land and were desperately tumbling around. But nothing but pain all over my body. Her parents asked her again, “Why?” She also said, “I can't get out with my clothes on; I only roll out when I'm naked.” Her parents took off their clothes and were desperately tumbling around Tsuen Ma Tei for the treasure. She took a chance and threw a broom into Tsuen Ma Tei. Her parents roll around, their whole body is rotting. They just roll out a broom and scold her: “Why did you roll? Why can't we get out of the treasure.” She wanted to punish her enough. She told her parents about the real situation, such as her brother who had run away and when she went to the riverside to wash clothes three years later. They arrived at the riverside on time. Sure enough, a dragon horse picked them up. The dragon horse cameled them to the Dragon King family. The Dragon King family was very rich and rich. The Dragon King's family entertained them with the best wine and mountain delicacies. They stayed at the Dragon Palace for 3 days before taking them away. When they left, they each took a box of lunchtime. My sister's box was full of delicious food. His dad had a snake in the box, and his mother's box was a nest of earthworms. When they left, the brother said, “Let mom and dad ride a horse together, and my sister ride alone.” At noon, my sister got off the horse to eat; when her parents were older, they rode on horses to eat. After my sister got off the horse, her parents walked away. Her mom said to her dad, “What do you have in the box? “Eat yours first” was an endless argument. In the end, his dad had to agree to eat what was in his box first, then opened the lunch box, and a giant snake crawled out of the box and wrapped his parents tightly together, making them unable to get off the horse. His parents were afraid and hungry, and her dad also said to her mom, “Open up and take a look at what's in your box.” Her mom opened the lunch box, and a swarm of earthen bees flew out, stinging them both parents and horses. The horse was stung and jumped around when shocked. The parents' bodies were wrapped tightly by snakes, and after being dragged by the horse, they waited until the horse returned to the Dragon Palace, and the parents didn't even know where they had been left. Right away, there was only one foot left. After burning these two feet to ashes, the brother picked them up and threw them up, thinking they turned into mosquitoes and flies in their mouths, then sprinkled three times down their mouths and turned them into fleas and bugs. Since then, there have been harmful mosquitoes, flies, fleas, and bed bugs in the human world.
