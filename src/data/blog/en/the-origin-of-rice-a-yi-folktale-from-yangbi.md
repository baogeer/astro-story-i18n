 Long ago, humans didn't grow rice because there were no grains.
Heaven wanted to pass on millet to humans, but people couldn't go to heaven to harvest grain, so they left this heavy responsibility to a dog to complete. The dog waded through mountains and water, went through all kinds of hardships and risks, and finally reached heaven. Seeing the fairy drying the millet, the dog explained its intention to the fairy who guarded the millet. Immortal was very moved. They agreed to give the dog grain, saying, “You can take as many as you can!” However, the dog had no tools to carry grain. As soon as it had a clever move, it rolled over the valley, covered its whole body with millet, and returned to the world. On the way, they passed through three rivers and four lakes. When people saw the dog, the millet was gone. Fortunately, there were still seven grains of millet on the tip of its tail that had not been soaked in water. People just use these seven grains of millet as seeds, and sow them day by day, year after year, so that rice can develop and become the main food for humans.
From then on, people will be able to eat rice, keeping in mind the benefits dogs bring them. In the old days, when rice was harvested for the first time, new rice had to be fed to the dog before eating it. These are just some local customs; in fact, they express people's gratitude for dogs.
