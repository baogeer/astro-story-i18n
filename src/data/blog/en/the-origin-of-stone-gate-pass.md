 One kilometer away in front of Shimenguan, lies a beautiful small hill in the shape of a long strip. Locals call this hill Wangjia Mountain or the back mountain of a small village.
According to old people, there was no such hill here in ancient times; of course, there was no Shimen Pass back then. One year, for some reason, there was a dark and vicious battle between the Little Yellow Dragon and the Big Black Dragon in the Erhai Sea behind Cangshan Mountain. Amidst the cheering cries of the local people beating the gongs and drums, the kind Little Yellow Dragon finally defeated the once unbelievable Big Black Dragon and blinded the eye of this evil guy. The big black dragon, which escaped from desolation, escaped to the top of Cangshan Mountain in pain. It stood on the high Malong Peak and looked: Ho, the Yangjiangshan River, which is surrounded by mountains on all sides, is lying quietly at its feet like an empty giant basin. What made this devil even more happy was that at the foot of a mountain not far away, there is also a Yangjiao River that is currently experiencing a surge of flooding where waves and waves are tumbling and tumbling...
When Big Black Dragon saw the situation, he couldn't help but come up with another evil idea that hurt nature and hurt reason. When people were quiet in the middle of the night that night, this animal that completely ignored the death of the local people quietly cut a long strip of rock from under Malong Peak. They are preparing to carry it to the narrowest point in the lower section of the Yangjiao River, and tightly block the river outlet that is raging east, so that the fields of Yangli can become a smoky water town, Zeguk, so that it can continue to thrive and fight hard. Fortunately, this sinful plot was discovered by the unknown prophet, the old mother of Guanyin. The compassionate old mother of Guanyin thought to herself how many fields and villages would be flooded if this damn evil dragon were to succeed. What's more, I don't know how many people will become ghosts in the water house. As a result, Guanyin's old mother, who was rescuing hardships, immediately flew from the South China Sea to this side of the land where disaster was about to occur.
Guanyin's mother knows that as soon as the rooster crows, the big black dragon will think it will soon dawn, and will not dare to continue carrying out her sinful plan. She then hid in a bamboo grove near the village and learned “ooh, ooh...” while hitting the dustpan to make the sound of a rooster fan flapping its wings. Hearing the roosters chirping from the bamboo grove, the roosters in the village also chirped one after another with “ooh, ooh...” At this point, the big black dragon was carrying a large block of sunken rocks slowly moving step by step towards the Yangliang River. Suddenly, he heard a rooster cry. I couldn't help but be taken aback. I thought the sky would soon be bright. As a result, it had to leave the rock on its back in a hurry and quickly flee in the direction of Yongping and Baoshan. In this way, the idyllic village on this side was finally preserved, and the people were saved. Early the next morning, the villagers got up to take a look and were shocked to discover that such an earth-shattering mutation had occurred overnight: not only did a deep gorge appear without any reason on the slope of Cangshan Mountain behind the village, that is, today's Shimen Pass; furthermore, an abruptly long, small hill in the shape of an abrupt strip popped up next to the village, that is, Wangjiashan Mountain behind the current Jinniu Village.
After everyone learned all the details, in addition to being happy, in order to thank Guanyin's mother for her act of righteousness in saving the local pastoral village and reviving the great blessings and virtues of the people, the villagers donated funds to build a Guanyin Hall on the right side of the mountainside of Shimenguan. Inside the temple, a statue of the old mother with a thousand hands and compassion was shaped. How many years have passed since then, the villagers are still busy going to Guanyin Temple at 4 o'clock, which is today's Jin'an Temple, to burn incense for the old mother of Guanyin.
