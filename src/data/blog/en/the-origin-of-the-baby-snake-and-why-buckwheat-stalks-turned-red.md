 Once upon a time, there was a village located on the edge of a large ditch at the foot of an old forest deep in the mountains. Every day, as soon as the sun set, it was completely dark. There is a savage in the deep mountains — the “old sick woman”. She often walks out of the forest to eat people, threatening the lives of villagers. Many families have moved out of this area one after another in order to survive, and in the end, there are only four members of the family left (parents and two sisters). My father disappeared again one day, and no bones were found.
One day, her mother went to the field to pick up the bales, and suddenly heard someone speaking. She looked closely. It turned out that an old woman with a strange appearance was walking up to her. Her mother knew this was the legendary “old sick woman,” but it was too late to run away. After the “old patient” walked up, she said to her mother, “Help me brush my hair, I'll brush your hair later.” Her mother had no choice but to do the same. She combed the “old sick woman” first. Her hair was yellow, dirty, thick, hard, and messy. She couldn't straighten it out at all. It was difficult and barely finished. The “old sick woman” also combed for her mother. As soon as the mother's head was off, the “old sick woman” used her fingernails to grab the mother's scalp, suck human blood, eat human flesh, and after eating, change into her mother's clothes and walk to their cottage.
It was almost dark, and the two sisters were anxious and afraid to close the door because they had heard their parents talk about an “old sick woman” who looked like someone could talk and eat people. When the “old sick woman” came to the door, they learned the mother's voice and said, “Daughter, open the door soon, mom is back.” As soon as the two sisters heard the wrong voice, they said, “You're not our mom; my mom's voice isn't as thick and dumb as yours.” The “old sick woman” couldn't fool them; she had to leave. After a while, they came back and pretended to have a different tone to trick them. After hearing it, the sisters also said, “If you were our mom, stick your hand through the slit of the door and let us recognize it.” The “old sick woman” stuck her hand in. As soon as the two sisters touched it, they were shocked when they saw it. She also said, “You are not our mom. My mom has white, tender hands, no long hair, and she still wears silver bracelets on her hands.” When the “old patient” saw that they still couldn't fool them, they left, washed and found the child's mother's silver bracelet on her hand, and once again fooled the two sisters. The two sisters were tricked into opening the door. As soon as the “old patient” entered the door, she chewed and ate her sister. The sister took the opportunity to escape in a panic. The “old patient” then chased out. The sister had nowhere to hide and couldn't run, so she climbed a plum tree. The “old patient” chased under the tree. Since she couldn't climb the tree, she just screamed under the tree. My sister couldn't get out, so she tricked the “old patient” and said, “The plums on the tree are sweet, crunchy, and delicious. You raise your head, close your eyes, and open your mouth wide. Pick it and throw it away for you to eat.” The “old sick woman” took the opportunity to be deceived and ran desperately out of the mountain by jumping off a tree. Only then did the “old sick woman” learn that she had been fooled. Her sister ran to a dozen soba restaurants and saw that some buckwheat bars were piled up, so she went in and hid. The “old sick woman” chased her to the buckwheat farm and couldn't find her sister. She jumped and screamed on the buckwheat pole and shocked the people. Everyone rounded up with shotguns and wooden sticks, and shot the “old sick woman” to death.
Everyone lifted the buckwheat pole and didn't find her sister, but they found a baby snake in a small underground hole. It turns out that my sister was afraid of being caught and eaten by an “old sick woman, so she tried desperately to drill down, and when she got underground, she became a baby snake. However, the buckwheat stem was originally not red, but later turned red after being stained with the blood of an old sick woman.
