 Legend has it that a long time ago, there was a scholar in Gujin Garden in Caozhou, whose name was Zhao Ming Banning. Both parents died as children, and there was only one elderly housekeeper in the family. Bonning is gifted and smart. He loves flowers and plants. I heard that there are strange flowers and plants there. No matter how far it is, you have to find them to plant in the garden.
On this day, he came to Laoshan on the East China Sea and wanted to find some nice flowers to bring back, but he looked all the way up and down the mountain, and he didn't like it. Disappointed, he came to a small riverside and suddenly discovered a strange peony with purple flowers, dark purple flower stems, fuchsia petioles, and greenish purple leaves next to the riverside cottage. Only the flower pistil is yellow-green, curved, and extremely eye-catching. It is clearly a rare variety. It's just a little shrunk, and the leaves roll up and sag, like a young girl in old clothes. Bonning thought to herself that this flower would have to be watered right away, otherwise it would die. However, there were no homes nearby, and the only cottage door was locked tightly. Where can I find a water lifting tool? Bonning was helpless and had to pick up the river with both hands and pour drop by drop on the shriveled peony. He took more than a dozen trips in one go, and he had just wetted the ground. Later, he took off his coat, soaked in the river, and screwed water under the peony flower. He went on trips over and over again, and his underwear was all sweaty. He didn't want to sit back and rest until he saw the peony flower that had gained water and gradually became refreshed.
Unwittingly. The sun has set. The owner of the cottage has returned. The owner is an old man in his 80s. Bonning greeted them in a hurry. The two met as soon as they saw each other. After the hustle and bustle, they started chatting. It turned out that this old man was old and sick, and his daughter took him to stay for a few days. He thought about this peony all the time, so he rushed back to take a look. When Bonning asked about this peony, the old man said, “This peony has been around for as long as I can remember. I don't know how many years it has been. It's blooming and leafy every year, making people love it. Too bad I'm older, my hands and feet aren't easy, and I can't take much care of it...” The old man looked at the peony and sighed deeply. When Bonning heard it, she told the old man how she loved flowers like her life. He expressed his desire to use everything on his body in exchange for this peony. When the old man saw that Bonning was loyal and honest and a person who loved flowers, he gave him this beloved peony.
Bonning was so happy that he was busy prodding to the old man. When Bonning returned to his hometown, he asked the old man for a large flower pot, planted this peony with roots and soil in the pot, and decided to drive back overnight. But it's a long way, trekking mountains and water, how can you get there? Use a car to pull it. What if the road is uneven and the basin is shaken? Should I use livestock to carry it? If the animals don't listen, what should you do if you throw the pot down? Bonning was worried and thought left and right. He thought it would be better to take insurance. Having decided on the idea, I packed my luggage in the car, picked up the flower pot, and hit the road.
On this day, Bonning missed a place to stay because he was in a hurry. Seeing that it was too late, I didn't go to the village before, and didn't rely on the store after, so I had to work hard and keep going. After walking for a few more miles, I was so tired that I couldn't support it. My eyes were dark, and the flower pot she was holding in her arms fell to the ground. Bonning climbed up, couldn't help but feel pain surges on her body, and hurriedly picked up the peony. The broken blood on her head dripped into the heart of the flower. He still wanted to move forward, but the flower pot was gone. In order to preserve this peony, he planned to plant it on the ground; he touched the ground with his hands and applied it wet. I thought to myself, flowers really shouldn't have been saved. Bonning threw his hand and dug a hole. I planted peonies in. ' He prayed to Peony: “Peony, peony, would you like to stay here? OK then.” Bonning set up a hermitage next to the peony and stayed there.
Besides, Bonning's old housekeeper waited a year and didn't see Bonning come back; he was very anxious. In the spring of the following year, I commissioned Bonning's cousin to take the silver coin and drive all the way to find it. Finally, in this wild countryside, 'I found Bonning's address. ' This fuchsia peony has been cultivated by him over 100 trees. However, he worked too hard to cultivate peonies. More than a month after his death, his cousin transplanted these 100 peonies into pots and brought them back to his hometown of Caozhou. At the time, Caozhou didn't have this kind of decoration. In order to miss Zhao Bangning, people called this kind of Dan Bong Ning purple.
