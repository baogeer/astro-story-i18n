 Legend has it that in ancient times, there was a hardworking and brave Buyei descendant named Aron. His parents had long since passed away, leaving him alone — and he had no plot of land or half of his house. He loved birds and sparrows since he was a child, and had no fortune. However, the birds caught couldn't fill their stomachs. There was no choice but to herd cows for a wealthy family in the stockade. It just so happened that the rich owner also loved to eat bird meat, so every day, in addition to watching cows, he was also limited to catching ten birds.
Since then, Aaron has gone out early and late every day to return to the mountains to herd cows and catch birds for the rich.
When Aaron was 19, one afternoon, I had no idea where the birds had gone; he couldn't even see the shadow of a single bird. He anxiously thought: if he doesn't catch the bird, he will be severely beaten by the rich owner when he goes back. He was just thinking, crying,... Suddenly, a turtle dove appeared in the sky. He was so happy that he hurriedly boarded his arrow and pulled out his bow. The turtle dove was hit by a “whistling” sound*. However, as soon as the turtle dove fell from mid-air, it disappeared. He's looking for it! Look for it! He started crying with sadness when he kept finding it when it was almost dark, but he couldn't find it. Suddenly, a string of crisp smiles flew out from behind a big rock. Next, a young and beautiful girl walked over. She was holding the speck that Aron had shot dead in her hand and said to Aron with a smile and groan, “Ago, you really are an archer!” At this point, Aaron's face reddened to the root of his ear, and embarrassed to say to the girl, “Sister Ah, please give me the turtle dove!” The girl said, “Yes, but you have to promise me one thing.” What is Aron busy asking about? The girl shy* said, “If you take me back to your house, we will become married.” Ah Long was also happy and sad when he heard it. What was happy was that he was able to get a fairy-like girl as his wife. What was sad was that he had no home, no fields, and was also a cow herder from a wealthy family. Where would he take the girl? He wanted to think about it, and finally said to the girl, “No, Sister Ah, I'm so poor that I don't even have a broken bowl to eat. How can I get married to a girl as beautiful as you?”
After being persuaded three or five times, Aron still did not agree. The girl cried and cried very sad. Ah Long watched it and was heartbroken. He had to agree to the girl's request. It was dark, and Alon and the girl cheerfully shouted the cow back to the owner's house. Along the way, Aaron talked a lot and a lot with the girl. The girl told Aron that she was the Seven Fairies in the Sky. Because Wang's mother and daughter saw Aron's hard work in the sky, every day they went up the mountain to herd cows and catch birds for the rich. After talking with the Seven Fairies, Aron arrived at the treasurer's house.
It was dark, and the rich man didn't see Aaron come back; he clenched his teeth with hate. He was coming out of the house and was shocked to see Aron and a girl born like a jade flower come back. At this point, Aaron stepped forward and said to the rich owner, “Grandpa, now that I have started my own family, I want to go back and set up a family business with my wife. Thanks to you for raising me over the years, thank you for your love!” The rich man hurriedly said, “You can go, but leave the girl next to you to me, otherwise Laozi won't let you go and ask you to ascend to heaven.” Aaron did not agree; the rich owner asked the family members to tie up Aaron, carry him up the mountain, and lock up the Seven Fairies in a well-bought house. The Seven Fairies are worried about Ah Long and cry every day! Cry, there is a poor servant who sympathizes with her. One night, while she was sleeping, they let her go. After the Seven Fairies escaped, she spent the whole day searching for Aaron, but she still couldn't find it. She cried and became tearful. It was dark and she cried. When she was lying on the hillside, she lost her mind. In her dream, she saw Aaron, and lived a sweet and happy life with Aron.
When she woke up the next day, she suddenly saw Aaron being tied up to a big tree about two feet away from her. She ran over and watched Aaron's body crying bitterly. She cried for seven days and seven nights, crying all her tears. Because she was so sad, she got angry and died under that big tree.
The death of Aron and the Seven Fairies moved the elderly of the land. He is determined to help Aaron and the Seven Fairies to avenge them. It is very precious for the elderly in the land to have a red cloth. This red cloth can both transform people and revive people from death. However, after using this red cloth twice, it didn't show any effect. And even myself became something else.
On this day, the old man from the land came to the rich owner's house and set fire to the rich owner's house. He then read the spell on the red cloth, and when he finished reading the spell, he threw the red cloth on the head of the rich owner's family. Immediately, the rich owner's family all turned into mice. After the rich man became a hahahahaha mouse, he had to go into the mountain stream and couldn't even get food. The old man from the land came to the mountain again and gently rubbed the bodies of Aron and the Seven Fairies with his red cloth. The bodies of Aaron and the Seven Fairies slowly moved. They both opened their eyes and looked at each other, and thought it was very strange: who saved us and brought us back to life from death? Everywhere they looked, they didn't see a single person; they only saw a red cloth hanging around a stone man's neck. The Seven Fairies understood that the old man from the land saved her and Aaron. In order to repay the kindness of the elderly in the land, Aron and the Seven Fairies raised the stone statue of the old man of the land to the foot of the slope and made a small house for the stone statue. This day happened to be June 6. Aaron and the Seven Fairies went up the mountain* and hunted birds and wild boars to feed the elderly. Later, they built a wooden house near the small house to sacrifice food for the elderly.
Since then, the couple have worked diligently, cultivated land, had children, and lived a happy life. Every year until June 6, Alon, the Seven Fairies, and every household in the Buyei tribe have to kill pigs and kill chickens to feed the Bodhisattva. This custom has continued to this day.
