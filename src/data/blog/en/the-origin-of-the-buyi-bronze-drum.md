 The bronze drums of the Buyei people are both sacred and solemn, and very expensive. Legend has it that in the past, the old Buyei people died; they always went down to the 12th floor to become an immortal; they only went down to the 12th floor of the sea to become immortal.
Taibai Xing from the sky is a charitable old man. One night, he drove Bu Meng to Buyi's ancestor, saying, “The old man in the world died. If you want to go up to the twelve levels of heaven and become an immortal, it's not difficult. As long as you go to the sky and ask Tenjin to go down the bronze drum, and if the old man dies, play three bronze drums, because only the sound of the bronze drums is loud and can spread far away. In this way, when Tenjin heard the sound of the bronze drums, it would have sent the immortal people down. The dead led up to twelve levels.” Taibai Xing shaved her beard and said, “Bujie, you are a talented person who can go to heaven and earth. Just climb up to the sky from the tall horse mulberry tree at the top of Qinglong Mountain and ask Tenjin for a bronze drum.” Having said that, I drove * clouds back to the sky.
The next day, Bujie woke up and followed Taibai Xing's instructions to the top of Mount Qinglong. He also climbed and crawled for nine days and nine nights. He was stabbed with blood all around him, but he clenched his teeth and continued climbing up for three days and three nights. Tenbai, who was guarding the gate, saw it and wanted to stop him. Seeing that it was a talented person who had captured Lei Gong before, he hurriedly flashed off a path and let him go into the Heavenly Court.
Bujie went into the heavenly court and told Tenjin about his intentions over and over again. Tenjin wants to test him to see if he has filial piety or is good at praising him. He said, “You can go to heaven if you want to go to heaven with a bronze drum, but I want to ask you one thing. If you answer right, I'll send it to you; if you don't answer right, I won't give it to you.” Bujie hurriedly said, “Just ask:”
Tenjin said, “Heaven and earth, which one is bigger?”
Bujie listened and thought to himself, “This god wants me to flatter him and praise him for being great? No, I can't say anything that is out of conscience for the sake of blessing the drum!” If you think about it, just seriously say; “Heaven and earth, parents are big!”
Tenjin heard it and was happy, but he deliberately raised his face and said, “You didn't answer right! You should count me as big!”
Bujie didn't change his voice; seriously, he said, “No! No matter how old you are, you were born to your parents. If you don't have parents, where are you from?”
Tenjin listened, shrugged his white beard and laughed, and the wrinkles on his face stretched a little bit as he smiled. He said, “He seems to be a real filial person. He doesn't tell lies. OK, I'll give you a bronze drum! Remember: From now on, as long as the old man dies, I will hit the bronze drum three times, and I will send a fairy to take him to heaven.” Bujie thanked Tenjin, carried a bronze drum, and walked out of Nantianmen with joy. Because he was so happy, he stumbled upon the threshold of the Southern Sky Gate. As soon as he fought, he fell to the ground and died. The bronze drum was also broken through a hole. To this day, the hole in the bronze drum still remains.
Since then, there have been bronze drums everywhere. People beat the bronze drums and surpassed Buje to heaven and become an immortal.
Since then, when the old Buyei people died, they played three bronze drums, and Tenjin knew that everyone had died. When the “open road” surpassed the dead, and nine more bronze drums were played, and Tenjin sent immortals to take the dead to the 12th floor of the sky and become immortals.
After a long time, every Lunar New Year, New Year's Eve on July 30th and the first day of the new year, some Buyei people had to play three bronze drums. Their ancestors, who became immortals in the sky on the 12th floor, heard that future generations of people invited them to celebrate the New Year, and Bujie also took the lead.
Because the bronze drum was sent by Tenjin, and Buje used * in exchange for his life, it is both very precious and solemn. It is not usually easy to knock, and it must be treasured respectfully. Furthermore, it is strictly prohibited for anyone to walk over it. It can only be used when the dead elderly are excessive and when they ask for their ancestors during the old year.
After a long time, there were poor and rich people everywhere. The hearts of the rich, * they also created the biggest and biggest bronze drum. They carried the sound far and wide. Wherever the sound spread, they said that the fields, mountains, rivers, and trees were all owned by them, so they took over those places. Therefore, the bigger the bronze drum of any company, the more it shows that he has money and power.
After a long time, the bronze drum became a signal for a stockade or tribe. Whenever there is an emergency in the village or invaded by foreign enemies, the village elders hit the bronze drums. When the crowd heard it, they gathered to discuss the enemy together. While fighting with the enemy, the village elder hit the bronze drum again. The villagers or people of the tribe listened to it; they were all extremely brave.
