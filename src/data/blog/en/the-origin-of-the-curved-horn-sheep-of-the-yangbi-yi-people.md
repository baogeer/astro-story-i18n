 Legend has it that when Grandpa Zinu was born, he exorcised demons, fought against violence and nurtured good fortune. At the end of his life, Grandpa Hades sent a police officer to push him back to Yin and was resisted. In the end, Hades personally came to recover. He said, “You can go, but you need to ask his sheep.” Sheep are always “tomorrow, tomorrow”. In the end, they were forced to hit the road, but slow to act, and Hades was helpless and promised to exchange mounts and costumes. As a result, Grandpa Zinu arrived in Yin Fu early. The doorman couldn't tell if it was real or false, so he let Zinu go to Yin Fu. The old man ordered the doorman to say, “If you arrive late, let's kill him.” As a result, the doorman actually killed Hades who arrived later. In this way, Old Senino took the throne of Hades. Since then, the old man has paid special attention to the undead of the Yi people. The Yi people used sheep in funeral sacrifices to honor and commemorate the mount he went to Yin Cao for his merit.
