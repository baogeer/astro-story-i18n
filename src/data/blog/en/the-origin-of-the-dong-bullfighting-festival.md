Although rhinos are rare, old people who love cows don't want them. He asked everyone to slaughter it, so that the townspeople were happy to eat a full meal. This day is the 9th of September of the lunar calendar. With Baojia beef, Dongzhai has had good weather and rain all year round. Grain is plentiful, and both people and animals are prosperous. To commemorate the victory of Baojia's bullfight with rhinos, every year on September 9 of the lunar calendar, old people who love cows lead the townspeople to herd and fight at the rhino pool to celebrate the harvest.
