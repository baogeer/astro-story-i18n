 Story ② The origin of Monjin
Yuhuang Temple is one of the three major attractions in Yuhuangding Park. When you walk into the Yuhuangmiao Mountain Gate, there are two left and right gate generals inside. There is also a historical legend about the origin of these two generals. These two generals, one named Wei Chi Jingde and the other was Qin Qiong. They were all famous generals during Tang Taizong and Li Shimin, and were very favored by Li Shimin. Legend has it that when Tang Taizong won the world and took the throne of the emperor, he was often terrified by murdering countless people during the founding of the country. He slept at night and had nightmares that made him feel uneasy by ghosts. Later, when he went to the DPRK, Li Shimin complained to the ministers. Qin Qiong and Wei Chi Jingde then volunteered to drive away evil spirits for him. Sure enough, Tang Taizong had a very sweet sleep that night. Since then, the two have been holding doors for him every day. Soon after, Tang Taizong felt very sorry for the two. He couldn't bear to fight during the day and stand guard for himself at night, so he thought of a way to find someone to draw images of Qin Qiong and Wei Chi Jingde and post them on the left and right doors. Later, Qin Qiong and Yu Chi Jingde became gatekeepers to exorcise evil spirits, as well as the two most widely spread and most influential demons.
