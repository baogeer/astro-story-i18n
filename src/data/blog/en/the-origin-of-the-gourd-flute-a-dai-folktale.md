 A beautiful girl often goes to the street to sell gourds. A handsome young man bought gourds from the girl. Because the girl was beautiful and the gourds were delicious, the young man bought gourds from this girl every day. Over time, the two fell in love.
Once, the young man wanted to write a love letter to confess his love to a girl, so he cut a hole in the gourd he had bought and hung it under the roof to dry it, ready to put the letter in the gourd and give it to this girl.
After the gourd dries, it makes a “buzz - buzz” sound when the wind blows. The young man holds this dried gourd in his hand and blows it to the hole once with his mouth. The young man also makes a “buzz” sound. The young man was very happy and ran to tell the girl. As a result, a couple of lovers made that dried gourd into shredded gourds, and since then they have had shredded gourds.
