 Legend has it that the Baizhushan Temple Fair was jointly organized by Dragon Street, Baizhu, Yoshimura, and the tile factory. Sheba, Mizutakutsubo, Tomisho, and Yoshimura Gaiji Ancient will each take the lead in rotation. Every year on the 14th day of the Lunar New Year, there is an endless flow of people from four villages and eight miles to help the elderly and bring their children to Chaoshan at Baizhu Temple to worship the gods. May the whole family stay safe and sound. Since Baizhu Mountain is located in the center of the four sides, the mountains and clear water are beautiful, the trees are deep, and the gods are very spiritual, and it is a place where young local men and women meet and have a romantic encounter, it is very crowded and very lively. People sang and drank, had fun, and it took four or five days before they dispersed.
Because Yoshimura and Dragon Street are a long way from Baizhushan, the people there have to get up and go on the road every time they walk to Baizhushan. By the time they walk to Baizhushan, the sun is already sloping west. Not to mention the public festival held by the Quartet, not to mention the public festival held by the Quartet, not even a place to rest their feet or cook with a pot. People who arrived early in the morning also read a book on the temple gate: “Baizhu Ling Mountain has four feet; some people don't shrink their feet”, mocking people who are far from arriving in time. People from Lu Yuan were outraged when they saw it, and they gathered to discuss the golden body and position of Guanyin from Baizhu Temple to Guikou in Yongping to build another new temple.
On the 14th day of January, 20 pairs of young guys and big girls selected in advance from Yoshimura and Dragon Street were singing and playing songs at the entrance of the temple, attracting people's attention. Taking advantage of the opportunity of people watching and singing in the empty space of the Qu Temple, the other two strong guys, who were as strong as cows, secretly turned around and ran, walking like flying, all the way to Guikou on Longjie Street.
Strangely enough, when the two young men carried the Buddha down the mountain, they were as light as swallows and didn't feel heavy at all; when they ran to Fuchang Linggang in one go, they felt like they were carrying a big mountain, so they were out of breath, making it difficult to walk every step of the way. The young man then placed the Buddha on the hill and sat down to rest. When the two wanted to walk away from the Buddha again, it was like ants shaking a big tree, and the statue didn't move at all, as if it had taken root. The young man felt strange, so he told the old people in Fuchang Village about the incident. The old people said, “This is Guanyin Bodhisattva's spiritual guidance; it must not go against the will of the gods.”
As a result, people built houses and built a temple in Fuchang Linggang to place the Buddha's body. The day the temple was built happened to be February 19 of the lunar calendar, the auspicious day of the zodiac, which was set for worship. Since then, people from Yoshimura, Yongping, and Shuizhuping villages have never gone to Baizhushan to have fun, because the Fuchang Guanyin Temple Fair on February 19 every year is just as lively as the 14th of January in Baizhushan.
