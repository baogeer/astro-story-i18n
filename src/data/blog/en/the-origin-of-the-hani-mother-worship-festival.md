 Mother's Day is a traditional sacrificial festival of the Hani people. The time usually falls on the first day of the cow in February of the lunar calendar.
Legend has it that a long time ago, a young widow cut down on clothes, worked hard to raise her son, and married his daughter-in-law to become a family. However, the well-intentioned mother didn't get good news. Not only did the son not honor his mother, but he also scolded his mother every day and ignored her life. The mother couldn't stand the torment, so she threw herself into the river.
The son's filial piety had serious consequences. The son did not become rich by reducing his mother; instead, he became poor day by day, and in the end he had to ask for food for a living. It was then that my son suddenly realized his mistakes and decided to change his past mistakes. Because the mother threw herself into the river on the day of the cow, her son designated that day as Mother's Day. To miss his mother's kindness, the son chose a big tree near the stockade as his mother's symbol.
The traditional “Mother Festival” of the Hani people is formed every year. On Mother's Day, people wear gorgeous costumes, bring sacrifices, gather in front of a big tree on the side of the stockade, pluck banana leaves, put them on the ground, lay out sacrifices such as wine, meat, chicken, sugar cane, candy, etc. brought by various households, and play drums and gongs.
The song says, “I had my mother tree in front of me. Seeing the tree, I burst into tears. Singing hundreds of songs, I can't finish singing my mother's kindness. Mountain springs are constant, and there is no motherly affection. The heroes are all raised by their mothers, and there is also a mother who asks for food...” After singing, people sit around drinking and eating together according to their age group. During the meeting, the elderly talked about the past and the present, and taught young people to respect the elderly and care for children. Everyone discussed and judged how good or bad each family in the cottage treated their parents. Good praise and bad reviews don't end until the sun sets.
