 Once upon a time, there was someone who wanted to have a good life in heaven and invited someone to give him the medicine to ascend the sky. After the medicine was prepared, he mixed the medicine with the meal and didn't take it right away. He wanted to wash his whole body before taking it, so he took a bath. At this point, an old hen came to his rice bowl with a bunch of chicks and ate all the food. When he finished bathing and came to eat, there was only an empty bowl left, and this bunch of chickens went up to heaven because they had taken the elixir of heaven. Since then, a bunch of constellations have appeared in the sky.
Narrator: Luo Guiying (Yi Ethnic)
