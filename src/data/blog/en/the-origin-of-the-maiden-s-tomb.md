 Legend has it that Usun has a Kazakh tribe called “Bai” (that is, the rich owner). Their beautiful daughter fell in love with Baiken, the son of a poor herder. The marriage was strongly opposed by the “Bai” family, and they placed their daughter under house arrest.
The girl used Akan's singing event to sneak into the forest. After three days and three nights on horseback, she finally found Baiken and became a couple.
“Bai Yi” sent out all the riders to find them, left Baiken off a cliff to feed the wolves on the spot, and forcibly married her to the Wu Sun nobleman Dalu.
On the night of her wedding, the infatuated girl jumped off a cliff to herself.
To miss the girl, when people pass by this place, they pick up a few beautiful stones and place them on the cliff. Over time, a large stone tomb was formed here, which is the origin of the girl's tomb.
