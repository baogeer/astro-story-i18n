 Changshou Village, Fuheng Township, Yangyi County, has two cliffs. One cliff is as round and rich as a steamed bun, while the other is steep. The two cliffs are next to each other and interconnected. They are known as Husband and Wife Cliff, also known as Gongmu Cliff.
There are many explanations about the origin of Gongmu Cliff; what I'm talking about now is just one of them.
Legend has it that there is a demon who has a backstage in the demon world and wanders the world. He specializes in drinking the blood and bone marrow of boys and girls to enhance his magic.
The devil sent out many devil demons to search for boys and girls, causing anger from heaven and people.
One day, a little demon drove over the Lancang River and saw an old woman playing along the riverside with a pair of boys and girls. The little demon was overjoyed, contained the limelight, landed on the ground, and turned into an old man with white hair and silver beard. The little demon had a wide robe and big sleeves and swayed in front of the old woman. The little demon is close to the old woman and wants to take the plane and take those two boys and girls into their sleeves.
The old woman's old eyes were blurry. She looked over and over again when she got goosebumps all over her body. The devil thought to herself, what happened to me? Is it because of the exhaustion of the road that it didn't work out? In the eyes of the little demon, that pair of boys and girls was just a dish. On weekdays, as long as he secretly keeps his cuffs open and works in the blink of an eye, the pair of boys and girls will be sucked into his sleeve. However, right now, he did it three times in a row, and the boys and girls are still playing, and there is no reaction at all.
The little demon was taken aback and didn't know what went wrong. In a hurry, they took the boy and girl by hand. However, no matter how the little demon flutters, the boys and girls hide and seek with the little demons in the middle of the big rock on the riverside, and the little demons can't even touch the bodies of the boys and girls. Instead, I was so tired that I let out my breath.
The little demon squinted at the old woman's relaxed and comfortable appearance. The old woman softly said, “Put away evil thoughts, reform yourself, protect living beings, and naturally become a Buddha.” As soon as the demon heard it, he knew that he had met a tall man and was afraid to stay. He immediately drove the demon wind and went back to his life.
The devil's training has reached a critical point, and there is an urgent need for a large number of boys and girls. After hearing the little demon's report, the devil got angry and said, “How dare the old monster teach this devil a lesson. Bad me a good thing. Big demon, go with the little demon right away, grab the boys and girls for me, and destroy the old monster by the way.”
Two demons, big and small, flew over the Lancang River. I saw that there were countless boys, boys, and girls along the Lancang River. The devil laid down his cape in the air, and the wind blew up. Sand and stones flew away, and it was dark. The boys, girls and boys were all sucked into the cloak. The two demons tightened their cloaks, drove the demon style, turned the limelight, and rushed back to enjoy them.
The devil couldn't help but be overjoyed when he saw his bursting cloak. However, when the monster unraveled his cape, what came out were large and small stones, and there were shadows of boys and girls. The devil was furious and screamed: “OK, big demon, how dare you make fun of me. Little ones, quickly send me to the Demon Furnace and turn him to ashes.”
The demon shouted wrongdoing. The demons couldn't help but talk about it, and threw the demon into the Demon Refining Furnace. After a day and night of hard work, the demon will go out of smoke.
The little demon hurriedly testified for the big demon and said, “I watched with my own eyes that the boy, the girl, and the old monster were sucked into the cape. It must have been that old monster messed up again. Please check with the king.”
At this point, a small frog jumped out of the pile of stones. I only heard the little frog shouting, “Devil, you demon who has destroyed your life, immediately change evil to the right, and you will have time to achieve the right results. If you don't get carried away and continue to do evil, you will be punished.”
The demon was so angry that he screamed “wow wow” and ordered all the demons to catch the little frog in a loud voice. A group of demons swooped at the little frog, and the little frog jumped back and forth, and no one could catch them. For a while, it became a mess. The little frog jumped and kept shouting, “If the devil is vicious and does not change, he will be punished.”
The devil was about to destroy the little frog, and suddenly the little frog disappeared.
The demon shouted in the Demon Furnace, “King, you can get rid of that old monster by following a plan.”
The devil called out the giant demon, and listen to his ingenious plan. Although the monster only spent an hour in the Demon Refinement Furnace, it has become a fireball. The demons put out the fire on the demon's body, and only heard the demon say, “With the magic power of the king moving the mountain to reclaim the sea, getting rid of that old monster is not as easy as twisting an ant to death. I see, as long as you block the Lancang River, you can drown that old monster alive.”
The demons joined in unison, and the devil thought it was a clever trick, so they let the big demon lead the way and rage headlong into the Lancang River.
That old woman is Guanshiyin Bodhisattva who saves suffering. Seeing that demons have repeatedly maimed lives, she transformed into an old woman to persuade the devil to change evil and return to justice. However, the devil not only did not repent, but instead intensified, making evil worse. If demons block the Lancang River, countless creatures will be destroyed. Bodhisattva Kanzeon resolutely applied the law to demons and demons, set demons and demons halfway, and stopped the devil's sinful acts.
As the years passed, demons and demons became cliffs. Since the demon has been burned in the Demon Furnace, not only is it round in shape, but even the body fluid that comes out is hot. However, in the eyes of the devil, his magic power was about to reach its peak, and it was suddenly stopped. The body fluids that came out were naturally cool and cold.
The big demon is slightly round and flows warm water, and is called the mother cliff; the devil is slightly taller and flows cold water, and is called the gong cliff.
Looking in the direction of the Lancang River, the first thing I saw was the Mother Cliff, because the demon led the way ahead.
Descendants appreciated the great grace and virtue of the Buddha and built a temple of Guanyin on the top of a small hill in front of Gongmu Cliff. That small hilltop is also called Guanyin Mountain.
