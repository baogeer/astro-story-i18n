 The ancestors of the Hmong started only with wooden drums, also known as leather drums. The bronze drums were sent down from heaven. When it comes to bronze drums, everyone knows that only the old fairy, Wu Hou, has it. It is said that she participated in pioneering the world and achieved success, and the king of heaven specially gave her this precious gift, asking her to take it back to the world and have fun with everyone. This bronze drum has a detailed pattern. When you knock it, the valley vibrates. When people listen, their hearts are excited; when birds listen, they want to sing. Who knows what Mu Hou brought to the world, but he took over it alone. She also specially fed two evil dogs, and has guarded the bronze drum for many months as an adult. The two vicious dogs were ferocious and unusually, and no one dared to stand next to each other.
Every time the holiday season arrives, when everyone is happily dancing around wooden drums, they naturally think of Wu Hou's bronze drums. If you get a bronze drum, how great would it be! One year, the Lunar New Year was here. In Longtouzhai by the Qingshui River, a young man and woman took advantage of the holiday to get married. They made a reservation for many guests and got ready for a lively event. Everything is ready, and it's best to borrow some bronze drums from Wu Hou to knock on it, then I'm really satisfied!
Everyone came up with a solution: gather money to find Wu Hou to rent a bronze drum for a day. As a result, the people who were sent got in the way of something, and everyone was very upset. At this point, a junior named Hasonga stood up. He said, “Friends and family! With such a grand festival and such a lively wedding, you can't live without bronze drums! I'd like to take another trip and find a way to go to the complaint room and the office game ①.” Everyone agreed, and Bosonga then went to the lawsuit hall and the office competition to talk about everyone's wishes. Gu Tingla and Uchiang Sai gave Bosonga three handfuls of rapeseed, three handfuls of water, and three handfuls of rocks, and taught him how to use these things. Bosonga immediately went home and found two daikon radishes, baked them half-cooked in a fire pit, hot to the point of boiling hot, wrapped in rotten cotton, and packed them in a small wooden box. Put the things given to you in the lawsuit room or office game in your purse. Everything was ready, and he immediately went to Wu Hou to borrow some bronze drums.

They swirled around on the ground, and their teeth were all burnt out. Busonga stepped into Wu Hou Nie's mansion on three legs and walked away with a bronze drum on her back. Because I was in a hurry, I forgot to look at the road, and fell. The bronze drum hit the stone, and the sound shook the valley and spread to the sky. Wu Hou Nie heard the sound of bronze drums and immediately rushed home. As soon as I entered the house, I saw that my beloved bronze drum was gone. The two dogs had lost their teeth and screamed loudly. Wu Hou was so angry that he stomped his feet. She immediately took off the two hand rings and placed them in the mouths of the two dogs. The two dogs immediately had sharp teeth. Wu Hou Xie immediately chased after him with his dog.
Bosonga ran with a bronze drum and suddenly heard a dog barking and knew it was Wu Hou who was chasing him. The dog's cry was getting closer and closer. He immediately pulled out the three handfuls of rapeseed from the lawsuit office and sprinkled it on the side of the road. Three tender, tender vegetable seedlings appeared in front of him.
