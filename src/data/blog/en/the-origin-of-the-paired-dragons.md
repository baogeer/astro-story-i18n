 Taiping Township in Mingguang City has a wide surface about 15 miles south along the Huai River. The water flow is smooth in the west and the water flow is raging in the east. This slow and rapid water situation often forms large swirls here, and the depth of the water below the whirlpool is difficult to measure. A farm on the west bank of the river is called “Duolong”. The swirl where the dragon is facing each other is known as the “Old Dragon's Nest”. There is a local legend about the origin of these two place names.
A long time ago, there were two young dragons in the Yellow Sea who were very playful and played at the Huanghai Dragon Palace all day long. After being tired of playing for a long time, I don't know whose idea it was. They both sneaked out of Dragon Palace and wanted to play in rivers and lakes on land. They both first went to the Yellow River. They felt that the water contained too much sediment. They couldn't open their eyes, and it wasn't fun, so they arrived at Hongze Lake. Hongze Lake is a great place to visit. The surface of the lake is wide, you can see fish clearly, and there are rhombus horns and lotus roots everywhere on the shoals. At the beginning of July, the white, pink lotus gave off an intoxicating fragrance. The purple diamond flowers bloomed on the tip of the waves, the little fish playing between the rhombus flower and the diamond leaf, and the white sails dotted with fishing songs in the distance, making people feel like they were in a wonderland. The scenery of the lake was so refreshing and unforgettable. The two young dragons swam to the fishing boat for a while, then fly to the place where the lotus flowers are in full bloom. After a while, they peek out half of their dragon bodies and look down at the reflection of the green mountains in the lake. As they rushed to the fishing boat, the fishermen were startled, and the fishermen were full of grievances; when they rushed to the lotus, the beautiful petals fell into the water, revealing their naked showerheads; when they discovered half of their dragon body looking down at the green mountain, the waves stirred up the green mountains in the water. The two young dragons were making such a fuss in the water that shocked the Hongze Lake Turtle Palace. The Turtle King sent his men to find out. Soon, a group of shrimp soldiers came to report that two young dragons were bothering in the lake. King Turtle ordered everyone to go out and stir up the lake water to drive this young dragon away. As a result, shrimp, and crabs at the bottom of the lake will be dispatched at the same time to stir up the sediment at the bottom of the lake. The lake water will begin to change from clear to mixed, and the turbidity will increase. This turtle king doesn't know anything. The two young dragons are from the Yellow Sea Dragon Palace and have just come from the Yellow River. How afraid of the sediment at the bottom of the river? For three days in a row, the two young dragons didn't care about the turbidity of the lake. On the contrary, most of the small fish and shrimp in the lake died and were injured. The lotus leaves were not green, the rhombus flowers were not purple, the lotus flowers lost their petals, and the center of the lake had no sails. The beautiful scenery was stirred up and dark. When King Turtle saw that he was unable to deal with the two baby dragons, he had to order the military to be drafted.
Hongze Lake has been ruined unbearably. It has lost its former beauty, and the two baby dragons have no intention of continuing to play. They both went all the way west, out of Hongze Lake and into the Huai River. Not far from the entrance to the Huai River, there is a high hill in front of it, so it's a bogang. The Huai River flows from here. The main stream runs west to the main channel of the Huai River via Bogang, and the other goes south around Bogang. It is a tributary. When the water is plentiful, the surface of the river is wide, and boats go deep. During the dry water period, the surface of the river narrows, and the water is covered with dense reeds.
After the two young dragons swam into the Huai River, they turned their heads towards the south. The willows were green and shaded, and the reeds were scattered. On the lakeshore, there was an inexhaustible sight of villages, farmland, flocks of sheep, people singing, dogs barking, and chirping. The two young dragons have been laid-back, relaxed, and free. Before they knew it, they had already gone through the bench. As soon as they stared at the prawn essence along the way, the two young dragons were still traveling forward. They immediately ran back to Hongze Lake to report to King Turtle. As soon as Wang heard, he quickly summoned his subordinates to discuss an urgent matter, and couldn't come up with a good solution. He was in a hurry. Still, Hercules came up with an idea and said, “Why don't you take a mountain to the river to block the way, then the dragon will definitely come back; at the same time, send shrimp, and crab to cut down the reeds north of Zhangtai, leaving sharp stubble. If the two young dragons had to swim, they would definitely peel open their flesh. This way, there is no way back and forth, so I can control them in the middle.” King Turtle was overjoyed and immediately agreed to Hercules Turtle's proposal.
The Hercules turtle chose a potato-shaped stone mountain at the foot of Mount Dragon and did its best to carry it into the river, blocking its way.
Furthermore, the two young dragons crossed the Huayanzui River and headed east. When they saw that the water in front of the river was blocked by a mountain, they thought they had reached the end, and the two young dragons swam all the way back. This moment is not the other time; this path is no longer the way to go. First, the water level has dropped. Second, 10,000 mu of reeds north of Zhangtai have been cut down, leaving behind dense reed stubble that is like a sword, and it is no longer possible to survive. The two baby dragons are trapped on a shoal, in a dilemma. Seeing that the water level is still falling, they will be trapped to death if they don't try to do it sooner. The two young dragons made a concerted effort to save their lives and choose a place with a wider surface of the water to roll over and tail, throwing the soil on the riverbed ashore with force. The two young dragons kept turning over, throwing up blocks of earth, and the waves that stirred up were spectacular on the surface of the river for a moment. People from both sides of the Strait rushed to the top of the shore to watch. The two young dragons were busy for three days and three nights. They built a mountain-like tall package on the east bank of the river, pulled out an unfathomably deep nest that was nearly half a mile long, and the two young dragons swam to the west bank to rest in the river.
Since then, the two baby dragons have often appeared here. Every day, thousands of people come here from all sides to watch dragons. Because they are two, people call them “opposing dragons.” It wasn't until the water level rose in the second year, and the reed stubble died after a winter, and tender new reeds grew from the roots. The two young dragons only swam back to the Yellow River, and they never dared to come out again.” It was then that the allusion that “Dragon's Dead Shoals can't be used” spread.
Later, people named a small village on the riverbank where the dragon rested. The deep pool where the dragon hid across the river. People called it “Old Dragon's Nest” until now.
