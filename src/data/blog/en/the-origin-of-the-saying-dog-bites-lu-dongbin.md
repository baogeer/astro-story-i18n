 Lu Dongbin used to be a book reader before becoming Xian Dao. One of his friends is a fellow countryman named Gou Yi. Gou Yao's parents both died. Her family was poor, but she was loyal. He was an old gentleman, and was very diligent in studying. Lu Dongbin was very appreciative of him, befriended him as the Jin Lan brother, and invited him to live in his own home. He hoped that he would study hard and have a good time in the future.
One day, a guest surnamed Lin came to Lu Dongbin's family. Seeing that Gou Yue showed her talent and hard work, she said to Lu Dongbin that she wanted to match her younger sister Xu to Gou Yi. Lu Dongbin was deeply afraid that being greedy for the joy of being in bed would be wrong with Splendid Future, so he hurriedly promoted it. Unexpectedly, Gou Yao herself heard that Miss Lin was beautiful and insisted on agreeing to this family affair. Lu Dongbin thought for a long time and agreed. He said to Ke Yao, “Now that my brother has made up his mind, I won't stop him, but after getting married, I will sleep with the bride for three nights.” Gou Ya was taken aback when she heard it. Why can't I bow my head when I'm under someone's fence? Besides, all the expenses for the wedding have to depend on the Lu family. Who left themselves destitute? After thinking about it, I clenched my teeth and agreed.
On the day Gou You became a family, Lu Dongbin was full of joy, taking care of everything before and after the run. However, Gou was faceless in his face; he simply hid to the side. At night, the guests were sent away, and Lu Dongbin went into the cave room. I saw the bride cover her head in red and sit leaning back on her bed. Lu Dongbin didn't lift that red hijab, didn't talk; he just sat under the lamp and read his head. Miss Lin waited until the middle of the night, and her husband still didn't go to bed; she had to sleep in her own clothes. When I woke up, my husband had long since disappeared. It's been like this for three nights in a row. But Miss Lin was ruined. Looking back, she had a hard time getting through three days. She had just entered the cave. She was sad and in tears. She looked down and cried, “Why didn't Lang Jun go to bed and sleep together for three nights, but only studied under the light. When it was dark, he left in the morning?” I was dumbfounded at this question. The bride looked up and was even more astonished: Why did her husband change someone? After half a day, the couple suddenly realized it. Gou Yao waved his feet and laughed out loud: “Turns out my brother was afraid I was greedy and forgot to read. He used this method to inspire me!” Miss Lin is also very happy and has a lot of respect for Lu Dongbin. Both husband and wife said, “Brother Lu must repay this favor in the future.
A few years later, Gou Yao certainly got a title on the Gold List and became a senior official. The couple tearfully parted ways with Lu Dongbin's family and took office. Eight years have passed in a blink of an eye. In the summer of this year, the Lu family caught fire by accident, and a huge portion of the family's wealth was reduced to ashes. Lu Dongbin and his wife had no choice but to live in a hut made of broken bricks. Needless to say, life was difficult enough. Lu Dongbin had no choice but to go out and get help from Gou Yao. After a lot of hard work along the way, I finally found Gouyofu. Gou Yao was very sympathetic to Lu Dongbin's family fire and warmly received him, but he just didn't mention helping him. Lu Dongbin stayed for a few months and didn't get any money. Lu Dongbin looked up at the sky and sighed: “People are as thin as paper. As soon as you broaden your face, it changes.” In one fell swoop, they said goodbye.
Back in his hometown, Lu Dongbin saw his broken cottage replaced with a new tiled house. He was very surprised: far away from home, his young wife is weak, how can he prosper in civil engineering? Approaching the house, the Three Spirits were even more shocked: there was a blank sheet of paper on both sides of the door. Someone died in the family? He hurriedly entered the house and saw a coffin parked in the house. His wife, Pima Dai Xiao, was crying. Lu Dongbin was stunned for half a day: Who is Dai Xiao for? Shout softly, “Ma'am.” When the lady looked back, she was terrified and shivered and shouted, “You, are you a human or a ghost?” Lu Dongbin was even more surprised: “Why did the lady say this? I'm back in good shape, how is it a ghost?” It took half a day for the lady to believe that Lu Dongbin actually came back and said, “Oops! It really scared me to death! Wouldn't this be a dream?” It turns out that soon after Lu Dongbin left home, a group of people came to help him build a house, and when he finished building the house, he left. At noon the day before yesterday, another group of people brought in a coffin, saying that Lu Dongbin had died of illness in Gou Yao's family. As soon as my wife heard it, the sky fell to the ground, and she cried to death. I was crying today; I didn't want Lu Dongbin to come back.
Lu Dongbin understood from the bottom of his heart: it was all just a trick for fun. He wielded an axe and slashed the coffin hard. With a “click”, the coffin broke open; it was actually all gold and silver treasures, as well as a letter. Lu Dongbin opened up the letter and read, “Gou Yao is not a bad person; he sent the gold and silver family to build a house. You left my wife in an empty room, and I left your wife crying.” Lu Dongbin woke up like a dream and smiled bitterly: “Brother, your gang can help me so much!”
Since then, Lu Gou's family has become even more affectionate. This is what is commonly known as the saying, “Go to Lu Dongbin, don't know a good person's heart”. Because “Gou Yu” is synonymous with “dog bites,” it has been passed down as “dog bites Lu Dongbin, doesn't know good people's heart.”
