 The origin of the incident is that, according to legend, the three ancient brothers split up and scattered all over the place, and Guanshan's blockade prevented them from meeting each other on weekdays. As a result, the brothers met on their leisure days after cutting rice and were close to each other to celebrate the harvest and not forget their hands and feet. They chose this day to reunite because they shared their home in “Hai”. Since then, “Hai” Day has passed since then, and it has become an aquarium festival that has been passed down from generation to generation.
In order to allow siblings, family, and friends to visit each other on “Hai” day, the rule of taking turns has formed in the history of aquariums. From late August to early October of the lunar calendar, from north to south, they pass on “Hai” day every 12 days.
