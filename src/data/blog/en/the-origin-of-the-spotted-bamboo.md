 Legend has it that Shun Yuan came out to fight against evil dragons, but there was no news. Empress Empress Ei learned of Shun's death while visiting the Yangtze River area. She washed her face with tears every day, and the surrounding bamboo grew spots, and is known as Xiang Fei Bamboo.
