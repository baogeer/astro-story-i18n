 On the eve of the festival (Tiger Day), every household must carefully clean the house, yard dam, and scatter indoor and outdoor dust into the rice fields. There is a folklore about the origin of this custom: in the early years, when the ancestors of aquariums led people from the south to the upper reaches of the Duliu River, they did not avoid hardships, built mountains and fields, and founded homes. However, the rice they sowed was repeatedly damaged by pests, and everyone was worried that they couldn't think of a way to get rid of the insects. One night, in the dream of our ancestors, they were instructed by Gong Lu Duo, the founder of Xianren Zao Shui Shui Shu, and told them to pour the dust from cleaning the house into the fields so that pests could be avoided. Everyone did what Gong Lu Duo showed in his dream, and this year was a good harvest. Since then, people have been cleaning houses on this day, praying for a good harvest in Gong Lu Duo, and slowly deepened into a festival.
