 Legend has it that this day is the day when the birth mother and daughter give their inheritance to the world, so the local Han people call it the “Women's Day.” During the holidays, a place of sacrifice should be set up in the village. The chairman worships the birth mother “Ni Hang,” also known as “honoring the teeth,” and a side seat for wild ghosts to “heal their teeth.” According to folklore, once upon a time, when there was an epidemic in aquarium villages, babies died a lot, and the laughter of the dolls was almost impossible to hear. Fortunately, at this time, the fairies in the sky, Yahwa-ri, Yahwa-long, and Yahwa-jutsu were performed, eliminating the plague, saving the world, and sending blessings to children, making Aquarium Village flourish again. To commemorate the blessings of these four fairies, Aqua Village set up a festival on the day of their birth, and followed suit as a folk festival.
