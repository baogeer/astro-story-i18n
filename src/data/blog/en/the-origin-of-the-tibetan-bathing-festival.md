 Every night in late summer and early autumn, a very bright star appears over southeast Lhasa. At this point, the Tibetan people began their annual bathing festival. Legend has it that the star only appeared seven nights. Why do people mark the seven nights when the stars appear as a bathing festival?
A long, long time ago, there was a famous doctor on the prairie. His name was Uto Yundan Gongbu. He has excellent medical skills, and he can treat any difficult or complicated condition. Therefore, Zao Akamatsu Toku praised him as a royal doctor and was responsible for treating the Zao king and his concubines. However, after Uto entered the palace, he still couldn't forget the people on the prairie in his heart. He often uses the effort of going out to collect medicine to treat people's diseases.
One year, a terrible plague spread. Many herders were bedridden, and some took their lives. At this point, Yutuo ran across the vast prairie to treat a family of sick herders. He takes all kinds of medicines from snowy mountains and old woods, and whoever gets sick will get better. I don't know how many patients who are close to death have recovered their health.
Dr. Yu Tuo's name is spread all over the prairie, and people call him the King of Medicine. Unfortunately, Dr. Uto passed away.
After his death, there was another terrible plague on the prairie. It was worse than the previous one, and many people died. The herdsmen, whose lives were at risk, had to kneel down and pray for heaven's blessings.
Coincidentally, one day, a woman who had been tortured by an illness for the rest of her life suddenly had a dream. In her dream, Dr. Yutuo said to her, “Tomorrow night, when a bright star appears in the southeast sky, you can go down to the Jiqu River to take a bath. Your illness will get better after taking a bath.”
Sure enough, after this woman bathed in the Jiqu River, her illness was immediately eliminated. A yellow and thin patient became a healthy person with a red face after taking a bath. After this news spread, all the patients came to the river to bathe. Every patient who took a bath got rid of their illness and recovered their health.
People say that this strange star was transformed by Dr. Yu Tuo. Dr. Yu Tuo saw in heaven that the prairie people were also attacked by the plague, and he was unable to come to the world to treat people's diseases, so he turned himself into a star, turned the river water into a potion using starlight, and made people bathe in the river to get rid of diseases. Because the Emperor only gave Yu Tuo seven days, this star only appeared for seven days.
Since then, the Tibetan people have designated these seven days as a bathing festival. Every year at this time of year, herders go to the nearby river to bathe. It is said that after bathing, people are healthy and happy, and do not get sick.
