 Zuo Wenhan's oral description u2002 collected and sorted by Zuo Dehui
In the past, children were frightened, or had yellow hair. Adults said that the child's soul was lost, and the mother had to call the child Soul; otherwise, the child's life would be difficult to protect. The time for a mother to cry out for her child is when the sky is about to brighten, or when it is just dark. The mother held an old, unwashed, sweaty, and dirty coat under her left hand, and stood in the middle of the middle of the middle house door with a smoking firewood stick in her right hand and shouted: “Come back -- come back! My kid so-and-so is back! Don't go in a place where there's no cooking smoke, don't go where chickens don't bark, don't where dogs bark! Come back--, come back! My kid so-and-so is back! There is no road in the sky; step on colorful clouds; there is no path on the ground; ants are coming on their way! Come back--, come back! My kid so-and-so is back! Go back to the place where the cooking smoke is burning; go back to the place where the chicken barks, go back to the place where the dog barks! Come back--, come back! My kid, so-and-so, is back!” It is said that boys need to call for 9 mornings or 9 nights; girls only need to call 7 mornings or 7 nights.
Why can Yi mothers save their children's lives by calling them souls? Not to mention the mother and child being connected; let's just say that the Yi people have an ancient and amazing legend.
A long time ago, a group of defeated generals dodged enemy hunts in a tile kiln. One person heard his mom calling him at the doorstep of Wayao. It felt very strange. It was thousands of miles away from home, and it was impossible for his mother to come here at all. However, his mother's urgent cries kept coming, and he thought: “Let's keep him 3721, why not run out and see.” Thinking this way, he sprinted out of the Wayao at a speed of 100 meters. As soon as he ran outside the Wayao, the Wayao fell down and buried all the other generals alive. The man who came out went through a lot of hard work, trekking mountains and water, and only learned that his mother had saved his life when he got home. It turns out that after he went out to join the military, his mother was very worried about his safety. Every morning and evening, she held his clothes and kept calling, every 9 days, taking a break for a few days, and continuing to call until her son came back.
Since then, the custom of mothers calling their children souls has spread.
