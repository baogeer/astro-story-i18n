 Once upon a time, there were two parents and a son. A son did ahbi in heaven, and a father did ahbi on earth. There is a well with yin and yang water on the ground. If there are three green leaves floating in this well, it indicates the death of young people; if there are three yellow leaves floating, it indicates the death of the elderly.
One day, my son came down from heaven to work with his father on earth. I was thirsty when I walked home from the Yin and Yang Well after doing it, so I went to the well to find water to drink. When my son arrived at the well and saw three green leaves floating around, he knew he was about to die, so he said to his dad, “Dad, drink some water first.” His dad also said, “You can do it in heaven; you're better at it than me; you should drink it first.” My son also said, “Drink today, I'll hold you when you drink water” (because of the depth of the well, you need someone to hold your feet when you drink water; you can only drink if you lean down). As a result, the father drank it first. After drinking, he held his son and asked him to drink, but when the father was too old to hold him, he fell into the well and drowned. His father is very sad and misses his son very much. Every time he eats, he takes meals to the well to see his son, and he can vaguely see his son's shadow in the well. But no matter how much the father cries, the son never talks to his father. In order to see his son clearly, the father set up a fishing net in the well. If he wanted to catch his son, he caught a scarecrow as soon as he caught it. The father took this scarecrow home, made a noodle man in the shape of his son using soba noodles, placed it on the scarecrow, made a small coffin, and put the scarecrow and noodle man in the coffin together. Then pick him up as he did from a dead person (that is, his father blows into the mouth of a hermit), and finally take his soul home from the well.
Since then, after the death of the Yi people, grass-roots mourning activities have begun.
