 Narrated by Zuo Zhihong and Wu Fuhai, collected by Yang Dengtang
In the Bimo Sutra of the Yi people, it is recorded that once upon a time, there was a demon from mixed worlds. The devil called “Xie” in Yi slaughtered the world everywhere, bullied men and women, and did nothing evil to live a peaceful day and suffer in the deep heat of the water. At this point, one of our Yi ancestors had a hero named Ah Wu. He had great ingenuity and great strength. He was specially designed to fight back the people. Everyone loved him. He learned that there was a “little girl” who was harming the people of the tribe, so he just subdued this demon. After hundreds of rounds of competition, “Xia” was no match for Abou's power and escaped. After that, “Xia” had no way to escape, and finally “Xia” had no way to escape. At the end, “Xia” had no way to escape and penetrated a cave in Cangshan. One end of this cave led to the Three Pagodas Temple on the eastern slope of Cangshan Mountain, and the other led to Yangyang Shimen Pass. Abu trapped the demon “Xia Xia” in the cave and was not allowed to harm humans. Asking him to go out a few times a year to get out to breathe. In the end, Abu promised him to go out in Dongpo for the day and Xipo for the night, and ordered him to go out to Dongpo The day is March 19 of every year, and the day when Xipo comes out is June 25 of every year. By March 19, Abu mobilized people to hold a material exchange meeting near Dongpo. People rushed rakes and furniture to trade, and got ready “carefully”. After looking at it, it was full of people at the foot of Cangshan Mountain. Turtles had to huddle in the cave. The feed machine came out in Xipo on the evening of June 25.
On the evening of June 25, Abu organized the ethnic group in Xipo to light torches and sing and dance, and got ready to go out carefully. After looking, Manshan Katano torches illuminated the forest house in red. Men and women danced with big swords and had a blast dancing with big swords, and they were afraid to come out. Since then, “Little Me” never had a chance to harm the world, and has been stuck in Cangshan Cave forever. The people of the tribe have lived a happy and stable life without being harmed by demons, and the Torch Festival has continued from generation to generation.
Circulation area: Wachang, Longtan, Chicken Street, Shunyi and other places
