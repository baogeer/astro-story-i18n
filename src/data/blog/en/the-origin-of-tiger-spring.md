 Longjing tea (one of the top ten famous teas in China) and Hupaoquan are known as “the best in Hangzhou.” How did Toraizumi come about?
It is said that they had two brothers a long time ago. The older brothers are named Otaka and Ergo. The two are so big. One year they came to Hangzhou and wanted to settle down and live in the small monastery where they are now. The monk told them that draining water was difficult here, so they had to climb a few ridges to fetch water. The brothers said, “As long as we can stay, we will take care of picking up water, so the monk took in the brothers.”
One summer, there was no rain or rain, and the creek dried up, making it even more difficult to drink water. One day, the brothers remembered the “Tongziquan” wandering past Heng Mountain in Nanyue. It would be nice if they could move Tongziquan to Hangzhou.
The brothers decided to go to Hengshan to move to Tongziquan. They ran all the way and passed out when they reached the foot of Hengshan Mountain. The storm broke out. After the wind stopped and the rain stopped, they both woke up and saw a child standing in front of them with willow branches in their hands. This is the little fairy who manages “Tongziquan.”
After listening to their stories, Little Fairy Man spilled water on both of them with a finger. Instantly, the two brothers turned into two colorful tigers, and the child jumped on the back of the tiger. The tiger shouted at the sky and headed straight to Hangzhou with the “Tongziquan”.
The old monk and villagers had a dream at night. They dreamed that the big tiger and the two tigers had turned into two tigers, and moved the “Tongzi Spring” to Hangzhou, and the spring water will be there at dawn.
The next day, the sky was shining brightly, and two tigers fell from the sky. Tigers were in the bamboo garden next to the temple, and soon they dug a deep pit. Suddenly, the storm and rain raged. After the rain stopped, they saw a clear spring gushing out of the deep pit. Everyone understood that it must have been the spring water brought to them by the giant tiger and the two tigers.
To commemorate the big tiger and the two tigers, they named the spring “Tiger Shaved Spring.” It was later called “Tiger Pao Spring” for the sake of goodwill. Using Hupao Spring to brew Longjing tea, the color and aroma are excellent. Today, you can enjoy this “double” drink in Hupao teahouses.
