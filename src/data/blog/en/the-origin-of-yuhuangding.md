 Story ① The origin of “Yuhuangding”
Yuhuangding is actually originally called Yuhuangding. According to rumors, since Emperor Huizong of the Song dynasty was Fengguangyan, the son of King Jingde of Miao Leguo, was the supreme emperor of the Jade Emperor, and issued orders to build temples and statues all over the world to be enshrined. Since then, many alpine peaks across the country have built Yuhuang Temple, or Jade Emperor Peak.
The current name of Yuhuangding dates back to the 19th year of Qing Guangxu (1893 AD). At the Yantai Qishan Institute at the time, there was a master named Liu Ciyuan. He often visited Yuhuangding. He was fascinated by the beautiful scenery here every time, but he always felt that the name “Jade Emperor” was indecent. On the 1st, Liu Ciyuan said to his fellow travelers, “Strange, the name Jade Emperor Ding is not an affront to dignity? Furthermore, when this mountain range comes south to the top of the tower, it winds. If you are fighting for it, you can look at it from afar, like thirsty people rushing to the spring. To the east, there is a group of peaks in Saga, which stretches for three to five miles. To the west, there are layers of mountains, which are exuberant and obtrusive. The left ring is surrounded by the right, and it is in the middle of Duxi Mountain, which is beyond dusty and immaculate. This giant town in our village is truly a treasure trove of precious flowers. What's more, Yuhuang?” Everyone agreed. As a result, “Yu Huang” was changed to “Yu Huang,” and he also wrote the couplets “Yu Xiu Zhong Ling Di Doesn't Love Bao; Huangcong Pu Yu Mountain Glows by Himself”. The meaning is that nature does not spare its treasures; it condenses all natural beauty into this, so it can be called an otherworldly splendor. Here, the meaning of “Yu Huang” is both summarized and explained in detail: “Yu” means nurturing and nurturing; “Huang” is a kind of exquisite jade, which also highlights the beautiful and gorgeous characteristics of the mountains here.
