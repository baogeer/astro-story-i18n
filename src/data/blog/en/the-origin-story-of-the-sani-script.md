Stone Forest Folk Tales: The Origin Story of the Sani Script
I heard from my grandpa that the Sani people have two types of script, “Northern script” and “Southern script,” and the southern script is also collectively known as “Ajirni Shishi” (transliteration) ①.
The origin of the “Ajirni Master” has been passed down from generation to generation: a long, long time ago, there was a Sani, named Lao Tu Chu, who lost his parents at an early age and was so ugly that he was thrown on a mountain rock by his sister. This mountain rock Sani was called the “Doer Bandit.”
A female tiger's cry attracted a female tiger. It lovingly carried the baby to the tiger's den in Shiyan. When the baby touched the tiger's milk, the female tiger suckled the baby...
A few years later, Lao Tzu and his mother tiger walked alone and spent their days picking wild fruits and hunting deep in the mountains. He is brave and extremely clever, but he is very bitter.
He lived in a mountain stream, and when he heard the Sani girls singing while grazing in the mountains, he hid beside him to eavesdrop and record. In order to write down the suffering of the Sani people, he shot the wild goose, took the guts of ink, used the goose's beak as a pen, spread his wings on paper, drew many graphic symbols to indicate language, and made it into Sani.
After writing 120 Pairs of Goose Wings, he couldn't write enough about the bitterness and hate in his heart. When one of Orima's older sisters-in-law learned about his situation, she kindly sent burlap as a book to help him continue writing songs of blood and tears of the Sani people. I wrote down 12 epics of Sani, counted 12 books, a total of 24 books, and spread my fame. Later, Muer, the leader of the Sani people, only knew more than Ripa. He was furious, “There can only be one king in a swarm, and a family can only have one wife. I am an official in charge of the world, and I will never allow Ar to crawl on my head.” As a result, the faction arrested the minions and scolded Ah, hoping to kill him.
The people of Sani admired the old man who wrote the Sani script and denounced Al, and secretly reported to him to protect the old man against Al. They had to run away to the southwest mountains and left behind 24 scriptures, which are still in circulation to this day. The Sani are commonly known as the Azabu Sutra.
① “Ajil Division”, translated from Sani into Chinese. In the southern part of Mount Gui, “Er” means “city”; “Nishi” means “Sani character”; “Ajir” is the chief's residence and was once the political, economic, and military center of the Sani people. “Achi” is the chief, the main character in “Ashima.”

Who Invented the Sani Script?
The transmission of culture is inseparable from words. The Sani are the only ethnic group in the Yi tribe that has its own unique script. Cang Jie invented Chinese characters, so who invented the Sani script? Is he just a servant? His name is synonymous with the Sani civilization.
Legend has it that at the foot of the majestic “Gubao” (Old Guishan), there is a Sani village with beautiful mountains and clear water — black. The village is home to Iwakinu, the famous big toast from near and far. He is in charge of the land for tens of miles, including scattered villages and hardworking farmers on this land. Iwaki is insidious, vicious, and cunning. Every night, he is arrogant, unbelievable, proud of the world and staring at one side.
The poor people in the village were all slaves of Iwaki's slaves. The poor were deterred by his indecent power and allowed him to take advantage of them. They were greedy early, and it was still difficult to fill their stomachs when the wind came and went in the rain. Poor people actually wake up earlier than chickens, sleep later than dogs, dry more than cows, and eat worse than pigs.
Where there is oppression, there is resistance. This is an immutable truth. Iwaki's cruelty aroused a crowd revolt among the poor. In a situation where the few were defeated, he made a plan to get the poor to recommend someone to negotiate with him. At this point, the poor naturally thought of being honest, wise, and unafraid of power. Just like that, Nobi relentlessly fought for reason in front of Iwaki, and won many benefits for the poor. However, when he wanted to fulfill his promise, Iwaki turned back, saying that there was no such thing. He also asked his family to spread rumors outside, saying that every time they went to Iwaki's house, they knew they drank and ate meat.
Over and over again, over and over again. Doesn't anyone believe in Nobita any more? He has no prestige, no foothold. He began wandering in the village of Sani. He was lying on the ground in the morning. The meteor passed through the sky, dragged his long tail, and the beauty disappeared in an instant. He jumped up. Right, Toast's beautiful promises aren't just like meteors; they can't be fixed or recorded; you say it's falling all over the place, and there's no foundation. And who believes it? He's not like a tree on a mountain; he can be counted by everyone; unlike a crack in a stone, he can show it to everyone. If there were a different symbol representing a different meaning, and everyone understood it, then no matter how crafty Toast was, no matter how crafty he was, he wouldn't be fooled by him.
As a result, Nubi Quan began a difficult calligraphy process. When he saw a mountain, he drew the shape of a mountain; when he saw that the river was rippled by the wind, he used a few lines to represent the river. He kept thinking and painting during the day. At night, he went to the village to show everyone what this represents and what that represents.
