 Meng Longtan in Jiangxi is a guest house in Beijing with Zhu Juren. They came to a monastery by chance and saw that the temple house was not very spacious; only an old monk who traveled all over the world stayed there temporarily. When the old monk saw a guest entering the door, he arranged his clothes to greet them and guided them both to explore the temple. In the main hall, there are statues of Shiko, whose hands and feet are shaped like bird claws. The murals on both sides of the wall are exquisite, and the characters on them are very lifelike. On the east side wall, there are pictures of celestial girls with scattered flowers. There is a girl with hanging hair in the middle. She draws flowers with her hands and smiles. Her little cherry mouth looks like she wants to talk, and her eyes twirl. Zhu Juren kept a close eye on her for a long time, and was immediately immersed in her loving contemplation.
Suddenly, he felt his body fluttering leisurely. It was like driving in the clouds, and he had arrived at the mural. Seeing the temple and pavilions repeated over and over again is no longer a sight on earth. There was an old monk preaching Dharma, and many monks were all around to listen. Zhu Juren also mixed up and stood in it. Soon, it seemed like someone was secretly holding his collar. Looking back, it turned out to be the girl with loose hair, walking away with a smile. Zhu Juren immediately followed her. After passing the tortuous fence, the girl entered a small house. Zhu Juren stopped and was afraid to go any further. The girl turned back, raised the flower in her hand, and greeted him from afar. Only then did Zhu Juren follow in. Seeing that there was no one in the house, he went to hug the girl. The girl wasn't very resistant, so he became affectionate with her. Soon, the girl closed the door and told Zhu Juren not to make any noise by coughing. She came back at night. After two days like this, the female partner discovered it. They found Zhu Juren one by one and joked with the young girl, “How old is the child in her stomach. Do you still want to learn to be a virgin?” They all brought hairpins and earrings to urge her to change her hairstyle to a young woman's hairstyle. The girl was too shy to speak. A female partner said, “Sisters, let's not wait here too long; I'm afraid they will not be happy.” The female companions left laughing. Zhu Juren looked at the young girl. Her hair bun, which is shaped like a cloud, is towering, and the phoenix hair hanging low. It is even more beautiful than when her hair was hanging down. When he saw no one around him, he gradually became close to the girl. The scent of orchid musk was refreshing, and the two were immersed in joy.
Suddenly, I heard the loud sound of leather boots walking, accompanied by a loud roar of rope locks. Immediately, there was another round of loud arguments. The girl was shocked. She secretly looked outside with Zhu Juren. She saw a divine man wearing a gold armor, dark face like paint, holding a rope lock, and carrying a hammer. Many women surrounded him. The god Golden Armor said, “Have you reached everything?” The women answered, “It's all there.” He also said, “If anyone is hiding a mortal from the world below, you must report it immediately, and don't blame yourself!” The women all said in unison, “No.” Golden Armor turned around and looked around fiercely like an osprey, as if to carry out a search. The girl was very afraid. Her face was so frightened that she panicked and said to Zhu Juren, “Hurry up and hide under the bed.” She herself opened the small door on the wall and fled in a hurry. Zhu Juren was lying under her bed, afraid to leave the atmosphere. Soon I heard the sound of leather boots coming into the room and walked out again. After a while, the hustle and bustle of the crowd gradually faded away, and Zhu Juren's mood calmed down a little bit. However, there are always people talking and talking outside the door. He lay uneasy for a long time, and felt that his ears were chirping and his eyes were burning, and he could hardly bear it. But I just listened quietly and waited for the girl to return, and I didn't even remember where I came from.
At the time, in the main hall, Meng Longtan suddenly disappeared Zhu Juren, and strangely asked the old monk. The old monk smiled and said, “I went to listen to the Dharma preaching.” Meng Longtan asked, “Where?” The old monk answered, “Not far.” After a while, the old monk bounced his finger against the wall and called out, “Master Zhu Shi has been playing for so long, why hasn't he come back?” I immediately saw a picture of Zhu Juren appearing on the mural. He was standing with his ears bent on his side, as if he had heard it. The old monk called out again, “Your travel companion has been waiting a long time.” Zhu Juren then casually came down the wall, standing discouraged, and startled. Meng Longtan was taken aback and asked him slowly. It turns out that Zhu Juren was lying under his bed just now and heard thunderous knocking on the wall, so he left the house to listen and take a look. At this point, they looked at the flower girl in the mural again. Her hair was already tall, and her hair was no longer hanging. Zhu Juren was surprised to greet the old monk and asked him what was going on. The old monk smiled and said, “Illusions are born in the mind; how can poor monks solve them!” Zhu Juren's heart was depressed and uncomfortable, while Meng Longtan's heart was shocked. The two immediately got up and said goodbye, then stepped out and left.
