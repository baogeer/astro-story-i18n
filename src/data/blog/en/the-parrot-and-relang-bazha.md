 Once upon a time, there was a woman named Geran Bazar who had sex with other men whenever her husband went outside.
Gélambazar fed a parrot. It was very unfamiliar with the hostess's behavior, and often talked about the scandals of Gélambazar in the presence of many guests.
Gélambazar was very annoyed and ordered the maid, Zaci Zhuoma, to take the parrot to a place where no one else could see it and quietly kill it.
Zaci Zhuoma thought: The parrot is not guilty; I can't kill it. He then brought the parrot to the edge of the Lhasa River and said, “Parrot! A parrot! Fly far away, fly to your hometown of Gongbu Forest! If the hostess Geran Bazar knew, I wouldn't do you any good.” When the parrot said “lasso,” it flew into the sky. Actually, it didn't go far; instead, it flew into the Jokhang Temple and hid behind the hat of the Buddha statue of Shakyamuni.
Although Geran Bazaar does unseen things at night, he still visits the Jokhang Temple every morning to worship Buddha. On this day, the parrot pretended to be Shakyamuni's voice and said, “Hey! “Gérambazar, would you like to go to heaven?” Geran Bazaar thought Buddha had spoken up. He danced with joy, and even nodded in gratitude: “Think! I want to! I even want to dream.”
“That's great!” The parrot said, “If you really want to go to heaven, throw away all your property within three days, give half to your maid, Zaci Zhuoma, and give the street scoundrel, and vow not to take it back.”
“Lasso! Lasso!” The woman quickly agreed.
“Today for one day, tomorrow for two days, the day after tomorrow, when the sun rises, you wear a broken robe and come here with a stick. I will help you walk to a happy paradise.”
Gélambazar heard the parrot and was so happy. When he met someone, he said, “Haha, Shakyamuni is going to overtake me! haha! I'm going to Paradise in Gélang Bazaar!”
By the third day, Geran Bazaar had run out of his family's possessions. He came to Buddha and nodded his head and said, “Buddha, I did what you ordered. Please help me ascend to heaven!”
At this point, the parrot came out from behind the Buddha statue and loudly said, “Bah! Shameless women like you still want to go to heaven, don't dream! Hurry up and bring your stick to Pajiao Street to ask for food. Parrot, I'm going back to the green forest of Gongbu! Ha, ha, ha!”
After speaking, as soon as I spread my wings, I couldn't even see the shadows.
