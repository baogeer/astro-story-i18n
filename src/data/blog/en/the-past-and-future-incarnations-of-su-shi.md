 Successful writers in the history of literature have always been the focus of people's attention. They have been studied a lot, and many have been attached. As a titan in the Northern Song literary scene, almost every aspect of Su Shi's literary creations and bits and pieces of his personal life have been swept away, and even the problems behind his predecessor, such as where he came from and where he went, have not escaped people's attachment.
Regarding where did Su Shi come from, according to the Northern Song Song Shi Huihong's “Leng Zhai Night Tales” section 7, “Dreaming of the Five Ancestors,” Su Zhe (written from) was relegated to the Gaoan Salt and Liquor Tax. There were Yunan monks residing in Dongshan, and Conzen Shi lived in Shusheng Temple. Overnight, the three had the same dream of welcoming the Five Ancestors. “It's been a long time since Dongpo Shu arrived and said: 'It's been a while, so we can meet on the evening. '” At the time, Su Shi was transferred from Huangzhou to serve as a deputy assistant for the Ruzhou team training. Despite the long journey, he turned to Gao An to visit his younger brother Su Zhe. After receiving Su Shi at Kenzan-ji Temple, 20 miles outside the castle, “then they all interpreted their dreams in words. Po said, “When Shi was 89 years old, I had a dream of being a monk, traveling back and forth between Shaanxi and the right. Also, when I first became pregnant, I had a dream about a monk coming to the boarding house, and I remember it in a blink of an eye. ' Yunan was shocked and said, “Ge, a native of Shaanxi, and lost sight of it. In the twilight years, I abandoned my five ancestors to visit Takayasu, and finally I was very foolish. ' The inverse was built 50 years, while Dongpo was 49 years old.” Looking at many aspects, this record shows that Su Shi's predecessor was the Five Ancestors of Zen Buddhism.
The so-called Five Ancestors, whose real name is Shikai Zen Master, are named after having lived in the Wuzu Temple in Huangmei, Qizhou for a long time, and are sometimes abbreviated as Master Zen Master or Master of the Yunmen sect of the Northern Song dynasty. Shi Huihong's determination that he was the predecessor of Su Shi later became a subject and was repeatedly transcribed in jungle quotes, writer's notes, and poems. In particular, in the novel “Yu Shiming Yan: Master Ming Wu Chasing the Five Commandments”, Feng Menglong from the Ming Dynasty recounted the story of the Five Zuji Zen Master who was reincarnated as Su Shi after breaking the color ring. The audience was wide and well-known, further expanding the spread of the idea that Su Shi was a monk's predecessor.
Su Shi's literary genius is also credited to the fact that he was previously a monk. Shi Huihong said in “Shimen Alphabet Zen” volume 27, “Dongpo Gai, the successor of the Five Ancestors, Zen Master, was so well-understood, so his writing was as loose as the quality of water. It spread widely, then its waves were naturally written. Gai is a non-linguistic script; they all make sense. It didn't come from Prajna; why did it come from it. It is written by Meng Ke, Zuo Qiuming, and Taishigong; there is only one person.” According to Shi Huihong, the reason why Su Shi's articles are outstanding is because her former name was a high monk, a master of Zen science, and has good roots.
Su Shi is also happy to accept the statement that his predecessor was a monk of the Five Ancestors. In his letter to the monk Unan, he said, “It's ridiculous that Ke Monk is ignorant and has made a strong comeback. It is a great blessing to have a contract with the law, but it can be painfully honed to return to the old rules.” Moreover, they are even wearing Taoist uniforms. There is a record of “Su Shi's Daoist Daoist Clothes” section in volume 7: “Zhe Zong asked you, Chen Yan: 'What clothes did Su Shi wear with the Emperor Zhang? ' Yan said, 'It's a suit. '” He also once said, “But when I went back to the jade situation, I suspected that I was the Five Immortals.” Also, in his poems, Su Shi repeatedly mentioned that his former name was a monk. In the poem “Nanhua Temple,” he said, “I am an ascetic and have been refined for three generations. If you lose your mind in the middle, you will be punished for 100 years.” This is not only a recognition of what he said as a monk, but also an introduction to Feng Menglong's novel “Master Ming Wu Chases the Five Precepts”. In the poem “Three Extraordinary Words with Zhang Zi Ye: A Journey Through the Past,” he said, “I have been to Hangzhou in my past years, and everywhere I go, it's like going on an old trip.” However, the article “Answer to Chen Shizhong's Master Book” is like a detailed explanation of this poem: “Shi also spent four or five years dreaming of the West Lake at the age of one. Most of these are secular so-called pioneers. When I tried visiting Shouxingyuan in Hangzhou, I realized that I had arrived once upon a time. I can say that the back hall of the hospital is a stone place, so I heard the phrase 'past lives have arrived. '” Su Shi's predecessor was recognized by others as the Master of the Five Ancestors and by himself as a high monk of Hangzhou Shuxingyuan, so how can the two be unified? Very simply, by combining the two independent elements of the Five Zuji Temple and Hangzhou Shuxingyuan, the Zen Master has become a Hangzhou high priest in people's minds.
In addition to being a monk, Su Shi also believes that his predecessor was Tao Yuanming. In the phrase “Jiang Chengzi,” Su Shi said, “I woke up drunk in my dream. Only Fuchi Akira is a former born. I've traveled all over the world, but I'm still struggling.” After the “Wutai Poetry Case,” Su Shi was relegated to Huangzhou, “dedicated his work in Dongpo and built a snowy place to live.” This kind of life experience is very similar to Tao Yuanming, who cultivated mu for a long time. According to incomplete statistics, Su Shi wrote 124 poems with Tao in his lifetime, and in “The Return with Tao,” he said, “Shi Yuan Ming's elegant performance, and 100 new poems. I have no doubt that I will be blessed with the return of a clear guide.” In terms of the value dimension of “returning home,” Su Shi and Tao Yuanming are highly consistent, so he claims that he is Tao Yuanming's successor. Su Shi and Zeng Fuzi said to himself, “I am from Yuanming; why don't I like his poetry alone? As if it were a human being, I was really impressed.” One of the words “feeling” reflects the sympathy between Su Shi and Tao Yuanming. It shows that he is in spiritual connection with Tao Yuanming, and also means that he has a sincere admiration for Tao Yuanming.
Now that you know where Su Shi is from, where is he going? There are also different opinions about Su Shi's successor. One explanation can be called Buddhism, and the other is an explanation of poetry.
According to Buddhist interpretation, Su Shi's successor was Yuan Hongdao from the Ming Dynasty. The “three yuan” of public security in the late Ming dynasty: the three brothers Yuan Zongdao (Zi Boxiu), Yuan Hongdao (Zi Zhonglang), and Yuan Zhongdao (Zi Xiaoxiu) all had literary names, while Yuan Hongdao was famous, and he was promoted as the leader of the public security faction. In the title of “Seeing the Snow and Photographing the End of the Volume”, he said, “Xiao Xiu met an old monk in his dream. It is said that Yu is the heir of Gong Po.” Kao Zhi Yuan Zhong Dao's “Book of Shuxue Photographs”: “At the beginning of the fall, Jia Chen visited the Heye Mountain House. Soon, Zhong Lang and Xueshao, Leng Yuner, Zen Master, and Yun Xin Ju arrived. It's already, and so has the cold.” “It was night. The moon was picturesque, and Duke Tan Feng was in full swing. Due to a false sleep, I went to a place where I saw an old monk Pang Mei and said, “Does the Lord want to know about the fate of the world? Zhong Lang's predecessor was Son Gong Zi Zhan; Gong is Zi Yuya. '” It can be seen that the determination that Su Shi's successor was Yuan Hongdao, just like the recognition that his predecessor was a monk of the Five Ancestors, all came from dreams. So what is Yuan Hongdao's attitude towards his younger brother's assessment? In the article “Seeing the Snow and Looking at the End of the Volume”, he said, “Ming Jiao said, 'Then the old monk called Gong the heir of Po; what is the cloud? ' Remaining: 'Yes. I have heard that people who are extremely rich and extravagant in the Bible are now born poor. Prince Shinpo is extremely extravagant; now it's okay to report it as being blunt and honest. '” Although there is a self-humble attitude in the article, Yuan Hongdao is clearly happy to accept the claim that Dongpo is the heir.
The story that Yuan Hongdao was the heir of Dongpo is also seen in the records of other writers. Yuan Hongdao once wrote “The Chronicles of Public Security,” and Lei Sipei said in “Gongan County Chronicles”, “Rumor has it that Lang is looking after his son, hehe! Zizan doesn't dare to write the history of the Three Kingdoms, but if Zhong Lang is the history of one country, why are elves from another world even more beneficial?” The term “rumor” not only indicates that the claim that Yuan Hongdao is the successor of Su Shi cannot be confirmed, but it also suggests that this statement was already widely spread at the time. Perhaps history is really caused by layers. The longer the legends have accumulated, the more convinced people have become. During the Qing Dynasty, Sun Xifan said in “Gongan County History: The Biography of Yuan Hongdao”, “There is no doubt that Zhong Lang is watching his children,” but the tone is certain; there is no doubt about it.
As a great scribe, it is certainly a great honor for Su Shi to be able to attach his predecessor to him. Today's scholar Gong Pengcheng said in one of the seven songs “Spring Trip to the West Lake”, “Clear mountains, clear water, blue bones, and peach blossoms on the willow bank. My predecessor was Master Su, and now I'm here to meet Manhu Yun.” In Gong Pengcheng's eyes, he is Su Shi's successor. Zhao Yoshi of Southern Song said in volume 4 of “The Record of Departure”: “Kaixi Bing Yin, Meizhou Revised Picture Book,” the last volume, “Jiang Xiang Zhi,” and the final volume, “Miscellaneous Gate”: Every time you live in a famous mountain, when you meet Su Wen's loyalty day in July, you must collect his apprenticeship and recommend his apprenticeship. This record is clearly untrue, but it is enough to show his extreme respect for Su Shi.
