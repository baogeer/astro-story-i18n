 The Qiang people, known as Erma, are one of the oldest ethnic groups in the Chinese nation and descendants of the virtuous Yandi Shennong clan. In ancient times, the Yandi tribe was sealed in Jiangshui, and the Huangdi tribe was sealed in Jishui. The two brother tribes worked together to defeat the Luyou tribe. Later, due to Neijiang, Yandi and Huangdi fighting with each other, it ended with the victory of the Huangdi tribe. After the defeat of the Yandi tribe, the Qiang people fell into slavery, and the Huangdi tribe used Qiang women as birth spouses to reproduce offspring. Famous historical figures such as Dayu, Xia Yi, Jiang Taigong, Jiang Wei, and Yuan Hao are all Qiang people.
Luerping in Shinyu Mountain, Mianyu Town, Wenchuan County, is the place where King Dayu was born. The mountain where Gaodian Village intersects Yangdian Village, 5 kilometers south of today's town, is Mount Shiniu. The mountains are cliffs, and the three characters engraved on the stone Mount Niushan still exist. Facing Guanliang, the flat ground in the middle of the mountainside at 1,600 meters above sea level is Luerping. Dayu led his people to establish the earliest Xia dynasty in our country. At the time of the Five Hu and Sixteen Kingdoms, Li Yuanhao, a descendant of the Qiang people, restored power in Western Xia. After the fall of the Xia dynasty, the Qiang people declined. During the Spring and Autumn Warring States, a group of Qiang people migrated from Gansu and Qinghai regions to the upper reaches of the Minjiang River, where they merged and flourished with local residents. Since Qin and Han, the Ran tribe of the ancient Qiang people lived in northwest Sichuan. During the Tang dynasty, part of the Qiang people assimilated to the Tibetans, part assimilated to the Han people, and some continued to survive and develop.
Today's Qiang people are mainly distributed in places such as Wenchuan, Maoxian, Lixian, Heishui, Songpan, and Beichuan County in Mianyang City in Aba Prefecture, Sichuan Province. Over a long period of history, the Qiang people have created a splendid history and culture, which is particularly worthy of being passed on and developed.
