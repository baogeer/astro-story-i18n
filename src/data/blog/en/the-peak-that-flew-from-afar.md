 Legend has it that on Mount Emei in Sichuan, there used to be a small mountain that could fly. It flew east for a while, and flew west for a while; wherever it flew, it crushed many houses there and crushed many people.
At that time, there was a monk at West Lake Lingyin Temple. People called him a crazy monk because he was insane all day long and didn't abide by the Buddha's rules. One day, the mad monk learned that at noon, the strange flying mountain would fly down to the village in front of Lingyin Temple. He feared that the mountain would crush many people. He even climbed up, rushed into the village, and went door to door and told, “There is a mountain that is about to fly up to this village at noon today. Everyone move quickly; it's too late!”
The old man shook his head when he heard it: “Crazy monk, you're looking for happiness again. Mountains are heaviest things. Who has ever seen a mountain that can fly!”
When the family heard it, they sighed: “Where are our poor tenants moving! If a mountain actually falls, I'll have no choice but to blame my life!”
The young man listened and hummed: “Don't make up lies, it's scary! When the mountain comes down, we put our shoulders on it; we're not afraid!”
The little kids followed him in a hug and clapped their hands to watch the fun.
When the Mad Monk's family went in and out, the whole village's 110 families took care of it. He had broken lips and dry saliva, but no one believed him, let alone that the family was ready to move.
The sun is getting higher and higher, and at noon, the crazy monk is in a hurry. At this point, he suddenly heard the sound of “hitting, hitting” and quickly followed the sound. At first glance, great. It turned out that one family got married, people came and went, and it was very lively. When the mad monk scratched his scalp and thought about it, he pushed the crowd and rushed to the front of the hall. Regardless of whether it was 3721, he put the bride on her shoulder and snatched out of the village.
The red shawl on the bride's head hadn't been removed. Suddenly, she was confused and made to run away on her back. She didn't know what had happened; she was just terrified and wow. The crazy monk robbed the bride, it's worth it! People grab the door latch, grab the door latch, swing the hoes, lift the rake, and catch up with their lives. While chasing, shouting:
“Catch the crazy monk!”
“Stop him in front and don't let him run away!”
At this point, everything was shaken up. It doesn't matter if it's a relative or not, whether it's a friend or not, male, female, old, or young, the whole village chased them out. Only the owner of the village east didn't move. Instead, they stood in front of the door, watched the lively scene, and spoke cool words:
“Going out of the family to rob my new daughter-in-law is something really new, hehe!”
The crazy monk carried the bride on his back and ran forward one by one. He runs really fast! Everyone has been chasing him for more than ten miles, and they haven't caught up with him yet. Wait until the sun shines, and the crazy monk stands still and doesn't run away. He put the bride down on his back, took a seat on the ground, and shook the fan to cool off. People arrived in front of him and were about to grab and beat him, but suddenly it was dark, and they couldn't reach for five fingers, and the wind blew loudly. There was a sudden “boom”, and everyone was shocked. Everyone climbed up and took a look. The wind had stopped and the clouds had stopped. The sun had just shone above their heads, but they saw a mountain that had just fallen on their village. Only then did people understand that the crazy monk robbed the bride to save everyone's lives.
The village was crushed to the bottom of the mountain, and everyone was left homeless. Some people were in a hurry to pat their chest and burst into tears. The crazy monk said, “Why cry! What you don't know is that the rich owners of the village have been crushed to death at the bottom of the mountain. In the future, each of you will cultivate your own fields, and I'm afraid you won't be able to build a house!”
People were happy to be told that they wanted to go away with joy, and the crazy monk spoke again: “Don't walk, everyone listen to me. Since this mountain can fly from anywhere else, it will also fly away from here; it will fly away from here; it will fly away from here; it will kill many people's lives. If we cut 500 of its stone Luohan in the mountain, we can stay in the mountain town and not let it fly to other places to harm people. Are you optimistic?”
Everyone listened. Once they said it was okay, they got their hands on it right away. “Ding Ding Dang Dang Dang” was busy all night long, and the five hundred stone Luohan were completely excavated. The mountain and bottom were covered with stone niches and Buddha statues. It only cut out Lohan's body, but it was too late to cut out his eyebrows and eyes. The crazy monk said, “I have a way, let me come!” Instead of a hammer, he used a chisel; he just used his long fingernails to stroke Shi Luohan's face. After half a day of hard work, I was able to put all the 500 pieces of stone Luo Han in the eye.
Since then, this small mountain peak has never been able to fly anywhere else; it will stay in front of Lingyin Temple forever! Because it flew in from somewhere else, it's called “Feilai Peak.”
