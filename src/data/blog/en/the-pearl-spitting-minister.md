 There is a king in the North, and he has an amazing minister under him. As soon as he smiles, he spits out a string of sparkling pearls. As a result, not only are the kings of the northern country getting richer, but the lives of ordinary people are also getting easier, causing envy and envy in many countries. The king of the South, in particular, spent all day and night asking about the secrets of the prosperity of the North.
One day, King of the South summoned ministers to discuss an old topic that could never be discussed. The king said, “Let's talk about the population about the same as the northern country, and the size of the country. Why are they so rich and we are so poor?” A courtier who has just been to the North said, “King, I heard that there is a courtier in the North who can spit pearls in his mouth. This is the root cause of their wealth.” The king listened and was very reluctant. He hurriedly dispatched four envoys, brought all kinds of precious gifts, and personally wrote a letter. The letter said, “I greet the king of the North, and sent four envoys to express my wishes. Please lend me the Minister of your country to spit pearls for one year, and I will definitely return them when the time comes.”
The king of the North received the letter and gift, and agreed with a smile. At the same time, he said to the four envoys, “Please tell your king that the minister can lend it to your country. As soon as he smiled, he was able to spit out a string of pearls. However, whether he will laugh depends on you; because of this minister, he can't laugh easily”.
Soon, four envoys accompanied the minister who spit out pearls and returned to the court of the South with various jewels returned by the King of the North. The king of the South China was elated. He personally sent someone to build a gorgeous palace, built a comfortable garden, selected beautiful women from all over the country to sing and dance for him, and specially found a few clowns to make him laugh in various ways.
However, time passed day by day, January and January, and the king of the South China had exhausted all methods. The minister still had a dark, cloudy sky with no smile at all. Of course, he didn't spit out half a pearl.
When the year was about to run out, the king of the South China had no choice but to endure the pain and wrote this letter to the North; “King, I am very grateful to you for taking care of me and sending the minister who spit pearls to stay in our country for a year. Since I don't have the fortune to get rich, the minister has never laughed once here. Now that the specified time has come, I don't want to break my original promise and have decided to let this minister return as scheduled. For the sake of a peaceful trip, please send four envoys to our country to welcome him.”
An envoy from the northern country arrived as scheduled, and the minister who spat out pearls is about to leave. Despite his disappointment, the Nankoku King held a grand feast. While the king was toasting, he accidentally touched the queen's finger. Unexpectedly, the princess immediately changed her face. Not only did she spill the wine in her hand on the king's face, but she also scolded the guests and ministers with foul language. The king still had a smirk on his face. He kept apologizing to the princess and finally slapped himself three times in public...
When the minister spitting pearls saw this kind of scene, he suddenly wanted to laugh. At the beginning, he laughed softly, then laughed loudly. As the laughter followed, Pearl Dingding went all over the place, and the servants couldn't win. The king, whose face was being made sad by his princess, saw so many pearls that he immediately turned to joy, and couldn't stand still laughing. My mouth kept shouting, “Laugh! Laugh! Laugh hard!”
Returning to the residence, an envoy from the northern country asked Minister Pearl, “You haven't laughed for a year since you came to the South. Why were you laughing so happy just now?” The minister said, “The concubine's attitude towards the king just now reminds me of something I saw with my own eyes half a month ago.”
“What's the matter?” The envoys asked in unison.
“That night, I was very sad. I was walking alone in the palace garden. Suddenly, I saw this princess, clad in thin clothes, climbing out of the king's bedroom window, just like a thief. I was skeptical at the time and quietly followed her. The princess turned a corner, ran into a dirty and smelly horse ring, and fell on her knees before the horse died. When Ma Yi saw the princess, not only did she not have any surviving words; instead, she picked up a wooden stick and slapped her on the head and body. While hitting, she scolded: 'Mai! Cheap goods! Why did you get to Cheng now? ' Instead of resisting, the princess held Ma Yi's feet and kept crying for forgiveness:
'The king just got drunk, and I couldn't get out. I'm a little late, beat me up! Hit me so hard! ' The princess was so powerful to the king, and so pathetic in front of Ma Yu, I couldn't help but laugh.” When the minister spitting out pearls said this, the envoys not only didn't laugh, but instead sighed and said, “Alas! There really are all kinds of strange birds in the forest; in the palace, there are all kinds of strange things!”
