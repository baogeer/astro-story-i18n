 There was Beilong Mountain, also known as Peony Mountain, near ancient Luoyang. Peony flowers grew all over the mountain. Every fall and winter, local farmers harvest peonies and cut them for firewood. There is a young man who loves peonies. Every time the flowers bloom, choose the best varieties, move them to your own home to carefully cultivate them, and build hedges to protect places with many peonies in the mountains. Under his careful protection, the peonies there grew as good as they grew, and their fame grew. On the night of the Mid-Autumn Festival, a beautiful girl came to the young man's house to give him a quilt embroidered with peony flowers and a handkerchief. She said she would like to express her gratitude. The young man asked her where she came from, but the girl disappeared. There's a poem on the handkerchief:
My name is Luoyang Flower. Lushanling is my home.
Thank you for your kindness and kindness. I will see something wonderful next spring.
Sure enough, in the second spring, many gorgeous peonies of different colors and shapes bloomed all over Manshan. Since then, no one has gone to Yushan to gather firewood. This story is still circulating among Luoyang people until now, and it is also being spread all over the world. My seniors answered why peonies came out of Luoyang and talked about this story.
