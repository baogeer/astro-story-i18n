 In Peony Township, Caozhou, there is a saying, “Old plum blossoms, few peonies.” This means that the older the plum blossoms, the more abundant and bright they bloom; peonies, on the other hand, bloom only when they are young (meaning that they have been planted for five or six years) when they are most gracious and luxurious. However, people say that in the early years of the Republic of China, on the south side of Zhaolou Village in Caozhou, there was a peony tree that had grown for over 150 years, called Lipid Red. This peony tree is two tall and the branches are eight tall. The trunk is as thick as a bowl, and its flowers are red like rouge. It is known as the “King of Peonies.”
The peony king flowers bloom for hundreds of flowers, red glow, and the fragrance is amazing. In summer, the elderly sit under the tree to cool off, and the children climb the tree to play. This incident spread as a strange story in Shilihuaxiang. It's been a long time; indeed, no one knows about the local public officials, and no one knows about it. During the rainy season, Peony King is in full bloom, and even people from outside the bridge come to see it. At the time, Shi Lulangzhai, the head of the town of Caozhou, had healed “King Peony” for a long time. It was said that this person lived up to his name and was unskilled, and was also bossy. The local people hated him. He repeatedly proposed to buy “Peony King.” The flower farmers insisted on not accepting it and had to stop doing it.
Yuan Shikai, the thief of the country, is preparing to become emperor in Beijing. As soon as Lu Langzhai saw that the time had come. In order to please Yuan Shikai, Qing went straight ahead and wanted to use this “King Peony” as a tribute. This spring, peonies bloomed again. Lu Langzhai led a group of people to the Zhaolou Peony Garden with brilliance. I saw Wang's colorful sea of flowers, and the “Peony King” stood tall like a group of chickens. The red light was dazzling, and the rich scent of flowers was very refreshing in my heart. Lu Langzhai thought in his heart: A good peony king. If I can get my hands on it and go south, I don't know if I can get it to the south, I don't know if I can get rich and prosperous? Thinking of this, the mission man robbed “King Peony.”
After hearing the news, the flower farmers painstakingly pleaded: “No! This “Peony King” is a treasure handed down from generation to generation in my flower town! I can't dig it up!” Where is Lu Lang willing? Amidst protests and insults from flower farmers, Lu Langzhai forcibly removed the “Peony King.”
Lu Langzhai got the “Peony King,” and was so proud that he sent a shuttle bus to personally escort him to Beijing, where he met Yuan Shikai. Yuan Shikai was overjoyed when he saw him. Lu Langzhai's official position was upgraded to three levels.
Later, Yuan Shikai also ordered Lu Langzhai to guard and plant the “Peony King” difference in Henan Province (present-day Anyang City) in Yuan Shikai's mansion. It didn't take long for “King Peony” to die at Yuan's mansion. Yuan Shikai, who had been the emperor for 83 days, was also ousted by the people of the whole country. Flower farmers in Taiwan's Peony Township learned the news that “King Peony” had died and were heartbroken. Someone asked for a song:
