 In Wanhua Village in Mudan Township, Caozhou, there is a Zhao Laohan. Zhao Laohan's ancestors grew flowers for generations and learned how to cultivate flowers. Peony flowers, in particular, have not caught up with his family's many varieties, and none have caught up with him. The old man saw the age of the flower beetle. His old partner died prematurely. Right in front of him, there was only one little daughter, named Jui Yu. I just turned 18. I was born to be handsome and pious, well-versed in the world, smart, and filial pious to my dad. Father and daughter love flowers and are dependent on each other. With a few acres of gardens, they can barely survive.
One spring, the old man went out to sell flowers and saw a famous traditional painting at a large house called Phoenix Opera Peony. Some local artists gathered in front to comment. Some say, “This painting has a novel layout and a realistic shape.” Others say, “This brush is skilled and has excellent colors. It must be Danqing's masterwork”; others say, “This painting is very careful. The phoenix is the king of all flowers, and the peony is the king of flowers. Combining the supremacy of flowers and birds, it has become a symbol of wealth, prominence, and outstanding beauty on earth.” The old man secretly thought for himself. Naturally, the peonies he cultivated with hard work and sweat were praised by people. Naturally, he was infinitely relieved.
In the spring of the following year, the old man went out to sell flowers again. In a county town, he saw a very exquisite and very fragrant, oh, those majestic stone monuments spanning the street, several feet tall, imposing, and quaint in style. When he took a closer look, he was even more surprised. It turned out to be an extremely finely carved monument. Each part of the monument is engraved with large and small lions and peony flowers in various positions. Not only is the shape realistic and the technique is detailed, but since the level of carving is unexpected, it naturally reminds people of the technique of carving more than ten layers of ivory balls. Therefore. This monument no longer looks like a bluestone relief, but rather a three-dimensional combination of many stone lions and stone peonies. The old man saw a stone carved peony flower. Each leaf was so vivid. Although it had no color or fragrance, it made bees hover and fly. As a result, the old man grew even more affectionate with peonies, and felt that being able to cultivate famous flowers for the world was his greatest happiness.
In the fall of one year, a person from Wanhua Village who had just returned from Guangzhou found Zhao Laohan. They said that Guangzhou hosts a grand flower market every year during the Spring Festival. All kinds of famous flowers from around the world are available, but unfortunately we aren't there
Peony flowers in Caozhou. If we can make our peonies bloom there on time, not only will our income be impressive, but our Caozhou peonies will also be famous at home.
Neighbors in the village heard this news and naturally wanted to go out with peonies. But when you think about the long way, peonies from the north are planted in the south, can the soil and water adapt to the climate? The normal flowering period for peonies is in March of Yangchun. Can you make them bloom early until the Spring Festival? Legend has it that Empress Wu Zetian of the Tang dynasty ordered flowers to bloom in full bloom during the harsh winter months. Peonies were burned by fire because they disobeyed the sacraments, and then devolved to Luoyang. Now, can we make things that even the emperor couldn't do come true? The townspeople wanted to come and go. I still think of Zhao Laohan. Everyone is here to persuade them that you have the most experience in cultivating peonies. For the sake of the reputation of our peonies in Caozhou, and to open up a path of wealth for the townspeople in Peony Township, you are advising the elderly to break through first.
Zhao Laohan has only one daughter around him. If he goes to Guangzhou alone and leaves her daughter at home, he won't be relieved. Take her with you. In the years when the army was in turmoil, girls were out of the house, which was very inconvenient. At a time of distress, the daughter stepped forward and said, “Dad is already old. When he goes out alone, the daughter doesn't feel at ease. The child wants the girl to dress up as a man and go with her father.”
Zhao Laohan's father and daughter packed 300 peonies and left the townspeople. Since then, Jade has been dressed up as a young man and changed her name to Satsuki. An old and a young man trekked through the mountains and water, and went through all the hardships. They came to Guangzhou. They found a garden in the suburbs and stayed there. Zhao Laohan agreed with the owner of the garden to borrow the site in his garden and wait for the peonies to be successfully cultivated. After selling them at the flower market, the two parties divided them into four or six*
From then on, the father and daughter of the Zhao family began to work hard. When he was in his hometown, he used a heated shed to grow a few peonies during the Spring Festival. Now in the southern country, how to control the temperature so that the peonies can bloom sooner or later, at the Spring Festival, how can they succeed. How can the father and daughter take turns caring for the baby day and night, and take care of them day and night. The dryness and humidity of the soil, and the temperature are all strictly regulated according to the rules of peony growth. The father and daughter endured almost 100 days and nights. 300 peonies sprout, draw branches, and grow leaves, but the steamed buns have blossomed. The hard work of a father and daughter will soon blossom into the flower of hope in the southern country. Not to mention how happy they are.
Unsurprisingly, the owner of this garden is a ruthless and toxic flower bully. In order to get rich, I don't know how many acts that hurt nature and harm him. He saw this old and young man thousands of miles away from Caozhou, Shandong. There are no relatives, no reason. Although people are honest, they have a set of tricks for growing flowers. Seeing that the peonies I have cultivated will be on the market during the Spring Festival is certain. Seeing that a sum of money is about to be obtained, I had a crazy idea in my heart. Over the past few days, Huaba has had to go to the peony bushes nurtured by the Zhao family's father and daughter, and is particularly interested in this young student, Satsuki. He always has to talk to Xiaoyue for a few words. It seems that he has already discovered some mystery in Xiaoyue. Smart Satsuki always cleverly evades the entanglement of flower bullies.
In a blink of an eye, it was already the middle of the month of July. All 300 peonies had grown strong and leafy. The flower bones had grown into large kernels. On this day, Satsuki was busy among the peonies. Huabao walked over with a smile and said, “No wonder your Caozhou peonies are so famous. Seeing your thin skin and tender flesh, and a smile on your face is really more beautiful than peony flowers.”
When Satsuki saw that he was rude, she simply told him, “You are the owner of the garden. You specialize in growing flowers. But don't lose the virtue of being a flower grower.
Huabao didn't think it was fun to suffer; instead, she shamelessly gathered up and said, “Everyone loves beauty. You have such a beautiful face, but it's a pity that you were a man in vain. If you stay in this southern country with four seasons of spring all year round, stay with me to protect your endless glory and wealth.” While talking, he punched them up and spread rudeness. In a panic, Satsuki slapped him in anger and ran to his dad's hut crying and crying.
When Zhao Laohan heard his daughter's complaint, he was furious and immediately sought a defense from Huabang. On second thought, the father and daughter are far from home and have no family and no reason. If they talk out, they will reveal their daughter's identity even earlier. Let's not argue; this tone is really unbearable. The old man really wanted to destroy all the 300 peonies that were ready to be released, and then return to his hometown with his daughter. But how could he bear to ruin months of hard work. Seeing that the Caozhou peonies are going to shine at the Guangzhou Flower Market. This is a statement about peonies; how can he bear to take action?
After a few days of angry torment, combined with a sudden wind chill, Zhao Laohan became ill. When the daughter saw that her father was in serious condition, she became more and more nervous and was unable to do anything. But he didn't dare to cry and cry in front of his dad, causing him to feel sad. No matter what, it's still important to find a way to treat your father's illness. When they found a local flower to save money, the flower bully intended to harass them and pretended; “Originally, we both had something to say. As soon as the Spring Festival peonies bloomed, we divided them into four or six parts. One penny is quite a bit. Right now, peonies are expected to bloom, but after all, they haven't bloomed. Peonies aren't blooming, I don't have any money, and I can't help them.”
Satsuki angrily said, “My family has grown flowers for generations. Seeing flowers is more important than life. If peonies don't bloom on time, we would never dare go a few miles away to become ugly.”
Huabang playfully said, “In front of me, don't pretend to be chess, if you rely on me for one thing... · I'll protect you...” Huabao approached Satsuki and wanted to pinch her face. Satsuki hurriedly dodged and shouted; “If you see death, you can't help it; it's still so barbaric and rude!”
Little Platform ran back to her dad's hut in tears. Despite all the grievances, she silently hid it in her heart for fear that Dad's condition would worsen. She only used words of relief to persuade the elderly family.
Zhao Zehan observed the child Shen Qian and anticipated that the flower bully was devising tricks and tricks. If I had a bad time and left my daughter alone and had no relatives, wouldn't that hurt my child? He had no choice but to beat up his spirit and persuade the child; “Dad's old arms and legs are old; a small illness or a minor disaster is nothing. After another three or five days, Peony passed the final level, and it was all open. At that time, we watched the peony flowers in Caozhou receive praise from people at the Guangzhou flower market. Both of us have been working in the South for months in vain.”
After the stove festival day, the Zhuangdan flowers bloomed one after another. As Zhao Laohan's condition worsened, he saw that his life was difficult to save, but he was secretly happy and thought to himself, this old man went thousands of miles away and gave up his life in vain. He also grew so many rare peonies. It's not enough to make a fortune, and this tomboy, who looks like a jade flower, has also become a prey in my hands.”
Before his death, Zhao Laohan said to his daughter who cried to tears, “The most precious thing about peonies is to give up their lives. No matter how difficult it is for us to grow flowers, we won't bow down to evil forces. After I died, I would definitely want to take my bones back to my hometown and tell the people in my hometown how our peony flowers in Caozhou bloom in the southern country”.
Satsuki followed her father's will and finally survived until all the peonies were in full bloom. Caozhou peonies have become a treasure that people scramble to buy at the Guangzhou Flower Market.
When Huabao packed the money into his pocket and was about to reach out to this lonely flower grower, the jade under the pseudonym Satsuki had disappeared, and even Zhao Laohan's corpse buried in the garden flew away.
Soon after, the peonies bought from the flower market gradually withered, their faces and leaves withered, and soon they all died.
According to people's legend, the Peony Fairy showed her spirit and saved the Zhao family's father and daughter. The flower tycoon scared a serious illness and almost died.
Since then, flower farmers in Caozhou have been cultivating peonies in Guangzhou year after year. Although they bloom year after year, they die one after another. Until now, flower farmers in Caozhou have to go to Guangzhou, Hong Kong and Macau every fall to cultivate peonies, but after the flowers bloom, they die, and the next year they have to be transported from Caozhou to be cultivated again.
