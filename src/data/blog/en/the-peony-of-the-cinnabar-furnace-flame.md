 In Peony Township, Caozhou, there is a famous type of peony called “Danlu Flame”. Its flowers sparkle red and have a dazzling color, like a blazing fire. Speaking of the origin of the name, there is another story.
Legend has it that there is a flower farmer named Wang Laohan in Mudan Township; his ancestors grew flowers for several generations. When Wang Laohan arrived, he not only built a peony garden, but he also had all kinds of famous products. Every year after the valley rain, peonies bloom. In front of Wang Laohan's Garden, there is always a crowd of people watching the flowers.
One day, the Governor of Caozhou took a car to enjoy the flowers. Seeing that the Wangjia Garden was full of flowers, shining brightly, and was full of praise. Under the prefectural government, there was a bad boy nicknamed “Long Tongue.” Seeing that the owner liked the peonies here so much, Xian Mei said, “What would you think if the peonies from here were planted in the garden at the back of the government?”
The governor nodded; “Hmm; OK. Still, you thought it through. Then go ahead and do this,”
With a long tongue, he nodded and said, “Yes, yes. I will definitely do my best.”
With a long tongue, he personally led a gang of thugs into the garden. Without saying a word, they shaved their hands.
Wang Laohan arrived when he heard the news and wanted to stop him, but he was determined to be old, and he couldn't stop the gang of thugs with three punches and two feet. He was already lying in the garden and couldn't move. Wang Laohan was furious, “Strong melons aren't sweet. If I wanted to grow Shedan this season, I wouldn't be able to survive.”
Long tongues finally led people to forcibly cut hundreds of peonies, offered them to their owners, and planted them in the back garden of the government office;
However, in the spring of the following year, not only did the peonies that were planted not bloom, but all the trees withered. The governor blamed Long Tongue for not being able to do his job well. Remembering what Wang Laohan had said, he couldn't help but get annoyed and angry, so he came to Juwang Laohan to settle the accounts.
He had a long tongue and waited for his subordinates to call Wang Zehan, then split his head and asked, “Why aren't your peonies blooming! huh?.
Wang Laohan smiled and said, “Peonies are spring flowers. When the spring breeze blows, the flowers are even more beautiful. It's as cold as winter in Yamen. The spring breeze can't blow in, and there's no spring. How can peonies bloom? What's more, they stole it again!”
The long tongue was prickly when I heard Wang Laohan's words, and it was even more furious: “Come on, burn all his peonies!” The people he brought swarmed up, piled up dried firewood, and burned down the royal garden. Wang Laohan saw the peonies that his generations had painstakingly nurtured and instantly turned to ashes. He was very sad. A long tongue takes people far and away.
The more Wang Laohan thought about it, the more sad he became, losing his voice and becoming painful.
A miracle happened. Wang Laohan's tears instantly turned into torrential rain falling from the sky to put out the fire. Who knows, that peony, which had been burnt to ashes, came back to life. Soon, the garden was full of spring colors. The blooming peony flowers were just like the red flames in Lao Jun's alchemy furnace. People have given this type of peony the name “Danlu Flame.”
