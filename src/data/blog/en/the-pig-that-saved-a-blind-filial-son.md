 Life, good fortune, and big damage means that the same bad luck has befallen, and a disaster has arrived. Most people saw their ancestors or fell flat ever since. But there are just a few people who will stand out from it and escape death. And often this kind of person will have extraordinary achievements in the future. So what kind of life is a blessing? There are only a few types: kind people, people with filial piety, people with a heart for the world, etc. “Reading Weiwei Thatched Cottage Notes” records the story of a blind filial son who was saved by chance by a pig and never died.
Wei Jiazhuang has a blind man with the surname Wei. His father died early, and he is dependent on his 80-year-old mother. On New Year's Eve in the year of Wu Wu, he walked the streets to sing for others. Every family gave him some New Year's supplies to get through the New Year. After singing all over the house, the blind man went home carrying food.
It was getting late, and the blind man lost his footing and fell into a dry well. The dry well was far from the main road. There were no people on the way to New Year's Eve. The blind man was hoarse, and no one answered. Fortunately, the bottom of the well is quite warm, and there is food you can carry with you to eat. Blind people took a bite of fruit when they were thirsty, but they persisted for a few days without dying. But the only thing he couldn't relax about was his old mother.
On the fifth day of the fifth day, a butcher named Wang Yisheng used a rope to hold the pig and drive the pig home. About half a mile from the dry well, the pig suddenly broke the rope and ran all the way in the direction of the dry well. Came to the side of the well and dove headlong into the well. The butcher caught up and planned to hook the pig up with a hook. When he went down the well to hook up the hook, he found a blind man dying in the well. As a result, blind people were saved.
After being rescued, the blind man said, “All my thoughts were empty at the time. My heart was like death. I just thought about my old mother, and I couldn't rest assured. The old mother is sick in bed and is being fed only by her blind son. Now she doesn't even see her blind son; I'm afraid he will starve to death within a few days. Thinking about this place is heartbreaking and painful.”
The dry well was not on the butcher's path. There seemed to be some coincidence. The pig seemed to have deliberately run over to save the blind, and deliberately made the butcher discover the blind. Could it be that the blind man had filial piety and moved heaven and earth. Did the gods help guide the pig to save him? Of course, only pigs know about this.
