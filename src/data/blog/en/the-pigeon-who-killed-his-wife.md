 If you don't carefully separate the good from the bad,
As soon as they get angry, they hurt the other person;
Try watching a pigeon kill his wife by mistake.
The partner's sorrow was in vain.
In a forest, a pair of pigeons live. In the fall of this year, male pigeons let their mother pigeons guard all the greenery they found in the summer, and flew elsewhere to find food themselves. When it returned to its forest looking for food, it saw that not to mention the fruit on the tree, even the leaves were yellow and sparse. He then blamed his wife: “Why have you eaten all the fruit?” The pigeon's wife explained, “I haven't even eaten one!” The male pigeon couldn't help but say that he beat his wife to death in a fit of anger.
After thawing the following year, the fruits that had fallen off the ground sprout and grew again, and the branches grew all over. After careful consideration, the male pigeon suddenly realized: “Oops! My wife really didn't steal a single piece of fruit!” My heart is full of remorse. A few young Tenjin in the forest laughed and said:
A dove lives in the woods,
Flowers have wings and red eyes;
There is no distinction between good and bad,
How sad it is to kill my wife.
