 A long, long time ago, there was a Yi cottage on the banks of the Yangjiao River in the Ailao Mountain region. There was a cropper named Ai Jia in the stockade. His family planted more than ten acres of slopes, but only one farm cow. Every year during spring farming and fall planting, Alga fights hard to quickly plow more than ten acres of cultivated land.
Spring ploughing this year, the slope was not finished, and the cows were so tired that they couldn't pull the plow. Aga was very anxious. Fearing that he would make a mistake, he dropped his whip and went to the ground to beat the cow, and scolded the cow while beating: “You playful cow, 360 days a year, you are selling some energy during the spring farming and fall planting these days. Doesn't my family spend the rest of the time taking care of you at leisure? After only a few days of suffering, you don't want to use force. Laozi will kill you, kill you!”
After a few hits, the poplar bar was interrupted, and Alga went to the side of the slope to cut down the beef tendon tree. Beef tendon bars are very tough. They are very powerful when used to fight livestock, and they won't break no matter how you hit them. As soon as Argan hit the cow with all its strength, it convulsed when it hit the cow. It tried desperately to take two steps forward, and then couldn't move again. Aga hit again, causing scaly wounds all over the body of the beating. They couldn't even see the birds in the trees on the ground. They screamed one after another.
The crow called, “Don't do it, don't do it!”
The magpie called, “You can't fight, you can't fight!”
The thrush shouted, “The cow is dying, the cow is dying!”
The woodpecker shouted, “So miserable, so miserable!”
Qingji shouted, “If you want to get into trouble, get into trouble!”
Alga didn't listen and continued to fight hard. Niu was struggling to take a few steps forward, and was unable to walk any further. Agia couldn't stop beating the cow. At the end, the cow struggled a bit. It was interrupted by a “knock” sound, and the cow was lying on the ground and couldn't get up again.
Alga went back to fighting cows and scolded while beating: “You pretend to die, you pretend to die! Get up and plow the ground, or Laozi will break your ribs!”
The wife who sowed the seeds in the back couldn't watch it either. Before running up, she advised her husband, “Agar doesn't want to fight; it's also fate; if you hit it again, you're dead.”
Agia said, “It's pretending. If you want to be lazy, the cow won't plow the ground for you if you don't fight.”
The wife said, “What are you saying? People have to sleep after a hard day, so let them rest and eat some grass. You see, the cables are all broken; I think it did its best.”
The wife took the beef tendon from her husband and then pulled out the plow again. It turned out that the plow tip of the plow was stuck on a stone. The wife scolded, “Ah Jia, you're really ruthless. Your plow is all stuck in stones. How can you pull the cow? You're still hitting it with anger.”
The cow was so sad that it was fatal. In the middle of the night, it escaped from the circle and went into the woods of Houshan with all his wounds. The more she thought about it, the saddened, and her eyes couldn't stop crying. It wanted to hide and be lazy for a few days, heal its wounds, find some nourishing grass, leaves, and eat in the mountains, wait until it has healed its wounds and gained strength, then go back and plow the land for its owner. But where to hide? The owner will soon find you nearby, and you will encounter wolves, tigers, and panthers in the deep mountains and old forests in the distance. What can you do about this?
As it walked, it looked up and saw that the Mountain Temple was right in front of it, and it had an idea in mind. Or ask the mountain god; maybe the mountain god will bless it. So it walked into the temple and knelt in front of the shrine in tears. When Yamagami saw a tearful cow kneeling in front of him and took a close look at the scars all over its body. It must have been beaten, and said to the cow, “Poor scalper, if you have any grievances or pains in your heart, just tell me.”
“Dear Lord Yamagami, you are the protector of all living creatures in the mountains. I'm really wrong! I do have a lot of pain to tell you. I have been plowing the land for my owner for decades. The owner has 18 acres of slope. I have to plow once every year for spring farming and once for fall planting; all of them are plowed by myself. Everyone has a double cow plow, but my owner couldn't bear to add a cow to plow with me. Every year, one of my cows pulls the plow for him, exhausting me to death. The owner couldn't stop whipping me. Yesterday, after plowing the ground until the sun set, the owner didn't finish his work. He pulled forward a few steps with a whip, then ploughed the dark stones and broke all the cables. I fell down because I was tired, but the owner also said I was lazy and beat me to death. Later, I was dissuaded by the kind hostess; otherwise, I was killed alive. Tomorrow, I'll have to go pull the plow, but I really can't pull it. I wanted to take a break from it for a few days, so I escaped in a circle. However, I can't hide anywhere; nearby owners will soon find me. Deep in the mountains and jungles in the distance, there are also wolves, tigers, and panthers. Lord Yamagami, I'm really out of my way, so I'm asking you to ask your merciful Grandpa Yamagami to save my suffering scalper.”
The mountain god heard the story of the scalper's sad experience, and his eyes were hot and sour, and he was happy with the scalper: “Don't worry, don't worry, I will definitely save you; just hide behind my house. There is a fat grassland there. You can eat fresh grass there to heal your wounds. That grass can heal your wounds and heal your body soon.”
It was only after the scalpers thanked them that they got up and hid behind the Mountain Temple.
At dawn, Adan went to chase cows and plow the field. He saw in front of the ring only to learn that he had run away while cultivating cows. He was in a hurry to look for cows everywhere. He went to the front and back of the village to search for it, to find it in the mountains, along the big and small rivers, and in the mountains. He searched everywhere, but he didn't see any signs of a scalper, so he was in a hurry to catch fire in his heart. Later, he found the area around the mountain temple and went to the back of the temple to look for it. Although the cow wagged its tail leisurely grazing on the grass behind the temple, he went back and forth dozens of times around the cow, but he couldn't see his scalper, so he had to hang his head down and go home feeling discouraged. As soon as I got home, I scolded: “Why can't you find the cow on its back? Tomorrow, Laozi will look for it again. To make fun of Laozi's fire, if you find it, you have to kill it and put it down in a soup pot!”
His wife advised, “Let's stop the fire, who told you to beat it to the death? Can it stand it? Let's ask Abi to take a look at the fire.”
Ah Gia listened to his wife and asked Abi to take a look at the fire. Abi told Abi, “You beat the cow so badly. The mountain god got angry, and the mountain god hid your farming cows. You must go to the Mountain Temple to offer three sacrifices, burn incense, and pray for the forgiveness of Grandpa Yamashin.”
Aga and his wife went to the Mountain Temple and offered three kinds of wine and tea to Grandpa Yamashin, burning incense and knocked their heads.
Yamagami pointed to Arga and asked, “Are you just Aga? Alga answered: “Little people are Arya.”
Yamagami reprimanded, “Ah, I know why you came to ask me. How can people treat livestock like you do? You work in the field with the cow; it pulls the plow and supports your plow. Although you have worked hard, it is much harder than you. Think about it. If you eat meat, eat alcohol, and cows eat grass, they honestly pull the plow so hard for you, but you beat it so hard. How can it stand it? You're so relentless!”
Agia nodded like garlic and prayed, “Please forgive Grandpa Yamagami! Please forgive Mr. Yamagami! I will definitely change and treat my cattle well in the future.”
Yamagami also said, “Remember, Agia. Cows help your family cultivate, plow, and plow. You two are labor partners, and you should be kind to cows as brothers and sisters. You are not allowed to beat cows and curse cows from now on, and you have to sing plow songs when plowing the ground. You are not allowed to raise a single cow to plow the ground; you must raise a pair of cows. Also, you can't lose sight of cultivating a cow when it comes to food; not only do you feed it grass, but you also have to feed it beans.”
Aga nodded and said, “I will definitely do what Grandpa Yamagami said. Xiaomin dared to ask the mountain god, how do you sing this cow song?”
Yamagami said, “Sing the song you plow the ground. After feeding the cows in the morning, they sang “Open Lap Song” when they open the cows, sing “Plow Song” on the way to the ground, “Set the Cow Song” when they reach the ground, and sing “Relief Song” when they finish the work and release the cows. Remember that?”
Arga still didn't know what words to sing in these four songs, but he didn't dare to ask any more. He even said, “Remember, remember.”
Since Alga got home, she was always worried about the lyrics of those four songs. But his wife, who is an expert singer, said to him, “What are you worried about?
Did you forget I'm one of the best singers in this area?”
Adami smiled and said, “OK, let's write the lyrics for you.”
That night, my wife composed the four songs of the plow song. She sang it to Alya a few times, which fits the meaning of Ejia. At the same time, she also memorized the lyrics of the four songs of the plow song.
After two days, the scalper came back happy. He was fat, fit, and in good spirits. The two of them couldn't help but be happy, and now they rewarded it with a bunch of soaked broad beans. Seeing that the owner's attitude towards it changed, the scalper was satisfied. While eating, it wagged its tail and “yelled”. The couple went to the neighboring village to buy a farming cow as a partner, and they no longer plow the land with a single cow.
Early the next morning, the two of them ate breakfast. Alga carried a bunch of soaked broad beans to the cow ring, poured broad beans into the cow's grater to eat them all, and laughed and said to the two farming cows, “I won't beat you again from now on, and I'll have to sing the plow song to you when plowing the ground.” After speaking, break the whip in front of the cow and throw it far and wide. The two farming cows saw it, wagged their tails in satisfaction, and called out “whimpered.”
After the two farmed cows had enough to eat, Alya opened up and sang the first song “Open Circle Song” of the plow song:
Ah, the hard-working cow,
Have you had enough for breakfast?
If both of you are full,
Please work hard for a day.
After listening to “Open Lap Song”, they were very impressed. They wag their tails and shouted “” to express their satisfaction. Ah Jia opened the door and set out carrying a plow and shouting. His wife followed behind him with a bag of bracken seeds. They walked down the red clay road in Qingpo. As Ah Jia walked, she sang:
Ago and Ah Cow went down to the ground together.
Let's work together to plow up the slope.
Sister Ah then sowed the seeds.
There is a lot of joy in the fall when grains are plentiful.
Ah, the hard-working cow,
Our good lives depend on strength.
This is “The Plow Song”. I heard the two farming cows wagging their tails and shouting “whistling”, moving forward cheerfully and cheerfully. On the ground, Ah Jia sang “Stand Up Song” while carrying a cow:
Ah, the hard-working cow,
You have to walk straight ahead when plowing the ground.
When you get to the bottom of the ground, you go back.
Rest your feet when you're tired.
If you plow stones and tree stumps, you stop.
Avoid straining your lower back due to tuberculosis.
After singing “The Stand Up Song,” the cow stand is ready. The two cows set out and walked forward. Aga steadily supported the plow and followed Niu Shun Shun as the area moved forward. His wife left a furrow in the cultivated field. When they plow the ground, the man who plows and the woman who sows can improvise. In addition to calling for the cow to step on the ditch, turn back, and stop, they can also sing 24 solar farming songs, sing about world events in heaven and earth, sing family history, and even sing about anything troubling them.
After plowing the ground and plowing until the sun sets, I finished the work. While slacking the cow, I sang “Unraveling the Song”:
Ah, the hard-working cow,
We plowed the soil for a day,
Ah Niu, you two have worked hard.
Even though Grandpa and Auntie have worked hard,
Ah Niu works harder than brother and sister.
Let's go home to the thatched hut.
After a few days, Alga's plow song went viral. The people in the stockade felt happy and interesting to sing the plow song and cultivate the land. At night, many people came to their families to learn to sing the plow song with their family. Later, farmers all over the village sang the plow song while plowing the land. The plow song spread to neighboring villages the following year, and plow the ground and sang the plow song. After a few years, all the cultivated land in Yizhai in the Ailao Mountain region sang the plow song. The plow song has been passed down from generation to generation. Until now, singing plow songs while cultivating land has become a custom in all Yi villages in Ailao Mountain.
