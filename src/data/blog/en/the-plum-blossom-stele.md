 In the early years, Hangzhou had old stonemasons with ingenious minds. This old stonemason has been chiseling stones all his life, carving stones all his life, and his beard hair is white. There was nothing else; they left behind only good craftsmanship. They are famous in the three provinces of Hangzhou, Jia, and Lake.
The old stonemason is old, has camels on his back, and his eyes are dazzled, but he still goes up the mountain every day. One day, the old stonemason found a white stone at the foot of Nanshan Mountain. The stone seemed to reflect the shadow of a tree. The old stonemason was doubtful about himself, rubbed his eyes and then took a closer look, but it wasn't! The shadow of a plum blossom is clearly reflected, as if it were growing on a stone. The old stonemason reached over the stone and touched it. It was flat and shiny. The stone was like a white enamel embroidered by a girl's daughter-in-law, so he didn't embroider the pattern yet. The more the old mason saw it, the more he loved it, and the more he looked, the more he couldn't bear to leave, so he used his whole strength to dig up the stone and back home one by one.
The old stonemason looked at the stone for three months, touched it for another three months, and thought about it for another three months before carving it on the stone. This stone is so hard! Once you cut it down, it only fell apart; if you knocked it down, only a few pieces of Mars came out. However, the old stonemason was not discouraged, and only carved one by one. Hammer and chisel, carve a petal on the 10th, carve a flower in 100 days. After January and January, and year after year, the old stonemason kept carving day and night, and finally carved that plum blossom into the stone.
The plum blossom is carved. Beautiful plum blossoms bloom like white jade in the face of the spring breeze and towards the morning glow. The old stonemason went to waste. The old stonemason died in Meihuabian.
The old stonemason had no children, no industry, and everyone respected him, so they buried him in a common plot of land and erected the plum blossom monument he had carved at the top of his tomb.
It's been a long time, and strange things have happened! The plum blossoms on the stone monument bloom. Every spring, the plum blossoms on the other trees only have buds, but the plum blossoms on the stone monument are already in full bloom; in summer, the plum trees on the stone monument are already in full bloom; in autumn, when not a single leaf is left on the other trees, the plum trees on the stone monument begin to fall; in winter, the northwest wind messes up the other plum trees and tilts. Only the plum tree on the stone monument stands still there.
This stone monument can also forecast the weather: when the sky is about to clear, the stone monument is bright and bright; when the sky is overcast, the stone monument is foggy, halogen and halogen; when it is about to rain, the stone monument is overcast and wet. People can tell from this stone monument that the weather is seasonal; the weather is clear and rainy. With this stone monument, the farmhouse can plow down the ground without making a mistake in the arrangement; those who go out should take a break and be sure to go. Everyone loves this stone monument and treats it as a baby.
One spring, a senior official came to Hangzhou. The chief official has heard of this marvelous stone monument a long time ago. Shortly after he arrived in Hangzhou, he brought a group of his subordinates to the old stonemason's graveyard. As soon as he saw, the plum blossoms carved on the stone monument were in full bloom. He was extremely happy. As soon as he went back and discussed it with Master Gutou, he built a government gate next to the old mason's graveyard, built a fence, surrounded the stone monument in the back garden, and put up a notice saying, “This is a public land. The common land is owned by the government, and the common people are not allowed to enter.
Strange to say, it's strange. This stone monument was surrounded by the official's garden. In less than two days, the plum blossoms on the monument gradually disappeared. After that, no matter whether it was sunny or rainy, the stone monument was always gloomy and wet. Slowly, the stone monument was covered with moss. Not only did it not have a hint of brilliance, but it also became extremely unsightly. Because of this, the chief official was so bored that he didn't want to eat, and was so sad that he couldn't sleep. He spent the whole day going back and forth around the stone monument. Master Gutou saw it and offered a plan: “Grandpa, I think this is because the ground is humid. If a fire starts at the foot of the stone monument to dry it, the drying humidity will be fine.” The official made sense when he was busy asking someone to move to dry firewood and charcoal. Burn it on top of the old mason's grave.
As soon as the flames licked the stone monument, it burst open, and the raging flames spewed far, far away; after a moment of effort, the government office and garden all burned down. The Grand Officer and Master were burnt to death inside, even if they wanted to escape, but were unable to escape. The fire burned for three days and three nights, burning the government gate to rubble, leaving only a half-burnt flagpole in front of the gate.
This is how this marvelous stone monument was destroyed! Today, there are still two place names left in Dongcheng, Hangzhou: one is called the “Plum Blossom Monument”; the other is called the “Cao Flagpole.”
