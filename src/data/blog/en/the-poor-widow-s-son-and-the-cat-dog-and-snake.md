 A long time ago, there was a widow's son on Mount Aini. Seeing everyone else go into business made my heart itch. One day, he begged his grandmother to borrow some money and let him do business with others. After listening to her son's words, the widow had to go everywhere to borrow money for her son. She traveled all over Aini's cottage, and no one would lend her money. She had no choice but to go down the mountain to Bazi to borrow money from Dai leaders. This time, the head lent him a thousand and two dollars and let his son carry on his back to do business.
My son left for a few months. Grandma was in a hurry at home. She prayed for God's blessings every day and stood at the door and watched. One day, her son returned safely with a dog.
When I got home, my son said, “Grandma, I earned this dog by doing business this time. Keep it on a leash and feed it well.” Grandma did as her son said; the mother and son were still poor.
After the spring season began the following year, people in the stockade went out to do business again. The son also wanted his grandmother to find a place to borrow money. Grandma went to borrow another 1,200 yuan from the Dai leader and handed it over to her son.
A few months later, my son came home with a cat. He said to his grandmother, “Grandma, this time I went into business and earned a cat, so take good care of it.” Grandma said, “Boy, the more people do business, the richer they get, but if you earn a dog and a cat, how can you get rich every day.” Even though Grandma said this, she raised the cat well and fished some small fish in the river ditch from time to time to feed it. Dogs and cats are also very obedient and stay with their owners every day. Another year went by like this, and the family was left as poor as ever.
In the spring of the third year, the businessman went down the mountain again. The son also asked Grandma to think about borrowing money. The widow was imprisoned by her son, so she had to go down to the dam again and borrow another one thousand or two coins from her boss and hand it over to her son. My son went out again with the money.
A few months later, he brought back a snake. Grandma scolded her as soon as she saw it: “My son with a wet back, why aren't you arguing, and ask Grandma to pay back the boss's money with anything?”
The son also admitted that he was unlucky and angrily hung the snake through his nose from the girder. One day, the snake hanging from the girder suddenly said, “Let me go, I'll bring you gold and silver.” My son believed it and let it go.
Once the snake sent a circle on the ground, it turned out a bracelet and gave it to the owner. As soon as my son wears a gold bracelet, it becomes gold and silver as soon as his hand takes it. Since then, the mother and son have lived a rich life.
When the widow brought the money back to the boss, the boss was surprised. They didn't know where they had obtained so much money to pay off the debt. So, after a few days, the head personally came to the widow's house to find out.
As soon as the leader entered the door, all he saw was gold and silver. The honest mother and son told him about the gold bracelet. The greedy boss thought that if I had a gold bracelet, I would be Quan Meng's tycoon. With his ingenuity, he came up with a ghost idea. He pretended to say to the mother and son, “God bless you; God keep you rich and healthy forever! However, if one person is blessed, everyone must remember. People from all over the village should be invited so that everyone can come and see this treasure.”
The mother and son thought it was reasonable, so they invited everyone from all over the village. The widow's son wore a gold bracelet and reached out to the crowd for everyone to watch and touch. Watching and touching, all of a sudden, the gold bracelet disappeared, and no one could explain what happened.
Mother and son are very sad to lose their baby. At this point, the dog and cat suddenly spoke to the widow's son: “The owner doesn't need to be sad; the gold bracelet was stolen from the crowd by greedy leaders. Both of us will definitely go get the gold bracelet back for you.”
As soon as the mother and son listened, their worries turned to joy. After talking, the dog and cat hurriedly hit the road. When they arrived at the dam's edge, a large river blocked their way. Dogs can swim, cats can't. Dogs let cats bite their tails and carry cats across the river.
When they arrived at the Dai Zhaizi side, the cat made the dog wait by the village, and first ran into the village to find the head man's home. The cat found the head of the house, only to see that the door was closed and the surrounding walls were dead, and it couldn't get in. The cat looked up and saw a mouse nest in the corner of the roof, with a female mouse sitting next to the nest. The cat said to the mouse, let it quickly gnaw through a hole and go in on its own; otherwise, it would eat the mouse's child. The mouse quickly responded, gnawed a hole in the roof, and let the cat break into the house.
On this day, the head man closed the door and sat alone in bed smoking while admiring the gold bracelet he had just stolen. The gold bracelet was placed on the despicable table in front of him. At this point, the cat jumped three and two jumps to the scammer table, picked up the gold bracelet, and jumped to the roof and drilled out of the hole.
The dog picked up the cat by the river, and when the dog helped the cat swim to the middle of the river, unbeknownst to me, a mountain chicken essence flew in from there and knocked off the gold bracelet, causing the bracelet to go to the bottom of the river. After crossing the river, the dog and cat found the chicken's nest and said to Mountain Chicken Jingjing, “Go pick up the gold bracelet and lose it to us, otherwise they'll smash your nest and bite your children to death!”
Chicken's nest hurriedly flew into the river, picked up the gold bracelet, and handed it to the dog and cat. The dog and cat handed over the gold bracelet to the owner again. Since then, the widow's mother and son have lived a carefree and happy life with their dog and cat.
