 Piglet dragons are produced in Jiangxi. They are shaped like dragons, but they are shorter than dragons. They can fly sideways, and often fly out of the water along the riverbank to catch geese and ducks to eat. Sometimes when someone catches one, they kill it and sell the meat to Chen and Ke's families. Both surnames are descendants of Chen Youliang. They have eaten pork belly since their ancestors passed it down; people with other surnames are afraid to eat it.
One day, a guest came from the west side of the river, caught a pig, and tied it to a boat. The boat was parked by the Qiantang River because it didn't tie the pig dragon tightly, so it ran away and plunged headlong into the river. After a blink of an eye, the river was full of waves, and the boat immediately turned over.
