Zhang Juzheng, a famous minister of a generation, called for wind and rain in front of him and scolded the situation, but while he was brilliant, he hid the crisis behind him. Zhang Juzheng died of illness in Wanli in June '10, and his opposition began to attack in December of the same year. Zhang Juzheng was closed down and raided, and all officials thought to have joined the party with Zhang were cut down for investigation and prosecution. As for the new policy he worked so hard to establish, it went to waste even more. Why did Zhang Juzheng painstakingly run the successful reform business for more than ten years, and once it was disrupted, he had no power to fight? The emperor's attitude played a decisive role in this.
Emperor Wanli was only a ten-year-old child when he took the throne. Zhang Ju was originally from a college student. He was very educated, so he was appointed by the Empress Dowager as the Little Emperor's teacher. He treated his emperor's students very strictly. They had to be monitored and supervised by teachers in their daily lives and every move. Zhang Juzheng always used the requirements of the sages to regulate the behavior of his students, and he must have the style of an emperor in every word and action. Under this strict teaching, Emperor Wanli respected and feared this teacher from the bottom of his heart, and left all major events in the DPRK administration to Zhang Juzheng.
This kind of close relationship between teachers, students, and rulers could have been discussed beautifully, but things never went as well as expected. Being a partner is like being a partner tiger. Zhang Juzheng overlooked that what he taught was not only a child, but also a student; he was also his superior, an emperor of the 95 superiors. Zhang Juzheng was too strict with him. Emperor Wanli learned calligraphy from an early age, and his handwriting was excellent. He often writes letters for close ministers around him, and Zhang Juzheng is also a person who often receives letters from the emperor. The little emperor treated calligraphy as a hobby and was very proud, but after two years, Zhang Juzheng advised the emperor not to get too addicted to calligraphy on the grounds that calligraphy was only a final skill, and also forcefully removed the calligraphy class the emperor must take every day. As can be seen from such a trivial matter, Zhang Juzheng's tough, one-size-fits-all style of work has hurt the little emperor's heart. Then, once again, the little emperor was drunk late at night with the two eunuchs around him and messed around in the palace. As a result, on the second day, Zhang Ju made his own decision and removed all the eunuchs who played with him. He also forced the emperor to post a “Sin Himself” article to the people all over the country. The wording was very harsh, making the little emperor feel really unbearable. Amidst such attacks time and time again, Emperor Wanli's feelings for Zhang Juzheng slowly changed from awe to resentment. ue004
In the ten years of Wanli, Zhang Juzheng, who had always influenced the administration of the DPRK, died. The 20-year-old Emperor Wanli was full of confidence and began his own government. However, Emperor Wanli soon discovered that even though Zhang Juzheng had died, his shadow still influenced the imperial court. Emperor Wanli is still unable to handle the administration of the DPRK according to his own will. The DPRK and China are full of officials trained by Zhang Juzheng. Moreover, he gradually discovered that Zhang Juzheng, who had always been regarded by him as a sage and also required the emperor to be a sage, was not as sacred as his appearance seemed. Zhang Juzheng himself likes women, and he also loves the secret tricks of the house, which he has always criticized. Zhang Juzheng is also greedy for money, and has also had the experience of taking bribes. All of this took the divine aura off his head. The emperor began to feel that Zhang Juzheng treated people with two completely different standards for himself. He asked others to be frugal, but he was extremely extravagant; he demanded that others were not close to women, but he was greedy for beauty, etc. Moreover, if we want to be self-governing now, we must remove Zhang Ju's shadow in the middle of the DPRK and eliminate the influence he left behind. ue004
As a result, he first attacked Feng Bao, a staunch supporter of Zhang Juzheng and the general manager who helped him communicate with the harem. At the beginning, Empress Li had extremely strict restrictions on Zhu Yijun's study and practice, supervising her everywhere, and often punishing her kneeling and punishing her. Most of them stem from Feng Bao. As Empress Dowager Feng Bao's eyes and ears, he often reports Zhu Yijun's problems to the Empress Dowager. The Empress Dowager always has to be punished. For this reason, Zhu Yijun was very afraid of Feng Bao. Sometimes he had to please Feng Bao, but deep down he planted the seeds of hatred against Feng Bao. Now that he has power, he immediately seizes some of Feng Bao's misdeeds and devalues him to Nanjing. Zhu Yijun, who had tasted the sweetness of the whole person, pointed the finger at Zhang Juzheng after his death. He wants a “double sculpture with one arrow”. On the one hand, he can use this to establish his authority and take control of the dynasty; on the other hand, he also wants to collect a large sum of money from Zhang Juzheng's family. In March of Wanli 11, Zhu Yijun ordered Zhang Juzheng to pursue the title of “Shangzheng” and “Master Master” under the pretext that Zhang Juzheng was an dictator when he was an official and used power for personal gain. He also ordered the pursuit of his nickname “Wen Zhonggong” and ousted a number of courtiers who were closely related to Zhang Juzheng back then. Zhang Juzheng's son was relegated to a common man. Afterwards, he sent someone to raid Zhang Juzheng's home, causing more than a dozen children and grandchildren of the Zhang family to be locked in the house and starved to death. In the end, the Zhang family found only 10,000 yuan of gold and 100,000 yuan of silver. The eunuch who was sent to handle this matter felt that the outcome was not easy to explain to the emperor, so he arrested Zhang Juzheng's eldest son, Zhang Jingxiu, the head of the Ministry of Rites, and severely tortured him. Under torture, Zhang Jingxiu bit it indiscriminately, saying that there are still 300,000 pieces of money hidden in the homes of three people including Zeng Sheng. As a result, these three families were also victims of this house raid, and they were all raided. Zhang Juzheng's son was forced to commit suicide, and the disaster at home made the government and field tremble.
In August of the 12th year of Wanli, Zhu Yijun issued a royal decree announcing Zhang Juzheng's so-called charges, and distributed his brother, son, and Sun Tong to the border. At this point, a farce about the house hunt came to an end. Zhu Yijun also finally escaped the shadow Zhang Ju was casting on him psychologically. It's just that Zhang Juzheng's efforts to reform went to waste.
