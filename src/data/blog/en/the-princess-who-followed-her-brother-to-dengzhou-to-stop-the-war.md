 Legend has it that when Li Shimin, the second lord of Tang, marched to the East, an imperial sister strongly dissuaded him. The emperor did not listen and insisted on going to war, so she went all the way to Dengzhou. There was physical evidence of this incident. “The Chronicles of Qixia County” records: “Under Mount Ai's right ear peak, there used to be an Huanggu Pagoda, but now it has been abandoned.” As a result, according to the general location indicated in the county history, people began searching for the Huanggu Pagoda in Mount Ai to explore its history. At the beginning of October 2002, we drove into the late Ai Mountain and entered the North Gate of Ai Mountain Forest Farm in Penglai. The general location of the “Princess Tower” and “Princess Palace” was clearly marked on the newly made guide map on the side of the gate. This message dawned on our eyes. Isn't the princess the Queen? Master Xu Qinglin, who has been working on a forest farm for more than 20 years, led us up the mountain in order to get the water out of the water.
 
 Mount Ai, at an altitude of 814 meters, is steep and has several peaks connected, like an ink painting hanging on a canopy. We walked in a detour along a winding mountain path. After walking for a while, I saw a small, broken stone pagoda standing in the weir. Master Xu told a story that has been around for a long time: “Rumor has it that when Emperor Tang dynasty drove the Eastern Expedition to Goryeo, there were many objections, including the emperor, who was very prestigious in the DPRK and China, who also strongly discouraged them due to lack of reasons for this Eastern Expedition. They directly persuaded them to go to camp at the foot of Mount Ai in Dongnae County, and never persuaded the Imperial Brother to return to Nepal. Many years later, she was buried on a mountain in the palace where the princess died, and later this tower was built as a memorial. It is also known as the 'Huanggu Tower' because the pagoda was built later.”
 
 Take a close look at this tower; there are still three floors above ground. The lower two floors are trapezoidal. They are towers, hidden in Egypt. Starting from the third floor, there is a finely carved round tower. From the legend of the Princess Tower, another story of “Princess Hermitage” was drawn up.
 
 Heading west from the “Huanggu Tower”, climb a few floors of terraced fields. On a relatively flat floor, there is a set of houses covered with old gray tiles. A square stone carving of the “Eight Road Weapons Factory” is clearly embedded on the wall on the left side of the house door. Master Xu said that this is the site of the “Princess Palace.” At the end of the 30s of the last century, the Eighth Route Army Weapons Factory, under the leadership of factory director Zhou Jilong, chose this hidden and safe place, used the site where the thatched hut had collapsed to build a weapons factory to manufacture weapons such as guns, bullets, mines, and grenades for the Eighth Route Army, and even made mortars, making great contributions to the war of resistance. Later, the site of the weapons factory was discovered by Japanese devils, and troops were sent to destroy it. Factory director Zhou Jilong heard the news and organized workers overnight to disassemble the machines, carry the parts over the Aishan Pass, and move the factory to the early stages of Aishan. There were only a few dozen people left in the original factory to cover the army after it was cut off. The next day, the sky was overshadowed, and the devil took over the top of the mountain on the west side of Gongzhu Palace, set up machine guns and started shooting. There was heavy fog all over the day, and no one was seen within five steps. Guns shot like rain, but the fog rotated with people, and never hit a single person. The devil became angry and wanted to burn down the factory building, but then a sudden shower of rain fell, putting out the fire, and the devil was so angry that he stared. Local people clapped their hands and applauded, saying, “The princess has always opposed the war. When she first discouraged the emperor's brother from going to war, her brother didn't listen and would rather leave home to Nepal; today, demons came to kill people and set fire, and she also showed up to protect the weapons factory.”
 
 Looking back at the position of Princess Tower, it is right behind the peak on the west side of Ai. Isn't this “below the peak of the right ear”? However, visitors can't help but ask: did Emperor Tang really have an imperial sister who advised her brother along the road? The answer is: This is just a legend, as there are many legends about Tang Er conquering the East, but there is no record in history that he set foot on the land of Jiaodong.
