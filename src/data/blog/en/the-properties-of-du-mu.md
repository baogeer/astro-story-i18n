 Du Mu is Du You's grandson. Du Mu was already ten years old when Du You passed away in 812. “The Old Book of Tang” volume 147 records: Du You used to work in Yangzhou, “The family has huge wealth. The first one is in Anren Li, and Ducheng has a villa. Tatebayashi Pond is the largest in Seongnam.” Therefore, Du Mu experienced and remembered the bustle of the past. However, Du Mu's father, a young son of Du You, died a long time ago. Du Mu's uncle Fang, but not necessarily Du You's eldest son, died in 822. History says, “Ji Dai is depressed and has few illnesses. Every recipe he makes his own recipes. He drinks medicinal food and water. He doesn't use traditional recipes; he doesn't get into his mouth. From Yu Yao's death, I cried all the time, and it was almost impossible to win.” It is also said that Du You has three children. After Du You, “the son lost his son and eventually became the son of Nong Shaoqing”; the teacher was probably Du You's eldest son or heir. There is no explanation for the teacher's loss in history; Du Mu did not mention it in his own poems. If you think of it, it's rather mediocre, and lackluster.
Soon after Yu Xian's death and his uncle Shifang's death, the three bedrooms of the Du family may have split up soon. Du Mu later said in “The Supreme Prime Minister Seeks the Second Revelation of Huzhou,” “He was orphaned and poor. An Ren's old house was placed at the end of the Open New Year. A certain house had 30 rooms, went to the yuan and the end of the day, paid for interest and owned by someone else, so they moved out.” Also, “The Supreme Prime Minister Seeks Hangzhou Enlightenment” says, “Somebody in Beijing and China is nothing but Anren's old 30th branch office.” It can be seen that the house in Anrenli of the Du family was built before Du You—the Jingzhao Du Clan has been in the Gaomen clan since Wei Jin—only improved and expanded the scale after Du You developed. Naturally, this number one is divided into several units. Among them, there are 30 houses under the name of Du Mu (Du Mu has an older brother and younger brother). They have their own courtyards and are relatively independent. That's why it's also possible to switch hands to others.
Du Mu's cousin, Du Wei, the son of his uncle Fang, married Princess Qiyang in 814. He is Captain Ma. However, Shikifang has four sons, and they all deserve to be the first in An In-ri. Du Mu became an orphan due to the death of his father, and there were few people to take care of him after his uncle's death; after setting up separate households, they became economically independent and sat and ate the mountains. The inherited Anin-ri property was used to pay off the debt, and after that, “every ten people moved out of their homes in eight years. I have a vertical, hundreds of volumes of books, and nurture them. Running through hardships, there is nothing to hide from. It died at Yanfu's private temple; the pillars were destroyed and destroyed.” (“The Supreme Prime Minister Seeks the Second Revelation of Huzhou”) Yanfuli and An In-ri are not in the same place. However, it is a private temple of Du Family, and it is also a Du family business. Therefore, when they are extremely poor and bored, Du Mu's family can take shelter there for a while.
Citing the legend of Du Youben, “Du Cheng has villas; the pavillion pond is the largest in the south of the city.” This villa is outside the city, in the so-called Fanchuan area. Du Mu's “An Essay on a Close Friend” says, “There is an old house at the bottom of Zhongnan Mountain, and there are quite a few water trees. Let's put pen and ink in between.” Du Mu also wanted to live and study there. Mainly, I like the beautiful landscape there. In “Changxingli: Sending Summer to the South Neighbors to Escape the Heat”: “Yongri Pillows, Forbidden Mountain, Yunshui Town.” While taking a nap, I also dreamed about Villa Fanchuan.
In addition, the Du family also has real estate in Dongdu, Luoyang. Du Mu in “Bushi Dongdu Lives and Tracks Road, Goes to Chuan Yin Liu Shilang, and Goes to the Forty Rhymes”: “Give Dai to become an official house, and a helper to start a guest booth.” The bottom of the sentence reads: “A certain sixth generation of ancestors lived in Renhe-ri at the beginning of the country; the search already belonged to a government house. I'm currently living in Trudaofang.” I don't know when the Du family's house in Luoyang City fell into disposition. Anyway, when Du Mu came to Luoyang as an official, he had to rent a house. I couldn't help but be surprised.
Regarding An In-ri's house, Du Mu described it to his nephew: “The old Kaizumon Gate is in the center of Chang'an City. There was nothing in it; it was full of 10,000 books. The 200-edition Family Collection takes the reins and outs. Most of them were written in Fuzhou; now, the Five Ji Qiang. It's OK to read it well; to help is good.” (“The Winter Solstice: A Song for My Little Nephew A Yi Shi”) Du Mu is not focusing on this house, but on a collection of books inherited from his ancestors. In “The Revelation of the Confidant Self”, Du Mu also said, “There are old titles on it; there are only ten thousand volumes.”
All of these are industries left over from Du Mu's ancestors; although Du Mu didn't take it seriously, they were actually gradually being consumed. But that wasn't Du Mu's intention. When he was an official in Jiangnan, he owned a business in Yangxian on the west side of Taihu Lake. “Xu Qishi Abandoned His Official and Returned to the East. He was chic in Jiangnan. He was quite comfortable. He had high hopes for the Autumn Festival, and presented the Ten Rhymes with his poem” Cloud:
“Dust is fascinated by the present and the past, and the cloud sentiments are a part of Shu. He was in the middle of snow, and Yang Xian visited our house.” Note: “There is a watershed near Yixing County.” “Li Shilang is rich in spring stones in Yangxian, and the shepherd is also rich in Yangxian, recounting the old story because of his long words and four rhymes”: “Zongnanshan Diao Quan Cave and bought fishing boats in Yangxian Creek.” The Yixing mentioned in the note and Yang Xian mentioned in the poem are all in the same place. Feng Jiwu, a native of the Qing dynasty, quoted Ni Zan's “Preface to Jingxi” in “Notes on Fan Chuan Poems”: “Su Zizhan said: Tang Du Mu's structure is beside the stream, and history can be examined until now.” The Yangxian Creek mentioned in Du Mu's poem is Jingxi; Jingxi flows into Taihu Lake from west to east, and Yixing is on the north side of Jingxi. Feng also quoted “The Chronicles of One Dominion”: “Du Qiao is outside the east gate of Yixing City. One person went up the bridge, commonly known as Toad Bridge. Legend has it that it is the former site of Dumu Shuixie.” It can be seen that Du Mu's property here is very close to Jingxi, and it is close to the city. (For Jingxi, Yixing, and Yang Xian, see Gu Zuyu's “Notes on Reading History and Public Opinion” volume 25)
In the latter poem, Yang Xian and Boshan are combined with the old house in Zhongnan. Therefore, the industry that Du Mu established in the south was also dominated by landscape and water. “In the beginning, Xing Qun was rewarded, the assassin of Shizhou”: “In the Ming dynasty, you had to use a knife and ruler. I had a career in seclusion. In the midst of the wind, smoke and envy, take a break from the turtle and go to Fei Nao.” Du Mu made it clear that he would like to enjoy “a valley of smoke and envy” after his family leaves office. Unfortunately, our poet was not blessed, and did not end up suffering from the warm waters of Wu Nong Soft Mountain. According to Du Mu's “Self-Written Epitaph,” the poet was 50 years old and finally moved to An Renli's old house. There is a question here, that is, Du Mu's real estate in An Renli is already owned by someone else in order to repay his debts — how can he go back to living there?
There are two possibilities. First, after Du Mu became an official, he redeemed his old house. Because selling ancestral real estate is a typical act of losing one's family in our traditional culture. Therefore, the first change in the Three Changes of Xiao Zi was, “Locusts eat their farmhouse.” Naturally, Du Mu is not willing to be seen as a locust. What's more, he is also interested in buying a property — since he can buy a property in Jiangnan, of course, he can also buy a property in Beijing. Second, Du Mu lives with his brother. He retired to live in Beijing at the time of his brother; or at his cousin's family, such as Ma Duyu's family. Whether it's a cousin or brother, they all belong to Anin-ri's house. We have analyzed this before. Judging from Du Mu's poems to his younger nephew Ah Yi, Du Mu had a good relationship with his cousin or brother as if they had never split up.
In short, Du Mu died in the Du family's former home. This was in 853. However, his cousin Du Yi previously worshipped the minister and gave Shang Shu the right servant of Shang Shu Shu as a servant, and settled the law with Chung Shu. Du Mu was able to live at his old house, showing that the Du Mu brothers were not unlucky with their grandfather Du You; they were able to overcome their superstition. Sadly, the poet was well known and didn't show up. He passed away after a thousand years. After a thousand years, it still saddens our generation.
