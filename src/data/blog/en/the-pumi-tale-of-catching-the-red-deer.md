 Legend has it that Tenjin gave the giant Jianzu a red dog, a white dog, a gold dog, a silver dog, a bronze dog, and an iron dog.
These six dogs helped him find the red deer and shot it with an arrow. He cut off the red deer's head and turned it into the sky, his eyes turned into the sun and the moon, his teeth turned into stars, and the blood that came out became the ocean: he also cut off its skin, the skin became the earth, and the fur on the skin became plants; he also dug out its heart, liver, and lungs became a mountain, liver, and lungs; the intestines he took out turned into rivers, and the small intestine became roads.
