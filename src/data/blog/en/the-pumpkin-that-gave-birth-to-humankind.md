 It is recorded in the Bimo Sutra of the Yi people that in the ancient, desolate times, there were no people on earth, only fairy activities in the sky. One day, the god of heaven bit off a tooth when biting a walnut and fell on the earth. After being moisturized by rain and nourished by the soil, a pumpkin tree grew. This melon tree had a solitary melon. Emperor Yu Huang of the sky knew that this melon nurtured human life, so he gave the earth a golden file and spread human life on the earth, but no animal creature dared to open this melon. At this point, two brothers, mice and Diao Lingzi, came out and said, “We can open it, but what are the benefits. Emperor Yu Huang said, “If you open this pumpkin, if you have humans, you can let mice live in humans Upstairs, they let Diao Ling Zi cook the food in the field where people grow it and let them eat it first. As a result, these two brothers opened this pumpkin with a golden gourd. Since then, humans have appeared, and mice and Diao Lingzi have followed God's will. One eats food on the ground and the other on the upper floor. There is no way for people to continue until now.
