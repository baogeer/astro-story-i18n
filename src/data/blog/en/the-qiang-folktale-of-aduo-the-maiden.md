 June and July of the lunar calendar happen to be the season for corn to sprout and grow red. Every time there are frequent droughts at this time, people have no choice but to watch the sky and hope for rain in Abamubita to help the grains recover.
At noon one day, the warm and gentle voice of a girl from the family vaguely sounded on Mount Jiguo: “Grandma, it's about to rain, so pack up the food on the roof.” As soon as the old couple had packed their food, a heavy rain poured down until dark. The next day, the old couple made an appointment with their neighbors and the young people in the stockade to search for the daughter they thought about day and night in Jiguo Mountain. They traveled all over Mount Jiguo, and still didn't see the girl. The old couple looked at the sky in disappointment, complained about the suffering of their own lives, and lost the pearl in their pocket. They cried more and saddened. The tears flowed unstoppably. At around noon, the sky changed abruptly, clouds were rolling, thunder was loud, and a torrential rain was imminent. At this point, the girl called out again from the top of Mount Jiguo: “Ah... Dad, it's about to fire into the air; it's about to rain.” The man left behind at home hurriedly fired shots in the sky, instantly dispersing the dark clouds and seeing the hot sun again. A disastrous torrential rain was avoided. The people looking for people successfully searched the top of the mountain at the location the girl called, and finally found the body of the girl Ho at the top of Mount Jiguo. Her face was rosy, and there were many marks of injuries caused by intense fighting on her body.
It turns out that the king of Mount Jiguo had a strange temper. The Qiang people went up the mountain to dig for medicine, hunt, and graze, and people came and went frequently. As long as there was a little loud shouting or drinking, he would get angry, bring down the torrential rain and wind, causing disaster for the people. Girl Ho is a mortal under a fairy. She is kind at heart and is happy to solve problems for mortal people. For the benefit of the Qiang people, she went up the mountain to negotiate with King Jiguo Mountain and advised him to accumulate more virtue and do good deeds to reduce disaster on earth. King Jigosan did not ask for his advice. He believed that the girl was competing with him for power and against him, and then used military force to launch a vicious battle with Ho Girl. King Jiguo of Mount Jiguo used his strength and martial arts to launch a vicious battle. At this point, the old king Liang Zishan saw King Jiguo Mountain's vicious attack. How could Ho Girl defeat King Jiguo Mountain. She picked up a machete and cut off King Jiguo Mountain's head. Because she was too strong, she slashed the head of Jiguo Mountain into the black water river of Sergu, and asked him to let the river wash away his sins, thus subduing King Jiguo Mountain.
Furthermore, Lady Ho was rescued and ascended to heaven by the old king Liang Zishan. Her corpse was just Ho Girl's shell. Later, on the recommendation of the old monarch Liang Zishan Wang, the father Mubita named Ho Girl as King Jiguoshan. People often hear girls shouting, “Ah -- Grandma, today's weather is good, let's dry our food.” Every time during the droughts, the Qiang people in the stockade are led up the mountain to seek rain. People burn scented cypress and smoke, respect the mountain gods, and keep calling, “Lady Adoya, the crops are dry. Let it rain soon, your children and grandchildren will be hungry.” They went back while calling. As soon as they got home, the rain started pouring down. After a long time, it became a Qiang folk custom to go up the mountain to ask for rain. The beautiful name of Adoya's sister has always been celebrated.
