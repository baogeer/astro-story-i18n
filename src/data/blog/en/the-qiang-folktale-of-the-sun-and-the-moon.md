 Legend has it that a long time ago, the ground was very flat, with no ravines or mountains.
At that time, there was a type of tree called the Marsan tree, which was as tall as the sky. One day, a group of boring apes climbed down the branches to the Tiangong Palace and accidentally pushed it to the tank filled with water in the Tiangong Palace. It immediately rained like a spring, and the flood was raging. They soon reached Nantianmen Gate. Seeing this situation at Tenjin Mupita, they saw this situation, and only then did a lot of water flow away. However, fireworks have been cut off, and there are ravines on the ground. Seeing this situation, Mubita sighed. Suddenly, one day fell to report that two brothers and sisters, when they saw water rising from a flat ground, they went into their pockets together and floated all the way down the water to Tiangong. Mubita was happy for a while and rushed back to the palace, only to see that the brother and sister were smart and cute. They went up and asked them what their names were. The man said his name was Moon, and the woman said her name was Sun.
Mubita told them the story of the world and the heavens. She said, “Now that the floods are raging, there are no traces on earth. I just want you brother and sister to do good deeds again.” Mubita's gentle explanation made them become a family. After listening to Mubita's words, the brother and sister didn't agree to anything.
Mubita couldn't do it; they had to let them each hold a grinding stone and drop it from two distant mountains. If the two millstones met, they became a family. The brother and sister each went to the top of the mountain and put down the millstone. As a result, the two millstones just met at the bottom of the mountain.
The brother and sister are still refusing to obey. Mubita was very moved to see their insistence, but for the sake of the prosperity of the world, they only aggrieved them. Then he said, “Well, you two each burn a fire in a far away place. If the blue smoke is mixed in the sky, your brother and sister will become a family.” The brother and sister started the fire as ordered. As a result, the blue smoke actually mixed up in the air. The brother and sister knew it was God's will, and they were moved by Mubita's care for the world, so they became close friends.
Soon, my younger sister became pregnant and gave birth to a bunch of meat. After Mubita found out, they had them turn the mass of meat into small pieces of meat, one in the east, east, north, and south. A few days later, they only saw fireworks all over the world. Mubita turned the pieces of meat scattered on the stones into villains one by one.
The sun and moon have made an immortal contribution to the prosperity of the human world. Mubita decided to leave their lives in the Heavenly Garden, but the two pandas didn't want to. They wanted to protect their children, but because the pandas became a family, they had no face to face at this point, and asked each other from now on. Mubita was very appreciative of the brothers' and sisters' virtues, so they took turns taking care of their children during the day and night, while the older brother Moon knew her sister was ashamed, and asked her to go out at night to take care of her, because no one would see her at night. However, the younger sister was afraid of the dark, the older brother let her go out during the day, and the younger sister was afraid of laughing at her, so the older brother came up with an idea for her to go out with a bunch of silver needles.
Since then, they have never been able to see each other again. Every time the younger sister's sun is about to set, her brother escorts her on the top of a distant mountain, and when she rises from the east, she wants to call her brother, but the moon has disappeared from the western canopy, so they illuminate the world with the pain of parting ways.
At the same time, Mubita ordered that brothers and sisters were prohibited from becoming married in any room, and the horse sang tree, which was the same height as the sky, died three feet away from the ground.
