 “Cloud Cloud Shoes” are handmade cloth shoes loved by the Qiang people. The shape of the shoe is quite small. The tip of the shoe is slightly curved, the sole is thick, and the upper part is embroidered with colorful clouds and azalea patterns. There is a story going around about the origin of Yunyun shoes.
In ancient times, a beautiful and moving carp fairy lived in a clear lake. She looked at a clear-eyed young barefoot shepherd. Fairy Carp devotes her rich thoughts and emotions to the interpretation of cloud patterns, using clouds in the sky and azaleas on the lakeside to embroider beautiful Yunyun shoes. She also embroidered love on “Yunyun Shoes,” and gave them to a barefoot shepherd boy as a token of love for both parties. The young shepherd fell in love with the carp fairy. He had a lot of fun wearing the “Cloud Cloud Shoes” embroidered by the girl himself. On her first day of marriage, the carp fairy happily sang a series of ancient narrative songs with the young shepherd, calling it “Flower Night.” Among them, there is a love song that sings: “I heard that my brother is going to travel a long distance, Aimei can't give up; what about sending my brother a pair of Yunyun shoes? Teng Yun will come back soon after driving in the fog.” There is also a love song that says, “I gave Grandpa a pair of Yunyun shoes. Does Grandpa wear Love Love? The shoes are hand-embroidered by Aimee, and can't be exchanged for a cash cow; I gave Grandpa a pair of Yunyun shoes; Grandpa doesn't need to hide them; you can walk along the road, just don't forget your sister.” These beautiful and emotional “Flower Night” songs all show that the carp fairy gave her “Yunyun Shoes” embroidered with thousands of stitches and threads to her lover, treating them as a very precious gift to support the infinite affection and love of both parties. After their marriage, the carp fairy and the young shepherd became a happy and happy couple; because of this, “Yunyun Shoes” have spread around the world, and the Qiang people are also known as a nation on the clouds because of this.
