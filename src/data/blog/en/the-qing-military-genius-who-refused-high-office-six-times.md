 Author: Liu Jixing
Peng Yulin was a famous politician and military figure in the Qing dynasty. Along with Zeng Guofan and Zuo Zongtang, he is known as the “Three Great Masters of the Qing Dynasty,” along with Zeng Guofan, Zuo Zongtang, and Hu Linyi. He was the founder of the Xiang Army Marines, the founder of the modern Chinese Navy, Governor of the Liangjiang River, Minister of Trade, and Shang Shu of the Military Department. He has resigned as a senior official six times, which is rare in the world.
Originally from Hengyang, Hunan, Peng Yulin was born in Anqing, Anhui in 1816. Her character is Xueqin (later known as Xueshuai), and he calls himself the owner of the Imperial Palace. His father, Peng Mingjiu, was an “inspector of Liangyuan Town in Hefei”. Although he was a very low-ranking military officer, he received extremely high praise from Li Hanzhang (brother of Li Hongzhang), the governor of Anhui at the time. After Peng Mingjiu's death, he personally passed down, and “promoted Peng Mingjiu as the most patrol officer in Anhui.” However, Peng Ming died young. Coupled with the fact that he was an official with a clean slack, he did not leave any possessions behind. Coupled with the fact that some bad members of the family also bullied their orphans and widows, Peng Yulin was unable to look back on her teenage years in her hometown of Hengyang, Hunan. There was little fun, and a lot of sorrow.
What is infuriating is that their orphan and widow's only small birth was stolen by villains in the family. Peng Yulin's younger brother, Peng Yuqi, was almost driven into the river and drowned. One day, the mother, Mrs. Wang, called her two sons, Peng Yulin and Peng Yuqi, crying and saying to them, “We are always being bullied and pressured; we can't stay in this place for long. You haven't reached adulthood, so stay away from trouble. Remember, from now on, you must be self-reliant, wait until you have achieved something, and come see me again!” My dear mother burst into tears, and Didi was sad. The poor children were sensible for a long time, so 13-year-old Peng Yuqi went to the long-distance water business with others, and the news disappeared for a long time. Sixteen-year-old Peng Yulin studied at Shigu Academy in Hengyang City. He is smart, studious, and particularly able to bear hardships. He never complains about Tianyu. At the time, he “lost his coat and ate no more than three meals, but he defended himself and never sighed in hunger or cold.”
Due to her difficult life, Peng Yulin had to interrupt her studies, get a career equivalent to a paperwork in the military camp, and talk about raising her widow. During this time, Peng Yulin met the first person in his life, Gao Renjian, who was the governor of Hengyang. By chance, Gao Renjian saw a document written by Peng Yulin in the military camp and greatly appreciated his literary talent and calligraphy. Later, he also recruited disciples and invited him to study at government schools, and became a brilliant talent. However, if it were to stop there, at most, Peng Yulin would only be a famous person in Hengyang City, seeking an official position through scientific studies. Like his father, he was only able to live in the countryside for the rest of his life despite his wisdom and ability.
In the 30th year of Daoguang (1850), Li Yuan initiated an uprising in Xinning, Hunan, and Peng Yulin accompanied the Hengzhou Coalition to suppress it. A small riot of only a few hundred people was quickly put to an end. Peng Yulin came to prominence in this battle. He was awarded the Blue Ling Dowager, but he was unable to resign. He went to a pawn shop in Leiyang, Hunan to become a treasurer. I'm afraid at this point, the intellectuals' arrogance is still deeply rooted in his heart, and he clearly disdains the acts of military generals who earned fame through murder.
In the 4th year of Xianfeng (1854), the Taiping Empire flourished, and the decaying Eight Flag Army was defeated. Zeng Guofan formed sailors in Hengzhou and Xiangtan to recruit talents. First, Zeng Guofan's younger brother, Tsang Kwok-po, praised Pang Yu-lin, and then Chang Yi-an recommended that Pang Yu-lin was brave enough. Tsang Kwok-fan then published a book calling him to visit. However, Peng Yulin just lost her mother and was unable to stay at home. Tsang Kwok-fan once again persuaded him and said, “With the help of the village, father and son don't protect each other, can they keep the hill tomb for a long time?” This was said at a very high level. Peng Yulin felt his sincerity and readily applied for the military. When 43-year-old Zeng Guofan and 38-year-old Peng Yulin met this time, they began an extraordinary life of fighting against the stormy Qing dynasty.
After joining the Xiang Army, Peng Yulin fought bravely and well in battles with the Taiping Heavenly Army, and was extremely intelligent and brave. Xiang Army coach Zeng Guofan asked him to found the Xiang Army Sailor Division, buy foreign artillery, build large ships, and train generals. Peng Yulin's military rule is extremely strict. In the Xiang Army sailor division, his boat is a speed boat with a red flag on it. The three in the battalion are not allowed to “brawl, gamble, or pick opiates.” If there were violators, dozens of canes were allowed, and heavy people landed on the ground. Therefore, under Peng Yulin's rule, military discipline was number one in the Xiang Army, far superior to Zeng Guoquan's department, who lured people to death with money, alcohol, and beautiful bait. In “Tales from the New World,” Yi Tsung-mui claimed that Peng Yulin at the time “looked like an idle wild crane, and his voice was so subtle that it was indistinguishable. But every time they are angry, those who see it shudder”, praising his majesty in governing the military.
He lived up to Zeng Guofan's high hopes and led the Xiang Army sailors to defeat the Taiping Army in the Battle of Xiangtan. He later captured Yuezhou with the army and defeated the Taiping Army sailors in a row in Wuhan and Tianjiazhen. Later, the Xiang Army sailors he led were defeated by Shi Dakai in Hukou, Jiangxi. As a result, he rectified the sailors and cooperated with the Army in defeating the Taiping Army in places such as Zhangshu Town and Linjiang. Next, the Xiang Army sailors led by him captured Hukou, Jiujiang, and Anqing with Yang Zaifu's troops, and was promoted to the rank of mariner admiral and the right servant of the military department. In the battle to capture Tianjing (present-day Nanjing), he personally led sailors to help Zeng Guoquan's Lu Shi go east along the Yangtze River and block the mouth of the Tianjing moat. The following year, he and Yang Zaifu conquered Jiangpu, Jiuzhou, and Pukou, cut off the Tianjing Food Trail, and became a major hero in the capture of Tianjing. After defeating the Kingdom of Taiping, he dedicated himself to the construction of the Qing Army's Yangtze River Sailor Division, working hard and devoting himself to the Qing army's sailors. Later, the Yangtze River Mariner, who poured out Peng Yulin's countless efforts, was fully accepted by Li Hongzhang and became the most important force of the Beiyang Sailors. The modern Navy started in Sri Lanka.
Peng Yulin once wrote a link. People relished it. The upper link was “Martyrs' Lives, Famous Men, and Daredevil,” and the lower link “Murder Saves People's Hearts.” He is known for being “lethal,” and “Japan fought for life in the midst of a thicket.” He often said, “I won't let a general die alone, nor let a timid man live alone!” In the Battle of Xiaogushan as recorded in historical materials, when the sailors of the Xiang Army attacked Xiaogushan, the Taiping Army “fired artillery on the coast, shot like rain”. If the ships were unshielded, the sailors and soldiers had no choice but to “avoid artillery.” But a lot of tried methods didn't work. Peng Yulin came up with a trick to “use flesh and blood to build a boat head; avoid it if you can avoid it; listen to the inevitable.” He personally took the lead in “planting the bow of the boat” and only said, “Today is the day I die. A general should not be allowed to die alone, nor should a timid man be left alone.” As a result, he personally led the Xiang Army battleships forward courageously, hitting Xiaogu Mountain and firing artillery on the Pacific coast. The Xiang Army “showed his quick skills and sharp eyesight”; if they could hide, they hid; if they couldn't, they all laughed at it; they all laughed at it and were ashamed.” Under heavy shelling by the Taiping Army, the Xiang Army was still “destroyed in two days of war,” although many people were killed and wounded. Peng Yulin was very happy and wrote a poem: “The scholar laughed and led the battleship, the flag on the river shines brightly, and 100,000 pixiu sang in unison, and Pang Lang took back Little Girl.” Also make a poem notebook and distribute it to friends. His iron-blooded pride in the face of danger is admirable.
Peng Yulin abhors corruption in the official arena and is determined not to cooperate with corrupt officials. When he was the admiral of the Yangtze River Mariner and the military commander, he acted impartially, acting as evil, severely punishing evil forces, and even offended senior Qing court officials Zeng Guofan and Li Hongzhang. Tsang Kwok-chuen is the brother of Tsang Kwok-fan. Pang Yuk-lin discovered that Tsang Kwok-chuen's army was undisciplined, and also captured two opium-smoking generals under Tsang Kwok-chuen. Tsang Kwok-fan is Pang Yu-lin's mentor, and he can be described as being very gracious. However, despite the friendship between teachers and students, Peng Yulin resolutely proposed impeaching Tsang Kwok-kuen three times, causing Tsang Kwok-fan to write angrily to him questioning where his younger brother had offended him.
One year, Peng Yulin passed by Anqing, and suddenly someone stopped him and called a lawsuit against the local bully Li Qiusheng. Li Qiusheng is Li Hongzhang's cousin. With the power of Li Hongzhang's power and opposition, he runs rampant in the village and kidnaps wives and women. Local people dare not speak up. After investigation, Peng Yulin obtained sufficient evidence and arrested Li Qiusheng for interrogation. Li Qiusheng actually disregarded Peng Yulin and was afraid to confess what he did. Peng Yulin unequivocally ordered: “Without this person, it will be difficult to have peace in Anqing.” At this point, the Anhui Governor heard the news and rushed to plead for help. Peng Yulin greeted him and ordered his men to quickly decapitate Evil Shao. The governor is still deliberating over words; Evil Shao has already ordered to go to Huangquan. Afterwards, Peng Yulin wrote a letter to Li Hongzhang, which only understated: “It made my nephew make a bad voice for my husband, and I felt sorry for him; I have already dealt with the government.” Li Hongzhang has always hated Peng Yulin, but he still has to write back and say thank you!
During her tenure, Peng Yulin also successively impeached and disposed of more than 100 corrupt and incompetent government officials. Among them, there was no shortage of senior officials. Furthermore, one of Peng Yulin's nephews, who served as the governor, was also killed by him due to a military aircraft mistake. What he did was a popular saying at the time: “As soon as Peng Gong came out, the river and lake were awe-inspiring.” An alternate lieutenant general Hu Kaitai summoned a prostitute to kill his wife, and Peng Yulin angrily killed him; Hubei Vice General Tan Zulun abducted his friend's wife and killed him. State and county officials colluded with him, and even the Governor protected him, but Peng Yulin was brought to justice.
Peng Yulin joined the Xiang Army sailor as a scholar. Due to his intelligence and bravery, and his repeated achievements in battles against Tianjia Town and Hukou, he was gradually promoted to the commander of the Sailor Division and became the commander of the Xiang Army Marines. He was successively awarded by the imperial court as the Governor of Jinhua, Guangdong Province Inspector General, Governor of Anhui Province, Prince Shaobao, Governor of Caoyun, Admiral of the Marine Division, Chief Minister of Trade of the South Seas and Minister of Commerce of the Nanyang Province, and Sho Sho of the Military Department. He sees wealth as a cloud, and has been a senior official six times in his life. Therefore, at the time, there was a popular saying, “Peng Yulin desperately resigns, Li Hongzhang desperately becomes an official.”
The first time was to resign as governor of Anhui Province (equivalent to governor of Anhui Province). At the time, it was in the 11th year of Xianfeng, and Peng Yulin's official position was Anhui's administrative envoy (equivalent to the vice-provincial level) and commander of the mariner. Zeng Guofan served as the capital of the Liangjiang River, and appointed three of his cronies as the governor of the three provinces to which he belonged, and made Peng Yulin the governor of Anhui. However, he resigned three times in a row. The reason was that he had become accustomed to military camps and neglected civil affairs, and asked the imperial court not to abandon its strengths and weaknesses. The imperial court had no choice but to take back his fate and replace him as military commander, and still stay on the front line to supervise and bring sailors. Only then did he accept it.
The second time was in February of Tongzhi 4, when the imperial court appointed Peng Yulin as acting Governor of Caoyun. Shipping is a water transportation business that transports food goods to Beijing. Governor Caoyun is in charge of Caozheng in the eight provinces of Lu, Yu, Jiangsu, Anhui, Zhejiang, Gan, Xiang, and E. It is coveted by everyone. How many people dream of it and can't do it. However, Peng Yulin treated it as bad and declined it twice. The reason was that, in addition to not understanding the administration of Cao, he also had an anxious temperament and foolish attitude, and would not get along well with all parties. Claiming that “the Emperor came with the Han Shi; may the Han Shi return.” Qing Ting was very strange about Peng Yulin's stubborn refusal to resign as an official. At the time, people in the official arena were extremely ununderstanding. Some even made suggestions to Qing Ting to punish him because he was not ordered and almost pretentious. Tsang Kwok-fan heard the news and came forward to speak out for him. He said, “Cha Peng Yulin joined the chancellor camp at the beginning of three years. Since then, he has kept his promise and is unwilling to become an official. Since then, he has repeatedly defended and made no excuses. Every time an official is removed, he has insisted on opening a vacancy. In the 11th year of Xianfeng, he was appointed as the governor of Anhui. He abstained three times. Chen also made a statement on his behalf once and invited for approval. This time, he personally took the favor and acted as Governor Caoyun. When Lang heard his fate, he was terrified and went out of his way. After coming to Jinling, he described the depth of his illness, and then asked for a vacancy. They stayed with each other for a long time, knowing that they are brave in cause, less than prosperous, unwilling to do anything to stop them.” Qing Ting read Zeng Guofan's back-and-forth before settling his doubts and granting Peng Yulin's request.
The third time was in June of the 7th year of Tongzhi. Peng Yulin asked to resign as military servant for 67 years. The reason is that when he joined the military, his three-year mother died for only one year. Now that the country is stable, he should have been disarmed and returned to his fields to make up for the remaining two years. This time, the imperial court saw that he was earnest and sincere, and agreed to his resignation.
Fourth, after leaving office for three or four years, the imperial court also appointed him acting military servant and suppressing minister at Emperor Tongzhi's grand wedding ceremony. As soon as the ceremony was over, he immediately asked to resign as acting military commander. After the imperial court accepted it, it was handed over to him as an agent to inspect the Yangtze River sailors once a year.
The fifth time, Peng Yulin was appointed by the Imperial Court as acting Governor of Liangjiang and Minister of Commerce of Nanyang in July of the 7th year of Guangxu. The two rivers cover a vast area, and are also rich in products. The Nanyang Minister of Commerce and Commerce is even more powerful and responsible. He has never been a famous lieutenant and cannot be appointed. Brother Zeng Guofan, senior minister of the DPRK and China, Li Hongzhang, Liu Kun, and others have all held this position. Letting 66-year-old Peng Yulin become Liangjiang Governor shows that the imperial court relied heavily on him, but he simply did not accept this sentiment. After taking the order, he immediately declined to resign, and then sacked again the next day. The imperial court was helpless and had to hand this over to Zuo Zongtang.
The sixth time was in the 8th year of Guangxu, when the imperial court appointed Peng Yulin as military secretary (equivalent to Minister of National Defense). As in the past, he resigned immediately after receiving the order, and the imperial court did not approve it. Soon, the Sino-French War broke out, and the imperial court ordered him to lead old generals and recruit new troops to quickly go to Guangdong to deploy coastal defense. He believed that his mission was in danger at this point, and that it was not appropriate to quit. He went to Guangdong with the intention of leading his department to the front line in the South China Sea, refurbish the Humen Fortress, strengthen coastal integrity, and defend Qinzhou and Lingshan. The main battle was postponed many times, and strict preparations were made after the war to prevent future problems. After the successful end of the Sino-French War, Guangxu ordered his resignation as military secretary in March 11, but the imperial court did not accept it. He also requested his resignation four times in a row in August of this year, August of the second year, July of the third year, and June of the fourth year. Given his persistence, the imperial court had no choice but to accept it.
In addition to “don't die” and “don't want officials,” Peng Yulin also has a major virtue, namely “don't want money.” In the winter of the fourth year of Xianfeng (1854), after Peng Yulin led the Sailors of the Xiang Army and cooperated with the Lu Division to capture Tianjia Town, a key location of the Taiping Army, Qing Ting awarded him 4,000 silver, but he instead used it to save his hometown. In a letter to his uncle, he said, “If I want the suffering people and relatives in my hometown, it is just convenient and fun to use this money.” He also asked his uncle to take out some money and two to set up a school in his hometown, hoping to “create a few talents” for his hometown. He is demanding of himself and his family, but he is very strict. When he learned that his son spent 2,000 copper coins to repair his family's old house, he wrote a letter and harshly reprimanded him: “I am deeply shocked by Ho Eho Philos.” He said that he has always regarded “building a house and buying a field as a bad habit, and he has vowed not to do it. Unexpectedly, you didn't write to me to inform you that civil engineering was booming; after starting civil engineering, you were unexpectedly extravagant. Outsiders don't know; they say I'm abnormal; if I can't put it into practice, then I'll see people like me!” In fact, his son's old renovated house was nothing more than three earth-walled and tiled houses.
Peng Yulin is very self-disciplined, but he is very generous to others. His own money is often used for Zhou Ji's poor family and friends, gifts to Triumph's subordinates, and also actively sponsors public welfare undertakings. Currently, Chuanshan Academy in Hengyang City was donated by him. Before her death, Peng Yulin donated all of the official's income from decades of government salaries, maintenance, expenses, etc. added up to millions and two to pay for military expenses.
There are two great paintings and calligraphy in the Qing Dynasty. One is Zheng Banqiao's “Ink Bamboo”, and the other is Peng Yulin's “Plum Blossom.” Peng Yulin is good at painting works, especially painting plum blossoms. The plum blossoms are bold, with branches like dragons, and the flowers are splashed with blood. They are known as “Soldier Plum Blossoms,” and many people thought it was a masterpiece at the time. His love for painting Mei comes from his long-standing “Love for Mei Gu”, which he portrayed with his rare obsessive tenderness: when she was a child, Peng Yulin lived at her grandmother's house in Anqing, Anhui, and loved to play with her grandmother's adopted daughter, Mei Gu. Although Mei Gu is only slightly older than Peng Yulin, from a generational point of view, she is Peng Yulin's aunt. The two are infatuated with each other. They are happy and have a lifelong private life. However, their relationship was opposed by their parents because the eight characters didn't match. Later, Peng Yulin moved back to Hengyang with the whole family. He and Mei Gu had to endure the painful separation. However, after 14 years of separation, Peng Yulin had already married his wife and started a family.
When Peng Yulin was 30 years old, he heard that his uncle had died in Anqing and that his grandmother and Grandma had no support, so he quickly sent his younger brother to Anqing and brought his grandmother and Mei Gu to live in Hengyang. However, it wasn't long before Mei Gu arrived at Peng's family. Peng Yulin's wife hated the relationship between Peng Yulin and Mei Gu, and instigated Peng Yulin's mother to marry Mei Gu. Peng Yulin thought about stopping this incident, but because the decision was too late, he missed the time for a final recovery. After four years of marriage, Mei Gu died of a difficult delivery. Pang Yu-lin was so sad that she swore in front of Mei Gu's tomb that she would spend her whole life painting plum blossoms to commemorate her with 10,000 plum blossoms. Peng Yulin later wrote in her poem, “Most of the early days were misguided, and all regrets were delayed because of decisions.”
Peng Yulin said he did it. It took him a full 40 years to complete his promise to commemorate Mei Gu with 10,000 plum blossoms, and he never married after losing his wife. He painted plum blossoms: dry like iron, branches like steel, flowers like tears. During his resignation and seclusion, he built a hall next to the mariner's Zhaozhong Temple in Hukou and planted plum blossoms all over the place, known as “Plum Blossom Dock,” a famous tourist attraction in Shizhongshan today; second, he built a “Huishengan” in his hometown and painted poems in the hermitage. The number of plum paintings reached over 10,000, and each painting had to be self-titled. None of them were the same, and the meaning must be trusted. Every picture must be stamped with a chapter saying, “A sad person should have no arms; a lifelong confidant is a plum blossom”. It can be seen that Peng Yulin's love for Mei Gu is so unforgettable. It can also be seen that he is an extremely infatuated person and left an independent world.
In March of the 16th year of Guangxu (1890), Peng Yulin died of illness as a civilian at Huisheng Temple in Hengyang City. He died at the age of 75. After his death, he was honored as Prince Taibao, a special temple was built to commemorate his death, and given the honorary title Gangzheng. He is the author of “Peng Gang Zhigong's Manuscripts” and “Peng Gang Zhigong's Poems”. Zhang Zhidong, the former Governor of Liangguang and Governor of Huguang, described it as “Don't worship the government, ride a donkey on the lake for a long time; follow the command and swear to be a crocodile in the sea” and wrote a five-word long poem for him: “Shenzhou runs through the Yangtze River, and its southern border rises to the sea. Jiang Hai is blessed, and the mainstay is safe today...” Yu Yue, a contemporary of Peng Yulin and a famous scholar who worked as an editor at the Hanlin Academy, called him “the only person who has always conquered the hearts of the people since Xian Feng and Tongzhi. He is the only person who has never had any sense of regret”. The high evaluation shows the importance of Peng Yulin's fame. Chen Baozhen, who was Hubei's administrative envoy at the time, wrote in mourning Peng Yulin: “Don't want money, don't be an official, don't live. It's a powerful phrase in life.” (Liu Jixing)
