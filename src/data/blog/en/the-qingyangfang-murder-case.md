 One summer in the early years of the Republic of China, a family of three moved into the old residence of the Zhu family in Qingyang Square. The owner of the house is an old man in his sixties. His wife is still very young, about 30 years old. She is small and beautiful, and has a very attractive appearance, yet their son is less than one year old. He stayed in the ocean when he was young, then opened a large factory in Shangyi. He used to have a rich family. Recently, the factory went bankrupt and went out of business, so I brought my young wife and young son back to their hometown, Qingyang Square, Shimen County, to repair their original ancestral home and settle down. However, within a few days, Detective Zhong Xinli received a report saying that Mr. Zhu Guoli had been killed in his home. Zhong Xin immediately brought his assistant Qi Pumin to Qingyang Square. Mrs. Zhu pointed to a closed door and cried, “Mr. Police, my husband was killed in the heater!”
Zhong Xinli pushed the door, but the door couldn't be pushed open. Mrs. Zhu saw it and hurriedly said, “The door is locked!”
Zhong Xinli asked, “The door is locked, how do you know Mr. Zhu has been killed?”
Mrs. Zhu said, “About 20 minutes ago, my husband said he wanted to get aggressive with the heater. Shortly after he entered the house, I heard a gunshot from inside the house, and I knew there was an accident. He asked while pushing the door, but the door was locked from inside. I looked through the lock hole and saw my husband reclining on a wicker chair. The blood from his head was still flowing down. I was so frightened that I called the police in a hurry.”
Zhong Xinli listened to it, then bent down and looked inside from her lock hole. Sure enough, she saw Chocolate reclining on a wicker chair, her head slumped to the side, and blood dripped to her face.
Zhong Xinli and Qi Pumin had no choice but to pry open the door and enter the house. The chocolate had already run out of breath. The furnishings in the house are extremely rudimentary. Other than two wicker chairs and a table, there is only one stove for heating in winter. The furnace is coal-fired, made of tin, and a pipe on it extends to the roof. Since it's summer, not the time to burn fires, the stove doesn't start a fire, but the furnace door that enters the coal is open, and the chocolate dies on the rattan chair in front of the furnace door. Apparently, he died while sitting in a wicker chair when he was shot at his temple.
Who is the murderer? Why kill an old man who has gone bankrupt?
After taking a close look at the scene, Zhong Xinli was even more confused. It turns out that the doors and windows of the heated room are all plugged in from the inside. If the murderer kills someone and then goes out of the door or window, the door and window cannot be inserted inside. In other words, if he kills a person, then how did the murderer leave the house after killing the person? If the murderer were to shoot inside from outside the house, the distance was wrong, because as can be seen from the victim's wounds, the muzzle and head were next to each other. If it was a suicide, then where did the gun that Chocolate used to kill himself go?
Zhong Xinli fell into confusion.
Three days later, Zhong Xinli suddenly received an official letter from an insurance company in Shanghai. It turns out that chocolate was previously insured by an insurance company in Shangyi
Huge life insurance. In other words, once he dies unexpectedly, his heirs can receive a huge amount of compensation. In the letter, the insurance company requested the Shimen County Police Department to investigate the cause of death of Chocolate within 10 days and issue a death certificate. If the chocolate was homicidal or died from other normal causes, the insurance company will have to pay compensation. However, in the case of suicide, the insurance company can refuse to pay this huge sum of money.
Zhong Xinli was holding an official letter from the insurance company and meditating. At this point, his superior, the Chief of Police, invited him to the office. The chief saw Zhong Xinli and kindly asked him to sit down. After saying three words, it came to the Chocolate case. The director told Chung Xinli that Mrs. Chu visited him several times at the police station and asked for the case to be resolved as soon as possible.
Zhong Xinli understood that in a case like this, if the murderer cannot be found, it can generally only be suspended as a pending case, and the insurance company only needs to have a copy of the outstanding case certificate, and this huge amount of compensation will also have to be paid. Since the family of the deceased and the director urged, and the circumstances of the case had no clue, they had no choice but to settle the case.
However, before the case was settled, Zhong Xinli decided to check out the chocolate heater again. He took a close look at the four walls of the heated room. He really couldn't see anything strange, so he sat on a wicker chair, lit a cigarette, and thought about preparing to write a closing report.
At this point, the director and Qi Pumin also arrived. Mrs. Zhu followed them both to get the heater. At first sight, the director looked laid-back and relaxed. “Detective Chung, don't empty your brain. If you can't find the murderer, just treat it as a pending case. Write out the proof first and hand it over to Mrs. Chu!”
Zhong Xin saw the director arrive. He was busy standing up. He was about to answer. He saw that a spider had fallen from a beam. The spider had a thread hanging in front of him. When it was about to fall to the ground, the spider suddenly swayed and climbed up like a thin wire. In a blink of an eye, it returned to the beam.
Zhong Xin immediately looked at the spider, looked down at the open furnace door, and suddenly understood: “I understand...”
Qi Pumin asked, “Detective, what do you understand?”
“Mr Zhu committed suicide!” As soon as Zhong Xinli made this remark, everyone in the house was taken aback. Mrs. Zhu immediately cried “proud”: “Old man, you died wrongly. You were killed. The police couldn't catch the murderer, and they also said you committed suicide...”
The director was also unhappy and said, “Detective Chung, you think Mr. Zhu committed suicide, so where is the gun he used to commit suicide?”
Zhong Xinli said, “If I had a good guess, the suicide gun must have been in the exhaust pipe of the heater!” While talking, he picked up the iron bar that leaned on the side, stuck it in the exhaust pipe of the stove, and smashed it gently. After a while, the iron bar seemed to have caught something. Zhong Xinli slowly pulled it down. Sure enough, he was hooked to a small Browning pistol, and a fine rubber band was attached to the handle of the gun.
Seeing the handgun, Mrs. Zhu suddenly stopped crying, and her face changed.
It turns out that although Chocolate has a well-off family, he is almost sixty years old and has no heirs. After the death of his original wife, he married a young and beautiful wife. A year later, the young wife actually gave birth to him a son. However, soon after his son was born, the chocolate business went bankrupt, and he had to bring his wife and son back to his hometown of Shimen County. Taitai Zhu only married this old man because she loved chocolate's million-dollar fortune. Now that the old man has gone bankrupt, how can she still live this kind of hard life! Later, she learned that Chocolate had taken out a huge amount of life insurance with an insurance company. If Chocolate dies, she can get an insurance benefit as an heir. As a result, she threatened Chocolate several times, saying that she would not be able to live this day anymore and that she would run away with her son. Chocolate always had good luck; how could she let her take her son away? However, when the company went bankrupt, he was really disheartened. In the end, he was forced to feel helpless, and the idea of death developed. Because of her death, my lovely wife, Aiko, will be able to get that huge amount of insurance compensation. However, according to the relevant regulations of insurance companies, if the policyholder commits suicide, the insurance company is not responsible for compensation. As a result, he and Mrs. Zhu planned a few times, emptied their minds, and finally came up with a “trick”: leave a pending case for the police. After getting started with the heater, he first locked the door and window, then attached a rubber band to the exhaust pipe of the heater, pulled one end of the rubber band out of the stove and attached it to the handle of a small Browning pistol. Then, when he died, Chocolate sat on a wicker chair and hit his temple with the gun. When he died, his hand loosened. As soon as the rubber band contracted, he pulled the small pistol back into the exhaust pipe.
The truth is clear. Residents of Shimen County are accusing Mrs. Zhu of being too toxic. Soon, Mrs. Zhu sold the Zhu family's old house and ran away with her toddler. Since then, her whereabouts are unknown.
