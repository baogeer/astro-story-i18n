 At the end of the Ming Dynasty, there were many bandits in various parts of Jinan Province. Every county set up an army, and when they caught the robbers, they killed them. There are especially many bandits in Zhangqiu County. There is a soldier in this county's military. The knife he wears is extremely sharp, so it doesn't take much effort to kill people. One day, the government army captured more than a dozen bandits and took them all to the court to be beheaded. One of the bandits recognized the soldier and hesitantly rushed over and said, “I heard that your knife is the fastest, so you don't have to cut your head a second time. Please kill me!” The soldier said, “OK. Stay with me and don't leave me.” The robber followed the soldier to the execution site. The soldier cut it off. The bandit's head bone fell off with a bang and rolled a few steps away. While spinning on the ground, he also claimed and praised, “What a fast knife!”
