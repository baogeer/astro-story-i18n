 In spring, male goat Jep Reguisangjie and ram Tuoguo Yun Dan Xirou go to Salt Lake to drink salt water and eat green grass. Throughout the winter, heavy snow continued to fall, and the northerly wind kept blowing. They hid in an earthen cave. They were cold and hungry, and they all lost weight.
On the way to Salt Lake, you have to go over a very high snowy mountain. They crawled wobbly with their eight hooves, just like drunk people because they were too weak. It was so easy to climb to a snowy mountain pass. I didn't expect a big bad wolf crouching on a big rock. The wolf showed its snow-white teeth, stuck out its blood-red, blood-red tongue, and said, “Welcome! I'm still waiting for you to have breakfast!” The male goat Jeep Rejisangkie was so frightened that he quickly climbed over. The male goat Togo Yun Dan Xirou greeted him with a smile and said, “Look, look, we are both so thin. Apart from our hair, we have bones. If we eat it, it probably won't stab you in the throat or break your belly; if you eat it, it probably won't stab you in the throat or cut your belly; if you eat it until autumn, when the fat seeps out of the croissants, it tastes good. That's it.” The Big Bad Wolf thought about it and felt that there was some truth in its words, so he agreed to let them pass, but he had to write documents and make an oath to settle. The ram was in the snow. He wrote a letter of guarantee with his hooves, and also signed his name with a pair of short horns.
Male goats and rams are on the shores of Salt Lake. After a full summer, drinking salt water and eating green grass, their bodies are getting stronger day by day. When I went back to my hometown, I still had to go through that snowy mountain because there were no other roads. As soon as they got to the bottom of the mountain, they saw the Big Bad Wolf walking around the snowy mountain pass, waiting impatiently. There is no way for male goats and rams. Let's go find the most knowledgeable rabbit, Luo Zhu praised it.
Luo Zhujian praised the goat and sheep's request and shook his ears three times and said, “This big bad wolf on the snowy mountain ate a lot of rabbits. I'm thinking of ideas to deal with this devil! Now that you're here, you've brought my little bunny's method too.”
“What's the way?” the goat and the sheep asked dumbfounded.
“Uncle Goat, you have a nice beard and are very powerful, you should be my horse; Master Sheep, you are very handsome. Like a gentleman, just be my messenger!” The rabbit jumped to their ears and grunted for a while. The three picked up some red, yellow, and green leaves and dressed themselves up a bit. Soon, the rabbit rode on a male goat, led the way by a ram, and walked majestically towards the snowy mountain. As the sheep walked, they shouted, “Our king wants to take 100 sheets of tiger skin, 100 sheets of leopard skin, and 100 sheets of wolf skin! Now it's just one wolf skin left! “Grey Wolf, don't run, let me peel your skin off.”
As soon as Big Bad Wolf heard it, he got scared and ran away. Rabbits, goats, and sheep chase as they roar. The wolf ran too fast. He fell off a very high rock, was seriously injured, and took refuge in a cave. None of the three partners had any way to kill the wolf, so they brought in lots of stones and blocked the hole. The rabbit said, “Uncle Goat, Uncle Sheep, stay here and watch! After three days and three nights, this devil will die.”
Goats and sheep had no choice but to take turns guarding the entrance of the cave to prevent the Big Bad Wolf from escaping. At the beginning, it was a male goat on guard. It heard the big bad wolf howling in the cave and shivered in horror. The wolf asked, “What's the noise outside?” The male goat was afraid to answer. The Big Bad Wolf asked three times, and the male goat had no choice but to say, “I'm afraid; I hit my knee a lot!” The Big Bad Wolf knew that male goats were timid, so he pushed a stone with his head and wanted to rush out.
At this point, the sheep came to set their guard. It used its horn and constantly rubbed the stone. The Big Bad Wolf asked, “What's the noise outside?” The sheep said, “I'm sharpening your knife.” After a while, it puffed up a bubble of urine. The wolf asked, “What's the noise outside?” The sheep said, “I'm pouring water to boil you.” Finally, the sheep pooped, and the wolf asked, “What's the noise outside?” It answered loudly, “Big Bad Wolf! Big bad wolf! I'm burning your cow dung!”
After listening, Big Bad Wolf never dared to run out; he starved to death in the cave.
