1. Female heroes and famous generals against the Qing dynasty
1. Strange ambition since childhood, wentao and martial arts.
Qin Liangyu, a female heroine in the late Ming Dynasty, Zi Zhensu, was born in the 2nd year of Daming Wanli (1574) on the banks of Mingyu Creek on the outskirts of Xilutian Town in Zhongzhou, Sichuan (now Zhongxian County, Chongqing). Surrounded by mountains and water, the terrain is majestic, and the scenery is beautiful. It has been a place where the Qin family has lived for generations. Her father, Qin Kui, read a lot of poetry and is very knowledgeable. He is a famous person. He is a patriotic Si Gong Sheng. “Ding is troubled, happy to talk about soldiers”. It trains children from an early age to learn Wen Tao martial arts, and encourages them to grow up: defending society.” Liangyu has three siblings. Liangyu is in third place, with older brother Bang Ping, Bonhan, and younger brother Min Ping. Her father is particularly fond of her. He believes that even though she is a girl, she should practice military self-defense so as not to be “an outcast” in the midst of war and war. As a result, in addition to studying chapters, verses, and history since childhood, Qin Liangyu was also able to practice martial arts with her brother with her father. Not only did she learn the excellent martial art of riding a sword, but she is also familiar with military history, is good at strategy, and has shown extraordinary military talent. The father said with emotion, “If you don't listen to it, your brothers won't be able to do it either.” Qin Liangyu doesn't feel inferior because she is a daughter's family. She has few ambitions and often uses the achievements of famous patriotic generals and national heroes in history to inspire herself. She magnificently stated, “Letting children take charge of the military, Lady City and the Women's Army are not enough.”
Later, Qin Liangyu and Ma Qiancheng became husband and wife with Shi Zhu Xuanping. After General Fu Bo, a famous general in the Eastern Han Dynasty, Senjo was originally from Fufeng, Shaanxi. Because of his ancestral achievements, he was crowned Shi Zhu Xuanfu, and his official position has been passed down from generation to generation. Senjo is handsome and Yan Yi, “I can't help but shudder when the whole army is here.” He is very fond of and respects Qin Liangyu. His husband and wife treated each other like guests, and they also discussed matters relating to military use in border control. Shi Zhu is located in a remote area inhabited by ethnic minorities. However, Qin Liangyu proposed to her husband, “Should a man seek thousands of miles of luck, and Xi should sit back?” Based on her lofty vision of serving the country, she helped her husband to take the lead. This stone pillar soldier uses white wood to make a unique kind of spear “with a hook at the end of the spear and a ring at the end of the spear”. It is mobile and flexible, brave and good at fighting. According to history, Qin Liangyu “was under severe control. Every time an army was ordered, the army was in awe. The department name 'White Guan' is afraid of near and far”.
2. Go on a trip with your husband and start to shine.
In the 27th year of Daming Wanli (1599), Banzhou (present-day Zunyi) encouraged Yang Yinglong to seize the region, fish and meat villages, and transfer him east to fight against Japan and aid the DPRK. Not only did he refuse to step out, but instead took the opportunity to incite the rebellion. Banshu's terrain is difficult, the mountains are high and the water is dangerous. Relying on natural barriers, the rebels raged for a while. In February of the following year, the Imperial Court assembled a heavy army to encircle the rebels. Ma Qianheng also led 500 elite soldiers. Due to the White Guanmen's special equipment and long-term strict mountain training, they were very handy in the Banzhou War and often dealt unexpected blows to the rebels. No matter how high the mountains were, the White Army was able to surprise, like a divine soldier falling from the sky, making the rebels feel discouraged.
In the end, the rebels mobilized all their forces and stuck in the city of Banzhou. Outside the city, they set up five checkpoints, namely Dengkan, Sangmu, Wujiang, Hedu, and Loushanguan. Each level was defended by elite soldiers. Yang Yinglong wanted to use this as his amulet. The attack on Deng Kan was led by Qin Liangyu and the 500 White Troopers as the main force. When Deng Kanshou saw that Yang Chaodong's military strength was low, he prepared to be swallowed up in one fell swoop, so he pulled all of his 5,000 elite soldiers to their positions and lined up a tight formation. Qin Liangyu was fearless in the face of an enemy force ten times larger than her own. She rode a peach horse, and slashed with dignity. She only saw her slash left and right, and rushed east and west. Enemy soldiers were killed everywhere they passed, like the autumn breeze sweeping away fallen leaves. The enemy flocked to her like the tide and surrounded her layer by layer. Unexpectedly, she became more brave and wielded her spear like a flying dance wheel, making her invincible. Caught in the enemy line, Qin Liangyu remained undisturbed. While slashing the enemy soldiers around her, she slowly moved closer to enemy general Yang Chaodong. When she came close, she suddenly jumped forward. She had not yet waited for those around her to see clearly. She had already held Yang Chaodong on the back of her horse, wielded a spear with her right hand, and held back the enemy general firmly with her left hand. When the enemy soldiers saw their leader captured, their positions were immediately disrupted. Qin Liangyu's Baiganmen took advantage of the victory and hunted down. Without a single meal, the enemy soldiers died, were wounded, and escaped. The 5,000 people were all destroyed.
After conquering Dengkan, the anti-bandit army then successfully conquered the three gates of Sangmu, Wujiang, and Hedu, and went straight to Loushanguan on the periphery of Banzhou. Loushan Pass is a natural barrier outside of Banzhou City. The mountains are high and dangerous. Only one path passes through the gate, so it can be described as a place where “everyone can do everything”. The main task of attacking Loushanguan fell on the hands of Bai Jianping. Limited to narrow roads and unable to pass through a large number of soldiers and horses, Qin Liangyu helped her husband come up with an ingenious plan. Early this morning, Qin Liangyu and her husband Ma Qiansheng ran side by side and attacked the gate along the right path. They only saw two spears flying up and down. The enemy soldiers in the way fell one by one, and the reinforcements from behind were unable to rush up. However, when Qin Liangyu and his wife fought side by side, and the enemy army gathered more and more, thousands of Baigan armies suddenly surrounded them from both sides of the gate. The enemy was defenseless and fled in desolation. It turns out that, taking advantage of Qin Liangyu's and his wife's frontal attack, which attracted the enemy's attention, the other White Pole soldiers climbed the upper level from the cliff on both sides of the checkpoint, using white pole spears connected at the beginning and end, and dealt an unexpected blow to the enemy. After conquering Loushanguan, the rebels lost their amulets, and the anti-bandit army worked hard to conquer the rebel city. Yang Yinglong's family burned themselves to death, and the rebellion completely subsided.
When talking about merit, Shi Zhu and the White Pole Soldier performed well in battle and was ranked as the number one meritorious army on South Chuan Road. Qin Liangyu participated in the war for the first time and made great contributions. In addition to being highly praised, the fame of the “female general” was broadcast far and wide. In the war against the rebellion, Qin Liangyu first showed off, “breaking gold and building seven blocks, taking Sangmu Pass, and ranking first in the battle for the Nanchuan Road.”
3. Invite thousands of miles to fight with blood in northern Xinjiang.
In the 41st year of Wanli of Daming (1613), Ma Qiancheng was framed by the eunuch Qiu Chengyun and wrongly died in prison in Yunyang. Because of Qin Liangyu's repeated achievements, the dynasty ordered an attack on the post and led Shi Zhu as an envoy. Since then, Qin Liangyu has removed her dress and easy crown belt. The Maid Guards are all in military uniform. They are fighting from the south to the north, and their power is impressive.
The day Qin Liangyu took control of the military came at a time when the female Zhen tribe rose up in Northeast China, posing a serious threat to the Ming Ting. In the 47th year of Wanli of Daming (1619), the Ming army was shocked by the late Jin army's defeat in Saarhu. The army was shocked. The officers and soldiers in eastern Liaoning “heard the alarm and were all shocked” and “pretended to live and refused to go to war.” The Imperial Court rushed Yongshun, Baojing, Shizhu, Youyang and other Turkish soldiers to Liao for rescue. Qin Liangyu sent troops to the front with his brother Bangping and his brother Minping leading thousands of soldiers to the front line.
In the first year of the Qin Ming Dynasty (1621), the Qing army took over Shenyang, and the momentum was even more aggressive. Qin Liangyu's big brother Min Ping fought a fierce battle with the Qing army in order to recover Daming's losses. Bang Ping died in battle and Min Ping fell under heavy siege; after hearing the news, Qin Liangyu personally led 100 Bai Jianmen to cross the river and rescue his brother's body in desperation. Although the bloody battle of the Hunhe River was defeated due to the loss of the crowd and Bang Ping was unable to win, it killed thousands of enemies under extremely difficult conditions, severely damaging the Qing army, and was hailed by the division as “the first bloody battle from Liao Zuo.”
His brother died and his son was wounded, and Qin Liangyu's grief and anger were mixed. Emperor Tianqi was deeply moved. Emperor Tianqi was deeply moved. He gave Qin Liangyu a second quality official uniform, appointed his son Ma Xianglin as the commander, and chased Qin Bangping as the governor of the capital. He also rewarded General Bai Jianbing.
After the bloody war in the Hunhe River, Qin Liangyu was immediately deported to the capital, rushed to make 1,500 winter clothes for pensioners, and put the rest in order. I personally led 3,000 elite soldiers straight to Yuguan (Shanhaiguan). Yu Guan was the main route the Qing soldiers had to take in Liaoyang and explore the Central Plains. The Qing army repeatedly sent heavy soldiers to break the challenge. Qin Liangyu was not moved; he only ordered his subordinates to reinforce their defense. In the end, the Qing soldiers were unable to succeed. Once, when Qin Liangyu's son Ma Xianglin was carrying troops to patrol the customs, he was hit by an arrow from the enemy. He withstood the pain and pulled out a cluster of arrows, fired three arrows in a row, and shot three enemies. The Qing soldiers were shocked. Since then, they have been afraid to come back easily to Shandong Customs for provocation. Qin Liangyu sat down in Yuguan. On the one hand, he provided relief to the hungry people inside and outside Guan and calmed people's hearts; at the same time, he strengthened his weapons and worked hard to guard, effectively curbing the influence of the Qing army invading the south. Under the auspices of Qin Liangyu, the defense of Yuguan was as strong as gold, and became an insurmountable barrier for the Qing soldiers.
4. Return to Sichuan and rebel, beautiful woman.
Soon, Qin Liangyu was ordered to return to Sichuan to expand his military aid to Liao. It was only one day after arriving in Shizhu. It coincided with Yongning's proclamation to force the Luyi people to rebel against Liao. In the name of Fengzhao leading 20,000 aid to Liao, Za Chongming entered Chongqing. He was stationed for a long time and called himself King Daliang. Instead, they took advantage of false advances to besiege Chengdu, seriously disrupting the anti-Qing situation. After Fan Long, the party leader of luxury, took over Chongqing, I heard that Qin Liangyu brought his troops back to Shizhu and immediately sent someone to contact her with gold and silver gifts; Qin Liangyu was furious; “I was blessed by the DPRK and seriously wanted to serve the country; how could I join the rebels!” They immediately slashed the thieves and quickly sent troops back to Jiangxi to Chongqing. Unexpectedly, they defeated Fan Long's forces and captured Chongqing. Immediately after that, Qin Liangyu went west to the rescue, and sent Min Ping, his nephew Yi Ming, and Gong Ming to go all the way, and went west with 6,000 elite soldiers and drove all the way west to Chengdu to drive away the people who besieged Chengdu. They successively took down several large villages such as Hongya Dun, Guanyin Temple, and Qingshan Dun, and completely destroyed the rebels. It allowed Chengdu to break the siege smoothly and take back Chongqing in one fell swoop. When the siege of Chengdu was lifted, and Qin Liangyu led Bai Jianbing into the city on horseback, the citizens of Chengdu rushed to the streets to help the elderly and bring children together to compete for the style of female generals. At this point, Qin Liangyu was already in her fifties. Her decades-long career not only did not cause her to age, but instead refined her more and more heroic. I saw her riding a peach blossom horse. Her cheeks were red and full, her eyes were bright and bright. She was in an upright position and energetic. She had the style of a general, yet she did not lose the beauty of a mature woman. People in Chengdu simply regarded her as a god, and one after another burned incense and prayed along the way she walked.
Governor Zhu Xieyuan set up a feast for Qin Liangyu and his family. Qin Liangyu had plenty of refreshment. He sat at a table with senior local officials and had a drink. While the wine was in full swing, a police officer sitting in the seat was probably fascinated by Qin Liangyu's red face. He unwittingly extended a hand from under the table and held her corner; Qin Liangyu felt very annoyed, quietly pulled out her sword and violently cut off the corner of her clothing being dragged. The people present were shocked, but Qin Liangyu remained silent. He still raised his glass and laughed. Instead, the disgruntled official left his seat in shame.
After the recovery of Chengdu and Chongqing, reports were reported in the morning that they awarded Qin Liangyu as the superintendent of the capital, worshiped Chief Military Officer Shi Yi, and rewarded her for her bloody exploits. She became King of Luo Dian, recruited troops and bought horses, and occupied a land of thousands of miles west of Guiyang. The imperial court also ordered Qin Liangyu to lead the Baigan army and the Qianping Rebellion. Qin Liangyu led his team into Qian. He soon calmed down the rebellion and killed An Bangyan, but also sacrificed the life of his younger brother Qin Minping.

6. Protect the country and protect the people, and stop the peasant army three times.
In the 6th year of Daming Chongzhen (1633), after the four cities of Yongping were recovered, she was transferred back to Sichuan from the Beijing Division, left behind Qin Yiming and stationed in Jingxi, defended the Beijing Division, kept Ma Xianglin, and Zhang Fengyi on guard in Beijing, and ordered Qin Liangyu to return home to “specialize in Shu thieves.” They fought successively against peasant forces such as Luo Rucai and Zhang Xianzhong.
In the 7th year of Daming Chongzhen (1634), Zhang Xianzhong Ke Meizhou was the gateway to Sichuan. Since ancient times, it has been a major military town. In the 13th year of Daming Chongzhen (1640), Luo Rucai settled in Huguang to advance into Sichuan. In May, transfer to Mount Wushan, enter Qu Gorge, and go straight to Quzhou. Qin Liangyu went to interception again and fought fiercely with Luo Rucai in Majiazhai, Rumayu, and Xiansiling. Defy General Luo Rucai from Wushan and Shuai Dongshan Tiger from Tan Jiaping.
In September of the same year, Zhang Xianzhong and Luo Rucai joined forces with their second brother into the river to shoot Zhang Ling, known as the “archer,” in Zhujiuping (northern part of the city of Kuizhou). At the time, Qin Liangyu and Zhang Ling were at odds with each other, but due to “being too late to be saved, they went back in battle and lost, and the 30,000 people in their team were slightly exhausted.” Qin Liangyu had no choice but to ride alone to meet with Sichuan Superintendent Shao Jiechun and said, “It's urgent! 7. The national spirit lasts a long time.
After 1644, Jin entered customs, and the situation changed abruptly. Manchurian serf owners replaced the Daming dynasty, quickly moved west and south, and carried out a series of barbaric and cruel policies of ethnic oppression through massacres. As the ethnic conflict escalated into a major conflict, the rest of the Dasun and Daxi peasant army cooperated closely with the Nanming administration, and instead there was a situation where they united Ming and fought against the Qing dynasty. Qin Liangyu also stopped fighting with the peasant army and rejoined the anti-Qing forces. In August 1646, the Nan Ming Long Wu administration gave Qin Liangyu the title of “Daming Taizi Taibao”, and sent Shi Zhuang to fight against the Qing dynasty. Qin Liangyu resolutely accepted the “Prince Taibao General Town Security” bronze seal at the age of 70, and went on the expedition. However, due to Zheng Zhilong's rebellion, the Nan Ming Long Wu regime immediately fell and was unable to take effect. After the Duanyang Festival in the 5th year of Qing Shunzhi, 75-year-old Qin Liangyu had just stepped down on the Peach Blossom Horse after reviewing Bai Jianbing once. Her body suddenly became crooked and left the world, ending her extravagant career of fighting against horses and running through the frontier. Although Qin Liangyu did not directly confront the Qing soldiers in his later years, he always insisted on fighting against the Qing dynasty and carried out reclamation in the Shizhu area to protect the country and the people. At that time, there were many wars in the Sichuan region, leaving thousands of miles empty, desolate and ruined. Hundreds of thousands of people took refuge in nearby states and counties. By the time of Qin Liangyu's death in 1648, there was still a large amount of grain in Wanshou Mountain, 50 miles southeast of the city.
