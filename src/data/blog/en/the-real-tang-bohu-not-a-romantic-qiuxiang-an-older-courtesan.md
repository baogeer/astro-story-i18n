April 4 - During the Ching Ming Festival, the reporter braved the rain to visit Tang Yin Cemetery in Suzhou, which has been reopened to the public, to worship the well-known “Jiangnan's Most Merry Man.”
The newly renovated Tang Yin Garden covers an area of tens of acres. The quiet environment reveals a bit of elegance, silence and elegance. The Taohuaxian Hall is an architectural style from the Ming Dynasty. Tang Bohu lived in Taohuadu in Suzhou in his later years, and also wrote the song “Taohua-an Song”. This museum took its mood and also had a “Peach Blossom Master”; the name Mengmo Tang comes from Tang Bohu once built a Dream Mo Pavilion in Taohua-an; Liurutang was named “Liu Ruju Shi” in Tang Bohu's middle age.
From the “Peach Blossom Fairy Hall” through the “Dream Museum” to the “Liurutang,” the reporter didn't meet a single person. There was only an isolated statue of Tang Bohu, wearing a scarf, holding a brush in his hand, and had an indifferent eye, showing mockery on his lips, showing how unruly Tang Bohu was in his life, laughing, and proud. Accompanied by the exciting sound of a guzheng, this former famous Gangnam's biggest talent is really lonely!
When I arrived at Tang Yin's tomb, a talented person lay dormant for a long time under a high ground seal. Amidst the pouring rain, only an old man guarding the tomb obscured Tang Yin in obscurity. He told the reporter that there are few people who go to Tang Yin's cemetery to burn incense on weekdays. However, it is slightly more popular during the Qingming Festival, but most people who visit to worship are elderly people, and there are almost no signs of young people. They probably don't know that Tang Yin, Xu Zhenqing, Wen Zhiming, and Zhu Yunming are called the “Four Talents of Jiangnan,” but only saw the famous teenager Tang Bohu in TV dramas.
In fact, the real Tang Bohu is not a good-natured person. He went to the Nanjing township test at the age of 29 and got Xie Yuan in first place. He was imprisoned and later reprimanded. He lived a rough and poor life. He made a living by selling paintings, and was good at painting landscapes, flowers, birds, and women. However, Qiu Xiang did have someone. She was a famous Qinglou woman at the time. She was ten years older than Tang Bohu. Although “Tang Bohu Dives Autumn Fragrance” is a folklore, it has been passed down through the ages and endures for a long time. People would rather accept the fictional artistic image of Tang Bohu than accept the real Tang Bohu, who is only talented and unmerciful.
In AD 1523, Tang Bohu, who was only 54 years old, died. Due to his poor family situation, he was buried in his former residence in Taohuawu, then moved to the current site in Jiajing in '22. A generation of talented people. Lonely after death, Tang Yin's tomb grew more and more deserted for more than 400 years. Until the 50s of the last century, ponds and stone houses were built in the graveyard. In 1985, the old building was restored, and the Peach Blossom Fairy Hall, Dream Museum, and Rikujudo were added. The following year, a display room commemorating Tang Yin was opened at the south of the tomb, which was opened to the public under the name “Tang Yin-yuan” along with the tomb to show remembrance and encouragement for future generations.
In 1993, an “Autumn Garden” covering an area of 15,000 square meters was built on the west side of Tang Yinyuan. Tang Bohu, who had been snubbed for a long time, finally had Qiu Xiang's company, and “broke the mirror and came back to life” 500 years later. Some people commented that the construction of the Autumn Flower Garden gave lonely talents a little more warmth and care.
Cemeteries reflect the cultural landscape of a country or region's history, culture, and art from one side. Suzhou has always been a place of personal excellence. Not only are there many tombs of famous people, but they are also well-maintained. These tombs of famous people scattered all over the place are a valuable cultural tourism resource, and a very rare place of education for young people. However, the reporter discovered that while the tombs of celebrities are properly protected, they have not been fully developed and used. The number of people who visited the tombs of famous people to worship during the Qingming period only accounted for a very small proportion of the million tomb sweepers in Suzhou, which is unfortunate.
