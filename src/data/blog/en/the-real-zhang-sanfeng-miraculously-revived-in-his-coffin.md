Zhang Sanfeng, a sloppy Taoist who came out of Liaoning
There is an amazing sloppy man in Jin Yong's novel, Zhang Sanfeng. In the novel, Zhang Sanfeng's martial arts predominate. He is otherworldly, and the dragon never ends when he sees it. He founded the Wu-Tang school and invented tai chi to make Zhou Zhiruo immediately repent by death. However, he came back to life, leading a group of young people traveling all over the world. Since then, their whereabouts have not been known.
Jin Yong wrote novels, but Zhang Sanfeng was a real person. Historically, many records have been preserved about Zhang Sanfeng. There are many magical legends about him around Zhongnan Mountain and Wudang Mountain. However, while more and more people know about Zhang Sanfeng, more and more people have forgotten where he was born. Some people even know it and pretend they don't know. What they don't want to accept is, how could such a big figure spanning the past and present be born in an alien place where “people are neither outstanding nor effective”?
Divine at birth
In present-day Tayingzi Village, Fuxin Mongolian Autonomous County, near National Highway 101, there is a “Zhang Sanfeng Xianju” covering an area of more than 100 acres. This antique building consists of a large hall, a side hall, a bell tower, and a drum tower. Inside the temple, the bronze statue of Zhang Sanfeng stands tall. It is awe-inspiring.
Tayingzi Village is the location of ancient Yizhou, and there are still ruins of a 100-meter-long city wall. Yizhou was a major town in the north. During the Liao Dynasty, Empress Dowager Xiao married her three granddaughters.
Zhang Sanfeng was born here.
However, the birthplace of all famous people is called their former residence, so why is Zhang Sanfeng's birthplace called Xianju?
This is because in the eyes of people in his hometown, Zhang Sanfeng is not a mortal; he is a god; he is an immortal.
According to historical records, Zhang Sanfeng's grandfather claimed to be a descendant of Zhang Ling, one of the founders of Taoism. If this statement is true, then Zhang Mitutong can be said to have great roots, because Zhang Ling's ancestors helped Liu Bang take over the world, Han Liu Hou Zhang Liang. Zhang Ling is originally from Longhushan, Jiangxi. During the time of Emperor Shun of the Eastern Han Dynasty, Zhang Ling founded the “Five Dou Rice Road”, known as Zhang Tianshi. After Zhang Ling's death, his grandson Zhang Lu spent more than 30 years spreading the “Five Dou Rice Road.” In the 20th year of Jian'an (AD 215), Cao Cao attacked Hanzhong, Zhang Lu avoided Bazhong, and soon surrendered to Cao Cao, general Zhennan, and conquered the Central Marquess of Langfang. The “Five Dou Rice Road” has gained legal status and has been publicly disseminated, which has had a great influence on Japan.
Zhang Sanfeng's grandfather moved to Liaoning with his family at the end of the Song dynasty. His father joined the ranks during the Yuan Taizong era and later returned to Yinyizhou. Zhang Sanfeng was born at this time.
Zhang Sanfeng's mother's name is Lin. She gave birth to four sons in her early years, but unfortunately they all died early. At the age of 40, Mother Lin gave birth to two more sons. The youngest son was Zhang Sanfeng.
There are many legends about Zhang Sanfeng's birth; the most amazing thing is this statement. Speaking of Jiuzi at the beginning of April 1247, Lin, who was close to being born, suddenly woke up in her sleep. She quickly woke up her husband Zhang Ju-ren. I said it was a dream where a fairy crane flew from the East China Sea and fell on the roof of our house. Zhang Ju-ren was overjoyed and said, “This is a good sign. Is our son about to be born?” As soon as the speech came to an end, Lin suddenly felt a sharp pain in his stomach. Zhang Ju-ren hurriedly called for her midwife. After about a meal, a huge boy was born. As the boy cried loudly, a crane suddenly sounded in the yard. Zhang Juren ran out of the house and saw a fairy crane flying from the roof of the house and circling the yard three times. It made a loud noise, soared through the sky, and disappeared into the vast sky. Zhang Ju-ren picked up her newborn child and saw that she had big droopy ears, round eyes, bright, and a strange person, so she named her son “Full One” and hoped that his son would become a perfect person in the future. Lin felt that “all” was being obnoxious, so she gave her son another nickname, Jun Bao. In “The Legend of Yitian to Kill the Dragon,” when the young Zhang Sanfeng first entered the river, his name was Zhang Junbao.

Zhang Sanfeng has been brilliant since childhood. At age three, he was able to read the Four Books and Five Sutras, and he never forgot. When I was four, I had a hundred Tang poems backwards. When Zhang Ju-ren saw that this son was capable of success, he invited famous teachers and dedicated himself as a professor. He hoped that he would study Confucianism well, gain fame, and glorify his ancestors with Guangzong.
Unexpectedly, Zhang Sanfeng suddenly developed an eye disease when he was five years old. Despite seeking medical treatment from many sources at home, there was no effect. As a result, their eyes “fainted for a long time,” and they were almost unable to see anything. The parents were extremely anxious. They heard that there was a “stranger” called Zhang Yunan from Biluo Palace not far away. They brought Zhang Sanfeng to Biluoguan and asked Zhang Taoshi to treat his eye problems. Zhang Daoshi claims that in addition to taking medication, his patients must also practice Daoism, so they can do more with less. Just like that, while treating his illness, Zhang Sanfeng began practicing Daoism.
Half a year later, Zhang Sanfeng's vision gradually became clear, and he “learned through reading” the Taoist Sutra, and “read it as you like, and you'll get through with it.” When Zhang Yunan saw that Zhang Mitutong was so smart and thought he was an immortal talent, she took him as an apprentice. Since then, Zhang Sanfeng has been studying Daoism and Confucianism. As a teenager, he laid a deep foundation in Taoism, Buddhism, and Confucian doctrines.
After studying at Biluogong for seven years, Zhang Sanfeng returned to his home in Yizhou. Only by studying books and painstakingly studying Taoist classics — Laozi's “Tao Te Ching” and Zhang Ling's “Laozi Wants to Note” did they learn even better.
Mitsumine Tadashi is famous as Mitutoyo
In the first year of Central Unification (1260), the Yuan dynasty implemented a policy of “showing diversity”. Zhang Sanfeng was 13 years old and had outstanding literary talent, and was listed by the local government as a Wizards and reported to the imperial court. In the fall of 1264, Zhang Sanfeng visited Yanjing, just in time for Kublai to issue a decree to solicit talents. As a result, Zhang Sanfeng was appointed to the Zhongshan Boling (present-day Dingxian County, Hebei) Order. While Zhang Sanfeng was in charge of the Zhongshan Boling Order, he learned that there was a local Taoist temple, Ge Hongshan, a famous Taoist. As a result, I often used my free time to visit the Ge Hongshan Temple and practice area to visit and wander around. Explore the true meaning of Taoism with Ge Daoren. Meanwhile, Zhang Sanfeng read medical and Taoist books such as “Jade Han Fang”, “The Legend of the Immortals”, and “The Legend of Incognito” in Ge Hongshan Taoist Temple, and gained a deeper understanding of the true meaning of everything in heaven and earth.
The following year, Zhang Sanfeng died due to his parents' illnesses. As a result, he quit his job and returned to his hometown, personally sent his parents' coffins back to Liaoning Province (present-day Liaoyang City, Liaoning) for burial, and kept the tomb of Dai Xiao for several years in his hometown. Meanwhile, Zhang Sanfeng recited the scriptures every day at home.
One day, a Taoist named Qiu visited with admiration. Qiu Daoren discussed Taoist theory with Zhang Sanfeng, opening up his mind. As a result, the idea of monasticism breaking all ties came into being. As he said in his poem, “It's easy to find a long way to live here; it's hard to live without saying anything in the world. A thought without life is freedom; having nothing in mind is a fairy Buddha”.
After Qiu Daoren left, Zhang Sanfeng decided to set off in disguise, travel the world, and visit famous Taoist teachers. As a result, he handed over the family's field products to his family, ordered them to manage and operate on their behalf, and also swept graves and sacrificed for both parents. Afterwards, he left home wearing silver and clothing, and traveled all over the famous mountains and rivers in the north and south to worship, learn, and practice. When he visited Baoji Jintai, he saw that the mountains were quiet, and that the mountains were green, and that there were Sanjian Mountains, and that the three peaks were beautiful. It was actually a wonderland. He stayed here, so he called himself a Mitutoyo resident.
Snores like thunder when he talks about being in a dead coffin
In 1314, 67-year-old Zhang Sanfeng left Jintaiguan and ended up in Nanshan, where he met Chen Yi, a Taoist master and real fire dragon. In his early years, Chen Yi was promoted to seclusion. He began to live in seclusion at Jiushuyan in Wudang Mountain. Later, he went to Yuntai in Huashan to live in seclusion and became friends with hermits Li Qi and Lu Dongbin. Chen Yi taught Zhang Sanfeng “Infinity Map,” “Innate Map,” and the 64-triangular pattern, and also taught him “The World's First Genesis” sleeping gong, making Zhang Sanfeng Road clear. As a result, she changed her name to Hyun So and named herself Hyun Hyun Zi. After studying, practicing, and studying the Daozong scriptures with Zhang Sanfeng and Chen Yi for four years, he headed south to Wudang Mountain to continue his training.
In the spring of 1324, Zhang Sanfeng waded south to Wudang Mountain, lived in a cottage, and spent nine years painstakingly practicing in Wudang Mountain. The so-called “nine years of nothing and no poetry, silent and ignorant”. Unbeknownst to him, he reached a state of transformation, and his morality began. At this time, Zhang Sanfeng had an amazing appearance. According to historical records, “He had a magnificent posture, turtle-shaped crane bones, big round ears, and must be bowed like a bow.” “With a ruler in hand, the only thing to wear in the cold and heat”. Rumor has it that he ate an amazing diet, “easily run out of food, or eat one meal for a few days, or not eat for months.” His whereabouts are mysterious and unfathomable. Some people say that he “can do thousands of miles a day,” mountaineering is like flying, or lying in the snow in the middle of winter, snoring like thunder.
In 1341, Zhang Sanfeng returned from Wudang Mountain to sweep his parents' tomb in his hometown in Liaoyang. Afterwards, he returned to Yuandadu (present-day Beijing), and also visited the regions of Qin, Shu, Jingchu, and Wu Yue. One day, he “went on a trip with the Sun God,” said to himself that he should die, and died. While the people in the county settled down to prepare their coffins and were being buried, the crowd suddenly heard thunder snoring in the coffin. As a result, he hurriedly opened the coffin and discovered that Zhang Sanfeng was alive again. The locals thought he was a true spiritual practitioner, and they were amazed.
Founding a school of Wudang Taoism
In the early years of Ming Hongwu (1368), Zhang Sanfeng returned to Wudang Mountain. He recruited disciples in Wudang Mountain to promote Quanzhen Taoism. Wudang Mountain has verdant peaks and mountains. The scenery is amazing, and the weather is amazing. He said to his disciples, “This mountain will surely rejoice every day.” So together with the others, they “went to Jingzhen, swept away the rubble, and created a grassland to live.” After the Caolu Palace was completed, he had his disciple Qiu Yuanjing live in Wulong, Lu Qiuyun in Nanyan, Liu Guquan and Yang Shan to stay at Zixiao, while Zhang Sanfeng himself “lived in Zhanqifeng North. This is called True Palace. Zhang Sanfeng and his disciple built a hermitage in Tucheng, called Huixian Hall, so Disciple Zhou was able to protect it”. As a result, his descendants concentrated on studying Taoism and contributed to the further development of Taoist theoretical doctrine. Zhang Sanfeng has deep ideas and has written many books throughout his life. His “Dao Dao Theory,” “Explaining Mysteries Directly,” “Hyun Yao Bian,” “Taoist Tales,” and “Tiangu-Bian” are very persuasive. Cite the classics, go deep, and explain morality. The opinions put forward in the book can be described as clearly prominent, and they pioneered the Wudang Taoist sect. He proposed that Emperor Zhenwu be revered as the ancestor of the Wudang faction. He spoke of filial piety, loyalty, poverty, and disgrace of character. He said, “Loyalty, filial piety and honesty are all humane. Naturally, the Sendo is not far away. ” He uncovered and criticized all evils: “As for the Sentence Sutra, the Imperial Lady takes Yin, subjugates the Three Yellow, and burns her ears with Eight Stones; there is no action on the side.” He focuses on practicing Inner Dan, that is, internal skills. “All thoughts are shared, and one spirit lives alone, called mindfulness.” He advocates insisting on “staying focused” when practicing, which means getting rid of distractions and practicing hard work to achieve the goal.
Magpie and Snake Brawl to Realize the Fist of the Inner Family
Zhang Sanfeng not only founded a series of Wudang Daoism theories, but also founded Wudang's internal skills and martial arts. There is a legend about Zhang Sanfeng's founding of Wudang Naijiajian. It is said that while Zhang Sanfeng was practicing on the “sloppy cliff” of Wudang Mountain, he saw a magpie fighting with a snake. The magpie flew up and down, while the snake dodged its sharp edge and dodged softly. After a long period of time, the magpie was exhausted, and the snake saw the opportunity and hit it with one blow. Zhang Sanfeng saw it. He was greatly inspired and understood how wonderful it is. Finally, after repeated training, a unique style of Wudang Naijia Boxing was created. This boxing technique is similar to the position taken by a snake during attack and defense. “Use soft force, brake with static brakes, strike at the back, identify mm in size, and seal the enemy by grappling.”
What is most valuable is that when Zhang Sanfeng created boxing, he integrated Taoist theory with Taoist practice, so that the name, number of ways, play style, and characteristics of Naijia Boxing were all branded as Taoism. The Taijiquan, Baguaquan, Xing Yi Quan, Wu Xing Quan, Mixed Yuan Quan, Xuanwu Stick, etc. founded by Zhang Sanfeng all draw the essence from the Taoist scriptures and are derived from the Taoist scriptures. The boxing techniques founded by Zhang Sanfeng all have a common characteristic: they focus on internal skills and changes in yin and yang, focus on coordination and unity of purpose, energy, and strength, calm movements, subtle posture, strong energy, and laid-back mind. All of these characteristics are consistent with the Taoist proposition of being quiet, weak, and indifferent, and the Taoist “Three Treasures of Practice” (refining and purifying gas, refining the gods; refining the devil). The inside is healthy, but the outside is evil. It can be said that it is a precious historical and cultural heritage left to future generations.
Famous Ariake generation
Due to Zhang Sanfeng's military prowess, his influence on Taoist doctrine was greatly appreciated by the Ming dynasty emperor who respected Daoism at the time. In the 17th year of Ming Hongwu (1384), Zhu Yuanzhang, the founder of the Ming Dynasty, issued a decree requesting Zhang Sanfeng to preach the sutras in Beijing.
In the 8th year of Yongle (1410), after Zhu Di, the founder of Ming Cheng, subdued the Mongolian rebels, he returned to North Korea and returned to Nanjing. At this time, Zhu Di was in a very bad mood due to Empress Xu's death, the Mongolian military rebellion, and a series of other worries. Professor Hu Guang said that Zhang Sanfeng, a Taoist from Wudang Mountain, is “profoundly moral and has great wisdom.” It would be better to ask him to go to Beijing to preach the scriptures, which can resolve his displeasure. As a result, Zhu Di immediately ordered Hu Ying to “visit” Zhu Xiang during the incident. Zhang Sanfeng, who has already enjoyed the vicissitudes of the world, of course knows that “being with a king is like being with a tiger,” so he avoided traveling. Hu Ying and Zhu Xiang traveled all over the mountains and “not met Mitutoyo for years.”
In February of the 10th year of Yongle (1412), Zhu Di, the founder of Ming Cheng, went to Wudang Mountain to see Zhang Sanfeng and gave him a poem written by the Imperial Dynasty. Among them was the phrase “If you meet a true fairy, stay with Sauvignon Blanc” to express his longing for him, but Zhang Sanfeng still avoided seeing him. In the 14th year of Yongle (1416), Zhu Di was disappointed that Zhang Sanfeng never showed up. He angrily said, “We invited you three times. Why did Zhang Sanfeng refuse to see him? ” Hu Guang saw that the emperor was really angry. On a starry night, he rushed to Wudang Mountain to burn incense and cry, and invited Zhang Sanfeng to show up. Zhang Sanfeng “saw Hu Guang's affectionate love, but his majesty saw him enter the DPRK” and gifted him with the Seven Rules “Answer to the Yongle Emperor”. The poem says, “It's useless for a minister to live in Kusamakigahara; if the emperor asks if he has feelings for it. Daring to listen carefully is a long life if you don't want to be clear”. After seeing him in the morning, Zhang Sanfeng drifted away.
Later, Zhu Di expressed the importance he attached to Zhang Sanfeng and Wudang Taoism. While building Empress Xu's Shou Mausoleum and Beijing's Xindu Forbidden City, supervisors Ding Fu led Ding Fu to complete more than 300,000 civil engineering projects in Wudang Mountain, “costing millions”, and successively reconstructed and newly built Jingle, Yuzhen, Yuyu, Zixiao, Nan Yan, Chaotian, Taihe, etc., with a total construction area of more than 1 million square meters. After the Gongguan Temple in Wudang Mountain was built, Zhu Di gave it the name “Taihe Taiyue Mountain” and “built an official seal to guard it.” It can be said that the main palace, temple, and hermitage buildings that currently exist in Wudang Mountain were all built by Emperor Yongle. Among them, the Golden Hall was installed in Wudang after being cast in copper and plated with gold in Beijing.
After the construction of the Wudang Mountain Palace Guanmiao was completed, Zhang Sanfeng began traveling all over the world. As he said in his “Qionghua Poems,” “Qiongzhi and Yushu belong to a fairy family. Unknown people have this flower. It is beautiful and not exposed to rain, and has a high standard of ancient smoke. He has been so old over the years, and the world is unrivaled. Wanting to take it back to heaven is like borrowing spiritual power from Bao Wang”. This poem can be described as Zhang Sanfeng's seclusion and death in seclusion.
Over the next 200 years, successive emperors of the Ming dynasty revered Zhang Sanfeng. In 1459, Ming Xizong and Zong Tianshun proclaimed Zhang Sanfeng three years ago; in 1486, Ming Xianzong's Zhu Jianshen sealed Zhang Sanfeng as “Taoguang Shang Zhi Zhen Zhen”; in 1563, Ming Shi Zong Zhu Houcong named Zhang Sanfeng as “Qingxu Yuan Miao Zhenjun”; in 1623, Ming Xi Zong's school proclaimed Zhang Sanfeng as “Feilong Xianhua Hongren Ji Zhen Jun”. At that time, Zhang Sanfeng was already “uncertain whether it existed or not.”
Zhang Sanfeng was not only a master of Taoism and martial arts, but also an accomplished writer, leaving behind many masterpieces of poetry and literature throughout his life. After his death, his mentor wrote books such as “Mitoyo Collection”, “Mitutoyo Poetry Collection”, and “Mitutoyo Alchemy Collection”, which are still in circulation.
