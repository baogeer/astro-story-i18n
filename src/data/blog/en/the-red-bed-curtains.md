 If a freshman's bedroom needs to be furnished, the dormitory manager will buy bed curtains. The school director didn't go to the city because he didn't want to go to the city, so he bought it at a cloth store in front of the school. Perhaps the owner likes red. There are only all kinds of red cloth in the store, and even the owner who sells cloth wears red clothes. The chief had no choice but to pick a nice red cloth with a cartoon on it.
The cloth was cut into curtains and drapes hung. The entire bedroom was painted red, and passers-by felt a bit depressing when they looked at it. Strangely enough, since then, the sisters in the bedroom have gotten sick one by one. Today, either this cold or that cough. One day, a roommate became seriously ill. While the head of the ward accompanied her to the hospital to see a doctor, she accidentally fell, broke her knee, and was bleeding more than once. As soon as the doctor wrapped the gauze on her, they were all dyed red, replaced with a new one, and were all dyed red, and the head of the hospital was hospitalized. The classmate accompanied her, and it took a long time for her blood to stop.
When I returned to the bedroom the next morning, all the sisters in the bedroom looked at them with a bleak face. The dormitory manager felt that the bedroom was also a bit different. ah! Their mattress curtains were all white and brushed white, and everyone had a blob of dark blood in front of their bed. The head of the dormitory was so frightened that they ran all over the building and shouted, “That's my blood! That's my blood!” Later, I heard from others that the owner of that cloth shop committed suicide by cutting her wrist before they entered school. She had blood all over her blood and dyed all the cloth red. Since then, the cloth shop has never been opened.
