 Once upon a time, there was a person whose parents passed away one after another when he was three years old. He was cared for by the neighborhood and grew up eating hundreds of family meals. When I became an adult, my family was so poor that I was often unable to keep up, couldn't get married to a daughter-in-law, and lived alone. Fish all day, month, and year for a living. If you fish, you have something to eat; if you can't fish, you're hungry. Adulthood has lived a bitter life of hunger and cold for many months.
One day, he went to the riverside to fish. After a day of hard work, he didn't have any fish or shrimp, so he carried his net home angry and hungry. Suddenly, he stepped on a stiff object and looked down. He saw a big screw on the beach. He picked it up. When he got home, he put it in a broken bowl and went to bed hungry.
The next day, he went to the riverside to fish as usual. He was really unlucky. After another day of hard work, he came home hungry and tired. He was unable to push open the door and saw a miracle. He only saw a table with delicious and rich flavors on the table. It felt very strange. I thought it was strange to see that I hadn't eaten in two days. He was very hungry and had enough time to eat delicious food. light. After a full meal, I fell into bed thinking about staying overnight, and still couldn't figure out who did it.
When he woke up in the morning, he still went fishing as usual, and when he got home, he had plenty of meals on the table, which really puzzled him for a few days. One day he dressed up to go fishing, and halfway back, hid quietly in a hidden place, and sneaked out who had brought him the meal. Suddenly, he saw a big, gorgeously dressed, flower-like girl popping out of the broken bowl and cooking for him with great movements. He finally found out the truth of the matter, and was surprised and happy. Unsure where the courage came from, he knelt down and held the girl's feet and painstakingly prayed: “Benefactor, thank you for everything you've done for me these days, so I can eat such a delicious meal. Tell me what kind of girl you are from, can you marry me, and save me, this poor poor bastard.” The girl couldn't get rid of him; she had to bend down and pull up her kneeling and said, “I think you have a really poor life. I can marry you, but if you think about it, I'm ugly. Don't look young and beautiful now. I will definitely grow old and ugly in the future, and I will also grow an unsightly large sarcoma on my neck. At that point, you will definitely dislike me for being ugly.” The young man shook his head and swore, “Absolutely not, you saved me from suffering. You are my benefactor. I am definitely not an ungrateful person. Truly, I will be grateful to you and repay you for the rest of my life...” In this way, the girl believed in him and became close to him.
The next day, as soon as he woke up, he discovered that his broken hut had become a big white tile house. The circle was filled with pigs, cows and sheep, the open space around the house was covered with fruit trees, and the yard was full of chickens, ducks, geese, and whatever he wanted at home. After that, they also had children and lived a very rich and happy life. He stopped fishing and working; he spent the whole day at leisure, eating, drinking and having fun. The daughter-in-law really didn't get used to it, so she advised him not to forget the basics. He must work hard and be frugal in life. No matter where he likes to listen to these words now, he still does his own thing. One day I lost a lot of money at the casino. When he got home, he was ready to gamble again. His wife stopped him and advised him not to go and cherish today's good days. Instead, he screamed and said, “You're old and ugly. Everyone makes fun of me. How could a millionaire marry a woman like you? I'm so shameless in front of others. You still dare to take care of me today. I don't want you, get out of here.” The wife was also sad and saddened to hear that it was impossible for him to repent. She was so helpless that she had no choice but to say to her, “Well, if you set up a swing in front of the house early tomorrow morning, I'll leave you.”
Early the next morning, the husband actually set up a swing. The wife put on beautiful clothes and jewelry that had been treasured for many years, and walked out of the house and climbed onto the swing and said, “Come to the house, pigs, cows, sheep, chickens, ducks, geese, animals, and animals, come with me.” As soon as I finished speaking, there was no trace of it. Everything he was rich in is gone, and he is living in a thatched house, wearing tattered clothes, and living the bitter days of not being hungry before. Since then, there has always been a person sitting in front of a thatched house and muttering to himself: “I broke my promise and was ungrateful. I really deserved the crime of suffering such consequences today.”
