The meaning of “to return” is to return. Gappu's Pearl is back. It's an example of a person going back and forth, or losing something and recovering.
In the Eastern Han Dynasty, pearls were abundant along the coast of Hepu County; pearls produced there were round, large, and pure in color, and have always been famous at home and abroad; people call them “Hepu pearls.” The local people all make a living by picking pearls in exchange for food in neighboring counties. Pearl harvesting is highly profitable, and some government officials have taken the opportunity to corrupt the law and abuse the people of Zhu under the guise of artifying the name. In order to catch more oil and water, they ignored the growth rules of mussels and simply invited the people of Zhu to go fishing. As a result, mussels gradually migrated to neighboring Koji County, and fewer and fewer can be caught in Gappu. Fishermen along the Hepu coast have always relied on pearl harvesting for a living, and few people grow rice. There is a lot of pearl harvesting, and the income is high, so I don't care if I spend some money to buy food. Today, there are few pearls, and income has been drastically reduced. Fishermen don't even have money to buy food. Many people starved to death as a result.
After Emperor Han Shun Liu Bao succeeded to the throne, he sent a person named Meng Shao as the Taishou of Hepu. After Meng took office, he soon discovered the reason why the local fishermen had no food; he ordered to eliminate the shortcomings, abolish the illegal regulations on scrap, and prevent fishermen from harvesting indiscriminately in order to protect the resources of pearl mussels. Less than a year; mussels have proliferated again, and Hepu has become a place rich in pearls.
