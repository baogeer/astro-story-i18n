 The situation at Conscience Pawn Shop is not as good as day by day; it seems like it's about to close its doors. Zhang Yipin, the owner of a conscientious pawnshop, lost his eyesight due to an illness, so he had to leave the pawnshop to his son Zhang Sanbao to take care of. One of these three treasures doesn't know how to operate; the other is ignorant of goods. Not long ago, due to wealth, I mistakenly received a few fake antiques in a row and lost more than 2,000 dollars.
On this day, an old man brought a blue and white porcelain vase to the Pawn Shop of Conscience. The dude called Zhang Sanbao in a hurry. Zhang Sanbao took a look at the blue and white porcelain vase and said, “Take it, take it! Use anything for it too!”
The old man said, “Don't you believe this dead director? This is my family's ancestral heritage!” Afterwards, the old man spoke with Zhang Sanbao in detail. It turns out that the old man is sick and is in urgent need of money to buy medicine. He asked Zhang Sanbao to take over this blue and white porcelain vase, saying that only 22 silver would be enough. The old man couldn't stop coughing. It seems that the old man used this porcelain vase as a life saver.
Zhang Sanbao thought that in the past, I was too unscrupulous, so God punished me, and pawnshops were not as good as day by day. Just as the pawnshop is closing its doors today, why don't you just give him 22 dollars. As a result, Zhang Sanbao took the porcelain vase and asked his buddy to take the money for the old man. The dude panicked and said to Zhang Sanbao, “Boss, this won't die, it's not even worth a penny or two!” Zhang Sanbao said, “I told you to pay, just pay!” The dude sighed; he had to take 22 pieces of silver for the old man. The dude saw Zhang Sanbao go into the back room with a porcelain vase and said in his mouth, “Obviously it's a fake. I think this pawn shop is closing!” After speaking, I shook my head and sighed.
No wonder, after ten days, the old man came again. This time, the old man brought another blue and white porcelain vase. As soon as my friend saw the blue and white porcelain vase, it was different from the last one. Dude didn't dare, so he quickly called Zhang Sanbao. When Zhang Sanbao saw this blue and white porcelain vase, he couldn't help but brighten his eyes. He touched the porcelain vase with his hands and said, “How much silver do you want?” The old man said, “Five hundred two!” Zhang Sanbao was taken aback and said, “Just 500? Let's do that, I'll give you a thousand two!” The old man said, “It doesn't have to be that many, just 500 or two is enough!” Zhang Sanbao was happy to receive the porcelain vase and asked his buddy to pay for it. The old man finally redeemed the blue and white porcelain vase from last time.
Ten days later, the redemption date arrived, but the old man never came. That blue and white porcelain vase is even from a pawnshop. This porcelain vase is worth two thousand two thousand silver, to say the least. I didn't expect it to make a profit in the end.
Soon, Zhang Sanbao had another plan and gave the pawnshop to Boss Liu of Xinglong Pawn Shop. Early this morning, Boss Liu brought two friends to the Pawnshop of Conscience to check the inventory. At first, Boss Liu wasn't convinced. When he saw the blue and white porcelain vase, he couldn't help but be amazed. He hurriedly said to Zhang Sanbao, “I still don't want your pawnshop...”
Zhang Sanbao thought it was strange: “How did you get out of here...”
Boss Liu pointed to the blue and white porcelain vase and said, “Just this porcelain vase is worth 5,000 silver. Are you kidding me that your pawnshop can't keep running?!” When Boss Liu finished speaking, he rolled up his sleeves and left with his two friends. Zhang Sanbao stood there. For half a day, he stepped forward and held a blue and white porcelain vase and said, “This porcelain vase is worth 5,000 silver, that much!”
Because of this valuable blue and white porcelain vase, the Conscience Pawn Shop is still open, and the business is getting more and more prosperous.
At the end of the year, with a single account, the pawnshop turned a loss into a profit. Excluding various expenses, it also earned 3,400 thousand dollars.
That night, Zhang Sanbao shared this happy news with his father Zhang Yipin. Zhang Yipin listened and smiled and said to Zhang Sanbao, “Pawn shops can be brought back to life; apparently they still rely on that porcelain vase!” Zhang Sanbao said, “Yes! For a porcelain vase of such value, that old man only spent 502 pieces of silver; he never came to redeem it!” Zhang Yipin said, “They saw that you have a conscience and sent it to you!” “For me?” Zhang Sanbao was inexplicable.
Zhang Yipin looked at Zhang Sanbao and smiled and said, “The first porcelain vase the old man brought was clearly a worthless fake, but you still gave him 22 pieces of silver. You saw that the old man was sick and pitied him. It can be seen that you still have a conscience, so later they gave you a real porcelain vase worth 5,000 silver!”
Zhang Sanbao couldn't help but wonder, “Dad, how can you know that clearly?” Zhang Yipin smiled and said, “Because I am that old man...”
“You...” Zhang Sanbao was so surprised that he couldn't speak for half a day.
Zhang Yipin continued, “I know the pawnshop will have to take care of it for you sooner or later, so. I became blind right after my illness and asked you to take care of the pawnshop. I thought you had good money, were in a hurry to make money, had no conscience, and lost two thousand two thousand dollars in less than a month. Because of this, I was very angry, but I didn't want the pawnshop to lose, so I pretended to be a sick old man using a fake porcelain vase. I didn't expect your kid to have some conscience and gave me 22 silver, so I found an antique porcelain vase that I had treasured for many years to buy for you. As a result, no one dared to buy this pawnshop, and you gained confidence.”
After listening to his father's explanation, Zhang Sanbao's eyes were blurry: “Thank you, Dad! “If it weren't for you...” Zhang Yipin smiled and said, “Thank you, or thank yourself. It was your conscience that saved you and saved the pawnshop. When I first named the pawnshop a conscientious pawnshop, I wanted to be a conscientious person and run a conscientious pawnshop. Because only a conscience can win people's hearts and win business!”
Zhang Sanbao couldn't help but nod. He finally understood why his father had been running a pawnshop for decades without making much money.
