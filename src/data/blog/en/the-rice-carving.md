 In the late Qing dynasty, a family surnamed Zeng moved to a village in Jiangnan. As a matter of course, although this village is small, it is also rich in soil and rich in products. The villagers all had a delicious life, but the Zeng family is strange. Three years have passed, their family and the villagers are out of place, and the economic situation is the same as when they first arrived. They are poor, and sometimes they can't even eat three meals a day.
What's even more strange is that Zeng's family of three can't pick shoulders, can't lift hands, and don't have any livelihood skills. As a result, some people speculate that the Zeng family is a declining aristocrat, but if that were the case, they should have learned something? I can't think of any of the family members who don't know a single character; none of them can read and study literature.
The strangest thing is that they would rather stay hungry for several days rather than accept the villagers' kindness. On weekdays, they only rely on old man Zeng and his son to pick up some cow dung or dead branches, and sell some money to make ends meet.
Just like that, in the midst of many discussions among the villagers, the Zeng family always maintained a sense of mystery and became a source of discussion for the group after dinner.
By the tenth day of the third year, it was rare that old man Zeng had no work. He woke up early and carefully carried a small bag of copper plates to the town. He walked into the best rice shop in town and told his friend that he wanted to buy the best white rice in the store.
The rice shop partner paused and immediately became happy: “Dad Zeng, I suggest you take a look at the rice here. For your little money, you can only buy less than half a catty of fine white rice. The brown rice here, even though it's a little bit smaller, can carry back a bag. I'm even more hungry than white rice.”
Old man Zeng sank and said, “If you say anything nonsense, I want white rice, and I want the best from you here. Give me your name!”
Dude, I had no choice but to start taking stock of the money in Zeng Laodou's bag. That copper plate was all polished to a shine. Seemingly, I don't know how many times each copper plate has been turned over and over in Old Man Zeng's hands! The dude got the money, found a very small pocket and filled it with white rice.
Old man Zeng took the rice bag, and his eyes were filled with tears. He carefully hid the rice bag in his arms, bowed his body, and left.
That night, old man Zeng called his wife and son to bed and solemnly took out the small bag of rice. As soon as his wife saw the white rice, she couldn't help but cry. Old man Zeng's tears also “ticked” down. Only Zeng Yang, son of the Zeng family, was in his teens. Naturally, he didn't know anything.
It turns out that the Zeng family was originally one of the richest families in the imperial city. Old man Zeng saved a car by chance a long time ago, so he was rewarded by the emperor. The Zeng family has been shamed by their ancestors for generations. Up until the generation of old men Zeng was poor and extremely extravagant. Not only did their children and grandchildren not learn or advance, but even their relatives relied on the name of the Zeng family and were overbearing outside the world.
The imperial city is at the foot of Tianzi; how could the Zeng family be so pompous? After a long time, the Zeng family had already made countless enemies. After the new emperor took the throne, although he was able to think for a moment about the Zeng family's rescue efforts back then, he was unable to withstand the impeachment setback and countless hours of spit. Within two days, he stabbed the Zeng family.
Old man Zeng said to his son Zeng Yang, “Three years ago today, the emperor rebuked his father in front of a hundred military officials, saying that his father had no studies or skills and wanted to have fun. If he were away from his ancestors, I'm afraid he wouldn't be able to live even a year. My father was in a hurry. He actually argued with the emperor, saying that our Zeng family didn't starve to death that easily; let alone a year, even three years.”
With tears in her eyes, Zeng Niang said, “Thanks also to your father's words at the time, the emperor gave our family one last chance.” Said, she handed over white rice to her son. “The emperor promised that as long as our family is not supported by others, we can live on our own feet for three years, and we will not blame the past and resume the monarchy.”
Old man Zeng sighed: “I have enjoyed all my glory. I never thought it would be so difficult to earn a living abroad. Over the past three years, your mother and I have all been exhausted. If not for this kind of hope, how can we support today? Now that we're all old, we have a long way to go to Beijing, we can only rely on you...”
Old man Zeng solemnly handed over the bag of white rice to his son and ordered, “Back then, the emperor said that our family was like a rat. Generations didn't work; I don't know how much rice the country ate for nothing. If we return to Beijing after three years, we will have to bring back fine white rice to verify that our Zeng family can support themselves without royal food. This bag of rice was saved by my dad over the past three years, so take it to Beijing; our family depends on you!”
The next day, Zeng Yang brought that small bag of white rice to Beijing amidst the expectations of her parents.
On the way, the food was draining, and life was naturally difficult. However, Zeng Yang never dared to touch that bag of white rice; he only went up the mountain to pick up some firewood in exchange for food. He was hungry and full, and walked through village after village.
On this day, Zeng Yang arrived in a small town, empty-handed, and had to go up the mountain to collect firewood. From when the sky was bright to when the sun was in the air, I had a hard time picking it up. After replacing five copper plates, he was preparing to buy a pancake to fill his stomach. Unexpectedly, he reached over and touched it and discovered that the bag containing rice had been cut open. There was not much left over when the bag full of rice had been left!
Zeng Yang was taken aback. Didn't the sky collapse and fall to the ground? How good is this? Zeng Yang ran out of ideas all of a sudden. Pity his one and a half children, he was so scared that he crouched on the side of the road and cried loudly, crying so much that he forgot he was hungry.
There was a jujube seller on the side of the road. Seeing that Zeng Yang cried pitifully, he came over to ask. Zeng Yang cried and talked about his thousands of miles to Beijing and told him that his most important token was gone.
Are jujube sellers strange: “What kind of token?”
Zeng Yang mixed up and said, “A bag of rice.”
Jujube sellers are happy: “Haha, how difficult is this? I have money, just buy another bag.”
Zeng Yang wiped away her tears and raised her head and said, “No, my dad said, even if I had no choice but to do anything, I wouldn't be able to accept any charity from others.”
The jujube seller was stunned and couldn't help but smile. They thought this kid had this kind of backbone, but it was rare, so they decided on him. The jujube seller thought about it and said, “Since you said that, I'm not reluctant. Otherwise, if you buy me a green jujube, I can help you without giving to you. What do you think?”
Zeng Yang took a look at that person and also looked at the basket of green dates. He felt that the person in front of him didn't look like a liar. It's just that the jujube in the basket was actually not very good; it looks like they're still fresh. I don't think he's opening a store today.
Zeng Yang thought about it for half a day, and there was no way around, and even if he was scammed, it was just a piece of green jujube money, so he doubtfully bought one, put it in his mouth, bit it, and immediately spit it it out. Wow, it was so sour that he fell out of his teeth! The jujube seller laughed “haha,” picked up the jujube on the ground, and said, “I'll lend you this jujube core for 10 days. I'll change the trick for you. After 10 days, you'll come here to find me.”
Just like that, Zeng Yang was uncertain and uneasy. He stayed in the town for ten days. Fortunately, the jujube seller did not break his contract. He met Zeng Yang as scheduled, and handed over the jujube kernels to Zeng Yang. Once Zeng Yang saw it, he couldn't help but be amazed: the jujube core had been transformed. It's only been three days. This unremarkable jujube core has been transformed into a beautifully carved painting! Before his family was ruined, Yang had seen this kind of play. It was a miniature sculpture!
The jujube seller said that he had already retired from seclusion, but this time it was an exception. He asked Zeng Yang to take this miniature sculpture to the Xijie Wenwan Store, saying that it was given to him by a jujube seller surnamed Song in the east of the city. In this way, he was able to exchange a bag of white rice, and maybe even the travel expenses to Beijing.
Zeng Yang heard it and knelt down in front of the jujube seller, “Master, please take me as a prisoner!”
The jujube seller said that he had been blocking the sword for many years and refused to take followers. However, after several days of entanglement, and with great sincerity, the jujube seller finally agreed.
After March of hard work, the jujube seller said to Zeng Yang, “Get started with your master. The practice depends on everyone.” After leaving this sentence, the jujube seller couldn't keep his doors closed. No matter how much Ren Zengyang asked for it, he wouldn't want to teach it any more. As a result, I prayed to my mentor and continued to go to Beijing while practicing. He walked and stopped along the way, practiced his craft diligently, passed through thousands of mountains and waters, and experienced ups and downs.
Finally, five years later, Zeng Yang arrived in Beijing and fell down on his knees in the Jinluan Palace. He claimed that he had an appointment with the Holy Son, and now he is back in Beijing.
After thinking about it for half a day, the emperor finally remembered something like that. He sneered and looked at Zeng Yang, who was like a beggar below the stairs, and said, “I have something to say. You are not allowed to beg, but you have broken the contract?”
Zeng Yang knelt down on the ground and sang loudly, “Little people have never received any charity.”
“Also, what about the rice you promised us?”
“Xiaomin finally lived up to the Holy Ghost's expectations. Xiaomin's rice doesn't dare to be called the best, yet it's hard to find in the world.”
As soon as the emperor heard it, he was interested: “Seriously? Show it to us.”
Naesai presented a tray. In the huge tray, there was only a single grain of rice alone. The emperor saw it and was furious: “You mischievous people, dare to make fun of us!”
Zeng Yang was not in a hurry and said, “Please watch the emperor carefully.” The emperor took a close look. It turned out that the grain of rice was actually a beautiful miniature sculpture: a light boat moving with the wind, a fisherman lying on the boat and taking a break. The craftsmanship was amazing. The work came to life and was ingenious!
The emperor was delighted and couldn't help but sigh, “This is indeed a rare rice. One grain can weigh a kilogram!”
Since then, he has made a name for himself, opened a school to receive many apprentices, and is full of peaches and plums, and has become a generation of microcarvers. The Zeng family's children and grandchildren all passed on their craft, and the family really prospered...
