 Legend has it that a long time ago, Aquarian had two mothers and children living in a cave. The mother is in her sixties and has hazy eyes, almost like a blind man. The son's name is Li Mengjin. He is capable and strong. The whole family lives by cutting firewood in exchange for rice.
On August 29 of this year, Li Mengjin hurriedly rushed to the streets with a load of firewood. He had just arrived at the side of the pond and was already tired and sweaty. He had to put down his chai to rest, take off his pants and jump into the water to take a bath.
Seeing that lively frog, he couldn't help but think: Mom hasn't eaten meat in a long time, so catch a few to make up for her body.
After a while, he caught a bunch of frogs, tied them to his pants on the shore, and continued to catch them.
Suddenly, there was a sound coming from behind. Li Mengjin looked up. It turned out that an eagle was holding that string of frogs and his own pants, spreading its wings and flying away.
How do you go to the street without pants? While anxious, Li Mengjin saw two plantain trees by the pond, and ran past, waved a wooden knife, cut down many banana leaves, surrounded his lower body with a green wisteria string growth dress, shouldered the burden of firewood, and continued on his way.
Li Mengjin picked firewood and entered the market. It was almost noon; it wasn't until dusk that he had to sell it. He bought six pounds of rice and went to the meat stand. He calculated in his mind: Tomorrow is the end festival, and he needs to get some pork back. He pleaded again and again before Yu Butcher promised to give him three catties of pork on credit.
Li Mengjin took the pork and left the bustling city in a hurry.
Unexpectedly, as I passed through the entrance of the state capital, a hasty cry came from behind: “... stop! Stop!”
Li Mengjin turned his head around, but what followed him was Yu Butcher. Without waiting for Li Mengjin to answer, Butcher Yu snatched the pork in his hands.
Coincidentally, the scene where Butcher Yu brazenly snatched pork happened to be seen by Miss Lei Xuejiao, the master of the state government. On this day, she was kicking a chicken feather. Hearing the cry of “stop,” she ran down the street to watch.
She was very close to her heir wearing a banana leaf skirt. After seeing his pork being stolen, they didn't argue and just stood in silence for a while. She looked at him carefully and suddenly discovered that he had a bright red “dragon” growing on his heel.
Suddenly, she thought in her heart, “I have a 'dragon' on my feet, and I won't be poor later.” While watching, she was fascinated before she knew it. She didn't go back until her later life had gone a long way.
