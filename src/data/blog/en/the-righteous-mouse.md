 Yang Tianyi said, “I saw two mice burrowing out of the hole. One was swallowed by a snake, and the other glared like peppercorns. I was very angry, but it just stared at me from afar and was afraid to move forward. When the snake had eaten its stomach, it meandered into the cave; as soon as it had climbed halfway, the rat swooped in and bitten the snake's tail violently. The snake was furious and hurriedly exited the cave. Mice are already very agile, so they run fast. The snake couldn't catch up and went back into the cave. The mouse ran back and bit like last time. This is how the snake got bitten by the mouse, and the snake ran out of the rat, over and over again. Finally, the snake climbed out of the hole and spit the dead mouse it had swallowed on the ground, and that mouse stopped. It sniffs its companion with its nose and squeaks in sorrow. Then, they whispered to the dead mouse. My friend Zhang Liyou wrote an article “Prosthetic Mouse Trip” for this purpose.
