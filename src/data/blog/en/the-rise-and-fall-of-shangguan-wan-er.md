Fourteen years later, Shangguan Waner became famous for her literary talent and was summoned by Wu Zetian. Since then, she stayed with Wu to handle paperwork. Her position was equivalent to that of a secret secretary, and then gradually participated in the drafting of the Royal Decree and participated in the drafting of the Dharma. At this time, she was only 19 years old.
Among these, Wu Zetian abolished Prince Li Xian's edict, which was written by Shangguan Waner. At the time, Shangguan Waner was 17 years old. Lee Hyun, on the other hand, is said to be her first love.
