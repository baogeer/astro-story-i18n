 Once upon a time, there were two young people who were deeply in love with each other. The girl is as gentle as a beautiful pigeon, and the young man is as strong as an eagle spreading its wings. Everyone called the young man Yannick, and the girl was Hanlushka. Hanlushka's father was a wealthy little aristocrat, but Jannik's father was a poor shepherd. The naive Hanlushka didn't care about Jannik being poor, but liked that he was strong, hardworking, and good-looking. “But what would your father say?” Yannick is worried that her father won't approve of their marriage. One day, Yannick was dressed beautifully and went to Hanlushka's house to ask if the little aristocrat wanted to marry his daughter. After hearing his marriage proposal, the little aristocrat said to him: “Listen, Yannick, if you want to marry my daughter, first ask why the sun doesn't shine on me like during the day; then ask the moon why it doesn't shine as hot during the day as it does at night. If they tell you to come back, I'll give you all my daughter along with her possessions.”
After listening, Jannik put a wide-brimmed hat on his head, said goodbye to Hanlushka, and hurriedly set out on the road to find the sun. One day, he came to a remote castle, and since it was dark, he went to ask the owner there to spend the night. The owner gave him a place to sleep, asked him to sit at the table and ask him where he had come from. When he told the owner that he wanted to find the sun and moon, the owner asked him for help. Don't forget to ask them why his pear tree hasn't produced pears in recent years; in the past, its pears have always been the sweetest. Yannick would be very happy to ask him about this. Early the next morning, he went back on his way.
He also crossed mountains and mountains, drifted across the river, and arrived at a village where there was a shortage of drinking water. They asked where Jannik had come from. When they heard that he was looking for the sun and moon, they asked him to ask the sun for them why the water in their delicious well suddenly stinks. Yannick promised never to forget to ask him questions and keep going. After being away for a long time, he finally found the sun. When Yannick walked towards it, it was in the east.
“Hey, wait! I have something to ask you!” Yannick shouted to the sun.
“If something happens, hurry up! Because I still have to go around the whole world!” The Sun answered.
Jannik quickly walked up to the sun and asked, “Why aren't you as hot and glowing at night as you are during the day?” “If I had the same heat and light at night as during the day, the Earth would be burnt to ashes.” The Sun answered. Janick asked him about pears and well water again, but the Sun said to him that he would have to ask his brother Moon to know about these things. The Sun finished talking and got busy with it. Yannick continued on his way until he met the moon. “Wait, Moon, I have something to ask you!” Yannick shouted.
“Let's talk about it then! The world is waiting for me!” The moon said and stopped walking.
“Moon, why don't you shine as hot during the day as you do at night?”
“If I had the same exposure during the day as at night, my crops wouldn't be harvested. I don't have a fever, but I give dew, so the crops are much more comfortable.” When Moon answered, she wanted to keep chasing it, but Yannick stopped it and asked it why the pear trees in the castle garden aren't growing pears now.
“When the oldest daughter in this family was a virgin, the pear tree kept growing pears, but when Pear discovered that a child was buried under it, it didn't bear pears. If the child's body is dug up and buried elsewhere, and the eldest daughter is married, the pear tree will bear pears again.” When the moon said it was leaving, Yannick kept it and asked it why that well in the village no longer had good water.
“This is because there is a rock at the bottom of the well, and there is a toad under the stone. It has been soiling the water in the well over the years. You have to pull out the stone, catch the toad and kill it, and the well water will be as clear, sweet, and clean as before.” When the moon said it, he hurriedly chased it away, and Jannik didn't delay it any longer. He knew everything he wanted to know, so he headed home.
As soon as the residents of the village where the well suddenly stinks, they ran to greet him and waited impatiently for him to bring good news. Yannick walked to the well with them and told them what to do. They actually found the toad under that rock. After killing the toad, the water became clean and drinkable again. Everyone sent many, many things to thank Yannick, and Yannick continued walking home. When he arrived at the castle, the owner greeted him with joy.
