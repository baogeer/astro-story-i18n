 On July 7 of the lunar calendar in 1926 AD, the day the cowherd and the weaver met, there was a marriage in Beihai Park in Beijing that people had been discussing for a long time. Groom Xu Zhimo and bride Lu Xiaoman have long been at the center of public opinion. At the wedding, Xu Zhimo's teacher Leung Kai-chiu's congratulations made everyone present feel embarrassed, except himself. Not only did he not say anything auspicious, but instead criticized the bride and groom, blamed them not to treat the marriage as a child's play, and finally said, “I wish you all this was your last marriage!” Afterwards, Leung Kai-chiu wrote to his daughter, Leung Ling Han, saying, “I did something I was extremely unwilling to do yesterday, which was to testify for Xu Zhimo. His bride is Wang Geng's wife. It is extremely immoral to get divorced from Wang Geng only after falling in love with Shima. When Shima finds someone like this as a partner, he is afraid that pain will follow in the future. Therefore, I did not hesitate to give it a loud voice and drink it in the hope that there will be some awareness so that Shima will not be killed in the future. I learned a great lesson in the wedding hall. The couple and the guests were all impressed. This is probably an unheard of wedding in China and abroad.”
Lu Xiaoman is a famous social bloomer in Beijing. Her father, Lu Ding, was originally the head of the Department of Taxation of the Ministry of Finance. Later, he abandoned politics and became the general manager of Zhenhua Bank. He is considered a man of fortune. The Lu family has money, and when they can't bear to let their baby daughter go to school, they invited all the teachers they needed to their home. So even though Lu Xiaoman never went to school, apart from being highly skilled in the Chinese script, the oral translation into English and French was smooth and natural. In terms of art, with the exception of brush characters drawn on a handkerchief, all kinds of Chinese paintings, Peking opera, and dance are fine. In addition, she has been smart since she was small and looked like a little fairy, and she has earned the reputation of “the greatest beauty of the ages.”
Lu Xiaoman's first husband was Wang Geng, mentioned in Liang Qichao's letter. He studied at Princeton University and West Point in the US. When I married Xiaoman Lu, I was teaching at Peking University. Shortly after their marriage, the lifestyle and lifestyle habits of the two became different. Wang Geng works seriously and responsibly, and often devours research to prepare for lectures. Lu Xiaoman is an affectionate person and has been outside for three days to have fun. As a result, Wang Geng thought that Lu Xiaoman had not fulfilled her responsibilities as a wife and had not kept her duty as a woman; Lu Xiaoman believed that Wang Geng was not considerate enough, and that people who had drank foreign ink were still so old-fashioned. At this point, Wang Geng's friend from studying in the US, Xu Zhimo, a colleague at Peking University, quietly stepped in.
Lu Xiaoman is from Wujin, Jiangsu, and Xu Zhimo is from Jianjiang. He is one year younger than Wang Geng and six years older than Lu Xiaoman. He studied at Peking University, Clark University in the US, and Cambridge University in the UK. He was born extremely smart, and later studied under Liang Qichao. His essays and new poems blend classical Chinese literature with Western literature. They are thorough, flamboyant but not greasy, and beautiful but not vulgar. On the eve of the “May Fourth” campaign, he nominated the Deputy Editor-in-Chief of the “Beijing Morning News”, and later founded the “Crescent Bookstore” to publish “Crescent Magazine.” Fueling the new cultural movement, he became an idol adored by fashionable people. Xu Zhimo is outstanding, has a wide range of interests, and is elegant. He had a wife, Carina Cheung for a long time, but he desperately pursued Lin Huiyin while studying in Europe. Naturally, this kind of person is loved by Lu Xiaoman.
Xu Zhimo is Wang Geng's best friend, and he gradually became familiar with Lu Xiaoman. As soon as Wang Geng had trouble or was too lazy to leave, he asked Xu Zhimo to accompany Lu Xiaoman out on a mountain tour, play in the water, or dive into places where the lights were red and green. At that time, Xu Zhimo was in the midst of falling out of love. Lin Huiyin, whom he had worked so hard to pursue, looked down on him and married Leung Sai-shing, the eldest son of Leung Kai-chiu. As a result, she transferred her full tenderness to Lu Xiaoman. It just so happened that Wang Geng was hired to be appointed police chief in Harbin. Lu Xiaoman is alone and lonely. Lu and Xu are like A and B glue; the more they stick, the tighter.
In terms of traditional morality, Xu Zhimo's pursuit of Lu Xiaoman is considered to be unfaithful to his wife Carina Cheung and unjust to his friend Wang Geng. However, Xu Zhimo has always acted with pleasure and is unfettered. Once he “meets his admiration and embraces each other,” he can't care about anything outside of his body. Both the Lu family and the Xu family think that they are unfaithful children; it is a scandal; they are trying their best to stop it. Xu Zhimo and Lu Xiaoman said, “True love is not a crime. It is the same as martyrs martyrs martyrs and religious martyrs.” Xu Zhimo declared to the world, “My willingness to risk the world is to seek a settlement of conscience and independence of personality. In the midst of a vast sea of people, I was lucky enough to visit my soul mate; that's all!” In the beginning trend, the two were encouraged and cheered by young men and women, and proudly embarked on the path of “seeking a new marriage without getting old.” For example, Yu Duff said, “Shima is passionate, and Little Man is as gentle as cotton. When the two meet, they will naturally burn into a ball. They can still take care of the rules of ethics and disregard the patriarchal family style.”
At this point, Wang Geng was invited by Sun Chuanfang to Nanjing to be nominated to the post of Chief of General Staff within the five provincial coalition headquarters, a position of high authority. Rumor has it that his wife acted differently. In a quick way, she wrote a quick letter to Lu Xiaoman, claiming, “If you miss the love between husband and wife, immediately go south to reunite. If they belong to someone else, I will never stop them.” After a few twists and turns, Xu Zhimo and Carina Cheung got divorced, and Wang Geng and Lu Xiaoman also went through separation procedures. Wang Geng said bitterly to Lu Xiaoman, “They are a married couple. If they don't get along, they split up. I voluntarily relinquished to complete all of you. I hope you have a happy life.”
With the help of a group of friends such as Hu Shi and Yu Duff, Xu Zhimo actively prepared for the wedding. The Xu family and the Lu family's elders hated the events of Xu Zhimo and Lu Xiaoman, and they never attended the wedding. So even though Xu Zhimo's teacher, Leung Kai-chiu, objected to the union of the two of them, he definitely had to be invited. Despite repeated exhortations from Hu Shi and others, to put it bluntly, Leung Kai-chiu finally agreed to attend the wedding. The wedding took place as scheduled. Leung Kai-chiu said the earthly horror words mentioned earlier; unexpectedly, they were so spiritual. Shortly after her marriage, Lu Xiaoman proposed to move to Shanghai, saying that she wanted to use the colourful colors of the Ten Mile Yangchang to dilute the obscurity he had built up in Beijing.
They built a nest of love in Siming Village on Fuxi Road. The two had a very sweet time for a while. In the spring of the following year, the warm wind was enchanting, and flowers bloomed. At last, Lu Xiaoman couldn't hold back her insatiable sense of emptiness, and began to return to her old ways. Having just joined the social circle, it immediately caused a huge stir. Shanghai is a place of hidden dragons and tigers. Most of them are old people from Manqing, Wang Sun Guizhou, wealthy business magnates, and children from rich and idle families. As a result, some invited her to dinner; others invited her to dance; and others came out and encouraged her to vote for a charity show. The limelight seemed to be out of the blue. Time, spirit, and money were all lost. Lu Xiaoman thought it was worth it, but Xu Zhimo secretly cried out a lot of suffering.
There is a native of Suzhou named Weng Duanwu. He has a rich family, an affluent family, eats, drinks and plays, doesn't do his job properly, is a mainstay of the Elegant Ge box office, and also has medical skills in massage. They met Lu Xiaoman through a ticket show, so they showed great hospitality in front of Lu Xiaoman. The two often got lots of applause from the show. At one point, Lu Xiaoman performed on a major axis. Singing was tiring, and once fainted. Weng Duanwu performed his massage stunts and kneaded Lu Xiaoman, which actually relieved Lu Xiaoman's fatigue. As a result, Lu Xiaoman often asked Weng Duanwu to massage her. She felt completely comfortable, and the relationship between the two gradually improved. Weng Duanwu also taught Lu Xiaoman to eat opiates. In this way, Weng Duanwu put his hands up and rubbed Lu Xiaoman for a while; after a while, he lay back on his back and forth with Lu Xiaoman, spitting fog at the lamp, and couldn't even be seen by anyone else. However, Xu Zhimo still defended his beautiful wife with a sense of infatuation. He explained, “The relationship between a couple is love. The relationship between friends is love. It is a cure; Hibiscus spits on the pillow and swallows up the clouds. At most, they can only talk; at best, they can't have sex.” As a result, Lu Xiaoman stepped forward and didn't pay attention to Xu Zhimo at all. In front of Xu Zhimo, they confronted Weng Duanwu, and even acted personally. What Xu Zhimo did back then seems to have been done more thoroughly now by Weng Duanwu; what happened to Wang Geng back then, Xu Zhimo also experienced bitterness at this time. What a retaliation. Xu Zhimo is now falling bloodily amidst the thorns of the reality of his life.
There was a rift in the couple's relationship, and Lu Xiaoman still didn't care at all; they were playing in a dark place. In order to supply his wife's bottomless extravagance, in addition to earning money by teaching and writing in Shanghai, Xu Zhimo also traveled to Beijing to open source in bad weather. Sometimes Shanghai, sometimes Beijing, both sides are busy.
On November 17, 1931, Xu Zhimo returned to Shanghai from Beijing and chatted with a few friends at home in the evening. Lu Xiaoman still came home very late, and was so drunk that his eyes were hazy. The friends left one after another, and Xu Zhimo's stomach was filled with anger. The next day, Xu Zhimo patiently enlightened and persuaded Lu Xiaoman. Lu Xiaoman couldn't listen at all, so the two got into a big fight. Lu Xiaoman was addicted to opiates while on the smoking bed. Suddenly, the young lady got angry, grabbed the smoke lamp and smashed it on Xu Zhimo. Although it didn't hit Xu Zhimo in the head, it flew past the corner of his forehead and knocked off Xu Zhimo's glasses. Xu Zhimo was completely desperate and quietly left home to Nanjing. On the 19th, he flew to Beiping on the China Airlines Jingping Line's Jinan flight. I flew to Dangjiazhuang near Jinan and was met with heavy fog. The plane mistakenly touched the top of Kaishan Mountain. The plane crashed and people died. Xu Zhimo's hands and feet were burnt to coke. The death was extremely terrible.
Xu Zhimo's death was extremely shocking. Friends came from all over to organize his funeral. Yu Duff wrote The Tale of Salvation:
Two new volumes of poetry, old friends for 20 years. When they met, it was the end of the world, just for the sake of beautiful people;
As soon as the river was full, there was smoke at a few points, and the crane returned to Huabiao, worried that it would be too cold in high places.
However, the most prominent and moving connection in the spiritual hall was that of Xu Zhimo's former wife, Carrie Cheung, and Xu Zhimo's father, Xu Shenfu, who announced the separation of father and son. Xu Shenfu was deeply unworthy of his son's death. He cried:
According to the epic story, Shen Xiang caught the moon, and the literati died on the sidelines. They were all sad and aloof. When they met Gang Feng, they were also horrified;
Since I was a child, I have been studying and studying, and my husband and wife have maintained their only child. My mother has now passed away, so I can endure the desolate old father to revive his soul.
Carina Cheung originally divorced her because of Xiaoman Lu. However, she is deeply in love with Xu Zhimo. Xu Zhimo's father, Xu Shenfu, also felt that Carina Cheung was a rare good daughter-in-law, so Carina Cheung still lives in the Xu family. This is how she cried Xu Zhimo:
Wanli Kuai Fei Peng lost his way when he was alone in the dark clouds;
As soon as I was shocked, I took pity on the weak to enchant souls.
At the spiritual hall, Xu Shenfu didn't want to see Lu Xiaoman, but Carina Cheung didn't shy away from her suspicions and went to comfort Lu Xiaoman. Lu Xiaoman discovered in his conscience that his regrets were mixed. The two faced each other at a distance and cried to death. In the spiritual hall, the long light under Xu Zhimo's feet was dimly bright and flickering.
Xu Zhimo and Lu Xiaoman were married for five years and sacrificed a precious life. Teacher Leung Kai-chiu's concerns have become irretrievable facts. Xu Zhimo was 36 years old at the time of his death. He was a promising year. Lu Xiaoman was just 30 years old at this time; she was in her most splendid age. However, in the face of the title of Xu Zhimo's widow, she naturally had to settle down in social settings, and society didn't understand her very much. The average person tried to avoid getting involved with her. She spent her bleak years studying painting today and Wang Xingbo studying poetry tomorrow. She simply lived with Weng Duanwu. In the end, life forced her to even sell the copyrights to Xu Zhimo's “Little Love Me” and “Shima Diary” to Chenguang Publishing.
“Beautiful women have been like famous generals since ancient times; they are not allowed to see white heads on earth.”
Lu Xiaoman was lazy and sleepy all day long. He couldn't keep up his spirits, and quickly became exhausted. She died in Shanghai when the 1965 Cultural Revolution was imminent.
