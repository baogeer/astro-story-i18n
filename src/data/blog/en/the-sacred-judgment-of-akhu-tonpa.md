 In the evening, as the sun sets, tired birds return to their nests. Agutomba, who often travels east and west, doesn't know where he went today; this effort has only come back.
As soon as I entered the village, I saw a crowd of people shouting and angry at the entrance of the herder Zaxidonzhu. At this point, I was confronted by a child. Agutonbatar heard from him: It turns out that the village leader is bullying Zaxidonzhu again.
Here's the thing:
Zaxidon Zhu returned from grazing today. While passing through Tsuomgang (mountain name), he found a piece of pine snail under a tree stump on the side of the mountain path. Somehow, this incident was learned about by the head and chief shepherd, who was greedy for money, ignorant, and used to bullying the people. As soon as Zaxidon Zhu stepped into the house with his right foot and had not had time to keep up with his left foot, he rushed in menace. As soon as they met, he didn't say a word. The first sentence was:
“Give it to me, damn it!”
Other than orders, in this sentence, it's just insult. Zashidonzhu still hasn't figured out what happened. The villain punched Zaxidonzhu in the chest again, then shouted like a wolf:
“Give me the pine ear stone, give it to me soon!”
Only then did Zahidon Zhu understand; it turned out that this was what he wanted. Of course, Zahidon Zhu didn't want to give him what he had picked up, just said:
“Master, I picked this pine fungus from the mountain! It's not yours, why give it to you?”
“OK, let me ask you, what mountain did you pick it up?”
“Mount Zom.”
“That's it. Mount Zomu is my mountain. Every plant and tree on this mountain belongs to my family. Can't this pine snail come back to me?”
“This pine snail didn't grow on your mountain...”
“Shut up! Anyway, since I picked it up on my mountain, I have to give it back to me!”
When Agutomba walked past, they were still arguing! When people saw him come, they thought this matter should be resolved reasonably.
When both parties to the dispute had to seek justification from Agutomba, Agutomba waved his hand:
“I already know everything. When I passed by Mount Zom, all the mountain gods there told me and asked me to tell the two of you, if you really don't get along, invite him and he will make a sacred judgment.”
As soon as the shepherd heard that his mountain god called him, then his judgment must be beneficial to him. Zahidun Zhu thought to himself: As long as Agudonba is present, there will definitely be no loss. As a result, both parties expressed their willingness to go and indicated that they would abide by the verdict.
As a result, the three of them arrived at the bottom of the mountain, followed by many people watching the lively scene.
Agutomba made a majestic and pious gesture, demanding that both sides of the dispute kneel down and waved to the crowd behind him:
“Quiet! I am now starting to ask Yamashin to make a sacred judgment.”
“Does this pine snail belong to the shepherd? Or should it belong to herder Zacidon Zhu?” When he asked this sentence, he deliberately lowered the voice of the first half of the sentence, and when it came to the second half, he also deliberately raised his voice higher. Therefore, as soon as his question fell off, the echo in the mountains was transmitted very clearly, and people only heard:
“It still belongs to herder Zaxidonzhu!”
For the sake of prudence, Agutomba asked three times in a row, and the answer was: “It should be owned by herder Zacidonzhu!”
In the face of this mountain god's “divine judgment,” the shepherd had to bow down and confess defeat and was speechless. Finally, I saw him bow up, wipe the dust off his lap with his fat sleeves, and walk towards his place in a hurry.
