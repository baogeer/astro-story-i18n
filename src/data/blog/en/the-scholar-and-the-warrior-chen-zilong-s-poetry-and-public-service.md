 Chen Zilong, Chu Ming Sui, is a native of Huating County, Songjiang Province. He is a famous writer and poet during the Ming season. He is known as the “Ming Dynasty Dianjun” and also known as “the first lyricist of the Ming Dynasty”. He co-organized the restoration of the Society with Zhang Pu and others.
The Restoration Society is not only a poetry club, but also an important party organization. As the leader of the restoration of society, Chen Zilong played a major role in the political situation in the late Ming Dynasty. He served many times during the war against the Qing dynasty, and his unyielding spirit and style are worth remembering.
Associations and accession to the WTO
In the Ming Dynasty, warriors formed cultural clubs to discuss their studies and strengthen their character. Political disputes became increasingly intense at the end of the Ming Dynasty. The Cultural Society actually participated in politics because it was passionate about commenting on political affairs, opposing empty talk, and concerned about society, and gradually took on the color of a political party.
In the second year of Chong Zhen, Chen Zilong, Xia Yunyi, Du Linzheng, and others promoted retro in Songjiang. “There were a few revivals, but they learned the meaning of God,” so they formed several societies, known as the “Cloud School” at the time. In the same year, Zhang Pu and others gathered several local cultural societies to form alliances, such as Jiangbei Nansha, Jiangxi Zeshe, Zhongzhou Duan Society, and Laiyang Yixie, to create a nationwide political cultural society restoration society.
Most of the reinstatement members are young men and women. In total, there are more than 2,000 people, and their popularity has spread all over the country. Most of these young men are full of political enthusiasm, calling for retro and practical use. Their works focus on reflecting social reality, exposing powerful and traitors, sympathize with people's livelihood and suffering, express pride in serving the country, and are contagious. Among them, Zhang Pu's essays and Chen Zilong's poems are outstanding representatives.
Early Qing poetry leader Wang Shizhen commented on Chen Zilong's poem in “Notes of Xiang Zu”: “Shen Xiong is magnificent. Modern writers are unrivaled; almost all of them are ancient talents.” 20th century lyricist Long Yusheng commented, “Linguistics declined in the Ming Dynasty, when Zilong came out, and Zong style flourished, so there has been a boom in rhetoric since the beginning of 300 years.”
Academically recognized by the academic community, Chen Zilong's style of speech is gentle and rich. “He doesn't use shouting and scolding as a sign of strength and festivity,” yet he is deeply moved because of his national spirit. “He has great talent and strong writing, and is well deserved.”
The first task after the establishment of the Resurgence Society was to fight for the field. In the third year of Chong Zhen, Chen Zilong went to Nanjing to take the township test, winning 75th place. The following year, Chun Yu returned home, had a bad mood and “missed the sound wine”, and met the talented girl Liu Ruze. In the 7th year of Chong Zhen, Chen Zilong lost the field for the second time. He had a close relationship with Liu Ru, but was opposed by his family and had to break up. Chen Yinke examined the situation in detail in the book “The Legend of Liu Ru Shi Bye”.
Fortunately, Chen Zilong did not calm down; instead, he actively hosted poetry activities and compiled a selection of essays, publishing “Selected Texts of Renshen” in the 5th year of Chong Zhen, compiling “Chen Li Changhe Collection” with Li Wen in the 6th year, and Chong Zhen writing more than 100 herbs of Ancient Lefu in the 7th year, etc.
In Chong Zhen's ten years, Chen Zilong went to Beijing for the third time to test, ranking 17th in the top three. Although Chen Zilong is a newcomer, because he is the founder of the Restoration Society, his fame can be described as prominent. The Duke has discussed it a lot, so he is “regarded as the party leader” by his political opponents. In the summer of the same year, Chen Zilong went south to take office as Huizhou's procurator; halfway through, his stepmother Tang died, and he returned to Songjiang to maintain the system.
In the 11th year of Chong Zhen, Chen Zilong, Xu Fuyuan, and Song Zhengbi organized 146 people from all over the country to co-compile the “Compilation of the Imperial Ming Dynasty”, which included 500 works from the Ming Dynasty, including cabinet aviation, general military administration, border and coastal defense, public security, imperial court courtesy, fiscal taxation, judicial security, and water conservancy construction.
The purpose of compiling this book series is to find a way out for the Ming Dynasty, which is suffering from internal and external worries. It is a “knowledge of financial governance” contributed to the country by colleagues in the recovery of society. Soon after the book was completed, Chen Zilong sent a book to Ni Yuanlu and Zhang Guowei requesting a preface to be printed. Incidentally, Xu Guangqi's “Complete Book on Agricultural Administration” was copied to Zhang Guowei, hoping that the rulers could gain experience in saving the country.
Xu Guangqi is a representative of the ideas applied to the world in the Chong Zhen dynasty. He is also a leader of Chen Zilong and the Fosha people. In the sixth year of Chong Zhen, Xu Guangqi died of illness in Beijing; Chen Zilong happened to have come from the North to test and took the initiative to take on the affairs behind him. In the 8th year of Chong Zhen, Chen Zilong obtained the “Complete Book on Agricultural Administration” from Xu Guangqi's grandson. After recording, he presented it to Fangyuegong, the governor of Songjiang Province. In the 12th year of Chong Zhen, Chen Zilong also organized members of several clubs to edit the “Complete Book on Agricultural Administration”, and also compiled a collection of essays by Prime Minister Tang Yu Lu and Li Deyu.
The Path of Eunuchu and Qi
In the 13th year of Chong Zhen, Chen Zilong completed his term of office, and was appointed as Shaoxing's commissioner in June, which quickly showed extraordinary economic skills. At the time, there were years of flooding and droughts in Zhuji, and hungry people were looting everywhere. Chen Zilong was commissioned for county affairs. While calming the unrest and raising food and rice to help the poor, the situation improved rapidly. In the 15th year of Chong Zhen, Chen Zilong was the governor of Shaoxing.
Chen Zilong also has outstanding military skills. Chuzhou is located in Tongqu in the three provinces of Fujian, Zhejiang, and Jiangxi. It is known as the birthplace of the rebellion, and the government and army have not been able to make peace for a long time. According to historical records, Chen Zilong and others were ordered to attack the state, and the unrest quickly subsided.
In the 16th year of Chong Zhen, Li Zicheng went south to Chengtian and De'an, and Chen Zilong actively built the capital of Shaoxing and rectifying military equipment. Zheng Sanjun, the chief secretary of the Ministry of Public Affairs, recommended Chen Zilong as “the leader of integrity”, fully affirming his political achievements.
In the winter of this year, Zhang Xianzhong's army went south to Yuanzhou and Ji'an in Jiangxi, and shipping was blocked again. The situation in Nanjing was critical. In December, Chen Zilong raised 70,000 koku of grain and rice from Shaoxing and personally escorted them into the warehouse, solving the urgent need for Nanjing to cut military pay in March.
Before Chen Zilong left Beijing, he heard that the Dongyang people had rebelled. During the month of the Moon, they had already broken through Dongyang, Yiwu, and Pujiang, and invaded Jinhua, a “great earthquake all over Zhejiang.” At the time, Zhejiang Province had no leader. Chen Zilong led the Shaoxing Army with thousands of people to guard and win the battle, and Xu Dubing was defeated and beg to surrender. Xu was in disarray, and the imperial court gave Chen Zilong a military command in the middle of the incident, and Beijing fell.
On the eve of the fall of Beijing, Chen Zilong wrote Zheng Xuan, the imperial history of Nanjing, and Shang Kefa. He proposed sending personnel north to welcome Taizi from the south of the sea, and also proposed that the Songjiang Sailors go north to take over.
Chen Zilong resides in Shaoxing, but he has become a mainstay of stabilizing the situation in Zhejiang and even Jiangnan, and is the main implementer of the strategy of restoring society based in Jiangnan to save the world. Regrettably, at the end of Chong Zhen's period, politics became increasingly corrupt, and party disputes became more intense. After all, the restoration of society failed to save the world.
In June of Hongguang's first year, Chen Zilong and others set up troops to resist in Songjiang, and the military called “Zhenwu.” On August 3, the Songjiang River fell, and Chen Zilong's first anti-Qing struggle failed.
In the second year of Shunzhi, Chen Zilong took refuge in Taozhuang, trusted a monk, and still led the anti-Qing forces in the Jiangnan region. However, as the Qing army went south again, the Fujian and Zhejiang armies were defeated, and the anti-Qing forces of the Soviet and Song dynasties once again disintegrated, and Chen Zilong's second anti-Qing operation failed.
In April of Shunzhi 4, Admiral Wu Zhaosheng of the Qing Song River rebelled, and Chen Zilong was arrested. On May 3, Chen Zilong fell into the water and died.
Chen Zilong, who died at the end of the Ming Dynasty, was recognized as a poet in late Ming. His style of poetry was either sad and bleak, echoing the national spirit; or magnificent and intense, comparable to Qi Liang's early Tang dynasty, completely forming a magnificent and unique style, which is impressive to read.
The former, for example, “Swords rang out in the box last night, Yan and Zhao's songs were the most uneasy” and “their eyes were full of bliss. Dongfeng used to be popular. There is only a few smokes, and only flowers are hard to protect”; the latter is “testing the fruit under a small peach branch, leaving behind the scent. “The Jade Wheel Flattens the Herb, Half Annoyed with Red Makeup” and “I first dreamed of a red mansion last night, surrounded by peach blossoms and trees” are all masterpieces of family music from ancient and modern times.
At the same time, Chen Zilong and the members of the Fushe Society attached importance to the study applied to the world and actively worked in the southeast to save the world, reflecting the valuable character of a writer who helped the world. In the struggle to defend his country, his patriotism and literary style were further promoted.
