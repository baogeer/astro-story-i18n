 At the end of the Qing dynasty, there was a talent named Ke Kuangsheng in Shaozhou. He was proud and loved to teach village children to read, so everyone in the village respected him as “Mr. Xiucai.”
On this day, Ke Kuangsheng was teaching the village children to read Tang poetry at home. Suddenly, Yao Laohan from the neighboring village walked in and “knelt” in front of him. Ke Kuangsheng hurriedly put down his collection of poems and picked up Yao Laohan and asked, “Yao Laohan, aren't you compromising my life? Get up quickly, sit down and say something slowly.” After Yao Laohan sat down, he told the story of his son being wronged by others.
It turns out that Yao Laohan's son, Yao Genping, makes a living selling tofu. A few days ago, Yao Genping carried a bag of tofu and sold it along the village. It wasn't until sunset in Xishan that he sold out the tofu. He didn't dare to slow down. He hurriedly packed up his goods and rushed home. However, when he walked to the back garden of the Zhang Family in Yuncun, it suddenly started raining heavily. He was sad that he had nowhere to hide from the rain, but he happened to be seen by Miss Zhang from the embroidery room.
As soon as Miss Zhang saw Yao Genping, she suddenly remembered that when she went to Yuqing in the spring to pay her wish, she met criminals on the way. Thanks to Yao Genping's rescue at the time. Right now, when she saw the heavy rain pouring down, how could she bear to let her benefactor endure the wind and rain, so she hurriedly sent her maid, Mei Hua, downstairs and invited Yao Genping to stay in the house to shelter from the rain. When Yao Genping saw that the rain was getting heavier, he made no excuses and entered the Zhang family through the corner gate of the back garden.
The two walked downstairs to the embroidered house, and the maid stopped them and said, “This is a back garden. Outsiders are not allowed to enter. I'll ask the young lady where the boy should be sheltered from the rain.” After talking, I went to the embroidery house.
Miss Zhang saw Yao Genping as a lifesaver. Without hesitation, she said to the young lady, “It's raining a lot at this time, so I can't help telling my father. Let Gong Yao go upstairs to take shelter from the rain, then report it to my father.”
As a result, the maid went downstairs and invited Yao Genping upstairs. Yao Genping saw that it was Miss Embroidered House. Wherever he dared to go, he had to leave when he was burdened with goods. As soon as the maid saw a hurry, she took Yao Genping and walked upstairs. Upstairs, Miss Zhang saw that Yao Genping refused to go upstairs. Knowing that he was a nuisance to men and women, she brought rain gear downstairs.
Who knows, just at this point, Zhang Yuanwai happened to walk out of the room. As soon as she saw her daughter, the maid, and Yao Genping tugging on the stairs, they immediately rushed forward in anger and scolded the three. After scolding him, he called Jia Ding, kidnapped Yao Genping, and escorted him to the county government to be charged.
The county commissioner was a confused official, and he also had dealings with Zhang Yuanwai. He preferred to listen to Zhang Yuanwei's remarks and did not allow Yao Genping to argue. He convicted him to death when he arrived, and promised that Zhang Yuan would be executed when he arrived.
Hearing this, Ke Kuangsheng couldn't help but ask, “Yao Laohan, how do you know about these things?”
Yao Laohan said, “After the incident happened, Miss Zhang visited my family's home and came to let me know.” Ke Kuangsheng nodded and said, “That's how it was.”
With tears in his eyes, Yao Laohan also said, “The old man learned that the child had been wronged and wanted to save the child, but he had nothing to do, so he ventured to come and ask Mr. to take pity on the child's innocence and save his life. This is some money. It's not a matter of respect. I hope Mr. will laugh.” He said he pulled a few pieces of silver out of his arms.
Ke Kuangsheng said, “When power and money were the only way to get things done, I was poor, but I was definitely not that kind of greedy villain; I didn't get a share of the money. But to save Ling Lang, this money can come in handy.”
After listening, Yao Laohan asked inexplicably, “I don't know if Mr. Xiucai has any ingenious ideas?”
With this effort, Ke Kuangsheng already had an idea. He smiled sly at the old man, and then told Yao Laohan all over again. Yao Laohan listened and even said, “Wonderful, wonderful!”
This morning, Yao Genping was about to be executed. Zhang Yuanwai has already arrived at the courtroom. The county grandpa carried out routine duties, read the prison department's approval to the prisoner, and asked the prisoner Yao Genping what else he wanted to say. Yao Genping shouted, “The old man from the county is clear, the villain is really wrong!”
The old man from the county shouted loudly, “Shut up, Diao Min. You already confessed during the last interrogation in this county. Why are you retracting your confession today?”
Yao Genping said, “The last time the county boss was interrogated, he was partial to Zhang Yuan's comments and forced the villains to draw confessions. The villains couldn't be punished; they had to succumb to the trick. I wanted to appeal for a retrial in the future; as expected, they would have to be executed today! Grandpa, now it's unclear whether it is right or wrong. There is no way to tell the difference. It's really a big mistake for a villain to die like this!”
“Bold!” The old man from the prefecture angrily shouted, “Could this prefecture have made a mistake?”
“Grandpa, Rong Xiaoren dared to ask, is it true that a female person also does the kind of act of looking for flowers and asking Liu?”
“Oh, how do you say this?”
“The old man doesn't know. The villain is the Taoist Gu. The head of Yuqing Horse Trail can testify!”
“You, what are you talking about?”
Yao Genping repeated what he had just said. The county grandpa couldn't help but stared straight at Zhang Yuan when he heard it.
Zhang Yuan looked over and looked at Yao Genping. Yao Genping also asked, “Other than Zhang Yuan, how can my female generation do such an elegant thing with your young lady? You are so unbiased between men and women and kidnapped me to the county government office to prosecute the crime. Isn't the lady in your family guilty of the same crime as me?”
“Huh?” Zhang Yuan was both shy and annoyed. He hurriedly stepped forward and said to the county grandpa, “This diaotic people are talking nonsense, old man...”
