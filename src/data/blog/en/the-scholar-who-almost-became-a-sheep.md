 A certain Duke from Shaanxi was a gentleman during the Xin Chou years and was able to remember the events of his past life. It is often said to people that his previous birth was a book reader, and he died in middle age. After his death, I saw Hades judge the case. There was a boiling oil pan in front of the main hall, just like the world said. On the east side of the main hall, there are several shelves. The shelves are covered with fur from livestock such as pigs, sheep, dogs, and horses. The official in charge of the book of life and death read the person's name and read that someone was punished as a horse, or punished as a pig, so the kid stripped him naked and took this kind of skin off the shelf to put it on him.
When the bookkeeper thought of a certain lord, Grandpa Yan said, “You should punish him as a sheep.” So the kid took an Aries skin and draped it over his body. At this point, the bookkeeper said, “This man saved a person's life.” After listening, Hades reviewed the records and said, “Forget it! He has committed many evils, but saving people can atone for his sins.” The kid took the sheepskin off of him again, but the sheepskin was stuck to his body at this point and he couldn't take it off. As a result, the two ghosts took him by the arms and pressed him on his chest, so that the pain was unbearable. The sheepskin was torn off piece by piece, and it never came off. There was still a small piece of sheepskin on the shoulder, the size of a slap. After a certain father-in-law was born, a bunch of wool still grew on his shoulders. It was cut, and it was still long.
