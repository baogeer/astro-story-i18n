After listening to it, Qin Zhenyou couldn't help but be happy and rushed home without stopping. In order to get home as soon as possible, he walked down the street.
But it didn't take long for Qin Zhenyou to find out that he had lost his way. He came to a village with beautiful scenery, which felt like a hideaway, but none of the people here were dressed in dynasty costumes.
What surprised Qin Zhenyou the most was that apart from children, there were no old people here.
