 On a historic island in the East China Sea, a variety of winter-resistant flowers of five colors grow, and flowers bloom all year round. The island has been uninhabited since ancient times, and it is an inaccessible place.
Zhang Sheng, a native of Dengzhou, likes to explore and find seclusion, and loves hunting. I heard that this place has beautiful scenery, so I prepared a drink and went there by boat alone. At that time, the flowers were in full bloom, and the fragrance drifted for miles. It takes more than 10 people to surround the thickest tree trunk. The pleasant scenery made Zhang Sheng linger and was very comfortable. So I opened the bottle and drank it myself, regretting not bringing anything along. Suddenly, a beautiful woman dressed in a red dress came out of the flower. Seeing Zhang Sheng drinking alone, she smiled and said, “I thought I was extremely interested. I didn't think there was anyone who was more interested and got ahead of me!” Zhang Sheng was surprised to ask her who she was. The woman answered, “I'm a prostitute from Jiaodong. I just came here with Haigongzi. He went somewhere else to play Range Rover, and I couldn't move, so I stayed here and waited for him.” Zhang Sheng was suffering from loneliness. He was very happy to be accompanied by a beautiful woman. He hurriedly invited her to sit down and drink together. The woman spoke softly and made people speechless. Zhang Sheng is very fond of her. He is afraid that after Haigongzi comes, he will not be able to enjoy himself, so he hugged her and made a friendly face. The woman leaned down happily. The two were affectionate. They overheard the furious wind, and the plants broke and made a noise. The woman hurriedly pushed Zhang Sheng up and said, “Sea Boy is here!” Zhang Sheng hurriedly tied up his belt. Looking back in surprise, the woman had no idea where she was. Next, I saw a large snake that was thicker than a bucket and sprang out of the trees. Zhang Sheng was afraid and hurriedly hid behind a big tree, hoping the snake hadn't seen him. The snake came close and entangled its body a few times with people and trees. Zhang Sheng's two arms are wrapped in the middle of his hips, and they can't be bent at all. At this point, the snake held its head high and pierced Zhang Sheng's nose with its tongue. The nosebleed continued to drip down, forming a small hole on the ground, and the snake bowed down to drink blood. Zhang Sheng thought he would die. Suddenly I thought of Poison Fox's medicine in the purse bag tied around my waist. I just picked up the medicine with two fingers and broke it into the palm of my hand; I turned my head and looked at the palm of my hand and let blood drip onto the medicine, and in a blink of an eye, it was filled with blood. Sure enough, the snake drank blood from its palm. Before it finished, it suddenly straightened its body, swung its tail violently, making a thunderbolt sound, and all the trees it touched were stopped and swept off. Soon after, it fell to the ground like a roof beam and died. Zhang Sheng was so frightened that he fell to the ground and couldn't stand up. After a while, he woke up and carried the snake back in a boat.
When he got home, he had a serious illness and only recovered a month later. He suspected that woman was also a snake spirit.
