 The beautiful mythical story “In Search of Happiness” tells the story of a young Maonan ethnic group living in poverty in ancient times. He wants to ask the “Immortals of the Three Realms” and seek happiness from him. Along the way, he first promised an old man to help him ask the “Three Realms Immortals,” why her daughter still wouldn't speak at the age of 20; then he promised a farmer to ask the “Three Realms” why the peaches and plums in his home only bloom and don't bear fruit; finally, he also promised the “Big Carp” to ask the “Three Realms Fairy,” why can't they jump to Dragon Gate when they're so big, etc.
This young man went through a lot of hard work, and I don't know how many miles he has traveled, and finally met the “Three Realms Immortals.” But when he asked those three things clearly for others, he forgot that he had come to the “Three Realms” to find happiness. On the way back, one by one, he told others what he had ordered him to do. When he arrived at the old man's village, the girl who couldn't talk saw him coming and suddenly turned around and ran back to the house, laughed and shouted “dad” a few times. She has been talking since then. The old man was very moved by the young man's lofty virtues. He was happy to have his daughter as a parent, and those looking for happiness finally found happiness.
