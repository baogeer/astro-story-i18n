A feud at a banquet
Brazil, the capital of Brasilia, during a dinner party after the World Biological Sciences Conference, Professor Ellis of the United States casually said, “There is no greater animal on land than an elephant!”
Professor Oteris of Brazil smiled and objected: “No, our South American Amazon River monkey is bigger than elephants! ”
Although Ellis is a scholar, he has always been famous for his arrogance. As long as he thinks his views are correct, he is not allowed to raise objections from others. At this point, he saw how dare Otris oppose his views, and immediately smiled back and said, “Dear Professor Autris, as far as I know, the Amazon River Monty Python is only as good as an African elephant's leg!”
Otris was undeterred: “In our Brazilian biology textbooks, it was recorded that the Amazon River giant python weighed 6.5 tons, while the heaviest recorded African elephant was only 6 tons!”
At this point, experts and scholars from various countries also joined the debate, but no one was convinced by the results. Finally, Professor James from England proposed, “Should we give Professor Autrice a year to provide him with a giant python that is larger than an African elephant to make a specimen to prove that the Amazon River giant python is indeed larger than an African elephant!”
Instantly, everyone focused their attention on Professor Otrice, because everyone understood that giant pythons living in the tropical jungle along the Amazon River aren't easy to annoy, not to mention that there are jaguars, lions, crocodiles, and even poisonous spiders that are more ferocious than these animals, as well as the carnivorous plant Sunflower, which changes the color of people.
However, Professor Autris refused to show weakness. He brought his assistant Cole, a shotgun and camera, as well as medicine such as food and sulfur powder to prevent the python attack, and embarked on a journey to find the Amazon River giant python.
Forced to back out
The Amazon River is located at the equator and is the largest river in the world. Otris and her assistant Cole discovered a large number of exotic animals unique to the tropical jungle in the jungle on the banks of the Amazon River. They also discovered many tree pythons and giant pythons, but these giant pythons were not that big, and Cole seemed a bit discouraged.
This afternoon, the two arrived in front of a steep mountain rock. Professor Autris and Cole carefully climbed to the top of the rock. Below is a huge cliff. The wide Amazon River is like a bright silk belt, flowing eastward under their feet.
“What a spectacular view!” Cole was genuinely amazed. Suddenly, his foot slipped and his heavy body shook a bit. He hurriedly shouted, “Professor!”
Otteris was quick eyed and quick. He grabbed Cole's shoulder. Cole took advantage of the opportunity to grab a thick tree vine, but the backpack strap on his shoulder broke. The backpack rolled over and collided with a prominent rock and fell to the bottom of the cliff.
“Oops! The backpack contains sulfur and dry food to protect against pythons!” Cole screamed in a hurry, but looking at the backpack under the cliff, the two were helpless.
“Looks like we just have to go back, Cole!” Otris smiled bitterly and shrugged her shoulders.
A chance encounter with a giant python
The two returned to the expansive marshland and saw that it was already evening, so they set up their own tents and prepared to camp.
Originally, after the tent was set up, a circle of sulfur powder had to be sprinkled around to prevent attacks by pythons and other toxic animals, but now the sulfur powder has been lost. Just be careful this night.
Otris is boiling water on the outside of the tent. Cole is carrying his only shotgun and is preparing to fight some small animals to fill his food. When Otris brought the water to a boil, she took out her diary and pen to keep a diary. Suddenly, he heard a long roar coming from afar. He looked up and was so frightened that he shivered. It turns out that on the other side of the river, a jaguar with seven- to eight-foot feet was staring at itself with a pair of green, quiet eyes. This type of jaguar, known as the king of the forest, is extremely ferocious. It can swim, climb trees, and as soon as it hits its prey, it relentlessly tracks and hunts until it catches its prey.
At this point, the jaguar yelled again, and couldn't wait to jump into the rushing river and swim here.
Professor Otrice had forgotten his fears at this point. He thought he would die anyway, so why not leave a few precious pictures. He raised his camera with his hands and pointed the camera at the King of the Forest who was swimming towards him.
Who knows? Just at this moment, the river in the camera looked like boiled water, but all of a sudden it boils. The jaguar on the surface of the river chirped and yelled, and quickly sank into the water. Professor Otrice knows that even though jaguars are known as “good swimmers,” they don't scuba dive. What is the matter?
When Professor Autris was shocked, he suddenly saw a huge dark, dark, phosphorescent head suddenly discovered in the turbulent stream of water.
“Giant python!” Professor Oteris couldn't help but scream. I saw that giant python vomited and had a long, red tongue. After twisting it a few times in the water, it swam ashore.
Professor Otrice woke up and went through all the hardships and risks, and the giant python he wanted to find was right around the corner, right in front of him? Only then did he remember the camera in his hand. He knew his life was at stake, but he still raised the camera and pressed the shutter towards the monster.
The monkey climbed ashore. Professor Autris looked at the monkey's head. It was the size of a writing desk. It was no shorter than 80 feet, and its thick stomach was slowly squirming. Professor Otrice knows, of course, that part of the peristalsis is the unlucky jaguar that had just been swallowed in the water struggling.
Otris didn't run away. He understood that if he wanted to run on his two legs, he would be overtaken by Monty Python. As long as the monkey stuck out a bright red long letter six to seven feet from him, his entire 150-pound body would be sucked into the Monty Python's stomach to be accompanied by the jaguar, the unlucky king of the mountains. The only thing I can do now is take a few more pictures of Monty Python before being devoured by Monty Python, and then quickly throw the camera away to save the film inside the camera.
However, the monkey wasn't moved by the camera's flashing light at all. After climbing ashore, it actually curled up on a soft sand about 40 feet from the professor, yet those two green, quiet eyes still looked at Professor Autris intentionally or unintentionally.
Professor Otrice suddenly realized that the giant python had just swallowed the fat jaguar and was waiting for the jaguar to digest it in its stomach. During this time, the giant python generally did not eat or attack other animals; unless another animal invaded it, it would fight back.
brave crocodile mother
While Professor Autris was filming, suddenly, another scene left him dazed. He saw a giant crocodile no less than ten feet long, unaware of where it was burrowing out, staring at two frightening eyes and slowly approaching the giant python. At this point, the giant python was focusing its attention on Professor Autris, and he didn't notice any danger was coming upon him.
The crocodile swam furiously, and at the same time opened its big mouth like a giant gate, and bit the monkey's tail in one bite.
The giant python was suddenly attacked and couldn't help but be furious. As soon as the giant head flutted, it shot like an arrow at the giant crocodile. The giant crocodile cleverly flashed and fled.
The monkey flung its injured tail and confronted the crocodile. Strangely enough, the crocodile didn't run away, but slowly circled around the monkey. The monkey also curled up in a circle, erected a huge head, spit red letters in its mouth, and turned around with the crocodile.
Right here, Professor Otris' assistant Cole came back whistling. He carried a shotgun on his shoulder and a colourful mountain chicken in his hand. The local community couldn't help but be amazed when they saw this confrontation between monkeys and crocodiles.
Professor Otrice discovered that in this duel, the crocodiles always took the initiative to attack. Generally speaking, an alligator cannot fight a giant python. Normally, as soon as an alligator sees a giant python, it always retreats without fighting. Today, this crocodile is a bit abnormal. It has had a few chances to escape, but has not escaped; instead, it has repeatedly launched strong attacks on giant pythons.
Finally, using its advantage, Monty Python looked at the fighter and rolled its tail around the crocodile. It swung around the crocodile, wrapped its body around it, wrapped the crocodile for a few laps, and quickly tied it up.
“The crocodile is finished!” Otris and Cole almost lost their voices in unison.
What puzzled these two spectators, however, was that usually only crocodiles are entangled by giant pythons. After a moment of effort, the crocodile will get entangled in its tongue and spit out of its mouth, and return to the West for the rest of its life. But this time, Monty Python seemed to have tried its best, but the crocodile still tenaciously twirled its neck, opened its big mouth, revealed sharp teeth like a dagger, and bit it on the back of the giant python.
Just like that, after fighting between the crocodile and the monkey for a full 20 minutes, the neck area below the monster's head was almost bitten by the crocodile, revealing Morimori's white bones. Finally, the monkey loosened up. As soon as the crocodile got involved, it climbed out of the monkey's encirclement circle and struggled to climb towards a field of grass.
Professor Autris and Cole both breathed a sigh of relief, and the two quickly got together. Cole asked puzzled, “Professor, I really don't understand why this crocodile is so ferocious, and this giant python seems so useless!”
Otris laughed, “This question is not difficult to explain!” He said, “Pull out the dagger, grab a few steps, and get to the monster's dead body. In the place where the giant python's stomach is slightly raised, he slashed it with force. The monkey's belly was cut open, and the dead body of a huge jaguar was revealed.
Cole understood: “No wonder the monkey originally had a jaguar in its stomach, so when it wrapped around the crocodile, it didn't use enough strength and left a gap, giving the crocodile an opportunity to take advantage!” Colton nodded and said, “However, professor, there's something I don't understand. This crocodile doesn't know that a jaguar in the belly of the giant python is blocking it. Why would it attack the monkey undead?”
Professor Otrice laughed, “Wait a while and you'll know the truth of the matter, because this unlucky monkey invaded the crocodile's home by accident!” He said, picking up Cole, “Let's get out of here and wait until the crocodile has had enough rest and regains its energy, and it will come back!”
After the two arrived at a big tree, Otris changed the camera to another film.
Sure enough, soon, the recovered crocodile came back. I saw the crocodile come to the side of the giant python's body and pick up the soft sand. After a while, some crocodile eggs came out.
Cole slapped his thigh and sighed, “Alas, it turns out that this unlucky monkey had no intention of intermittently resting in places where crocodiles lay their eggs. In order to protect their children, crocodiles can only fight to the death with giant pythons that are stronger than them. Hi, motherly love, how powerful is this great motherly love!”
A week later, when Professor Syotris and Cole arrived by helicopter to the place where the monkey and the crocodile were fighting, the monkey's dead body had become thick bones, and its irony was eaten clean by unknown animals.
Based on the python head and its body more than 80 feet long, it can be estimated that the total weight of this Amazon python is over 6.5 tons. It can be said that it is the largest animal on land.
