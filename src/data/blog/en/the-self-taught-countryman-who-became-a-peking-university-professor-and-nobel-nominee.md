 Elementary school graduates who have never been university professors don't deserve to be nominated for a Nobel Prize.
There is a master of literature who claims to be a country native. He only studied at elementary school in his hometown for a few years, worked as a clerk in the military when he was a teenager, and earned a meager salary. He became Beipiao in his early 20s. Later, he went to college to teach, married one of his students, and married one of his girlfriends, creating a wonderful story of teacher-student love. In the end, he became a professor at the highest university in China, and was nominated twice for the Nobel Prize in Literature.
To put it bluntly, it sounds like a no-brainer.
That's right. He is Shen Congwen. For the sake of love, he can keep his original intentions behind; he is the lowest-educated professor in the Republic of China, yet he is a great writer, praised by Hu Shi: the best novelist in China. His work is delicate and beautiful, like a landscape scroll. After reading it, it was as if the fragrance of a flower filled his heart; his life was full of ups and downs, suffering, and eyesight.
From the text, these two words, I know that his life belongs to literature. According to people, if you want to be famous in history, either be a “feudal official” or go “write a book and set up a story,” these are roughly the two paths. Shen Congwen apparently chose the path of “writing books and setting up stories.”
Looking at modern Chinese literature, almost everyone has a good family background, giving these writers the opportunity to study at a university or study abroad, and further their studies to become a mainstay in the history of Chinese literature. For example, Lu Xun, who was born in Shuxiangmeng, had the opportunity to study in Japan; Qian Zhongshu, who had excellent family conditions, befriended a talented girl like Yang Jiang; in addition, famous literary figures such as Xu Zhimo, Liang Shiqiu, Guo Moruo, Ba Jin, and Hu Shi were all the same; the only exception was Shen Congwen.
This article talks about his literary works, youth experiences, and love and marriage.
Life
Shen Congwen, formerly known as Shen Yuehuan, was born in Fenghuang County in western Hunan Province in December 1902. He ranked fourth out of nine siblings.
At the time, it was in the late Qing dynasty, where corruption was rampant. The current situation was turbulent. He went to the military to work as a scribe before he graduated. Because of his good handwriting, some talent for writing articles, and good looks, he was considered a cultural person in the environment at the time. His army later lost the battle; fortunately, he is still alive.
He decided to go to Beijing to break through. He plans to work and study there. He imagined that he could get into a university through an exam. He said, “Compared with knowledge and power, I am willing to gain wisdom. I am determined to walk into a raw world, put my life on the line, and bet on it.”
Everything was more difficult than expected in Beijing. In the days of the Northern Expedition War, many people were displaced. He went looking for his eldest sister Shen Yuexin and brother-in-law Shinichi Tian. His brother-in-law was unable to find a secure job even after graduating from college. He said, “If you want to ask for some funding, the country people's pride is too much for me.” They didn't pay him, but they gave him a very useful phrase: “If you came for faith, you have to stick to your faith, because other than that, you have nothing.”
One of Shen Congwen's distant cousins found him a small house with briquettes through a relationship where he could stay for free. Shen Congwen's house was moldy and humid. He happily called it a “narrow and moldy little snack”. With no guaranteed income, he was only able to save money. He prepared two steamed buns a day, a little pickles, and when he was well-off, he could have two meals a day, just like that.
He took the entrance exam for the two-year Mandarin class at Yanjing University, but he didn't know anything during the exam. The examiner was very sympathetic to him and refunded the two yuan application fee. It was so easy to get admitted to the Sino-French University, but he couldn't pay the 28 yuan accommodation fee... From this point on, Shen Congwen lost his heart about officially enrolling. At the time, Peking University implemented an open school policy, and the doors of the school were open to all interested people who wanted to learn. Not only were they allowed to freely attend classes, but students were also allowed to use the Peking University library's book fair.
In his dealings with young literary people, he developed a desire to write. To write a novel, he must not only learn modern punctuation, but also learn vernacular from scratch. The manuscripts I wrote were rarely used. When I went to the newspaper to ask questions, I had to be asked for a dime tip from the doorman. With less and less money on his body, Shen Congwen's life became extremely difficult. In his words, he had to do his best every day to “find something to digest.”
Many years later, when he recalls these years, he still feels a great deal of emotion:
“When I arrived in Beijing, I didn't even know punctuation. When I went there, I wanted to get rid of the original environment. Actually, my plans were very small. I wanted to sell newspapers and read books. As soon as I arrived at this place, I learned that there are no opportunities to sell newspapers. Selling newspapers is divided into shares; selling newspapers is not OK. I later discovered that it's not OK to even ask for food. Beijing has very strict rules for asking for food. One street is one street, so you can't 'rebel' at all!”
He even thought about becoming a soldier. One day, Shen Congwen, who was unbearably hungry, saw that “Beijing had a direct warlord at the time, a platoon leader or something, and put a 'recruitment commissioner' flag at their muzzle,” and he followed them and walked to the side of the Tianqiao Juggling Shed. If you press a handprint, you can get the meal expenses. At the end of the day, Shen Congwen slipped away.
At the brink of despair, Shen Congwen tried to write a letter asking for help for writer Yu Duff. After receiving Shen Congwen's request for help, Yu Dafu decided to visit Shen Congwen. It was snowing heavily that day. Seeing Shen Congwen shivering in the cold room, Yu Dafu took off his wool scarf and draped it over Shen Cong's body.
They said a lot this morning. Shen Congwen talks; Yu Duff listens.
Shen Congwen told Yu Dufu that he came to Beijing mainly to obtain the title of a national university. According to Shen Congwen, as long as he can graduate from a national university, at least his future livelihood problems can be solved. Shen Congwen also talked about his family, saying that he hasn't seen his mother or sister in four or five years, and he doesn't even know their lives or deaths.
Shen Congwen said a lot to Yu Duff, and Yu Duff listened quietly. In a later article, Yu Duff explained, “I visited your apartment today to see how you look, and I don't think I can say anything.”
Yu Dafu invited Shen Congwen to have a full meal. At the time, Yu Duff paid 5 yuan to remove the 3 yuan triangle plug from his meal and got Shen Congwen's hands. At that time, Yu Duff's salary in January was only 30 yuan, and Shen Congwen's manuscript fee was only 7 cents at most.
I was deeply moved to meet Yu Duff this time. I went back and wrote an “Open Letter to Youth”:
“It's admirable, but the simplicity and foolishness of your thoughts is just as amazing... After college, you can eat later. Which book did you read this theorem?”
“An old, white-faced, and unreliant literary youth like you, even if they ate bread and tears and diligently stayed under the university window for five or six years. Could it be that the day you get your diploma, a rain of pearl white rice will suddenly fall from the sky? Can you keep things coming to you when you graduate?”
“I've been talking about this for half a day, but I just want to shatter your dream of studying and graduating from college. Now it's up to you. The best strategy is to find something to do.”
Yu Dafu's letter to Shen Congwen shows how difficult it was for the writer to survive at the time. The situation of the whole society at that time was a mess, indicating that an old society needed to be broken down to create a new world. There was no way out of the darkness before dawn. A group of aspiring writers were in a state of confusion, helplessness, distress, and destitute.
A meal, a light gray wool scarf, a phrase “write it down well,” and an excellent article gave a wandering spirit left behind in the cold winter a burning firewood. Shen Congwen's fate has changed since then.
More than half a century later, Yu Dufu's niece Yu Feng recorded when she visited Shen Congwen: “Mr. Shen Congwen was already in his 70s when he said this to me, but he smiled so naively and excited. He said he would never forget that scene for the rest of his life: 'Later, he paid five yuan to go out to eat with me, and he gave me all the money he had recovered. Five bucks back then! '”
In December of this year, under Yu Duff's introduction, Shen Congwen finally published his first article “Distant Night” in the “Morning News Supplement”, which was appreciated by Peking University professor Lin Zaiping.
In 1927, Shen Congwen's first novel collection “Tangerines” was published, marking his official entry into the literary world. He wrote more and more works, and he also got to know more writers later. Xu Zhimo, Zhu Ziqing, and Hu Shi all appreciated his articles.
Later, an acquaintance told him that Sun Fuyuan, the editor-in-chief of the “Morning News Supplement,” used to stack unpublished articles he sent in a pile at the editorial meeting, saying that it was the work of the great writer Shen, then formed a group and threw it away on the spot.
There are always people who appreciate it and others who slander it.
In August 1929, due to the introduction of the poet Xu Zhimo, Hu Shi, the Chinese public school principal, unusually hired Shen Congwen as a lecturer in the Department of Chinese Literature to focus on new literature research and novel writing. This year, Shen Congwen was 26 years old. It was also here that Shen Congwen met his later wife, his student Zhang Zhaohe, but his pursuit was in vain, and he did not change his original intentions.
26-year-old Shen Congwen falls in love with 18-year-old Zhang Zhaohe at first sight
“I've walked bridges in many places, seen clouds many times, and drank many kinds of alcohol, but I only loved someone who was the best age.”
This phrase is just as beautiful in use today.
Since then, it has also become a classic phrase in love letters from the Republic of China.
The “Four Zhang Sisters” in modern Chinese history are second only to the “Three Sisters of the Song Family” in terms of popularity. The four sisters (Yuanhe, Yunhe, Zhaohe, and Chenghe) were all affected by their father Zhang Wuling. Ye Shengtao also said, “Whoever marries the four sisters of the Zhang family will be happy for the rest of their lives.
Zhang Zhaohe is a young lady of three. She is familiar with reading the Four Books and Five Scriptures. She is familiar with the Song Rhythm, Xi Kunqu, and Hao Danqing. Beautiful, charming, and talented.
Zhang Zhao is not necessarily a “school flower” in Chinese public schools, but in Shen Congwen's eyes, he is not one of the most beautiful. The chin is slightly sharp, well-defined, beautiful and unconventional...
One day, Zhang Zhaohe suddenly received a thin letter. After unpacking it, he learned that it was written by his teacher Shen Congwen. The letter only wrote one sentence: “I don't know why I fell in love with you all of a sudden?” Zhang Zhaohe did not reply, followed by a second and third letter. The fourth letter was soon sent. Zhang Zhao couldn't help but be stunned. There are people in the world who “can't help themselves”! When will this endless stream of emails come to an end? Just let him ignore it and see how long he can hold on!
Shen Congwen followed closely and was very tiring, while Zhang Zhaohe was just silent. She thought silence was the best way to say no, but Shen Congwen, regardless of this, still diligently writes his love letters and sends love letters, one a day, or even a few times a day.
A man is confused because of love, and no education can make him smarter, unless that love is dishonest.

I wish I could learn to be a man and love you without bothering you. I love you for a day, always have to live a serious day, and try my best to save you an uneasy day. In order to have people in this world that I will always love, I must work hard to be an individual.
The letter was written too many, too long, and so bold that Zhang Zhaohe finally couldn't stand it. She found Principal Hu Shi's home and told the principal that it was not good for Teacher Shen to write letters to students like this.
Hu Shi wanted to match this talented couple at the time. He advised Zhang Zhaohe to:
Shen Congwen loves you stubbornly!
Hu Shi painstakingly advised Zhang Zhaohe: “If you give him a little love, you can save his soul, not to mention that he is so talented. Why won't you do good deeds?”
Zhang Zhaohe: “But I stubbornly don't love him!”
Shen Congwen went to Zhang Zhao and his roommate Wang Hualian cried that if Zhang Zhaohe did not agree to be in a relationship with him, then he had only two paths to follow. One was positive Kangzhuang Avenue, and the other was when he committed suicide by hanging up a building; he would always have to breathe a sigh of relief.
This is a marathon of unrequited love. Everyone knows he's chasing her, and everyone knows he's madly in love with her. After three full years, his infatuation left her with no way back to face the persuasion of others and Shen Congwen's persistence. Zhang Zhaohe's rock-solid heart was finally shaken.
“Whether his passion is sincere or embellish it with words, I always feel like I did something wrong myself, and therefore fell into the grief of others. But his unwavering love deeply moved me. Before I left this world, I always remember that one day in my mind, I always remember that there was someone in this world who lost the balance of my life and who worked hard on himself in sorrow because of me abandoning a stable life.”
So far, Zhang Zhaohe wrote down this passage in her diary, and since then she has acquiesced in Shen Congwen's passionate feelings.
Zhang Zhao said to him, “I want to ask my father what he meant. If the father agrees, I will report it to you.” Shen Congwen said, “OK, if the results are good, let me as a country person drink sweet wine,” so Zhang Zhaohe later reported: Country people, drink a sweet bar!
After 4 years of intense pursuit, they actually became husband and wife. In September 1933, they had a simple wedding at Beijing's Central Park.
Anyway, this is a love won with love letters.
After her marriage, Shen Congwen continued to use love letters to warm the hearts of her lovers, and continued to use love letters to express her unfinished feelings.
Shen Congwen went to visit her mother in Xiangxi alone. Along the way, Shen Congwen wrote many gentle, emotional, and beautiful sentences.
Zhang Zhaohe was worried: “Will the wind in Changsha also roar so uneasily, blowing my second brother's body to ice? I'm very worried because of this wind, just because I'm sitting in a warm house at this time, and the wind blew my heart cold. I don't know how my brother supports it.”

When Shen wrote about girls in Xiangxi, they always had dark skin when it came to writing. Look at Cui Cui in “Frontier City”. Her skin is dark and dark; Yao Yao in “The Long River” is also dark and attractive. These are all characteristics of Zhang Zhaohe's skin color.

In the first few years of the full outbreak of the War of Resistance against Japan, their love withstood many tests.
After the fall of Beijing, Shen Congwen and several intellectuals went south, and Zhang Zhaohe stayed in Beijing with his two children. In his letter, Zhang Zhaohe repeatedly stated that his reasons for staying in Beijing were: the child needed care; it was not convenient to leave Beijing; Shen Cong had too many letters and manuscripts, which needed to be cleaned up and protected; the whole family followed Shen Congwen, which would drag him down.
Shen Congwen is different. He doesn't seem to care much about the difficulties of real life, and he is still obsessed with his feelings — thoughts, doubts, fears, and anxiety about Zhang Zhaohe, all kinds of intertwined feelings.
Everyone has their own values and code of conduct. Shen Congwen's childlike innocence in her feelings may have worried Zhang Zhaohe. As a young woman, she had to deal with this big man and writer's rambling like a big sister: “I don't like to listen to this kind of nonsense in the letter, what is not free. I don't like to listen to it, and I won't let you talk about it in the future. ... If I write something like that after that, I won't reply to your letter.
Zhang Zhaohe once wrote to Shen Congwen saying, “Don't force me to wear high heels and perm my hair; don't ask me to wash and do my work because I'm afraid I'll make my hands rough. It doesn't matter if what you eat is good or bad, it doesn't matter if you pay attention or not to pay attention to what you wear; being able to survive is already a creation.”
Some people say that Zhang Zhao doesn't love Shen Congwen; if they don't love her, how can they marry him? However, she is a new-style woman. She received a new style of education. When Zhang Zhaohe married Shen Congwen, even if he didn't love her as much, she definitely loved her.
After marriage, one of her, Miss Qianjin, can live a difficult life with him. She can wear simple clothes because she has no money, and she can use her hands to cook him a thick tea and light meal. This is not love; what is love?

What a person looks like is his person. Shen Congwen gives the impression that he is gentle and shy. He is not pompous, not modern, uncombative, friendly, and always smiling, just so calm. Like water, but his personality is also very strong and stubborn, like a mountain. Shen Congwen's literary career is about devoting his life to his ideals.
Ma Yueran, a lifelong judge of the Nobel Prize in Literature, said that Shen Congwen was the greatest writer in China in the 20th century. The more I learned about his greatness, the more I was saddened by the loneliness of his life.
Feng Tang said that Shen Congwen, who only attended elementary school, contributed more to Chinese culture than the current 100 PhDs. To experience the beauty of Chinese, Shen Congwen is a must read.
His work focuses on the world of Xiangxi, which has not been destroyed by urban civilization. He said, “People in the city are in a hurry. Their ears and glasses touch too much sound and light. Other than their sense of lust, their other senses are a bit numb, and they can't discover the essential beauty of life. This is a misfortune for urban people.
Li Jianwu's comments on Shen Congwen's work: “All the characters are cute, as if they meant it or not. He wants readers to leave their worries behind and walk into his ideal world, a real world where they have the courage to meet. Each of these lovely characters has a simple and kind soul”
Amidst beautiful nature and simple humanity, Shen Congwen developed particularly sensitive sensory abilities and superhuman memory and imagination; the ignorance of the townspeople and the cruelty of the warlords also gave him a sense of pain and compassion. This confrontational memory came too early and too strong. In his writing, he always subconsciously avoided this kind of psychological trauma. He will use many things, such as beautiful images of women and simple hometown customs, to ward off the cruelty of the real world. This became an important theme in Shen Congwen's novels.
The sun shines on the earth, and as far as the sun goes, I look up, but I feel that the people and trees in the house and the pool of fresh water all have a lot to do with each other. However, a person's life is like a deep sense of isolation, with nothing to convert to, and nothing to be attached to it. Essay “Underworld”
In real life, there is nothing else that can make life flourish. Only literature “The Void Is Silent, the Reader's Soul Is Music, the Void Clear, and Light and Clean” can always discover the beauty of nature, trace it, and be more appreciative. When words pass through his hands, they can always fly high. The closer a person is to nature, the lighter the state of life. Light versus heavy, simple versus complex, his writing shows a peaceful and liberal personality.
His student, Wang Zengqi, is a writer I really like. His writing is similar to his teacher Shen Congwen. Regarding Mr. Shen's lectures, he said, “Mr. Shen reads a lot of books, but he never quotes the classics. He always speaks according to his instincts. He never says what Aristotle said, what Flubai said, or Tolstoy.”
“Mr. Shen's lecture was very humble and very self-controlled. He doesn't use gestures; he doesn't have any white stage tone; he doesn't have the slightest sense of popularity. He was very sincere and even naive.”
Wang Zengqi said, “I wrote a novel and had many conversations. I tried my best to write the conversation in a beautiful, poetic, and philosophical way. Mr. Shen said this wasn't a conversation between you; it was a fight between two clever brains! Since then, I've learned that dialogue is just plain words spoken by characters, and I want to keep it as simple as possible. Don't be philosophical, don't be poetic. This is the only way to be real.
“For many years, Mr. Shen has been trying to find places to publish others' works. It can be said that there are countless manuscripts introduced by him. The postage he spent on writing articles for others in his life is also quite a considerable amount. In order to prevent being overweight and save on postage, he mostly cut off the paper edge of the original, leaving only the paper core. This certainly doesn't look good. However, during the war of resistance, everything was expensive, so we had to make this little calculation.”
The “Regulations on the Qualification of University Teachers” issued by the Ministry of Education at the time stipulated: “Anyone with special academic research without a degree may act as a university teaching assistant or lecturer after being decided by the university's council. Shen Congwen has taught at Chinese universities, Qingdao University, Southwest Union University, and Peking University.
Along the way, they were supported and helped by other people of insight, including Yu Duff, Xu Zhimo, Hu Shi, Yang Zhensheng, and Zhu Ziqing. There are also opposition voices and bitter voices, such as Qian Zhongshu, Mu Dan, and Liu Wendian. Qian Zhong wrote a novel that harassed him. After the July 7 Incident, Beiping fell, and intellectuals all went into exile in the southwestern provinces. In the spring of 1938, Shen Congwen also traveled back and forth to Kunming. Seeing that Shen Congwen had no career, everyone contemplated hiring him as a professor at Southwest Union University. I just graduated from elementary school, had no experience studying abroad, and didn't even have a high school diploma. Wanting to stand out at the talented Southwest Union University is by no means an easy task.
Through the efforts of many professors, at the 111st meeting of the Standing Committee of the Southwest Union Congress held at the end of that month, one issue was to decide to hire Shen Congwen as an associate professor in the Department of Chinese Literature at the Union Master's University. Although it was passed unanimously since then, voices of opposition have persisted for a long time.
On May 3, 1946, all teachers and students of the Chinese Department of Southwest Union University took a group photo in front of the classroom.
In July of the following year, the university's executive meeting decided to “replace Mr. Shen Congwen as a professor in the Department of Chinese Literature at the University's Normal College, with a monthly salary of 360 yuan. At this title evaluation meeting, Professor Liu Wendian was furious: “Chen Yinke is the real professor; he should have paid 400 yuan. I, Liu Wendian, should have paid 40 yuan; he, Zhu Ziqing, should have taken 4 yuan — he, Shen Congwen, is only worth 4 cents!”
A few days later, there was a running alert. Shen Congwen happened to be brushing his shoulder by Liu Wendian. Liu Wendian showed disdain: “I ran to preserve the national quintessence; the students ran to preserve the hopes of the next generation, but damn it. Why did you run Shen Congwen?”
Does he care about these rumours?
After victory in the War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression, Shen Congwen moved with his troops to Beiping and became a professor at Peking University.
Shen Congwen has written extensively throughout his life. The “Complete Works of Shen Congwen” has just been published in 32 volumes, with more than 10 million words
In the mid-30s, Shen Congwen wrote quite confidently: “... To be fair, I'm really superior to some of today's so-called writers. My work will go above and beyond. My work will go on longer and broadcast farther than the work of these people. I have no way to say no.” (“From the Book of the Family: Brief History of Xiang Xiang”) Sure enough, Shen Congwen's work withstood the test of the times and time and became classics, and Shen Congwen's prophecies became reality
