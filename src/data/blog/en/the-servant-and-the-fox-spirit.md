 It is said that there was a servant at Koroku-ji Temple. One night, he was sleeping in front of the court. Suddenly, he saw a bright light upstairs, like a star. After a while, the light floated like a firefly, slowly came to the servant, fell to the ground, and became a fox. The servant was very strange, but he didn't think much about it. The fox made a hint to the servant, then walked inside.
When I got to the backyard, this fox turned into a woman. Only then did the servant know that it was a fox fairy. The servant pretended not to know, went back to his house and lay down ready to rest. The woman actually walked over and leaned over the servant and shook him. The servant pretended to have woken up and asked her who she was, but the woman didn't answer. The servant said, “Are you the bright light upstairs?” The woman answered, “If you know, why should you ask?” As a result, the two took a break together.
After a long time, the two of them got used to it; that woman came every night. The servant's owner was very angry when he found out about this, so he called two other servants to put them in the middle. But when people woke up, they found that both of them were under the bed, and they didn't know when they fell.
The owner was even more angry and said to the servant, “If she comes back, take her back; otherwise, I want you to look good.” The servant thought, “How could I catch her? But if I don't catch her, I'll be fired.” Thinking back and forth, he suddenly remembered that when a woman slept, her close-fitting clothing was a small red shirt that had never been taken off. That must have been her key; getting this dress would definitely threaten her.
At night, the woman came as always and asked, “Did your landlord ask you to arrest me?” The servant answered, “He did say this, but why would I do it?” After falling asleep, the servant secretly grabbed her clothes. As a result, the fox fairy shouted and instantly broke free. She disappeared without a trace, and never appeared again.
Later, the servant went to work abroad. On the way back, he saw the woman. He walked up and covered her face with her sleeve. The servant said, “Why should you do this?” The fox fairy woman said, “Looks like you haven't forgotten me. Since you haven't forgotten, I can forgive you. After all, it was an order from your master; I don't blame you. But you and I are at an end. Why don't you go to my house to eat before leaving? Think of it as a farewell.”
This is the harvest season. The sorghum grew very tall and dense. The fox fairy walked in with her servant. Soon after, they saw a tall house, and they started drinking it. When the sun went down, it was time for the servant to go back, so he got up and left. When I came out to take a look, it was still a sorghum field, and the big house was gone.
