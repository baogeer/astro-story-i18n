 Author: Su Zinong Chen Qingsong
In March of Yangchun, Vientiane was renewed, pink and willow green. A few daughters of Emperor Jade were stranded at home. After taking a nap, I arrived at the sparkling and brightly colored Yixin Island in the Tianhe River. They either sang and danced, or wrote the right poem, and wandered freely in the fairy boat for half a day. It wasn't until late in the evening that I gracefully returned to Tiangong's Shenyuan boudoir under the guidance of my elder sister.
After not leaving the palace for a long time, the sisters seemed to have no end of fun. They remember drinking and groaning in Yixin Island, lazing in the boat in the Tianhe River, and talking nonstop in the boudoir.
Suddenly, the elder sister frowned and said, “Not good!” When the sisters saw the situation and didn't know what had happened, they all hurriedly leaned over to the elder sister. I saw the elder sister say, “My little sister is already suffering in the mortal world. If you take a close look, it's a difficult scent that refreshes your nose and clears your mind.” Sisters — Smell it, it's definitely a bad smell.
It turns out that this difficult fragrance was prepared by the barefoot daisen using 981 flavored herbal elixir and later given to the royal mother. It's a woman's strength book. If you run into an accident, you can't defend yourself. If you burn an incense stick and read the true mantra of difficult incense, you can also ask for help from your loved ones. Mother Wang thought to herself, I've been with Emperor Jade all the time, and I have the protection of the Heavenly Soldier. What else can I do? It's just that the seven daughters aren't around, and sometimes they don't feel at ease. Why don't you give them one or two help when the time comes. As a result, I gave this difficult fragrance to my eldest daughter.
When the Seven Fairies were handed over this time, the eldest sister drew three and handed them over to her and explained the method she used. Tonight, when I suddenly smelled bad, I knew something was wrong with the Seven Girls.
Under the arrangement of the elder sister, the six sisters prepared in a hurry, so they took advantage of the night and headed all the way to Fujiawan. When the seven sisters met, it was too late to be friendly and have a conversation, so they all got busy. Because Fu Yuan went to the Seven Fairies, they had to weave a bunch of silk into ten silk silk before the chicken barked three times.
It's still Fairy Girl's ingenuity. Before the chicken barked three times, the ten brocade silk were certainly well woven, and she also woven one more pocket for the little baby.
Seeing that the time had come, the sisters reluctantly left the Seven Fairies. Just a short walk away, the eldest sister suddenly thought of something: when she left Heavenly Court, she also brought a pack of peerless striped tea seeds to Seven Sisters, and she forgot to throw them away for Seven Sisters.
It turns out that all kinds of tea trees in the sky were given to the world by Emperor Jade, but the essence of this striped tea tree is still in the sky. No other reason, just because the leaves of this tea tree don't have veins. The Jade Emperor simply did not allow tea tree essences over and over again, saying that tea was not tea, no tree, and the leaves were as bright as gourds, and had no good appearance.
This tea tree essence was very sad, and there was nothing to be done. One person had to go back to the deep mountain in a daze and continue to practice it. It thought: I'm not afraid you, the Jade Emperor won't let me go down to earth until the aroma lasts for a long time, the soup is bright yellow and green, the taste is mellow and refreshing, and the aftertaste is rich and sweet. Thinking about it this way, tea tree essence calms the mind and can practice without hesitation.
I don't know how many years have passed, and the tea tree essence feels that it has been completely repaired. One day, it came to Tiangong again and found an old friend, General Ling Xiaoden, and wanted the general to take it to the Jade Emperor to make a statement. General Rolling Curtain agreed.
We haven't met an old friend for a long time. We had a great time talking. Unsurprisingly, the weather was too late. The general said he couldn't go today; let's talk about it tomorrow.
At the time of the light, tea tree essence falls asleep, but at first sight, there are no futons or mats.
The general said, “I mostly have bamboo curtains here, which can be used as a mat. As for the mat, aren't the leaves on your body ready-made?”
As soon as I heard the tea tree essence, I thought this was a good idea, and all the leaves were brushed and spread around the wall. The general took a bamboo curtain and spread it over the tea leaves, and the tea tree essence fell asleep.
