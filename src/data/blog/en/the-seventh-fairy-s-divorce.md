 Author: Xu Aihua
On this day, Emperor Jade Emperor was discussing matters with the gods in the Heavenly Court. When he saw the Seven Fairies spreading their beak, she ran away crying and screaming to let her father decide for her and divorce Dong Yong.
Emperor Jade hurriedly asked her daughter, “Why is this? Aren't you getting along well with Dong Yong? When you first fell in love freely, I think Dong Yong is a different kind. I don't agree with your marriage, and I don't want to leave a name for ages. A few years ago, I was forced by your mother and stepped in. As a last resort, we approved your marriage, and arranged a 'human to Africa' for Dong Yong to enroll him in the kingdom of immortals. Otherwise, you wouldn't be on the side of heaven and wanted to wear it?”
The Seven Fairies said, “That's true, but Dong Yong only knew that they had made a fortune by cultivating land and watering. Seeing that everyone was making a fortune, living in a mansion, riding in a car, eating fresh, and having fun, he didn't know how to make money. As a result, the family was poor. Even the money my child went to school was borrowed from my mother in a cheeky manner. I scolded him for nothing. He ignored it and hit me when he said he was in a hurry. Is it reasonable for you to say this is still reasonable?”
Emperor Jade sarcastically said, “In the beginning, you said that husband and wife were both bitter and sweet, but now you can't blame anyone else.” He sank his face and said, “Your father won't let you get divorced from Dong Yong. Do you think the divorce was a joke? If this were to spread, it wouldn't be a big joke!” The Seven Fairies fell into the arms of the Jade Emperor and swallowed: “In the past, I blamed my daughter for being ignorant and blindly pursuing... unrealistic love led to a lifelong mistake. Who knows the taste of being poor is so painful!” Emperor Jade was not moved by her daughter's tears, and did not want to be reprimanded. “Jade Emperor also slowly blamed the Seven Fairies. I think Dong Yong really doesn't know the current affairs. He should have thanked Dade and worked hard to have a good life for the Seven Fairies. Unsurprisingly, he was so ignorant. It's really abhorrent.”
Emperor Yu meditated for a moment and said, “Ai Qing cannot say this. There are times when it is difficult to clear the government about household chores, so let's take a closer look and then talk about it.” The gods were very moved when they saw that Emperor Jade was so reasonable. They even shouted, “My emperor is holy!” Chang'e, the female secretary of the Jade Emperor, interjected, “Why worry about trivial matters? My emperor doesn't need to worry about the Seven Sisters; it's up to Chang'e to take care of it.” Emperor Yu thought about it and felt that she didn't have any good options for a while, so let her go.
Chang'e felt that the Seven Fairies were mainly rooted in the word “poor.” If the Seven Fairies were to become rich, she wouldn't have to die and get divorced. However, how can the Seven Fairies become rich overnight? Chang'e suddenly remembered the repair project for the Imperial Palace Ling Xiao Dian, and immediately had an idea.
Chang'e brought Dong Yong in person to explain the occasion. I saw Dong Yong walk in, wearing a thick cloth jacket, looking depressed. He was so embarrassed in front of Chang'e, dressed in brocade. Chang'e said, “I heard that Dong Yunma had some family problems recently?” Dong Yongwei never burst into tears and said, “It's not about the money yet.” Chang'e said thoughtfully, “If the couple quarreled over the money, you should put more effort into your money and want your wife and children to have a good life.” Dong Yong was aggrieved and said, “Who doesn't want to be rich? But I'm a farmer, one with no special skills; two, I can't beat horses or speculatively drill in the official field; I can only cultivate land to weed and pick water to pour vegetables. Where can I make foreign money?” Chang'e sympathically said, “I have a small fortune here. I want to help the horse. I don't know if the horse is interested or not?” Dong Yong dawned in front of her eyes, rushing Chang'e to talk about where she was rich, and vowed that if there was money to be earned, she would go to Tang to the Fire.
Seeing that the fire had arrived, Chang'e said, “Construction of the Ling Xiao Temple repair project is about to begin. We are looking for a construction team. I think you can also pull up a construction team to package the project. Isn't it a trivial matter to make a fortune?” Dong Yongyi hurriedly shook his head when he heard it and said, “This is impossible. I'm empty-handed, what kind of contractor? Furthermore, the palace repair project has to be tendered. More than a dozen construction companies are already competing to win the project, so I'm afraid it would be more difficult than going to heaven!” Chang'e said with confidence, “I'll give you the technology and capital, and secretly give you the bottom of the bid during the tender. I'm not afraid you won't win.” Dong Yong hardly believed his ears: “Is this a success? But... why are you helping me like this?” Chang'e said, “What's wrong? I made up my mind with the Jade Emperor and contracted the project to whoever saw it. If you want to say why I'm helping you, I blame you for being soft hearted. Too bad you're poor; you can just pick up my Moon Palace when the time comes.” She paused for a moment and said meaningfully, “I really should repair my Moon Palace this month.” Dong Yong nodded as if he understood.
It turns out that Emperor Jade recently went around Fanjian and discovered that over the years, construction of many magnificent high-rise buildings has been carried out. Compared to the imperial palace he came from home, it was much shabby. It wasn't even as good as the tombs of the rich, and his heart was very unbalanced. If they wanted to build a new imperial palace, it would cost an astonishing amount of money, and were afraid that some immortal would get in the way, so they discussed an alternative method with Chang'e to change the construction to repair it. Although it is a repair, the size of the Imperial Palace will increase several times, and the investment is huge. When many construction companies heard this news, it was as if mosquitoes smelled blood and swarmed to find their own way and get their hands on the project. It's true that the Eight Immortals are each showing their ingenuity in crossing the sea. In order to be fair, Emperor Jade arranged a public tender; in fact, it was a secret operation in his own box. As a result, Chang'e came up with the idea of a double arrow and contracted the project to Dong Yong. This would not only help the Seven Fairies get out of poverty and please the Jade Emperor, but she could also sail smoothly and take the opportunity to repair her Moon Palace.
Dong Yong got a good fortune from Chang'e, rushed back to his hometown Dongjia Village overnight, raised a big flag to recruit workers, and put together a “Dong's Construction (Group) Corporation” and became the chairman and general manager. Then I went to Chang'e to imitate their construction company's bid, made a construction plan, and naturally won the bid. Dong Yong knew that his “Dong Company” couldn't do the project, so he subcontracted it to another construction company and collected the profits himself. On the day the foundation was laid, Dong Yong, under the direction of Chang'e, invited Emperor Jade and the gods to visit the scene to cut the ribbon. Amidst a burst of “crackling” firecrackers, construction began, and the atmosphere on the construction site was lively!
One day, Emperor Yu suddenly remembered the divorce of the Seven Fairies, and asked Chang'e how it was, and said it was still good to be inseparable. Chang'e laughed, “Now, even if you force the Seven Sisters to divorce Dong Yong, she won't get divorced.” The Jade Emperor was about to ask Ming's original party. She didn't want the Seven Fairies to suddenly run in, crying and saying that Dong Yong wanted to get divorced from her. She didn't agree, so Dong Yong hit her. As soon as Emperor Yu heard it, he was confused: “You proposed a divorce before. Isn't Dong Yong's agreement right for you? Why aren't you happy anymore?” The Seven Fairies cried, “It used to be before, now it's now. Now that Dong Yong has earned a lot of money, we are about to have a good life. Who still wants a divorce?” Emperor Jade was even more confused: “Then why did Dong Yong get divorced again?” The Seven Fairies said, “Dong Yong made a fortune by contracting the Ling Xiao Dian project. He spent the whole day eating, drinking, and fighting horses. He recently hired another female secretary. What kind of female secretary? Isn't she just a mistress? The two have become a pair every day, and they haven't even gone home. I told him, not only did he not listen, he hit me, but now he is threatening to divorce me.” Only then did Emperor Jade understand, and he thought with hate. Dong Yong, this guy was really disgusting, and then said, “I didn't expect Dong Yonghui to be this kind of poor person; just wait for his father to fix him. But a secretary is a secretary, so why must she be a mistress? Don't talk nonsense in the future.” The Seven Fairies knew for a moment that they were extremely angry. They accidentally touched their father, the emperor and Sister Chang'e. They couldn't help but throw up their tongues and blush. But after hearing the father emperor say they want to clean up Dong Yong, the Seven Fairies are in a hurry again, saying, “Father and Empress, as long as I don't get divorced, don't treat him harshly.” Chang'e, who is on the sidelines, interjected, “This is easy. Some way is that you have to be a bell person. Dong Yong was promoted by me. He still can't listen to what I say.”
A few people were talking. Suddenly there was a loud bang at the back of the palace, like a thunder bombing, and shocked the Jade Emperor. He couldn't help but complain: “This old man, Thunder, you should say hello in advance. He always cuts the afterplay first, and it's getting less and less like talking.” At this point, Taishang Laojun rushed in in a panic, gasped and said, “That's not good... the big deal is bad! The old man inspected the Ling Xiao Dian project and accidentally sneezed. The newly built large hall actually collapsed a hole, killing many workers. I've already said that you can't hand over such a big project to Dong Yong, who doesn't understand architecture. You don't want to listen; this is fine; let's see how it ends.” After listening, Chang'e immediately sank down and said, “What does Laojun mean? At the beginning, Dong's Construction Company was selected through public tenders; in your opinion, it seems as if Emperor Jade cheated.” Taishang Laojun sneered, “Chang'e, don't brag about the Jade Emperor. If that's the case, you should check how Dong Yong bid. Whoever chose him, I think there must be something wrong with this.” Chang'e also had to argue. No matter how the Emperor Yu heard it, she stopped and said, “Okay, it's too early to draw a conclusion. Let's have Chang Aiqing take the lead in organizing an investigation team to investigate and then talk about it. The accident is shocking. It must be investigated to the end. The person responsible, even King Laozi, must be punished according to law.”
After Taishang Laojun and Chang'e left, Emperor Yu said to the Seven Fairies in a daze, “This is fine; if you don't want to get divorced from Dong Yong, you have to leave!”
The Seven Fairies puzzled why. Emperor Jade said, “Don't you understand this yet? The Ling Xiao Dian project stabbed Lou Zi this big. Dong Yong must have been to blame. I wanted your husband and wife to earn two. I can't imagine that the house this kid built couldn't stand a sneeze. Under such circumstances, you're still tied to him, and even I have to get involved. Now we have to do whatever it takes to kill the family!” “But father and emperor...” The Seven Fairies still had to argue about. The Jade Emperor shook her head and seriously said, “There is no need to discuss this; Dong Yong must be brought to account; otherwise, I wouldn't be able to explain it to the gods!”
When the Seven Fairies saw their father and emperor's tone so firm, they had no room for maneuver at all, and couldn't help but cry out loud...
