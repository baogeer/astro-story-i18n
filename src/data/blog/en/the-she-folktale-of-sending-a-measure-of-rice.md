 Once upon a time, there were many mountains and villages around Maoyang Jitiao in Jingning. They cultivated wasteland to grow sweet potatoes and rice fields.
At that time, there was a mountain old man in a village, and the family had only one grandson. Shanha Laodong usually builds bridges and paves roads, and is very hardworking. No matter the wedding or funeral of the family, the Shanha old man will go to help. The village, young and old, have great respect for the young and old in the village.
There is July in the lunar calendar, which is the month of the Great Desert. Shanha Laodong brought a few Buddha babies to build a wooden bridge at the entrance of the village. The old man from Shanha was presumptuous. If he was careless, he fell off the bridge and died.
After the death of Shanha Lao Lao, a funeral will have to be held. His rice bowl in his house is only a few litres of rice. What should I do about the funeral? As the old saying goes, “Ten people can help one.” In the same village, Shanha, each family sent a bucket of rice to carry out Shanha's funeral.
Later, Shanha felt that this was a good habit, and learned to deliver rice every time someone died. After a long period of time, the custom of “sending rice” was formed and continues to this day. However, now in addition to delivering rice, people also send alcohol, money, etc.
Year of the Pig
Every year on New Year's Eve, Shanha slowly burrows a section of the tree in the ashes of a pot stove. It is called the Year of the Pig. Why does Shanha want to raise New Year's pigs?
Legend has it that a long time ago, the Shanha people never raised much pork. Pigs are raised to 340 pounds. They get sick and die. People are busy from the beginning to the end of the year, and they can't even eat a piece of pork. There was a dream flower woman somewhere, and she took advantage of this opportunity to say that Guanyin gave her a dream. As long as you go down a large cedar tree called Sow Mountain near Liao to worship Guanyin, Guanyin will bless that pigs can grow to 300 kg on the scale. All of them eat pork in Mount Liao.
Everyone heard it and believed it was true. On the morning of December 4th of that year, Menghuapo first climbed up Sow Mountain, went under the big cedar tree, set up an incense case, and lit incense candles to worship Guanyin. When Shanha got home, Menghuapo collected three large gifts and brought them home. She had a great time.
Unexpectedly, in the second year, Shanha was busy from the beginning to the end of the year, and still couldn't eat a piece of pork. There is a Buddha baby in the dorm. There are no parents or siblings under him. He was furious when he heard his dream house. He climbed to Sow Mountain on the evening of December 23 of the 3rd year of the lunar calendar, cut down a large cedar tree into the propulsion ditch, dug up a tree brain, and carried it back to his house. On New Year's Eve, he placed the tree brain in the ash of the pot, covered it with Martian furnace ash, and burned it to relieve his heart.
I really didn't expect that the pig raised by Buddha in the second year actually weighed 300 kg. Everyone says this is because Buddha gave birth to pigs in the ashes of the pot on New Year's Eve to celebrate the New Year. As a result, everyone followed the example and gradually became a custom. It spread throughout the village and has continued to this day. Later, even the Han people living in the dormitory learned to do this.
