 In the past, when Shanha went to relatives and friends to celebrate the New Year, they all used the second day of the first day of the first day of the new year. Legend has it that a Buddha gave birth to a baby. He has prepared gifts and plans to go to his mother-in-law's dorm to celebrate the New Year in the second year. As the old saying goes, “People don't count the world.” He started with stomach pain on the morning of the second day of the first day of the first month, and the pain didn't stop until the evening of the fourth day.
On the 5th day of the sky, this Buddha baby went to his mother-in-law's house to celebrate the New Year. At noon, he went back to inspect himself. He had to take advantage of the sky to grow potatoes. Halfway through, he had a stomach ache so much that he needed to enlarge his stool. When the Buddha saw so many people on the road, he put them in a small pit on the side of the road. When he didn't want to put down his poop pants, he dropped a pair of cloth shoes that Pu Niang gave him into the pit.
Buddha's pups were unhappy and unwilling. They climbed to the edge of the pit to take a look, and they couldn't see their shoes. There was no choice but to touch the Buddha's cub in the pond. As a result, they touched a pair of golden shoes.
Buddha's son brought these gold shoes back to the dorm, exchanged them for silver, built a new dormitory, and married back to his family.
As the old saying goes, “There are no impermeable walls in the world.” Many mountains know that this Buddha baby got a pair of golden shoes for the first five years of his New Year greetings. Shanha began studying in the second year. Instead of celebrating the New Year on the second day of the first month, they changed to the fifth day of the first month. In this way, the custom of praying for the first five years of the new year was formed.
