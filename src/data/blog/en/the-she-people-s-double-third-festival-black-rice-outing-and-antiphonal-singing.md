 The Qingming Festival on March 3 is one of the traditional folk festivals of the Chinese nation. It is associated with sacrificing ancestors and tomb sweeping in Qingming. In some places, it is also known as the “Youth Outing Festival.” In the Shes, on the other hand, they also impart other content and customs. This means that they not only sacrificed their ancestors to sweep tombs, but also went on a green outing to sing songs, and also had to eat a kind of “black rice.”
 
 
 
 Speaking of eating “black rice,” not only do the Shes have this custom, but in places such as Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi, Sichuan, Guizhou, and Anhui in Jiangnan, people still maintain the ancient custom of eating black rice in April of the lunar calendar. Some places eat during the Qingming Cold Food Festival, while more are eaten on April 8. Umihan is made by using glutinous rice, collecting the leaves of the wild plant black rice to make soup, then soaking the glutinous rice in the cooked soup for half a day, then scooping it out and steaming it in a wooden pot. After cooking, this kind of meal is dark, fragrant, and delicious. It not only increases appetite, but also removes wind and detoxification, and prevents mosquito bites.
 
 
 
 As to why to eat “black rice,” there are two versions in Jiangnan and ethnic minorities. First, a long time ago, the wife of a rich owner was mean and vicious, and not only did many acts unjust to her neighbors. Also, eating poultry, livestock, and seafood is extremely cruel. She said that when she ate pork, she first locked a live pig in an airtight room. The floor of the room was covered with an iron plate. The bottom of the room was covered with an iron plate. The bottom of the pig's feet was so hot that she couldn't stand, so she had to run hard and run until exhaustion and fell before ordering the family to cut off the pig's buttocks. Because the pig's blood was concentrated on the hips at this time, the meat was tender and delicious. Also, her way of eating fish is ingenious. She hangs a large live fish upside down, put boiling oil in an iron pan underneath, then knock on the fish's head to let the fish's brain drip into the pan, fry it and eat it dipped in sauce...
 
 
 
 Since she committed too many crimes during her lifetime, she was thrown into the 18th floor of hell after her death. There was no food. However, she had a son named Mulian. He was very filial and brought meals to his mother, but the meals were gone before they reached his mother's mouth.
 
 
 
 why? Because you've been sent to hell 18 times, you have to go through 18 levels, and all the little ones guarding the levels have eaten up all the food. Magnolia was in a hurry and cried on the road. The cries shocked the nearby mountain god. The mountain god asked the reason. She was moved by Magnolia's filial piety. She pointed to a type of tree called “black rice” on the mountain, and told him how to make black rice. Magnolia immediately followed the instructions to prepare black rice. The next morning, Mulian delivered the cooked black rice to the place where her mother was imprisoned. On the way, all the guardians guarding the checkpoint saw this dark and immature thing, and no one dared to risk eating it. As a result, Mulian's mother was able to enjoy this delicious meal on earth. Because this day just happened to be April 8 of the lunar calendar. In order to commemorate Mulian's filial piety and pass on China's filial piety culture, the family ate this special significance of black rice on this day, and it continues to this day.

Another explanation is the folklore of the She people. In the Shes, it is widely accepted that “March 3” is Gumi's birthday, and they eat “black rice” on this day to celebrate Gumi's birthday. There's also a very moving story here. It is said that on March 3 of the ancient year, due to a pest infestation a few years ago, the Shes people almost reached a situation where their families lost their food. Seeing that spring planting had begun, the Shemin teamed up to borrow grain from the mountain owners. Instead of borrowing, the mountain owner released evil dogs to bite the Shemin people. At this point, a young Shemin man named Lan Tianfeng stood up. He and several partners planned to take advantage of the quiet night to kill the evil dog, break the lock, and distribute a bag of grain to Shemin. The Shemin took advantage of the night when no one interfered and planted the seeds in the field overnight.
When the owner of the mountain discovered that the granary had been stolen and robbed, he stormed into the stockade with his family and fought against Shemin. To save Shemin from being charred. The blue sky stepped forward and took matters into his own hands. This day just happened to be March 3rd. Lan Tianfeng was imprisoned in a dungeon by the owner of the mountain. They didn't give him food, and he had to starve him to death.
According to the news, the parents and townspeople of the She ethnic group all visited the prison one after another. They made rice balls from planting leftover food and sent them to jail. However, I didn't eat a single bite of these rice balls; instead, I was eaten by the prison guard's crooked mouth. This was learned about by Zhong Xiu, the most beautiful singer in Shexiang, and she thought of a way to deal with her crooked remarks. She picked the leaves of the black rice tree to cook the soup, prepared the rice balls and placed them under a bamboo basket, and put a pocket on it. Some poisonous ants were placed inside, and the food was delivered. Looking crooked, I saw that a beautiful woman was here to deliver the food; she had no good intentions and wanted to check it out. When he stuck his hand in his pocket, the poisonous ants inside were attacked, and they all climbed onto their crooked hands and struggled to eat their bites. Eventually, his crooked mouth was bitten and poisoned by poisonous ants and died.
Since then, the She family has been picking black rice leaves from the mountains to make a meal. Judging from the outside, these meals look like groups of poisonous ants. The prison guards didn't dare to make them again due to their crooked rhetoric; they closed one eye and it was over. Later, Lan Tianfeng stayed in prison for three years. Not only was he not starved to death, his wounds healed, and his health got better and better. Finally, he was rescued by the rebels and promoted as the leader of the rebels.
In order for future generations to remember that Shejia's black rice was hard to come by, “March 3” was designated as the birthday of Gummi. Because black rice is glutinous rice with white flowers cooked through the leaf juice of the black rice tree to give it a dark and bright appearance, the Shes family also likes to use dark blue as the base color for their clothes, making the flowers green and green in their outfits, making it a modern dress with the characteristics of the She family.
