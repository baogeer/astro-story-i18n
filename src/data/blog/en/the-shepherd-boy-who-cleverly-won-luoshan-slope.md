 In the Guxian Subdistrict Office of the Development Zone, there is a verdant mountain, known locally as “Rockhill Slope”. There is a spring at the foot of the hillside, and the water flows continuously throughout the year. This is where the legend of “shepherds make a fortune falling down a hill,” which is circulating in the Fukuyama area.
In the late Yuan dynasty, a couple surnamed Wang in Dali, Yunnan, moved to live in Guxian Town, 30 miles northwest of Fushan County in Dengzhou, Shandong, to avoid war and chaos. Soon, the couple surnamed Wang died of illness one after another, leaving behind a young child. After being introduced by a neighbor and a kind clerk, they went to large families in the town to become cow herders.
One day, a Southern Feng Shui man and his wife passed through the downhill slope. He was surprised to see the spring water spilling out from the foot of the mountain. He looked around and couldn't help but clap and be amazed: it's a treasure of Feng Shui! The next day, he and his wife went back to Loxanpo and quietly placed two raw eggs in the spring. He told his wife that if the spring had a lot of energy, the eggs would be fully cooked when the sun rose the next day, then he would have to buy this place and move his ancestral tomb. He will definitely be able to prosper his family and business, and Ding will be an official for generations.
This scene was seen by a shepherd named Wang, who herded cows not far away. After they left, the shepherd came to the fountain, reached out to take out the eggs, and put the eggs back in the spring early the next morning. The next day, Mr. Feng Shui came to the spring alone. He was surprised to see that the eggs were actually undercooked. Confident that he wouldn't look away, he summoned the shepherd from afar to the front: “Doll, the old man put this egg in the spring water. I stomped three feet on the downhill slope. If you see if the eggs in this water bounce, be sure to tell me the truth.” Let's just run to the top of the mountain. The little shepherd really saw it; he saw that every time Mr. Feng Shui stomped, the eggs bounce. Mr. Feng Shui asked loudly, and the clever shepherd boy waved his hands in response, saying that the eggs didn't move at all. Mr. Feng Shui suspected that the shepherd was cheating, so he went down the mountain and gave the shepherd a few coins to stomp three feet at the top of the mountain. He watched on his own by the spring. The shepherd clearly stomped three feet hard on the mountain, but unfortunately, the eggs in the water didn't move at all. Where did he know that the shepherd only lifted his legs hard, but settled down gently! Mr. Feng Shui confessed his weakness and turned away.
However, it was said that the little shepherd immediately requested from the owner's family that he was willing to use his grazing wages over the past few years in exchange for a falling mountain slope with spring water in Nanshan to be a burial place for his parents. The downhill slope was not a field; the landowner was happy to agree. The little shepherd bought a sea bowl of soju, steamed the only goose raised in his hut, packed it in a wicker box and gave it to the neighbor, Mr. Wenshi, and asked him to write a document for notarization. Mr. Wenwen measured the food box sent by the shepherd and wrote, “One sea wine, one river goose, in exchange for a downhill slope; if you want to take back the downhill slope, you must return me a sea wine; you must return me a river goose.” Well, the owner didn't take a close look and signed the picture.
I don't know if the document is written like this, which is amazing! Just imagine, a bowl of wine becomes a sea of wine, and a goose in a food box becomes a river goose. In this way, even if the owner regrets it later, they won't be able to lose the shepherd's wine from the sea and the goose from the river!
After the little shepherd bought the hillside, he moved his parents' graves next to the springs at the foot of the hillside. Later, the shepherd married his wife and formed a beautiful family. The family business gradually prospered and developed, and eventually became a family of famous families and officials in the Fukuyama region.
According to historical records, after the Wang family moved from Yunnan to the end of the Qing dynasty, from the Ming dynasty to the end of the Qing dynasty, they went through 16 generations, having 6 Han Lin, 24 knights, 58 people, 58 Gong Sheng, and 357 Xiucai; there were three feudal rulers; they also had a brilliant family of “father and son Sanhan Lin and brothers raised many people”. It is worth mentioning that Gu Xian Wang's family also had a famous figure with a remarkable reputation: Wang Yirong, the discoverer of Oracle.
