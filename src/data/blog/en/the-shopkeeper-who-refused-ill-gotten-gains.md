 During the Qing and Xuantong years, there was a Wang Ketong in Nansha Hebei Street in Tengxian County. He opened a grocery store called “Enrichment Store.” This person spent his whole life being kind and often used the money he earned to help the townspeople.
One year, a young man from the South came to Beijing to rush to take the exam. He fell ill on the street. After Wang Ke found out, he took this raised son home and asked Lang Zhong to see a doctor with him. The soup is boiled twice a day, and three teas and four waters await. After a few days, my son recovered from his serious illness, but he still didn't see a smile on his face. I asked him why he was so sad that he only spoke his heart when he raised his son. It turned out that I had no pennies in my pocket. It was over 1,000 miles away from Beijing. The exam period was coming soon, and I couldn't get to Beijing on foot. Wang Kekong said, “Don't worry, I'll pay to hire a horse-drawn carriage to take you to Beijing.” He saved up enough money and spent the money to hire a superior horse-drawn carriage, and ordered the chaser to say, “You have to drive day and night, rain or shine, don't miss the test period, and come back and enjoy a bag of millet.
As a result, those in the car rushed on their way and continued to Beijing day and night; indeed, they didn't miss the test period.
Soon, after being interviewed by the emperor and a civil servant, he was placed in the provincial capital of Shandong. Another man, Ma Xuntian, left Beijing, and the governor ordered him to drop off the bus in Tengxian County. He wanted to meet the visitors at the Nansha River.
After leaving the car in Tengxian County, the governor walked to the Nansha River in plain clothes and showed up at the Zenghi store. After more than a month of parting ways, the two met very cordially, and they talked about parting ways. At this point, I only heard that there were soldiers and horses passing through the street. For some reason, Yu Ke Yu was busy visiting. The governor asked him to hang a yellow lantern on the doorbell. Soon, officials from all states, provinces, and counties arrived. Only then did Wang Kekong learn that he is in high school and has become a senior official.
The next day, Zhi County asked Wang Ke, how many petitions did he receive? Wang Ke said that there are 10 prefectures, 100 singles and 8 prefectures in Kyushu, and a total of 127 books have been handed over. The prefectural government asked how much was the total number of gifts received? Wang Ke said he didn't get a penny. The governor said, “You've lost my kindness. I think this move can make you a millionaire in a blink of an eye. You are a very kind person.” Wang Kekong said, “My lord! Villains don't dare to take unjust wealth.”
The governor returned to Jinan and sent two packs of tea leaves. Wang Kegong is busy with his business; how can he take apart the tea bag? It took more than three months to remember the two packs of tea sent by the governor. They hurriedly asked my son to pick them up and open the bag to take a look. Where are the tea leaves? It turned out to be two test papers from the provincial test takers.
