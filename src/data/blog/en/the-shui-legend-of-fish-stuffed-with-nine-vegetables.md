 The purpose of the Aquaria Festival is to celebrate our ancestors with fish wrapped in filet dishes. The intention is to miss our ancestors and not forget our national roots. Here's a story about “Fish Wrapped in Fillet”.
Aquarians are not native to this area. Legend has it that when the ancestors of aquariums migrated from south to north, an old man brought a pack of food to the travelers: “Bring it, this dish will become a hometown dish for you to enjoy after building your new home.” On the way, this ancient ancestor was tired and hungry. When he opened what the old man had sent him, it turned out to be a pack of boiled fish with nine kinds of vegetables. After eating, he felt full of energy, so he rushed forward and finally settled on the banks of the beautiful Duliu River. Later, this ancient ancestor spread this dish, and it became a dish for the completion of a new aquarium home, a holiday feast, and used it to celebrate ancestors in the New Year to commemorate loved ones in their ancestral home in the south.
