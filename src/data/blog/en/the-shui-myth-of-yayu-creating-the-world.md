 1. Kaiten Matsumata
In ancient times, the sky was connected to the earth, the earth was connected to the sky, and the sky and the earth were connected. There was no difference between day and night. No trees, no rivers, nothing. At this point, she was a goddess.
Yomata is a giant. She is extremely powerful, can think, calculate, and has ambition.
That year was the year of the child, and Yomata decided to open the world. But how did she open up the sky? I saw her stretch out her arms, grab the top with one hand, and hold the bottom with the other hand. I heard a “wow” sound. The top and bottom were immediately split. Then the hand rocked up and up high, and the upper part was taken to the top. This is the sky; then, I saw her fly furiously and kick it past the part below. All of a sudden, the one below was kicked 70,000 feet. This is the ground.
However, she can calculate, and she will think that if she let go, heaven and earth will come together again. She is very smart. She immediately holds the stomach of the sky with a bronze rod, an iron to hold the center of the earth, and then uses the bones of four crocodiles to support the four sides of the sky and the four corners of the ground.
Crocodiles have the hardest bones. This support is stable in the sky and firm on the ground. Like a rocky mountain, it won't collapse in a thousand years, or ten thousand years.
Heaven and earth have been separated, yet the space between heaven and earth is still chaotic. Grandma Mata can do the math. She really has great talent. When she saw that the world was so chaotic, she let out a gust of wind, and the wind blew. She saw that chaos split into two layers, one layer of clean air, and the other layer was cloudy.
She breathed out again, only to see the turbidity slowly sinking down to the ground and turning into dirt; the clean air was rising upward and rising all the way to the sky. Immediately, the whole sky was clear, quiet, and blue.
2. The day and moon built in Tsurumata
The sky is separated from the earth, yet there is no light in the sky, the earth is not bright, the sky is blurry, and the earth is dim and dark. In this way, everything cannot survive, and everything cannot grow. Grandma Azumata also thinks about math. She took clean air, used clean air to make the sun, and the moon.
She created the first sun, and the sky immediately showed a slight light; she felt that it wasn't bright enough, so she made three more suns, and suddenly brightened up ten provinces; Minamata had a whim and became two more suns, which immediately brightened up eleven countries; she made two more suns, which lit up the whole world; and so happy that it made two more. She made another one, made ten suns, and the world was so sad.
There are ten suns, ten more moons, and they all happily hang in the sky, and Minamata has created countless more stars, all scattered in the sky. Tsunomata is tired. She is too lazy to do it any more, and the sky is filled with big and small stars.
3. Sagimata Day
Ten suns and moons are making a mess of the world. The dirt has turned to mud, and the rocks have turned into magma. The fish in the water were so thirsty that they couldn't get close to their mouths. They cried and sued Minamata: “Save us!” They couldn't even find anything to eat, and their stomachs were all dead. They also sued Minamata and shouted, “Save us, King God.”
This surprised Minamata. Both the sun and the moon had already been released and ran in the sky. The only way was to shoot it with an arrow, and she took out an arrow, but who would shoot it? No one has that much strength, and no one has that much skill. There's no choice, she's the only one to shoot the sun.
Shoot the head one,
The world is still hot;
Shoot five,
The world is still hot.
She couldn't. She shot it again. She shot 9 suns, leaving only the 10th sun to give to the ground. Since then, there has only been a sun and a moon hanging in the sky, and the earth is bright and warm. However, the Sun is a mother, and she is very shy, so Hamata has a rule that she is not allowed to see it; the Moon is public, so you can watch it with everyone.
At six o'clock noon,
The moon hugs the cub;
At 6 o'clock at night,
The sun embraces grandchildren.
The stars are all sisters, and they can be allowed to wear jewels; it is also stipulated that the broom stars are used as lanterns to accompany the moon in the light of the stars.
4. Yomata Mountain Construction
There is the sun, moon, and stars in the sky, yet there is nothing on earth, and it is still very desolate. The first grandmother of Yurimata decided to cultivate the land.
What is on the ground? To make everything on earth, we must build mountains. To make mountains, we can have mountains and plains, and canyons, mountains, and rivers.
In the year of the Ox, she built a mountain. Wudamata built the Five Great Mountains, Mount Taishan in the east, and she landed it in Fujian (original text); Mount Huashan in the west, she placed it in Yanguo (original text). On the south side and north side, she each built a mountain. The north side is called Mount Heng, and the south side is called Heng
Mt. There is also a mountain called Songshan, which is located in the middle. Five large mountains, each in one country. Finally, Yamamata also divides each mountain branch. In this way, mountains are all over the world, there are mountains everywhere, flat land everywhere, and rivers everywhere. However, the mountain was still bare, and nothing was visible.
Trees don't grow,
bald mountains;
Grass doesn't grow,
The ground is burnt yellow.
The original grandmother of Yomata would think that she could calculate that if she wanted the mountains to look good, and if she wanted the earth to grow everything, she created seeds and scattered them all over the mountains and plains. On the hillside, she sowed 30,000 seeds, and on the small hillside, she also sowed tens of thousands of seeds. As soon as spring arrives, grass grows, trees grow, and the earth is green, which is very beautiful.
5. Zoujin Tsurumata
Grandma Azumata created people again, and people will manage this world. However, the person who just created it is very poor.
There are no fields, no land,
No cotton, no rice.
Ancient people,
Eat only the grass roots.
They had no place to sleep, so they slept on the grass and were riddled with conflict. Wisteria is used as a belt for the body, and banana leaves are used for clothing. They only cover the body and protect against rain, but they cannot warm the body.
People at that time, regardless of gender, were clueless; they only looked for food; they didn't know anything else: they couldn't think or count. Follow tigers, get along with dragons and snakes, and swim everywhere in groups with wild animals. He is also a friend of Lei Bus, calling him a brother and brother. It's very foolish.
Grandma Azumata was a bit disappointed. In order for humans to manage the earth, she later separated people from animals.

Since then, humans and animals have all been separated from the inside to the outside: the tiger's fur has a pattern, which is very beautiful; the dragon's body is covered with armor, which is very beautiful. Yes, tigers are good-looking, but they are very ignorant and can't talk; dragons are beautiful, dragons can't talk, and they are also very stupid.
Because they are foolish, they are slaughtered by humans, and only then shot by people with arrows.
Kill a tiger with a knife,

Arrow-shooting dragon,

People also hated Lei Gong. Setting a fire shocked Lei Gong and jumped into the sky.

6. The Arisu Clan's Master's Nest
Humans have gained space and taken over the world, yet they don't know how to reclaim places, and haven't got the hang of it. They still hunt around in groups every day looking for food. It's dark, find a cave or something to sleep in. Ancient people back then:
I don't know love,
Nor do they hate it.
Women don't have husbands,
Wouldn't look for it;
A man without a wife,
Nor will I want it.
I can't tell my older brother from my younger brother, I can't figure out my sister and sister, I don't know what's beautiful or what's ugly.
Born at ease,
regardless of whether you are fat or thin;
nourish yourself,
It doesn't matter if it's alive or not.
The number of people is slowly increasing, and they are all scattered all over the place. They didn't have a house to live in, and settled in a cave. At this point, a man called the Aru Chao clan appeared. He taught humans to build houses. If you don't have an axe, what would you use to cut down a tree? I don't have a chisel, what do I use to cut a pillar? When there is a nest king, he taught people to use broken trees, random photo frames, and branches.
They set them up randomly, and they also taught people to build fences to form a house.
