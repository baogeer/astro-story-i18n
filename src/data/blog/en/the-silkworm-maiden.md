 Legend has it that the Silkworm Lady's family lives in a ditch in the middle of the mountains.
In the early years, in the Lifoqiao area of Hangzhou, there was a smart and capable little girl named Ah Qiao. When Ah Qiao was nine, her mother died, leaving her and her four-year-old brother behind. Dad asked for a stepdaughter. The Scorpion's heart, which was born later, is terrible when it comes to Ah Qiao's sister and brother! In the middle of winter this year, one day, my daughter-in-law named Ah Qiao carried a bamboo basket and braved the north wind to cut sheep grass. When the weather is cold and freezing, where is there still green grass! Ah Qiao ran from morning to dusk, found a mountainside by the river, but didn't find a hint of tender grass. She was cold and scared, so she sat in the middle of the mountainside and started crying. Crying and crying, I suddenly heard a voice above my head saying:
“Cut the grass, half a ravine!
I need to cut grass, half a ravine!”
Ah Qiao looked up and saw a white-headed bird that fluttered towards the ravine. She then stood up, wiped away her tears, and followed the white-headed bird. After turning a corner, the white-headed bird disappeared. I saw an old pine tree standing at the mouth of the mountain. A green elephant covered the mizoguchi with a large umbrella. Ah Qiao pulled out a branch and went around the pine trees. Suddenly, her eyes lit up, and she saw a curved creek flowing murmuring. The flowers on the banks of the creek are red, green, and beautiful as spring.
When Ah Qiao saw the grass, it looked like she had found a baby. She was busy crouching down and slitting her body. She cut by walking and getting farther and farther. Before she knew it, she reached the end of the creek.
She cut a bamboo basket full of green grass and stood up to remove sweat from the corner of her forehead, but not far ahead, she saw an aunt in white, wearing a white dress, waving at her with a fine basket in her hand. The aunt in white smiled and said to Ah Qiao:
“Little girl, that's a rare guest. Let's stay at our house for a few days!”
Ah Qiao looked up and saw another world in front of her: in the middle of the mountainside, there was a neat row of houses with white pink walls and white tiles; in front of the house was a dwarf forest, the green leaves were bigger than a slap; and there were also many aunts in white, all carrying small baskets, laughing and singing, picking the fresh leaves in the dwarf woods.
Ah Qiao was so happy that she stayed here.
Afterwards, Ah Qiao, along with her aunts in white, picked young leaves in the dwarf woods during the day and fed the leaves to a snow-white bug at night. Slowly, the bugs grew and spit out silk to form snow-white little walnuts one by one. My aunt in white taught Ah Qiao how to draw these snow-white little walnuts into shiny silk thread, and how to use the tree to dye the silk thread in color: the green one dyed blue thread, the red one dyed the red thread, the yellow one dyed the gold thread... The aunt in white also told Ah Qiao that these little snow-white bugs are called “sky bugs,” and the leaves that feed the sky bugs are called “mulberry leaves”; this colorful silk thread embroidered dragons for the emperor and made clouds for the weaver.
Ah Qiao lives in a ravine. She picks mulberry leaves with her aunt in white, feeds celestial bugs together, and draws silk together. She lived a very busy life. Three months passed in a blink of an eye.
On this day, Ah Qiao suddenly remembered his younger brother and asked him to come and have a good life here too! As soon as it dawned the next day, she couldn't tell her aunt in white, so she took care of herself and went home.
When she left, Ah Qiao also took a blank sheet of paper covered with celestial bug eggs. Also, I packed two bags of mulberry trees. Along the way, I lost them all the way. I thought to myself: I'll walk back with the mulberry trees tomorrow.
Ah Qiao went home and took a look. Her father is already old, and her younger brother has grown into a young man! When Dad saw Ah Qiao back, he was happy and sad and asked:
“Ah Qiao, why did you go for 15 years before you came back? Where have you been all these years?”
After listening, Ah Qiao told her father how to go up the mountain and how she met her aunt in white. Neighbors on the left and right got it. They all came to see her and said that she had met a fairy.
Early the next morning, Ah Qiao wanted to go back to the ravine and take a look. Having just walked out, she looked up at a green dwarf forest along the road. It turned out that the mulberry trees she had left behind had all grown into trees. She walked along the woods all the way to the ravine. The old pine tree at the mouth of the mountain is still covered like an umbrella; if you want to go inside, you won't be able to find your way.
Ah Qiao was in a daze at the old pine tree and saw that the white-headed bird flew out from behind the old pine tree again, shouting:
“Ah Qiao stole the treasure!
Ah Qiao stole the treasure!”
Only then did Ah Qiao remember that when she left, she didn't talk to her aunt in white. She also took a celestial bug egg and two bags of mulberry trees. The aunt in white must have been mad and hidden the road and wouldn't let her go again. As a result, she went back to her home, hatched the celestial bug eggs, picked young mulberry leaves to feed them, and raised the celestial bug at home.
Since then, there have been celestial bugs in the human world. Later, people combined the word celestial bug and called it “silkworm.” It is said that the aunt in white that Ah Qiao met in a middle of a ravine is a silkworm flower lady who is in charge of silkworm cocoons.
