 My husband often scolds his wife for not being human. One day, I invited a few people to plow the ground and let my wife cook. At 1 or 2 p.m., they didn't ask for breakfast. The male owner invited the plowers a few times to rest, but they kept on plowing. Finally, it was called to eat. When I opened the steamed rice bowl, it was all filled with noodle dolls made from Aba noodles. It turns out that the wife wanted to prove to her husband in front of others to see if she could be a human. My husband was annoyed and furious when he saw it, and he couldn't have imagined such a stupid woman. She slapped her in the face with the bow left and right. She was very aggrieved. She covered her hot face with her hands and cried and said, “Every day I curse that I won't be a person, I won't be a human, I will beat people.”
