 A long time ago, there were two sister dragons in Shiping's Sang Gu Nao Qing. Her sister is called Ergu Milk Dragon. She has a gentle temperament and lives in Hot Water Palace; her younger sister is called Sang Gu Milk Dragon and has a cold temperament and lives in Lengshui Palace. Legend has it that Ergu Milk Dragon spews hot water all year round. The local people built a temple next to the Hot Water Palace and built a bathing pool for local people to bathe. People coming from all sides to take a bath are constantly coming and going.
It was so lively for several years. Then, one day, a millionaire from Xiangyang Village brought a group of people to take over the bath, so they got into a fight with the local people. After a fight, the rich were defeated and fled back to their hometown. The millionaire couldn't hold back his breath and came up with a trick to get rid of the dragon. The idea was set. On a dark night, I quietly brought two family members to the Hot Water Palace to kill a pre-prepared white dog and throw it into the hot tub. The white dog's corpse weighed down on Ergu Milk Dragon. Under the influence of Ergu Milk Dragon, he moved to Jiangxi Bridge overnight. Since then, the Hot Water Palace has become a dry mud pond, and the locals are no longer able to take a hot bath.
Also, cold water dragons have an extremely cold temperament because they spew cold water all year round. It has raised some dragons and often releases little dolls to catch ordinary people. People around them hate this dragon, but there is nothing they can do about it. One day, a Taoist passed by here and saw that most of the people's little dolls were sick. After asking why, he learned that the Three Gu Milk Dragon was a harmful dragon. After the Taoist summoned the old people in the village to discuss it, he spent three days beside the Three Gu Milk Dragon before subduing this cold water dragon. Since then, this dragon has been afraid to endanger people around it, and the people have finally lived a stable life.
Narrator: Li Jiawen, Su Shaowen
