 In the battle against the US and aid the DPRK volcano, a battle team of only 6 people fought against two battalions commanded by the US military, creating a brilliant record of 0:360. The team leader who led this combat team to create a miracle is Li Fei. This 22-year-old combat team leader of the 6th Company of the 68th Army Corps of the Volunteer Army had already won the title of Grand Archer long before entering North Korea.
After the “Autumn Offensive” launched by the US military in 1951 was defeated fiercely on the eastern front, four divisions concentrated on the western front to launch an attack on our defensive positions on the first line of Jincheng in mid-October. Li Fei's troops were ordered to engage in interdiction battles in the Jiaoyan Mountain and Feng Volcano zones. After the battle began, Li Fei led his team to stick to the top of Yuefeng Mountain. At 20:00 on October 17, after leading the team to the position in accordance with the chief's orders, Li Fei determined the enemy's possible route of attack on me based on hostile conditions and terrain, and formed an interdiction plan for various situations.
Dare to hit the enemy close to 30 meters, making the enemy's aircraft artillery and suppressing firepower completely ineffective
At dawn on October 18, two battalions of the US 7th Division (including the Dutch battalion under their command), which confronted me 300 meters from the front of my position, launched a frenzied and continuous attack on our Yuefeng Mountain position.
More than 80 enemy soldiers, covered by fire and heavy machine guns, rushed up from the front like a pack of wolves. Li Fei calmed everyone down. When the enemy reached 340 meters, Li Fei's combat team attacked with machine guns, assault robbers, and hand grenades. I only heard the enemy down the hill crying and the wolf howling, all dead and wounded.
At this point, a large group of enemies climbed up from the left again. Li Fei still has an old idea: if you don't see a rabbit, don't throw an eagle, you don't have a hundred percent certainty, and never shoot a bullet easily. When the enemy reached a distance of only 30 meters, he shouted, “Fight!” Rows of grenades were thrown out in one fell swoop. Of the 340 American devils, only four or five went back.
After deliberately reaching the left side of the position to expose firepower, then quickly run to the right side to hide, causing enemy shells to empty all over
Li Fei estimates that if the enemy has suffered such huge losses, they will definitely carry out retaliatory bombardment. In order to divert enemy fire, he led his comrades to a position on the left side of the traffic ditch. After hitting a shuttle bullet, they quickly ran to the position on the right side of the traffic ditch. This trick really puzzled the enemy. The enemy reacted quickly and bombarded my left-hand position with concentrated artillery fire for 20 minutes.
Once the artillery fire stopped, a new round of enemy group charges began again. In order to get closer, Li Fei's team deliberately threw stones and pushes stones at the enemy group. The enemy shouted with joy, probably saying, “Haha, their bullets have finally run out, let's go boldly.” The enemy actually raised their bodies and rushed upward with great might. Unbeknownst to me, when I was about ten meters away from my position, the enemy was swept away by Li Fei's team's sudden oncoming fire and blood, and dead bodies spread all over the field.
Boldly extend the front of the position to the “tip of the enemy's nose.” Destroy a large number of enemies while charging halfway up the hill
When the US military was unable to attack for a long time, they became annoyed and furious. At noon on the same day, 4 planes and 6 tanks concentrated artillery fire to bombard, fire, and throw gasoline bombs at our position for 30 minutes. A huge fire broke out at my position, and most of the construction was completely blown up.
In order to preserve vitality and seek time to annihilate the enemy in close combat, Li Fei used the enemy's opportunity to exchange bullets and cleverly circle to the mountainside ahead along the favorable terrain, boldly extended his position to an unknown plateau “below the tip of the enemy's nose”, and used crates as hidden positions.
Another group of enemies rushed up, but they never expected to be violently killed by Li Fei's team when they were halfway there. The enemy in front fell to the ground in pieces, and the enemy in the back was stunned. They couldn't guess which direction the bullets were flying from, and quickly climbed back to the ground.
Charge and “race” with the enemy's multi-way group. Shoot left and right and “decapitate” each other
The number of dead enemy corpses on the ground continues to increase. The US military dogs quickly jumped against the wall and did their best. Under the cover of air, tanks, and artillery, they launched a group charge on our unnamed plateau in three ways, in an attempt to advance left and right and encirclement on three sides.
Li Fei's team quickly seized favorable terrain on the left rear side of the unknown plateau. They took turns to “decapitate” the three enemies according to the severity of the threat to my position: first knock out the aggressive one that is closest to me, and so on. After several circles of “beheading”, Li Fei's team returned to their original position after defeating the enemy's three-sided siege.
Until the friendly forces took over, Li Fei's team fought fiercely with the enemy for a total of 17 hours, repelling 11 enemy attacks and annihilating 360 enemies, yet I had no casualties, and the enemy was unable to move forward. After the war, Li Feiji paid a special service to Li Feiji at the headquarters of the Volunteer Army.
