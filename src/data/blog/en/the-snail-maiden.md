 Farmer picks up rice
Once upon a time, there was a lonely young farmer who lived by cultivating land for the landlord, and worked hard every day at sunrise, cultivating at sunset. One day, he found a very large snail in the field. He was very surprised and very happy. He took it home, placed it in a water tank, and carefully kept it with water.
Ecluse girl cooking
One day, the farmer went to work in the field as usual, but when he got home, he saw fragrant rice on the stove, delicious fish and vegetables in the kitchen, and boiling water in the teapot. The next day he came back like this again. Two days, three days... Every day was like this. The farmer decided to figure things out. The next day, the chicken called again. As before, he carried a hoe down the field to work. As soon as it got dark, he rushed home and wanted to see what kind person he was. From a long distance, he saw that the chimney on the roof of his house was already cooking smoke. He sped up his steps and wanted to see with his own eyes who was actually burning the fire to cook. However, when he crept his hands close to the door and looked inside, there was no movement at home. When he walked through the door, he saw that the food on the table was fragrant, the fire in the stove was still burning, the water was boiling in the pot, and he hadn't had time to scoop it up; only the enthusiastic cook was gone. Another day has passed. The farmer woke up early in the morning, called the chickens, and rushed home when it was not bright. The cooking smoke at home hadn't started yet, so the young man quietly approached the fence, hid in the dark, and watched everything in his house with full attention. Soon after, he finally saw a young and beautiful girl slowly walking out of the water tank. Her clothes were not slightly damp from the water. This girl moved to the front of the stove and started cooking.
The young man saw it really attentively. He hurriedly ran through the door and walked to the side of the water tank. As soon as he took a look, all that was left was an empty shell of the large conch he had recovered. Surprisingly, he took an empty shell and looked again, then walked to the front of the stove and asked the young girl who was cooking, “May I ask this girl, where are you from? Why help me cook?” The girl didn't expect him to show up at this time. She was taken aback, and when she heard him question her origin, she didn't know what to do. The young girl wanted to go back to the water tank, but she was blocked from going. The young farmer asked questions over and over again. The young girl had no choice but to tell him the truth; she was just a conch girl. The young people liked the Conch Girl very much, and they got married later.
Locusts are messing up the game
Locusts are very fond of Girl Eclus. Seeing that Lady Eclat and the peasants are so nice, they are very jealous, and decide to take the Ecleca Girl. As a result, they pretended to be a fortune teller to defraud the Conch Shell from her blind mother-in-law. With the Eclus Shell, the Eclus Girl received it and couldn't get out of his cave. The farmer and their partner went to the Locust Girl but were defeated by Locust Essence. Later, they thought of a way to use salt to sprinkle salt on Locust Essence. Locust Essence was most afraid of salt, and finally died in pain.
happy ending
The Conch Girl and the farmer lived a happy life. A year later, she gave birth to a fat boy. In a blink of an eye, she was 5 or 6 years old. One day, he was playing in the water by the riverside, and was later scolded by his fellow children as being a child of Echelojin, “Pile, pile, whack, what amu field conch shell, ding, ding, what amu field snails.” His son listened to what others said and hid his mother's shell, and the Eclaw Girl never went back to being an Echidna.
