 There is a person in Higashi-gun who plays snakes for a living. He used to domesticate two snakes. They are all blue. The big one is called Daqing, and the younger one is Erqing. Erqing has a red spot on her forehead. She is particularly smart and tame, and she is very happy to direct her to the circling performance. Therefore, snake people love it more than any other snake.
After a year, Daqing died, and Snake Man wanted to find another one to fill the gap, but he never took care of it. One night he stayed at a temple in the mountains. At dawn, when I opened the bamboo box and took a look, Erqing was gone. The Snake Man was so annoyed that he wanted to die. He searched for calls in bright and dark places, but never even saw a shadow. Previously, every time I went to a place full of plants, I let the snakes out, let them feel free, and soon they would come back on their own. Because of this, Snake Man still wanted it to come back on its own, so he sat and waited. I didn't leave happy until the sun rose high and I felt hopeless.
After walking a few steps outside, the snake man suddenly heard the sound of rice crunching in the cluttered grass. He stopped and looked astonished. Erqing had returned. The Snake Man was very happy, as if he had received a priceless treasure. Placing his burden on the side of the road, Erqing also stopped. Another look at the back of it was followed by a small snake. He caressed Erqing and said, “I thought you had run away. Did you recommend that little guy?” Speaking of which, take out feed to feed it, and also give the little snake some. Although the little snake didn't leave, it hesitated and didn't dare to eat it. Erqing fed it with her mouth, as if the owner were entertaining a guest. When the Snake Man feeds it again, it eats it. After eating, the snake followed Erqing one by one into the bamboo box.
Snake Man was brought back to training. The little snake circled and bent according to requirements, which was not much different from Erqing. Therefore, I named it Xiaoqing. The Snake Man brought them both, and the four sides performed their skills, and earned a lot of money.
Snakes usually played with by snake hunters are no more than two feet. If they are bigger, they are too heavy, so you have to replace one. Because Erqing is very gentle, the Snake Man didn't replace it right away. After another two or three years, Erqing had grown to more than three feet long. He lay in a bamboo box. The bamboo box was filled to the brim, so the Snake Man decided to let it go.
One day, Snake Man came to Dongshan in Zichuan County, brought out the best food to feed Erqing, blessed it a bit, and then let it go. Erqing left, but after a while he came back, meandering around the bamboo box. The snake man waved at it and said, “Let's go! There is no feast in the world that lasts for 100 years. From then on, you'll hide in the deep mountains and valleys, and you'll definitely be able to train as a dragon in the future. How can bamboo boxes live for a long time?” Erqing only left, and Serpent sent him away. But after a while Erqing came back, and the Snake Man didn't drive it away. He also hit the bamboo box with his head. Xiaoqing kept moving around in the bamboo box. The Snake Man suddenly became enlightened and said, “Do you want to say goodbye to Xiaoqing?” Speaking of which, open the bamboo box. Xiaoqing came straight out of the bamboo box, and Erqing spat with it, as if they were instructing each other to speak. The two snakes then snuggled up and walked together. Snake Man is thinking that Xiaoqing won't come back. After a while, Xiaoqing came back alone and climbed into a bamboo box to lie down.
Since then, snake people have always been looking for new snakes, but there has never been a suitable one. However, Xiaoqing also gradually grew up, and it was not easy for her to perform. Later, Snake Man got a snake, and he was also very tame, but in the end, he wasn't as good as Xiaoqing. At this point, Xiaoqing had grown thicker than a child's arm.
Previously, Erqing was in the mountains, and people gathering firewood often saw it. After a few more years, Erqing grew several feet long, the mouth of the bowl was so thick, and gradually came out to chase people. As a result, pedestrians and travelers all warned each other not to follow the path where it appeared. One day, the snake man passed by, and a snake suddenly flew out, acting like a gust of wind. The Snake Man was terrified and ran desperately. The snake chases even faster. Looking back, he had almost caught up. Suddenly, he saw that the snake had a red dot on its head. Only then did he understand that it was Erqing. He let go of his burden and shouted, “Two green, two green!” The snake stopped, held its head high for a long time, and dragged forward and entangled the snake man, just like in the previous performance. The Snake Man noticed that Erqing had no intention of harming him; he was just too heavy to withstand being entangled by it himself. He had no choice but to fall to the ground and prayed loudly, so Erqing let go of him. Erqing touched the bamboo box again with his head. The Snake Man understood what it meant and opened the bamboo box to release Xiaoqing. As soon as the two snakes met, they immediately became tightly entwined and stuck together like caramel, and it took a long time to separate. Snake Man blessed Xiaoqing and said, “I've wanted to be separated from you for a long time. I'm with you today.” He also said to Erqing, “Xiaoqing was originally brought by you, but you can also lead him away. I would like to advise you one more sentence: there is no shortage of food and drink in the deep mountains, don't disturb passers-by, and avoid being punished by heaven.” Both snakes bowed their heads. As if they had taken his advice, they immediately jumped away. Erqing was in front and Xiaoqing was in the back. Wherever they passed, the trees and grass were separated and fell to both sides. The Snake Man stood there for a long time and watched until he couldn't see it before leaving. Since then, pedestrians have passed through that area safely and safely as before; they don't know where those two snakes have gone.
