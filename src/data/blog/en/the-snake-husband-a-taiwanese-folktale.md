 A long time ago, at the foot of the mountain, three poor sisters lived. The eldest sister is 18, the second sister is 17, and the third sister is 16. My parents have just passed away. Every day, the three poor sisters take turns working to support themselves. One washes and cooks at home, one goes to the fields to water and fertilize, and the other goes to the mountains to pick up firewood and mountain goods.
The eldest sister was about to run back and forth. She heard Silver Snake speak, “Don't be afraid, girl, girl. Snake Lang proposed marriage under the tree. The golden mountains and silver mountains are all yours. Ask the girl if she will get married or not?”
The eldest sister shook her head through the waves and drums, threw her axe at Snake Lang, and turned back home.
The next day, it was the second sister's turn to go up the mountain. The second sister carried two large baskets, followed the path the elder sister said, and went straight to the bottom of a lychee tree full of fruit.
Snake Lang Kun waited there with his elder sister's axe and sang a song to the second sister:
“Girls, girls, don't be afraid. Snake Lang proposes marriage under a tree. The golden mountains and silver mountains are all yours. Ask the girl if she will get married or not?”
The second sister shook her head through the waves and drums, threw the basket she carried over at Snake Lang, and turned back home. On the third day, the three sisters wanted to go to the mountains, and the two sisters advised that lychees must be picked home.
The three sisters came empty-handed under a lychee tree full of fruit. Snake Lang Kun waited there with the older sister's axe and the second sister's basket and sang a song to the three sisters:
“Girls, girls, don't be afraid. Snake Lang proposes marriage under a tree. The golden mountains and silver mountains are all yours. Ask the girl if she will get married or not?”
The third sister answered, “Pick up an axe to cut firewood, pick up the basket and pick the fruit. If Snake Lang has real skills, the third sister will follow Snake Lang.”
Snake Lang hit the tail, and wild fruit jumped into the bamboo basket; Snake Lang hit the second ring, and the firewood flew in bundles; Snake Lang hit the tail three times and turned into a handsome boy.
At noon, the three sisters came home with firewood and wild fruit, and the snake turned into a silver snake lying on the firewood. The three sisters said to the two sisters:
“The elder sister and the second sister take great care. The third sister wants to marry Snake Lang to spend time in the mountains. My older sister and second sister are affectionate, and they even go down to the lychee tree to see me.”
The eldest sister crooked her mouth, and the second sister laughed and bent over. This is the first time a girl has married a snake. The third sister and Snake Lang just left the house amidst the ridicule of the two sisters. Snake Lang led the third sister back under the lychee tree. The third sister put the quilts she had brought from home to the floor, and tears burst into her eyes.
Snake Lang comforted his three sisters: “Don't cry, Snake Lang has a house. does my sister want a money store? Does my sister want a silver store?”
The three sisters burst into laughter: “Just a thatched house.”
After taking the three sisters around the lychee book, the snake hit the third ring. The litchi tree had a large, one-person tall tree cave. The cave had colorful ladders leading directly to the ground. The third sister followed Snake Lang into the cave and down the stairs. Two large houses opposite the door appeared in front of them. One was golden and the other was shiny silver.
Snake Lang became a human being, led his three sisters into the gold house, and also took her to meet the lady weavers next door. The third sister saw that all the furniture was complete, and she was accompanied by a lady. She stayed safe and became Snake Lang's wife.
During the day, Snake Lang all went out hunting. The third sister weaved and cooked with her lady to make a family routine, and was happy for a month.
At noon this day, the third sister missed the two older sisters in the family. She didn't know how their lives were and whether they lacked food or clothing, so she set out on her way home alone and wanted to have a good life with her older sister and her second sister.
The third sister came home, and the eldest sister went out to visit her relatives; the second sister was the only one left. The third sister told the second sister everything that had happened this month, and invited the second sister to stay at Snake Lang's house for a while. The second sister followed the third sister under the lychee tree with half her belief and half doubt.
The second sister looked at the yard, which was full of beautiful flowers; the second sister looked at the house, which was full of famous antiques; the second sister looked at the table, which was filled with delicious dishes; the second sister looked at the cupboard, which was covered with gorgeous satin.
At night, the third sister and the second sister went to the well to get water ready to cook. The second sister was mischievous. One pushed the third sister into the well. The third sister swims around in the water. The second sister hit the fish and made it burnt brown, and was afraid that Snake Lang would come back and recognize herself, so they put on the clothes of the third sister and dressed up like the three sisters. As soon as the meal was served, Snake Lang came back.
Snake Lang looked at the second sister: “The face of the third sister turned white and yellow, and Asako was added.”
The second sister said, “I haven't seen the sun turn yellow in a long time. When cooking, the sesame in the pot jumped on my face.”
When Snake Lang heard the voice, he also asked, “Why was the third sister's voice originally detailed and loud?”
The second sister argued, “I was not careful when I drank water at noon; I choked and coughed in my throat and hurt my body.”
Snake Lang wanted to ask again. The second sister urged him to eat and rest. Snake Lang ate fish, which was very tasty; the second sister ate fish, and her mouth was full of gravel. The second sister was so angry that she threw the fish in the yard and buried it. A bamboo grows where the fish is buried.
Snake Lang went to Takeshita to cool off and practice in the fresh breeze; the second sister went to Takeshita to cool off, burning herself like a fire. The second sister was so angry that she split the bamboo into two bamboo chairs. Snake Lang sat in a bamboo chair and was steady; the second sister sat on the bamboo chair, and people looked up and turned upside down. The second sister was so angry that she took the bamboo chair apart.
As soon as it dawned the next day, Snake Lang went out hunting again, washed his face by the creek, and the green bird flew over and stopped at Snake Lang's bow and arrow.
Snake Lang said, “It's my third sister, flying over my shoulder; it's not my third sister, walking fast and far.”
The kingfisher made a very nice cry, flew up softly, and stopped at Snake Lang's shoulder. Snake Lang immediately understood that the three sisters had been assassinated. He started crying and asked the kingfisher, “Who harmed my wife, and who pretended to be my wife?”
The kingfisher shed tears and couldn't speak. Snake Lang quietly walked past the house with the kingfisher and went to Lady Weaving's house. Lady Weaving left the kingfisher and asked Snake Lang to bring the three sisters' clothes and a pair of chopsticks at midnight to meet the three sisters again.
Serpent came home and drove his second sister out of the house in a fit of anger and turned into a crow. Today, we can often hear the second sister crying in repentance at dusk.
At midnight, Snake Lang came to Lady Weaving's house and heard the loom squeaking and ringing. When I took a closer look, it turned out that the kingfisher was busy.
Lady Weaving called Snake Lang to guard the door. She took the chopsticks brought by Snake Lang and bundled them in a bundle. She threw them over to the kingfisher, and the chopsticks became the real body of the third sister. Lady Weaving also threw her clothes at the Third Sister. The three sisters' songs came up on the loom:
“Thanks to the lady for her kindness. The three sisters returned to their original appearance. In order to repay the blessings of their parents, they knitted brocades for the lady.”
