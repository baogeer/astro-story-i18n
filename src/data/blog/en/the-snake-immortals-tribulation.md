 In Heiniuyingzi Township, Chaoyang County, Chaoyang City, western Liaoning, there is a mountain, Dagu Mountain, which stands majestically above the mountains. It looks like the mountain of Big Brother.
As long as there is enough moisture, the top of it will be covered with clouds. The famous temple “Sosenji” in the middle of the mountainside also looms there, giving people a sense of mystery.
The book returns to its original origin:
Folklore tells the story that a long, long time ago, next to the twin springs on this mountain, a pair of snake sisters practiced fairy arts. They don't interfere with people's lives here; on the contrary, they help people get rid of disasters and diseases. They sprinkled elixirs in the spring water. People can cure diseases by drinking this water.
Now when people go to Shuosenji Temple to burn incense, they can't forget to bring back a bottle of spring water to drink for the family.
After thousands of years of training, the sisters are able to fly in the sky. If they want to become a fairy, they must go through thunderstorms.
On this day, the two sisters were practicing flying in the sky. Suddenly, lightning thundered, and they were both surrounded by lightning. They cast spells and fled to the sea. If they wanted to hide under the sea, why didn't they have enough mana to reach the sea, they were struck by a thunderbolt from Lei Gong's electric mother. Once the two sisters were crushed to pieces, their souls were scattered.
The practice of a thousand years was destroyed. Unfortunately, the heist failed.
Because they have done good deeds for people, they have now built temples to pay them, and they are paid as immortals!
