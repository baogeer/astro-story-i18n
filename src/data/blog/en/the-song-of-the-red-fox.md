 Author: Shen Siwu
There is no way to tell which generation the story takes place in which generation. However, it was said that at the time, there was a person named Zhang Jingqiu who loved to compose songs and wrote hundreds of songs, none of which caused any stir in the music industry. Discouraged, he decided to take a trip to find some inspiration.
One day, he walked through the forest. It was dark, and he climbed a big tree to sleep. Suddenly, after a strong wind, a boy in red, about six or seven years old, ran to the open space under the tree, held a flute, and eagerly played a very inflammatory and combative song. Zhang Jingqiu heard it with enthusiasm. Soon, a 15- or 6-year-old boy in yellow came after him with a long sword. The teenager acted viciously and stabbed his opponent at critical points with all his tricks. The boy in red only dodged and had no power to fight back. The situation was very critical.
Unpacking the bag, Zhang Jingqiu took out his self-defense dagger and threw it at the young man in yellow. The boy in yellow hid by his side, and still pressed the boy in red to keep going. The boy in red kept on playing the flute while avoiding concessions. At this point, a few people in red ran wild and frantically besieged the young man in yellow. The young man in yellow was left and right, overwhelmed, and had to flee into desolation. The boy in red proudly stopped the flute. The few people in red, who had just been ferocious and abnormal, immediately woke up and strangely asked each other, “Why are we here?” The boy in red ignored them. He walked under the tree, looked up and thanked Zhang Jingqiu for his help, and invited him back home with him.
Zhang Jingqiu, who is not well-educated, has an excellent memory and can memorize countless famous songs. However, he had never heard the song The Boy in Red played. What's more, a piece of music has so much magic. If he hadn't seen it with his own eyes, no matter who told him, he wouldn't believe it. He was curious and wanted to know what kind of person the boy in red was. He climbed down the tree and followed the boy in red.
Zhang Jingqiu followed him one step deeper and one foot in the back, and the place they went was getting more and more remote. After walking for a while, he looked up and found a gorgeous house in front of him. An old man with white beard was standing at the entrance of the house. The man in red stood respectfully standing his hand. The boy in red shouted “dad,” and whispered a few words to the old man. The old man immediately invited Zhang Jingqiu into the house.
The old man claims that his last name is Hu, and he lives in seclusion here. He asked if there were any good songs in the world and asked Zhang Jingqiu to write them. Zhang Jingqiu wrote more than 100 songs from memory. The old man was very happy. He ordered someone to bring in the music score for his wife, and ordered the servant to host a feast. The boy in red toasted and served the meal, and was very attentive. Zhang Jingqiu asked him for the score of the song just now. The boy in red said, “It's my improvised work; it's not worth anything.” When you're done, pick up the pen and you're good to go. The name of the song is “The Red Fox Song”. Zhang Jingqiu felt a bit puzzled. Since he loved that song so much, he couldn't help but remember it. The old man came over and took a look. He changed his face and had to burn the score.
The score instantly turned to ashes, and Zhang Jingqiu was shocked to ask why. The old man sighed, “Inuko is gifted and smart. Her understanding of music is extraordinary. However, she is smart and doesn't need to be on the right path; she loves to write such outrageous songs.” The boy in red couldn't help but chime in: “This song saved the child's life.” The old man was furious: “Have you offended Prince Wang again?” The boy in red pouted and whispered: “Then let him kill the kid?” The old man shouted: “Killing you is also natural; you must not disobey at all.” The boy in red bowed his head and stopped talking.
Zhang Jingqiu was left in the fog by the father and son's remarks, and it was not convenient to ask questions in detail; he had to bury his head and eat.
After eating, the old man asked the boy in red to walk around the garden with him. At the edge of the pond, the boy in red looked down and played in the water in displeasure. Zhang Jingqiu took out hundreds of songs he had written and asked him for advice. The boy in red immediately flared up. He left two songs. The rest all said it didn't work, and threw them all into the pond. Zhang Jingqiu was so embarrassed that his face turned red. The boy in red said, “What do you want that waste for? I've changed these two of your songs, so no one else in the world can do it.” He is half-convinced and half-skeptical. The boy in red took a pen, changed his manuscript for a while, and handed it to him. As soon as Zhang Jingqiu watched it, he couldn't help but be overjoyed: it really turned a stone into gold! The Boy in Red also taught him songwriting techniques, which made him feel unattainable fame and fortune. At the moment, it's like exploring a bag.
This night, Zhang Jingqiu couldn't sleep no matter what. As soon as the rooster began to cry for the first time, he couldn't wait to say goodbye, and the old man didn't hold back.
Outside the door, he met a beautiful middle-aged woman claiming to be the boy's mother. She said, “I think my husband has a great memory. I may have written down “The Red Fox Song”. Remember not to play this song, let alone pass it on. If you can do that, sir, our whole family would be grateful.” Zhang Jingqiu was in a hurry and nodded in agreement. The beautiful woman was still not at ease. She took a token with the word “Hu” engraved on it from her sleeve and handed it to him, saying it was for self-defense.
Zhang Jingqiu went straight to Beijing. The first thing I did when I arrived in Beijing was to write “Red Fox Song” and frame it. Take it with you all the time, and play silently in your heart every day.
Two years later, he became a famous composer and a disciple of the Ten Kings.
Zhang Jingqiu, who is famous for his success, has not forgotten the boy in red. He has prepared a generous gift and is ready to go to the Hu family. While walking, he visited the places of interest and monuments along the way. On this day, he met an acquaintance. He heard that the Ten Kings would host a songwriting competition in advance, and they are sending people everywhere to find him. Zhang Jingqiu knew he couldn't get away with it; he wrote it at the hotel that night. In the middle of the night, he wrote, threw away his pen, and went to bed with his kimono. Wake up at dawn, pack up your baggage, and prepare to enter Beijing to make ends meet. It didn't matter if he cleaned it up; he discovered that “Red Fox Song” had disappeared.
Zhang Jingqiu was in a cold sweat and didn't know what to do. People sent by Prince 10 found him and said that Grandpa Wang had ordered him to be escorted back to Beijing. Disappointed, Zhang Jingqiu followed his official mission to Beijing. For two days in a row, Grandpa Ten personally accompanied him; he only had a strong fighting spirit. On the third and fourth day, Grandpa Ten did not come. I heard the servant say that he had a song unrivaled in the world. Zhang Jingqiu didn't care; he just wanted to get in touch with the Hu family to find that crazy score. He asked his servant to tell Grandpa Ten that something happened in his hometown and that he had to leave for some time.
Zhang Jingqiu took a horse-drawn carriage and quickly left Beijing. As soon as the horse-drawn carriage arrived at the entrance of the city gate, a notice was posted next to the city gate. Gong Zhi met him and smiled and said, “Grandpa Zhang only finished second this year, which is a pity.” Zhang Jingqiu laughed, “Strong has its own midfielder. What is the name of this number one song?” What Gong Za said frightened him and almost fell off the carriage: “Red Fox Song.”
Zhang Jingqiu hurriedly ordered the chauffeur to turn over the carriage and went straight to the Ten Kings Palace. He asked to see Prince 10, but Prince 10 was very busy.
After lunch, Zhang Jingqiu heard from his servant that Grandpa Ten had rested and that he would have time to see him at night. He walked around the garden in a hurry and couldn't think of a way. Walking outside a pavilion, I was about to sit in. I found that the windows on all sides of the pavilion were tightly closed. I was afraid I would run into someone else's privacy. He quietly wanted to go back. After listening to a teenager's voice referring to the “Red Fox Song,” Zhang Jingqiu stopped, then listened to the teenager continue saying, “Now, the Hu family can't wash up even if they jump into the Yellow River.” An adult voice: “Prince Wang is an amazing plan! How did you know that idiot had 'Red Fox Song' in his bag?” The young man proudly said, “What don't I know? The children in the Hu family never paid attention to me, huh! I want him to try it out great this time.” The adult said, “The Hu family still doesn't know. The villain servant came to Beijing to go shopping, and he really did find his own way.” The young man smiled and said, “As soon as the 'Red Fox Song' is played, the servants of the Hu family will go crazy and kill people. At that time, the emperor will not pursue the Hu family, and God will not let them go.” The adult suddenly shouted, “Someone.” Zhang Jingqiu quickly dived into the flowers and was afraid to move at all.
Zhang Jingqiu recognized the boy in the gorgeous costume who walked out of the pavilion. It was the person who fought with the boy in red back then. He followed the brightly dressed adult in yellow to look around and find the eavesdropping person. The young man in yellow stopped him: “Don't lose your age because you are young.” The two walked out of the garden.
Zhang Jingqiu frantically filled the street asking if the people in red were servants of the Hu family, but not a single one was.
In the evening, Grandpa Shi ordered his servants to take him to the dance hall in Wangfu, Lixiangyuan. Outside of Lixiangyuan, Zhang Jingqiu heard that the band had already started playing “Red Fox Song”. He was shocked and stumbled in. A beautiful dancer dressed in red and danced together to a song. He just shouted, “Can't play.” A group of people in red have already stormed in and are slaughtering and killing in a burst of chaos. Zhang Jingqiu held the token and went straight to the Ten Kings. Although the man in red is close to crazy, he still has some concerns about the “Hu” token. Seeing him protect the Ten Princes, he is afraid to take action. The poor performers kept playing until they were all killed. As soon as the music stopped, the people in red looked at each other as usual: “Why are we here?” When they saw the house full of dead bodies, they were shocked and rushed out the door. When the bodyguard arrived to hear the news, there was no trace of the man in red.
Grandpa Ten, who was initially frightened, looked down at the token Zhang Jingqiu had in his hand. Zhang Jingqiu immediately hid it in his sleeve. Despite the astonished eyes of Grandpa Ten, he picked up a musical score and set it on a candle to burn.
Grandpa Shi's house was brightly lit, and his minions were busy carrying corpses. Zhang Jingqiu was not at ease with the Hu family. He said goodbye to Grandpa Ten. Grandpa Ten had a hint of murder in his eyes, but soon he agreed with a smile and ordered someone to prepare a fast horse. Zhang Jingqiu left Beijing overnight. The night was full of thunder and heavy rain, so he had to stay at a hotel.
Confused, he saw the boy in red standing in front of him. He hadn't seen him for several years. The boy in red had grown quite tall, lost a lot of weight, and looked depressed. Zhang Jingqiu quickly got out of bed and told him about the loss of “Red Fox Song.” The boy in red cried and said, “I'll tell you the truth. Our family is all vixens, then Wang Gong is just a tiger spirit. We foxes are bound to be bullied by tigers for generations, and even if they become elves, they have to be mad at tigers. I didn't agree; he treated me as his enemy. At that time, I was in a hurry and made a song to save my life. That song can make those vixens who don't go deep enough exude extraordinary energy. I didn't expect it to end up hurting everyone.”
I blame myself for extracting the “Red Fox Song”! Zhang Jingqiu's heart was full of remorse. He knelt down on the ground to ask for forgiveness from the boy in red. Suddenly, a lightning bolt came in from outside the window and hit the boy in red violently, and the boy in red's clothes immediately ignited. A strange wind blew open the closed door of the house and swept away the boy in red. An anxious voice from the boy in red came from the air: “Run away! Run away! Grandpa Ten is already suspicious of you.”
Zhang Jingqiu wanted to save the boy in red. Who knows, as soon as he took a step, he fell down a cliff a lot. Surprised, he woke up suddenly and had forgotten most of his dreams. At this point, the sky was already clear. It was a fresh sight after the rain. Zhang Jingqiu rode on horseback and discovered that Grandpa Ten's entourage was secretly following him. In order not to expose the Hu family, he went through a few circles, got rid of the followers of the Ten Kings, and only then went back on the road.
After walking for nine days and nine nights, I finally arrived at the forest where I met the boy in red. On the way to Hu's house, there were several burnt red fox corpses. When it comes to the Hu family, where are there any luxury residences? It was clearly a huge fox cave that had been destroyed by lightning. There were three burnt fox corpses in the cave.
Zhang Jingqiu suddenly remembered the scene in his dream. It turned out that the Boys in Red family were really vixens. The poor boy in red also risked his life to find him and let him run away quickly. Zhang Jingqiu picked up the small fox's dead body from the rubble and couldn't help but cry bitterly.
Having buried all the dead foxes he could find, Zhang Jingqiu cried for three days and three nights at the grave of the Boy in Red family before leaving the forest. Legend has it that he later saw a beautiful and beautiful lake with 108 small islands in the lake. Local natives call it Weixia Lake. Those islands are indeed like tiny imperfections in jasper. He then settled down on one of the islands, fasted and prayed all day long, and didn't know anything about the world until he was 108 years old, and ended up without illness.
