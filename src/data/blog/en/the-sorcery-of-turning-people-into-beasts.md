 It can be said that there are many types of witchcraft that pretend to be ghosts and deceive people. Some witchcraft uses deliciousness as bait to lure you into eating it, and then you will lose your mind and can't help but follow him. This is commonly known as “whipping”, and the whole area of Jiangnan is called “teasing.” Children are ignorant, often deceived, and profoundly victimized. There is also a type of witchcraft that can turn people into livestock, called “animal making.” This kind of witchcraft is rare in the northern part of Jiangbei; it is common south of the Yellow River.
One day, at a hotel in Yangzhou, a man came in, holding five donkeys, and chained them under a stable. He ordered the store partner to say, “I'll be back soon,” and ordered, “Don't give them water to drink.” When I finished speaking, I went out. The donkeys were so irritated by the sun that they kicked and barked. The owner took them to a cool place. As soon as the donkey saw the water and was struggling to run past, the owner let the donkey drink enough. In a blink of an eye, I saw the donkey rolling around on the ground. In the midst of the dust, it immediately became a woman. The owner was very surprised and asked the woman what was going on. The woman had a hard tongue and couldn't say anything. The shopkeeper was busy hiding the woman in the house. After a while, the owner of the donkey came back and tied the five sheep he had brought back to the yard. When I found out that the donkey was missing, I panicked and asked the owner. The owner hurriedly pulled him down and ordered someone to bring him the meal, relieving him, “Eat first, the donkey will be here soon.” The shopkeeper went out and let the sheep drink enough water. As soon as they rolled around, they all became children. So I secretly sued the county about the incident. The government immediately sent someone to apprehend the sorcerer, and then beat him to death with a bang.
