 Some people once said that you can tell that Mulan is a woman by reading the phrase “East City buys a horse, West City buys a horse, South City buys a horse, North City buys a long whip.” Because of men's shopping habits, they usually go straight back and forth and don't run to four markets in a row. By the way, what was the view of the ancients on consumption? You may be able to find some answers from the poems of the ancients.
Among the poets of the Tang dynasty, Li Bai was undoubtedly the most elegant and very lucky. Li Bai was born in the first year of Wu Zetian's Dazu (701). Apart from catching up with the “Anshi Rebellion” in his later years, he basically lived during the Shengtang period, so his psalms are mostly free-spirited. Among Li Bai's poems, nothing is more impressive than his love for drinking and traveling.
Li Bai is a “poetry fairy” and a “wine fairy”. He is a good drinker, and he also writes many famous poems about drinking, “One pot of wine, no blind date”, “Ten thousand gold barrels of fine wine, the rice bowl is worth ten thousand dollars”... Li Bai's masterpiece “Will Drink Wine” is almost an exhortation. “You must enjoy your life to the fullest; don't let the Golden Barrel fly to the Moon” “If you are happy to eat sheep and cows, you will have to drink 300 cups.” “In ancient times, all the sages were lonely, only drinkers were lonely. Leave your name alone”. At the end of the drink, “Five Flowers, a Thousand Gold, Hu'er will exchange fine wine and sell all the ancient sorrows with him”, but it was luxurious enough.
Furthermore, Li Bai is also a “travel expert.” He has traveled to many places in his life, and wherever he went, he was warmly received by friends from all walks of life. For example, the famous phrase “The water in Taohuatan is a thousand feet deep, not as deep as Wang Lun gave me love”, and even the owner, Wang Lun, is famous. Among Li Bai's many travel poems, there are scenes such as “Phoenix Tour on Phoenix Terrace, Phoenix Goes Wild to the Taikong River”, and others include food and drink, such as “Lanling wine and tulips, jade bowl full of amber light”. In current terms, these are all contributing to the tourism economy.
Li Bai's family is in a good state of affairs. His previous and subsequent wives were also granddaughters of the prime minister, so he was quite well-off most of the time. Because of this, even though Li Bai spends money like running water, his concept of money is very weak. In his words, “Everyone knows that life is expensive; why do we need money and money?” Furthermore, Li Bai also has quite a bit of confidence in his talents. The so-called “I was born to be useful, and I will return after losing my money”. It is this arrogance that makes Li Bai have the arrogance that “An Neng destroys powerful people and makes me unhappy.”
Compared to Li Bai, Du Fu, known as the “poet,” was not so liberal. Du Fu was born in the first year of Tang Xuanzong's birth (712). He is only 11 years younger than Li Bai, but his situation is much worse than Li Bai. For example, when drinking, Li Bai used “ten thousand gold barrels of fine wine,” but when he arrived at Du Fu's place, it became “street alcohol prices are often too expensive, and drinkers from outside are very drunk; if you drink it quickly, it's 300 bronze dollars.” Obviously, the alcohol Du Fu drank was much worse than Li Bai in terms of grade.
Judging from the psalms left by Du Fu, he himself also loves to drink, but more often he is poor and has no alcohol, and uses alcohol to relieve his worries. Related verses are not uncommon in Du Shi, such as “Go back to the sun and get drunk in the river every day”, “Now that the 9th has arrived, I am conscious about drinking alcohol,” and “Shu wine is prohibited, and there is no money where to spend it”. Because he has no money to buy alcohol, Du Fu had to rely on a pawn or credit account, and later “rely on his Sus business and always beg for money.” This was because he was able to continue drinking with his friend Susie.
According to his origin, Du Fu is also considered the son of a government official. He lived a rich life as a teenager, saw big scenes, and visited Wu Yue and Qi Zhao successively. However, after becoming an adult, Du Fu had to go to the elite to try to donate, but to no avail, because he failed the exam many times. In the end, after being stuck in Chang'an for more than ten years, Du Fu was depressed and unmotivated. He laughed at himself, “Dulingye's guests were even more stubborn, and they were browned short and silky. “When I bought five litres of rice in Taicang, I went to the same period as Zheng Lao”. This made me so embarrassed that I wanted to buy Taicang rice at a reduced price to eat.
It wasn't until the 14th year of Tianbao that Du Fu, who was over 40, was awarded a minor official such as Lieutenant Hexi (not in office) and Right Guard leading the government soldier Cao to join the army. Just this year, the “Anshi Rebellion” broke out, and Du Fu was in the midst of war and turmoil, which was very embarrassing. As the famous “Ascend Me” poem says, “It's hard and bitter to hate heavy ice cream, so I can't even drink alcohol at all.” Not to mention this, Du Fu's newly built cottage was also hit by strong winds, “in the fall of August, the wind rolled over my roof three times”; then, there was another torrential rain, “the bedside house had no place to dry, and the rain didn't stop.” This day was really uneasy. According to Du Fu, his personal plight is actually also the plight of the people all over the world, but in the midst of his plight, he is still worried about the country and the people, and he has the will of the world to “settle down tens of millions of Guangzhou buildings and make everyone happy.” At this point, if Du Fu were a high-ranking official, he would probably also run out of his family's wealth to save his life!
In Tang Dynasty cities, people with poor consumption levels were not only book readers, but also poor people with lower social status. For example, “Selling Charcoal Men” by Bai Juyi: “Selling Charcoal Makes Money, Which Business Runs, Eat Your Clothes in Your Mouth. The poor shirt is just right, and I'm worried and wish the weather was cold.” The consumption expenses of charcoal sellers are mainly food and clothing, and they cannot even meet their basic needs for warmth in the cold winter; their poverty is known.
As a great poet after Li Bai and Du Fu, Bai Juyi used to visit famous people when he was obscure. The latter joked with the name “Bai Juyi”: “Everything in Chang'an is expensive; it's not easy to get big.” However, when I read, “When you leave your roots, you prosper when you are one year old. When “The wildfires are burning, and the spring breeze is blowing again,” Gu Quang couldn't help but appreciate the shooting: “There is such a saying, how difficult is it to be in the world! The old man's ear for the joke!”
As Gu Quang said, Bai Juyi's life in Chang'an certainly went well. At the time, the “Anshi Rebellion” had settled down, and social life was gradually recovering. Bai Juyi was also far luckier than Li Bai and Du Fu in his career. After winning his studies, he was soon promoted to the rank of Secretary and Provincial School Shulang, and soon to Zuo Shi Ye of the Metropolitan Public Prosecutor's Office and Cao of Beijing and Zhaofu to join the military. Bai Juyi's income has continued to rise due to a happy career. Even though he was later evicted from Beijing, he had a very good life. In addition to his official salary, Bai Juyi also had huge expenses. For example, when he wrote “The Memoirs of Henan Yuan Cemetery” for Yuan Yi, he earned 700,000 dollars for 1,400 words. According to the records in “Zhi Ge Ge Ji Ge”, Chang'an is very hot in summer, and many senior officials often buy ice cubes to cool down the heat. Since the ice cubes were stored last winter, the price was very high. Not to mention that ordinary people can't afford it; that is, officials or wealthy businessmen only buy small plots of land, and Bai Juyi “takes it from the basket and doesn't pay the price anymore,” and this is true every day. This consumption capacity is quite high.
Because he has always been an official, Bai Juyi has also accumulated quite a bit of wealth throughout his life. According to “Dazai Rakuten”, Bai Juyi disposed of his family property in his old age, “first selling the ten-acre garden in Nanfang and then selling five hectares of fields in Dongdu. Then I sold my house as if I had won 23,000.” The reason for this is because “I am now 71 years old, and my eyesight is blurry. But I'm afraid I won't be able to run out of money, so I went back to Night Spring first.” It can be seen from this that Bai Juyi is quite open in terms of money, which is why he has the feeling of “life and death are everything, until it is Bai Rakuten”!
