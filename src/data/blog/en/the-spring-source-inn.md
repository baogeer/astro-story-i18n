 During the Jiajing period of the Ming dynasty, there was a “Yuanquan Inn” in Luzhou. The owner was named Liang Jiazhu. This year was the year of big time. For a while, the Yuanquan Inn was filled with students arriving from all over the province.
This evening, Leung Ka-chu, who had finished his work outside, hurriedly rushed to the hotel. As he passed through an alley, he suddenly heard a cry for help, then hurriedly followed the sound and ran past.
It was only recently that Leung Ka-chu discovered that a few gangsters were robbing a scholar, and he knew the scholar, whose name was Xia Shanlai, had been staying at the Yuen Quan Hotel for over half a month because he was in a hurry to take the exam.
Leung Ka-chu hurriedly drank loudly, waved his fists and rushed forward to drive away the few bastards. I learned later that Xia Shan went out for a walk in the evening and didn't want to run into those bastards.
More than a month passed quickly, and the day of the leaderboard came. Xia Shan came to be famous as Sun Mountain. The students left the Yuanquan Inn one after another. On this day, after saying goodbye to Leung Ka-chu, Xia Shan rode on horseback and embarked on the journey home. Leung asked a friend to clean up the room Xia Shan had stayed in. Soon, the dude came to Leung Ka-chu with a small wooden box and said he had found it while cleaning up his room. Apparently, it was left behind by Xia Shan.
Leung Ka-chu took the small wooden box and placed it in his room. In his opinion, after Xia Shan discovered that he had lost the small wooden box, he would definitely go back to the hotel soon to search for it. At that point, returning it to Xia Shan would be considered a return to its original owner. Unexpectedly, after a full day of waiting, and not being able to wait for Xia Shan to come, Leung Ka-chu couldn't help but sigh to himself: Xia Shan was really careless when he came to this young man!
After another three days of waiting, they still haven't been able to wait for Xia Shan to come. Leung Ka-chu knows that Xia Shan can't wait for the time being. In desperation, he had to put that small wooden box in one of his cabinets for a while. Unexpectedly, when the cabinet door was just opened, he lost his hand for a moment. The small wooden box actually fell to the ground. The case cover was broken, and a silver ticket with a value of 52 was lying inside.
The days slowly passed for three years, and now it was the year of Dabi, and many students rushing to take the exam stayed at the Yuen Quan Inn one after another. Leung Ka-chu waited at the front door of the hotel every day, but he waited until the day of Dabi, and never saw Xia Shan come. Leung Ka-chu was in a hurry, so he looked for it day and night in other inns. But who knows, after searching all the inns in Luzhou city, I haven't been able to find Xia Shan.
Leung Ka-chu is even more anxious -- the 502 silver ticket is not a small amount! He wanted to return the cash ticket to Xia Shan's home, but he only heard Xia Shan say that Xia's family lives in southern Anhui, but Xia Shan never told him the exact location of Xia's family. The mountain is high and the road is long. Where should I go to find Xia's family? He had to quit.
One day after half a year, Leung Ka-chu returned from going out and circled around her room, looking very restless. After a bit of hard work, he stomped his feet and made up his mind to take out the small wooden box from the cabinet, take out the silver ticket, and muttered in his mouth, “Dude, Yamakai, I want to use this 52 silver to do business. If the business loses, even if I borrow 502 of money from you, I will definitely pay back the amount; if I make a profit, then even if we make a profit, the two of us have partnered to do business, split it in five or five, and when I see you the next time I see you, I'll pay back the money and profits together!”
It turns out that recently, Leung Ka-chu unexpectedly encountered a good deal and made a lot of money, so he started thinking about doing business. The transaction required the principal amount of 1.2 silver, but Leung Ka-chu had only 502 silver on hand, so, in desperation, he planned to use the 52 banknote from Xia Shan...
Soon after, the sale was completed, and Leung Ka-chu earned quite a bit of money. Since then, he was unable to accept it. His business grew bigger and he earned more money. Five years later, he became a very famous wealthy businessman in Luzhou City. However, the “Source Inn” was always open, but unfortunately, he never saw Xia Shan again.
In February of this year, Leung Ka-chu felt that he really couldn't wait any longer, so he brought a large number of cash tickets and arrived in southern Anhui with many friends. There are more than ten counties in southern Anhui. Liang Jiazhu bought a plot of land and then built an inn in each bustling area of the county town. A sign hangs above the main door of every inn. Without exception, the four characters “Yuanquan Inn” are engraved on the signs. Underneath those four large letters, these lines are also engraved: Branch of Luzhou Yuanquan Inn.
Why did Leung Ka-chu do this? It turns out that this is his plan: since I don't know which county the Xia family lives in southern Anhui, let alone the detailed address of the Xia family, I have to open a “Source Inn” in every county in southern Anhui, hoping that one day in the future, Xia Mountain will discover one of these inns, so I can think of the past, walk into the inn, and ask the treasurer and guys in the hotel about my recent situation. In this way, I have found him...
Since then, every few days, Leung Ka-chuk will let go of his business affairs and rush to southern Anhui to stay in each “Yuen Quan Inn” for a few days, waiting for Xia Shan to show up. Of course, he has long described to his treasurer and friends what it looked like when he passed Xia Shan in detail.
One day in April of this year, Liang Jiazhu arrived in Jingxian County and stayed at the “Source Spring Inn”. After lunch, he just wanted to take a break. Suddenly he heard a dude shouting outside the hotel door: “What are you looking at? What's so pretty? Go, go, get out of here!” Leung Ka-chu thought that guy was really out of touch, so she called the guy into the hotel to inquire about the situation.
The dude complained with a big grin that just now, a beggar stood in front of the front door of the hotel, looked up at the sign, kept wiping tears and wouldn't leave for a long time. He feared that the beggar would affect the hotel's business, so he shouted and drove the beggar away.
Liang Jiazhu raised his face and harshly reprimanded the dude for a few words, then asked him to take a plate of food and give it to the beggar. The dude was about to go out with his plate. Leung Ka-chu suddenly stopped him and asked what the beggar looked like. The friend said, “The owner, I can't see the beggar's face clearly at all because of the dirt on his face. However, judging from the fact that he kept wiping tears, that sign seemed to make him very sad.”
After hearing this, Leung Ka-chu hurriedly took a plate from her friend and walked out of the hotel in a meteoric manner. However, there were no beggars at all in front of the gate. Leung Ka-chu was anxious. The dude suddenly said, “It's him, he's the beggar just now!”
Leung Ka-chu looked in the direction of her partner's finger, only to see a raggly dressed man walking at a steady pace, holding a rice bowl in his hand. He hurriedly ran past and stopped in front of the beggar.
After carefully identifying the face of the beggar, the plate in Leung Ka-chu's hand suddenly “popped” to the ground. I saw him shudder his hands and burst into tears: “Dude Yamakai, it was you! How did you look like this? After all, we haven't seen each other in ten years!” The bowl in the beggar's hands also immediately fell to the ground: “Brother Liang, why did you come to Jing County?” It turns out that the beggar is from Xia Shan.
After hugging her head and crying, Leung Ka-chu took Xia Shanlai's hand and entered the hotel. According to Xia Shan, his family was originally a wealthy family, so when he went to Luzhou City to catch the exam ten years ago, he carried quite a few cash tickets. After falling off the list, he rushed back to Jing County and returned to his home. He didn't want his father to die of serious illness. As the only child in the family, he had to take over all of the family's businesses. Due to being young, energetic, and inexperienced, the business lost money year by year. Three years ago, his family's property was completely destroyed...
After listening to Xia Shanlai's remarks, Leung Ka-chu couldn't help but feel sad again. He placed an abacus on the table and shouted, “Brother Yamakai, it's a blessing for us to meet today; otherwise, I don't know when I'll be able to return your money. Today, let's split the money!” Xia Shanlai was confused: “Divide the money? What kind of silver?” Leung Ka-chu hurriedly explained in detail the fact that he had initially started a business using the 52 silver vouchers from Xia Shan as capital. Xia Shanlai was taken aback: “Brother Leung, I intentionally 'left those 52 silver tickets in the hotel to thank you for saving them that evening. How can I split your money?” But Leung Ka-chu said, “Brother Shanlai, the so-called saving grace is actually just a matter of showing a hand. How can I use gratitude? I must distribute the money that belongs to you!”
Speaking of which, Leung Ka-chu “crackled” the abacus... In the end, Leung Ka-chu calculated that Xia Shan should have earned 58,600 dollars. As a result, the Xia family made a comeback...
