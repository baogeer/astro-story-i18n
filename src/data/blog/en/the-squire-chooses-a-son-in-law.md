 Once upon a time, there was an only daughter in his family, and his wife loved her very much.
Life is going by so fast. The young lady outside the staff is 20 years old. She really looks gorgeous. As a result, Yu Wai thought she should find a son-in-law for her, so she asked a matchmaker to help introduce her.
After a few days, the matchmaker introduced a young man named Wang Gui. Although Wang Gui has little knowledge, his father is a local official and quite powerful, so the staff is quite in agreement with this family affair.
However, Mrs. Yuan Wai strongly opposed it, because she had a long crush on a young man named Cai Cai, who was a millionaire young man.
As for the young lady herself, she is not interested in these two family matters chosen by her parents, because she has always liked the classmate Zhao Yi she met while studying at a private school when she was a child. Although his family is poor, Zhao Yi is very educated. When he was 22 years old, he was already selected for talent, and he is still working hard to advance. So the young lady thought he was a promising young man.
In the winter of that year, the parents of the three young people all officially invited someone to come and talk to the rest of the staff. Mr. Wai was unable to resolve this marriage for a while. While he was feeling very nervous, I had no idea that all three young people had already gone to the county government office one after another.
After Zhi Prefecture learned about this incident, they called all three young people to the government for questioning. In the face of Zhi Prefecture, all three young people claimed that they were the son-in-law. They hoped that the prefectural lord would uphold justice, but no one would give up. Outside the staff, his wife, and the young lady also believed that the young man they chose was the best candidate. The prefecture really didn't know what to do about this complicated case, so they asked them to go home first and have a separate investigation tomorrow.
When Chi-ken returned to her official residence to rest, Chi-ken's wife saw that her husband had a puzzled look on her face and inquired about the reason. She learned that she had a headache in order to resolve this marriage dispute. Mrs. Chigen is a very smart woman. After thinking for a while, she suddenly came up with a good idea and told Chigen what she meant.
“This is a fantastic idea! ” Chigen couldn't help but applaud.
The next day, before going up to the church, Chi-ken called the young lady next to her and quietly told her about her plans. Shortly after the interrogation began, the young lady suddenly screamed for stomach pain, and then she was struggling on the ground.
Chiken immediately called the left and right servants to take the young lady to her official residence for first aid. After about an hour, the officers came back and told the county, “After the doctor's first aid, there was still no way to save the young lady's life. She was finally exhausted.”
After listening quietly to the sergeant's report, Zhigen expressed a serious expression and declared, “As everyone has just heard, the young lady died suddenly due to a sudden illness. If the three of you really love her, go buy a coffin right away and take her dead body back to cook for later!”
The three young people were dazed for a moment and didn't know how to respond. After a while, Wang Gui first spoke up: “County Grandpa, I want to marry a living man; it's no use getting it from a dead person!”
“Of course! What about a fool in the world who asks a dead man to be his wife?” Cai Cai went on to answer like this.
“No! Mr. County, I am deeply in love with the young lady. Whether she is alive or dead, I want her to be my wife. Please give me her body and I'll go buy a coffin right away.” When Zhao Yi said this, she immediately had to run outside.
Only then did Zhi Prefecture stand up, put a smile on her face, and declared, “Zhao Yi, please wait. Now I see that you are the son-in-law who really loves the young lady. I am really moved by your words and actions. Honestly, the young lady didn't die of a sudden illness; I asked her to pretend to be sick to test your sincerity. Here, the judge ruled that the young lady should marry Zhao Yi as a wife.”
Hearing the verdict from Zhigen Prefecture, Wang Gui and Cai Cai were so ashamed that they couldn't even lift their heads, and quietly left the county government office. The young lady soon married Zhao Yi, and both of them lived very happy lives since then.
