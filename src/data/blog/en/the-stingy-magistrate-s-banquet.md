 Once upon a time, there was a sect (meaning county official in Tibetan). His home was full of moldy food, but he couldn't bear to give it to the people. He was so stingy that he wanted to use a large amount of water; he was so aggressive that he wanted to peel off the skin on other people's bodies to wear as clothes.
The county governor's family has a smart servant named Tudeng. He often thinks about how to get some oil out of his master's hands. One day, Tudeng announced to the people, “I want Zongben to set up a feast for a few days. Please keep your ears up and wait for him to post!”
The people laughed and said, “Can wolves still bleed in their mouths? If Sōmon, who loves money like his life, sets a feast for us, unless the sun comes out from the top of Mount Xisan1”
Tudeng smiled and answered, “Don't you believe it? Let's wait a few days and see!”
There is only one spring in the prefecture that people can drink. Strangely enough, this spring is hot as soon as winter arrives, and it becomes clear and cool in summer. Munemoto took possession of the spring water, saying that it was sacred water given to him by Lord Buddha.
One day, Munemoto was walking on top of the castle and suddenly saw Tudeng lying on a big tree by the spring, staring blankly at the spring water. He shook his head for a while and bent over for a while, as if he were arguing with someone. Munemoto walked down the castle and came to Tudeng and asked, “Why are you pointing fingers here?”
“Ahhh, Honourable Munemoto!” Tudeng smiled and said, “Who knows, you are a kind and kind person. You worship the Three Treasures and give back to the people.” At this point, he suddenly put his smile away and angrily said, “But today I saw a dragon in the spring water, unscrupulously slandered adults, and said a lot of bad things about you.”
Munemoto hurriedly asked, “How can dragons slander me?”
Tudeng slowly answered, “Jiaolong said that Zongben made the people perform hard labor all year round and obtained countless wealth from it, yet he couldn't bear to give even a bite of chopsticks. A stingy person who loves money like this will sooner or later be like a hungry ghost dying of thirst in the sea; it will not end well. When I heard this, I was so angry that I shivered all over my body. I countered that we had been busy and busy all day long, all for the comfort and happiness of the people. He's definitely not the kind of person you said. Let's not say give a bowl of delicious pickles, just ask him to set up a feast for the people for a few days, and he will agree without hesitation.”
Munemoto grinned with joy and even nodded, “Right! Right! You're right!”
Tudeng went on to say, “Jiaolong was not convinced. He took out gold the size of a goat's head and threatened that if Zongben was unwilling to make a fortune and set up a feast for the people, then it could set up a feast for a few days. If its words flow away like this spring, it will give this piece of gold to Zongben.”
As soon as Munemoto heard it, he hurriedly went home and discussed it with the housekeeper. The housekeeper said, “I think we can set up a banquet. Dragon has no food or meat, only tasteless water, and you will definitely beat it.”
Sōben had no idea. Thinking that it would cost so much to set up a feast and let those poor people eat and drink for nothing, it was like someone cutting the flesh on his body with a knife. I felt a sharp pain in my heart. But when he thought about getting gold the size of a goat's head, he couldn't help but take it right away and hold it in his arms. The tea didn't taste good for a few days and nights. After much deliberation, I finally decided to endure the pain and set up a feast for a few days.
Servant Tudeng dressed up in holiday costumes and kindly brought tea and wine to the people. Seeing that everyone was happy to eat, Tudeng asked, “Are there any bleeding from the wolf's mouth? Is today's sun rising from the top of Mount Higashiyama, or is it coming out of the summit of Mount West?” The people laughed out loud, “Your words really worked, but I don't know how you ended up?”
The feast wasn't over yet, and Zong Ben hurriedly asked Tudeng to invite Jiaolong to set up the feast. Like the other day, Tudeng climbed to the big tree by the spring, paddled and danced for half a day, then bowed down and stretched out his tongue, touched his temple and said, “The dragon is sliding...”
He was shocked by the battle and was afraid to go further. Munemoto yelled in a hurry: “What are you doing in a daze? Speak up quickly!” Dudeng pretended to be afraid and whispered, “The dragon said it had already set up a feast with Sōbon. What was eaten and drank wasn't made of water flowing out of the dragon's den? Munemoto took out food and meat, and Dragon took out water. It tells me that if Zongben can set up a feast that doesn't use water, he'll give you a piece of gold the size of a goat's head.”
Munemoto glared and gasped in his mouth, and his face became like a gray monkey.
