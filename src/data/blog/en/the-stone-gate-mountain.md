 Thirty miles northwest of Mingguang, there is a large mountain that winds ten miles from northeast to southwest, called Shimen Mountain. The western slope of the mountain is within the boundary of Fengyang County, and the eastern slope is within the boundary of Mingguang City. Although Shimen Mountain isn't that big or tall, legends say it was infested with immortals in ancient times, so each hill has allusions, and each spring has its own aura. This story has been passed down from generation to generation.
On the southeast slope of the mountain, there are two upright boulders, about five or six meters high, and about two meters apart. They hold a huge crossbow stone on the top, which looks like a gate from a distance. Ordinary people also call it “Shimen Mountain”; this mountain is also called “Shimen Mountain.” Legend has it that this stone gate was often open a long time ago. There was a stream inside the gate, and there were immortals who practiced in the cave. Farmers who worked under the mountain and on the hillside often walked in and out of Shimen from there. The white-haired old man treated people kindly and got along well with the surrounding villagers. One day, a farmer named Xu Sanjiu was picking corn in the hot sun. He had a heat stroke and passed out in the field. He happened to be met by an old white-haired man who had returned from a trip outside and was preparing to enter the cave. Seeing that the white-haired Lao Dao gently brushed the dust in his hands, and a cool breeze blew through, Xu Sanjiu woke up. The white-haired old man had a big smile on his face. He wanted Xu San-jiu to follow him into the cave, but he didn't know what it was like or what was inside? Now that Bai Hai-lao Dao invited him, Xu San-jiu was unkind and followed him into the stone cave. The inside of the cave was cool, and it didn't feel like summer at all. Looking around for a week, Xu Sanjiu saw that the cave was more than three feet high, two feet wide, and seven or eight feet deep. There were stone beds, stone tables, and stone benches in the cave. There are two piles of dazzling snow-white silver bowls on the stone table. Next to the table is a two-story stone pot. A thick stalagmite is stretched out from the top of the cave, and crystal spring drops “tick, tick” drip off the tip of the bamboo shoot. Xu Sanjiu said to himself, “This cave used to be so big and so beautiful.” The white-haired old man pointed to a stone bench and asked Xu Sanjiu to sit down, then picked up a silver bowl and scooped half a bowl of water over, “Please drink spring water.” Xu Sanjiu got up politely and took two sips of “Gu dong, Gu dong” with both hands. He only felt that the spring water was cool and sweet. Xu Sanjiu drank two bowls in one go. The old man with white hair said, “The heat is unbearable. Picking is hard work. I'll give you a jacket to block the scorching sun.” Xu Sanjiu took his hands and saw that it was a small cloak made of reeds. He didn't pay much attention. He put his hands on his head and got up and said goodbye. On his way out, the old man with white hair said, “If there are any difficulties in the future, you can burn incense outside Shimen before sunrise and hit this door nail with a stone when the incense is half burnt. If the stone gate opens on its own, I will come out.” Xu Sanjiu nodded and remembered. After only hearing the phrase “not far away,” Shimen “dang dang” shut down.
Xu San-jiu returned to the village, talked about his adventures when he met people, and kept showing everyone a reed hat. Everyone didn't believe it. His wife also thought that he ran to sleep in the shade of that tree because he was afraid of getting sunburned. Now he's back and made up a false story to deceive people. Once he knocked up his hat, he fell at an angle on the courtyard wall. Xu Sanjiu hurriedly ran over to pick up his hat and rolled his eyes at his wife. The wife glared in a circle. She looked straight at the wall. Xu Sanjiu followed her gaze. Something strange happened. Just in the place where he had just dropped a powerful umbrella, it turned out that a few artemisia trees had dried up and died. Suddenly, the green branches and green leaves shook slightly on the wall with the wind. Xu San-jiu also took the hat in his hand and covered it over the other dried artemisia. One burnt artemisia tree turned blue-green. Xu Sanjiu's wife hurriedly picked up the jacket, carefully wiped the dirt on the hood with the collar, and happily said, “This turned out to be a treasure!”
Xu Sanjiu got a treasure coat. It seemed like it had grown wings and spread all over the village and quickly spread to neighboring villages. Xu Sanjiu told everyone what the white-haired old man said when he was about to leave the house. There are patients in the village who have been ill and have not been able to recover for a long time. They knock incense with incense sticks in front of the sun. Halfway through incense burning, they hit the navel-shaped stone nail on the stone gate with a stone. Of course, Shimen opened wide. Those who asked for a fairy explained their intention. The white haired old man gave him a Xian Dan pill. The patient recovered less than two days after taking it.
The news spread to Xixu Village in the south-east corner of Shimen. There is a big owner in the village called Xu Caimi, so people love to take advantage. When getting along with people, they always have to beat others a little bit. People in the village all call him “Xu Cai Fan.” This fan of Xu Cai and Xu San-jiu are siblings and the elder of Xu San-jiu, 10 years old. When he heard the legend, he rushed to Xu Sanjiu's family to call “big brothers” one by one, and repeatedly inquired about the situation in the cave and how to get into the cave. The next day, after burning incense, Xu Cai knocked on the stone gate. As soon as he entered the stone cave, he stared at the two piles of silver bowls on the stone table. Using his gaze, they counted 50 in total. One was worth a bar of silver. Xu Cai's fan quickly calculated: 50 bars of silver can buy dozens of acres of land; immortals don't worry about eating or drinking; they can change if they don't have to. What's the use of this silver? Why don't you get it in the name of borrowing it and not pay it back. As a result, Xu Cai's fan tried and proposed to borrow those two piles of silver bowls. Lao Dao, who had no trouble at all, agreed to borrow the bowl. Xu Caiman took off his coat and wrapped up the 50 silver bowls and left. Xu Cai's fan replaced silver bowls with silver ingots and began buying land, buying cows, and building houses. Half a year passed in a blink of an eye, and the white-haired Lao Dao didn't see Xu Caifan back. He was very angry and left Shimen Mountain to move to another place. I heard later that they moved to Mount Nakaga.
The old white-haired road has left. Since then, the stone gate has never been opened. Difficult farmers in the surrounding area came to burn incense and hit the stone nail with a stone. Over time, the stone nail was leveled, and the stone nail area was hit into a big recess, and the stone gate never opened. People all complained and blamed Xu Caifan. Xu Cai's fan hasn't had a good life for a few years. Either the cow died of illness, or the pig took it away by the wolf, or the family became seriously ill. After a few years, Xu Caifan's family's possessions have been exhausted, and there is nothing left. Later, Xu Cai realized the reason. Every day he went to Shimenkou to burn incense, and when he met people, he said, “You must return what you borrow from others; you won't be retaliated.”
