 “In 1947, it was the last time I saw Meng Xiaodong as Cheng Ying at a church meeting. Xiao Dong sang the word 'Tiger' with a strong back in his head, and the whole atmosphere immediately became tense and solemn...” This is a memory written by Peking Opera historian Xu Jichuan, a former secretary of Mei Lanfang, in 1986. He praised Meng Xiaodong as a “mature actor,” which is much more valuable than the “famous artist” title that is scattered all over the place now. In this article called “What I Know About Meng Xiaodong,” Mr. Xu has the subtlety of an old school writer, without mentioning Mei Lanfang, or Du Yuesheng — although the 1947 church meeting mentioned above was held for Du Heshou.
Everyone says that writing Mei Lanfang can omit Meng Xiaodong, but writing about Meng cannot fail to mention Mei; similarly, the legend of Du Yuesheng can live without Meng Xiaodong, but Meng Chuanli cannot bypass Du. Indeed, among the two famous men, Xiaodongzong is number one, but he can only take it with shame; he will always be attached.
Stunning woman to join the Prime Minister
Meng Xiaodong is not usually beautiful. Yuan Shikai's son-in-law Xue Guanlan once compared dozens of Kunling, famous for their beauty, to Meng Xiaodong, and concluded that “nothing can match Meng Xiaodong.”
Meng Xiaodong came from the Liyuan family. Both his grandfather and father were old Peking opera students. Family-learned roots. At age 7, I joined a class to perform in Wuxi twice; from age nine, my uncle and grandson (Ju Xian) sent an old student, Qiu Yongxiang, to learn to sing old students; at age 14, I joined a class in Shanghai to sing at the Qiankun Grand Theatre.
Old Peking Opera players are nothing but loyal generals and brave servants. In the past, old students only specialize in “The Emperor's Generals.” From the age of nine, Meng Xiaodong imitated and compared the image of a man among these men: once he raised his hand and put his foot forward, he was demure and calm; every word he sang was bleak and passionate. As an adult, Meng Xiaodong likes to dress up in men's clothing, and in terms of his style of work, he is also manly. He is bossy and uncompromising. Back then, she worshipped Yu Men and indulged in Yu Pai's singing voice every day, but she didn't let her sister, who was also an old Peking opera student, sing at home. She said that she had a bad voice and was afraid it would affect her. She became generous. It can be said that she spent a lot of money. After 1949, she went to Hong Kong and saw that Yu Shuyan's friend Sun Yangnong had a difficult life, and proposed to collaborate with him to write a book recalling Yu Shuyan. She was paid more than 100,000 yuan for the manuscript. She didn't give Sun Yangnong a penny.
If Meng Xiaodong were plain and dressed like a man, he would be in his right place. The problem is that Meng Xiaodong's appearance is not as beautiful. Yuan Shikai's son-in-law and drama critic Xue Guanlan once compared Meng Xiaodong's appearance to ten Kunling from the late Qing dynasty, including Xue Yanqin, Lu Sujuan, and Lu Lanchun. The conclusion was “nothing can match Meng Xiaodong.”
Wang Huang is in the same position as a perfect match
As you can imagine, one is the Emperor of Susu, and the other is King of Danjiao. What a perfect match for the two.
At the age of 18, Meng Xiaodong performed from Shanghai to Tianjin and Beijing. At that time, Meng Xiaodong was already famous in the South. When she appeared on stage in Wuxi at age 12, she had already performed in over 30 shows. Later, she toured Shanghai, Hankou, the Philippines, etc., all of which received rave reviews. However, for actors in Beijing opera, famous actors from the South seem to always have a wild path if they are not recognized by northern audiences, no matter how famous they are. At the time, Peking opera performers had a saying: “I'd rather spend tens of thousands of dollars a day in the North; I wouldn't want to spend thousands of yuan per month in Shanghai. Three hundred people from Shanghai said yes, it's definitely not as good as those who know the North.”
In April 1925, Meng Xiaodong took the stage in Beijing. His debut play was “Shiro Visits His Mother”, which was an instant hit. “Yan Jing Sanren,” which wrote the drama review back then, had a detailed description and review of Meng Zang: “Meng Xiaodong was born with a good voice. The rarest thing is that it doesn't have a female voice. This is rare among tens of millions of people. In the maiden world, I don't dare to say that there are no people after; at least, there are no ancient people.”
In August of the same year, Meng Xiaodong starred in “Up to the Sky” and met Mei Lanfang, who appeared in “Overlord Farewell” on the same day. “Liu Xiu”, dressed in a dragon robe, saw “Yu Ji” and called it “Grandpa Mei” — this was the first time the “hero” and “beauty” met.
At a later meeting, some people arranged for Mei and Meng to perform together in “Shiro's Mother,” one playing the gentle and clear Princess Fanbang, and the other as Yang Jiajang, an exiled fanbang. As you can imagine, one was the Emperor of Susu, and the other was King of Danjiao. What kind of union were the two. By the time they co-starred in “Journey to the Dragon and the Phoenix,” not only did the two parties actually do some fake movies, but even the Mei Dang people were eager to try it out and plan a realistic marriage for this “Fake Phoenix” pair — even though Mei Lanfang already had two wives, and one of them, Mrs. Fu Zhifang, was also born in Peking opera, and is known as “Amanohashi Mei Lanfang.” However, people in the Mei Party have their own methods: Mei Lanfang passed on to her uncle's family when she was a child. Theoretically, it is possible to have a two-bedroom daughter-in-law. As for Grandma Wang Minghua, she is already seriously ill with lung disease. On August 28, 1926, a reporter wrote an article on “Beiyang Pictorial” saying, “Mei Zhifan's wife, Wang Minghua, has never liked Fu Zhifang, so she definitely made her husband make an appointment for Meng Xiaodong as her stepfamily.” This could be a footnote on harem politics.
The fake Phoenix and the fake Phoenix are real
After getting along with Meng Xiaodong, Mei Lanfang was in a happy mood; even, she was a bit excited.
After Mei and Meng were combined, Mei's stage performances were as numerous as social events, yet Meng Xiaodong was “hidden.” The “perfect match” that people had anticipated did not appear on stage. After all, Meng Xiaodong is married to Mei Lanfang, the king of the Ling world. Does Mei's wife want to make money by singing?
What did Meng Xiaodong look like at the right time? Qi Rushan's son Qi Xiang once recalled in her old age, “I usually don't think she was overly dressed. Her clothes were normal, the colors were elegant, she was slender, and she had a solemn attitude. Sometimes she looks down at paintings and calligraphy, and others greet her. As soon as she looks up, her eyes shine brightly. Sixty years have passed, and her natural beauty and radiance are just now.” As can be seen, Meng Xiaodong is no different from an ordinary bride: beautiful, dignified, and gentle, and Mei Lanfang, who is over ten years older, should also be very caring and caring for Meng. There is an interesting photo of Zhen. Mei Lanfang in casual clothes is very lively. She is projecting her hands on the wall to make animal shapes. Meng asked on the right, “What are you doing there?” Mei answered on the left, “I'm acting as a goose shadow here.” Mei Lanfang has always been elegant; she is really rare in such a lively manner. As can be seen, after being combined with Meng Xiaodong, Mei Lanfang was in a happy mood, even a bit excited.
Although Meng Xiaodong sometimes hangs her voice at home and sings the passage “The Promiser Plays for the Han Dynasty, Chu and Han Fight for the Sword”, if it weren't for those events in the future, she might slowly downplay the “imperial general” in her body and live a long life as a gentle wife.
Peacocks fly southeast
Mei Lanfang has the cowardice and selfishness that all men share when dealing with emotional problems. Only now did Meng Xiaodong know that what “two big heads” is nothing but a mirror flower, water, and moon.
Mei and Meng got married soon after the Spring Festival in 1927. This happened in September of that year: an admirer of Meng was furious and wanted to tie Mei up; as a result, he mistakenly hurt others. The young man was also shot dead by the military and police, and he showed up for the first time.
Although the Plum Tying Incident was alarming and dangerous, rumors spread all over the place in the tabloids for a while. Some said that Meng Xiaodong was originally the young man's fiancee, and that a certain celebrity had taken people's love. You need to know that Mei Lanfang's name is entwined with the murder scandal. This is a huge obstacle and danger to his development. Not only did the large number of artists gathered around Mei Lanfang not get involved, but even Mei Lanfang himself was dissatisfied. Meng Xiaodong went to Tianjin to sing in concert with Kun Ling's famous dan Xue Yanqin. This “comeback” has taken away the popularity of the old days, so it's actually not popular. “Tianjin Commercial Daily” not only featured the “Meng Hua” column, but also called Meng Xiaodong the “Winter Emperor” in the poem, meaning the emperor of the living, and shouted “Long Live My Emperor”! During the performance, Meng Xiaodong wore men's clothes to socialize and accept people's worship of the “Winter Emperor.” He was so energetic that he couldn't see the slightest bit of the angry concubine.
By 1929, Mei Lanfang will perform in the US, causing another trouble: who actually followed Mei Lanfang to visit the US and appear as Mrs. Mei in front of the world? Qi Xiang, the son of Qi Rushan, recalled, “Among the gifts to perform in the US, there were also small handicrafts such as ink cartridges and inkstones. The cartridges all had images engraved on them. What impressed me the most was the costume statue of Meng Xiaodong. She was originally an old actor, but this picture is of a woman in costume, which is very beautiful”. Is this also a sign that Mei intends to take Meng Xiaodong out?
However, in order to be able to visit with Mei, Mrs. Fu decided to ask a Japanese doctor to have an abortion. Things got to this point, and it was just bloody. In the end, Mei Lanfang had no choice but to not bring either of them.
Then came the filial piety scandal. On August 5, 1930, as soon as Mei Lanfang returned from a visit to the US, she received news of the death of her grandmother (great-aunt) as soon as she arrived in Tianjin. In the old days, the mourning period was a time when various family disputes concentrated and erupted. According to the rules, as Mei Lanfang's wife, Dai Hsiao should receive the condolences of the four guests in the Xiaotang. When Meng Xiaodong arrived at Mei's house, he was stopped outside the door by someone called “Miss Meng.” It just so happened that Mrs. Fu was pregnant again. She said that if Meng wanted to enter the door, she would risk two lives. Grandma Fukuji, who sings in Tsing Yi, naturally knows how to subdue men better than Little Winter who sings Sunae. Mei Lanfang, on the other hand, has the cowardice and selfishness that all men share when dealing with emotional problems. Only now did Meng Xiaodong know that what “double cheeks” and “two big heads” are all mirrored.
According to Yu Shuyan's daughter, Yu Huiqing, recalled, “As far as I know, the Bangmei Group was also divided into the Bangfu faction and the Bang Meng faction because of the relationship between the two concubines. When Mei's original wife, Wang Shi, was alive, Meng Xiaodong was a good match with her; after Mrs. Wang passed away, the former had the upper hand in the contest between the Fangfu faction and the Beng Meng faction. Meng Xiaodong was unwilling to continue being a concubine, so she got divorced and ran away.”
Meng Xiaodong once told people in her later years in Hong Kong that because Mei Lanfang couldn't agree to a fight in the end, she slipped her feet. Looking back many years later still sounds a bit light-hearted and witty, but the situation back then was not so understated. After breaking up with Mei Lanfang in 1930, Meng Xiaodong went on hunger strike, fell ill, took refuge in Jingu, and even converted to Buddhism for a while. By the time Mei Lanfang returned to Kyoto a few years later, Meng Xiaodong already regarded Mei Lang as a stranger and had never spoken in his whole life.
Worship Yu Men
She couldn't help but miss the loud and loud sounds of gongs and drums, as well as the years of studying drama and running on the pier. Even those of us outsiders a few years later can't help but feel sorry for her blindness and unworthiness in the past: she should not have succumbed to being a concubine; she should not have left the stage lightly.
Fortunately, there is also Peking Opera. Meng Xiaodong himself said, “I've been singing in the arts since elementary school, but it wasn't until I arrived in the north that I really understood the joy of singing and became addicted to drama.” The truth is that as soon as Meng Xiaodong arrived in Beijing, he was fascinated by Yu Shuyan's art.
However, praying for Yu is not that easy. Yu Shuyan's own art is hard to come by; of course, she doesn't want to pass it on easily to others. Yu Shuyan once said to people, “When I was studying drama with my teacher (Tan Xinpei), the teacher was happy to smoke in bed, so I sat up and talked about something. As for how to understand and learn after speaking, that is my own business. I'm a teacher's apprentice, but when I go to the theater to watch the teacher's shows, I spend my own money to buy tickets...”
Due to his obsession with Yu Pai, Meng Xiaodong once asked someone to worship Yu Shuyan. Unfortunately, Yu's wife, Chen Shuming, did not agree, so she had to quit. In 1934, Mrs. Chen passed away. Meng Xiaodong once again proposed to pray for Yu, taking into account Mei Lanfang's relationship — Mei Lanfang called Yu's “third brother,” so she promised to go to her friend's house to talk about drama for Meng. At this point, Meng Xiaodong was the only woman left in Yu Men. She not only had good artistic talents and skills, but also had no choice but to immerse herself in the art of the rest of the world. Yu Nu Yu Huiqing recalled, “Meng Xiaodong's memory was slightly poor. When her father taught her, we copied drama lyrics for her.” Meanwhile, Meng Xiaodong treated Yu Shuyan's two daughters as well. Later, when they got married, Meng Xiaodong gave them a full set of furniture.
The five years Meng Xiaodong studied drama with Yu Shuyan were the last five years of Yu Shuyan's life. Yu Shuyan did her best to teach on her sick bed. She was often sweaty to exemplify her posture. Meng Xiaodong also didn't untie her clothes and took the side of the waitress. Naturally, this aroused Mrs. Yao's jealousy. After Yu died, she burnt everything that the Liyuan community regarded as precious, such as secret ancestral screenplays written by Yu Shuyan, and the Gongzhuku score book. Some people say the burning was to take revenge on Meng Xiaodong.
Du Yuesheng gave her a name
Zhang Boju once wrote a poem for Meng Xiaodong: “Liyuan should be a wise woman. Yu Pai's vocal voice can also be passed down. They all dream of hell and paradise, and celebrate the good year in a smoky cave.” It seems that Zhang Boju is very sorry that Meng Xiaodong married Du Yuesheng.
When Meng Xiaodong reached middle age with stiff cheekbones, it can be seen from his face alone that he has gone through countless hurdles. Yu Huiqing said, “After Meng Xiaodong's divorce from Mei Lanfang, she told our sisters that she would never get married in the future, and that if she didn't get married, she would marry someone who stomped her feet (that is, has power and power).” Du Yuesheng started as an apprentice at a fruit stand and became a famous person at sea. In Shanghai dialect, he really “turned a dragon into a dragon.” On the day Meng Xiaodong met Du Yuesheng, Du naturally already “stomped and trembled.”
At the right time, Meng Xiaodong took the stage in Shanghai. Jin Rong said to Du Yuesheng, “This actress, I think she is beautiful, has a chic demeanor, and is also a rare girl. Why don't you try to get her in. In the future, you can open a theater. She can not only be your wife, but you can also earn money by singing and take care of your affairs. You have to get started early on this kind of beautiful thing.” After listening to this suggestion, Du Yuesheng invited Jin Rong's wife Lin Guisheng to come forward and persuade Meng Xiaodong in every way. Xiaodong, who had gone through many disappointments with Mei Lanfang, was already somewhat disheartened. On the one hand, she felt that Du Yuesheng's love for herself over the years, and Yao Yulan, the fourth wife of Du Yuesheng and Meng Xiaodong's Jin Lan sister, had matched over and over again, so she thought that would be it.
Du Yuesheng, of course, is not a good person, but he is a person you can rely on. Not to mention that for Xiaodong, in addition to Mu Se, he appreciates his talent even more. In her later life, she gradually learned another side of Du Yuesheng as the underworld boss: he was not a bad person; even to a certain extent, he was Meng Xiaodong's only confidant. What more could a woman who sang in the olden days ask for?
Unfortunately, at the time, Du Yuesheng was already over a year old. Since entering Dumen, Meng Xiaodong naturally took on the burden of serving Du Yuesheng. In a large family, the two wives live under the same roof. Even though Yao Yulan and Meng Xiaodong love the same sisters, they sometimes bite their teeth and tongue. Du Mansion also didn't hear a burst of laughter all day long because the male owner was seriously ill. These bleak and bleak days were really cruel to an outstanding woman like Meng Xiaodong.
Ashamed of Meng Xiaodong's many gifts, and there are too few places where she can do her best. In her daily life, Du Yuesheng always respects and loves Meng Xiaodong, endures her own pain, talks softly and carefully, usually calls her like her own children, and warmly shouts “Mommy”: “What does' Mommy 'want to buy and eat?” As soon as Meng Xiaodong made a brief revelation, he kept busy ordering people to do it quickly. Back then, there were people in Hong Kong who witnessed the lives of the two of them, saying, “If the Shanghai dialect were to be translated into Mandarin, I'm afraid “affectionate” wouldn't be enough.
Since the rise of the military in the War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression, Du Yuesheng left his hometown of Shanghai until he returned to Shanghai after victory in the War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression. After a short period of singing and dancing, the civil war immediately flared up. However, the relationship between Meng Xiaodong and Du Yuesheng was caught in the midst of the torrents of the times. It was in a hurry, and there were no rules. Until he left the mainland and went to Hong Kong with the Du family in 1949, Meng Xiaodong had no name. When Du Yuesheng wanted to move to Europe and buy passports for people close to him in 1950, did Meng Xiaodong say softly, “I followed. Is this considered a girl or a girlfriend?” Du Yuesheng was stunned. Only then did he have a 63-year-old groom and a 42-year-old bride. At this point, one of the key words in Meng Xiaodong's story, was finally settled.
At the beginning of this article, Du Yuesheng's concert in Daqing in 1947 is said to have been an unprecedented event. Later, the five-day drama was repeated again because the drama was really wonderful. On the day of the official performance, the front door of the China Grand Theatre was crowded and full of traffic. It was really hard to find tickets, so much so that Ma Lianliang wanted to watch the movie that night, but had to add a chair in the aisle. The flower baskets given away by people from all walks of life were a mile long, and the total number of flower baskets discounted was as much as 1.2 billion dollars (old currency). However, those who were lucky enough to watch it live and listen to it on the radio had nothing to say, apart from the “This song should only be found in the sky”. Meng Xiaodong's two “Searching for Orphans and Saving Orphans” were also known as “The Incredible Sounds of Guangling.”
Afterwards, according to Mei Lanfang's manager Yao Yufu, Meng Xiaodong performed two shows of “Searching for Orphans and Saving Orphans”, and Mr. Mei listened to two radio broadcasts at home...
