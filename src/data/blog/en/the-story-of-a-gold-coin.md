 Once upon a time, there was an old man. He had a son who spent a lot of money and was very lazy.
The old man worked hard all his life, saving money and raising his son as an adult. When I got old, I saw my son still like this, and I couldn't stand it anymore. Lying in bed, he called up his wife and said, “We can give our assets to whoever we want, just don't give them to our son. This lazy person doesn't do any work, and he can't earn any money. ”
The mother felt very uncomfortable when she heard it and defended her son: “How could he do anything? ”
The old man resolutely said, “OK, if he can do it, let him make money! Even if I earned a single coin, I gave him all my assets. ”
“OK. “My wife said.
She went to her son, gave him a gold coin, and taught him, “If you go shopping outside, go wherever you want. Come back in the evening and give this coin to your dad, that is to say it's money you earned. ”
My son did just that. He came back in the evening and taught his dad about coins. Dad took the coins and threw them in the stove. “This isn't money you earned,” he said.
The son laughed and walked away.
Mom gave her son another gold coin and said, “Tomorrow you'll go shopping in the mountains. By the evening, you'll have to run for two Russian miles, get sweaty, and then go to your dad and say to him, “It's not easy to earn this coin! ”
My son did just that. By the evening, he was exhausted. Sweaty, I ran to my dad and said, “Dad, look, I'm soaked all over my body! Earning this coin isn't easy! ”
Dad took the coin and threw it in the stove.
“Don't lie to me, kid. “Dad said, “You didn't earn it. “My son laughed again and walked away. Mom knew things weren't working, and she said, “No, kid, you can't lie to your dad anymore. You have to earn your own money and find a job. Even if you earn two cents a day, hand over the money you earn to your dad, he will believe it. ”
My son listened to his mom and left. He actually worked for a whole week. He helped this harvest the crops and built the house, earned enough coins, and brought them back to his dad. The old man took the coin and still threw it in the stove.
“No, kid, you didn't earn it either! ”
My son couldn't stand it. He rushed to the front of the furnace, grabbed the coins out of the blazing fire with his hands, and shouted, “Dad, you're crazy! I worked as a cow and horse for others, and worked hard for a whole week before I earned this coin! And you just throw it in the stove and burn it! ”
At this point, the old man said, “Now, I believe this is money you earned yourself! ”
