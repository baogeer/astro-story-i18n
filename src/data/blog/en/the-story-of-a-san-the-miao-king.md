 Once upon a time, there was an old Miao worker who had endured all his life. At the end of his life, when he couldn't do much for the rich owner, the rich man just gave him a small amount of work and sent him home.
When the old worker got home, he shouted his three sons in front of him and said, “Do the three of you guys still have to go to the rich owner's house to commit crimes like me? I've been working all my life, and I haven't saved much money. Now I'm sharing it with the three of you. You can get ready, go out and learn all your skills, and be happy to mix a bowl of rice and eat in the future.”
After two days of preparation, the three sons each went out with simple luggage. When they left, the old boss ordered, “You three must arrive at home on the 30th night of your third year to celebrate the New Year.” They left tearfully remembering the old man's words.
The three brothers traveled together for two days. During the two-day walk, we walked and talked about what sample things we each wanted to learn. The boss said, “I want to learn some blacksmith skills. In the future, in addition to repairing my own agricultural tools, I can also help the townspeople get busy.” The second brother said, “Big brother got it right. I want to learn carpentry skills so I can build a good house in the future.” Ah San was the only one who didn't get angry. After half a day, the boss asked, “Ah San, don't you want to learn either?” Ah San slowly answered, “I want to learn the art of killing people.” Old Dalian hurriedly said, “Ah three, you're crazy. If you don't learn any other craft, you learn the craft of killing people.” Ah San explained to the boss, “I learned the art of killing people specifically to kill rich owners. You think Grandma died at the rich owner's house, and Grandpa suffered so much that he couldn't do his best before being driven home.” The boss made the elder cry after saying a few words, but he still advised Ah San. What you said made sense, but can you learn this craft? Ah San affirmed, “Yes.” On the third day, the three brothers each left.
On the evening of the 30th of the third year, the boss picked up blacksmith tools and happily told the old man, “Dad, I went out for the past three years to learn the blacksmith craft. I graduated in two years. I worked for another year, and I saved 52 bucks to give to my dad.” The old man had just picked up the money, and the old man came home with carpenter's tools. “I studied carpentry. I studied carpentry for 2 years, worked for another year, and saved 52 pieces of silver to give to my dad.” The old man took the money and looked out of the door sadly. After half a day, he still didn't see Ah San back. The old man anxiously asked the boss, “Do you know which path Ah San followed and what kind of craft he learned? Why haven't you come back yet.” The boss and the old man didn't dare to tell the old man that Ah San wanted to learn the art of murder; they only promised they didn't know.
At dusk, everyone thought Ah San wouldn't come back today, so they locked the door and got ready to eat. The old man poured three bowls of wine. The father and son each picked up a bowl of wine and said to the boss, “Your ancestors blessed your return, but it's still worse than Ah San, but Ah San is smarter than both of you. He won't suffer any losses; he will definitely come back.” As soon as the old man's voice came to an end, Ah, the three harrier turned over and fell in the yard and immediately picked up the old man's words: “Dad, I'm back.” The father and son immediately got up to greet Ah San and invited Ah San to take a seat. The old man happily poured a bowl of wine and handed it to him. Ah San took the wine and was very embarrassed to say to the old man, “Dad, the craft I learned was not the same as my older brother's second brother, but the craft I learned was enough to deal with the rich.” The old man had a long time in his mind when he saw a harrier roll over and fall in the yard. Having said just one sentence, I'm afraid of losing money in the future. Ah San said, “Please don't worry, Dad.”
On the second day he got home, it was the first day of the new year. Ah San was preparing to clean up the biggest local treasurer that night. This rich owner has ten horses and ten big mules. They have a total of 20 pounds of silver. Other assets are innumerable. They must find a way to get the rich owner to give the money out in a friendly manner.
That night, Ah San first broke into the county official's residence. The county official was sleeping. A three pointed at the county official and lifted it up. The county official stood up like a wooden man. When I woke up, I wanted to shout for help. Ah San drew a circle on his face with his finger, and the county official couldn't say anything. A San said to the county official, “If you wear casual clothes and take a trip with me to Dacai's house, I can spare your life.” Something went wrong with the county officials; they had to follow Ah San obediently.
At 3 o'clock in the middle of the night, all the rich owners were asleep. Ah, 31, turned over and leaped into the rich owner's house and opened the door to let the county officials go in. Ah San took out the dishes from the kitchen of the financial owner's house and ate and drank a bowl of wine. Ah San deliberately hit the wine bowl on the ground and woke up the rich man. The old man of the fortune shouted, “There are thieves, there are thieves.” Ah San jumped on the roof. The five sons of the rich man climbed up and saw someone, so they hung them up and beat them. After a few beatings, the long-time workers outside called the stable on fire; the stable was on fire. The five sons rushed out to fight the fire. When they stormed out, Ah San jumped from the roof again, put down the county official, and hanged the rich man. The five sons came back after saving the fire. The person in charge shouted, “Hurry up and put your dad down.” When the five sons heard it, they were anxious and angrily said, “You are a thief, and you still dare to call yourself a father.” In the midst of the hustle and bustle, another round of chaos led to the death of the rich man's dog. The little grandson who had just beaten someone to death and slept with the rich man woke up and touched that his dad wasn't there. They shouted bitterly and started crying. The five sons heard that the situation was not good. I ran into the house to see that there were no rich owners. When I came out and took a close look, the person who was beaten to death was my own father.
The next day, the five sons of the rich man thought the family was ugly and wanted to hastily settle the death of the rich man. Just about to be mourned, Ah Sanhe County officials arrived and asked, “How did the rich man die?” The five sons answered in unison: “They died of illness.” The county official said, “It's impossible. I've never heard of anyone getting sick. Open a coffin for examination.” The five sons were helpless. Only the opening of the coffin. As soon as the inspection was carried out, the financial owner's foot was broken and his lower back relieved. My stomach opened up and my head broke. The county official judged, “Your five sons were personally killed; you must kill your five heads to pay for your lives.” The five sons immediately knelt down in front of Ah Sanhe County officials to ask for forgiveness and no judgment. The county official said, “OK, remember the relationship between your family and me in the past. I will spare your life. You are limited to using ten horses, ten mules, twenty loads of silver to Azan within three days. Even Ma Lian will hand over the silver to Ah San, without delay.”
On the third day, twenty piles of silver were delivered to Azanzhai. Ah San gave all of these assets to the Hmong brothers in neighboring Zhaizi. Since then, he has also punished many wealthy people, big and small. The Miao brothers have great respect for him and affectionately called Ah San the king of Miao.
