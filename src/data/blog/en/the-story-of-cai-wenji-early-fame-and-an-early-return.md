 Author: Hou Hongbin
Cai Yan, Zi Wenji, from Chen Liuyi (Qi County, Henan). Cai Yong's daughter, a famous writer, musician, and historian in the late Han period. History books say that she is “erudite, capable of discerning, and good at musical rhythms.” Tsai Wen-ji married Wei Zhongdao when she was 16. Her husband died. She was taken prisoner to Southern Hungary during the war, married Zuo Xianwang, redeemed 12 years later by Cao Cao, and married to Lieutenant Tian Dong. Her fate was difficult. Guo Moruo has a historical drama about Wenji's return to China, including Wenji singing “The Eighteen Beats of Hu”. According to general research, Wenji only left a five-word “Poem of Sadness and Anger,” which was passed down from generation to generation.
The Cai family is a famous local family. Wenji's father is Cai Yi, a famous Eastern Han scholar, and her mother, Zhao Wuniang, also knows Shu Dali. Unfortunately, when Cai Yong was 45, Zhao Wuniang only gave birth to Wen Ji. As an only child, Tsai Wenji is loved.
In fact, as a child prodigy, Choi Man-ji wrote enough books of “Chen Liu Girl Tsai Wenji”. Her parents should have carried her around a long time ago to give lectures, talk about how to train their daughter, and start a “Success Studies” course. At age 10, Bunji showed her musical talent. Cai Yong was playing the piano outside. Inside, Bunji heard her father's broken string and immediately said that the second string was broken. Cai Yong was very surprised. She also deliberately broke the fourth string, and Moon Hee was able to tell right away. Cai Yong began teaching her daughter to learn piano. Two years later, Wenji became a piano artist, and won her father's most cherished Jiao Tieqin. At the age of 12, Wenji's calligraphy was already handed down by Cai Yongzhen. Legend has it that Cai Yong's characters were taught by gods and people, passed down to Wen Ji, then from Wenji to Zhong Yi, then to Mrs. Wei, and Mrs. Wei to Wang Xizhi... At age 14, Wenji's literary talent was already shining brightly. Everyone knew that there was Moon Hee and Cai Yi. There was no need to publish a book or hype; Tsai Man-ki became famous thanks to her word-of-mouth reputation.
At the age of 16, Moon Hee married Wei Zhongdao of the Hadong family. Wei Zhongdao was also an excellent college student. The couple were very affectionate. Unfortunately, less than a year later, Wei Zhongdao died due to hemoptysis. Tsai Wen-ji never gave birth to one and a half daughters, so proud, she had no choice but to return to her mother's home. Her father later died in prison, and Bunji was kidnapped by the Huns. At the age of 23, Moon-hee was accepted as queen by King Zuo Hyun. She lived in the Southern Huns for 12 years and had two children.
Wenji, a genius, is unlucky enough. But things aren't over yet. I have just fallen in love with Zuo Xianwang. I have just lived a stable life. With her beloved child, Cao Cao, who has gained strength, it's not too late or too early. I think of her. As a writer, Cao Cao respects talented women, and hopes to use this to win people's hearts and create the image of a cultural power. As a result, they spent enough money and used “one pair of white balls, one thousand two thousand and two gold” in exchange for Moon Hee. It is estimated that if they fail to claim it, they will have to send troops to rob them. The Huns had no choice but to return Wenji. There was no way for Moon Hee. Not only did she miss her country and desire to complete her father's unfinished career, but she was also unable to give up her own life and her husband.
Alas, it's really hard to be a turtle. Back or not? Tsai Wenji wrote “Poems of Sadness and Anger” and “Eighteen Shots of Hu” in the midst of her intellectual and emotional struggles. They each became one of China's most outstanding poems and one of the most outstanding pop songs.
Cai Wenji returned to her hometown of Chenliu County on a special train, but the wall was broken and there was no place to live. Fortunately, as a distinguished scholar brought in from overseas with outstanding contributions, the government not only solved housing issues and household registration issues, but also solved spousal issues — honestly, she found a husband. Under Cao Cao's arrangement, Wenji married Captain Tian Dong Si. This year, she was 35 years old, and Dong Sizheng was a very young man. At her peak, she was gifted with talent. She knows the history of books, knows sound rules, and has a great sense of self-respect. At first, she wasn't very satisfied with this marriage, but in the second year after the marriage, Dong Si committed a crime. She couldn't help but take a break. Peng Shou leapt to Cao Cao's prime minister's house and pleaded with Cao in front of many guests. Cao Cao immediately sent someone to step up the task, recover the letter, and pardon his crime.
Geniuses like this are out of the world. For example, Cao Cao is very envious of the original collection of books in Tsai Wen-ji's family. Tsai Wen-ji said, “The original collection of 4,000 books has been lost after several wars and turmoil, but I can still recall 400 books.” Cao Cao was overjoyed and immediately said, “If that's the case, I'll send ten people to copy what you have written down.” Choi Man-ki said no need. Soon, she wrote 400 articles from her memory, all without leaving a trace. Only this kind of person can sit quietly and finish “Continuing the Han Dynasty”, just like that old lady Ban Chao.
Look, you need to be famous as soon as possible, and you have to return home early or late, and you don't even have a place. Where can Cao Cao have so many talented young people to be your husband?
