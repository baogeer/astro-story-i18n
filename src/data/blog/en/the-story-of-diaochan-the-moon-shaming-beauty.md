 “Closed Moon” is a synonym for Diao Chan.
She is a singer and prostitute of Wang Yun, the minister of Emperor Han Xian of the Three Kingdoms. She can sing and dance well, and is loved by Wang Yun.
At the time, Dong Zhuo's dictatorship took hold of Tianzi to make the princes and ministers angry and dare to speak out.
Wang Yun is bored and unhappy every day. She doesn't drink tea, and doesn't eat. Diao Chan is very worried about her owner.
On a moonlit night, he knelt down in the back garden and prayed for the owner, “Moon ah, moon, even though you are as clean as clean, how can you know the troubles in our old man's heart! Oh heaven, sky, even though you are so deep, it's hard to let our old man feel like he's burning like fire. I am the old man's servant. I want to serve the country and the people, and die forever.”
By chance, Wang Yun also came to the garden to relax. Immediately, he was emotional and hurriedly walked up to lift Diao Chan up.
Wang Yun said, “Can you share my worries. Do you know where my worries lie?”
“I know, adults.” “Then would you like to help me get a national thief and kill Dong Zhuo?”
“As long as adults can trust slaves, slaves will turn their brains out.”
Wang Yun listened, put his hands together, and immediately said goodbye to Madam Minao.
Since then, they have been compatible with Diao Chan as father and daughter.
More than a year later, Wang Yun first gave her daughter to Dong Zhuo, then to Lu Bu.
Dong and Lu were jealous, and a fire broke out, and Dong Zhuo was killed.
This is Wang Yuncao's serial plan, one woman and two hires to kill national thieves.
So how is Diao Chan called a “closed moon”?
While Diao Chan was praying for the moon in the back garden, a light breeze suddenly blew, and a cloud covered the clear bright moon. That's when Wang Yun saw it. To promote how beautiful his daughter is, Wang Yun said when she met someone, “My daughter is more beautiful than the moon.” She quickly hid behind the clouds. As a result, Diao Chan was also called a “closed moon.”
