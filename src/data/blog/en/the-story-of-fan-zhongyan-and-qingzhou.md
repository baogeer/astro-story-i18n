 When you think of a city, many people first think of the historical celebrities associated with it.
Qingzhou is located in the heart of the land of Qilu. It has a long history. It is one of the famous ancient Chinese cities of Kyushu and a national-level historical and cultural city. During the Northern Song dynasty, Qingzhou developed into a famous Oriental metropolis near and far, as the capital of Jingdong East Road and the administrative center of peace. The imperial court attached great importance to knowing the candidates from Qingzhou. The so-called “Haidai district has great expectations; Yue Mu is in charge, and the state is very well elected.” As a result, a group of historical figures associated with Qingzhou emerged — Kou Zhun, Cao Bin, Wang Zeng, Xia Wei, Fu Bi, Fan Zhongquan, Wen Yanbo, Pang Ji, Ouyang Xiu, Zhao Wei, Zeng Bu, Liu Zhi, and Huang Shang. In the 3rd year of Huangyou (1051), the arrival of Fan Zhongyuan brightened the ancient state the most.
There is no need to say much about Fan Zhongyuan who spent his whole life supporting the country, defending Western Xia, and implementing the new law to celebrate the coup. There is no need to say much about building excellence. The phrase “Born to worry and worry, enjoy the world after tomorrow” is his permanent monument. Fan Zhongyuan is in charge of Qingzhou. Five years after writing “Yueyang House Diary,” he is 63 years old. This is the final stop for his career. It was only a year before and after in Qingzhou, but it mostly benefited the government. First, in the face of the Qingzhou disaster, they asked the imperial court to save enough military food for a year, and provide all the rest for disaster relief. Second, the people of Qingzhou needed to pay for their land in Bozhou (Liaocheng). Fan Zhongyuan discounted the field and sent government officials to Bozhou to buy food on their behalf. Not only did they save the people of Qingzhou from the labor of long-distance transportation, but they also paid back the remaining money. “The young people are in trouble because they stand like a temple.” Third, there is a legend that red eye disease was prevalent in Qingzhou at the time. Fan Zhongyuan used water from Liquan to make herbal medicine to contain the spread of the epidemic. Fan Zhongyuan showed the image of a parent and official who did their best and lived up to his life.
During the Ming and Zhengde years, the book collector and Zuo Yudu Yu Shi Shao Baofu's poem “Giving Your Servant Wang Dehua to the East” was a strong reminder to his best friend, Tobe Shilang Wang Qiong, who is about to leave Beijing for disaster relief: “If you go to Ma Yuqin in Shandong, the people should know the heart of the messenger. Wild people are like beasts, and Tudou vegetables are as rich as gold. Tiankai Dai Yue added a new view, and in spring, Kom Tong regained its old shadow. If Qingzhou had to hang ancient times, the rock springs under Fangong Temple were deep.” In Shao Bao's mind, on the journey of thousands of miles from Beijing to Shandong, only the Qingzhou Fangong Temple has the most weight and is the most important place of interest and monument to be honored.
The outstanding thinker Gu Yanwu visited Qilu and visited Qingzhou, not forgetting to visit the famous Fangong Temple. This patriot lamented the tragedy of Daming's death and wrote the song “Fan Wenzheng Gong Temple”: “Former dynasties were also sad about Yuan Hao. Who are the courtiers like Fan Gong? It has already been seen that Gan Ge is in the sea, and it still remains in Jiangdong. In the abandoned field with a frosty evening spike, in the ancient temple of Noishi Hanbori. I want to do something big with public funding; until now, my worries are endless.” Gu Yanwu couldn't help but feel that there were no outstanding wise men like Fan Gong in the Ming dynasty who resisted foreign aggression, defended the community, and expressed a desire to see the wise in common. “The rise and fall of the world, everyone is responsible”, which originated from Gu's ideology, shines through the patriotic feelings that have shaken the ages, and is enough to strengthen the soul of the Chinese nation, just like Fan Zhongyuan's “Born to Worry and Worry, and the Future Will Be Happy”.
Six to seven hundred years after the death of Gong Fan, Emperor Yongzheng, who worked as far back as Beijing, highly praised the historical achievements of the Qingzhou province: “For example, Fan Zhongyuan ruled Qingzhou, Guangshi Huizheng, and Xize rushing to Lequan. These are all recorded in the books. Those who believe and seek are not our praises.”
After the “9/18” incident, the Chinese nation faced a crisis of extinction. In the spring of 1934, Feng Yuxiang, a servant of dust, came to Fan Gongting in Qingzhou. He wrote a couplet with a handwritten couplet and engraved a stone to commemorate: “In the bosom, he taught him to ride and fly, and he was also afraid of Mr. Fan Xiaolaozi; he is worried and concerned about the world. I hope today's people will be excited and learn from Mr. Xiucai.”
Since the Northern Song Dynasty, Fan Zhongyuan's charisma has inspired Qingzhou for a long time, inspiring generations of Qingzhou people to keep the world in mind and continue to improve themselves. In the more than 100 years from Zhengde to Chongzhen, there were outstanding figures in Qingzhou. Among the officials, there were as many as ten senior court ministers, Chen Jing, Shi Maohua, Wang Ji, Xing Yi, Feng Qi, Zhong Yuzheng, Zhao Bingzhong, Dong Kewei, and Cao Yi. They flourished for a moment and became a new spectacle in youth history.
The famous Fan Gong Ting Park in Qingzhou started when Fan Zhongyuan learned about Qingzhou. The park's buildings mainly include Fangong Ting, Fan Gong Jing, and Fan Gong Temple. Among them, the Jingting is also known as the “Fan Gong Jing Ting”. The Fangong Temple was originally a temple built by the people of Qingzhou to appreciate Fan Zhongyuan and empathize with the people. The three have been scrapped many times in history. Today, the main building is in the style of the late Qing dynasty. The park is full of Chinese cabbage and pine acacia. The pavilions are patchwork, and the scenery is pleasant. In the book “Talks of Mianshui Yan” written by Wang Bizhi of the Northern Song Dynasty, it is recorded that Fan Zhongyuan presided over the construction process of Fan Gongting and Fan Gongjing: “In Huangyou, Fan Wenzhenggong Zhenqing, and the Longxing Monk House, there is a 'Li Quan' gushing out of the South Yangxi River. It is engraved on the stone. Later, young people thought of Gong's virtue, called “Fan Gongquan” (later called “Fan Gongjing”). The surrounding springs are covered with ancient trees, and there is no trace of dust. It only takes a few hundred steps to get to the city, which is like being deep in the mountains. People often sing the piano and make tea on top of that. The sunshine is beautiful, the birds are rare, and the trip outside of the real world seems like it's not on earth.”
Although Fan Gongting Park is located in the Nanyang River Valley depression in Qingzhou, it is a spiritual highland. A thousand years after Fan Gong left, the people of Qingzhou are still deeply attached to him. Fan Zhongquan, a great man from a generation, immortalized with Fan Gong Ting, Fan Gong Jing, and Fan Gong Temple. (Li Sen)
