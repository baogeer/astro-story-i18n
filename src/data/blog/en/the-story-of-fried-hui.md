 Fritters were originally called “deep-fried Japanese cypress,” and it is said that Lin'an people first made them.
During the Southern Song dynasty, Prime Minister Qin Hui, and his wife, Wang, made a drug plan in the east window and burned Marshal Yue, who served the country faithfully to life, to death in the storm pavement. When the news spread, ordinary people were all not convinced; restaurants, teahouses, and streets and alleys were all discussing this matter...
At that time, under the Zhonganqiao River, there were two food stalls next door; one sold sesame and scallion pancakes, and the other sold deep-fried glutinous rice balls. On this day, the morning market had just broken up. Wang Er, who made pancakes, cleared the stove and sorted out the leftover green onion pancakes. Seeing that there were no buyers, he sat on a bench and rested. At this point, Li Si, who made glutinous rice balls, had also packed up the oil pan and crouched there to smoke.
After greeting each other, Li Si walked over. The two of them sat down opposite each other and talked. After talking for two, they unwittingly discussed the incident of Qin Hui killing Yue Fei. Li Si was furious and couldn't help but clench his fist and knock hard on the bar: “Traitor! “I can't hate you...” Wang Er laughed and said, “Brother Li doesn't need to be in a hurry; look, I'll clean them up!” Speaking of which, they took two pimples from the bar, pinched the ball, and kneaded the ball into two people; a man with raised eyebrows, and a woman with a crooked mouth. He grabbed a face-cutting knife, stabbed the big-cheeked man in the neck, and stabbed the crooked woman in the belly, and said to Li Si, “What do you think?”
Li Si nodded and said, “But it's still cheaper for them!” After speaking, he ran back to his stand, brought the frying pan to Wang Er's pancake stove, re-kneaded the two cut-cut noodles, glued them back to back, and threw them into the frying pan. One side of the fried noodles one side shouted, “Let's take a look at deep-fried Japanese cypress! Let's take a look at deep-fried Japanese cypress!”
Passers-by heard “deep-fried Japanese cypress” and thought it was so fresh; they all gathered around. Everyone saw these two people in the frying pan, and they were frying so loud that they understood what was going on. They were so excited that they all shouted along; “Look, look, fry the Japanese cypress! Look, look, deep-fried Japanese cypress!”
At this point, I only heard the sound of a gong. As it happened, Qin Hui was sitting in an eight-lift coupe, retreated from the imperial palace back to his home, and passed through Ongyo Bridge. Qin Hui heard loud cries in the car. She felt that the sound was wrong, so she stopped the car and immediately dispatched his own soldiers to arrest people. Soldiers rushed into the crowd and brought Wang Er and Li Si. Even the frying pan was brought to the front of the car. Qin Hui saw the dark fried noodles in the frying pan. He was so angry that his beard all turned upward. He walked out of the car and yelled, “So bold! Do you want to rebel?”
Wang Er pretended to be OK and answered with a smile, “We have a small business. Where did the opposite happen?”
Qin Hui said, “If that's the case, how dare you misuse your official name?”
Wang Er said, “Ah, Prime Minister, you are the 'cypress' next to the tree; I am the 'stew' next to the fire! ”
At this point, everyone shouted, “Right, right, the sound is different!” Qin Hui had nothing to say. He looked at the two people floating in the frying pan and shouted, “Don't bother! How can I eat this fried thing like black charcoal! They are clearly two troublemakers, gathering together to deceive the government!”
Hearing Qin Hui say this, two people immediately stood up from the crowd and said, “If you want to fry like this, you have to fry like this!” Scoop up the noodles in the pan one by one. They even said, “Yummy, yummy! My teeth are getting itchy the more I eat, I can't help but swallow it!” This made Qin Hui cry and laugh. He had no choice but to stare at his eyes and walk away in a daze.
The imposing butler had quite a crowd; this incident shocked Lin'an City. People rushed to Zhong'an Bridge one after another, and they all wanted to eat “deep-fried Japanese cypress.” Li Si simply stopped making glutinous rice balls. He brought over an oil pan, set up a stall with Wang Er, and partnered to sell “fried Japanese cypress.”
Originally, “deep-fried Japanese cypress” were two people back-to-back. However, the noodles need to be kneaded one by one, and it takes a lot of effort to make a “deep-fried cypress,” which is really expensive. Later, Wang Er and Li Si came up with an easy method. They rolled out a large piece of dough, cut it into many small strips with a cutting knife, and took two sticks. One was considered Qin Ju, and the other was Wang's. Press and twist them together with a stick to fry them in an oil pan. They still call it “fried juniper.” That way, it's much easier to do.
In the first place, ordinary people ate “deep-fried Japanese cypress” to dispel hate. However, once eaten, it was delicious and the price was cheap, so more and more people ate it. For a while, all the biscuit stalls in and outside Lin'an City learned how to do it. Since then, it has spread all over the country.
Since then, “deep-fried Japanese cypress” has become a favorite food for everyone. Later, when people saw that “deep-fried Japanese cypress” is a long strip, they called it a “fritters.” Since fritters were first made at biscuit stands, until now, the original custom has been preserved, and pancakes and fritters are always made in the same stand.
