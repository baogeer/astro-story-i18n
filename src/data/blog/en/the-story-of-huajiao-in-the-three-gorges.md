 As far back as the floods were raging, Dayu led thousands of troops and tens of thousands of horses to clear the Three Gorges of the Yangtze River. The people of Limin, who were devastated by the floods, returned to their homelands one after another to reclaim their pastoral life.
In the flood control army, there is an old man who follows Daewoo, with a little granddaughter. This girl is gorgeous and smart. She is named Pepper. She has been running around with her grandfather all day to treat diseases for migrant workers and local people. Dayu cleared the Three Gorges and was about to move to another location, but there was an eye disease epidemic here.
A girl who is good with Huajiao is so skinny due to eye pain. After being treated by Grandpa Pepper, her eye condition finally got better. This girl is extremely grateful. However, people continued to suffer from eye diseases and various diseases. Seeing this situation, Pepper couldn't bear to leave here with her grandfather's transfer, and proposed to Grandpa to stay in the Three Gorges. Grandpa missed her when she was 13 and 4 years old. She was small and weak. He couldn't rest assured and insisted that she follow the expedition.
Pepper made up her mind, so she went to Uncle Da-woo to make love to her grandfather. Da-woo saw that this innocent little girl had a heart for the people and really liked it, but when she wanted to test her medical skills on the spot, she asked her for prescriptions for several difficult conditions, and she was able to administer medication according to the symptoms. She then treated one person alone for one person's eye disease. She was able to actually do a good job of rejuvenating, taking medicine to get rid of the disease, and quickly cured the patient's illness.
Grandpa nodded and said yes. Daewoo was satisfied and persuaded Huajiao's grandfather to leave her in the Three Gorges.
Pepper took away her grandpa and the flood control team. She was carrying a small medicine kit with her grandpa, going to the owner's house and west family. Whichever family was sick and treated it over a long period of time, cured many patients. Local people all love her and call Huajiao a “magic doctor girl.”
Later, due to the dense forests in this area, the land was wet, and hot and humid eye diseases were widespread. In severe areas, people went from door to door and moaned from house to door, and some were even blinded. Seeing this situation, peppercorns were distraught, and one person couldn't get busy, so they mobilized everyone to find a medicine that bloomed white flowers, made kids, and had a strong taste. The curative effect was remarkable. It cured one by one. Since then, it has become an effective medicine for eye diseases.
In order to make picking easier, people transplanted these saplings to the front and back of their houses. In order to prevent eye diseases, they also add small, fragrant, and fragrant red seeds to the three meals a day. Over time, these little red seeds become a seasoning. Together with chili peppers, they have become a unique flavor of the dish — spicy flavor.
Since then, it has been passed down from generation to generation. Later, people called this unknown little red seed “peppercorns” to commemorate the peppercorns girl.
