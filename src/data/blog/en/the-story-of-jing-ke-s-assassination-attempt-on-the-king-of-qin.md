 Not far southwest of Yi County, west of Yanxiadu, there is a village called Jingke Village. There is an ancient tower on the mountain package next to the village. Seen from afar, it looks like a Qingfeng sword straight into the sky. This tower is the famous Jingke Tower. People pass by here, and I can't help but talk about it with great taste
Starting with the origin of Jing Keta and the story of Jing Ke's assassination of King Qin.
As early as the Warring States period, the Qin State used big pressure to bully the weak with strength. Yan Guo is the one that often suffers from it. Yan Zhao built the Golden Platform, recruited the world's heroes and talents, determined to enrich the country and strengthen its people and resist Qin. It was then that Jing Ke was heavily recruited by Taizidan Yan.
Jing Ke is Korean. After being hired, Yan Taizidan built a very beautiful house for him, called the Jing Guan. The current Jingke Village is said to be the location of Jinggwan back then. Jing Ke ate delicacies from the mountains and the sea, and she was also surrounded by beautiful women. After raising him like this for a year or two, Prince Zidan didn't say why. Jing Ke wanted to help Yan Guo, but for another moment there was no way to do anything. At this point, Prince Yan came to Jingguan, explaining that he wanted to send him to assassinate King Qin. Jing Ke agreed and bravely took on this task.
The set date has arrived. Prince Zidan hosted a feast for him by the Yishui River, and Jing Ke brought Fan Yu Qi's head. (Fan Yuqi was originally a minister of the Qin State; he surrendered to Yan because he was dissatisfied with Qin's tyranny. In order to successfully assassinate King Qin, Fan offered his own head so that Jing Ke could gain the trust of Qin Wang). At the same time, he also carried a map of Yan Guodu Kang, with a dagger hidden in the picture. Warrior Qin Wuyang followed him and went straight to Qin Ting.
King Qin was singing and dancing with the concubines and court ladies in the palace, ignoring reports from internal ministers: “Jing Ke, the envoy of Yan Guo, presented the head of Fan Yu Qi and a map of Dukang.” King Qin was delighted and ordered to receive the messenger at Xianyang Palace.
Xianyang Palace is splendid and powerful. Qin Wangduan was seated in the center, and Wenwu Baiguan stood on both sides. Jing Ke first presented Fan Yuqi's head. Qin Wang laughed out loud when he saw it. Next, Jing Ke held the map of Duke Kang high above his head and paid tribute to Qin Wang. At this point of view, Qin Wuyang was timid and panicked, and his legs and stomach were straight and shaky. Jing Ke swept this scene from the corner of his eye, fearing that his feet would be revealed.” He was busy moving forward, hoping that Qin Wang would quickly pick up the picture so he could take action. Qin Wang took over the map, and Jing Ke made up the first few steps to help Qin Wang display the map while pointing it out. The exhibition was over, and the dagger was revealed. This is what people often call “trying to be poor”. Thanks to this effort, Jing Ke hurriedly pulled out the dagger and stabbed King Qin. I didn't expect Qin Wang to have goggles. Jing Ke was a little further away from him. The dagger stabbed him, and his strength was low. I only heard a beep, but it wasn't stabbed to death. As a result, Qin Wang hurriedly flashed his robe to the back of the Big Red Pillar, and Jing Ke hurriedly hunted down and killed him. The two turned around the pillar.
There were many ministers of culture and martial arts standing on both sides of the palace, but no one carried weapons. Why don't they bring weapons? Because of the Qin State regulations, the Minister of Culture and Armed Forces is not allowed to bring weapons to North Korea. Besides, no one dared to do anything without Qin Wang's orders. At this point, Qin Wang panicked and had no choice but to run. I even forgot the sword I was carrying, so how could I order it! Minister Zhao Gao reminded and shouted, “King, you have a sword too!” Only then did Qin Wang suddenly realize it. However, he was wearing a Dragon Quan sword. He was in a hurry and couldn't pull it out. Zhao Gao made another gesture of drawing a sword from behind; only then did he pull out his sword. Jing Ke is a strong warrior. When King Qin pulled out his sword, it wasn't Jing Ke's opponent either! He was so scared that his face went blank; he had no power to fight back. As soon as Zhao Gao saw it, he was in a hurry: “King, give orders quickly!” He just ordered it. Manchurian civilization and martial arts only captured Jing Ke and Qin Wuyang with seven hands and eight feet, and cut them both into meat sauce on the spot.” Later, the people of Li Min in Yan Guo built a Jing Ke pagoda on the hill next to the Jing Guan to commemorate Jing Ke. Jing Ke's corpse was gone, so he put his crown with feathers inside. Jing Ke Tower is actually Jing Ke's costume house.
(Note: According to historical records, the Jingke Pagoda was originally a sanctuary tower in Shengyuan Temple, also known as the Seri Tower. It was built in the Liao Dynasty.)
