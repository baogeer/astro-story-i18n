 In her later years, Lin Yi lived in seclusion in Xiaogushan, West Lake in Hangzhou. 365 plum trees have been planted on the mountain. Weeding, fertilizing, and hard work on weekdays. When the plums were ripe, crowds of hawkers came to buy his plums. Instead of selling plums by weight, he made judgments based on how many plums were grown per tree, and the price was fair. Therefore, vendors all like to buy his plums. He also prepared 365 bamboo tubes, and put the money sold from each tree separately into the bamboo tube and numbered them. It doesn't matter if there are guests, no guests, or many guests or few guests. Live on the money of a bamboo plum a day, and never spend more than a penny.
He also kept two white cranes. When the guests arrived, the gentleman was on the sidelines. White Crane immediately flew in and stood in front of the gentleman. He packed money and notes in a bag, hung it on the white crane's neck, and made the white crane fly to the city to buy fish, meat, wine, and dishes. When those vendors saw Hakutsuru flying in, and when they learned that the customer had arrived, they paid for the goods issued on the note and handed over to Hakutsuru to bring them back.
