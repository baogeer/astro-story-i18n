 Once upon a time, there were two brothers. The eldest was Loba, and the second was Loche. Because my family is very poor, I have been growing crops with my parents since I was a child.
Loba is smart, hardworking, and helpful. He took the initiative to help out of people around him whenever something happened, and he was very popular. The local chief security officer dispatched him to work as a sergeant at the county government office. In the past three years, he has witnessed the extravagant corruption of the government office and the miserable lives of poor people, and he is reluctant to continue to work in the dark. However, I had many opportunities to run at street fairs. I made many friends, increased my knowledge, and learned some words for the first time, so I continued to stay at the county government office. My younger brother Loche still sells crops with his parents at home.
lose one's job and become a family
At one point, Loba went on a business trip to Guanyin Temple and met a few rogue guys molesting a girl named Ah Yu, and stopped them. The scoundrel knew he was a sergeant at the county government office, and when they didn't dare to offend him, they left quietly. Girl Ah Yu was very grateful to him. Ah Yu's parents are small and famous wealthy people in the local area. Seeing that Lobba is smart and capable, they invited Lobba to dinner to express their gratitude.
Shortly after, Loba heard that Ayu had been forced to get married. She went to visit her without her parents' orders a few times to find her death. Ah Yu cried and said, “A bandit leader in Daqingshan heard the hooligan's accusations and sent his parents a treasure and forged silk. He had a time limit of 10 days to complete the dowry. At that time, he will have to get married as a suppressing lady. Living with bandits will not only cost you your whole life, but it will also tire your relatives and friends. My parents are afraid that the bandits are evil, and the other is that they value the money and supplies sent by the bandits and don't hesitate to betray themselves. They can't even die themselves. They really have no way to go to heaven, and there is no way to enter the land. Loba listened to the situation and meditated for a long time before saying to Ayu, “If you want to drive, don't hurt your health. Although the bandits are rampant, in order for your loved ones not to be harmed, you must first endure it; I will go to Daqingshan to save you.” “Can you really save me?” Ayu asked excitedly. Loba said, “Don't worry, I won't let you down; you must take care of yourself.” Ayu Lian nodded in agreement.
Ten days passed quickly. Hu Qisan, the leader of the bandits, brought a group of minions and arrived at Ayu's house early in the morning carrying a large car. After they had enough food and water, they forced Ah Yu to hit the road. Ah Yu sat uneasy in the car, hoping that Loba would arrive soon. I couldn't stop my tears rolling down when I thought about my future being difficult to predict.
At dusk, the car was carried to the front of the wooden fence gate of the Daqingshan bandit cottage. Hu Qisan hurriedly jumped off his tall horse. He was about to help Ayu get off the bus. A dark horse with a yellow mane flew in. The bandits showed up their weapons and shouted, “Who are you?” The person who came flew down and said, “Who is the king?” Hu Qisan asked, “What's wrong?” A visitor said, “My name is Loba, and it's been three years since I was forced by the Chief Security Officer to work as a sergeant at the county government office. Mr. County was ordered by Shangfeng to destroy you. He has been searching for you for many days. Today, he learned that the king had married a bride back to the mountains, and sent various soldiers and horses to attack Daqingshan tonight. I know that you are poor and have no food or clothing, so you have been forced to embark on this dangerous path. Your family, young and old, are still watching you raise your family. I can't bear to let you die Yokoyamano, and I deliberately risked my life to report the news to you. Currently, soldiers and horses on the east-west and northwest roads have surrounded Daqingshan. There are only soldiers and horses on the south road, and they have not reached the bottom of Daqingshan due to steep slopes and dense forests. You will only survive if you break south immediately.” Some of the bandits knew Loba. Many people had heard of Loba's character for a long time, and had no doubt about what Loba said. While they admired Loba's strong spirit, they anxiously advised Hu Qisan to quickly come up with the idea. Hu Qisan was half-convinced and half-skeptical about Loba's words, but he saw that his subordinates were already anxious and uneasy. He thought that if they didn't follow the crowd's emotions, once they were surrounded and repulsed, his situation would be even worse. It's just that she couldn't think of a solution and wasn't sure about Ah Yu's safety. Lobba also said, “The Black Siege will attack the mountain. Time is short. To prevent the girl Ayu from suffering, you have Ayu riding my horse, and I will escort Ah Yu home as a county government officer. The siege won't doubt it. Just wait until the king escapes and pick her up again.” The bandits all agreed. Hu Sanqi had no choice but to push the boat along with the river, let Ah Yu ride on the donkey's horse, and leave the bandit cottage with Donkey Ba. The bandits went to the cottage to gather their weapons, food, and grass, and quickly evacuated south.
After leaving Daqingshan for a while, Loba and Ayu quietly returned to the bandit cottage. Seeing that the bandits had already left early, they destroyed the cottage, carried useful items on the horse, and destroyed the cottage with a fire. Ah Yu was extremely sad because her parents only cared about her own safety and were greedy for wealth and disregarded her future. They vowed to spend the rest of their lives with Loba and were unwilling to return to their mother's family. The two went straight to Loba's home. Loba's parents and Loche were very happy. They immediately killed a rooster to give it to their ancestors, and Loba and Ayu became friends.
Loba has long been unwilling to work as a sergeant in the county government office. Now that he is full for three years, he has refused to go home... When I met someone close to the bandit, I spread the word that he lost his job because the county government suspected a bandit. Some bandits also realise that being a bandit isn't a long-term solution and is not going to be a bandit anymore.
Large-headed ants arrive at red deer
After Loba became married, Ah Yu was like a flower, gentle and virtuous, honored her in-laws, and maintained a thrifty family. The family had a very happy life. Loba didn't feel at ease with her father-in-law and mother-in-law. Ash Yu went back to visit. Ah Yu knew that her parents were strenuous and that it was not convenient to go back for a while, so she let Loba visit first.
Ah Yu's parents already know that after Loba and Ah Yu became married, they lost their job due to banditry, and the family was very poor, and they didn't want to recognize this family. Seeing Roba arrive, I deliberately despised him. When Lobba entered the house, she smelled a strong scent of bacon, but when eating, she only placed a plate of fried tofu and a pot of vegetables. Loba understood that her father-in-law and mother-in-law suspected his family was poor, so she deliberately said, “Ah Yu told me over and over again, that the bacon in your family tastes very good, and asked me to bring her some.” My father-in-law made a secret calculation in his mind: It's nothing to give him some bacon. If you give him this, he will want that. How many sheep will catch up with the mountain? It's better not to give anything, and let him die of this heart. He answered, “Although the bacon in our family tastes good, there aren't many. I ate it when King Shanda came to marry Ayu as a guest. Look, we also just use browned tofu to add bacon.” Only then did Loba understand that Ah Yu didn't want to go back to her mother's house was not that Ah Yu was gambling with her parents, but that her parents were really unscrupulous. For parents like this, we also have to suffer a bit of being unkind to them. As a result, after talking about my house, there is Daqing Mountain. Red deer often appear. Deer antler is a famous medicinal herb. Eating venison regularly can support longevity, and if you catch live deer, you can also sell it for a big price. The more my father-in-law listened, the more enthusiastic he asked, “How can I catch live deer?” Loba said, “If you want to catch live deer, you must first set up a net at the mountain crossing where red deer must go. There are people who are dedicated to it. Starting at Qingbian at the foot of the mountain, several groups of people with hunting dogs shouted and hit the stones. Red deer were frightened and fled, and they could be caught by drilling into the net at the mouth of the mountain where they had to pass. This is called the mountain.” My father-in-law asked, “Who can lay the net?” Loba said, “I'm just an expert in netting, but I'm not a person who runs mountains.” My father-in-law couldn't wait to say, “I can go ask my relatives and friends to go to Kakeyama right away. How about you go lay the net?” Loba said, “I can set up the net within three days. Take someone to start driving the mountain early in the morning of the fourth day.”
Early in the morning of the fourth day, my father-in-law brought more than 10 people to the bottom of the mountain. Loba divided them into three groups. They started driving down the mountain from the side of the Qinggou under the three mountain beams, and met until they met at Shanyakou, the highest peak, and the net was spread there. Loba confessed over and over, “I'm guarding the net at Shanyakou. I can see the direction the red deer is running. I'm telling you which way you go, you have to go that direction. If you don't drive the red deer to Yamayakou, you won't be able to catch the red deer.” When the crowd understood, they split up and went to the mountain.
Loba came to the bottom of a marijuana chestnut tree in Shanyakou and cut off a piece of bark with a machete. The ants under the tree had to go through this peeled trunk to climb up and down the tree. Lobba moved a stone and sat under a tree and could see all sides of the hill. The people who saw the mountain had already driven to the middle of the mountainside. When it was still early, they shouted, “The red deer ran down, the red deer ran away!” When the people who left the mountain heard it, they went back to their heads and drove off down the mountain. After a while, Loba shouted again: “The red deer is running up, the red deer is running!” The people in the mountain heard it and went back to the top of the mountain. After a few repetitions, the people in the mountain were so tired that they panted one by one, and their mouths were dry and hungry. But the donkey ate tartary buckwheat noodles under the chestnut tree and drank the cool water from the gourd.
At the end of the afternoon, Loba stabbed the two large ants with two tangerine pears, and shouted, “The idiot caught it, the idiot caught it!” When the people in Kakiyama heard it, they all happily rushed to Yamayakou and asked Loba, “Where are the red deer?” The donkey pointed to the ant poking at the chestnut tree and said, “Red deer is here, red deer is here.” Some people said, “That's a big headed ant, why is it a red deer?” Loba answered, “My father-in-law can add fried tofu to bacon, why can't I beat giant ants to red deer?” Everyone was aware of his father-in-law's usual behavior. Unbeknownst to him, his father-in-law smiled. He blushed, couldn't speak, and went home with everyone in a humming manner. After this mountain attack, Roba's relationship with her father-in-law's family came to a standstill for several years.
Autumn eyes, treatment of eye diseases
There is a pig butcher with the surname Qian in the market. He often buys sick pigs at low prices and sells sick pork at high prices. The more money he makes, the more he is greedy. Lobba wants to teach him a lesson.
One day, Loba met a farmer from a neighboring village looking for money and said that three pigs in the family were sick. They asked the butcher if they wanted to buy them, so they went and watched it together. Money butchers have always been suffering from eye problems that cannot be cured for a long time. As soon as I heard that there were three sick pigs, I felt that the opportunity must not be lost, so I went to buy sick pigs from the farmers. When Lobba saw the opportunity, she called for help. On the side of a small bridge that the money butcher had to go through on the way back to buying pigs, Loba drilled into a sackcloth pocket and asked Loche to hang himself on a branch of a watermelon tree on the side of the bridge, so that Loche could hide and watch from afar.
The butcher rushed three sick pigs to the bottom of the watermelon tree. Lobba kept shouting, “Autumn eyes, cure eye diseases.” The money butcher heard it clearly and asked out loud, “The gentleman in the tree, what are you doing?” Loba said, “My eyes hurt, and a famous doctor told me a secret recipe. This is a special treatment for eye diseases.” One sentence hit the heart of a money butcher, and I eagerly asked, “How effective is this secret recipe you used?” “It worked pretty well,” Loba said. The money butcher said, “Can you introduce me the secret recipe to try the downside?” Loba said, “Famous doctors told me not to spread secret recipes.” Qian butcher Yang Qiu said, “I haven't been able to cure my eye disease for a long time, and I've lost a lot of business. If you help me this time, I won't be disappointed in you.” Loba said, “I often buy the meat you sell, so let me put it down first,” the money butcher put down the donkey. After the money butcher drilled the bag and hung it on the branch of the watermelon, Lobba only taught him, “You should always think about autumn eyes, treat eye diseases, don't stop. After three days, your eye problems have healed, and naturally someone will let you go. I was treated like this for 3 days, and when my eye disease got better, you came. Don't you think my eyes are healing properly?” The money butcher said, “Yes, it's just my pig...” Loba interrupted Money Butcher's words and said, “Don't worry, I'll help you get your pig back to your house. You can concentrate on curing your eye problems.” After speaking, Loba waved to Loche and rushed away with the three sick pigs.
The money butcher shouted for half a day and felt dry mouth, numb feet, hungry and panicked, and unbearable eye pain. It took less than a day to complain about Loba. It was cold and hungry at night, and the money butcher's voice was hoarse, and he fell asleep in a state of confusion.
The next day, Loba came under the watermelon tree and saw that the money butcher didn't make a sound, so he put him down. The money butcher is dying. Lobba asked, “Why didn't you speak up last night?” The money butcher answered, “I really can't shout.” Loba taught him, “You haven't been able to heal your eye pain for less than three days. It's just because you usually treat people with dishonesty, too much wealth, and an open eye when you see money, so Buddha and Bodhisattva can't help you either. If you buy sick pigs at low prices and sell sick pork at high prices, you have made money, but you have harmed meat buyers, causing meat buyers to get sick from eating sick pork. What is your conscience? In the future, you should be honest as a human being, and stop focusing your eyes on money. Your three sick pigs, I've helped you get to your house and fed them medicine. You've only been treated for an eye problem for a day. It definitely won't heal for a while; I'll take you home too.” Looking back on the immoral things he had done for a long time, he also felt guilty, and said, “Thanks for your teachings, I can slowly go back on my own without bothering you.” Loba stuffed two tartary buckwheat noodles and a gourd with cold water to the money butcher.
When the money butcher came home, he saw that the three sick pigs were well-kept, well-fed, and much better. He didn't sigh and said to himself, “Loba is a nice person! He is not greedy for money, and he advises me not to be greedy; I really should learn from him to be a human being.”
I'm hungry for honey
When Loba was working as a sergeant at the county government office, she was very diligent and quick. When he got home, County Governor Meng always felt that other missionaries were not as good as Lobba. One day, Grandpa Meng sent a sergeant to find Loba and asked Loba to come back to the county government to work as a janitor for a few years. Lobba said that parents are old and their children are young. They need to take full responsibility for themselves, are inseparable, and cannot take their lives. Grandpa Meng's repeated advice didn't work; he had to ask for the truth: “When you were a sergeant, I loved honey, and you can get it for me any way you can; now the sergeant isn't as good as you. Can you get me some more honey?” Loba knows that Grandpa Meng loves honey and can't bear to spend money; in fact, he distributed it to himself. It just so happens that there is a rich owner with the surname Wang who is very good at calculating others and is very greedy. Why don't you let Grandpa Meng rule over Wang Yizhi's treasurer? He said to Grandpa Meng, “I will definitely do my best. It's just that the basic honey currently on the market is spring honey. Poor flower impurities can easily be poisoned, so it will take some time to make winter honey.” Grandpa Meng said, “Then please don't worry; I'm waiting to hear from you.”
The owner of Wang Cai's family is rich and stubborn. The land is large, and there are many tenants. As soon as the crops were ripe, he led his accomplices, brought evil dogs, and forcibly collected rent from tenants. If there is a confrontation, hit the dog and bite it. The tenants hated and feared him; there was nothing they could do about it.
Loba found an opportunity to ask Wang Cai's helper about the situation. She learned that Wang Cai's car can enter the back door. It is located in the back yard of the warehouse. The keys to the back door are kept by the housekeeper, and the keys to the warehouse door are kept by Wang Caizhu himself. Every night, the housekeeper arranges for people to go on a night tour in shifts with evil dogs, and they are closely guarded.
If you don't get into the tiger's den, how can you get the tiger? Loba met a few helpers from Wang Cai's family. Taking advantage of the housekeeper's employment, she was hired to work at Wang Cai's house, and gradually understood Wang Cai's family's personnel situation, housing, and traffic routes. One day, Wang Caizhu and the housekeeper hosted a feast for the guests. They drank until late at night and went to bed drunk. Loba sneaks into the bedroom of Wang Caizhu and the housekeeper, and uses the ants he had already prepared to lay out the key style. After careful imitation, they also took the opportunity to test open the door of the house, and entered the warehouse to inspect the materials inside. Sure enough, there are some good old honey stored in wooden barrels. After thorough investigation and thorough preparation, Loba returned home.
The next day, Loba went straight to the county government office to meet with Master Meng. Grandpa Meng was waiting anxiously. Seeing Loba coming, he hurriedly asked, “How did it go?” Loba frowned and said, “Grandpa, things are really not easy to do. Poor people can't save food at all, and rich families don't take it out even though they have it. I've traveled a lot and ended up being empty-handed, so I'm so sorry to see you.” Grandpa Meng understood that it wasn't out of stock; it was just that the wealthy family had it and wouldn't take it out, so he asked, “Who has the goods and wouldn't take it out?” Loba said, “I've already asked from Wang Caishu's helpers. The best winter honey is stored in the warehouse in Wang Caishu's backyard, and Wang Caizhu heard that you want to use it. He bluntly said that his family doesn't have honey. Isn't this because I couldn't get along with you?” As soon as Grandpa Meng heard that Wang Caizhu didn't take it out, he angrily said, “He thinks he is rich; he still underestimated me a bit!” Loba quietly said to Grandpa Meng, “You can only get rid of his arrogance if you come out in person.” Grandpa Meng asked, “How to spend it,” Loba spoke in detail, and Grandpa Meng even claimed it was wonderful.
Coming out of the county government office, Loba waved over to Wang Caizhu's house and said to Wang Caizhu, “The old man from Meng County had to collect some aged good winter honey. There is no limit to the quantity. I wonder if Wang Cai's house has it in stock?” As soon as Wang Caizhu heard that it was a collection stand, he even declined to say, “No, no, go to another place and ask.” Wang Caizhu's answer was expected by Loba. “Sorry to bother you,” Loba said. I just quit.
Three days later that night, Grandpa Meng brought a group of close friends and joined Loba. Loba asked Loche to carry a wooden ladder to the outside of Wang Caishu's backyard. Loba climbed the wooden ladder, went up and down the pear tree in the fence to the ground, opened the back door, asked Loche to take the wooden ladder bar home, and brought Grandpa Meng and his team into the backyard, leaving two people to hold the back door, and the others split up the main road. Loba led Grandpa Meng into the warehouse. Grandpa Meng checked the supplies in the warehouse and recorded it on cardboard using wicker charcoal. It was still early when Loba met, so she ate honey with Grandpa Meng and had enough. At five o'clock, the night rangers arrived in the backyard and found someone, and when the back door was wide open, they shouted that there was a thief. When Wang Cai led his family, Ding Wensheng, he was taken aback. He was taken aback and asked, “I don't know what happened to Grandpa. He came to the door from afar, and was often too lazy.” Grandpa Meng's close friend scoffed and said, “We are not thieves; we are private visits from the old man.” Wang Caishu nodded and said, “Please forgive my sins, please forgive my sins!” Loba said, “Are you guilty of Wang Caizhu not reporting the goods and deceiving Shangfeng?” Wang Caizhu was in a hurry and couldn't say anything. Grandpa Meng covered the cardboard with the records in his hands and slowly said, “You are gathering to demand goods, defraud the market, deceive and conceal it, are greedy for profit, and endanger people on one side; they should have been severely punished. If you committed the crime for the first time, and don't send it to the government court for trial, just confiscate some of your assets!” Wang Caizhu had no choice but to nod his head and thanked him; he didn't dare to say much. Grandpa Meng assigned two close friends: “Go find a car.” A close friend should have left. At that time, the sky was already bright. Grandpa Meng thought about the number of products on the ticket. The crowd carried it to the back door. Hou Niu pulled a big truck and got on the bus. After getting on the bus, of course, the good honey was also among them. Wang Caizhu suffered the loss of being dumb and even shouted, “Unlucky, unlucky!”
