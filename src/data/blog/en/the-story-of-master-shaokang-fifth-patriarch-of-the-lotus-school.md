 Shaokang (c. 736-805 AD), also known as Shankang, commonly known as Zhou, is a native of Xiandushan, Jinyun in the Tang Dynasty. It is the fifth grandmaster of Buddhism that is widely accepted by all other denominations and has the greatest influence among the people — the Pure Land sect (Lotus) [note 1] [note 2] of the seven ancestors [note 2] (Song “The Book of the Ancestors” vol. 26). He went to great lengths on his own and spared no effort. It enjoys a very high position in the annals of Buddhism, especially the Jodo sect. Therefore, Master Shaokang is one of our famous Chinese and foreign celebrities in Jinyun, both ancient and modern.
Shaokang was born out of good health
In the Tang Dynasty, Kaiyuan Tianbao Room, there was a small village on the banks of Jiuqu Creek and at the foot of Xiandu Mountain in Jinyun. The village's mother-in-law, Yiyiyi, and Dinghu Peak were upright, and the mountains were green and the water were beautiful. It really was a wonderland on earth. In other words, there was a descendant in the village whose surname was Zhou and married his wife Roche. Men farm and women weave, husband sings and women, and live a harmonious and comfortable life as a child. In particular, Zhou Luo's kindness and kindness were highly praised and admired by their neighbors on the left and right. The downside of the US and China is that Zhou Luo and his wife have been married for several years and have never had children. For this reason, Zhou Luo and his wife often go to Lingshan Temple to pray for gods and Buddha.
In the afternoon of one day, Roche was tired for a moment and couldn't sleep. As soon as I closed my eyes, I fell asleep. Seul, apparently invited by a fairy and leading the way forward. Roche leisurely followed him to where he was, but he saw Qisheng (that is, Dinghu Peak) directly inserted into the sky. The beautiful water was crystal clear, the sky was fragrant, and colorful fairy flowers. At the place where the colorful clouds opened, the golden girl, and many female fairies were surrounded by a female Bodhisattva with beautiful eyes that looked like two stars, smiled, and majestic, and slowly floated down the clouds. I saw the female Bodhisattva arrive with a golden blue lotus on her chest. Luo's heart was taken aback — isn't this just the Buddha of Mercy and Peace that is often seen when praying Buddha at Lingshan Temple? They hurriedly clasped their hands and even knelt down to pray. The female Bodhisattva called Lady Jade to lift up Roche, then handed over the green lotus flower to the Roche family, and ordered, “This green lotus flower is an auspicious thing. I am giving it to you now. Be a Takako, and I want to take care of it.” Roche took the green lotus flower with excitement and held it tightly in his arms. While I was so thankful, I actually woke up. Since then, the signs of Roche's pregnancy gradually became apparent, and along with it, her stomach slowly grew larger. Zhou Luo and his wife have always been grateful to the female Bodhisattva for sending their children, and praying the Buddha has become a top priority for her family. After Roche became pregnant, she was also in good health. It's just that fish and meat are fishy, and I can't eat it at all. It's my favorite thing to eat on weekdays. As long as it's meat, I get nauseous as soon as I see it; if I barely eat a little, I even vomit bitter bile. This is also a common occurrence for some pregnant women, so no one should be surprised. Day by day, winter and spring are here, and before we know it, Roche's puerperal period has arrived. On this day, many colorful birds gathered on the big tree in front of the house and chirped like singing fairy music. The inside of Roche's delivery room is full of blue light and exudes a faint scent of lotus flowers. Soon after, Roche gave birth to a son. This kid is good-looking, loud, and very cute. Zhou Luo and his wife became pregnant after dreaming that a female Bodhisattva gave her lotus flowers. They also had a lot of lotus fragrance during delivery, so they nicknamed their son Lien Er.
Shaokang monk
Since Lian Er was born, Zhou Luo and his wife have been cheerfully surrounding their son all day, busy and nurturing them carefully. Lian Er, who is white, fat, and has a full court, a square shape, blue lips, and is loved by everyone. They all say that he is very blessed and will definitely be rich and noble in the future. Lian Er was not playful when she was a child. She often sat laughing. She only grew up to seven years old and had never spoken yet. Her parents were so confused.
One day, Roche took him to Lingshan Temple to worship Buddha. Inside the treasure house, Lian Er looked at this Buddha, prayed to that Buddha, and finally stared hand in front of a giant Buddha and refused to leave for a long time. When Roche was surprised to see it, he casually asked, “Do you know this Buddha?” Unbeknownst to her, Lien gave a small finger and suddenly said, “This is Shakyamuni Buddha.” Lian Er hasn't spoken since birth, and she hasn't seen a Buddha statue before. This was the first time I saw a Bodhisattva, and the first time he spoke, he said the name Buddha Zunzun. Roche was very surprised. He hugged Lian Er, and burst into tears. On the way home, Roche recalled a dream she had before she became pregnant... In the evening, Roche discussed with her husband and agreed that Lian Er is well-rooted and has a relationship with the Buddha. He chose an auspicious day and reluctantly sent Lien Er to Lingshan Temple on Mount Xiandu to become a priest. The old monk took the Dharma Ren'er as Shaokang.
While Shaokang dedicated himself to praying Buddha at Ryozen-ji Temple, he also often tempted friends who accompanied adults to worship the Buddha and herd cows on the hills and riverside meadows around the temple to pray for “Amitabha Buddha.” Time really is like water, and time is like an arrow. Unbeknownst to Shaokang, he has reached the age of 15. Through the old monk's teachings and his own personal research, Shaokang is already able to understand the mysteries of the five major sutras, including the “Lotus Sutra” and the “Leng Yan Sutra.” The old monk knows that Shaokang is very knowledgeable about Buddhism, and advised him to go to a larger temple early to study and promote Buddhism. As a result, Shaokang Yiyi reluctantly said goodbye to his master and went to Jiaxiangji Temple in Huiji (Shaoxing, Zhejiang) to study the Ministry of Law. Five years later, when he was a weak teacher, he went to Longxing Temple in Shangyuan (Jiangning, Jiangsu) to listen to “Huayan Sutra” and “Yoga Theory,” and relentlessly studied the principles of Buddhism in the morning and evening. Over the next few years, I was less fortunate and traveled a long distance, traveled all over the place, and studied Buddhism.
Determined to be pure
At the beginning of Tang Zhen Yuan (AD 785), Shaokang visited Baima Temple in Luoyang, the earliest Buddhist temple in China, which he had long admired. One day, when he prayed to the Buddha in the temple, he suddenly saw a Buddhist scripture shining in the Grand Hall. Shao Kang was astonished. He hurriedly took a closer look and discovered that it was a “Westernized Introduction” by the two ancestors of the Jodo sect [Note 3]. Shaokang was extremely happy when he saw this scene. He thought that the master mentor was a high priest of the Jodo sect. He also admired the Pure Land teachings. Being able to see the Buddhist sutras shine today must be a good sign. It is convenient to pray silently in one's heart; “If I really have a relationship with the Pure Land, let this scripture shine light up and get me started.” After praying, the Buddhist scripture certainly shone once again, and countless Buddha statues of Bodhisattvas were revealed in the light. Shaokang hurriedly threw himself to the ground and vowed, “If you steal a stone, you can wear it out. My pledge for the pure land of Hongyang will never change.” Since then, Master Shaokang has established a firm ambition to study the Pure Land Dharma and explain the Pure Land Dharma.
After Shaokang made a vow, he immediately let go of Wan Yuan, and with a sense of respect, he made a special visit to Komyo-ji Temple in Chang'an, the famous temple of Master Zen at the time. One day, Shaokang admired the portrait of Master Zendō in the temple in the temple. In the theater, he prayed, hoping to see the true face of the Master Good Mentor. At the time of the ceremony, I noticed that Master Shanshou really appeared in the air, and advised Shao Kang to say, “If you follow our teachings, you have a connection. He will succeed day by day, and he will live well.” After listening to the teachings of Master Shanshou, Shaokang became more determined to study the Pure Karma and apply the Pure Land teachings for the rest of his life.
Quan Qiao-chaohua
Since then, Shaokang has sought out the classics of the Pure Land, devoting himself to practicing, and practicing Buddhism has increased day by day. In order to further promote Pure Land Buddhism, Shaokang worked tirelessly to promote Pure Land teachings from Chang'an, Shaanxi, south to Jiangling (Jingzhou) in Hubei.
One day, Shaokang visited Gwan-ji Temple in Gangneung. When entering the temple to worship the Buddha, I met an old monk Yan with a crane. After greeting each other, the old monk said to him, “If you want to teach all living beings, you should go to Xinding. Your Buddha relationship is over there!” Shaokang just wanted to ask about the details. Suddenly, he didn't see it; he only saw the fragrance go west. Shaokang realized that this was the Buddha's guide, so he decided to go to Xinding [Note 4] to reward people from other places.
When Shaokang first arrived at the new time, since no one practiced the Pure Land Dharma in this area, monks and lay people didn't know much about the Pure Land doctrine, and the fact of enlightenment was very difficult. Shaokang thought about it day and night, and finally came up with a way to facilitate all people living in Puru. He begged every day in the city, and after getting the money, he induced the children to say, “'Nanwu Amitabha' is your master teacher. I will give you a dime every time you read the name Buddha.” The children first thought it was fun, and the other was to get money, so they scrambled to say the Buddha name “There is no Amitabha.” The fact that children get money by praying for Buddha had a great impact in Xindingcheng. Men, women, and children all over the city were worshiping the Buddha, some out of taking money, and others out of joke... As soon as they saw Shaokang, they prayed endlessly. It can really be described as “everyone is holding hands, everyone is a master.” Shaokang never rooted in everyone's motives for praying the Buddha; they all smiled and gave money one by one as a sign of encouragement. Later, there were too many people praying, so Shaokang had to switch to praying the Buddha ten times and get a reward of one dollar. After Shaokang taught this method without hindrance for a year, the towns and villages of Xinding County, whether young or old, respected or inferior, when they saw Shaokang — and even when they met an acquaintance, they bowed down the saying “Nanwu Amitabha”, making nenbutsu a local fad, and the sound of Nenbutsu often filled the streets. The “Buddhist Meditation with a Name” [Note 5] was popularized by the public.
Takazuki Dharma Altar
After a period of indoctrination and education, Master Shaokang thought the opportunity was ripe. In the 10th year of Tang Zhenyuan (AD 794), he built a third-level Buddhist altar at the foot of Wulong Mountain [note 6] near Xinding County, built Yuquan Temple [Note 7], and carefully carved a statue of Amitabha Buddha on the “breathing stone” [note 8] in the temple, and officially built the Pure Land Dojo to gather the public to practice the puja together. Every day of Ramadan, good men and women from all over the four sides gather. Master Shaokang ascended to the throne, prayed loudly, and thousands of Buddhists chanted along the altar. What is particularly strange is that as soon as Master Shaokang prays a Buddha, one Buddha floats out of his mouth, and ten bodhisattvas floats out with ten sounds, like a string of Buddha beads, one after another. Therefore, Master Shaokang is called a “later good guide” [Note 9]. Master Shaokang told his disciples, “When I'm praying, if you see a Buddha floating out of my mouth, it means that you have taken a certain amount of steps, and you will definitely be able to live in Western Bliss in the future. If you haven't seen the Buddha yet, don't worry; as long as you truly believe in the Pure Land and do your best, you will be able to see the Buddha in the future, and you will definitely be able to live in Western Bliss.” The disciples listened to the master's teachings, and they were all more earnest in their belief and desire to pray. They all felt sad and blamed themselves, worked hard, and worked hard to practice the Buddha. Master Shaokang gathered the crowd to practice Buddhism with such fanfare, setting off a boom in praying for life in the Pure Land of Buddhism in Jiangsu and Zhejiang. As a result, people have a desire for good, no evil will be committed, and there will be no disputes between neighbors. As a result, people are praising Master Shaokang as a “living Bodhisattva” who came to the world to help people.
Since Master Shaokang spared no effort in promoting Pure Land Buddhism, the Pure Land Dharma spread rapidly, and famous teachers and teachers have emerged, and are now popular at home and abroad, forming a beautiful scene where Buddhists from all over the world practice together.
End the silence
Master Shaokang promoted the Dharma by himself, focusing on educating Pure Land scholars, and compiled 3 volumes of “Pure Land Theory” and co-authored 1 volume with Shamen Wenyi “Lost in the West” [Note 10], which spread throughout the world. “Past Life in the Western Pure Land: Rui Should Be Deleted” was also included in the “Great Tibetan Sutra” (Vol. 51), and is an important document for studying Pure Land Buddhism.
In the first year of Tang Yongzhen (805 AD), Master Shaokang was 69 years old. On October 3 of the lunar calendar of this year, the master predicted that his death limit had reached and told his disciples, “Be happy in pure land; in Yan Futian, you get tired of centrifugation.” At the end of the day, sit back, let in some light, and relax. At this point, the weather changed abruptly, and I saw strong winds, dark clouds, birds screaming, and the top of Wulong Mountain instantly turning white. God also wanted to greet Master Shaokang.
After Master Shaokang died, there was an endless stream of morals and desolation. Four disciples built a stupa for him at Taiziyan in the east of Yanzhou (present-day Meicheng). For this reason, Master Shaokang is also known as “Master Taiyan.” Legend has it that it was unknown at the time who discovered that the dirt on Master Shaokang's tomb tower was curable, so many state residents burned incense and used it to treat diseases. After more than a century, the tower was greatly damaged. Master Shaokang's tomb tower was rebuilt in the 3rd year after Han Qianyou (950), more than 10 years after entering Tiantai Mountain to promote the law. Later generations called it the “Houshan Guide Tower.”
Based on Master Shaokang's efforts to go his own way, sparing no effort to make an outstanding contribution to Hongxuan Jingzong, he is listed as the fifth ancestor of the Seven Ancestors of Lianzong in “The Chronicles of the Seven Ancestors of the Lotus Zong”. Buddhism and the Annals of Jingzong's history will forever preserve Master Shaokang's extraordinary glorious work.
Remarks:
[Note 1] Pure Land sect: It is a Buddhist sect that specializes in the Pure Land Dharma of Amitabha. It is also known as the “Lotus sect” because its founder, Master Huiyuan from Dongjin, planted white lotus at Lushan Donglin Temple and founded the Lotus Society to promote the Pure Land of the Past. The actual founder was a master mentor of the Tang Dynasty. The main classics and teachings are the “Three Sutras and One Theory,” namely the “Murangshou Sutra,” “The Amitabha Sutra,” and “The Theory of Past Life.”
[Note 2] Seven ancestors: There is generally no direct inheritance relationship between the ancestors and masters of the Pure Land sect. Master Shi Zhi Zongxiao of the Song dynasty listed previous masters such as Huiyuan, Shandao, Shang Yun, Dazhao, Shaokang, Longshou, and Provincial Standing, who had made significant contributions to promoting the Pure Land Dharma, as the seven ancestors of the Lianzong. As of the Ming dynasty, Yunqi Zhu Hong and Ou Yizhi Xu were the 8th or 9th ancestors, while the Qing dynasty had strategies, Sheng An, and Che Wu as the 10th, 11th, and 12th ancestors; in modern times, Master Guang was promoted as the thirteenth ancestor.
[Note 3] Master Shandao: (613-681), a native of Linzi, Shandong, a senior monk from the Tang Dynasty. He is the second-generation ancestor of the Pure Land sect and the actual founder of the Pure Land sect. He became a priest when he was a child. In the 15th year of Tang Taizong (641), he went to Xihe Genchu-ji Temple and prayed as a teacher. Later, he entered Komyoji Temple in Chang'an and handed down the Pure Land Dharma, and his disciples were innumerable. Legend has it that he wrote 100,000 volumes of the “Mida Sutra” in his lifetime and is known as the “Avatar of Mida.” He is the author of “Guan Wuliang Shou Jing”, “Praise for Past Lives”, “Dharma Dharma”, “Praise the Law of the Dharma”, and “Pang Zhou Zan”, etc.
[Note 4] New: Within present-day Chun'an County, that is, Suian County in the Tang Dynasty. Its county city, Shicheng, was flooded in 1958 when it was used as the Xin'an River Reservoir (cultural relics of the underwater city still exist today). At the time of Dongwu Sun Quan, it was divided into Xinding County, Wuqiang Township, Shexian County; in the first year of Taikang in Western Jin (280), Suian County was merged into Xin'an County; in the 9th year of Sui Kaihuang (589), Suian County was merged into Xin'an County; in 1958, the State Council decided to merge Suian County into Chun'an County.
[Note 5] “Meditation with a name”: This is the method of praying with a focus on the name of the Buddha. It is one of the four methods of praying Buddhism in the Jodo sect.
[Note 6] Wulong Mountain: On the north side of present-day Meicheng Town in Jiande City, 25 kilometers above sea level, it is more than 890 meters above sea level. The mountain is majestic, green, and the water is beautiful. It stretches for tens of miles. It is known as “Little Qi Yun” and is an important symbol of Jiande County.
[Note 7] Yuquan Temple: Also known as Yuquan Temple, commonly known as Shibutsuao and Shifengan, it is located at the southern foot of North Wulong Mountain in Meicheng Town, Jiande City. In the 10th year of Tang Desong (794 AD), Master Shaokang, the five ancestors of the Pure Land sect, founded the mountain as the “Good Guide Monk Mida Dojo.” There is a stone in the temple, which is more than seven feet tall. It is five feet tall on Wednesday and has Amitabha Buddha engraved on it. Legend has it that it was planted during the Tang Dynasty. There are hills in the east of Teranohigashi, such as the castle, and the famous stone castle, and the clouds don't even show the beginning and end of the sky. There is a spring in the west, with turbulent snowfall. It is a jade spring. In Song Shi, Yu Quan built a pavilion, which is called Jade Spring. At its peak, the temple had 100 buildings and 170 acres of good fields. Incense has flourished throughout the ages. In 1942, Japanese invaders invaded Meicheng and bombarded the Wulong Mountain jungle. Yuquan Temple was damaged. In the “Cultural Revolution,” all the remaining temple buildings were demolished, and the stone Buddha was bombed without being destroyed; they survived. It was rebuilt on its original site in 1991. Today, there are the Amitabha Buddha Hall, the Emperor's Hall, the Yuantong Hall, the Nembutsu Hall, the bell tower, the monument gallery, the Zushi Tower, and other buildings. It covers an area of more than 30 acres and is quite large. It is a sacred place for Yan Ling and a pure land for the world.

One sank into the sea, and one stood at the top of the five peaks of Wulong Mountain (about 50 miles from today's Yuquan Temple). One day, the Amitabha Buddha Road passed through the top of the five peaks and kicked the “breathing stone”. The “breathing stone” rolled all the way to the current site of Gyokusen-ji Temple before stopping. When Master Shaokang founded the mountain, he carved a “breathing stone” into an Amitabha Buddha. Strangely enough, even though this statue of Amitabha was bombarded by the Japanese invaders in 1942 and exploded with explosives tied to the head of the stone Buddha during the “Cultural Revolution,” it was completely undamaged. [Note 11] Master of Dezhao: Dezhao (891-972), a native of Longquan, Zhejiang (Jinyun). At the age of 15, he became a monk from Longgui Temple, and later went to Linchuan, where he taught Wenyi (855-958) to fully assist Master Wenyi in founding the Dagen sect of Zen Buddhism. Later, in the 3rd year of Tang Qingtai (936), he entered Tiantai Mountain to build a dojo at Tongyuan Peak to study the Daxing Dafan. In the 12th year of post-Han Tianfu (948), Qian Hongchu became Queen Wu Yue, who sent an envoy to visit Dezhao to Hangzhou, and was granted the title of “Master Zen Master” and respected as a national teacher, so he was called the Master of Dezhao. Dezhao lived in Tiantai Mountain for 37 years and built 13 dojos. During this time, he also helped the Taiwanese monk Yi Xian (919-987) do a great deal of work to complete the Taiwanese classics lost since the disaster.
