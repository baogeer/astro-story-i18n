Take charge of the Qing dynasty
On the first day of August 7, the 6th year of Tang Zhenguan (632), the 72-year-old Master Kanchou summoned thousands of monks from all over the temple to solemnly announce that Zhi Wei will preside over Guoqing Temple, the fundamental dojo of the Tendai sect, inherit the Dharma lantern of the Tendai sect. Thanks to Zhi Wei's advanced, law-abiding efforts, and good presidency, Emperor Taizong Li Shiminzhao made Zhi Wei a “Master” and gave him the title of “Master” in the 20th year of Tang Zhenguan (646).
However, since entering the Tang dynasty, the development of the Tendai sect and the survival of Guoqing Temple have gradually presented many difficulties and tests. From the perspective of the imperial court, the first is that the Tang dynasty emperor adopted Daoism founder Laozi Li Yi as the ancestor of the royal family, thus adopting a series of policies and measures to “go before the Buddha.” By the 3rd year of Gao Zong Xianqing (658), Taoism was clearly defined as the “national religion”, which undoubtedly had a certain impact on the development of Buddhism; second, since the Tiantai sect and the ancestral court Guoqing Temple entered the Tang dynasty, they completely lost the full political and economic support provided by the royal powers during the Chen and Sui dynasties. The lives of thousands of monks in the temple and the daily survival and maintenance expenses of Guoqing Temple were difficult and stressful, and sometimes seriously jeopardized the survival and development of the Taizong and ancestral home, Guoqing Temple. From within Buddhism, the first is the rise of Buddhist sects such as the Cien sect, the Shingon sect, Tantra, Zen, and the Pure Land sect. Many Buddhist sects used Chang'an, the capital city of the Tang dynasty, as their center to promote the law, and even a few Buddhist sects voted for their favors and favors the imperial court... The new Buddhist sect is greatly superior to the Tiantai sect, which is far from Chang'an by thousands of mountains and waters in terms of geographical location and other development advantages. Therefore, during the Chen and Sui dynasties, the Tiantai sect, which had a monopoly of the dynasty, completely entered an era of competitive survival with obvious disadvantages; second, the Tiantai sect was divided into two major factions at this time: the “Guoqing” system based on “watching” and the “Yuquan” faction, which focused on “precepts,” and the power of the Tiantai religious group was seriously divided and weakened. It was under such difficult circumstances that Master Zhi Wei painstakingly planned, encouraged and worked hard to promote the Tendai sect of Buddhism and Guoqing Temple to advance in the midst of difficult misfortunes, so that the status and role of the Tendai sect and its ancestral home, Guoseiji Temple, were further consolidated and developed.
