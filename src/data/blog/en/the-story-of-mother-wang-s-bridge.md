 There is a village called Puzigang in Dongerli Town, Mingguang Qiaotou Town. There is a bridge called “Wang Mama Bridge” at the east end of Puzigang. At first, it was just an unnamed wooden bridge, but later the bridge was damaged. Villagers from several villages near the bridge donated funds to build a single-hole stone bridge on the original site. After the stone bridge was built, villagers named the bridge “Wang Mama Bridge” to commemorate Mama Wang, who originally set up a tea stand at the head of the bridge. The ruins of the old bridge still exist.
Legend has it that a long time ago, a family lived in Dongtou of Puzigang. There were two old couples. The old man's last name was Wang, known as Grandpa Wang, and his old partner was called Mama Wang. Both families were in their sixties, with no one man or half a woman under their lap. There is a creek on the side of Puzigang that separates the two sides of the Strait. In summer, people take off their shoes and rolls and wade through the water. In spring, cold, autumn, and winter, it takes a long detour. Wang Laohan and his wife saw it in their eyes and decided to use their years of savings to build a bridge in front of the gate where the surface of the stream is narrow. Every day, Wang Laohan built an entire autumn and winter bridge foundation from the foot of the mountain with stones and the foundation of the bridge. Wang Laohan also traveled all over the surrounding villages and spent money to buy a large bowl of acacia trees. He bought a total of 48 trees. He brought in a few carpenters. After three days of cutting and cutting and cutting belts, they built a three-pier wooden bridge with a width of 2 feet and a length of 4 feet. Wang Laohan worked on earthen paving the road for over a month, and the wooden bridge was ready for traffic. The bridge has been built, the road is clear, and it is convenient for pedestrians to come and go. They want to donate money to him. Wang Laohan refused to accept it. Later, a gentleman suggested, “Puzigang is more than two miles from the town, and three or four miles from the nearby village. It's just a good place to take a break while rushing from the street to and from the street. Mama Wang might as well set up a tea stand here to quench their thirst. Everyone can pay for tea, you and the old one can add salt and soy sauce. Vinegar or something.” Everyone said it was reasonable, and Wang Laohan thought it was possible. As a result, Mama Wang set up a tea stand at the bridge. The average person, Mama Wang only charges one dollar; some elderly and frail people don't take any pennies. In addition to being responsible for road construction, Wang Laohan also planted an octagonal rice field of about 2 acres. Strangely enough, as long as they are planted, there will definitely be a good harvest. Not a single grass tree grows in the field, so the old couple had a pretty pleasant life.
The sky is full of ups and downs, and people have both bad luck and bad luck. Wang Laohan lost his job early one morning and was burdened with a basket to build a road. Early this morning, Mama Wang had just woken up. An old man from the previous village covered his stomach and passed by the bridge.
