 The marriage between the Chinese and Tibetan tribes
During the Tuban dynasty in Tibet, Chizun, the concubine of Zanpuchide, gave birth to a prince, who was as powerful and handsome as a god. Since his mother was a Qiang girl, he was named “Jiang Zulawen,” meaning a nephew of the Qiang people and a descendant of Tenjin. The prince grew up little by little, and when it was time to adopt a family, Zanpu summoned the Minister of Culture and Wu to discuss the election of a princess. He said, “Our ancestors, Song Zang, and Gan, were very talented. They married Princess Wencheng, the daughter of the Tang Dynasty, so that the two countries lived in harmony and made Tuban prosperous and stable. Now that the prince has grown up, he should have a beautiful and wise girl as his wife. I heard that Princess Jincheng, the daughter of Tang Zhongzong, was beautiful and I wanted to marry her”. As a result, an envoy was sent to Chang'an with Zhong Li to ask for marriage. After reading the letter and wedding ceremony, Tang Zhongzong decided to assign Princess Jincheng to Prince Tuban. After Princess Jincheng found out, she didn't know if she was happy or worried. Tuban was thousands of miles away, far from her hometown, let alone the prince's personality. Fortunately, the princess has a mirror that can reflect the future and things in the distance. From the mirror, she saw that the Yalong River Valley in Tuban was beautiful and rich, and the prince was amazing. So full of joy, she promised to marry Jiang Zuan Rawin.
The princess is sad and wrestles the treasure mirror
Princess Jincheng took the emperor's instructions and went to Tibet with a large number of items, but halfway there, an unfortunate incident happened. After hearing the news that Princess Jincheng was married to Tuban, everyone was very happy. In particular, Zanjiarawen rode his horse and was happy to greet Princess Jincheng with his entourage. Who would have guessed that the prince accidentally fell off his horse while hitting a Mercedes-Benz on the way, and lost his life in Huangquan. When the princess arrived at the junction of the Chinese and Tibetan ethnic groups, she suddenly felt bogged down, so she took out her mirror and watched. Who knew that the young and handsome prince in the mirror would disappear in the future. Instead, he was replaced by an old man with a beard full of beards. The princess was puzzled and heartbroken. Unbeknownst to her, the mirror fell out of her hands, fell in half, and became two mountains, namely Sun Moon Mountain in Qinghai. As the saying goes, the girl who gets married spills the water. Princess Jincheng thought to herself that she had already married Tufan. Even though the prince died, how could she go back to her hometown, let alone that the father and emperor repeatedly warned that something must be done for the friendly unity between the Chinese and Tibetan people, so she arrived in Tuban and married Zuzan of Zanpuchede.
The princess lost her little prince
Later, Princess Jincheng gave birth to a son. Zampu heard that Xun was happy and hurriedly rushed back from abroad. Unexpectedly, when he came back, Princess Jincheng's son was taken by Heedeng, the concubine of the Na Nang family. When Heeden came to grab the baby, Princess Jincheng was angry and cried, “This is my child.” At the same time, they also got a breast certificate with milk. Who would have thought that the concubine had already tried to rob the child, applied medicine to her breasts beforehand, and also squeezed out the milk, so everyone couldn't figure out who actually gave birth to the baby. In the end, they were robbed by their concubines. In order to determine who actually gave birth to the child, Zampu came up with a way to place the child at the other end of the palace and let the two concubines hold it. Whoever held the child first would own the child. Princess Jincheng tried desperately to get there first, take the child in her arms, and when she arrived, she was anxious and hated when she saw the child being held by Princess Jincheng. She thought: If the child dies, it will die, and you can't hold it. They just rob the princess in her arms, alive or dead. In other words, Princess Jincheng was afraid that the child would be snatched and hurt, so she shouted, “The child was born to me. You vixen, don't snatch the child and hurt him; let you take her.” Just like that, the child was taken away by Heeden. Everyone saw it, and it was clear in their hearts, but there were so many families that had power, and no one dared to say it clearly, and there was no good solution. Little Prince Ming argues that his uncle has been around for a year. The prince is already one year old and is about to hold a celebration. Zang Puxin wants to take this opportunity to find out about the prince's real mother. As a result, Han family and friends and family and friends of the Nanang family were invited to participate. When everyone was seated, Zampu picked up a gold cup, filled with wine, then handed it to the prince, and said, “Give this glass of wine to your real uncle, and determine who your real mother is from this.” People in the Na Nang family teased the little prince with all kinds of things they liked, but the little prince didn't even look at it and said, “Akamatsu Tokuzan is a good nephew of the Han family. How can the Na Nang family be my uncle!” After speaking, I raised my wine glass and steadfastly walked over to Han's uncle's side. When Princess Jincheng saw this scene, she shed tears of excitement and even called “My Good Son,” and the little prince finally returned to Princess Jincheng's arms.
