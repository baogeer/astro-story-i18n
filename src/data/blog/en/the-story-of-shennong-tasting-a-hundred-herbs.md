 The story of Shennong Benyao Tasting Baicao is a famous ancient Chinese myth and legend.
Shennong was originally one of the Three Emperors. He was born in a stone cave in Lieshan. Legend has it that he was a bullhead. Because of his special appearance and hard work and bravery, he was promoted as a tribe leader when he grew up. Because his tribe lived in the hot south and called the Yan people, everyone called him Emperor Yan. At one point, he saw birdseed breeding, which led to the invention of grain agriculture. Because of these outstanding contributions, people also called him Shennong.
He saw that people were getting sick, and he went to Duhirogino to get dried herbs from the Tiandi Garden and was given a magic whip by the emperor. Shennong walked all the way back to Lieshan with this magic whip. Shennong was poisoned many times by tasting moxa, all thanks to tea to detoxify. He vowed to try all kinds of herbs, and finally died because he tasted broken gut herbs. To commemorate his benevolence and exploits, people worshipped him as the god of Yao Wang and built the Yao Wang Temple at 4 o'clock for sacrifices. Legend has it that at the junction of Sichuan, Ou, and Shaanxi in China, it is a place where Shennong tastes herbs, called the Shennongjia Mountain Area.

Everyone who saw it said that God sent it; he brought the Jiuyan Well. As a result, when he grew up, everyone nominated him as the leader of the tribe because they lived in the hot south, so they called themselves the Yan people and called him Emperor Yan. Once, Yandi saw a red bird with a string of things that looked like seeds.
