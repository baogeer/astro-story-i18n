 Su Shi paints and loves to paint dead wood, bamboo, and stone. On his cousin's memoir, Ke, he praised, “Bamboo stands out in the cold, the tree lives in the barren, and the stone is written as a friend of the three interests.” He has collected Qiuchi Stone, Xuelang Stone, and Lingbi Stone shaped like an “elk's neck,” and gave friends the Dengzhou “Magpie Vortex” stone, which is like crushed jade, and used cakes in exchange for colorful jade pebbles from the Qi'an River in exchange for Buddha Yin Zen Master. He also wrote poems praising Tang Yuan's stone, the “Drunken Master” stone, “Jiuhua in a pot”, “Jiuhua in a pot”, the twelve stones of Beihai, and the Qixi stone. His phrase, “Among the bad, the best; if a stone is ugly, all are beautiful” became an aesthetic guideline for appreciating stones. He has been associated with stones throughout his life, and stones have also become a symbol of his spiritual temperament.

In 1047 AD, 12-year-old Su Shi was still studying at the Meishan Shajiao family. One day, he and his friends were playing a game of digging for treasure in the garden and suddenly dug up a light green stone shaped like a fish. The stone is dotted with tiny silver stars. It is gentle and bright, and it also makes a loud, metal-like sound when hit. Su Shi tried to polish the ink, and it was still very good at emitting ink, but there were no holes for storing water on it. Father Su Xun said, “This is 'Tianyan. ' There is a function of ink, but it's just that it's not perfect in terms of shape.” He advised Xiao Su Shi to take good care of his collection, and said a profound remark: “It is also a blessing in writing.” Su Xun also personally carved a dent adapted to the shape of the stone on the wooden board, so that the carpenter used this as the bottom of the box to make a box so that the stone could be placed properly.
As a result, Su Shi named this stone “Tianshi Inkstone” and wrote an inkstone inscription: “Once you get it, you can't change it. Either the Lord is above morality, or everything is perfect. They are both, Gu Yu An. There are many such things in the world.”
A sentence from his father was a great inspiration for young Su Shi. A stone without an inkstone pool and cannot be called an inkstone, yet it was given the mission and meaning of inkstone, and it is also a peaceful object that has become a splendid literary talent for all young people. Since then, this stone has accompanied Su Shi's growing and wandering career, from Meishan to Kaifeng, to Fengxiang, to Huangzhou, to Hangzhou...
A few inscriptions by young Su Shi are often misinterpreted by contemporary people, so it is necessary to clearly translate them: once given and created by the creator, they cannot be changed. Some are mainly based on moral connotations, and some are perfect in terms of physical appearance. Combining the two aspects of virtue and shape. What aspects will I focus on capturing? Aren't there so many inkstones in the world that have an ink pond on top and inkstone feet on the bottom?
Su Shi made it very clear. What he valued was the “virtue” of the ink, not the “shape.” No matter what form a talent takes, it must have the quality and virtue of ink. Becoming a person who lives up to the “epitome of writing” is the ambition of young Su Shi.
Writing is his blessing, and it is also the cause of his bad luck. By studying diligently, Su Shi studied wealth and was talented. He entered high school at the age of 22. Together with his younger brother Su Ze, Emperor Renzong gladly praised “Today we have two prime ministers for our children and grandchildren.” Later, because of differences in political opinions from those in power, many of his poems were interpreted as implying slander. At the age of 44, he was attacked by political opponents, imprisoned, and demoted to Huangzhou. When he was released from prison, he wrote poems lamenting “I am tired of writing my whole life.”
Amidst the chaos of displacement after being demoted, Su Shi thought he had lost Tianshi. He hadn't found it in Huangzhou for 4 years, yet he was pleasantly surprised to find it when he left Huangzhou to Dangtu's boat and opened the bookcase to find a book. At this time, Su Yi was 49 years old. She was the world-famous Su Dongpo. Looking at the bright Tianshi ink and the marks engraved by her father in the box, Su Shi was both happy and moved. She called her sons Su Yi and Su Guo, and solemnly handed over the inkstone to them.
“The Complete Works of Dongpo” includes 27 inkstones written by Su Shi. At the end of his life, he also wanted to use Zijin Inks of Rice for burial. He has collected and used many inkstones; on the other hand, he is very fond of this unusable Tianshi inkstone. This stone, which gathered her father's high hopes, became Su Shi's “original intention,” and he also wanted to pass this trust on to his children.
Two strange stones
In 1059, 24-year-old Su Shi had a strange dream during her mother's death and death at home. In my dream, a strange man with a strange appearance and the appearance of a ghost came to me and had a conversation with him. It turned out to be the spirit of a strange stone standing next to the bamboo house. This strange stone is useless. Whether it is used as an anvil, as a pillar, as a whetstone, or as an inkstone, as an inkstone, as an arrow, or as a stone monument, it's not enough material, so Su Shi really hates it and has wanted to throw it away for a long time. Strange Stone made a loud “thumping” sound in his mouth. After careful identification, he gradually understood what he was saying. He quoted the classics, laughed, and listed his similar achievements in his books to show how extraordinary the Strange Stones are. He said, “The stones you call useful, are scattered all over the mountains, and they use their injuries to destroy themselves to serve the world. Even though they are of minor use, what can they count as? And if you think a strange rock like me is useless, isn't it beneficial to you? “I haven't moved, no words, no polish, no shine; the Zi Xing Festival is as strong as I am.” Wouldn't it be a great gain for me to give these to you? Why should you blame me? When Dongpo was ashamed to say something, the strange stone disappeared.
This strange stone is the second stone that Meishan Homeland gave Su Shi spiritual motivation. Its most typical personality is “”. External forces can't change it at all; it cannot be engraved; it cannot be polished smoothly; thunder and bombardment; it is independent; wind, rain and snow; it is difficult to change its steadfastness.
This strange stone is actually the personification of Su Shi's own spiritual pursuit. Although he was a young man at this time and had not experienced many hardships in his life, he got his own answers from strange stones on how to stand up in the future and how to maintain his true nature in the midst of the storm in the Lianhai Sea.
Yes, Su Shi's personality, on the one hand, is as smart and gentle as Tianshi, and on the other hand, he is also an angular stubborn stone. He is never afraid of wind or frost, and never conceals his true self. He is bright and honest, and straightforward. If you encounter something unwell, “if there are flies in your food, you just throw up”. As a result, he became an “eternal opposition” in the dynasty, and was repeatedly attacked and belittled as a result.
The stone is the epitome of a mountain. It preserves the strength and strength of the mountain, and contains the cliffs and strangeness of the mountain. Su Shi's ambition was to be the cornerstone of the country and use what he learned in his lifetime to govern the country and the world. “The golden voice of jade is in the stone”, “standing still and no one is as good as Zun”. This is the undertone of Su Shi's personality and the main theme of changes in his life.
Sanchou Ikeishi
In 1091, when Su Shi was Taishou in Yingzhou, he had a dream. In his dream, someone invited him to stay in a government office, and the words “Qiu Chi” were clearly written on the plaque. When I woke up, I remembered a poem by Du Fu: “Ancient Qiu Chi Den, a secret path through the skies”. Qiu Chi is the name of Mount Xian in Taoism. Su Shi dreamed of falling into the Immortal Realm, and his mind felt extremely happy, so he never forgot about the dream.
The following year, Su Shi was transferred to the position of Taishou of Yangzhou. His cousin Cheng Deru brought him two strange stones, one white, one green, and one from Lingnan. Strange green stones and mountains were piled up, and caves were accessible on top, showing a magical situation of ingenuity in every inch. Looking at the exquisite, transparent stone, Su Shi couldn't help but store it in a large copper pot cast by Goryeo, and lined the stone with white stone from Dengzhou, which looks like crushed jade he had harvested before, calling it a “rare treasure.” Looking at Strange Stone, he seemed to have seen the Qiu Chi Wonderland he loved, so he named it “Qiu Chi Shi.”
Later, his friend Ma Wangyi, who had been implicated in the “Wutai Poetry Case,” heard about Qiu Chi Shi's great name and wanted to borrow it for fun. Wang Yi has always been on the “List of Untrustworthy People.” When he gave it to him, he definitely didn't return it. As a result, Su Shi got into several pen-and-ink lawsuits with him. In return for poetry, he proposed that Grandpa Ma actually wanted to borrow them, and asked Grandpa Ma to exchange two of Han Gan's personal collection. When I finally thought about it, I couldn't bear to let Wang Yi take a look.
Su Shi once said in “The Chronicles of the Treasure House” for Wang Yi, “A gentleman can mean things, not pay attention to things.” “Meaning something” means falling in love with something, and “paying attention to something” means being addicted to something. When it comes to cherishing Qiu Chi Shi, Su Shi seems to have gone against his attitude of attaining his views. Why is that? Let's also take a look at the poem he wrote for Qiu Chi Shi:
To dream is to recognize the wrong time,
Pumping water and burying the basin makes a fool of oneself.
But when I saw that Jade Peak was too white,
From Bird Road, I was able to choose Emei.
The autumn breeze and the smell of smoke,

The old man really wants to live in the pool of feud.
Because he lives with Tao Yuanming in his heart, he spent most of his life watching the ups and downs. Qiuchi was his peach blossom source. In the spring of 1082, Su Shi, who worked in Dongpo, Huangzhou, wrote in the song “Jiang Chengzi,” “I woke up drunk in my dream. Only Fuyuan Akira is a former life”. He began studying Tao poetry in middle age, and later relegated to Huizhou and Danzhou, and mixed over 100 pottery poems. In “Coming Back with Tao,” he said, “Shi Yuan Ming's elegant performance, and a hundred new poems. I have no doubt that I will be blessed with a clear lead in my footsteps.”
Su Shi successively served as Taishou of Mizhou, Xuzhou, Huzhou, Dengzhou, Hangzhou, Yingzhou, Yangzhou, and Dingzhou, and was demoted to Huangzhou, Ruzhou, Huizhou, and Danzhou. He lamented, “I am currently drifting and waiting for red geese; there is no permanent shelter in Jiangbei, Jiangnan.” In the midst of exile and migration, he nurtured his mentality. Although he was on his way, he was able to transcend things and be aloof. Everywhere he went, he was able to fall in love with the landscape, adapt himself with a sense of seclusion, and be saved in Laozhuang's philosophy. But he also adheres to the Confucian spirit of helping the world and strengthening oneself, just as a mountain is strong and a stone is strong. Therefore, in his stone, he has both stubbornness and the presence of pillars and stones, as well as a sense of seclusion and liberation.
Four Snow Wave Stones
In 1093, Su Yi, then in the back garden of Dingzhou, discovered a strange stone in the back garden of the government office. The texture was as gentle as the flow of a river, and it also had the shape of a sea beast in the waves. He named it “Snow Wave Stone”. He specially had the mason carve a white marble basin to place it, engraved it on the pot, and wrote Shi Yong Huai, saying that this strange stone came from the exuberant Taihang Mountain. It once competed with Mount Taishan, and was later transported to guard the city as a “flying stone” The cannon shocked the soul of Tianjiao”. Today, during the Taiping season, it can only sit still beside dead elm roots, yet its magnificent texture still shows its strangeness. Su Shi sings Xuelang Shi; isn't he also writing about his own experiences? He was also at the height of the temple, and had a high energy to get rid of evil; he was also imprisoned, criticized day and night, and confused between life and death; he also despised his poor country, had no clothing or food, and was “unwilling to live in the cold, lonely and cold in the sand.” Su Shi distributed verses by Yong Xuelang Shi to his family and friends to express his joy at meeting a strange stone, causing his younger brother Su Ze and his friends Qin Guan, Chao Buzhi, Zhang Yi, Li Zhiyi, and Shen Liaozi to sing along. This stone became a star in the stone world for a while.
From the snow and waves, Su Shi saw in a trance the Minjiang River flowing from his hometown. “This is a self-illusion or not a dream; let's talk about the landscape of his hometown.” At this point, Xuelang Stone is connected to the Strange Stone Spirit in my hometown. After being away for half his life, he hasn't forgotten his original intention.
This famous Xuelang stone is still preserved in the Temple of Literature in Dingzhou, Hebei. Wang Shiyi of the Qing Dynasty once saw it and commented, “There is nothing strange about Shi. He admired Po Gong for thousands of years, and was blessed by nature.” “(Su Yi) has a collection of poems such as “The Drunken Taoist Stone,” “Strange Stone,” and “Dendrobium,” all of which have gone viral. Taking snow waves as an example, it's not necessarily surprising.”
Wang Shiyi was right. Judging from the morphology, Su Shi's stones are probably all normal, but when Su Shi's spiritual projection is injected into them, they have divinity, and they can increase their brightness and increase their price and be immortal for a thousand years. Look, there are so many stones. The ten stones engraved on the stone drum claim to be our number one cultural relic in China. The value cannot be measured; aren't they also stones?
It's been more than 900 years since Su Shi left us, and we are still looking forward to having the chance to admire Qiu Chi Shi, Xue Lang Shi, or the strange stone at his home in Meizhou. In his poem to mourn Su Shi, he said, “Empty Xuelang Zhong Shi, left with the people of the country for a long time.”
