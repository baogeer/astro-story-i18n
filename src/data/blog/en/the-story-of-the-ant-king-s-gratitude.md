 In ancient times, a Fuyang native named Dong Zhaozhi once took a boat across the Qiantang River. When the boat arrived at the center of the river, it suddenly discovered a three-foot-long reed floating in the river. There was an ant in a hurry on the reed, crawling from one end to the other, and apparently it was about to fall into the river and drown.
Dong Zhaozhi is a good-hearted person. He brought the boat close to the reeds and wanted to pick up the reeds along with the ants. The people on the boat objected: “This is a poisonous bug that stings people. If you dare to take the boat, we'll stomp it to death!” Dong Zhaozhi couldn't help it, so he tied the reeds with a rope at one end, tied them to the side of the boat, and dragged the ants ashore. The ants escaped from death and quickly ran away on land.
This night, Dong Zhaozhi had a dream where hundreds of people dressed in black were looking for him. The first man in black thanked him and said, “I am the king of ants. I accidentally fell into the river today. Thanks to you, you saved my life. If you need anything in the future, be sure to let me know and I'll do my best to help you.”
After Dong Zhao woke up, he felt that this dream was absurd, so he ignored it.
A few years later, Dong Zhaozhi offended the government for defending his rights and was taken to jail. There was no way to file a complaint, and he was unable to get out. In desperation, he remembered that absurd dream and thought it might be better to try it out; maybe it was a solution. He found a few ants in his cell and said to them, “I am Fuyang Dong Zhaozhi. I'm in jail. Go tell your king and let him find a way to save me!”
The ants paused for a moment and hurriedly crawled away.
However, after that, there was no movement for three days. Dong Zhaozhi was a bit hopeless, and even felt that he was so bored that he was foolish to talk to ants.
Three days later, in the middle of the night, there was heavy rain and lightning thunder. In his sleep, Dong Zhaozhi also dreamed of the Ant King in black. Ant King shouted, “Hurry up! Hurry up!” Dong Zhaozhi woke up violently and looked around. Suddenly, the wall in one corner of the cell collapsed out of a hole with a “buzz”, and tens of thousands of ants crawled away densely through the hole. Dong Zhaozhi knows that this is an escape route where Ant King led the ants out of the cavities. He prayed to the ants with gratitude and escaped from prison on the night of the thunderstorm.
Since then, Dong Zhaozhi has always warned his descendants that building virtue and doing good deeds should start little by little; don't do anything small.
(“Qi Concord”, “Art and Literature Collection”)
