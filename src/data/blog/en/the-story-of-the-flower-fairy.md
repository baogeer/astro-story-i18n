 Deep in the forest, there is a family where their older sister Lili lives, and their elderly grandmother.
Lilac is always active. Every day, she has to go to the forest to pick some vegetables and fruits for food, and also pick a basket of flowers to grow in the garden. Of course, purple lilacs and pink lilies are essential. Lily is not like her sister. She has a very quiet personality and doesn't like to make noise. But every day, she keeps her home in good order, cleans it clean, and doesn't forget to loosen the soil and water the flowers in the garden. My grandmother can cook good dishes and changes the flowers in the vase regularly. The family's life was not rich, but it was a blissful one.
One day, the sisters were eating fruit in the living room and suddenly heard a cry: “Save my life, let me go!” Lilac opened the curtain and saw that a girl the size of a butterfly was surrounded by a large flock of birds and couldn't get out. The birds threw her in the air for a while, pecked her on the shoulder for a while, and ripped her hair loose for a while. Lilac was outraged by the girl. She pushed the door open and ran to the place. Lily followed closely behind her with a stick. The two made a concerted effort to scatter the flock of birds and fly back to the sky. Lily carefully picked up the frightened girl, placed her on the bed, covered her with a small blanket, and warmed her body.
After careful care, the little girl finally woke up. Grandma saw it and hurriedly brought out the freshly baked bread and placed it in front of her. The little girl saw it and devoured it. My grandmother asked with concern, “What's your name? How could it fall into the hands of those flocks?” As soon as the little girl heard it, she cried with sadness: “I was originally a flower fairy. I lived in a peony flower, but this peony was pecked by that group of birds. I couldn't find a place to hide, so they took me away.” Lily said to Flower Fairy, “Just stay with us, we'll take care of you. I don't have anything else at home; I just have a lot of flowers.” The flower fairy nodded and smiled brightly. Since then, Flower Fairy has lived happily with them.
But one day, Flower Fairy disappeared, leaving everyone in a hurry. Lilac found a note on the table, only to see that it said: Grandma, sister, I have to leave you. I don't belong to this world, I still have to go back. There's nothing to give you in return; I'll give you a calendar. Just tear down one of these pages, state your wishes, and you can make them come true. Sorry, I'm leaving.
The family saw that magical calendar, but no one used it. When I picked it up, I saw a flower fairy.
