 Before the Kada Keikodo Festival begins, the Kodang tower was arranged. A long wall map of the “King of Grain” is hung on the upper wall. Two square tables are placed below. The “head family plaques” are placed in the center of the table. The statues of the social director, mother, Mushi Lang, and Lady Harvest Mushi are placed on the left and right sides of the plaque. The statues are made of square wooden boards, and the words “country”, “tai”, “people”, and “an” are written on the back. Place incense burners, bamboo gouge, pork head, rice wine, and fruit in front of the plaque, and place charms such as the head of the zhangtan master, dharma suit, master sword, and brand stamp before offering.
The Katian Qinggudang Festival places “Shegong” and “Shemu” in important places, second only to “King Wugu,” because the Miao compatriots believe that “Shegong” and “Shemu” are the ancestors of Miao society, “Sheng Gong” and “Our Lady,” just like the “Fu Xi” and “Lady” of the Han people.
When I was a kid, I often sat around the fire at night listening to grandparents and uncles “talk plain.” At that time, what we were most curious about was how humans came about. Every time we asked this question, the old people would tell us the story of “The Old Man from Dongshan and Little Sister Nanshan” (also known as the story of Jiang Liang and Little Sister). We were fascinated and amazed by this beautiful myth.
Legend has it that in ancient times, there was an autumn year, when the sky was dark and red rain began on August 8. The flood flooded Tianmen Gate on August 13, and 99 streams of water all flowed to Dongting Lake. The torrential rain fell for almost a month, and floods poured into Tianmen three times. A young man named Jiang Liang in Dongshan (now Chengbu Jinzishan) was rescued by a giant python. He came to Tianmenkou with a bronze stick and knocked on the sky drum. The bronze bar was broken, and the sky drum was broken. Only then did Tianting send someone to act as a director. After hearing Jiang Liang's complaint, they invited Emperor Jade to stop the flood.
After the flood, Jiang Liang returned to the mortal world. He looked around and saw that all the mountain tops were empty slopes. People in the mortal world had died. He was so angry that his heart was in pain, and his eyes were full of tears. There is only one young girl who survived Namsan (now Shirobu 100,000 Furuta Oyama). Seeing this disaster on earth, she cried with sadness and fell to the ground. Taibai Xianren saw it. They quickly took out Xian Dan, rescued her, and intended to use Little Sister Jiang Liang as a race, and wanted the two of them to form a couple. Jiang Liang was very happy to listen to it, but the little sister was shy and left. Misty and hazy, it's good to hear someone say to him, “If you ask Kim Fu Torang to be your matchmaker, you can get married as you wish. But where can I find Golden Woman Momoro? While feeling sad, he saw a golden turtle walking out of Taoshu Mountain. Jiang Liang hurriedly went to catch the golden turtle. I was about to get started, and I heard someone behind me shouting, “Don't take my wife.” Who's shouting? It turned out to be the tortoise's owner, Momoju Senjin. After Jiang Liang apologized, he confided in Xianren that he had received instructions from Taibai Xianren to marry Little Sister Nanshan, but he was tormented by no one to act as a mediator. Peach Tree Fairy said he would be willing to act as a mediator for Jiang Liang. At first, Mrs. Golden Turtle went to talk to the media. The little girl was shy and pretended not to agree. After that, Momoshu Fairy ran for the horse herself, but the little sister still refused. Jiang Liang also doesn't want to be difficult for strong people. He thinks it's better to do things voluntarily for men and women; leave it up to God to decide if they have a chance or not. So I agreed with my little sister to use the fireworks encounter to determine if the two are related. The little girl didn't believe that Dongshan and Nanshan were two mountains so far apart, and the smoke would be entangled, so she promised Jiang Liang to see if she had a relationship with Jiang Liang. They started the fire on time and place as agreed. Strangely enough, Higashiyama blew the northeast wind and the southern mountain blew the southwest wind. The two waves of firewood smoke were entangled and gathered together. What a blessing! So they became a couple. Three months after the marriage, Nanshan's sister became pregnant. At nine months, she quickly had a lot of meat. She had no hands or feet, and no adults, so the little sister threw away all the flesh. It just so happened that the people of Damo Xian passed by, breaking up pieces of meat into 364 pieces with a knife. As human seeds, they were scattered all over the place. The three dynasties became people, and they grew up in the four dynasties. Wherever there are chunks of meat, there are humans. In the 364 places it visited, it became 364 surnames, and since then it has given birth to humans.
