 Legend has it that there used to be a family with only three people, that is, two young people with one child. The couple are affectionate, and the kids are very smart and clever. It can be called a happy family. However, as the saying goes, “A loving couple is out”. After a few years, the daughter-in-law had a very strange illness and was bedridden. Her husband was extremely anxious about this. He went to great lengths to pray to God and ask for medicine, but since the daughter-in-law had a terminal illness that no one could cure, the herbal medicine had no effect...
On this day, my daughter-in-law, who hasn't even had soup for many days and had been in a coma for a few days, suddenly woke up from her slumber. She knew that her limit was near; it was time to look back. As a result, she called her exhausted husband to her hospital bed to say her final goodbye. She clasped her husband's hand and asked him to personally promise her two things: the first thing was to beg him not to say anything nice about herself in front of the doll's old lady in the future, and preferably not even mention her. The second thing is about educating my son. She said that even though dolls don't fight or curse, they're not adults. However, if her son wanted to hit him, he could only hit him with her own hands; she would never allow anyone else to hit him. Therefore, after her death, she asked her husband to cut off her right hand and store it in storage, and only take it out to educate his son when he is disobedient. Even with tears of grief, the hardhearted husband nodded deeply and agreed to these two somewhat unusual things. The daughter-in-law was then relieved to close her eyes. The husband then sawed off his daughter-in-law's right hand according to her will, dried it, and treasured it.
A few years later, the young man, who was unhappily widowed, married another stepmother for the doll. This old lady treated her ex-wife's son in good and bad times. Generally speaking, before her child is born, she should care about and love him more, and when she has her own biological son, she will inevitably become more aggressive towards him. Overall, though, it's not too abusive. What only puzzled her was that every time she wanted to hit the son left behind by her ex-wife, her husband took a dry hand out of the big cabinet and asked her to play only with this strange hand, but never told him why that was the case. But over time, she became accustomed to it. Every time she wanted to educate her former mother's son, she naturally went to the big cabinet and pulled out that dry hand.
The stars have changed, and the son of the former mother has grown into an adult in the blink of an eye. On the morning of the day after he married his daughter-in-law and started a family, he had just climbed out of bed and hurriedly went to the large cabinet to remove the dry hand that scared and hated him. His dad then asked him what he wanted to do, and when he couldn't help it any longer, he angrily answered, “I've been punched by this hand on my back since I was a kid. Now I'm going to burn it to ashes in order to get rid of the hate in my heart!” His dad couldn't help but sigh heavily, and only then slowly told him about his poor daughter, who died early, and the painful history of this hand. When the son listened, he couldn't help but clasp this dry hand, which was soaked in so much love from his mother, close to his chest and blurted out, “Mother, mother, my poor daughter!” I lost my voice and started crying...
On the night of the same day, this girl, who lost her daughter as a child, was deeply shocked for the first time since starting a family. She carefully made a small cabinet, carefully put in her daughter's dry hand, and then respectfully and respectfully provided it to her family altar with great piety. Since then, this tear-jerking hand has not only become a family heirloom passed down from generation to generation; it has also become an excellent textbook for them to teach future generations to honor their parents.
Luo Tinghong narrates
