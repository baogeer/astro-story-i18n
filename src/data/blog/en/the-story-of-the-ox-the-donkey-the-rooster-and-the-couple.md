 Once upon a time, there was a hardworking young man named Solan Jasi. He has very few possessions; he has only one scalper, one donkey, five chickens, and a small field. Later, he found a wife, and he didn't want to do anything with a sharp job. He only knew how to eat and eat every day. Others gave her a nickname, Sheba Zhuo.
One day, the scalper came back from cultivating the field and kept sighing. The donkey felt very sad, and came to comfort: “Hey, friend, are you tired?” The scalper shook his head and answered, “Alas, work is my job; I don't care if I'm tired. It was the woman of Shebatshaw, who not only didn't let me get enough to eat, but she hit me with a stick, and I really couldn't stand it.”
“This woman, she treats us all the same!” The donkey shouted in a fit of anger; “Friend, I'll teach you a way to just lie down and don't eat anything. The owner thinks you're sick and will ask you to rest.”
Scalpers follow the method taught by donkeys and lie in the cow ring and pretend to be sick. Solangazi was very heartbroken after watching it, so he put on a donkey and let it take the place of scalpers to cultivate the land. After a day of work, the donkey was tired to live. It thought, “This work on cultivated land is really painful. I still have to find a way to let the scalpers do it themselves.”
At night, the donkey limped, walked to the scalper, and made a fuss and said, “Big brother! There's some bad news I can't help but tell you. When the owner cultivated the land, he said, “If scalpers get sick like this, they can sell them to butchers!” The scalper was frightened to death, and its legs trembled: “Oh my god!” The donkey said, “Big brother, it's okay! Starting tomorrow, if you eat well, work hard, scream straight around your neck and wag your tail in the sky, the owner won't sell you.” The scalper listened to the donkey's words and obediently farmed the land.
Solangzahi worked hard for two or three years, and his life slowly became rich. He had some meat, ghee, barley, and pickles in the warehouse. One day, Shebatore said to her husband, “Hey! Solang Jasi, during the Wangguo Festival, I want to invite a few relatives to eat and drink for a week full of pain.” Solangzahi hurriedly exhorted, “Ah Jia, this stuff isn't easy to come by. I think I'll keep it and eat it slowly.” Sheba Zhuo angrily said, “Bah! You're still heartbroken when you eat something like this. Let me tell you, if you agree, we'll get on with it; if you don't agree, we can get divorced!” When Solangazi heard about the divorce, she was so frightened that she had no choice but to change her voice and smile and said, “Ah Jia, don't be in a hurry; let's discuss it again!”
The couple's argument was all overheard by the big cock in the house. The next day, Solangzahi put up scalpers and donkeys and plowed vegetable fields in the backyard. While drinking tea, the big rooster came and said to the scalper and the donkey, “Hurry up! Our owner, Solang Jasi, really doesn't have the temperament of a husband! Yesterday, Shebatshaw said that if you want to eat up all the things in the house, or you can just get divorced and break up with him. My hands were itchy when I heard it, and the owner still whispered about discussions. Friends, look at me. There are four ladies around me, and none of them don't listen to me. When I go out looking for food during the day, they all follow me; when I go to bed at night, they all ask me to go first.”
The donkey said, “Sheba Zhuo only knew how to eat. He didn't understand us at all. We couldn't eat enough. Maybe the owner didn't know at all.” The scalper lay on the grass and slowly said, “Yes! Our owner couldn't find a way to call Sheba Zhuo a labourer, just knowing that he was tired and tired. If Sheba had understood the hardships of labor, he wouldn't abuse us, nor bully my husband like this.”
While drinking tea, Solangazi listened to conversations between roosters, and scalpers, and donkeys, and felt very educated. On this day, he picked up his work early, personally fed the scalpers and donkeys with peas and herbs, and fed the roosters and hens with pickles. Sheba Zhuo watched it. He was very unhappy. He asked, “Hey! You finished work so early today and fed the animals yourself. “What does that mean?” Solang Zaxi smiled and said to her, “My wife, Chabazeul, do you know how the good life of our family came about?” Sheba Zhuo answered, “Huh, of course your good work brought me good fortune!”
“Well, who is cultivating our land”
“Of course it's farmed by scalpers.”
“Well, who is carrying our manure”
“Of course it was carried by a donkey.”
“OK!” The husband also asked, “Who gave the eggs we ate and sold?”
“Of course it's raw from hens.” Sheba answered quickly.
“That's right! Without scalpers cultivating fields, donkeys delivering dung, and hens laying eggs, we wouldn't have a rich life. Not only do you not feed them enough, you hit them at every turn, so that's not appropriate.” Sheba bowed his head and couldn't say a word.
“Ah Jia, starting tomorrow, I think you'll send fat to the vegetable field too! There are five trips in the morning and five in the afternoon. If you have more, you'll get tired.” Solangazi said in a consultative tone.
“What's amazing about this!” While Sheba Zhuo answered, he prepared a basket for delivering feces. After three days of defecation, she fell back and forth from exhaustion, her shoulders were swollen and tall, and a piece of skin was worn out on her lower back. The pain was unbearable. At this point, she thought, “I've only been working for three days, and I'm so tired. Solang is all over the world. I don't know how tired my body is, donkeys carry things every day, I don't know how much pain their back hurts, scalpers cultivate land every day, and I don't know how uncomfortable their necks are”
Since then, Sheba Zhuo has become hardworking and kind. She is considerate and kind to her husband; she is just like her own children. No one else is envious of such a harmonious and wealthy family!
