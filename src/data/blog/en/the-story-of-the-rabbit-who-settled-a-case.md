 Once upon a time, there was a young man named Li Yong. He was an orphan. His family was very poor and depended on using buttons to catch wild animals for a living. Because he is brave and clever, he often catches many wild animals. This can make the landowner's son two dogs hungry. He stalked Lee Yong to take him to catch them together, and Li Yong had no choice but to agree.
They arrived in the mountains this day. Li Yong put the button down in a place where wild animals often appear, but the two dogs just wanted to play and hung the button on a tall tree.
“When it wasn't bright the next day, the two dogs secretly ran to the mountains to see the buttons. I saw that Li Yong's button caught an obese fawn, and his own button only caught a woodpecker. The two dogs turned their eyes and came up with a bad idea. He put the fawn in his own clasp, exchanged the woodpecker for Li Yong, then quietly slipped back home.
After breakfast, the two dogs went up the mountain with Li Yong. As he walked, he said, “I had a dream about Emperor Jade last night. He said my buttons would definitely catch a fawn.” As soon as he walked to the place where the button was placed, Li Yong was taken aback. He saw that the two dog clasps had caught a fawn and wandered around while hanging it from a tree; his only holding a woodpecker was his own.
“How can fawns run to trees, and how do woodpeckers get to the ground?” Li Yong began to doubt. “This is God's will. Emperor Jade helped me.” The two dogs kept talking nonchalantly. Li Yong wasn't a fool. He quickly understood what was going on, so he told the villagers. However, the rich people in the village all turned to the two dogs because their father was the landlord and the poor were afraid to speak out.
Li Yong was not satisfied and decided to go elsewhere to find a just person. On the way, he met a rabbit. He thought: I heard that rabbits are very smart, there must be a way for this. So I told Minzi about the circumstances of the incident and asked Bunny for help. The rabbit cheerfully said, “No problem. When the sun first showed up on the morning of the third day, you summoned the whole village to the head of the village and let me judge you.”
The sun hadn't come out yet on the morning of the third day. Li Yong, Ergu, and the whole village gathered at the head of the village. However, they waited from morning until noon, and from noon to afternoon, and didn't see any rabbits. Everyone chatted and began to complain about Li Yong. The two dogs were even more proud. I didn't see rabbits swallowing it until the sun was about to set.
The rabbit said to everyone, “I'm really sorry. I just woke up in the morning. Unexpectedly, the sand caught fire. I used a bamboo basket to fill the fire with water, and didn't put out the fire until noon. I ran down here and met another group of goldfish picking honey in the trees and asked me to help, so I arrived late.”
As soon as the rich people heard it, they all laughed and angled their eyes and asked the rabbit, “You notary are really smart! How can sand catch fire?
How can bamboo baskets be filled with water? How can goldfish harvest honey? It's all nonsense.”
The rabbit smiled slightly: “Yes, this is nonsense. So, please ask gentlemen, how can fawns climb trees, and how can woodpeckers go to the ground to find food?”
As soon as the rich heard it, they knew they had been caught in the rabbit's trap, and their faces blushed red. The two dogs also fled back home in a hurry.
