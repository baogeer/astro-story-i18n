 Once upon a time, there was a king, and the family supported a monk. This monk is a bit clever and can predict many things. One day, he said to the king, “King, allow me to report a very scary thing to you. The city will soon be flooded, and you and your subjects will have to stay underwater like fish. The only way to predict the arrival of a flood is to send people to visit the Shishi market every day. If there is blood in the eyes of Shishi, then the flood will arrive in less than seven days.” After speaking, no matter how the king held him back, he left on his back.
The king took the monk's word and sent his three daughters every day to take turns buying meat at the market. Actually, it was to check Shishi's eyes. Right next to Shishi, there are five famous meat sellers. They were very surprised that the princess bought the meat herself. When they were together, they said to each other, “Oh my God! There are hundreds of male and female servants under the king. How can the princess be called to buy meat? There must be a reason for this.” One day, they posed their question to the youngest princess. When the little princess saw that there was no one next to her, she honestly told the story of the Shishi shedding.
After the little princess left, the five merchants got together. You discussed it word by word. They wanted to seize this secret and make a huge fortune. At night, they secretly painted cow blood and sheep blood in the eyes of Shishi. The next day, the Grand Princess came to buy meat. She saw that Shishi had blood on her eyes. Terrified, she couldn't take the meat, and ran to report to the king in a panic. The king hastily summoned all the ministers and announced this terrible news, auctioned off the palace's property at the lowest price, and fled to the mountains with his subjects.
There were only these five merchants selling meat, laughing secretly in their stomachs. They used very little money to buy countless properties and houses. They killed many cows and sheep, cooked a large pot of wine, and each family took turns hosting guests for three days to celebrate that they had become the richest man in the city overnight with their ingenuity and wisdom. While they were drinking and having fun, there was real blood in Shishi's eyes. However, since the upper part was covered with cow blood and sheep blood, it was not easy to see. After seven days, a flood flooded the entire city, and the five merchants, along with their possessions, were swept away by rolling waves and lost track of their whereabouts.
