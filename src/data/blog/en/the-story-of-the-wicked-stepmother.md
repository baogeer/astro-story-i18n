 Siber folk tales spread throughout the Chabchar Siber Autonomous County in Xinjiang.
Once upon a time, there was an individual whose wife suddenly became ill and died, leaving behind a 17-year-old daughter.
The father went home less often because he was away from home and had trouble caring for his daughter. Later, he married a widow.
The widow has a daughter who is eight years old. Soon after, the mother began abusing the child and treated her daughter as a baby.
The older and more beautiful the daughter of the former wife, and the older and uglier the daughter of the second daughter, the daughter-in-law became angry and pushed her into the well and filled it out.
When the father came back, the daughter-in-law falsely claimed that her daughter had died of a sudden illness.
The grieving father discovered that a willow sapling that had grown from the original Inoguchi had grown into a big tree in a few days. There was a beautiful bird singing 99 songs, which was extremely sad.
The father put it in the birdcage, and the daughter-in-law killed it with a poison meter and threw it in the oven and burnt it to ashes.
The next day, my neighbor's lonely grandmother found a bright emerald in the ash, and the emerald later became a beautiful girl.
One day, the girl prepared a table and asked her grandmother to invite her father and daughter-in-law as guests.
During the meeting, the girl told a sad story. The father suddenly realized that he was furious, and immediately sent his stepmother to the government for severe punishment.
A story circulating in the Chabchar Siber Autonomous County in Xinjiang tells the story of a mother and daughter who framed their ex-wife's daughter and was duly punished.

The mother and daughter pushed the daughter of the former wife down the well and buried it.

Later, a willow tree grew in the filled Inoue, and the daughter's ghost became a little bird, singing on the branches every day.

The daughter-in-law burnt the bird to ashes and scattered it outside the gate.

The lonely lady next door saw a shining jewel in the ashes, so she picked it up and put it in the closet at home.

The jewel changed back to being a girl. She lived with the old lady. The old lady helped her avenge her hate and was reunited with her father.

The daughter-in-law and daughter were beheaded in public by the county officials.
