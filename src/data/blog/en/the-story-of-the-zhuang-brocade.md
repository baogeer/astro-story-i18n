 In ancient times, at the foot of a mountain, a Zhuang mother was dependent on her three sons.
Mom is a skilled weaver. After years of hard work, she has woven a magnificent picture with houses, orchards, fields, chickens, ducks, and sheep.
On this day, a gust of wind suddenly blew in, sweeping the sky far away in the East.
Turns out, it was a group of fairies from there who went to tapestry in the shape of Zhuangjin.
The old mother cried blindly because of the loss of Zhuangjin. She sent her oldest son and second son one after another to find Jin, but they all feared the hardships of the road and ran around the city to enjoy the blessings.
Finally, the third son, with the help of Oishi Ma, crossed the volcano and the ocean and found the fairy in red, who was making a pattern of jangjin.
The senior took the opportunity to steal back Zhuangjin and rushed back to his old mother on horseback. Under the sunshine, Zhuangjin gradually grew into a beautiful home.
The old mom also saw the light again. What Lao Si didn't expect was that the fairy in red really liked Zhuang Jin, so she secretly embroidered a portrait of herself on Zhuang Jin, and unexpectedly, she was taken home by Lao Si. As a result, the older three became a couple with the fairy and lived a happy life.
