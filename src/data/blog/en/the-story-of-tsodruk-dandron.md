Grandma said, “My daughter is still young and I don't plan to get married.” The devil said, “If you don't say yes, I'll cry.” After speaking, I rolled my eyes the size of two wooden bowls and started crying. Today one day, tomorrow two days, the day after tomorrow, when the sun rises, I'll pick up my family.”
After three days, the devil certainly came. Laughter shook the house, and the rafters fell off one by one. Grandma was afraid and had to agree again. The devil stopped laughing and said, “That's right. Today one day, tomorrow two days, the day after tomorrow, the day after tomorrow, when the East turns white, I'll pick up my family again.”
After another three days, the devil came early. The old grandmother cried in tears and said, “Please spare my daughter. They walked into a big, very big house. Two dogs the size of a yak were squatting at the door, and they were eating people's bones. The girl fed each dog a piece of “hemp pine.”
At the bottom of the stairs, there is an old woman with a rotten eye. She has many keys hanging around her waist and is weaving a blanket using human hair. The girl gave her a handful of fried barley.
Since then, Cuozhu Danqiong has become the devil's wife. Every day the devil goes out early and comes back late. Cuo Zhu Danqiong walked east and west of the house every day, sometimes helped the old lady weave a blanket and sang some beautiful songs to her.
The terrible days passed day by day. Cuo Zhu Danqiong stayed here for 29 days. On this day, the old lady was dozing off. The girl secretly removed the key to her waist and opened one locked iron door after another. Terrified, she quickly covered her eyes with her hands. Because these houses are full of human blood, human flesh, and human bones.
Cuozhu Danqiong opened the last room. There were many women of all ages lying in a row. Their faces looked like dead leaves and their bodies were like broken wood. If her eyes hadn't been able to turn, the girl would have thought it was a house of dead corpses! Cuo Zhu Danqiong boldly asked; “Old mom, big sister, why are you lying here!” It took a long time before one woman reluctantly answered, “Girl, we are all devil's wives.
