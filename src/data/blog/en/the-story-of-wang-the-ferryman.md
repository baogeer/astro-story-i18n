 There is a ferry crossing in the southwestern part of Mingguang Mountain Lake. The south bank is Shaogang Mountain North Village, and the north bank is the Fengshou Polder Embankment. It is Nushan Lake and a key point for merchants from the Huaihe River to go to Liuhe and Pukou. This ferry crossing is very famous from ancient times to the present, and is known as the “King Ferry”. This “Wang Ferry” is named after a person and has a history of more than 1,000 years.
Legend has it that in 906 AD, Shandong was born in Daishui. A young man named Wang Yan settled here and had to transition south to seek a living. The person ferrying on the lake was a lonely old man. He was sixty to eight years old. He is known as the Wei ferry. Wang Yanzhang arrived at the ferry crossing this day. Since no one was coming, Wei Shuyu was already resting in the cabin to take a nap. Wang Yanzhang politely called out, “Grandpa, I want to make a transition.” Wei Pangdu got up to help and sent Wang Yanzhang for the transition. As the boat reached the center of the lake, Wei Shuang had a severe cough and then vomited blood in his mouth. Wang Yanzhang hurriedly went over to support Wei's ferry: “What happened to the old man?” Wei Baidu had a yellowish face and was powerless and said, “Little brother, I've been sick for over half a year, and after taking medicine a few times, I haven't gotten better. It seems like I haven't been able to do this ferry trade for a long time. Unfortunately, I have no one to replace me. Hi there! As soon as my boat stopped, passers-by on both sides of the Strait didn't know how many detours it would take...” I had another severe cough and vomited a few sips of blood. Wang Yanzhang hurriedly supported the old man to the dock and asked, “Does the old man live on the south bank or the north bank?” Wei Baidu said, “I live in Weidu Village on the North Shore.” Wang Yanzhang said, “Old man, I'll take you home to rest and recuperate. I'll take a few days on this ferry. When you get well, I'll leave. What do you think?” Wei Baidu was overjoyed to hear it. He took a look at the young man in front of him. He was very happy to see that Wang Yanzhang was tall, had a big waist, round shoulders, and was very happy, but I don't know if this person knows anything? Wang Yanzhang explained, “I am from Liangshan County in Shandong Province. I grew up by the lake. I know how to play with water, can fiddle with paddles, and have practiced martial arts since childhood. I can do a few tricks with all 18 weapons. My parents and siblings were affected by the floods in my hometown. I heard that my mentor and brother have a martial arts gym in Jinling. They want to run there and find a place to stay.” As soon as Wei Ferry heard it, he was even more happy. He said OK in the cabin. Wang Yanzhang stayed to take over the ferry for the time being.
The next day Wei was bedridden. Wang Yanzhang asked Lang Zhong to grab the medicine, and his neighbor's sister-in-law helped make the medicine. Wang Yanzhang has officially taken over the ferry service. This has been around for more than three months. Wei Shuangdu's condition not only did not improve; instead, it worsened day by day. A few days later, Wei Fangdu became ill. Since Wei Ferry's family had no savings, Wang Yanzhang borrowed some money from a village neighbor to bury Wei Ferry. Since then, this Wei ferry has become the king ferry.
One day, a cow dealer sold one and a half rhubarb on the southern shore of the lake to transition. When it boarded the boat, this cow had a grumpy temper; no matter how you dragged, pulled, or knocked it, it wouldn't want to get on the boat. The cow dealer was in a hurry and sweated. Wang Yanzhang put down her penny and rolled up her sleeves while saying, “I'll try it.” I saw him hugging the front of the scalper with his left hand and the back of the scalper with his right hand. With both arms, the half of the rhubarb suspended four hooves in the air and was picked up by Wang Yanzhang. As the saying goes, “I'd rather lift a kilo of stone than hold 200 animals.” Strangely enough, the scalper seemed willing to cooperate with Wang Yanzhang and was taken to the boat in response to the post. The cow seller and a few transitioners looked dumbfounded. Since then, Wang has been extremely powerful in ferrying. The story of hugging a cow aboard a boat has been spread, and it has spread further and farther, more divine.
Wang Yanzhang had excellent arm strength, and the legend of his superb martial arts spread hundreds of miles away to Xuzhou. At the time, King Zhu Quanzhong of Houliang abolished Emperor Tang Ai and became emperor on his own. The country name was Liang, known as “Houliang” in history. In order to consolidate his power, Zhu Quanzhong is recruiting troops everywhere and recruiting talents. In 908 AD, Zhu Quanzhong heard that there was a secluded half-immortal in Xuzhou who could learn about the major events of the 500 years before and after. Zhu Quanzhong personally brought someone from the north to Xuzhou. He stayed for a few days without hearing any trace of the Half Immortals. While searching for Half Immortals, I heard rumors about Wang Yanzhang. Zhu Quanzhong was eager to seek wisdom, so he took four or five companions day and night to Wang Ferry.
On this day, King Zhu Quanzhong of Houliang arrived at the ferry crossing. At that time, it was Pan Cun's big gathering. There were a lot of people passing by. Zhu Quanzhong and a few of his companions were not busy getting on the ferry. Instead, they sat under a big tree at the ferry crossing and watched Wang Yanzhang's every move. Wang Yanzhang also saw a few people under the tree. The boat went back and forth on the lake, but these few people still sat under the trees and couldn't board the boat. Wang Yanzhang was a bit puzzled, but in the past there were so many people that he was so busy that he never cared about it. The sky gradually fell into the sky. There were few pedestrians in the past, and a few people under the trees on the shore began to transition aboard the boat. Since Zhu Quanzhong has been watching Wang Yanzhang for a while, watching him help the elderly, hold children, and carry goods, the more he likes it. The more he sees it, the more he likes it, and thinks to himself: this person is kind and has a kind heart. If he were really as powerful and talented in martial arts as in the legend, he would set the world over the generals.
When the boat arrived at the center of the lake, Liang Wang Zhu Quanzhong wanted to test Wang Yanzhang's reaction, so he deliberately used his left force, causing the hull to shake violently. Zhu Quanzhong followed the trend and held out Wang Yanzhang's soft spot under the elbow of his right hand. I saw Wang Yanzhang flashing to the side; at the same time, he reached over and grabbed Zhu Quanzhong's right hand and said, “Please sit still, sir.” As soon as Zhu Quanzhong saw Wang Yanzhang's quick skills, he was even more affectionate. However, the surface is quiet, and I still want to test Wang Yanzhang's strength. Then he said, “I'm walking south through north and south. I don't know how many ferries I've been to; you are the only one who set up the ferry the slowest. Isn't this urgent? Even if you give it to me, I won't pay you anything.” When Zhu Quanzhong said this, he wanted Wang Yanzhang to use his strength to support him; he could tell by watching the speed of the boat. As soon as Wang Yanzhang heard this remark, she thought to herself: These people didn't want to get on the boat for a long time; they just sat under a tree and made comments. I already guessed that they showed a leg in the clouds — not just a foot (horn). Also, I think this person just wanted to make trouble, but he made extraordinary moves, and now he wants to get money. What kind of business is this? Wang Yanzhang looked upset and raised the sound of a “whistling” of the boat in her hand. The arrow went deep into the bottom of the water, showing only a little more tip for half a foot, and picked up both hands: “If you can pull up this boat, I don't need a penny. After disembarking, go to the biggest pub in town to set up a table. If you can't pull it up and leave the boat money behind when you go ashore, don't force me to act recklessly.” Zhu Quanzhong laughed. The commander and his entourage pulled out hard. Those few people gathered to the side. The boat floated on the surface of the water and swayed. A few people were unable to do their best and almost knocked over the boat. The boat was motionless in the water. Back Liang Wang Zhu Quanzhong waved his hand, and his entourage stopped. Wang Yanzhang took a look at the few people on board. He grabbed the boat's tip with one hand and gently lifted it. Bunny brought out half of the mud out of the water. Zhu Quanzhong was secretly happy to see the situation. After disembarking, Zhu Quan called Liang Ming's identity, indicating his intention. Wang Yanzhang is worried that all his skills are nowhere to be used. Now I hear that Houliang Wang admires his talent. He came from hundreds of miles away to invite him. Is there any reason not to rely on him? Houliang Wang Zhu Quanzhong said, “Wang Zhuangshi is extremely powerful. You can see that just now when you supported her.” Wang Yan smiled and said, “There was a fraud just now. “The bunny is inserted into the mud, and if one person pulls it, it may be possible to pull it up, but when everyone pulls the boat, the center of gravity shifts to the side, and the hull swings, making it impossible to use force.” While talking, I tied the cable to a stone that was half a person tall. After the cable was fastened, my right hand grabbed the corner of the big stone. Using force, a stone the size of a dog's head was torn off. Seeing this, the crowd is even more convinced of the magical power of the legend.
Houliang Wang Zhu Quanzhong hosted a banquet for Wang Yanzhang at a tavern in town. Wang Yanzhang said to Zhu Quanzhong after eating and drinking, “Please allow me for three months. After three months, I will go to Houliang to the best of my ability.” Zhu Quanzhong inexplicably asked, “Why don't the brave men go with them?” Wang Yanzhang said, “When Wei Baidu passed away, I borrowed money from my hometown neighbors to handle the aftermath. I haven't paid back some of this money. I'm going with you. I'm afraid it will be difficult to pay back this money; why not leave behind a name called.” When Zhu Quanzhong heard Wang Yanzhang say this, he was even more impressed by Wang Yanzhang's personality. He asked, “How much do we still owe?” Wang Yanzhang said, “Not much; I still owe less than two.” Zhu Quanzhong and his entourage laughed “haha.” Zhu Quanzhong said, “How much do I think it is?” Speaking to his entourage, he said, “Take 50 pieces of silver and share it with the villagers.”
Wang Yanzhang paid back his money and decided to go with Zhu Quanzhong to go to Ziyang Mountain to make a 120-kg long spear using superior Ziyang red iron. After that, Liang Wang Zhu Quanzhong fought between North and South, and achieved many successful battles. The following year, he became Zuo Longxiang's military ambassador, and his rear officials paid tribute to the general. In 923 AD, Hedong Province destroyed Li Keyong and his son Li Cunxun after the fall of the Tang Dynasty. Wang Yanzhang was taken prisoner during the battle. Later, Tang Wang Li Cunxun also admired Wang Yanzhang's personality and talents, persuaded him to surrender many times, and Wang Yanzhang never died. He later died in prison.
More than a thousand years have passed, and Wang Yanzhang's fame has been passed down from generation to generation by ordinary people. Today, the name of the ferry crossing “Wang Ferry” is still in use. At the beginning of the Republic of China, there were also old people who saw the big stone that had been torn off by Wang Yanzhang at the entrance, but then didn't know where it was going. So far, in Shaogang and Pancun, when they talk about “good horses don't carry two husbands, good generals don't have two masters”, they often talk about the story of Wang Yanzhang's death.
