 Speaking of the fishing house, there is a story about Seisen asking visiting fishermen.
According to reports, during the Jiajing years, Jinyun joined Fan Xianke to serve as an inspector general in the imperial court. He was not afraid of power and sent Yan Song's protégé, Kun Hu, the local bully, to the guillotine. He is known as the “Iron Man.”
Soon after, Fan Xianke left his job and returned to Jinyun. He lived a carefree life in Ni Wengdong, calling himself “Doosan Cave Heaven.”
One day, his friend Sen Se visited him because Mount Sentou has more than 100 large and small peaks, and there are so many strange caves in it.
After boating for a long time, Sesen got lost. He met an old fisherman and asked Fan Xianke's place of residence. The old fisherman said, “There is a Doosan man living here.”
As soon as Seisen heard that Mr. Doosan was his good friend Fan Xianke, the three met. Mr. Doosan took out his wine, and the fisherman took out the fish he had just caught.
The three were drinking while recounting old stories.
When the fisherman got up and said goodbye, Sesen offered him 52 silver, which the fisherman categorically refused to accept.
Se-Xian deeply admired this fisherman, so he handed over these 52 pieces of silver to Fan Xianke and built such a pavilion on this stone, named “Asking the Fishing Pavilion” to commemorate his “visit to lost friends, ask fishermen, meet old friends, and say thank you for the bonus.”
