 Wu Tang, Zi Zhongxuan, is a native of the Three Realms of Mingguang. Our ancestors moved from Huiji in the middle of the Ming Dynasty, and they first lived in the Three Realms. They have been cultivating and reading each other for generations. Wu Tang has been studying hard since childhood, working hard on her own. Her family is poor, and she often studies hard under the moonlight. He was selected for talent when he was a teenager, and later, in the 15th year of Daoguang (1835), he was an exhibitor in the Jiangnan Township exam. I went to the seminar five times, all of them famous for Sun Mountain. Daoguang passed the “big pick” in 1844, and was appointed by the imperial court as Qinghe (present-day Huaiyin) Zhi County, and has been working since then.
Ng Tang is from a poor family. He has been strong throughout his life. He is a clean and honest official, and is loved by the people. However, corrupt officials, rich and wealthy people regard him as enemies. This has left many popular stories in Wu Tang's life. The two short stories collected and compiled below are widely spread among people in the Mingguang area.
(1) Cixi never forgets Wu Tang's blessing to send silver
Legend has it that before taking office in Qinghe Zhi County, Wu Tang was waiting to work in Anqing. At the time, Yi Guifei's father, Hui Zheng, was an alternate minister in Anhui Province. He had been out of service in Huizhou for nine years. He had not been able to actually hold an official position and had a difficult livelihood. Wu Tang was very sympathetic to Hui Zhi and often sent some money and goods. Tang made up vacancies in Qinghe Zhi County. On one day, Wu Tang was reviewing official documents at the county government office. There was a news report: “I stopped a funeral boat on the riverbank and hanged the dead. He wanted to meet adults through this place and talk about the old days. It is inconvenient to visit the government office because you have filial piety, so adults are invited to enjoy a drink on the boat.”
Wu Tang arrived at the shore. The people on the boat had been waiting on the shore for a long time. When they arrived recently, they turned out to be friends in the same window when they were young. Wu Tang learned of the death of her friend's father. Since this old man helped Wu Tang as a teenager, the old man has now passed away. Wu Tang is planning to give away two hundred and two silver memorial stones as funeral expenses. But I don't have that much cash on hand. When people come to know something in a foreign country, it is essential to have some arguments. The two talked and drank while talking. One drink for you and one for me until late at night. Both of them were nine minutes drunk, so you sent them to me. I took you back and forth to pull the saw back and forth, and Wu Tang went back to her home.
As soon as I woke up, it was already a trifecta of sunshine. Wu Tang climbed up in a hurry and had them take 300 silver dollars to the riverside, and repeatedly ordered that it was a funeral boat.
I sent someone trotting all the way to the riverside with 300 dollars. Coincidentally, a boat was lost on the riverside, and the boat family and their employer were in a standoff over boat money and were unwilling to leave again. A beautiful little girl on the bow of the boat, wearing a horse and daixiao, was painstakingly begging for more money. There were already many onlookers on the shore, accusing the boatmen of admitting money or not admitting anyone. The deceased were also standby workers. The skinny camel was bigger than a horse, and you could run out of money when you arrived at the location. The shipman blushed and argued, “Everyone didn't know. A dozen or so people arrived at the location and got 52 silver, to say nothing, to say nothing. Right now, I also have no food in the warehouse, so I can't afford this amount of money.” After speaking, my eyebrows and nose were wrinkled, and I squatted on the bow of the boat and sighed. The poor man was very angry when he saw this kind of scene. He opened the crowd and jumped on the boat and shouted, “Don't be afraid, my old man has already delivered this 32 silver.” Speaking of taking the money out of her arms and giving it to the crying little girl, the girl took the silver with both hands and asked the caretaker, “Is your family name your first name?” The person sent an answer: “Wu Tang, the mayor of Qinghe County, Mr. Wu.” Is this Mr. Wu Tang, who was waiting to work in Anqing a few years ago?” Someone nodded: “Exactly.” The girl was grateful and said, “I heard my dad say that Master Wu helped my family a few years ago, and now we are in trouble. Mr. Wu is sending money again. Magnolia will one day be well rewarded, and the Yehenara clan will always remember this great benefactor.
I had to wait a long time for the boat to go before returning home to report Wu Tang. Wu Tang asked, “Did the adults on board say anything after picking up the bank?” Inhumane: “It was a girl who picked up the money.” Wu Tang was shocked and thought to herself that she hadn't seen any girls on board. At first sight, Mr. Wu was taken aback. He hurriedly held a standoff between the shipowner and his employer over the money for the ship when the money was delivered. The adult lifted the siege and waited 150 for detailed reporting. The more Wu Tang listened, the more confused she became. She decided that the money had been sent by mistake. Wu Tang got angry and scolded, “It's really useless. I sent it by mistake! Can't you remember which funeral ship it was? How far has it gone? Can we catch up?” I was so frightened by this questioning that he knelt down and stammered on the ground with a soft “thud” and stammered, “Well... that girl's name... is Magnolia, and what leaf... what kind of clan is Yehra. They also said that adults helped her when they were in Anqing... their family.” As soon as Wu Tang heard it, she said in her heart, “Can Huizheng pass away? Almost, I heard that he has a daughter named Yulan, whose last name is Ye Henara.” I was in a hurry and got sweaty and said, “My lord, the ship was dead in less than two hours. If you want to catch it, you can still drive it up.” Master Wu Tang shook his head and said, “Forget it, forget it!”
It turned out that Wu Tang's friend and friend were desperate, and they set sail as soon as the sky dawned. Sending someone to deliver silver to the riverside happened to be met by another funeral boat parked on the shore, and the silver was delivered by mistake. It was also due to Wu Tang's ingenuity in sending the wrong money. In the future, Wu Tang was able to take the lead and become governor, because the girl who took the money was not someone else; it was the famous Empress Dowager Cixi who hid the sky in the future.
(2) Drinking alcohol causes bad breath
Wu Tang is an upright person, clean and honest. He often said, “You must not lose your duties as an official, you must not lose your job, be diligent in government affairs, and act for the people.” Qinghe County was treated as a “hideaway” by him. In the dark official arena, Wu Tang was constantly bombarded with open fire and dark arrows. Once, some corrupt local officials falsely accused Wu Tang of “eating cao and swallowing”, charged them, and went to Beijing to jointly file a lawsuit. At the time, some officials in various departments and offices in Beijing were highly qualified but had no real jobs; they relied on local officials to confess cases. When they met owners with poop butts, they were able to get back one thousand two hundred and two hundred silver at a time. Sometimes you work hard, aren't you trying to take someone down? If you don't buy a few hundred or two pieces of silver, the light ones ask you to roll up and go home; the heavy ones ask you to move with your head. Mr. Yang, the governor of Qinghe Caoyun at the time, was Wu Tang's friend. He was very good-natured and wanted to sell thin noodles in front of Lord Chincha to break the siege for Wu Tang. Wu Tang thinks that she is in good shape and is not afraid of being shadowed. There is no corruption, and the other is no extortion. How can money be used as a bribe?
