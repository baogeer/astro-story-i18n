 “Sinkfish” tells the story of Shih Tzu. During the Spring and Autumn Warring States period, Wu Yue fought. Wu Guobing strengthened Ma Zhuang, quickly defeated Vietnam, and took Yue Wang Goujian and Prime Minister Fan Li as hostages.
In order to avenge the destruction of the country, Yue Wang temporarily took a stand under Wu Wang's lap and pretended to be very honest and loyal.
Once Wu had a stomach ache, he didn't notice any illness when he invited Lang Zhong. When Yue Wang Goujian learned about it, he personally tasted Wu's feces in front of his husband and said, “Dawang isn't sick; it's fine to drink hot wine while warm.” As Wu Wangzhao said, after drinking some hot wine, it was fine.
When Wu Wang saw Gou Jian's loyalty, he was released back to Vietnam.
After Gou Jian returned home, he accepted Fan Lixian's three plans to restore the country; one was to set up troops and step up martial arts training; the second was to set up fields to develop agriculture; and the third was to select a beautiful woman to give to Wu Wang as an insider.
At the time, there was a woman named Shih Tzu. She had good facial features, pink and peach blossoms, and was very good-looking. When she was on the riverside, the clear river reflected her handsome figure, making him look even more beautiful. At this point, the fish saw her reflection, forgot to swim, and gradually sank to the bottom of the river. Since then, Shih Tzu, a synonym for “sinking fish,” has spread around the neighborhood.
After Shih Tzu was selected and sent to Wu Guo, Wu Wang saw that Shih Tzu looked so beautiful. He was addicted to amusement all day long, ignoring state affairs, and his national strength was exhausted.
Yue Wang Goujian took advantage of the situation and sent troops to attack Wu Guo, achieving the goal of restoring the country and taking revenge. There was a great deal of credit from Shih Tzu here.
