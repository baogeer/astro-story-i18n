 The Year of the Horse was supposed to be about horses, and those who paint horses should take the lead, Chinese painting master Xu Beihong. Xu Beihong has thoroughly studied the appearance and habits of horses. He has drawn more than a thousand sketches of horses. He was able to express realistic horses using Western painting techniques and Chinese brush and ink. Under his brush, he created an image of a horse with a distinct personality.
Today, however, I'm going to focus on painting horses, Xu Beihong, who painted pigs.
In the art world, there are pigs that are not part of the tradition of painting. Throughout the history of painting, famous paintings of cows, horses, chickens, dogs, tigers, etc. are all popular all over the world, but pig paintings have not spread to this day. Pigs give people a dirty and lazy vision. Although their sense of smell is not bad, it is difficult for a brush to reflect. What's more, some people ask for paintings and draw pigs to give away; isn't that cursing people! There are many reasons, the famous painting Pig, none.
Despite this, not everyone has ever drawn pigs in the art world. Xu Beihong, a famous master of Chinese painting, has painted pigs 3 times.
Xu Beihong's first picture of a black pig came out in the middle of the winter of 1934. At the time, “China Times - Picture Weekly” asked Xu Beihong to draw a picture of a pig for the upcoming Year of the Pig. Beihong graciously agreed. Soon, Beihong drew with a large brush of light ink and a fine pen, and also painted the back of the pig's head with a large ink jet and pencil, drawing a black pig walking from the opposite side. At the time of writing, Beihong faced this painting, and after thinking for a while, he also wrote the eight characters “Beihong paints pigs, strange story” on the painting. He immediately signed the “Year of Ehai, Written by Beihong” and sealed a round “Xu” seal.
It didn't take long, and Master Beihong painted the second ink pig. The composition and pen and ink of this painting are essentially similar to the first one. If I had to say anything different, I wrote a wonderful poem in the upper right corner of the second ink pig painting: “Young children have stabbed their thighs, and they don't walk away in vain. “The gods are invincible, and paint ink pigs in the scent of bathing.”
About 10 years after the first ink pig painting came out, Master Beihong painted the 3rd ink pig painting when he returned from Guiyang to Chongqing. This painting is a standing picture of an inkjet cloud mountain. On a shaded road with a close-up view of the bottom, there are two drumsticks carrying a big fat pig with a slide rod. The pig is a black and white flower pig with four legs facing the sky and sleeps steadily on a slide pole. At the top of the painting, Beihong wrote two intriguing poems: “Two people carry a pig; the pig comes from the depths of Baiyun.” The donation was “Spring Festival 32: A Journey Back to Life”. Obviously, this painting and this poem are extremely ironic. Also, as people say, drawing pigs is meant to curse people. At the time, the phenomenon in the painting was often seen in many scenic spots.
Unfortunately, the first picture of the ink pig made by Mr. Xu Beihong has been lost. Thankfully, the second picture of the pig and the third pig are now stored in the “Xu Beihong Memorial Hall” in Beijing, and published in the “Xu Beihong Color and Ink Paintings” collection.
