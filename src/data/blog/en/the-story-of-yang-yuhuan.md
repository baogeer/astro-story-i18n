 Yang Yuhuan (719-756), a native of Yongle, Puzhou (present-day Yongji, Shanxi), daughter of Yang Xuanyan, the owner of Shuzhou. The noble concubine of Tang Xuanzong and Lee Yong-gi.
Yang has a rich posture, good singing and dancing, and vocal rhythm. In 734 (the 22nd year of Tang Xuanzong's founding), Na was the queen of Xuanzong's 18th son, Shou Wang Li Mao. Yang was 16 years old, and Li Mao was also about 16 years old. In 737, Empress Wu Huifei, who was loved by Xuanzong, died, and there were thousands of gongs in the harem, none of which satisfied Xuanzong. To please Xuanzong, Collix recommended Princess Yang Yuhuan to Xuanzong. In 740, Xuanzongxing Hot Spring Palace summoned the Yang clan to the Palace of Prince Collix and made them go out of his family. He took the name Taizhen and lived in Taizhen Palace. In 745, Zuo Weizhonglang also established Wei Zhaoxun's daughter as Princess Shou, and Xuanzong canonized Yang as his royal wife. “The father took his child and his wife,” which became a major mystery in the Tang dynasty.
Xuanzong personally wrote “The Neon Harlequin”. When he summoned Yang Guifei, he made the musician play this new song, gave Yang a golden hairdo, and personally inserted it into Yang's hair. Xuanzong said to the people in the Harem, “We have Queen Yang, if we have a treasure” (“Secrets of the Ancient and Modern Palace” vol. 3), replicating the new song “Debaozi” is enough to show how lucky he is. When there was no new empress in the palace, everyone in the palace called Yang a “maiden,” and she actually took a backseat. Zheng Chuyi told a story, saying that after Yang Yuhuan was promoted to be a royal princess, there was a white parrot in Lingnan that could imitate human language. Xuanzong and Yang Guifei really liked it. They called it “Snow Flower Girl,” while the left and right in the palace called it “Snow Flower Lady.” Xuanzong's commandment is taught in psalms. After a few times, this white parrot can sing praises, making people love it. Every time Xuanzong played chess with Yang Guifei, if the situation was not favorable to Xuanzong, the attendant's lieutenant called “Snow Flower Girl,” and the parrot flew into the board and clapped its wings, “to disrupt the queue or peck the hands of the kings, making it impossible to argue.” (“Miscellaneous Records of the Ming Emperor”) Later, this lovely “Snow Flower Girl” was pecked to death by an eagle. Xuanzong and Yang Guifei were so sad that they buried it in the Imperial Garden, called the “Parrot Mound.” Yuan dynasty poet Yang Weizhen's “An Untitled, Hidden Poem for Business”: “Jin Li recently took Qing Haijun, and the snowsuit girl first appeared in Jinlong.” (“Iron Cliff Collection”) is Yong, Xuanzong, and Yang Guifei's pet white parrot. Xuanzong still cherishes his pet white parrot, and his love for Yang Guifei goes without saying.
Yang Guifei has three sisters, all of whom are national colors. They are also called to the Palace. They are named as Mrs. Korea, Mrs. Guo, and Mrs. Qin Guo, and they each receive 100,000 dollars of fat powder every month. Mrs. Guo Guo is ranked third. She is naturally beautiful and has no fake fat powder. Du Fu's “Mrs. Guo Guo” Shiyun: “Mrs. Guo Guo took the lead and went straight to the gate. But they don't like the color fat-pink, so they blush their eyebrows and go to supreme.” (“Detailed Notes on Du Shi” vol. 2) is a reflection of the facts.
Due to Yang Guifei's popularity, her brothers all gifted senior officials, and even her distant brother Yang Zhao, who was originally a scoundrel. Due to good planning, Xuanzong and the Yang sisters gambled, causing Yang Zhao to calculate gambling accounts, give him the name Guo Zhong, and manipulate the government of the DPRK. Xuanzong traveled to Huaqingchi, and used the Yang family as his henchmen. Each team wore one color, and the five families joined in a variety of colors. Jewellery was dropped all over the place along the way, shining brightly, and its luxury was beyond compare. The Yang family married two princesses and two county governors. Xuanzong also personally wrote a temple monument for the Yang family.
At one point, Yang Guifei was proud of her arrogance, offended Xuanzong, and was condemned by Xuanzong to her mother's family. However, after the royal wife left the palace, Xuanzong didn't eat well, and Gao Litu had to bring her back. In 750, the royal queen stole the purple jade flute from Jugoro (split) and played it for herself. As a result of the incident, they were sent out of the palace again due to disobedience. After leaving the palace, a drop of blue silk was cut off, and Zhang Taoguang was brought to Xuanzong. Xuan Zong was shocked, and Collix also recalled her. Zhang Hu's “Dividing Wang Xiaoguan” Shi Yun: “Fortunately, no one saw Jin Yi. He secretly blew up the Fen Wang Xiaoguan.” (“Midnight Tang Poetry Collection” vol. 5) praises this. Yang Guifei knows that Xuanzong doesn't have her, and is even more arrogant. The Yang family “doesn't ask for entry and exit; the Beijing division chief looks on him.” There is a saying, “Don't be sad when you have a girl, don't be happy when you have a boy”. (“Yang Taizhen: The Legend of Yang Taizhen)) Li Zhao said, “Yang Guifei was born in Shu and loves lychee. Born in the South China Sea, they are better off than those from Shu, so they advance by leaps and bounds every year.” (“Supplementary History of Tang” volume) Du Mu's “Crossing the Huaqing Palace” Shi Yun:
Changan looked back at Xiuchengdui, and the top of the mountain soon opened at the top of the mountain.
As soon as I rode the Red Dust Feizi Pass, no one knew it was Lychee
(“Mid-Evening Tang Poetry Collection” vol. 6)
This is the case of Yongling Nan Gong Lai. Later generations of Lingnan lychee had a “concubine smile,” and it is said that it was named after this.
In the middle of Tianbao's middle age, Fan Yang made Mount An Lushan cross the border, and was favored by Xuanzong, making the Yang sisters brother and sister with Lushan, while Yang Guifei recognized Lushan as her son. Under the name of entering the palace to meet Gan Niang, Lushan blatantly provoked Yang Guifei to come.
Yang Guifei celebrated her last birthday in Chang'an. On June 1, 755, at Huaqing Palace, Xuan Zong had fun in Liyuan and played a new song at Changsheng Temple. There was no title for the song. It was suitable for lychee to arrive in the South China Sea, so she named the song “Lychee Fragrance”. In November of the same year, an Lushan rebels and Xuanzong hurriedly entered the river. The following year, he passed through Ma Yi Station (west of present-day Xingping County, Shaanxi Province). The military mutinied and forced Xuanzong to kill Yang Guizhong and gave Queen Yang Guifei to commit suicide at the age of 38. Bai Juyi's “Song of Eternity” is a tragic story about Xuanzong and the Imperial Consort.
Yang Guifei's poetry, “All Tang Poems” includes her song “Giving Zhang Yunrong Dance”:
Luo Shou was full of fragrance, in the autumn smoke of the Red Sow.
The wind shook at Qingyun Ridge, and the water first blew by the tender willow pond.
This is a dance style written by a woman. Compared to the autumn smoke, it is vaguely visible; it is even more like a willow swirling in the water, graceful and gentle, and lined with a cropped sleeve, which is amazing. There are many stories reflecting Yang Guifei in her poetry. Du Mu's “Passing the Huaqing Palace Excerpt” said:
Xinfeng's green trees started yellow dust and drove back to Yuyang several times.
The neon song A Thousand Peaks comes to an original state in the middle of the dance.
(“Mid-Evening Tang Poetry Collection” vol. 6)
It's just that Queen Yong Guifei let go. As for the three songs in Li Bai's “Qingping Diary”, the famous phrase “The Cloud Wants Clothes, Flowers, and the Spring Breeze Blows Down a Journey to China” (“The Complete Works of Li Taibai” vol. 5) has become a masterpiece song throughout the ages. After the death of the noblewoman, Xuanzong's native Shu, “walked to Fufeng Road... and then to Shiegukou. It was Linyu involved. It heard a bell in the rain on the boardwalk, which was right across the mountain. “Shangji paid tribute to the Imperial Consort because of her voice as “Yu Lin's Bell Song”.” (“The Legend of Yang Taizhen”) This is the origin of the later Song lyric “Yu Lin Ling”.
There are more stories of Yang Guifei in dramas. Bai Pu of the Yuan dynasty wrote the mixed drama “Tang Ming Dynasty: An Autumn Night with Sycamore”; the Ming dynasty had the legend of Tu Longlong's “Scary Chronicles”, the legend of Wu Shimei's “Scary from Dust”; the Qing dynasty had Hong Sheng's “The Legend of Changsheng”; “The Drunken Princess”, “Taizhen Gaiden”, “Ma Yupo”, etc. The list is endless. In particular, the Peking opera “The Princess Is Drunken” starring Mei Lanfang is famous at home and abroad for her ingenuity and outstanding acting skills. Novels include “The Legend of Yang Taizhen,” Chen Hong's “The Legend of Song of Eternity,” and “Sui and Tang Romance.”
Today, there is the tomb of Yang Guifei in Xingping County, Shaanxi, covering an area of 3,000 square meters. There are monuments of poetry from the past, including Li Shangyin, Bai Juyi, and Lin Zexu on the side of the tomb. At the northern foot of Lishan Mountain in Lintong, there is Huaqing Pool, which is said to be the site of Yang Guifei's “Spring Cold Bath Huaqing Pool, Hot Spring Water Scrubs Gel”. Among them, “Guifei Pool” is known as Yang Guifei's exclusive bath, so it is also known as “Fei Zi Tang”. There is a “gazebo” on the pool side, which is said to be a place for nobles to cool down and brush their hair. Because of their close relationship with Yang Guifei, a famous ancient beauty, these places of interest have attracted many Chinese and foreign tourists, and have become famous tourist destinations.
