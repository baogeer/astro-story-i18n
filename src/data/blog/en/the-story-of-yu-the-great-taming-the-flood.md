 In ancient times, floods raged, and people were often affected by floods. When Emperor Yao was in power, another terrible flood disaster occurred: the earth was covered in a vast ocean, houses were destroyed, fields were flooded, and dead piles were piled up; people helped the elderly and young, and fled everywhere. When Yao saw that the people were suffering so much, he felt worried and uneasy, so he sent an agent to control the floods. He led everyone to control the water, using the method of blocking the blockage. As a result, they blocked it back and forth. This side blocked it, and the other side broke down again. The laborers lost their wealth for 9 years, and nothing was achieved.
Yao Huishun became the leader of the tribe. He saw that the water management had not improved. He delayed the matter, so he ordered the execution of Lu, and also ordered Yu, the son of Yu, to take care of the water. Yu knows that water management matters are very important, and if it is not treated well, he will lose his net worth and his life. However, seeing that the floods were raging and people were suffering unspeakably, Yu bravely accepted this heavy responsibility.
Yu carefully summed up the lessons of her father's failure, and felt that the method of blocking would not work, so he boldly conceived a water treatment plan that was contrary to her father's — unclog the river, follow the flow, and drive the water away.
After the plan was drawn up, Yu decided to travel all over the world, check out the terrain, discover rivers, and divert water downstream. He led a large number of assistants to the nine flood-affected states, measured the elevation of the site, and erected separate wooden stakes as markers. At that time, most places were deserted and uninhabited. Yu Feng ate and slept outdoors, often braving heavy wind and rain, and running in harsh environments.
Then, based on the data obtained from the survey, Yu began flood control in the area most affected by the flood. Together with everyone, Yu used rudimentary tools such as stone axes and stone knives to dig rivers and build embankments. His hands are covered with calluses, the soles of his feet are covered with foot pads, and his face is often unkempt. Since he had been soaked in water for many years, all his toenails fell off, and people were moved to tears when they saw it.
In order to fix the water, Yu was not married until the age of 30. Later, he married a girl named Nu Jiao. Just 4 days after the marriage, he bid farewell to his newlywed wife and was busy treating the water.
Daewoo treated the flood for 13 years, and even though he passed through his house 3 times, he didn't even bother to go in and take a look. Passing through his doorstep for the first time, he heard that his newborn son was crying, and his wife was also groaning due to the pain of childbirth. His assistants all advised him to go in and take a look. Yu also wanted to go in and take a look, but he had important things to do. He was afraid of delaying his work, so he just didn't go in. When I passed through the house for the second time, Yu's son was already able to call his dad. The little one shouted hard at his dad in his mom's arms, and Woo just waved affectionately to his wife and children and walked away. The third time, when Yu passed through the house, his son was already in his teens. He ran over to pull his dad home. Yu caressed his son's head and told his son to tell his mom, wait until the water had healed before reuniting, then left in a hurry.
After 13 years, Yu went through many hardships and risks, opened a ditch and built a canal, and finally overcame the flood disaster, promoted agricultural development, and enabled the people to live and work peacefully. Yu was named the heir to the throne by Shunli and became the first monarch of the Xia dynasty because of his success in managing water, so historically, he was known as Xia Yu or Dayu.
After Dayu became the leader of the tribal alliance, he was still not greedy for pleasure. He worked tirelessly to visit various places and did many useful things for the people.
