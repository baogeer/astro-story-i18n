 “Fallen Goose” is the story of Zhaojun leaving the village. During the reign of Emperor Han Yuan, the North and South had to fight, and the border must not be quiet. In order to appease Northern Hungary, Emperor Han Yuan chose Zhaojun and formed a marriage with Shan Yu to ensure that the two countries would always be at peace.
On a cool autumn day, Zhaojun bid farewell to his hometown and set sail north.
Along the way, Ma Xiyan shattered her heart; her sense of sadness made her feel uneasy.
On top of her mount, she stirred the strings and played a sad parting song. The giant geese flying from the south heard this pleasant sound of the piano and saw this beautiful woman on horseback. She forgot to swing her wings and fell to the ground.
Since then, Zhaojun has been nicknamed “Fallen Geese.”
