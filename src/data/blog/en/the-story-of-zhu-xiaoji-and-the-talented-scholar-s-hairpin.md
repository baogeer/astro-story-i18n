 The moon set and the sky was full of frost, and Jiang Feng was fearful and sleepy.
At Hanshan Temple outside Gusu City, the bell rang to the passenger ship in the middle of the night.
This poem made Gusu City very famous in history. The author of this poem is Zhang Ji. During the years of Emperor Yuan Shun, another talented woman, Zhu Xiaoji, appeared in Gusu City. Her talent and charisma added another good story to Gusu.
An affectionate merchant family lives on the side of Canglang Pavilion in Gusu. The owner travels between Suzhou and Wanluo to sell groceries from North and South; the wife lives a life of calling slaves and wearing golden clothes and jade food. One day, the Zhu family was taking a nap. Suddenly, she dreamed that a god had thrown a rhinoceros in her arms, so she became pregnant and gave birth to a girl, named Xisheng. She is later Zhu Xiaoji. Zhu Xiaoji has been smart since childhood. She can read characters when she was four years old. The Zhu family even hired someone to teach her. She had a certain foundation for quite a few years. Neighbors all said, “The daughter of the Zhu family is extremely smart, and her future will definitely be limitless.”
During the time of Emperor Yuanshun, the world had become menacing, and thieves revolted everywhere. Father Zhu never went back on his way to do business. This year, Zhu Xiaoji was ten years old. The village was heavily looted. The mother and daughter of the Zhu family were bullied. Finally, under the extortion of the family and the powerful, she quickly fell into her family, making it difficult to maintain her livelihood. After living under the fence, Zhu Xiaoji's mother relentlessly sold her to Yu Family, a wealthy family in Gusu, and changed her name to Yu Kui. This year Zhu Xiaoji was 12 years old.
The Yu family is a poet from Gusu. It can be said that they have no contacts with Bai Ding; they talk and laugh, and Hongru. Famous people from near and far love to stay in the Yu family's garden, Panhuan. Gradually, due to Grandma Yu's old age and poor health, a few of her sons were unable to overcome their skills; instead, Jiaji Yu Kwai replaced them with poetry and literature from famous people. Zhu Xiaoji is a girl with a personality. Her poems are all signed by Zhu Xiaoji, and she doesn't like to pretend to be Yu Kui. Most of these poems are written on raccoon paper. The handwriting is delicate and the meaning is clear. At this point, Zhu Xiaoji was already very charming and had a snowy face. Unfortunately, none of the Yu family's sons were impressed by Zhu Xiaoji. Zhu Xiaoji, who had already settled down her style, often used to burn incense drums and harp behind closed doors and prayed for the sound of a phoenix for Ai Feng. No one was amazed when they heard it. Some of the poems she left behind at this time all showed her this kind of feeling.
Luojitang and Li Shuishui patted the embankment, and feared the fans;
Ruthless is the bird on the tree; it doesn't matter how sad people are, it just chirps.

Isn't there someone Zhu Xiaoji doesn't like at all? No, at least one person is deeply engraved in Zhu Xiaoji's heart and cannot be forgotten. This person is Zheng Hanqing, who has traveled to Jiangnan, visited Yu's family a few times, and has several relationships with Zhu Xiaoji.
Zheng Hanqing, “Ghost Recorded Book,” said that he once “used Confucius as an aid to the Hangzhou Road Officer. He was a direct person and didn't want to socialize with people. He is famous all over the world, and is known as Mr. Zheng Lao by the smart generation, and everyone knows him as Dehui.” He is the most famous talent at the end of the Yuan Dynasty. He wrote 18 kinds of miscellaneous dramas such as “Fu Nu Goes Away”. He wrote that the girl is nostalgic for her lover. It is so gentle, gentle, beautiful and moving:
I'm most concerned about devil sickness. It's like being addicted to alcohol and sleeping in spring. On the outside of the carved eaves, the sun is low, and colorful clouds are flying along the side of the painting building. The twelve railings stand, and the railings lie outside the sky. Naturally, such an emotional man is favored by ladies, not to mention a talented girl like Zhu Xiaoji.
At the beginning, amidst the turbulent world situation at the end of the Yuan Dynasty, Jiangnan was still able to maintain an unruly situation, but gradually Gusu also smelled chaos and flames, and Zhu Xiaoji moved to Wulin (present-day Hangzhou) with the Yu family. In late spring and March, grass grows in Jiangnan, mixed peanut trees, and warblers fly in droves. Zhu Xiaoji often sits in a painting house, wandering around the beautiful lake and shuttling through the lotus leaves in the fields. One day, Zhu Xiaoji suddenly saw someone in the green shade of the embankment waving to her frequently. She came close and took a close look. It was actually her lover that she was concerned about day and night. It was very kind to meet again in a different place. The completely unfamiliar and fresh environment completely dismantled the concerns and barriers of the past. They smiled cheerfully and said goodbye to each other warmly. That night, Zhu Xiaoji accompanied Zheng Hanqing back to Hanzhuang in Seoling, where he lived. As evidenced by the bright moon, the two actually developed in an unusual, unexpected, and crazy way. As Chen Boru, who can clearly see it on the sidelines, said in a poem given to Zhu Xiaoji:
When I met Kang Dao, I didn't get carried away; I stole people and left the song untuned;

At this point, the old lady of the Yu family had passed away. Zhu Xiaoji didn't seem to be much restrained by the second generation of the Yu family. In addition, when she first arrived in Hangzhou, the Yu family was unfamiliar, and the Yu family also lost their former momentum and lineup. Zhu Xiaoji simply greeted the Yu family and stayed in Hanzhuang, Xiling for over a month. Zheng Hanqing is compassionate, and Zhu Xiaoji is even more tender. Zheng Hanqing once gifted a rhinoceros whip. After seeing it, Zhu Xiaoji was amazed: “My mother is dreaming, the two of us are in trouble. Could this be God's will?”
Zhu Xiaoji impressed Zheng Hanqing with her “Tianyi,” which is also her ingenuity. Seeing that this was already the case, Zheng Hanqing paid a lot of money to hire the Yu family. Zhu Xiaoji was separated from the Yu family in the form of redemption and officially became the Zheng family's daughter-in-law. She took off her gorgeous costume, took care of the housework herself, sang with her husband on the shores of Xizi Lake, and lived a happy life on earth.
