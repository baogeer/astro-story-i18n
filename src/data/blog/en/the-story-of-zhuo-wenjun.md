 That year, Zhuo Wenjun was 17 years old. She was fair, charming, talented, and good at playing the piano. Unfortunately, her fate didn't go well. Heaven made a big joke with her. She died at a young age, her unhired husband died, and she became a famous widow. She has a beautiful old age and is confined to a lonely girlfriend, but her heart is like a peach blossom blooming in March, and she shines brightly on a spring day. Sima's appearance, like the silent spring rain, suddenly sparked the flower buds of her love.
Once Zhuo Wenjun's father Zhuo Wang Sun invited guests to a banquet, the personable Sima Xiang came and brought his song “Phoenix Seeks the Phoenix”: Phoenix Wings Back to Her Hometown and Traveled All Seas to Seek the Phoenix. No time has met, nothing can be done, so now it's time to get away with Sheng Shi Tang! There was a gorgeous lady in the boudoir, and the room was full of poison. He Yuan crossed the neck with a mandarin duck, and Hu Jiexi soared together!
Sima's bold and straightforward confession suddenly ignited Zhuo Wenjun's passion for love. She has the appearance of Zhu Yan Yu. He is extremely poetic. The two talented and beautiful people met and instantly collided in a lightning-fast love.
She fell in love with him at first sight. He is in love with her, and she is committed to him. The two were very happy with each other, but they were strongly opposed by Father Zhuo. Why would Father Zhuo marry the flower Qianjin to a poor scholar? Helplessly, Zhuo Wenjun had to run with Sima at night.
When you're in love, love is a drink to quench your thirst. Even if you get poisoned, you have to drink it in the most beautiful way. When you're in love, no matter how high the mountains are and the path is deep, you have to follow him closely, even if you walk the path of Tianya. Just like that, Zhuo Wenjun gave up on his wealthy family and left with poor students.
Sima is like a family member with four walls. Zhuo Wenjun had to let go of Miss Qianjin and make a living selling alcohol. Sima was like doing miscellaneous goods. Thinking back then, she sold alcohol to accompany him and keep each other together. As long as they were together, they didn't envy God or immortal; the couple were affectionate and sweet. Thinking back then, the two shared hardships; they should have been happy and happy.
Zhuo Wenjun's small hotel is not far from his hometown in Linqiong. How dare Zhuo Wang Sun, who wants to make his daughter feel wronged and make people gossip? In desperation, Father Zhuo had to recognize Sima Xiangru as his son-in-law and distribute the gold and silver assets to the young couple.
Zhuo Wenjun and Sima are similar in their original love story. It's like a classic legend, making many people stunned and amazed! A strange woman from ancient times, with her beauty, poetic talent, and courage, made me feel deeply ashamed thousands of years later.
When we're in love, everyone thinks they have a lot of love in their hands. It's like DiDi Ganlin sprinkled from heaven; it always moisturizes and delights our hearts. In this way, the two are happy. They look at each other every day, are dependent on each other, and can hold hands and grow old with their children. However, after experiencing the vicissitudes of love, the people they were in love with broke up. How impenetrable are people's hearts! Feelings are also so fragile!
How easy is love when it comes to money, power, and status! It's as light as a strange flower; if you don't notice it, it will be destroyed by the storm; as light as a cloud in the air, it will be scattered by the breeze before you know it.
How did Zhuo Wenjun feel when he learned that Sima was like a concubine? Her heart must have been broken like a grain of sand, burning in the hot sun. Ruthlessness is not like much suffering; an inch is still tens of thousands of threads. An inch of tenderness in the intestines and tears all turned into heartfelt pain. Some people say that marriage is the tomb of love; does marriage really bury the legendary romantic love between Zhuo Wenjun and Sima?
After getting married to Zhuo Wenjun, Sima Xiangru became famous all over the world with his words. His official fortune flourished, and fame and fortune followed. He's no longer the shockingly poor scholar back then. He became famous. He was crowned Zhonglang General in Chang'an City. His wings are strong and he is famous. He has forgotten his former sea and mountain alliance, and he has forgotten his past love. What are those, but just a game and a dream.
He forgot all about it and wanted to throw it into the ocean, so he wanted to leave his wife and take a concubine.
Sima Xiangru said it bluntly. He sent someone to send it to Zhuo Wenjun. The content of the letter was “1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 90 million.” He implied his thoughts in the numbers. Of the 13 numbers, only one word “100 million” (memory) was missing. Wouldn't that be an “unintentional” hint? How could the clever Zhuo Wenjun not understand? After reading the letter, she burst into tears and felt as cool as water. With a feeling of great grief, she wrote back to him with a pen and wrote the song “The Bad Man's Poem.”
After saying goodbye,
The two places are intertwined,
Let's just say it's March and April,
Who knows another five or six years.

There is nothing to pass on in the Eight-Line Book.

One, two, three, four, six, seven, eight, 90 million, looped back and forth, and full of sorrow. The two parts of the poem, from ten thousand to ten thousand, from ten thousand to one, closely reflect the thirteen numbers in Sima Xiangru's letter, and vividly write the acacia that one wants to wear after separation, the helplessness that goes back and forth, and the sad feeling of loneliness that one is cold and cold. And the last sentence, “I can't help but come for a woman, I am a man” is not only a humble complaint about Sima, but also a powerful accusation of the male power society.
When Zhuo Wenjun wrote this poem, what kind of liver damage should he have done? The sound of the piano in the past was pleasant, and once abandoned and ran away from home. The love and love that used to be, the yin, yin, and gorgeous, all became colorful clouds under the sunset and passed away with the wind. Only a cold heart fights quietly in the twilight.
There is a poem that says, “I have poor green sides, just to love myself for old age.” But who can keep the beautiful scenery of love? Who can never get old for love? How difficult it was for men in the old days to do this!
They all say that obsessive women lose heart. How did Sima Xiangru feel when he saw this poem? A famous young lady and a talented scholar played out a legendary romantic romance through the ages, yet he still lost her. He already has someone new by his side. Spoiled love is like stewed stinky tofu. Although it has a different flavor, he doesn't like it anymore.
If it were a false statement from parents, if the marriage went smoothly, maybe being abandoned would not be so painful. But after all, she paid too much for him. She is heartbroken and still somewhat obsessed with love. She's not happy!
Zhuo Wenjun's admiration is not only her courage to elope and her extraordinary talent; what is even more invaluable is her determination to turn around in the face of loss. Her “Bald Head Gin” is proof of this.
Covered like snow on a mountain, as clear as the moon in the clouds.

This poem is written with equal emphasis on strength and softness. It has both the tenderness of growing old and the courage to turn around. In the poem, Zhuo Wenjun uses Bai Xue and Jiaoyue as a metaphor for the purity and elegance of his personality. Since love is no longer there, let us go our separate ways. Since he is leaving her, she is never sad and desperately begging him to change his mind. The beginning of a romantic passion, a gorgeous and bleak transformation, so much tenderness and tenderness, and so determination!
What she wants is a persistent and beautiful love that “wants to be one person and never separates from each other”.
Wouldn't it be OK for such a smart and strong woman to spend time with others? She just wants him to be hers. How can a woman like this not make Sima so impressed? According to the “Miscellaneous Notes of Xijing”, “Sima Xiangru will hire a Maoling woman as his concubine, and Zhuo Wenjun committed suicide by writing “Baitou Yin”, and so on.”
After reading Zhuo Wenjun's Grievous Letter, Sima Xiangru couldn't help but marvel at his wife's outstanding talent. He was extremely ashamed. Thinking back in the days, Sima was like playing the qin. Zhuo Wenjun was running at night. The poor couple sold alcohol. They had a lot of emotion and a lot of repentance. Since then, he has stopped talking about his wife and taking concubines.
If you think about it, Zhuo Wenjun is an extremely intelligent woman. She didn't win back a man's heart by crying, two screaming, and hanging three times like some women today. She used her wisdom and poetic talent to keep her husband's love. She managed her love and marriage with all her heart, and finally came to the end.
After experiencing a thousand turns of love, after ups and downs, I finally saw a rainbow. Zhuo Wenjun is a talented woman who has ideas, courage, and dares to love and hate. Throughout her life, she appeared in a gorgeous manner, decided by being abandoned halfway, and left a good story of love by working together to live an old age.
Women are like this for the rest of their lives, so what more can a husband want?
