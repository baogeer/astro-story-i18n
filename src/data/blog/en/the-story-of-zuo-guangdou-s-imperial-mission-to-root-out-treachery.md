 Author: Hong Yinglong
During the Xizong years of the Ming dynasty, the castor Wei Zhongxian and Donglin had no compatibility with each other, and they set up the Dongchang factory to frame Loyalty. There was a bloody storm in the morning hall, and the dark tide was raging. At the time, in the Cao family on the west side of Tianhong Town in Pengze County, there was a person named Cao Rimei who worked as an official in the Ministry of Public Affairs. He was unscrupulously unattached to Wei Kaoru, nor did he rely on the Donglin Party; he just wanted to be a good official.
In Wei Jiren's eyes, ministers all have to stand on the sidelines. If they are not friends, they are enemies, and no one is allowed to swim ashore without getting wet. Therefore, it is not easy to see Cao Ri-mei.
Official Cao Ri-mei has a good voice; Xi Zong has heard of it for a long time. One day, I heard that Tianhong Wushan, was the place where my ancestor, Grandpa Hong Wu, was enlisted in Jiangnan, and was later designated as Zhu Jiafu's hometown. It was a whim to proclaim that Cao Ri-mei ascended to the throne. Xi Zong said, “Cao Aiqing, I didn't expect you to be a Tianhong person. This is the place of Longxing. Tell me the story of Grandpa Hong Wu back then!” Cao Rimei then talked about “Ganzi Bridge,” “Zhu Yuanzhang's Night Visit to Hongru,” and “Wushan Military Training.” These are all popular stories. Cao Rimei lived in Tianhong since she was a child, and she is familiar with it. Xi Zong listened to Long Yan Dayue and ordered the eunuch to give tea. Seeing Xi Zong's joy, Cao Rimei said, “Emperor, Tianhong is a Feng Shui treasure. The mountains have 99 springs, and small village bridges flow through them. Whether it's hot or cold, the four seasons are like spring. Unlike Beijing, the climate is dry and windy. Back then, Poyang Lake triumphed, and Grandpa Hong Wu built the elite army in Wushan, and built a golden dragon palace, only because the Buddha kept his dream and said, “Tiancheng Emperor's house, Buddha will be a monk again, and the Zhu Family's palace is in Stone City.” Only then did Grandpa Hong Wu set foot in Nanjing. Today, the abandoned temple still exists. At what time did you drive south to see the sky red. Not only can you enjoy a different landscape from Beijing, but you can also enjoy the appearance of an old palace.” Xi Zong was overjoyed and said, “Cao Aiqing, your hometown is as good as you said; we will move Beijing over.” Cao Rimei only wanted to please the emperor. Hearing Xi Zong say he wanted to move Beijing over, even though it was a joke, she was shocked and sweaty. As the saying goes, too many lose. Cao Ri-mei's remarks to Xi Zong also laid the foundation for the future.
Cao Rimei was favored by the emperor and was given a gold medal. The eunuchs were not allowed to stop her from entering the palace, and the city gate officials were allowed to leave the city at any time. This annoyed Wei Jiaoren. His ability to look after his family is to compete for favors; he can't let others snore under his bed. Since then, Cao Rimei has been viewed as a thorn in the flesh, a nail in the eye, and quickly pulled out.
Soon, Xi Zong's dragon was unwell. Rumor has it that Wei Jiaoren went up to the temple and said, “Wei Aiqing, I had a dream the night before yesterday that there were two suns in the sky. My one was dim and dull, but the other one was very bright. I was so nervous that I didn't think about tea. Aiqing, what does this mean?”
Wei Jiaoren is famous for his brilliant mind. Remember two years ago, Xi Zong had a strange dream. He was terrified and worried all day long. He went up to the temple and said, “Wei Aiqing, I dream of the sunset, the mountains falling, the sea drying up, and the thunderstorm. What evil sign is this Lord?” As soon as he changed his mind, he said, “Congratulations to the emperor. This is an auspicious sign. At sunset, the emperor's star appeared, the mountains fell flat, the sea dragon showed claws, and the flower was reunited. The minions are about to report that Donggong's wife is already secretly pregnant.”
Xi Zong sounded like a good sign. Donggong was pregnant again. As soon as he was happy, there were no more illnesses. I felt refreshed. Since then, Wei Jiaoren has also become the number one expert in interpreting dreams in the dynasty. This time, the emperor wanted him to interpret his dream again, and I thought it was a good opportunity to get rid of Cao Rimei. So he said, “Emperor, the other sun is fake. That is Thief Star, “Sun Moon Star Cao.” This “Cao” is a star generation; if it becomes the Sun, it is a thief star who commits crime against the Sun. Minions have been watching for a long time; Liburou's central sergeant Rimi is this thief star.” Xi Zong was taken aback and said, “Not long ago, this dog minion scolded us to move the capital to his hometown...”
“Empress, Cao Rimei didn't keep her duty as a courtier and misled the Lord. This was to rebel; this thief should be caught immediately!”
Xi Zong was convinced of Wei Caoren's words and immediately sent Wei Caoren to arrest Cao Ri-mei. Wei Jiren made every effort to get rid of Cao Rimei and didn't want to bear the notoriety of framing Zhong Liang, so he handed this errand to cabinet minister Zuo Guangdou; he needed to do two things with one arrow. Wei Jiren proposed the purpose of Dai Xi, using Zuo Guangdou's instructions to kill rapes.
Zuo Guangdou, a native of Zongyang County, Anhui, was a marshal of Wanli in the 35 years of the Ming dynasty. He is an important member of the Donglin Party. He is an outspoken person and impeached Wei Kaoren many times. Although Zuo Guangdou took the lead, he knew it was a ghost carried out by Wei Jiaoren. He didn't want to see a good minister slaughtered, nor did he dare to disobey the Holy Order. His heart was very conflicted. When I left the palace, I suddenly thought of a solution that had the best of both worlds, and secretly sent Cao Rimei a basket of fresh peaches. By the time Zuo Guangdou summoned officers and soldiers to capture Cao Rimei, Cao Rimei had already left the city overnight. It turns out that on weekdays, Cao Rimei had no contact with Zuo Guangdou. Suddenly, Zuo Guangdou sent someone to send a basket of fresh peaches. He learned that the last visit had caused trouble. Fortunately, he had a gold medal as a gift, called Kaichengmen, and fled to Yunnan with his family.
Cao Rimei fled, and Zuo Guangdou led the officers and soldiers along the way to Jiangxi. The officers and soldiers were used to eating beef and lamb from the north, but by chance they switched to pork from the south. They were not satisfied with the soil and water one by one; most of them were thin. When it arrived red, it was three days later than expected.
The news that Zuo Guangdou planned to commit adultery spread to the Cao family in the west. People in the Cao clan panicked and scolded Cao Rimei for leaving the people behind, leaving the family alone to run for their lives. The rebellion was a major crime against the Jiu Lian family. It appears that the Cao clan is in trouble. Just when everyone couldn't do anything, a daughter-in-law who had just passed through the door said, “Isn't the emperor going to kill someone with the surname Cao? We don't have the last name Cao!” The patriarch said, “Cao's house has a history of hundreds of years. If you don't say the surname Cao overnight, who believes it?” The patriarch's grandmother said, “A dead horse has the power to be a living horse doctor. As long as the officers and soldiers are fooled, they may be able to escape a robbery.”
Everyone said it made sense. A teacher said, “Change your last name to Zhao Hao, Zhao Qian, Sun Li, and your last name is Zhao Weida!” The patriarch said, “The last name is Ouyang. The Ouyang family was crowned by the emperor. Back then, Grandpa Hong Wu gave the couplet 'Go a few thousand miles to Dajiangdong and come to the first family in the west. ' The Cao family and the Ouyang family are often close. We're just pretending to be this 'first home'.” As a result, everyone made a concerted effort to erase and rewrite all the plaques, couplets, and ancestral cards on the ancestral hall. To prevent children's words from being spilled on the open air, all children who can speak are sent back to their grandmother's house. If officers and soldiers don't leave, they are not allowed to return.
On this side, Zuo Guangdou visited the Cao family in the west. From afar, he saw a big flag flying in the wind. “First family in the west in white” was written on it. I know this village's surname is Ouyang. It was once sealed by the emperor, so I don't dare to invent it again. Strangely enough, everyone says its surname is Cao; you can find out if you go to the ancestral place. Officers and soldiers swarmed into the temple and discovered that all of the ancestors had their surname back to Ouyang. Suspecting that I had gone to the wrong village, I continued my search in the direction of the front of the temple. Arriving at Dongjianong Bay, this was the last village. The villagers discovered that the officers and soldiers had all taken refuge in the woods. There was only one cow herder looking out on the side of the road. Zuo Guangdou called to ask a question, and his last name was Ouyang. Zuo Guangdou asked the cow herder: “Baby, is there a village with the surname Cao nearby?” The cow herder said, “Grandpa Guan, the Western Yang family's last name is Cao. Cao is Yang, and Yang is Cao.” Zuo Guangdou saw that it was too late; the officers and soldiers were already exhausted. He decided not to look for it anymore, and returned to Beijing to restore his life. At this point, one of the officers and soldiers, a Jinyi guard sent by Wei Kaoran, suddenly went out of his way and immediately attacked, and asked Zuo Guangdou to lead the officers and soldiers to the west to capture members of the Cao clan. Zuo Guangdou yelled, “How can you believe the kid's words? Haven't you heard that Yang is Cao? This is Cao Rimei's hometown; they all set this village on fire!” Under Zuo Guangdou's order, officers and soldiers all set fire, and dozens of families instantly turned to ashes.
Zuo Guangdou dedicated his efforts to kill the rapes. Not only did he release Cao Rimei, but he also refused to let Zhu Lian be innocent. Even though he ordered the burning of more than a dozen families, ordinary people all counted this account on Wei Jiaoren. History has humiliated him. Till now, Dongjianong Bay is still called Huoshao Bay.
