 During the Yongzheng years of the Qing dynasty, it was a dark and windy late night.
Directly in the study room in the back yard of the Governor's Office, the broken candle fluttered. Governor Tang Zhiyu is still reading books. This directly subordinate governor, who was a member of the Kangxi era, has always been a clean and honest official, and has a very good reputation.
Suddenly, there was a burst of miserable crying from outside the screen. In the middle of a quiet night, it sounded heartbreaking. Tang Zhiyu called his servant to open the door and inspect. As soon as I opened the door and looked in the backyard in fear, I screamed and fell to the ground. Tang Zhiyu shook up his spirit and went out of battle. Under the shadows of the trees at night, I saw a person kneeling eerily. His face was green and red, and his nails were about two inches long. This is clearly a monster! Tang Zhiyu had an earthy face, but then changed his mind: I don't lose my heart; I'm not afraid of a midnight ghost knocking on my door; furthermore, I am a proud member. How can I be afraid of a little ghost in the district? So I tried to calm down and said loudly, “The devil who knelt down, can there be any grievances? Also, speak to the master.” That ghost Youyou said, “My name is Feng Desheng. My family lived in Wuqing County during my lifetime. While doing business, I was killed by strong people. The unscrupulous official of the Wuqing County Commander arrested an innocent citizen, but allowed the real culprit to go unpunished. I look up to them in the name of their youth. I'm bothering you tonight, and I hope they will redress me.” Tang Zhiyu hurriedly asked, “Who is the real culprit?” The devil said 16 words word for word: “A mouth of heaven, a mouth of dirt, a river behind the house, and willows on the side of the house.” End of speech, go over the wall.
Early the next morning, two police officers from Wuqing County sent a prisoner to the Governor's Office to report the murder. Tang Zhiyu was surprised to hear the circumstances of the case. It turned out that this was the murder of Feng Deshan. Remembering last night's misdemeanor, Tang Zhiyu hurriedly asked the sergeant, “Where were the dead killed? quot; The sergeant answered that it was near Liujiazhuang in Wuqing County. Tang Zhiyu also asked, “Can there be a river behind this Liujiazhuang?” The sergeant answered: “Exactly. Tang Zhiyu was shocked and told, “Bring someone named Wu Ji from Liujiazhuang as soon as possible!” Shu Yi listened and meditated: “One mouth of heaven, one mouth of earth, isn't it Wu Ji? I secretly admired Tang Zhiyu's ingenuity.
Furthermore, Wu Ji was arrested and brought to court. After some interrogation, Tang Zhiyu immediately ordered him to be taken to death row. Tang Zhiyu also brought the murderer originally escorted by the police officer to the court and said to him, “This officer has always decided the case impartially; your grievances have now come to an end. I burnt a sheet of paper today to allow the dead spirit of Feng Deshan, who sued you, to file a lawsuit within three days. Can you send a letter to your family for the time being, report safety, and let go when the official has filed a complaint? quot;
Three days later, on the night, the ghost appeared again. Tang Zhiyu took the complaint filed by the devil and suddenly tore it apart and shouted, “Give me the best of this guy!” The garrison, who had been ambushed from all sides, swarmed up and captured the devil on the spot.
After interrogation, the truth came out: Actually, this “ghost” pretended to trick Tang Zhiyu. After killing Feng Deshan, the murderer was apprehended and brought to justice. In order to save his life, he spent a lot of money to buy a thief who was good at flying over the wall, posing as a false soul and blaming others.
The government officials and Xiao Shuyu were both surprised and admired, and asked Tang Zhiyu how they discovered that this “wrong ghost” had been impersonated. Tang Zhiyu laughed, “My boss never believed that ghosts really exist in the world. What's more, I observed that there were obvious footfall marks on the backyard wall. Would ghosts come and go that clumsy? As a result, I calculated it, brought out this “ghost,” and revealed the truth.
The innocent Wu Ji was sent back to Liujiazhuang, and the murderer and flying thieves were severely punished according to law. This story of Tang Zhiyu's plan to solve the “Wrong Ghost” case has been handed down as a good story among the public.
