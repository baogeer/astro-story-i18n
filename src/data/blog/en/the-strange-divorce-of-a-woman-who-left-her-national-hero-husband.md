 Author: Pei Yu
Qi Jiguang, left to history in four words: people, nationality, English, and Xiong
His wife, Wang Shi, also left the four words to history: dare, love, dare, hate
There are two very peculiar women in Chinese history. One is Wen Xiu. She voluntarily divorced and left her husband, Pu Yi, the last emperor of the Qing Dynasty. The story of Wenxiu is well known. There is another woman, who also resolutely divorced her husband, Qi Jiguang, a national hero and famous anti-Japanese general. This woman is “Wang Shi.” Why should she take a break from her long-famous national hero husband?
Speaking of Qi Jiguang's marriage, it's very intriguing. He married at age 13 and married Wang, the “daughter of General Wanhu Nanxi” at age 18. This woman, Wang, is no ordinary woman. She is extremely virtuous and takes great care of her husband. At that time, Qi Jiguang wasn't well known. Her family was very poor. At one point, she cooked fish for dinner and left the fattest part of the fish for her husband Qi Jiguang to eat. What about herself? She only ate fish head and fish tail. Qi Jiguang later learned about this situation. Holding his wife, he was moved to tears!
The Wang clan was not only virtuous, but also brave and resourceful. Qi Jiguang led the army to fight against Japanese vassals in Taizhou. Family members of Wang and “Qi Jiajun” lived in Xinhe, and there were very few guards. At this point, suddenly, a large number of Japanese troops made remote raids and surrounded the small Xinhe City. Everyone inside the city was terrified, because there were too many Japanese people outside the city. Under the critical situation, Wang convinced the officers and soldiers who guarded the city and mobilized the women and children in the city to wear “Qi Jiajun” military uniforms. The square is listed in the city. The Japanese army outside the city, looked up and saw that above the city wall, there were many Chinese soldiers everywhere. The Japanese pirates first thought there was Qi Jiajun's main force in the city, and immediately turned their head and retreat. Wang staged a realistic version of the “Empty City Plan,” which vividly deterred the vicious Japanese.
Later, Qi Jiguang went back to his team a thousand miles and returned to Xinhe. Seeing that the Japanese army had already retreated, this man, who had gone through hundreds of battles, simply couldn't believe his eyes! Since then, Wang's fame has shaken the entire army, and even Beijing knows Wang. This woman, who is both virtuous and gentle, intelligent and brave, Qi Jiguang also deeply admires her brave and resourceful wife!
Wang took care of her in her life and assisted her in her career, and helped her husband Qi Jiguang completely defeat the Japanese and defend the southeast land of China during the Ming Dynasty. Qi Jiguang was then transferred to Jibei by the imperial court to guard the fortress of the Great Wall and became a general that the country and all people depended on. At the time, Qi Jiguang was known as the country's Great Wall of China.
Wang's marriage gradually led to death as Qi Jiguang became a national hero. Wang has a problem: she is infertile and unable to support Qi Jiguang's offspring. As a result, Qi Jiguang adopted a concubine, Shen Shi, when he was 36 years old. Wang was deeply saddened, but he also accepted this fact; after all, he had no children. Who knows that Qi Jiguang doesn't know how to settle down. He is a national hero. Naturally, his life and state of mind are not as good as when he was obscure. In less than a year, he took in another concubine, Chen. At 48, he was also greedy for beauty, and accepted Yang, a third concubine. He completely ignored Wang's feelings and greatly hurt Wang's family!
Wang was unable to bear it. One day, he “practised white sword every day, hope to save less and be willing”, holding a knife and planning to kill the ungrateful Qi Jiguang to relieve his hate. Qi Jiguang understood his losses and took refuge in Tibet. However, the absurdity of his private life did not change at all. In her old age, Wang “took all her savings and returned to the kings”, that is, she voluntarily divorced Qi Jiguang, went back to her mother's family, and relinquished her national hero husband in a dignified manner.
Wang didn't want to sink into a marriage of death and chose to divorce and break up. We can't help but admire the art and courage of Wang's life. She's not a humble woman. She let her husband eat fish body and eat fish head herself, which has been passed down as a beautiful story of history; she is also not a weak woman; she can stage an empty city plan to scare the Japanese army; let alone a little woman, traveling all over the country with her husband leading the army. Nanping the Japanese invaders, and rejecting Mongolia in the North. How can a little woman eat it?
Wang is virtuous, strong, and smart, and has her own self-respect. In her husband's ruthless and absurd family, she finally left her husband who was a national hero and returned home after divorce, which shows that she really “dared to hate and dare to love”!
