 Zhou Daoru has been a full-time tutor in Yuan Shikai's family for more than ten years. The relentless years have eroded her splendid years. In the three years of proclamation and unification, a wave of revolution surged, and the Qing government was struggling to find a way. When Yuan Shikai took the throne, Zhou Daoru was 36 years old and still kept his word. Even Yuan Shikai, a great hero with a heart of tigers and wolves, felt that he owed too much to her.
Zhou Daoru is a native of Yixing, Jiangsu. Her father unfortunately died early after reaching the top spot in the academic exam. As soon as she graduated from Beiyang Women's College, where she studied at Lübicheng, she stayed at an affiliated elementary school to help her family with her meager salary.
Lu Bicheng has a special appreciation for Zhou Daoru, a student who believes that she has pure character, is knowledgeable, and has good manners. Zhou Daoru works hard as a teacher at an attached elementary school. Lu Bicheng felt that Zhou Daoru was being overpowered and undertreated. It just so happened that Yuan Shikai, who was nominated as governor at the time, wanted to hire a tutor, so Lu Bicheng recommended Zhou Daoru to Yuan Shikai. The Yuan family's children, daughter, daughter-in-law, and even Yuan Shikai's Miaoling concubines all became Zhou Daoru's students. More than ten years have passed, and they get along well with each other and are as close as family. In particular, Yuan Kewen, the second-youngest grandpa of the Yuan family, also known as Yuan Hanyun, had the most affinity with Zhou Daoru. That year, Yuan Shikai was suspected and ostracized by the Qing government. He resigned from the imperial court and went home to take care of his illness on the grounds that “he is currently suffering from a foot disease and is struggling to walk.” The Qing government was forced to leave the imperial court as soon as possible and granted immediate permission for Yuan Shikai to return to Yin Huan. Zhou Daoru is preparing to resign from Yuan Shikai. It was Yuan Kewen who took a group of younger siblings and painstakingly retained them, so that Zhou Daoru couldn't bear to leave them behind and go to Huan with them. Yuan Shikai, who landed in Pingyang, was extremely grateful to Zhou Daoru. Zhou Daoru is a person that everyone in the Yuan family respects.
Back then, Yuan Shikai trained the new army at the Tianjin Station. The four most powerful subordinates were Wang Shizhen, Duan Qirui, Feng Guozhang, and Liang Huadian. Wang Shizhen is in charge of the engineer, Duan Qirui is in charge of the artillery, Feng Guozhang is in charge of the infantry, and Liang Huadian is in charge of the cavalry. Later, Liang Huadian drowned during night exercises. By the time Yuan Shikai was promoted to the post of Minister of Beiyang, Wang, Duan, and Feng had risen in height, there was the saying “Three Great Dragons, Tigers, and Dogs of Beiyang.”
Yuan Shikai stole the fruits of the 1911 Revolution. After becoming president, he began to dream of being an emperor. Helplessly, some of the old Beiyang people showed great indifference. Feng Guozhang, in particular, is sitting in Nanjing, with a heavy army in his hands. Although he is a pillar in the southeast, it is indeed difficult for Yuan Shikai to reassure him. He is deeply afraid that these brave soldiers will be used by others or that they will not lose their way.
Zhou Daoru has been in the Yuan family for more than ten years. The Yuan family's children have all grown up and started families and businesses. The Yuan family's daughter-in-law has also become Zhou Daoru's student, and Zhou Daoru is still alone. Everyone hopes that Zhou Daoru can find a good husband. However, Zhou Daoru's marriage is indeed a difficult problem: people with poor character positions are naturally afraid to rise high; if the family has a wife, they ask Zhou Daoru to settle down to a side room; don't even think about it. But how many others were able to match Yuan Shikai's tutor at the time and did not start a family in the same way as Zhou Dao, a family member? I'm afraid Zhou Daoru doesn't disdain the generally unbearable Cydia.
Yuan Shikai's oldest brother, Yuan Keding, and his second son, Yuan Kewen, are two people with very different personalities, interests, and personalities. The second prince, Yuan Kewen, has an elegant temperament. He is passionate about piano, chess, calligraphy and painting, antique poetry, and is indifferent to fame, and opposes his father's imperial dream. Grand Duke Yuan Keding is a cunning person. He is passionate about fame and fortune. He is actively running for his father's imperial dream, making suggestions, and living the dream of being a prince. Yuan Shikai was very nervous about how to contain Feng Guozhang during the Spring Lotus Festival. Yuan Keding suggested, “Feng Huafu (that is, Feng Guozhang) has been broken for a long time. There is only one girl close to her aunt and wife. Wouldn't it be great if you could marry Mr. Zhou as his wife!” After hearing about it, Yuan Shikai called out the shooting, so he mobilized his daughter-in-law and concubines to lobby Zhou Daoru. When they had an eye for it, Xia Shoutian, a secretary born in the lead, wrote an affectionate letter to Feng Guozhang to talk to the media. They said that this was Yuan Shikai's beauty. Zhou Daoru's personality is learned. Feng Guozhang has heard about it for a long time. I heard that Yuan Shikai wanted the media to marry Zhou Daoru. I was overjoyed; I felt very disappointed. She immediately wrote back to Xia Shoutian, expressed her sympathy to Yuan Shikai, and sent someone to send her a wedding ceremony. Feng Guozhang also knows that Yuan Shikai is already wary of him. Now that Yuan Shikai has married Zhou Daoru, he hopes to make good use of this opportunity, show loyalty to Yuan Shikai, and win Yuan Shikai's greater trust. A few times he wanted to go north to welcome his family, but Yuan Shikai told him not to leave his post easily.
After Yuan Shikai's second son, Yuan Kewen, learned about this incident, he first asked his father why he was doing this, saying that his father would avenge his revenge and conceal his bad intentions, making Yuan Shikai furious. Yuan Kewen then went to Zhou Daoru's bedroom to see his teacher. He felt that the teacher was a kind-hearted person. He didn't plan to tell her the truth of the incident, but he finally couldn't help but preach: His father took his older brother's advice and married Feng Guozhang in order for her to spy on Feng Guozhang. He also hoped to use this incident to make Feng Guozhang appreciate the pictures and be loyal to their Yuan family. Yuan Kewen finally said, “Once the matter has reached this point, it is irretrievable, so I want teachers to take good care of themselves. Zhou Daoru nodded.
On the day they got married, Yuan Shikai specially invited Zhou Daoru to his Spring Lotus Festival for a long conversation. First, I thanked Zhou Daoru for his dedication to his family over the years, and then slowly talked about Zhou Daoru's marriage. Yuan Shikai said, “Nanjing is a major city in the southeast in terms of military affairs, and the essence of the country is economically. Feng Huafu is an old person from Xiaozhan. Using Teacher Zhou's economics, it is enough to make up for Feng Huafu's shortcomings. If he can protect himself with wisdom and seek loyalty to the country, his fame and wealth will always be second to none in the country.” Zhou Daoru could understand Yuan Shikai's implicit remarks. She seemed to feel that she was being used as a spy, and she felt very uncomfortable; she also felt that her fate was still in Yuan Shikai's hands; she was just a pawn in the Yuan family.
The marriage of a female teacher in the Xinhua Palace to General Wang Feng of Jiangnan was a momentous event. The Yuan family, including the female family, gave gifts to Zhou Daoru: jewelry, Chinese clothes, and beautiful objects, which were sturdy and filled dozens of large boxes; Yuan Shikai gifted Ocean 50,000 yuan of cash; only Yuan Kewen did not send Oriental paintings. When Yuan Shikai's fourth lady (that is, her third aunt, Min, a beautiful lady), accompanied Zhou Daoru in a float and headed south from Beijing, Yuan Kewen stood at the top of Xiangshan Mountain and looked in the direction Zhou Daoru had gone in the distance. For a long time, there was no movement.
The floats drove to Fengmen, Nanjing. Feng Guozhang used the ceremony to greet the President and sent Zhou Daoru to the West Garden of the Supervisory Office, which had already been cleaned up, brightly lit, and had a splendid “Zhou Gongguan” plaque, as a temporary place for Zhou Daoru to stay.
