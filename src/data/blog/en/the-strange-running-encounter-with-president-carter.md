 For several days in a row, every time David ran in the morning, he always saw an old man running slowly and quietly in front of him. This is an old man, almost 70 years old. His face was covered with wrinkles, and sweat trickled down the wrinkles. Despite being out of breath, the old man was still holding on, as if he were competing with someone.
David wanted to go up and say hello to the old man, but before he came forward, he was stopped by a strong man who followed the old man. The strong man actually glared at him, and his eyes filled his eyes with an unfathomable expression.
Early that morning, wearing a few steps, I was only one step away from the old man. I was trying to catch up. Suddenly, 78 men flashed out from behind and in front of the old man, and surrounded him with a group of Davies. David yelled, “Who are you and what do you want to do?”
The strong man who took the lead actually rushed up and knocked David to the ground. Meanwhile, a few other men bent down and scoured David's body, and also found the string of keys on his waistband.
David felt extremely humiliated and struggled to shout, “I want to call the police! Call the police!”
The old man, who had already run a long distance, looked back and waved at a few big men. Only then did the big men let go. The one who took the lead said harshly at David, “Don't run on this path in the future!”
“Why?”
“No matter what, don't run on this path anyway!”
David couldn't help but yell. He was still yelling. The old man turned around, walked over to him, smiled and said, “I'm sorry, I'm so surprised.”
David looked at the old man angrily: “What are you doing!”
“Like you, I am a sports lover, an old man who is not well enough to participate in the exercise.” He extended his skinny hand to David and said, “Let's be fine. I apologize to you.”
A few strong men looked at the old man's attitude. They no longer dared to be rude; they just looked at David with his eyes wide open. David was furious and snorted: “It's so baffling!”
Seeing that David is still angry, the old man said, “Young man, don't compare yourself to an old man. Would it be better for us to compare and be optimistic together?”
David looked at the old man's sincerity and never said anything; he just nodded softly: “OK.”
The old man pointed to the street in front of him and said to David, “Let's just run forward along this road and run all the way to the park entrance at the other end of the street to see who is the winner.”
David thought it was funny. This street is at most 1,000 meters long. Can a young man in his 20s not run like you, an old man?” He proudly waved his hand: “Let's get started!”
The two used a brick seam as the starting line. As soon as they were ready, the old man shouted, “Ready - Ready - Run!”
They actually started running. Before they were halfway through, David took the lead. He looked back, turned his body around, stepped back, waved his arms at the old man, and shouted, “Come on! Go for it!”
A few strong men also followed and watched the old man. David thought to himself, isn't this old man a New York tycoon; aren't these strong men his bodyguards? Forget it, no matter who you are, since you asked me to compete, we're equal. As long as I don't touch the old man, what can you do about me? When he thought about it this way, a taste that winners often have came out in his heart.
The old man had a hard time running and kept panting with his mouth open. After running for more than half the distance, it seemed like I couldn't run anymore, but I was still working hard to move forward. David suddenly felt that the match with such an old man was too weak. Even if he won, what was the point.
David slowed down and retreated to the front only a few steps away from the old man, as if leading the old man forward.
The entrance to the park arrived, and David reached the finish line first.
The old man followed up and held a tree to take a breath. The sweat was shining brightly in the morning sun, making the old man's face, which had gone through the wind and frost, like a layer of oil paint, making it look even more vivid. David was so moved by the old man's spirit that he couldn't say anything. It took half a day to spit out: “Old man, you're really not easy.”
The old man smiled bitterly: “You're the winner, I'm old, I can't do it anymore.”
David didn't know what to say.
After stopping for a while, the old man said, “Thank you, young man. You have led me this far for the first time. I'll be back tomorrow morning...”
David looked back at the strong men in front of and behind the old man and quietly said, “I can take you to run, but on one condition: don't bring so many bodyguards.”
When the old man heard it, he suddenly laughed out loud. The laughter was so familiar, but David couldn't even remember where he had heard it.
At night, David was used to turning on the TV. Suddenly, a familiar figure appeared on the screen. Oh my gosh, isn't that US President James Carter? He is visiting the Middle East. Isn't that happy laughter exactly what he heard in the morning? He opened his eyes to the boss and softly said to the president on TV: “Don't forget, early tomorrow morning, I'll have to race against you!”
Since then, David has met people and said that on March 10, 1979, he ran with the President of the United States.
