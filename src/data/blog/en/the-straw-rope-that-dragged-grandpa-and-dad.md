My grandfather is old and his ears are deaf. He has been ill in bed for over half a year, leaving only a little bit of gas left. My son felt that the old man was too much of a drag; why don't you send him up the mountain sooner.
One day, my son rubbed a very strong string, tied the old man to a wooden board, then called his baby son, Grandpa's grandson, Chun Tsai, and said, “Drag Grandpa up the back mountain and throw it away!”
Chun Tsai was very obedient. Sure enough, he dragged the old grandpa to Houshan Mountain and threw it away. When he turned around and wanted to go down the mountain, he thought it would be a pity to use this herb only once. Chun Tsai took the herbs home.
When Grandpa saw Chunzai bring back the sloppy rope, he thought it was strange. He asked, “Why are you bringing back this sloppy rope?” Chun Tsai smiled and said, “Keep it until you get old, I'll save myself some more trouble.”
Grandpa shouted “ah” in surprise, but couldn't say anything. Chun Tsai thought Grandpa still didn't understand, and explained, “Drag Grandpa in a nutshell, drag your dad, keep you old and drag you up the mountain!”
Grandpa had a red face when he heard it and felt ashamed of his disobedience. Stand up and say out loud, “Chun Tsai, go up the mountain.” “Do what?” “Bring Grandpa back.”
So the father and son went to Houshan and brought Grandpa back.
Grandpa hugged his old man and cried bitterly. Since then, I have changed my past mistakes and served my critically ill Grandpa wholeheartedly.
