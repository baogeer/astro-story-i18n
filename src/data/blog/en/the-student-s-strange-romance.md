 Once upon a time, a student went to school. On the way, he met a mountain craftsman and caught a white fox that was very beautiful. When he saw it, he bought it from a mountain master. Seeing that he was a student, Tashan Master said to him, “You can't afford to buy this rare white fox.” The student said, “It doesn't matter, you go home with me. My family has money. My parents will give you money when you get home.”
Takumi Uchiyama also followed the student back home. The student's parents asked why, and they saw that white foxes were really beautiful, so they paid to raise them. However, white foxes are wild animals that cannot be kept at home; they escaped after a few days. The student's parents scolded him, and the student ran away from home in one fell swoop.

The student had to send the young man in white and arrived at the intersection. The young man in white took the student's hand and said, “I'm a white fox. So he had to go to the king's palace alone and find the princess. The princess was born as beautiful as a flower. He was stunned. However, when he said to the baby, he mistakenly said that he wanted to go to the big forest when he went home, so the baby took them both to the big forest.
The princess thought it was inexplicable how suddenly the same strange young man came to the big forest and asked the students, “What kind of person are you and why did you take me to the big forest?” The student took out the baby and explained the reason to the princess. The princess was half-convinced and half-skeptical. I took the baby and looked at it and said, “I want to go home.” The baby brought the princess back to the palace, leaving only one student in the deep mountains and forest.
The student had no choice but to walk home without a baby. After walking for three days and three nights, he suddenly smelled a scent. After looking around, he discovered that it was a red fruit tree, full of red fruits, so he climbed up the tree and picked two to pack, and picked two more to eat. While eating, a pair of long horns grew on his head. He thought, how can we meet people in the future, but what are the ways?
He had no choice but to walk away again. On the fourth day, he smelled another scent. After taking a close look, it turned out to be a tree full of black fruit. He climbed up the tree and picked two more and ate them. Strangely enough, as soon as he finished eating, a pair of horns on his head fell off. The students were very happy and kept on their way.
