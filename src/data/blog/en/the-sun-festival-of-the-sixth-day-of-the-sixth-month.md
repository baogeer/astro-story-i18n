 Today, in Yangqiao Township, Cengong County, there is a village called Zhong Ling. There are about 100 families living in it, all from Tujia. Every year on June 6, the village is very busy. Men, women, young and old wear holiday costumes. Chicken wakes up when it's called 5, and climbs to the top of Jigong Mountain, which is the tallest place near the village. Facing the East, everyone clasped their hands, waited for the sun to come out, and kept on reading, “The Oriental Sun is rising fast, and everyone in the world is happy. Standing on Rooster Mountain today, the rooster is shouting at you.” When the sun came out, everyone cheered and jumped. The adults threw the poop and candy into the woods for the dolls to grab them. Everyone laughed, and finally gathered around and danced a hand dance. It wasn't until the sun went down that people went home. This is the Sun Festival of the Tujia people in Sizhou.
In ancient times, ten suns suddenly appeared in the sky at the same time, illuminating the earth red, the trees burnt, the fields burnt, the Longao River dried, and the stone walls on both sides of the river cracked. Seeing that life on both sides of the river was about to be destroyed, the men, women, and children in Zhong Ling Zhai all looked sad and didn't know what to do.
One day, a descendant named Asan came back from studying the law from outside and asked Zhai Lao, “There are so many suns in the sky, so big, you should find a way! If it continues like this, will everyone still be able to live?” Zhai Lao said with a sad face, “We can't think of a way.” Asan said, “I have a solution, which is to have a few talented people accompany me to Sun Mountain, take ten magic arrows, and shoot all the sun down.”
The next day, Zhaishang selected three strong descendants, carrying dry food and a steel knife, to accompany Asan to Taiyangshan. Everyone beat the gongs and drums and carried them all the way to the top of a mountain near Zhaizi. Facing the East, Asan shook hands and thought:
Heaven's Gate opened, Earth Gate opened, Tujia sent me up the mountain.
If there was a big fire today, everyone on the ground would be affected by disaster.
The Jade Emperor quickly sent a blessing of clouds, and the Buddha of the Earth led the way,
Take me to the east coast and lead me to Mount Sun.
Dragon King from the East China Sea used large water to take the divine arrow from Mount Sun.
The immortals quickly save me and bless the safety of all beings.
Suddenly, a red cloud floated from the East China Sea sky, connecting Asan and them to the sky. After 7 749 days, they arrived at Sun Mountain. The Sun Mountain was like a fire, burning their sweat, burning their skin red, and curling their hair. They couldn't help but look for it. Finally, they saw the Ten Divine Arrows at the foot of Mount Qingne. Everyone jumped for joy. They just wanted to throw over and get the arrows. They found a giant python plate there. They held their heads high, opened their mouths, and guarded the Divine Arrows. Asan pulled out a steel knife, rushed up and slashed it with a sword. The three descendants who went with him swarmed up and got into a fight with the giant python. Finally, he cut off the giant python's seven inches and took the magic arrow. Everyone was happy to return to Zhong Ling Zhai.
Early the next morning, Asan arrived at Rooster Mountain with an arrow on his back. When the sun rose, he shot nine suns in a row with his bow and arrow. The youngest Sun was so scared that it ran back and never dared to come out. Since then, the ground has become dark and getting colder, and people are unable to live. Ah Sang saw something wrong, and he also said to the people in the village, “It's not OK if there's too much sun, it's not OK if there's no sun; we still have to find a way to invite the one that is hiding out.” Everyone thinks this statement makes sense, but they don't know how to invite the sun out. One day, Asan, young and old from all over the village, led cows and horses and rushed to Zhonglingba to ask for the sun to come out. They shouted for three days and three nights, and the sun didn't come out. Asan said, “I'm afraid this place is far from the sun. Besides, everyone shouted in unison. The sound was chaotic and loud. The sun was frightened and afraid to come out. I want someone to go to the East China Sea to shout when the sun rises; it will definitely come out. Let's see who can do this!” People look at me, I see you; no one dares to accept this. Suddenly, a duck volunteered to say, “I'll shout. If you can't, hit me in the mouth!” After speaking, it spread its wings and swam in the direction of the East China Sea. While swimming and shouting, it swam for seven, seven, forty-nine days and shouted all the way to the East China Sea. Its legs were limp, its voice was hoarse, and the sun still didn't come out. In the end, the ducks had no choice but to swim back uninteresting. Everyone was very disappointed. They stepped on one foot angrily, stepped on the duck's mouth and stepped flat on their feet. Until now, ducks have all had flat mouths, and they have swayed back and forth when walking. The goose said, “I'm louder than a duck. Let me try it. The rooster said, “I can't swim, I can't fly, how can I call the sun out? But trust me, I can try it”. The rooster came to the top of a nearby mountain, faced the east, flapped its wings, and shouted with all its strength: “Tai-Yang - Brother, come out -- come -- Luo!” It shouted three times in a row, and was awakened by the sun it was hiding in. Everyone was scrambling to throw bread, fry rice, and wrap grains at the rooster, and the dolls scrambled to hold the rooster. This day is June 6th in the lunar calendar.
