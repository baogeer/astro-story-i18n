 A Siber folk tale, spread in the Chabchar Siber Autonomous County in Xinjiang, tells the story of swallows paying back their love.
An injured swallow came to the house of the old lady in the west. The old lady healed it and let it fly.
In the spring of the following year, the swallow left a pumpkin seed for the old lady. The old lady planted pumpkin seeds in her backyard and grew a big pumpkin full of gold and silver in the fall.
Since then, the lives of poor old women in the western neighborhood have been getting better day by day.
The rich old lady on the east side was very red-eyed. She also caught a swallow, broke her left leg, and let go.
In the spring of the following year, Swallow also lost a pumpkin seed to her. After planting, she grew a pumpkin containing a giant python and bit her to death.
