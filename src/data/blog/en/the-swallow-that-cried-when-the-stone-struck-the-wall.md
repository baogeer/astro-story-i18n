 Legend has it that in ancient times, a pair of swallows built a nest in Rouyuan Gate in Jiayuguan. Early in the morning of the day, the two Yens flew out of customs. At nightfall, the female Yan flew back first, and waited until the Xiong Yan returned. She screamed and hit the wall and died screaming and hit the wall. Because of this, the female Yan was heartbroken and made a “twirling” sound of swallows from time to time, and continued to scream until death. After death, his spirit persisted. Every time someone hit the wall with a stone, he “chirped” the sound of Yan and confided in others. In ancient times, people regarded being able to hear the sound of Yan Ming in Jiayuguan as an auspicious voice. When generals left customs, their wives would hit the wall to pray. Later, before generals left customs, they brought family members and children to the corner of the wall to pray together, so it became a custom.
