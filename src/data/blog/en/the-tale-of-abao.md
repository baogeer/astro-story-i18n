 There is a person named Sun Zichu in the east and west of Guangxi. He is a famous person. He was born with six fingers, has an honest personality, and is slow to speak. When others lied to him, he believed it. Sometimes when he met a singer in his seat, he saw a faraway turn away and left. When others knew he had this kind of temper, they tricked him into teasing and persecuting him by prostitutes, and his face was so embarrassed that he blushed all the way down his neck, and sweat went straight down. Because of this, everyone made fun of him, described him as stupid, and gave him an ugly nickname, “Sun Idiot.”
There is a big businessman in this county, but he is richer than the king. His relatives are all rich and powerful people. Dafushang has a daughter named Abao. Her parents are busy choosing a son-in-law for her. Many children of famous aristocrats scrambled to ask for a family, and none of the merchants liked it. It just so happened that Sun Tzu Chu's wife died. Some people made fun of him and advised him to go to Dafu's business to get married. Sun Zichu didn't even think about his situation; he actually asked the matchmaker to go. The businessman also knew Sun Zichu's name, but he thought he was too poor and didn't agree. When the matchmaker was leaving, he happened to run into Abo. When Abo asked what the matchmaker meant, Abo jokingly said, “If he can cut off that extra finger, I'll marry him.” The matchmaker told Sun Zichu this story. “It's not difficult,” Sun said to himself. After the matchmaker left, Sun Zichu cut off his sixth finger with an axe. The blood was flowing so much that the pain almost killed him. After a few days, as soon as I was able to wake up, I went to the matchmaker's house and showed her the hand that had cut off her six fingers. The matchmaker was shocked and went to the wealthy merchant to tell Abao. Ah Bao was also surprised. He made another joke that Sun Zichu still had to get rid of that infatuation. After listening, Sun Zichu argued loudly, saying that he was not demented, yet he had no chance to confess in person to Abao. On second thought, Abao isn't necessarily as beautiful as an immortal; why should he raise his own worth so much. As a result, the thought of asking for a family cooled down.
It just so happened that the Ching Ming Festival has arrived. According to customs, this day is a day for women to go on a green outing in the countryside. Some skimpy teenagers also accompanied them to comment on the women and make fun of them at will. A few writers and poets also insisted on their grandson Chula. Some mocked him and said, “Wouldn't you like to see your lovely beauty?” Sun Zichu knew that everyone was teasing him, but because he was mocked by Ah Bao, he also wanted to see what kind of person Ah Bao was, so he was happy to walk and watch with everyone. From afar, I saw a woman resting under a tree. A group of villains surrounded her like a wall. Friends said, “This must be Abba.” I ran over to take a look; it sure was Abao. It's really beautiful and very moving when you take a closer look. After a while, there were more and more onlookers, and Abo got up and left in a hurry. The crowd was turned upside down and made comments, and they just went crazy; only Grandson Chu remained silent. Everyone walked away, but looking back, Sun Zichu was still standing there in a daze, and he didn't agree to shouting. Everyone pulled him up and said, “Did the soul go with Abao?” Sun Zichu still doesn't speak. It's not surprising that people speak little because he's usually dull. Someone pushed him, someone dragged him, and went home.
When Sun Zichu arrived home, he fell down on his bed. I couldn't sleep all day. I felt like I was drunk, and I couldn't wake up shouting. The family thought he had lost his soul, and calling him out in the wild didn't work. When I asked him by patting him hard, he vaguely said, “I'm at Abao's house.” When I asked him further, he was silent again. The family were terrified and didn't know what happened.
At first, when Sun Zi Chu saw Ah Bao gone, she felt very sad. She felt that her body had gone with her, gradually leaning on her belt, and no one scolded her. So I went home with Abo. They all sit and lie down with Abao. At night, they have fun with Ah Bao, and they are very friendly and happy. But I just felt really hungry. I wanted to take a trip home, but I didn't know the way home. Every time Abao dreams of getting along with someone and asking him his name, he says, “I am Sun Tzu Chu.” Abo feels very strange, but it's not convenient for him to tell others.
After lying down for three days, Sun Zichu saw that he was about to lose his breath. The family was anxious and afraid, so they asked someone to go to the merchant's house to pray for Sun Zichu's soul. The businessman smiled and said, “I don't usually have any contacts, why would you leave your soul in my house?” The Sun family prayed so much that the businessman agreed. The witch brought Sun Zichu's old clothes and straw mat to the merchant's house. When Ah Bao found out the reason, she was extremely afraid. She didn't let the witch go anywhere else; she took the witch directly to her room, and left after letting the witch greet her for a while. The witch had just returned to Sun's doorstep, and Grandson Chu was already groaning in the bed inside the house. After waking up, he was also able to clearly say the names and colors of Abao's interior furnishings, which is great. After hearing it, Abao was even more afraid, but she also felt Sun Zichu's deep feelings in her heart.
After Sun Zichu woke up, whether sitting or standing, he was always thoughtful and in a trance, as if he had lost something. I often listen to Abao's news, and I hope to see Abao again. On the day of the Buddha Bathing Festival, I heard that Abao was going to burn incense at Suigetsu Temple. Sun Zichu waited on the side of the road early in the morning. Seeing that he was dizzy, Ah Bao didn't come until noon. Ah Bao saw Sun Zichu from inside the car, pulled up the curtain with her slim hands and looked at him intently. Sun Zichu was even more moved and followed suit. Abo suddenly asked his mistress to ask him his name. Sun Zichu said it very kindly. His spirit was even more shaken. He didn't go home until after the car had left.
After Sun Zichu got home, his old illness recurred. He didn't eat or drink. He shouted Abao's name in his sleep, and hated that his soul couldn't go back to Abao's house like the last time. He had a parrot in his family, which suddenly died. The kid played with it at the bedside. Sun Zichu saw what he thought to himself. If I could become a parrot, I could spread my wings and fly to Abao's house. I was thinking in my heart that my body had turned into a parrot. It flew out and flew all the way to Abao's room. Abo grasped it happily, tied its legs with a rope, and fed it with hemp seeds. The parrot suddenly spat out and said, “Sister, don't tie me up, I'm Sun Zichu!” Abo was shocked. He was busy unraveling the rope, and the parrot didn't fly away. Abo prayed to the parrot and said, “Your love is engraved in my heart. Now that we have different kinds of people and birds, how can a good marriage be restored?” The parrot said, “My wish to be by your side has been fulfilled.” Other people feed it and won't eat it; it will only eat if Abo feeds it. When Abo sat down, the parrot crouched on her lap; when Abo lay down, the parrot snuggled up next to her. After three days like this, Ah Bao felt sorry for it, so he quietly sent someone to visit Sun Zichu. It's been three days since I saw that my grandson Chu was lying in bed without gas, but his heart was still a little warm. Abao also prayed, “If you can become a human again, I will be with you even if I die.” The parrot said, “You lied to me!” Abao immediately swore an oath. The parrot slanted its eyes, as if thinking about something. After a while, Abo wrapped her feet and took off her shoes under the bed. The parrot rushed down violently, picked up the shoes with her mouth and flew away. Abo called it quickly; the parrot had already flown far away. Ah Bao sent an old mother to visit; of course, her grandson Chu has already woken up.
When people in the Sun family saw that the parrot had brought an embroidered shoe, they fell to the ground and died. It was strange that Sun Zichu had already woken up. As soon as I woke up, I started looking for shoes, and no one knew what was going on. It just so happened that Abao's old mother had arrived. When I entered the room and saw Sun Zichu, I asked him where his shoes were. Sun Zichu said, “The shoes are a token of Abao's oath. Please let me know for me that my grandson Chu will never forget her golden promise.” When the old mother came back and talked about it again, Ah Bao was even more strange, so she asked the maiden to divulge the accident to her own mother. After her mother inquired in detail and understood, she said, “Sun Zichu is still talented and famous, but he is poor like Sima Xiangru. After choosing a son-in-law for a few years, this is actually what I got; I'm afraid it will be laughed at by the nobles.” Abao refused to marry him because of her shoes; her parents had no choice but to listen to her. Some people ran to tell Sun Zichu the news. Sun Zichu was very happy and got well immediately.
Abao's parents wanted to bring their grandson Chu over. Abao said, “My son-in-law should not live with my father-in-law's house for a long time. What's more, his family is poor, and people look down on them if they live for a long time. Since my daughter has agreed to him, she never complained about staying in a thatched house and eating a full meal.” Sun Zichu then went to marry the bride and got married. The two met as happy as a couple separated from another world to be reunited.
Since then, Sun Zi Chu's family has taken Abao's dowry, increased their wealth and household assets, and their lives have improved. Sun Zichu is just fascinated by books; he doesn't know how to manage his family and finances. Ah Bao is a person who is good at being a family. Sun Zichu doesn't have to worry about anything in the family. After three years, the family is richer. However, Sun Tzu Chu suddenly fell ill and died. Ah Bao cried very sad. The tears in her eyes never dried up, she never slept, she didn't eat, she didn't listen to anyone's advice, and she hanged herself at night. After the girl found out, she was in a hurry to take care of her and only woke up, but she never ate anything. Three days later, the family summoned family and friends to prepare for Sun Zichu's burial. When I heard a moan coming from inside the coffin, I opened it and took a look. Sun Zichu came back to life. He said, “When I met Hades, Hades said that my life was simple and sincere, and ordered me to become a minister. Suddenly someone said, “Sun Bu-cao's wife is coming.” Hades checked the birth and death books and said, “It's not time for her to die.” Another person said, “I haven't eaten for three days.” Hades went back and said, “Your wife's act of kindness has moved me; let you live a better world again.” So I sent the groom to take the horse and bring me back.”
Since then, Sun Zichu's health has gradually returned to normal. This year coincided with the hometown exam. Before entering the exam room, a group of teenagers teased Sun Zichu and drafted seven bizarre test questions together to trick Sun Zichu into a secluded place, saying, “This is a test question someone got through a relationship, and I secretly told you because of our friendship.” Sun Zi Chu believed the truth, thought about it in black and white, and prepared seven and eight articles. Everyone laughed secretly at him. At the time, considering that the questions from the past might be plagiarized, the main examiner deliberately went against the rules. The test questions were actually exactly the same as the seven questions Sun Zichu had prepared. Sun Zichu won the first place in the hometown test. The next year, I got a juror again and entered the Hanlin Institute. The emperor heard about such strange things as Sun Zichu and summoned him to inquire. Sun Zichu explained everything truthfully. The emperor was very praising and happy. Afterwards, he summoned Abao and gave him many things.
