 There is an iron bar lama in Zhebang Temple. He has always been pretentious and arrogant. One day, he came back from a large manor house, riding a big yellow horse, wearing a pair of high-ranking leather boots, and carrying a foreign gun. That style was simply imposing. People couldn't help but exude amazement... While walking, he suddenly bumped into Agutomba.
“Hey, Agutomba, where are you going?”
“I'm going to the kid's uncle's house.”
“Why?”
“They want me to tell them stories.”
“Oh right, I hear you can tell jokes, but you can also deceive people, right?”
“Where, where!”
“You're welcome! Since we met here today, it's a rare opportunity, so you can fool me!”
“How dare, how dare!”
“Don't use garlic! If I want you to cheat, you have to deceive; if you don't lie, it's OK! I must teach you how clever you are to deceive today!”
Agutomba looked at his arrogant attitude, pretended to be timid, and hurriedly shouted at him: “Dear Buddha, spare me! Since ancient times, owls have only dared to howl in the dark; how can a little devil appear in front of a god...”
“No nonsense! Whether you want to live or die depends on whether you cheat or not. Don't you know my temper yet?”
“If that were the case, then I wouldn't dare disobey my order. But I really can't do it today because I didn't have that trick kit with me, so please be kind and allow me to change the date!”
“Where have you lost your trickery tools?”
“At home.”
“I'll wait for you here, go back and get it!”
“It's still a long way from my house. It's not good if you wait too long.”
“Let's do it, ride my big yellow horse and go back right away!”
Agutomba got on the horse helplessly and walked a few steps. He deliberately strangled the grade rope and stopped the rhubarb horse. Agutomba got off his horse and came back and said to the Iron Rod Lama, “Your horse is a rich horse. It looks so shabby as I am; it just doesn't want to carry me around!” The Iron Rod Lama listened to Agutomba's words and was extremely happy to take off her gorgeous coat and the shiny leather boots on her feet and hand them to Agutomba: “Put them on. Now it can make you look dazzling all over your body!”
Agutomba changed into gorgeous clothes, rode a “rich horse”, and walked for a while. He strangled the slow rope again, jumped off the horse, and ran back to the Iron Rod Lama and said, “Ahhh! It's not going away! Your horse is not only a 'rich horse', but it's also a 'shogun horse'; it must have seen that I don't carry a foreign gun... Lord Buddha, you can simply lend me a gun on your back too!”
Iron Rod Lama thought to himself: Aguton Bazhun wouldn't dare to cheat on me, but instead found ways to use excuses. I might as well give him the gun, shut him up, and tell him that he can borrow it for no reason; there's nothing you can trust! Let's see what else he has to say. As a result, I resolutely handed over the gun to him.
At this point, Agutomba was already fully armed. I saw him whip his horse and gallop leisurely. After riding across the vast Nianchu River, he turned around, wrapped his hands around his mouth, set up a microphone, and shouted at the Iron Rod Lama: “Hey! Bye! Bye— see you—”
Only now does the stupid Iron Rod Lama understand that he has been scammed! But is there any way? If you go on a hike to catch up, it will only seem even worse!
