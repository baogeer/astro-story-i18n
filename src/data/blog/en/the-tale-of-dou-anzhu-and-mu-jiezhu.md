 Legend has it that Tenjin Kobita had three children and three daughters. The older son herds sacred cows in Niluo Mountain; the second son herds magic horses in Huanghopo; the eldest daughter Xu is married to the Jingu Palace; the second daughter is married to the Dragon Palace; the third child and the third daughter are not yet married; they are herding sheep in Wenshan.
Of the few sisters, the third daughter, Mu Jiezhu, is the most beautiful. Her eyes are like clear autumn water, and her moving voice often makes birds stop their wings and forget to return home. Whenever she carried shavings of wool and a rope in her hand to herd sheep, flocks of birds flew with her, flowers paved the way for her, and the flocks cheered and jumped in front of and behind her. Even the small tree on the side of the road gently caressed her red face with the tips of its branches.
One day, Dou Anzhu, a brave young man from the Qiang tribe, came to the grassland in Qianshan Mountain, and suddenly heard a moving voice of singing along with the wind. He was fascinated by the song and followed the song in search of it. Shortly after walking, he saw a beautiful shepherd girl sitting on a large rock package in the center of the meadow. This is Mu Jie-zhu.
While hanging wool, Mu Je-zhu sings to the cloud-like flock of sheep and the picturesque Wenshan Pasture. As soon as she looked up, she saw a young man standing in front of her, watching herself. Dou Anju's handsome face and tall, athletic body made Mu Jie-ju very fond of her.
The two fell in love at first sight. The happiness of their first love was sweetened with honey. They sang and shared their love with each other until the sun set. It wasn't easy to meet and even harder to say goodbye. Dou An Joo was in love with Mu Ge Joo. Before leaving Mu, Sister Mu took off the bleached foot wrap on her leg and gave it to Dou An Joo.
Since then, every day when the sky wasn't bright, Dou An Zhu came to the grassland to wait for Mu Jiezhu. She didn't wait for the sun to come out, so she rushed her flock to land on Mount Wenshan.
They were inseparable. They fell in love with each other on a blind date. The wooden comb on their heads was used instead, they carried buckets of water in exchange for their backs, one for each tree, and the two sat on one stone. Their love songs echoed on the grassland, and images of them falling in love were reflected in the mountain springs.
The water in the ravine was rising and disappearing, and the moon was round and lacking. It dazzled until September 29, Tenjin's Prayer Day. On this day, they met on the grassland. Mu Jie-zhu lost her old laughter, and her face was covered with sadness. With a trembling voice, she asked Doo An Joo: “Brother An Joo, do you know who I am?”
Dou An Joo answered honestly: “There was no sound of your voice on the singing hall in the village, and I didn't see you next to the roaring bonfire. I visited Shanshanzhai village, and every village house said I didn't know your name. Are you a Heavenly Goddess?”
“You know that I am the third daughter of Tenjin Mubita; do you know that after tomorrow's Tenjin Prayer Day, I will go back to Heaven and stop shepherding in Wenshan.” Miss Mu was in a hurry and immediately revealed her true identity and the reason for her worries.
After listening to Mu Jie-zhu's words, Dou An Joo was unaware that there are thousands of Buddhas and ancestors in the sky, and Mubita is respected. How could he allow his daughter to marry a mortal? However, when Dou Anju thought of his deep feelings and the sweetness of getting along with Sister Mu in the morning and evening, she strengthened her determination to fall in love with Mu Jie-ju.
Dou Anju said, “Tenjin Mubita is the god of all gods. He has many natural soldiers and endless mana, but I have a loyal and firm heart. For the sake of love, I dared to go up the mountain to fight tigers and go down to the sea to capture dragons. As long as we both really love each other, we will definitely be able to use wisdom and courage to get your father and prince to agree to our family affairs!”
“Brother An Joo! My sweet brother.” Mu Jie-zhu attached her head to Dou An Joo's chest, cut a strip of blue silk from her head and held it to Dou An Zhu with both hands; Dou An Zhu also cut off a black stick from her ear and sent it to Mu Ge-ju's hand.
Mu Je-joo said to Dou An Joo, “We'll go see our father tomorrow and ask him to agree.”
That night, Dou An Zhu rode through Tianmen, mixed up with Mu Jiezhu's flock and arrived at the heavenly court. He hid next to a sheep pen and waited until dawn.
The rooster crows, and the sun shines. The morning light in Tiangong was bright and colorful. When Tenjin Kobita met the gods, she smelled the smell of mortal people, so she went back to the Tiangong Palace and asked the two eldest daughters: “Can you bring mortals to Tiangong?”
Both daughters claimed that they had returned single, without any human being. Mu Jie-zhu bravely brought Dou An Joo to the palace without waiting for her father and king to inquire.
When Mubita saw Mu Jie-zhu bringing a mortal person, she was furious. Without waiting for Mu Jie-zhu to report it, she shouted and asked Dou An Zhu: “What kind of person are you, who dare to run into heaven?” The sound made the main hall vibrate.
Dou An Joo calmly presented the ceremony and answered with an unchanging look, “Dear Tenjin Mubita, I went to Tiangong to ask for your kindness and approval for me to become a family with your third daughter, Mu Jie-ju.”
“What? You want to marry my Tenjin Kobita's daughter?” Mubita was astonished, but when he saw Dou An Zhu's strong body and fearless look, he couldn't help but lower his voice by three points. “If you dare to come to Tiangong to ask for a family, you must be very capable. OK, tomorrow when it's not bright and the clouds haven't dissipated, you wait for me under the ice cream tank. We'll work hard. If I think you're a mortal, I don't need magic powers; as long as you can keep up with what I've left behind, I'll give you three daughters.”
“Okay!” Dou An Joo agreed without hesitation.
When Mu Je-ju, who was on the sidelines, saw that Dou An Joo had agreed to her father's requirements, she was very anxious. As soon as the gods dispersed, she said to Doo-an-joo, “Brother An Joo, how can you agree to your father's requirements? He wants to drop rolling wood first, then drop stones, can you catch it?”
Dou An Joo caressed Sister Mu's dark hair and said, “Sister Mu, for our happiness, I'm not afraid even if it's a sea of fire. Why are you afraid of this rolling wooden rock!”
Once again, Mu Jie-zhu was deeply moved by Dou Anju's sincerity: “Hey, my smart and stupid An Joo Ge. Brave people need wisdom and ingenuity to act!” As a result, she quietly told Dou An Joo a good idea.
Early the next morning, Dou An Joo acted according to plan and hid under the ice trap early in the morning. Soon after, I only heard a loud noise. A trough of rolling wood rolled down, and the stones hit the trough flew in the middle of the air. Immediately after that, there was another loud thunder, with large and small stones falling down the trough, shattering the ice that was a foot thick above the ice.
Dou An Joo hid under the trough and wasn't hurt at all. While he was eating the dry food Mu Jiezhu gave him, he listened attentively to the sound in the trough. When he heard no sound of rolling stones, he looked up and just happened to hold Mubita who had scrolled down the trough to check the results. Dou An Joo said, “Dear Mubita, I caught the wood and stone you put down, and now I'm catching you again.”
Mubita was dumbfounded, but he was still unwilling and made another plan: “If you won this time, you really have the skills! Then please cut down the big trees and vines in the Jiugou Fire Field in one day, and I will fulfill your wishes.”
The Jiugou Fire is full of trees and vines all around it. Dou An Zhu has great talent, and he can't finish cutting it down in a day. At this point, Mu Jie-zhu came to him again and softly comforted him and said, “Brother An Zhu, don't worry. Tomorrow morning, you should take down the four trees at the four corners of the Jiugou Fire Station, and hide behind a big rock and wait for the results. The tiger howled and the wolf howled not to show up; only when the voice calmed down did they look up.”
At dawn this day, Mu Jie-zhu prayed to the gods of Jiugou Mountain on the back of the waterway and asked them to help Dou An Zhu. At this point, Dou An Joo had cut down four trees at the four corners of the fire and hid behind large stones. Time stopped, the earth shook the mountains, and the wind raged. With the sound of the wind, there were sounds of tigers howling, wolves howling, and large trees falling to the ground, and vines breaking. Soon, all the sounds disappeared. He looked up and saw that the Jiugou Fire Site had become a flat river.
He completed the task of cutting down the Jiugou Fire and happily returned to Mubita. Mubita was very surprised. Thinking that she couldn't even cure a mortal person, wouldn't she make the gods laugh? As a result, another problem came up: “If you can cut down the big trees and vines in the Jiugou Fire Field in one day, then it will take another day to finish burning the Jiugou Fire Field.”
“Okay, dear Mubita, I'll use it for one day!” Dou An Joo thought that she had set fire to a place on earth with her townspeople. This was unbearable, so she readily agreed.
But where did he know, the big trees and vines connected to the mountains in the sky just ignited a fire. He used a white iron sickle to fire the firewood and threw it into the tree heap. He only heard a “huh” sound. The big tree was burning, and the vines were burning. A big fire broke out in the sky. The mountain wind was strong, and the wind fueled the fire, so that Dou An Ju had no place to hide, and finally fainted next to a large rock.
At this point, the huge fire was seen by Mu Jie-zhu, who herded sheep. Her heart was burning, and unable to catch up with the flock, she immediately invited her second brother-in-law, Shui Bolong Wang Shiyu, who came to the Dragon Palace to put out the fire.
Shuibo arrived, and heavy rain fell, reducing the fire. Mu Jie-zhu braved the heavy rain and stepped on the burning firewood pile to rescue Dou Anju. Dou An Joo's eyes were closed, and her skin was burnt and rotten all over her body. Mu Jie-zhu cried as soon as she saw it. Crystal teardrops fell on Dou Anzhu's bubbling face. Doo-an-joo woke up, and he regretted that he hadn't told Mu Je-ju about this.
Mu Jie-zhu also regrets that she went to the grassland early in the morning without questioning An Zhu. With tears in her eyes, she said, “Brother An Joo, go back to the world soon! You must remember to pick up three stones at the three-way intersection in the grassland and take them back, take a pot, and then use peach branches to boil a pot of water. Put the burnt iron flower head in the water, steam the body, then bathe in boiling water, and your wound will heal.”
Dou An Zhu returned to Wenshan and did as Mu Jie-zhu said. The burn was certainly better. He hurried back to Tiangong again.
At this point, the lights were shining brightly in the Tiangong, and the drums were blaring in unison. Tenjin Kobita is celebrating her victory over Doan Joo. But suddenly he saw Dou An Joo standing in front of him safely and unharmed, and was stunned for a moment. Still unwilling to fail, he reversed his promise once again and said to Dou Anju, “Now that you've burnt the fire, help me grow rapeseed! Nine buckets of nine liters of rapeseed must be planted within a day. Otherwise, don't come see me!”
Dou An Joo was very angry at Mubita's repeated harassment, but she had trouble getting sick, so she had no choice but to nod and agree. Hearing her father's words, Mu Jie-zhu was also very anxious. At this point, the Eight Daggers, who were on duty in the Heavenly Court, were moved by their loyal love, and also angry that Mubita harassed the young people, and took the initiative to help them regardless of the King of Heaven's will.
Early the next morning, Dou An Zhu followed the instructions of the Eight Daggers, filled nine cowhide pockets with rapeseed, placed one in a trench, sprinkled a handful on each piece of fire, then hid behind a big rock to wait for the results.
The Eight Daggers drove the birds and beasts to the Jiugou Fire Site, and soon spread the nine dou, nine liters of jiuhe rapeseed evenly. After Dou An Joo thanked the Eight Kings, he went to see the King of Heaven.
Mubita saw Dou Anju ascend to the palace and shouted, “How? Have you finished growing rye seeds as required?”
Dou An Zhu went one step further and said, “According to your requirements, I have planted the Jiugou Fire Field. You should have given Mu Sister Zhu Xu to me!”
Mubita was speechless and still unwilling to stop. He looked down and thought for a while, then cunningly said, “You really deserve to be a good man in the Qiang tribe. I agree to marry Mu Jiezhu. But I don't have a marriage partner to give you, so I'll give you nine buckets of nine liters of jiuhe rapeseed. If you can get back a grain of rapeseed in a day, I'll have a wedding for you: if you can't get it back, don't blame me for being ruthless!”
Dou An Ju believes that wisdom must be used to defeat Shubita. He told Mu Je-zhu about this. Mu Jie-zhu was very angry at her father's harassment and said, “Brother An Joo, you've worked so hard for your sister. I really don't know how to thank you for your love for me. Father King couldn't beat us this time either. Let's go ask for help from the Mountain God and Bodhisattva; we must let Father King's trick come to naught!” Having said that, I went hand in hand with Dou Anju to find the mountain god.
After Doo An Joo left, Mubita couldn't help laughing: “Okay, Doo An Joo! You don't even want to think about it. Well, nine dozen nine liters of jiuhe rapeseed is as small as a flea. It's spread all over the Kujo Valley, and even if you have the skills to do it, it's hard to take back more than one grain. If you take one less, I'll cut your head off, making you speechless... ha! ha! ha!”
Unbeknownst to me, with the help of the mountain god, Dou An Zhu mobilized thousands of birds, and quickly took back the rapeseed. Mubita ordered people to pick up, lift, and load the scales to inspect the rapeseed.
Dou An Zhu arranged the nine cowhide pockets in a row on the main hall, and let Mubita personally measure the bucket and lift it over the scale. With each bucket at a time, each liter and a liter pack, Mubita's face is getting more and more ugly. Suddenly, Mubita shouted: “Come on!”
A few warriors hurriedly stepped forward and knelt down. Mubita clapped the dust on his hands and loudly said, “Push Dou An Zhu to the head at noon!”
“Why?” Mu Jie-zhu and all the gods were taken aback.
Pointing at the scale, Mubita proudly said, “Hey, I don't know how tall the sky is, how dare you compete with me. Look, this scale falls down, and it's still less than rapeseed. I've already stated in advance that rapeseed is not enough to be cut!”
“Slow!” Without waiting for the divine soldiers to take action, General Zhideon from the Eight Daggers stepped forward and said to Mubita, “Dear Mubita, sow this rapeseed for a day, and the bug eats the bird's peck! It's almost understandable. You should give Dou An Zhu another half day to recover the lost rapeseed. If you can't find it, it won't be too late.”
“Hmm...” Seeing that God would intercede for Dou An Joo, Mubita feared that the crowd would not be offended, but she had to say, “OK! In the eyes of the gods, I'll give you another half day.” When you're done, brush your sleeves and go
In half a day, it was really difficult to retrieve the bad rapeseed in Jiuzuoling in Jiujianggou. However, Dou Anju was not deterred by the difficulties. Carrying the dry food Mu Jiezhu had prepared for him, he carried a strong bow and a hard arrow, and hit the road feeling refreshed.
Arriving at Jiugou Fire Ground, he glanced at a pheasant with a long tail and asked, “Beautiful pheasants, have you ever pecked rapeseed on this fire?”
The pheasant flapped its wings and answered, “Dou Anju, smart and brave, we just flew from that mountain top and haven't eaten your rapeseed!”
Dou Anju walked forward again, saw a few red-billed eagles on the cliff, and asked loudly, “The old magpie with a red mouth and black coat. Have you ever eaten rapeseed on this ground?”
The old red-billed cockatoo croaked a few times and answered, “Dou Anju, smart and brave, we haven't eaten your rae seeds!”
The sun was only one pole high from the top of the mountain. Dou An Zhu was very anxious, and climbed higher up the mountain. He went over mountain top after mountain top, crossed one big ditch after another, and never found the rapeseed's whereabouts. Arriving at the top of the ninth hill, Dou An Zhu saw a large group of wild pigeons looking east and west to peck on a fire. He rushed over and asked loudly, “Wild pigeons busy looking for food, have you ever eaten rapeseed from Jiugou Fire Field?”
Hearing Dou An Zhu's question, the group of pigeons flew up in flames. While flying, they said, “How many pieces of rice seeds from Jiugou Fire Field have we eaten!”
“Alright, you guys hurt me so hard to find!” Dou Anju raised his bow and arrow to shoot down the pigeon. But he changed his mind: Pigeons don't know about this, how can I hurt them?! So he relaxed his tight bowstring and shouted to the wild pigeon, “I want to take back all of these rice seeds. Please help me!”
After listening to Dou An Joo's words, the group of pigeons fell next to Dou An Joo one after another and muttered, “Dou An Joo, smart and brave, your generosity and kindness make us extremely grateful. We will repay you for not killing!” After speaking, several wild pigeons that had eaten rapeseed crashed into a cliff and immediately hit and died.
Dou An Ju was sad to pick up the dead pigeons. The pigeons gathered around him. The pigeon king said, “Dear Dou An Ju, don't be sad; go ahead and cut open your stomach to get the lotus! Otherwise, as soon as the moment arrives, you won't be able to take back the seeds, and the king will not let us get married, and it will also disappoint our intention to repay our kindness!”
When Dou Anju saw that the sun had set, she had no choice but to endure her grief, pull out a hunting knife from under her waist, and find rapeseed in the dead pigeon's salt bag...
The scales are flat, and the bucket is full. Dou An Joo and Mu Jie-ju won! Mubita had nothing to say but to say, “Dou Anju, smart and brave, you have won. My three daughters will definitely be happy when they marry you!”
Mubita called the emperor's grandmother, recalled the children, gathered all the gods, and discussed family matters in the main hall. He said to Mu Jie-zhu, “Three girls, you won't get married in Dragon Palace. Now that you're married to a farmhouse, just run agriculture!”
Dou An Joo and Mu Jie-zhu smiled happily, and the gods smiled in comfort.
Love in exchange for bravery and wisdom is happy and sweet. The entire Heavenly Palace was brightly lit up, and all the gods were delighted with the couple's union.
Doo An Joo and Mu Jie-ju's wedding was more solemn than that of her sister's brother. Mubita and the gods gave gifts of all kinds of food seeds, such as buckwheat, barley, corn, and wheat; all kinds of tree seeds such as cypress, fir, and black thorns; all kinds of clothes such as: dragon and phoenix clothes, multicolored cloud embroidered shoes, linen white shirt, white cloth baotoupa; all kinds of livestock such as cows, sheep, pigs, horses, dogs, and rabbits. etc.
At the time of their farewell, Mubita advised them both to listen to their parents; to work hard to master the family; burn incense 15 at the beginning; pray for the Spring and Autumn Newspapers to be clean; be good for the people; and don't look back after leaving the Tiangong Palace...
Dou An Joo and Mu Jie-ju returned to the world with rich gifts and the wishes of the gods. Along the way, they sang and danced like happy little birds, and you chased me.
At Wenshan Pasture, they recalled the scene of their first love; on Dashibao, they remembered the place where they snuggled up and whispered; beside the murmuring mountain spring, they also saw a reflection of their dependency: at the three-way intersection, they thought of fighting wisely with Mubita...
Talking all the way, singing along, watching, and thinking along the way, I even forgot Mubita's instructions at the time of farewell. I looked back and looked back at Tiangong, which was blocked by clouds and fog, but unexpectedly the beasts that followed them ran away. So there are wild boars, bison in today's sloppy jungle...
Dou Anju and Mu Jie-ju spread fir seeds at the top of the mountain, and scattering black thorn seeds at the foot of the mountain. Immediately, the red cedar at the top of the mountain grew into a giant green umbrella, the cypress trees on the mountainside were green, and the thorns at the foot of the mountain grew to protect the family and protect the animals.
Together with the townspeople, they used their hard-working hands to cut back the sequoia and cypress stands, covered the roof with thick soil, and built a tall village building. Sow buckwheat, barley, corn, and wheat seeds on the fire and cultivate diligently. Cows, sheep, chickens, and rabbits were fed on the grassland next to the village house...
Since then, the lives of the Qiang people have been getting better and better.
