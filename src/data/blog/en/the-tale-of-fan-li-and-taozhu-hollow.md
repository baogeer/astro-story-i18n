 In Qianshan, Bilian Baiquan Township, beside the Nanxi River, there is a Shantou Ao Cave. Legend has it that during the Spring and Autumn Warring States period, I don't know where a large family moved from, picked a plot of land in the mountains to build a house and live there. His parents are an old man named Tao Zhu. He has a very good heart and often does good deeds. Everyone respects him and calls him Tao Zhu Gong.
Tao Zhu Gong does three things a day: writing books, practicing martial arts, and raising chickens. He and his family dug about 30 trenches there. Each ditch is about ten feet long, three to four feet wide, and one foot deep. After digging, put firewood, cover with fine mud, and raise termites. After the termites are raised, the chicken is placed in the ditch to eat. Chickens are big and fat, and they lay a lot of eggs.
Every time there is no green or yellow, Tao Zhu Gong often goes to the Waiao and Changtan areas to see a few villages under the mountain. If cooking smoke does not rise on any of the roofs, he will send food and eggs.
In the fall of a year, there was good rain and a good harvest of food. The villagers prepared many gifts for Tao Zhu Gong. Unexpectedly, when they arrived at his house, there wasn't a single person in the whole house. Everyone was surprised. As soon as I inquired, I learned that Tao Zhu Gong had already moved to another place.
Later, the businessman who came back from outside said that Tao Zhu Gong was Dr. Fan Yu, Yue Wang Goujian. When Yue Wang Goujian defeated Wu Wangfu with his army, Fan Li resigned from office and went into seclusion deep in the mountains, raised chickens, cultivated fields, and lived a fairy life. Later, not knowing why, he moved to another place with his family. People in Baiquan Village, in order to thank Gong Tao Zhu for his kindness, named the place where he lived in seclusion called Tao Zhu Keng.
