 Legend has it that in Wanhua Village in Peony Township, Caozhou, many people specialize in growing flowers and make ends meet by selling flowers. There is a small garden on the south side of Wanhua Village. The Chaimen fence is very elegant. The person watching the garden is named Chun Bao. He is 18 years old. When he was a child, his parents both died due to illness. Little Chunbao lived with her grandfather. Little Chunbao is smart, hardworking and studious, and loved by everyone. But unfortunately my grandfather was just after he became an adult. I just gave up. Chun Bao felt very sad. Every month hangs high and the night breeze blows, he blows up the bamboo flute left by his grandfather to support his condolences. ' On this day, Chun Bao had just eaten dinner and picked up a bamboo flute. His melodious and pleasant sound of the flute wafted over Wanhua Village. Hearing it, it had an indescribable charm. I don't know when the sky competed to see rain falling from the ground in Zhejiang. He moved the bench inside the house and looked at the drizzly night outside the door. Chun Bao sank into her deep thoughts about her grandfather. Suddenly, a man stumbled from outside and didn't wait for Chun Bao to speak up. The first one said: 'Sorry to bother you, let me take shelter from the rain here, okay?
Hearing the sound, it was a woman. Chun Bao quickly turned on the light. Under the light, Chun Bao can see clearly. This girl looks beautiful and has a beautiful appearance. The rain poured down the tips of her hair, and her beautiful, white-faced pet was even more impressive.
Chun Bao didn't know what to say for a moment. I thought it was raining so much outside, a woman came to ask for help, but she couldn't help but agree. But the house is so small. What should I do if I leave her?
The girl saw that he wasn't talking and said, “If you're really bothered, I'll find another one.” I just turned around and had to go out.
Chun Bao hurriedly stopped the girl and said, “Girl, please stay! Girls, if they don't like the mess here, stay sheltered from the rain.” He thought to himself, if the girl actually left, then I wouldn't be too messy; just stay sheltered from the rain.” He thought in his heart that if the girl actually left, then I would be a woman of benevolence. The girl was very happy and even thanked.
When Chun Bao saw that the girl was all wet, her body trembled a bit. But he didn't have any clothes for her to replace. He thought about it and said to the girl, “You stay warm in the house first; I'll go see the flowers in the garden.” I picked up the coat hanging on the wall and went out the door and left the girl alone in the house. Chun Bao walked around the garden for a while, and saw the rain with no intention of stopping. Walking around the garden, he unwittingly walked to the front of the house and saw that it was already closed. He was about to go ahead and lock the door, but he didn't think it was right. Thinking back and forth, he leaned in the doorroom under the roof and waited.
Just like that, Chun Bao stood outside the gate all night wearing a coat. The rain finally stopped when it was almost bright.
At this point, he heard a “squeak” from the door. The door opened, and the girl walked out and saw Chun Bao wearing a mask, with a tired face. He was very uneasy. He said, “I'm bothering you. I'm really sorry to go. I'll definitely come back another day to thank you.”
Chun Bao repeatedly said, “No, no need.” Looking at the girl's background, I don't know why. Chun Bao feels a bit lost in her face.
Three days later, on a clear night, Chun Bao played the bamboo flute again. The flute sounded melodious and wafted through the night sky. The sound of the flute seemed to have a bit more charm. I don't know how long it's been blowing, and suddenly I hear someone behind me saying, “Brother Chun Bao, your bamboo flute is so good!” Hearing the sound, Chun Bao felt a bit familiar. Chun Bao looked back. Sure enough, it was the girl who came to shelter from the rain that night. The girl held a bamboo basket in her hand and smiled at him slightly.
“It's you! When did you get here? How do you know my name?” Chun Bao asked several questions in a row.
The girl smiled and said, “I've been here for a while now, and I'm so excited to see you playing the flute, I didn't dare to bother you. As for your name, I won't tell you.
The girl took out some fragrant meals from the basket in her hand and took out a pair of brand-new cloth shoes. Chun Bao doesn't know what to say. Since his grandfather died, no one has treated him with such consideration.
When they met again, the two seemed like old friends, and there was no such sense of restraint anymore. At the end of the moon, next to the flowers, they chatted freely. When asked, they both showed their love for each other. Chun Bao didn't want the girl to leave in a hurry, and the girl was always delaying and not leaving. Unbeknownst to me, the day was bright, and the girl had no choice but to get up and say goodbye. After leaving temporarily, the girl said to Chun Bao, “I live in a fence garden in the south of the village. My last name is Lan. If you go to my house in the morning and face this hairpin, we'll be married.” After all, I took a golden jade hairpin off my head and gave it to Chun Bao. It floated away, leaving behind a fragrance.
“It dawned.” Chun Bao recalled what the girl said, and kept thinking about the village south and the fence garden... Suddenly he realized something and was busy walking towards the peonies in the garden. However, when I saw a dudan tree showing up, it was suggested that there was a peony with varieties called Blue and B5. It was dry and loose all over. There was not a drop of dew on it. Chun Bao immediately thought that the girl's last name was Lan. He had already guessed that this blue girl must be a peony fairy. Another thought: Yes! This blue girl stayed in my house all night, and naturally there was no dew. I hurriedly picked up the flower buds. At first glance, the competition was one less. He took out a pair of jade hairpins that the girl gave him, and the peony tree instantly became the Blue Girl from last night: Chun Bao was surprised and happy: Girl Blue! Lan Tianyu, I found you!” Miss Lan also said, “Being able to find hardworking and smart descendants like you is also a lifelong dependable source.”
Since then, the two have become husband and wife. The couple were respectful and loved each other, and had a happy and beautiful life.
