 Duke Chen Baoyao of Qingzhou Dao is from Fujian. One night, he was alone in his study reading a book. A woman lifted the curtain. Chen Gong looked up and didn't recognize this woman. However, she was gorgeous and dressed in palace costumes. The woman smiled and said to Chen Gong, “Don't you feel lonely sitting alone in the middle of a cold and clear night?” Chen Gong was surprised to ask her who she was. The woman said, “The concubine's house is not far away; it's next to your west.” Chen Gong thought she might be a ghost, but he really liked her, so he walked over and took her by the hand and asked her to sit down with her. The woman spoke in an elegant manner. Chen Gong was very appreciative. When she sat next to the woman and hugged her, the woman didn't really refuse. She looked around and said, “Isn't there anyone else in the house?” Chen Gong hurriedly closed the door and said, “There is no one else.” Then I urged the woman to strip and go to bed, but she was very shy and afraid. Chen Gong took off her clothes. The woman said, “Although the concubine is 20 years old, she is still a virgin. It's unbearable to be rough.” As a result, the two were happy, and there was a little bit of blood on the bed sheet. Since they were having a heart-to-heart conversation at the bedside, the woman herself said that her name was Lin Siniang. Chen Gong asked her about her life again. She said, “I have been strong all my life, and now I'm being scrupulous enough by you. If you love me and want to be good friends forever, why do you have to ask more?” After a while, the chicken called Tianming, she got up and left.
From then on, girls must come night after night. Every time they came, they both closed the door to drink and talk very speculatively. When it comes to musical rhythm, Lin Si-niang is very proficient. Gong Chen said, “You will definitely sing a song.” Si Niang said, “I learned a bit when I was a kid.” Chen Gong asked her to sing a song. She said, “I haven't sung for a long time. I've forgotten most of the rhythms of the scale. I'm afraid singing is an insider joke.” Chen Gong requested it over and over again before she bowed her head to beat the rhythm and sang Yi Liang's song, and her tone of grievance softened. After singing, I started crying. Chen Gong was also moved by her. He was sad and saddened. He stepped forward and hugged Si Niang to comfort him and said, “Don't sing songs from the dead country; it's depressing.” Si Niang said, “Music expresses people's feelings. A sad person can't call him happy, just as a happy person can't call him sad.”
Gong Chen and his fourth wife are very close; they are like a married couple. It's been a long time, and everyone in the family knows, and they all come outside the house to eavesdrop on the song. Everyone who has heard her sing doesn't shed tears. Mrs. Chen has secretly seen the Four Wives. She doubts that there are no such enchanting women in the world. She thinks it's either a ghost or a fox. Fearing that Chen Gong will be haunted by demons, she persuaded Chen Gong to become divorced from the woman. Gong Chen didn't listen. He still wanted to ask Si Niang for understanding, so he asked Si Niang again about her life. The fourth woman was unhappy and said, “The concubine was a court lady from Heng Wangfu back then. It has been 17 years since she died in a disaster. Because of your elegance and righteousness, I am on good terms with you, and I really don't dare to hurt you. If you doubt or are afraid of me, we'll break up.” Chen Gong immediately said, “I'm not skeptical, nor afraid. Now that we're on good terms, I must not know your truth.” Gong Chen also asked about what was happening in the palace at the time. Si Niang's recollections are detailed, well-organized, and very moving. When it comes to the decline of Wangfu, I choked and cried; I couldn't make a sound.
Siniang doesn't sleep much at night, and she gets up every night to read the Junti Sutra, the King Kong Sutra, etc. Chen Gong asked her, “Can you surpass yourself under Jiuquan?” She answered, “Yes! The concubine wants to be destroyed for the rest of her life, and she wants to be good in life.” Si Niang also often comments on poetry with Chen Gong, criticizing bad sentences, and singing quietly for good sentences. They are affectionate and merry, making people forget they are tired. Chen Gong asked her, “Have you ever written poetry?” Si Niang replied, “I also wrote it occasionally when I was alive.” Chen Gong asked her to give her a poem. She smiled and said, “How can a child's poem be called a master joke?”
I stayed for another three years. One night, four women came to announce goodbye to Chen. Her face was miserable. Chen Gong was shocked and hurriedly asked, “What happened?” Si Niang said, “Hades asked me to give birth to the royal family because I was innocent during my lifetime and didn't forget to read the scriptures after my death. We are parting ways today, and we will never see each other again.” To put it bluntly, describe it as sad. Gong Chen also shed tears and immediately arranged drinks to see off the fourth lady. While drinking alcohol, the four ladies sang generously. The lyrics and songs were sad and bleak, turning word by word. When they reached the point of sadness, they choked and couldn't make a sound. At the end of the day, I finally finished singing a song. Si Niang was in a bad mood and wasn't very interested in drinking. She got up and wandered around and wanted to say goodbye. Chen Gong couldn't bear to part ways and held her for a while. It wasn't until the chicken sang Tianming that the fourth lady said to Gong Chen, “You must never stay any longer! Every time you blame me for refusing to write a poem, today we will say goodbye forever. I should write a poem for you to commemorate your farewell.” So I picked up the pen, swung it out, and said, “I'm sad, I can't scrutinize it, the syllable is wrong, don't take it out and ask others to read it.” After speaking, cover up and walk down.
Chen Gong sent his four daughters out and disappeared in a blink of an eye. Gong Chen was disappointed for a long time. When I read the poem written by Si Niang, the font was correct and the writing was neat, and I collected it with great treasure. The poem is: “After being locked in the deep palace for 17 years, who will ask the country to Qingtian? Take a look at the arbors in the garden and cry at Wang Hua's rhododendron. The waves of the sea were shining at sunset, and the Han family was beeping and blazing. It is difficult to do anything if her face is weak; Hui Zhi is sad and only meditates. Recite hundreds of sayings a day and take a moment to read two or three Bayeux essays. Sing the Liyuan song instead of crying; please listen to it alone and it will blow your mind.” There are repeated disconnections in the poem, and I doubt that there seems to be a misunderstanding.
