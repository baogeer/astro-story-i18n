 When Lady Meng Jiang fell over the Great Wall, it just so happened that Emperor Qin Shi Huang visited the Great Wall of China. Seeing that Meng Jiang was beautiful, he wanted to be considered a concubine. No matter how willing the woman Meng Jiang was to obey, she couldn't help but fight hard with this tyrant. However, at this point, she was nine months pregnant, and in order to preserve Wan Xiliang's roots, she turned around and fled. Qin Shi Huang sent a team of gozen warriors to catch up closely.
Lady Meng Jiang fled to the edge of the Wu River. There were no boats before, and there were chasers later. My stomach ached for a while. I really couldn't. I jumped into the Wujiang River as soon as I got involved.
On this day, fisherman Xiang Liang was fishing on the Wujiang River. He saw black crows on the surface of the river in the distance and slowly drifted towards the fishing boat. As soon as they “beep” in front of the boat, a group of crows flew up, and a newborn baby surfaced on the surface of the water. There is also quite a bit of fluff sticking to my body. Xiang Liang hurriedly hugged the child and saw another woman knocking her head in the water and disappeared. Xiang Liang adopted this child. There was no milk on board, so Xiang Liang fed it with fish broth. Kids get along well when they suck fish soup. Xiang Liang named the child Xiang Yu. Xiang Yu grew up, and became the enemy of Qin Shi Huang.
