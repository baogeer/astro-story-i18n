 There is a very small, very small house in the valley. A very old, very old couple lives in the house. Their hair is white like a conch, and they don't even have pearl-sized teeth in their mouths. One day, I didn't know why. The old couple got into a fight. The more they fought, the worse they got, and they were still arguing about breaking up. In other words, their family is so poor that they can play with sticks; they don't have anything decent to share; they only have an old and thin goat. The old man said, “Fuck! Fuck! I'll grab the sheep's head, you grab the sheep's tail. If you see who the sheep follows, the goat will go to who, OK?” The old lady didn't figure anything out, so she nodded in agreement. When the two pull it up, of course the goat follows the old man! The old lady knew she had been fooled and held on by the goat's tail. In the end, she broke the goat's tail and fell to the ground, watching the old man take the goat away. She got mad and threw the goat's tail in the cow dung heap.
In winter, the north wind howled around the cottage, and snowflakes knocked on her doors and windows. The old lady was cold and hungry. She looked east and west in the landlord to see if there was anything to fill her belly. Finally, in the cow dung heap, I found that goat's tail again; it had already dried very hard. The old lady is holding it like holding a baby:
“Haha! Hot show! Hot show!”
Careful, she put the dried goat's tail in a millstone, calculated to smash it to pieces, put in a little green onion, and boil a can of earthen to keep her body warm. Who would have thought that the stone had just hit it, and the goat's tail jumped “miso”, and kept beating around the old lady, and shouted, “! Fuck! Don't hit me! Don't hit me!”
A goat's tail actually speaks human language, and the old lady is almost frightened and paralyzed. I had no choice but to throw down the stone, sigh, and say, “Oops! My life is so bitter that I don't even have the blessing of eating a sheep's tail!”
“Fuck! Fuck! Don't cry! Don't cry!” Reksu Kanba said, “Mo” jumped into the old lady's arms, jumped up again to help her wipe away her tears, and quietly said, “You can treat me like a son! There will be ghee; there will be beef; there will be pickles”.
The old lady smiled bitterly and shook her head in disbelief: “Alas, when you grow up, my bones will rust a long time ago!”
On the hot show, she said to the old lady, “Huh! I'm gone!” It sneaked out through a slit in the door with a “whistling” sound. In the middle of the night, the old lady was sleeping and heard a hot show knock on the door from outside: “Huh! I'm back, open the door! Open the door quickly!”
“You haven't got a big mouse yet, get in through the door! Fly in from the skylight!” The old lady froze and shivered. Her stomach was so hungry that she lay on a broken mat and didn't want to move.
“Fuck! I brought something to eat, brought something to drink, come and carry it! Hurry up and carry it!” Hot Show Ganbo shouted with vigor.
When the old lady hummed and opened the door, she actually saw a beef leg, a loaf of ghee, and a big bag of pickles on the outside. She laughed and almost dropped her chin, grabbed the goat's tail and kept kissing it on the face.
It turns out that the goat's tail flew outside a large manor house and saw a few thieves punching a hole in the wall. Some were carrying beef, some were carrying ghee, some were carrying pickles, and the goat's tail quietly followed them. The thieves were bent at the waist, and their legs were shaking when they were tired. Looking east and west, they were terrified. As soon as they walked past the old lady's door, the goat's tail shouted, “Catch the thief! Catch the thief!” The thieves thought the people in the manor had chased them, left their stuff, and fled for their lives like rabbits. Ghee, beef, and pickles were obediently returned to the old lady.
Shortly after, the princess, who lives in the castle, asked her old lady to wash her hair. While brushing her hair, the concubine said, “Fuck! Your robe is getting more and more greasy, and your face is getting better and better. Looks like you've split up with the old man and had good luck!” In a few words, the princess said, the old lady's heart was sweet, her tongue was itchy, and Yang Yang proudly said, “My son is very talented! No matter what the baby is, he can get it!”
These few words made the princess jealous. She sat on a treasure mat. She didn't drink tea, nor did she drink alcohol. Her mouth was so small that she could hang a snuff bottle. This frightened the king. Asking from the left to the right, and from the east to exhort the west, the princess tearfully said, “Thanks to you, you're still the head of a country, and you haven't even had a hot show!”
The king figured out the situation, sent someone to call the old lady, and scolded, “Bah! You three-tongued old lady whispering all that nonsense in her ear! Your son is really good at showing off, so let him steal my life tonight! If you steal it, I'll give you whatever you want. If I can't steal it, I'll kill you black crow!”
The old lady was terrified to death, and she didn't know how she got out of Wangcheng. Along the way, she also punched herself in the mouth three times and said, “Old lady who can't control her tongue, now it's too late to put nine locks on her mouth.” While cursing herself, she quickly ran away. When I got home, I didn't see a goat's tail. I had to cry and shout, “Come on! goat tail! Come on! 1 Hot Show!”
“Fuck! Fuck! What's the matter? What's the matter?” The goat's tail was sleeping on a cow dung cake, and it stretched out a slack. The old lady talked about the events in the palace from beginning to end. When the king wanted to kill her, she cried again with sadness.
“Fuck! Don't cry, I'll go get the king's life root jade!” When the goat's tail was over, it jumped over to the old lady and pulled back.
Also, after the old lady left, the king thought, “Hey, if you lose your life, it's no joke. Whether or not her son is good at showing off, I have to be careful.” He dispatched 500 guards, who were strictly guarded under the city wall; prepared 500 fast horses with good saddles; also brought in 500 muskets full of gunpowder; and brought out 500 vicious dogs and tied them tightly at the entrance of the city. At the same time, ask the maids to hold torches in their hands, and the male maids carry gongs and mallets. As soon as the heat wave hits, the gongs are lit immediately, and the whole city is on the move. He thought, “Hot show dry waves just have bird wings, and they can't escape the king's palm.
In the middle of the night, it flew into the royal palace with the sound of “miso” in the middle of the night. Seeing the king's arrangement, I “popped” and laughed. It brought 500 horses into the bullpen and brought 500 cows to the horse chain; it drove 500 dogs past the sheep fence and chased 500 sheep out of the leash; it sent 500 muskets into the firehouse and left 500 firewood sticks where the guns were fired. While the maid is sleeping, wrap a large handful of straw around her head; while the male maid doesn't pay attention, scatter some peas under his feet.
Once these things were done, it flew secretly into the king's bedroom. When I saw the Gem of Life, the title was in the king's mouth. The king was sleepy and handed it to the concubine; when the concubine was sleepy, she handed it to the king's mouth and handed it back and forth. The more I watched the hot show, the more interesting it was. He then slipped into the middle of his two mouths and picked up the jade lightly and smartly. With a “whistling” sound, it flew out of the window, and flew over the city wall, and flew all the way back to the old lady's house.
The king and his concubine disappeared. The two ran to the roof and shouted, “Arrest people quickly! Hurry up and catch someone! The hot show season is here!” As soon as the maid heard it, she hurriedly lit the fire. She didn't expect to burn the straw on her head. The male maid ran to knock on the gong and stepped on the peas, and the family rolled down the stairs.
When the 500 Guards heard the order, they quickly got on the horse, picked up the musket, let go of the evil dog, opened the city gate, and chased it down without life. They shouted loudly, “Go One” and “Ho One,” and kept hitting the horse. However, the horse wouldn't run, and the dog wouldn't bark at all; the gun didn't fire at all. At dawn, the guards themselves laughed because they were riding cows, carrying firewood sticks, and carrying old sheep.
The king had no choice but to make peace with the old lady. He summoned the old lady and even praised her: “Aahehe, your son has shown great success. He's an amazing hero! Please ask him to give me back the Gem of Life, what do you want. I'll give you anything!”
The old lady and the goat tail hidden in her sleeve all laughed out loud.
