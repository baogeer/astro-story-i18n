 Sanniang is a beautiful girl from a poor background and is very kind. She is deeply in love with a young Zhuang people named “Land”.
Later, when a senior official, the Meng Guan, saw the Three Wives, he salivated. He wanted to force the “Three Wives” to be his concubine. The “Three Wives” were harshly rejected by the “Three Wives,” so the Mongolian government killed the “land” and robbed the “Three Wives.” The “Three Wives” fought back heroically. Under the cover and help of their maid, Aunt Zhang, they killed the Mongolian government, took revenge, and escaped from the tiger's mouth.
When she arrived in front of the tomb of the “land,” the tomb suddenly collapsed, and she jumped into the cave; a cloud of white smoke immediately rose, and a bunch of white honeysuckle flowers bloomed at the entrance of the cave.
It praises the steadfast love between young men and women and the fighting spirit against the evil forces of the feudal system. Today, the Yifeng Brigade in the Maonan region also has a village called “Sanniang Village.”
