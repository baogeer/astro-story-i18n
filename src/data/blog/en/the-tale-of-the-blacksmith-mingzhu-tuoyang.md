 On the blue Lhasa River, there are three golden waterfowl. They are in love with each other on a blind date. If you want to fly, fly together; if you want to land, land together. Of these three waterfowl, one is an avatar of the blacksmith Pearl Tuo Yang, one is an avatar of Miss Lang Ruo, and the other is an avatar of their two children. The old people said, “Speaking of these three birds, there is also a sad legend!
Legend has it that many years ago, there was a young blacksmith on the banks of the Lhasa River. This young man was strong and bright, with snow-white teeth and dark, dark eyes. He was happy from morning to night, and smiled when he saw the stones. Not to mention his skill as a blacksmith. A hammer is like a magician, so people call him Gala Pearl Support Center.
He had no parents on him, no wife around him, like a free-spirited bird, like a cloud that appears every now and then. The little donkey, carrying blacksmith tools, wanders all over the long Lhasa River.
As the saying goes, there are no flying birds in the sky that don't land, and there are no unattended serfs on earth. The leader of Pearl Tuo Yang is Chief Lang Ruoben, who has power like a majestic snowy mountain and a raging fire. Every year in spring and autumn, he usually has to go to the Chancellery branch to beat the iron. To soak up the sweat he shed here, it's enough to pack a few cowhide boats.
In the spring of this year, the cuckoo called, and Pearl Tuoyang arrived as usual. At the wheat farm in front of the governor's office, two tents were set up, a black cow hair tent, where Zhu Ming and Tuo Yang worked iron; a blue flower tent with a white background, where the owner supervised the work of the blacksmith.
The chief's grandfather was very uneasy about the blacksmith and sent his own daughter to monitor him. He said, “Daughter! You go to that white tent and watch the boy from Pearl Tuo Yang work. Don't let him be lazy; watch out for him stealing things. Remember: a blacksmith's soul is dark, and his bones are also dark. Whoever's shadow is reflected in a blacksmith's body will have to shed mold for the rest of his life; you must never talk to him casually!”
At first, the young lady was as afraid of Pearl Tuo Yang as she was afraid of the plague. Pearl Tuo Yang spoke to her, but she was afraid to answer; Pearl Tuo Yang walked up to her, and she quickly dodged. Pearl Tuo Yang doesn't care about this, but is still happy to work and sing happily. After a few days, the young lady felt that her father was wrong; she secretly fell in love with Little Blacksmith. She thought the figure of the blacksmith was as cute as the prince; the sound of the iron smashing sounds as good as Xianle.
When eating, the blacksmith ate green tea and pickles, and the young lady ate milk tea and sesame seeds. The young lady then sang to the black cow fur tent:
Please listen to it.
Blacksmith Pearl Tower:
It's not interesting to sit alone,
Please sit down with me!
It's tasteless when eaten alone,
Please come to me to eat and drink!
Pearl Tuo listened and answered with a laugh and singing voice:
Please listen to it.
Grandpa Lang Ruo's girl:
Blacksmith, I'm used to sitting alone,
It's also comfortable to sit like this;
Blacksmith, I'm used to eating alone.
It's also sweet and savory when eaten like this.
Pearl Tuo Yang didn't come. The young lady was empty. She didn't want to eat pine, and she didn't want to drink milk tea. She walked over and got into the black cow wool tent, touched it, fiddled with it, and sang:
Please listen to it.
Blacksmith Pearl Tower:
A thunderbolt hammer,
Shall I do the whipping? Blacksmith!
A wind-like bellows box,
Shall I come to Lala? Blacksmith!
Lightning pliers.
Shall I take the palm of my hand? Blacksmith!
After singing, I'll have to work for him. Pearl Tuo Yang was in a hurry and sweated and sang:
Please go away quickly,
Grandpa Lang Ruo's girl:
You've stained your shirt,
I can't pay compensation, miss!
It hurt your irony,
How can I take it, miss!
It's exhausting your body,
The old man will take me to his cell, miss! No matter how the Pearl Tuo Yang advises or blocks her, the young lady always stays by the blacksmith's fireside every day. No matter what, she takes three trips and nine trips; she doesn't have to say three words, she says nine sentences. When the young man was working on an iron, the young lady helped him pull the air box; when the young man was eating chopsticks, the young lady poured him tea. One day is short and two days long. When Pearl Tuo Yang left the Chancellery, the two felt that no one could live without anyone.
The days of parting ways are getting longer day by day, and their thoughts are getting deeper day by day. The young lady heard that Zhu Tuoyang was living in Grandma Nimalazhen's house, so she secretly escaped from the government office and came looking for him. Pearl Tuo Yang saw the young lady and was so happy that she sang:
Please listen to it.
Grandma Mara Jane:
Miss Lang Ruoben is here,
Please lend me a porcelain bowl;
Miss Lang Ruoben is here,
Please lend me a pair of card pads.
While entering the door, the young lady sings:
Please listen to it,
Blacksmith Pearl Tower:
I don't want any card pads,
It's good to pad your sheepskin;
I don't want any porcelain bowls,
Just drink your wooden bowl”.
Just like that, they had a simple wedding in the blacksmith's shed. Pearl Tuo Yang's craftsmanship is excellent. They are rushing to get their iron tools everywhere. The two had a lively and lively life. Soon, the young lady became pregnant again. They are looking forward to the birth of a baby every day, and their lives are even more interesting.
One day, the young lady came back from outside and saw that the blacksmith shed had been torn down, the blacksmith furnace had been destroyed, and the blacksmith's Pearl Tuo Yang had disappeared. Grandma Nimala Jane told her that Elder Lang Ruoben sent a bunch of people to arrest the little blacksmith.
The young lady quickly ran home. Grandpa ignored her, Grandma ignored her, and the housekeeper Morimoto ignored her. She searched upstairs, downstairs, in the front yard, and in the back yard. Finally, her old grandmother, who was milking milk, secretly told her that the Pearl Tower was in the dungeon. She ran to the entrance of the dungeon and locked four large iron locks on the door. The young lady found an axe and smashed it, smashed it once, twice... Suddenly, her stomach hurt. That's when the poor kid was born.
She climbed into the firewood pile, cut off her umbilical cord, tore a piece of clothing to wrap the child, and came to find her beloved husband, only to find the cell door wide open. There was a pool of blood in the cell and a pool of blood outside the cell. Pearl Tuo Yang had unknowingly taken there by them.
How sad the lady is!
Carrying the baby, she ran outside with the blood stains. On the path in front of the door, she saw the housekeeper riding a horse. The young lady quickly took off “Ba Zhu” from her head and sang:
Please listen to it,
Chief Lang Ruo's steward:
“Bazhu” that works for you,
I'll give you one;
Words that worked for me,
Please answer:
Ironsmith Pearl Tuo Yang,
Where is it locked now?
The housekeeper reached over and grabbed “Ba Zhu” and arrogantly said, “I don't know, go ask Morimoto! “After speaking, I hit the horse and left without going back.
The young lady walked forward, walked forward, and met Morimoto riding over. She hurriedly took off the “Gau” on her chest and sang:
Please listen to it,
Chief Rongruo's Morimoto:
“Gau” that is useful to you,
I'll give you one;
Words that worked for me,
Please answer:
Ironsmith Pearl Tuo Yang,
Where is it locked now?
Morimoto tucked “Gau” into his sleeve and as he walked, he answered, “I don't know, go ask the donkey man! “It just went away in a big way.
Shortly after the young lady left, she met a donkey man. She repeated what she had just said and gave him the coral rosary in her hand. The donkey stammered and said, “Poor blacksmith, beaten to death by the old man; his body was just thrown into the Lhasa River! “When I finished speaking, I ran away like I was running for my life!
When the young lady heard the bad news, she suddenly passed out on the ground. It took half a day and a half a day before she woke up. She cried and climbed. I don't know how long it took to finally get to the edge of the Lhasa River. I saw the rolling river flowing, making waves layer by layer. Pearl Tuo Yang's corpse swirled and wandered on the waves.
Miss Lang Ruo stopped crying because her tears dried up; the Lang Ruo team was not worried because the grief had come to an end. She brushed her hair, straightened her clothes, nursed her children, and sang happily:
Are you waiting for me? Are you expecting me?
I'm here, here I am,
I came with iron tools.
I'm here, here I am,
Here comes the baby with his heart on his back,
Since it's hard to be a couple in this world,
Then go to heaven to form a pair.
After singing, hold the child and leap into the rolling river. At this point, Pearl Tuo Yang's body followed suit and sank deep into the water. Next, music sounded at the bottom of the river, and golden light flashed in the water. Three golden birds flew out of the golden light and circled and sang on the blue Lhasa River.
