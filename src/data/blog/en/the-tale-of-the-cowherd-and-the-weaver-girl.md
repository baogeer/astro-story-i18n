 The cowherd and the weaver are mythical characters derived from the star names Morning Glory Star and Weaver.
A long, long time ago, an orphan lived with his brother and sister-in-law. My brother and sister-in-law treated him very badly. They told him to eat leftovers, wear clothes, and rush him up the mountain to herd cows every day when it wasn't bright. He doesn't have a name; everyone calls him a cowherd.
The cowherd looked after that cow very carefully. Every day he herds a cow, he always picks the best grass and lets it eat the fat and tender grass; when the old cow is thirsty, he takes it to the upper reaches of the creek to drink the cleanest stream. That old cow is also very close to him. He often looks at him with a gentle eye, and sometimes sticks out his tongue to lick his hand.
One night, the cowherd walked into the barn and suddenly heard a “cowherd!” , who called him? Looking back, under the faint starlight, it turned out that the old cow was talking. Lao Niu said, “Tomorrow at dusk, you will go over the mountain on the right. There is a lake on the side of the mountain, and there is a forest of woods by the lake. In the woods, you'll meet a beautiful girl. Don't miss this opportunity!”
At dusk the next day, the cowherd turned over the mountain on the right and came to the woods by the lake. Suddenly, there was a light laughter in the distance. The cowherd followed the laughter, only to see a few girls playing by the lake. After a while, one of them left his partner and walked towards the woods. Who is this girl? It turns out that she is the granddaughter of the Queen Mother. She is a master weaver. Her name is Weaving Nu. Every morning and evening, Mrs. Wang adorns the sky with colored brocade that she weaves; that is, brilliant clouds. This afternoon, Mrs. Wang drank a few more glasses of wine and fell asleep on her throne. When the weaver and the fairies saw a rare opportunity, they flew to the world together.
The cowherd and the weaver met in the woods. During the conversation, the cowherd understood the identity of the weaver, and the weaver also learned about the cowherd's experience. When the weaver saw that the cowherd had a good heart and was able to bear hardships, she decided to stay on earth and be the cowherd's wife.
Since then, cowherds have cultivated fields, and weavers have woven at home. The two worked hard and had a very happy life. In a blink of an eye, three years passed, and they had a son and a daughter.
One day, the cowherd went to feed the cow. The old cow spoke again, and tears filled his eyes. It said, “I can't help you work on the ground anymore! We're about to break up! After I die, you peel off my skin and keep it; in case of an emergency, you put my skin on...” The old cow died before he finished talking.
Furthermore, when Wang Mu Niang learned that the weaver had married on earth, she was furious and vowed to take the weaver back to heaven and severely punish her.
One day, Mother Wang took advantage of the cowherd to work in the field and led General Tianbing to break into the cowherd's house to catch the weaver. The two children ran over and grabbed their mom's clothes to death. Mother Wang and her mother pushed hard, and the two children fell to the ground. Mother Wang dragged the weaver and flew to Tiangong together. While struggling, the weaver looked at the two children and shouted, “Go find Dad soon.”
The cowherd was distraught when he learned that Weaver had been taken away by the princess's daughter. But how can heaven save them? Suddenly, he remembered what the old cow said before he died, so he quickly found the cowhide, draped it on his body, then placed one son and one daughter in two bamboo baskets, picked it up, and ran outside. As soon as he left the house, he flew up. The faster he flew, the faster he was about to catch up with the weaver. Mother Wang took off the jade hairpin from her head and stroked it behind her. Suddenly, a Tianhe appeared in front of the cowherd. The Tianhe River is so wide that the cowherd can't fly past it.
Since then, the cowherd is on this side of the Tianhe River and the weaver is on the other side of the Tianhe River; the two can only see each other across the river. After a long time, they became morning glory stars and weavers on both sides of the Tianhe River.
Every year on the night of July 7 of the lunar calendar, a group of magpies fly in and build a “magpie bridge” on the Tianhe River, so that the cowherd and the weaver meet on the bridge. On this day, there are indeed a lot fewer magpies; it is said that they have all gone to the Tianhe River to build a bridge.
