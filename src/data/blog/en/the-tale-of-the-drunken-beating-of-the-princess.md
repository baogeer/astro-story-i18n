 Guo Neng, the sixth son of Guo Ziyi, a hero in the Tang dynasty, was recruited by Dai Zong as his wife and married Princess Shengping. Princess Shengping is proud of herself, and her husband is restrained by royal court rules and royal court rules everywhere. Although Guo Nan was dissatisfied, there was nothing she could do about it. One day, Guo Ziyi made a living. Guo Nan's brother and sister made fun of Guo Wenfeng and feared for not coming to pray for her life because she was promoted. Guo Wen was furious and went back to the palace to evaluate and slap the princess. The princess was furious. When she went home, she cried to her father, king, and mother, demanding that Guo Wen be punished for getting mad at her. After understanding the cause of the little couple's quarrel, Daizong and the Empress blamed their daughter for not praying for her birthday. However, the princess refused to admit her mistakes and was simply pompous. Dai Zong pretended to be serious. He wanted to get mad at Guo Wen, which frightened the princess and ran out of ideas. Guo Ziyi learned that Guo Wen had beaten the princess, and kidnapped her to the palace to plead guilty. Dai Zong's unwillingness to take care of his children's private affairs hurt the monarch's peace. Not only did he pardon Guo Wen for his faults, but he also promoted her official position to show respect, and ordered the princess to worship her father-in-law for her life. Finally, the emperor ordered that the young couple be exempted from all court rules and royal ceremonies, and persuaded the little couple to make peace as before.
