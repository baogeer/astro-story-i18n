 Legend has it that the Prince of Shule Guo was injured while hunting in the mountains. He was saved by a beautiful shepherd woman named Yu, and often used the reason to hunt and date shepherds; the shepherd woman was also moved by the prince's sincere feelings and fell in love with the prince. King Shule was furious when he learned about this incident. He trapped the female shepherd prisoner in a maze in the back mountains of Shule Guo without the prince's knowledge. The shepherd spent the whole day in the valley sad and in tears because she missed her prince, but she had no way out of the maze.
When the prince found out, he only went to the valley to find his loved one. He called out the name of and searched many valleys. He finished eating the dry food and drank all the water. After searching for seven days and seven nights, he still hasn't found his loved one. However, the prince continued his search in the valley and was exhausted. The desperate prince poured all his strength into the valley and shouted the name of the mountain. The sound shocked the mountain. The mountain instantly split a canyon, and the prince fell unconscious beside Lianxin Creek. At this point, the shepherdess also heard the prince's call. Her tears welled up like a spring. Tears poured into a stream and flowed down the valley to the prince. The prince felt Yu's tears. He knew he was not far from him, so he picked up his spirits and continued his search along the stream, but due to lack of physical strength, the prince fell. Trapped in a valley, she kept crying for 49 days and 49 nights. Her tears were dry and her eyes were blind. In the end, she became a stream, hoping that one day the prince would follow the stream to find her. Due to the irrigation of the stream, a dense euphratica forest grew in the place where the prince died. The stream and euphratica forest, which have not changed for thousands of years, have been entangled in this valley forever. Descendants named this valley the Valley of Love in memory of this loyal couple. To remember this loyal couple, posterity called this low recess between the two mountains “Love Valley,” and the creek incarnated as a shepherd was called “Lianxin Creek.”
