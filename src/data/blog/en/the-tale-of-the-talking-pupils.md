He saw that the curtains of the car were being pulled open. There was a woman sitting in the car. She was sitting in the car. Her makeup was very gorgeous; she had never seen it before in her life. Dazzled and unconscious, Fang Dong followed the front and back of the car and couldn't bear to leave. He followed him for several miles. I overheard the girl in the car calling her maid to the side of the car and said, “Put down the curtains for me. Where did such an arrogant scholar come from so often to take a peek.” The maid put down the curtain and looked at Fang Dong in anger and said, “This is Shichiro's bride from Fuyu City returning to her mother's house. At this point, Fang Dong felt extremely remorseful; he was very ashamed of his misconduct.
