 The story of the White Dragon River (1)
A long time ago, the eastern part of Laiyang City was a vast ocean, which fell under the control of Dragon King in the East China Sea. Dragon King of the East China Sea is very greedy and very harsh on the people. Every year, when Emperor Jade Emperor distributes rain to the world, he has to cut back on it. As a result, the earth often experiences droughts, leaving the world in trouble.
Lao Dragon King has five sons, the eldest son Bai Long, the second son Black Dragon, the third son Qinglong, the fourth son Xuanlong, and the fifth son Fulong. Among the five sons, Bai Long is hardworking and kind, and he wants to do more good things for the people. Black Dragon is delicious and lazy to cook. He often makes waves on earth, damaging crops and seedlings, and the people hate and fear him. As the eldest son, Bai Long harshly taught his older brother every act. However, instead of listening, the Black Dragon is holding a grudge, and the two dragons often make trouble. Although a few younger brothers had feelings for the older brother, helplessly, their father always favors the Black Dragon.
There is a village by the sea called Chujiazhuang. Zhuang has a first-time father and first-time mother. The older couple had no children under their lap and lived a poor life. When Bai Long saw that the two old people were poor, he often went ashore to help the elderly plow fields and cultivate land. Whichever family in the village built a house or wall, there was a red and white happy event, and White Dragon also went to help. The people in Zhuang were very affectionate to Little Bailong like a family. Black Dragon was sick and jealous, so he went to the old dragon king to confess his appearance and talk bad about it. Lao Long Wang was very angry when he heard it. He scolded Bai Long for being the prince, mixing with the people and not doing anything. Finally, the old dragon king sighed and said, “I'm getting old too. How can I be relieved to take over the throne like you.”
The last words of the old dragon king shocked Black Dragon's heart.
Spring has arrived. One day, Old Dragon King will travel all over the world with Dragon King of the West Sea. Before leaving, Lao Long Wang called the brothers Bai Long and Black Dragon to the front and said, “I'm going out for a while now, and you two will be in charge of everything in the Dragon Palace.” Finally, the fire cards, water cards, rain cards, and wind cards in Dragon Palace cannot be used indiscriminately.
The old dragon king took off. The black dragon looked at the fire card and wind card and evaded the circle in his heart. He thought in his heart that I would use fire cards and wind cards to hurt the people. Bai Long will definitely think about saving them. At that point... haha! Thinking about this, Black Dragon couldn't help but show a wicked laugh on his face. In the middle of the night, Black Dragon saw that everyone was asleep. He secretly removed the fire and wind cards from the treasure pot, and came ashore, shook the second card, and cast a spell. Suddenly, the heatwave surged with the wind, and rolled up the leaves. Seven days in a row, every night. At this point, the hard working crop farmers panicked. Crops are the lifeblood of the people, huh! Seeing dried up seedlings, people were distraught, so they helped the elderly and children, knelt down and prayed in front of Longwang Temple to pray for rain, causing much suffering.
Seeing the people in pain, Bai Long was extremely anxious and uneasy to sit and lie down. The Black Dragon, on the other hand, swims leisurely, which is a surprise.
Suddenly, Bai Long thought of his father and king's rain cards, and sincerely asked his younger brother, Heilong, to discuss saving the people with rain cards. When Black Dragon saw White Dragon's plan, he was overjoyed and pretentiously said, “The people were really pitiful, but the big brother had to think twice about using rain cards. Have you forgotten your father and king's instructions to go ahead?” When Black Dragon said this, White Dragon hesitated for a moment: Yes, his father's life must not be violated! When Hei Long saw White Dragon hesitating, he said with ulterior motives, “However, the father and king are not at home. The elder brother is better than his father. Saving people is like fighting a fire; the older brother just watched.” As soon as Bai Long thought about it, he resolutely took out the rain sign. When walking at night, the southeast wind blew up and down, and the people suddenly heard the sound of rain loud at night, and they all climbed up and watched with joy. The next day, after the rain cleared up, people beat the gongs and drums, carried the three sacrifices, and went to the seaside to thank Dragon King for saving his life.
Unbeknownst to me, half a month has passed, and the old dragon king has returned to the palace. When Black Dragon heard it, he hurriedly went to Lao Long Wang to file a complaint with the former villain. It was obvious that Bai Long didn't listen to persuasion and used Rain Pai Yunyun without permission. Lao Youyuan Wang didn't ask anything clearly. All of a sudden, he shivered all over his body, and his sweat began to rise, and he couldn't speak clearly: “Huh, huh? ... A traitor who created evil, how can I explain this?” It turns out that these Three God cards must be handed down by the Emperor Jade before they can be used; otherwise, it is a crime of deceiving the king. At a time when the old dragon king was helpless, suddenly a white cloud fell from the sky. Above the clouds, Taibai real Xuanyu Emperor's motto: Xuan Dong, Hai, Dragon King went to Heaven to question the unauthorized use of the Three Gods of Wind, Fire, and Rain. After listening, the old dragon king suddenly fell to the ground and shouted angrily: “Get the White Dragon out of me; he will never return!”
Bai Long was forced out of Longting and evicted from the East China Sea. He was forced to be helpless. As long as he wandered around the field as a student with a white face, he walked and went to his familiar home. On the big river at the head of the village, he was hungry and thirsty. He threw up all the stars in front of him and fell all over the river. Everyone knows that dragons are inseparable from water. In the water, White Dragon regained its strength. After fighting this, Bai Long settled down in this river. During the day, he went ashore as a young man to help people work, and returned to the river at night. Because of him, the river was cleaner, and the water was more plentiful than before.
Soon, Black Dragon learned about this again. One night, Black Dragon quietly stole the fire card and came to the river, shook the sign, cast spells, and burnt the river. Thanks to the kind Phoenix Mountain God who lives upstream, he discovered Black Dragon's intentions early on and released more spring water, which saved White Dragon's life. However, White Dragon's ordinary jade scales have been burned off, and they can no longer be turned ashore to help the people with their work.
Since then, White Dragon has never left this river. The river is murmuring. The four seasons are clear, the water source is rich, and it continues to flow. The people on both sides of the river live and work peacefully, and Lin Mao is rich in food. In order to repay White Dragon's kindness and to commemorate White Dragon, people built a temple called the White Dragon Temple on the mountain beside the river. This river is also called the White Dragon River. As for the Black Dragon, it was also because he steals magic cards and maimed people. He was discovered by Emperor Yu Huang and distributed to the far north to guard the Heilongjiang River. However, he also regrets it and wants to go back to his hometown to repay the people's forgiveness for him!
The story of the White Dragon River (2)
A long, long time ago, there was a blue dragon in the Bailong River. It was a famous strong dragon near and far. Normally, after eating and drinking enough, Qinglong lies under the water and sleeps soundly. As soon as he wakes up, it's been over a month. Along both sides of the river, the seedlings are half dead and dying. It hurriedly took a big sip of water, shook its head and tail, and soared up in the air. Suddenly, dark clouds overturned, the wind was loud, and heavy rain poured down. People on both sides of the river are full of grievances.
Suddenly, another little white dragon flew into this river. Its body was thin and long, its scales flashed silver all over its body, and its eyes were bright and bright. Little White Dragon often flies to the sky to carefully inspect crops on both sides of the Strait, or for three days and five days, it swims lightly at night, slowly spitting clouds, and the drizzle silently warms the green mountains and penetrates the fields.
After dawn, Little White Dragon returned to the river. The sky changed from rain to clear, windy and sunny. As a result, there was plenty of forest and food on both sides of the river, and happy laughter came from the family.
One night, a village patriarch suddenly discovered a small white dragon leaping out of the river. Soon, heavy rain fell. He quickly knelt down on the ground and knocked his head three times. The next day, they also led young and old from the village to the riverside to burn paper and incense. Some people also threw steamed buns into the river to wish Bailong a long life, bless the people along the river, and live and work peacefully.
However, when Qinglong learned about this incident, he was annoyed and became angry, so he often provoked trouble and wanted to put Little White Dragon to death
Little White Dragon didn't show weakness. He said to Qinglong, “We might as well decide between male and female, but whoever loses has to leave here and never come back.” Qinglong didn't pay any attention to Little White Dragon, so he agreed. Bai Qingerlong is scheduled for the morning of three days from now, and the river will be divided into highs and lows.
Little White Dragon held up a dream to the village chief at night, saying that one day, at some point, it will have a decisive battle with the Blue Dragon and hope that the patriarch can help. It said to the patriarch, “When the river turns over white waves, then you throw steamed buns into the river; when the river turns black waves, I feel more energetic; when the river turns over black waves, that means the blue dragon is hungry, so you throw stones into the river!” When the patriarch woke up, he knew that White Dragon had given him a dream, and the next day, he made an arrangement. However, that night, Lao Dao in the mountain temple also had a dream. He dreamed that Qinglong said to him, “At some point, I would duel with White Dragon. When I saw black waves and white waves turbulent, you had to say, “The Blue Dragon has taken over White Dragon's nest.” When Lao Dao woke up, he knew it was Qinglong's dream. He didn't dare to slow down, didn't eat simple meals, and didn't sleep. He muttered and practiced that trick.
On the morning of the third day, men, women, and children from the village all came to the riverside. Steamed buns and stones were placed in front of each of them. As soon as the moment arrived, the two dragons started fighting inside. When ordinary people saw white waves twirling, they threw steamed buns; when they saw black waves rolling up, they threw stones. Meanwhile, the old man from Shanmiao saw white waves and black waves rolling around and hurriedly said “White Dragon has taken over the Blue Dragon's nest”! As a result, Qinglong was defeated and fled, so they never returned.
Since then, the river has been clear and calm, and Li Min has lived and worked peacefully. This river is also named the White Dragon River. Of the five rivers in Laiyang, there is no Qinglong River alone.
