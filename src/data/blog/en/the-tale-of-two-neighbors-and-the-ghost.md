 Once upon a time, there were two people, one named Rob, who had the fortune of 100 silver coins, and was more powerful than a yak; the other, Ta Qing, was so poor that he had only one copper coin in his family and was not strong, but he was frighteningly bold; Hades was not afraid when he came. They are both connected by a fence between them. Every night, Rob has to count 100 silver coins. “hissing” and “hissing” can be heard from afar. Ta Qing also made a jingle on the copper wire and spread it to Rob's ears, thinking that he has more money than himself, so don't underestimate him.
One night, Rob was counting money, and suddenly a ghost came in. There was no irony, just bones all over his body. Rob was terrified and trembled, and all the coins fell to the ground. The devil touched his face and disappeared. Rob woke up in the morning to find that he had lost his silver coins and his mouth was crooked. I had no choice but to secretly cry bitterness; I didn't even dare to go out the door.
The next night, this ghost appeared in front of Ta Qing again, carrying a leather pocket on his shoulder. Pei Qing hurriedly greeted him: “Hey, friend, what's wrong?” The ghost said, “There's an old man at the door who almost died. Hades sent me to get rid of his gas. Please help me, OK?” Ta Qing said, “Of course!” After speaking, I went out with the devil.
Along the way, they walked and talked, just like old friends. Ta Qing said, “Stop playing this game. This is my first time doing it; you have to teach me.” The ghost took the leather pocket and scratched it and said, “This is the easiest thing to do. If you put it under the patient's nose and slowly let the air out, his whole body will cramp, his eyes will roll up. If you tie your leather pocket with a rope, this person's life is over.”
“Whoops! Terrible! Isn't there any way to return your soul?” Ta Qing pretended to be afraid and asked.
“Of course it is.” The ghost said, “Put the air pocket under the dead person's nose and slowly open the rope. If you send air to his nose, his eyes will become active, his eyes will slowly light up, and the person will live.”
“Speaking of which, you ghosts aren't afraid of anything!” Ta Qing asked again in a daze.
“No! We are afraid of barley! The barley hit us on the head, just like a lightning strike, and the pain was fatal.” The ghost is familiar with Ta Qing; he talks about everything.
“Alas! We are all too; we are most afraid of gold and silver. If someone smashes gold the size of a goat's head and silver the size of a sheep's head, I'll have to die.” Ta Qing walked while talking. Ghosts keep these secret memories in their hearts.
They went into the yard of the old man who was dying, and the devil called Ta Qing hiding under the stairs and went upstairs themselves. Soon, a woman was crying upstairs, and she came to the ground proudly. Her pockets were too heavy, and the devil called Ta Qing carrying them. The two walked along, passing through a large field of barley. The barley was golden, and the scythe was about to open.
Carrying an air bag, Ta Qing immediately dived into the barley field. The devil looked back and saw Ta Qing, so he looked around the crops. Ta Qing picked up a handful of barley grains and beat them at the devil. The devil couldn't help but smash gold the size of a goat's head and silver the size of a sheep's head. The two fought for a while in the barley field. There was a rooster cry in the village. The devil had several bones broken by barley grains, and they didn't get their hands on the air bag, causing a smoke to escape back to the ghost cave at Sanya-ji Temple.
When the sun was rising on the snowy mountains, Ta Qing made a lot of gold and silver, and came out of the barley field carrying an air bag. He was met with two women, one old and one younger, crying on the side of the road. Ta Qing asked, “What happened, you guys are so sad!” The young girl choked and said, “My grandpa died last night, leaving Grandma and me behind. How was his life?”
Ta Qing said, “If that's the case, maybe I have some other options.” They just took out the gold and silver, and let the mother and daughter carry their own gas pockets all the way to the old man's house. He followed the devil's instructions and certainly brought the old man back to life. Needless to say, the whole family is happy. The old grandmother said over and over again, “Young man, if you don't dislike it, stay in my house and be my son-in-law!”
Since then, Ta Qing has stayed at the old man's house and lived a full and happy life. That devil, because he lost his gas pocket, there is no way to hurt people anymore.
