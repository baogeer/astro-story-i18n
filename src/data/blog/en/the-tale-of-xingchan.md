 On the edge of West Lake, there used to be a Xinghua Village. There was a smart and capable girl named Xing Chan in Xinghua Village.
When Xing Chan was seven or eight, on an early summer afternoon, she herded cows on a meadow in an apricot grove in front of her village. At this point, the apricots on the tree were ripe. One red apricot fell, just in front of Xing Chan's feet. She picked up the apricots and wanted to eat them in her mouth, only to hear a clear voice saying, “Little girl, don't bite, let me go.”
Xing Chan looked around and quietly saw no one. Who was talking to her? She was shocked. As soon as her hands were loose, the apricots fell to the ground. Strangely enough, the apricot turned into one of the few beauties in the sky. Standing in front of her, it turned out that the Apricot Fairy came out!
Xing Xian pulled a dazzling gold chain from her head, handed it over to Xing Chan, and smiled and said to her, “Hardworking and kind little girl, give you this golden bracelet. When you have an emergency, just knock on Jinchai and call Xing Xian Xian, and I'll help you.” After speaking, the apricot fairy became another big red apricot and flew to the tree.
When Xing Chan grew up, she married Father Song's ninth son as a daughter-in-law. She married the Song family. The younger couple had a very peaceful life, and her in-laws liked her. Anything else is fine; it's just that the family is so upset. If you want to go east, he wants to go west; if you want to eat sweet, he wants salty. My father-in-law is an honest person; he can't care for his nine grown and older sons; the mother-in-law is easy to talk to, and she can't be the master of the daughters-in-law. When Xing Chan sees justice, she has many things to think about. She often mentions one and a half words. Her ideas are always right and good. If the mother-in-law forgets anything, she can remember everything. Therefore, my in-laws love to discuss anything with her.
When this came, it made the eight daughters-in-law unhappy. They thought their in-laws were hurting their little aunt, so they secretly muttered.
One day, it was Heng Chan's turn to cook. She had just cooked a wok of rice and a wok of old tofu, and her older sister-in-law waved to her at the kitchen door and asked her to cut the shape of her shoes. As soon as she left, her second sister-in-law walked into the kitchen lightly, added a few large pieces of firewood to the stove, and sprinkled a few pinches of salt into the tofu. By the time Heng Chan returned to the kitchen, she only smelled a pungent smell of burning. When she uncovered the rice wok and took a look, every pot of good food had been burnt. Lift the lid of the wok and taste the bitter taste of tofu. As soon as she guessed, she understood everything, but without making a sound, she added a spoonful of water to the rice wok to make a wok of charred porridge; then added some water to the tofu wok, mixed in some rhombus powder, and cooked it into a wok of tofu soup. It was time to start the meal, everyone who went down to the field came back, and the kids were busy carrying tables and benches. Eight sisters-in-law stand on the side.
I cuddled and waited to eat burnt rice and salted tofu, and watched jokes. At this point, Xing Chan brought out the meal with a grin and said to everyone, “The weather was hot, so I cooked rice porridge for everyone to quench their thirst. Everyone is tired of eating this old tofu, so I changed the method and cooked it into tofu soup for everyone to change the taste.”
My father-in-law, grandma, grandpa, little nephew, little niece... The whole family enjoyed eating it. While eating it, they boasted that the tofu soup was delicious, saying that the pot of burnt porridge was fragrant and quenched their thirst, and ate the two wok of the meal perfectly.
After this time, the sisters-in-law also really admired Xing Chan. They also saw that she respected her in-laws, was considerate of her husband, and treated people kindly, so they publicly promoted her to become this family and let the elderly in-laws ease their burdens.
Since Xing Chan became a family, she never took matters into her hands. She always discussed everything with everyone and made proper arrangements for the field and household chores. Those nine brothers are dedicated to living in the field, so they don't have to worry about family matters at all. At home, there are nine, spinning and weaving, sewing clothes and making shoes, and arranging meals well every day. The mother-in-law is in charge of those young children, and the father-in-law is in charge of rushing to the streets. Those older cows also have things to do, such as herding cows, cutting grass, cutting firewood, and picking up dung. In this way, the family didn't worry about food or clothing. Life slowly improved, and the house was renovated.
The butler Xing Chan was very fair. What she ate and wore was never thick or thin; everyone always had something to eat. The whole family is young and old. The old love the younger ones, and the younger ones respect the elderly; there is also a peaceful atmosphere among the brothers and sisters, and even the little ones have become nice.
Xing Chan is also most happy to help others. When her neighbors lack chai rice or utensils, she always lends them to them without waiting for them to speak up. Therefore, people in the surrounding villages all admired Xing Chan. When people teach their daughter's daughter-in-law, they always say, “Look at their Xing Chan!”
You boasted about Xing Chan, and I was also proud of Xing Chan. It has been passed down, and the emperor in the imperial palace also learned about it. The emperor didn't believe that such an able daughter-in-law really existed, so he sent a Minister Chincha to send an almond to Dad Song's family to eat, and see what Xing Chan did with it.
Father Song and his family were all shocked when they heard the Holy Scripture. Only Xing Chan took the almonds from Minister Chincha in a hurry and said, “Dear Chincha, you have worked hard. Please sit in the hall and watch our family eat these almonds before leaving.”
Xing Chan brought in the bricks and built a stove in the house on the spot. A large wok was placed on the stove, and a full pot of boiling water was burnt. She put the almonds in the wok and boiled them, then added some brown sugar to the wok, and served spoon by spoon. ha! No more, no less, just one bowl per person, and the whole family ate almond tea!
Minister Chincha rewarded the emperor and also said, “This Xing Chan is not only smart and capable, but she is also a beautiful woman like a fairy!” The emperor heard that there was such a beautiful woman, regardless of whether it was 3721, and asked Minister Chin Cha to take the 3,000 Imperial Forest Army and rush Xing Chan into the palace.
This large group of people came to the West Lake in droves and surrounded Father Song's house. Minister Chin Cha went into the house to read out the sacrament. Dad Song's family cried: the women were holding Xing Chan's sleeves and crying, and the men argued with Minister Chin Cha, arguing with Minister Chin Cha, and it became noisy and noisy. Xing Chan stopped her family with both hands and said to Minister Chincha, “Please wait outside the door for a while, let me pack up, change my clothes, and leave with you.”
Xing Chan walked into the room, unplugged my Jinchai from my head, knocked on the edge of the table, and called “Xing Xian” three times. Xing Xian was standing in front of her. She then said to Xing Xian, “Xing Xian, now that I'm in a difficult situation, please help me!”
Xing Xian said, “OK, let me move your home to the bottom of the West Lake and live a safe life forever!”
Xing Chan nodded. As soon as Xing Xian softly brushed his sleeve, the wind blew in, and blew Dad Song's whole family out of his mouth, even the house, cows, sheep, farm tools... all into the West Lake.
The wind blew Minister Chincha and the Three Thousand Imperial Forest Army to a total of seven hundred and eight. By the time the wind calmed down, when they rushed to the lake to take a look, they saw a chimney peeking out on the lake! After a while, the chimney disappeared, and Dad Song's family sank safely and steadily to the bottom of the West Lake.
Since Xing Chan moved to the bottom of the lake, neighbors have often missed them. A neighbor wanted to see if they were still alive at the bottom of the lake, and just shouted Xing Chan by the lake and lent her a plow to cultivate a field. After a break of light, a plow actually floated onto the surface of the water. Later, people in the village lacked any object application, so they used this method to borrow it from the bottom of the lake. If you are a stranger from abroad and want to relax after a trip to the West Lake, you only need to speak to Xing Chan, and a table, chair, and bench will soon float up for you to sit on. This has been the case for many years. At one point, I don't know which greedy person borrowed four benches from Xing Chan's family and actually moved back to his own house. Xing Chan was probably mad. Since then, people have been unable to borrow anything from the lake.
