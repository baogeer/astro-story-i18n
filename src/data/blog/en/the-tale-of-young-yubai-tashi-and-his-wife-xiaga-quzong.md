description: "The young man Yu Bai Zaxi fell in love with Xia Ga Qu Zong, a girl from a poor family, and married under the guise of being sick as a lama. His mother was afraid of being poor and abused his daughter-in-law while he was out carrying tea, causing her hair to burn and disfigure. When Yu Bai Zaxi returned, she did not recognize her wife. After learning the truth, she left home with her wife and left her mother to release the donkeys alone. The story criticizes fear of being poor and loving the rich, and praises loyal love."

 There is a young man in the valley named Yubai Zaxi. There is a girl from Hirakawa called Xia Ga Qu Zong. The two of them have been very good since childhood; it seems like tea leaves are inseparable from salt. Looks like this marriage is settled! But, no! Yu Bai Zaxi's grandmother is an old lady who hates the poor and loves the rich. She feels that her family is a rich businessman who runs the arrow furnace every year, and she should find a young lady from a rich and powerful family to be her daughter-in-law. Yu Bai Zaxi said, “Grandma, my son is in charge of my son's marriage; you don't need to worry about your old man.”
Yu Bai Zaxi came to Hirakawa to discuss marriage with Xia Ga Qu Zong. The girl was embarrassed and said, “Ahhh! My parents and grandma didn't agree to our marriage.” Yu Bai Zaxi asked, “Why?” Xia Ga Qu Zong answered, “First, your family is too rich; second, our family is too poor.” Yu Bai Zaian consoled her and said, “Girl, don't worry. I'll make your parents' clothes for the rest of my life, and I'll feed and drink for the rest of my life.” At the same time, they also came up with an ingenious idea called Xia Ga Qu Zong Sui Si
When Xia Ga Qu Zong got home, she fell on a mat and pretended to be sick; Yu Bai Daxi dressed up as a Yunyu Lama and entered the door rocking bronze bells and drums. He pretended to be crazy for a while and clapped and screamed, “This woman's illness is a demon that has hit a snowy mountain. You can only get rid of the disaster and get rid of illness if you go to the Rikoko Temple in the valley and pass 7 749 days.” The old couple took Yunyu Lama's words and packed up her things and sent her to Koko-ji Temple. Just like that, Xia Ga Qu Zong came to Yubai Zaxi's home, and the two happily became a couple.
There was only Yu Bai Zaxi's grandmother, who was very unhappy to look at Xia Ga Qu Zong. The more she looked at her, the more displeased she was; the more she looked at her, the more displeased she was. The old lady treated her as sand in her eyes and spikes on the soles of her boots, and made up her mind not to let her have a good day.
After three days of marriage, the old lady yelled in the yard: “My son Yu Bai Zaxi! Son Yubai Zaxi! The Tuocha upstairs is sold out, so it's time to go to the arrow oven to transport the tea leaves!” Yubai Zaxi answered, “Grandma! Grannies! The tuocha upstairs is gone; there is still plenty of brick tea downstairs!” The old lady opened the tea cellar, used brick tea for firewood during the day, and fed the livestock with brick tea at night, and soon destroyed the brick tea. After three days, the old lady shouted in the yard again, “My son Yu Bai Zaxi! Son Yubai Zaxi! The brick tea downstairs is sold out, so it's time to go to the arrow furnace to get the tea leaves!”
Yu Bai Zaxi had no choice but to pack up his mules and horses and go to the arrow furnace. Xia Ga Qu Zong heard that her husband had gone far away and that it would take almost a year to go back and forth. Her stomach was full of sorrow, and she was afraid to speak in front of her old lady. I had no choice but to shed sad tears, chew the horse that caught Yubai Zaxi, and follow him back and forth. The old lady was very angry. She gave a hard whip to Yubai Zaxi's horse's butt. The horse was like a sharp arrow and quickly ran past the hill in front of her. The old lady screwed the flesh on Xia Ga Qu Zong's tender face again and clenched her teeth and scolded, “Mai! Raksha girl! My son went out to make money, why are you crying? If my son were three long and two short, I'd peel your skin off like slaughtering a goat!”
Since then, the old lady has sought ways to torture her daughter-in-law every day. She used burning firewood to burn Xia Ga Qu Zong's dark hair as soft as satin; she used a wool iron brush to cut through Xia Ga Qu Zong's white face like a bright moon; she used a barbed stick; and hurt her lower back and limb as slim as Xia Ga Quzong's willow tree. He also harshly said to her, “Mai! Raksha girl! Other people want to ask you why your hair is broken; just say donkeys gnaw when you sleep! Other people want to ask why your face is bad, and you say you get burned when frying broad beans, understand?!” After speaking, I rushed her to the mountain to set up her donkeys. Every day, I only gave her a bowl of cottage cheese water and a bunch of sour wine.
One day, Xia Ga Qu Zong stood at the top of the mountain and saw a gang of caravans on the east side of the road. She quickly ran to the side of the road and sang with a full stomach of hope:
Welcome! welcome!
The merchant who came from the archery furnace!
Are you thirsty, businessmen,
Please drink some cottage cheese water;
Are you hungry, merchants,
Please eat a little sour wine tank;
Please ask Yu Bai Zaxi,
Are you back? Are you back?
When the merchants saw that she had no hair on her head and thought she was Huayuan's nun, they immediately fell into debt, gave her some tea, and sang:
Thank you, huh! Thank you!
Ani from the roadside flair!
I'm not thirsty, I'm not thirsty,
Just drank tea and wine;
I'm not hungry, I'm not hungry,
I just ate a pickle.
Yubai Zaxi's caravan,
Right behind us.
Xia Ga Qu Zong waited for the second batch of caravans and gave the same answer as the previous merchant. Next, she waited for the third batch of caravans. Yubai Zaxi was inside. The girl was extremely happy. She quickly ran up to greet her. She stopped Yubai Zaxi's horse head and sang:
Welcome, welcome!
Youth Yu Bai Zaxi,
Are you thirsty, Zaxi,
Drink some cottage cheese water quickly;
Are you hungry, Zaxi,
Eat some sour wine soon.
You've worked hard on your way,
Hurry up and take a break, take a break!
Who knows, Yu Bai Zaxi, like any other businessman, treated her as a Huayuan nun and sang:
Thank you, thank you,
Ani from the roadside flair!
I'm not thirsty, I'm not thirsty,
Just drank tea and wine;
I'm not hungry, I'm not hungry,
I just ate chopsticks;
The body is not tired or tired,
My house is right in front of me.
After singing, she gave her some tea and left in a hurry. Xia Ga Qu Zong was very sad because her husband, whom she had loved since childhood, also treated her as Huayuan's nun. She ran to the edge of the spring and looked down at her shadow. What she saw in the water was an ugly woman with no hair on her head and scars all over her face. She didn't dare to recognize herself. She hurriedly chased the donkey home. She closed the door and hid in a donkey ring alone, and cried sad and disappointed.
When Yu Bai Zaxi walked through the door, the first thing she did was ask his wife, where is Xia Ga Qu Zong? The old lady didn't make a sound for half a day. Yu Bai Zaxi was anxious and said, “Grandma! Grannies! Where is your daughter-in-law, Xia Ga Quzong? Is she sick? Where is the patient lying? Is she dead? Where are the bodies buried?” Only then did the old lady say, “She's not sick or dead. She's living very well. She's herding donkeys in the mountains!”
Yu Bai Zaxi quickly ran to the mountain, didn't find his wife, and quickly ran back to the donkey ring. When she saw that the ring was closed tightly, she hammered the door with both hands and shouted; “Xia Ga Quzong, open the door! Xia Ga Quzong, open the door!” The girl hid in the corner of the wall in silence and cried very sad. Yu Bai Zaxi climbed over the wall and saw his wife, who turned out to be a woman like a nun he had met on the road during the day. He was in a hurry and hugged Xia Ga Qu Zong and asked, “Girl! girl! You, how did you become like this?!” Xia Ga Qu Zong didn't say anything. Yu Bai Zaxi pulled out the knife and placed it on her chest and said, “If you don't talk about it anymore, I don't want to live!” The girl took a knife from her waist and said, “My grandmother didn't burn my hair; I broke it myself; my grandmother didn't hurt her face; I damaged it myself!”
Yu Bai Zaxi understood everything and thought to herself: “Mom, hehe, mom, your heart is too fierce! If I beat you up too, the townspeople will say that I'm not being filial; you can still do your own suffering. Maybe this will make your heart more charitable.” Then I ran to my mother and was very kind and said, “Grandma! When my son arrived to carry tea in the arrow oven, his wife was beaten by Raksha, not like a ghost. People are not like people. Now I want to take her out for medical treatment. This group of donkeys, just ask your old man to graze.”
Since then, Yubai Zaxi has taken his beloved wife to the home of the Kamihaga Qu sect in Hirakawa. In the valley, there are only hardworking old ladies and a bunch of donkeys.

Note: This story was recorded from the mouths of three Tibetan women, and their stories are basically similar. It's just that the ending part is different. Niqiao said that the old lady was later eaten by a leopard, and Nimapendo said she was killed by her son with an arrow. Also, in the original story, the one who pretended to be sick was Xia Ga Qun Zong's grandmother. We changed to Xia Ga Qun Zong herself, which seems a bit reasonable.
