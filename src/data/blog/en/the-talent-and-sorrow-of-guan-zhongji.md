 After the original ancestor Kublai unified the world, he implemented the Huairou policy towards Han culture and Han intellectuals. They ordered a search for Yu Yi. A group of Han readers couldn't resist the temptation of fame and fortune, and put aside their sense of benevolence, courtesy, morality, loyalty and patriotism, and put them into the arms of the Mongolians. The most famous of them is Zhao Mengyi.
Zhao Mengyi, the character Zi Ang, also known as Song Xue, also known as Ou Bo, Crystal Palace Taoist, etc. He is the eleventh grandson of Song Taejo Cho Kuang-yin and a descendant of the Eight Philosopher King Cho Defang. When the Southern Song Dynasty was still alive, he joined the army as a supporter of Zhenzhou (Jiangsu Yizheng) in the 14th year. Its calligraphy is known as “Zhao Ti,” and it was called a “divine work” at the time. He is highly skilled in painting and is very comprehensive. He suggests that painting should have an “ancient intention” and promotes the inclusion of calligraphy. He is a leading figure in the painting world. His influence extended to the Yuan 4 dynasties, and even everyone in the Ming and Qing Dynasties. He is also fluent in music, is good at judging, and is also good at poetry and writing.
In the 23rd year of the Yuan Dynasty, which is the 7th year after China was unified in the Yuan Dynasty, Emperor Cheng Ju of Xingtai brought Zhao Meng to see the ancestors of the Yuan Dynasty. The ancestors of the Yuan Dynasty looked at their ingenuity and brilliance. Like Zhao Mengzhao from the Immortals, they were overjoyed. First, they sealed him up as prefectures in Jinan and Fenzhou, and then promoted by Jiangsu and Zhejiang Confucianism. At the time of Yuan Wu Zong, he was also a bachelor's degree student in Beijing Shi Han Lin. At the time of Yuan Renzong, he was summoned as a master lecturer at Jixian Temple, Dr. Zhong Feng, and soon worshipped Master Han Lin to take over the mission. Dr. Rong Rong Lu, and the official Zhipin. “The Five Dynasties of Rongji are famous all over the world.”
Due to her wife and husband's fame, Zhao Mengdi's wife was also named the wife of Wu Xing County.
Zhao Mengti's wife, Guan Zhongji, joined forces with Zhao Meng Tuzhu. On that day, Guan Zhongji, a young girl who was soaking up the spirit of Huzhou's landscape, went to Zhanbutsu Temple to drink incense. The monk had already heard her name and asked her to leave an ink treasure. Guan Zhongji was a little bit embarrassed, but only promoted the feelings of the monk, so she waved a huge “bamboo repair map” on the east wall of the Buddhist temple. The local boy came to watch one after another, making Zhanbutsuji very lively for several months. Sadly, they left Nyorai Buddha and Guanseyin in the cold. Even Zhao Meng, whose eyes were too high, was drawn in, but seeing that the bamboo in the painting was sparse in detail, young and old, they were all wonderful. This “Bamboo Repair Map” led to a marriage between the two.
Guan Zhongji accompanied Zhao Meng to Dadu. The most prominent officials and wealthy businessmen and travelers in Beijing were proud to be able to obtain the calligraphy and paintings of Zhao Mengdi. Guan Zhongji was idle at home that day. Suddenly, she received an edict from her ancestors asking her to write a “Thousand Characters”. “A Thousand Characters” is best written by Zhi Yong of the Sui dynasty. “A Thousand Characters” is a four-word article with more than 1,000 characters, making it easy for beginners to recite and read, just like the “Three Character Sutra”. Written by Zhou Xingsi under the order of Emperor Liang Wu, it is the most widely circulated. “A Thousand Characters” written by Zhi Yong was called by Su Shi as “a deep and stable backbone, excellent in body, capable, and counterproductive and laid-back.” It is already an excellent item in calligraphy. Guan Zhongji received an edict from her ancestors asking her to write “A Thousand Characters,” which cost a lot of money. She only writes 100 words every day, strives for excellence, and finishes after 10 days. Yuan Shizu saw her slim and beautiful handwriting and greatly appreciated it, and said, “From now on, the world will know that I have talented women who are good at calligraphy.”
Zhao Mengdi and his wife were favored by the emperor as Han people, especially as descendants of the Song dynasty, causing strong dissatisfaction among Mongolian ministers. Attacks and crackdowns followed one after another. After the fall of the Southern Song Dynasty, a large number of masters and doctors were influenced by the idea that loyal subjects had nothing to do wrong. In particular, they felt the deeds of people such as Wen Tianxiang, Zhang Shijie, and Lu Xiufu. They were secluded and even secretly opposed to Yuan Yuan. As a descendant of the Song dynasty, Zhao Mengping became an official in the Yuan dynasty as a descendant of the Song dynasty. He was looked down upon by the masters and doctors. In particular, he shamelessly patted the Yuan regime, capitalizing “The past is no longer something to talk about, and he directly reports his loyalty to the imperial yuan.” It also aroused outrage from the Southern Song administration's villains, lone ministers, and left behind, and scolded him for not having any backbone. This often left Kan Chung-hee in a state of guilt and pain.
On that day, she was invited to the harem to give lectures to those concubines. The queen and concubine were forced to learn from her, so they had to ask her for advice and show respect. However, she also vaguely felt that, as a Han woman, she was always in the midst of a kind of hidden contempt. She sat on the car she came back and thought about the meaning of life over and over again. There are all kinds of people in the world: people live to satisfy their desires, but they should also be different. Another dusk, drizzle continued to fall on the sycamore tree. The fat sycamore leaves were soaked in moisture by the rain, forming drop by drop of rain that dripped onto the stone in front of the steps. Staying alone in the house almost suffocated Kan Chung-hee to death. She thought about how comfortable her life was back then. She thought her life was absurd and foolish. She spread out her pen and paper and wrote four “Fisherman's Words” in one go:
First:
Imagine a mountain house numbering plum trees; Ling Han and Yu Rui are branching south; the moon shines in the mountains, and the evening breeze blows, just for the sake of fragrance and bitterness.
Second:
Looking at 4,000 Wuxing Road from the south, when are you free to go to Yunshui? With fame and fortune, Fu Zhitian laughed and drew a fishing rod on a boat.
Third:
Being near the imperial residence in Yanshan Mountain, I remember Dongwu day and night; drinking fine wine and fresh fish, but it's always not as good as being relaxed.
Fourth:
People are extremely noble in life, and they are not free to be famous; they fight for the same, go back to work in a flat boat and go back to work for a long time!
These four songs, “Fisherman's Father's Words,” are filled with Kwan Chung-hee's tiredness of official life and bitter memories of her hometown. She read these four songs “The Fisherman's Father's Lyrics” slowly to Zhao Meng. Zhao Meng listened quietly, and her face also revealed a yearning for an unruly and unruly life. After all, he is also an artist, poet, etc. But after all, he didn't say a word. He walked silently into the room and looked at his official uniform for a long time. I'm also thinking about another layer of his inconvenient secrets.
On that day, he headed home. Because he was bored, he didn't have a car in particular, and wanted to walk around and relax. While walking, I suddenly heard a pleasant female voice shouting at him. He looked up and noticed that his eyes were bright in front of him. The car curtain picked up, and there was a beautiful woman sitting in the car: wearing a pink embroidered shirt on her upper body and an emerald green lake crepe skirt; her white face was lightly blush, like peach; her eyebrows were dark and curved, like a painting; his dark eyes radiated with an intoxicating aura; his dark hair turned into a hibiscus cloud A silk flower was placed on the side of the clouds, making her look even more gorgeous and charming.
He looked like crazy and couldn't remember who this beautiful woman was for a moment?
Instead, the woman in the car was introduced first. She said that at a singing party five years ago, she sang the wrong song, and the owner wanted to pull her out and beat her. It was he who interceded for her, rescued her, and immediately composed a new song of lyrics for her, which won a lot of applause.
He remembered that this woman was a diva named Choi Yun-young.
