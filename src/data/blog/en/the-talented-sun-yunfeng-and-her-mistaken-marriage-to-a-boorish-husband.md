 During the Qianlong period of the Qing Dynasty, Jiangnan had a great poet, Yuan Mei. She had an elegant temperament and pioneered a large number of female disciples. Thirteen of them were outstanding women, all of whom were famous for a while. Of these 13 proud female disciples of Yuan Men, three are from the same family, namely the three Sun Sisters from Hangzhou.
The three Sun sisters grew up in a family of government officials. Not only are they outstanding, but they are also more beautiful than each other. It can be said that they have made the most of the limelight among famous people and girlfriends in Hangzhou. The three sisters are in that order, the eldest sister Yunfeng, the second sister Yunhe, and the third sister Yunpeng. The age difference between them is three years. Since childhood, they have all been smart and well-behaved. When they joined Yuan Mei's family, they all learned poetry and vocabulary, and they all became outstanding characters. The best of the three sisters is the eldest sister Yun Feng. Not only is she slightly superior to the three in beauty and talent, but she is also the leader of the three. Every time she has a girlfriend's poetry party, she takes her two younger sisters to attend. If the Sun family starts the party, she hosts it, so out of the three sister flowers, she is the most eye-catching one, and is known as a “smart person.”
Sun Yunfeng is precocious. When she was nine years old, she had a guest in her family who loved to sing strange poems. Seeing that Xiaoyun Feng was so cute, she took her little hand and jokingly said, “Guan Guan, Suijiu.” It was due to the excitement of the guests, but unexpectedly, Xiao Yunfeng answered in a serious manner: “Yong Yong Ming Yan.” The guests couldn't help but be amazed. They didn't expect that she was so quick to think at a young age. They praised Xiao Yunfeng a bit, and immediately said they would take her as a disciple in the future. This guest was the famous poet Yuan Mei. Soon after, he quit his job and went back to his hometown. Sun Yunfeng, along with his two younger sisters, became one of his first female disciples.
When the Sun sisters were girls, they visited many places along with their father Sun Jiale, who traveled all over the place. Recently, the north and south of the Yangtze River, as far as Sichuan and Yunnan, have left their beautiful footprints. This not only enriched their experience and broadened their poetry; it also spread their psalms to various places. Every time they stayed, there was bound to be a boom in girlfriends studying poetry there.
Along the way, Sun Yunfeng carefully observed the scenery along the way, tasted his own inner feelings, and wrote many travel poems with mixed scenes. For example, “Xiaoxing” Shiyun:
Waning Moon Dawn Bell, Horseshoe Yellow Leaf Road;
There were no people at sunrise, and the sound of the stream was isolated from smoke trees.
Between heaven and earth filled with morning fog, only a path covered with fallen leaves stretches in front of me. There were few bells and a clear, monotonous sound of horseshoes coming from the distant skyline, knocking out a secluded and quiet mood for pedestrians. In just 20 words, the scene of a traveler acting astonishingly evocative is fascinating.
Leaving the charming land of Jiangnan and going west to the unfamiliar Shudi, Sun Yunfeng felt a bit sad and a bit novel in his heart. She is used to migration. She is not as gentle and nostalgic as the average girl in her girlfriend, but after all, she has traveled far away and is uncertain. This mixed state of mind is revealed in her “Journey” poem:
In spring, the wild animals on the river have returned; I am still driving in the wrong direction;
Fragrant herbs are extremely mysterious. Where is Baiyun located in the township.
Dibei City is mostly surrounded by water, and small families in the county live half in the mountains;
I heard that there are many stone trails going west, and I am so happy that there is no dust.
After traveling around for a long time, the Sun family moved back to Hangzhou City. At this point, Sun Yunfeng had already passed the year of tea and was still waiting for his girlfriend. Famous people and talented women gather in Hangzhou. Sun Yunmin is not willing to be lonely. On weekdays, the girls who are in silence gather on the West Lake painting boat. While visiting the lake and watching the scenery, tasting tea and singing, laughing and making noise, it is a rare form of relaxation and indulgence. It is a popular form of activity for ladies and girls.
Sun Yunfeng's fame soon spread in the city of Hangzhou. Many elegant boys often saw the timing of girlfriends' gatherings and hired boats to follow the girls' painting voyage and compete to see Sun Yunfeng's gorgeous, flowery appearance, and dignity. There was an endless flow of match-makers in the Sun family. The Sun family was open-minded and respected her daughter's opinions on marriage matters, but Sun Yunfeng had a different opinion. She believed that most of these rich boys seeking a family showed off their inner workings and were not in line with their wishes. She was determined to marry a talented Ruyilang. In the 55th year of Qianlong, Yuan Mei returned to Hangzhou to sweep the tomb. After Sun Yunfeng heard the news, she invited all Yuan Men's female disciples in Hangzhou to set up a feast at the Fengyu Tower on the shores of West Lake to wash off the dust for the teacher. Dozens of famous women and girlfriends gathered in one place. The iron light was fashionable, and the fat powder was very fragrant. Everyone drank and presented poems, and Yan used curse. The scene really envied the tourists. It just so happened that seven or eight male disciples of Yuan Mei also arrived at the lake. For a while, everyone invited upstairs to have a dinner together, temporarily breaking the rule of “not being friendly between men and women.”
Sitting next to Sun Yunfeng was a handsome boy with an embroidered hat. Seeing his laid-back demeanor, he must have been a high-ranking official. He was very attentive to Sun Yunfeng. He poured wine, delivered plates, introduced dishes, and his words were full of warm care. During the conversation, Sun Yunfeng learned that his name was Cheng Maoting; of course, he was the son of an official.
