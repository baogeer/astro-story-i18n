 Dong Mo-an worked as a waitress in the middle of the DPRK. His home is disturbed by fox spirits, and bricks, tiles, and sand often fall from the sky like hail. The whole family dragged the old and the little ones to hide, and waited until they calmed down before they came out to work. Dong Gong was deeply concerned about this, so he borrowed Sima Sun Yiting's house to stay temporarily. However, the fox spirit was still disturbed, just like when he was at home.
One day, while waiting to go to the hospital, Dong Gong told his colleagues this strange thing. A minister said, “Guan Dongshi, Jiao Wei, now lives in the inner city and presides over the spell to bring down demons. I heard it was very clever.” So Dong Gong visited Jiao Daoshi and asked him to help defeat demons. Jiao Daoshi wrote a sign with a red pen and asked Dong Gong to go home and post it on the wall. After Dong Gong went home to do the job, Fox Jing wasn't afraid at all; instead, throwing bricks and stones became even more powerful. As a last resort, Dong Gong had no choice but to tell the Taoist again. Jiao Daoshi was furious. He personally went to Dong Fu and built an altar to perform spells. When he didn't do much, he saw a big fox lying under the altar. Dong Fu's family had been victimized for a long time, and they had always bitten their teeth. One of the maids went up and hit the fox, but she suddenly fell to the ground and lost her breath. The Taoist said, “This thing is so rampant that I can't even surrender to it all of a sudden. How can this woman easily offend it?” He then said, “Exactly, I can use this woman's mouth to ask the fox.” Then she put her finger on the maid and read a mantra in her mouth. Suddenly, the maid got up and knelt under the altar. The Taoist asked where it lived? The young lady spoke the fox's words: “I was born in the Western region. I have been in Beijing for 18 years.” The Taoist also said, “This is the Beijing city where the imperial court lived. How can you let these things stay for a long time? Hurry up and go!” The fox didn't answer. The Taoist was furious and clapped the table and said, “Do you still want to disobey my orders? If it's delayed any longer, Daofa won't let you!” The fox only then frowned and looked a little scared, saying that he was willing to follow the instructions. The Taoist urged it to go fast again. At this point, the mistress fell down again, and it took a long time before she woke up. Then I saw four or five pieces of white balls rolling down the eaves, following one by one, and in a blink of an eye, all the hard work rolled away. Since then, Dong Gong's family has settled down and done well.
