 There is a scholar with the surname Wang in the county. He is in 7th place. He is the son of a family of government officials. He has been envious of Taoism since childhood. He heard that there were many immortals in Laoshan, so he carried his luggage and went to visit the Immortals.
He climbed to the top of a mountain and saw a Taoist temple. The environment was very quiet. There was a Taoist sitting on a futon with white hair and a shawl, and his eyes were full of joy. Wang Sheng went to see the ceremony and had a conversation with him. He felt that the Taoist's point was very mysterious, so he asked the Taoist to take him as a disciple. The Taoist said, “I'm afraid you're used to being stubborn and lazy; you can't bear hardships.” Wang Sheng answered, “I can suffer.”
There are many apprentices of the Taoists, and they all gather in the evening. As soon as Wang Sheng had a meeting ceremony with them, he stayed in Taoist Temple.
Early the next morning, the Taoist summoned Wang Sheng and handed him an axe, and asked him to go chopping firewood along with the Taoists. Wang Sheng graciously agreed. After a month, Wang Sheng's hands and feet all had thick calluses. He could no longer bear such suffering, and secretly thought of going home.
One evening, he returned to the hall and saw two guests sitting and drinking with the master. It's late and the candles haven't been lit yet. The master cut a piece of paper in the shape of a mirror and stuck it on the wall. After a while, the paper turned into a bright moon illuminating the room, shining brightly. All the disciples were running around and waiting.
A guest said, “The night was beautiful, and the fun was endless, and we couldn't help but enjoy it together.” As a result, he picked up the flask from the table, distributed the wine to the disciples, and ordered them to drink as much as they wanted. Wang Sheng thought to himself, how can seven or eight people drink a pot of wine? As a result, everyone was looking for a cup and a bowl and scrambled to drink, but they feared that the wine in the pot had dried up. However, the crowd kept pouring back and forth, and there was actually quite a bit of alcohol in the pot. Wang Sheng was very puzzled.
After a while, a guest said, “Thanks for the moonlight to illuminate us, but drinking like this is still a bit lonely. Why not invite Chang'e?” So I threw my chopsticks into the moon. I saw a beautiful woman floating out of the moonlight. At first, it was less than a foot, and when it fell to the ground, she was just like a normal person. She wiggled her slender waist and beautiful neck and danced the “neon dance.” Then she sings, “Fairy, why are you confined me to the Guanghan Palace when you have returned to the world!” The song was clear and melodious, as beautiful as playing a flute. After singing, it swirled around and jumped to the table. While everyone watched in amazement, it had been reduced to chopsticks. The master laughed with the two guests.
Another guest said, “Tonight was the happiest, but I'm almost drunk. Would you two accompany me to the Moon Palace for a glass of candied wine?” As a result, the three moved their seats and gradually entered the Moon Palace. The disciples looked up at the three and sat in the Moon Palace drinking. They could see all of their beards and eyebrows clearly, just like the shadows of people shining in a mirror.
After a while, the light of the moon gradually faded, and the disciples lit a candle, only to see the Taoist sitting there alone, and the guests didn't know where they were. The kernels of the dishes on the table are still there. The moon on that wall was just a sheet of paper round like a mirror. The Taoist asked the disciples, “Have you drunk enough?” Everyone answered, “That's enough.” The Taoist said, “If you have had enough, go to bed early; don't delay getting firewood tomorrow.” The disciples agreed to retreat. Wang Sheng was amazed and envious, and the thought of going home was immediately dispelled.
Another month passed, and Wang Sheng really couldn't bear this kind of suffering, and the Taoist still didn't teach a single spell. He really couldn't hold back his mind, so he said to the Taoist, “Disciples came hundreds of miles away to study with the Immortal Master. Even if they can't get spells that last forever, if they can learn some small spells, it can comfort me in seeking advice. Now it's been two or three months, but I went out to get firewood in the morning and went back to bed at night. My disciples have never experienced this kind of suffering at home.” The Taoist smiled and said, “I always said you can't suffer, but that is true now. I'll take you home tomorrow morning.” Wang Sheng said, “My disciples worked here for many days. I asked my master to teach me a little bit of magic. I didn't take a trip this time in vain.” The Taoist asked, “What spells would you like to learn?” Wang Sheng said, “Normally when I see the master go, no wall can stop me. As long as I can learn this spell, I'm content.” The Taoist agreed with a smile. So I taught him the secret, and when he finished reading it himself, the Taoist shouted, “Go into the wall!” Wang Sheng faced a wall and was afraid to go in. The Taoist also said, “Try walking inside.” Wang Sheng then walked forward calmly. When he reached the front of the wall, he was blocked by the wall. The Taoist said, “Keep your head down and advance, don't hesitate!” Wang Sheng certainly left the wall a few steps and rushed past. When he crossed the wall, he felt empty and empty; looking back, his body was definitely outside the wall. Wang Sheng was very happy. He went back and thanked his master. The Taoist said, “Be clean and love yourself when you return, otherwise the magic won't work.” So I gave him some travel expenses and sent him home.
When Wang Sheng returned home, he boasted that he had met Immortal Road, and the strong walls couldn't stop him. His wife didn't believe it. Wang Sheng then imitated every move of the day. He left the wall a few feet, ran and rushed away, hit his head against a hard wall, and suddenly fell to the ground. The wife picked him up and took a look. There was a big bag on his forehead, like a big egg. His wife mocked him. Wang Sheng was ashamed and angry, and scolded the old Taoist for not being at ease.
