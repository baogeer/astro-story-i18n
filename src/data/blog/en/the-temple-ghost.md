 Xincheng Xiucai Wang Qihou is the great-grandson of the government envoy Wang Xiangkun. One day, Wang Xiucai saw a fat, dark, and unattractive woman walking into the house, smiling and sitting close to him. She looked very slutty. Wang Xiucai was busy driving her away, but the woman decided not to leave. Since then, whether sitting or lying down, Wang Xiucai has always seen that woman in front of her. He made up his mind and never got moved. The woman became angry and raised her hand and hit Wang Xiucai in the face. Wang didn't feel any pain. The woman also tied a belt to Liang, grabbed Wang Xiucai's hair, and forced him to hang him with herself. Wang Xiucai couldn't help but follow the beam, stick her head into the hook, and take a hanging position. Some people saw Wang Xiucai stand straight and upright in the middle of the air without getting his feet on the ground, yet he was unable to hang himself.
Since then, Wang Xiucai has been suffering from insane illness. One day, he suddenly said, “She's going to jump in the river with me!” After speaking, he rushed towards the riverside. Thanks to someone finding out, they dragged him back. It's like this every day, with a lot of trouble, and it happens several times a day. People in the family asked for medicine, but it didn't work. One day, I saw a samurai with an iron chain rushing in furious anger and scolding the woman, “How dare you bully such a simple and loyal person!” Afterwards, they wrapped a chain around the woman's neck and pulled her out of the window. After being dragged to the yard, the woman became a monster with eyes like lightning and a big blood bowl. Some people suddenly remembered that of the four mud ghosts in Chenghuang Temple, there was one very similar to this monster. Since then, Wang Xiucai's illness has improved.
