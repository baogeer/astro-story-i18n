 Academic examinations were the only way for most book readers to become government officials in ancient times, and the winners in academic examinations were highly admired by all. Since the Sui dynasty founded the scientific research, there have been 592 winners. From Sun Fu Jia in the 5th year of Tang Gaozu's martial arts, to the end of Liu Chunlin in the 30th year of Qing Guangxu, there were 592 people. Among these 592 winners, each had its own merits. Ten of them are now excerpted for the benefit of readers.
Zheng Hao, the only triumphant in Chinese history that can be tested

He had to give him his beloved daughter, Princess Wanshou, and Zheng Hao preferred not to love the princess, and not to marry Miss Lu, who had a close relationship with him. The most confident champion -- Hu Dan
In the first year of Song Taizong, a scholar visited a county in Shandong. When the adults of the prefecture learned that the scholar was quite literate, they hosted a banquet. The boy from Chigen really despised that scholar and asked him what his specialty was. The scholar talked about work poetry. The boy then asked him to recite one song, and the student recited one. The highest status champion - Zhao Kai
Song Huizong's third son, Zhao Kai, secretly took the exam in the first year of Zhong He. Since he was really talented, he was invincible all the way and went to the palace exam. As a result, Zhao Kai became the highest ranked champion in Chinese history.
