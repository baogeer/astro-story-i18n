 “Since ancient times, there has been a lack of life.” When people read this phrase, it is mostly associated with power, fame and fortune. In fact, this phrase is also suitable for ordinary families and ordinary people.
Liu Lanzhi was a jasper from a small family in Lujiang County in the late Han Dynasty. “13 can weave, 14 learn tailoring, 15 drapes, and 16 recite poetry books.” Seemingly, she was a girl in the Gu Ge who was strict in her parenting, versatile, and knowledgeable about books. When I was 17, I married Jiao Zhongqing, a civil servant in Lujiang County, as his wife.
The Jiao family has a simple population. Other than her husband, there is only a mother who has been widowed for many years and a little girl. It is also considered a well-off local family. After Liu Lanzhi married to the Jiao family, she went to bed early and late, worked hard with household chores: fetching water, cooking, laundry, and weaving. She kept a family of four in an orderly manner from day to night.
When Jiao Zhongqing saw it in her eyes, she was delighted. In her spare time at work, she whispered, had lots of love, and occasionally played the kite and sang a song in a quiet voice. The couple were affectionate and very happy. The neighbors were very envious of this young couple with Lang Cai, but she was very unhappy with her. Jiao's mother, on the other hand, increased her daughter-in-law's workload quite unreasonably. She was then extremely picky. She finally lost her rationality. She thought that the daughter-in-law was simply a vixen that destroyed the Jiao family's harmonious atmosphere, and forced her son to take Liu Lanzhi back to her mother's family.
The reason why Mother Jiao wanted to take a break from going to Liu Lanzhi at the time was that she thought that her daughter-in-law had no manners and loved to make her own claims about everything, making my old family unhappy. This would seem like a big joke today, but in ancient times, this was an important reason. In ancient times, in addition to the so-called “seven outs,” you can get rid of a wife according to any of these. “Rite Chronicles: True Destiny” says, “Women have seven destinies: disobeying their parents, having no children, being obscene, being jealous, being sick, talking too much, and stealing.”
From a psychological point of view, Jiao Mu has been widowed for many years, and it has become a long-standing habit for mother and child to be dependent on each other. Suddenly, there is an extra daughter-in-law in the family, causing a situation where mother and son are dependent on each other, which immediately caused drastic changes, loss of mental balance, and anger at the daughter-in-law. At the time, Jiao Zhongqing thought that her daughter-in-law's behavior was not inappropriate; why wasn't she cared for by her mother? He objected to his mother doing this and vowed in front of her: “If I send my daughter-in-law, I will never marry again!” However, Jiao Mu used the final killer weapon, threatening death with a snot and tears. At the end of the day, Jiao Zhongqing lost the battle and succumbed to her mother's intentions.
On the night of the same day, the husband and wife had tears in their eyes. Jiao Zhongqing repeatedly explained his embarrassing situation and promised that over time, the situation would definitely improve, and advised his wife to be patient for a while and welcome them later; however, Liu Lanzhi was afraid to take this luxury. It was completely a situation where they would not have to be permanent. She cried like a tearful person. She continued to insult her husband to leave behind items for women such as embroidery, skirt, bucket, sachet, mirror case, and silk rope. Set it up so as not to see physical injuries and increase distress.
The tears hadn't dried up yet, and the sky was almost bright. With a feeling of sadness and anger, Liu Lanzhi woke up and cleaned up her clothes. When she put on her clothes and socks, every little thing repeated four or five times. Each time touched her infinite affection for her husband. If she wanted to stop, she wanted to stop talking about it! She walked out of the house in style and quit her job to Mother Jiao. She was serious and dressed in elegant attire, which was a kind of protest against the mother of focus. However, this holy expression turned into tears when it was her turn to resign to the little girl. Her stomach is full of bitterness and she can't help it anymore in front of a young girl who is the same woman and the same age as herself. She had to leave and couldn't bear to leave this house!
Time to go. A carriage carrying Liu Lanzhi left the Jiao family. Jiao Zhongqing rode a white horse and drove them back on. Every turn of the wheel seemed to shatter two broken hearts and couldn't help but feel unbearable pain. Jiao Zhongqing got off his horse and got into the car, and the two hugged and wept again, pointing to God, and they swore that they were not at all against each other; “You were treated as a rock, and the concubine was treated as Pu Wei. Pu Wei is sewing like silk, and the rock is not transferred.” It means that the sea is dry and rocks are rotting, the two love is happy, and they will never change their heart. When I got home, it was time to break up. “Show your hands and work for a long time, and be dependent on each other.”
However, the development of things is never driven by human will. When Liu Lanzhi returned home, her kind mother looked at her daughter, who had “no face in the way”, and was greatly devastated. However, Lau Lan-chi also has an aggressive older brother. As for her older brother, Lau Lan-chi had been mentally prepared for a long time. On her way home, she knew, “I have parents and siblings, and sexual violence is like thunder. I'm afraid they won't let me do anything, so they scolded me.”
Sure enough, after Liu Lanzhi got home, the first thing the county government sent to the media to ask for a family for his third son, who had just turned 18. Being a mother, she understood her feelings, and declined on her behalf. Soon, County Mayor Taishou Zao proposed marriage to his Five Young Grandpa. When the mother was preparing to say no to her daughter again, her older brother intervened. In the old society, the elder brother took over the father, and the family was dominated by men, so he agreed to the marriage, accepted employment, and chose a good day to welcome her. Liu Lanzhi kept quiet; she only covered her crying with a hand towel. Tears welled up in tears. What she called “I want to marinate and cry when I'm sad when I go out.”
When Jiao Zhongqing heard the news of Lau Lan-chi's remarriage, she quickly rushed to Liu's house. It was already twilight. The sound of loud noises was the sorrow in his heart. I saw that a “green house” had already been set up in front of the door. It was a large draped tent. It was used by the bride to spend the night before leaving the cabinet. Seeing Liu Lanzhi, Jiao Zhongqing was in a hurry and said, “I'm like a rock. I haven't moved for a thousand years, but what about the resilience of your Pu Wei? Why did everything change from day to night? What about our Hai Si Shan League! All I can do is congratulate you on climbing the high branch and getting better day by day.” Liu Lanzhi broke down and sobbed, “Life isn't as good as I would like. It's hard to put it into words, so why should you talk like that! I was persecuted just like you, and only one death showed my intentions.” Liu Lanzhi cried and ran back to Qinglu. Jiao Zhongqing also turned his head around and set out on his way home with all his thoughts in vain. There were thousands of hardships in the world, but there were no differences between death and life.
It was already a cold winter day. The cold wind hit the trees, and the leaves drifted away. Gradually, at Dusk, Ji'an An An was silent. The moon was cold, and the ground was covered with severe frost. Occasionally, one or two lone birds' screams came from the air. Liu Lanzhi stumbled away from Qinglu. Taking advantage of the lack of preparation, she leapt into a pond outside the village and used her life to interpret the steadfastness of love.
Over there, when Jiao Zhongqing returned to her home, she went to the church to worship her mother and said some farewell words, “You can't take good care of yourself.” His confused and bossy mother is still comforting him: “You are a big son; you live in the Taiwan Pavilion; be careful when you die as a woman. Why are you so ungrateful. There are wise women in the owner's family, and the city is beautiful. Grandma looked for you, and then it came back to the day.” No matter how persuaded her mother made up her mind, Jiao Chuqing had already decided to die at this point, so I could hear that. They wandered around the courtyard that night. After three years, crows flew by in groups. Jiao Zhongqing had a different feeling. He thought his beloved wife had already been martyred and was waiting for him to join him on Huangquan Road! As a result, he unfastened his belt, tied him to a branch in the garden, and hanged himself.
It's amazing that Liu Lanzhi didn't go to the rich house with “the golden car and jade wheel, the blue horse, and the golden saddle” and was willing to die out of love.
After dawn, news of the death of Jiao Zhongqing and Liu Lanzhi was already shocking in nearby villages. Jiao Mu grabbed land and was deeply saddened by the death of her only son; the elder brother and father of the Liu family even regretted that he had no choice but to throw water to protect her sister, who had no way; ordinary villagers were even more sympathetic and indignant. They gathered in front of the two families to incite and curse, and called for the two to be buried together at the foot of Mount Huagai.
Jiao Zhongqing and Liu Lanzhi's graveyard, planted pine cypress in the east and west, and planted sycamores in the north and south. A few years later, the branches were thick and shaded. There was a type of mandarin duck that flew up and down the night, singing softly; young men and women visited the cemetery to pray for a good relationship. Today, Huagai Mountain in the south of Shucheng County, Anhui Province also has ruins of mandarin duck graves! Jiao Zhongqing is a junior governor of Lujiang County, around present-day Lujiang County, Qianshan County, and Shucheng County in Anhui Province.
This is a sad, tragic love story. A folk poet wrote a five-word poem “The Peacock Flies Southeast,” and Xu Ling of the Southern Dynasty collected it in “Yutai New Song.” The poem describes Liu Lanzhi's appearance like this: “If you cut an onion root, your mouth is like Zhu Dan, and you take fine steps, it is unrivaled in the world.” When it comes to her costume, she described it like this: “Footsteps on her feet, tortoiseshell lights on her head, a slender waist, and earring the moon.”
A lot of pen and ink was used in “The Peacock Flies Southeast.” The purpose of describing Liu Lanzhi's talent, character, beauty, and attire is to emphasize that such a rare beauty cannot win the favor of her mother-in-law, and increase the world's sympathy and regret.
More than 1,700 years have passed, and today, on the screen and on stage, this heart-wrenching story is still constantly being played. It can still be seen that the reason for its immortality is definitely not an accident or a disaster. “Peacocks fly southeast and wander for five miles.” “Life says goodbye to death; hate is fine.” “Thank you to future generations, and be careful and don't forget it.”
