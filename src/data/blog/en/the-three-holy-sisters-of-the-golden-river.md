 Legend has it that the three daughters of King Dragon King of the Jinsha River visited Cangshan Mountain and Erhai Sea. When they arrived at Lianhua Mountain in Fengyi County, the mountain was in the midst of a drought. The eldest sister asked the two younger sisters to go back to the Dragon Palace first and stay alone to solve the water diversion problem for the people. The eldest daughter turned into an old woman. She went deep into the fields and villages to check the droughts. When she saw that there was no water for planting, it was difficult even for people and animals to drink; the villagers ate barley meal. As a result, she created a clear spring with a thick bucket at the foot of Lianhua Mountain, so that the dam at the bottom of the mountain was planted, and there would never be a shortage of water. In order to thank the eldest daughter for her blessings, people in the three villages of Huaying, Puhe, and Banzhuang at the bottom of the mountain built the main temple, shaped her statue, and respected her as her owner. Enshrined as Our Lady of the Jinjiang River.
Dragon King's second daughter looks like a flower and jade. While in Dali, she met Luolong of the Dong family, a prince in the east of Shanghai. Dong is a lustful person. Seeing her second daughter's beauty, she placed a flower on her head. The second daughter felt that Dong Jia Luolong showed her ugliness in public; she went to Haidong with her third sister to punish Dong Jia Luolong. When the younger sisters arrived in Shizi Mountain in Haidong, they were experiencing a major drought. The second daughter couldn't bear the suffering of the hardworking and kind people. It rained a stream of rain, causing a clear spring to pour out from the mountainside, so that the bottom of the mountain would never be arid. After punishing Prince Dong, the two sisters returned to Shizi Mountain and saw the beautiful scenery, so they stayed in Ciyunxi on the mountain. A few villages at the bottom of the mountain know that they have built a main temple for her; they respect her as their master. Enshrined as Our Lady of Tsz Wan Purun Jinluan.
After her second daughter became her boss, she was afraid that her younger sister would be alone and sent Grandpa Wu Zhong, who was close to her, to stay with her sister in Qinglong Mountain. After my sister arrived at Qinglong Mountain, she was also revered as her master for diverting water and rain to local villagers. Sealed Our Lady of the Golden Spirit. Later, King Jin Jiang Long visited his three daughters; seeing the beautiful scenery of Cang'er, the three daughters were also a bit lonely, so they stayed in Qinglong Mountain.
Every year from the 5th to the 25th day of the Lunar New Year, it is the main festival of the Three Sisters of Jinjiang. At that time, their ten villages will have to sing big songs, play lions and dragons. Since the three Jinjiang sisters love spring trips, during the main festival, they have to take turns picking them up in a car from village to village, wait until they finish visiting ten villages, and then return them to their respective main temples.
