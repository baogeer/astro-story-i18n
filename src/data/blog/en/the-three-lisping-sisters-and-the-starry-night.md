 Legend has it that in the past, there was a family like this. They had no sons and only had three daughters. Unbeknownst to me, these three daughters have all grown to the age where it is time to discuss marriage, yet none of them are married yet.
Speaking of these girls, even though they aren't as talented as flowers, they are also very good-looking and hardworking. The only thing that's lacking in the US is talking so the young people who came to go on a blind date immediately withdrew once they heard what they had to say. As the saying goes, “Women don't stay in college”. Seeing that the girls are one day young and two days old, yet the mother-in-law's family hasn't settled down for a long time. Naturally, this has become an increasingly serious mental illness of being an old woman. On this day, another matchmaker led a few more people to go on a blind date. Since these people are from the mountain side, they will definitely have to take a night off at their house in the evening. Learning the lessons of the past, the old lady advised her three daughters over and over again the first night, saying, “Tomorrow, when the guests come, you can just do things in a dull voice; don't make any noise to the old woman!”
The next day, in front of the visitors, a few daughters certainly listened very much to the old lady. They didn't say anything all day; they just kept busy inside and out quickly with their feet. People who have gone on a blind date are very satisfied to see that these few girls are so diligent and capable. After dinner, it was dark. The eldest daughter and the second daughter, sitting in the courtyard, suddenly looked up and saw the sky full of stars. At this point, the eldest daughter, who had been holding back for a day, suddenly popped up the phrase: “Listen to my brother-in-law (today's star will be fine)”. The second daughter heard it and immediately said, “Don't listen to Ah Tingquan (tomorrow will be fine).”
The old woman, who was sitting with the guests in the hall, was in a hurry as soon as she heard her two daughters speaking. She couldn't help but scold, “Damn girls, who taught you to pee and spit there, and you can't put up your butt and mouth with a piece of linen!”
Hearing the old lady's angry reprimand, the youngest three daughter quickly made a loud statement and said, “Wow, wow, take it in bad breath. Uzzai will be enough to buy me to go to Grandma (Mom, Mom, I didn't say it; it was the older sister's second sister who said, I'm washing the dishes and dishes behind the stove!”). The guests resting at their house saw this scene, and naturally understood everything. The next morning, these people quietly quit when they didn't even say hello to them.
