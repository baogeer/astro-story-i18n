 The story of the Tiger Brothers is: Once upon a time, there was a small village in the middle of a mountainside in a mountainous area in southern Fujian. The front and back of the village were full of woods, and wild animals often appeared. There is a family in the village. Their parents died early. The three brothers went up the mountain to cut firewood during the day and picked them to the market in exchange for food rations. At night, they followed their ancestral teachings and practiced martial arts and defense together. Over time, they also practiced good fists.
Due to the poor family situation and difficulties in earning a living, the boss crossed the ocean with the townspeople to work in Nanyang. A few years later, the second and third elders in their hometown all started families one after another and lived separately. For a while, tigers were often spotted in the village, and the villagers were fearful and uneasy. Seeing this situation, the two brothers discussed going up the mountain to fight tigers and exterminate them for the people. Just ask a blacksmith to make a pair of steel forks and a pair of iron daggers as weapons to fight tigers.
So the two brothers went up the mountain with steel forks and iron bars to ambush them. Near dusk, a cold wind passed, and I saw a tiger breaking out of the jungle. The elder three were young and energetic, and rushed to the tiger with steel forks. Tigers stand and stare at people when they see someone blocking the way. The older three swayed their steel forks a few times in front of the tiger, causing the tiger to shake its power, “roar,” leap its front legs, and bow down condescending. The old three didn't lose the opportunity to point the fork at the tiger's neck. The tiger was forked in mid-air by a steel fork and kicked in the foreleg. At this point, the old man hurriedly used an iron bar to discount the tiger's two front legs. The tiger broke his front legs and couldn't stand any longer. The two brothers raised both iron forks and iron bars and stabbed the tiger. Soon, the tiger lost its breath, and the two carried the dead tiger back to the village.
Since then, the two brothers have made a living by fighting tigers, but their lives have been quite happy. One night, the elder told his wife how the two brothers cooperated and made a tacit agreement when fighting tigers. The wife was not convinced when she heard it. She felt that every time her husband tried hard to kick the tiger's neck, the second brother saved a lot of effort, and actually shared the benefits equally, which is really unreasonable. Her husband couldn't stand her encouragement, and he thought he was at a loss. In this way, the couple made a small plan and decided to go up the mountain and fight tigers together in the future, and not invite their second brother.
The next day, my sophomore had something to do with the house. The older couple went up the mountain alone with steel forks and iron bars, and ambushed for a while. A tiger walked slowly and leisurely from the depths of the woods. As soon as the tiger came, the husband picked up a steel fork and skilfully circled the tiger a few times before forking the tiger. At this point, when the wife saw the tiger, she was terrified. She couldn't stand steady, and almost fainted. Seeing that his wife was unable to help him, the husband panicked and shouted for help. He understood in his heart: if he couldn't break a tiger's front leg, it would be rare for him to spend the rest of his life. At the moment of crisis, I saw that his older brother was carrying a big axe and his second brother both arrived with an iron rod.
It turns out that the boss earned his living in Nanyang for many years, but he was homesick and invited back to his hometown with several townspeople. The boss just walked into the house, and the elder followed him. The brothers met but couldn't find the older couple. After asking about the second-in-law's wife, they learned that they had gone up the mountain to fight tigers. After hearing it, the brothers had a hunch that something was not going well. The older brother hurriedly picked up the big axe that had cut firewood back then. The second brother picked up the iron stick that fought the tiger and took the trail to catch up the mountain. When they saw people and tigers holding each other, the boss and the older three waved the axe and iron bars respectively, discounting the tiger's front legs, one left and one right. The tiger fell down and died, and the third person was unable to lie on the ground to breathe.
As a result of this lesson, the third-generation wife no longer dared to play with right and wrong. The relationships between siblings and siblings have become even closer. The phrase “fight tigers and brothers” spread all over southern Fujian.
