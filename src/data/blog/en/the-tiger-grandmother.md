 Once upon a time, there was a family where Mu's mother asked her son and daughter to give her mother ① rice and dates ②. The mother's family lived in another village, and there were several miles of mountain roads.
The sister and brother walked laughingly on the street.
Halfway through, I met a tiger.
The tiger asked the sister and brother, “Where are you going?”
The sister and brother answered, “Let's give my wife rice dates.”
The tiger said, “I am your wife.”
The sister and brother didn't believe it and said, “Our wife's face ③ has six spots and seven moles. You have ④ moles on your face; it's not our wife.”
The tiger said, “I've just sifted rice. I have gray on my face. I can see it when I wash it in the creek.”
After speaking, the tiger went to the riverside and picked up a few snails and put them on his face to show it to his sister and brother.
The sister and brother believed it and followed the tiger home.
Before walking to a cave, the tiger said, “Arrived, I'll stay here tonight.”
The sister and brother are a bit unconvinced.
The tiger said, “I never knew you were coming, but now ⑤ the sky is dark ⑥, my old man is walking slowly and I can't get home.”
After supper, the tiger puzzled the sister and brother.
If the younger brother wanted to sleep with his “wife,” the older sister had to sleep with the other one.
When she was puzzled, my sister touched “Maiden,” and strangely asked, “Maiden, why are you hairy?”
The tiger said, “My old man's house is afraid of the cold, so it's covered with wool.”
My sister didn't doubt it; she fell asleep without understanding.
In the middle of the night, the tiger ate his sleeping brother. My sister was woken up. When she heard the sound of “maiden” eating, she asked “maiden”, “Maiden, what are you eating?”
The tiger said, “I'm hungry. I'm eating the rice and dates you gave me.”
My sister said, “Ma'am, I want to eat it too.”

The tiger said to her, “There are tigers outside. Don't take too long. I use a rope to tie your foot. If you pull it off, you'll have to come back.”
Speaking of which, the tiger tied her sister's foot with a rope and held it in her own hand at the other end.
