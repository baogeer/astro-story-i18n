 Hangzhou Shangcheng has an “Anle Bridge” and three “Tofu Bridges” side by side on the same river. These four bridges were all built during the Southern Song dynasty, and now it's been over 800 years.
When Yue Fei defeated the Golden Army, one of his subordinates was a general named Wang Zuo. Using bitter tactics, he defeated Lu Wenlong and defeated Jin Wu Shu by a big defeat. Since then, he has lacked an arm and can no longer fight. Yue Fei protected him as King of Peace, and the emperor also promised him to build a royal residence for the elderly in Hangzhou.
Anle Wang's palace was built by the river. When construction began, sediment, lime, slate, and timber were piled up on the harbor. There was no bridge on the river. The original ferry was also occupied by craftsmen transporting bricks and tiles. Ordinary people were unable to get around. They were very angry, so they composed a song to sing:
“King Anle, King Anle,
I'm doing my best to keep you all happy!”
Wang Zuo came back to Hangzhou from the military and learned about this incident. He thought: Why would I want such a big palace alone! Why don't you use these materials to build a bridge over the river to make it easier for everyone. As a result, he ordered the craftsmen to pick solid blue brick slabs and build a bridge over the river. Using the remaining materials, he built two houses at will to make the royal palace.
People heard that if they don't build the Wangfu, build a bridge first; everyone is here to help. With so many people, it took less than a month to build a wide, flat bridge over the river.
There is a bridge over the river, so it's very convenient to get to and from. As soon as the common people are happy, they also compose new songs to sing:
“Anle Wang, kind hearted,
Zouza on all sides of Ohashi Street.”
You sing to me, after passing through two, Anleqiao is becoming more and more famous. The news blew into Prime Minister Qin Hui's ears. Qin Hui was very jealous. He said, “Building a bridge is also famous! I've now built three of them. One is wider than the other, and one is taller than the other. Fight with you and Wang Zuo to get rich. Let's see if you're better or me!”
When Qin Hui moved his mouth, the following officials ran off their feet: increasing donations and taxes, arresting husbands and sending workers, and forcing ordinary people to build bridges day and night. After a full three months, three bridges were built side by side on the same river at Anle Bridge. Sure enough, one was wider than the other, and one taller than the other. Qin Hui was very proud. He personally named these three bridges, “Doufu First Bridge,” “Doufu Second Bridge,” and “Doufu Three Bridges.”
Bridges are walkable; having a bridge over a river is enough. Why do you have to build so much! The common people hated Qin Hui to death, and they all gambled not to walk away from his three bridges to wealth.
Because the voices of people in Hangzhou “fighting for wealth” are similar to “tofu,” ordinary people made fun of Qin Hui and called those three bridges “tofu bridges.” I've been calling it until now.
