 Living in the southwestern part of Yunnan, at the foot of Wanming Mountain, there is an ethnic minority village - Tuanshan Village, Guodazhai Township, Fengqing County. It is 15 kilometers from the government site and has a land area of 5.4 square kilometers. The entire village covers 15 villager groups with 529 farmers, including 427 households with 1,704 people from the Li Yi ethnic group, involving 14 villagers groups, 11 pure Liyi groups, and 1 mixed living group with the Han ethnic group, accounting for 79.6% of the village's population. The whole village has steep mountain elevations. It is a mountainous and mountainous area. The average annual temperature is 16.5℃ and the average annual rainfall is 1,450 mm. It has a cool climate in the Central Asian tropics. The geographical conditions are poor, and living conditions are extremely difficult. Most Liyi people are still below the subsistence level. At 3 kilometers from Tuanshan Village Committee, there is a green mountain called Wanming Mountain. It is 2,905 meters above sea level. The top of the mountain is snowy in winter. If you climb to the top, you can enjoy a panoramic view of the mountains. There is an ancient temple ruins on the mountain, and there are many visitors today. The Li Yi people living in the country are able to sing and dance, and are very hospitable. They still maintain traditional folk customs, their own language, and traditional village architecture. Traditional festivals include the temple festival (April 8 in the lunar calendar), the torch festival (June 20 to 26 in the lunar calendar), July 30, and the October dynasty. The legend of the origin of the Li Yi people, the legend of gold and silver rice, and the legend of the torch festival are spread.
June 24 of the lunar calendar is a grand festival for the Yi people and many other ethnic minorities, commonly known as the “Torch Festival.” The Yi Liyi people who live in Tuanshan Village, Guodazhai Township, Fengqing County, have followed ancient customs and been passed down from generation to generation, forming a unique festival cultural landscape. Li Yi people regard the Torch Festival as the biggest festival of the year. When the festival is coming, each family must take back their married daughter. Everyone wears holiday costumes, sacrifices their ancestors, sets off torches, sounds a big gong, sings torch songs, and dance torches to wish the people good fortune. On the day of the holiday, several families gathered to kill a sheep or kill a chicken to cook meat. On the 24th, they offered a mountain god to sacrifice the field. After dinner on the 25th, they set up torches, played songs, and drank alcohol at the singing hall.
The origin of the Fire Festival
There is such a beautiful legend in the hearts of the Liyi people: it is rumored that a long time ago, there was a beautiful and kind girl in the Li Yi tribe. Many surrounding tribes were dumbfounded by the beauty of the happy girl. They had already coveted their mouths high. They wanted to attack the Li Yi tribe by force and take over the happy girl. The happy girl didn't want to see her family and loved ones humiliated and ravaged by the war. She resolutely dedicated herself to the sea of fire to repay her family with death. Later, the people of the tribe lit a bonfire, sounded the sound of gongs, waved torches, and danced the big gong as a sign of remembrance, so they followed the tradition.
There is another legend: long ago, everything in the world had languages, and humans were able to communicate and talk with all animals and plants. One day, seven suns appeared in the sky, and the earth was so hot that all living things couldn't live. There were raging fires and smoke everywhere, and everything faced a threat to survival. As a result, humans worked with animals and plants to discuss countermeasures. Everyone recommended that the White Monkey King go to heaven to find Dragon King. Who knows, Dragon King only has a large tank full of water. That is just the rain that the human world needs. When he gets angry, he overturned the table and the water in the tank poured out of the earth. This rain was torrential rain for 7 749 days. The whole world was flooded, and no sound was heard on the earth. It was completely dark, day and night, and humans were unable to live. There was a big gourd floating on the river at the time, and a sister and brother were hiding inside. As a result, Emperor Jade sent a mouse to find a human spirit to gnaw out a hole and save the sister and brother. Later, the sister and brother became a couple. In order to make a living, the husband went to the mountains that were not flooded to retrieve the seeds of fire and was tied to the head of a cow to cultivate. In this way, humans received light and lived for generations. Later, to commemorate the couple, people designated June 24 and 25 of the lunar calendar as the Fire Festival every year, which continues to this day.
Fire Festival customs
(1) Sacred field crops: From June 20 to 25 of the lunar calendar. Gong dancing officially began at dusk on the evening of the 20th. The gong dancing team lit torches and hit the big gong. The villagers each sacrificed their ancestors at home or went to the fields, and in groups entered each household and danced around the clock until they walked through the village.
(2) Dog sacrifice: June 25 of the lunar calendar. Feed your dog before eating to remember your ancestors. Liyi people still maintain the custom of not eating dog meat.
(3) Mountain Song Duet: June 26 of the lunar calendar. The Liyi Song and Dance Company from Tuanshan Village performed in 6 shows, followed by a mountain song duet. It was a good day for young men and women to talk about love.
(4) Fire torches: The evening of June 25 to 26 of the lunar calendar. Local leaders led the villagers from all over the village to the fields and corners of the village to throw torches and ask for good fortune.
