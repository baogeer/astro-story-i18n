 Although there are many explanations for the origin of the Torch Festival, its origin is probably most directly related to the natural worship of fire. Its purpose is to use fire to exterminate insects and protect crop growth. Here is a legend: Legend has it that in ancient times, there were six suns and seven moons in the sky, exposed to the hot sun during the day, and bright lights shone at night. The land was barren, demons were rampant, and everything in the world faced extinction. At this moment, the Yi hero Zhi Ge'alon shot dead the scorching five suns and six moons, tamed the last remaining sun and the last moon, conquered the raging floods, and destroyed all kinds of demons that have ruined the world. Since then, the world has been beautiful and rich in aquatic plants, and the people of the Yi ethnic group have begun to live and work in peace, like a hideaway.
However, the god Enti Guzi, who rules everything in the world, saw that the world was so prosperous and rich, and was dissatisfied, so every year he sent his son, Hercules, the Heavenly Soldier, to the world to levy heavy taxes. Wherever the Heavenly Soldiers went, they burned and looted, and did nothing evil. Good people are also being spoiled by the gods and demons. People live in deep heat, and the suffering is unspeakable. Later, there was a Yi hero in Zhigalong's hometown called the Black Mule. He was extremely powerful, smart, brave and fearless. He was able to walk thousands of miles a day on a horse, and if he stepped on his feet, he could walk a cliff and walk against a wall. He often solved problems for various tribes, got rid of violence and was loved by the public. One day, a black mule went up the mountain to hunt, stood on a high mountain, and was enchanted by the beautiful scenery. He couldn't help but sing a loud song to express his love for nature and his desire for a beautiful life. The high-pitched song drew the melodious and gentle singing of Ni Zhang, a girl shepherding on another mountain. They sang love songs for each other across the alpine river until dark. Since then, they have fallen in love with each other on a blind date and swore an oath.
The powerful god Szeaby, who has long coveted for Ni Zhang and Aji, heard about their relationship. Her heart was mixed with anger and jealousy, and she always wanted to find a chance to put a black mole to death. It didn't take long for Sereabi, who couldn't stand the jealousy, to take on the challenge of wrestling and duel with the black mole. As a result, in a wrestling duel, he was knocked to death by the Yi folk hero, the black mole. Tenjin was furious, and released all over the earth with insects (locusts) to destroy mature crops.
The clever Ni Zhang, Azhi, climbed the mountain and found a highly respected Big Bimo on the edge of the sky. Bimo read the Tianshu and told Ni Zhang, “To exterminate the locusts, you need to use a torch. Ni Zhang, Aji and the black body mule led the people up the mountain to use artemisia torches. They lit torches for three days and three nights, and burned torches for three days and three nights. Finally, they burned all the celestial insects to death and preserved the crops.
Seeing this scene, the abominable body bursts like thunder. Use mana to turn an overworked black body mule into a mountain. Ni Zhang and Ah Zhi watched all of this, sad and desperate. Amidst the prayers of Big Bimo, she turned herself into a mountain full of beautiful soma flowers blooming all over the mountain that had turned into a black mule.
The Fire Festival generally lasts for three days and three nights, usually from June 23 to 25. The first day was to welcome the fire. On this day, villages and villages will all fight cows, slaughter sheep, and kill pigs, greet the god of fire with meat, and sacrifice their ancestors. Women also have to make soba and chopsticks. People outside all have to go home to eat reunion dinner, drink home-brewed wine around the fire pit, eat tuotou meat, and share joy and happiness together. As night falls, people near the village will set up an altar at a location chosen by the elderly to ignite the torch by hitting flints in the traditional way, and the Khimo chanting the fire. Afterwards, each household, had the elderly connect torches made of artemisia poles from the fire pond, and let the children and grandchildren take the torches from the old man's hands. First, they illuminated every corner of the house, then walked over the fields and mountains, and used the flames to exterminate the disease disaster. Finally, they gathered on the hillside to play torches, sing and dance, and play all kinds of games.
The second day of the Torch Festival praises and praises fire, which is the climax of the Torch Festival. As soon as it dawned, men, women, and children all dressed up for the festival, brought cooked pork and soba, gathered at the altar under the torch to participate in various traditional festival activities. Tens of thousands of people gathered to organize horse racing, wrestling, singing, beauty pageants, pole climbing, shooting, bullfighting, sheep fighting, cockfighting, etc. The girls were dressed in beautiful clothes and sang and danced. On this day, there is no more important event than the Yi Family beauty pageant. The elderly should select beautiful men according to the legendary image of a bold mule who is hardworking, brave, handsome and chic. Choose beautiful women who are kind, smart, beautiful and generous like Ni Zhang and Ah Zhi. When evening came, tens of thousands of torches formed a line of fire dragons, which flocked from all sides to the same place, and finally formed countless bonfires, burning the sky red. People danced and sang around the bonfire until late at night. The scene was grand and full of joy, so it enjoyed the reputation of the “Oriental Carnival”. When the bonfire was about to go out, a pair of affectionate young men and women walked quietly to the hillside, walked into the woods, and under a yellow umbrella, played the moonlight, and shared their thoughts. Therefore, some people also refer to the Yi Fire Festival as “Oriental Valentine's Day.”
The third day of the Torch Festival is to send fires. This is the end of the entire Yi Fire Festival. When night fell on this day, after sacrificing the god of fire, families lit torches one after another, walked to the agreed place, set up a fire stand, held a fire ceremony, prayed to the god of fire, prayed to the god of fire, prayed for ancestors and Bodhisattvas, and gave the children and grandchildren health and happiness, and gave the world a good harvest and joy. People danced with torches and sang congratulations, “Burn to death the plague, burn to death from hunger, burn to death the sick, and burn out a year of peace and good harvest” to pray for the safety of their homes and the prosperity of the six animals. At this point, they also had to burn the chicken wings, chicken feathers, etc. that were slaughtered on the first day, and the elves, which symbolize evil, and the evil god, were also burnt down. Then find a larger stone and press the lighted torches, feathers, etc. under the stone, as a metaphor for holding back the devil and protecting the family Ding Xingwang, rich in grains, and fat cows and sheep. Finally, torches like dragons from villages up and down the mountain gathered and lit a big bonfire to show that everyone was united to defend against natural disasters.
The time for the Yi people to celebrate the Fire Festival varies from place to place; everyone spends it from June 23 to 25 in the lunar calendar. Most of the Yangyi Yi people are on June 25 of the lunar calendar, and the surrounding area of the county and the county town are also used to passing the 25th. The autonomous regulations of Yangyi Yi Autonomous County stipulate that local ethnic customs are respected, and that the Fire Festival is a three-day holiday every year.
