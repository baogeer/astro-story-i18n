 A long time ago, Jiuxian County (now Nushan Lake) was a large castle called Liuzhou City. Later, due to changes in the earth's crust, the ground sank, and more than half of Liuzhou City sank to the bottom of the lake, leaving only the southern half of the city, which was later called Zhaoxin County. Then, during a flood, Zhaoxin County also sank to the bottom of the lake, which is now Qili Lake. What I want to talk about here is a sad story of Luo Chengma, the hero of Wagangzhai, a man from Sui and Tang, who was shot dead by the Sui Army in random arrows the second year after the northern half of Liuzhou City sank.
In other words, Luo Cheng led a team of people and horses fighting day and night in Xuyi and the Sui army, and eventually fled due to a massive defeat due to a single force and no reinforcements. Luo Cheng rode out of Jianxi on a jade zebra and went all the way into the mountains. He was rescued by a group of egrets in Pangyu Wa. He escaped being chased by Yang Lin's ghost in the sky that night and rushed to the foot of Qiyun Mountain to join up with his brother Qin Qiong to discuss how to break the enemy. The two agreed that Qin Qiong and Pegasus would join Shan Xiongxin in Haozhou, and Luo Chengfeima would join Cheng Yaojin in Sizhou. Early the next morning, the two held a fist ceremony at the Ishimura fork and parted ways.
Luo Cheng sped all the way on his own. As soon as he passed through Jinli, he heard someone shouting “Little thief Luo Cheng bring it”. Luo Cheng looked up and saw that it was the ghost of the thief Yang Lin who was searching in the sky. Mana's enemies have a narrow path. Luo Cheng pinched his legs in the horse's belly. The jade zebra understood the owner's intention and sped in the direction of Liuzhou City with its four hooves raised. Well, Yang Lin also kept up his ghost, beating drums in the air, waving flags and shouting, and closely following Luo Cheng. The sound of gongs and drums and cries soon attracted a large number of Sui Jun. The jade zebras were running around and came to a temple called “Duliang Temple” (Song Huanghu changed its name to “Kaihua Temple” in the 2nd year, and Jiahu changed its name to “Jiahu Temple” in the first year). Luo Cheng immediately got off his horse and took refuge in the temple. Elder Tolyang-si Tōyuan learned that Luo Cheng, a rebel general, had been chased to take refuge here, hurriedly welcomed him, and hid Luo Chenglian in the backyard. Elder Tong Yuan clasped his hands: “The general took refuge in the backyard and waited for the old monk to go around with Sui Jun. It would be a blessing if he didn't search the temple; if he wanted to search the temple, the general could escape from that back door.” After speaking, Elder Tongyuan went to the front yard.
Well, under the leadership of Deputy General Wang Lei, the Sui Army rushed to the front of the temple by riding less than a bag of smoke. Seeing dust flying all the way to Duliang Temple, Na Luo Cheng must have taken refuge in the temple. Wang Lei was also a good man on a daily basis, and he also had a relationship with Elder Tong Yuan of the temple. He waved and told all his subordinates to get off the horse, then took a few of his subordinates to the hospital and told the elders that they wanted to search the monastery and arrest Luo Cheng. Elder Tong Yuan was trying to conceal that he hadn't seen anyone enter the hospital, but Yang Lin's ghost shouted in the sky: “I'm taking orders from the general to stop gossiping with that bald donkey. The thief Luo Cheng has already taken refuge in the backyard.” As soon as Deputy General Wang Lei of the Sui Army did not listen, he withdrew from the monastery and his commanders immediately surrounded the monastery. Just as Wang Lei commanded his soldiers to surround the monastery, Luo Cheng opened the back yard gate and flew to the north of the city. The horse crossed the Shilong Bridge and had no choice but to drive the horse into the muddy river. Na Luo Cheng was riding on the back of a horse and wielding a long gun in his hand. It turned like a round of iron. There was no clear wind or rain, and all the flying arrows fell into the muddy river. Wang Lei laughed as soon as he saw it: “Good boy, see how long you can hold on!” After speaking, the commander's men took turns firing arrows in groups of five. Poor Luo Cheng, a handsome young man who had just spent 20 years in the world, died tragically at the hands of a small lieutenant general before starting a family.
A few days later, a group of Wagangzhai heroes such as Cheng Yaojin, Shan Xiongxin, and Qin Qiong heard that their beautiful brother Luo Cheng had died horribly at the hands of Wang Lei, a junior deputy general in Sui's army, and issued a toxic vow: not killing Wang Lei to avenge Luo Cheng would be a man.
