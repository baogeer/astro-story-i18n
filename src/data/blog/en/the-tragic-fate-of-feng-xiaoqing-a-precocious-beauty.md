 There are two beautiful tombs beside West Lake in Hangzhou: one is the solitary tomb of Su Xiaoxiao, a famous poet from South Qi, located on the banks of Xileng Bridge; the other one houses Feng Xiaoqing, a grievous woman from the early Ming Dynasty, buried in plum trees at the foot of Gushan Mountain. The two isolated graves covered with green grass add a bit of poignant color to the shores of Xizi Lake. People who visit here can't help but think back to the poignant story of the two desperate people.
Feng Xiaoqing was originally the daughter of the Guangling (present-day Yangzhou) family. His ancestors followed Zhu Yuanzhang's southern expedition to the north, defeated the Daming River and Mountain, and established great credit. After Dingding Nanjing in the Ming dynasty, the Feng family enjoyed senior officials and honors, and Feng Xiaoqing's father was crowned Taishou of Guangling. Feng Xiaoqing spent his childhood in Taishoufu in Guangling. Those days can be described as golden clothing and jade food; he called for slaves. Since childhood, Feng Xiaoqing was beautiful, elegant, smart, and loved by her parents. Feng Xiaoqing's mother is also a great girl. She is good at dancing, writing, and playing the piano. She is the only baby girl like Feng Xiaoqing. Naturally, she looks heavier than the eyes. I carefully nurtured her from an early age and hoped that she would grow up to be a talented girl. When Feng Xiaoqing was 10 years old, Taishou Fuchu had a friendly old nun. This old nun was dressed in a spotless grey cloth and had a kind eye. Seeing that Feng Xiaoqing was smart and cute, she called her to her side. Feng Xiaoqing felt that the old man Nicole was friendly, so he stood in front of her very happily. Lao Ni caressed Feng Xiaoqing's head and slowly said, “The young lady has a bright face and an extraordinary life. Let me teach you an article to see if you like it?” Feng Xiaoqing is very curious. Hearing her say she wants to teach her own articles, nods with interest, and looks up intently at Lao Ni. Lao Ni cleared his throat, closed his eyes and joined hands, and read a large section of the Buddhist sutras. After Lao Ni finished reading it, I opened my eyes and looked at Feng Xiaoqing. Feng Xiaoqing knew that he was examining himself, and immediately closed his eyes and repeated the Buddhist sutras that Lao Ni had just read. It turned out to be no bad word.
Lao Ni looked surprised. She immediately shook her head, chanted “Amitabha,” and turned to Feng Xiaoqing's mother and solemnly said, “This girl has a poor life and is willing to beg to be a disciple; if you can't bear to give up, don't let her read and read; she may still have 30 years of good life!” It means that if Feng Xiaoqing can't bear to leave home and teach her to read and read, then she won't even live to be 30 years old.
Mother Feng was taken aback by the news, but after all, she is someone who has seen the world. She thinks that with her family's conditions, Feng Xiaoqing can live a comfortable life no matter what. On the one hand, the old nun determined that Xiaoqing's life was weak; he must have made a mystery for some reason. How can we be convinced! After sending Huayuan Lao Ni away, Mu Feng continued to train her daughter, and she hasn't seen anything unusual yet.
Who would expect the unexpected to happen; people have both bad luck and bad fortune. In the fourth year of Jianwen, Yan Wang Zhu Di took the throne of Emperor Jianwen under the name “Jingnan.” When Zhu Di marched into Nanjing, Feng Xiaoqing's father, as the minister of Emperor Jianwen, led troops to resolutely block it. When Zhu Di took over the world, the Feng family naturally became his devil and slaughtered the whole family. The old Feng Xiaoqing went out with Mrs. Yang, a relative from far away at the time. She survived the disaster. In the midst of panic, she fled to Hangzhou with Mrs. Yang.
In the city of Hangzhou, Feng Xiaoqing had no relatives; he had to stay with his family, Feng Yuanwai, who had a relationship with Father Feng once. Feng Yuanwai is Fu Yin, who runs a silk business. Her family has a big business. Seeing that Feng Xiaoqing is alone and poor, she was taken in.
Having stayed in Feng's family, there was no need to worry about food or clothing. However, overnight, she fell from Taishou Qianjin to an orphan girl under the guardianship, leaving Feng Xiaoqing always immersed in grief and melancholy. In a blink of an eye, it was the Lantern Festival. Feng Yuan's house was brightly lit, so it was very lively. Mrs. Yang, who came to the Feng family to walk away for her relatives, was the lady who brought Feng Xiaoqing to Hangzhou. Seeing Xiaoqing sitting alone in the room, she pulled her out and looked at the lights. Feng Tong, the eldest grandpa of the Feng family, is a Confucian businessman who is proficient in literature and ink. He took advantage of the festival lantern festival to make many riddles and hang them on the lights. By the time Feng Xiaoqing came out, most of the riddle had already been guessed. As she took a closer look, she was fascinated by the mystery of a riddle:
Speaking of having a family in Yubashan, I shed tears when I met people and talked about the end of the world;
The red color was accompanied by three more rain, and constant sorrow and falling flowers.
The mystery of this lantern riddle was guessed by Feng Xiaoqing all of a sudden, but what attracted her even more was this puzzling puzzle, which seemed to be a picture of her state of mind at this time, and she couldn't help but stand in a daze.
Feng Xiaoqing's unusual expression was seen in the eyes of the mystery Master Feng Family, and he couldn't help but feel a sense of pity. He knew for a long time that there was a young lady who was killed in his family. He heard people talk about how she had both talents. Unfortunately, she had a husband with a woman, and was afraid to visit at will. Seeing Feng Xiaoqing just now, he immediately guessed her identity.
Feng Tong approached Xiaoqing and softly asked, “Did Miss guess this lantern riddle?” Feng Xiaoqing was suddenly awakened. He turned his head around. He was an elegant young boy. He couldn't help blushing and whispering, “Could it be a red candle?” Feng Tong smiled and praised, “Miss is very understanding.” Feng Xiaoqing was embarrassed to walk away.
A few days later, it snowed in the city of Hangzhou in spring. Snowflakes are falling, and they are covered in plain silver everywhere. There are several white plum trees outside Feng Xiaoqing's house. At this time, the plum blossoms were picking up the snow, and the courtyard was filled with fragrance. Since childhood, Feng Xiaoqing has loved plum blossoms, especially white plums. A large plum tree is planted in front of her boudoir in Guangling's old house. Every time the plum blossoms are fragrant, she always likes to stay there and enjoy that elegant charm. Falling into a foreign country, she saw the familiar plum blossom and snow again. Her dull mood flashed clear. So I found a porcelain pot and walked out of the room to the plum trees in the courtyard. I carefully collected crystal snow from the plum petals and prepared it to burn plum tea. It's also a fun thing she used to do.
At this point, Feng Tong, who also loved Mei, walked into the courtyard. He came to see the plum blossoms on purpose. The two Aimes met unexpectedly under a plum tree in the snow. They didn't seem surprised; they just smiled heartily. As a result, Feng Tong began helping Xiaoqing sweep away the plum snow together, while laughing at interesting stories about plum blossoms and Gin Mei's poems in scattered ways. Before I knew it, I received a pot full of plum blossoms and snow. Feng Xiaoqing slightly shyly invited Feng Tong into the house to cook and taste plum snow tea. Feng Tong gladly took the lead. The two spent a pleasant afternoon together, burning snow, tasting tea, and talking about poetry.
After that conversation, Feng Tong couldn't help but want to find an opportunity to get close to Xiaoqing. Xiaoqing felt that Feng Tong was gracious, polite, and kind and heartwarming, so she was also very happy to see Feng Tong. As a result, for three days, Feng Tong secretly visited Xiaoqing without his wife Cui Shi. Xiaoqing's cottage was full of life ever since. Eventually, the relationship between the two was in full swing, and they couldn't bear to meet each other in secret and separate from day to day. When spring came, Feng Tong requested his father to accept a concubine. Feng Yuanwai originally had a crush on the smart and charming Xiaoqing. In addition, Feng Tong's original wife, Cui, did not have children for three years after their marriage, so they happily took advantage of Feng Tong's marriage to Xiaoqing as his concubine. Although Choi is stubborn about this, since the old man nodded, she had no choice but to secretly hate her teeth.
Xiaoqing and Feng Tong had a proper relationship, and they stayed with each other day and night. Feng Xiaoqing, a famous person, married Shang Jia's family as a concubine. Speaking of it, she was a bit aggrieved. Feng Tong loved her so lightly, so she was very content. She thought the disaster was over, and didn't come to her best. On the shores of the beautiful Xizi Lake, I have regained my happy life.
Unexpectedly, the good times didn't last long. As soon as the honeymoon was over, Feng Tong's original wife, Choi, developed and displayed the majesty of her grandma. First, Feng Tong's actions were strictly restrained, then he chose three things about Feng Xiaoqing's life. Xiaoqing has a light flavor and is not used to the Feng family's greasy diet, so Feng usually asks the chef to cook additional side dishes that match the taste of Xiaoqing. On this day, the chef's separate stir-fried dish for Xiaoqing was seen by Choi. She deliberately reprimanded the chef and said, “The Feng family has big fish and big meat. Who wants you to cook these non-greasy dishes? You want to lose face of the Feng family? Don't burn it again!” After speaking, I poured those two dishes hard into the cesspool.
Due to being constrained by the Cui clan, Feng Tong rarely had the chance to spend time with her in Feng Xiaoqing's house. Xiaoqing once again fell into loneliness. Because of that short period of time, her loneliness now became even more unbearable. In a dry house, Xiaoqing had no choice but to use poetry to relieve her worries. On this day, she felt a sense of emotion and wrote these two amazing sentences:
First:
The drapes are only worried about the good scenery and the rolling curtains are afraid of the wind;
It's difficult to hang the bottom of a roller curtain; if you're upset, who knows!
Second:
Xueyige no clouds are flowing; old clouds are pressuring new clouds;
Let's turn the pen upside down and drop it out of the window. Shu Matsuarashi is in our building.
The poem talks about the helplessness of her situation, and is also a metaphor for Choi's oppressive tendencies. After writing, the poem was spread out on the table, and I fell asleep. At this point, it just so happened that Choi passed by and saw that the house was quiet and silent. She sneaked in with bad intentions and snooped in, and unwittingly found an undried poem note on the desk. Even though Cui was crude, he understood the meaning of those two poems. He knew they were secretly sarcastic about himself, and immediately became aggressive and loud.
Cui's cry shocked many people up and down in the Feng family. Seeing that Feng Yuanwai and Feng Tong both arrived, Choi took advantage of the opportunity. She burst into tears. “I can't control this family anymore. Some people don't like it. Why must they slander me in the poem, so that everyone knows that the Feng family has no rules...” She sounded the first foot as if she had been greatly offended.
After catching a hint of control, Cui never let go of it. She didn't force Feng Tong to send Xiaoqing out of the house; otherwise, she would seek death and find life on her own. Due to the spiciness and barbarism of Cui's family, and her mother's family is a wealthy businessman in Hangzhou. As an inconvenience, Feng Tong had no choice but to send Xiaoqing to live in a Feng family villa in Gushan.
Gushan is located on the shores of Xizi Lake. The scenery is beautiful and quiet. Feng Xiaoqing was accompanied by only an old servant. Facing the morning glow and sunset of the West Lake, the flowers and trees were beautiful, and Feng Xiaoqing was not at all interested.
