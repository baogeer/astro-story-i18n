These two bad words come from different people, yet they are steeped in the same grievances and frustration, because together they tell a poignant love story — Tang Wan's dream with Lu You and Shen Yuan.
Lu You was a famous patriotic poet during the Southern Song dynasty. He was born into an affluent family in Shanyang, Yuezhou. When he was a child, during the Golden People invaded the South, he often fled everywhere with his family. At this time, his mother, Tang Cheng's family, had many relationships with the Lu family. Tang Cheng has a daughter, named Tang Wan, whose name is Huixian. She has been well-behaved since childhood, but she is not good at speaking but is very understanding. The feelings of Lu You, who are similar in age, are very similar. The two are infatuated with each other. Despite the chaos of the military, the two young people who are unfamiliar with the world still spend a beautiful, pure and flawless time together. As they grew older, a feeling that haunted their hearts gradually grew in the hearts of the two.
In their youth, Lu You and Tang Wan are both good at poetry. They often use poetry to share their feelings. Before and after a month, the two were singing each other's songs in pairs, like a pair of colorful butterflies fluttering in the middle of a flower, and their eyebrows are full of happiness and harmony. The parents and friends of both families also thought they were a match made in heaven, so the Lu family used a beautiful family heirloom as a token and arranged a close family marriage in the Tang family. As an adult, Tang Wan became the Lu family's daughter-in-law after a night of candles in the cave. Since then, Lu You and Tang Wan have been even more happy and deeply affectionate. They have been immersed in the world of the two. They don't know what evening this evening is, leaving their studies, fame and fortune, and even their family members in the sky for a while. Lu You has taken the cover of Deng Shiro at this point, but this is only the first step in becoming an official. However, Lu and Tang were deeply affectionate and were unable to look back, and there was no significant improvement in the situation. Lu Mu was so disgusted with her daughter-in-law that Tang Wan was actually the Tang family's broom star and would delay her son's future. Due to her mother's death, Lu You had no choice but to promise to send Tang Wan back to her mother's family. This situation seems out of place in today's eyes; the relationship between the two cannot be interfered with by others. However, in ancient Chinese society where filial piety was advocated, motherhood was a sacred purpose, and the Son of Man had to follow it. Just like that, a pair of affectionate mandarin ducks will be brought to life the eight characters of unfounded filial piety, worldly merit, and fictitious fate. In desperation, Lu You had no choice but to clean up all her grievances. Under her mother's supervision, she studied science and studied hard for three years. At the age of 27, she left her hometown of Shanyin by herself and went to Lin'an to take the “Lock Room Test.” In Lin'an, Lu You won the admiration of examiner Lu Fu with his solid academic skills and talented writing skills, and was recommended as the leader.
