 Once upon a time, there was a very poor old man and old woman. Soon, the old lady passed away. Winter is here and the weather is very cold.
The old man went to his family and friends to help dig a grave. Everyone knows he's poor, and he didn't agree. The old man went to the priest. This priest was greedy for money. Doing business is not ethical.
“Please help me bury the old lady, old man.” The old man said.
“Do you have money to pay for the burial? Let's be honest!”
“I'm afraid to lie in front of you. I don't have a penny. Please allow me to temporarily owe it. I am willing to pay high interest, and I will definitely pay it back!”
The priest didn't want to listen to what the old man said.
“If you don't have any money, don't come here!”
“There's no way,” the old man thought. “I went to the graveyard and dug a hole and buried my old lady myself.
He came to the graveyard with an axe and a shovel and dug a pit by hand. He used an axe to split the frozen soil and shoveled it away with a shovel. He dug around and unearthed an iron can, filled with gold coins, shining brightly. The old man was overjoyed.
“Thank God! Now I have the money to bury my wife.”
He put down the pit and didn't dig it up, and carried the tin can home.
It was really rich to make the devil work; everything went well. He hired a few people, dug the graves, and prepared the coffins. He asked people to buy alcohol and food, buy things to use for the funeral, and go to the priest with gold coins himself.
As soon as he entered the door, the priest said to him:
“To be clear to you, old man, don't come if you don't have any money!”
“Don't be mad, old man.” The old man spoke in a friendly voice. “I gave you coins to help me bury my old partner. I will never forget your kindness for the rest of my life!”
The priest took the money and didn't know how to receive the old man, so he could sit well, and his tone of voice changed. “Old man, don't worry, everything will be fine.”
The old man bowed to the priest and went home. The priest and his wife told the old man's strange words: “Look, this old fool says he's poor, but he can bring money! I've buried so many dead people in my life, and I've never had this much money...”
The priest brought a group of children and buried the old lady according to canon rules.
The old man invited the priest to eat at the house. They sat down at the table with wine, food, and all kinds of snacks. When the guests saw something so good, they ate it in big sips. After the guests finished their meal, they left one by one, and the priest stood up. The old man sent him out of the yard. Seeing no one else around him, he said to the old man, “Listen to me, darling. You have to be honest with me as if you were before God. How did you get rich? You used to be so poor, where did your money come from, who did you kill and who did you rob?”
“What are you saying, old man! I promise you, no stealing, no robbing, no murder. The money came upon it.”
The old man recounted the circumstances of the incident.
The priest listened and became jealous. When he got home, he didn't do anything. Day and night, he thought, “This old man who didn't make good use of it got such a huge amount of money. Is there any way to trick him into his money pot?”
“Listen to me, buddy, don't we have a goat?”
“There's a goat.”
“OK, let's do it at night.”
At night, the priest brought the goat into the house and killed him, peeled off the sheepskin and headband all over his body, immediately put it on himself, and said to his wife:
“Old partner, quickly get the stitches and sew so that the sheepskin doesn't fall off.”
My wife sewed the sheepskin with a thick needle and thread.
In the middle of the night, the priest walked to the old man's house. He walked under the old man's window, hit the door with his claw, and grabbed the door. The old man heard the noise and sat up and asked, “Who?”
“Demon!”
“We don't invite ghosts here!” The old man shouted, crossed the cross, and said in his mouth God bless, God bless.
“Listen, old man,” the priest said. “No matter how you draw a cross and ask God to bless you, you won't escape my hands. You'd better hand over my money pot, or I'll be unkind to you! I feel sorry for you. I showed you the money pot. I thought you would only use a small amount of money for a funeral, but I didn't expect you to take them all!”
The old man looked out of the window and saw a goat head with horns and a beard, like a devil. “Let him take all his money,” the old man thought to himself. “I didn't have any money before, and I can live without money in the future.”
He took out the money pot, held it outside the house, threw it on the ground, and quickly burst into the house himself. The priest picked up the money pot and rushed home.
He went home and said, “I got the money! “My old partner, quickly grab a quick knife, cut the thread and remove the goatskin before you can see it.”
The wife picked up the knife and cut along the thread, and blood came out. The priest shouted:
“It hurts, it hurts, don't cut, don't cut!”
My wife cut it off from another place, and it's still the same. Goatskin is grown on my husband's body.
The couple thought of many ways to return the money to the old man, but it didn't work. As a result, the goatskin on the priest's body never came off. This is God's punishment for greedy devils.
