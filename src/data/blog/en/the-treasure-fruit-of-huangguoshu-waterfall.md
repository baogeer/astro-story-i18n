 Many years ago, on the hillside of Huangguoshu Waterfall, an old man growing crops lived with his wife. The older couple are both in their sixties. They have no children, and they work all year round. They never take a break from the weather, but their days are always very difficult. The older couple is often sad and relatively sigh when they think about working hard all day and not being able to enjoy a full life.
Since the old man came here young and strong, he cut down his own trees, cut thatch, built a thatched house, and lived alone as a family. He planted 100 yellow fruit trees in front of and behind the house. Over the years, these trees have grown into forests, and the clusters surround his small thatched hut. The old man sits in front of the house and smokes leaf smoke when he's okay. His door is facing the big waterfall flowing down in front of him.
This waterfall didn't have a name originally. It was about ten feet wide, rushing straight down from a hanging rock three to forty feet high. The thunderous sound of an earthquake sounded day and night. The water droplets were like a drizzle of cow hair, flying a few miles away. In the morning, when the sun shines on the waterfall, a colorful rainbow appears. At night, when the moon shines on the deep pool below the waterfall, the pool shines brightly again. This is how the old man enjoys the wonderful scenery of the waterfall every morning and night. In addition to growing crops, I took a look at Huangguoshu and spent his years.
One year, the 100 yellow fruit trees planted by the old man were somehow very different from previous years. This year, every yellow fruit tree bloomed more and bigger than in previous years, and the fragrance can be smelled a few miles away. Lao Han and his wife are very happy. He calculated that this year's yellow fruit harvest must have been higher than previous years. Of course Luo! It also sold for much more money. Every time the old man thought that his income would increase, he always grinned and said to his wife what he had unknown many times: “Old partner, when Huangguo sells for money, you should get a new one.” His wife also repeated the phrase, which she had never said before: “You can go to the market and buy a few pounds of meat for a dental festival.”
After the Huangguohua was over, the days passed day by day. The old man looked at this tree every day and looked at that tree. It seems that after looking at it for more than ten days, he never saw a tree growing yellow fruit rice. At this point, the old man was both sad and disappointed. He didn't want to say anything, couldn't eat food; he was just smoking one bag after another. However, one afternoon, when he was drowsy at home, his wife suddenly exclaimed in surprise outside the door: “Come and see, Huang Guo!” The old man had his butt pinched by a needle. As soon as he jumped up, rubbed his eyes, and ran outside the door. At this point, his wife was holding a bundle of freshly picked firewood and was looking up at a yellow fruit tree.
“Look, what a big yellow fruit!” His wife pointed to the tree and said.
“Huh, strange, why have I never seen it?” The old man saw that there was actually a yellow fruit growing in the leaves and strangely said, “This yellow fruit is a bit strange. It only took ten days, and it grew bigger than a fully ripe one.”
“Look around and see if there's anything else.” His wife put down the chai in her hand and said.
As a result, the two found one tree after another. They carefully searched for 100 trees, but apart from this yellow fruit, they were unable to find a second yellow fruit.
“Don't look for it.” The old man said to his wife, who still wanted to find it again, “The lives of poor people always suffer; they can't find them even if you look for them again.”
A few days later, the old man's family had a rare guest. He came from a few hundred miles away after hearing the legend about Huangguo. This guest was only in his thirties. The thin man looked like a tuberculosis ghost, yet his eyes had a strange light. People who know him all call him Shibao's Shaanxi Lao ①, yet the old man never knew him. When the old man from Shaanxi arrived at the old man's house, his first words were: “Old man, are you selling yellow fruit or not?”
“There were a lot of yellow fruits in previous years. You could buy a few hundred pounds. It's just that this year they didn't grow well; they only bought one in total.”
“I just want to buy this.” Shaan Lao said.
“This is grown; I'm not selling it yet.” The old man answered casually.
“Sell it, I have money.” The old man from Shaanxi looked at the old man with temptation.
“Is there money? How much can you do?” the old man asked skeptically.
“How about 200 pieces of silver?”
“Two hundred and two?” The old man's heart “throbbed”. Although he had seen some scattered silver, he still didn't really know how much 200 was. If he wanted to buy it, it must have been more than a lot of silver! As soon as he thought of the word “many,” he thought Shaanlao was having fun with him, but looking at Shaanlao's face, he was serious again; it didn't seem like he was deceiving.
“Are you too short of 200?” Shaan Lao said, “Let's do it. I'll give you 1 thousand two. This is a fixed payment.” Shaan Lao took out a 52 silver ingot from his pocket and handed it to the old man.
“No, no.” The old man looked at Bai Shengsheng's big bar of silver and didn't know what to say.
“One thousand two is enough, take it.” The old man stuck the silver ingot into the old man's hands. The old man was really a bit confused at this point. His wife seemed to remember something and hurriedly said, “If you sell it, sell it, wait for me to pick it.”
“Don't be busy, don't be busy.” The old man from Shaanxi hurriedly stopped him and said, “I don't need this yellow fruit now; I don't have enough money.”
“So when do you want it?” the old man asked.
Shaan Lao first walked under the tree to look at it for a while, then pulled up his finger and did some math, then said, “After 100 days, 100 days, I'll pick yellow fruit. But you must remember that during these 100 days, regardless of day or night, you must guard this yellow fruit; no one is allowed to touch it, nor eat it to birds or animals...”
“Don't worry!” The old man chimed in and said, “It's been a rare time for me to have someone here for a year and a half. I'm afraid birds and animals will eat it; just make a cage to cover it.”
“No, you can't cover it; let it grow.” Shaan Lao said, “You must guard day and night; you must not be negligent at all; otherwise, I won't be able to buy your yellow fruit when the time comes.”
“Why is that?” the old man asked.
“If you promise not to tell anyone else, I'll tell you.”
“My old partner and I dared to gamble, even though my three-year-old kid didn't tell him.” The old man patted his chest and spoke honestly.
“This is a -- yellow -- fruit -- this is a -- treasure!” The old man from Shaanxi lowered his voice and spoke softly in the old man's ear. Actually, no one heard him shouting, because the waterfall across the door was very loud, and the old man's family lived alone on the hillside, with no one on the left or right.
“What's the use of it?” The old man asked a question.
“Hmm, this...” Let's talk about it later!” The old man didn't want to say one more word, and the old man couldn't ask again. After he nodded and listened to Shaanlao's instructions, he watched him leave.
Since then, the old man and his wife have taken turns guarding under this yellow fruit tree every day. Even at night, they are afraid to close their eyes. In the old man's arms, the heavy 52 yuan made him forget his fatigue; as soon as he thought of “one thousand two,” an unimaginable number, he always took that ingot out to touch it.
Look, 100 days are almost here. The old man and his wife are also exhausted and almost sick. When he stayed for 99 days, the old man was no longer able to support him. His hard-working waist bar bent like a lobster, and his red eyes just wanted to close. He thought, “I've kept it for 99 days, and the yellow fruit is fully ripe. It doesn't matter if I don't keep it for a day.” But he also thought, “If you don't keep up this day, why don't you lose your previous achievements if you get eaten by birds and animals?” The old man thought it over and over again, and finally decided to take it home and put it away to prevent accidents.
The next day, Shaanlao certainly arrived as scheduled. He didn't carry any money; he only carried a rope ladder made of wire. As soon as he entered the door, he asked, “What does Huang Guo look like in the old man's house?”
“It's ripe; I took it off yesterday.”
“Taken it off?” Shaan Lao asked in surprise, “Let me see it.”
The old man brought out the yellow fruit. This rare yellow fruit in the world is fragrant and big, just like a pumpkin. After watching for a while, Shaan Lao sighed and said, “Unfortunately, on this bad day, I didn't have enough strength.”
“Speaking of which, what's the use of this yellow fruit?” the old man asked.
Shaan Lao pointed his finger at the opposite waterfall and said to the old man, “The deep pool below this waterfall is a treasure pit. Some people know that there are many gold and silver jewels in the pool, but there is no way to get them. This yellow fruit is just the key to opening the deep pool. Unfortunately, it's still a day before you take it off. I'm afraid you haven't had much strength yet, so you won't be able to open it. But we can try it.” After speaking, Shaan Lao picked up Huangguo, carried a rope ladder, and walked to the deep pool at the bottom of the waterfall. The old man and his wife helped him tie a rope ladder to a large rock by the pool. After the bundle was ready, the old man from Shaanxi picked up the Huangguo Chaotan center and lost it. The strange thing immediately happened: the thunderous waterfall suddenly stopped flowing, and all of a sudden the deep pool below was dry. The old man and his wife looked into the pool, only to see the yellow and white shiny gold, silver, and precious stones. The bottom of the pool was piled up like stone sand, and there were still quite a few large and small iron boxes sandwiched between them. Shaan Lao threw the rope ladder into the pool with a happy face and slipped to the bottom of the pool. He quickly picked up the yellow fruit at the bottom of the pool and held it, and immediately hurriedly climbed up along the rope ladder with another small iron box. Just when he was halfway up, suddenly, there was a loud noise that seemed like the sky was breaking apart, frightening the old man and his wife. It turns out that the waterfall above was very ferocious, and the deep pool below filled with water in the blink of an eye. When the old man was clear, apart from the rope ladder in front of them, they couldn't see any trace of the old man from Shaanxi.
The old man shook his head, sighed, and took the silver that had shone from his arms, threw it into the deep pool without hesitation. He looked back and said to his wife, “This is not something we crop farmers deserve; there is no use in keeping it.”
Since then, the waterfall has been known as Huangguoshu Falls. Although people know that the deep pool under the waterfall is still full of gold and silver treasures, people can no longer find the key to open it.
