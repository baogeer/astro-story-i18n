After waking up, the couple told each other that they thought this was an immortal sympathizing with their situation and guiding them out of the bitter sea of poverty, so after discussions, the couple prepared simple clothes, brought hunting tools, and went out to find a stone cave containing gold and silver treasures.
During the day, Gole and Callie run in the mountains to hunt and dig holes to find treasure; at night, they rest in caves deep in the mountains and old woods, cook their prey to fill their stomachs, and spend the night heating.
