 One family lives on this side of the mountain, and another family lives on the other side of the mountain. I don't know if it was a marriage in a past life or a blessing from a god or Buddha. The two housewives were pregnant with children at the same time. At a time of good fortune, both women went to the top of the mountain to pray for good fortune. After praising God, the two of them sat on the lawn, toasted and poured tea with each other, and had detailed heart-to-heart conversations at the same time. The more they talked, the more intimate they became. When they broke up, they agreed with each other: if they were all boys in the future, they would marry them as brothers; if they were girls, they would be considered sisters; if they were a man and a woman, they would become a couple.
Soon after, the woman in front of the mountain gave birth to a male doll; the woman at the back of the mountain gave birth to a little girl. Not only does this little girl look cute, but she also has a different characteristic: as soon as she smiles, a snow-white lotus flower blooms on the ground. Not to mention how happy the boys were when they learned about these situations at home. They felt that finding such a daughter-in-law was a result of their past lives.
Who knows that when the little girl was still an adult, her grandmother had an incurable illness. Before she lost her breath, she called her husband over and over and over again about her daughter's marriage; she also took out a pair of pearl death necklaces and tied them around the little girl's neck and advised her not to leave the necklace. When I finished speaking, I left the world sad.
Time passed year by year, and the male doll in front of the mountain grew into a strong young man; the little girl at the back of the mountain also grew into a beautiful big girl. One day, the man sent a messenger holding an auspicious Hada to propose marriage to the woman. At this point, the little girl already had a stepmother and brought a younger sister who was a little younger than her. When the stepmother heard that the family in front of the mountain was very rich, she became angry and whispered in her husband's ear: “I see! Our oldest daughter is so naive. Being a daughter-in-law at someone else's house would be a loss. I think we should marry our younger daughter to them!” The husband said, “No! When her mother was alive, I personally agreed to this marriage; I can't feel sorry for the dead.” As a result, he was happy to receive the marriage proposal messenger and soon set a date for marriage.
On the day they became married, the stepmother woke up early and took her daughter to the family. The three of them walked and arrived at the blue Lake of God. The stepmother said, “Bride, you've just arrived at your husband's house. Wash your face and brush your hair soon!” The girl listened to her, kneeled down by the lake to wash her face, and sat by the lake to brush her hair. The ruthless stepmom took her necklace with one hand and propelled her into the lake with one hand.
The stepmother drowned her ex-wife's daughter, put on a necklace for her daughter, put on a new dress for her daughter, personally delivered her to a house in front of the mountain, and became close friends with the young man.
A poor old man and his wife live beside Lake. This night, when the old lady went to the back of Lake Kamiwa and saw a beautiful pearl tree growing in the water, she stumbled over and called the old man. The old man was so happy that he jumped into the water to pick pearls. The old lady shouted loudly, “Old man, don't pick. Pull it out quickly, carry it home, and do it slowly!”
The old couple spent a lot of energy and didn't bring the pearl tree into their cottage until dark. The pearl tree was placed in the corner of the house, and suddenly moved gently, and eventually became a beautiful girl. The old couple were so scared that they thought they had met the devil. They both knelt down on the ground and bowed their heads and prayed. The girl said, “Two old people, I'm not a devil; I'm the bride from the house in front of the mountain. My stepmom stole my dream necklace, put it on her daughter, and pretended to be a friend of the young man in front of the mountain. Now, my little sister is sleeping, and the necklace hangs on a pillar so I can live. Tomorrow morning, she wears a necklace, and I'm going to die again.” The two old men were very unhappy with the girl and said, “Girl, don't be sad; we will definitely help you get your necklace back.”
The girl listened and smiled, and a snow-white lotus bloomed out of the house with a “jingling bell”. The lotus flower shines brightly, and the fragrance flows through the house. The girl took the lotus flower and handed it to the old man and said, “Please sell this flower to the young man in front of the mountain! When he asked how much it was, you said it must be no less than 100 coins.”
The next day, the old man held flowers and sold them under the young man's window. The young man stuck his head out of the window, and his eyes were immediately illuminated by lotus flowers. He thought: They all talk about my wife, and when she laughs, white lotus flowers bloom on the ground. Since yesterday, she has been giggling and laughing. Why don't you see a flower grow? Besides, where did this old man's white lotus come from? While thinking, the young man went downstairs and bought this snow-white lotus with heavy money.
As soon as it dawned on the third day, the old man was holding another white lotus flower and selling it under the young man's window. The young man quickly took 100 coins and went downstairs to buy this flower. The old man hurriedly shook his hand and said, “I don't want gold coins this time; I want to get a pearl necklace.” The young man wanted to know the origin of the white lotus flower, so he went upstairs. While the fake bride was still sleeping, she quietly took off the necklace and exchanged it with the old man.
The old man was holding the necklace and happily returned to the cottage and hung the pearl necklace on the pearl tree. The tree swung softly, and suddenly became a beautiful girl. The young man in front of the mountain was following in the footsteps of the old man. Seeing this situation, he thought he had bumped into a priestess and shivered all over his body.
The old man cheerfully shouted, “Hey, lad! This is your real bride. The one in the family is fake, fake!” The old two explained everything, before and after, with your words and my own words. Needless to say, the young man was happy to have found his real wife. The girl, when she saw her husband, was extremely happy. Her laughter was like a silver bell, and she laughed in the little house. Snow-white lotus flowers bloomed everywhere. They were beautiful and fragrant.
After two days, the stepmother was so proud that she brought barley wine and gifts to see her daughter. Seeing her, the young man shouted, “Girl, come out and pour tea!” It wasn't anyone else who responded; it was her oldest daughter who killed her. She was so ashamed that she blushed and couldn't say anything. The young man said, “Your heart is really worse than that of a Raksha girl! I'm not going to beat you, I'm not going to scold you, just take your daughter and go! At the same time, I want to say, don't want to walk into my house in this life!”
The stepmother had no choice but to slip away with her crying daughter like a caught thief. Since then, the young man in front of the mountain and the girl from the back of the mountain have formed a happy couple and lived a happy life.
Narration: Pingtou Buchi, Chengguan Town, Shigatse
