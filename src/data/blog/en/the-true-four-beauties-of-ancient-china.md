 Author: Yue Chu
In the traditional sense of the word, the classical “Four Beauties” of Chinese classics — Shih Shi, Wang Zhaojun, Diao Chan, and Yang Yuhuan — are the most popular for two reasons: they have a special political background: Xishi gave up his life to help Wu, and Zhaojun endured humiliation to “Heqin,” and Diao Chan separated Dong Lu, and Yuhuan caused the “Anshi Rebellion”; the second is that all kinds of literary works relating to them have endured throughout the ages, “Sinking Geese, and Shy Flowers” are natural. It has become synonymous with classical beauty. Therefore, the classical “Big Four Beauties” carried more of a cultural burden!
In fact, the prototype of “Shen Yu” was not Shih Tzu; Mao Yu of the same generation as Shih Tzu should have been more perfect than her; Wang Zhaojun was probably just an ordinary beauty in the Han Palace, and was unlikely to stand out in the palace and be ignored by the emperor; Diao Chan had no one; Yang Yuhuan was an ancient song and dance artist, and her appearance was mostly in line with the aesthetics of Li Tang, who is of Northern Hu descent. The real “Big Four Beautiful Women” in ancient China should have had someone else, or an alternative list.
So, who were the real “Big Four Beautiful Women” in ancient China?
An alternative list of the ancient “Big Four Beautiful Women” provided by me is: Mao Qiang, Xia Ji, Li Zu'e, and Zhang Lihua.
I think Mao Gong in the late spring and autumn period should have been the “first beautiful woman” in ancient China, at least during the pre-Qin period. There is no specific record in history books. I only know that she is an Ai Ji who was the late spring and autumn hegemon, Yue Wang Gou, and is roughly the same age as Shih Tzu and Zheng Dan. But we can still know from the praises that future generations gave her; she was the embodiment and symbol of beauty in people's minds in the first place.
The hairtail is the prototype of a “sinking fish.” “Sinking Fish Falling Geese” is derived from “Mao Qiang and Liji, Where People Are Beautiful, Fish See Deep, Birds Fly High, and Elk Sees Decisive” in “The Legend of Zhuangzi Qi”. Zhuang Zhou's original intention was that regardless of whether they are recognized as stunning beauties like Mao Qiang or Li Ji, fish and birds don't know how to appreciate them. Facing beautiful women who are enchanting the country, they either sink to the bottom of the water to swim on their own, or fly high above ground. Zhuang Zhou used this to show that everything is in harmony in heaven and earth. Later, Tang dynasty poet Song Zhiwen Yu Shiyun: “Birds are amazing pinellias; fish are afraid of lotus flowers.” As a result, the world described women as beautiful as “sardines falling geese.” Therefore, the original “sinkfish” refers to “Mao Qiang.”
Among the praises of beautiful women from later generations, Mao Qiang and Shih Tzu appeared at the same time, mostly after Mao Wei took the lead and Xi Shi died. “Han Feizi: Xianxue” says, “The beauty of Shih Tzu is not beneficial to our face. Using fat and fondant is double that of the beginning.” In “Guan Zi: Short Name,” there is “Mao Qiang and Xi Shi, the beauty of the world. They are also full of grievances; you can't think of them as good.” “Huai Nanzi” says, “Today's husband Mao Qiang and Xi Shi are beautiful people in the world.” It can be seen that Mao Yu lived before Shih Tzu. However, due to the reasons mentioned in the opening article, Mao Yi was gradually forgotten, and Shih Tzu became a symbol of beauty, ranking first among the classical “Big Four Beautiful Women.”
The second person on the “Big Four Beautiful Women” alternative list. When promoted as the number one gorgeous woman in Chunqiu, it can also be said that Xia Ji was the number one “popular lover” in ancient times. Xia Ji, the daughter of Zheng Mugong, was born with an apricot face, peach cheeks, and phoenix eyes. Growing up, she was even more like a spring willow, stepping out of a lotus flower. She had the beauty of Liji and Xi Qi, and also had the beauty of being blessed. She was a stunner in the world who envied many noble boys. Later generations called her the “Enchantress of the Generation.”
It is rumored that in her year of remission, Xia Ji tasted forbidden fruit in a trance with a great alien, and obtained the art of healing yin and yang to return to old age and stay young forever. As a result, she was both famous and notorious. The family had no choice but to marry Chen Guo's Uncle Xia Yu as his wife. This is where the name Xia Ji came from. Uncle Xia Yu died when she was in her prime. Some people say it was Natsuji's “healing technique.” After being widowed, Natsuhime was “beautiful and unrivaled, old and resilient.” According to rumors, until her forties, Xia Ji still has a delicate face, delicate skin, and maintains the appearance of a young girl. However, this incredible woman in history has indeed fallen in love with her rare and unique charm. History books say, “If you fight over the king, you won't be confused and disappointed.” She was a queen for three and a wife for seven. It can be said that there were no ancients before, and no one came later. Chunqiu's death in several countries was linked to her.
Li Zu'e, the third member of the “Big Four Beautiful Women” alternative list, is the empress of Emperor Wenxuan of Northern Qi and Gao Yang. “Northern Qi Shu: Wenxuan Li's Succession” depicts Li Zu'e as “very beautiful.” In “The Old Fox Talks About Beautiful People of the Past” by Ehu Eshi of the Qing Dynasty, it is stated that “the old woman Li Zu'e was beautiful and beautiful,” and included Li Zu'e as one of the “Five Great Beauties” in Chinese history. The Goose Lake Issy believes that Li Zu'e was “unfortunately born in Ji Shi, and married to Gao's disrespectful family. She was humiliated over and over again, to the point where the jade flower was ruined.” As a result, future generations almost forgot about her beauty. In fact, Li Zu'e is “favored by the autumn waves, and the divine light is inspiring”, which is “something that has never existed; it is also a beauty that has endured through the ages.”
Emperor Wen Xuan of history, Gao Yang was a famous sexual sadist. He had a penchant for whipping concubines and even killing them in public. Only Jo-ah Lee has always been treated with great courtesy and respect. It can be seen that in Gao Yang's mind, Lee Jo-ah is an absolute beauty that no one can replace. After Gao Yang's death, her son Gao Yin took the throne and honored Zu'e Li as Empress Dowager. Soon, Gao Yin was abolished, and Gao Yang's brother Gao became emperor. Gao Yan died early, and Emperor Wu Cheng's Gao Zhan (the other brother of Gao Yang) succeeded to the throne. Gao Zhan has coveted the beauty of this brother and sister for a long time. After taking office, she forced Jo-ah Lee to have sex with her, and she also became pregnant. After Lee Jo-ah gave birth to a daughter, she was left to death. Gao Zhan immediately killed Jo-ah Lee's son, stripped off Lee Jo-ah's clothes and beat them with a stick, and finally sent her to Miaosheng Temple to become a nun. After the fall of Northern Qi, she was captured and taken to Chang'an, Guanzhong. Perhaps it was Li Zu'e's unfortunate experience that diluted her beauty of “transcendent beauty through the ages.”
The fourth person on the “Big Four Beautiful Women” alternative list is Zhang Lihua, the royal wife of Queen Chen Shubao. The world once called the “Chang'e on Earth.”
According to historical records, Zhang Lihua is elegant, elegant, and like a fairy. “Her hair is seven feet long, her light can be learned, she is sensitive, and beautiful.” Every time she looked forward to staring, she looked even more radiant, reflecting from side to side, which made Queen Chen turn her spirits upside down. Also, according to the “Fireworks Chronicles”, the Jieqi Pavilion where Zhang Lihua lives is also known as the “Gui Palace”. Chen Shubao “made Zhang Lihua constantly train a white rabbit. Lihua was dressed in plain clothes, put in white buns, and pregnant with Baektong, and walked alone in the middle, the so-called Moon Palace.” Every time Shubao Chen comes to have fun, “Hulihua is Zhang Chang'e.” Perhaps in Chen Shubao's eyes, Zhang Lihua really is a “Chang'e fairy” under the Moon Palace. Chen Shubao's famous song “Yushu Backyard Flowers” praises Zhang Lihua. Back then, when the Sui Army destroyed Chen, Yang Guang, who had long admired Zhang Lihua and was beautiful, once ordered to leave behind this “human “Chang'e,” but Gao Yi, who was the chief marshal of Sui Jun at the time, said, “In the past, Taigong masked his face to slay his own face; how can we keep Lihua now!” As a result, Zhang Lihua was slaughtered in Qingxi. This move also became a major reason for Gao Yu's murder later.
