Princess Roujia in history didn't have any romantic stories in TV dramas
The stone monument at Princess Roujia's Tomb is mostly well preserved
Married at age 6, married at age 12, died at age 22 and buried in Mentougou —
In the drama series “Young Kangxi,” which is currently being broadcast on Beijing TV, there is a character that is very popular with everyone — Bing Yue Ge Ge, who grew up in the Wangfu compound since childhood. In the TV drama, she and the teenage Emperor Kangxi were deeply in love with the teenage Kangxi, and later became Princess Shuoroujia. In the end, she reluctantly married Geng Juzhong, a good friend she didn't love and grew up with Emperor Kangxi since childhood.
Historically, there are two figures, Princess Heshuo Roujia and Geng Juzhong, but the historical Princess Heshuo Roujia was not that romantic, let alone fell in love with Kangxi. Instead, she became a victim of politics like many princesses in the Qing dynasty. She married Geng Juzhong when she was 6, got married at age 12, and left the world at age 22. It's impossible for her to have any love story with Kangxi at the age of 22.
Princess Roujia is buried in Longquan Town, Mentougou
After their death, Princess Heshuoroujia and her husband Geng Juzhong were buried together in Longquan Town, Mentougou District. The burial site is commonly known as the “Princess Tomb” or “Geng Wang's Tomb” by locals.
The surrounding scenery of Gongzhu Tomb (Geng Wang Tomb) is beautiful, the Feng Shui is excellent, and the tomb regulations are higher than those of the same level. The entire tomb faces west and east, about 200 meters from east to west. Currently, there are a pair of Optimus Prime (commonly known as Huabiao), which are 5 meters tall. In 2001, an Optimus Prime beast was stolen. There are traces of destruction left on the tomb; the three-hectare Baodong was already destroyed during the “Cultural Revolution.”
There are five tall Dao Long monuments on the cemetery, namely the 15th year of Shunzhi (1658), “The Inscription of Geng Juzhong Ebi”, the 3rd year of Kangxi (1664), the “Princess Heshuorou Jia Monument”, the 12th year of Kangxi (1673), “Princess Heshuorou Jiayu Sacrifice Monument”, Kangxi 26 (1687), “Geng Juzhongyu Monument”, and the first year of Yongzheng (1723), “The Prophecy of Princess Heshuorou”.
Huabiao of Princess Roujia's tomb is in the yard of an oil company
There are no other buildings on the ground of Gongzhu Tomb (Geng Wang Tomb) except for a stone monument with a pair of Chinese watches and a stone monument, but compared to other Gongzhu tombs in western Beijing, this Gongzhu tomb is quite well preserved. Today, Princess Heshuorou Jia's tomb is also the only Qing dynasty princess tomb with an inscription in western Beijing.
Princess Roujia, the daughter of Shunji, married for political needs
Princess Heshuo Roujia is the adopted daughter of Fu Lin, the Emperor Shunzhi of the Qing Dynasty. She was originally the second daughter of Wang Yuele of An County. Princess Roujia was born in May of the 9th year of Shunzhi (1652). She has always been raised in the palace. Shunji loves this daughter.
At the time, in order to appease King Jingnan, one of the three feudal states, due to political needs, in the 15th year of Shunzhi (1658), Princess Heshuo Roujia was married to the grandson of Jingnan Wang Geng Zhongming, Geng Juzhong, the son of King Geng Zhongming of Jingnan. Geng Juzhong was crowned a third-class son and a master. However, the marriage was not complete until November 2 (1663), and Princess Shuoroujia was only 12 years old when they got married.
In the first month of the 3rd year of Kangxi (1664), Kangxi joined Prince Geng Juzhong, Taizi Shaobao, and later joined Taizi Taibao. However, after all, the political marriage was not chosen by oneself. Also, since she was too young, the marriage with Princess Shuo Roujia was not very happy after getting married. She died in the 12th year of Kangxi (1673), when she was only 22 years old.
In the second year of the princess's death, in the 13th year of Kangxi (1674), Geng Juzhong's older brother Geng Jingzhong responded to Wu Sangui's rebellion. Geng Juzhong and his brother Geng Zhaozhong led their son to die and stand by at home. In July of the 14th year of Kangxi (1675), Emperor Kangxi called him to surrender Geng Jingzhong.
In the 19th year of Kangxi (1680), Geng Jingzhong's rebellion was quashed, and Geng Juzhong went to Fuzhou to deal with the aftermath. In February of Kangxi 26 (1687), Geng Juzhong passed away. In April of the same year, Emperor Kangxi sent the priest of the Ministry of Rite, to sacrifice Geng Juzhong on his behalf and gave him the nickname “Que Min.”
It is worth mentioning that Geng Juzhong and Princess Heshuo Roujia have a daughter, Geng Shi. Later, she married Naran Kuixu, the younger brother of Nalan Mingzhu, a famous lyricist from the Qing dynasty, who was famous and powerful in the dynasty. Just like that, Geng Juzhong and Princess Roujia also became the father and mother of the family of Nalan Shengde, the first lyricist of the Qing Dynasty.
