He killed himself
Yang Xin, the resourceful and courageous head of the Serious Crimes Team, became famous for unraveling many old cases and death cases in a row. A poor colleague laughed, “Once Yang Shen arrived, the criminal couldn't escape.” Who would have guessed that as soon as “Detective” came out, it actually became Yang Xin's nickname. In his later work, once a major incident occurred, the leader always made him take responsibility. Of course, Yang Xin made no deductions, and took the lead everywhere.
You can imagine how nervous Yang Xin will be in her future. But every time he brought the criminals to justice and saw the families of the victims cry bitterly, he didn't forget to bow and thank him, and the leaders' admiration, and a deep sense of relief in his heart, making him secretly determined: no matter how difficult it was, never give up on any case, so that good people were wronged and bad people escaped justice.
Since Yang Xin has decided to spend most of her time at work, she is poor at caring for her wife and daughter. Not a few days ago, just because he left halfway for an indoor murder at his father-in-law's birthday party, he was so angry that his wife Zhang Hong simply stayed with her daughter and never came back. After several days and nights of investigation and evidence collection, it was actually his brother who was the murderer due to birth pain.
When Yang Xin dragged her tired body back home, it was already 4 a.m. on June 11. He fell down on his bed and was planning to get a good night's sleep before going to his father-in-law's house to pick up his wife and daughter. However, things in the world always backfire. Just as Yang Xin was sleeping soundly and was suddenly awakened by a sudden ringing of the phone... Yang Xin was only slightly stunned. Due to her professional instincts, she immediately realized that another accident had happened, and quickly picked up the microphone: “Hey, I am, OK — I'll be here in 15 minutes!”
By the time he got back to the station, his assistant Xiao Wang was already waiting for him to leave next to the police car. As soon as Yang Xin waved, let's talk about it again when we get in the car. Yang Xin leaned back in her seat, closed her still trembling eyes, and quietly listened to Xiao Wang's brief account of the case: The deceased was Zhang Lao, a professor who had published many articles and was very famous in the medical community. “Just that single old scholar who is old and still has rumors about rhymes all the time?” Yang Xin still hadn't opened her eyes. She seemed curious and asked Xiao Wang. Xiao Wang even said, “It's him, that's right. The person who reported the crime was a young girl, who said it was his family's little babysitter, named Rolian.
The police car arrived at the scene. Yang Xintai looked at his watch. It was only 6:01 at this point. Yang Xin's first impression of being on the scene was that the deceased was a person who enjoyed life very well and had excellent taste. The staff were busy and meticulously carrying out various tests. They only saw that the deceased had no signs of struggling to resist on a small bed in the study room. It was probably the poison he had taken. Because the small medicine bottle that fell off the ground, after technical inspection, only his own fingerprints, and the room was clean and orderly, and there were no traces of outsiders walking around at all. It was initially determined that it was a suicide. The word suicide lingers in Yang Xin's mind for a long time, like a big question mark. Yang Xin greets Xiao Wang again to the first witness
——Babysitter Rolian inquired about what happened, but she watched the situation herself. The little babysitter's answer was as accurate as the report: I woke up at about 4 a.m., and went to the bazaar to buy groceries. When I came back, I was ready to clean the room first. Zhang was older. In addition to being busy working and sleeping late at night, he never ate breakfast, but hygiene must be cleaned before he woke up. Today was no exception. I pushed open the door to the study room and saw him lying in bed like that, and I felt something was wrong. I tried screaming a few times that they didn't agree, and didn't dare to go ahead and destroy the scene, so I hurriedly went to report the crime.
On the way back, Xiao Wang was driving and leisurely exhaled: Brother Yang, it would be great if all the cases were as simple as this one, and my sister-in-law wouldn't be mad at your mother's house because you only cared about work.
full of doubts
Yang Xin stabs her throat like a fish and doesn't vomit; do you really think the deceased committed suicide? Xiao Wang asked: What, are you wrong with your previous conclusion? Yang Xin nodded and said it wasn't right; I couldn't figure it out. He continued to express his thoughts: With his superior living conditions and conduct, he can continue to enjoy life. How could he commit suicide? Also, Lo Lian, with her temperament and unsurprised demeanor, doesn't look like a working girl at all...
Xiao Wang was also moved: Yes, after careful consideration, I really think this should not have happened, Brother Yang, so what should we do next? At this point, the phone that was not around Yang Xin's waist suddenly rang loudly; when I picked it up, it was actually Luo Lian calling: Captain Yang? There's a new discovery I'd like to tell you. While I was compiling Zhang Lao's data, I saw a few lines of incoherent words, which seemed to have been newly written by him before his death. Can you take a look? “I'll be there soon!” Yang Xin felt refreshed and waved to Xiao Wang: turn her head around. Xiao Wang would like to say: OK. Lo Lian, who was waiting in front of the door, looked more serious than Yang Xin, and wasn't cold. She took them directly into the study room. Yang Xin saw all of this in her eyes and quietly paid attention, but on the surface, she seemed careless. At this point, Luo Lian handed over a card with the words “6.11,9125 days, it's too late to regret it!” large manuscript paper.
Luo Lian was eagerly awaiting his next reaction. Unexpectedly, Yang Xin was very unconvinced after watching it: “We are very grateful for your active cooperation, but this doesn't explain anything; Zhang Lao's suicide is a foregone conclusion.” “What, defined as suicide? You're a detective, and you think so too!” Yang Xin's indifference finally made Luo Lian excited: with his good life, he is also a person who enjoys it. How can he bear his old life, even if he commits suicide, someone must have forced him...
Well, who do you think would push him where? Yang Xin was overjoyed and didn't lose the opportunity to ask. This... maybe... his daughter forced him to do it. In response to Yang Xin's question, Luo Lian flickered her words and then said this. Yang Xin's eyes brightened: He has a daughter, I haven't heard of it. Have you seen it? Lo Lian slipped through a bitter smile that was hard to notice and asked Yang Xin: Zhang Lao hasn't even met his daughter himself. How do you think I can meet his daughter? Next, Luo Lian shared a story: Yesterday, when I came back from shopping at the department store, I saw Zhang Lao sitting in a sofa chair looking depressed, so I walked over and asked him with concern. What happened, he sighed and said that his daughter had arrived in the city. A daughter is here, should father and daughter be happy to be reunited? Xiao Wang couldn't help but ask. Lo Lian continued, “I asked the same question at the time, but he was in tears and only said in half an hour that his daughter had just called and said that she could never forgive him and couldn't hate him to die right away. What else? Yang Xin still reacted indifferently. Luo Lian was really anxious: No, I've said everything I know. I think you should take a trip to Zhang Lao's hometown. His family must have been more detailed than I know. This is a great suggestion. I'll consider it. When I left, Yang Xin said it lightly. On the way back, I couldn't help but be complacent about my ingenious methods. Xiao Wang is even more unhappy: the little girl, pretending to be deep, has never escaped your brother's aggressive generals law! I said Brother Yang, what should we do next? Yang Xin cleared up her head and told Xiao Wang: If you want to see that fog clearly, you also have to wait for me to return; don't fright the snake.
Yang Xin kept on going to Zhang Lao's hometown, a mountain town more than 200 miles from the city of Li Sheng. He first went to the local police station, explaining that the young police officer was assigned and very warmly accompanied Yang Xin to see Zhang Laoshang's cousin — an old lady who had trouble even walking.
It turned out so
Without waiting for Yang Xin to speak up, the little police quickly explained that this comrade is from the provincial bureau and wants to know about your cousin's situation. I hope you know what to say.
Did something happen to him again? The old lady wasn't surprised by the police's visit: it's been over 20 years since he left his hometown, and I was able to find out what happened to him. Yang Xin nodded politely: Let's just talk about his previous situation. Before, OK... before that, the old lady first sighed: our older brother, was really untouchable. He only got married to a foreign girl who didn't know his details. Everyone hoped that he could have a good life. Who knows his fatality hasn't changed. Later, his daughter-in-law got poisoned to death. The most pitiful thing was his daughter, who was only two or three years old, and was also given by him to a family with the surname Luo. Now I don't know if it's life or death.
“How old is his daughter this year?” Yang Xin asked. Oops... the old lady thought about it for half a day and then affirmed, “Born on June 11, '75, that's right. “6.11, is the day of birth,
9125 days is the total number of days in 25 years. They are all listed correctly, that's right.” Yang Xin was so excited that her heart beat faster. She quickly called Xiao Wang: The mystery has been solved, and I'll go back to find Luo Lian right away. Xiao Wang shouted bad on the other side: she had already gone back, but she seemed to have guessed that you would find her, so before leaving, tell me that if you want to find her, go to her hometown, which is a neighboring town of the town you are in.
Without exception, Yang Xin had to go to the local police station to learn about the situation. An old police officer heard that she knew Luo Lian. While looking for information, she said, “The little girl's experience was very unfortunate. When she was young, a couple with the surname Luo adopted her from a neighboring town. Later, her foster father died again, and her foster mother took her back to marriage. Twenty years have passed, and the family was doing well. Unexpectedly, his stepfather's brother broke up the family. One day soon after, his stepfather was killed in his house. Case solved? In response to Yang Xin's inquiry, the old police officer bluntly said how easy it was. Although everything pointed to his brother's family, after many investigations, they were unable to find any evidence of the crime. Soon, Luo Lian's foster mother took drugs because she couldn't keep listening to everyone's gossip. It's been almost ten years since the case was handled, and the mood in the face of all kinds of murder scenes has long become dull and normal. However, when I hear this now, I can't help but feel a convulsive pain in my heart.
Accompanied by the old police, when she arrived at Luo Lian's house, there were already thousands of lights. Forgetting to go through the window, Luo Lian was standing in front of her foster mother's portrait burning incense. She wasn't at all surprised by Yang Xin's sudden arrival, until Yang Xin said in a tone that was both full of blame and concern like a big brother, “You went out of your way to get me here because of the murder two years ago. You're really stupid; you can go directly to me! At this point, Lo Lian couldn't help but burst into tears: You're so famous and busy, I'm afraid I won't touch you... My two mothers died so horribly one after another, so I hate my father! Luo Lian then revealed the story of Zhang Lao's suicide: when I told him I was his daughter, I deliberately provoked him to say that I would never forgive him, so he committed suicide. I watched him take drugs. At the time, I wasn't sad at all, and I didn't even want to stop... Rolian, who gradually calmed down, said bluntly: If this were a violation of the law — then I pleaded guilty.
 
 Guilty or not? That is a matter for future judges; now I just want to take over this murder case. “What you said is true, did I hear it right?” Luo Lian opened her eyes in disbelief. When she saw Yang Xin's solemn expression, she shed tears again with excitement, then bowed respectfully to Yang Xin: Well, thank you in advance — Detective!
 
 “Detective,” it seems I can't live up to this nickname! Yang Xin said to herself with full confidence.
