“Three washes” is a very important ceremony in ancient China's birth ceremony. On the third day after the baby is born, a bathing ceremony will be held to gather family and friends to wish the baby good luck. This is called “washing the child for three times,” also known as “washing the child three times.” The purpose of “washing three” is to wash away the dirt and avoid disasters; the second is to pray for good fortune.
