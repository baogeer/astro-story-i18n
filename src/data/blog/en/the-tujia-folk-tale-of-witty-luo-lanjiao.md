 Roland Jiao is a very talented woman. She is smart, witty, spirited and generous, and people still tell her story to this day.
1. On the way to marriage
When Roland Jiao was 18, she married a hardworking and kind farmer. On the day they passed through the door, the matrimony team was halfway through and suddenly stopped. It turns out that a bridge had just been built before, and they were waiting for the old man from the county to “step on the bridge”. Only after he had passed, were ordinary people allowed to pass through.
“Lu Du-Guan” (also known as “Mr. Bu Li”) made a few negotiations. Master Mason was very embarrassed and feared that he would let Rolanjiao and his entourage take offense. Roland Jiao thought, “As long as the mason's heart is brought to life, they will also take some risks. Let me confess a few words to them.”
So Roland Jiao opened the curtain and said to “Lu Duguan”, “Please tell Master Stonemason, how about I give them a few words?” The stonemason had long heard that Rolanjiao was a beautiful woman. Beautiful, generous, and eloquent, I've never seen them. I had a good chance today. I was able to meet her, so I agreed.
Roland Jiao got out of the car, climbed the bridge in one step, and confessed; “The newcomer stepped on the new bridge. The new bridge is ten thousand years old. Moon floats to the bottom of the water, and a rainbow runs through the riverside. With Mount Taishan over, Ishibashi is better than Iron Bridge”. Master Mason listened. His eyes smiled like pea horns one by one, and hurriedly invited Rolanjiao over. Later, there was a motto in the mouth of the common people: “When the girl goes back to her mother's house, she can't step on a broken teppan bridge.” I heard it was just the last two sentences in Roland Giorgio's greeting that changed.
2. Children don't hate their mother's ugly
One day, Rolanjiao was washing her clothes in the river. A few playboys saw her in shabby clothes. After muttering for a while, they wanted to scold her a bit. Each one of them said, “A Zi B is ugly.” The second one said, “Bing Zi Ding is ugly.” The third one said, “Boko is ugly.” The fourth one says, “Gengzi is spicy and ugly.” Then they all said, “Ren Zi Gui -- Ugly”! I dragged the ugly “word” to the boss for a while, then haha.
Roland Jiao didn't even think about it, so she said, “Boys, you've forgotten the ugly one.” A few playboys were amazed as soon as they heard it. I was busy asking, “Why did you forget the ugly one?” Roland Jiao replied, “Children don't hate their mother being ugly.”
3. Zhidou County Officials
Rolanjiao is very famous. Later, some people with eyes and noses said that there was a big “Never ask for anyone” plaque hanging on Rolanjiao's front door. After hearing about it, the county official went to Rolangio's house.
The county official said, “I've heard that you don't hire anyone for anything. The prefecture has come to borrow four things: one is higher than the sky; two are deep; three are hard like iron; and four are soft as cotton. You can only send it within 3 days”.
Rolangio said, “Master, you can take it with you today. Those who are superior to heaven are the hearts of the people; those that go deep into the sea are government officials; those who are as soft as iron are molesters; and those who are as soft as gentlemen. I don't have the first three in my family. There is a picture of Emperor Guan. I don't know when Grandpa wants it?”
The county official also said, “This prefecture also wants to ask you, what people walk early (in native language, lucky) and who travel late? What is the right time to walk? Who lives a lifetime in autumn (native language, unlucky)?”
Roland Jiao spoke up and answered, “Gan Luo left early; too late; the old man went at the right time; he didn't dare say it when it was autumn.” The county official said, “It doesn't hurt to say it quickly; this county pardons you without crime.” Luo Lanjiao hurriedly came forward and offered a 10,000 blessing to the county grandpa and said, “Under the earth, ordinary people carry autumn.”
