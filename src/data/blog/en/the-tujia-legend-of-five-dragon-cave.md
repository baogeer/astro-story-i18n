 Legend has it that a long time ago, a female dragon and four little dragons lived in a horse cave on the rocky side of Bamian Mountain Paradise Village and Chongqing. These five dragons were very uneasy and ambitious. They threatened to turn Hunan and Sichuan (now Chongqing) into the East Ocean, and the female dragon was in charge of the Dragon Palace.
When Emperor Jade heard it, he sent a dragon slayer to dispose of the five dragons. After the dragon slayer passed down, he arrived at the Five Dragon Cave, caught the female dragon alive, and slaughtered all the remaining four dragons. When the last little dragon was killed, the mother and child hugged and cried bitterly. The dragon slayer felt compassion. They only cut off the tail of the dragon and used spells to destroy it deep in Wulong Cave, while the female dragon was taken to Jiangxi. Mother and child can only meet once every year on May 20, and it must be a storm when they meet. This is the May 20 “Dragon Sang Order,” which is currently being handed down in folklore.
