 A long, long time ago, there was a flood in Changqi, and humans were on the verge of extinction; only a few people survived the disaster by riding a large ship. When the big boat drifted to the current Mount Hachimen, it was unable to withstand the wind and waves and capsized. Almost all of the people on board were buried in the water. Only two brothers and sisters escaped with a large, hollow gourd (now Mount Bamian is the overturned boat). Later, the brother and sister stayed at the bottom of this boat. Since brothers and sisters cannot get married and have children, seeing that humans have since died out, Emperor Jade Emperor sent an immortal to induce brother and sister to get married, so that humanity can continue. However, the brother and sister refused to get married no matter how persuaded them. The fairy was very anxious and asked, “How can you get married?” The brother and sister said, “Unless you roll a stone mill each in the opposite direction, the two stone mills can only get married if they coincide.” As a result, the fairy and the two rolled the two stones in the opposite direction. This happened miraculously. The two stone mills rolling in the opposite direction coincided. The location was Mozi Rock in the current Ikuhashi Village.
