 In 1938, when the Kuomintang moved from Nanjing to Chongqing, Chiang Kai-shek left a backbone for future development and handed over a secret mission to transport some of the gold and silver jewels to the Babian Mountain Swallow Cave on the border with Sichuan (now Chongqing City). The military chief then sent a regiment leader to carry out the operation. The regiment leader sent a platoon of soldiers to transport this batch of treasures to Rieye. In order to keep it secret, they traveled around the clock and night to transport the treasure to the swallow cave in Bamian Mountain. It took more than 30 people three and a half months to complete the shipment. After being transported to Swallow Cave, it was stored in a hole in a cave, mixed with tung oil and lime, then sealed, filled with large and small stones, painted a pattern on the top of the treasure site that only they could understand, and painted a picture of the treasure and handed it over to Chiang Kai-shek, and everyone involved in the incident was solved. At this point, Chiang Kai-shek became the only person who knew about the treasure. After Chiang Kai-shek fled to Taiwan, he always kept in mind this batch of treasures. He also planted a flag on his military map for Bamian Mountain, and airborne agents several times to check this batch of materials, but they were unable to do so. After the liberation of the Chinese people, it also attracted great attention, dispatched a team of soldiers, stationed in Bamian Mountain for a long time, and set up a dedicated telephone line directly to the Guangzhou Military Command.
How did such a secret matter come out? Let's start with the dinner after the treasure hunt. At the time, a soldier had a bad stomach due to gluttony. When the troops gathered in the early hours of the next morning, he had diarrhea. Since it wasn't bright, and the company leader didn't count the number of people, the soldier escaped, fled to Guizhou, went to Guizhou, went incognito, and survived. Later, he also wanted to go back to Bamian Mountain to dig for treasure, but due to tight policies at the time, strict control over the expatriate population, he was always able to do so. After the reform and opening up, with the relaxation of policies, restrictions on people's activities were lifted, and the soldier thought of a treasure again, but when he was too old, he explained the incident to his son. How could he have heard from the wall and was known to another person. His son murdered him on his way to Bamian Mountain with a treasure map and took it away.
When he overheard it, he knew that if he wanted to dig up the treasure, the project was huge, and the cost was not something he could afford, so he sold this picture to a mine owner in Huayuan County. In 1997, the mine owner organized 20 people, brought generators and essential tools, and excavated in Swallow Cave for nearly four months, and did not find any treasure. It ended because of the huge cost.
At this point, the Swallow Cave treasure has become a mystery.
