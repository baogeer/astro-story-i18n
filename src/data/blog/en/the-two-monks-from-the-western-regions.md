 It is said that two monks came from the western region. They were very interested in the famous mountain of the Central Plains, Okawa. One monk wanted to go to Mount Wutai, and the other wanted to go to Mount Taishan. Their costumes, language, and appearance are not the same as those in the Central Plains; you can see their characteristics at a glance. People were very curious when they saw these two monks and asked them what they had worked so hard to come here.
The monk said, “When I arrived here, I have passed through the Fire Mountain, which is full of smoke, and as hot as a grill. If anyone wants to go over this mountain, they must concentrate and not have distractions when walking on the mountain after the rain, keep their eyes on the ground, lift their feet gently, and land slowly. If they accidentally step on a hot stone, they will get burned.”
The monk continued, “Other than that, when you get here, you have to go through the Quicksand River. There is a crystal mountain in the river. This mountain is very steep, with cliffs leading directly to the sky. The peak is very clear on all sides, without a hint of dirt. It seems to be transparent. There is also a gate here. It's wide and narrow enough for a car to pass through. A dragon guards here on both sides of the pass. Anyone who passes through here has to pray. If the dragon allows you to pass by, it will open its mouth. The dragon is all white, and its scales look like crystals.”
Another monk said, “We've been on the road for 18 years. When we first left our hometown, there were 12 people, and only 2 people were able to get here. According to Western legends, there are four famous mountains in the Eastern Central Plains. One is Mount Taishan, the other is Mount Wutai, and the other is Mount Loga. Legend has it that these mountains are full of gold and silver jewels. The legendary Buddha and Guanyin Bodhisattva live here. Everyone who comes here will become a Buddha and can stand for a long time.”
After listening to what the monk said, it felt amazing. On second thought, this isn't just a reason why we admire the pure land of the West here. If people who have traveled to the West meet people who have come to the East to seek Buddha and they talk about these aspirations together, what kind of scene would it be?
