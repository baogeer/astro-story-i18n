 Once upon a time, we had a family with two daughters. The oldest daughter had a dark face, thick and clumsy figure, thick and long fingers, and was very stupid when doing things. She walked very hard, and her body was still shaking, so I knew from afar that she had arrived. The second daughter has a white and tender pink face, a slim and handsome figure, and a pair of dexterous hands. Any handicraft or housework is her strong point. When my sister reached the age of 25, no one came to raise her family. My younger sister was only 18 years old. There were many people who raised her. She married Ruyi Lang Jun when she was 19. Although the younger sister is married, the two sisters are still in touch all the time. The brother-in-law is a hardworking man with a kind heart. He falls in love with his wife on a blind date and is very considerate to his wife. The younger couple had a delicious and happy life. My sister was upset and jealous every time she saw it.
One day, my younger sister went back to her mother's house. When she left, my sister insisted on sending her home. The younger sister thought it was a good intention, so she agreed. Along the way, my sister always made up mistakes and lies to stir up the relationship between my younger sister and her brother-in-law, but the younger sister explained everything and still didn't change her original intention. The sister was helpless. When she walked to the side of a pond, the sister asked her sister to take a break, brush her hair with the pond, and ask her sister to change her clothes after brushing her hair. Facing the figure, the two sisters didn't look alike. The older sister took advantage of the younger sister's defenselessness to push her sister to the pond and drowned. Afterwards, she dressed up as her younger sister and went back to her sister's house. Her brother-in-law was surprised to see what she looked like, and asked, “Why did you get fat when you went back to your mother's house?” She answered, “It was because I got sick that changed my body and appearance.”
One day, my brother-in-law went up the mountain to herd sheep, and another bird surrounded him and called out: “Strange, really weird, sister and brother-in-law are in the same family.” He felt so strange that he brought the bird home. At home, the bird kept calling: “Strange, really weird, sister and brother-in-law are in the same family.” After hearing it, my sister felt uneasy and feared that the incident would come to light. The next day, she took advantage of her brother-in-law going up the mountain again to herd sheep, kill the birds and eat them, and throw the bones in the garbage. When my brother-in-law got home, he found a pair of scissors next to the trash can and he put it in the closet. I opened the closet the next day and took a look. All of my sister's clothes had been cut; only my sister's clothes were in perfect condition. My sister was so angry that she took the scissors and buried them a long distance away.
One night, my brother-in-law had a dream and his beloved wife cried to him, “I miss Fu Jun so much. I miss Fu Jun. He became a bird to see you. He was killed and eaten by my sister, then turned into scissors and buried by my sister. Now I'm cold and hungry, and I'm a lonely wild ghost. Fu Jun, I miss you so much, come and save me.”
After waking up from her dream, my brother-in-law finally learned the truth and couldn't bear it. He angrily killed the sister who had cheated and posed as his wife, and retrieved the scissors.
Since then, he has kept his scissors and lived a sad, angry, and lonely life.
