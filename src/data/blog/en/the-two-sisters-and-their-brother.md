 A long time ago, there were two older sisters and a younger brother in Cheolhyung's place. Due to the premature death of their parents, the two sisters had to take on the obligation to raise their younger brother. The eldest sister is very good-looking and attractive, but she is ruthless and loves to kill, just like being reincarnated by the devil. The second sister has a kind heart. She respects her brother, and regards her little brother as the grease in her heart and her eyes. Seeing that the little brother is getting smarter and more beautiful day by day, the older sister has long been salivating him and is always looking for opportunities to eat him.
On this day, the older sister asked her younger sister to herd sheep, saying that she was cooking at home and taking care of her younger brother. The younger sister discovered her sister's trick and insisted on refusing to leave the younger brother. She said, “Sister, go herd sheep as usual. I'll definitely cook a large pot of the kind of meat you want to eat.” My sister was very unhappy and went herding the sheep.
The younger sister left a sheep from the sheep pen beforehand. When the sister came back, she hid her younger brother and brought out the cooked lamb and said to her, “Eat it! This is my brother's meat.” As soon as my sister heard this, she danced with joy and greedily chewed lamb legs. After eating, she discovered that it wasn't human meat, but she didn't make a sound. The next day, my sister asked my sister to herd sheep. The younger sister had no choice but to hide her brother and went up the mountain.
When my little sister came back in the evening, my sister took out one of her brother's fingers: “You lied to me yesterday; I killed my younger brother to eat today. I'll just leave you with this finger, eat it!” Hearing this, my little sister was sad and angry, but she couldn't do anything about the devil's sister. The younger sister cried sadly. She took her brother's finger and went up a tower on the hill. She placed her brother's finger in the tower and kept reading and praying to bless the younger brother's safety in the next life.
The next day, my sister herded sheep and came to the hill. She discovered that the small pagoda had turned into a small temple. Just when she was stunned, a gust of wind suddenly blew through and blew a sheep towel from the “park” in her arms into the temple. My sister followed her into the temple gate. I saw a zaba (monk) in a red and yellow dress at the temple come to her and give her many treasures such as agate and turquoise.
When she got home, the sister saw that her sister had so many beautiful jewels around her neck, and she hurriedly inquired about the origin of the item. The younger sister told her the truth.
The next day, the devil's sister also learned from her younger sister and came to the temple with a sheep towel. The wind blew several times, but the sheep towel in her arms remained motionless, and her sister had no choice but to throw the sheep towel on top of the temple. She walked into the temple. Yesterday, Zaba came out to receive her, and also adorned her head and neck with jewels. My sister gladly walked home.
The devil's sister came to a mirror-green lake and couldn't wait to see what she would look like after decorating it. When she saw herself in the lake, she was so frightened that she fell into the deep lake and drowned. Turns out what kind of jewelry she saw was a bunch of poisonous snakes and bugs hanging around her chest.
