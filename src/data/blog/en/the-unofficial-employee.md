 There is a vast expanse of pristine forest on the northern border of France. A railway runs through it. Every morning and evening, two trains of trucks facing Cairo pass here on time. There is a small station not far from the forest. There was only an old man named Charlie in charge of the small station. Charlie had no family and no room. He worked here alone for 40 years.
It was a harsh winter day. There were several heavy snowfalls one after another, and almost all of the small red brick stations were covered in white snow. In the evening, Charlie sent off the evening shuttle bus, went back to the cottage, and sat by the bursting stove to warm up. The potatoes baked on the stove gave off an enticing scent. He took a sip of the wine bottle from the closet and took a sip, feeling refreshed, and didn't care about the wind whistling outside the house. Just like this pair, there was a cry coming in from outside the door. It was very weak and poignant. Charlie immediately stood up and opened a gap, and the raging wind swept in a burst of snow. Charlie shivered, and at the same time, a furry thing covered with snowflakes all over his body “whoop” rushed into the house. This thing made a “yo-yo” cry in his mouth. Charlie wiped his eyes before he could see clearly that it was actually a little gorilla. Charlie closed the door. At this point, the little one had already shaken off the snow and hid in the corner of the wall, and his eyes were blinking, staring both timid and hungry at the few potatoes baked on the stove. Charlie had a feeling of kindness on his face. He took a potato in his hand and handed it to him. The little one quickly grabbed it. As soon as he hid while hiding, he “hishehehehe” and gnawed at Charlie hehehe.
From this day on, the little gorilla became a member of this house. Spring came, the snow covered up, and the pristine forest showed a lot of dark green, but this little guy had no intention of leaving. Although he always had to walk in the forest every day, he always came back to Charlie's cabin at night, and sometimes brought Charlie a bunch of wild fruit or something.
Little by little, the little orangutan became big guys, dark fur, and shiny. Old Charlie gave it a very nice name: Genie. Every time Charlie called “Genie,” he immediately “whimpered” and promised to run to Charlie's side, behaving like a sensible kid.
Genie is very playful. She has to imitate everything. Sometimes when the train enters the station, it actually rushed to break the turnaround for Charlie, and even commanded the train passing by while holding a flag. At first, the train driver was taken aback when he saw this strange flag bearer. Slowly, he became accustomed to it, and raised his hand to say hello to him.
It's been a few more years. Amarimi is very old, her back is more bent, and her gait is getting more and more sluggish. Thanks to having a “helper” like Genie by her side, he is still able to hold on to his position as a small stand.
Unexpectedly, on a cold winter morning, old Charlie left his car alone to pick up firewood in the nearby forest. A sudden blizzard buried him forever in the snow. When Ginny slept by the fireside and got lazy, she looked around without seeing Charlie, but never saw any trace of her old friend. Ginny seemed to feel an ominous feeling. Facing a vast snow-covered forest, it was just a whistle. The blizzard fell on it and immediately turned it into a snowman, but it stood upright there like a snow statue.
In the evening, the blizzard stopped, and the sound of a roaring train came from afar. The driver of that truck drove off the train, and he thought there would be an accident. It wasn't until he picked up his flute near the small station that he suddenly felt that the situation was wrong; there was no signal at the small station! He braked in a hurry, but because of inertia, how could the train stop? It was just screaming and rushing forward. At this point, the train driver was so frightened that his body was cold, his spirit was out of his way, and he closed his eyes and waited for the train to break through an untouched turnstile. However, just at this critical moment, the train driver heard a “dumbfounded” sound in a trance, which was the sound of a turnout being pulled! The train stopped steadily with a breath. The train driver opened his eyes like a dream and only came to a clear mind when he saw Shino. He hurriedly jumped out of the train and walked through thick snow to the station hut. He found that the cabin was empty. Looking at the fork, he immediately saw a snowman-like Ginny with the flag raised one by one in his hand, but there was no figure of Old Charlie.
Everyone realized that something had happened to Charlie and immediately went to find them separately. Ginny followed suit, crying hysterically in the face of the pristine forest, making everyone feel tight. However, everyone finally understood that Genie just couldn't break the turnaround, saving the train and everyone's lives. While grieving over Charlie's death, the train driver hugged Genie tightly with gratitude and muttered, “Thank you, good brother!”
The train returned to Lyon. People reported the news of Charlie's disappearance to the General Administration, and also reported on the Genie rescue car. The news spread and shocked the entire media in Lyon. Many reporters rushed to this small, inaccessible border station to interview this spiritual orangutan, and Genie became a star. However, Genie just didn't want to leave the hut and still waited for the train to pass through the station on time every day.
The General Administration of Railways, while re-dispatching a janitor at the small station, issued a special order: to give the station an additional employee's salary. A regular employee at the Genie Small Factory Station. Orangutan employees should be considered a unique miracle in the history of world railways.
