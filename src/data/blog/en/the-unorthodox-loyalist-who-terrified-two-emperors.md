 In March 915, Yang Shihou died of illness in Zhenzhou after Liang Tianxiong's military government. Emperor Zhu Youzhen gifted him as Master and abolished the dynasty on the 3rd to celebrate his death in the palace.
Yang Shihou is the best military general in Houliang. The reason Li Cunyi didn't dare to attack Hebei was because Yang Shihou and the “Silver Gun Saves the Army” he founded in 912. Everyone in the Houliang dynasty knew this truth, and of course Emperor Zhu Youzhen knew it too. However, when such a general who supported half of the country died, he was not only not sad, but why was he very happy?
All of the problems are due to Yang Shi's good health.
Yang Shihou is a native of Anhui. When he was young, he worked under the supervision of Li Han, a deputy procurator in southeast Tang. Li Hanzhi is not a knowledgeable person, so even though Yang Shihou is very brave, he can only be a small soldier. In 884, Li Hanzhi was attached to Li Keyong. Li Keyong lacked brave soldiers willing to send him to death, so he invited Li Hanzhi for someone. Li Hanzhi then gave over 100 soldiers to Li Keyong. Among them was Yang Shihou.
Soon, he fled to Zhu Wen. Zhu Wen was very knowledgeable and appointed him the assassin of Caozhou.
In July 903, Yang Shihou followed Zhu Wen and fought against Li Maozhen. Yang Shihou defeated Li Maozhen. Soon, during the Pinglu period, Wang Shifan rebelled in Qingzhou. Tang Zhaozong ordered Zhu Wen to subdue Wang Shifan, and Zhu Wen handed over this task to Yang Shihou.
Wang Shifan definitely owed Yang Shihou three hundred dollars in his last life. After three battles, Wang Shifan was beaten up and resolutely surrendered. In 904, Yang Shihou was awarded the post of Commander and Envoy of Ma Budu of various military camps by Zhu Wen.
Yang Shihou at this time had already become the supreme commander of the strongest mainland army during the five generations. His every move directly affected the political situation in the late Tang Dynasty.
Then there was the Battle of Yangyang in August 905. Yang Shihou and Zhao Kuangning, another outstanding military commander from the 5th generation, began a decisive battle in Tongshan near Xiangyang. Yang Shihou defeated Zhao Kuangning.
Zhu Wen asked Tang Zhaozong to appoint Yang Shihou as the chief ambassador of Xiangzhou and handed over half of the main power to him to run Xiangzhou. Yang Shihou built Nanluocheng before Zhu Wen became emperor.
In 907, Zhu Wen became emperor and promoted Yang Shihou to the position of the most prominent military general. Moreover, Zhu Wen was in great need of military genius like Yang Shihou at this time because he was at war with Jin Wang Li. Every time Yang Shihou becomes a pioneer in war, the army he leads is the elite that Zhu Wen values the most.
In 911, Zhu Wenhui fought against King Li of Jin. Yang Shihou won the most proud battle of his life—the Battle of Zaoqiang. This battle took more than three months. After invading the city, Yang Shihou slaughtered the city for three days because he hated the tenacity of the city's guards.
Hebei was immediately in the hands of Zhu Wen, but at this point, it was impossible to launch a second round of attack. Zhu Wen then returned to Dongdu and ordered Yang Shihou to be stationed in Weizhou to guard against Jin Wang Li's clan.
In the Tang dynasty, the general guarding of Weizhou was called Wei Bo Zedu Envoy. Since he had a strong Tianxiong Army, he was also called Zedao of the Tianxiong Army. When Yang Shihou defended Weizhou, although he had a strong elite army, he was not a military commander in Weizhou. This is due to Zhu Wen's suspicion of him.
He was in such a position, and anyone would be suspicious of him. Thankfully, Zhu Wen was killed by her son Zhu Youyi before she had time to take action against him.
Yang Shihou “responded positively” in Weizhou, killed the Weizhou dental general, and drove Luo Zhouhan out of Weizhou. Zhu Youyi had no choice but to sign him as Tianxiong Army's ambassador.
Just this year, Yang Shihou recruited thousands of people from the Ya Army to step up military training and “save the army with silver fire.” This was the most powerful army during the five generations. Precisely because Yang Shihou controlled the entire army of Houliang, Emperor Zhu Youyi feared that Yang Shihou would rebel.
He wants to get rid of Yang Shihou. As a result, he asked Yang Shihou to come to Beijing to discuss something. Yang Shihou's staff advised him not to go. Yang Shihou smiled coldly: “I've been helping the Zhu family beat the world for over 20 years, and there's no place where I feel sorry for them. If I don't go now, isn't that proof that I'm selfish? I can tell you what kind of person the emperor is. He wouldn't and wouldn't dare to do anything to me.”
Don't think Yang Shihou is a lone hero. He came to meet Zhu Yougui, but he still has more than 20,000 soldiers behind him.
Zhu Yougui was so frightened that he hurriedly gave Yang Shi money, then sent him away with alcohol and meat. Since then, Zhu Youyi has been even more afraid of Yang Shihou, always worried that he will rebel. But this isn't the scariest thing; the scariest thing is that Yang Shihou simply did not fight back.
But this fear didn't last long, as his bad behavior was cursed from behind bars. His younger brother, Zhu Youzhen, is ready to kick him off the dragon chair and sit on his own.
During the plot, Zhu Youzhen's staff member Zhao Yan told the master: “If you want to become an emperor, you only need to do one thing, and that is to recruit Yang Shihou from Weizhou. If he agrees, this will happen; if he doesn't, even if you remove the emperor, your position as emperor will pass away in a blink of an eye.”
 
 Zhu Youzhen then sent someone to find Yang Shihou. Yang Shihou and his subordinates pretended to be his grandson: “When Zhu Youyi rebelled in the first place, I should have defeated him immediately. Now, there are people who have rebelled again. I have been loyal for the rest of my life; it is the only time to fight back the betrayal.”
 
 His subordinate immediately said, “Zhu Yougui was a son, but he killed his own father; he was also a courtier and killed the emperor. Everyone has a reason to chastise such a person. If you think about it, Zhu Youzhen is preparing to walk for the sky; this will definitely work. If you don't do something now, when they have taken control of the world, what will you do?”
 
 Yang Shihou ordered, “Wang Shunxian quickly went to Luoyang to discuss related matters with Zhu Youzhen!”
 
 They then sent troops into Beijing and were stationed in Huazhou to block reinforcements that might help Zhu Youyi. Zhu Yougui was successfully defeated by her brother Zhu Youzhen.
 
 As soon as Zhu Youzhen ascended the throne, Yang Shihou was crowned King of Ye, and he was not mentioned in the royal decree. This is a great deal of respect, but Joo Yoo-jeong doesn't respect him. Although he discussed everything with Yang Shihou, this was not about respect; rather, he was afraid.
 
 In the five generations, trying to make one person respect another was simply more difficult than ascending to heaven.
 
 Yang Shihou holds a heavy army and guards Weizhou. Every move concerns the fate of Houliang. When he was there, Li Cunyi was afraid to attack Hebei; when he was there, Zhu Youzhen had no choice but to be afraid.
 
 A sword hanging over one's head gives people far greater fear than a flying axe. Yang Shihou would rather make that sword than an axe.
 
 You need to know that in five generations, strong soldiers and horses could become emperors. However, this statement seemed hemiplegic to Yang Shihou. Although Yang Shihou searched for people's money and lives in Zhenzhou, he was very loyal to Houliang. Although he turned Zhenzhou into an even more majestic place than the imperial city, he still considered himself a general of the Liang dynasty.
 
 It's rare! Having someone like this in five generations is simply invaluable!
 
 Why did Yang Shihou make both Emperors Houliang afraid? The main thing is the army in his hands. Why didn't Yang Shihou become an emperor? Probably due to Zhu Wen's kindness in getting to know him.
 
 Yang Shihou's true career began with Zhu Wen. Zhu Wen was good at using people and dared to use people in the process of fighting the world. This is the reason he became an emperor. Yang Shihou was never taken seriously by Li Han or Li Keyong. There's no need to elaborate on the reasons here. A person's success often has many coincidental factors. When these chance factors contribute to a person's success, everyone will regard these coincidences as inevitable.
 
 Yang Shihou said it very well. I haven't felt sorry for the Zhu family over the past 20 years, killing East and Xipin. He thought so, and did the same. Because Zhu Wen promoted him by chance, he regarded this as an inevitable factor in his success.
 
 In order to repay this inevitability, he gave his “loyalty” to the Zhu family. Even if this kind of “loyalty” is only for Zhu Wen alone.
 
 Yang Shihou was a very special person during his five generations. First, he doesn't have any nostalgia for the Tang dynasty; second, he doesn't have any affection for the Liang dynasty, but he has no sense of betrayal. Perhaps, in his opinion, he has a heavy army to protect himself and keep himself at ease. He probably understood that the emperor was afraid of him, but there was nothing the emperor could do about him.
 
 Historically, none of China's military generals have been able to die easily. The reason is that these generals have pure “loyalty”. This kind of loyalty is “loyalty” like a dog, not “loyalty” with clear reasons.
 
 Zhu Yougui wanted to kill him. If he was a loyal minister, and the king made the minister die, then the minister would have to die. However, he was not like this; he led the army to meet Zhu Yougui.
 
 However, he was also a loyal minister, because from the time he took control of Houliang's main forces until his death, he never thought about rebelling against any of Houliang's bastard emperors.
 
 There was no “foolish loyalty” in five generations; this is a major step forward in China's program. However, the emperor was unable to keep this progress moving forward. Shortly after Yang Shihou's death, Zhu Youzhen divided Zhenzhou into two towns for fear that another Yang Shihou would appear. He wanted to scatter Yang Shihou's military strength. However, Yang Shihou's soldiers didn't follow this pattern; they “save the army with silver bullets” did not listen to the command, and surrendered to Li Cunyi in a daze.
 
 Hebei was lost in this way, and Zhu Youzhen soon paid the price of dying and destroying the country.
 
 In Yang Shihou's view, I beat the world for you, and I enjoy the blessings I deserve in a regular manner; this is what I deserve. But if you want to kill me, that's definitely not OK.
