 When Genghis Khan fought against the world, his generals were all good heroes. Among them is a general named Dayu, who is even more resourceful. The army he led went south and was able to drive straight in for a long time without a drop of blood on the blade. His army occupied an important city and village. He has an excellent method of intelligent warfare.
When General Dayu marched out, he didn't have many soldiers and horses compared to other generals, but he had an amazing barracks, which was a camel team. Wherever his army went, they neither stayed in stores, nor harassed people in urban or rural areas. As soon as nighttime arrived, he pulled one battalion of camels into a dragon and then formed a circle. The whole army's soldiers and horses played wild games in the middle. They set fire to cook. After they had enough to eat and drink, they lit pine branches and lit up a bunch of fires to brighten up the camp. Let the soldiers form a circle and kick the ball. They kicked mid-air high and had a great time. Tired of kicking and having played enough, they just lay on the felt to sleep.
They woke up early the next day, cooked on fire, packed up the cowhide balls, threw down the big iron ball, and left with the camels. After General Dayu's army was deployed, the local residents saw that they dropped a few large iron balls at the place where they were playing camp. They couldn't move them even when they moved, let alone kick them with their feet. As a result, legends immediately spread, saying that General Dayu's generals were strong and that a few people were unable to move a large iron ball. They kicked it to the middle of the air. If they fought against such an army, the God Soldiers wouldn't win.
When Song Jun heard this remark, some of the military were terrified at the time. There was also General Nakayoshi who didn't believe the legend; they brought the army to battle. However, whether it was a head-on battle or a night raid to steal the camp, they were all killed by the Mongolian army led by General Dayu, and sometimes no one soldier escaped.
Song Jun no longer dared to go to war. As soon as he heard that the heroic Mongolian army came forward, he was immediately shocked and softened. All the generals had to do was bring soldiers, stand in the hatchback, and claim the throne.
General Dayu led the army until Yunnan and further south. It is said that the Mongolians in Dali today are all descendants left behind by the military from the past.
