 Among the widely spread Tibetan folk tales, there is a legend about the Himalayas: “A long time ago, this was an endless ocean. The waves rolled up waves and hit the coast covered with pine cypresses, hemlock, and palm, making a loud noise. Above the forest, there are mountains covered with greenery and clouds; the forest is full of strange flowers and plants, and groups of zebra deer and antelopes are running; groups of three or five rhinos are walking at a leisurely pace and drinking water by the lake; cuckoos, thrushes, and larks leisurely jump around the treetops to sing beautiful songs; rabbits run carefree on the green, lush green meadow... What an attractive, peaceful and stable picture! One day, a giant five-headed poison dragon suddenly appeared in the sea, messed up the forest, stirred up thousands of waves, and destroyed flowers and trees. The birds and animals that live here all anticipate that disaster is imminent. They jumped to the east, and the forest fell and the grass flooded; they surged to the west, and there were also strong waves on the west side, leaving no one breathless. Just when the birds and animals had no way to go, suddenly five colorful clouds floated over the sea and became five smart flight carriers. They reached the seaside, exerted endless mana, and surrendered to the Five Poison Dragons. Demons have been conquered, and the sea is calm and quiet. The deer, wildeers, monkeys, rabbits, and birds that live here worship the fairies and thank them for saving lives. Zongxing wanted to resign and return to the Heavenly Court. How could all living beings make painstaking demands that they stay here for the benefit of all living beings. As a result, the Five Fairies showed compassion and agreed to stay and share the day of peace with all beings. The five fairies caused the sea to retreat, so the east side became a dense forest, the west side was 10,000 hectares of good fields, the south side was a garden full of flowers, and the north side was an endless ranch. Those five fairies became the five main peaks of the Himalayas, namely: Xiang Shou Fairy Peak, Cui Yan Fairy Peak, Zhenhui Fairy Peak, Guan Yong Fairy Peak, and Shi Ren Fairy Peak. They stand on the southwestern edge and guard this happy paradise; the Cuiyan Fairy Peak, which is headed by that, is Mount Everest. It is the highest peak in the world today. The local people affectionately call it “Goddess Peak.”
Note: The transformation of the Tibetan Plateau from a sea has been proven by more and more scientific research and discoveries. However, the plateau did not form overnight; it changed quite slowly. It was only changes in the earth's crust over the past few million years that caused the uplift of the high source to rise sharply. Measurements of Mount Everest, the main peak of the Himalayas, in recent years have shown that the plateau is still rising. This rate of rise is astonishing in the history of the Earth's rescue, but it has only risen 1 or 2 centimeters in just one year.
