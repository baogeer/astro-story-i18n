 When Qiu Gong of Zhucheng County became an official in Zunhua, there were a lot of foxes in the government office. On the floor at the end of the office building, large groups of foxes live on it. They have become the fox's old nest. They also often come out to harass people. The more they drive them away, the more trouble they get. In the past, everyone who served as a palace here placed offerings and prayed respectfully for them; no one dared to offend these foxes.
After Qiu Gong took office here, he heard that something like this happened, and he was very angry. These foxes were also afraid of Qiu Gong's strong temperament, so they became old wives and told Qiu Gong's family, “Please tell the adults, let's not be feigned. In three days, we led the whole family, young and old, to move out.” Gong Qiu listened but didn't say a word.
By the next day, Qiu Gong had finished his military parade. He told everyone not to disband. He asked the soldiers to carry all the artillery battalions and suddenly surrounded the building behind the station. Qiu Gong ordered thousands of artillery guns to be fired in unison. In the blink of an eye, the tall building was destroyed to the ground. The fur and flesh of a flock of foxes fall from the sky like rain. I saw a drop of white gas rushing through the sky in the billowing smoke. Everyone looked at the sky and said, “A fox has escaped!” Since then, however, the department has been safe and sound.
Two years later, Gong Qiu sent some money from his powerful servants to Kyoto. He planned to entrust someone to handle the promotion. Before the matter was settled, he temporarily hid the money in Banji's home. Suddenly, an old man went to the palace to complain, saying that his wife had been murdered, and he also revealed that Qiu Gongke withheld military salaries and bribed senior officials. The money was now hidden in someone's home, so it can be checked. The emperor ordered the old man to go to the banjiya for inspection, but no matter how he searched, he couldn't find any money. The old man used a foot in the ground and sent someone to understand what he meant. Once he dug up the ground and took a look, he certainly dug up the silver. “A certain county settlement” was still engraved on the silver. Then look for the old man, but he's gone. The government took the local account register and wanted to find this old man, but there was no one. Prince Qiu was executed in this case. People only know that this old man is a fox that ran away back then.
