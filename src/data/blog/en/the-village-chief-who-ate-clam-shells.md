 A long, long time ago, villagers in the mountains went on their first trip in their lives with the village chief.
Also, I stayed at a luxurious inn and had clam soup for dinner.
Since they live in the mountains, the villagers saw clams for the first time, and no one knew how to eat them.
As a result, everyone asked the village chief how to eat it, but the village chief also saw clams for the first time, and he didn't know how to eat them.
“Well, how can I eat something that hard. ... But no matter how hard they are, they're all in the soup, so I can only bite them. But the soft stuff inside is offal, so don't eat it.” Having said this, the village chief threw away the delicious clam meat and began to eat it with a squeaky feeling of chewing on the clam shell.
“Yes, that's how it was eaten?” So everyone else tried hard to bite the shell and eat it.
