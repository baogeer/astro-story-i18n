Qian Lou is a native of Lu Guo. He lived during the Warring States period. At this time, Zhou Wang gradually declined, and the division hierarchy gradually collapsed. Academic ideas flourished, forming a heyday situation of “100 families fighting”. People from all countries gathered talented people by their side. Civilians began to climb the political stage, and often formed a “clothed” situation.
Qian Lou is also a knowledgeable person. He has written four books explaining the main theme of the Taoist family. Despite being a family member, he has been highly praised for his inspirational hardships, happiness, and poverty, and he does not participate in that kind of battle for fame and profit.
Qianlou came from a poor, civilian family, but Mrs. Qianlou is from an aristocrat family. Mrs. Qianlou is named Shi Liangdi. She is well-educated, beautiful and dexterous, and can be called extravagant. His father's official residence “Taizhu”, the so-called “Taizhou”, is the official position of holding ghost sacrifices for the imperial family. “Taizong,” “Bu Zheng,” and “Taishi”, “Taizong” addressed demons on behalf of the priest; “Taizong” managed the temple and arranged sacrifices; “Bu Zheng” specialized in divination; and “Taishi” recorded current events, observing the sky, and protecting documents. In the Shang Zhou period, when people were superstitious about ghosts, these were four very important official duties.
These four official positions in charge of devil sacrifices have been passed down from generation to generation. Due to the large number of children and grandchildren, the foodstuffs were not enough to make a living, so they took their professional talents away from the Zhou royal family and gradually split into various countries to seek development. This is how the Shi Family came to Lu Guo, and Shi Liangti's father also became Lu Guo's “great blessing.”

According to the patriarchal system of the Zhou Dynasty, women had no patriarchal status in the mother's family, let alone any inheritance rights. Therefore, no matter how prominent a woman's mother is, once you get married, it has nothing to do with it; it just depends on your husband's family's situation. The so-called “marry a chicken, marry a dog.” Your mother's family is only your temporary place of residence; your husband's family is your permanent destination. The four chapters of “Qianlouzi” are aimed at explaining Taoist law. The Fuxi clan uses tortoises to show the number of gossip in the sky and study the principles of the formation of heaven and earth, focusing on the philosophy of operation from heaven and earth to find the rationale for cosmic change. Its meaning is divided into two major parts: one is the “mathematical” part, such as the two techniques of Taiji, the four phases, and the four-phase gossip, which is then converted into 64 triangles and then 384, thus further deriving 1,520 strategies, which are the “constants” of everything in the universe; the other is the “chemistry” part, such as the five elements, yin and yang, odd, and reverse, always, the “variation” that makes up everything in the universe. Qian Louzi believes that “mathematics,” the total number of everything, is the “nature” of everything. “Chemistry” is the “quality” of everything in the form of everything. It is innate in nature and becomes its quality. From the intangible to the tangible, it is a step in the evolution of everything.
