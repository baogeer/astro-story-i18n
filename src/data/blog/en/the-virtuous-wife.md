 In the old days, there was a man named Qi Gong. The family is poor. Thanks to the financial support of his wise wife and mother, he started a trade selling grain and oil. After a few years, he earned a family business and became a rich local person.
After becoming rich, Qi Gong discovered that his wife was old, her hair was mostly white, her graceful figure had lost shape, and her face had lost its smoothness. His looks don't match him. Also, looking at his business friends, they are all three wives and four concubines. He is itchy in his heart, and he also has thoughts of being a concubine.
Once he went out to buy goods, he met a diva in Yanjie Liu Alley. Not only did she sing small songs well, but she also looked like a flower, and was loved by people. As a result, he spent a lot of money to marry this diva back home. The wife was very angry, but she was also helpless. She had to endure her sadness and arrange for the concubine to stay. That night, Qi Gong and his wife were discussing business matters in the bedroom and overheard someone knocking on the door. Qi Gong angrily asked, “Who?”
The diva timidly said, “Master! It's windy outside, and I'm afraid to sleep on my own. Would you sleep with me?”
Qi Gong was very happy to listen to it. He hurriedly bid farewell to his wife and went back to the house hugging the diva, completely ignoring his wife's feelings.
After Qi Gong left, his wife cried the whole night. The next day, she voluntarily moved down to sleep and gave them her main house. Since then, she has never left the front door without leaving the door without leaving the door, praying the Buddha every day, and no longer managing family affairs.
However, in Qi Gong's eyes, only a diva, he had no sense for his wife. When she wanted to stay out of the house, she went with her! I don't care about the couple's relationship at all.
After a few years just like this, one day the wind suddenly became very strong, causing a storm. The storm was raging. It overturned Qi's main house in the blink of an eye. The collapsed wall just hit Qi Gong's body. He screamed in pain. He wanted to run, but his leg was crushed to death and unable to move.
I had to shout for the diva to save him, but I saw that the diva was getting ready to run away. He was angry and hurriedly scolded. The diva looked back and said, “If you don't die, I'm entrusting you with the intention of being rich, but now your house has collapsed. If you rebuild it, you will definitely spend all your savings, and I don't want to stay with you to suffer.”
After speaking, I hugged Si Soft and ran away.
Just when Qi Gong was completely desperate, his wife rushed in in a panic and went to remove the bricks on his feet without saying a word.
Qi Gong was ashamed to have a wife to save himself and said, “I treated you like that before, why are you still helping me! Let's go! This house is about to collapse, don't worry about me.”
While moving the bricks, the wife said, “You're my husband, how can I leave you alone, is that still a human?
After listening, Qi Gong bowed his head down even more ashamed. Since then, Qi Gong has never married a concubine.
