 Mizumo is a poisonous herb. It grows like kudzu, and its flowers are purple, like lentils. If people eat this poisonous herb by mistake, they will die instantly and become a “water maniac.” According to folklore, this kind of demon cannot be reincarnated; it must be replaced by someone who has been poisoned before it can give birth. Therefore, in the Taohua River area in central Chu, there are a lot of such water scoundrels.
People in central Chu refer to people of the same age as “the same age.” When visiting, they called each other Geng Brother Geng Di, while their children and nephews called them Geng Bo. This is a local custom.
