 In the old days, Dragon King of the East China Sea left the palace to go on a cruise.
On this day, Dragon King cruised to Yuanxin Lake and discovered that there were many sparkling water droplets on the lake. Dragon King asked his companions to pick them up and put them in the palm of their hands. The drops were like pearls. Dragon King watched and tasted with love. The drops were slightly sweet and fragrant and delicious. Since then, Dragon King has called this water pearl water.
When Dragon King returned to the Dragon Palace and was determined to find the pearl water source, he gave an order for the fishermen and shrimp to go all over the world to find it. Fish and shrimp will run all over the world and five lakes, and they haven't found them, so they have rewarded Dragon King. However, Dragon King was still not satisfied, and gave another order so that the fishermen would search for shrimp again.
Dragon King's fishermen will finally find a high mountain, Changbai Mountain, in a remote location in the northeast corner. Seen from a distance, there is a pool of clear water at the top of the mountain. Pearl water flows from this deep valley.
When Dragon King knew where the pearl water came from, he wrote a book to Emperor Jade. Emperor Yu was very happy and sealed this pool at the top of Changbai Mountain as Tianchi. The good news that there was pearl water in Tianchi soon spread in the Tiangong.
On the morning of this day, the South Tianmen Gate opened with a “loud cry,” and seven fairies dressed in colorful clothes came out of the door. They drove Caiyun to Tianhe in Changbai Mountain.
Seven beautiful and handsome fairies are shown on the clear blue surface of Tianchi. After watching the beautiful scenery surrounding Tianchi, she took off her colored coat and went down to Tianchi to take a bath. Fairies swam around the pool and have fun. A fairy accidentally took a sip of Tianchi water and thought it was really sweet and refreshing. She thought, “How good would it be if I let this water flow down the mountain and let everyone in the world drink Tianchi water. She shared her thoughts with her sisters, and the family all agreed. As a result, the seven beautiful fairies scooped up Tianchi water with seven wooden spoons and splashed it to the north. The spoons of water passed through the dragon gate and went straight down the rock cliff, turning into a waterfall, and flowed in twists and turns into the pine forest. In spring, pine blossoms fall on the surface of the water and drift down the mountain. Later, people named it the Songhua River. The fairies also scooped the water to the east. As soon as the water flowed down, it was blocked from its way by a towering mountain peak. This stream of water turned its head down the crevices of the stone. It flowed a long way before it gushed out of the valley to the ground and continued to flow east. People named this river the Tumen ① River. The fairies also scooped water and spilled it on the southwest side. This stream of water was blocked by overlapping mountains, then swirled around the peaks and flowed all the way to the sea. People named it the Yalu ② River.
Since then, Tianchi in Changbai Mountain has nurtured three rivers — Songhua River, Tumen River, and Yalu River.
These three rivers flow freely in the three directions of north, east, and west. Over the years, it has nourished the fertile land and nurtured thousands of people on this land.
-----------------------------------------------------------------
① Tumen: In the family language, it means to run away.
② Duck green: A family word that means passing through many mountains and steep mountains.
