 At the time of Xie Qian's rebellion, the houses of the government and lieutenants were all occupied by thieves and became a den of thieves. There is a student named Wang Qixiang. There are especially many thieves living at home. After the officers and soldiers destroyed the city, they swept away the bandits. The dead corpses all filled the steps, and blood flowed down the door.
Wang Xueshi entered the city, returned home, and ordered someone to carry out the thief's body and wash off the blood stains. Only then did he stay. However, I often see ghosts during the day. At night, phosphates fly under my bed, and there are often demons crying in the corner, so I'm not at ease.
One day, there was a scholar named Wang Yidi who borrowed from Wang Gong's house. At night, I heard a quiet cry from under the bed: “Doody! Doody!” After a while, the voice grew louder and said, “I died so bad!” Then I started crying, and then there was crying all over the yard. After Wang Gong heard it, he went to Wang Sheng's house with a sword and shouted, “Don't you know I'm Wang University?” I only heard the devil scoffing and sneering.
As a last resort, Wang Gong set up a water and land dojo. He ordered monks and Taoists to read the sutras excessively, and cooked meals and left them in the yard for the devil to eat at night. At this point, I saw phosphorous spots in the yard; they were everywhere.
Previously, a person surnamed Wang, who was watching the gate for Wang Gong, was very sick and had been unconscious for a few days. On this haunted day, he suddenly stretched out and felt like he had woken up. His wife brought him a meal when she saw this situation, but he said, “I don't know why the owner just cooked the meal in the yard, and I ate it with everyone. It wasn't until I came back full, so I didn't feel hungry.”
Since then, demons have all died out. Is it true that Taoists play music, and monks go overboard and give food, really spiritual?
