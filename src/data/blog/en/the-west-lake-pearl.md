 Legend has it that in ancient times, in the grottoes on the east side of the Tianhe River, there lived a snow-white jade dragon, and in the large woods on the west side of the Tianhe River, only a colorful golden phoenix lived.
Yulong and Jin Feng are neighbors. Every morning, they each drill out of the grotto, and the other flies out of the woods, and they are busy doing various things. One day, they both fly in the sky, and the other is on the Tianhe River, fly, swim, and before they know it, they come to a fairy island. They find a shiny stone on the island. Jin Feng liked it so much that he said to Yulong:
“Yulong Yulong, look how beautiful this stone is!”
Yulong also liked it, so he said to Jin Feng:
“Jin Feng Jin Feng, let's think of it as a bead?”
Jin Feng nodded and agreed, and they both started construction. Jade Dragon caught it with its claws, and Jin Feng pecked it with its mouth. Day by day, year by year, they both actually turned the stone into a round, round bead. Jin Feng happily flew to the top of Xianshan Mountain, and many drops of dew dripped onto the beads; Yulong happily swam to the Tianhe River to absorb a lot of fresh water and spray it on the beads. Drop, drop, spray, spray, slowly this bead becomes bright and shiny.
Since then, Yulong loved Jin Feng, and Jin Feng also liked Yulong; both Yulong and Jin Feng loved their pearls. Yulong didn't want to go back to the grottoes on the east side of the Tianhe River, and Jin Feng didn't want to go back to the woods on the west side of the Tianhe River. They both stayed on Fairy Island in the Tianhe River and guarded their pearls day and night.
This pearl is really a jewel. Wherever the pearl shines, the trees are evergreen, the flowers are in full bloom, the mountains are bright and the water is beautiful, and there is a good harvest of grains.
As soon as Mother Wang walked out of the palace, she saw the treasure of this pearl. She was very envious. At midnight, she sent a close friend of the Sky Soldier to quietly take this pearl while sleeping with Jade Dragon and Jin Feng. Mother Wang got the pearl. She loved it so much that she couldn't bear to even look at it, so she hid it inside the Fairy Palace, closed the Kuju Gate on the outside, and locked it with nine locks.
Yulong and Jin Feng woke up to find that the Pearl was missing. They were both really anxious! Looking east, searching west, and Yulong searched every grotto under the Tianhe River; Jin Feng searched every corner of Xianshan Mountain and didn't find them. They were both extremely sad, but they were still searching everywhere day and night, trying to get their beloved pearl back.

At this point, Yulong and Jin Feng were looking everywhere for their pearls. Jin Feng discovered the bright light emitted by the pearl, so they were busy calling them Yulong:
