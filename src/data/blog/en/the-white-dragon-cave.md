 Long ago, the southwest of Miroku County had endless blue water. The lakeside was full of green mountains, fishing songs in the lake, and people of all nationalities lived in an environment full of flowers and sheep. The jealous and ferocious Black Dragon used magic to destroy the peace and quiet. Instantly, the lake dried up, the seedlings died, and the townspeople were unable to live.
Yulian, a naturally beautiful and very kind dragon girl, cruised across the southern sky. Seeing that there were many prayers on the shores of Miroku Lake, she was determined to bring clouds and rain to benefit her. However, the vicious Black Dragon blocked her and stole her sword that called for wind and rain.
A young man named Ah Qing led everyone to dig a well to save themselves. After a few twists and turns, they finally dug up a clear spring, so that every hungry person can drink a little bit of spring water to continue their lives. After 982 days, the clear spring suddenly disappeared; however, I saw a white slit fish breathing by the pool. Ah Qing saved the white mackerel with only a little bit of water. The mackerel instantly became a young girl in a white dress, and she had a hard time telling Ah Qing about her experience. It turns out that after losing her sword, Yulian went into exile and spit water with her weak body to save the lives of the hungry. She had no choice but to live for a long time, had no physical strength, and finally got trapped in the water. After speaking, he handed over the coral treasure to Ah Qing and advised him to continue digging the well. Ah Qing finally dug up a clear spring, and Miroku Dam restored the clear, pink, and green scenery of the lake. Dragon Girl is happy to stay on earth and bond with the kind and hardworking Ah Qing.
The ferocious Black Dragon hates the beautiful scenery of the world and the beauty of Yulian. Once again, he secretly devised poison schemes. At once, sand and stones were scattered everywhere. Unfortunately, Ah Qing was murdered. In order to save lives, Yulian struggled to remove the scourge with a spoon. Hongxi Dam came back to life, yet the kind and beautiful Magnolia has since left the world.
I don't know how many years and generations have passed, and people have finally found the underground palace, the White Dragon Cave, carefully sculpted by dragon girls in the mountainside of Hongxi Mountain, which is full of cane groves and fragrant flowers and fruits.
