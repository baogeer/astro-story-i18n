 Legend has it that there is a little white dragon in Laguolongtan. It can transform into an adult body, both a man dressed in white and a woman dressed in flowers. It is said that people with good fortune and good luck will live long and rich when they see dragons that turn into human bodies; people who see dragons with long horns, long turtle shells, long limbs, and bright gold will live a short time, and those who don't die will also suffer for the rest of their lives.
People in ancient times had to go to Longtan to burn a set of incense, paper, and fire for Dragon King, so they could borrow a golden bowl, golden spoon, and gold chopsticks from Dragon King. It doesn't matter if you accidentally use it; if you return the broken one, it won't blame you; if you don't return it after using it, you won't be able to borrow it again. If you go to the side of Longtan and pay attention to hygiene, it will show you its scary dragon body and scare you to death. When a good person passes by it, it becomes a man or woman walking in front of you or stumbling behind you.
According to the old man, some abominable people went to the side of Longtan to kill dogs and eat sheep. Since then, you can't borrow any golden bowls, golden spoons, or gold chopsticks. The water in Longtan is small, and they even now give you a dragon body with long horns, long limbs, and a long turtle shell, making it difficult to save your life.
