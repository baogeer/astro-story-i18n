The old man said that this is a bird of repentance. It regrets the mistakes it made back then and said, “My fault, my fault.”
What did the penitent bird do back then?

The newly married wife has a very bad heart. She treats her two sons very kindly. She grins in front of the boss and is just scolding at every turn; and for her own biological son, it is Bai Yi Bai Shun. If you want to give anything, even if you want stars, you have to shoot them down with a rod.
One night, my stepmom said to the boss, “You're not too young; you should do something. On the plot of land at the foot of Houshan Mountain, your brothers and sisters went to grow sesame seeds. Whoever grew the seedlings came back to see me. If they can't grow it, they won't come back. This is a sesame seed, one bag per person.”
Her stepmother got up in a crooked manner. She gave a bag of fried sesame to her boss and raw sesame to her brother. She thought that if the old ripe sesame seeds don't sprout and grow into seedlings, they won't be afraid to come back; it's best eaten by wolves in the mountains.
The two brothers hit the road. The older brother is usually used to eating it. While walking, he grabbed sesame seeds to eat.

The boss usually uses the old one; he replaced it. Once replaced, the cooked sesame was sold to the old used one.
My older brother planted ripe sesame seeds, but not a single tree grew. However, the boss's sesame soon broke ground. The boss was kind. Seeing that the old man's sesame had not been grown, he went back with his brother.
As soon as the stepmom saw that the boss wasn't dead, she was not happy. When she slept at night, she gave the boss a stone pillow, gave the brother an embroidered pillow, and asked the brothers to sleep in a bed. They planned to cut the boss down at night. They came all the time.
However, the weather was hot, and the old man couldn't sleep on the embroidered pillow. He felt that the boss's stone pillow was cool, and proposed to switch to the boss. The boss didn't hesitate to switch pillows with his brother again.
At night, my stepmom quietly touched the house, and when it was dark, she didn't turn on the light, so she touched her pillow. I first touched the embroidered pillow, thought it was my son, then let go, then touched the stone pillow. Without saying a word, a “click” on the neck was a knife. The poor old man was speechless and was hacked to death by his own mother.

Only then did the stepmother learn that she had killed the wrong person. Her intestines were all blue in regret. Thinking about her dead son, she suddenly got angry.
