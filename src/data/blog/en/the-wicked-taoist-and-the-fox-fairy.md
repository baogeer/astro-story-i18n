 The Will of Liao Zhai, a New Chapter of Jingyue | Praise the Feelings of Ancient Times, Be Decent in the Present
During the Wanli years of the Ming dynasty, there was a Shuxiang family with the surname Chen in Laizhou. From generation to generation, the Chen family had a simple academic style and an extremely noble family, and many people were promoted. In Chen Tian's generation, they are even more prosperous.
As the eldest son of the Chen family, Chen Tian has been brilliant since childhood. At the age of 17, he took the lead in his hometown.
After being sent off by the people of Laizhou, he rode a high-headed horse to Beijing to take the exam. Before leaving, Father Chen quietly stuffed him with 32 silver ingots so that he could eat and stay well on the way, and not treat himself badly.
Father Chen advised his son not to reveal the money; otherwise, it could be a financial disaster. Chen Tian resigned after he got down.
On this day, Chen Tian came to the foot of a mountain on horseback. Seeing the beautiful surrounding scenery, he stopped and planned to enjoy it.
When he came out of his bag with wine and vegetables, he accidentally spilled the silver bars in the bag. At this point, it just so happened that a Taoist in a tattered suit was passing by and said to Chen Tian, “The client was in Beijing to rush to take the exam.”
Chen Tian claimed yes, and the Taoist also said, “The top of the mountain here is a good Feng Shui treasure. If the donor is willing to donate some money to build a Taoist house on it to worship God, I will bless your high school champion.”
Chen Tianzhen was young and naive; he agreed without much thought, then donated a penny of silver to the Taoist. The Taoist smiled and invited Chen Tian to stay at a nearby hotel as a witness, then proceeded to ask someone to build the Taoist Temple.
Chen Tian stayed for a few days, enjoyed the beautiful scenery nearby, and then went up the mountain to say goodbye to the Taoist.
At this point, the Taoist Temple at the top of the mountain had already built the foundation and walls, and the Taoist said to Chen Tim, “The two pieces of silver given by the client are only enough to cover half. Why don't good things help to the end, then donate two more silver!”
Chen Tian was a bit embarrassed to hear it, but when he saw that the Taoist had a serious expression, he gave him two more bars of silver.
After receiving the money, the Taoist once again forcibly left Chen Tian behind, saying that he would attend the opening ceremony of the Daoist Hall; otherwise, it would not work. Chen Tian planned out the dates for his next trip. Seeing that there was still some time left until the exam, he agreed.
After ten days in the blink of an eye, a brand-new mud hall was finally built. However, the three real celestial statues in the temple are nothing but clay statues. The Taoist went to Chen Tian again and said, “If the statue needs to be painted with gold today, why don't the client give me 22 more dollars to get this done?”
Chen Tian refused. When the Taoist saw that he didn't give, he cursed him, and when he saw no one, he tried to beat Chen Tian.
Poor Chen Tianshen is proud and rich. He has never seen a wicked person since he was born until he grew up. He was so frightened that he hurriedly gave all of his money to the villain, and then wanted to say goodbye as soon as possible.
Unexpectedly, the Taoist reached over and grabbed him and harshly said, “I still don't have a Taoist in this Daoist, so stay and be my Taoist. In the future, it's better than being an official in the exam.” At this point, Chen Tian burst into tears and begged the other party to let go of himself. But the Taoist didn't listen and dragged him into a firewood house and locked him up after a few moments.
From autumn to winter, half a year has passed, and the academic exam has already been completed, and the scholar Chen Tian is still being imprisoned by evil spirits.
One day, Chen Tian fell asleep in the firewood room. He was suddenly woken up by a Taoist and ordered him to go up the mountain to cut firewood. Chen Tian was afraid that he would beat him; he had no choice but to listen. After getting up and drinking two bowls of porridge, I went to the mountain to cut firewood with a hemp rope firewood knife.
After being cut for a while, Chen Tian's hands felt a lot of pain, so grief came from it, and she cried right away.
Chen Tian was sad for a while. Her stomach was unbearably hungry. Thinking about not eating again, she might starve to death. So I had to pick up a wood knife and get ready to start cutting firewood.
At this point, on the mountain road in front of me, an incredibly beautiful woman suddenly walked over. The woman came to Chen Tian and looked at him, “Well, scholar, why didn't you study to cut firewood in the mountains; are you being bullied?”
Chen Tian hurriedly wiped away her tears and told how she had been deceived.
The woman looked at him and said, “I can't see it. I think it was Xiao Xiu who was scolded by her daughter-in-law? Are you still acting alone? That Taoist is also so abominable. Do you want me to help you get revenge?”
Chen Tianyi listened and nodded, “If my sister is willing to help me get revenge and save me from a sea of suffering, I will definitely repay you.”
However, Chen Tien also had doubts in her heart. Although this woman is very beautiful, she looks weak. Is she the daughter of a powerful family? Can't she fight that evil on her own?
The woman smiled and said, “I'm a mountain fox fairy. It's easy to save you, but I want to know how you can repay me? You need to know that there are no free lunches in the world, and saving you is a great blessing.”
Chen Tian was shocked to hear that he was a mountain fox fairy. He said that his family is very rich, and if you can save him, you can thank him for a thousand and two.
The woman scoffed and said, “That Taoist is greedy for your money, why wouldn't I care about such vulgarity? OK, after I saved you, I put forward a condition to guarantee that you can do it, and it won't be a bad thing. Can you agree?”
Chen Tian quickly agreed, then took the fox fairy woman back to Daoguan.
At this point, the Taoist was walking out of the door. Seeing that Chen Tian had brought back a beautiful woman, he angrily scolded, “Okay, you little bastard. I told you to cut firewood; instead, you are molesting a woman from a good family.”
Just throw out the whip and pick Chen Tian. At this point, the fox fairy recited the mantra. A white smoke fell from the sky and instantly tied the Taoist's body. The Taoist couldn't control it. He slapped his arms left and right, and kept slapping himself. Soon, his face swelled like a pig's head, and lost a few teeth.
As soon as the fox fairy pointed a finger, the Taoist was tied up with a rope and flew towards the mountain in front of me.
Moments later, there were a few tiger roars and the screams of Taoists in the mountains. Chen Tian was frightened and looked bleak. She thought that although this fox fairy was beautiful, she had a bit of a scary heart, but it wasn't easy to say anything when she came looking for her herself.
After the incident, the woman clapped her hands and said to him, “Let's go and repay the favor with me!”
Chen Tian was helpless and had to follow the woman deep into the mountains and into a cave.
The woman looked at him and said with emotion, “Boy, you're my person for the next year. Is it OK for us to have fun as a couple? I've been training for thousands of miles in the mountains, and I'm about to soar. I haven't experienced the joy between men and women, and I want to try it out so I don't have any regrets. Can you agree to this condition?”
Chen Tian was taken aback. There was some reluctance in his heart, but he had just seen how powerful the Fox Fairy was. Thinking about it, it was better than the Taoist beating him; he had to agree with a stiff head.
The woman stepped forward and hugged him, and the two fell into a haystack in the cave. They were very happy.
A year later, Fox Fairy gave birth to a son. She is very affectionate with Chen Tian. However, when summoned by the superior, they had no choice but to fly away. Chen Tian was holding her toddler who had just reached full moon. She was sad and helpless. After leaving the mountain, she had no choice but to go back to her hometown.
Father Chen saw his son return with his grandson in his arms. He was very happy! After a few years, Chen Tian rushed to the exam again, and finally got into the exam.
