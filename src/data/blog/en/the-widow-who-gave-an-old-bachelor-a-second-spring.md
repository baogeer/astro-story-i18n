 At the end of the Qing dynasty, Wang Erzhuang, a small rural village in southern Anhui, was a real old bachelor. One person was well-fed and the whole family wasn't hungry. To be honest, in those days when supplies were scarce, they saved him quite a bit of food rations, and even had some surplus that could be exchanged for small alcohol. I'm just a person who's been a bit lonely for too long, so I haven't been honest in my thoughts. In particular, a new widow recently appeared in the village, making Erzhuang's food unsweet and unable to sleep.
In fact, it's not that Wang Erzhuang hasn't had a daughter-in-law; it's just that his life is suffering; his daughter-in-law, who never passed through the door, suffered even more; she became ill even more. Even so, Erzhuang married her home. Unfortunately, she stayed in bed for a year and then left. After that, it wasn't that no one told him the media. Perhaps they were disheartened by their thoughts about his deceased wife. They were all rejected one by one, but they all waited to slow down, and it was too late when they got older.
Gradually, when Wang Erzhuang had already lost his heart, he never thought the opportunity would come again. Her name is Dafeng. Speaking of it, it's actually very similar to what happened to Erzhuang. One married a sick wife and the other married a paralyzed husband. It's just that one survived a little longer, and the other got released a little faster.
Therefore, Dafeng has not had a good life in the village over the years. She looks good. At first, someone had an idea when her husband hadn't left, but now she's a widow. Naturally, it's like a broken egg, and flies are flying all over the sky. Of course, this includes Erzhuang.
However, no matter how many times Erzhuang has taken it back, Dafeng never nodded in agreement, yet did not refuse; he just said wait. To be honest, in that era, a widow didn't have much love to talk about; she either lived alone for the rest of her life or just found someone to marry. Because their village has such rules, when a woman's husband dies, they can find a family within 3 months, but they can't get married; they have to keep the spirit for 3 years. Otherwise, after 3 months, no one dares to get married; if not, no one dares to get married; they have to set up an archery of chastity.
This really made Erzhuang very angry. In this village, she is strong, and the family are all good players at home and abroad. Not only is she hungry, but she is also rich; what are her concerns? It's nothing more than a partner to live together and pick something to do. Therefore, Erzhuang wanted to go to the door and ask questions in person. If Dafeng really didn't agree, he would just stop thinking about it himself.
Annoyed was instead annoyed. After all, this was a major marriage event, so Erzhuang went out early in the morning and deliberately wanted to buy some rouge gouache that women liked in the city. Maybe Dafeng was happy and agreed to him. But he never expected that as soon as he left the village, he ran into a sneaky giant phoenix.
An hour and two strong hearts are like knocking over a bottle of five flavors; it's not a taste. Out of anger and curiosity, Erzhuang followed along the way. No wonder he misunderstood, because the path Dafeng took was to break the temple outside the village. But what happened next made Dazhuang rush in with anger.
What's more, I didn't expect this scandal to be seen by Erzhuang. I only saw Dafeng being held in his arms by a man, and Dafeng kept asking for forgiveness. This is strange. Everyone with a clear eye knows that this is a private meeting, and Dafeng took the initiative, but why are they asking for forgiveness again now?
Erzhuang, who couldn't keep watching, broke in and kicked that person away with one foot. If you take a closer look, he also knows this person; isn't he just a tiger from the village next door? The point is that this guy has a wife. The two were so scared that their faces went blank for a while, and it's no wonder they were afraid, because in his time, this kind of thing had to be soaked in a pig cage.
The tiger bowed and asked for forgiveness, but Dazang didn't listen at all. After punching and kicking him, he let him go. At this point, looking at the giant phoenix still crying, Erzhuang shook his head with a wry smile and said, “Is that why you didn't agree to me? Forget it, I won't say it, go!”
I didn't expect Erzhuang to just turn around, but he was hugged by Dafeng and wouldn't let go. “Brother Erzhuang, that's not the case; he hurt me.” It turns out that Tiger Zi is rich and often harasses Dafeng. Once, when Dafeng went out to buy medicine for her husband, she didn't expect to be stopped by Tiger Zi, that is, in this broken temple, and treated lightly by him. Afterwards, every time Tiger used this to blackmail Dafeng into following suit.
Nor was it that Dafeng disagreed with Erzhuang; it was that he lost his innocence and had no face to agree. After listening to this, Erzhuang was extremely distressed, “Isn't it just a tiger? I'm still afraid of him, look how I can clean him up.” After speaking, I hugged Dafeng tightly. “Will you marry me now?” Dazhuang saw her heart unraveled, so she asked again.
“I'm afraid you'll dislike me and it won't be too late. How could you not want to? Brother Zhuang, you're a good person.” In this way, the two finally got together and lived a happy life. At this point, it seems that Erzhuang saved Dafeng. If this scandal were to be discovered, then Dafeng would have only one outcome.
Unfortunately, Dafeng met her paralyzed husband and Tiger Zi, but she was lucky because she met Erzhuang again. However, not all women are big phoenixes and can have a good ending, so as a man, be kind to the women around you.
