 In the Seven Rebellion, there were many murders. Li Hualong, a country native, had escaped from the mountains and was running into a soldier who had spent the night. To avoid being killed by soldiers, he was in a hurry and had nowhere to hide, so he lay down in a pile of dead people and pretended to be dead. After the army was over, Li Hualong didn't dare to climb up. He opened his eyes and suddenly saw dead bodies with broken arms. They all stood up, like a small grove. One of the dead bodies, with its broken head still attached to his shoulder, said in his mouth, “Wild dogs are here, what should I do?” The other corpses also noisily said, “What should I do?” In an instant, they all popped down, and then there was no sound at all.
Li Hualong was frightened and wanted to climb up. He saw a monster in the shape of a beast head lying in a pile of dead corpses eating human heads, sucking people's minds one by one. He hid his head under the corpse for fear of being eaten. The monster came to shake his shoulder and wanted to eat his head, so Lee lay down hard on the ground. After not being able to get his head several times, the monster pushed away the corpse covered with a plum head and exposed his head. Li was terrified. Slowly scouring his waist with his hands, he touched a stone, the size of a bowl, and held it in his hand. When the monster found Lee's head, he lay down and wanted to kill him. Lee suddenly jumped up, shouted, and hit the monster on the head with a stone. As a result, he hit it in the mouth. The monster screamed like an owl, covered its mouth and ran away in pain. It vomited some blood along the way. Li Hualong checked it and found two teeth in the blood. They were curved in the middle, sharp at the end, and over four inches long. I took it back to the village and showed it to others; no one knew what kind of monster it was.
