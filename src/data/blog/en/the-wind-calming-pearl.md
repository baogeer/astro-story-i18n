 Once upon a time, a young man made a living by cutting firewood. One day he was cutting firewood in the mountains. Suddenly, the wind blew so hard that people couldn't stand still and couldn't open their eyes. He hurriedly hid behind a big rock to take refuge. At this point, he saw a huge black spider slowly climbing onto the bridge in front of him, then spitting out a large round bead from its mouth and rolling it on the bridge. At this point, the wind suddenly stopped, and the wind and the sun shone in an instant. All of a sudden, the hearts of young people became excited. Is this what the old people used to say about setting the wind? He held his breath and didn't dare to move at all, fearing that the spider would be shocked. After a while, the spider had enough sun and slowly climbed off the bridge, and the beads remained on the bridge. Seizing the opportunity, the young man took advantage of the spider's distance and quickly ran to the bridge, picked up the beads, ran home in one go, and carefully hid the beads.
He heard the old man say that Dingfeng Pearl can shelter from the wind and water, and if you have it, you can visit Dragon Palace in the East China Sea as a guest. As a result, he chose a sunny day to say goodbye to his old mother and took Ding Fengzhu on the road. He walked all the way east, all the way to the East China Sea. He took out the beads and continued walking forward; only the seawater before the meeting automatically separated from the two sides, giving way, and he continued walking inside all the way to the Dragon Palace. When the old dragon king saw a mortal come to his dragon palace, he was surprised to know that young people were only able to walk in because of the role of Ding Fengzhu, so he warmly entertained him. The young man lives in the Dragon Palace. The old dragon king entertains him with delicious food every day, making him visit the Dragon Palace and enjoy the amazing beauty of the seabed. After ten days, the young man had eaten enough, had enough fun, missed his old mother at home, and said goodbye to Lao Long Wang and wanted to go back. Dragon King couldn't hold back and agreed. Before leaving, Lao Dragon King gave him a plate of jewels to wrap around. He didn't want it. Lao Dragon King asked him to bring him another plate of gold ingots; he still didn't want it. The old dragon king asked him what he wanted? He also said that as long as the Dragon Palace had what he wanted, he would definitely agree. The young man said he only needed the most beautiful pot of flowers on the window sill. That pot of flowers is an incarnation of Lao Dragon Wang's favorite little daughter, Little Dragon Girl. Although Lao Dragon King couldn't bear it, he had already said it and had to agree. So the young man cheerfully carried the pot of flowers home.
When they got home, young people went up the mountain to cut firewood every day. There was only one more thing, which was to take good care of the potted flowers. After a few months like this, Little Dragon Girl was moved by the young man's sincerity and came out of the flower and became a couple with him. Since then, young people have gone up the mountain to cut firewood every day, and the little dragon girl took care of her mother-in-law at home and cleaned up the house to cook. Her humble cottage was kept clean, tidy, and spotless by her. One day, Miss Xiaolong said to the young man, “Look, our house is too broken. We should build a new house so that my mother-in-law can enjoy it too. It just so happens that there is a plot of land in the east of the village that needs to be sold, so go buy it. The young man said, “I also want to build a new house for my mother to live in so that her elderly family can enjoy the happiness, but where can I get the money? Little Dragon Girl said, “Don't worry about this. I brought some money when I arrived. It's enough to use; you just have to buy that plot of land. The young man agreed and went to the owner of the plot of land in the east of the village to discuss the purchase of the land. The man saw that the young man wanted to buy the land and knew that his family was not in good condition, so they sold it to him at a bargain price.
One night a few days later, after dinner, Little Dragon Girl said to the young man, “Take me to see that plot of land.” As a result, the young man carried Little Dragon Girl to the plot of land in the east of the village. After arriving, the little dragon girl circled around that plot of land, then pulled a hairpin from her head and drew a picture of a house on the ground. After painting, she breathed and stomped her feet, and a house with a green hall wreck appeared in front of the young man. They quickly went back to pick up their old mother, and spent the night moving everything in the family to their new house and settling down.
A few days later, an official who took office abroad passed by this place and saw that a splendid house had been built in a vacant lot a few days ago. He thought it was unusual. It was not the work of a mortal person, so he stopped and asked his entourage to inquire. A follower came back to report that a young man bought this plot of land, built this house, and married a wife who looked like a fairy. Officials were even more certain that the house was not built by a mortal, so they stopped to eat at the young man's house and named the young man's wife to serve. The young man refused at first. Little Dragon Girl comforted him and said don't worry; she has her own options. As a result, Lady Xiaolong brought good wine and good food to serve the officials and his entourage until they were intoxicated. Then Little Dragon Girl asked the young people to take off the official uniforms and put them on, and pack up the official seals and belongings brought by the officials, then the two took office with their possessions. Before leaving, the little dragon girl breathed and stomped her feet. In the blink of an eye, the house disappeared; there was only an empty space left.
After half of the day, the official and his entourage woke up, only to see them lying in an open space, with no official uniforms or personal belongings on them. Officials had to confess that they were unlucky, let their entourage go to the village to find clothes to wear, and then went home in a mess.
