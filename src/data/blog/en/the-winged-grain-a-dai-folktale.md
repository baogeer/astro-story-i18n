 In ancient times, grains were the size of eggs and had a pair of wings. Fly exclusively to hardworking people. There was an old girl who was lazy and hungry. She saw that millet flew to other people's homes and not to hers. She was so blushing that she broke the millet's wings with a bamboo pole.
She thought the grains were too big, so she was too lazy to chew them, so she crushed the millet. Since then, the millet has stopped flying, and the grains have become smaller.
