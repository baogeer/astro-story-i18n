 There is a farmhouse in the middle of the mountainside. Farmers rushed to plow the land and cultivated many terraces on the mountain.
There is a wolf living on the mountain, and it watches all this in the dark every day: the old cow is wrapped in a lasso, pulls down the rake and plows the smooth hillside into a small ditch; then the farmer sows some seeds in the small ditch, and young seedlings soon grow.
The wolf thought it was strange and funny.
One day after cultivating the field, the farmer untied the cow that had pulled down the rake, threw the cow on the ground, and took the cow to the riverside to drink water. The wolf then quietly walked to the rake and watched closely, first licking the cow's cover. Then, unwittingly, I stuck my neck in little by little, and I couldn't get it back. The wolf was a little flustered and had to walk around the hillside with his rake.
The farmer came back from the riverside with his cow. Seeing what a wolf looked like, he picked up an axe and chased him past.
The wolf was afraid and said, “Good farmer, please don't hurt me! I'll never steal chickens and do bad things from now on; I want to cultivate fields and cultivate land for you every day like an old cow!” The wolf pretended to be crying and begging, but the farmer saw in its eyes that it had no good intentions towards the old cow, and caught up and slashed the wolf to death. The cow nodded happily.
