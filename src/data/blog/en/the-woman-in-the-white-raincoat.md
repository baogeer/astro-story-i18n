 It kept raining for a moment, and it was as fine as a needle. The sky is dark, and the earth is silent and bleak. I was alone running wild forward in this endless rain, and a woman in a white raincoat was chasing after me...
I'm too late to look back. No, I don't even dare to look back; I can only intuitively feel the existence of the “white raincoat.” I clearly felt that behind my back, that thing that was fast moving with the wind was getting closer and closer to me... A sense of coolness gradually came, and I used all the strength of my whole body on both legs and walked fast... Unfortunately, it was too late, and I was suddenly caught by one hand...
My legs left the ground and my whole body flew upward. I tried to turn my neck, and I wanted to look back and see that “face,” but my neck was like a splint, and I couldn't move at all... I was struggling so hard that my hand suddenly loosened. I was like a sandbag filled with lead. I felt like a sandbag filled with lead, a “knock” sound, and went straight down from high altitude...
“Ah...” I screamed, opened my eyes clear, reached over and touched the sweat on my forehead. It was another damn dream. I scolded and slowly got out of bed. My wife was awakened by my cry and asked me with her sleepy eyes, “What time is it?” I didn't raise my head and muttered, “6:30”. My wife said “Oh,” and after a second, she seemed to have been suddenly given stimulants. She jumped out of bed and asked, “Did you have that dream again?” I didn't answer her remarks. Sitting on the couch next to me and lighting a cigarette would definitely be amazing.
My wife shivered and reached for the knob of the radio next to her and gently turned on the radio. The radio is broadcasting the weather forecast “... today will be overcast and light rain, northeast wind level 3 to 4...”
My wife looked at me in a daze with her pale eyes widened. This has been an immutable rule for 25 years. As soon as I have that dreadful dream, I must wake up at 6.30 in the morning, and it must rain every day. There has been no error in this rule for 25 years. I buried my head on the couch and painfully recalled that rainy day 25 years ago...
I had just entered the third grade of elementary school that year, and there was a toilet on the south side of our school playground. On this day, I played on the playground with a few of my best friends Xiaoqiang, Ah Fei, Big Tou Yong, and Ermao. I don't know how long we played. Gradually, all the students in the playground ran out, and the five of us were still playing like crazy. The sky gradually became darker, and light rain began to drift, but none of us cared; we were still kicking hard.
I received a good ball from Xiao Qiang. After leading the ball left and right, I looked up and got ready to pass it on to the next person. At this point, I vaguely saw a person in a white raincoat walking out of the corner of the school fence through the fog. He kept his head down and couldn't see his face clearly, but I could tell it was a woman by her slim figure and walking posture wrapped in a raincoat. But I didn't think much about it at the time; it just felt a bit strange. After a short glance, I threw the ball out steadily...
The ball went to Ah Fei's feet. A Fei made a long pass to pass the ball to Ermao, but the ball had long eyes, drawn a beautiful arc in the sky, and flew directly into the women's toilet from the air. All of us looked in the direction the ball was moving. As soon as the ball flew into the women's toilet, the person in the white raincoat also turned into the women's toilet almost at the same time...
As soon as they watched the ball and got kicked into the women's restroom, they all complained about Ah Fei. Ah Fei was forced to feel helpless and had to agree to pick up the ball herself, but how could boys go into the women's restroom? Ah Fei asked everyone to find a way for him. While everyone was scratching his ears, Dadou Yong suddenly slapped his thigh and popped out a sentence: “How difficult is this? Didn't a woman in a white raincoat just go to the bathroom? Why don't we just wait for her to come out and let her take it for us?” Ah Fei slapped her head, “Ah, that's right. Then let's just wait outside the bathroom and when she comes out, why don't we just ask her to pick it up for us?” As a result, the five boys stood about five meters away from the toilet in boredom, staring blankly at the toilet exit.
After about five minutes, the woman hadn't come out yet. At this point, it was even darker, and the rain seemed to have gotten more dense under a mysterious call. Xiao Qiang couldn't help but sneeze. Only then did everyone feel a bit cold from the rain. Ah Fei and Ermao couldn't help but bounce back and forth a few times. While bouncing around, Ah Fei was still complaining: “Women are just annoyed that they haven't come out in such a long time.” Xiao Qiang took up the conversation: “Hey, I said she wouldn't come to 'Big'!” Everyone who said this laughed out loud. When Ermao saw this scene, he quickly placed his index finger around his mouth and made a “hush,” saying, “Quiet, let her hear it; it would be bad if they didn't get us the ball.” Only then did everyone calm down again.
The sky was getting darker, and the drizzle continued for a moment. All five of our clothes were soaked, staring at the exit of the women's restroom all over, waiting for the woman in the white raincoat to show up... A minute and a second passed, and about ten minutes passed, and that woman still hadn't come out. At this point, the playground became extremely quiet. There was only the sound of drizzling rain, and the five of us crammed into a crowd. In this dark, rain-filtrated space, none of us said a word anymore, as if we were in another world, listening to God's story...
I don't know how long it took for a flash of lightning to pass through the sky, and we woke up like a dream. Everyone, you look at me, I look at you. I also looked at the exit from the women's restroom, but there was still no movement. It's completely dark all around us. In an empty playground, we are like five lost lambs, lonely and helpless in this chaotic world...
“What is that?” Dadou Yong screamed hoarsely because he was nervous, and everyone looked in the direction of his finger...
A dark shadow hurriedly rushed towards us from the direction of the school gate, as if wearing a huge hat...
“Damn...” I don't know who shouted in a different tone.
The five immediately heard the order to retreat, like timid soldiers on the battlefield, and ran off...
“Stop, stand...” A woman's hoarse voice came from behind me shouting, Xiao Qiang heard a voice holding me back, “Isn't that Grandma Lee?” , I just took a look, didn't I? Isn't that Uncle Lee's old partner who showed the door to our school?
“Hey, you're all back, it's Grandma Lee.” Xiao Qiang yelled at the others.
Grandma Lee rushed to us panting with a black umbrella and complained, “You kids, I called you guys out loud, why are you running? I've just been watching you in the window for a long time. It's raining, you're not going home, and I haven't finished looking at the women's toilet here. What do you want to do when you're young? Go home soon...”
“No, Grandma Lee, you misunderstood,” Ermao argued. “Yes! “We just wanted to get the ball and go home, because we accidentally kicked the ball into the women's toilet, and we didn't dare to go in and get it. We just saw a woman go in, so we wanted to wait for her to come out and have her pick it up for us,” Xiao Qiang interjected.
“Really?” Grandma Lee still has half faith and half doubt about us.
“But, that woman went in, and she hasn't come out yet...” Dadou Yong's voice just fell, and a red lightning flashed through the sky, followed by a dull thunderbolt that scared us all over our bodies.
“Then I'll go in and help you get the ball back.” As soon as Grandma Lee finished speaking, she turned around and went to the bathroom.
I stared dead at the toilet exit with five pairs of eyes, and my heart was full of nervousness and anticipation...
Suddenly, another flash of lightning flashed through the sky, reflecting our five white little faces. At this point, a person flashed out of the toilet. It wasn't anyone else; it was Grandma Lee. Grandma Lee had a bleak face and had strange eyes. Her eyes were straight on the faces of the five of us.
“Grandma Lee, how are you? Help..., did you get the ball for us?” Ah Fei asked a little timidly.
“No ball” was simple and clear; how could Grandma Lee's voice become so cold.
“No ball?” We were amazed almost at the same time.
“Grandma Lee, well..., then can you help us ask if you've seen that woman?” Ah Fei almost prayed and suddenly, Grandma Li's face was dark and her eyes were full of bloodshot, and her voice became more ferocious and low. “I said, no ball, let alone anyone.”
The last few words came out of Grandma Lee's mouth, and everyone couldn't help but shiver...
“No one? no one? Then we saw it...” Ah Fei was talking to himself, saying, he suddenly pulled out his leg and ran. The others also suddenly came back to their senses and scattered, desperately running home...
The next day, when we arrived at school feeling uneasy, we heard that Grandma Lee died of a sudden illness last night, and it is said that her death was extremely horrible. We were scared for many days, unconscious and listless.
After two weeks, a group of police officers picked up a decayed female corpse from the school's women's restroom. The female corpse was unrecognizable. The only thing that was clear was a white raincoat wrapped around her body...
We only heard later that on a rainy day a month ago, the woman was raped and murdered in the woods after going through school after going through the night shift and then dumped her body in the women's restroom. By the time we were about to go on winter vacation, Mr. Li was also fired from school. The only reason was that some people thought he was crazy. He often walked around the playground alone at night while still thinking “Retribution! “Retribution...”, the frightened neighbors were afraid to sleep at night.
By the next semester, all five of us had transferred to other elementary schools one after another. Since then, none of the five of us have mentioned what happened on that rainy day.
In a blink of an eye, we grew up and married and had children. Fifteen years later, by chance, I met Dazo Yong on the street, and I only learned from chatting with him that he often had the same dreams as me. At the time of parting ways, Dadou Yong mysteriously said to me, “Do you know why Grandma Lee died?” I shook my head and whispered in front of me, “When I heard that woman was murdered, I called for help from Grandpa Lee and Grandma Lee. It's just that Grandma Lee didn't let Uncle Lee take care of his affairs, so Uncle Lee didn't go. Otherwise, maybe...” I heard a long sigh. It turns out, I also thought of Uncle Lee's voice in my ears, “Retaliation! Retribution...”
After that incident, every time it rained, I had the same strange and strange dream. Every time I woke up, the clock always accurately pointed at 6.30, and I don't know what year and month it will stop. As for what did Grandma Lee see in the women's bathroom? That is probably something only she herself knows; for us, it's a mystery we'll never be able to solve.
