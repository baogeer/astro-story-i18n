 Legend has it that the Nishikura Bridge over the Xue River was built during the Ming dynasty. This three-hole stone arch bridge is said to be a royal project. The scale is very large. Many skilled workers have been recruited, and more migrant workers have been requisitioned. When the bridge was built, the construction site was very lively; it looked like a small bazaar.
One day, an old white-haired stonemason came from abroad to buy a stone house for his family in Hebei. He fought for several days, and when he was resting, he wandered around the construction site to watch people carving water animals, stone lions, and stone pillars on the bridge. Gao Liaoxing also talked about Masonry Gate a few times. Even the leaders at the construction site snuggled up and nagged a few words. Everyone said that this old man is dumb, that dogs bite mice — he is nosy.
The old stonemason made a stone nest. He said to the owner, “If you have so much stone, I'll make another stone pier for you. It's so convenient to sit and scoop food.” The owner was very happy, so he continued to play.
When hitting this stone pier, the old stonemason wasn't too careful. He measured and weighed. Some people asked him, “Why does it take so much effort with a stone hammer?” The old stonemason listened. If he just laughed and didn't talk, he knew he would bow his head and work. On the day the stone was built, it was just in time for the bridge's stone arch circle to close the dragon gate. I don't know why, when I got to the end, I just lacked a small stone and couldn't touch the stubble. It was very overcast at the time. It looked like it was going to rain heavily. The stone arch circle wouldn't fit right away at Longmen. With a heavy rain, months of work were in vain! But it's too late to make it now! The masons and the officials in charge of the project were in a hurry; they looked like ants on a hot pot. Just when everyone was in trouble, this old stonemason walked around again. He looked at the gap and said to the engineers, “I have a stone trap. You can see if it's appropriate to put it here.” The stonemasons listened. They immediately moved the stone with the old stonemason and placed it in the middle. It was not bad at all; it was tightly sealed.
The bridge was completed, and the officials were very happy. When I think of going to the old stonemason to say thank you, there are still shadows of old people! I saw a big red note just in front of me, and there was a big red note on it:
There was an error in the bridge arch during construction.
Luban came to help. Silver thanked the farmhouse.
After reading the notes, people learned that the old stonemason was secretly helping the royal project by Luban. Officials followed Luban's orders and rewarded the house that fought in Shiusuwo with 22 pieces of silver.
