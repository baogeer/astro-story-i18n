 Even a general with thousands of troops and horses in command may be demoted or lost his life as long as the emperor is a little displeased. Who ate leopard gall and would dare fight with the emperor as a prostitute?
There is one person in history, not Takefu who plays with a sword and a stick, but a writer who is good at writing flattering words, named Kunihiko Shu.
Zhou Bangyan lived during the Song Huizong era. Our ancestor of the Zhou family had no power. He didn't have much money, but he was very brave. The woman he likes is Li Shi Shi, a famous prostitute who became very popular in the morning, a beautiful woman who risked fighting against the hero Song Jiang and had to sneak into the capital city for a while.
Shi Li was originally the daughter of a small family. Due to changes in her family, she was taken in by the owner who runs a brothel since she was four years old. She read, read and read, and blow-and-sing. By the age of 13, she had already gone out of her way to pick up customers. For a while, a gentleman in the capital seemed to have lost her soul. After making such a big move, Huizong, who lives deep in the imperial palace, knows it. Who dares to come close to the woman the emperor is interested in? However, there was an outsider with strong martial arts called Jia Yi who didn't believe in evil. He actually ate the emperor's vinegar and quietly wrote the song “Nan Xiang Zi” after meeting with Li Shiyu ——
In front of a small building, I saw a beautiful woman who looked like a fairy; I thought that Sheng Fei was like a dream, chasing joy and holding hands, and Lan Fang was free to talk about alliances all night long. It was reported that they returned early in the morning and returned to Luang in the late morning, leaving behind mackerel as an abode.
Just because of this phrase, Jia Yuanwei almost lost his head. Despite being interceded, he was relegated to Hainan Island, and stipulated that he would never return to Beijing.
This means that Li Shi Shi was none other than the emperor; other men could only see but not eat. Whoever wants to have bad thoughts will lose their head!
Chow Kong-yan, the ancestor of our Zhou family, has learned good words and can compose music. Shi Li also likes to sing songs he wrote. In today's terms, they can be considered musicians and are in a circle. Master Li only allowed the emperor to have fun alone. Of course, she thought it was boring. She had a talented son, Zhou Bangyan, in her heart.
Once, Song Huizong caught a cold, and Li Shi Shi felt that the opportunity had come, so he made a secret appointment with Zhou Bangyan. This week, no matter how many heads he had, he sneaked into Li Shishi's home as promised. Unexpectedly, Song Huizong came all of a sudden. Amidst the panic, Zhou Bangyan hid under his bed. Huizong operated with illness. Although Zhou Bangyan under the bed was frightened and sweaty, he wrote down all the conversations between the two. After Song Huizong left, Zhou Bangyan had a strange feeling and composed a series of “Junior Tour” ----
They went hand in hand, and Wu Yan won the snow, breaking a new orange. When the bromelons are warm, the scent of animals continues, and they sit on a kite. whispering: Who are you staying for? Shi Li learned to sing this song in less than two days. While serving the emperor, he proudly sang it and told the emperor who wrote this song. The emperor was so smart. The content of the song was a conversation he had with Master Li when they were having fun that night. In other words, Zhou Bangyan was on the scene at the time! Couldn't the emperor allow him to immediately gather evidence of Zhou Bangyan's crime and exile him from Beijing.
What is quite interesting is that Jia Yuanwei almost got killed, but Zhou Bangyan was not convicted. What's even more ironic is that since Li Shi Shi missed Zhou Bangyan, and his beauty grew weary throughout the day. Song Huizong was distressed by the beautiful woman, so he sent Zhou Bangyan back to Beijing. The two also often exchanged their experiences in writing lyrics together, just like buddies.
