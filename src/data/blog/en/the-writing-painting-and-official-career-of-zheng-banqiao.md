 Zheng Banqiao (1693-1766), a famous painter from the Qing dynasty, was born into a poor school family. His father was a private school teacher. Zheng Banqiao has been literate and studied with his father since he was three years old. History says that he was “born a genius, has a strong personality, and is unfettered.” Zheng Banqiao himself said that studying at a young age “can work hard, self-anger, establish oneself, and live up to the same customs.” As a “Kangxi talent, Yong Zheng raised people, and Qianlong advance,” Zheng Banqiao was able to consciously approach the original Confucianism. As Confucius requested, he struggled to break free from the “little man,” returned to “gentleman,” and acted as a maverick. He wrote, painted, and acted as an official, all amazing acts and extraordinary achievements.
◆Resonance of history and art
Be a useful person in heaven and earth
According to the “Zheng Banqiao Chronology”, Zheng Banqiao entered the exam in the first year of Qianlong (1736), when he was 44 years old. The following year, I returned from Beijing south to Yangzhou and met my friend Gu Wanfeng. Gu Wanfeng wrote the poem “Zheng Dajin of Giving Banqiao”. One of them said, “There are also disputes that are inexplicable, and raging rhetoric is frightening. To write the unashamed “Six Scriptures”, it takes a thousand years of merit.” Zheng Banqiao's “crazy rhetoric” and “fanatical rhetoric” directly expressed his feelings. For Wen Jing, he must go after the original Confucian “Six Scriptures”, and he also had to build a meritorious career as an official. He is famous throughout the ages.
Confucius taught his disciples, and taught the six kinds of talents, mostly practical science that enabled his disciples to establish and use the world at the time, and advised the disciples to “aim for the way, be guided by virtue, rely on benevolence, and play in the arts.” Confucius himself did not shy away from saying “many abilities and despicable things,” and also confessed, “I am rich and can be sought after. Even though I am a person in charge, I did it too.” Zheng Banqiao strongly refused to be misled by Cheng Zhu, inheriting the glorious tradition of valuing talents in primitive Confucianism. He opposed reading dead books and writing empty texts, and angrily asked, “What is the result, “What is the heart of the world and the lives of all people”? Rebuke: “Everyone who is called a brilliant person is a waste of the world”! He thought, “Aren't people who read and write just words? You will be happy and wise, nurturing from within and helping from the outside. If you have ambition, you can give it to the people; if you don't have ambition, you can stand alone. You can also teach your children by turning into the township party.” Like primitive Confucian pioneers such as Confucius and Mencius, we must actively do things that benefit the country, the people, and the hometown. Therefore, he is a pragmatic and versatile person. He not only knows how to “take credit for the world and protect the people”, but also works in government, but also works in poetry, writing and drawing, “foraging for food”, and is a country and himself. They all have great uses to rely on. He has a wonderful and tasty life, which is what makes his life wonderful and tasty.
Zheng Banqiao learned to write lyrics at age 16 and began painting at age 22. Shortly thereafter, he became famous and often wrote and painted for people, “selling 100 dollars a day and cultivating crops for generations.” At a young age, I was able to relieve my family's plight by selling calligraphy and painting. He quit his job and returned to his hometown when he was 61. Zheng Banqiao went back to Yangzhou to make a living selling calligraphy, paintings, poetry, and literature. As described in his poem, “Twenty years ago, I carried wine bottles, and the spring breeze relied on Zuzhu Xiting. Now they grow Yangzhou bamboo again, and Huainan is still green.” “Officials are empty and cold, and talk about selling paintings to complement Korean food.” It was the solid and beautiful cultural talents that nourished the glorious evening scene of Zheng Banqiao, and also raised the height of the life of a gentleman in that era.
◆Never be out of date
Created since ancient times, only one's own
Zheng Banqiao consciously resists the retro style, strives to teach nature, and is based on reality. In his paintings, he advocates “using creation as a teacher,” arguing that “the situation is the scene,” and his thesis argues that it is “suitable for daily use.” His own works must “come from their own mind” and forge a new style for themselves. “There are also people from ancient times who are Tang and Song dynasties. Itabashi easily abuses them, saying, “If I pass it down, it's a Qing poem and a Qing text; if I don't pass it down, it won't be a Qing poem or a Qing text. Why is it old saying that”!
Zheng Banqiao advocated that “scholars should set their own standards.” He wrote a letter encouraging his friends Jiang Yu and Jiang Yi to say, “If you two can establish your own style and endure for a long time,” I would like to wear military hats and military uniforms, “carry water and fire sticks, and run for strength.” In fact, Zheng Banqiao has already set a creative purpose unique to me for his own poetry, calligraphy, and painting. In “Rampant Orchids, Bamboo, Stones, and Wang Xilin,” he said bluntly that his work “shook the earth, shook, thunder, and talk about ghosts. There are no paintings that have never been seen before; they are not even in an ordinary eye. Before painting, there is no grid; once painted, no grid is left”. Therefore, Zheng Banqiao's works, poems, lyrics, books, and paintings are “all independent of the world and have formed their own family.”
Zheng Banqiao's poems and lyrics are sometimes called “out of tune,” and there are many “words of love.” Poetry is emotional, compassionate and moving, regardless of physique; if you are excited, it becomes a success. Words are also better than poems; they refuse to use familiar words; they are particularly good at winning. His articles are particularly grand and majestic. In particular, the family books he has written are all old and loyal. They are all sincere and deeply moving, and “all words are indelible.”
Zheng Banqiao's calligraphy combines the three elements of slavery, kaiji, and execution. He claims to be a six-and-a-half-minute book. He is extremely thin and stiff, and occasionally uses painting. Jiang Shiquan praised the poem, “Itabashi's writing is like writing an orchid, and the Crucifixion is very ancient. Itabashi writes orchids as if writing calligraphy, and Shuhua Shuhua looks like a figure.”
Zheng Banqiao's paintings are extremely gifted. Orchids, bamboo, and stones are freely waved, all of which are extraordinary. Bamboo painting also came out of nowhere. It is out of the ordinary. It is extremely beautiful. It is of a high standard, and no one can match it. The written essays and paintings are full of truth, true meaning, real interest, and brilliant insight. They are not only in the art world, but are also masterpieces of essays. Whoever reads them can't help but be enchanted by the beauty.
◆ Take responsibility and dare to act
Creating a world where people are friendly and governed
Mr. Xu Beihong said, “Mr. Itabashi is one of the most outstanding figures in China in the past 300 years. His ideas are amazing, his writing is amazing, and his paintings are particularly amazing. The poetry, calligraphy, and painting are not only elegant, but also merciful and wonderful, especially rare in ancient times and today.” Mr. Xu Beihong can be called Zheng Banqiao's closest friend. In one word, he expresses the highest level of Itabashi's poetry and painting pursuit and its immortal roots — “mercy is miraculous.” Indeed, in the face of Itabashi's works, we are often infiltrated by a feeling, beginning with compassion, and then excited to do something, like great primitive Confucian pioneers, to “save people's water and fire” and “save people's hunger.”
Rice ears and cabbage are considered the most common grains and the cheapest vegetables. My friend Li Yi drew a picture of rice ear cabbage. Zheng Banqiao's inscription gave it the meaning of “sky-high price”: “The yellow ears of rice fill the stomach; the green leaves make soup. The flavor is bland and and long. Ten thousand lives, two things in charge. A few minutes wet with ink, a big article.” A few bits of ordinary ink, a small painting, just because it depicts the food and vegetables that ordinary people consume on a daily basis that can bear the lives of “10,000 people,” even if it is a big article, made for people's livelihood, that is, it is the heaviest painting between heaven and earth. In Zheng Banqiao's mind, what position does people's livelihood occupy! Judging from this standard, he really looked down on the famous Wang Wei and Zhao Mengyi. He said, “If Wang Mojue and Zhao Zi were older, but if you had heard the Tang and Song painters, try their life poems, but once said something painful about people's lives!” It clearly stated, “Everyone who paints orchids and paints bamboo and stones is used to comfort the workers of the world, not for the peace of the world.”
However, “in the way of people's livelihood pain and pain,” after all, it is better to simply relieve people's livelihood pain and itch. Zheng Banqiao, a candidate in the entrance examination, said while waiting for the Ministry of Government to be elected, he said that he should be a villager who is friendly to the people in order to help the poor. Therefore, Gu Wanfeng's poem “Giving Banqiao to Zheng Dajin” says, “There are so many people in the world, who can only take advantage of it. He has a natural nature to read articles; he can be kind to his people.”
In the 6th year of Qianlong, Zheng Banqiao was appointed as the county commissioner of Fanxian County in Shandong. The first action he took office was astonishing. He ordered that the walls of the county government be punched in hundreds of holes, saying that it was “bad customs from former officials.” In fact, he announced to the public that his county order was to remove the traditional separation between the county government and the people. When you leave the government office to do business, you often get rid of the routine of shouting and shouting quietly, and go directly into the village to visit social conditions and folk customs. “I can't help but drink Pai Ya Nga-lai, and it's common practice to go to Lin Shen. “I'm ashamed to go by in a wasteland with a glass of white water and foolish people.” “Cloth socks and blue shoes are the chief officials. White Yu, Wen, and apricots grow Chuncheng. A few times I came to Dafu to ask questions, and Longshang slept while watching Kuo Geng.” Friendly to the people, diligent, impartial, and clean. Five years in Fangxian County, history books described Zheng Banqiao's administration like this: “Loving the people is like a child; there is nothing left behind. Officials often hang out with writers; there are those who forget them as elders.” It was the ultimate state of government that Confucianism aspired to.
In the 11th year of Qianlong, Zheng Banqiao was transferred to the post of Wei County Commander. Like Fangxian County, they often go to the countryside in plain clothes and go deep into the public. “Spring rain embankment goes to Mailong, and the autumn wind ancient temple asks about melon fields.” One night, I heard a student studying in a cottage. They were very engaged in their reading, and I really appreciated it. I learned during the visit that their family was very poor, so they were often given living allowances. The student later went to the entrance examination, namely Han Mengzhou, a famous Confucian and civil servant. In the midst of summer desolation, poppies are worth hundreds of thousands of dollars, and people eat the same thing. Zheng Banqiao immediately opened a position and was dissuaded by the government. They said that according to convention, they had to ask for instructions from their superiors and obtain approval before they could do so. Zheng Banqiao said, “When is this? Once the declarations are repeated, the people will be left alone! There are punishments, I'll leave it up to me.” A batch of food was quickly distributed, and the citizens of the county were able to receive vouchers and lend them. At the same time, they are working hard, building cities and digging ponds, and luring hungry people from near and far to go to work. A large owner in the village started a factory to cook porridge and feed it on wheels. Defend the House of Gummu and blame them for calming them down. Several types of relief have been combined to save more than 10,000 people. There was no harvest in the fall, and the people were unable to repay their summer loans. Zheng Banqiao paid out of his own pockets, “donating money for money,” and even when he went on strike and returned to his hometown, he “took all the vouchers and burned them.”
What Zheng Banqiao did when he was in charge of Weixian County was very frightened by the local wealthy businessman. He scolded him for “happy events” and caused trouble in order to get things done. He was accused of “lawsuits being right-handed and left rich businessmen left”. When the case was decided, the case was not biased in favor of the rich, but towards the poor, according to government practice. What's more, they “don't do anything with the rich” and publicly humiliate businessmen who have obtained the status of supervisors. In the Ming and Qing Dynasties, students at Chung-Ang University were called “supervisors” and had quite a position in society. Some rich people then used their money to buy a “mentor” name, use local power as a blessing, and take advantage of privileges. Some wealthy merchants in Weixian County did the same, but Zheng Banqiao didn't buy it. When this kind of supervisors went to the county government to see him, they were disrespectful, and according to the case, they scolded: “What kind of begging for money and donkeys? This isn't enough for you to worry about!” They also ordered Soap to take off his hat, or drive him away by stepping on it or shaking his head in the face.
