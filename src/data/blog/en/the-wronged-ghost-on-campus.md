 Author: Ya Wei
This evening's self-study class was in the large staircase classroom. The students in the two classes were together, but it still showed that the classroom was empty. It's winter, and the weather is so cold. Many students are staying in their dormitories and don't want to leave. Anyway, there's no harm in being absent. In college life, all they want is freedom.
I was sitting in the penultimate row of the classroom, under a dusty ceiling fan. There was no one around me, and my classmates all squeezed out “warm” in front of me. The “Old Man” in the “Ancient Literature” course is a bald, chunky, middle-aged man. He was talking about bizarre stories in “The Legend of Liaozhai” on the podium. He was also staring at a few beautiful and petite girls with fascinated eyes, while talking about finding a “The Moon Is Dark and the Wind Is High” to show us the movie “Liao Zhai” at night.
I'm used to seeing his face. I held my elbows on the table in annoyance, covered my ears with the palm of my hand, and looked down to concentrate on reading my “Don't Poison People” (article in Wang Shuo's collection). A cold wind suddenly burst in through the dilapidated window, and I couldn't help but have a cold war.
Right, I forgot to mention one thing, something very important.
Our school is quite big. There are many tree gardens, and there are also a few creepy groves. The large staircase classroom is very old. Like an old man who is struggling, he is looking forward to the day it ends. Perhaps its fear tormented it to no longer survive because it was located next to one of the most remote groves in the school.
When we first entered school, we heard school girls tell a story that was forbidden to tell. The school forced the students to revisit this past story, but the school girls were afraid that our “ignorance at a young age” would be ruined, so they gathered a few of our little girls and talked about that painful past in a mysterious way.
In other words, a few years ago, a girl came to our social science department. Not only was she beautiful, but her grades were excellent. She caused a lot of shock at school, and followed a bunch of boys behind her butt every day. Speaking of how those boys chased her, it was really amazing. They had all kinds of moves, which simply surpassed all the moves of the various boxing factions that competed in the Jianghu Heroes Club.
Here, let's not go into much detail; I won't go into the process of boys chasing girls. Anyway, in the end, a handsome boy defeated the other opponents and became the girl's official boyfriend. The two were so sweet together, and the people around them were so jealous when they saw it.
Suddenly one day, after the boy and girl had been together for two months, a boy from a foreign school came looking for a girl. I learned later that the boy at the foreign school turned out to be the girl's high school boyfriend. They didn't break up, but have always been in touch. When the boy learned that the girl had quietly made a boyfriend behind his back, he was furious and took a day to drive for ten hours to quietly arrive at the girl's school. Once he confirmed the truth, he “calmly” wanted to ask the girl out and talk.
It was dusk, and when all the sun had disappeared to the edge of the sky, they arrived at the most remote grove in the school. The boy asked the girl why she lied to him, and the girl said she couldn't help it. The boy excitedly grasped the girl's shoulders and said to her melancholy, don't leave me, I need you. The girl was already weak. Being shaken by the boy caused pain in her shoulders, and she couldn't help but break free from the boy's hands. The boy tried harder. He clasped the girl's shoulder tightly, shouted that I really love you, and rounded his lips over. The girl dodged, but she felt a sudden breathing sound that came closer and closer. The boy frantically kissed the girl's face, lips, and neck. The girl was really angry. As soon as she slapped the boy in the face, the boy was stunned, but in just two seconds, his eyes glowed fiercely. He thought he loved her so much and was so loyal to her, yet she betrayed herself and never forgave her. The boy lost his mind. Like an angry lion, he swooped up and held the girl's neck tightly with both hands. The girl struggled to resist, but her movements became lighter and lighter. In the end, her hands fell down powerlessly. The boy sneered, knocked the girl down on a pile of fallen leaves, frantically tore off the girl's clothes, pants, bra, and underwear, and swooped up like a wild wolf. He doesn't know anything now. All he wants is to punish the woman who betrayed him. Suddenly he screamed and stumbled out of the grove.
The boy was naturally caught later, but he got nervous. According to the legends of the school sisters, there are various explanations for the boy's scream. Some say they saw the girl's current boyfriend suddenly appear, while others saw the girl suddenly open her eyes and stare hard at him. Despite all the opinions, the girl is dead, and the boy is insane after all.
After hearing this story, we were all very scared and silently admonished ourselves to never play relationship games. Therefore, every time we go to the Big Ladder Classroom in the evening, we walk together and leave in a hurry after class. We haven't met any wrongdoers ever since.
OK, back to that. As I mentioned earlier about a cold wind rushing in through a broken window, I couldn't help but shiver coldly. The “Old Master of Ancient Studies” is still painting and talking endlessly about his Liaozhai story. A few of his classmates are drowsy. My eyes were also hazy, and my head was a little dizzy. I couldn't help but blame myself for staying up all night without taking my contact lenses off last night, rubbing my eyes, and still couldn't read the words on the book clearly. My whole body was also soft and powerless. I think I definitely didn't sleep enough. The first thing I did when I got back to the dorm room after class was to get a good night's sleep.
Confused, I felt like I stood up and silently walked out of the classroom, step by step, in the direction of the depths of the grove. I suddenly remembered the tragedy that happened in the grove, and I couldn't help but sweat. Why am I here alone? No, I have to get out quickly. I started running, and it became harder and harder to breathe. My feet felt like two sandbags were tied, and I fell so hard that I couldn't take a step.
I run and run, but the more I run, I feel like there are more trees in the grove. It's spooky. It's scary. A sense of coolness flew from the bottom of my heart to my throat. I wanted to calm myself down, but I couldn't because I found that no matter how I walked, I couldn't get out of the shady grove.
Suddenly my feet stumbled, and I looked down and looked down, wow! A girl lying naked all over her body was lying on the ground. Her hair was messy, her mouth was wide open, her white teeth were eerie, and her eyes stared violently at me. There was thick dark red blood on the corners of my eyes, nostrils, and outside of my ears. I felt so disgusted that I almost fainted to the ground.
However, my instinct to survive drove me to take a double step, turn around and run away quickly, and I kept running to places with bright lights in the distance. The light was getting brighter, and I was about to run out of the grove. At this point, I heard the girl crying intermittently. After the crying gradually subsided, I heard the girl talking like nothing. She said in a trembling voice, “I've always wanted to find an opportunity to say sorry to him, but I'll never get a chance; everything is my fault. If you see him, I'll definitely say sorry and thank you for him.
The girl's voice disappeared. I looked ahead and happened to have run out of the grove myself. I had already seen the lights in the classroom on the stairs. I walked quickly to my seat and saw my classmates. They were still chatting, listening to songs, and dozing off. The “old man” on the podium had just finished telling the Liaozhai story, but he was still addicted to his own story.
The bell rang appropriately after class, and I woke up in surprise. Oh, it turns out I was just dreaming; I couldn't help but sweat it out for myself. The students quickly packed up their pens and waited for the “old man” to announce the end of class. I quickly packed up my stuff and walked out of the classroom with the crowd.
After returning to my dorm room, I had washed up and was getting ready to get into a warm bed, but my roommates started talking about the Liaozhai story told by the “old man” in class. I said if you guys are bothered, aren't you sure listening to ghost stories one night is enough? The timid Xiaoli also agreed with me, “Yes, don't talk about this anymore; listen for a while, I won't even dare to go to the bathroom at night. At this point, Xiao Juan suddenly asked me. Right, when I was in class, why didn't you even take a day off and ran to the bathroom alone? I'm shocked, what? Are you saying I walked out of the classroom when I was in class? Xiaomei also went on to say, “Yes, we are still worried about you. Fortunately, the “old man” didn't see it.
I was dumbfounded, how is this possible? I'm clearly dreaming, how could I have left the classroom? So did I actually go to the grove? Is everything I've seen and heard true? I was so scared that I couldn't talk anymore. I covered my head tightly with a quilt, and thought to myself, should I take a chance to see that insane boy and pass on the girl's words to him?
