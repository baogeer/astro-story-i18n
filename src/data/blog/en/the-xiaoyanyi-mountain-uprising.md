After two more days, Nanzhaozhi County published a list of wanted rewards. The list stated, “Whoever captures Li Fuyi, Ma Guofang, Xu Tianshun, Wu Liniang, or Cao Ronglian will be rewarded with 120 silver. Anyone who has dealt with or hid rebels is the same crime as the rebels, etc...”
The leader of the Xiaoyanyi Rebel Army thought that the officers and soldiers had been defeated, so he let his guard down. After a few days, three, three, two, two, three, two, two, three, two, two, and went to the bottom of the mountain to recruit troops.
On the first day, Xu Tianshun brought ten military personnel to the Yangshudi area and met two acquaintances. Xu Tian fell to the ground in response. Li Guang stepped forward with a hawk-billed machete to cut off Xu Tianshun's head and went to the county to ask for credit. The rest of the volunteers were all surrounded by officers and soldiers. Their seclusion and impenetrability laws were broken by swords and guns with “confinement straw.” Some of the volunteers were killed, and some escaped.
In order to guarantee the simultaneous uprising of various rebels on September 9, Li Fuyi went out for a few days to discuss related interests with local leaders. On the first 6 September, he took two soldiers. When he returned to Yunyi Mountain, he did not dare to walk the main road and walk back up Jianping Donggou. He had just reached the mountainside, was captured by ambushes, and taken to Nanzhaocheng overnight to guard.
County Governor Xu Peitang learned during the interrogation of the two pro-soldiers that all of them will revolt on September 9. On September 7, it was overcast, and the northeastern wind blew. Xie Laodao led a foreign gun company. Xuzhi County surrounded the Xiaoyishan Regiment with a strong police group, and immediately sounded the command to attack the mountain.
Marshal Ma encouraged the soldiers to keep their guard up. After tomorrow, the 13 counties will have nothing to be afraid of.
Superintendent Cao Ronglian, pioneer Zhang Kun, and Wu Liniang are even more energetic. They each guard their positions and are ready to roll the stones, enough to handle the attacks of the officers and soldiers.
The cunning Xie Laodao only feigned an attack. The sound of foreign gunfire and shouting shook the valley, but the attack was very slow. The blazing fire set Little Entertainment Mountain in flames In the mountains, countless rebels, women and children were burned alive on the mountain, and volunteers who were driven by the flames had no choice but to jump off the cliff. On the morning of September 8, Little Entertainment Mountain was still bursting with smoke from the northeast. There was light rain in the sky. Xie Laodao laid down a temporary execution ground at Mizoguchi in Kirijo, surrounded by clean soldiers. At noon, Xie Laodao ordered the execution of the rebels. The executioners cut off the heads of Guo Zongyu and Cao Ronglian, who had death flags with the words traitor written on their backs. The rain was getting worse, and a few owls screamed miserably in the mountains...
Xie Laodao threw the heads of Cao Ronglian and Guo Zongyu into the five-acre pool, and hung the heads of Li Fuyi, Xu Tianshun, and Wu Liniang on the high poles of the city gate tower to warn the crowd.
Xie Laodao ordered the killing of most young adults in Pongtiaogou in order to cut down the roots, and also imprisoned all of Li Fuyi, Ma Guofang's son, daughter, and other family members. They were not released until the Republic of China.
The leaders of various rebel forces in the surrounding area learned the news that the rebels had crashed in the South, and they all cancelled the September 9 rebellion plan, and local rebel groups also disbanded.
At that time, after Xie Laodao set up a execution ground on the Mizoguchi dam in Kirijo, a heavy rain destroyed the dam, and it was never repaired.
