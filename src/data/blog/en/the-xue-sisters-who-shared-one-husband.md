So I pinned all my hopes on the two daughters. They hired famous teachers to teach them since they were young, and specially built a painting house for the two daughters after staying at home, and invited the monk of Chengtian Temple, who is good at painting ink and flowers, to paint huge Lan Hui on the pink wall, and named the building “Lan Hui Lianfang Tower”. The two daughters sang and studied upstairs day and night, talking about the past and present. When I was thirteen or four years old. The two of them are already famous for their poems, and their “Sudai Bamboo Branch Lyrics” have all been appreciated by the great contemporary writer Yang Tieya.
