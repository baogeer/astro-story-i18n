 “Passing through Guan Lidi”: Legend has it that when the Yi people made heaven, six of the seven fathers and sons who made heaven had names and one had no name, so later people used three-pronged pine branches to represent unnamed people, and chestnuts to represent the six gods with names. There is an important ceremony when welcoming an abnormal death and burial or suspending their spirits on Mount Fen to pay their respects. That is, the six chestnut tree sticks are arranged side by side in a group of six, divided into two rows. Each row is connected to the top, middle, and bottom with three ropes made of white thatch. A channel particularly like a gateway is formed in the middle of the two lines. A hot red hot stone, artemisia branch, cypress, etc. is placed in the aisle. At the entrance, vinegar was poured on the vinegar soup pot to evaporate the fog, so as to remove the dirt from the dead, and also to clean up the dirt and decontamination of Takako. The place where chestnut poles are planted is what is known as the ground that passes through chestnut. This is a necessary procedure for Abi during the whole process of mourning for her seniors who died abnormally.
