 This is a true story. Dianzhu and Changshou Village, Fuheng Township, Yangyi County, is a great place with beautiful mountains and clear water. Before the founding of the People's Republic of China, the gang Zhang Jieba, which had long been entrenched in the Baoshan, Yongping, and Yangyi regions, led gangs to burn, loot and loot in Quan Fuheng territory, cruelly harming innocent civilians in these regions. In particular, Shizhu Village and Changshou Village in Fuheng were very harmful. Under the ravages of the bandits, the local people lived a deep and hot life. The people of Fuheng couldn't stand it. There was a local Yi guy named Huang Haisheng. He was smart and intelligent. Under unbearable circumstances, Huang Haisheng faced danger without fear, broke into the gang, gained the trust of the gang, and became a squadron leader. When the time was right, villagers were secretly organized at the Afghanistan Society and Dagushuangsha to warmly entertain the remaining pigs and sheep, and secretly organized villagers' armed forces. While the bandits were enjoying drinking and being unconscious, they led the villagers to use muskets, spears, and bows to destroy all the remnants of the bandits with muskets, spears, and crossbows. It has done immortal credit for the Yi people in the Baoshan, Yongping, and Yangyi regions to relieve their future problems. After the founding of the People's Republic of China, under everyone's election, Huang Haisheng became the chairman of the Fuheng Agricultural Association, and also served as a township chief for more than ten years.
