 Legend has it that at the end of the Qing Dynasty, a wealthy merchant went to Fukuoku-ji Temple in Shimen to pray for good fortune and hope that the Buddha would bless his business prosper. If he wishes, he will donate more than half of his dividends to the restoration of Fukukoku-ji Temple. Later, this wealthy businessman certainly made a fortune year by year. Seven or eight years later, they sent the housekeeper to escort more than 20 packs of silver and copper coins, stayed overnight, and traveled thousands of miles to Fukukoku-ji Temple to pay back their wishes. On this day, they traveled from Xiaguan to Shimen Mountain at the foot of Jinniu Mountain. They only had to walk two or three more miles to Fukoku Temple. Instead, they met a mountain taker on the side of the road. His family lived in the village at the foot of the mountain, carrying a crossbow and two or three pheasants in their back basket, and had just returned from hunting in Shimen Mountain. The housekeeper greeted him and asked, “I wonder how far this place is from Fukukoku-ji Temple?” Takumi Uchiyama looked at more than 20 wooden boxes with a pair of wolf eyes: “What is the duty of a guest official to go to Fukukoku-ji Temple?” The housekeeper was straightforward and readily answered, “Send Gong De Qian.” Takumi Uchiyama was overjoyed, and he secretly thought that Grandpa Fortune had arrived! Then he told a big lie: “It's still a day away from Fukukoku-ji Temple.” After a few discussions with the six groomsmen, the housekeeper decided to find a horse shop to stay at before dusk. Takumi Uchiyama observed the situation and took the opportunity to say, “Follow this fork for eight or 900 steps eastward. There is a Longhua Temple. It has had no monks, no nuns, and no incense for many years. Although it is in disrepair, it can shield from wind and rain. In the past, merchants often stayed at the temple. The guest officials might as well rest at the temple. I'd like to lead the way, so I won't be late tomorrow.” The housekeeper and groomsmen listened to Takayama's ingenious words. It seemed like they were drowsy and hit a pillow. Naturally, their hearts were happy. After a day's drive, people were stuck and running out of horses. There was a burst of shouting and drinking, and followed the mountain craftsman and headed east at the fork. Unexpectedly, they embarked on a path of no return.
Master Uchiyama led his team to Longhua Temple, and by the way, sold the three pheasants he had hunted to the housekeeper. After receiving the copper money, he left in a hurry. The housekeeper ordered to unload the horse and boil water to cook...
At night, the moon was dark and windy. The mountain breeze blew east and west through the surrounding woods, like a group of ghosts dancing their claws, making a terrible hustle and bustle. The housekeeper and the five groomsmen all slept, and soon made a snoring sound. Just let a groom stay up all night by the fire pond to prevent thieves from stealing things from horses.
It was late in the middle of the night, and the groom who was on duty had been yawning for a long time. It was time for him to change his shift and go to bed. He got up and planned to wake up his partner. Unexpectedly, his throat was suddenly stuck with his hands behind him, then a cold knife stuck in his chest, and fell when it was too late to struggle. More than a dozen dark shadows flew into Longhua Temple. A burst of sword and blood flew, and the sound of snoring disappeared. The bloody laughter, accompanied by the dreary cries of owls, wandered through the deserted mountain temple.
It turns out that behind Shanjiao Village, there is a strip of white water. There are many deep and shallow stone caves in Qingli, which have become wolf burrows and snake caves for thieves to hide and distribute stolen goods. Takumi Uchiyama is the leader of this group of thieves. After settling the housekeeper, groom, and his team to rest at Longhua Temple, he flew to Baishuiqing, and with more than a dozen thieves in the cave, they took advantage of the night to sneak their lives into Longhua Temple's fortune. The poor housekeeper and the six husbands didn't know that Gong De Road was dangerous. They lost their money and lives in paralysis and sleep.
Master Uchiyama ordered four thieves to go to the back of the temple to dig a pit and bury the bodies to clean the temple of blood and relics. He and the other thieves brought out more than 20 mules and horses, covered them in saddles, and carried all the paddles full of silver and money to Baishuiqing. Before dawn, the silver coins were hidden in the Baishuiqing Deep Cave and buried with earth and rocks. They then set fire to the saddle stand and the wooden box containing the money. More than 20 mules and horses are temporarily tethered in Baishuiqing. Afterwards, they all rushed to Pingpo Street, Yangyi Street, and Maidi Street to sell separately. Keep your feet clean and tidy.
This group of vicious thieves are ruthless, and they kill people without blinking an eye. They didn't even let go of donating money to Fukukoku-ji Temple; I don't know how many crimes hurt nature and harm they have committed.
The mountain craftsman and the thieves sat on the ground and shared their affairs. Overnight, they were rich and fueled one by one. Takayamatsugu is a thief and has the highest merit. Of course, he has much more money than anyone else. He and his wife are planning to buy land, buy a walnut house, build a tiled house, and walk all over the mountain with mules, horses, cows, sheep, and pigs, chickens, ducks, and geese. Since then, I've been wearing silk grosgrain and eating delicious mountain and seafood. When Bear Million, Shi Tosi, and Yang Toosi saw him, they all had to bow and say... The more they thought about it, the more beautiful they were. It seemed like they had fallen into a wine can and a honey bottle, intoxicated with the sweet honey.
However, there are gods on the three-foot head.
Takumi Uchiyama and his wife had only been having fun for more than ten days, and strange things came one by one. His hands and feet were inexplicably stiff, and they were getting stiff, and he couldn't even hold his rice bowl; he was so panicked that he jumped and jumped. I was restless the whole time. As soon as he fell asleep, the mistakenly dead housekeeper and the six groomsmen gnawed their teeth one by one, ripping him and biting him again and again. He often woke up shocked by furious beating and scolding. Terrified, his wife was also shivering all over her body. I don't know how many rounds it took. Not only does it have no effect; instead, Ghosts are getting more boisterous and more aggressive. I had no choice but to be bold at night, and I couldn't drive away the bad spirits... I struggled until dawn every day. The two Uchiyama craftsmen have reached a dead end.
