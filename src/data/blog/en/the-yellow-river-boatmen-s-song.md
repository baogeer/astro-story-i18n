description: "Boaters on the Yellow River ride cowhide rafts through the steep rivers of Jiuqu Eighteen Bay. They traveled from Ningxia to Tongguan to seek a living at the crossroads, but they asked questions about how many bays and how many boats the Yellow River actually had with their majestic Xintian tour. One song at a time showed the tenacity and open-mindedness of the sons and daughters of the Yellow River fighting against wind and waves, and sang their awe and love for the Mother River."
