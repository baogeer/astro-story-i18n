 The legend dates back to the 70s of the last century. Villagers living near both sides of the Yellow River at the time. In order to drink pure river water, a few young men went into the water to clean up the silt at the bottom of the Yellow River, and actually dug up a dragon coffin from the bottom of the river, which frightened them all. When the most famous Mr. Feng Shui in the neighborhood arrived at the scene, he suddenly knelt down and was afraid to take a step forward. The surrounding villagers all asked him what happened, but when he died, he didn't say much. He immediately got up and prepared to leave from here. Furthermore, he also said that the coffin was Luo Shu's coffin; no one should touch it; otherwise, he would die very much.
