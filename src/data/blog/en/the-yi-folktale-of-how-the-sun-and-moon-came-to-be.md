 A long time ago, a couple planned to abandon their two biological children and let them carry a gourd with them to fetch water.
The two siblings didn't know what the plan was. They jumped to the water well with a gourd in their hands, scooped a full gourd of water, and walked back. After walking a few steps, all the water was leaking out. The sister and brother went back to the well to scoop up a gourd of water and walked back. After not walking a few steps, all the water leaked out again. In this way, I have been pumping water over and over again for three days (there was no sun or moon at the time; three days is equivalent to the current three years), and I still haven't been able to mention home. The sister and brother couldn't make up for it, and finding mud to make up for it didn't help. At this point, a crow shouted at the top of the tree, “Ah-ah-ah-ah! Make up for the glue, make up for the mess, your mom and dad have run away!” “Ah-ah-ah-ah! Make up for the glue, make up for the mess, your mom and dad have run away!” “Ah-ah-ah-ah! Make up for the glue, make up for the mess, your mom and dad have run away!” When the crow first called, the sister and brother didn't pay much attention. They called many times. The sister told the younger brother to go home after losing the gourd.
The sister and brother returned to where they used to live. The house has long since become a broken wall. The courtyard is full of greenery and wild animals. The sister and brother affectionately called out, “Dad — where are you? Mom, where are you?” Valley echoes: “Where — inside — inside —”, but there is no response from the parents. The sister and brother cried for three days and three nights, and there was still no response from their parents. Suddenly, the younger brother said, “Sister, sister, look, our chickens are back, and so will mom and dad.” My sister wiped away her tears and took a close look: “Brother, this isn't my chicken. My chickens have turned into pheasants.” The younger brother suddenly discovered: “Sister, sister, look, my little cows are still there; mom and dad must still be nearby.” “Little brother, this isn't my little cow; it's suede. Mom and Dad must have gone a long way; we must find them; otherwise, how can we live?”
My older sister was only six years old at the time, and my younger brother was only four. My sister is wearing full Yi clothes tied around her waist, pants, and a black headscarf. The younger brother wears matching clothes, has a bare butt, a bare head, and a small amount of hair on his forehead. It is said to be a hiding place for the soul. The sister and brother both hit the road barefoot to find mom and dad.
The sister and brother walked wow. I don't know how many roads they have walked, how many mountains they have climbed, and how many rivers they have crossed. I don't know how many blisters my sister's feet have, and I don't know how many times my younger brother's feet have shed blood. The footprints along the way were redder than the color of the seal. The sister and brother helped each other, tremble step by step, tremble step by step, tremble step by step, move forward...
“Dad — where are you? Mom, where are you?” Valley Echo: “Where — inside — inside —”.
One day, the sister and brother met an old man herding cows on the hillside. The sister came forward and asked, “Dad the cow herder, have you seen my parents come here?”
“I'm here, you brought my cow in front of me, and I'll tell you.”
The sister and brother stumbled and had a hard time driving the cow in front of the herder, but the cow herder said, “The cow is going to lift your butt; you won't have to leave yet.” My brother was terrified and cried. The sister asked again, “Dad the cow herder, are you doing a good job. Tell us where mom and dad have gone?” “Don't know, don't know!” The herder waved his right hand very impatiently.
The sister and brother had no choice but to go on their way to find out. “Dad — where are you? Mom, where are you?” Valley Echo: “Where — inside — inside —”.
The sister and brother came to the bush again. They met a young man herding sheep. The sister came forward and asked, “Big brother, have you seen my parents come here?”
“I'm here, you brought my sheep in front of me, and I'll tell you.”
The sister and brother stumbled and had a hard time driving the sheep in front of the shepherd, but the shepherd said, “The sheep are going to lift your butts; you won't have to leave yet.” My brother was terrified and cried. The sister then asked, “Brother Cow Herder, are you good to go. Tell us where Mom and Dad have gone?” “Don't know, don't know!” The shepherd shrugged his right hand very impatiently and said.
The sister and brother had no choice but to go on their way to find out. “Dad — where are you? Mom, where are you?” Valley Echo: “Where — inside — inside —”.
The sister and brother came to another house. They saw an old lady sitting in the hospital kneading twine. The sister came forward and asked, “Old lady who washes the thread, have you seen my parents come here?”
“Here you go, look for lice on my head first. If my scalp is itchy, I won't win; I'll tell you when it's done.”
The sister and brother finished looking over the old lady's hair, but they didn't see a single lice. My sister put her left and right thumbs together and pressed down hard. Her nails “ticked” a bit, and the old lady was very comfortable and said, “Ah, it's so comfortable, I'll come back. Ah, that's right.” The sister told the younger brother to find some grass seeds and spread them on the old lady's head. The sister kept pressing the sound of “click - click - click -”. The old lady thought that the sister and brother had strangled him to death and crushed many lice eggs. The old lady was so comfortable that she dozed off. It took a long, long time for the old lady to wake up. The sister asked again, “Old lady who washes the thread, are you good to go. Tell us where mom and dad have gone?” The old lady took out a string from the bamboo basket next to her seat and handed it to her sister and said, “You carry the wire group to the top of the highest mountain. You follow the wire. Wherever the wire group finishes rolling, your parents will be there.”
The sister and brother took over the tour and, following the old lady's instructions, had a hard time climbing one of the highest mountains nearby. The sister and brother rolled off the group and followed along the way. The group finished rolling by a pond at the foot of the mountain. The sister and brother looked at the full pond and shouted in disappointment, “Dad — where are you? Mom, where are you?” Valley Echo: “Where — inside — inside —”. Suddenly, the younger brother shouted, “Sister, look, that ugly monster is laughing at us!” My sister turned her head and looked at it. It was a toad. My sister stepped forward and said, “Daddy Toad, mom and dad are in the pond, we can't get in. What do you think we should do?” The toad said, “I've sucked up the water in one go, so you can go in.” After speaking, open your mouth and take a bite to soak up all the water in the pond. My belly was bloating. My younger brother laughed when he saw it: “This ugly monster has a really big stomach.” As soon as the toad heard the fire, it spilled water into the pond again. My sister prayed, “Grandpa Toad, my younger brother is not sensible and offended you. You don't remember being a villain. Please forgive my younger brother. I apologize to you for your younger brother. Little brother, come kneel down and knock your head!” The sister and brother kept knocking their heads at Toad. “Alright, alright, I'll just help you dry it!” When the toad finished speaking, it sucked up the water in the pond again. Strangely enough, there was no water in the pond, and a path led to a house. The sister and brother quickly walked to the house.
Dad is doing wood work. The plow rack is ready and dismantled, then dismantled and redone. Mom is doing needlework. The clothes are sewn and removed, and then sewn again. They were both focused and never noticed that their children had arrived. “Mom and Dad, we're looking for you!” My sister pounces on my mom, and my brother runs to dad. Mom and Dad looked up in surprise and said in unison, “Who are your parents; you have recognized the wrong person!” The sister and brother thought their parents were joking with them. They held their parents' hands in a playful way and shook their hands and said, “That is, you are the mom and dad we think about day and night.”
My sister said, “Mom, my name is Ami. You are careful of your liver, have you forgotten?”

Mom said, “Ami, you're my daughter, just swallow this needle.” When I said that, give Ami a big needle.

Mom said, “This Ami is our daughter.”

The relentless father yelled, “This little bastard is not our son. Your mom immediately brought sewing thread, sewed the little bastard's eyes, put him in a pig pen, raised fat, and killed the Lunar New Year pigs.” While talking, he twisted his brother's hand and pressed it to the ground. My younger brother cried while kicking and pedaling his little feet.
Mom actually picked up her brother's eyes with stitches. The sister cried and knelt down to ask for the younger brother to be released. Mom scolded, “Go away, or I'll sew you together.”
The younger brother shouted with all his might: “Dad — where are you? Mom, where are you? Save me! Sister-save me!” Valley echoes: “Save — save — me —”.
Since then, my younger brother has been kept in a pig pen as a New Year's pig feed. My parents fed it with more bran and less noodles, and my sister hid rice and meat under the bran when she fed it. Winter Moon has arrived, and my younger brother is getting fat, and my parents are about to kill my younger brother.
One day, the mother said, “Ami, it's almost the New Year. My New Year pig is getting fat, but the barbeque pig hasn't got loose hair yet. Go to the mountain and pile up some loose hair, but be careful, don't have poop on the loose hair.”
Sister Ami wanted to save her brother for a long time, but she never found a chance. Don't have poop loose hair; this is Ami's chance to save her little brother. When my sister went up the mountain, she secretly brought glutinous rice flour from home, mixed it with water and mixed it into the noodle paste, and spilled it in her mouth on pine wool from a nearby mountain. The pine hairs were all white spots one by one, just like they were poop. There wasn't enough flour, so I asked for some more from my neighbors to dye all the pine hairs in the nearby mountains white.
When Ami got home, she said to her parents, “Grandpa and Grandma have been looking for pine hair for a day. The pine hairs in the nearby mountains all have poop, and I'm afraid to go far away.”
Parents don't believe it. Dad said, “Let's go find it. Keep this pig at home, don't let it run away!”
