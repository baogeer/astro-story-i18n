 Ma Zizhong
Legend has it that in very ancient times, there were no Shimen Pass in the western foothills of Mount Cangshan; there were only high cliffs and high mountains; there was no Shimen River; there were only mountain springs flowing through crevices.
The crop farmers in the mountains are very poor. They are so poor that they cultivate land in Pantian and don't cultivate cows; they all have to dig up the seeds one by one. Yuezi had a really hard time, and the croplers' faces were sad like tartary buckwheat. When Guanyin's mother saw it, she ordered the golden cows from Dali Guanyin Hall in the eastern foothills of Cangshan Mountain to plow in spring and fall farming for the people.
This year, during the busy farming season, golden cows flew over Diancang Mountain at night and landed at Bazi along the Yangyi River to plow land for the people.
When the crop farmers woke up in the morning to work in the fields, they all saw that the pears in the field were deep, carefully raked, and grateful and strange. They all say that the gods who saved suffering gave blessings. In order to repay his favor, an old farmer prepared a can of home-brewed soju and meat, and crouched at night by an uncultivated field and waited. After a stroke of smoke, I saw a golden light falling from the top of Diancang Mountain and falling on a field a hundred steps away from him. Yo, it turned out to be a golden bullhead! It had sharp horns. I saw that its neck was bent and its head rushed down. The horn stuck in the mud and fluttered on all fours. The mud behind it looked like rolling waves. With just two cigarettes, more than 100 acres of fields were plowed. The old farmers were dumbfounded. While watching, they kept nodding praising and admiring them. They saw chickens barking over and over again. The golden cows had already plowed tens of thousands of acres of fields. I saw it stop raising its head and panting. He knew the golden cow was tired, so he hurriedly mentioned that can of soju and meat. Run to the Taurus. Unexpectedly, the Taurus was shocked, and it went up into the air. Seeing the situation, the old farmer hurriedly knelt down to say thanks, while pointing fingers at wine cans and meat. The golden cow stood tall in the middle of the sky. It understood the old farmer's feelings. It nodded and “shouted” for a long time, and it turned into a golden light hiding in the air.
Since then, crop farmers have learned that the golden ox of Dali Guanyin Tang came to cultivate fields and plow the land at night. Every household set up a Guanyin sign in the house, burning incense, and praising. Since then, the golden cows have been cultivating year after year, and ploughing the little spring, year after year. Crop farmers are rich in grains every year, and the six animals prosper. Every household likes it and laughs like it.
Another year of spring farming has arrived. The golden cow flew to the western slope of Diancangxi. It just happened that the big black dragon was making waves amidst the storm. The waves were raging and rolling into the fields. The Taurus couldn't help but be furious. His eyes were full of anger, and the stars shouted in the sky, swooped into the wind and waves, raised their horns, and attacked the big black dragon. When the Black Dragon was cold, he got caught two holes by his horn and hurriedly fought. Back and forth, there were hundreds of rounds of vicious battles. The sound of tearing and impact was like thunder and flashing, and the mountains were shaken, and the stars and moon had no light, regardless of victory or defeat. Suddenly, the sky was full of turbulence. The old mother of Guanyin came to help. She poured the nectar water from the clean bottle to the Taurus. The golden cow drank the nectar water and increased its strength a hundred times. The more it fought, the braver grew, and the armor of the Big Black Dragon was broken. The blood splashed violently, and the skin split all over her body. The pain was unbearable, and gradually became unbearable. Know that if you don't retreat, you will die and there will be no place to die! It made a long noise, crashed into the cliffs of Diancangshan Mountain, and “thundered” an earth-shattering sound. The big black dragon crashed through a stone gap and fled all the way through bloody storms...
The stone slit that the Big Black Dragon crashed into became an ancient stream in Shimen Guan. Snow, rain, and spring water from the western slope of Mount Cangshan converge down the ancient stream and become the water of the Shimen River.
In order to protect the crop farmers and prevent the Black Dragon from committing evil acts, the old mother of Guanyin ordered that the golden cow be turned into a stone cow to protect the land and water while farming, so as to keep the people safe forever. The people were grateful to Jinniutun for protecting agriculture, eliminating harm and helping farming, and named this place Jinniutun.
Shimen Pass is located east of Jinniutun, and beside the Yangliang River Ancient Trail west of Jinniutun, there is a natural stone cow. Villagers and past merchants often put grains or vegetables, melons, and fruits in the mouth of the stone cow, burn incense, and make sacrifices, and pray for whatever they want. As time goes by, the custom continues. When Yang (Shen), the champion of the Ming Dynasty, passed by here, he sang the “Golden Bull” poem: Strange Stones are born just like cows, and I don't know if they have experienced thousands of years. The wind blew all over my body, and I felt sweaty in the rain. It's hard to lay the grass flat, and you can't go back if you leave the golden whip alone. The shepherds played the flute in vain; heaven and earth did not accept it during the night.
