Legend: In Yangyi a thousand years ago, the Yi people lived. Most of their places were between 1,600 and 2,000 meters above sea level, and a few were above 2,000 meters above sea level. The Yi people living in these areas all have high mountains and steep slopes, but the mountains and clear water are beautiful, the forests are dense, and the pastures are good, and the seasons are like spring. There is a Yi family living in Mouji in the country. The older couple gave birth to the Longfeng sisters, and the whole family consists of four members. The two elders have three meals a day. The man hunts and the woman cultivates the land to do housework. The two sisters herd pigs, cows, and sheep, and hunt and cultivate the land as caretakers year after year. One summer afternoon of the year, the sisters rushed pigs, cows, and sheep to Qingbian to drink water in Qinggou. The sisters cool off under an unknown fruit tree in Qingbian. My sister suddenly heard a pig eating fruit. Unlike the usual sound of eating, my sister looked up to the pig. The fruit that the pig was eating had just fallen from this tree, and my sister picked up a few of them everywhere. I wanted to see the truth. No matter how I hit it, I couldn't open it. Finally, my sister found a stone and smashed the fruit food a few times. Each fruit was divided into four cells, and each compartment contained about one centimeter thick kernels. My sister could eat any pig. We also tried it. It was fragrant, crispy, and crunchy. It was really delicious. When I got home from harvesting pork, beef, and lamb late, I told Grandpa and Grandma. I heard from your grandmother that there is a type of fruit in the mountain forest called “sami” in Yi. The kernels in it are edible, but it is very laborious and difficult to open, and there are few kernels in it. After picking them up, dry them, smash them, remove the hard shell part, and add water to cook the oil. The oil is really fragrant.
The sisters are 18 and 9 years old. They are very beautiful and beautiful, have a kind heart, and are loyal and respectful. Since they live alone in a single village and rarely go out, no one has come to the house to bring up family. They chased pigs, cows, and sheep all day long. Year after year, day after day, and in the summer of the second year, the sisters were still rushing pigs, cows, and sheep to Qinggou to drink water one afternoon. They were able to cool off under the “Sabi” tree in Qingbian. Each time, they picked up a few “Sami” under this tree to eat. After eating, I fell asleep. When I woke up, my sister asked her to sleep well. My sister answered that she had a good night's sleep and had a dream. My sister said that I had a dream too. I had a dream where an old lady with gray hair said; your sisters are so tired of chasing pigs, cows, and sheep all day long, and they pick up a few “Sami's Poop” and smash them every day. The fruit is hard and hard to break, and the number of kernels is small and laborious. The old lady gave it to both of you and your sisters. In the future, you will be able to eat nuts like “Sami”. They can be opened without much effort, and the skin will be thick and thick. But you have to wait for a few years, and do as I said. There is a similar tree growing on the side of the “Sami Gou” where you chased pigs, cows, and sheep, and went to Qinggou to drink water, cool down, and sleep; it won't bear fruit. Look for the two trees, each with a fork attached to each other. The wind rubbed the two branches. The bark was torn, and tears were still shed. You find a few pieces of earth and hemp, pinch the worn parts of the two branches together, and then wrap them tightly with ground hemp. Gradually, one branch will die naturally. After a few years, this tree, which is alive, will bear full branches and fruit. At that point, you won't have to work hard; you can open it with a pinch, and it's two and a half thick, and the peel is thin and thick. It's delicious when you eat it, and it's delicious. You often eat it to make your hair darker, moisturize your lungs, and grow more beautiful and beautiful. In the future, you'll have to name it because it's a marriage with “Sami Maid,” then change the latter word “mother” to “Sami Mother”! (The dream my sister had is no worse than the dream my sister had).
Since the old lady gave them dreams, the sisters herded pigs, cows, and sheep while digging up a few “Sabi” seedlings and planted them on the front and back of their house. By the beginning of spring the following year, the sisters did as the old lady said, cut off a few “Sabi Mama” tips, cut off a piece of skin from the bottom 5 inches to 1 foot, cut off a piece of skin of the same size at the bottom of the “Sami Mother” brace, and attached the cut skin close to the “Sabi Mu” peeling area, and held them tightly with an earthen horse. In order to make the “Sa Miyu” toothpaste hair more fat, every year, the “Sa Miyu” teeth are held tightly. The young shoots cut it off. After four or five years in the fall, the sisters discovered that the whole branch of the tree was covered with fruit, just like the old lady said. It opened in a pinch, and the peel was thin and thick. It was fragrant, crispy, and crunchy in the mouth. In the fall of the sixth year, the two sisters cultivated and grafted one after another and formed the same “grandmother.”
Since then, “Sami Mother” has spread in Yangyi, where the Yi people live. People from the Yi ethnic group in Si Xiang came to see the truth. More and more people came to watch. The two sisters also attracted the attention of Yi guys. The sister recruited a Yi guy as her son-in-law, and her younger sister also chose a Yi guy to marry. This explains the origin of “Sami Mother” soaking walnuts.
