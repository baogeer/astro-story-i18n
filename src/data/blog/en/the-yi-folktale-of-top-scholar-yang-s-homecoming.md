Yang Zhuangyuan wanted to resign and go home for a long time. He told the emperor several times, but the emperor did not agree.
One day, a tribe sent two jade barrels, which the emperor really liked. If someone wanted to hurt Yang Zhuangyuan, they would bet with him whether they would dare to break a jade barrel. Yang Zhuangyuan said there was something he didn't dare to say. When I lifted the jade barrel, I smashed it on the stone steps. The jade barrel instantly turned into gravel. The emperor later learned that he was furious and wanted to be punished. Yang Zhuangyuan said, “The world can only be ruled by one, not two barrels. I smashed a barrel just to preserve the unity of the country; what was my crime?”
After a few days, someone made another bet with Yang Zhuangyuan and asked him if he dared to smash the remaining jade barrel. Yang Zhuangyuan said what he didn't dare to do. Talking, he smashed that jade barrel again. The emperor learned that Yang Zhuangyuan's head was cut off. Yang Zhuangyuan said, “The tribe that sent the jade barrels had no good intentions. They sent two jade barrels to separate the country from the emperor, so I smashed one. Later, I heard that they had set up an army to rebel and take over the world. So, in one fell swoop, I smashed the jade bucket they sent.” The emperor listened: “What you said makes some sense, but you shouldn't have smashed the jade barrel without my consent. Forget it, let him go.” The person who bet with Yang Zhuangyuan said, “Capital crimes are exempt, life crimes are not exempt. If everyone finds a reason to smash something in the palace, then that's OK?” The emperor said, “What you said also makes sense; then distribute the frontier. However, you can choose any location.” Yang Zhuangyuan said, “I'd rather go 800 miles to the desert than go to Bijiguan in Yunnan.” The emperor asked, “Isn't Bijiguan in Yunnan your hometown, why don't you want to go back?” Yang Zhuangyuan said, “Yunnan Bijiguan is not a place for people. A mosquito weighs half a pound; wild artemisia and grass can bite people.”
The emperor sent someone to investigate, and when he came back, the report said, “Yang Zhuangyuan said that it is true that there are many and big mosquitoes in Yunnan. If they bite people, they will rot to pieces, and they will stare at people every grass or tree.” It turns out that Yang Zhuangyuan secretly sent people to put old earthen bees into the house where people sent by the emperor lived and brought them to the nettle field. Every time a grass was pulled, they stung people.
Later, Yang Zhuangyuan made up his wish and returned to Bijiguan, Yunnan.
On the way, a group of beautiful girls' daughters-in-law were scavenging. The person escorting Yang Zhuangyuan said, “Everyone said that Yang Zhuangyuan is smart, witty, and eloquent, but none of us have seen it with our own eyes. Today, if you have the ability to kiss all those girls' daughters-in-law, we'll be satisfied.” Yang Zhuangyuan said, “This is simple. I kissed them. They are all just willing; they don't believe it. You go watch it with me.”
Yang Zhuangyuan came to the girl's daughter-in-law and said very seriously, “Yesterday, I lost a sheep. I doubt one of you killed it.” The girls' daughters-in-law all said I wasn't. Yang Zhuangyuan said, “The rhetoric is unfounded. People who have eaten lamb taste like lamb meat. I can tell if they have stolen sheep by smelling it.” The girl's daughter-in-law scrambled to say, “Look at it first, I got it; I'm not afraid.” Yang Zhuangyuan personally smelled his face, nose to nose, and mouth to mouth. In the end, Yang Zhuangyuan said that none of them were sheep thieves; the girl's daughter-in-law cheered and went scavenging the seedlings.
The family who escorted Yang Zhuangyuan and his entourage laughed when they couldn't stand it any longer.
