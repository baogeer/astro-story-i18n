The dragon looked up in February. Legend has it that the Jade Emperor ordered no rain within three years. People starved to death due to the lack of rain, crops withered, and many people starved to death. People asked for rain from heaven. This moved Tianlong. This moved Tianlong. Tianlong carried the Jade Emperor to drop heavy rain on the earth, mitigating human disasters. After learning about it, the Jade Emperor was furious and crushed Tianlong under the mountain. It was impossible to return to the Heavenly Garden until the golden beans blossomed.
People are looking for golden beans everywhere in order to rescue Tenryu, return to heaven, and serve the public, but by February 2 of next year, no golden beans have been found. People only grow corn seeds. When some people say, these corn seeds look like or not. Everyone says, like, fry corn seeds in a pot to blossom, isn't that just golden beans blooming? As a result, people used corn seeds to fry the flowers in a pot, put them on the table and sincerely pray, saying that the golden beans have already bloomed. Please ask Emperor Yu to take good care of the dragon and let go back to the heavenly court!
Emperor Jade was moved by people's actions, so Tianlong was released, and Tianlong was able to continue serving humans. The day Tenryu made his comeback happened to be the second day of February in the lunar calendar. On this day, there was heavy rain and thunder, heralding the beginning of a new year of spring farming. To commemorate Tenryu's comeback, people regard February 2 as the day the dragon lifts its head. Many babies have to wait for this day to shave their heads for the first time, which indicates the day when they grow up to adulthood here.
