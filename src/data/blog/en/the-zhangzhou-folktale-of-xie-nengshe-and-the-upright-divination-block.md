 Xie Nengshe's father, named Xie Minglian, was a scout in the Ming dynasty. Before he raised anyone, his family was very poor. He had to work as a teacher, set up a school in Yanglao Alley in Zhangzhou, and used to cook a village. Since his salary only covered three meals of porridge and had no spare money to buy good ingredients, he used small wood to cut a wooden chicken leg and use it to cook rice with soybean oil, lest people say he had no food.
One day, he discovered that there were many termites in the bookcase eating his books, so he left the books empty on the side of the road. It just so happened that some chickens in the village saw termites, and they all scrambled to peck, knocked over the bookcase, and were covered in the bookcase. Soon, the sky fell “rice spoon rain”. Xie Lian hurriedly packed the books in the bookcase and returned to the school. She didn't notice the chicken in the bookcase.
In the evening, Cuobian discovered that one chicken was missing, and there was no way to retrieve it. Some people argued that Mr. Jiaoshu-san doesn't make much money a year, but he eats chicken thighs with meals every day. How dare he steal chicken from others to eat? Cuobian felt it made sense, so I asked Xie Lian. Xie Lian vowed, “I am a book reader, and I would never do such a despicable thing!” He denied stealing a chicken. Everyone encouraged the owner to search him, and the lost dead chicken was found in his bookcase. Xie Lian was so ashamed that she couldn't help herself. He argued again and again, and people argued. Some people said, “Otherwise, you go to the Temple of Garan with everyone to throw a cup. If you throw a holy cup, it means you're not stealing a chicken on purpose. If you throw a cup or a laughing cup, it means that you are stealing the chicken with you.” Xie Lian had no choice but to go with everyone to the Jialan Temple to throw a cup.
Coincidentally, Grandpa Tianjialan was not in the temple. His men didn't dare to make a decision, and “stand out”. Everyone said, “Grandpa Garan didn't give you proof; Mr. must have stolen the chicken on purpose. Xie Lian had to swallow the sound and roll up the floor to earn a living elsewhere. Later, he studied hard and advanced in his studies by leaps and bounds. He went back to his hometown to sacrifice his ancestors and rode around the city to show his glory. He was tied to the back of a horse and dragged along the street, and after walking around the street, he relentlessly threw the statue into Nanmen Creek. Strangely enough, the wooden statue actually sank into the water as soon as it fell into the water. You can't even salvage it.
Later, Xie Tanhua's wife gave birth to a son, known by the mononym Neng. He was smart from an early age, but he acted wobbly and made many mischief that made people cry and laugh. In the end, he destroyed his family and only then jumped into Nanmen Creek to commit suicide, so people all say that Grandpa Jialan couldn't bear the wrath of being dragged around the streets. He was reincarnated and transformed into Xie Nengshe, the guy who came to Xie Lian.
The large plaque “Zhuo Li” still hangs high in Yang Lao Xiang's Garan Ye Temple. It was destroyed until the temple was demolished.
