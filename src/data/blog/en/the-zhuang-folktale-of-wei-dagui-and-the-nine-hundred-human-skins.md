 A long time ago, there was a man named Wei Dagui in Zhuangxiang who was minister under a Turkish emperor. Da Gui is not very old, but he is very knowledgeable. Moreover, he is very concerned about the suffering of the Zhuang people. The emperor gave him all his salary back to his hometown and distributed it to the people. He had nothing left.
One year, there was a great drought in Zhuangxiang. The townspeople asked Dagui to give the Turkish emperor free of royal food. Da Gui knelt down and said, “The people of Zhuangxiang had no harvest. I went to the inspection for a thousand years.” Da Gui accompanied him to Zhuangxiang. He saw that the fields were cracked, the ears were burnt, and a group of people with yellow skin and thin skin knelt on the mountain path to suffer. Da Gui knelt down and said, “See you in a thousand years, hopefully free of food.” The Turkish emperor had no choice but to get rid of the emperor in his hometown.
After the attack, he hated Da Gui to the bone, but due to Da Gui's popularity, he couldn't find any trouble to get rid of him, so he devised a toxic plan and plotted to frame Da Gui.
One day, he summoned Da Gui to his front: “Da Gui, you've always been very capable, and now I want you to do something for me.” After listening to Da Gui, he knew that the Turkish emperor had no good intentions, but he calmly said, “Please come over a thousand years old.” Emperor Tu squinted and said, “I'm building a pavilion. There is a shortage of tiles. I heard that the skin can protect against the cold and heat, waterproof and fireproof, and is durable. I want you to get me 900 zigzag skins to use as tiles within two months. I would greatly appreciate it. If it doesn't work, it must be severely punished.” Da Gui easily answered, “When that happens, please ask Chitose to personally order and inspect the goods under the city gate.”
A month has passed, and Da Gui hasn't moved. Fifty-nine days have passed, and Da Gui still hasn't moved. When the deadline was reached, Da Gui summoned 900 powerful men. Each brought a catty of glutinous rice, a catty of rice wine, and a catty of pepper noodles to the foot of the imperial city to cook glutinous rice, cook chili vegetable soup, take off their coats one by one, sit shirtless, drink alcohol and drink chili soup. Just when they were full of wine and full of sweat, the Turkish emperor arrived in a large sedan in eight.
Da Gui hurriedly stepped forward and said, “The goods have already been delivered to Chitose, but all of these poor bones are leaking water. Can they be used?” When the Turkish Emperor got out of the car and took a look, their dark and red skin was all wet. It looked like it had been taken out of water, and the smell was pungent. The Turkish emperor covered his nose with his hands, frowned, and stepped back a few steps: “It's leaking and smelly. You can't use it, you can't use it.” After speaking, I transferred the car back to the palace.
