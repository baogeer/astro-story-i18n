 Legend has it that Brodda and Milocia were the ancestors who were sent by the Emperor to create man-made creatures and created the world of mortals. He also created Ganzhuang Mountain in Naguantun, Liulian Village, Baiyu Town, Tianyang County. It used to be a mecca for him to live and reproduce for future generations. The following story is about the legend of Prado and Mount Dang.
Brodore and Milocia were sent by the Emperor and set off on the first day of February. On that day, Brodore picked a pair of radish baskets, one with five children on one end and futons and clothes on the other; Milo Gia carried a hoe on his shoulder and a kaisan sickle in his hand, and slowly drifted down on two leaves taken in front of the Tiandi Palace. ... one day, two days. On the 19th day, Brodda and Milocia floated over Tianyang. At this point, dark clouds suddenly changed, and lightning thunder, which had never been seen before on the ground, accompanied by heavy wind and rain, and exploded deafly over the earth. Amidst the sound of thunder, the flat on Brodore's shoulder broke down. In an instant, the two baskets flew down to the ground. Brodore and Milocia panicked. They immediately threw off the broken shackles, hoes, and sickles in their hands, and slammed to death at the child. But it's too late. In just a blink of an eye, the two baskets fell ten miles from east to west, and three today, respectively. The basket containing the futon fell on the east side. Where the futon fell to the ground, a pile of dirt suddenly towed up, and soon formed a mountain. This is the first mountain in Zhuangjia, “Dare Zhuangshan.” That gap between the futons, hole by hole, later became a cave in the mountain.
The couple from Prado landed on “Daredevil Mountain,” and they both searched for half a day without seeing any sign of their child. They both immediately rushed to the top of Gan Zhuang Mountain and stood at the highest point in the west to look around from afar. They still haven't seen the falling basket or the five children. As a result, another stone block was moved to a platform. This platform was later called “Wangzidai.” Mr. and Mrs. Brodda climbed to the lookout and looked south. They saw that a large curved trench had been dug in the place where the hoe and sickle fell. After falling, the rain from the sky all flowed into the gutter, forming the Youjiang River. Brotto and Milocia had no intention of looking down on the rivers and mountains; they looked west. I saw five children squirming in Hirakawa, ten miles away. Miloga excitedly shouted “kid” at first sight. As soon as the shouting fell, the five children immediately sat motionless, and the kung fu of a bag of smoke turned into five hills. People call them “Wuzi Mountain.” Because of the homonym “child” and “finger,” they were all later called “Wuzhishan.”
Suddenly, the five living sons became five mountains, and they could no longer hear their parents' calls. The Brothos and his wife cried silently, plucked up the dirt under their feet like crazy with their hands, and threw them around Gan Zhuang Mountain in a “whoop, whoop” manner. It should be known where these earthen blocks have reached, and soon it has grown into another mountain, connected to Mount Wuzi, and surrounded by Ganzhuang Mountain. Since then, the land of Zhuangxiang has had the Ganzhuang Mountain Range, with “Dang Zhuang Mountain” as the main mountain.
