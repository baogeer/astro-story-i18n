 There is a beautiful legend about hydrangeas in Zhuangxiang. I'm talking about a poor family living in a small village under Jiuzhou Ancient Town in Jingxi County more than 800 years ago. Grandpa, the son of a poor family, is in love with Ah Xiu, a girl from a neighboring village. Ah Xiu is beautiful, good-natured, and deeply in love with her honest, hardworking, and brave grandpa. One spring, when Ah Xiu was once running for polders, he was looked upon by a rich and powerful villain in town. He wanted to marry Ah Xiu. Ah Xiu threatened each other with death and refused to do so.
When Eu Shao learned that Ah Xiu was deeply in love with his old brother from the neighboring village, in order to make Ah Xiu die, he turned his eyes around and made up his mind. He bribed the government, imprisoned Grandpa in a dungeon on “unnecessary” charges, and sentenced him to death, awaiting execution in the fall. After hearing this news, Ah Xiu felt like a thunderbolt from the blue. She washed her face with tears all day long and went blind in tears. After Ah Xiu cried and went blind, Ah Xiu began sewing hydrangeas stitch by stitch for Grandpa, who was about to be cut after the fall. The needle broke my hand, and blood flowed on the hydrangea. After being stained with blood, the flowers on the hydrangea were more colorful, the leaves were greener, and the birds were more vibrant. After 981 days, the hydrangea filled with Ah Xiu's deep love for Grandpa was ready.
After Ah Xiu Bian sold her own jewelry, bought out the prison guards, and was accompanied by her family, when she felt her grandpa, who thought about day and night in a dark and humid dungeon, but had been tortured to the bone, Ah Xiu was desperate and tried to take a hydrangea out of her body and put it on her brother's neck. At this point, seeing a flash of light, Ah Xiu, Grandpa, and the family floated down at the foot of a beautiful and rich mountain far from the devil.
Later, Ah Xiu and Grandpa got married, had a son and a daughter, and lived a happy life with her hard-working hands. After passing through ten, and ten to 100, hydrangeas slowly became the mascot of the people of Zhuangxiang, a symbol of the love between young men and women in Zhuangxiang. Later, there were folk activities such as throwing hydrangeas and lion rolling hydrangeas.
Today, throwing hydrangeas is still a popular traditional sport for the Zhuang people. Its history can be traced back more than 2000 years, when it was used to throw an ancient instrument, the “flying mule,” which was cast in bronze, and was mostly used in combat and hunting. Later, the flying ball gradually developed into the current embroidered cloth bag, or hydrangea. People often throw things around to entertain each other after tea, which plays a role in communicating feelings.
It has now evolved into a way for young men and women from the Zhuang ethnic group to express their love. Song Zhufu's “Laughter in the Mountains” says, “For a few days, a man and a woman split into two pairs of friends in the wild, each using five colors of beans and millet, back and forth.”
