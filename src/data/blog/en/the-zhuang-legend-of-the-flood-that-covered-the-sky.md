 Once upon a time, there were people on the ground. One year, Grandpa Lei made tricks and tricks in the sky and deliberately didn't rain creatures on the ground. It has been dry for six months without a single drop of water. All the trees on the mountain died, and so did the wild animals starved to death. People had no choice but to ask for Zhang Baobu. Zhang Baobu can do spells, and even Grandpa Lei is no match for him. He listened to what people said, and when he complained about them, he invented spells. He said, “If it doesn't rain for three days, I want Lei Gong to fall.”
After a few days, it really rained. Grandpa Lei was very angry because Zhang Baobu on the ground bothered him. He then attempted to kill Zhang Baobu. At that time, Zhang Baobu was studying spells at home. Lei Gong cut it down and didn't hit him, so he covered up Lei Gong with a chicken mask.
The news of Grandpa Lei's arrest reached the public's ears, and everyone said they wanted to kill him to eat it. Zhang Baobu accepted everyone's opinions and went to the street with the crowd to buy ingredients and prepare to kill Grandpa Lei. Before going to the street, Zhang Baobu said to his son and daughter Fuxi brothers and sisters, “Never let Grandpa Lei drink tea or clean water at home.”
After Zhang Baobu went to the street, Grandpa Lei said to Fuxi's brothers and sisters, “Boy, bring me some tea.” Brother and sister Fu Xi said, “My father told me when he went. He wouldn't give Lei Gong tea to drink.”
Grandpa Lei had no choice. After thinking about it, he wanted to say, “If you don't give me tea, just give me some water (to feed the pigs)!” Brother and sister Fu Xi thought, “Don't give him tea. My father said that. Why don't you give him boiled water? My father never said that. The water is so dirty, you can probably give him a drink!” So the brother and sister brought a bucket of very dirty water to Grandpa Lei. Grandpa Lei was covered in a chicken hood and couldn't come out to drink water, so he asked Fuxi's brother and sister to take a straw and drink the water from the bucket from the hood.
Grandpa Lei took a sip. The chicken mask moved slightly. After taking the second bite, the chicken mask moved a lot. After taking three, the chicken mask broke, and Lei Gong moved. It turns out that Grandpa Lei can use his magic weapon when he has water; when he doesn't, he can't use it. Now that he has water, he can break through this chicken hood and come out.
Grandpa Lei pulled out a tooth from his mouth and said to Fuxi's brother and sister, “You take this tooth and grow it. When it bears fruit and is ripe, you take it off, dig out the heart inside, and get well. When the water comes one day, you'll dive in.” After speaking, Teng Yun drove off in the fog.
Soon after, Grandpa Lei was furious because Zhang Baobu had embarrassed him, and ordered Rain God to rain all day and night. There was a lot of rain. The river rose, first flooded the plains, then flooded the village, then the mountains. Finally, the flood continued to flood the sky.
Furthermore, when the Fuxi brothers and sisters saw that the water was coming, they just followed what Grandpa Lei said, they drilled into that gourd, floated on the surface of the water, and floated all the way to the sky. Suddenly there was a noise. When they knew they had reached the sky, they called, “Grandpa Lei!” Lei Gong asked, “Who?” They answered, “Fuxi brothers and sisters.” Grandpa Lei also asked, “Have all the people on the ground died?” Brother and sister Fu Xi said, “All the people and creatures on the ground are dead. Only my father, Zhang Baobu, is still riding behind me.” Lei Gong was furious: “Hey, hate, everyone in the world has died, why only my opponent?” So he took out his sword and waited for Zhang Baobu to arrive. When Zhang Baobu arrived, he had a fight with Lei Gong. Lei Gong stabbed Zhang Baobu in a fight. Zhang Baobu had no recourse. He was defeated by Lei Gong, and the flood receded.
Later, Taibai Baixing scolded Grandpa Lei and said, “If you do this, everyone in the world will die. Who else will burn incense for you in the future?” Prince Lei didn't think it was right either. Taibai Jinxing called the Fuxi brother and sister to become a couple, and they were born again as humans. Brother and sister Fu Xi said, “No, you want us to get married. If you cut a bamboo into pieces, and if you can connect them together, we'll be husband and wife.” Immortals are capable, so they attached bamboo together. Bamboo originally had no knots; when cut and picked, it became a knotty plant.
After the Fuxi brother and sister became husband and wife, two years later, they gave birth to a monster, which was a sharpening stone. The two were so angry that they broke this sharpening stone and scattered it from the mountain to the ground. Those that fall in the river become fish and shrimp; those that fall on the mountain become birds and animals; those that fall in the village become ordinary people. Since then, the world has had people and creatures again.
Myths and legends about the Fuxi brothers and sisters breeding and breeding humans are still circulating among the Sichuan people. It is about the same as those spread among ethnic minorities in the southwest. It can be seen that they all have the same origin.
