 A long time ago, in a village at the southern foot of the 100,000 mountains, there lived a pair of young Zhuang people who were in love on a blind date. The man's name was Nongduan, and the woman's name was Nongya.
One day, the two heard a pleasant “ding-dong” sound coming from the cave at the entrance of a cave. They both wanted to bring this beautiful, moving and strange sound back to the cottage and share it with the townspeople.
As a result, the two found a gourd shell in the mountains, a wooden pole, and inserted the wooden pole into the gourd to stretch the thin strip of Ueno wisteria. Sure enough, a “ding-ding” sound similar to “ding-dong” popped up. After hearing it, the townspeople all clapped their hands and praised it, calling it “Ding Ding.”
The melodious sound of the piano soon spread to the sky, and the Jade Emperor sent immortals to summon Nong Duan and Nong Ya, and also named them immortals.
In order to dispel the thoughts of the townspeople, every year from the 1st to the 15th of the first month of the first year, when Tianmen Gate opened, they both rushed back to the mortal place to sing and dance with the townspeople. This form of activity was later named “Sky Jump.” The “Ding Ding” is the only instrument in the “Sky Jump” event, so it is called the “Tianqin”.
