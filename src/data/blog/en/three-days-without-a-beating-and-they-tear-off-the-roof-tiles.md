 Bao has two very playful kids. They either chase chickens or chase dogs every day; otherwise, they just mess up the food in the field. Bao is so angry that he has to beat them up every day. After being beaten, the two brothers were only a little more honest. The next day, it was still messing around again.
When the wheat was harvested, Bao's husband broke his leg while doing business outside and was unable to go home. All the work in the field fell to Mr. Bao alone. She cuts wheat during the day and plays at night. She is so busy that she has no trouble caring about her two mischievous sons.
After harvesting the wheat, Bao was so tired that he couldn't even straighten his back. When he lay down in bed, he fell asleep crying out.
It rained heavily in the middle of the night, and Bao was woken up by rain leaking from the roof. She was very curious where the water came from. After turning on the light, she discovered that several tiles had been removed from the roof. No wonder the rain had leaked out.
Bao knew it was her two mischievous sons, and yelled at them, “Okay, you two little bastards. If you don't beat you for three days, you guys will go to the house and remove the tiles.” Naturally, the two mischievous ghosts were beaten up hard.
Later, people described children's playfulness by not fighting for three days and going to the house.
