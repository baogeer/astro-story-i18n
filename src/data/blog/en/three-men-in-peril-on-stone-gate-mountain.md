From ancient times to the Qing dynasty, there were not only incidents of tigers, bears, wild boars and wolves injuring people in the dark and dark forest of Cangshan Mountain, but also incidents of wild people, humans, and bears being mutilated together. Legend has it that Lang Zhong, who is in his 50s, went to Shimen Mountain alone to collect medicine and never returned home after dark. The family was in such a hurry that ants were spinning around on a hot pot and didn't meet their eyes overnight. The next day, Lang Zhong's son Ah Qing invited Ah Bao, a mountain master from the same village, to go to Shimen Mountain to find his father. Ah Qing followed Ah Bao along the winding mountain path through the jungle, climbing steep cliffs, and crossing Shenqing, and kept calling “Grandpa, Grandpa...” as she walked. I walked about 15 or 6 miles along the mountain road. I saw no one alive, and no corpses when I died. The duo's mood is getting heavier and heavier. They have an ominous feeling that they don't want to talk about it. Ah Qing couldn't stop crying; she still choked and kept calling out for her father. A desolate cry echoed in the deep mountains and old woods. The higher you go, the denser the woods, the more eerie and scary. Ah Qing walked, wept, and cried. “Don't make a sound!” Ah Bao, the leader, suddenly raised an alarm: “Look, this pile of human and bear feces. The color seems to have just disappeared. Humans and bears are probably close by.” Having just finished speaking, there was a crackling “clap” in the woods. It was the sound of dead branches being trampled on. The two followed their popularity and vaguely saw the shadow of a beak shaking. “No good, it's humans and bears!” “Brother Ah Bao, what should I do?” “Run downhill!” The two were so nervous that they quickly jumped out of their mouths. “You're in front, I'm in the back; the faster you run, the better!” They both ran furiously along the way.
