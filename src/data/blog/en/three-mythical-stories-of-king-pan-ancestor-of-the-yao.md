 The folklore literature of the Yao people is rich and varied. Most of it is passed down by word of mouth, and some have been copied in Chinese. It comes in many forms, including myths, stories, legends, songs, riddles, and sayings. So do you know any folk stories about the Yao people?
1. The legend of the birthday of a Yao cow
There is a legend in the Yao family: “Pangu opened the sky, Luban built the wall, He Wang sent He, and the Niu Wang cultivated the fields.” According to the ancient Yao books “The Big Song of Pantian” and “Roller Mountain List,” Yin Mao experienced heavy flooding in two years, and everything in the world was flooded; only the Fuxi brothers and sisters were left hidden in the water. Only then was it possible to continue as a human being. Later, Pan Wang ascended the temple and gave the people of Yao the world's mountains to be cut down and allowed to live and work peacefully. However, in the Yaoshan region, poor mountains and bad water, despite people's hard work, they are still uncertain. One year, Taibai Baixing visited the public situation and saw that the Yao family had lived in Junling Mountain for generations, cultivating fires, and had a difficult life, so they reported to Heaven. Emperor Jade then sent King He to send Rice to the world, and King Cow came down to cultivate the fields. Since King Niu came to the world, the Yao family has been cultivating fields and planting crops. The weather and rain have been smooth year by year, and grains are abundant. In order to thank King Niu, the Yao family used the day King Cow went down a fan (April 8 of the lunar calendar) as the traditional holiday Ox's birthday. The Yao family has a folk song, “April 8, throw the plow; in July and a half, the barn is full. Take back 10,000 tons of food, all with the help of cows.” On the first day of April 8, the Yao family takes care of cows the most. They use cows as gods to worship and worship, and let them leave their rakes and rest for a whole day. On this day, no one is allowed to whip cows, fight bulls, let alone kill cows, or even curse cows. On the first day, every household had to bathe the cow once, brush the whole body clean, clean and tidy the cow fence, lay out fresh grass once, draw on red paper or cut a Buddha charm and stick it on the cow fence to exorcise evil and scare the plague, and protect the cow's health. On this day, what the cows ate was even more unique and exciting. Early in the morning, people scrambled to release the cows to eat dew grass. The sooner the better, “stealing the head.” In the morning, use glutinous rice lees to cook eggs for the cows. At Yao's family, children have a habit of eating eggs on their birthdays. There is a saying, “People live, eat carrots; when cows live, eat bitter ginseng.” In the afternoon, boil loach with bitter ginseng to feed the cows to keep them healthy and live a long time. At night, the Yao family also had to select the best and strongest cows for the party. The Yao family dressed in festive attire, surrounded by a roaring bonfire, knocked on long drums, and danced with cheerful songs.
II. The legendary story of the Panwang Festival of the Yao people 1
In ancient times when heaven and earth were floods, there were two equally tall Baoshan mountains in the mountains. They were one mile apart and faced off against each other. The one on the left is called Mount Brocy, which looks like a powerful man; the one on the right looks like a young woman in a dress, named Mount Milodha. Every year, these two mountains move one foot closer to each other. After 995, they* became 995 feet closer, and then moved 5 feet closer, and the two mountains were connected. One day, May 29 of the lunar calendar, an earth-shattering thunder suddenly sounded in the sky. The two mountains split two slits at the same time. Then, a tall man walked out of Mount Brocy, and a strong woman walked out of Mount Mirota. This day was the birthday of a man and a woman. They later became a happy couple. The man's name was Brocey. The woman's name was Millotha. They are the first pair of hardworking and intelligent parents who created everything in the world. After they created the world, they let their three daughters each go out to earn a living. The eldest daughter carried a rake to the plains to plow and rake fields, and became the current Han people; the second daughter picked up a load of books to study and became the current Zhuang family; the third daughter took a bucket of valleys and a bronze gong that Mirotuo gave her, and went to the mountains to cultivate land. Since then, the three daughters have lived and worked peacefully in the mountains and have become the current Yao ethnic group. Therefore, the Yao people have lived in Takayama and Shenqing for generations, cultivated land, and Causeway has become the family heirloom of the Yao people. Mirothor is getting older. One day he brought back his three daughters and said to them, “October 16 is my birthday.” At that point, you will “feed” me. He also specifically ordered his third daughter to say, “You don't have any rich gifts. At that time, you only need to make a pot of rice wine and bring it to everyone to taste. On October 15, you should bring a causeway to the scene in advance.” Since then, the Yao people have had the “Panwang Festival”, which starts on October 15 and ends on October 17.
3. The legendary story of the Panwang Festival of the Yao people 2
In ancient times, King Gao invaded, and King Ping took the lead. Whoever can defeat King Gao at the first level will marry the princess. This remark was heard by the dragon dog Pan Yu. Pan Yu took off the gold list and crossed the sea to go to Gao Wang's side. Panping pretended to be attentive and was favored by King Gao. One day, Pan Yu took advantage of Gao Wang's drunkenness to bite off King Gao Wang's head and dedicate it to King Ping, making great contributions, so he married the Three Princesses. Later, Panyu wanted to become a human, so she asked the princess to steam it in a steamer for seven days and seven nights, and the princess did the same. After steaming for six days and six nights, the princess had drowned her husband. When she secretly uncovered the lid, the pan actually turned into a human. It was just that in less than seven days and seven nights, there was still a lot of hair dust falling off her head and calves. Later, she wrapped her head and calves with a cloth. After Pan Yi became a man, King Ping sent him to Huiji Mountain to become king, known as King Pan. After their marriage, Pan Wang and the Three Princesses gave birth to six boys and six daughters. King Ping each gave them one surname, making them the first twelve surnames of the Yao people. Although Panyu is already king, he still lives a simple life, teaching children to work and hunt, and earn a living through hard work. At one point, Panwang and his six sons went up the mountain to hunt and chase an injured goat. Unfortunately, they were wounded by a sheep horn. They fell down a cliff and died. The body was hung on a tree. The children cut down the tree to make drums, peeled off the sheepskin, and covered it. They carried long drums and sang while beating and crying in memory of Panwang. To this day, the Yao people still maintain the habit of not eating dog meat, and sacrificing Panwang is also an important part of the Panwang Festival.
