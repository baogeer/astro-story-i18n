 The legend of the cowherd and weaver is one of the four major legends in China. Therefore, there are many different stories about this legend. They either have similar plots, the main character's name is different, or the main character's name is the same, and the plot is different. Furthermore, in the process of word of mouth, legends often incorporate local colors, such as the location where the story takes place, etc. Intersectionality is an important characteristic of folk literature. Here are three different texts from The Legend of the Cowherd and the Weaver.
one
Legend has it that the parents of the cowherd died young and were often abused by his brother and sister. He was accompanied by only one old cow. One day, Lao Niu devised a plan to marry a weaver as his wife. On that day, the beautiful fairies certainly went to the Milky Way to bathe and play in the water. At this point, the cowherd hiding in the reeds suddenly ran out and took the weaver's clothes. The frightened fairies hurriedly went ashore, dressed up and flew away; only the weaver remained. At the request of the cowherd, the weaver promised to be his wife. After marriage, the cowherd and the woman weave, the man farmed, and fell in love, and lived a very happy and full life. The weaver also gave birth to the cowherd a son and a daughter. Later, when the old cow was about to die, the cowherd was advised to keep its skin and put it on for help in case of emergency. After the old cow died, the couple endured pain, peeled off the cow and buried it on the hillside.
When the Jade Emperor and Princess Mother of Heaven learned about the marriage between the weaver and the cowherd, they were furious and ordered Tenjin to take back the weaver. Tenjin took advantage of the cowherd's absence, and took the weaver. The cowherd went home and didn't see the weaver. He hurriedly put on cowhide and chased after him with two children. Seeing that they were about to catch up, Mother Wang was in a hurry. She pulled off the golden hairpin from her head and stroked at the galaxy. The galaxy, which was once light, instantly became muddy and raging, and the cowherd couldn't get by anymore. Since then, the cowherd and weaver have only been tearful. They have been facing each other across the river for a long time. Emperor Jade Emperor and Lady Wang were allowed to meet once a year on July 7. Legend has it that every July 7, magpies on Earth have to fly to the sky and build a magpie bridge for the cowherd and weaver. Also, when people are quiet in the middle of Tanabata night, people can also hear the pulse of the cowherd and weaver in the sky under grape racks or other melon and fruit stands.

three
Legend has it that a long time ago, there was a Niujiazhuang in the west of Nanyang City. There was a smart, loyal, and hardworking child named Ling Er in Zhuang. Due to the premature death of her parents, she had to live with her older brother. Her sister-in-law, Ma Shi, is vicious and often abuses Reiji. In the fall of one year, just after harvesting, Ma forced Ling Er to go to the mountain to herd cows and said to him, “Give these nine cows to release. You'll have to wait until you have ten cows before you come back!” After Ling Er heard it; there was no choice but to rush the cow out of Niujiazhuang.
People from the village arrived after hearing the news and expressed deep sympathy for Ling Er. Some brought food, others brought clothes. Ling Er thanked them all, then walked alone to the mountains with the cow.
When he walked to the grassy, heavily forested mountain, he stopped to rest and sat under the tree and shed tears with sadness. Thinking to myself, how many years and months will I have to wait until I have ten heads? At this time, an old man with white beard suddenly appeared in front of him. The old man patted him on the shoulder and kindly asked, “Kid, why did you go to this remote place alone to herd cows? Why are you so sad?” Ling Er talked about abusing her sister-in-law and forcing him to herd cows all the time. The old man smiled and said, “That's what happened! Don't be sad. There is an old cow in Mount Funiu. Feed it well, and when the cow's illness gets better, you can drive the cow home.” After speaking, the old man disappeared. The spirit seemed to be dreaming. He then followed the old man's orders and walked to Mount Funiu.
Ling Er walked and went over mountains and streams, and finally found the sick cow. It was lying on a large flat rock. Ling Er immediately fell down, knocked her head three times in a row, and shouted “Big Brother Niu.” The old cow opened his tired eyes and closed them again for a while. Ling Er looked at the old cow's lack of energy. She thought in her heart that she was probably hungry, so she hurriedly went to scavenge the old cow bundle after bundle of grass. After three days and three nights of feeding, the old cow was finally full. I suddenly looked up and said to him: “Good boy, I was originally the Great Grey Bull from heaven. I broke the rules of heaven by secretly removing grain seeds from the world. I was kicked out of heaven by Emperor Jade. I broke my leg, and I couldn't move. If you can wash me with Baihua Dew for a month, I'll be fine.” After listening, Xiaoling stayed at Mount Funiu. She ate some wild fruit when hungry, drank some spring water when she was thirsty, and snuggled up next to the cow at night. Every morning I go to pick flowers and wash old cows with the dew on the flowers. After a full month, the old cow really healed. It went home with Ling Er.
When Ling Er got home, her sister-in-law saw that he had actually brought back ten cows. She insisted that one of them had stolen someone else, and beat him up. It was only after advice from the townspeople that it was counted as such. Since then, Ling Er herded cows during the day and slept next to cows at night. Everyone in Zhuang Shang called him a “cowherd.” The old cow that was saved by the cowherd is very close to the cowherd. Once, my sister-in-law put poison in the dish, and the old cow told the cowherd not to eat it. My sister-in-law got angry and wanted to break up with the cowherd. The cowherd didn't want a house or a field. As long as that old cow, two broken cars, and a crappy suitcase, they left the house and settled in a straw shed under a mulberry tree. The old cow spit out a tea bean from its mouth. The cowherd planted the tea beans in front of the hut and broke ground the next day. The leaves grew on the third day, and the cowherd was busy setting up the stand. After a few days, the beanstalk was full of bean vines. Lao Niu said, “Boy, if you hide under a tea bean stand at night, you can see the fairies in the sky, and the fairies can see you. If anyone peeks at you for seven nights, she just wants to be your wife. At that time, I used a car to take you to heaven, take her to the sky, and marry you.”
After listening to the old cow's instructions, the cowherd dived under the tea bean stand and looked up at the sky. He only saw a group of fairies bathing in Jade Pond. When they left, a fairy peeked down at him. The next night, I saw the fairy come alone to Jade Pond and boldly watch the cowherd. On the third night, I looked at the cowherd with a slight smile. On the fourth night, I nodded at the cowherd, brought out a basket of silkworms on the fifth night, stole a loom on the sixth night, and waved to the cowherd with a weaving shuttle on the 7th night. The cowherd and the weaver, one in the sky and the other underground, came and went. The cowherd looked forward to the weaver leaving, and the weaver hoped that the cowherd would reunite soon. On July 7, a magpie flew in from the sky and fell on the old cow's head and chirped, “The weaver sent me and asked you to go get together soon. Go get together, go get together soon.” The old cow smiled and nodded. The cowherd got in the car and sat down. The old cow emptied his feet and arrived at Jade Pond for a while. The cowherd got out of the car and carried the loom onto the car. The weaver carried a silkworm basket to the car, and the cowherd also jumped into the car and sat with the weaver. Lao Niu Tengyun drove the fog and got home after a while.
When the townspeople learned that the cowherd had become a family, they all came to congratulate them. The weaver distributed the celestial silkworms she brought from heaven to all the sisters to teach them how to raise silkworms, handle silk, and weave satin.
As a result, everyone in the village knows that the cowherd married a wise wife. They can raise silkworms, can draw silk, and can also weave bright and bright satin. They all say that the weavers' looms are brought from the sky, and the woven satin is made into clothes, warm in winter and cool in summer. This news spread, attracting silk merchants from the mountains, south, and the sea, all scrambling to buy Nanyang silk. This incident shocked thousands of families on both sides of the Baihe River and the Funiu Mountain area, sending their own girls to learn how to weave. Weavers have a kind heart and are happy to teach others. In less than two years, every household learned how to raise silkworms and make silk and satin.
By July 7 of the third year, Weaver gave birth to a boy and a girl. The man's name was Brother Jin, and the girl's Yu Mei. The cowherds cultivate the fields, and the weavers weave, and have a very peaceful life.
