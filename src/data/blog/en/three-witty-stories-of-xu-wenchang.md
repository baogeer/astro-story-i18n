 Xu Wenchang has been good at using his brain since childhood. He is smart, witty, and full of fun.
Xu Wenchang's uncle liked him very much. He always thought of ways to make fun of him and test his ability to think. At one point, my uncle led Xu Wenchang to the edge of a narrow and soft bamboo bridge with water, filled two buckets with water, and said to Xu Wenchang, “I want to test you. If you can cross the bridge with these two buckets of water, I'll give you a present.”
When the teenager Xu Wenchang thought about it, he took off his shoes, tied the keg with two ropes, and then put the wooden barrel filled with water into the water. In this way, he walked across the wooden bridge with two ropes.
My uncle also wanted to use a more difficult method to make Xu Wenchang difficult. He said, “Now that you've crossed the bridge, of course I want to give you a present, but you have to get it according to my requirements.” Speaking of which, he hung the gift from the top of a long pole and told Xu Wenchang, “You can neither stand on a high place such as a bench to pick it up, nor can you roll down the bamboo pole.”
My uncle thought there would be no way for Xu Wenchang now.
However, Xu Wenchang touched the back of his head and immediately figured out how to get the present. I saw him hold the bamboo pole and walk all the way to the edge of a well, then drop Takeshige into the well. When the bamboo top reached the wellhead, he successfully received the present.
My uncle was stunned by the clever Xu Wenchang. He couldn't help but clap and praise: “Xu Wenchang is really smart!”
