This trifecta of three and four is throwing darts. They have Yang Zhi's ability to wear 100 steps. In other words, within 100 meters, whether they fly in the sky or walk on the ground, as soon as their darts are shot, they are guaranteed 100 hits. Just when Master Shoufu was happy, Master Ikkyu said, “Master Ikyu promised to help you, but I can only send one apprentice down the mountain to help you, because neither of my apprentices have taught, and you can't walk the river or lake without a master. Considering that catching thieves is a major event to appease the people on one side, let's make an exception and let one go first.”
Lord Morifu said, “If that's the case, it's OK for me to go down the mountain alone to help me.”
Next, Master Yi Xiu invited Shoufu to watch Zhang San Li Si's entrance exam. Master Shoufu readily agreed and said, “I just happened to be able to open my eyes.”
The exam will take place on the mountain. Two scarecrows were placed on top of the mountain. Zhang Sanli and Si each stood 100 meters away with three darts. After the exam started, Zhang San and Li Si each threw a dart. Zhang San's dart hit the scarecrow's wrist, and Li Si's dart hit the scarecrow's eyebrow.
The second dart, Zhang San hit the scarecrow in the neck, and Li Si shot the scarecrow in the chest.
The third dart, Zhang San shot the scarecrow in the lower abdomen, and Li Si shot the scarecrow in the throat.
Although they each had three darts in the middle, Zhang San's darts were clearly not as good as Li Si's darts. Li Si's darts and darts hit the key points, which can be described as being highly skilled.
Obviously, Li Si's departure was a foregone conclusion.
Just as Lord Shoufu and the crowd cheered for Li Si, Master Yi Xiu smiled meaningfully. After laughing, he announced, “My apprentice Zhang San left his class and immediately went down the mountain with Shoufu Daredevil to catch the thieves and get rid of violence and peace.”
Everyone was taken aback by Master Ikkyuu's words, and they stayed there with their mouths wide open.

Master Ikkyu smiled and shook his head and said, “The old man is not eccentric. Keep practicing; you haven't reached the level of becoming a master.”

Master Yi Xiu smiled and said to Master Shufu, “My old man, even though his old eyes are faint, he's not so faint that he can't discern the two of them. The old man really didn't make a mistake. Zhang San can actually leave the class; Li Si still needs to continue his training. Trust the old man. If you take Zhang San down the mountain, he will definitely live up to expectations.”
Master Shufu took Zhang San down the mountain in doubt.

It was no different from the position Li Si had shot at the time.
Lord Shufu was dumbfounded.
Master Ikkyuu said, “Have you seen it, sir? This is Zhang San's ability.”
