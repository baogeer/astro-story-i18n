One day, when they were cutting firewood, they picked a small pot of gold. Rekichi said, “Friend, let's split up. One and a half.” When Zehan saw the gold, he felt selfish in his stomach and said, “Brother, can't do it! Who knows if this thing was placed by an immortal? Or is it hidden by the devil? I simply took it home and kept it for a few days. Let's make another bottle of barley wine, wait for you to drink at my house, then split the gold, OK?”
Rekichi agreed to Zehan's proposal, and the two went home each carrying firewood. Zehan entered the door and quickly locked the gold in the box, got a few pieces of rotten, yellow wood, and stuffed them in a clay pot.
Three days later, Rekichi happily arrived to split the money and saw Zehan sitting at the door with a sad face. Reji said, “Friend, you have no patients upstairs, and no dead animals downstairs. Now, there's only some rotten wood left in the earthenware pot, and I don't know where the gold has gone.”
Reyoshi thought to herself, “It's clearly Huang Chengcheng's gold. Why did it become rotten wood after three days, it's really strange!” He was going to ask Zehan for a review, but he changed his mind and smiled and said, “If that's the case, let's just say that's not the case! Come on, let's have a drink!”
When Rekichi got home, the more she thought about it, the more she felt fooled. Then I came up with an ingenious idea to take revenge on Zehan.
