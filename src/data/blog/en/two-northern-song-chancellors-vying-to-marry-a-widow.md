If there is a product, there is a price. The price at which a product is traded on the market is a monetary expression of the value of the commodity. It reflects the state of supply and demand, and can be viewed as a socioeconomic barometer. Studying prices in the Song Dynasty is useful for understanding the extent of commercialization, commodity production and consumption conditions in the Song Dynasty, and for understanding social and economic life, and the living standards of families in different classes. Since past research focused on objective investigation ①, this article focused on examining the specific prices of specific products, and also provided a reference for price comparison for other historical research.
Prices have obvious temporal and spatial characteristics. There is a big difference between the same product at different times and places, and this must be accurately reflected based on historical materials. At the same time, it is also necessary to eliminate the interference of extreme situations, such as wars, disasters, etc., and certain prices in individual special locations, which have no universal significance. Products from the Song Dynasty are almost all inclusive, and it is difficult to list them all; only one can examine the main products and labor prices. The Song people used a mixture of the equivalent terms of span, thousand, and. This article uniformly defines continuity in the discussion.
1. Land

At the same time, social factors such as local population density and class relationships also play an important role.
