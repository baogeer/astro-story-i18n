 There is a Wujiapo near the Little Wild Wild Goose Pagoda in the south of Xi'an. There is a dilapidated cave with the title “Guhan Kiln” on the edge of the cave. Legend has it that Wang Baochuan kept the cold kiln for 18 years and waited for her husband Xue Pinggui to return. This is where it happened. There is also a temple in front of the window. Statues of Wang Baochuan and Xue Pinggui are enshrined in the temple, and there is a couplet on the pillar:
There was no wave in Furui in the 18th year; in order to be born again from the martyr woman Sadahara;
A cold kiln that is over a thousand years old to the sun. Seeing the water flowing from the Qujiang River here, I want to see Bing Xin.
Wang Bao Chumbon is the daughter of Wang Yun, the prime minister of the DPRK during Tang Yizong's reign. Wang Yun doesn't have any sons; she has only three eldest daughter, Bao Jin, and Su Long, the servant of Xu Paibing, as his wife; the second daughter, Bao Yin, is also married to Jiumen Admiral Wei Hu; the third daughter is Bao Chuan. Of the three sisters, she is the most talented. Since both sisters are well-married, of course, the parents also want to find a happy son-in-law for their little daughter in the middle of their love.
Miss Three, Bao Chuan, seemed to be more picky than her parents. Many powerful nobles who came to raise their family were firmly rejected by her; everyone else thought that Shofu Qianjin was proud. In fact, Bao Chuan has her own set of criteria for choosing a husband. She doesn't admire the powerful, the other is not greedy for fame; she only wants to marry a talented and virtuous Ruyilang. Helplessly, behind those giants, it was either a playboy or a wine bag, how could they make her look good?
At that time, the area south of Chang'an City was surrounded by mountains and water, and the scenery was beautiful. Every time spring was warm and green, and the willows were dark and the flowers were bright, the royal family in Chang'an, Beijing, and the poor people loved to enjoy the flowers and spring. In the spring of this year, Wang Baochuan also brought a few girls on a green outing in the southern suburbs. Unexpectedly, he was followed and entangled by a group of merry boys of unknown origin. He hated it but couldn't get rid of it. At this point, a young scholar dressed in old clothes next to him couldn't see it; he boldly stepped forward to stop this group of people's disrespectful behavior. This group of boys in golden clothes didn't pay attention to this student at all. They shouted at him with seven hands and eight feet, and scolded, “Where did the wild boys come from, take care of the grandparents here!” The scholar was fearless and said, “If you see someone shoveling on the road and molesting a woman from a good family in broad daylight, how could this be a gift?” Of course, the boys in the golden dress were angry. I thought to myself that the kid you had eaten leopard gall, so they swarmed up and punched the scholar.
Wang Baochuan was on the sidelines worried about this loyal scholar. He didn't want the scholar to just sit on the stand and gently, knock the boy in the golden suit that he didn't like in seven or eight. He knew he wasn't the opponent of the scholar, and walked away with scolding and grin.
Bao Chuan secretly admired the scholar's hard work and courage. Seeing that group of merry boys walk away, they hurriedly stepped forward to thank them. The scholar was a bit shy and even said, “Reasonably so, the young lady doesn't have to be polite!”
The more polite the scholar was, the more Wang Baochuan admired him and thanked him. As soon as they went back and forth, the two became familiar. This scholar only said that his name was Xue Pinggui. Both parents died and his family fell into disrepair. He was the only one left, but he refused to tell the details of his family history. According to Wang Baochuan, not only is this scholar good at martial arts, but he is also well-educated. Although he is shabby, he can't hide his exuberance, and he can't help but fall in love with him. As a result, the two toured together. Along the way, the birds and flowers were fragrant, and the spring spirit invaded people. A warm feeling reverberated between them. Xue Pinggui learned that the young lady in front of her was Xiang Guo Qianjin. Not only was she beautiful, but she was also very elegant, unpretentious, and not arrogant in her speech and demeanor. It really fascinated him, but she was too unfit.
Unbeknownst to me, the two spent the afternoon together. Their words were very close. They were able to read a bit of love for each other. Because the mistress was with them, it was not convenient to say anything more deeply. When they broke up at Higurashi, their eyes were full of affection and reluctance.
Back home, Wang Baochuan was afraid to tell her parents about meeting good people during the Spring Festival trip. She knew that her parents wouldn't agree to give her permission to a disillusioned scholar with no merit; she had to spend her days feeling sad. Soon, the old father also urged the three daughters to quickly get married so as not to become old girls. Wang Baochuan made a clever move and proposed a way to decide a lifelong event by throwing hydrangeas. Bao Chuan thought that as soon as the news that he was throwing a ball to get married comes out, his lover Xue Pinggui will definitely come to participate. At that time, it will all be up to him to decide which side the hydrangea falls on. However, Father Wang saw that the persistent and capricious three daughters were getting older, and he was very anxious. Since she proposed throwing hydrangeas herself, there is an example of this in ancient times. Furthermore, it is also a sight to see the prince Wang and Sun scramble to gather in front of their house, so he agreed. It was just a secret decision that the courtyard gate should be tightened and only let in some boys with status. The rule of throwing a ball is “middle chicken marries chicken, middle dog marries dog,” but those poor kids can't be allowed to pick up the bargain.
As a result, they set up a tall lantern in the Wangjiayuan, and arranged for the Three Little Ladies to throw hydrangeas to choose a son-in-law on a lucky day. Minister Wang repeatedly invited the children of nobles from Beijing to participate. After the news broke, famous men from near and far rushed to the royal family, because everyone had already heard about the talents of Miss Wang's three, and they are also worth a fortune. If hydrangeas were lucky enough to hit themselves in the head, wouldn't they be happy to fall from the sky? So everyone wants to try their luck.
Of course, the Wang family's courtyard doors are strictly controlled; people with no head or face will never be allowed to enter. So why can't Xue Pinggui, who has no money or position, get in? Don't worry, the smart Bao Chuan already had plans. She had her close girl from her last spring trip and met Xue Gongzi's side to quietly search for Xue Pinggui outside the hospital, and asked her to take Xue Pinggui into the hospital through the side door.
The right time has arrived. After a round of gongs and drums, the drapes on the roof were gently pulled up, and a group of women surrounded by a young lady who looked like a flower, jade, and was dressed beautifully to show up. The young lady was holding a multicolored hydrangea on her hand. The boys, dressed in red and purple, in the downstairs courtyard were full of excitement. They all stretched their necks and looked forward to the good fortune of heaven falling on their heads. Wang Baochuan's powder noodles smiled on it. He seemed to have a strong chest, his jade wrist turned over, and the hydrangea had fallen on its head, unbiased, and was hitting the head of Xue Pinggui, the cloth boy in the corner of the courtyard, just like the song “Wang Sun Gong Zi 10 Million, Xue Pinglang in the Color Ball Single.”
Wang Yun took a close look. Her son-in-law, who was thrown at the hydrangea, was actually a ruined teenager. She immediately became angry and made up her mind to repent of her marriage. After returning to the house, Wang Yun resolutely said to Bao Chuan, who had just gone down to the cailou, “For my father, I don't agree to this marriage! The matter will be discussed again at a later date.” Bao Chuan, who was originally happy in her heart, was taken aback as soon as she heard her father's words. Soon, she understood that her father must have seen Xue Pinggui's poverty and did not hesitate to break the contract and repent of the marriage. At this point, Bao Chuan's mind was set. She decided not to rely on her father to fiddle with her lifelong events at will, and try to make a rational argument: “If you throw the ball and settle the marriage, then the chicken marries the chicken, and the dog marries the dog.” How can her father disregard his belief and backfire?”
The father and daughter got into a fight, and no one was able to convince anyone. In the end, Wang Baochuan insisted on marrying his sweetheart, Xue Pinggui, and Wang Yun broke off the father-daughter relationship with her in anger. After becoming Xue Pinggui's wife, she had to follow Xue Pinggui. At this point, Xue Pinggui had no place to live. She usually stayed at the home of a relative or friend. Now that she has a wife, they always have their own nest, so the two move into an old cave on Wujiapo. In the cold kiln, the couple lived a bitter life, a man and a woodcutter. Fortunately, the couple respected and loved each other and depended on each other, and had a very pleasant life. Although Wang Baochuan's father broke off his relationship with her, her mother, who is not far away, was unable to let go of this adorable little girl. From time to time, she sent people to visit them and send them money and goods to maintain their lives.
In the 9th year of Xiantong, a rebellion broke out in Xuzu in the border district of Guizhou. They gathered crowds as bandits, captured a major border town, and advanced north. The imperial court sent Kang Sheng Xun to lead the attack. In order to strengthen their military strength, they also ordered the Shatuo army to join the army to help the battle.
Shatuo was originally a nomadic tribe in the northwest border area of Datang. Due to the failure of the war with Tuban, the chiefs led the remnants to the Tang dynasty, and Tang Ting placed them in the Dingxiang area. After receiving the dispatch order, the Shatuo army first rushed to Chang'an and was ready to go to Guizhou at any time. Xue Pinggui, who has both martial arts and martial arts, saw the opportunity and decided that the time had come to build a meritorious career, so when the army gathered in Chang'an, Xue Pinggui joined the army in Shatuo.
