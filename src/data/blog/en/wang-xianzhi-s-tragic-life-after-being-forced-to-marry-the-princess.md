 Wang Xianzhi was the youngest son of Shusheng Wang Xizhi. He was famous for his calligraphy and cursive calligraphy. He was as famous as his father. Later generations also called them the “Second King.”
He is extremely talented, uninhibited, and graceful for a moment. In addition, they are handsome and pay attention to style decoration, and have the refined lifestyle attitude of the children of the family. It's no surprise that someone like this is secretly in love with Princess Xin'an.
Princess Sima Daofu of Xin'an is the daughter of Emperor Jin Jianwen. She has loved Wang Xian for a long time. Unfortunately, she is already married to Hengwen's son, Hang Ji. Later, when Heng Ji wanted to usurp his military power, Princess Xinan divorced him.
The princess, who is still young and beautiful, finally has the freedom to pursue happiness.
Originally, this was supposed to be a good relationship. However, Wang Xianzhi is already married to his wife Lu Daomao, and the couple are very close. Lu Daomao is the daughter of a famous family, and Wang Xizhi is the eastern son-in-law of the Lu family.
Lu Daomao is elegant, quiet, and talented. She is a woman with a very interesting lifestyle. She is also Wang Xianzhi's cousin. The two have known each other since childhood and are young. When they grew up, their families arranged marriages for them. Wang Xianzhi is not very passionate about her career. In addition, her father has set up a large number of farm estates in the mountains and forests, and her life is quite rich. After being blessed with a wise wife, she was even more indifferent to fame and fortune. She only wanted to spend her days in silence in the landscape and concentrate on her calligraphy.
According to Princess Xin'an, although Lu Daomao was married in the Ming Dynasty, she never left any children to Wang Xianzhi; she only gave birth to a daughter; she also died. If a woman has no children, she can be abandoned. Wang Xian must be happy to have married him in the honorable status of Jin Zhi Yu Ye. As a result, she painstakingly asked the Empress Dowager.
The Eastern Jin royal family was also quite appreciative of Wang Xianzhi's personality, and the Empress Dowager took the initiative to have Emperor Xiaowu order Wang Xianzhi as an outlaw.
Obviously, Princess Xin'an underestimated Wang Xianzhi's love for his wife.
It was a quiet day. Wang Xianzhi's sacrament to take a break from his wife and get married again shattered the peace of the Wang family. The young couple are affectionate and affectionate. They have agreed to take care of their children for a long time, and have never thought that they will be separated in this life. But this time, they didn't encounter a contract of life or death; they met the imperial power, an irresistible imperial power.
Wang Xianzhi watched his wife shed tears in silence and walked into the study room alone. It was bound to be a sleepless night.
What surprised everyone was that Wang Xianzhi walked out of the study room early the next morning and became lame. He couldn't bear to leave his wife, and he couldn't think of any way to resist. In desperation, I actually burned my feet with wormwood, leaving me disabled for life.
Holding her husband, Lu Daomao shed tears like rain. She felt every hint of her husband's pain. She can't stand it.
On weekdays, I need a husband who keeps his hair tidy at all. How deeply do I feel for her before I can hurt myself so hard?
Ai's fire was burning, yet his heart was so calm. If you can keep your loved one by your side, what kind of pain is this? He held her back and said, “Don't cry, we don't have to be separated. I'm a disabled person now, how should I be a horse?”
Obviously, Wang Xianzhi also underestimated Princess Xin'an's affection for him. She easily said she didn't care; even if he was lame, she would get married.
Wang Xianzhi was completely desperate.
Lu Daomao walked out, carrying a burden in his hands. He said softly, “Donate it, I'm gone, I won't let you hurt yourself any more.”
“No need!” If you don't have her in your life from now on, what's the point of being alive? “Let's meet the princess together, why would she split us up for life? Even if you lose your life in the Golden Temple, what are you afraid of?”
Lu Daomao held him: “No, donate it. It's not just about the lives and deaths of the two of us. If this infuriates the emperor, it will bring disaster to the whole family.”
For people with this kind of affectionate and righteous temperament, abandoning life for love is not difficult. But his life isn't just his own. He has too many responsibilities and too many burdens.
She left by boat, and all he could do was just across the river. At this point, her mother's family had split up, so she had to go to her uncle. As Wang Xianzhi watched her back gradually blur, she was so sad that she couldn't help herself. It's just like life or death. She's lost her father, lost her daughter, and now she doesn't have a husband. Since then, she has been left alone under a fence. How did she live this life?
Not long after Lu Daomao was suspended, he ended up depressed. Hearing this news, Wang Xian's heart was cut like a knife. He was tormented by grief and guilt all his life, and he never forgave himself until his death.
The person responsible for this tragedy is the person who is now his wife, Princess Xin'an. He is full of resentment against her.
As you can imagine, Princess Xin'an went to great lengths to grab the marriage, and she was not happy. He didn't have the slightest affection for her, and his ostensible courtesy revealed a hint of chill. But after all, he is someone she adores and admires. As his wife, you can see him every day, and she is also content.
Later, Wang Xianzhi had another concubine, Momoye, and spent the whole day being in love with her. It seemed that she was still afraid that people in the world wouldn't know, and often showed love to her by singing poems and lyrics.
Every time Momoye went out, Wang Xianzhi had to personally greet her at the ferry crossing, and also wrote a poem for her. Today, Nanjing still has the “Ancient Peach Leaf Ferry” stone monument, which is a testament to their love through the ages:
Peach leaves are mixed with peach leaves, and peach trees have peach roots. Being kind to each other makes me happy.
Peach leaves turn peach leaves, so there's no need to cross the river. But there were no hardships; I welcomed you myself.
Momoha also wrote poetry and answers, and since then she has sweetly called him “Danseiro.”
Princess Xin'an understood that this was his silent resistance. Her heart was full of bitterness, but since she made this glass of bitter wine herself, she could only resist drinking it. The once proud princess was submissive and tolerant because of her deep love. She didn't say a word of complaint, let alone go back to her mother's house to complain. She has always used her strength to protect her husband, and after his marriage, he went straight to the head and ordered the Chinese government. She hopes to one day inspire her husband and truly win his heart.
Momoye is an intelligent and versatile woman, yet she is only a shadow of Lu Daomao; she is also a tool for Wang Xianzhi to express his anger and dissatisfaction to the world. The only thing Wang Xian couldn't let go of in his heart was always Lu Daomao.
