 Since Liu Bang, the ancestor of Han Gao, “the wind rises and the clouds fly,” pioneered the “exchange of women for peace,” a large number of princesses have married far and wide. Since then, the rulers of the Han Dynasty have loved peace infinitely. Of course, the sacrifices offered on the altar of peace were innocent women. Wang Zhaojun, the second of the Big Four Beauties, was dedicated under these circumstances, forming a famous sacrifice.
At the time of Emperor Han Yuan, the king of the Huns called Han Xie Dan to seek beauty. Emperor Yuan was very pompous. All of a sudden, they sold five to each other. The foreman was Wang Zhaojun, who was outraged and asked for action. The helpless Wang Zhaojun went abroad in tears: looking at the Han Palace in the south made him even more sad... Wang Zhaojun was portrayed as a patriotic hero by later generations of writers, yet I don't know what kind of shame and humiliation is buried here! What goes down in the annals of history with the woman who died is the cowardice and disgrace of the Dahan man!
As we all know, the famous Battle of Troy was only started by Greek men to take back Helen, a woman who had been taken from her country, and it lasted ten years. In Western culture, protecting women of one's own nation is everything, that is, the whole reason for war. This reason is unique and sufficient, because behind women of their own nation is the dignity of men from all over the country. In contrast, behind the girl Zhaojun, there is anger, trade, and the collective cowardice of the male community!
