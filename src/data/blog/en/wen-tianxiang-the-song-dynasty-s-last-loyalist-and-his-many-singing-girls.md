The fall of the Southern Song dynasty dealt a huge blow to Wen Tianxiang. It was like an architect watching the building he had put his whole life and effort into collapse in an instant. For a person with ideals and discipline, the meaning and value of his life is the decisive reason for the existence of his life. Also, because the Yuanjun caretakers adopted a method of forcibly feeding, Wen Tianxiang was still unable to die after not eating for 8 days. What gave the escort a sigh of relief was that starting on the 9th day, Wen Tianxiang actually resumed eating on his own. Naturally, people's understanding of this transformation is not that Wen Tianxiang was unable to bear the pain of trying to die. Since the pre-Qin period, the life value orientation of the Chinese warrior class has basically revolved around the three levels of establishing morality, merit, and asserting opinions.
