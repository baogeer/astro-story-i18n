 Wen Zhiming, known as Heng Shan, was born in the 6th year of Ming Xianzong Chenghua (1470), a native of Changzhou (present-day Suzhou, Jiangsu). He is famous for his poetry and paintings. He is a representative figure in the field of literature and art in the middle of the Ming Dynasty. As a famous writer, calligrapher and painter of the Ariming generation, he is liberal, sophisticated, and highly respected. He was the leader of Wu Zhongzhan for more than 30 years. He graduated in Jiaqing in the 38th year (1559). He died at the age of 90. He was old and tireless in writing and writing, and left many works passed down from generation to generation. However, in works of opera literature and art, most of his images are hidden behind famous Wu Zhongcai sons Tang Bohu and Zhu Zhishan. There have always been doubts about his life history. There is a Mr. Zhou Daozhen in Wuxi. Since he studied Wen Zhiming's calligraphy at a young age, he has accumulated over 60 years of success. He has published academic monographs such as “The Ming Collection of Wen Zhi”, “Tang Yin Collection”, “The Chronicles of Wen Zhi Next Year”, and “An Examination of Tang Zhi's Next Year”, and “An Examination of the Tang Dynasty”. Therefore, “The Biography of Wen Zhi and Ming” written by Mr. Zhou can be called a “little book for everyone” in the field of character biography.
There are many praiseworthy stories in Wen Zhiming's life, but after reading “The Biography of Wen Zhi Ming,” what is profoundly impressive is the pain and joy of Mr. Heng Shan.
From time to time, people ridicule Wen Zhiming as a “talented person.” This is not only a fact, but also an aspect of Wen Zhiming's bitter life. Wen Zhiming lost her mother at age 7 and was raised with her grandmother in China and abroad. After the death of her 30-year-old father Wen Lin, she followed the arrangement of her stepmother Wu and settled down with her siblings. Life was difficult for a while, to the point where he was only able to make a living by borrowing money from his friend Chen Yao (in the poem “Sending Chen to Kill Rice”). When he reached the age of 42, he saw that Zhi Ming was still poor in his old clothes, and he also planned to take the opportunity to fund him. According to research by Mr. Zhou Daozhen, Wen Lin has three children and one daughter. However, the brothers Wen Zhiming did not have the same sincere feelings as the brothers Su Yi and Su Ze. Zhi Ming kept his brother's way and didn't compare things with it; for the most part, they just barely maintained it. While on his career path, although his father and uncle were all from undergraduate backgrounds, Wen Zhiming was stuck in academic examinations for 30 years. He failed all eight rural exams, and it was just his whole life. He was later recommended by Lee Yun-si, the senior secretary of the Ministry of Engineering, and was secretly mocked by his colleagues for “academic issues” after taking office in Beijing. It also coincided with the period of political turmoil caused by Emperor Jiajing's “Great Courtesy”. Wen Zhiming became more and more depressed and homesick, and wrote the poem “Empathize only that government is tasteless, and it is difficult to know about retreat.” Fortunately, no one remembers him as a “doctor of calligraphy and painting.” At the end of his three-year term, he went to Beijing and had no nostalgia. After returning home, 58-year-old Wen Daizhao was already famous all over the world. He “leisurely traveled to Lin Quan, and had no financial worries.” (Wen Jia's “The First Master's Guide”) However, at this point, many of Wen Zhiming's teachers and admired seniors, such as Wang Yi, had gone out of style and friends, such as Zhu Yunming and Tang Yin, who had lived in Beijing in the past few years. The “Ten Friends of Dongzhuang” were incomplete, and “Wu Zhong Si Zi” There is only one person left. The lonely Wen Zhiming became an “idle person” who hosted “Wu Zhong Fengya” for more than 30 years.
However, compared to his younger generation, Jiangnan talent Xu Wei, Wen Zhiming was also much luckier. Zhiming married Wu Yu's third daughter, Wu Shi, when she was 23 years old. They loved each other throughout their lives, and Mrs. Wu handled all the household chores of the Wenjia family. Wen Zhiming also had great respect for this woman who brought him the warmth of his long-lost family. He did not accept concubines or sex prostitutes, and left behind beautiful talk about “a life is different.” This was very rare in the social environment at the time. In the letterhead that has been preserved until now, Wen Zhiming reported all the people and events he met during his trip to his wife one by one, and also told him, “I don't need to keep track of household chores and cook for yourself. However, it is necessary to save gas and effort, take medication diligently, and put rest first.” At the end, the inscription “Zhiming wrote a book in Huai'an. Three young ladies read it.” (“To My Wife, Mrs. Wu”, “The Collection of Wen Zhi Ming (Expanded Edition)”), I covered up my face and laughed.
Wen Zhiming's development was slow when he was young, and he spoke late (if he wasn't smart). It's hard to say that it had nothing to do with being demoralized at a young age. Fortunately, his father, Wen Lin, was concerned about his further education and sought out three famous people from his hometown as teachers. Zhi Ming studied with Wu Kuan. Wu Kuan was the champion in the 8th year of Chenghua (1472). He was also a loyal elder. He admired Zhi Ming's talent and diligence, and often recommended him among the Gong Qing. Wen Zhiming was “less than a book, so he deliberately went back to learning”, so he worshipped Zhu Yunming's father-in-law, Li Yingzhen, to improve his calligraphy. Li Yingzhen was famous for his seriousness (suffering from sexual hardship) and “being careful with his disciples.” Li Yingzhen taught him brushwork one by one. Later, his son-in-law, Zhu Yunming, and his disciple Wen Zhiming all achieved great calligraphy achievements out of blue. Zhiming also studied painting with Shen Zhou of the “Ming Family”. Shen Zhou grew up with Dan Qing. He was deeply cultivated in poetry, literature, and books. This was the longest relationship between mentors and apprentices, and the deepest feelings. Until his old age, Wen Zhiming still missed his Wu Shi, Li Shi, and Shen Shi. He had good relationships with their descendants for generations and time. In addition to these three academic mentors, as well as the writer's old friends, were all invited for advice, making him more and more sophisticated and liberal. Wen Zhiming's relationships with these noble people made him pursue Confucian principles even more. He was not involved in a “cynical” or “cynical” attitude, and avoided many disasters. Compared to his friend Tang Yin, he withstood the temptations of Ning Wang Zhu Chenhao and graciously refused to give the power minister Yan Song a good fortune when he was old. These stories passed down from generation to generation were all extremely farsighted.
Suzhou, where commercial activity flourished in the middle of the Ming Dynasty, encouraged Jiangnan gentlemen to pursue spiritual enjoyment such as culture and art. Mr. Heng Shan was admired by the local people for his elegant style of work and gentle and honest personality, and all the squatters from all over made friends with wealthy businessmen. After his retirement, Wen Zhiming was active in the lower reaches of the Yangtze River as a central figure in Suzhou Wenyuan. “As a result, petitioners from all sides came, and Gong also responded, and he never got tired of it.” In the more than 30 years after his “retirement,” he followed suit, wrote books and posts, promoted the cultural prosperity of this rich land, and formed the peak period of the Wu Men painting school with Wen Zhiming and his children at the core. According to “Ming Shi,” “the people of Wu Men from Siyi Gongdao worshipped Mr. Wang and hated not being able to see Mr.”. There was an endless flow of people who bought Zhi Ming's ink treasures from his family and friends at a high price, so many of Wen Zhiming's paintings and calligraphy were distributed overseas. In 2020, at the Tokyo National Museum and Calligraphy Museum, there was a special group exhibition called “Wen Zhiming and His Era” commemorating the 550th anniversary of the birth of Wenzhiming. It can be said that the Wenzhiming Special Exhibition was very important in hosting the 2019 Yan Zhenqing Special Exhibition.
This story was recorded in “The Biography of Wen Zhi Ming”, and it was possible to get a glimpse of Wen Zhiming's popularity in Gusu. During the Jiajing years, there was a young man known as “Wen Jiayi, with characters like Zhong and Wang”. He won the championship but did not plan to become an official. He wanted to worship Wen Zhiming from his hometown as his mentor. Some people advised him that you had already won an official position; why did you still worship his teacher? Don't you know that some people now just call themselves the calligraphy and painting of Wenzhiming? The young man said, “You're wrong. Calligraphy and painting are just aspects that are easy for everyone to see. The moral knowledge is worth studying hard for me.” (When Reiburou resigned and invited him to step down, his friend ridiculed him, and Lu Shi Zheng said, “Mr. Fumi also uses art to learn.”) This young man, named Lu Shidao, later became the most famous disciple of the Wenzhu Ming Dynasty.
There are also many famous people who have dealt with Wen Zhiming as a reward. The author selects the stories of their dealings and details their lives, which is extremely valuable as a document. There are still some anecdotes that have been revealed that are mixed with truth or falsehood, or are transcribed from other people's old works, which are difficult to decipher. The author records them one by one for reference.
The author, Mr. Zhou Daozhen, did his best in search and research, and gathered his arms to explore the life trajectories of the amazing calligraphers and painters in Suzhou in the middle of the Ming Dynasty, and collect the calligraphy and painting books handed down from them. He enjoyed it without fear of hard work. Fragments of history hidden under the famous name of Wenzhu have been unearthed from countless relevant people and materials, so that true fragments of silence in the long course of history have been revived and recreated one by one. It opens the key for readers to explore the world of Wenzhiming's calligraphy and painting in a structured way. The individuality and value of people, their calligraphy, and their paintings”.
