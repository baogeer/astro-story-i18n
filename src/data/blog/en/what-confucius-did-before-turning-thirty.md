 September 28 is the birthday of Confucius, which is currently recognized by mainstream opinion in society, and many places will host a series of praying ceremonies for Confucius on this day. Saints have a lifetime of service. As Zhu Xi of Southern Song said, “Heaven never gives birth to China and Nepal; all ages last like night.”
Confucius wasn't born; he was the bearded old man that was handed down from generation to generation; he also came from his youth. At that time, although he didn't have the thrill of taking charge of Lu Guo's criminal administration and attacking the powerful “Three Huan” forces after middle age, he had no hardships and hardships to travel around the world and promote political ideas, let alone revise the Six Scriptures and establish a Chinese cultural system, but his homeland feelings for the country and the people had already taken root, and the responsibility and mission of doing what was impossible was already on his shoulders.
Unhappy native families
According to some current ideas, Confucius' native family is a problem; it is the type of person who runs high and low. His ancestors were originally part of King Song's family, but later his family moved to Lu Guo. Although his father, Uncle Liang, was a fallen aristocrat, he was famous among the princes; his mother, Yan Zhizhai, was the second wife of Uncle Liang Yi, over 40 years younger than her uncle Liang Yi; her family is the family name of Qufu, so the Confucius family should belong to the local upper class.
Before Confucius, Uncle Liang and his former wife Shi Shi had 9 daughters, and had a son, Meng Pi, who had foot problems with his concubine, and none of them were able to inherit Incense. Confucius is an old son of Uncle Liang Yi in his 60s. He is the hope of the whole family. He should have had a happy childhood that is regarded as a pearl in the palm of one's hand.
Unfortunately, when Confucius was 3 years old, his father died. Her mother, Yan Zhi, left her hometown in the countryside with 3-year-old Confucius and moved to live in Qeli in Qufu, the capital of Lu Guo, in order to avoid complicated family conflicts with the Shi clan.
Although it is a single-parent family, her mother did her best to create a good environment for Confucius as a child to grow up, and taught according to Confucius' natural talents. According to Sima Qian's “History: The Confucius Family,” Confucius had a unique childhood hobby. “Play with children, and set up manners”. He didn't like to play like the average child. Instead, he fiddled with rituals such as square and round zudou used to place sacrifices during sacrifices and banquets, placed them according to the etiquette system, and then practiced bowing his head.
In the political culture of the time, understanding manners was an important sign of distinguishing social classes. According to Zheng Huan from the Qing dynasty, Yan Zhi observed his son's interest and talent in studying etiquette. He used the money he saved to buy standard ceremonial instruments for Confucius, so he could learn the etiquette system, making it a step for him to escape the declining family status and return to the aristocracy in the future.
Being humiliated in public by powerful people
After Confucius was dependent on his mother for more than 10 years, an even greater blow came. When Confucius was sixteen and seven, his mother Yan Zhi passed away in her prime in her 30s due to excessive labor. Confucius treated his mother's afterlife according to strict etiquette and buried his parents together in Fangshan in eastern Qufu, winning praise from those around him.
The man who loved Confucius the most has left and will never come back. At the age of 16, he wants to study and earn a living independently. Soon, life taught him a tough lesson.
According to “Historical Records: The Confucius Family,” soon after the death of Confucius's mother, Ji Sun, the most powerful aristocrat in Lu Guo, held a banquet for “feast” and invited all the nobility “Shi” and above to attend the banquet. Although Confucius was still “demanding” Dai Xiao for his mother at the time, he was happy to “go with it” considering that he was the son of Liang Yi, a samurai uncle famous among the princes, who was eligible to attend this Lu Guo aristocracy gathering, and that he might revive the Kong family's voice at the party.
Unexpectedly, before seeing Ji Sun, Confucius was stopped by Ji's vassal Yang Hu at the entrance of Ji's family. Yang Huyi pointed out his courage and was extremely arrogantly scolded Confucius, “You don't dare to rejoice”. The Ji family is hosting a feast of aristocrats and celebrities today; they are not inviting you to Confucius. Yang Hu's remarks are tantamount to the Ji family expelling Confucius from the ranks of the nobility. Confucius was not recognized even as a lowest-class “taxi”; he “retired” and left the Ji family.
Yang Hu's arrogance did not kill Confucius; on the contrary, he made him stronger. The mission to revive the family is calling ahead, and what is most important right now is to earn a living.
Work hard in an ordinary position
According to “The Analects of Confucius: Zihan,” after Confucius became famous, Dazai, who was in charge of the king's court affairs, once said to Zigong, a disciple of Confucius, “Fu Zi Sheng and? “How capable”, Confucius is simply a saint, why can he master so many skills! Zigong, a divinified teacher, answered, “Gu Tianzong is holy and capable.” These are all things that God arranged for Confucius to become a saint, so that he has extraordinary skills.
Confucius heard Wen Zigong's answer and wasn't convinced. Instead of deifying himself, he revealed the truth, “Does Dazai know me? “I am young and weak, so I can despise many things”. This was all forced by life back then, and every skill was a painful yet inspirational past.
According to “Historical Records: The Confucius Family” and “Under Mencius: Ten Thousand Chapters,” even though Confucius, who was “poor and poor” back then, was humiliated by the Ji family, he had to bear the burden of humiliation and bow down to the Ji family. After all, the Ji family almost monopolized most of Lu Guo's industries. Confucius once worked as a “commissioner” for Ji. His responsibilities were to manage warehouses, increase material volume, and account and cashier. Confucius clearly calculated and registered all accounts of income and expenditure, and “accounting is enough.” Confucius later became an “official clerk,” or “riding field,” managing cattle and sheep animal husbandry affairs. Confucius raised and cooked carefully in the morning and evening. “It's enough for cows and sheep to thrive”, and fed the cows and sheep to be fat and fit.
Confucius knows that managing warehouses or herding cattle and sheep can only solve the most basic survival problems at the moment. He also has to master more skills and “open up more skill trees” in order to further develop.
In the aristocracy education system and political and social etiquette at the time, taxis were required to possess the six “six arts” of improvisation (etiquette), music (music), shooting (archery), defense (driving), books (calligraphy), and numbers (calculation). Confucius had already laid the foundation for etiquette when he was a child. He also developed the art of “numbers” when managing warehouses, and later mastered the other four arts one after another through self-study and asking for advice from others.
Although he knows all the “six arts,” Confucius is quite humble. According to “The Analects of Confucius: Zihan,” after Confucius became famous all over the world, a person in a place called Daxiang Dang heard that he was good at all “Six Arts” and praised “Great Confucius! “Knowledgeable and not famous”. Confucius is an erudite generalist, and the title of an expert cannot be used to limit his talents. When Confucius heard it, he said to his disciples, “What is my obsession? Persistent? Shooting huh? “I'm in control”. As a teacher, who has any skills, can you shoot arrows, can you drive a car, you just know a little bit about driving skills.
Be a gentleman, don't go with the flow
No matter how humble and low-key later Confucius was, his talents during his youth have gradually become known. Such a talented person naturally needs to be in the company of a beautiful person. According to “The Family Language of Confucius,” Confucius married Song Qiguan as his wife when he was 19 years old. The wife's family, like the Confucian family, all moved from Song Guo to Lu Guo in their early years, so the Song Qi Guan clan.
At the time, Confucius was already famous for his talents, and the country's monarch heard it. A year after Confucius married, around 20 years old, his wife bore him a son. Lu Guojun heard that Confucius was pleased with Takako and sent someone to send a carp to express his congratulations. Confucius then named his son a list with the characters Koi and Boyu to thank the king for his generous rewards.
The sovereign value is important, and the Chinese people value it. At a time when Confucius had a great future, he chose a path that few people took.
According to “Zuo Chuan,” in the 17th year of Lu Zhaogong (525 BC), that is, in the fall of Confucius's age 27, Juanzi, a small country attached to Lu Guo, came to see Lu Guo's monarch in the morning. Dr. Lu Guo Shouzi asked Juanzi about the government system during the time of the ancient Shao Hao clan. Tanzi answered in detail the ins and outs. Confucius “heard it” and immediately “learned by seeing Juanzi” to learn from him the history of changes in the ancient civil service system. After completing his studies, Confucius said to people, “I heard that 'Tianzi is out of office; I still believe it. '” I often hear people say that the royal power has declined, and the officials responsible for culture and academia are scattered all over the world; I didn't believe it before; today I saw it with my own eyes and verified it with my own ears.
Seeing the declining culture and academic decline, Confucius is determined to reverse this decline. At the age of 15, he was “interested in learning,” but what he learned before that was only to seek the survival of his own family. As he later said to his student Zi Xia, he was for a “little man.” Studying the “Six Arts” was only a way to earn a living. After that, Confucius wanted to become a “gentleman”, elevating his study of the specific techniques of the “Six Arts” to exploring its origins, strengths and weaknesses, and meaning, in order to transcend the times and connect with the present. He learned the qin from Lu Guole's teacher, and learned about Zhou Wenwang's personality and feelings for helping the world from the song “Wen Wang Cao”. Confucius' academic purpose was no longer “as for the Valley,” that is, to seek the good fortune of a senior official and revive the family's voice, but rather to follow the path of understanding and the way of life, his mission, and the temples of rivers and lakes.
Confucius began to study all kinds of rules and regulations with hunger and thirst, and did not miss any opportunity to ask for advice. According to “The Analects of Confucius,” Confucius went to the temple where Lu Guo sacrificed his ancestor Zhou Gong and “asked everything”; he had to ask clearly and clearly about everything. Some people sarcastically said, “Who is Zhiri, the son of a traitor? “When you enter Taimiao, ask everything,” they all say “a scoundrel,” that is, Confucius, the son of his uncle Liang, is an authority on the etiquette system, but he is required to ask others about everything after entering Taimiao.
When Confucius heard it, he didn't agree. He said, “It's polite,” and only asking if you don't understand something is a “courtesy.” Only then did my approach fit the academic attitude of “knowing is knowing, ignorant.” Of course, there are scholars such as Qian Mu who think that this conversation has another meaning. Confucius used an interrogative tone to satirize that many of the sacrifices at Taimiao of Lu Guo did not meet true manners, and used deliberate methods of asking others to teach them to awaken people's attention to all kinds of un“ ceremonial” acts at Taimiao.
Some scholars also believe that when he was young, Confucius also went to Luoyang, where Zhou Tianzi is located, to study Zhou Li and ancient literature. Prince Lu Guo gave Confucius a child servant, a car, and two horses to finance his journey to Luo. Confucius also met Laozi in Luoyang to discuss academics. Of course, the exact age at which Confucius went to Luoyang and whether he actually met Laozi is also debated in academia.
Establish civic education and set a lifelong ambition
In the process of studying, Confucius promoted himself and others, and began to worry about the education of people at the bottom. As a lofty aristocrat “mentor,” he could ask for advice and answers from people such as Tanzi, Shi Xiang, and Laozi. However, in the educational pattern of “studying at the government office” and the aristocrats monopolized academic culture at the time, there were no opportunities for ordinary children to receive education.
Confucius, 30, is determined to change this unfair situation. He began to set up private schools, teach apprenticeships, and widely recruit children from ordinary people with the concept of “there is education and no kind”, breaking the moat built by the declining aristocrats on education issues. As a result, he became the first vocational educator in Chinese history, and was respected as the supreme pioneer.
As stated in “Analects of Confucius,” “I have never tasted ignorance”. As long as you send a bouquet of dried meat as tuition fees, you can join Confucius for education. Confucius, who founded civic education, set a lifelong ambition to teach people how to stand up and cultivate feelings for their homeland. Early disciples such as Zilu, Yan Lu, and Zeng Dian joined one after another. Lu Guo's aristocrat Meng Xizi also sent his two sons, Meng Yizi and Uncle Nangong, to worship Confucius as teachers and learn from him.
There are stars ahead, light in the eyes, benevolence in the heart, and a long road ahead. Confucius, a young man who has stood at 30 and is gradually moving towards the age of 40, is no longer confused. He already has the strength and openness to face all the unknowns, blame, and inexplicable things in the future. He is about to illuminate the long nights of ancient times, such as “like the light of the Sun and the Moon” (Cheng Hao in the Northern Song Dynasty).
