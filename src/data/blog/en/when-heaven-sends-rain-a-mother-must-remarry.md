 A long time ago, there was a scholar named Zhu Yaozong. Her father, who was a little over one year old, passed away due to illness. Her mother feared that Zhu Yaozong would be abused by her stepfather; she did not remarry, causing the burden of life alone.
Chu Yiu-chung has been smart since childhood and loves to read. When the mother saw that her son was a student, she used the money she had saved to invite a teacher named Zhang Wenju to teach her son at home. Zhang Wenju is also a poor scholar, but
He is very knowledgeable, and he is even more meticulous in teaching Zhu Yaozong, and he is very careful. The two teachers and students are well-taught, and the other is smart and studious. As a result, Zhu Yaozong was selected for talent when he was 15, went to Beijing to rush the exam at age 18, and became the top pick in high school.
When Zhu Yiu-chung participated in the palace exam, the emperor saw that he was extremely talented. He showed his talent, and ordered him to be taken as a traitor. After Zhu Yaozong thanked the emperor, he couldn't help but think of his mother who worked so hard to raise him as an adult. As a result, he told the emperor about his difficult life of being dependent on his mother since childhood, how his mother painstakingly raised him alone in order not to be abused by his stepfather, and how she asked teachers to teach him knowledge and nurture him as an adult in an extremely difficult situation. The emperor was very moved after hearing it. He immediately decided to set up a “chastity arch” for the mother of the champion Zhu Yiu-chung, who had been widowed for many years and had never been remarried. Naturally, Zhu Yaozong was very happy to hear it.
According to convention, the winners of the new department have to go back to their hometown and province. When Zhu Yaozong returned home, the mother was so happy that her son had won the top spot. However, when she heard that the emperor had issued a decree to “establish a chastity archway” for her, she clearly showed an uneasy look. It turns out that the wife of Zhu Yaozong's mentor Zhang Wenju unfortunately passed away due to illness a few years ago. The two of them gradually developed a relationship during the days when Zhu Yaozong's family spent time with Zhu Yaozong's mother. Although Zhang Wenju left Zhu Yao's family after Zhu Yaozong entered Beijing to rush the exam, their relationship continued unabated. Recently, Zhu Yaozong's mother was thinking about officially marrying Zhang Wenju. Now that I hear that the emperor wants to set up a “chastity arch” for her, how can she not worry?
Zhu Yaozong was shocked when he heard that his mother wanted to marry his mentor Zhang Wenju. He “fluttered” and knelt in front of his mother and cried and said, “Ma'am, this must not be done. Your son has already told the emperor that you are not going to change your marriage. Now if you do, you are committing a crime of bullying the king. This is a serious crime to punish the nine ethnic groups!” Chu Yiu-chung's mother also felt confused after hearing it. Let's remarry; this is undoubtedly a crime of bullying the monarch; but who knows how many years she spent all these years alone living in an empty house and having a hard time at night? Thinking back and forth, Zhu Yaozong's mother couldn't help but sigh at the sky and reluctantly said, “Let's leave everything to fate.” After all, she took off a dress from her body and handed it to Zhu Yaozong, saying, “It's not easy for your mother to raise you. Tomorrow morning, you should show your mother a filial act. Wash this dress and dry it in the yard. If my dress dries before dark, I won't get married; if my dress doesn't dry, it means God meant it, and you won't have to stop me from getting married again.” Zhu Yiu-chung knew that it was not easy for her mother to raise herself; she had to follow her mother's orders.
The next morning, the sky was clear and the sun was blazing. Zhu Yaozong was secretly happy. He thought that in such good weather, not to mention a dress, even ten or eight pieces can be dried in the sun. I know that after washing his mother's dress and drying it in the yard, the clear sky was suddenly overcast and torrential rain. The dress dried in the yard was soaked by heavy rain, much more wet than after washing and wringing it dry. The heavy rain continued until the middle of the night, and my mother's dress was always like soaking in water. How could I do that? After dark, Zhu Yaozong's mother looked at the pouring rain outside the window and said to Zhu Yaozong, “Oh, if it's going to rain, my mother will get married. God must go against her!” Although Zhu Yaozong's heart was constantly struggling, God meant that he was helpless.
After returning to Beijing, Zhu Yaozong truthfully reported the marriage between his mother and mentor Zhang Wenju, as well as the situation where he washed his mother's dress and torrential rain in the world, and requested the emperor to punish him. After hearing it, the emperor was amazed and said, “It's raining, and my mother wants to get married. This is a match made in heaven. Let him go.”
Since then, people have used the phrase “it's going to rain, my mother wants to get married” to describe those irreversible things.
