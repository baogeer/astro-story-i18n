 Once upon a time, there was an old man with three sons under his lap. When the old man was about to die, he called his three sons and advised them to unite, be friendly, and live in harmony. He also left one of his treasured Ruyi Treasure Swords called “Sangzhu Rizhao” to his eldest son; left a piece of sparkling gold to his second son; and left eleven goats and sheep named “Stuttering Green” to his beloved little son. Then I closed my eyes and left the world.
One day, the three brothers saw a big fish in the river. The boss said, “The root of a fish's life is on its head; you should smash its head!” The second brother said, “No! The root of a fish's life lies in its lower back; you should stab it in the lower back!” The junior said, “You're both wrong! The root of a fish's life is in its tail; its tail should be cut off.” The three brothers quarreled over how to kill this fish, and finally sued Munemoto and asked for judgment.
At night, the boss lay on the mat and couldn't sleep at all. In order to win the lawsuit, he delivered the ancestral Ruyi Sword to Munemoto in the middle of the night and asked Sōben to agree to his claim during the judgment. Munemoto hurriedly nodded and agreed: “Of course!” Similarly, the older brother sent precious gold, and the older third sent rare goats and sheep, and asked Sōben to speak for himself. All of them cheerfully made promises.
After three days, the day of judgment has arrived. When the three brothers walked into the courtroom, they each used ingenious hidden words to remind Zong Ben not to forget his bribes. The boss made a statement and read aloud, “Put the sword in the scabbard, don't be afraid of any enemies. Master Munemoto, the root of a fish's life is only in the head!” When the elder came in, he also said, “The golden rule is underground, and the light shines on the sky. Master Munemoto, the root of a fish's life can only be found in the lower back!” The younger brother meant more clearly: “The sheep escaped deep into the mountains and left footprints in Hirakawa. Mr. Munemoto, if the root of a fish's life isn't in its tail, I won't agree”.
In the end, Sōmoto stood up from the throne and judged very seriously: “What the boss said made sense. I saw a group of spring fish going against the water upstream, all thanks to the strength of their heads. At this point, the root of the life of sea bream should be in the head. Boss, I declare you won!” After speaking, I picked up a poor quality hatta and asked the book to hang around Big Brother's neck. The older brother was so proud that he looked at the two younger brothers like a winning rooster. Munemoto coughed a bit, then said, “The old man said it was good too! I saw winter fish stacked one on top of the other in the fish hole, all resting on their hips. At this point, the root of a fish's life is, if not on the waist, where is it? Dude, you won too!” Based on the misconceptions of the original manuscript, the book also presented the older brother with a poor quality Hatta. At this point, the senior couldn't sit still and was about to have a seizure. Munemoto waved his hand and asked him to calm down and said, “What the senior three said is not unfounded. I saw summer fish jumping into the air one by one, thanks to the power of their tails. At this point, the root of a fish's life is probably on its tail. Senior, I announce that you also won.” As a result, the older third also got a poor quality Hatta.
After the verdict was completed, he announced that he had left the church, and went home to drink and eat meat.
