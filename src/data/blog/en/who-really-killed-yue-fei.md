Nearly a thousand years have passed, and people continue to question the cause of Yue Fei's death. People don't stop thinking about fealty and adultery. The most representative figure is Nan Huaijin, a famous Taiwanese scholar. He suggested that historically, Qin Hui killed Yue Fei. Where did Qin Hui kill him? Song Gaozong originally hated Yue Fei; Qin Hui only complied with Gao Zong's intentions; he only accepted the crime on behalf of Gao Zong.
There are two strongest evidences put forward by Nan Huaijin. One is that Yue Fei's slogan annoyed Song Gaozong; the other is that Yue Fei invited Prince Gao Zongli to make Gao Zong unhappy.
Yue Fei once proposed a famous slogan; “Go straight to Huanglong and welcome back the Second Saints.” Huang Long is Jin Jun's old nest; it's OK to smash it. But the two saints are the emperor's father, Huizong, and the other is the emperor's brother Qin Zong. Which of these two has returned and has no part in the current emperor. How can the current emperor not be annoyed? These two are still too late for Song Gaozong to hide. You and Yue Fei have to bring them back. Aren't Cheng Xin troubled by the current emperor?
The slogan seems to be quite loved by the Chinese people. It is simple and clear, and it is easy to gather people's hearts and work together. For example, defeating Chiang Kai-shek in the early years and liberating all of China, such as in recent years, focusing on economic construction, etc., they are all simple and powerful, and have miraculous effects. But don't mention slogans; if you don't get it right, it will distract people, blur your goals, and even lose your net worth and life.
The second thing Yue Fei did to annoy Song Gaozong was to appoint a prince. In 1137, Yue Fei heard that Jin Renren wanted to establish a son of the Zong in Bian Jing, so Shu Gao Zong invited a prince to calm people's hearts. It was meant to be loyal, but it was mistaken for someone with a different spirit. Gao Zong thought, you can just fight your battles well when Yue Fei is outside; is it up to you to become a prince or not? If you are urging to become a prince, don't you think I can't do it anymore; I have to rely on a new owner. At this point, Yue Fei only spoke 100 words, and was unable to explain it clearly. Yue Fei too. Should you be the only general to handle the abolition?
No wonder Yue Fei's death was followed by a poem of condolence: “Since ancient times, the loyal emperor doubted, Quan Zhong Zhen did not die.” In addition to the above two rules, in addition to the fact that the Southern Song dynasty was biased and determined to make peace, and Jin Wu Shu also said, “Yue Fei must be killed and then reconciled,” there is no doubt that Yue Fei is bound to die.
There is an unwritten rule in China. Whenever people are persecuted, the crime is mostly recorded on one or two traitors. In fact, traitors are certainly abominable, but without the emperor's acquiescence or encouragement, which traitor can become the climate? In the Southern Song Dynasty, an editor named Hu Quan objected to the peace and asked to kill Qin Hui. Qin Hui was in heavy power at the time, and he didn't dare to do anything about him. Yue Fei was a ministerial cadre at the time, and his position was much higher than Hu's. If Gao Zong didn't allow it, would Qin Hui dare murder him? It can be seen that the culprit behind Yue Fei was Song Gaozong; Qin Hui was nothing more than a high-ranking accomplice.
In China, an insightful person is not only often unmotivated, but is also often murdered. why? Because in the mind of the ruler, he himself is the most knowledgeable; others only need to do what they want. Therefore, before putting forward opinions, traditional ministers often had to figure out the emperor's mentality, not only to have a humble attitude, but also to speak nicely. As a result, it is extremely difficult to fully express one's opinion.

Some people even summed up the trick to becoming an official, which turned out to be; “just knock more, talk less.” With this kind of mentality, how could they not do anything wrong?
