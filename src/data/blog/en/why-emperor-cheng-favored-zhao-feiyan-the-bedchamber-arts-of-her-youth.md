The princess's lifestyle, like spring rain and nourishment, made Zhao Feiyan quickly become a beautiful girl. Her mature body had a strange feeling, accompanied by an inexplicable sense of desire.
Zhao Lin's family has a neighbor who is a bird shooting guard in the Yu Lin Army guarding the Imperial Palace. Let's just call him a bird shooter! When the Feiyan sisters were street beggars, bird shooters were very sympathetic to what happened to them. After not being seen for a few years, Feiyan actually came out like a flower and jade, much to the surprise of bird shooters.
When the girl first started out, she was curious, fresh, and exciting about all relationships between men and women. Driven by this complicated mentality, Feiyan was warmly seduced by people who shoot birds, and they wanted to refuse. On a moonlit night, the two achieved good things, which was absolutely wonderful. Since then, it has been out of reach. As long as Princess Yang Ah's family doesn't have a cabaret show, Feiyan often makes excuses to go out and meet birders.
According to Ye Shi records, Feiyan was smart and intelligent. In addition to singing and dancing, she was also familiar with ancient medical books stored in the Princess's Palace and learned the art of guidance.
One night, it snowed heavily, and it was extremely cold. Feiyan stood in the snow and waited for the shooter. Despite her thin clothes, she didn't have a hint of chill on her skin. It turns out that she used guidance techniques to hold her breath and secretly soothe her blood, so that the chill did not invade her skin. The bird shooter hugged Feiyan and was surprised to find that it was particularly warm and stretchy than before. He thought it was a fairy.
It can be seen that when Feiyan was a girl, she was able to guide Xia Ji's room, making her want to be immortal.
This is probably one of the reasons why she was so loved by Emperor Cheng since then!
Emperor Han Cheng Liu Wei is a playful and swanky emperor. He likes lewd fun, and is known all over the world. Often accompanied by a group of attendants, under the pseudonym Prince Zhang, they rode on horses in the market or in the countryside, speeding around the market or in the countryside, acting recklessly. Or watch cockfights and enjoy horses, or snatch folk women to satisfy their own preferences and fresh desires. When Emperor Han Cheng was in power, the great Han dynasty had already been blown away by rain and wind. A relative of the Wang clan, was crowned by nine people. Since he was addicted to wine and femininity all day long, the government of the Dahan dynasty gradually fell into the hands of the younger brother of his mother, Empress Dowager Wang Zhengjun. As a result, he simply did not ask about the DPRK administration and became Taiping Tianzi.
One day in the 3rd year of Han Hongjia (18 BC), he wandered around with Hou Cong again, and by the way, abducted to Princess Yang Ah's home. What an honor it is for the emperor to arrive. Princess Yang Ah was flattered. Naturally, she wanted to bring out all the best food, use, and see in Fuchu and entertain the emperor. Of course, Kabu Sara and beautiful yuzu are essential for drinking parties. While Emperor Cheng was drinking hot, he saw that Zhao Feiyan was full of love, and was infinitely shy.
“The cool breeze came from the sky, and Huai Jun Zi was unforgettable, and I felt a lot of relief.”
Zhao Feiyan's light, twisting lower back and legs are as gentle as the singing voice of a white cloud, soft as boneless, and like a weak willow to support the wind. She is beautiful and charming. The facial expression seemed to be shrouded in a layer of clouds that seemed to leave, making people feel sorry; the sense of joy revealed between raising hands and feet was just like a flower branch in the clouds, swaying in the wind, like a flying swallow, and was so beautiful. There is such a vivid description in “The Legend of Zhao Feiyan”: “(Feiyan) has a very thin lower back and is a good walker. If her hands tremble, no one else can learn.” She created an original dance technique called “walking down”. It can be seen that she has deep dance skills and understanding. Seen in this way, not only did Zhao Feiyan have a moving and extraordinary dance style, but her appearance was also outstanding. Emperor Cheng was insane for a moment and was dazed, as if she had lost her soul. I wonder if I'm in the Immortal Realm, but I don't know what night it is tonight!
Without waiting for the song and dance performance to finish, Emperor Cheng said to her sister, “This little girl is almost as beautiful as a demon. In particular, her body, which is as light as a swallow, reveals such a colorful dance language that only fairies have. Watching her sing and dance is such a pleasing pleasure!”
When Princess Yang A saw that the emperor liked it, she immediately dedicated Feiyan to Emperor Cheng. Emperor Cheng praised Princess Yang Ah. She immediately brought Zhao Feiyan back to the palace. She loved her without discussion, pampered her day and night, and indulged in fun.
Zhao Feiyan is an intelligent woman, and she did not indulge in comfort because of this. She knows that the harem is sinister, and the emperor's greatest virtue is that he likes the new and gets tired of the old. It was even faster than the seed germinated. In addition to his appearance, the reason why he was favored by the emperor was the main reason Cheng Emperor was moved by his dance posture and singing voice. Emperor Cheng favored her, not for her color, but for her art. Every woman who enters the harem is beautiful in the world. Emperor Cheng was used to seeing all the flower buds blooming in spring; there wasn't much that moved him.
If you want to talk about color, the only one who can fascinate the emperor for a long time is her beautiful sister, Zhao Hede.
Zhao Hede is just a miracle in the sky; there is absolutely nothing on earth. According to the history book, she was extremely beautiful, and everyone admired her! She knows that her younger sister has been talking longer than herself, and that her skin is more beautiful and creamy, as if the rouge would lose its color, and that the most beautiful jade in the world would also be shy in front of her. It is the most beautiful color in the world, enough to make her fall in the harem. In order to be able to pamper the harem, Zhao Feiyan recommended her younger sister, Zhao Hede, to Emperor Cheng, and boasted that she was gorgeous, in good shape, and pure and trustworthy.
Zhao Feiyan is a smart woman. She recommended her younger sister to Emperor Cheng, not so much for her to share her wealth as herself, but rather for her to join forces to control the emperor, deal with other beautiful women in the harem, and enjoy the emperor's love forever.
It's not easy to get anything good. Zhao Hede knows the art of getting away with the situation. Although the royal decree is up in a day, Zhao Hede is arrogant and unscrupulous. She thanked the messenger and said, “I only went if my sister called me. Other than that, I wouldn't dare to go, even with an axe.”
After Emperor Cheng heard it, he became more novel, and his desire to take it even more, so he used Zhao Feiyan's multicolored handkerchief as a token and then summoned Ho Te.
Only then did Zhao Hede seem to have followed the envoy to the palace with displeasure and reluctance.
Zhao Hede is naturally beautiful, has a unique charm, and has all kinds of styles. He can be speechless, but it also makes people fall in love and lose their spirits. When I saw Emperor Cheng and his left and right attendants, they were all shocked and dumbfounded. They didn't believe that there were such extraordinary women on earth. At the time, Dr. Pixiang's Naofang City during Emperor Xuan's time was already very old and had seen so many things in the world. After seeing Zhao Hede, he was also shocked and reminded Emperor Cheng, “This is also a disaster; putting out the fire is a must!” (Because according to the Five Elements Doctrine, Shang Chi of the Han dynasty inherited the virtue of fire, extinguished fire with water, and fought each other, so I meant to refer to Zhao Hede as the person who died of Han)
However, in the mind of Emperor Cheng, beauty was no longer important in the recent past, and hate couldn't eat it right away, so he didn't mind it. They immediately ordered the canonization of both Sister Zhao as Jie Yu. In the Han Palace, Jieyu ranked third among concubines, a title after Empress and Zhaoyi. Marquess Berriet. However, Empress Xu, who was originally loved by Emperor Cheng and the talented girl Ban Jieyu, have since been completely snubbed by Emperor Cheng.
Just like any upstart, they regard chance opportunities to develop as inevitable. And once your ambitions expand, you won't stop burning until you burn yourself to ashes. When Zhao Feiyan saw that Emperor Han Cheng was fascinated by the beauty of herself and her sister, she couldn't help herself, so she ambitiously wanted to seize the throne of the empress. She believes that due to the incredible colors of the two sisters, they deserve a higher position than Jieyu. Zhao Feiyan has her own reasons for thinking this way. Because in her opinion, if it really fulfills her wishes, then in the feudal era, where emphasis was placed on growing up and orderly, then the empress's crown was none other than her.
