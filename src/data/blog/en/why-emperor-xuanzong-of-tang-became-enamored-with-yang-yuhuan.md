 The love between Xuanzong Lee Lung-gi and Noble Consort Yang Yuhuan became a legend throughout the ages through the poem “Song of Eternity” by Bai Juyi. Yang Yuhuan is a native of Yongle, Bozhou (present-day Ruicheng, Shanxi). She is the fourth grandson of Wang's family in Suiliang County. Her father is Yang Xuanyan. When she grew up, she was hired as the queen of Xuanzong's son Shou Wang Li Mao. If Xuanzong hadn't been greedy, robbed her daughter-in-law and robbed her for love, Yang Yuhuan would probably live a quiet life and live a laid-back life as a noble lady, and she wouldn't be known to the world. However, Hyeon-jong lost her love and changed her life.
Hyunjong Lee Yong-gi is a charming person, and he is not as particular about relationships as people might think. He has loved many women, including when he had a crush on Yang Yuhuan, he couldn't forget his love for Mei Fei Jiang Caiping. At the same time, there was also a beautiful woman named Nian Nu who often accompanied him.
Every time Nian Nu sang on the board, he always had to give his eyes to Qiu Bo and convey all kinds of styles to Xuanzong, but Xuanzong was always happy to enjoy it. Later, “Nian Nu Jiao” became a type of song in the palace.
Before Xuanzong took office, he was a railway driver in Luzhou. He loved Consort Zhao. Afterwards, she fell in love with Consort Qian, Deyi Huangfu, and Liu Cairen until Consort Wu. Wu Huifei died in her forties. This year, Xuanzong was 52 years old. There were many beautiful women in the harem, but none of them appealed to Hyeon-jong. For Xuanzong, the seed of passion, this has no place to put down his feelings; it is nothing more than a form of torture. As a result, Xuanzong was depressed and often angry.
At this point, a recent minister said that Yang Xuanyan has a daughter, named Yang Yuhuan, who is now Princess Shou. She has a natural posture. Xuanzong was very happy to hear that, and she didn't care that the family was his own daughter-in-law, and immediately ordered to be invited to watch.
Don't look at it, don't worry. As soon as he saw it, Hyeon-jong was dumbfounded. Yang Yuhuan is extremely beautiful, and can sing and dance well. She is extremely intelligent, and extremely smart. There's no way a woman with such a rich, radiant personality, not to be tempted by a normal man, let alone the merry seed Lee Yong-gi? After being dumbfounded for a while, Lee Lung-gi relaxed and felt that this coveted beauty couldn't be hugged right away, because she wasn't a princess; she was her own daughter-in-law; at least she had to change her status first. So Xuanzong ordered that Yang Yuhuan be naturalized as a female Taoist first, given too serious a title, and then welcome back into the harem. Finally, the exclusive room was reserved, causing Fandai Rokumiya to lose its color.
When Shou Wang saw his daughter-in-law being taken by his father, he didn't even dare to say a word; he had to hold back and swallow the sound. In order to pay compensation, Xuanzong hired Wei Zhaoxun's daughter as Princess Shou. What can Shou do other than leave it at his mercy?
Yang Yuhuan is good at singing and dancing, knows sound rhythm, and is empathetic. Xuanzong liked it so much that he gradually fell in love with it and couldn't help himself. Soon, Yang Yuhuan favored the harem. The palace called her a maiden, and her manners were the same as those of an empress. In the early years of Tianbao, Yang Yuhuan was canonized as a royal concubine. Of course, there is a reason why Yang Yuhuan was so charming that Xuanzong's spirit was turned upside down. After a short time, the Spring Festival grew stronger, and since then, the king has not had an early morning. So people can't help but ask, how did Yang Yuhuan make Xuanzong so obsessed with her? Is it her natural beauty, with fair skin like a “gel”? Is it her charming demeanor of “Looking Back and Laughing, Bai Meisheng”? It's her feather suit, can you sing and dance well?
There is an opinion that Xuanzong is familiar with sound rules; this is considered the best person among the emperors of the Tang dynasty. He has loved music since childhood. He is highly qualified, can compose and dance. Many of his disciples have been trained by him in Liyuan. Meanwhile, Yang Yuhuan is in good shape, and is good at fast-paced Western dance. In addition, Yang Yuhuan is a master pipa artist. Ancient records say: Once, Xuanzong proposed using instruments from the mainland to hold a concert with 5 instruments from the western region. At the time, Xuanzong was full of interest. Holding drums, Yang Yuhuan played pipa, sang softly, and danced day and night. For Xuanzong, of course, Yang Yuhuan, who is good at vocals, is particularly attractive.
Yang Yuhuan has an outstanding figure. Not only is she in good shape, her skin is delicate, and her face looks like a peach blossom. This is also attractive to Xuanzong, who is more important than her voice. However, what can make Xuan Zong fall madly in love with Yang Yuhuan should be that she has excellent intelligence, is good at controlling men's mentality, and is empathetic. For example, she had no complaints when she was gifted to death, so how could Emperor Xuanzong not continue to hate him for a period of time until he died of depression!
According to another opinion, Xuanzong would have a crush on Yang Yuhuan. Of course, some of Yang's charms were at play, but more importantly, the social environment at the time and changes in the royal family should have played a decisive role. As the Tang dynasty entered its heyday, the emperor's extravagance would inevitably replace the desire for healing. Xuanzong gradually lost interest in politics, and he also developed a sense of burnout in political affairs. Under the patronage of the prime minister and lieutenants, he quickly became tired of government affairs. Later, Xuanzong took charge of the dictatorship of Li Linfu and others, and settled down at ease on his own. This gives you time to indulge.
