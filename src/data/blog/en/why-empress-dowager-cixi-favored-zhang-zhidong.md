 Author: Yue Chu
Looking at the elegant figures in the late Qing political world, Zhang Zhidong was undoubtedly an outstanding one. The reason why Zhang Zhidong was able to keep up in the official arena is, on the one hand, thanks to his extraordinary talents, advanced ideas, and superhuman political achievements. On the other hand, the “Three Super League” is also an important capital for promotion in today's political arena; on the other hand, it is inseparable from the favor of Empress Dowager Cixi, the actual “top leader” in the late Qing dynasty.
Zhang Zhidong's entrance can be described as having its ups and downs, but before he joined Cixi, he was listed with Cixi, leaving an important “first impression” on Cixi. The reason is that Zhang Zhidong's older brother, Zhang Zhidong's elder brother, was able to talk to Cixi in front of him. Zhang Zhi Wan is 27 years older than Zhang Zhidong. He was Ding Weike's champion in Daoguang in '27. He went on to become a college student. Therefore, Zhang Zhidong should be considered a “person in the DPRK”. Naturally, Zhang Zhidong was an important step for Zhang Zhidong to enter the political arena in the Qing Dynasty. However, in the beginning, these “people in North Korea” actually influenced Zhang Zhidong to participate in the “civil service” recruitment examination in the Qing dynasty. Zhang Zhidong was ranked first in the Shuntian Township exam when he was 16. He became the teenager “Xie Yuan” Lang. He was able to advance to first place, but he waited 11 years to get a place in the gold list.
Zhang Zhidong first helped his father, who was the governor in Guizhou, handle military affairs, and then married a wife and had children. After his father died in office, Zhang Zhidong “Ding Yu” remained filial pious for three years, and had no time to take the test. I didn't prepare to take the regular exam that year until I was 23 years old. Unsurprisingly, my brother Zhang Zhiwan was the same examiner for this exam. The Qing dynasty's academic examination regulations were “routinely avoided,” that is, candidates who are related to being examiners must avoid applying for the exam, and Zhang Zhidong lost his eligibility to take the exam. In the second year of the Open Enke Conference exam, the same examiner was Zhang Zhiwan. Zhang Zhidong was only able to “follow the rules” again. Zhang Zhidong was only able to take the exam in the first year of Tongzhi (1862). Although he was recommended by the same examiner Fan Hesheng, he became famous in Sun Mountain due to limited places.
Zhang Zhidong joined the ranks in the 2nd year of Tongzhi (1863). Legend has it that this year, Empress Dowager Cixi was planning to try Zhang Zhiwan as president, but one day she unwittingly saw a tabloid with an anecdote about “Zhang Jieyuan and Young Huiqiao”. Cixi, who usually likes to do couplets, was very interested in Zhang Zhidong, the 16-year-old “prodigy” in the article. She also saw that Zhang Zhiwan was from the same place, so she asked Zhang Zhiwan to ask if Fang Zhi was of the same generation. However, it was because of this level of relationship that he lost his eligibility for the exam twice and has not been able to join the military so far. As a result, Cixi decided to change the position of flag person Bao Jun as the president of the test, causing him to avoid Zhang Zhidong in order to take the exam. Whether this rumor is true is unknown, but one thing is certain, that is, Zhang Zhidong has already been recognized as a rare genius by Cixi before entering the ranks.
In the exam that followed, Zhang Zhidong successfully won the 141st Gong Shi. Fan Hesheng, who is still the same examiner, wrote a poem on this topic, and Zhang Zhidong shared his gratitude with the poem. It can probably be seen from this that the Zhang brothers have close personal relationships with Fan. Zhang Zhidong came in first place in the retest. His theory of the test was even more out of touch with the times and brilliance. The minister who read the paper seemed to think that this person was too arrogant and was preparing to downgrade him from first in the first class to the end of the top three. At this point, Bao Jun stepped up and placed Zhang Zhidong as No. 1 in the Second Division. After the big list was in Cixi's hands, Cixi, who had already taken the lead, soon placed Zhang Zhidong in the top and third place, commonly known as “flower exploration.” In the Qing Dynasty, each discipline was divided into three ranks, that is, the top three: champion, champion, and flower prospector, and first; seven in the second class, given to the rank of the champion; and several in the top three, given to the same rank. Weng Zeng Yuan, the winner of Cixi back then, was the son of a Changshu native of Weng Tongshu. His fourth uncle, Tongxu, was the champion of Xianfeng for six years and was the master of Emperor Guangxu; the runner-up was Wan Chong Hou, which usually took a bit off because the name “Wan Fang Chong Hou” means “Wan Fang Chong Hou”.
Rumor has it that after the awarding ceremony for new academics, Cixi left behind three people, Weng Zengyuan, Wan Chonghou, and Zhang Zhidong, and gave them an upper link. Whoever was right was selected as his protégé. Cixi's uplink is:
The Luoshui spiritual turtle lives a life. The number of days is 5, the number of places is 5, and the number 5 is returned to 25. The count is Dingyuan Shi Tianzun. I am sincerely impressed.
Zhang Zhidong was worthy of his ingenuity. Half a cup of tea turned out a subordinate that made Cixi very happy:
Danshan Caifeng is bright. There are six male voices, six female voices, and six six are all 36. The voice praises Empress Dowager Cixi, and there are no boundaries for a long life.
Zhang Zhidong's high-class fart captured Cixi Feng and Yan Dayue, and also opened the door to her career. He was accepted as a disciple of Cixi and taught as an editor at the Hanlin Institute. Since then, Zhang Zhidong's reputation has grown, and his official fortune has flourished, and he has risen step by step. From the head examiner of the Zhejiang Rural Trial University, Hubei Xuezheng, the governor of Shanxi, the governor of Liangguang, and Governor Huguang (Governor Huguang was temporarily appointed twice during this period), to the Minister of Military Aircraft, a Ti Ren Ge University student, and Taizi Taibao, he became the main representative figures of the Western Wu faction in the late Qing Dynasty.
Zhang Zhidong was stable as a rock in the turbulent political scene of the late Qing dynasty. First, he relied on the “Three Super League” in the beginning, second, on the support of his brother Zhang Zhiwan in the DPRK, and third, on the favor and special care of Empress Dowager Cixi.
