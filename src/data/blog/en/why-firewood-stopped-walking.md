 A girl falls in love with a young man, but this young man doesn't know. One day, they all accompanied the young people in the stockade to the mountains to cut firewood. The young man had a lot of strength and moved fast. Soon he cut enough firewood and rushed back and forth.
The girl moved quite slowly. When she cut enough firewood and discovered that the young man had already left, she rushed after the young man in a panic. However, Chai didn't listen to his call at all; he walked very slowly. As soon as she was in a hurry, she packed up a bundle of firewood and carried it on her shoulder to quickly catch up. Legend has it that since then, Chai won't be able to walk.
