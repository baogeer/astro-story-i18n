An utterly masculine name, devoid of romantic beauty, and without the simplicity of realism. I really don't know what thoughts and ideas did Xu Yushi's daughter and her son-in-law, Chenglong, have given their Qianjin such an unpoetic name.
The first time this name caught my eye, I decided without thinking that the owner of it was a man. After turning a few pages of the book, I learned that I had lost my eye. Xu Zongpu was not only a woman, but also the first wife of Li Bai, a genius poet from the Tang Dynasty.
My impression is that naming is inertial, but there is no absolute standard. In the beginning, the name was just a symbol of the birth of life, making it easy to distinguish and call each other. At the beginning, the names were arbitrary. For example, when a child was born, they looked up at the moon and saw a round of the moon in the sky; when they heard a dog barking in the morning, they were named Dog. At that time, men's surnames were passed down from generation to generation, so men had all names and surnames, while women were only responsible for childbearing; using their surnames for themselves was an end. Therefore, it doesn't matter if they are famous or not; many people share the same name. Of course, they all have small names, so they are easy to call. Later, when there was an aesthetic sense, men's and women's names were temperamental, and there was a difference between feminine and masculine. Later, some people began to think about fate and linked their names to yin and yang gossip and the eight characters of birth. Naming a name had a sense of mystery, and it became a major event close to sacred and solemn. At this point, famous households saw their names as huge, so the characters commonly used by men and women appeared in different categories, and powerful households also had different choices of characters commonly used in naming ordinary households.
The name Xu Zongpu obviously doesn't follow the rules for men and women, but after careful consideration, its education and connotations are extremely profound. After Xu Yushi was demoted for many years, her granddaughter became known as Zong Pu. In fact, I'm speaking in a different way, to repeat that my Xu family is rooted in Pu Yu, who is clean internally and externally, and that being buried and demeaned is a temporary Hecaogai Pearl. It can be seen that Xu Yushi's daughter and son-in-law are actually quite educated and thoughtful. Although the name is unattractive, the inclusion in it makes people who are in the middle of their homes puke their breath. Of course, this is just my subjective guess; in fact, it is most fundamental. I think it is inseparable from the confidence of the Datang dynasty and the confidence of the Xu family.
Confidence is an internal strength that comes from the bottom of the heart; it is backed by strong strength. I am who I am. Naturally gifted, naturally made, I can ignore everything. There is no need to use any disguise to enhance and dress up yourself, as we often say in our lives today: “A full bottle doesn't ring, half a bottle shakes”. Take a look at many of the phenomena in action. This is indeed the case. For example, real billionaires don't need to wear famous brands or cars to enhance and show off; they go shopping on Hanzheng Street or at stalls; if they play or wear cloth shoes, they are all praised for their frugal character, which is a kind of demeanor. On the other hand, the more they are afraid that others will look down on them, the more people with low self-esteem will die and be flouted. They like to punch people with swollen faces and fat people, and they also have to save money to buy famous watches to hang on their wrists when eating pickles. Or buy a used car at a low price from someone else and drive a lot of money all over the world.
According to this logical deduction, under the conditions of an open and prosperous society like the Tang dynasty, a strong country gave everyone confidence, and Xu Zongpu, the granddaughter of the prime minister, was also a beautiful woman with golden and jade. Going in and out in the eyes of a man is an open and natural aesthetic comparison, so Xu Zongpu will not discount her status and not be able to get married because of a man's name. Even though the Xu family was already beside Qingmen at the time, their skinny camels were bigger than horses, and they were still famous people in the local community. As long as they were rich and naturally beautiful, not to mention a male name, that is, women wear male makeup. At the age of marriage, beautiful women with female makeup walk down the street. Definitely, they are always making promises.
Seen from this perspective, the importance of a name varies from person to person. The masculinization of names does not change the essence of feminization of temperament. What's more, the foundation of life is a natural gift of parents, and names are gifted after parents' ideas. Therefore, one's personality is determined by one's own internal biological mechanisms, and is not influenced by a life symbol that accompanies oneself.
I believe this is true. I've summarized the materials I've read. There aren't many records of Xu Zongpu, but through the lines, I can conclude that she is a virtuous woman with great maternal beauty. She has an outstanding appearance and spiritual wisdom. She is the best friend of all women who are too ordinary in their studies and upbringing. Because she already has a strong exterior, she sees her inner practice as more important than her outer self.
Her ability to talk about her parents and greet Li Bai with an active attitude is due to her self-confidence in her personality. At the time of the meeting, she picked up the curtain from behind the screen and was recognized at first sight. Letting a marriage link one's own destiny with Li Bai's destiny is also the confidence in his personality.
Xu Zongpu is extremely discerning. She is satisfied with Li Yuyi's liberal and personable poets, but more importantly, she admired his poetry and talent, and chose his inner education and character.
As a result, in the 15th year of Kaiyuan, Da'an Village in Anlu, Hubei had a son-in-law. Soon, they built a new house next to Tonghui Temple under Baizhao Mountain. Xu Zongpu and his friendly official Li Bai lived a loving life here. Time did not disappoint them; soon, they had a son and a daughter.
After less than three years of relative calm and stability, Li Bai, who wanted to “soar through the sky and be astonished,” couldn't hold back. At the time, he was already in the name of backbreaking the door. He was unwilling to take on the fame of the Xu family. He began to rely on his ability to make friends, meet famous people, and seek recommendations, and actually “worked all over the lords and experienced the Qing Xiang.”
Exactly, Li Bai fell into the midst of so-called progress and became an uncompromising official fan. In order to connect to the antenna, he spent a lot of time in the midst of interlaced communication and interaction with dendrobium. He spent a lot of money and poured out drinkers. “Buying songs and laughing with gold, I was intoxicated by Prince Yueqing.”
Li Bai didn't do any housework. He carried a smart head on his shoulders and went out drinking and socializing all day long. Xu Zongpu, completely promoted to a housewife, used a woman's shoulders to carry the lion's share of life. She is confident that she is working hard for this family and is busy working for Li Bai's son and daughter. She is also confident that the person who can take Li Bai from her side hasn't been born yet, regardless of her family situation, her knowledge, or her looks. She ignored that dressing up is a woman's second face, that aesthetic fatigue is a common disease that men are prone to, and that men love the new and hate the old.
Confidence has made her more generous and patient than normal. She never asks Li Bai what she is busy with every day. She believes that the world of men has a kind of dignity and cannot be violated or controlled by women. A woman trying to control a man is confused and self-deceiving. “You can force yourself to die, you can't force a person to sleep”; a woman can never tie a man to her own cloak. In her philosophy, a man who was governed by a woman who was open, open-minded, and chivalrous would die. He must have no circle, no world. This kind of subjugation is either a lack of internal self-discipline, or a clever deception due to the dark side of the Ming Xiu Boardwalk.
Having worked hard in the midst of the hustle and bustle created by time, she has entered a state of selflessness, and acts more and more like a true housewife.
In Li Bai's eyes, she is a beautiful, gentle, and delicate wife, but she has lost her seduction and charm. He believes that being beautiful and sexy are two concepts. He believes that they are now husband and wife, and have successfully moved from a period of excitement and impulsivity to achieve a successful transition from love to family. He felt comfortable and comfortable with her, but he was unable to arouse excitement or impulse. Li Bai's gaze and interest began to shift strategically. He spent the whole day outside, serving feasts in Qin Mu Chu, and had many peach blossoms. He went home every day with alcohol and fatigue. He only knew pillows when he opened the door. Xu Zongpu became a high-quality leisure asset.
For a while, Li Bai was looking for flowers and the rumor that he was looking for flowers and asking Liu began hitting Xu Zongpu's eardrum. In fact, in the details of her life, she also felt something different. Li Bai often made excuses to go to Jinling and Guangling. At the time of the Tang dynasty, Nanjing and Yangzhou's Fireworks Alley were already famous all over the world, and became popular places for writers and rascals. She knows that Li Bai is nobler in this regard. Sometimes looking through poetry manuscripts he wrote after his travels, some sentences encourage suspicion and associations in her mind.
“Chisato was placed in a bottle of sake, carrying prostitutes and stayed with Hato.” ; “Cymbidium follows a bustling female artist, and the scenery is full of songs.” ; “I visited all the places on Falling Flower Road and laughed at Wu Ji's wine shop.” ; “When will we meet again, turn off the lights and unravel our clothes.”
Seeing these sentences for the first time, she was shocked and wondered if this was a picture of the official's debauchery outside. She immediately said no. After all, she is a famous girl who has read a lot of poetry books. Ming Dali knows the general trend. She thought that a writer's artistic imagination when writing was higher than and surpassed in life. Even if he was at fault outside, he should understand. She is confident that every time a man plays when he is working outside the home and occasionally finds manna when he is hungry, it won't cause him to forget his roots and abandon his roots. The key is for the home to become a source of living water and clean the source.
Xu Zongpu has roots in this way of thinking. Since she was a child, she has seen and heard the manners of many men and women in the court and the elite families. Let's just say the emperor, the seventy-second concubine of the Sannomiya Rikuin, but the empress is the empress; her name and rank are very clear. Looking at the long course of history, it is true that some people have bad intentions and seek chaos, but as long as the mother and mother the world are not in trouble, the sky is the sky, and the earth is the earth.
People's hearts are full of flesh. Xu Zongpu's generosity and hard work make Li Bai look anxious in his eyes. He felt guilty and wrote a song “Gift of Inner Poems”: “360 days, day was as drunk as mud. Even though she is Li Baifu, she is a regular wife.” It should have been said in a very sincere manner. However, Li Bai was worthy of being a genius. He was extremely cunning. He attributed his mistakes to alcohol and brilliantly erased other faults.
The most important thing is that Li Bai has a very sincere attitude, but he has never made any mistakes in actual action. He treated Xu Zongpu's generosity as acquiescence and indulgence. He gradually drifted away and lost himself in the realm of morality and character.
Looking back at Xu Zongpu, women's confidence also needs to evolve with the times. Being beautiful today doesn't mean you'll still be beautiful tomorrow. A famous girl is just a business card. The key is how you shape yourself or the person you want to shape. Generosity is a form of attitude. Letting a horse run on your territory is a form of letting go, but if you run out of your own meadow and go grazing on a neighbor's plot, then there's a less appealing name called wild.
At this point, it's not a question of grandeur, but of control. Therefore, being tolerant of Li Bai, Xu Zongpu eventually interpreted her short life as a tragedy. Li Bai ignored and snubbed her with her forgiveness. In the ten years he lived in Anlu, Li Bai spent six years loving the landscape, wandering around his hometown, and busy with fame. Suddenly, at the age of 37, Li Bai was still wearing a blue shirt, fresh air on both sleeves, and his future was in smoke, with no good omen. However, Xu Zongpu, who had worked so hard, had silver streaks on his sideburns and cured himself of an illness. It was the winter of this year, when she was so sick that she was put to an end by being medicated in a bed.
Only after Mrs. Xu died did Li Bai feel that the sky had fallen. Taking care of her two children, the necessary daily handling made him discover his wife's admiration in his reflection. The grief of remorse was amplified every day, and he constantly fell into self-blame. In order to escape the entanglement of sorrow, Li Bai and his two sons fled to Donglu in the summer of Kaiyuan 25th.
As an emotional poet, Li Bai's introduction of love and blood into her poetry thrilled and resonated with me countless times; as a husband, Li Bai's indifference to the Xu family and her indulgence in emotions really undermined her personality. In the words of ordinary people, this person is hardhearted enough; he is less humane!
Later, Mrs. Xu's tomb was covered with wild grass, and the wind in the past was inconspicuous. She often cried around the monument. What she cried was tears from heaven and earth. Unbelievably, the words on the stone monument were gradually brushed away.
Since there were no descendants to worship, over time, her tomb was erased. Those archaeologists have gone to the vicinity of Da'an Village to find no telltale signs. Currently, only the titles of Xu Zongpu and the Xu clan are in books and have been passed down from generation to generation, which is considered a kind of sacrifice.
