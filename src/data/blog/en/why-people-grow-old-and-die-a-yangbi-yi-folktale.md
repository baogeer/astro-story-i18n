 Legend has it that people don't die of old age in the beginning, because people change their skin, replace their old skin, and grow new skin, they become younger again. However, because the human body has limbs, head, and neck, it is not easy to molt, especially when decaying hands and feet. It is often painful to die due to difficult peeling. After this situation was seen by heaven, in order to relieve people's pain, they replaced their molting skin with snakes (because snakes have a smooth body and are easier to shed their old skin).
Since then, in addition to this pain, humans have also experienced life, old age, illness, and death; snakes have become animals that are not easy to die of old age through molting. It is said that snakes can live for over a thousand years, turn white all over, and become snake spirits. However, few people live to be 100 years old.
