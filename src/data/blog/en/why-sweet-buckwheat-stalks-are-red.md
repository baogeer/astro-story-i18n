 Sweet buckwheat is basically the same in appearance as tartary buckwheat, but the flavor of the resulting buckwheat is different. As the name suggests, tartary buckwheat is bitter, while sweet buckwheat is sweet. There are also differences in harvesting season. Tartary buckwheat can be harvested twice, summer buckwheat and winter buckwheat, while sweet buckwheat can only be harvested once. There are also differences in color. Sweet buckwheat stems are red, while tartary buckwheat is green. According to folk mythology, sweet buckwheat is a variant of tartary buckwheat.
