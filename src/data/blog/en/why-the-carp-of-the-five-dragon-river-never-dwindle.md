 The carp in the Wulong River is very famous. Naturally, many people catch them, but there are not many carp in the river. Why? There is a local story that goes around like this:
It is said that back then, there was an expert fish-catcher surnamed Zhang in Zhangjiaguan Village. He was seven feet tall, had a bear back, and a beard. He was a wonderful man. He followed his father down the river from an early age and learned a good skill in casting a net. Unexpectedly, when he was 20 years old, his father suddenly became bedridden and said to him, “I'm afraid I can't do it anymore. I'll give you the fishnet and fish basket!” Since that day, he has gone down the river to fish independently.
On this day, on the banks of the Wulong River, willows were like smoke and pear blossoms were like snow. Carrying a fishnet and carrying a fish basket, Xiao Zhang came to the riverside with pride. He saw a big girl and a little daughter-in-law doing laundry by the river, and playful children on the white beach. He thought: I want to show them off my hands! As a result, he took off his shoes and socks, rolled up the corners of his pants, and got into the water. The river is clear, and you can see the fine yellow sand at the bottom of the water at a glance. Speaking of rounding seven, I saw a large carp more than two feet long in front of me. It swayed its head and tail and swam towards him. The golden scales flashed, like a dragon. Looking at it again, around the big carp, there was also a group of small carps with long and short mouths that were so frightened that they wandered around. This made Xiao Zhang very happy. He lifted the fishing net with both hands and spread it “out”. This gesture is famous: the “magic circle” is the best technique he learned from his father. As soon as he mentioned the net outline, it was heavy, and by feeling, he knew that there were quite a few fish in the net. When he put the net away and took a look, not only was the big fish in the net, but the group of small fish was also knocked out by him. The riverside people saw this scene and shouted, “Ho, such a big carp!”
Xiao Zhang was very proud. He packed all the big carp and the little carp in the basket, then went home happy.
He heard his father moan on the couch. Then I put the fish basket on the edge of the pot, “Dad! Look!” As he spoke, he reached out to catch the big carp. The old man was certainly happy and had a smile on his face. Xiao Zhang took out two more small fish and showed them to his father, “Dad, look, the fish are tender!”
What do you know, Xiao Zhang's father's face suddenly changed, gloomy and very scary: “You bastard!”
Xiao Zhang was stunned! The old man said harshly, “Put the little carp back into the river!”
Xiao Zhang understood, because his father told him a long time ago: Little carp cubs with tiger mouths cannot be caught; catching them will reduce people's longevity. When fishing, my father always respectfully and respectfully released the little carp that came from the internet back into the river.
Xiao Zhang had no choice but to send the little carp back to the river.
Soon, Xiao Zhang's father died of illness. Xiao Zhang thought that his father's heart was so kind, but it didn't increase his longevity; he only lived to be 50 years old. Those fishermen, who often eat small carp, haven't died since they were quite old. Xiao Zhang didn't believe his father's words anymore. Later, when he caught little carp, he didn't release them; even those with two fingers long were not too small.
Xiao Zhang gradually became an old Zhang, and people can still see him cast a net fishing on the Wulong River throughout the year. In the winter, he also has to catch fish to eat when he opens the ice ring, and he is addicted to alcohol. While fishing, he is drinking while holding a wine gourd.
The third day of June this year is his 50th birthday. When he woke up early in the morning, he suddenly thought that his father had died at the age of 50, yet he was still strong and very happy. So he ordered his family to pick up a bucket of old rice wine. He wanted to go catch a few dragon carp and enjoy a nice meal to celebrate his birthday.
After breakfast, he arrived at the Wulong River. However, after casting a few nets, I didn't see any fish shadows. At this point, there was a kid mowing cows by the river and told him there were a lot of carp in “Piggy's Mouth Bay”. When Lao Zhang heard it, he followed the kid to Piggy's Cove. Lao Zhang told him that if he caught fish, maybe two fish would be given to him.
Arriving at the west bank of the bay, Lao Zhang quickly cast a few nets and hit more than ten pounds. Although it's not big, he is happy. He has been used to eating this little carp for the past few years.
On this day, the weather was very hot. Even walking and casting nets, Lao Zhang was already smelly sweaty. I decided to take a bath in the river and walk home. So he went deep into the water one by one. Who knows, his legs and stomach cramp at this point, and his body is like being tied to two large stones, sinking down one by one. After staying for a while, I felt as if my feet had landed on the ground. When I opened my eyes, it was a huge cave. The water is as deep as ink, and the blue rock is like a ghost. Seeing a large five-foot-long carp lying in the cave, he was overjoyed: if he had caught this big carp, today's birthday party would be rich. So I held my breath and approached the big carp. Who knows, just now that the carp is gone, there is a “prince” wearing a golden fish scale robe sitting in front of me. I saw him, his eyes were prominent, and he was majestic.” Oops, I must have bumped into Koi Sen today! :Lao Zhang was timid. He saw a few prawns, soldiers, and crabs coming over and ordered him to kneel down in front of Grandpa Wang and listen to his request.
“You... killed nine thousand nine hundred ninety-nine of my children, and today I'm asking you to pay for your life!”
Lao Zhang thought to himself that this was his own revenge for eating small carp.
“King, I'm confused; I didn't listen to my father's teachings. Today is my birthday, please spare my life! I want to release all the little brothers I've caught in the future, and I also have to convince others not to hurt these little lives!”
“OK, if you can persuade fishermen along the river to release seedlings and prolong your longevity...”
Lao Zhang looked up and saw that the king was gone. At this point, I felt that a giant carp was floating upward carrying him. After he went ashore, he dumped all the small fish he had just caught in the river.
Since then, Lao Zhang has gone to his fishing companions and insisted that they should take good care of the carp cubs. The old fishing brothers also all swore to heaven and never hurt the little carp again.
It is said that this is the reason why the number of carp in the Wulong River has not decreased over and over again. This story can be taken as a warning to those who “do their best to fish”.
