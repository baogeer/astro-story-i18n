 Everyone has heard about monkey butts being red, but why are monkey butts red?
When I was a kid, I heard from adults that once had a daughter, until when the daughter grew up, one day when there was only one daughter in the family, monkeys on the mountain snatched this daughter up the mountain, and the family couldn't find a daughter anywhere when they came back.
It took a long time for my daughter to sneak back from the mountain. I told my parents that they had given birth to monkeys on the mountain, but the parents had no other choice.
The next day, the monkey on the mountain carried the monkey to the family's door and sat on a slate and thought to the family: the monkey's mom, the monkey's wife, and the monkey wanted to eat some food. I keep thinking about it every day. As soon as the family chases the stick, the monkey runs, and never chases the monkey and keeps coming back.
The family really couldn't do it. After thinking about it this way, they were worried about their daughter's fame. In the end, they had to ask a gentleman for advice. As soon as the gentleman saw the situation, he said it was fine. You cut the bottom of this slate and burn firewood, and when the monkey came back at dawn, then the monkey's butt was burnt to the ground.
