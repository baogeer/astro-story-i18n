 A buffalo is a good helper to help people cultivate fields, but it is quiet and hoarse, and it also has a rope strangling mark on its neck. Why? Turns out there's another reason!
Legend has it that initially sheep cultivated land for humans. However, sheep are too weak, and when they cultivate the land, they are so tired that they are called “what?” After the situation was discovered by heaven, scalpers were sent down to the earth to cultivate land for people. Naturally, scalpers are much more efficient at work than sheep, but later the population increased, and scalpers were still unable to meet human needs, so heaven decided to send buffaloes to cultivate land for humans. The news was heard by water buffaloes playing in the pond, and they said, “Emperor Jade decided to send us to cultivate land in every corner. What should we do?” A buffalo said, “Our cry was earth-shattering. Everyone would be scared to death when they heard it. How could they surrender to us?” After the conversation was heard by the Dragon King, he went to heaven to tell the Jade Emperor. Guanyin then unfastened the foot wrap, tied the buffalo's neck, and carried it to the mortal world to serve as a human servant. As a result, the buffalo's cry became quieter, and there was a permanent strangulation mark on its neck. Since then, buffaloes have obediently worked for people.
