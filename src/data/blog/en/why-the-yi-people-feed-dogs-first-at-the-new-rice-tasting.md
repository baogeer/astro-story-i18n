 In the past, when the Yi people shook rice in August and September, they grilled it on a baking sheet. Once it was dried, it was immediately tossed in a pot. After drying the dust, the rice became rice. Cooking new rice and killing chicken to sacrifice one's ancestors is called tasting a new meal. If you want to try a new meal, you must feed it to your dog before humans can eat it. Why is that?
It is said that after pioneering the world, there were people on the ground, but there were no dogs or cats. People need animals to take care of their homes, and they also need animals to catch mice to protect food. When Tenjin knew about it, he was ready to send dogs and cats. However, these jobs are difficult and easy. There is no division of labor, and I can't get ideas. So I asked the dogs and cats to go to Hetian to plant, preparing to reward diligence and punish laziness.
On that day, dogs and cats wore overcoats, rain hats, and plow racks to drive cows to Hetian.
When they arrived in the field, dogs and cows started plowing the field, but the cat slept lazily on the field. The dog said, “I plow the field, you plow.”
The cat said, “Don't worry, it won't be too late when you have two plows and one rake.”
When the dog was raking the field, he said to the cat, “I've started raking the field, you can go plow the seedlings.”
The cat also said, “Don't worry, it won't be too late when you have three plows and two rakes.”
After three plows and two rakes, the dog said to the cat, “I've finished plowing the field plow, finished raking the rake, you can go plow the seedlings.”
The cat also said, “Don't worry, it won't be too late when you shovel and paste the field.”
The dog shoveled and pasted the field and said to the cat, “I've shoveled and pasted the field, so you can go pluck the seedlings.”
The cat also said, “Don't worry, it won't be too late when you have leveled the field.”
The dog cleared the field and said to the cat, “I've cleared the field, you can go pluck the seedlings.”
The cat also said, “Don't worry, when you've harvested the seedlings, why don't I go and plant the seedlings?”
The dog said, “Isn't it OK for me and the field to grow seedlings?”
The cat said, “That's what you said, I don't agree. It's unreasonable to divide your own and pick your own first. If not, I'll split it; you pick first. One person plows, one person planters, what do you do?”
The dog said, “Then I'll just pluck the seedlings!” The dog said it and pulled out the seedlings.
The cat still sleeps on his slumber.
Soon after, the dog finished pulling the seedlings and said to the cat, “I've finished plucking the seedlings, you can go plant the seedlings.”
The cat stretched out and said, “If you don't have to do the seedlings, you'll have to plant them. Anyway, you don't have much time; you can do good things without doing anything. I'll do it tomorrow, and you sleep for a day, OK?”
The dog said, “I have mud all over my body. You're still clean, so I'll plant the seedlings too, so I won't have to wash your feet and hands!” When I finished, I started planting.
The dog had finished planting, washed its body clean, put on a coat, a rain hat, carried a plow stand, and carried a plow, and raked, and rushed home with the cows.
Suddenly, the cat jumped up, jumped into the paddy field and rolled around, filling his whole body with mud water. The dog was puzzled.
The dog and cat returned to the heavenly court, and Tenjin asked, “How are you doing today? Are there any lazy ones? Talk about it.”
The dog said, “The cat slept lazily in the field all day and never did any work.”
The cat said, “It's just the wicked who filed the complaint first. The dog only sleeps on the field all day long; you can see that his whole body is clean and free of any mud. Look, my whole body is covered in mud water. Wise Tenjin, you must enforce the law impartially, with clear rewards and punishments!”
Tenjin was very happy to say, “The cat was right! In recognition of your hard work, go to the world to enjoy yourself: eat mouse meat exclusively, eat with people; sleep in the house; pull poop on the food pile; and pee upstairs.”
Tenjin stared viciously at the dog and said, “You're a lazy dog; you only need to eat human poop; you sleep outside the gate to take care of people in a nursing home.”
The dog is very unconvinced. When they meet people, they tell their wrongdoing, but no one can change the dog's unfortunate fate. I had no choice but to feed the dog first when trying a new meal. One was to express my sympathy for the dog, and the other was to thank the dog for their hard work all day growing seedlings in the field and bringing us raw rice to eat.
