 Legend has it that in the past, there was dew on both dry and rainy days, so why was there no dew on dry days later? Here's a story.
People everywhere have offended heaven, and Emperor Jade has blamed the Emperor, not letting the Dragon King rain for them for three years, planning to kill the crops here and starve the people here to death. The land here is very sympathetic to the people. He secretly made discussions with the god of the river, the god of the well, and the god of dew. Every night, the god of dew secretly lets the river tide for ten miles and the well water for eight miles. Every night, the god of dew falls on the dew. The tide floods the crops in this area and saves the people here. As a result, it hasn't rained for three years. The people have had plenty of clothes and food.
Emperor Yu Huang thought it hadn't rained in three years, and the people here had almost died, so he ordered Dragon King to rain the land here.
Besides, the people here haven't seen rain in three years. It's raining heavily at this point, how can we not be happy? So I bought fruit and firecrackers, burning incense and honoring Cangtian and Dragon King. As soon as Grandpa Ji saw that ordinary people revered God and Dragon King and put himself aside, he felt strange and panicked. He also summoned the god of the river, the god of the well, and the god of dew to discuss: “I said brothers, look how unhumane the people here are! Three years ago, they offended heaven. The Jade Emperor kept them from raining for three years. We secretly helped them, so they had plenty of clothing, food, and happiness. It's raining today. They don't thank us; they just respect God and Dragon King. This is really abhorrent.” Kawagami said, “In the future, we just won't give him wetland.” Dew Shui said, “That's strange that it was cheap for him. I think we're just like this. It's dry, so let's keep up with it. Everyone has a share of this kind of merit. The people admire them, and we share it from within. Isn't that good?” Everyone was amazed when they heard it! That's how they did it. Since then, the more flooded the day, the more water, the more water in the river, well water, and dew; the drier the day, the less well water, the river, and dew.
