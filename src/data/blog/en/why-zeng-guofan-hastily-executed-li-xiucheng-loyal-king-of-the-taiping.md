 Author: Zhao Yan
On July 28, 1864, Zeng Guofan arrived in Jinling. A few hours later, Tsang Kwok-fan interrogated Lee Soo-sung overnight. Li Xiucheng was captured a few days ago. When Jinling broke through, Li Xiuquan covered Xiao Tianwang and his entourage to kill out of the city, fell off his horse due to a foot injury, and hid in the mountains outside the city. As a result, he was caught by local villagers and bundled to the Xiang Army camp. A few days ago, during the interrogation of Lee Soo-shing, Tsang Kwok-chuen, who had an aggressive personality, remembered his new hatred. In an emergency, he stabbed Lee Soo-shing's arm and thigh with the tip of a knife. Lee Soo-sung was in a hurry and shouted, “Lao Jiu, we all have our own hands. Why are you suffering?” Tsang Kwok-chuen was ashamed and had no choice but to quit.
 
 Like all the victors, Tsang Kwok-fan sat on high ground. He didn't speak at first; he only used the dim lights in prison to carefully examine this important rival in his military career with his triangular eyes. Tsang Kwok-fan had no idea that this military genius, who did not have a high level of education, could be called the most outstanding military genius of the Taiping Kingdom, was so thin and not amazing. He was even as weak and slender as a woman. Tsang Kwok-fan spoke. His speech was calm and slow, just like Lee Soo-sung is leading a family. Tsang Kwok-fan asked Lee Soo-sung a lot about Taiping Heaven's situation and listened carefully to Lee Soo-sung's comments on some people and events. This conversation confirmed many of Zeng Guofan's questions about Taiping. Lee Soo-sung also answered the questions honestly. For most of the time, Tsang Kwok-fan was listening, while Lee Soo-shing laughed. Lee Soo-sung brilliantly described this meeting between the two sides in his self-statement a few days later, showing his deep gratitude to Tsang Kwok-fan between the lines. However, Zeng Guofan understated in his diary that day: “At the beginning of the year, the impostor king who was captured was personally judged and counted.” With just one stroke, it can be seen how slow Tsang Kwok-fan is to Lee Soo-sung. Tsang Kwok-fan was the winner; of course, he can be condescending. In addition to this, there is also culture and ideology that makes Zeng Guofan condescending; of course, there is also a possibility that this former rival greatly disappointed Tsang Kwok-fan. However, since seeing Lee Soo-sung, Tsang Kwok-fan has decided to kill Lee Siu-shing — the next day, Zeng Guofan wrote to his son Zeng Jizhe, who is in Anqing, saying, “The King of Hypocrisy has personally informed him once, and he plans to get the law right here.”
 
 Why did Tsang Kwok-fan kill Lee Soo-sung in such a hurry is always a mystery. Perhaps Zeng Guofan, who is a knowledgeable person, is not happy with Lee Soo-sung's face, words and actions. In his opinion, King Zhong of Taiping was extremely cunning, and enjoyed great prestige in Taiping. Only a quick kill would make Yu Dang completely cut off the idea of getting back on his feet. Of course, this is just one idea. The biggest possibility is that when Zeng Guofan interrogates Lee Soo-sung, Lee Soo-sung is likely to persuade Tsang Kwok-fan to set up an army and restore the Han dynasty, and promised to summon 100,000 old departments to help Zeng Guofan. In Lee Soo-sung's autobiography, there is also probably relevant content, which was later removed by Tsang Kwok-fan. This is arguably Zeng Guofan's most taboo. If Lee Soo-sung were to be released from custody to Beijing, the topic involved in this aspect, or if he said something bad for the Tsang brothers, it would make Tsang Kwok-fan very passive. Also, one thing that Zeng Guofan is afraid of is that if Lee Soo-shing is on his way to Beijing and has three strengths and two short legs, leaving the tiger back to the mountain, it will definitely cause a major disaster. All in all, if Lee Soo-sung were to be escorted to Beijing, everything would be out of control. There would be harm and no benefit, and he would be quickly executed in Jinling. Of course, another possibility is that while talking with Tsang Kwok-fan, Lee Soo-sung requested that he be quickly executed in Jinling to avoid torture. Tsang Kwok-fan fulfilled Lee Soo-sung's wishes. The execution of Lee Soo-sung in Jinling, at the very least, can make him die a happy one. When he arrives in Beijing, he will definitely suffer all kinds of suffering, and in the end, he will have to “die too late.” As far as Zeng Guofan's is concerned, he has such abilities. What can support this speculation is that after meeting Tsang Kwok-fan's face, Lee Soo-sung realized that his death period was imminent. With his crippled body, he wrote his autobiography almost at a rate of 7,000 words a day.
 
 On August 7, 1864, Lee Soo-sung had just completed his autobiography in the morning. In the evening, he was taken to court and executed. According to Zhao Liewen's later account, before his death, Li Xiucheng always said, “Be virtuous in the middle, don't forget, the present world is wrong, the next life is a picture report.” Yun Yun Yun seemed to be grateful to Zeng Guofan. Before his death, Lee Soo-sung talked and laughed a lot. Although he did not have a high level of education, he still wrote ten and a half sentences of absolute proverbs. Tsang Kwok-fan ordered: “Avoid late delays. It first spread to all provinces, and it was fortunate that they lost their lives.” Judging from the fact that Lee Soo-sung was spared, it is already quite difficult for Zeng Guofan. According to imperial court practice, these rebellious leaders have always been dealt with in a daunting manner. Tsang Kwok-fan executed Lee Soo-sung in Jinling. At least, Lee Soo-sung was spared physical torture and pain.
 
 Later field history records: On the day after Tsang Kwok-fan personally interrogated Lee Soo-shing, that is, on the evening of July 29, Tsang Kwok-fan had a long conversation with Tsang Kwok-chuen. For his ninth brother, Zeng Guoquan, Zeng Guofan has always been grateful. Since Xianfeng prepared the “Ji” camp to fight with Zeng Guofan in the 6th year, his younger brother has besieged the city and conquered the battle. Tsang Kwok-fan once asked Tsang Kwok-fan what he thought about Tsang Kwok-chuen's older brother. Tsang Kwok-fan's answer was: Killing people is like killing people and spending money like dirt. According to Tsang Kwok-fan, Tsang Kwok-chuen is considered a military genius, but he lacks wisdom in governing the country and the world, and appears quite immature. Now, to reduce the Xiang Army, the first thing we need to get is Zeng Guoquan's support. Ye Shi once described a conversation between the two brothers — after the two met, Tsang Kwok-kuen saw his brother's feelings and simply said, “The southeast half has no owner. Is my husband's intention?” This is actually just asking Tsang Kwok-fan, would he dare to rebel? Tsang Kwok-fan sank his face and said, “If you lose your head like this, you dare say it; it's really confusing! Zeng Guoquan seemed to disagree, arguing that the Governor of Liangjiang is you, the Governor of Fujian and Zhejiang is Zuo Zongtang, the Governor of Sichuan is Luo Bingchang, the Governor of Jiangsu is Li Hongzhang, and the three current governors and the five current governors are all members of the Xiang Army. The elder brother holds more than 200,000 elite soldiers of the Xiang Army. If necessary, he can beat Li Xiucheng, who is currently being arrested, and let him raise his arms and take 100,000 Taiping soldiers to rebel with you. In this way, we have more than 300,000 elite teachers on our hands. With these soldiers and horses, you can defeat the Beijing Division, restore the Han family's rivers and mountains, and become a generation of emperors. Big brother, who else is there for you!
 
 Tsang Kwok-fan shook his head and slowly said, “Lao Jiu, you know one or the other! In the Xiang Army, there are many people who can share suffering, but they cannot share wealth. Tso Tsung-tong was a hero of his generation. He wasn't willing to be subordinate when he was his mentor. Now he sits on an equal footing with me, can he be willing to bow down and praise the minister in front of me? I am sure that if something were to happen, the first person to set up an army to defeat me would be Zuo Zongtang; besides, Li Hongzhang, if all goes well, Li Hongzhang will always be my student; if things don't go well, Li Hongzhang will definitely fight back against Georgia. Li Hongzhang is so smart. He has a strong sense of fame and fortune. Of course, he will not easily lose his current power and position. Also, take a look at the current Xiang Army. After so many years of fighting, the elite have already been exhausted. Those excellent people have already died, and the army is in an old state; where can they fight again? Furthermore, Lee Soo-sung can raise his arms if he doesn't surrender, and his followers gather; once he surrenders, he's just a lackey; who still listens to him! A statement left Tsang Kwok-chuen speechless. Tsang Kwok-fan also said that when you become a soldier, you get rich when you raise a group of dogs. If you throw a bone, it will follow you; if someone throws a bigger bone, it may betray you. I look like this now, how many bones can I throw at them?
 
 Of course, this conversation between Tsang Kwok-fan and Tsang Kwok-chuen had elements imagined by Wild History. However, given the personalities and relationships between Tsang Kwok-fan and Tsang Kwok-chuen, the content of this conversation was entirely reasonable. Furthermore, after Zeng Guofan arrived in Jinling, many of his henchmen, including Peng Yulin, Zhao Liewen, and the famous scholar Wang Yunyun, who studied “The Study of the Emperor,” all discovered Zeng Guofan's roots one after another. They were very vague when they first spoke. Some took the opportunity to complain about unfair rewards from the imperial court, and others complained about Zeng Guofan because Emperor Xianfeng had a last word when he was about to die and promised to “defeat King Jinling.” But by the time the Zeng brothers had conquered Jinling, Empress Dowager Cixi and Emperor Tongzhi only spared in giving Zeng Guofan a “First Class Yiyong Marquess”. The difference between “Wang” and “Hou” was 180,000 miles! Tsang Kwok-fan was silent about the temptations of his subordinates and staff. He said nothing. Later, in order to avoid more and more trouble, Zeng Guofan simply wrote down a couplet with his own handwriting: “Relying on the sky, countless sea flowers, flowing water, high mountains, the heart knows” and hangs it in the middle hall of his residence in Jinling. In this way, everyone who comes to Zeng Fu to test the trend can already understand Zeng Guofan's feelings from this couplet.
