 Wu Gang is a character in Chinese mythology. In the TV drama “Chang'e,” Wu Gang cut down osmanthus trees to save white jade. Wu Gang's favorite Baiyu was murdered by four fake princesses. Wu Gang made a big fuss in the capital. He cut down the thousand-year-old laurel tree, changed life and death, and saved Baiyu, but Baiyu only had three days to live.
Legend has it that there is a 500-foot tall laurel tree on the moon. In the Han dynasty, there was a man named Wu Gang who made a mistake in the process of studying Xian. As a result, the Emperor was furious, detained him in Moon Palace, and ordered him to cut down the laurel tree in Moon Palace. He said, “If you cut down the Katsura tree, you can get magic skills.”
But every time Wu Gang cuts an axe, the axe rises, and the wounds on the tree heal instantly. Day after day, Wu Gang's wish to invade Gui is still unfulfilled. As a result, Wu Gang cut down laurels all year round in the Moon Palace, but he was unable to cut down trees. Therefore, posterity can see images of Wu Gang cutting down the laurel tree endlessly in the middle of the month. See “Tang Duan Dancheng Style Youyang Zazhuzan Roll for a Day”.
Legend has it that Wu Gang's wife had an affair with Boling. Wu Gang, the grandson of Emperor Yan, killed Boling in a fit of anger. This angered Emperor Yan, the Sun God, and was sent to the moon to cut down this immortal tree. However, the laurel tree was a perfect match. Every time Wu Gang cuts an axe, the branches and leaves cut down by the axe will grow back on the tree.
