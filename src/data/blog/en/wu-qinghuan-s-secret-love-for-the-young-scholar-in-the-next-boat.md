 The gentle spring breeze blows lightly on the quiet surface of the water. Half covering her face, the moon quietly climbed the mast and scattered the clear stars little by little on the waves of water and the bow of the boat. There is a vague sound of bamboo coming from afar. Is it a lover confiding her heart, or is it a mistress confiding in ghosts? In such a warm night, the young heart couldn't hold back the excitement. It was on a night like this that Wu Qingyi, an official who first started out in love, unwittingly confided a young girl's heart to Jiang Wenqing, a young talent in a neighboring boat. It all happened so naturally.
Wu Qinghuan spent his childhood and teenage years in Fuzhou. Her father, Wu Yunqing, was a former governor of Fuzhou, and has only one daughter, Qing Huan. Wu Yunqing is a Confucian official and attaches great importance to educating her baby girls. Following the compulsory courses that everyone's girlfriends learn, he made his young daughter familiar with books such as “Xiao Jing” and “The Commandment of Women” from a very early age. Xiao Qinghuan was smart and intelligent. She soon mastered these simple things, and also asked her father to step up. Wu Yunqing had no choice but to explain to her the classics such as the “Four Books” and “The Five Scriptures” that boys must study during their examinations. Xiao Qinghuan was actually able to quickly recite and integrate these chapters with esoteric content. Wu Yunqing was amazed and said, “If you were a boy, you must be a man of fame!”
After being enlightened by his father, Wu Qingqing became more and more motivated to read. He quietly found books such as “Tang Poets Law” and “Tang Poetry Collection” from his father's study room. He was fascinated by reading them. Slowly, he mastered the rhythm of poetry without a teacher, and began to quietly practice writing poetry himself. At first, she wrote scattered poems based on what she saw, heard, and occasional feelings, but they hadn't been combined into a single chapter. However, from these mere words, we can already see her splendid talent. For example, “Let the leaves appear in the garden, and the wind blows laurel flowers.” “Suga grass is green, and cherries fall red.” “Zhi Yan passed through the flower room, and Liu Wai Sheng didn't see the warblers.” “Spring water comes from the pouring basin, and personally irrigates caryophyllum flowers before the stairs.” Although the content of the poem is inseparable from the flowers in her small life, Ying Yan, the words are beautiful and have the charm of poetry.
By the age of 123, my understanding of things had become more and more comprehensive, and my skills in writing words and sentences had become more sophisticated, so I wrote quite a few complete psalms. Her girlfriend was fine, sitting back and cooking tea, and her laid-back and gentle mood led her to sing the song “Relaxation Under the Light”:
Cook fragrant tea in the small pavilion, and clean the curtains under the jade hooks;
The lights came out of the tripod, and the shadows fell down.
I want to get rid of the spring slumber and experience the twilight sorrow;
As a result of sitting for a long time, Gin Shi changed her makeup in the evening.
Although it is a casual poem, it is easy to tell and moving, showing Wu Qinghuan's talent and expertise.
As the years passed, the carefree little girl gradually grew into a young girl in love. The young lady from the official family, raised by an unknown girlfriend, had an increasingly bright appearance and quietly developing body in a private mirror. The spring romance turned into a faint sense of grievance. This mood vaguely appears in some of Wu Qinghuan's short poems:
At what point, plum blossoms grow in small pots, ice skin and jade bones accompanied by dusk;
Sitting in the window for a long time, I took time to remember the signs of the Moon.
Embroidered in the cold and never wanted to sleep, and listen to spring cuckoos on the pear branch;
In the Ming dynasty, it was Ching Ming Festival again, and I was sad to see people buying paper money.
Spring rain follows the wall of rheumatism powder, and garden flowers trickle down the human intestines;
How much do you know about red, grievous, and white; the water flowing through a long ditch is also fragrant.
The small window is free to embroider tonight, and sit across from the silver table;
No matter how far in the world has it reached the sky, the Moon Palace is still like a human being.
Wu Qinghuan wrote the poem for her own amusement, but unexpectedly, it was secretly transcribed by Ah Xuan, her gentle and careful handmaid, and brought outside to show off. Inadvertently, these poems went viral; they were actually copied by Chinese people in Fujian, and the name of Wu Qinghuan's poem spread along with it. The talented and good-looking girl has become a flower that the noble boys in central Fujian yearn for.
However, Wu's parents believed that their daughter was still young, and they couldn't bear to let her leave themselves too soon, so they never discussed marriage, making Wu Qinghuan an unattainable flower in the mirror.
In the early years of Wanli of the Myojin sect, Wu Yunqing was co-sponsored by the three directors of Fujian Province due to his remarkable achievements in governing Fuzhou and was promoted to the throne. At the beginning of the new year, Wu Yunqing brought his family north. People are refreshed when it comes to happy events, not to mention the season when the spring breeze brings warmth and the grass grows. While Wu Yunqing's family rushed to enjoy the mountains and water with great interest, they didn't notice any boating work at all. In March of Yangchun, I arrived at the border of Jiangnan. It was a season of dancing willows and apricot blossoms and spring rain. The scenery of Jiangnan was beautiful and pleasant. The Wu family hired a large boat in Huai Shui, placed a small purple flag “dedicated to entering Beijing” on the bow, and headed north majestically against the water. When the boat arrived at the Huai'an Gate, it stopped and waited for the wind to pass one after another. Dozens of boats, large and small, were parked around it. It seemed very lively, and the Wu family's big boat was particularly prominent in the boat group.
The boat was parked safely. Wu Yunqing and his wife brought some servants ashore for a tour. Since Ng Ching-hun is nearing her age, the eldest girl in Tachibana couldn't show up, so she and a few handmaids were left on the boat. Bored in the cabin and bored, Wu Qinghuan then opened the cabin window and welcomed a fresh breeze of the river. Her mood became much brighter. She pulled out a volume of poetry from her cage and sat down in front of the window to read it carefully.
Wu Qinghuan's every move in the cabin was extremely normal, but he unexpectedly made a young boy confused. That was Jiang Wenqing, a young boy in a neighboring boat. It turns out that there is a merchant ship parked next to the Wu family's ship. The owner is a silk merchant surnamed Jiang from Taiyuan. The merchant's son Wen Qing, is 16 years old. He had just passed the talent exam a few years ago. His father was happy for a moment, and he took him along the way when he came to Jiangnan to buy the goods. First, he was able to enjoy the beauty of Jiangnan's spring colors, and secondly, it also increased his knowledge. While he was sitting on the bow of the boat, he unwittingly discovered Wu Qingyi, who was on the boat. The girl raised her hands and feet, with a poetic charm, just like a rose swaying in the spring breeze. A sixteen-year-old teenager, Chunxin had just blossomed. An indescribable power drew his gaze tightly to the girl on the boat, and his heart floated away.
At this point, Wu Qinghuan's handmaiden, Ah Xuan, arrived at the side of the boat to clean up her laundry. Jiang Wenqing felt that an opportunity had come, so she pretended to be casual and flirted with Ah Xuan. He casually asked, “Where are you from and where are you going?” When Ah Xuan saw someone on the boat asking questions, she looked up. That person was young, good-looking, handsome, and friendly. It didn't seem like they were being mean. They only looked for someone to gossip when they were bored, so she told the truth about the Wu family's actions.
Jiang Wenqing answered the question. He was secretly happy. He also deliberately discussed topics such as weather and scenery that were not marginal, and also sent a few peaches he had brought to Ah Xuan. As Ah Xuan gradually became familiar with him, he took the opportunity to test; “Why can't you see your young lady's son-in-law?” Ah Xuan was a little shy and blushed and yelled, “How can my young lady have a husband!” As soon as Jiang Wenqing heard this remark, she felt deeply happy and secretly asked, “Is your young lady literate?”
This sentence actually came up with an idea. Ah Xuan proudly boasted, “More than just being literate; my young lady's poems were once popular in central Fujian!” This remark fell into the trap of Jiang Wenqing. He hurriedly picked up a pen and paper in the cabin, and asked Ah Xuan to say, “There are a few characters the kid couldn't understand. Please ask the girl to be the young lady to ask for advice. I mean, Jiang Xiucai from Taiyuan asked for advice!”
Ah Xuan turned to the cabin and handed over the paper to Wu Qingyi. After reading it, Wu Qinghuan seemed to have an enlightened smile, and still put a pen on the side of the letter—adding a comment for Ah Xuan to send it out, and laughed: “How can I not understand these characters!”
Ah Xuan not only sent Jiang Wenqing a paper, but she also relayed the young lady's words to him. Jiang Wenfervently thought: Now that I have guessed that it was meant to be teasing, but he is also willing to get caught, it seems like he is giving himself a chance! Immediately after entering the cabin, I wrote another poem and asked Ah Xuan to pass it on for me. The poem is written like this:
The sky is clear and the smoke is blazing, and Fan Jichun is full of red boats;
Meet Hebi Lanqiao Road and take a break from the waves.
This provocative poem came to Wu Qinghuan's hands. The rules in “The Commandment of Women” she read when she was a child came to her mind. She couldn't help but feel humiliated and angrily said, “If you meet someone in plain water, you can seduce people with flirty sentences. You lose your reading style!” Then she reprimanded Ah Xuan for not helping others to do such despicable things, and threatened to punish her parents. Ah Xuan was frightened and quickly knelt down and begged for forgiveness from the young lady. After Wu Qinghuan's anger subsided, he also took the opportunity to argue, “Looking at Jiang Gongzi, he doesn't look like an evil person. Admiring beautiful people is also a common occurrence!”
