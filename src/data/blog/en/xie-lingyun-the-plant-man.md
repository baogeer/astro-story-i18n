 Speaking of Xie Lingyun, I usually first mention that he is a poet, the founder of Chinese landscape poetry, and the originator of the Chinese landscape poetry school. Xie Lingyun loves the landscape, lingers in nature, and writes a large number of landscape poems. A person who loves nature must have a sensitive heart that loves plants. Xie Lingyun often revels in the world of plants. Various types of plants also give him endless creative inspiration and rich creative passion, making him a poet who lives with green mountains and water.
In the early fall of Song Yongchu in the 3rd year of the Southern Dynasty (422), Xie Lingyun came to Ren Taishou in Yongjia County. He planned to visit Yongjia Mountain and once visited Mount Chuitai. Mount Chuitai is the remainder of Donggong Mountain, which straddles the border between Zhejiang and Fujian. Qing Guangxu's “Yongjia County Zhi: Xushan” describes “Chuitai Mountain, 20 miles south of the city, flat as a platform. Legend has it that Prince Jin Chui Sheng”. You can see that this mountain is extraordinary. According to Xie Lingyun, “There are Takagiri on the platform, and they are all around. Yiyang lone paulownia, this is bad.” (“Journey to the Famous Mountains”). Paulownia, or sycamore, is a large deciduous tree. Compared to the unique sycamore tree that grows in Yishan, it is even worse. You can see the sycamore tree at the time of Mount Chuetai. The tree is tall, has a thick bust, and is impressive. Legend has it that it was also a good material for piano making. There has always been a folk legend called “Fengqi Sycamore,” which means that phoenixes only inhabit sycamore trees, so sycamores symbolize good fortune, good fortune, and good health and longevity. Today's Chuetai Mountain has the tomb of Sun Yirang. Sun Yirang was a famous master of Park Studies in the late Qing dynasty; monuments also include Chuishengtai and Drinking Crane Springs, etc., but it's hard to find tall and beautiful sycamore trees. The Tongxi Scenic Area, not far from Chuetai Mountain, is a Tongling Ancient Trail. It used to be the main passageway from Tongpu and Taoshan in Ruian to Wenzhou. The trees and stream flow along the way. Every spring and summer, sycamore flowers, paulownia flowers, and oil paulownia flowers bloom beautifully and beautifully against the young leaves.
“I say that being a governor is like good fortune, can make a country and a mountain like Yongjia.” (Song Su Yi, “On the topic of Xingzhou Chao Taishou: Opening a New Ancient East Pond”) Paulownia trees can also be seen everywhere in the Nanxi River in Yongjia, where Xie Ling Yun is in love. The breeze blows lightly, and the fragrance hits people, and before you know it, you'll be met with a beautiful rain of paulownia flowers. Xie Lingyun leisurely climbed from the lower reaches of the Nanxi River to the upper reaches on a boat. He wanted to see a strange mountain-shaped Mount Shimuro. He went ashore and didn't go far, but found the precious medicinal herb xanthine in a mountainous area. He wrote down “Mount Ishimuro Yellow Essence and Aster” (“Yumingshan Chronicles”). Xanthine leaves look like bamboo, and the root is like ginger. It is a plant in the lily family. The dried rhizome is a Chinese herbal medicine. Physicians have clearly studied its medicinal value: it nourishes qi and nourishes yin, strengthens the spleen and lungs, and also benefits the kidneys, and is in the same position as ginseng. Aster is an aster. Its root is also a Chinese herbal medicine and is a good medicine for clearing the lungs and relieving cough. Aster usually grows in the mountains. When it doesn't bloom, it's inconspicuous like an ordinary wild plant. Once blooming, the blue-purple flowers are bright and eye-catching.
Many plants are actually medicinal ingredients; xanthine was first discovered to satisfy hunger. In an age where food was scarce and food was scarce, many ordinary people would run to the mountains to find tender leaves, grass roots, flower petals, etc. to fill their stomachs. This is how xanthine happened to be dug up. Therefore, people also called it “fairy surplus food.” Aster is a plant with strong vitality. Commonly known as “return spirit grass” in folk medicine, it withers in winter and germinates and blooms in spring.
When Xie Lingyun was the governor of Yongjia County, he did not just indulge in the landscape. He had a compassionate heart that was sensitive to people's feelings, went deep into the grassroots level, inspected farmland, and vigorously developed people's livelihood undertakings. He advocated the development of agricultural mulberry and led ordinary people to grow mulberry trees on the outskirts of the city. To this end, he wrote the poem “Planting Mulberry”: “The poet Chen Tiaoke is also very beautiful. What is the cause of the previous works, and the results are determined by the later ones. Chang Peizhi and Fang Cheng are ashamed of the lessons learned by being rich. Fuyang Wu Jiayue, Yi Sang is idle. Close to Shulanfa, Changxingda Plaza. The wilderness begins at the beginning of the spring, and there are still traces of the hike. Let this grow and comfort me in overseas service.” In his poetry, he wrote not only about the historical significance of growing mulberry trees, but also about managing mulberry trees to prune branches; he also wrote about the relationship between plant growth and seasonal changes, as well as his own concerns about mulberry farmers and their ideals for governing the county. Mulberry trees had a unique position in ancient Chinese farming, and the ancients called agriculture “agricultural mulberry.” “On the Secret Life: Volume 1: Founding the People” has the phrase “the rich people take agriculture and mulberry as the foundation, and the tourism industry as the end.” During her tenure in Yongjia County, Xie Lingyun promoted agriculture, water conservancy, led academics, and had a sense of responsibility to “serve as an official for the benefit of one party”.
Xie Lingyun worked in Wenzhou for only over a year. During her time of being demoted, she wrote romantic and depressing poems, and unwittingly wrote many kinds of plants and plants. This not only showed the poet's aesthetic state of searching for the pure land of the soul, but also reflected the geographical environment and vegetation conditions in Yongjia County at the time. He used his poems to inject culture and energy into Yongjia's landscape, and also to calm his uneasy mind.
After Xie Lingyun left Yongjia, he returned to his hometown of Huiji Ning County (present-day Shangyu) and lived in seclusion. He still enjoyed the mountains and water, and was close to all kinds of plants. He discovered that everything in nature has its own time and space to grow, and learned about the growing regions, maturing seasons, and lifestyle habits of many wild plants. He walked through the beautiful world of plants, and had a great harvest. He completed his masterpiece “Mountain Abundance”. In “Shanjufu,” he mentioned a very rich variety of plants. Crops include hemp, millet, beans, cabbage, cabbage, fenugreek, radish, ginger, etc.; medicinal materials include persimmons, Tianmendong, maimendong, orchid, curly cedar, fulling, etc.; fruit trees include apricots, tangerines, peaches, dates, loquats, persimmons, etc., which were originally planted in the north; flowers include orchids, lotus flowers, etc., he wrote: “Aquatic plants include Algae, Zingiao, Celandine, and Rhododendron. Despite the beauty of the supplies, Dukfuqu is gorgeous. Sow the lush greenery, including the colorful colors of red shiki. It's hard to keep the scent of grievance; it's easy to keep it humble.” There are many beautiful plants, but only the lotus flower, which comes out of mud and does not stain, looks particularly gorgeous. Unfortunately, the refreshing scent of lotus flowers is difficult to stay on earth, and its sacred appearance easily fades away.

Xie Lingyun is definitely a “Mr. Plant.” He explores the mysteries of mountains and forests, discerns the wonders of hundred herbs, and has always been full of love for plants. The colorful variety of plants comforted Xie Lingyun's sentimental feelings of career setbacks and official disappointment. At the same time, the words he wrote also opened a door to the world of plants for readers.
Xie Lingyun has a sentence like this in the poem “Reward My Brother Hui Lian”: “Mountain peaches have red calyxes, and wild ferns are getting purple.” When the wild peach trees in the mountains have pink calyx, the wild ferns in the forest stick out their heads and develop purple leaf buds. Wild ferns are one of the oldest plant species on Earth, and can be found growing lushly everywhere, whether in sun-drenched pine groves or in the crevices of barren barren ravines. There is a record in “Shijing”: “Cheokbilanam Mountain says its fern. I'm worried that I haven't seen the gentleman. I saw it and stopped, I said it from the bottom of my heart.” The poem describes the feelings of a woman going up the mountain to pick ferns.
Climate change, rain lines move, and life in the world is changing. For example, ferns were mostly tall woody plants in the Paleozoic era, but today they have gradually evolved into short herbaceous plants. However, no matter how many years have passed, these plants, recited in Xie Lingyun's poems, still exude a bright cultural light, and still show a fresh and transcendent beauty today.
