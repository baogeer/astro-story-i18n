 During the Warring States period, Wei Wang sent the Ximenbao to make orders (present-day Linzhang County, Hebei). When the Ximenbao arrived in Yexian County, they saw that it was sparsely populated and desolate, so they asked some ordinary people what was going on.
A white-bearded old man said, “They all had trouble with Hebo marrying his daughter-in-law. He Bo is the god of the Zhang River. Every year, I have to marry a young and beautiful girl. If I don't send him, the Zhang River will flood all the fields and villages.”
Simon Bao asked, “Who said this?”
The old man said, “The witch said it. Every year, local stewards force people to pay money by hosting happy events for Hebo. They have to save millions of dollars every year, spend 2.3 million on happy events, and share the rest with the witch.”
Simon Bao asked, “Where did the bride come from?”
The old man said, “Whichever family's daughter is young and good-looking, the witch will take people to choose whichever house they choose. Rich people spend a little money and get away; people without money are unlucky. On the day Hebo married his daughter-in-law, they set up a reed seat by the Zhang River to dress up the girl.
Let her sit on a reed seat, put it in the river, and drift down the water. At first, the reed mat was still floating on the water, but after a while it sank. As a result, families with daughters have all gone abroad; the population here is getting smaller and smaller, and the place is getting poorer.”
Ximen Bao asked, “If Hebo married his daughter-in-law, would the Zhanghe River not have much water?”
The old man said, “It's still hair. The witch said that thanks to sending Hebo a daughter-in-law every year, it would have been much better if the Zhang River had not drained water.”
Simon Bao said, “When the witch said this, Heber is still a spirit! The next time he marries his daughter-in-law, let me know, and I'll give the bride away too.”
On the day Hebo married his daughter-in-law, the riverside was full of people. The Ximen Panther actually came with a guard. The witch and local steward hurriedly greeted them. The witch is over seventy years old, and she is followed by ten or so flirtatious female apprentices.
Simon Bao said, “Bring the bride and let me see how handsome she is.” The girl was brought in after a while. As soon as Simon Bao saw the girl's face full of tears, she looked back and said to the witch, “No, this girl isn't pretty. Please go to the river and say it to Hebo. Choose another beautiful one and send it to the witch in a few days.” After speaking, ask the guard to pick up the witch and throw her into the Zhang River. After waiting for a while, Simon Bao said, “Why hasn't Wu Zi come back? Let her apprentice host an event.” Another of her apprentices was thrown into the river. After waiting for a while, she threw her other apprentice into the river. After a while, Simon Bao said, “It seems that women can't do this. Please go talk to the local steward about it!” They said they wanted to ask the guard to throw the steward into the Zhang River. The supervisors in these places were frightened one by one. They hurriedly knelt down to ask for forgiveness, and their heads were all broken. Simon Bao said, “OK, wait and see.” After a while, he said, “Get up! Looks like Heber left them behind. Y'all go back!”
At this point, ordinary people were suddenly enlightened. It turns out that witches and local stewards all hurt people and defraud people of money. Since then, no one dared to mention Hebo's marriage to Quan. The Ximen Leopards mobilized ordinary people to excavate 12 large canals to channel water from the Zhang River into the fields and irrigate crops. Since then, both sides of the Zhang River have had good harvests year after year.
