 Consort Jiang Mei is a famous woman in the legends of the people of Putian. In fact, in history, the story of Mei Fei has been widely spread. It has attracted the attention of many famous painters, and is often used as an important subject for her creations. Qiu Ying, a famous painter from the Ming Dynasty (father, number Shizhou, from Taicang, Jiangsu) once painted a “Mei Fei Photo Map”, which is a masterpiece passed down from generation to generation. This work spread to the early 50s, and there was a moving story with Master Xu Beihong.
At the beginning of the 50s, an art exhibition was held in Beijing, and Xu Beihong visited. Xu saw a portrait painting “Mei Fei's Photograph” and saw at a glance that it was a masterpiece by Ming Dynasty master Qiu Ying. At this point, a foreign ambassador to China was bargaining with the owner to buy this picture. Xu Beihong didn't want this national treasure to go abroad, so he resolutely stepped forward and said, “I bought it, no return.” Originally, the ambassador to China had a personal relationship with Xu Beihong, and discussions have already been made to invite him to the ambassador's country; now the ambassador blames Beihong for not having enough friends and stealing his love. In one fell swoop, he cancelled inviting Xu Beihong to host an art exhibition, but Xu Beihong is very pleased and doesn't regret it at all. Xu Beihong truly regarded the country's famous paintings as his own life. In this case, he showed his strong love for the motherland and his distinct personality of adhering to the greatest extent.
