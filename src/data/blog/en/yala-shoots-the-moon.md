The sunburned skin turned red, and the crop seedlings were burnt, so of course, ordinary people couldn't live their lives. There is a hunter couple. The woman's name is Nie, and the man's name is Yara. The couple were worried about the people. Nie said, “You say the moon had nine angles, and all the crops died when it got sunburned! You're a good shooter, can't you shoot it down?” Yara said, “The sky is too high to shoot.” Nie said, “You stand on the top of the mountain and shoot in the morning!”
This morning, Yara aimed at the moon and fired an arrow. I shot it in the morning, but I couldn't shoot the moon. Yara was worried. A large mountain behind her suddenly split. An old bearded grandpa came out and said to Yara, “If I want to shoot the moon, catch the tiger in Kitayama and the big deer in Nanshan, use antlers as arrows and tiger tails as strings, then I can shoot the moon all around.” After speaking, the cracks in the mountains closed again, and I don't know where the bearded old man went.
Yara came home and told Nie about the original incident: “I shot one morning and it went to nothing. A long-bearded old man ignited me, saying, “Whoever wants to shoot the moon must catch a tiger in Kitayama and a wild deer in Nanshan, and use antlers as arrows and tiger tails to shoot the moon all around.” Nie said, “You're a good shooter, so why don't you just shoot the big deer in Nanshan and the tiger in Kitayama?” “How do you know, those animals are so old, their skin is so thick that they can't shoot.” “So what?” “If you want to catch the tiger in Kitayama and the deer in Nanshan, you have to form a big net to block their path before you can catch them.” “What do you connect to the net?” Yara said, “I'm worried.” Nina said, “Then make my hair!”

Yara also used antlers to carve arrows and tiger tails as strings to shoot an arrow at the moon. This arrow is like a sword; in one go it shoots the nine angles of the moon round off and becomes a round moon. But the moonlight still sunburned people quite a bit. Nina said, “What should I do about this?” Yara said, “If you had a large piece of brocade, tied to an arrow, and blinded the moon, it wouldn't have such a toxic light.” Nie said, “I happened to be weaving a silk brocade, and it's still on the loom. I woven a Thoreau tree, a white rabbit, and a flock of Aries on it. You can use it!”
Yara cut the large brocade on the loom, tied it to an arrow, and shot it on the moon with a “knock” sound, and blindsided the moon. Since then, there has been a Thoreau tree on the moon, and white rabbits and Aries under the tree. Her hair fell to the ground, and Yara grabbed her hair and climbed up.
