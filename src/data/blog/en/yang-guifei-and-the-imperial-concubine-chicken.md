 “Guifei Chicken” is a famous dish from Suzhou. Its method is to use fat chicken wings and stew them with shiitake mushrooms, mussels, young bamboo shoots, and bell peppers. The characteristic of royal chicken is that it has a bright color, a combination of green, milk yellow, black, and white, which is pleasing to the eye. It is tender and fresh when eaten, and has a pungent aroma. It is a rare dish.
As you can tell from the name, a royal chicken must be related to any royal princess. In fact, this is true. It turns out that this noble lady is not a stepping up person; it is Yang Yuhuan, who “looks back and smiles at Bai Meisheng; there are no colors in the Rokugong fan.”
More than 1,200 years ago, it was during the reign of Emperor Tang and Li Longji. When Lee Lung-gi first became emperor, he appointed Yao Chong and Song Jing to govern the country, bringing the Tang dynasty's social and economic development to its peak. It is known in history as the “heyday of the Kaiyuan era.” However, in his old age, Emperor Tang Ming was extremely faint, and the Tang dynasty fell from the peak of development. Pampering Yang Guifei is an important sign of his poor old age.
Yang Yuhuan originally fell in love with her after Lee Lung-gi had a crush on her and was unable to marry her, and she was crowned a royal concubine. After having Yang Guifei, Tang Minghuang spent the whole day and night looking for fun with her, handing over important national events to Li Linfu, Yang Guozhong, and other gang of rapists.
One day, Tang Minghuang drank alcohol and sang with Yang Guifei again, leaving her spirits upside down. After he got drunk, he even called “Good wine, good wine! It was a pleasure to eat!” Yang Guifei also obsessively shouted, “I want to fly to the sky!” Tang Ming made a mistake because he was drunk. He thought the queen wanted to “fly to the sky,” and immediately asked the eunuch to order the Imperial Dining Room to do it. After listening to the emperor's sacrament, the chefs looked at each other: they never said there was a “flying to the sky” dish. But if the emperor says it, you have to do it if he can say it. The chefs used their brains to meditate. One chef said that when eagles fly high, they probably “fly to the sky”! As soon as everyone heard it, they quickly made two braised eagles. But as soon as I tasted it, I discovered that hawk meat was sour! So the chefs restarted their minds. Among the chefs, there is a famous chef from Suzhou called “Su Kong”. He thought that chicken's scallops are the most tender, and using it to “fly to the sky” would definitely be delicious. As soon as he told everyone what he thought and everyone listened to it, it was just that. They scrambled to find a few young chickens, cut off their wings, and stew them with shiitake mushrooms, mussels, bamboo shoots, and bell peppers, even if they “flew to the sky.” As soon as everyone saw this dish, the color was fresh and fragrant, and only then did they make up their mind.
The eunuch “flew up to the sky” in front of the princess, and the noblewoman's eyes immediately lit up when the bar had woken up. Tang Minghuang also tasted it, and was even amazed. He was busy asking the eunuch what kind of dish it was. The eunuch hurriedly said, “Fly to the Sky” that His Majesty had just ordered. It was only at this point that Emperor Tang was reminded of the sacrament when he was drunk; it was inevitable that he would be embarrassed. At this point, Yang Guifei, who was enjoying “Flying to the Sky,” said, “This dish is colorful, tender, and fragrant. They are all similar to my Guiyang City Princess. Let's just call it 'royal chicken'!” As soon as Tang Minghuang heard it, he even claimed it was OK.
Later, when Su Kong went back to his hometown, he brought back the cooking method of “royal chicken” to Suzhou. This dish has also been passed down from generation to generation in the Suzhou region.
