Yang Yuhuan: Hao Taizhen, the noble concubine of Tang Xuanzong and Li Longji. A native of Yongle, Puzhou, the daughter of Yang Xuanyan, the owner of Shuzhou. Yang has a rich posture, good singing and dancing, and vocal rhythm. She was first adopted as the queen of Xuanzong's 18th son, Shou Wang Li Mao. Yang was 16 years old at the time. In 740, Xuanzong ordered Yang to go out of the family, and five years later, Yang was canonized as a royal consort. As a result, the Young family is distinguished. Empress Yang lived a luxurious life, and Xuanzong ignored politics ever since, which eventually led to the Anshi Rebellion. Consort Yang was forced to take her own life in Ma Yupo. There were many interpretations of this period of history by future generations.
In my opinion, Yu Jiaolong in “Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon” is an excellent portrayal of a new human being: smart, cute, unmotivated, capricious, and ruthless. She doesn't need any bad intentions, but because she is selfish and ruthless, hurting others is often comparable to a huge flood. You still don't know what to do with her, because she's simple, because she's innocent. Wang Xiaobo wrote in “The Joy of Thinking”: “I can't forgive myself if I am deceived by big evil people, mentally balanced, and deceived by kind people.” That's the truth.
If there is the term “red face and evil water,” I think Yu Jiaolong is the kind of woman: she has no subjective malice, but she has actually brought objective evil results. Instead, they painstakingly planned their fate like Wu Zetian, Cixi, and even Cao Qiqiao; they were so aggressive and evil that they became conspirators.
The person with the most disgusting temperament is probably Yang Guifei.
Tang Xuanzong, Lee Lung-gi, was a very active emperor in the early days. He promoted excellence, and the economy and culture of the entire society developed greatly. Historically known as the “Kaiyuan heyday,” it can be said that it was the peak of the prosperity of the entire feudal dynasty. At this point, he met Yang Yuhuan, who loved the country. As soon as the gongs and drums sounded loud, Xuanzong's soul was taken by this fat woman. At the time, Yang Yuhuan was his daughter-in-law, Princess Shou. He insisted that her son and Yang Yuhuan get divorced, let Yang leave the family, and get married again. Since then, “there are 3,000 beautiful people in the harem, and 3,000 pampering people all in one.” Actually, it's not accurate. There are 40,000 beautiful people in the harem, and 40,000 people love them all. Lee Lung-ki and Yeung Yuhuan are musicians, and the other is a dancer. The two developed a like-minded revolutionary relationship, which is in line with the view of love in the eyes of the proletarian class. They made outstanding and indelible contributions to the singing and dancing industry in the Tang Dynasty. At the same time, because Yang Guifei loved lychee, they also vigorously developed cultivation techniques and transportation techniques for this type of crop. ——But the problem is that Tang Xuanzong is an emperor. His job is not to be the CEO or technical consultant of an entertainment company, but to manage the country. He drove all dynasties to indulge in sound, and finally caused chaos in Anshi.
Why did successive emperors have so many concubines? In addition to being able to please the emperor with his voice as he pleases, I think one reason is very important: having countless beautiful women by his side makes it harder for him to fall in love with women, which ensures that women are only a tool for the royal family, not an equal lover. Why can't Tang Xuanzong think of such a smart person? As soon as Yang Guifei was jealous, he was frightened, and once in a while he drove her away and had to miss her. This love caused quite a bit of trouble. Yang Guifei's older brother, Yang Guozhong, and his three older sisters were all sealed wives, and the Yang family had power over the world. Of course, the emperor was stealthy once in a while, and was a bit unclean with Consort Mei and her three little aunts. However, in her prime, Yang Fei was not a vegetarian. She had an unusual relationship with Ning Wang, the emperor's younger brother. At the time, Fan Yang Festival made Mount An Lu highly trusted by Xuanzong. The emperor called it a “child,” and Yang Guifei also “washed the child” for this man who was more than ten years older than herself. Xuanzong was actually happy to appreciate Mount An Lu. This extremely ugly “belly hangs over the knee” An Lushan even stayed with Yang Guifei's girlfriend all night long. Everyone knew what had happened; the emperor was still silly! No wonder there is a saying “Tang turtle.”
At last, An Lushan rebelled. At this point, even the Imperial Forest Army, which is closest to the emperor, is in trouble: if you don't kill Princess Yang, we won't protect you! For the sake of his life, Tang Xuanzong had to let the noble lady commit suicide and die. Although the Anshi Rebellion has subsided, this was a turning point in the rise and fall of the Tang Empire, and the beginning of the decline of China's entire feudal civilization over a thousand years.
It was only through the decline of one country and one civilization that Li Yang's “love for the country” was achieved. Unfortunately, it didn't give them a moment of stability, which was less than that of Fan Liuyuan and Bai Ryusu.
To be fair, Yang Guifei is naive and indulgent, and doesn't have much political ambition, but she did hurt the balance of political power and hurt people all over the world. She is not without responsibility for the “Ma Yupo Mutiny.” This kind of person was born to be a loving animal and cannot be raised in an ordinary family; in an emperor's family, it is even more necessary to nurture her all over the world. It's not enough to just be a human being without bad intentions.
