 Brief analysis
This poem describes the love story between Emperor Tang Ming and Princess Yang Guifei. Only the first sentence uses Tang [Han Dynasty]. All other place names and people are real. The poem was written in Xian Zongyuan and the winter of the 6th year (806). The poet Zheng Renwu (present-day county governor) visited Xianyu Temple with Chen Hong and Wang Zhi. At the suggestion of Wang, he met with Chen Hong to write a work about the love story between Xuanzong and the pet concubine that was currently being shared by the public. Chen wrote the poem “Song of Eternity”, and wrote this poem in white. The poem began as a comedy and turned into a tragedy. Although the author's intention was to “punish a stunner,” it became an ode to love. The most successful aspect of this epic poem is its lyricism. It uses only a few refined sentences to explain the past, and the intense rendering details the intensity of the love between Emperor Tang Ming and Yang Guifei and the feelings of the two after the death of the noble lady. There are quite a few famous quotes that are deeply moving and have been passed down for thousands of years. The dramatic and mythical descriptions and strong romantic colors in the poem are also the reasons why it has a strong artistic appeal.
