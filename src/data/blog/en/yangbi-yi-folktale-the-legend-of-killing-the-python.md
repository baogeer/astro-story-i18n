 Once upon a time, there was a person doing business as a vendor.
One day, he passed by a mountain. There was a woman who was constantly singing mountain songs. Her voice was melodious, and her voice was clear and charming. A normal man had long been captured by this song, not to mention a single man who was far from his hometown and had traveled thousands of miles away. How could he resist this temptation, and let go of his voice. After singing for most of the day, the vendors saw that the sun was about to set, so they hurriedly rushed to a nearby village to stay.
He asked from the east to the west, and from the south to the north, no one let him stay. They said that he had sung with the devil snake, and that the devil snake would eat him at night, lest the owner get involved.
Vendors had no choice but to stay in a footstool house on the side of the village. They borrowed a few large pots, all filled with boiling water, and waited for the demon snake to arrive.
In the middle of the night, a gust of wind blew in, and the door of a large blood bowl with open feet reached in. They didn't wait for the demon snake to inhale. The merchant threw the large pot with boiling water in its mouth, and didn't wait for the snake to reflect that the second and third large pot had been thrown into its mouth. I don't know how good it is for a snake to get burned, and it's not good to swallow it, nor throw up. As the saying goes, “Fiery people are hardworking,” the demon snake pulled the snake head out of the door of the house in a panic. It swallowed three large pots with boiling water in annoyance. The demon snake rolled with pain and struggled to death, causing the entire cottage to shake mountains, strong winds, and lightning thunder, frightening everyone. I don't know what happened.
The next day, the vendor went to the village and told everyone that the monster snake had been killed by him. Men, women, and children from all over the village gathered at the foot house to watch the bustle and watch the demon snakes. That demon snake was too thick for two people to hold. It was as long as two or three houses. People use axes to cut the belly of demon snakes, which contain lots of gold, silver, jade earrings, and jade bracelets. Vendors kept some of these valuable items for themselves. Some of them were distributed to people who lent large pots, and most of them were distributed to people in the stockade.
You can't sing with a stranger, let alone agree to someone who says your name casually. The elderly always teach their children this way.
