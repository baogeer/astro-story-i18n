 Legend has it that in ancient China, there was a type of monster called the “year”. It had long tentacles and was ferocious and abnormal. “Years” have been at the bottom of the sea for many years. They only climb ashore every New Year's Eve, devouring livestock and harming lives. Therefore, every New Year's Eve, people in villages and villages take refuge deep into the mountains with their young to avoid being harmed by the “New Year” beast. On New Year's Eve this year, people in Taohua Village were helping the elderly and taking refuge in the mountains. An old man begging from outside the village only saw him with crutches, arms in his pockets, and flowing silver whiskers, looking like a star. Some of the townspeople sealed the windows and doors, some packed up their luggage, some raised cows and chased sheep, and people everywhere shouted, and there was a scene of haste and panic. At this point, who still cares about this old man who is begging. ; Only the old woman at the east end of the village gave the old man some food and advised him to go up the mountain to avoid the “old” beast. The old man laughed: “If my mother-in-law lets me stay at home overnight, I will definitely drive away the 'old' beast.” The wife took a close look and saw that he had a very young face, a strong spirit, and extraordinary energy. However, she continued to persuade and beg the old man to laugh without saying a word. My grandmother was helpless, so she left her home and went up the mountain to take refuge. In the middle of the night, the “New Year” beast broke into the village. It discovered that the atmosphere in the village was different from previous years: the old woman's house at the east end of the village had large red paper on the door, and the house was brightly lit with candles. The “Year” beast shivered all over its body, and the devil shouted. “Year” glared at the mother-in-law's house for a moment, then screamed and rushed past. Approaching the entrance, there was a sudden “bang bang” explosion in the courtyard. “Nian” shuddered all over his body and was afraid to move forward. It turns out that the “year” is most afraid of red, fire, and explosions. At this point, the mother-in-law's house opened wide open, only to see an old man in a red robe laughing out loud in the hospital. “Year” was shocked and ran away in a hurry. The next day was the first day of the New Year. People who had taken refuge were surprised to see that the village was safe and sound. At this point, the wife suddenly realized and hurriedly told the townspeople about the promise of begging the elderly. The townspeople all gathered at the grandmother's house, only to see red paper on the door of the mother-in-law's house, and a bunch of unburnt bamboo was still “popping”, and a few red candles in the house still had an afterglow...; To celebrate the advent of good fortune, the townspeople all changed their new clothes and put on new hats to say hello to their family and friends.
People discovered that the “New Year” was very afraid of firecrackers and red things, so every time it arrived on New Year's Eve, people would wear red to celebrate, and put off firecrackers when they were old and welcoming the new year. Later, “New Year” never dared to come again. This is the legend of New Year's Eve. The day after 30 is the Spring Festival on the first day of the Lunar New Year, also known as the “New Year.”
