 I don't know what era it is. On the edge of the Yangliang River, there is a village called Zhi Cun. A farmhouse couple lived in the village and raised a girl named Ah Xiang. The girl grew up to the age of 17 and was a beautiful woman who fell in love with Dou Chu Kai. The name of Girl Ah Xiang spread all over Yangjiancheng, and there was an endless flow of people visiting her home to ask for family. The front of his house was as lively as rushing to the street.
Meanwhile, in Heifung Cave in Cangshan Mountain, there is a lawless king who doesn't do anything wrong. He had a yellow face, red eyebrows and blue eyelids, and a fleshy face. One day. There was a thunder of the mountain. He went out of the cave to watch. It turned out that someone was seeking a daughter-in-law in Kikumura by the Xueshan River. When he saw that the new daughter-in-law was very beautiful, he sacrificed a magical mixed sword, whirlwind blew on the flat ground, shielded the sky from the sun, flew sand and stones, and ingested the new daughter-in-law into the Batu Caves. He wants to hang out with his new daughter-in-law, then he sucks the new daughter-in-law's blood and eats the new daughter-in-law's meat. This is his usual method. I don't know how many beautiful new daughters-in-law are buried under his claws.
At this time, Cao Kou Wang's new daughter-in-law was Ah Xiang. Her family lived in Zhi Village by the Yangliang River. She is beautiful and kind, can sing and dance well, and is particularly fond of playing the bamboo flute. In her dream, Ah Xiang got a bamboo flute given to her by her old mother Guanyin. Guanyin's mother said, “There is a small hole on the back of this bamboo flute. It's a magic hole. As long as you plug the magic hole and meditate for three more sounds, the sound of the flute will inspire excitement, like thousands of troops and horses, and mountains call a tsunami, and reduce the magic power of demons. If you cooperate with a '' hitting sound, you can also defeat demons.” However, the sudden attack by King Cao Kou caught Ah Xiang by surprise. As soon as the wind gusts were overcast, they were caught in the Batu Wind Cave. Well, the bamboo flute was still in her marriage chest.
Since falling into the village of Chaokou Wuying Village, Ah Xiang has been crying and crying every day. She has been crying and crying all day long. She was tired of crying this night, and slowly closed her eyes in sorrow and sorrow. I only heard fairy music, but I saw Guanyin's old mother driving up from the top of Cangshan Mountain, looking lovingly at her and saying, “Ah Xiang girl, your cries and people's grievances have already shocked the sky. Don't worry; early tomorrow morning, you only need to do what I say to catch thieves and get rid of them!” After speaking, let's just leave with a breath of fresh air. Cao Kou Wang woke up Ah Xiang's thunderous snoring. It turned out to be a dream. She carefully thought about her dream, and the more happy she thought about it, the more happy she was, so she carefully calculated how to capture the thief.
Cao Kou Wang has already transformed Batu Caves into a cave room with big red happy characters, and he himself has also transformed himself into a handsome and chic new girl. Cao Kou Wang ordered Ya Huan to serve wine and shocked the new daughter-in-law. He looked at the new daughter-in-law, pretended to be Sven, and raised a glass and said, “Dare to ask the little lady's name, who is her name, and where does her family live?” Ah Xiang Ming knows that he is the legendary devil, the Hermit King, but since she was enlightened by Guanyin's old mother, she has a strong sense and is not afraid of demons. She raised her glass and said, “The name of the slave family is Ah Xiang. They live in Zhi Village by the Yangyi River. The king abducted his family here. I don't know what that meant?” Cao Kou Wang has abducted countless people, yet I've never met someone who doesn't cry or make noise, and is calm and at ease. I can't help but secretly wonder and persevere and say, “You misunderstood what doesn't abduct, Ah Xiang girl. King Ben admired the beauty of Ah Xiang Girl. He specially invited Ah Xiang Girl to Dongfu to have a hundred years of friendship with Ah Xiang Girl. Come and have a drink, King Ben respects Ah Xiang.” Ah Xiang smelled a smell of blood in the cave, and saw skulls and white bones. She kept quiet and coaxed Wang Zhouxuan. She can't eat anything from King Cao to prevent King Herb from taking drugs. She said, “The king's kindness has given it to the slave family; the slave family won't drink alcohol. You should honor your ancestors with this glass of wine.” As I said, I poured alcohol on the floor. I saw white smoke rising from the ground. Cao Kou Wang saw that the drug had been taken down. He was annoyed and angry. He was about to have a seizure. He only listened to Ah Xiang and said, “Don't you want to listen to the slave family singing a song to help King Ya Xing?” Cao Kou Wang had no choice but to persevere and falsely said, “OK, OK, I didn't expect that Girl Ah Xiang would also sing.” Ah Xiang sang while dancing. With her gentle singing voice and graceful dance posture, I could see Cao Kou Wang's mouth flowing straight through her mouth. Ah Xiang suddenly stopped and sighed, “Alas, it's really not interesting to sing and dance like this.”
Cao Kou Wang was on the rise and asked, “So what?” Ah Xiang said, “It would be nice to have a bamboo flute. If you blow a bamboo flute, then it sounds good and looks good.” Cao Kou Wang was embarrassed to say, “Where can I find a bamboo flute for you?” Ah Xiang said, “I have a bamboo flute in a mahogany box, and the mahogany box is placed at the foot of the bed in the cave house in Changcun. Otherwise, I'll go get it.” Cao Kou Wang said, “Why use Ah Xiang Girl to work hard; stay still and don't walk around; Ben Dawang will come when he goes.” King Cao Kou went outside the cave to lay a magical mixed sword, and a wind blew straight to the cave house of Ah Zhu and Ah Xiang in Chang Village. Ah Xiang was secretly happy. As soon as she got the bamboo flute given to her by Guanyin's mother, she would have a treasure against King Cao Kou. However, she didn't expect it; Azhu helped her out of trouble. A bamboo can do good work, and is best at making baskets. Atake once dreamed that Guanyin's old mother said to him, “As long as you hoard three drops of blood from your middle finger, meditate three more times, and hit hard to stock up, then hoarding is magical. If you cooperate with the exhilarating sound of a bamboo flute, you can defeat demons.” Since being instructed by Kannon's mother in her sleep, Atake has carefully prepared a storage basket the size of a pen holder and carried it with her wherever she goes, just in case she needs it. However, on the day of his marriage, he didn't carry a hoard with him and let King Cao Kou take a picture of Ah Xiang. He really couldn't regret it. Ah Zhu is ready. He dropped three drops of blood from his middle finger to the bottom of the hoard to keep an eye out for attacks by King Coco at all times. When it was overcast and ferocious, Azhu meditated three times, patted hard to stock up, and made a “pooppoff” sound. Seeing that the wind suddenly stopped, the mahogany suitcase that Ah Xiang had married fell out of the cave window.
Taku said, “Thankfully, they were slapped and stocked up in a timely manner. Otherwise, this mahogany box would have been taken by King Cao Kou.” Ah Zhu went to the government office to ask for help. However, the government army had already learned how powerful Chaokou Wang was in magic, and no one dared to fight against King Caokou.
Azhu dripped blood from his middle finger on the bottom of ten large hoards. Ah Zhu ordered his family and neighbors to always be wary of the attack by King Cao Kou. As soon as there was any movement, they turned the hoard upside down and beat hard to stock up. After the arrangement was made, Ah Zhu brought a small basket and went to Cangshan alone to search for Ah Xiang. Cao Kou Wang has never been able to be broken by anyone. So why does Ah Zhu's “poo poo” sound have so much mana? He was puzzled and asked Ah Xiang, “What treasure is Ah Bamboo? It was able to break my mana.” However, Ah Xiang doesn't know what treasures Ah Chu has, just as Ah Chu doesn't know that Ah Xiang has treasures. Both of them wanted to tell each other their secrets and surprise each other on a candlelight night in the cave. When Ah Xiang heard Cao Kou Wang say that Ah Zhu has treasures, she is both happy and worried. The good news is that Atake also has treasures that can break Kou Wang's mana. What I'm worried about is that I can't get my hands on the bamboo flute. How can I fight against King Zao Kou? Ah Xiang naively said, “No, he is a craftsman. What treasure can he have?” Cao Kou Wang said, “He just slapped the basket of rice crackers and made a 'pop-pop' sound, and my mana was destroyed. It really infuriates me.” Ah Xiang was secretly happy and thought to herself, “If you can make a '' sound with my flute sound, wouldn't we be able to surrender to King Coco? Right now, the most important thing is to quickly get in touch with Azhu.” She came up with the idea, “Dawang, why don't we stay near Chang Village. I'm looking for an opportunity to dive into Ah Zhu's house and give Ah Zhu's treasure to the king. Why doesn't Dawang have any worries?” Cao Kou Wang laughed, “Hehe, you think I'm a three-year-old kid? Why should I take your word for it? This king is not afraid of enemies. King Ben now wants your body, drinks your blood, and eats your flesh.” When I said that, I turned to Ah Xiang. Ah Xiang was shocked. Originally, she was shouting “help,” but she made a “boo boo boo” sound. I saw that Cao Kou Wang retreated successively and couldn't get close to Ah Xiang's body. Cao Kou Wang has never been so embarrassed in front of a woman. He doesn't know what's wrong? He looked at Ah Xiang, who looked like a flower, and was so frightened that he immediately changed his mind and said, “Ah Xiang girl, King Ben is kidding; you don't need to be afraid.” Ah Xiang felt unbelievable about what just happened. She thought, “It must have been Guanyin's old mother who secretly saved her.” Ah Xiang had Guanyin's mother in her heart. She felt that she was full of strength, and strengthened her determination to subdue King Cao Kou. She returned to normal and said, “The king, with your magic power, want the slave's body, drink the slave's blood, and eat the slave's meat. It's not a piece of cake. The slave family fell into the hands of the king; whatever the king wanted, it all happened sooner or later. However, did the king think that if the king were to end up with the slave family now, no one would be able to take down the enemy for the king. Moreover, in the end, Azhu will oppose the king, and maybe even the king will be destroyed. If the king followed the slave family's words, it would take a long time plan and wait for the slave family to get the treasure for the king, then it won't be too late for the slave family. The slave family all thought about the king, and they wanted the king to think twice.” Ah Xiang is so clever. Cao Kou Wang heard it, and she said it bluntly. Cao Kou Wang thought to himself, “Ah Xiang is in my hands. It's impossible to escape. Why not use her to get rid of my heart.” Although Cao Kou Wang thought so, he was wary of Ah Xiang. He said, “If you can actually get the treasure for this king, then the king will let you go. However, you have to say the eight characters of your birth; otherwise, this king will lay down a magical mixed sword and burn you to ashes.” Ah Xiang knew that Cao Kou Wang wanted to use the eight characters of her birth to control her, so she signed up for a vacation. In order to verify the truth or falsehood, King Cao Kou decided to use the spells. Ah Xiang pretended to roll around all over the place, crying out for forgiveness for the king's life. Cao Kou Wang tried this a few times; he thought he had control of Ah Xiang in his hands. Cao Kou Wang brought Ah Xiang to the broken brick kiln at the head of the village of Miba Village. The interior was lit up and divided into two rooms and one living room. The exterior was still a broken brick kiln. Ah Zhu looked for Gougou Qingqing in Cangshan, but he didn't find Ah Xiang. Turns out, King Cao Kou was a treacherous and cunning guy. As soon as he got out of the hole, he hid the hole. All of those Ya Huan maids were made up of him on a makeshift basis. As many as he wanted, they were ready to go. Ah Zhu didn't know that Ah Xiang had returned to the vicinity of Chang Village, and she was still searching day and night on Cangshan Mountain.
