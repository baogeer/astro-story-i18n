 At a time when heaven and earth are mixed together, and day and night are difficult to discern, Fairy Ya used two hands to separate the closely connected heaven and earth. She also used advanced technology to make 70,000 tall bronze and iron columns to support the sky. Heaven and earth were built, but it was desolate and empty, so Ya Ewa transformed the leaves into human form. Then seal it in a crock pot. Originally, she was only able to open it after 10 days, but she couldn't bear to be lonely. On the 7th day, she opened it. The person who jumped out of it was not only short and thin, but also had an empty chest. These people are unable to cultivate land; they are threatened by animals when they go up the mountain to hunt; they are also bullied by lazy cats when going down the river to catch fish. She persevered and made the animals eat them. Then they created people again. This time, Ya Wa was patient and waited for ten days. This group of people grew up when they saw the wind, and finally dominated the world and became owners.
