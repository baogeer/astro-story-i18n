 A long time ago, there were more than a dozen families living at the foot of Longshan Mountain. There is an old man with the surname Yu called Uber. He is particularly fond of playing chess. He has two daughters and a son. The son's name is You Yun, and he hasn't started a family yet. The family is hardworking and has had a pretty good life.
In the summer of this year, Uber went to the mountains to repair trees. Because of the hot weather, after not much work, I was sweaty, thirsty, tired, and hungry, so I thought of taking a break at the big acacia tree in the southwest. When I got to the bottom of the tree, I saw two old white-haired men playing chess there. They had a teapot and two vegetable bowls next to them, so they walked over and looked.
Since he also often plays chess with others at home, he is very familiar with the chess game, so he can't help but talk to the two old people, and sometimes give them pointers. The two white-haired old men were also very enthusiastic about him and made Uber drink a bowl of tea. When I go down my stomach with an Uber cup of tea, I just feel a fresh fragrance rising from my stomach. If I'm hot, tired, or hungry, don't mention the feeling of being relaxed and at ease all over my body. Soon, in front of the stone table where the two old men were playing chess, I was confused and felt that the clouds on the side of the mountain were blurry, dark and white, and suddenly dark, and suddenly talked; in the ground under the mountain, it was all yellow and green. By the time he took a break, the two old men hadn't finished playing chess yet. The more I watched it, the more it was a bit creepy, and I couldn't even understand the final chess. As soon as I thought that my son You Yun would go to Jining Province tomorrow, I said hello to the two old people and hurriedly went down the mountain.
As soon as Uber got to the foot of the mountain, I felt that the path was getting worse and not the same as before. He doesn't know any local people, and no one knows him. According to my own impression, I found my own village, but the village is larger than yesterday, and there are more people than yesterday. A village full of villages looked for their own home. From the south to the north, from the west to the east, they found none. At this point, an old man with white hair came over. He hurriedly greeted the old man and asked him his name, age, and why he lived here. The old man told him that his last name is You. His ancestors have lived in this village for generations, and he is 93 years old today. Uber asked the old man again: Where did You Yun go? The old man told him: You Yun is his old man's grandpa. The old man was surprised and asked Uber, “How do you know my old man?” Uber didn't answer, so he went up the mountain. He wants to ask the two old people who play chess, what the hell is going on.
When Uber arrived on the mountain, he searched all over the mountain, and he didn't find the two old people who played chess and the giant acacia tree. The more he thought about it, the saddest he felt: his wife was gone, his son was gone, and no one in the world was close to him, and there was no one he knew. He couldn't help but lie where he used to play chess and cry
Come on. He cried from dark to dawn, and from dawn to dark, I don't know how many days he cried. The tears that fell through the stones underfoot formed a cave. When Uber was tired of crying, he fell asleep. While he was asleep, he heard an old man pat him on the shoulder and said, “Don't cry, kid. If you don't shed your tears to the East China Sea, this place will have long since become a vast ocean. When you watch chess, dark clouds are rain, white clouds are snow, and thick clouds are wind. The ground is yellow and green; that changes from season to season. This is your knife. Go down the mountain and do something good for humanity!” He woke up abruptly and there was nothing. Looking around, I found his knife. Although the knife was original, it had a golden glow and was extremely sharp.
Uber followed the old man's orders and went down the mountain. He lived in Longshan for many years and did a lot of good deeds against hegemony. Later, Emperor Jade recalled him to Tianting. In memory of him, people called the cave “Laoyou Cave.”
