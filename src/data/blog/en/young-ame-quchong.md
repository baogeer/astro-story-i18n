 Once upon a time, there was a young horse herder in the Aramco region, named Ami Qu Pou. He has kind parents and a newlywed wife, and is living a happy and full life. On the third day after getting married, the young man went out to herd horses. The young wife helped him prepare all kinds of food, took him out of the house, and sang:
My husband, listen to it!
Drive the stallion to the left slope,
Move the mare to the right,
A happy little horse,
Arrived at the sun-drenched mountain bed.
You need to wear a white and Tibetan robe.
Don't let go of your flower boots;
Pastoral whipping rice gudo (Gumigudo: the name of a shepherd's whip.) Huh,
Keep it close to your waist and can't be lost.
Don't sleep in a haystack,
Don't joke with the rubble,
Don't joke with crawling dogs,
Don't make fun of wind and thatch,
You are exactly twenty-five this year. (25: Tibetan custom, 25 for men and 21 for women are the “year of robbery”; it is easy to be harmed by ghosts.)
It was a miracle to meet a demon.
The young man came to the riverside, scattered his horse group, forgot his wife's instructions, and slept on a haystack. He dreamed that from Sanye Temple (Sanye Temple: In the past, in Tibet, people's lives and deaths were controlled by Sanye Temple.) Three Iron Men came, carrying gas pockets on their backs and a life trap in their hands, and said to him, “Ani Baigu, the goddess of Sanya-ji Temple, please take a trip.”
When the young man woke up, he had no strength all over his body, walked back and forth, and rushed back home. Only an air about the size of a pony tail came in and out of his throat. His father was sad, and so was his mother. His wife, who had just gotten married, cried to death. The young man said to Grandpa:
Please listen to it.
My strict father and father!
Your son wants to eat some meat,
Please cut the meat into small pieces,
Please boil the meat to a rotten state.
Grandpa quickly went to cut the meat. He sings to Grandma again:
Please listen to it.
My dear mother and grandmother!
Your son wants a cup of tea,
Please make the tea strong,
Please make the tea fragrant.
Grandma quickly went to get tea. He turned his face and sang to his wife:
Please listen to it.
My beautiful wife!
Your husband wants to see the flowers,
Please go pick a halo flower,
Please go pick a bunch of Gesang flowers.
Grandma was carrying meat, Grandma was carrying tea, and when the wife came in with flowers, the young man had already lost his breath. His soul left his hometown and wandered towards Sanya-ji Temple. On the way, he met a friend and returned from Sanya-ji Temple. He told the young man, “Friend, when you arrived at Sanye Temple, the goddess Ani Baigu told you to eat human flesh. You must not eat it; if you drink human blood, you must never drink; if you wear human skin, you must never wear it; if you do, you can restore your soul.”
The young soul came to Sanya-ji Temple and walked through the first tall and powerful temple gate,” the Iron Man guarding both sides sang in unison:
Young people in Ami listen:
When entering the temple gate,
You have to make three swords,
You have to knock three heads!
The young man entered the first door and then the second door. While entering the third door, the goddess Anibaigu came out. The young man asked her:
Please listen to it.
Bai Gu Sang Yeani!
Please let me go back,
I have a newly married wife,
I have elderly parents.”
Ani Baigu ignored the young man's request and ordered the kid to take out human flesh, human blood, and skin and sing:
Please listen to it.
Young people from the Ami region!
You must be hungry on the way,
Eat a little bit of human meat quickly;
You must be thirsty on the way,
Drink a little bit of human blood;
It must be cold on your way,
Put on a human skin.
Remembering his friend's advice, the young man quickly answered:
I haven't eaten human meat in the past,
I don't want to eat it now;
I've never drank human blood in the past,
I don't want to drink it now;
I haven't worn human leather in the past,
I don't want to wear it now either.
Ani Baigu got angry; threw down a soul bag and a life rope; one held an oxtail and ordered him to collect people's souls.
The young man brought three things back to his hometown and was meeting men, women, and children basking in the sun at the head of the village. He came to the old man's heap. The old people were sitting under the wall, waving a warp in their hands. One of them sighed and said, “Alas! Good people like Amequ are poor and leave when they are young. Why in the world keep our old bones?” The other old people listened and shook their heads and sighed.
The young man couldn't bear to take one of them, so he went to the youth heap; the young people were playing a game of throwing horns, and suddenly a young man said, “Alas! We had so much fun together, but unfortunately Wrangler's songs are running out.” When everyone finished listening, they all split up and had no intention of playing games
When the young man couldn't bear to take one of them away, he moved to the girls; the girls were kicking shuttlecocks, giggling, and hopping, without the slightest sense of sentimentality. There just happened to be a little girl who said, “Ah, the young Wrangler died. His wife is alone and so pathetic. Let's go see her!”
The girls have such a kind heart, and the young are even more intolerable. He wandered around the world and couldn't find a suitable person. Suddenly, he saw a dead dog and got rid of the dog's corpse. Ani Baigu said, “No!” He got another dead yak. Ani Baigu was very unhappy and sang:
Please listen to it,
Young people from the Ami region!
If you want to restore your soul,
Find a replacement first.
The height is about the same as you,
About the same age as you,
Your appearance is about the same as yours.
Found it, you can go home
I can't find you. I have to go with me.
Young people have no choice but to be searched everywhere on earth. Once, he came to the city of Lhasa and walked into the Gaxia government (Gaxia government: the local government of the old Tibet.) , I saw Duoren sitting on a tall cushion (Duoren: One of the greatest aristocrats in Tibet.) Little young man, he looked beautiful. Seeing that he was highly educated, he walked over and asked, “Young man! “Do you dare to enter the three major gates of Sanye Temple?” Grandpa Duoren said with full enthusiasm, “No matter how tall the temple gate is, it's not as tall as the Gaxia government's government; no matter how big the temple gate is, it's not as big as the Gaxia government's government; what are the three small gates of Sanye Temple?!”
The young man led Little Master Tani to Sanya-ji Temple. Walking through the first door, the little young man's face went blank; when he walked into the second door, the little young man shook his knees; when he walked through the third door, the little young man kept on shaking, saying, “The temple gate of Sanye Temple is much taller, bigger, and scarier than the government gate of Gaxia!” Ani Baigu walked out laughing and told him to eat human flesh; he ate it; told him to drink human blood, he drank; told him to wear human skin; he dressed obediently.
Since then, Master Daren has stayed with Ani Baigu. The young man went back to Ami and was reunited with his parents and wife.
Narration: Nimapenduo, Chengguanzhen, Shigatse
Note: In Zhalang County in the Shannan region of Tibet, there is a Sangdeng Temple. It was built in the 8th century AD during the Akamatsu Dezan period. It is the first temple in Tibet. It was a goddess in charge of life and death under King Baigal.
The name of this story is “The Story of Ami Qu Qu”. Ami is a place name, and Qu Qu is a god and a man. Therefore, we think it's almost a legend about the origin of the god Ami and Han.
