 Author: Hong Zhu
There is an adverb called “Yu Mei Ren” in Song lyrics. Li Hou once filled in the famous “When is the spring flower autumn moon” based on this. The so-called Yu Meiren is presumably referring to Yu Ji, the “little honey” of Xiang Yu, the king of Western Chu. Later, one type of flower was also named poppy. Whether it's reading the lyrics or enjoying the flowers, I can feel the memory of going through a thousand years of autumn.
Yu Ji has only been featured in history books (as one of the supporting actresses of the Chu-Han War), yet she has always lived very fully in the memories of the people. After all, she stained her hair with a hint of fragrance in that bloody battle between the sexes.
On the inclined balance, Yu Ji was unfortunately on the loser's side, but she was still an indispensable weight.
The phrase “What if Yu Xi Yu Xi Nai” made Xiang Yu's “Song of the Gai Xia” even more bleak. Is this his confession about beauty, about fate?
How can a little woman answer the huge confusion men face?
To comfort the hero in her mind, she had no choice but to endure her grief and dance, then pull her sword and fend for herself.
Yu Jina's final dance formed “Death of a Swan” in the Eastern style. She dances on a blade. Oh, the heartbreaking dancer never seemed to stop spinning. In the wind, under the banner of the Overlord, in front of the stage and behind the scenes... she spread her majestic beauty to everyone, including me tonight.
Yu Ji showed both the fragility of women and the strength of women to the extreme.
It can be said that when Yu Ji shattered like a hanging vase, Xiang Yu lost. Overlord lost his domination. His last fight, stained in battle, was nothing but a sleepy beast fighting. How sad is it that you can do your best, but you can't even protect your beloved vase?
Yu Ji's suicide was more or less to relieve Xiang Yu of shame. Even the beautiful people couldn't bear to witness his defeat.
Xiang Yu first said goodbye to Yu Ji, then said goodbye to Wu Ma. These are his two greatest loves. His world was left with very little left. Poorer than even any man.
However, Yu Ji herself is not a vase belonging to men only for viewing. She also has a fierce soul.
If the image of Overlord is a landscape with ink splashed on it, Yu Ji's death is a private chapter stained with blood left in an empty space.
Her gentleness dwarfs even the power of the Overlord. Yu Ji died in the bosom's arms. Overlord, on the other hand, has no place to die; he can only sigh: “It's not good for war; it's heaven's death for me too.” Men can't overcome fate in the end; just as women always surrender to love. Yu Ji died, and so did Overlord. All who survived were those who confessed defeat. Which is more hopeless, death or life?
It is said that before her death, Yu Ji became a tearful person and sang in tears: “Han soldiers are already quiet and singing all over. The king ran out of energy; how could a poor concubine talk about her life?” Are there any other options?
She can only lead the neck and become a victim of love.
Unfortunately, even her death couldn't inspire Xiang Yu's strength.
A beautiful woman's tears, a hero's blood, eventually mixed into a cocktail. It seems that fate can really be called a skilled bartender.
The name of this glass of wine is called Tragedy.
Yu Ji was born and died for a tragedy. She's such an unlucky beauty. She held out her hand to the sword and pointed the sword at herself. What gave her the last courage?
There are definitely more people who sympathize with Yu Ji than with Overlord. There's no one more innocent than her, and no one more helpless than her. She loved Overlord, but it wasn't her fault. Of course, if Yu Ji hadn't died, we wouldn't be able to realize the intensity of this love.
The most impressive thing about the ten-year war between Chu and Han was not the hate between Liu Bang and Xiang Yu, but the love between Yu Ji and Xiang Yu. Women's love makes men's fights pale and empty.
