 Yuan Mei was the “master of a generation of poets” during the Qianjia period, and the standard-bearer of the “sexual spirituality” in the poetry industry. His nearly 600,000-word book “Suiyuan Poetry Tales,” in which the aesthetic ideas and creative ideas of poetry are the words of a family, enough for future generations to study. “Poets of poetry, discerning syntax, preparing for the past and present, memorizing old and moral records, and mistakes” (“Xu Yanzhou's Poems”). After the Tang and Song dynasties, poetry writing flourished unprecedentedly, and poetic appreciative texts were also popular. By the Qing dynasty, they were already very popular. “Sui Yuen Poetry” uses essays and handwriting to demonstrate and highlight the aesthetic view of “spiritual speech” while selecting poems by poets from all walks of life. At the same time, it reflects the vision and courage of Yuan Mei, a connoisseur, poetician, editor, and publisher.
As a famous and talented poet who has spent time compiling poems, Yuan Mei is very particular about how and what poems to choose. In “Suiyuan's Poetry,” he said, “If you use talent, you must be broad and strict... try the slogan: 'If you want to use talent, you have to be crunchy; as long as it tastes sour and salty, it is fresh. '” (Vol. 7, 32) Hundreds of people from Hangzhou, Nanjing and other places presented their psalms at the time of compiling “Sui Yuen Poetry,” in order to be selected. They were satisfied even if they only took one or two sentences. Yuan Mei did not take poems from people, but rather looked at whether the poems themselves were “crisp” and “fresh” enough. Someone once asked to be selected for a poem by a “giant gong”. Yuan Meiyue was drowsy, then bluntly said, “The poem is very old and very hard; however, there is nothing wrong with it; there is nothing wrong with stabbing; there is no choice to choose from; there is nothing to choose from; there is nothing to pick.” (Supplement Vol. 2, 72) A certain Taishi, who “got along” with Yuan Mei, sent a collection of more than 40 volumes of poetry. Yuan Mei “suffered too much, and turned it over to Corporal Zhou Wutang.” After reviewing the title of the movie, Zhou Wutang said, “Why bother with the old lyricist scene, every chapter is being remodeled. Thirty thousand dendrobium is difficult to test.” Yuan Mei laughed out loud when she read it. She said, “Relax in the summer, read people's poems. Picking a poem is like choosing a color, and I always find it difficult to get moved.” (Supplementary Book 1 of 53)
Yuan Mei pointed out that “there are seven diseases of choosing the poetry of people close to one's family”, especially the seventh disease of “favoring oneself and listening to the requests of others”. “Remaining in “Poetry” is also unavoidable” (vol. 14 bis). However, most of the poems or excerpts selected for “Sui Yuen Poets” came from Poets of the Poor, and even quotes from many young girls and peddlers. This was extremely rare in the literary world at the time, and it also coincided with Yuan Mei's view of poetry as “the standard-bearer of the 'sexual spiritual' in the poetry world” and the original intention of compiling the poem. Yuan Mei has never been shy about saying “her favorite works of romance”, “Every time you go to the Soviet Union or Hangzhou, you must return with poetry; if you have sex, you grow old and grow old”. Various parties have submitted good quotes, and he “has many excerpts.” Of course, the selected verses, whether they are contextual, or evocative, must be unconventional. Compared to some famous figures, Yuan Mei praised “poor people have excellent poetry”, such as Chen Guyu's “The Rain, Dusky Alley, No Flame, the Wind Goes Through the Walls of the Poor Family” and “Accidental Poetry Gets Too Much to Eat”, Yang Sili's “Poor Family Makes My Wife Annoyed, Sick and Leisure Causes Mother's Sadness”, Zhu Caoyi's “Bedtime Burns the Night, Every Time I Borrow a Monk's Couch, My Wife Always Goes to Her Mother's Home”, Xu Lan's “Poor Girl Wears Clothes, Weeks Cry” etc., true feelings flow, showing a sense of poverty and distress (vol. 3-11).
Partnering with the author because she appreciates her poetry is also an act that Yuan Mei is accustomed to. One year, he asked someone to recommend Huang Sheng, a book copywriter. This person is really “Park Ye.” Once, Yuan Mei passed by Huang Sheng's desk by chance and saw him write “The Monk Who Broke the Temple Sells Street Tiles, the Dujing People Strive for the Evening Spring,” and was amazed. Immediately, he was rewarded with five buckets of rice. Since then, Huang Sheng, who received the reward, has worked even harder to write poems. There are quite a few famous quotes from Huang Sheng in “Follow the Garden Poetry”, such as “The Sun Goes Straight, the Rain Falls Round”, “Use the remnants of the pen together, and the ink is polished at the same time”, “The mud is still green in the old place, and the water is also fragrant when the flowers fall”, “The old one always stores water at leisure, and it is difficult to make up for a broken wall” (vol. 5 of 5).
Yuan Mei once expressed emotion: “Picking poetry is like throwing money away; it's better to go to waste. However, those who have not written their poems would rather lose the flood. Those who have already written the manuscript may as well leave it behind.” (Supplement Vol. 8, 25) When it comes to selecting poems, he always maintains an open attitude. While adhering to the standard of “winning by quality,” he also favors poets who have no chance to gather his own poems as much as possible. Coupled with decades of communication, style, and writing, he realized that “poetry often comes out with freaks and poor workers” (footnote 10 no. 38), so he selected a large number of poems from famous talents and even people at the bottom of society, such as “The Man Who Stitches the Ruins” (Tailor) Wu Kun's “Little Rain, Cloudy, and Stone Moss.” “Wandering around and sweeping the flowers, the flowers can't finish sweeping the rain”, “The bamboo bed makes fresh, fresh straw, the cloth doesn't warm the old cotton”; Han Ximen Yuan sells gluten, and his “Yongxue and Dongpo” has the phrase “Six strange flowers are difficult to embroider; where are beautiful women at the tip of the needle”. Yuan Mei believes that although they are “all poor workers, their poems are quite interesting” (Supplementary Book 8 32). In “Sui Yuen's Poetry” vol. 8, Yuan Mei wrote, “There are barrelmakers who are old; their son freezes all the time. Children and grandchildren are born again. The elderly love their grandchildren and always hold them in their arms. People laugh insanely. The old man Yin Yun: “I remember raising my son back then, and now my son is raising grandchildren. I'm hungry, I'm hungry because he's hungry, don't let my grandchildren starve me! '” It can be seen that this poem is profoundly intentional. He also wrote The Hometown Vendor (hawker). Even though he wasn't very literate, he learned the lyrics and wrote the crying mother Shiyun: “Cry once, cry, mother is used to hearing the sound of a child. How can a mother not do it?” Yuan Mei commented: “Even though the words are ridiculous, the person who hears it is moving.”
Yuan Mei chose poetry. Apart from seeking temperament, he gave special praise for incorporating household words and spoken words into his poems. As he said, “Familiar language is best put into poetry,” and for this reason, he selected the poem “A building is protected by its own red clouds, so why should a flower be protected by green leaves”, and the poem in Xu Guanshi's “The Concubine”, “Be kind, and let go of grievances; it's unhelpful and hurtful”. They are plain and easy to understand, and easy to sing and spread. As for the rhetoric, “It's the sound of heaven,” Yuan Mei cited Sun Songfen's “Winter Warm”: “When the grass marks return to Biliu and Shu Ya, I think the age order is bad. Unfortunately, the cold weather stopped again, otherwise the little peach blossoms would bloom all over.” “Bamboo Branch” by Huang Jiaomen: “I chose Liang Chen to go on a youth outing and invited my female partner to the fullest. I was worried and afraid of the coming rain; the east wind listened overnight.” There are also “Master doesn't follow style, good poetry only talks about eye feelings”, “No sycamore trees are planted before the steps, where does a leaf wind fly from”, and “Greedy for cool windows, autumn bugs fly on the reading lamp”, etc. These verses are all in plain language, but they are full of charm.
“Suiyuan Poetry” has emphasized many times that “poetry has natural beauty” and “fairy tales are always true words,” and also affirms children's poems. One day, Yuan Mei saw two children flying a kite. One child was overjoyed. The other Tong Yongshi said, “Please don't be surprised if the east wind is good; you can blow it down even if you blow it up.” Yuan Mei was “deeply pleased” after hearing it (Supplementary Book 7 2). Another boy scoffed at his teacher Yun: “Praise is a big trick, and they are afraid every day of corruption.” It has the style of Li Bai's “Mocking Lu Ru” (Supplementary Book 6 of 8). Yuan Mei believes that “poets are also those who don't lose their heart.” Good poems are often “wonderful in children's language,” and admits that “village children, mu shou, laugh at every word, they are all my teachers, and all good works are good sentences.” He once wrote of a dung bearer who accompanied the garden. One day, he reported under a plum tree: “It's enough to have flowers!” After hearing it, Yuan Mei was suddenly inspired, and went on to say, “Moon reflects bamboo with a thousand characters, and Shuang Gao Mei is pregnant.” (Vol. 2 4)
It's certainly not easy to achieve equality for all when choosing poems, but Yuan Mei knows the principle that “gentlemen don't use nonsense.” As a highly appreciative poet, he greatly appreciated the poems of Yan Song, Ruan Dacheng, and others, and selected some of their best poems to evaluate, even though they were notorious traitors of a generation. In volume 7, 52 of “With Garden Poetry: Addendum”, he said that Yan Song's “Qianshantang Collection” was “quite impressive, such as 'the curtain shook the mountain fog in, and the village chief heard the waterfowl cry 'the color of the sand, the yellow pine smoke in the waterside tower', they are all cute, too.” He also said Ruan Dacheng's poem “'Cool gathers bug language, good style settles the fireflies, 'the last five characters are quite patient.”
All in all, “Suiyuan Poetry Tales” has been widely read. The selected poems come from thousands of families inside and outside the poetry industry. They don't compromise on names, regardless of whether others are famous or not. Despite singing and writing works, it also depends on whether they can be read and whether they are durable to read. The selected poems may have different levels of character and temperament. Perhaps the lyric narrative has no deep meaning, but as long as it is interesting to read, it is enough. You need to know that what Yuan Mei presented to the world was poetry with her personal taste and value proposition. Poetry needed to be elegant and clear, and also vivid, and highlight the three characteristics of temperament, personality, and poetic talent. This is an essential element in poetry writing, and it is also the focus of “spiritual speech.” (Que Weihang)
