 Yue Fei (1103-1141), Southern Song military man, national hero. Zi Pengju is from Tangyin (now Henan) in Xiangzhou. In a short time, he was diligent and studious, and developed a good martial art. When I was 19, I joined the army to fight against Liao. Soon after, due to the death of his father, he retired from the military and returned to his hometown. In 1126, the Jin Soldier invaded the Central Plains in a big way, and Yue Fei joined the army again, beginning his career fighting against the Golden Army and defending his family. Legend has it that when Yue Fei left, his mother, the Yao clan, stabbed the four characters “serve the country faithfully” on his back, which became the creed that Yue Fei followed throughout his life.
After Yue Fei joined the army, he was soon promoted to Yoshirang due to his bravery. At this point, Song Kaifeng was besieged by the Jin Army. Yue Fei accompanied Deputy Marshal Zong Ze to the rescue. He defeated Jin Jun many times, and was praised by Zong Ze for his “wisdom and bravery; Gu Liang will not be able to live past it.” In the same year, the Jin Army invaded Kaifeng, captured Emperors Hui and Qin, and the Northern Song dynasty came to an end. In May of the second year of Jingkang, King Kang and Zhao ascended the throne to establish the Southern Song Dynasty in order to move the capital to Lin'an and establish Southern Song. Yue Fei wrote to Gao Zong to recover lost ground and was dismissed. Yue Fei then transferred to the Hebei Metropolis and was commanded by the Chinese army. He fought against the Golden Army in the Taihang Mountain area, and achieved many successful battles. Later, he returned to Tokyo to stay in Munesawa and transferred to Takerō for his military exploits. After Munezawa's death, he succeeded Du Chong, who stayed behind in Tokyo, to keep Kaifeng.
In the 3rd year of Jianyan (1129), General Jin led Wu Shu to invade the South again. Du Chong led his army to abandon Kaifeng and fled south, and Yue Fei had no choice but to go south. In the fall of the year, Wu Shu continued to invade the South, and Du Chong, who was replaced by Jiankang (present-day Nanjing, Jiangsu), left behind, surrendered without a fight. The Jin Army was able to get through the Yangtze River and soon conquered places such as Lin'an, Yuezhou (present-day Shaoxing), and Mingzhou. Gao Zong was forced to go into exile at sea. Yue Fei led an army and insisted on fighting behind enemy lines. He first attacked the Golden Army defender in Guangde and won six battles. Also, when the Golden Army attacked Changzhou, they led support and won four battles. The following year, Yue Fei ambushed Mount Niutou, broke the Golden Wushu, recovered Jiankang, and the Jin army was forced to retreat north. Since then, Yue Feiwei's fame has spread throughout the north and south of the river, shaking Heshuo. In July, Yue Fei was promoted to the post of governor of Tongzhou and the governor of Taizhou. He has more than 10,000 people and established the “Yue Jiajun”, a strong brigade with strict discipline and brave combat.
Three years in Shaoxing, Yue Fei won the “Loyal Yue Fei” pennant for defeating “military thieves” such as Li Cheng and Zhang Yong. In April of the following year, Yue Fei headed north to defeat the Golden Puppet Pseudo-Qi Army and take back six counties including Xiangyang and Xinyang. Yue Fei was also promoted to the rank of envoy of the Qingyuan Army for merit. In December of the same year, Yue Fei defeated Jin Bing again in Luzhou (present-day Hefei, Anhui), and Jin Bing was forced to return to the North. In the 5th year of Shaoxing (1135), Yue Fei led an army to suppress Yang Mo's rebellion and recruited 50,000 to 60,000 elite soldiers, greatly increasing the strength of the “Yue Family Army.”
In the sixth year of Shaoxing, Yue Fei once again marched north, capturing Yiyang, Luoyang, Shangzhou, and Guozhou, then besieging the Chen and Cai regions. However, Yue Fei soon discovered that he was an isolated army, with no reinforcements or food, and had to withdraw to Ezhou (present-day Wuchang, Hubei). In this northern expedition, Yue Fei's ambitions were unsuccessful, and he wrote the famous phrase “Manjianghong”, which has been sung throughout the ages:
Furious to the crown. At the back of the fence, take a break in the rain.
Looking up, shouting loudly and violently.
Thirty feudal feats of dust and earth, eight thousand miles of road clouds and the moon.
Don't wait idle. Blank the boy's head; empty and sad!
Shame on Jing Kang; it hasn't snowed yet.
When will the courtier's hate be destroyed?
Set up a long car and break through Helan Mountain! The champion was hungry for meat and laughed and was thirsty to drink the blood of the Huns.
Wait until the beginning, clean up the old mountains and rivers, and go to heaven!
In the 7th year of Shaoxing, Yue Fei was promoted to First Lieutenant. He repeatedly suggested that Gao Zongxing go north to take back the Central Plains in one fell swoop, but all were rejected by Gao Zong. In the 9th year of Shaoxing (1119), Gao Zong, Qin Hui, and Jin Yihe, and the Southern Song dynasty paid tribute to Jin. This made Yue Fei irresistible. The above form called for “dismissal of military service and withdrawal from Lin Quan” as a sign of protest. The following year, Wu Shu destroyed the peace agreement and invaded the South in a big way again. Yue Fei was ordered to send troops to fight back. They conquered Zhengzhou, Luoyang and other places one after another, and defeated the Golden Army's elite Iron Cavalry “Iron Floating Map” and “Gaizi Horse” in Yancheng, and took advantage of the victory to occupy Zhu Xian Town. It was only 45 miles away from Kaifeng. Wu Shu was forced to retreat to Kaifeng. Jin Jun's morale was depressed. He lamented “it's easy to shake the mountains, it's difficult to shake the Yue family”, and he was afraid to go to war.
In Zhu Xian Town, Yue Fei recruited troops and bought horses, contacted the Hebei rebels, actively prepared to cross the Yellow River to recover lost ground, and directly attack Huanglong Province. He excitedly said to the generals, “Go straight to Huanglongfu and drink it with all of you!” At this point, Gao Zong and Qin Hui sought peace. They handed out 12 gold medals in a row, and ordered Yue Fei to leave the military. Yue Fei couldn't contain her inner sorrow and looked up at the sky and lamented, “Ten years of success have been destroyed once and for all! Income states and counties, all at once! It's hard to be happy with all the mess! The world of Qiankun, there is no way to repeat it!” His ambition was difficult to achieve; he had no choice but to shed tears in his class.
After Yue Fei returned to Lin'an, he was dismissed from military power and served as Deputy Privy Council Deputy. In August '11 of Shaoxing, Gao Zong and Qin Hui sent people to seek peace from Jin. Jin Wu Shu demanded that “Yue Fei must be killed before peace can be agreed upon.” Qin Hui conspired against Yue Fei and imprisoned him. On December 29, the 11th year of Shaoxing (1142), Qin Hui killed Yue Fei at the Lin'an Fengbo Pavilion on the charge of “being unnecessary”. Yue Fei was only 39 years old. His son Yue Yun and general Zhang Xian were also murdered at the same time. At the time of Ning Zong, Yue Fei was able to pass away and was banished by King E.
Yue Fei is good at planning and has strict control over the army. His army is famous for “freezing to death, not demolishing houses, starving to death, and not being carried away.” During his career in war and horse, he personally participated in and commanded 126 battles without a single defeat. He was a true victorious general. Yue Fei has no special military works left behind. His military ideas and strategies for governing the military are scattered in books, verses, etc. Later generations compiled Yue Fei's articles and poems into “Yue Wu Mu's Legacy”, also known as “The Collection of Yue Zhongwu Wang's Essays”.
