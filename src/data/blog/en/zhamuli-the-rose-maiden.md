 A Siber folk tale, spread in the Chabchar Siber Autonomous County in Xinjiang, tells the love story between Fa Lishan and the fairy Zamuli.
Hardworking and kind hair saved a white snake. White Snake's grandparents gave Fa Lishan a Zamuri flower (moonflower).
Later, Zamuri Flower became a very beautiful girl, married with Fari Shan, and lived a happy life.
Grandpa Zhang Jing wanted to take possession of Zamuri; as a result, he even carried his horse into a snowy ravine and froze to death.
