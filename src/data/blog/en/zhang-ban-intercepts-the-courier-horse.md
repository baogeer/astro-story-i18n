 During the Qianlong period of the Qing Dynasty, there was a Confucian student called Zhang Dang in Houyan Village in Chaihudian Town. He is very slutty. He loves raising birds, playing with eagles, and training hunting dogs. Every day, he carries many maids, birdhouses, hunting dogs, and drives eagles to walk and hunt birds in the wild.
One day, he came to Daguan Road in the south of Chai Hu Dian and opened the thrush cage to listen to the thrush cry. By chance, a “newspaper horse” ran from the south. When the person delivering the official letter soon saw a group of people on the way to Daguan, they shouted loudly from afar. This is a signal to run “report horses”, so that people can blink off the road and allow the “bully horse” to pass smoothly. Who knows, Zhang disguised himself and waited for the “newspaper horse” to run over. He was screaming with thrashes. He was so frightened that he fluttered around in the cage and gave his wings. Zhang was very annoyed. He immediately called the maid to stop the “newspaper horse” and beat the sergeant who handed over the official documents. Now, not only did they miss the time to hand over the emergency official documents, but they also carried out the crime of killing the head in accordance with the rules of the Great Clean.
This incident was investigated in Beijing, and Zhang Fu had to leave his house business and flee to Xuzhou Province. As a result of this lawsuit, Zhang made his family go bankrupt. Fortunately, the person responsible for investigating and handling this case is the current surname Zhong from Zhongxin Village in Chaihudian Town. He has relatives with the last name Zhang. He told the emperor that Zhang was acting crazy. After countless twists and turns, the case was settled by spending money to buy up and down government offices.
Zhang went into exile in Xuzhou and shaved off a beard from his chest in order not to be recognized by others. On the day he shaved his beard, he wrote four limericks:
Clouds are light and light, almost in the afternoon.
The beard fell to Xuzhou Province.
They will be called teenagers who are secretly studying,
When people don't know what to do, they go to great lengths.
While escaping from Xuzhou, Zhang often went to the “Iron Cow Sleeping Place” on the banks of the Yellow River to pass the day.
