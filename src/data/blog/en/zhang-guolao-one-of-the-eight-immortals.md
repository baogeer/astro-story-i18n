 One of the Eight Immortals of Taoism. Also known as Zhang Guo. According to the “Book of Tang”, there is indeed a person who was originally a folk sorcerer, and was handed down as an immortal due to folklore. I live in Nakajo Mountain in the mountains. I say that I was born in Yao, and there is a way to live forever. Tang Taizong and Tang Gaozong (Wu Zetian's husband) recruited him from time to time, but they were all graciously rejected by him. Wu Zetian also summoned him out of the mountain. Zhang Guolao pretended to die in front of the temple. It was midsummer. Soon, his body rotted and stinky. After Wu Zetian listened, he had no choice but to quit. But soon someone saw him again in the mountains of Mount Hengshan.
Tang Xuanzong summoned him several times to ask for a way to live long and not get old. Tang Xuanzong saw Zhang Guolao's old Dragon Zhong. I just asked, “Mr. is a good person. Why is his teeth slack and old? Zhang Guolao said, “At the age of decay, there were no techniques to apply, so it became what it is now. It's really shameful. But if these loose teeth are removed today, wouldn't they be able to grow new ones? ” So he removed his hair in front of the temple and shot his teeth off. Xuanzong was a little afraid and was busy asking someone to help Zhang Guo Lao to rest. After a while, Zhang Guolao returned to the palace. Sure enough, he had a fresh face and bright blue teeth. As a result, the top officials and nobles at the time all scrambled to pray and ask for tips on returning to old age, but they were all rejected by him.
Once, Tang Xuanzong went hunting and caught a big deer. This deer is slightly different from an ordinary deer. The chef was about to slay the deer. Zhang Guolao saw it and immediately stopped him, saying, “This is a fairy deer. It's over 1,000 years old. When Emperor Han Wu first hunted, I followed him. Although Emperor Han Wu caught this deer, he later released it.” Xuanzong said, “The world is big. There are a lot of deer, and times have changed. Do you know this that he is the deer you said? Zhang Guolao said, “When Emperor Wu was released, a bronze medal was used to mark it under its left corner.” So Xuanzong ordered an investigation. Sure enough, there was a two-inch bronze medal, but the handwriting was blurry. Xuanzong also asked, “In what year did Emperor Han Wu hunt? How many years has it been until now? Zhang Guolao said, “It's been 852 years now.” Tang Xuanzong ordered people to check, sure enough, it was correct.
Zhang Guolao died soon after returning to the mountain. Tang Xuanzong built a “Qixia Guan” for him. Zhang Guolao has a quirk. On weekdays, he rides a white donkey and can travel thousands of miles a day. Of course, this donkey is also a “magic donkey.” It is said that when not riding, you can fold it up and put it in a leather bag.
