 In ancient China, it was not unusual for a man to be surrounded by a group of wives and concubines. However, only a few wives and concubines live in harmony and are very humble. If you keep a man, everyone wants to pamper them a little more, so fighting for jealousy is commonplace; if you don't get it right, you can fight back and forth. Lu Jihe's wife, Cheung Suk-yee, is unique. Her husband empathized with other women. Not only did she not block them, but instead actively matched them and became a popular medium for her husband. It made the three husband and wife very happy.
Both the Lu Family and the Zhang Family are prominent households in the Funiu Mountain area of Henan. Both families had their ancestors in the Ming Dynasty. After the end of the Ming and Qing dynasties, he quit his job and returned to his hometown to live and work peacefully in Mount Funiu. The Lu family lives in Sangpingtun in Funiu Mountain. This is a fertile basin in the mountains of the Chongshan Mountains. A well-watered Sangji River flows smoothly through, and mulberry trees form forests on both sides of the river. Because the soil is rich in water, the mulberry forest does not need to be managed and is naturally lush. Inherited by ancestors, most of these mulberry groves are owned by the Lu family. Nearby farmers all rent their mulberry trees to raise silkworms and peel silk, and the Lu family earns a lot of money as a result. People here raise silkworms unlike anywhere else. Everyone keeps silkworms in bamboo plaques in the house and picks mulberry leaves to feed them, but here they keep silkworms directly on mulberry trees in the wild and let them grow. In early summer, silkworms swallow silk to make cocoons. The snow-white and crystal cocoons hang all over the branch, just like pear blossoms blooming all over the tree. The scenery has a different flavor.
Passed down to Lu Ji and this generation, the time was already at the end of Qianlong. The wind and rain were smooth, and the country was safe and secure. Lu Ji and her bookkeeper are at ease and comfortable. They don't worry about their livelihood, nor do they think about living. They are relaxed, plain, and uneventful. They live like living fairies in the mountains. At the age of starting a family, she married Shu-yi, the daughter of the Zhang family in the neighboring village, as his wife. The two families are on the same side, and it is also a social exchange. This girl, Cheung Suk-yee, looks elegant. Not only can she write poetry, but she is also good at spinning and embroidery, and has the dual talents of being a girlfriend and a small family of jasper.
After marrying Cheung Suk-yee, Lu Ji not only had an additional partner in her life, but also added a good helper as a family manager. Based on the uneven thickness of local silk, Zhang tried to weave a unique kind of “pimple silk”. This kind of silk looks potholed and uneven on the surface, but it feels soft and smooth when worn. It has a soft luster, and is thick and durable. It has a simple and natural country flavor, and has become a popular local item for a while. It is fashionable for women to wear this kind of “pimple silk” sewn dress. Zhang mobilized the Lu family's tenants to weave “pimple silk,” and the Lu family concentrated on selling it, increasing the Lu family's income by a large amount of money, and the tenants also benefited quite a bit. Zhang has quite a strategy for managing a family. After she married the Lu family to take care of the family, the Lu family became more and more prosperous. In their spare time, the young couple read and sing poems together; or take a walk in the mulberry forest to see silkworm babies lying “rustling” on mulberry leaves. Their days are as moisturized and bright as Mulberry Creek.
However, it is always difficult to have perfect things under the world. Cheung Suk-yee did a great job in the Lujiayi family, but she was also not as good as expected. That is, she never had children for five years after her marriage. In Lu Ji and this generation, the Lu family passed on from one son to another. Now that there are no descendants, how can the Lu family keep burning? “There are three types of filial piety; nothing is greater.” Cheung Suk-yee feels more and more ashamed of the love of her husband and in-laws; she only complains that her belly is uncontentious. They are all very friendly people. My daughter-in-law has been infertile for five years and has never revealed the slightest dissatisfaction. The daughter-in-law is smart and capable, respects the elders, is considerate of her husband, and has no impeccable character. Therefore, she intended to accept a concubine for her son, but she was too embarrassed to speak up. Her husband Lu Ji and Geng never felt bad for his wife; instead, they often comfort her: “As long as we are happy, it doesn't matter if we have a son or not; we can take over for one when the time comes!” No matter how forgiving her in-laws and her husband were, Cheung Suk-yee herself always had an unbreakable pimp in her heart. She wanted to convince her husband to marry a concubine, but she didn't know where to start. After all, she had to leave half of her husband's love. She inevitably felt a bit sad.
From spring to summer, it's the Duanyang Festival. It was an excellent sunny day. Cheung Suk-yee had a good discussion with her husband. In the morning, we went outside the mountain to watch the dragon boat race together. When she woke up in the morning, Cheung Suk-yee was busy arranging for people to wrap Duanyang rice dumplings in the kitchen. Lu Ji He has already stopped working and is the same as Cheung Shu-yee's personal maid Ping Ping. My maid, Ping Ping, was married to the Lu family from the Zhang family. She was only 12 or 3 years old when she arrived. She was a thin, small, smart girl. Five years have passed, and she has suddenly grown into a big girl. She is in full shape and has red cheeks. She is like a freshly ripe peach, and exudes an attractive atmosphere. She is often associated with Cheung Suk-yee. Naturally, her demeanor is also infected. Her demeanor reveals that she is a bit more sensitive and capable. She is five years younger than Cheung Suk-yee, so she is a bit more shy than her.
Cheung Suk-yee couldn't get out of the kitchen for a while. Lu Jihe got bored and suggested playing a game of straw fighting with Ping Ping. Ping Ping nodded in agreement. Straw fighting is a game that locals often play before and after Duanyang. First, tear off a large handful of slender grass, and each person holds a bunch; during the game, each person pulls a stick of grass from their opponent's hands each time, which lasts longer than who draws it, and whoever draws it wins this round. In fact, this is a simple gambling game, and the stakes vary from person to person. Lu Ji He and Ping Ping made a bet: Whoever wins will gently hit the loser's palm. Having settled the rules, Lu Jihe turned around and dropped a handful of grass under the courtyard wall and distributed it to Ping Ping in half. The two went to the stone bench in the courtyard and started the game. The two were fighting in earnest. They didn't notice that Cheung Suk-yee had left the kitchen and came behind them. Cheung Suk-yee was not shocked; she quietly stood on the sidelines and watched the battle.
There is no need to use wisdom in the game of Straw; it all depends on luck, so the chances of winning or losing are roughly the same for both parties. After watching for a while, Cheung Suk-yee saw that her husband always lost a lot and won less, which couldn't help but be a bit strange. After taking a closer look, I finally discovered the mystery. It turns out that Lu Jihe deliberately made Ping Ping Ping. After he picked the grass, he often silently cut off a small piece of grass from his hand. This is always a bit shorter than Ping Ping's grass. He then willingly reached out and asked Ping Ping to gently hit him with a small wooden board. Looking at Ping Ping's demeanor, there wasn't much joy in winning; more, he was shy. Seeing that Lu Jihe always dodged and flickered, hitting his opponent with a board was cautious, light, and light, as if carrying a kind of love and distress. Could it be that the two of them have a heart-to-heart agreement? Cheung Suk-yee was skeptical, but judging from a weekday perspective, both of their servants followed the rules and were definitely not humble people. Cheung Suk-yee felt that was quite strange, but she was silent. She pretended that she had just walked over and greeted the two of them, and then rushed to watch the dragon boat race together.
The riverside at Duanyang Festival was very lively. People who came to watch dragon boats from far and near surrounded the shore and couldn't get through. There are also vendors selling snacks such as rice dumplings and salted eggs. The three of Cheung Suk-yee's master and servants had a hard time squeezing out a gap and stood on the riverside waiting for the dragon boat race to begin. Cheung Suk-yee was in the center, and Lu Ji and Ping Ping stood next to each other. Soon after, the gongs and drums rang in unison, and several dragon boats dressed in red, red, green, and green sailed out and rushed downstream like arrows in the midst of the earth-shattering cries of the crowd. The men on the dragon boat danced their wooden paddles hard, and the ten or so individuals moved in a neat and consistent manner, which was really interesting. While watching the dragon boat, Cheung Suk-yee inadvertently retracted her gaze and glanced at her husband, only to find that his gaze was on Ping Ping, who was on the sidelines. A burning desire was hidden in her gaze. Cheung Suk-yee couldn't help but “chuckle” and sink a bit. Lu Jihe seemed to notice his wife's reaction. He hurriedly turned his gaze to the surface of the river, and unwittingly showed shame on his face.
Are the two really interested? Seems like it's still looming. Cheung Suk-yee had no intention of watching the boiling dragon boat race again. She secretly thought about her husband's delicate relationship with Ping Ping, and couldn't help but set her sights on Ping Ping, who was right next to her. This girl really came out in style. What caught her most attention was her tall breasts and round hips. I've heard people say this is an embryo suitable for pregnancy and childbearing; why not...? She thought of the secret relationship between her husband and Ping Ping. Ping Ping grew up around her, and she understood her personality and temperament. She was indeed a good girl with a sense of duty and empathy. Having married her husband as a concubine suits both of them; she can also give birth to children for the Lu family; she also had one of her own heart problems; wasn't it a trifecta of her own?
