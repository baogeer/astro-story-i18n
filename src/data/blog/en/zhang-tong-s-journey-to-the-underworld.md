 At the end of the day, there was an old Zhang man in Nanhangtou Village in Pingxing County (present-day Henan) who hunted quails for his business. Locals jokingly called him a “quail.” He is very old and has only one son. Because he is young, he is known as Zhang Tong.
One day, Zhang Tong died suddenly. Zhang Laohan and his wife felt old and unreliable. They cried to death, and hated not being able to die with their son.
On the day of burial, Zhang Laohan dug one or two feet of land, built a tomb with bricks on it, and said, “My son is still alive.” People laugh insanely, and there are also those who feel sorry for the old man.
Three days later, Zhang Laohan and his wife went to the graveyard to cry again. Overhearing the groans in the tomb, they were surprised to say, “My son is still alive!” Hurry up and remove the coffin and take my son home.
After Zhang Tong woke up, he asked for soup and porridge and said, “At the beginning, the kid was brought to Meng Shi, and the child complained to Meng Guan: 'My parents are old and unreliable. Please let me serve for the rest of the year, wait for the funeral, and die without hate. '
The Mad Officer was very compassionate and said, “Let me go back and tell your father that if you can give up quail hunting, your life can be extended.”
When his father heard him say this, he immediately burnt all his hunting tools such as nets.
