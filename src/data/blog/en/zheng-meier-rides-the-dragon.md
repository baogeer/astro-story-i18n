 In the old days, there was a person named Zheng Meier in the Bilian district of Zheng Shan. He is stout, strong, loyal, and has an extravagant personality. He also praises Xu Zhenjun, the lord of the Caves, as his master. He is a master. He has a good martial art and is also extremely talented. He can bring down dragons and tigers, and is happy to do good deeds for the people. One year, the weather was arid, and the seedlings in the fields were dried and burnt, and the farmer felt like frying in oil. People are asking for dragon rain everywhere, but to no avail.
Zheng Meier thought to herself: Dragon doesn't rain for the people; I want it to rain. He thought of a solution: prepare a very bright vegetable oil lamp, take it to the top of the mountain at night, cover it with a bucket and turn on the mask every time to make the light blink and dim, really like lightning. When the dragon saw the light flashing, he couldn't hold back, so he peeked out. It wasn't like Xiao Ke; True Dragon's electric light flashed out. Zheng Meier could see clearly. Turns out the dragon had turned out to be as small as a bug, hiding in a bamboo shoot on a flat top. The next day, Zheng Meier went to the top of the bamboo where the dragon was hiding to catch her. When Dragon saw that Zheng Meier was ferocious, she had to flee back to her hometown, at the mouth of Zhoushan Mountain, a dragon pool in Hongyan Water. Another day passed, and Zheng Meier invited people from the village to go hunting dragons together. Zheng Meier said that she has the ability to catch dragons and can catch dragons and tie ropes. She calls dragons like buffaloes cultivating fields, so they can walk the clouds and rain in a friendly manner. They also ordered the people in the village to prepare gongs, drums, artillery, knives, and guns. When I arrived in Longtan and heard my Excalibur sound three times, you would blow the gongs and drums in unison and set off the artillery. The dragon will let me take it out, tame it, and it can walk the clouds and rain for us at any time.
If you don't enter Longtan, how can you lead a dragon? Zheng Meier bravely led the people in the village to Hongyanshui. He went to the Dragon Pool. The crowd waited for Excalibur to sound. Zheng Meier arrived in Longtan, caught the dragon, rode on the dragon's back, and prepared to tie the dragon to a rope. However, as soon as the body turned around, the Excalibur hit the stone beside Dragon Pool. The crowd heard the sound, and the gongs and drums were blazing, and the sound shocked the world. The dragon was frightened and flew out. Zheng Meier rode on the back of a dragon and was taken to the middle reaches of Xixi. At this point, the dragon stretched her head and opened her mouth. Afterwards, the dragon stormed into the Oujiang River and re-entered the East China Sea Dragon Palace. Zheng Mei-er was also taken to the East China Sea Dragon Palace.
Originally, the tide in the Oujiang River rose to Shangwu Qiaotou (that is, Wu Zhai) in Xixi, because the dragon was ridden out of Longtan by Zheng Meier. Immediately after that, heavy rain, flash floods, huge floods, mountains and sand poured down the water, filling the creek bed in the middle of Xixi, where the tide had risen, and turning it into a beach and plain. As a result, the current tide has only risen to the Korean port area under Xixi, and cannot rise to Shangwu.
