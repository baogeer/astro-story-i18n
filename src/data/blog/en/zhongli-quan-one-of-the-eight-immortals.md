 Zhong Li Quan's last name is Zhong Li, name Quan. Allegedly, he is Zhong Lizi from the Han Dynasty, so people also call him “Han Zhongli,” as if his last name is Han and his name is Zhong Li. Taoist propaganda aimed to achieve the effect that Guiwei was not as good as being a monk. Through books such as “The Legend of Immortals through the Ages” and “The Complete Biography of the Immortals,” the Taoist propaganda made up the life of a “General Tiger” for him. According to the book, Zhong Liquan is a native of Xianyang, Jingzhao (present-day Shaanxi Province). He later changed his name to Zu Dao, Gou He Zi, also known as Wang Yangzi and Mr. Yunfang. His father was a general in the Eastern Han Dynasty. His name was Zhong Lizhang, and he was a master of Yantai during his expedition to Beihu. His brother, Zhong Lijian, is a general in the middle. Zhong Liquan had strange signs when he was born. He had round and broad forehead, thick eyebrows, deep red eyes, and big cheeks. His lips and arms were long, like a three-year-old child. What's even more strange is that for six days and six nights, he was silent, didn't cry, and didn't eat. By the 7th day, he had plenty of energy to say: “I went to Zifu, famous book Yu Jing.” When he grew up, he did as many things as the scales say. His father treated him very seriously, so he named him “Kwon.” Recommended by his older brother Zhong Lijian, he became a general in the Han dynasty. He was ordered to fight against the Tufan soldiers who invaded the frontier. He won first, then defeated, and fled into the valley alone. At a time of despair, I was guided by a monk named Mr. Donghua and saw Mr. Donghua say, “Fame and wealth are always fuzzy. Wars are all about luck. I have seen how many permanent owners of rivers and mountains have, and what are the constants of wealth? In a blink of an eye, the strange shape made me feel like I was dreaming about Huangliang. Why should generals struggle with fame and fortune?” As I said, Zhong Liquan understood it in her heart. When she went home and went into the mountains to practice with her brother, she finally became a fairy. After becoming an immortal, Zhong Liquan was named a “real person of Taiji Zuogong” by Emperor Jade. After that, he went back to Jin and became a frontier general.
