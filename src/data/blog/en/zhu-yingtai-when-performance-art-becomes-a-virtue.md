Zhu Yingtai: The story of Liang Zhu is one of the four major folklore legends in China, and is known as the “Romeo and Juliet” of the East. This story has been recorded since at least the Tang Dynasty. By the Yuan and Ming dynasties, many of Liang Zhu's stories had already entered dramas. One of the earlier editions was the Ming Dynasty drama “The Chronicles of the Alumni”. At the time of the Eastern Jin Dynasty, Zhu Yingtai, a young girl from Futai, was eager to study. She dressed up as a man, went to Hangzhou to study, and formed an indelible relationship with Liang Shanbo for three years. The alliance between Yingtai and Shanbushan promised a lifelong private contract. Who knows if Mr. Wai Yuanwai assigned Yingtai's license to Ma Jiagong a long time ago. When Shanbo became depressed and fell ill, England and Taiwan were forced to get married, passed through Shanbo's graveyard, took a leap forward, and threw themselves into the tomb, and both turned into butterflies. There are not only operas and songs, but also outstanding piano concertos.
A brain teaser: “What happened after Liang Shanbo and Zhu Yingtai Huadie?” The answer was, “There were a bunch of green bugs.”
Usually, a man, Ma Wencai, is added to the remake. Actually, nothing happened to him; what interfered with the love between the two was the gatehouse system at the time of the two. Gatekeeper societies place great value on the family's social status and marriage relationships; they can only marry people of the same status. At the time, in order to adapt to the warrior gate system, a new category of historical studies, genealogy, came into being. It distinguishes between their last names and their bad names. Under the doorbell system, they appoint government officials, regardless of their abilities, but only ask for advice. Simply put, Zhu Yingtai's dad is from a family. If he marries his daughter from a family member, their family will be very unlucky, and they won't want to look up for the rest of their lives.
Xuanxue had a lot of fun, and Dong Jin started playing performance arts again, such as Fu Shi. Actually, it's Jinshi elixir. The exact ingredients are yet to be tested. However, this thing has two characteristics: one is that it is expensive, and only large families can afford it; the second is that it causes fever. Once you have a fever, your cheeks turn red. The hot flashes are unstoppable, and pretend to be crazy. Some people who are not aristocrats also say that I am feverish. Once, someone lay down in front of a vegetable market and pretended to have a fever. Even if his companion didn't believe him, he could accept the “stone.” This person had no choice but to deftly argue, “I bought rice yesterday at the vegetable market. There was a stone in the rice. After eating it, I had a seizure today.” Some famous people drank too much, some famous people pretended to be crazy, some famous people were naked and didn't wear pants, and some famous people not only didn't accept the grief for three years, but instead didn't shed a single tear. They are all throwing drugs and getting into performance art.
Zhu Yingtai met Liang Shanbo in this situation where physical expression of art is popular. Zhu Yingtai was studying at an art college at the time. He was doing a hard art job. He was dressed as a man, mixed up with his male classmates, and became an iron buddy with Liang Shanbo. Here, another person doesn't understand: is this possible? If it were today, of course not, but the two Jin dynasties were different. He Yan's face was like Fu Fan, and Wang Yifu's hands were white jade. Pan Anren, Xia Hou Zhan, Pei Linggong, Wei Wei, and Wang Rong were all super beautiful. “Looking like a good woman” was the standard for beautiful men back then. So, the male version of Zhu Yingtai is just a relatively beautiful one. Liang Shanbo then formed a brotherhood with Zhu Yingtai and rushed Zhu Yingtai's spring heart, which had already been stirred up.
He still promoted his personality during the two Jin dynasties. Zhu Yingtai successfully obtained his degree. The story of “We fell out of love together when we graduated” ended up like this: Liang Shanbo and Zhu Yingtai began to say goodbye at the Long Pavilion. Zhu Yingtai has always wanted to confess that she is a woman, from yin and yang to the five elements, from constellations to Ziwei's number. However, they only thought she was a brother, and the “Eighteen Wishes for Everyone” drama had no choice but to break up. Zhu Yingtai had no choice but to get married.
By the time Master Liang Shanshan had completely repented and followed up with his family to propose marriage, Zhu Yingtai had already been married to Ma Wencai, who was quite famous. Liang Shanbo was so sad that he vomited blood. ——After all, it was still a small income. I took too much stone. I couldn't lift it in one go, and I died at a young age.
The end of the love incident is martyrdom. If there is no way out of love, public opinion encourages everyone to be martyred. This is a tradition. Having been hit by this, Zhu Yingtai pretended to promise to marry Ma Wencai. On the way to the wedding, he went to Nanshan Mountain to lay the grave for Liangshan, and was also planted in the tomb. When affectionate comes to this point, life can die, and death can lead to life. There's nothing we can do about love. Later generations will say that Liang Zhuhua is a butterfly.
The story's storyline is a bit confusing here. The truth couldn't be so bizarre. Liang Zhu discovered an ancient tomb underground. He lived comfortably underground, raising children, studying martial arts, making ancient tombs, and by the way, doing some carving art. Their descendant was Lin Chaoying, the founder of the Ancient Tomb School.
